Definition 1 [α΄ · Def. I]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. folio:: Menge 2
   :page: 2

.. enunciation::

   Spaces, lines, and angles are called given in magnitude to which we can procure equals.

*Category*: **Magnitude (μεγέθει)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenMagnitude(X) := exists constructible X' with X' = X``
