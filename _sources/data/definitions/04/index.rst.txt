Definition 4 [δ΄ · Def. IV (cf. Def. A)]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Points, lines, and angles are said to be given in position which always occupy the same place.

*Category*: **Position (θέσει)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenPosition(X) := X is invariant in the fixed planar reference frame (and constructible)``
