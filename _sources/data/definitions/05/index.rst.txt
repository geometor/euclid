Definition 5 [ε΄ · Def. V]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A circle is said to be given in magnitude whose radius (straight line from the centre) is given in magnitude.

*Category*: **Circle in Magnitude** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenCircleMag(C) := GivenMagnitude(radius(C))``
