Definition 6 [ϛ΄ · Def. VI]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A circle is said to be given in position and in magnitude whose centre is given in position and whose radius is given in magnitude.

*Category*: **Circle in Position & Magnitude** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenCirclePosMag(C) := GivenPosition(center(C)) and GivenMagnitude(radius(C))``
