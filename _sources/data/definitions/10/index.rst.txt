Definition 10 [ι΄ · Def. X]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A magnitude is less than a magnitude by a given magnitude when, the given magnitude being added, the whole is equal to that same magnitude.

*Category*: **Affine Defect (δοθέντι ἔλασσον)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``LessByGiven(A, B) := exists GivenMagnitude(D) with A + D = B``
