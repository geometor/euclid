Definition 11 [ια΄ · Omitted (expanded verbally in Props. XIV–XXVII)]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A magnitude is greater than a magnitude by a given magnitude than in ratio when, the given magnitude being subtracted, the remainder has a given ratio to that same magnitude.

*Category*: **Affine-Ratio Excess (δοθέντι μεῖζον ἢ ἐν λόγῳ)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GreaterThanInRatio(A, B) := exists GivenMagnitude(D), GivenRatio(k) with (A - D) : B = k``
