Definition 13 [ιγ΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)] · *[Bracketed Post-Euclidean Scholium]*
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   [A dropped line (katēgmenē) is the straight line drawn from a given point onto a straight line given in position in a given angle.

*Category*: **Directed Ordinate [Bracketed Scholium]** · *Functional Predicate*: ``Kategmene(P, L, theta) := segment from GivenPoint(P) to GivenLine(L) at GivenAngle(theta)``
