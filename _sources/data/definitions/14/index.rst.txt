Definition 14 [ιδ΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)] · *[Bracketed Post-Euclidean Scholium]*
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   An erected line (anēgmenē) is the straight line drawn from a given point upon a straight line given in position in a given angle.

*Category*: **Erected Line [Bracketed Scholium]** · *Functional Predicate*: ``Anegmene(P, L, theta) := ray from GivenPoint(P) on GivenLine(L) at GivenAngle(theta)``
