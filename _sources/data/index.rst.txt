====
Data
====

.. rubric:: *Δεδομένα* — Bilingual Critical Edition (Menge 1896 & Simson 1806)

.. container:: title-page

   | **Author:** :person:`Euclid of Alexandria <euclid>` (fl. c. 300 BCE)
   | **Protheoria:** :person:`Marinus of Neapolis <marinus>` (c. 440 – c. 495 CE)
   | **Critical Greek Edition:** :person:`Heinrich Menge <menge>` (*Euclidis Opera Omnia*, Vol. VI, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1896)
   | **Critical Reconstruction:** :person:`Robert Simson <simson>` (Glasgow 1762 / London 1806)

Composed by :person:`Euclid of Alexandria <euclid>` alongside the thirteen books of the :doc:`Elements <../heath/index>`, the **Data** (*Δεδομένα*, *Dedomena*) is the foundational grammar of Greek geometric analysis: given certain magnitudes, ratios, angles, or loci in hypothesis, what further geometric entities are thereby uniquely determined (:term:`πόριμον <porimon>`)?

Functional Architecture of the *Data*
-------------------------------------

.. picture:: /_static/figures/data_08_functional_dag.svg
   :alt: Functional Dependency Architecture of Euclid's Data

Interactive architecture map: :g:`Protheoria & Definitions 1–15 <data_08_functional_dag:U0>` grounds the modes of givenness alongside :g:`Euclid's Elements I–VI <data_08_functional_dag:ELM>`. From these rise :g:`Magnitudes & Ratios (D.1–24) <data_08_functional_dag:U1>` and :g:`Position & Parallel Loci (D.25–38) <data_08_functional_dag:U2>`, converging in :g:`Triangle & Polygon Species (D.39–55) <data_08_functional_dag:U3>`, branching through :g:`Area Application & Cosine Metric (D.56–67) <data_08_functional_dag:U4>` and :g:`Proportional Figures (D.68–83) <data_08_functional_dag:U5>`, and culminating in :g:`Quadratic Systems & Circle Loci (D.84–94) <data_08_functional_dag:U6>`—powering :g:`Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge VII) <data_08_functional_dag:SYN>`.
