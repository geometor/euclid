Euclid of Alexandria
====================

*Geometer · Author of Elements, Data, Phaenomena & Optics*

Alexandrian mathematician called ὁ Στοιχειωτής ('the Writer of Elements') by Marinus and Proclus; authored both the synthetic Elements (13 books) and the analytical foundation Data (Δεδομένα).
