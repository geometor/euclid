Sir Thomas Little Heath
=======================

*Historian of Greek Mathematics & Translator of Euclid's Elements*

Author of The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements (1908/1926) and A History of Greek Mathematics (1921), whose propositional analysis of the Data bridges Menge's Greek text with Book II and VI geometric algebra.
