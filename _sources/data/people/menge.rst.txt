Heinrich Menge
==============

*German Classical Philologist · Critical Editor of Euclidis Data (1896)*

Edited Volume VI of the Teubner Euclidis Opera Omnia (1896), establishing the authoritative 94-proposition non-Theonine Greek text of Euclid's Data from Vat. gr. 190 alongside Marinus's Protheoria and the ancient scholia.
