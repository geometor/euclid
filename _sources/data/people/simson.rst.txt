Robert Simson
=============

*Professor of Mathematics at Glasgow · Critical Restorer of Euclid's Data*

Master of classical geometrical analysis who appended his reconstructed English edition of Euclid's Data to the second edition of his Elements (1762), introducing explicit fourth-proportional diorismoi, re-ordering Unit 1 and Unit 4–5 propositions, and appending new propositions by Earl Stanhope and Matthew Stewart.
