Theon of Alexandria
===================

*Alexandrian Mathematician & Editor of Euclid*

Prepared fourth-century teaching recensions of both Euclid's Elements and Euclid's Data; Menge (1896) demonstrated the superiority of the non-Theonine Vatican archetype (Vat. gr. 190, P) over the Theonine vulgate.
