.. _D.13:

Proposition 13 [ιγ΄ · Prop. XXIV]
---------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 24
   :page: 24

.. enunciation::

   If there be three magnitudes, and the first have to the second a given ratio, while the second is greater than the third by a given magnitude than in ratio, the first will also be greater than the third by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(A : B) = r, (B - F) : C = k) -> Determined((A - r*F) : C = r*k)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[V.11] <V.11>`, :just:`[V.19] <V.19>`

Let there be three magnitudes :math:`A, B, C`; let the ratio :math:`A : B` be given, and let :math:`B` be :term:`greater by a given magnitude than in ratio <dothenti-meizon-e-en-logo>` to :math:`C`. I say that :math:`A` is also greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`C`.

Since :math:`B` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`C`, subtract the given magnitude :math:`BD` from :math:`B` (writing :math:`B = BD + DC'` or dividing :math:`B` into given :math:`D` and remainder :math:`E`): specifically, in Menge's lettering, represent the three magnitudes as :math:`AB, CD, E`, where :math:`AB : CD` is given and :math:`CD` is greater by a given magnitude :math:`CF` than in ratio to :math:`E` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`). Make :math:`AG : CF = AB : CD` (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`). Since :math:`AB : CD` is given and :math:`CF` is given, :math:`AG` is given (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`). Moreover, since whole :math:`AB` is to whole :math:`CD` as subtracted :math:`AG` is to subtracted :math:`CF`, the remainder :math:`GB` is to the remainder :math:`FD` as :math:`AB` is to :math:`CD` (:just:`[V.19] <V.19>`), so :math:`GB : FD` is given. Yet :math:`FD : E` is given; therefore :math:`GB : E` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Since :math:`AG` is given, :math:`AB` is greater by the given magnitude :math:`AG` than in ratio to :math:`E` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`).
