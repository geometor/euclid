.. _D.16:

Proposition 16 [ιϛ΄ · Prop. XX]
-------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 30
   :page: 30

.. enunciation::

   If two magnitudes have a given ratio to one another, and from one of them a given magnitude be subtracted while to the other a given magnitude be added, the whole will be greater than the remainder by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(A : C) = r, Mag(d1), Mag(d2)) -> Determined(((A+d2) - (r*d1+d2)) : (C-d1) = r)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[V.19] <V.19>`

Let two magnitudes :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` have a given ratio to one another; from :math:`AB` let the given magnitude :math:`AE` be subtracted, and to :math:`CD` let the given magnitude :math:`DF` be added. I say that the remainder :math:`EB` is :term:`less by a given magnitude than in ratio <dothenti-meizon-e-en-logo>` to the whole :math:`CF`.

Since the ratio :math:`AB : CD` is given, make :math:`GB : DF = AB : CD`, adjoining :math:`GB` to :math:`AB` at :math:`B` (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`). Because :math:`DF` is given and :math:`GB : DF` is given, :math:`GB` is given (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`). Since :math:`AE` is also given, the sum :math:`AE + GB` is given (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`). Now since :math:`AB : CD = GB : DF`, the compound :math:`AG = AB + GB` is to the compound :math:`CF = CD + DF` in the given ratio :math:`AB : CD` (:just:`[V.12] <V.12>`). Yet :math:`AG = EB + (AE + GB)` exceeds the remainder :math:`EB` by the given magnitude :math:`AE + GB`. Therefore :math:`EB`, together with the given magnitude :math:`AE + GB`, has a given ratio to :math:`CF`; that is, :math:`EB` is less by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`CF` (:just:`[Data Def. 12] <D.def.12>`).
