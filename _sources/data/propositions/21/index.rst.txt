.. _D.21:

Proposition 21 [κα΄ · Omitted (cf. Prop. XVIII)]
------------------------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be two given magnitudes, and there be added to them magnitudes having a given ratio to one another, either the wholes have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater than the other by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(A), Mag(C), Ratio(x : y) = r) -> Determined(Ratio(A+x : C+y) | GreaterThanInRatio)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 14] <D.14>`, :just:`[V.18] <V.18>`

Let there be two given magnitudes :math:`AB` and :math:`CD`, and to them let magnitudes :math:`BE` and :math:`DF`, having a given ratio :math:`BE : DF` to one another, be added. I say that the wholes :math:`AE` and :math:`CF` either have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the other.

Since :math:`BE : DF` is given, and to the two magnitudes :math:`BE` and :math:`DF` having a given ratio to one another the given magnitudes :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` have been added, the wholes :math:`AE` and :math:`CF` either have a given ratio to one another or one is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the other (proved identically to :just:`[Data 14] <D.14>` by making :math:`BG : CD = BE : DF`, so :math:`BG` is given by :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`; if :math:`BG = AB` then :math:`AE : CF = BE : DF` by :just:`[V.12] <V.12>`, while if :math:`AB > BG` then :math:`AG` is given by :just:`[Data 4] <D.4>` and :math:`AE = AG + GE` exceeds :math:`GE` by given :math:`AG` with :math:`GE : CF = BE : DF`).
