Proposition 22 [κβ΄ · Prop. XI]
-------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If two magnitudes have a given ratio to some magnitude, the magnitude compounded out of both will also have a given ratio to that same magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(A : C) = r1, Ratio(B : C) = r2) -> Determined(Ratio((A + B) : C) = r1 + r2)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 6] <D.6>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[V.24] <V.24>`

Let two magnitudes :math:`A` and :math:`B` each have a given ratio to :math:`C`. I say that the compound :math:`A + B` also has a given ratio to :math:`C`.

Since the ratio :math:`A : C` is given and the ratio :math:`B : C` is given, the ratio :math:`A : B` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Therefore the compound :math:`A + B` has a given ratio to :math:`A` (:just:`[Data 6] <D.6>`). Yet :math:`A` has a given ratio to :math:`C`; *ex aequali*, the compound :math:`A + B` also has a given ratio to :math:`C` (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`).
