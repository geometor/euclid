.. _D.30:

Proposition 30 [λ΄ · Prop. XXXIII]
----------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If from a given point a straight line be drawn onto a straight line given in position making a given angle, the straight line drawn is given in position.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(A), Pos(Line BC), Mag(Angle ADC)) -> Determined(Pos(Line AD))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`, :just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`, :just:`[I.23] <I.23>`, :just:`[I.29] <I.29>`, :just:`[I.31] <I.31>`

From a given point :math:`A` outside a straight line :math:`BC` given in position, let a straight line :math:`AD` be drawn making a given angle :math:`\angle ADC` with :math:`BC`. I say that :math:`AD` is given in position.

Through the given point :math:`A` draw :math:`EAF` parallel to :math:`BC` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`); then :math:`EAF` is given in position (:just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`). Because :math:`EAF \parallel BC`, the alternate angle :math:`\angle EAD` is equal to :math:`\angle ADC` (:just:`[I.29] <I.29>`). Yet :math:`\angle ADC` is given; therefore :math:`\angle EAD` is given. Thus to the position-given line :math:`EAF` and at the given point :math:`A` on it, the straight line :math:`AD` has been drawn making the given angle :math:`\angle EAD`; therefore :math:`AD` is given in position (:just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`).
