.. _D.32:

Proposition 32 [λβ΄ · Prop. XXXV]
---------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 54
   :page: 54

.. enunciation::

   If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn making given angles, the straight line cut off between the parallels is given in magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(AB || CD), Mag(Angle BEF)) -> Determined(Mag(EF))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[I.29] <I.29>`, :just:`[I.31] <I.31>`, :just:`[I.34] <I.34>`

Between two parallel straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` given in position, let a straight line :math:`EF` be drawn making given angles :math:`\angle BEF` and :math:`\angle EFD`. I say that :math:`EF` is given in magnitude.

Take any given point :math:`G` on :math:`AB`, and through :math:`G` draw :math:`GH` parallel to :math:`EF` meeting :math:`CD` at :math:`H` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`). Because :math:`GH \parallel EF`, the interior angle :math:`\angle AGH` (or :math:`\angle BGH`) equals the corresponding angle :math:`\angle BEF` (:just:`[I.29] <I.29>`), which is given; hence :math:`GH` is drawn from the given point :math:`G` on position-given :math:`AB` at a given angle, so :math:`GH` is given in position (:just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`). Since :math:`CD` is also given in position, their intersection :math:`H` is given (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`). Because both :math:`G` and :math:`H` are given, :math:`GH` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Finally, :math:`GEFH` is a parallelogram, so :math:`EF = GH` (:just:`[I.34] <I.34>`); therefore :math:`EF` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`).
