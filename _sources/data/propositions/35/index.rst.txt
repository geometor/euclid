Proposition 35 [λε΄ · Prop. XXXIX (pt. 1)]
------------------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If from a given point onto a straight line given in position a straight line be drawn and cut in a given ratio, and through the point of section a parallel be drawn to the straight line given in position, that parallel is given in position.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(Pole A), Pos(Line BC), InternalRatio(AE : ED)) -> Determined(Pos(Line FEG || BC))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`, :just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`

From a given point :math:`A` outside a straight line :math:`BC` given in position, let a straight line :math:`AD` be drawn to :math:`BC` and produced to :math:`E` so that the ratio :math:`DE : AD` is given. I say that :math:`E` lies on a straight line given in position.

From :math:`A` draw :math:`AF` perpendicular to :math:`BC` meeting :math:`BC` at :math:`F` (:just:`[I.12] <I.12>`), and produce :math:`AF` to :math:`G` so that :math:`FG : AF = DE : AD` (:just:`[VI.12] <VI.12>`). Because :math:`AF` is drawn at a right angle from given :math:`A` to position-given :math:`BC`, :math:`AF` is given in position (:just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`), :math:`F` is given (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`), and :math:`AF` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Since :math:`FG : AF = DE : AD` is given, :math:`FG` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`), so the extremity :math:`G` on the position-given ray :math:`AFG` is given in position (:just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`). Through :math:`G` draw :math:`HGK` parallel to :math:`BC` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`), which is given in position (:just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`). Because :math:`AD : DE = AF : FG`, the line through :math:`G` and :math:`E` is parallel to :math:`FD` (:just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`) and therefore coincides with :math:`HGK`; hence :math:`E` lies on a straight line given in position.
