.. _D.37:

Proposition 37 [λζ΄ · Prop. XL (pt. 1)]
---------------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 62
   :page: 62

.. enunciation::

   If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn and cut in a given ratio, and through the point of section a parallel be drawn to the parallels given in position, that parallel is given in position.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(AB || CD), InternalRatio(EG : GF)) -> Determined(Pos(Line HK || AB))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`, :just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[I.30] <I.30>`, :just:`[I.34] <I.34>`, :just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`

Between two parallel straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` given in position, let any straight line :math:`EF` be drawn, and let :math:`EF` be produced to :math:`G` in a given ratio :math:`EF : FG`. I say that :math:`G` lies on a straight line given in position parallel to :math:`AB` and :math:`CD`.

Take a given point :math:`H` on :math:`AB`, draw :math:`HK` perpendicular to :math:`CD` meeting :math:`CD` at :math:`K` (:just:`[I.12] <I.12>`), and produce :math:`HK` to :math:`L` so that :math:`HK : KL = EF : FG`. By :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, and :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :math:`HK` is given in position and magnitude; since :math:`HK : KL` is given, :math:`KL` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`), so :math:`L` is given in position (:just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`). Through :math:`L` draw :math:`MLN` parallel to :math:`CD` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`), which is given in position (:just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`). Produce :math:`EF` to meet :math:`MLN` at :math:`G'`: by parallel transversals (:just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>` via the auxiliary parallel from :math:`H` to :math:`EF`), :math:`EF : FG' = HK : KL = EF : FG`, whence :math:`FG' = FG` (:just:`[V.9] <V.9>`) and :math:`G` coincides with :math:`G'` on the position-given line :math:`MLN`.
