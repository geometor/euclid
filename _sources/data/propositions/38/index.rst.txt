Proposition 38 [λη΄ · Prop. XL (pt. 2)]
---------------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn, and there be added to it a straight line having a given ratio to it, and through the extremity a parallel be drawn to the parallels given in position, that parallel is given in position.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(AB || CD), ExternalRatio(EF : FG)) -> Determined(Pos(Line HK || AB))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`, :just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[I.30] <I.30>`, :just:`[I.34] <I.34>`, :just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`

Between two parallel straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` given in position, let a straight line :math:`EF` be drawn and divided internally at :math:`G` in a given ratio :math:`EG : GF`. I say that :math:`G` lies on a straight line given in position.

Take a given point :math:`N` on :math:`AB` and draw :math:`NM` perpendicular to :math:`CD` meeting :math:`CD` at :math:`M` (:just:`[I.12] <I.12>`), so :math:`NM` is given in position and magnitude (:just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Divide :math:`NM` at :math:`L` so that :math:`NL : LM = EG : GF` (:just:`[VI.10] <VI.10>`); since :math:`NM` is given and :math:`NL : LM` is given, :math:`NL` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`), so :math:`L` is given in position (:just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`). Draw :math:`KLH` through :math:`L` parallel to :math:`CD`, which is given in position (:just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`). Since :math:`AB \parallel KLH \parallel CD` cuts :math:`NM` and :math:`EF` proportionally (:just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`), :math:`KLH` divides :math:`EF` in the ratio :math:`NL : LM = EG : GF`, and therefore passes through :math:`G`.
