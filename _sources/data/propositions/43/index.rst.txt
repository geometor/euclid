.. _D.43:

Proposition 43 [μγ΄ · Prop. XLVI]
---------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 76
   :page: 76

.. enunciation::

   If of a right-angled triangle the sides about one of the acute angles have a given ratio to one another, the triangle is given in species.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Angle(A) = pi/2, Ratio(hypotenuse c : leg b)) -> Determined(Species(Triangle ABC))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`, :just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`, :just:`[Data 31] <D.31>`, :just:`[I.47] <I.47>`, :just:`[VI.7] <VI.7>`

Let :math:`\triangle ABC` be right-angled at :math:`A` (:math:`\angle BAC = 1\text{ rt.}`), and let the sides :math:`BC` and :math:`BA` about one of the other angles :math:`\angle ABC` have a given ratio :math:`BC : BA`. I say that :math:`\triangle ABC` is given in species.

Set out a straight line :math:`DE` given in position and magnitude, and on diameter :math:`DE` describe the semicircle :math:`DGE` (:just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`), whose centre (the midpoint of :math:`DE`, :just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`, :just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`) and radius are given, so the semicircle :math:`DGE` is given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`). Make :math:`DE : F = BC : BA` (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`); since :math:`DE` is given and :math:`BC : BA` is given (with :math:`BC > BA` by :just:`[I.19] <I.19>`), :math:`F` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`) and :math:`F < DE`. With centre :math:`D` and radius :math:`DG = F`, describe a circle meeting the semicircle :math:`DGE` at the unique point :math:`G` (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`), and join :math:`DG, GE` (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Then :math:`\triangle DGE` is given in species (:just:`[Data 39] <D.39>`) and :math:`\angle DGE` in the semicircle is right (:just:`[III.31] <III.31>`). In :math:`\triangle ABC` and :math:`\triangle DGE`, :math:`\angle BAC = \angle DGE = 1\text{ rt.}`, :math:`BC : BA = DE : DG`, and the angles :math:`\angle ACB, \angle DEG` opposite :math:`BA, DG` are both acute (:just:`[I.17] <I.17>`); hence :math:`\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DGE` (:just:`[VI.7] <VI.7>`), so :math:`\triangle ABC` is given in species.
