Proposition 47 [μζ΄ · Prop. LI]
-------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   Rectilineal figures given in species are divided into triangles given in species.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Species(n-gon P)) -> Determined(Species(ear-triangulation T_1 .. T_{n-2}))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`, :just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 41] <D.41>`, :just:`[I.32] <I.32>`, :just:`[VI.20] <VI.20>`

Rectilinear figures given in species are divided into triangles given in species. Let the polygon :math:`ABCDE` be given in species (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`). I say that :math:`ABCDE` is divided into triangles given in species.

Join the diagonals :math:`BE` and :math:`EC` (:just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`). Since :math:`ABCDE` is given in species, the angle :math:`\angle BAE` is given and the ratio :math:`BA : AE` is given (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`). Therefore :math:`\triangle BAE` is given in species (:just:`[Data 41] <D.41>`), whence :math:`\angle ABE` is given and the ratio :math:`AB : BE` is given. Subtracting given :math:`\angle ABE` from the given polygon angle :math:`\angle ABC` leaves :math:`\angle EBC` given (:just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`). Moreover, since :math:`EB : BA` is given and :math:`AB : BC` is given (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`), the ratio :math:`EB : BC` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Thus :math:`\triangle EBC` has given angle :math:`\angle EBC` and given ratio :math:`EB : BC`, so :math:`\triangle EBC` is given in species (:just:`[Data 41] <D.41>`). For the very same reason :math:`\triangle ECD` is also given in species; therefore :math:`ABCDE` has been partitioned into triangles given in species.
