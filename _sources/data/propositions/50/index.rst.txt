Proposition 50 [ν΄ · Prop. LIV]
-------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines have a given ratio to one another, the rectilineal figures similar and similarly described upon them will also have a given ratio to one another.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(AB : CD) = r, Similar(E, F)) -> Determined(Ratio(Area(E) : Area(F)) = r^2)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[VI.11] <VI.11>`, :just:`[VI.19] <VI.19>`, :just:`[VI.20] <VI.20>`

Let two straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` have a given ratio :math:`AB : CD`, and on them let similar and similarly situated rectilinear figures :math:`E` and :math:`F` be described. I say that :math:`E` has a given ratio to :math:`F`.

Take the third proportional :math:`G` to :math:`AB` and :math:`CD`, so that :math:`AB : CD = CD : G` (:just:`[VI.11] <VI.11>`). Since :math:`AB : CD` is given, :math:`CD : G` is given; hence *ex aequali* :math:`AB : G` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Yet because :math:`E` and :math:`F` are similar and similarly situated rectilinear figures on :math:`AB` and :math:`CD`, :math:`E` has to :math:`F` the duplicate ratio of :math:`AB` to :math:`CD`, namely :math:`E : F = AB : G` (:just:`[VI.19] <VI.19>` Cor., :just:`[VI.20] <VI.20>` Cor.). Since :math:`AB : G` is given, the ratio :math:`E : F` is given (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`).
