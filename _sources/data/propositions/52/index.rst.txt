Proposition 52 [νβ΄ · Prop. LVI]
--------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 94
   :page: 94

.. enunciation::

   If on a straight line given in magnitude a rectilineal figure given in species be described, it is given in magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(AB), Species(P on AB)) -> Determined(Mag(Area(P)))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 49] <D.49>`, :just:`[I.46] <I.46>`

On a straight line :math:`AB` given in magnitude, let a rectilinear figure :math:`ACDEB` given in species be described. I say that :math:`ACDEB` is given in magnitude.

On :math:`AB` describe the square :math:`AFGB` (:just:`[I.46] <I.46>`). Because all angles of a square are right and all its sides are equal (:math:`1:1`), the square :math:`AFGB` is given in species (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`), and since :math:`AB` is given in magnitude, the square :math:`AFGB` is directly constructible equal to itself and so is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`). Now :math:`ACDEB` and :math:`AFGB` are two figures given in species on the same straight line :math:`AB`; hence the ratio :math:`ACDEB : AFGB` is given (:just:`[Data 49] <D.49>`). Because :math:`AFGB` is given in magnitude, :math:`ACDEB` is also given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`).
