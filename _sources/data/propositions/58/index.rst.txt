Proposition 58 [νη΄ · Prop. LXXXIII]
------------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 104
   :page: 104

.. enunciation::

   If a given area be applied to a given straight line, falling short by a figure given in species, the breadths of the defect are given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(S), Mag(a), Species(Defect)) -> Determined(Roots of a*x - c*x^2 = S) [diorismos]`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`, :just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`, :just:`[Data 55] <D.55>`, :just:`[II.5] <II.5>`, :just:`[VI.24] <VI.24>`, :just:`[VI.27] <VI.27>`, :just:`[VI.28] <VI.28>`

Let a given area :math:`AC` be applied to a straight line :math:`AB` given in magnitude, :term:`deficient <elleipsis>` by a parallelogram :math:`CDFB` given in species (:just:`[VI.28] <VI.28>`). I say that both the breadths and bases of the deficiency (:math:`BD, DF` and :math:`AD`) are given in magnitude.

Bisect :math:`AB` at :math:`E` (:just:`[I.10] <I.10>`). Since :math:`AB` is given in magnitude, its half :math:`EB` (:math:`= AE`) is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`). On :math:`EB` describe the parallelogram :math:`EFGH` similar and similarly situated to the species-given defect :math:`CDFB` (:just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`), and complete the diagram of :just:`[VI.28] <VI.28>`. Because :math:`EFGH` is given in species and described on the given segment :math:`EB`, :math:`EFGH` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`). By the gnomon identity (:just:`[II.5] <II.5>` generalized to equiangular parallelograms in :just:`[VI.27] <VI.27>`–:just:`[VI.28] <VI.28>`), :math:`EFGH` equals the applied area :math:`AC` together with the inner parallelogram :math:`KHLN` (which is similar and similarly situated to :math:`EFGH` on segment :math:`ED = EB - BD`). Since both :math:`EFGH` and :math:`AC` are given in magnitude (with :math:`AC \le EFGH` by the *diorismos* of :just:`[VI.27] <VI.27>`), the difference :math:`KHLN = EFGH - AC` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`). Because :math:`KHLN \sim CDFB` is also given in species, its side :math:`KH` (:math:`= ED`) is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 55] <D.55>`). Subtracting :math:`ED` from given :math:`EB` gives :math:`BD` (:just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`), adding :math:`ED` to given :math:`AE` gives :math:`AD` (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`), and from species-given :math:`CDFB` on :math:`BD` its breadth :math:`DF` (:math:`= CB`) is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`).
