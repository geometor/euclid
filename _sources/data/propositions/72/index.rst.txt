.. _D.72:

Proposition 72 [οβ΄ · Prop. LXVIII Cor. 2]
------------------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 136
   :page: 136

.. enunciation::

   If of two triangles both the bases be in a given ratio, and the straight lines drawn onto them from the vertices making either equal angles or unequal yet given angles with the bases be in a given ratio, the triangles themselves will also have a given ratio to one another.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(b1 : b2), Ratio(d1 : d2), Angle(phi1), Angle(phi2)) -> Determined(Ratio(△1 : △2))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 70] <D.70>`, :just:`[I.29] <I.29>`, :just:`[I.31] <I.31>`, :just:`[I.34] <I.34>`, :just:`[I.41] <I.41>`

Let two triangles :math:`\\triangle ABC` and :math:`\\triangle DEF` have their bases in a given ratio :math:`BC : EF`, and let the altitudes :math:`AG` and :math:`DH` drawn to the bases from the opposite vertices also have a given ratio :math:`AG : DH`. I say that the area ratio :math:`\\triangle ABC : \\triangle DEF` is given.

Complete the rectangles (or right-angled parallelograms) :math:`KBCG` of altitude :math:`AG` on base :math:`BC` and :math:`LEFH` of altitude :math:`DH` on base :math:`EF` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`). Because all right angles are equal (:just:`[Post. 4] <I.post.4>`), :math:`KBCG` and :math:`LEFH` are equiangular parallelograms with side ratios :math:`BC : EF` given and :math:`BG : EH` (:math:`= AG : DH`) given; hence :math:`KBCG : LEFH` is given (:just:`[Data 70] <D.70>`). Since each rectangle is double its triangle (:just:`[I.41] <I.41>`), :math:`\\triangle ABC : \\triangle DEF = KBCG : LEFH` (:just:`[V.15] <V.15>`) is given.
