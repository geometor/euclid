.. _D.89:

Proposition 89 [πθ΄ · Prop. XCIII]
----------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If on the circumference of a circle given in position a given point be taken, and from it a straight line be inflected to the circumference of the circle making a given angle, the other extremity of the inflected line is given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(PosMag(Circle(D, R)), Pos(B on circle), Mag(InscribedAngle BAC)) -> Determined(Pos(C))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`, :just:`[III.1] <III.1>`, :just:`[III.20] <III.20>`

Let a point :math:`B` be given on the circumference of a circle :math:`ABC` :term:`given in position <thesei>` (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`), and from :math:`B` to another point :math:`C` on the circumference let two straight lines :math:`BA, AC` be inflected at a variable point :math:`A` on the circumference so as to contain a given angle :math:`\angle BAC` (with :math:`C` on a specified side of :math:`B`). I say that the point :math:`C` is given in position.

Take the centre :math:`D` of the position-given circle :math:`ABC` (:just:`[III.1] <III.1>`); then :math:`D` is given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`). Join :math:`DB` and :math:`DC`. Since both :math:`D` and :math:`B` are given in position, the radius :math:`DB` is given in position (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Moreover, the central angle :math:`\angle BDC` is double the inscribed angle :math:`\angle BAC` (:just:`[III.20] <III.20>`, or :math:`2\,(2\\text{ rt.} - \angle BAC)` if :math:`\angle BAC` stands in the minor segment). Because :math:`\angle BAC` is given, its double :math:`\angle BDC` is given (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`). Thus at the given point :math:`D` on the position-given line :math:`DB`, the ray :math:`DC` is drawn making a given angle :math:`\angle BDC`; therefore :math:`DC` is given in position (:just:`[Data 29] <D.29>`). Since the circumference :math:`ABC` is also given in position, their intersection :math:`C` is given in position (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`).
