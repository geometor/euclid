.. _D.90:

Proposition 90 [ϙ΄ · Prop. XCIV]
--------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 176
   :page: 176

.. enunciation::

   If from a given point a straight line be drawn touching a circle given in position, the straight line drawn is given in position and in magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(PosMag(Circle(O, R)), Pos(D outside)) -> Determined(Pos(DA), Mag(DA = sqrt(OD^2 - R^2)))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 43] <D.43>`, :just:`[Data 53] <D.53>`, :just:`[III.17] <III.17>`, :just:`[III.18] <III.18>`, :just:`[III.31] <III.31>`

From a given point :math:`C` outside a circle :math:`AB` given in position, let a straight line :math:`CA` be drawn touching the circle at :math:`A` (:just:`[III.17] <III.17>`). I say that the tangent :math:`CA` is given both in position and in magnitude.

Take the centre :math:`D` of the position-given circle :math:`AB` (:just:`[III.1] <III.1>`), so :math:`D` is given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`), and join :math:`DA` and :math:`DC`. Since both :math:`D` and :math:`C` are given in position, :math:`DC` is given in position and magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Because :math:`CA` touches the circle at :math:`A`, the angle :math:`\angle DAC` is right (:just:`[III.18] <III.18>`); therefore the semicircle :math:`DAC` described on diameter :math:`DC` passes through :math:`A` (:just:`[III.31] <III.31>`). Since :math:`DC` is given in position and magnitude, the semicircle :math:`DAC` is given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`). Because the circle :math:`AB` is also given in position, their intersection :math:`A` is given in position (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`). Joining :math:`A` to the given point :math:`C`, the tangent :math:`CA` is given both in position and in magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`).
