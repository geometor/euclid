al-Faḍl ibn Ḥātim an-Nairīzī
============================

*Persian Mathematician, Astronomer & Commentator on Euclid*

Authored a major Arabic commentary on Euclid's Elements (translated into Latin by Gherard of Cremona) that uniquely preserves large lost portions of the commentaries of Heron of Alexandria and Simplicius.

**Cited Works:** *Commentary on Euclid's Elements (ed. Besthorn & Heiberg; Latin tr. ed. Curtze)*
