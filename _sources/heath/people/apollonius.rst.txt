Apollonius of Perga
===================

*The 'Great Geometer' of Antiquity*

Author of the monumental treatise on Conics (introducing the terms parabola, hyperbola, and ellipse from the Pythagorean application of areas) as well as works on Plane Loci, Tangencies, and Unordered Irrationals.

**Cited Works:** :ref:`Conics (Κωνικά) <work-apollonius-conics>`, :ref:`Plane Loci (Τόποι ἐπίπεδοι) <work-apollonius-plane-loci>`, *Tangencies*, *On Unordered Irrationals*, :ref:`Tactiones <work-apollonius-tactiones>`, :ref:`Plane Loci (Τόποι ἐπίπεδοι) <work-apollonius-plane-loci>`, :ref:`Comparison of the Dodecahedron and the Icosahedron <work-apollonius-comparison-solids>`
