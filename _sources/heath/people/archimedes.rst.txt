Archimedes of Syracuse
======================

*Greek Geometer, Physicist & Engineer*

Perfected Eudoxus's method of exhaustion and mechanical heuristic discovery to determine areas, volumes, and centers of gravity of curvilinear and solid figures.

**Cited Works:** *On the Sphere and Cylinder*, *Measurement of a Circle*, :ref:`On Conoids and Spheroids <work-archimedes-conoids-and-spheroids>`, *On Spirals*, :ref:`Quadrature of the Parabola <work-archimedes-quadrature-of-parabola>`, *The Method*, :ref:`On the Sphere and Cylinder (Περὶ σφαίρας καὶ κυλίνδρου) <work-archimedes-sphere-and-cylinder>`, :ref:`Liber Assumptorum (Book of Lemmas) <work-archimedes-liber-assumptorum>`, :ref:`On the Equilibrium of Planes <work-archimedes-equilibrium-of-planes>`, :ref:`The Method of Mechanical Theorems (Ἐφόδιον) <work-archimedes-methodus>`
