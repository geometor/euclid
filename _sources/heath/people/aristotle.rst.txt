Aristotle of Stagira
====================

*Founder of the Peripatetic School*

Codified the logic of demonstrative science (Analytica Posteriora), distinguishing definitions, hypotheses, postulates, and common axioms, and preserving key pre-Euclidean geometric proofs and definitions.
