Anicius Manlius Severinus Boethius
==================================

*Roman Philosopher & Transmitter of the Quadrivium*

Translated Euclid's definitions, postulates, and enunciations into Latin and authored the De Institutione Arithmetica and De Institutione Musica.

**Cited Works:** *De Institutione Arithmetica*, :ref:`De Institutione Musica <work-boethius-de-institutione-musica>`, *Ars Geometriae*
