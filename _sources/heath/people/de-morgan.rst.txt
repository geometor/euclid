Augustus De Morgan
==================

*British Mathematician & Logician*

Wrote penetrating critical analyses of Euclid's logic, Book V theory of proportion, and Book X theory of irrationals in the Companion to the Almanac and the Penny Cyclopaedia.

**Cited Works:** *Study and Difficulties of Mathematics (1831)*, *Connexion of Number and Magnitude (1836)*, :ref:`Formal Logic (1847) <work-de-morgan-formal-logic>`, :ref:`Articles in The Penny Cyclopaedia <work-de-morgan-penny-cyclopaedia>`
