Diophantus of Alexandria
========================

*Alexandrian Algebraist & Number Theorist*

Author of the Arithmetica and On Polygonal Numbers, pioneering syncopated algebraic notation and indeterminate analysis.

**Cited Works:** *Arithmetica*, *On Polygonal Numbers*
