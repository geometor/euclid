Eudoxus of Cnidus
=================

*Mathematician & Astronomer of the Academy*

Creator of the general theory of proportion in Book V (applicable equally to commensurable and incommensurable magnitudes) and the method of exhaustion in Book XII (proving the volume ratios of pyramid, prism, cone, and cylinder).

**Cited Works:** *General Theory of Proportion (Elements Book V)*, *Method of Exhaustion (Elements Book XII)*
