Carl Friedrich Gauss
====================

*German Mathematician ('Princeps Mathematicorum')*

Proved the constructibility of the regular 17-gon (extending Euclid Book IV) and was the first to recognize the logical consistency and possible physical validity of non-Euclidean (hyperbolic) geometry.

**Cited Works:** *Disquisitiones Arithmeticae (1801)*
