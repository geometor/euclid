Naṣīr al-Dīn al-Ṭūsī
====================

*Persian Polymath, Astronomer & Geometer*

Composed influential Arabic recensions (Taḥrīr) of Euclid's Elements and attempted a proof of the Parallel Postulate that directly influenced John Wallis and Girolamo Saccheri.

**Cited Works:** *Taḥrīr Uṣūl Uqlīdis*
