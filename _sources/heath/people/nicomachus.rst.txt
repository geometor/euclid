Nicomachus of Gerasa
====================

*Neopythagorean Mathematician*

Author of the Introductio Arithmetica, presenting Greek number theory (prime, composite, plane, solid, and perfect numbers) in an inductive, non-geometric style.

**Cited Works:** *Introductio Arithmetica (Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή)*, :ref:`Introductio Arithmetica <work-nicomachus-intro-arith>`
