John Playfair
=============

*Scottish Mathematician & Geologist*

Popularized 'Playfair's Axiom' (already stated by Proclus) as a modern substitute for Euclid's Fifth Postulate in his Elements of Geometry (1795).

**Cited Works:** *Elements of Geometry (1795)*
