Claudius Ptolemy
================

*Alexandrian Astronomer & Geometer*

Author of the Almagest (Syntaxis Mathematica) and a treatise attempting to prove Euclid's Fifth Postulate, preserved and critiqued by Proclus.

**Cited Works:** :ref:`Syntaxis Mathematica (Almagest) <work-ptolemy-syntaxis>`, *On the Parallel Postulate*
