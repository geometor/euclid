Bernhard Riemann
================

*German Mathematician; Founder of Riemannian Geometry*

In his 1854 Habilitationsschrift, generalized geometry to n-dimensional manifolds of variable curvature, distinguishing unboundedness from infinitude and introducing spherical/elliptic non-Euclidean geometry.

**Cited Works:** *Über die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen (1854 / 1867)*
