Giuseppe Veronese
=================

*Italian Geometer; Pioneer of Non-Archimedean Geometry*

Author of Fondamenti di geometria (1891) and Elementi di geometria (with Ingrami), developing rigorous synthetic foundations of geometry.

**Cited Works:** *Fondamenti di geometria (1891)*, :ref:`Elementi Di Geometria <work-de-paolis-elementi-di-geometria>`
