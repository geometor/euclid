.. _person-campanus:

Campanus of Novara (c. 1220 – 1296)
-----------------------------------

*Medieval Italian Mathematician & Editor of the Latin Euclid*

.. _person-thales:

Thales of Miletus (c. 624 – c. 546 BC)
--------------------------------------

*Ionion Natural Philosopher & Pioneer of Greek Geometry*

Introduced geometry from Egypt into Greece; credited by Eudemus and Proclus with propositions I.5, I.15, I.26, and the theorem that the angle in a semicircle is a right angle (III.31).

.. _person-theaetetus:

Theaetetus of Athens (c. 417 – c. 369 BC)
-----------------------------------------

*Geometer of the Platonic Academy*

Developed the general classification of incommensurable magnitudes (medial, binomial, apotome) codified in Book X and completed the theoretical construction of the five regular solids (octahedron and icosahedron) in Book XIII.

**Cited Works:** *Theory of Irrationals (Elements Book X)*, *Regular Solids (Elements Book XIII)*

**Mentions:** :ref:`Vol. I, p. 413 <folio-i-0427>`, :ref:`Vol. I, p. 37 <folio-i-0051>`, :ref:`Vol. I, p. 64 <folio-i-0078>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 3 <folio-iii-0007>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 11 <folio-iii-0015>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 19 <folio-iii-0023>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 30 <folio-iii-0034>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 438 <folio-iii-0442>`, :ref:`Vol. III, p. 442 <folio-iii-0446>`
