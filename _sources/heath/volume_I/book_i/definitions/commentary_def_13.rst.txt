Commentary on Book I Definition 13
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book I Definitions <I.def>`.

Ὅρος ἐστίν, ὅ τινός ἐστι :term:`πέρας <peras>`.

*A boundary is that which is an extremity of anything.*

----

:person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` also uses the words :term:`ὅρος <horos>` and :term:`πέρας <peras>` as synonymous. Cf. *De gen. animal.* II. 6, 745 a 6, 9, where in the expression “limit of magnitude” first one and then the other word is used.

:person:`Proclus <proclus>` (:cite:`p. 136, 8 <proclus:in-euclid:136.8>`) remarks that the word boundary is appropriate to the origin of geometry, which began from the measurement of areas of ground and involved the marking of boundaries.
