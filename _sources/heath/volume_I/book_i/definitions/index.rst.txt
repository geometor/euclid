DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _I.def.1:

1. A :term:`**point** <point>` is that which has no part.

.. _I.def.2:

2. A :term:`**line** <line>` is breadthless length.

.. _I.def.3:

3. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a line are points.

.. _I.def.4:

4. A :term:`**straight line** <straight-line>` is a line which lies evenly with the points on itself.

.. _I.def.5:

5. A :term:`**surface** <surface>` is that which has length and breadth only.

.. _I.def.6:

6. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a surface are lines.

.. _I.def.7:

7. A :term:`**plane surface** <plane-surface>` is a surface which lies evenly with the straight lines on itself.

.. _I.def.8:

8. A :term:`**plane angle** <plane-angle>` is the inclination to one another of two lines in a plane which meet one another and do not lie in a straight line.

.. _I.def.9:

9. And when the lines containing the angle are straight, the angle is called :term:`**rectilineal** <rectilineal-angle>`.

.. _I.def.10:

10. When a straight line set up on a straight line makes the adjacent angles equal to one another, each of the equal angles is :term:`**right** <right-angle>`, and the straight line standing on the other is called a :term:`**perpendicular** <perpendicular>` to that on which it stands.

.. _I.def.11:

11. An :term:`**obtuse angle** <obtuse-angle>` is an angle greater than a right angle.

.. _I.def.12:

12. An :term:`**acute angle** <acute-angle>` is an angle less than a right angle.

.. _I.def.13:

13. A :term:`**boundary** <boundary>` is that which is an extremity of anything.

.. _I.def.14:

14. A :term:`**figure** <figure>` is that which is contained by any boundary or boundaries.

.. _I.def.15:

15. A :term:`**circle** <circle>` is a plane figure contained by one line such that all the straight lines falling upon it from one point among those lying within the figure are equal to one another ;

.. _folio-i-0168:

.. folio:: 0168
   :page: 154

.. _I.def.16:

16. And the point is called the :term:`**centre** <centre>` of the circle.

.. _I.def.17:

17. A :term:`**diameter** <diameter>` of the circle is any straight line drawn through the centre and terminated in both directions by the circumference of the circle, and such a straight line also bisects the circle.

.. _I.def.18:

18. A :term:`**semicircle** <semicircle>` is the figure contained by the diameter and the circumference cut off by it. And the centre of the semicircle is the same as that of the circle.

.. _I.def.19:

19. :term:`**Rectilineal figures** <rectilineal-figure>` are those which are contained by straight lines, :term:`**trilateral figures** <trilateral>` being those contained by three, :term:`**quadrilateral** <quadrilateral>` those contained by four, and :term:`**multilateral** <multilateral>` those contained by more than four straight lines.

.. _I.def.20:

20. Of trilateral figures, an :term:`**equilateral triangle** <equilateral-triangle>` is that which has its three sides equal, an :term:`**isosceles triangle** <isosceles-triangle>` that which has two of its sides alone equal, and a :term:`**scalene triangle** <scalene-triangle>` that which has its three sides unequal.

.. _I.def.21:

21. Further, of trilateral figures, a :term:`**right-angled triangle** <right-angled-triangle>` is that which has a right angle, an :term:`**obtuse-angled triangle** <obtuse-angled-triangle>` that which has an obtuse angle, and an :term:`**acute-angled triangle** <acute-angled-triangle>` that which has its three angles acute.

.. _I.def.22:

22. Of quadrilateral figures, a :term:`**square** <square>` is that which is both equilateral and right-angled ; an :term:`**oblong** <oblong>` that which is right-angled but not equilateral ; a :term:`**rhombus** <rhombus>` that which is equilateral but not right-angled ; and a :term:`**rhomboid** <rhomboid>` that which has its opposite sides and angles equal to one another but is neither equilateral nor right-angled. And let quadrilaterals other than these be called :term:`**trapezia** <trapezium>`.

.. _I.def.23:

23. :term:`**Parallel** <parallel-straight-lines>` straight lines are straight lines which, being in the same plane and being produced indefinitely in both directions, do not meet one another in either direction.
