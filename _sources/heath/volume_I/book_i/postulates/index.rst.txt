POSTULATES.
===========

Let the following be postulated :

.. _I.post.1:

1. To draw a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` from any :term:`point <point>` to any point.

.. _I.post.2:

2. To produce a :term:`finite straight line <straight-line>` continuously in a straight line.

.. _I.post.3:

3. To describe a :term:`circle <circle>` with any :term:`centre <centre>` and distance.

.. _I.post.4:

4. That all :term:`right angles <right-angle>` are equal to one another.

.. folio:: 0169
   :page: 155

.. _I.post.5:

5. That, if a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` falling on two straight lines make the interior :term:`angles <plane-angle>` on the same side less than two :term:`right angles <right-angle>`, the two straight lines, if produced indefinitely, meet on that side on which are the angles less than the two right angles.
