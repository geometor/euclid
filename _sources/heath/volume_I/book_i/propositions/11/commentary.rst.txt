Commentary on Proposition I.11
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition I.11 <I.11>`.

.. folio:: 0283
   :page: 269

:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` remarks that this proposition, which is “to bisect the angle made by a straight line and its continuation” [i.e. a *flat angle*], should be a particular case of I. 9, the constructions being the same. This is certainly
worth noting, though I doubt the advantage of rearranging the propositions in consequence.

.. _folio-i-0284:

.. folio:: 0284
   :page: 270

:person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` gave a construction for this proposition (see :person:`Proclus <proclus>`, :cite:`p. 282, 8 <proclus:in-euclid:282.8>`) differing from Euclid’s in much the same way as his construction for bisecting a straight line differed from that of I. 10. Instead of assuming an equilateral triangle drawn without repeating the process of I. 1, :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` takes :g:`$D$ <0284.g1:D>` and :g:`$E$ <0284.g1:E>` equidistant from :g:`$C$ <0284.g1:C>` as in :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, and then draws circles in the manner of I. 1 meeting at :g:`$F$ <0284.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0284.g1.svg
   :alt: Apollonius Construction for I. 11

This necessitates proving again that :g:`$DF$ <0284.g1:/ D F />` is equal to :g:`$FE$ <0284.g1:/ F E />`; whereas Euclid’s assumption of the construction of I. 1 in the words “let the equilateral triangle :g:`$FDE$ <0284.g1:< F D E >>` be constructed” enables him to dispense with the drawing of circles and with the proof that :g:`$DF$ <0284.g1:/ D F />` is equal to :g:`$FE$ <0284.g1:/ F E />` at the same time. While however the substitution of :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`’ constructions for I. 10 and 11 would show faulty arrangement in a theoretical treatise like Euclid’s, they are entirely suitable for what we call *practical geometry*, and such may have been :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`’ object in these constructions and in his alternative for I. 23.

:person:`Proclus <proclus>` gives a construction for drawing a straight line at right angles to another straight line but from *one end* of it, instead of from an intermediate point on it, it being supposed (for the sake of argument) that we are not permitted to *produce* the straight line. In the commentary of :person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>` (ed. Besthorn-Heiberg, pp. 73—4; ed. :person:`Curtze <curtze>`, pp. 54—5) this construction is attributed to :person:`Heron <heron>`.

Let it be required to draw from :g:`$A$ <0284.g2:A>` a straight line at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0284.g2:/ A B />`. On :g:`$AB$ <0284.g2:/ A B />` take any point :g:`$C$ <0284.g2:C>`, and in the manner of the proposition draw :g:`$CE$ <0284.g2:/ C E />` at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0284.g2:/ A B />`. From :g:`$CE$ <0284.g2:/ C E />` cut off :g:`$CD$ <0284.g2:/ C D />` equal to :g:`$AC$ <0284.g2:/ A C />`, bisect the angle :g:`$ACE$ <0284.g2:< A C E )>` by the straight line :g:`$CF$ <0284.g2:/ C F />`, [I. 9] and draw :g:`$DF$ <0284.g2:/ D F />` at right angles to :g:`$CE$ <0284.g2:/ C E />` meeting :g:`$CF$ <0284.g2:/ C F />` in :g:`$F$ <0284.g2:F>`. Join :g:`$FA$ <0284.g2:/ F A />`.

.. picture:: 0284.g2.svg
   :alt: Heron Construction of Perpendicular at Extremity of Straight Line

Then the angle :g:`$FAC$ <0284.g2:< F A C )>` will be a right angle.

For, since, in the triangles :g:`$ACF$ <0284.g2:< A C F >>`, :g:`$DCF$ <0284.g2:< D C F >>`, the two sides :g:`$AC$ <0284.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$CF$ <0284.g2:/ C F />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$DC$ <0284.g2:/ D C />`, :g:`$CF$ <0284.g2:/ C F />` respectively, and the included angles :g:`$ACF$ <0284.g2:< A C F )>`, :g:`$DCF$ <0284.g2:< D C F )>` are equal, the triangles are equal in all respects. [I. 4]

Therefore the angle at :g:`$A$ <0284.g2:A>` is equal to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0284.g2:D>`, and is accordingly a right angle.
