PROPOSITION 21.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If on one of the sides of a triangle, from its extremities, there be constructed two straight lines meeting within the triangle,\ [#f1]_ the straight lines so constructed\ [#f2]_ will be less than the remaining two sides of the triangle, but will contain a greater angle.

On :g:`$BC$ <0303.g1:/ B C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0303.g1:< A B C >>`, from its extremities :g:`$B$ <0303.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0303.g1:C>`, let the two straight lines :g:`$BD$ <0303.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0303.g1:/ D C />` be constructed meeting within the triangle;

I say that :g:`$BD$ <0303.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0303.g1:/ D C />` are less than the remaining two sides of the triangle :g:`$BA$ <0303.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0303.g1:/ A C />`, but contain an angle :g:`$BDC$ <0303.g1:< B D C )>` greater than the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0303.g1:< B A C )>`.

.. picture:: 0303.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0303 Figure 1 - Proposition 21

For let :g:`$BD$ <0303.g1:[ B D ]>` be drawn through to :g:`$E$ <0303.g1:E>`. Then, since in any triangle two sides are greater than the remaining one, :just:`[I. 20] <I.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, in the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0303.g1:< A B E >>`, the two sides :g:`$AB$ <0303.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AE$ <0303.g1:/ A E />` are greater than :g:`$BE$ <0303.g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$EC$ <0303.g1:/ E C />` be added to each;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BA$ <0303.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0303.g1:/ A C />` are greater than :g:`$BE$ <0303.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0303.g1:/ E C />`.

Again, since, in the triangle :g:`$CED$ <0303.g1:< C E D >>`,

.. assertion::

   the two sides :g:`$CE$ <0303.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0303.g1:/ E D />` are greater than :g:`$CD$ <0303.g1:/ C D />`,

.. subclause::

   let :g:`$DB$ <0303.g1:/ D B />` be added to each;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CE$ <0303.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0303.g1:/ E B />` are greater than :g:`$CD$ <0303.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0303.g1:/ D B />`.

But :g:`$BA$ <0303.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0303.g1:/ A C />` were proved greater than :g:`$BE$ <0303.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0303.g1:/ E C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BA$ <0303.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0303.g1:/ A C />` are much greater than :g:`$BD$ <0303.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0303.g1:/ D C />`.

Again, since in any triangle the exterior angle is greater than the interior and opposite angle, :just:`[I. 16] <I.16>`

therefore, in the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0303.g1:< C D E >>`,

.. assertion::

   the exterior angle :g:`$BDC$ <0303.g1:< B D C )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$CED$ <0303.g1:< C E D )>`.

For the same reason, moreover, in the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0303.g1:< A B E >>` also,

.. assertion::

   the exterior angle :g:`$CEB$ <0303.g1:< C E B )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0303.g1:< B A C )>`.

But the angle :g:`$BDC$ <0303.g1:< B D C )>` was proved greater than the angle :g:`$CEB$ <0303.g1:< C E B )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$BDC$ <0303.g1:< B D C )>` is much greater than the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0303.g1:< B A C )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **be constructed...meeting within the triangle.** The word “meeting” is not in the Greek, where the words are ἐντὸς συσταθῶσιν. συνίστασθαι is the word used of constructing two straight lines to a point (cf. I. 7) or so as to form a triangle; but it is necessary in English to indicate that they meet.

.. [#f2] **the straight lines so constructed.** Observe the elegant brevity of the Greek αἱ συσταθεῖσαι.
