PROPOSITION 22.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Out of three straight lines, which are equal to three given straight lines, to construct a triangle:\ [#f1]_ thus it is necessary\ [#f2]_ that two of the straight lines taken together in any manner should be greater than the remaining one.\ [#f3]_ :just:`[I. 20] <I.20>`

Let the three given straight lines be :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`, and of these let two taken together in any manner be greater than the remaining one, namely,

.. subclause::

   :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>` greater than :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`,

.. subclause::

   :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>` greater than :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`,

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>` greater than :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`;

thus it is required to construct a triangle out of straight lines equal to :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0306.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0306 Figure 1 - Proposition 22

Let there be set out a straight line :g:`$DE$ <0306.g1:[ D E ]>`, terminated at :g:`$D$ <0306.g1:D>` but of infinite length in the direction of :g:`$E$ <0306.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$DF$ <0306.g1:/ D F />` be made equal to :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$FG$ <0306.g1:/ F G />` equal to :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, and :g:`$GH$ <0306.g1:/ G H />` equal to :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`. :just:`[I. 3] <I.3>`

With centre :g:`$F$ <0306.g1:F>` and distance :g:`$FD$ <0306.g1:/ F D />` let the circle :g:`$DKL$ <0306.g1:( D K L )>` be described;

again, with centre :g:`$G$ <0306.g1:G>` and distance :g:`$GH$ <0306.g1:/ G H />` let the circle :g:`$KLH$ <0306.g1:( K L H )>` be described;

and let :g:`$KF$ <0306.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0306.g1:/ K G />` be joined;

I say that the triangle :g:`$KFG$ <0306.g1:< K F G >>` has been constructed out of three straight lines equal to :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`.

.. _folio-i-0307:

.. folio:: 0307
   :page: 293

For, since the point :g:`$F$ <0306.g1:F>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$DKL$ <0306.g1:( D K L )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$FD$ <0306.g1:/ F D />` is equal to :g:`$FK$ <0306.g1:/ F K />`.

But :g:`$FD$ <0306.g1:/ F D />` is equal to :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KF$ <0306.g1:/ K F />` is also equal to :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`.

Again, since the point :g:`$G$ <0306.g1:G>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$LKH$ <0306.g1:( L K H )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$GH$ <0306.g1:/ G H />` is equal to :g:`$GK$ <0306.g1:/ G K />`.

But :g:`$GH$ <0306.g1:/ G H />` is equal to :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KG$ <0306.g1:/ K G />` is also equal to :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`.

And :g:`$FG$ <0306.g1:/ F G />` is also equal to :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the three straight lines :g:`$KF$ <0306.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0306.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GK$ <0306.g1:/ G K />` are equal to the three straight lines :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`.

Therefore out of the three straight lines :g:`$KF$ <0306.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0306.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GK$ <0306.g1:/ G K />`, which are equal to the three given straight lines :g:`$A$ <0306.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0306.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0306.g1:C>`, the triangle :g:`$KFG$ <0306.g1:< K F G >>` has been constructed.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.

.. [#f1] This is the first case in the Elements of a :term:`διορισμός <diorismos>` to a problem in the sense of a statement of the conditions or limits of the possibility of a solution. The criterion is of course supplied by the preceding proposition.

.. [#f2] **thus it is necessary.** This is usually translated (e.g. by Williamson and :person:`Simson <simson>`) “*But it is necessary*,” which is however inaccurate, since the Greek is not δεῖ δέ but δεῖ δή. The words are the same as those used to introduce the :term:`διορισμός <diorismos>` in the other sense of the “definition” or “particular statement” of a construction to be effected. Hence, as in the latter case we say “thus it is required” (e.g. to bisect the finite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0279.g1:/ A B />`, **I.** 10), we should here translate “thus it is necessary.”

.. [#f3] To this enunciation all the MSS. and :person:`Boethius <boethius>` add, after the :term:`διορισμός <diorismos>`, the words “because in any triangle two sides taken together in any manner are greater than the remaining one.” But this explanation has the appearance of a gloss, and it is omitted by :person:`Proclus <proclus>` and :person:`Campanus <campanus>`. Moreover there is no corresponding addition to the :term:`διορισμός <diorismos>` of **VI.** 28.
