PROPOSITION 36.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Parallelograms which are on equal bases and in the same parallels are equal to one another.

Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0345.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EFGH$ <0345.g1:< E F G H >>` be parallelograms which are on equal bases :g:`$BC$ <0345.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$FG$ <0345.g1:/ F G />` and in the same parallels :g:`$AH$ <0345.g1:/ A H />`, :g:`$BG$ <0345.g1:/ B G />`;

.. picture:: 0345.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0345 Figure 1 - Proposition 36

I say that the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0345.g1:< A B C D >>` is equal to :g:`$EFGH$ <0345.g1:< E F G H >>`.

For let :g:`$BE$ <0345.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$CH$ <0345.g1:/ C H />` be joined.

Then, since :g:`$BC$ <0345.g1:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$FG$ <0345.g1:/ F G />` while :g:`$FG$ <0345.g1:/ F G />` is equal to :g:`$EH$ <0345.g1:/ E H />`,

.. assertion:: [C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>

   :g:`$BC$ <0345.g1:/ B C />` is also equal to :g:`$EH$ <0345.g1:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   But they are also parallel. And :g:`$EB$ <0345.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$HC$ <0345.g1:/ H C />` join them; but straight lines joining equal and parallel straight lines (at the extremities which are) in the same directions (respectively) are equal and parallel. :just:`[I. 33] <I.33>`

.. assertion:: [I. 34] <I.34>

   Therefore :g:`$EBCH$ <0345.g1:< E B C H >>` is a parallelogram.

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`$ABCD$ <0345.g1:< A B C D >>`; for it has the same base :g:`$BC$ <0345.g1:/ B C />` with it, and is in the same parallels :g:`$BC$ <0345.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$AH$ <0345.g1:/ A H />` with it. :just:`[I. 35] <I.35>`

.. subclause:: [I. 35] <I.35>

   For the same reason also :g:`$EFGH$ <0345.g1:< E F G H >>` is equal to the same :g:`$EBCH$ <0345.g1:< E B C H >>`;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>

   so that the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0345.g1:< A B C D >>` is also equal to :g:`$EFGH$ <0345.g1:< E F G H >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
