Commentary on Proposition I.48
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition I.48 <I.48>`.

.. folio:: 0383
   :page: 369

Proclus' note (p. 430) on this proposition, though it does not mention Heron's name, gives an alternative proof, which is the same as that definitely attributed by :person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>` to :person:`Heron <heron>`, the only difference being that :person:`Proclus <proclus>` demonstrates two cases in full, while :person:`Heron <heron>` dismisses the second with a "similarly." The alternative proof is another instance of the use of I. 7 as a means of answering objections. If, says :person:`Proclus <proclus>`, it be not admitted that the perpendicular :g:`AD <0383.g1:/ A D />` may be drawn on the opposite side of :g:`AC <0383.g1:/ A C />` from :g:`B <0383.g1:B>`, we may draw it on the same side as :g:`AB <0383.g1:/ A B />`, in which case it is impossible that it should not coincide with :g:`AB <0383.g1:/ A B />`. :person:`Proclus <proclus>` takes two cases, first supposing that the perpendicular falls, as :g:`AD <0383.g1:/ A D />`, within the angle :g:`CAB <0383.g1:< C A B )>`, and secondly that it falls, as :g:`AE <0383.g1:/ A E />`, outside that angle. In either case the absurdity results that, on the same straight line :g:`AC <0383.g1:/ A C />` and on the same side of it, :g:`AD <0383.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`DC <0383.g1:/ D C />` must be respectively :eq:`equal <0383.g1:/ A D / = 0383.g1:/ A B /; 0383.g1:/ D C / = 0383.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`AB <0383.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0383.g1:/ B C />`, which contradicts I. 7.

   :alt: Folio 0383 Figure 1 - Proposition 48 Commentary (Proclus and Heron Alternative Proof)

Much to the same effect is the note of :person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` that there is here "an appearance of avoiding indirect demonstration by drawing the triangles on different sides of the base and appealing to I. 8, because drawing them on the same side would make the appeal to I. 7 (on which, however, I. 8 is founded)."
