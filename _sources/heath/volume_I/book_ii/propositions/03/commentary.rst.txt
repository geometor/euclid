Commentary on Proposition II.3
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition II.3 <II.3>`.

.. folio:: 0392
   :page: 378

If we leave out of account the contents of Book II. itself and merely look to the applicability of propositions to general use, this proposition and the preceding are, as already indicated, of great importance, and particularly so to the semi-algebraical method just described, which seems to have found its first exponents in :person:`Heron <heron>` and :person:`Pappus <pappus>`. Thus the proposition that *the difference of the squares on two straight lines is equal to the rectangle contained by the sum*
*and the difference of the straight lines*, which is generally given as equivalent to II. 5, 6, can be proved by means of II. 1, 2, 3, as shown by Lardner. For suppose the given straight lines are :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`, the latter being measured along :g:`$BA$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`.

Then, by II. 2, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />` is equal to the sum of the rectangles :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` and :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0393.g2:/ A C />`.

By II. 3, the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` is equal to the sum of the square on :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` and the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0393.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`.

Therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />` is equal to the square :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` together with the sum of the rectangles :g:`$AC$ <0393.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />` and :g:`$AC$ <0393.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`.

But, by II. 1, the sum of the latter rectangles is equal to the rectangle contained by :g:`$AC$ <0393.g2:/ A C />` and the sum of :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`, i.e. the rectangle contained by the sum and difference of :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`.

Hence the square on :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` and the rectangle contained by the sum and difference of :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`; that is, the difference of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />` is equal to the rectangle contained by the sum and difference of :g:`$AB$ <0393.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0393.g2:/ B C />`.
