PROPOSITION 10.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be bisected, and a straight line be added to it in a straight line, the square on the whole with the added straight line and the square on the added straight line both together are double of the square on the half and of the square described on the straight line made up of the half and the added straight line as on one straight line.

For let a straight line :g:`AB <0409.g1:/ A B />` be :eq:`bisected <0409.g1:/ A C / = 0409.g1:/ C B />` at :g:`C <0409.g1:C>`, and let a straight line :g:`BD <0409.g1:/ B D />` be added to it in a straight line;

I say that the squares on :g:`AD <0409.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`DB <0409.g1:/ D B />` are double of the squares on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

For let :g:`CE <0409.g1:/ C E />` be drawn from the point :g:`C <0409.g1:C>` at right angles to :g:`AB <0409.g1:/ A B />` :just:`[I. 11] <I.11>`, and let it be made :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ C E / = 0409.g1:/ A C / = 0409.g1:/ C B />` to either :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />` or :g:`CB <0409.g1:/ C B />` :just:`[I. 3] <I.3>`; let :g:`EA <0409.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`EB <0409.g1:/ E B />` be joined;

through :g:`E <0409.g1:E>` let :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />` be drawn parallel to :g:`AD <0409.g1:/ A D />`, and through :g:`D <0409.g1:D>` let :g:`FD <0409.g1:/ F D />` be drawn parallel to :g:`CE <0409.g1:/ C E />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. picture:: 0409.g1.svg
   :alt: 0409.g1

Then, since a straight line :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />` falls on the parallel straight lines :g:`EC <0409.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`FD <0409.g1:/ F D />`,
the angles :g:`CEF <0409.g1:< C E F )>`, :g:`EFD <0409.g1:< E F D )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. _folio-i-0410:

.. folio:: 0410
   :page: 396

therefore the angles :g:`FEB <0409.g1:< F E B )>`, :g:`EFD <0409.g1:< E F D )>` are less than two right angles.

But straight lines produced from angles less than two right angles meet; :just:`[I. Post. 5] <I.post.5>`

therefore :g:`EB <0409.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`FD <0409.g1:/ F D />`, if produced in the direction :g:`B <0409.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0409.g1:D>`, will meet.

Let them be produced and meet at :g:`G <0409.g1:G>`, and let :g:`AG <0409.g1:/ A G />` be joined.

Then, since :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ A C / = 0409.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`CE <0409.g1:/ C E />`, the angle :g:`EAC <0409.g1:< E A C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0409.g1:< E A C ) = 0409.g1:< A E C )>` to the angle :g:`AEC <0409.g1:< A E C )>`; :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

and the angle at :g:`C <0409.g1:< A C E )>` is right;

.. assertion:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore each of the angles :g:`EAC <0409.g1:< E A C )>`, :g:`AEC <0409.g1:< A E C )>` is half a right angle.

For the same reason each of the angles :g:`CEB <0409.g1:< C E B )>`, :g:`EBC <0409.g1:< E B C )>` is also half a right angle;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`AEB <0409.g1:< A E B )>` is right.

And, since the angle :g:`EBC <0409.g1:< E B C )>` is half a right angle, the angle :g:`DBG <0409.g1:< D B G )>` is also half a right angle. :just:`[I. 15] <I.15>`

But the angle :g:`BDG <0409.g1:< B D G )>` is also :eq:`right <0409.g1:< B D G ) = 0409.g1:< D C E )>`, for it is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:< B D G ) = 0409.g1:< D C E )>` to the angle :g:`DCE <0409.g1:< D C E )>`, they being alternate; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. subclause:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`DGB <0409.g1:< D G B )>` is half a right angle;

therefore the angle :g:`DGB <0409.g1:< D G B )>` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:< D G B ) = 0409.g1:< D B G )>` to the angle :g:`DBG <0409.g1:< D B G )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 6] <I.6>

   so that the side :g:`BD <0409.g1:/ B D />` is also :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ B D / = 0409.g1:/ G D />` to the side :g:`GD <0409.g1:/ G D />`.

Again, since the angle :g:`EGF <0409.g1:< E G F )>` is half a right angle, and the angle at :g:`F <0409.g1:< E F G )>` is :eq:`right <0409.g1:< E F G ) = 0409.g1:< D C E )>`, for it is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:< E F G ) = 0409.g1:< D C E )>` to the opposite angle, the angle at :g:`C <0409.g1:< D C E )>`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. subclause:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   the remaining angle :g:`FEG <0409.g1:< F E G )>` is half a right angle;

therefore the angle :g:`EGF <0409.g1:< E G F )>` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:< E G F ) = 0409.g1:< F E G )>` to the angle :g:`FEG <0409.g1:< F E G )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 6] <I.6>

   so that the side :g:`GF <0409.g1:/ G F />` is also :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ G F / = 0409.g1:/ E F />` to the side :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />`.

Now, since the square on :g:`EC <0409.g1:/ E C />` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ E C / = 0409.g1:/ C A />` to the square on :g:`CA <0409.g1:/ C A />`, the squares on :g:`EC <0409.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`CA <0409.g1:/ C A />` are double of the square on :g:`CA <0409.g1:/ C A />`.

But the square on :g:`EA <0409.g1:/ E A />` is equal to the squares on :g:`EC <0409.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`CA <0409.g1:/ C A />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion:: [C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>

   therefore the square on :g:`EA <0409.g1:/ E A />` is double of the square on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`.

.. _folio-i-0411:

.. folio:: 0411
   :page: 397

Again, since :g:`FG <0409.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ F G / = 0409.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />`, the square on :g:`FG <0409.g1:/ F G />` is also :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ F G / = 0409.g1:/ F E />` to the square on :g:`FE <0409.g1:/ F E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`GF <0409.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`FE <0409.g1:/ F E />` are double of the square on :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />`.

But the square on :g:`EG <0409.g1:/ E G />` is equal to the squares on :g:`GF <0409.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`FE <0409.g1:/ F E />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`EG <0409.g1:/ E G />` is double of the square on :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />`.

And :g:`EF <0409.g1:/ E F />` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ E F / = 0409.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`; :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`EG <0409.g1:/ E G />` is double of the square on :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

But the square on :g:`EA <0409.g1:/ E A />` was also proved double of the square on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`AE <0409.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EG <0409.g1:/ E G />` are double of the squares on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

And the square on :g:`AG <0409.g1:/ A G />` is equal to the squares on :g:`AE <0409.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EG <0409.g1:/ E G />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`AG <0409.g1:/ A G />` is double of the squares on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

But the squares on :g:`AD <0409.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`DG <0409.g1:/ D G />` are equal to the square on :g:`AG <0409.g1:/ A G />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`AD <0409.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`DG <0409.g1:/ D G />` are double of the squares on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

And :g:`DG <0409.g1:/ D G />` is :eq:`equal <0409.g1:/ D G / = 0409.g1:/ D B />` to :g:`DB <0409.g1:/ D B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`AD <0409.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`DB <0409.g1:/ D B />` are double of the squares on :g:`AC <0409.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CD <0409.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
