PROPOSITION 11.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To cut a given straight line so that the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the square on the remaining segment.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line;

thus it is required to cut :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` so that the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the square on the remaining segment.

.. picture:: 0416.g1.svg
   :alt: 0416.g1

For let the square :g:`$ABDC$ <0416.g1:< A B D C >>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`; :just:`[I. 46] <I.46>`

let :g:`$AC$ <0416.g1:/ A C />` be :eq:`bisected <0416.g1:/ A E / = 0416.g1:/ E C />` at the point :g:`$E$ <0416.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$BE$ <0416.g1:/ B E />` be joined; let :g:`$CA$ <0416.g1:/ C A />` be drawn through to :g:`$F$ <0416.g1:F>`, and let :g:`$EF$ <0416.g1:/ E F />` be made :eq:`equal <0416.g1:/ E F / = 0416.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$BE$ <0416.g1:/ B E />`; let the square :g:`$FH$ <0416.g1:< F H >>` be described on :g:`$AF$ <0416.g1:/ A F />`, and let :g:`$GH$ <0416.g1:/ G H />` be drawn through to :g:`$K$ <0416.g1:K>`.

I say that :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` has been cut at :g:`$H$ <0416.g1:H>` so as to make the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0416.g1:/ B H />` :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< H B D K > = 0416.g1:< A F G H >>` to the square on :g:`$AH$ <0416.g1:/ A H />`.

For, since the straight line :g:`$AC$ <0416.g1:/ A C />` has been :eq:`bisected <0416.g1:/ A E / = 0416.g1:/ E C />` at :g:`$E$ <0416.g1:E>`, and :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` is added to it,

.. assertion:: [II. 6] <II.6>

   the rectangle contained by :g:`$CF$ <0416.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` together with the square on :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EF$ <0416.g1:/ E F />`.

But :g:`$EF$ <0416.g1:/ E F />` is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:/ E F / = 0416.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$EB$ <0416.g1:/ E B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0416.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` together with the square on :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EB$ <0416.g1:/ E B />`.

But the squares on :g:`$BA$ <0416.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />` are equal to the square on :g:`$EB$ <0416.g1:/ E B />`, for the angle at :g:`$A$ <0416.g1:< B A E )>` is right: :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0416.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` together with the square on :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$BA$ <0416.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />`.

Let the square on :g:`$AE$ <0416.g1:/ A E />` be subtracted from each;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0416.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` which remains is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< C F G K > = 0416.g1:< A B D C >>` to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`.

.. _folio-i-0417:

.. folio:: 0417
   :page: 403

Now the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0416.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FA$ <0416.g1:/ F A />` is :g:`$FK$ <0416.g1:< C F G K >>`, for :g:`$AF$ <0416.g1:/ A F />` is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:/ A F / = 0416.g1:/ F G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0416.g1:/ F G />`; and the square on :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` is :g:`$AD$ <0416.g1:< A B D C >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FK$ <0416.g1:< C F G K >>` is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< C F G K > = 0416.g1:< A B D C >>` to :g:`$AD$ <0416.g1:< A B D C >>`.

Let :g:`$AK$ <0416.g1:< A H K C >>` be subtracted from each;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0416.g1:< A F G H >>` which remains is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< A F G H > = 0416.g1:< H B D K >>` to :g:`$HD$ <0416.g1:< H B D K >>`.

And :g:`$HD$ <0416.g1:< H B D K >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0416.g1:/ B H />`, for :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:/ A B / = 0416.g1:/ B D />` to :g:`$BD$ <0416.g1:/ B D />`; and :g:`$FH$ <0416.g1:< A F G H >>` is the square on :g:`$AH$ <0416.g1:/ A H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0416.g1:/ B H />` is :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< H B D K > = 0416.g1:< A F G H >>` to the square on :g:`$HA$ <0416.g1:/ H A />`.

.. assertion::

   therefore the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />` has been cut at :g:`$H$ <0416.g1:H>` so as to make the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0416.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0416.g1:/ B H />` :eq:`equal <0416.g1:< H B D K > = 0416.g1:< A F G H >>` to the square on :g:`$HA$ <0416.g1:/ H A />`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
