PROPOSITION 12.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In obtuse-angled triangles the square on the side subtending the obtuse angle is greater than the squares on the sides con- taining the obtuse angle by twice the rectangle contained by one of the sides about the obtuse angle, namely that on which the

.. _folio-i-0418:

.. folio:: 0418
   :page: 404

.. enunciation::

   perpendicular falls, and the straight line cut off outside by the perpendicular towards the obtuse angle.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0418.g1:< A B C >>` be an obtuse-angled triangle having the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0418.g1:< B A C )>` obtuse, and let :g:`$BD$ <0418.g1:/ B D />` be drawn from the point :g:`$B$ <0418.g1:B>` perpendicular to :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:[ C A ]>` produced;

I say that the square on :g:`$BC$ <0418.g1:/ B C />` is greater than the squares on :g:`$BA$ <0418.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0418.g1:/ A C />` by twice the rectangle contained by :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`.

.. picture:: 0418.g1.svg
   :alt: 0418.g1

For, since the straight line :g:`$CD$ <0418.g1:/ C D />` has been cut at random at the point :g:`$A$ <0418.g1:A>`, the square on :g:`$DC$ <0418.g1:/ D C />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />` and twice the rectangle contained by :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`. :just:`[II. 4] <II.4>`

Let the square on :g:`$DB$ <0418.g1:/ D B />` be added to each;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$CD$ <0418.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0418.g1:/ D B />` are equal to the squares on :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0418.g1:/ D B />` and twice the rectangle :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`.

But the square on :g:`$CB$ <0418.g1:/ C B />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$CD$ <0418.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0418.g1:/ D B />`, for the angle at :g:`$D$ <0418.g1:< C D B )>` is right; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

and the square on :g:`$AB$ <0418.g1:/ A B />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0418.g1:/ D B />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CB$ <0418.g1:/ C B />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0418.g1:/ A B />` and twice the rectangle contained by :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   so that the square on :g:`$CB$ <0418.g1:/ C B />` is greater than the squares on :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0418.g1:/ A B />` by twice the rectangle contained by :g:`$CA$ <0418.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0418.g1:/ A D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
