PROPOSITION 16.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The straight line drawn at right angles to the diameter of a circle from its extremity will fall outside the circle, and into the space between the straight line and the circumference another straight line cannot be interposed;\ [#f1]_ further the angle of the semicircle is greater, and the remaining angle less, than any acute rectilineal angle.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0044.g1:( A B C )>` be a circle about :g:`$D$ <0044.g1:D>` as centre and :g:`$AB$ <0044.g1:/ A B />` as
diameter;

.. _folio-ii-0044:

.. folio:: 0044
   :page: 38

I say that the straight line drawn from :g:`$A$ <0044.g1:A>` at right angles
to :g:`$AB$ <0044.g1:/ A B />` from its extremity will fall
outside the circle.

.. picture:: 0044.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram for Proposition 16

For suppose it does not, but,
if possible, let it fall within as :g:`$CA$ <0044.g1:/ C A />`,
and let :g:`$DC$ <0044.g1:/ D C />` be joined.

Since :g:`$DA$ <0044.g1:/ D A />` is :eq:`equal <0044.g1:/ D A / = 0044.g1:/ D C />` to :g:`$DC$ <0044.g1:/ D C />`,
the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0044.g1:< D A C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0044.g1:< D A C ) = 0044.g1:< A C D )>` to
the angle :g:`$ACD$ <0044.g1:< A C D )>`. :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

But the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0044.g1:< D A C )>` is right;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$ACD$ <0044.g1:< A C D )>` is also right:

thus, in the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0044.g1:< A C D >>`, the two angles :g:`$DAC$ <0044.g1:< D A C )>`, :g:`$ACD$ <0044.g1:< A C D )>` are
equal to two right angles: which is impossible. :just:`[I. 17] <I.17>`

Therefore the straight line drawn from the point :g:`$A$ <0044.g1:A>` at
right angles to :g:`$BA$ <0044.g1:/ B A />` will not fall within the circle.

Similarly we can prove that neither will it fall on the
circumference;

.. assertion::

   therefore it will fall outside.

Let it fall as :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />`;
I say next that into the space between the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />`
and the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>` another straight line cannot be
interposed.

For, if possible, let another straight line be so interposed,
as :g:`$FA$ <0044.g1:/ F A />`, and let :g:`$DG$ <0044.g1:/ D G />` be drawn from the point :g:`$D$ <0044.g1:D>` perpendicular
to :g:`$FA$ <0044.g1:/ F A />`.

Then, since the angle :g:`$AGD$ <0044.g1:< A G D )>` is right,

.. subclause::

   and the angle :g:`$DAG$ <0044.g1:< D A G )>` is less than a right angle,

.. assertion:: [I. 19] <I.19>

   :g:`$AD$ <0044.g1:/ A D />` is greater than :g:`$DG$ <0044.g1:/ D G />`.

But :g:`$DA$ <0044.g1:/ D A />` is :eq:`equal <0044.g1:/ D A / = 0044.g1:/ D H />` to :g:`$DH$ <0044.g1:/ D H />`;
therefore :g:`$DH$ <0044.g1:/ D H />` is greater than :g:`$DG$ <0044.g1:/ D G />`, the less than the
greater: which is impossible.

Therefore another straight line cannot be interposed into
the space between the straight line and the circumference.

I say further that the angle of the semicircle contained by
the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0044.g1:/ B A />` and the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>` is greater
than any acute rectilineal angle,
and the remaining angle contained by the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>`
and the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />` is less than any acute rectilineal angle.

For, if there is any rectilineal angle greater than the
angle contained by the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0044.g1:/ B A />` and the circumference
:g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>`, and any rectilineal angle less than the angle contained
by the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>` and the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />`, then
into the space between the circumference and the straight line
:g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />` a straight line will be interposed such as will make an
angle contained by straight lines which is greater than the
angle contained by the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0044.g1:/ B A />` and the circumference
:g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>`, and another angle contained by straight lines which
is less than the angle contained by the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>`
and the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />`.

.. _folio-ii-0045:

.. folio:: 0045
   :page: 39

But such a straight line cannot be interposed;\ [#f1]_
therefore there will not be any acute angle contained by
straight lines which is greater than the angle contained by
the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0044.g1:/ B A />` and the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>`, nor yet
any acute angle contained by straight lines which is less than
the angle contained by the circumference :g:`$CHA$ <0044.g1:( C H A )>` and the
straight line :g:`$AE$ <0044.g1:/ A E />`.—

.. porism::

   From this it is manifest that the straight line
   drawn at right angles to the diameter of a circle from its
   extremity touches the circle.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **cannot be interposed**, literally "will not fall in between" ($\text{οὐ παρεμπεσεῖται}$).
