PROPOSITION 26.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In equal circles equal angles stand on equal circumferences, whether they stand at the centres or at the circumferences.

.. _folio-ii-0063:

.. folio:: 0063
   :page: 57

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0063.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0063.g1:( D E F )>` be equal circles, and in them let there
be equal angles, namely at the centres the angles :g:`$BGC$ <0063.g1:< B G C )>`,
:g:`$EHF$ <0063.g1:< E H F )>`, and at the circumferences the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0063.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0063.g1:< E D F )>`;
I say that the circumference :g:`$BKC$ <0063.g1:( B K C )>` is equal to the circumference
:g:`$ELF$ <0063.g1:( E L F )>`.

.. picture:: 0063.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For let :g:`$BC$ <0063.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0063.g1:/ E F />` be joined.

Now, since the circles :g:`$ABC$ <0063.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0063.g1:( D E F )>` are equal,

.. assertion::

   the radii are equal.

Thus the two straight lines :g:`$BG$ <0063.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GC$ <0063.g1:/ G C />` are equal to the
two straight lines :g:`$EH$ <0063.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HF$ <0063.g1:/ H F />`;

.. subclause::

   and the angle at :g:`$G$ <0063.g1:G>` is equal to the angle at :g:`$H$ <0063.g1:H>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$BC$ <0063.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the base :g:`$EF$ <0063.g1:/ E F />`.

And, since the angle at :g:`$A$ <0063.g1:A>` is equal to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0063.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [III. Def. 11] <III.def.11>

   the segment :g:`$BAC$ <0063.g1:( B A C )>` is similar to the segment :g:`$EDF$ <0063.g1:( E D F )>`;

and they are upon equal straight lines.

But similar segments of circles on equal straight lines are
equal to one another; :just:`[III. 24] <III.24>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the segment :g:`$BAC$ <0063.g1:( B A C )>` is equal to :g:`$EDF$ <0063.g1:( E D F )>`.

But the whole circle :g:`$ABC$ <0063.g1:( A B C )>` is also equal to the whole circle
:g:`$DEF$ <0063.g1:( D E F )>`;
therefore the circumference :g:`$BKC$ <0063.g1:( B K C )>` which remains is equal to
the circumference :g:`$ELF$ <0063.g1:( E L F )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
