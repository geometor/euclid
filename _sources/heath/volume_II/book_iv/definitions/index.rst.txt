DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _IV.def.1:

1. A :term:`rectilineal figure <rectilineal-figure>` is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a rectilineal figure** <inscribed-in-rectilineal-figure>` when the respective :term:`angles <plane-angle>` of the inscribed :term:`figure <figure>` lie on the respective sides of that in which it is inscribed.

.. _IV.def.2:

2. Similarly a :term:`figure <figure>` is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a figure** <circumscribed-about-figure>` when the respective sides of the circumscribed figure pass through the respective :term:`angles <plane-angle>` of that about which it is circumscribed.

.. _IV.def.3:

3. A :term:`rectilineal figure <rectilineal-figure>` is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a circle** <inscribed-in-circle>` when each :term:`angle <plane-angle>` of the inscribed :term:`figure <figure>` lies on the circumference of the :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _IV.def.4:

4. A :term:`rectilineal figure <rectilineal-figure>` is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a circle** <circumscribed-about-circle>`, when each side of the circumscribed :term:`figure <figure>` touches the circumference of the :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _IV.def.5:

5. Similarly a :term:`circle <circle>` is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a figure** <circle-inscribed-in-figure>` when the circumference of the circle touches each side of the :term:`figure <figure>` in which it is inscribed.

.. _IV.def.6:

6. A :term:`circle <circle>` is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a figure** <circumscribed-about-figure>` when the circumference of the circle passes through each :term:`angle <plane-angle>` of the :term:`figure <figure>` about which it is circumscribed.

.. _IV.def.7:

7. A :term:`straight line <straight-line>` is said to be :term:`**fitted into a circle** <fitted-into-circle>` when its :term:`extremities <extremity>` are on the circumference of the :term:`circle <circle>`.
