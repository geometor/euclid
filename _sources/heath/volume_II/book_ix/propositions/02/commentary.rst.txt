Commentary on Proposition IX.2
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.2 <IX.2>`.

.. folio:: 0391
   :page: 385

If $ab$ is a square number, $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. (The converse of IX. 1.) For

$$a : b = a^2 : ab. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$

And $a^2$, $ab$ being square numbers, and therefore similar plane numbers, they have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

Therefore $a$, $b$ also have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

whence $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. :just:`[VIII. 20] <VIII.20>`
