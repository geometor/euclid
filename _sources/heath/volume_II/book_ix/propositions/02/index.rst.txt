PROPOSITION 2.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers by multiplying one another make a square number, they are similar plane numbers.

Let :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` be two numbers, and let :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` make the square number :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` are similar plane numbers.

.. picture:: 0391.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 2 Diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>` is square.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>` is square, and :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>` is so also,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>` are similar plane numbers.

.. assertion::

   Therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>`. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$D$ <0391.g1:D>` is to :g:`$C$ <0391.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` also. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   But, if one mean proportional number fall between two numbers, they are similar plane numbers; :just:`[VIII. 20] <VIII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0391.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0391.g1:B>` are similar plane numbers.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
