Commentary on Proposition IX.3
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.3 <IX.3>`.

.. folio:: 0392
   :page: 386

The product of :math:`a^3` into itself, or :math:`a^3 \cdot a^3`, is a cube. For

.. math::

   1 : a = a : a^2 = a^2 : a^3.

Therefore between :math:`1` and :math:`a^3` there are two mean proportionals. Also

.. math::

   1 : a^3 = a^3 : a^3 \cdot a^3.

Therefore two mean proportionals fall between :math:`a^3` and :math:`a^3 \cdot a^3`. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

(It is true that VIII. 8 is only enunciated of two pairs of numbers, but the proof is equally valid if one number of one pair is unity.) And :math:`a^3` is a cube number: therefore :math:`a^3 \cdot a^3` is also cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
