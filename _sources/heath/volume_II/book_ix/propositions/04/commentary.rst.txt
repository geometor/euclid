Commentary on Proposition IX.4
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.4 <IX.4>`.

.. folio:: 0393
   :page: 387

The product of two cubes, say $a^3 \cdot b^3$, is a cube. For

$$a^3 : b^3 = a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3 \cdot b^3. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$

And two mean proportionals fall between $a^3$, $b^3$ which are similar solid numbers. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

Therefore two mean proportionals fall between $a^3 \cdot a^3$, $a^3 \cdot b^3$. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

But $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is a cube: :just:`[IX. 3] <IX.3>`

therefore $a^3 \cdot b^3$ is a cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
