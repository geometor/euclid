Commentary on Proposition IX.5
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.5 <IX.5>`.

.. folio:: 0394
   :page: 388

If the product :math:`a^3b` is a cube number, :math:`b` is cube. By IX. 3, the product :math:`a^3 \cdot a^3` is a cube. And

.. math::

   a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3b = a^3 : b. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}

The first two terms are cubes, and therefore "similar solids"; therefore there are two mean proportionals between them. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

Therefore there are two mean proportionals between :math:`a^3`, :math:`b`. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And :math:`a^3` is a cube: therefore :math:`b` is a cube number. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
