Commentary on Proposition IX.16
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.16 <IX.16>`.

.. folio:: 0412
   :page: 406

If *a*, *b* are prime to one another, they can have no integral third proportional.

If possible, let

$$a : b = b : x.$$

Therefore :just:`[VII. 20, 21]` *a* measures *b* ; and *a*, *b* have the common measure *a*, which is contrary to the hypothesis.
