PROPOSITION 32.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Each of the numbers which are continually doubled beginning from a dyad is even-times even only.

For let as many numbers as we please, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, have been continually doubled beginning from the dyad :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`;

I say that :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` are even-times even only.

.. picture:: 0424.g1.svg
   :alt: Book IX, Proposition 32

.. assertion::

   Now that each of the numbers :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` is even-times even is manifest; for it is doubled from a dyad.

I say that it is also even-times even only.

For let an unit be set out.

.. subclause::

   Since then as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit are in continued proportion, and the number :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>` after the unit is prime,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, the greatest of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, will not be measured by any other number except :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`. :just:`[IX. 13] <IX.13>`

.. subclause::

   And each of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>` is even;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 8] <VII.def.8>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` is even-times even only.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that each of the numbers :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>` is even-times even only.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
