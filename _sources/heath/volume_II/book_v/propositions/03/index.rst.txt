PROPOSITION 3.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude be the same multiple of a second that a third is of a fourth, and if equimultiples be taken of the first and third, then also :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>` the magnitudes taken will be equimultiples respectively, the one of the second and the other of the fourth.

Let a first magnitude :g:`A <0147.g1:A>` be the same multiple of a second :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that a third :g:`C <0147.g1:C>` is of a fourth :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`, and let equimultiples :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />` be taken of :g:`A <0147.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0147.g1:C>`;

I say that :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />` is the same multiple of :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />` is of :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`.

For, since :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />` is the same multiple of :g:`A <0147.g1:A>` that :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />` is of :g:`C <0147.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as many magnitudes as there are in :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />` equal to :g:`A <0147.g1:A>`, so many also are there in :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />` equal to :g:`C <0147.g1:C>`.

.. _folio-ii-0147:

.. folio:: 0147
   :page: 141

Let :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />` be divided into the magnitudes :g:`EK <0147.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`KF <0147.g1:/ K F />` :eq:`equal <0147.g1:/ E K / = 0147.g1:/ K F / = 0147.g1:A>` to :g:`A <0147.g1:A>`, and :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />` into the magnitudes :g:`GL <0147.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`LH <0147.g1:/ L H />` :eq:`equal <0147.g1:/ G L / = 0147.g1:/ L H / = 0147.g1:C>` to :g:`C <0147.g1:C>`; then the multitude of the magnitudes :g:`EK <0147.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`KF <0147.g1:/ K F />` will be equal to the multitude of the magnitudes :g:`GL <0147.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`LH <0147.g1:/ L H />`.

.. picture:: 0147.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 3 diagram

And, since :g:`A <0147.g1:A>` is the same multiple of :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that :g:`C <0147.g1:C>` is of :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`,

.. subclause::

   while :g:`EK <0147.g1:/ E K />` is :eq:`equal <0147.g1:/ E K / = 0147.g1:A; 0147.g1:/ G L / = 0147.g1:C>` to :g:`A <0147.g1:A>`, and :g:`GL <0147.g1:/ G L />` to :g:`C <0147.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`EK <0147.g1:/ E K />` is the same multiple of :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that :g:`GL <0147.g1:/ G L />` is of :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`KF <0147.g1:/ K F />` is the same multiple of :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that :g:`LH <0147.g1:/ L H />` is of :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`.

Since, then, a first magnitude :g:`EK <0147.g1:/ E K />` is the same multiple of a second :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that a third :g:`GL <0147.g1:/ G L />` is of a fourth :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`, and a fifth :g:`KF <0147.g1:/ K F />` is also the same multiple of the second :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that a sixth :g:`LH <0147.g1:/ L H />` is of the fourth :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the first and fifth, :g:`EF <0147.g1:/ E F />`, is also the same multiple of the second :g:`B <0147.g1:B>` that the sum of the third and sixth, :g:`GH <0147.g1:/ G H />`, is of the fourth :g:`D <0147.g1:D>`. :just:`[V. 2] <V.2>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
