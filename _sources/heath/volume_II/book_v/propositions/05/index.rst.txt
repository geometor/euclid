PROPOSITION 5.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a magnitude be the same multiple of a magnitude that a part subtracted is of a part subtracted, the remainder will also be the same multiple of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.

For let the magnitude :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` be the same multiple of the magnitude :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` that the part :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` subtracted is of the part :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` subtracted;

I say that the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is also the same multiple of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0151.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 5 Diagram

For, whatever multiple :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, let :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` be made that multiple of :g:`$CG$ <0151.g1:/ C G />`.\ [#f1]_

Then, since :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is of :g:`$GC$ <0151.g1:/ C G />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 1] <V.1>

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$GF$ <0151.g1:/ G F />`.

But, by the assumption, :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is the same multiple of each of the magnitudes :g:`$GF$ <0151.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GF$ <0151.g1:/ G F />` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` be subtracted from each;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$GC$ <0151.g1:/ C G />` is equal to the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`.

.. _folio-ii-0152:

.. folio:: 0152
   :page: 146

And, since :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is of :g:`$GC$ <0151.g1:/ C G />`,

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$GC$ <0151.g1:/ C G />` is equal to :g:`$DF$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is of :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`.

But, by hypothesis,

.. subclause::

   :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is the same multiple of :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` that :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`.

That is, the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` will be the same multiple of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **let EB be made that multiple of** :g:`$CG$ <0151.g1:/ C G />`, τοσαυταπλάσιον γεγονέτω καὶ τὸ ΕΒ τοῦ ΓΗ. From this way of stating the construction one might suppose that $CG$ was given and :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` had to be found equal to a certain multiple of it. But in fact :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is what is given and :g:`$CG$ <0151.g1:/ C G />` has to be found, i.e. :g:`$CG$ <0151.g1:/ C G />` has to be constructed as a certain *submultiple* of :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />`.
