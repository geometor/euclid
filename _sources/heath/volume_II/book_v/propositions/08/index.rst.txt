PROPOSITION 8.
==============

.. enunciation::

   Of unequal magnitudes, the greater has to the same a greater ratio than the less has; and the same has to the less a greater ratio than it has to the greater.

.. _folio-ii-0156:

.. folio:: 0156
   :page: 150

Let :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` be unequal magnitudes, and let :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` be greater ;
let :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` be another, chance,
magnitude ;
I say that :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` a
greater ratio than :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` has to
:g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, and :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` has to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` a greater
ratio than it has to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0156.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 8 Diagram (Case 1)

For, since :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` is greater
than :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`, let :g:`$BE$ <0156.g1:/ E B />` be made equal
to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ;
then the less of the magnitudes
:g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />`, if multiplied,
will sometime be greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`. :just:`[V. Def. 4] <V.def.4>`

[Case 1.]

First, let :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />` be less than :g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />` ;
let :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />` be multiplied, and let :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` be a multiple of it which is
greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;
then, whatever multiple :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is of :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />`, let :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` be made the
same multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />` and :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` of :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ;
and let :g:`$L$ <0156.g1:L>` be taken double of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` triple of it, and successive
multiples increasing by one, until what is taken is a multiple
of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` and the first that is greater than :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>`. Let it be taken,
and let it be :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` which is quadruple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` and the first
multiple of it that is greater than :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>`.

Then, since :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is less than :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` first,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is not less than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`.

And, since :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` is of
:g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 1] <V.1>

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

But :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0156.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is of :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` that :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is of :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ; therefore :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`.

Again, since :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` is the same multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />` that :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is
of :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`,
and :g:`$EB$ <0156.g1:/ E B />` is equal to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` is equal to :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>`.

.. _folio-ii-0157:

.. folio:: 0157
   :page: 151

But :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is not less than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither is :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` less than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`.

And :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` together.

But :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` together are equal to :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`, inasmuch as :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` is
triple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, and :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` together are quadruple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, while
:g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is also quadruple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ; whence :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` together are equal
to :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`.

But :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is greater than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is in excess of :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`,

while :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is not in excess of :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`.

And :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`, while :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is
another, chance, multiple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` a greater ratio than :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` has to :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`.

I say next, that :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` also has to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` a greater ratio than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`
has to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

For, with the same construction, we can prove similarly
that :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is in excess of :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>`, while :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is not in excess of :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`.

And :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is a multiple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`,
while :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` are other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` has to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` a greater ratio than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` has to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

[Case 2.]

Again, let :g:`$AE$ <0157.g1:/ A E />` be greater than :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`.

Then the less, :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`, if multiplied, will sometime be greater
than :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>`. :just:`[V. Def. 4] <V.def.4>`

Let it be multiplied, and
let :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />` be a multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`
and greater than :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>` ;
and, whatever multiple :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />` is
of :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`, let :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />` be made the
same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0157.g1:/ A E />`, and :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>`
of :g:`$C$ <0157.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0157.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 8 Diagram (Case 2)

Then we can prove similarly
that :g:`$FH$ <0157.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>` are equimultiples
of :g:`$AB$ <0157.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0157.g1:C>` ;
and, similarly, let :g:`$N$ <0157.g1:N>` be taken a multiple of :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>` but the first
that is greater than :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />`,
so that :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />` is again not less than :g:`$M$ <0157.g1:M>`.

.. _folio-ii-0158:

.. folio:: 0158
   :page: 152

But :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />` is greater than :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$FH$ <0157.g1:/ F H />` is in excess of :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0157.g1:M>`, that is, of :g:`$N$ <0157.g1:N>`.

Now :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>` is not in excess of :g:`$N$ <0157.g1:N>`, inasmuch as :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />` also, which
is greater than :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />`, that is, than :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>`, is not in excess of :g:`$N$ <0157.g1:N>`.

And in the same manner, by following the above argument
, we complete the demonstration.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
