Commentary on Proposition V.12
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition V.12 <V.12>`.

.. folio:: 0166
   :page: 160

Algebraically, if $a : a' = b : b' = c : c'$ etc., each ratio is equal to the ratio $(a + b + c + \dots) : (a' + b' + c' + \dots)$.

This theorem is quoted by :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`, :cite:`*Eth. Nic.* V. 7, 1131 b 14 <aristotle:eth-nic:v-7-1131-b-14>`, in the shortened form "the whole is to the whole what each part is to each part (respectively)."
