Commentary on Book VI Definition 4
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book VI Definitions <VI.def>`.

.. container:: center

   Ὕψος ἐστὶ παντὸς σχήματος ἡ ἀπὸ τῆς κορυφῆς ἐπὶ τὴν βάσιν κάθετος ἀγομένη.

The definition of “height” is not found in :person:`Campanus <campanus>` and is perhaps rightly suspected, since it does not apply in terms to parallelograms, parallelepipeds, cylinders and prisms, though it is used in the *Elements* with reference to these latter figures. :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` does not appear to know altitude (:term:`ὕψος <height>`) in the mathematical sense; he uses κάθετος of triangles (:cite:`*Meteorologica* III. 3, 373 a 11 <aristotle:meteorologica:iii-3-373-a-11>`). The term is however readily understood, and scarcely requires definition.
