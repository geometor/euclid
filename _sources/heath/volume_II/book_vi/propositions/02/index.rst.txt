PROPOSITION 2.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be drawn parallel to one of the sides of a triangle, it will cut the sides of the triangle proportionally; and, if the sides of the triangle be cut proportionally, the line joining the points of section will be parallel to the remaining side of the triangle.

For let :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0200.g1:/ B C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0200.g1:< A B C >>`;

I say that, as :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`.

.. picture:: 0200.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 2

For let :g:`$BE$ <0200.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$CD$ <0200.g1:/ C D />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` is :eq:`equal <0200.g1:< B D E > = 0200.g1:< C D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>`;

for they are on the same base :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />` and in the same parallels :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$BC$ <0200.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[I. 38] <I.38>`

And the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>` is another area.

But equals have the same ratio to the same; :just:`[V. 7] <V.7>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` is to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` is to :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, so is :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`; for, being under the same height, the perpendicular drawn from :g:`$E$ <0200.g1:E>` to :g:`$AB$ <0200.g1:/ A B />`, they are to one another as their bases. :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also, as the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>` is to :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, so is :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`.

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   Therefore also, as :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`.

.. folio:: 0201
   :page: 195

Again, let the sides :g:`$AB$ <0200.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0200.g1:/ A C />` of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0200.g1:< A B C >>` be cut proportionally, so that, as :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`; and let :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />` be joined.

I say that :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />` is parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0200.g1:/ B C />`.

For, with the same construction, since, as :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`, but, as :g:`$BD$ <0200.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0200.g1:/ D A />`, so is the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, and, as :g:`$CE$ <0200.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0200.g1:/ E A />`, so is the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

therefore also,

.. assertion::

   as the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` is to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. subclause::

   Therefore each of the triangles :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>`, :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>` has the same ratio to :g:`$ADE$ <0200.g1:< A D E >>`.

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$BDE$ <0200.g1:< B D E >>` is :eq:`equal <0200.g1:< B D E > = 0200.g1:< C D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$CDE$ <0200.g1:< C D E >>`;

.. subclause::

   and they are on the same base :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   But equal triangles which are on the same base are also in the same parallels. :just:`[I. 39] <I.39>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$DE$ <0200.g1:/ D E />` is parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0200.g1:/ B C />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
