PROPOSITION 5.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two triangles have their sides proportional, the triangles will be equiangular and will have those angles equal which the corresponding sides subtend.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F >>` be two triangles having their sides proportional, so that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$AB$ <0209.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0209.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0209.g2:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   and further, as :g:`$BA$ <0209.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0209.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`$ED$ <0209.g2:/ E D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0209.g2:/ D F />`;

I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0209.g1:< A B C ) = 0209.g2:< D E F ); 0209.g1:< B C A ) = 0209.g2:< E F D ); 0209.g1:< B A C ) = 0209.g2:< E D F )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F >>`, and they will have those angles :eq:`equal <0209.g1:< A B C ) = 0209.g2:< D E F ); 0209.g1:< B C A ) = 0209.g2:< E F D ); 0209.g1:< B A C ) = 0209.g2:< E D F )>` which the corresponding sides subtend, namely the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F )>`, the angle :g:`$BCA$ <0209.g1:< B C A )>` to the angle :g:`$EFD$ <0209.g2:< E F D )>`, and further the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0209.g1:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0209.g2:< E D F )>`.

For on the straight line :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`, and at the points :g:`$E$ <0209.g2:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0209.g2:F>` on it, let there be constructed the angle :g:`$FEG$ <0209.g2:< F E G )>` :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< F E G ) = 0209.g1:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>`, and the angle :g:`$EFG$ <0209.g2:< E F G )>` :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< E F G ) = 0209.g1:< A C B )>` to the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0209.g1:< A C B )>`; :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

.. _folio-ii-0209:

.. folio:: 0209
   :page: 203

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$A$ <0209.g1:< A )>` is :eq:`equal <0209.g1:< A ) = 0209.g2:< G )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$G$ <0209.g2:< G )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0209.g1:< A B C ) = 0209.g2:< F E G ); 0209.g1:< A C B ) = 0209.g2:< E F G ); 0209.g1:< A ) = 0209.g2:< G )>` with the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>`.

.. picture:: 0209.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 5 diagram 1

.. picture:: 0209.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 5 diagram 2

Therefore in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>` the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and those are corresponding sides which subtend the equal angles; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0209.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`.

But, as :g:`$AB$ <0209.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />`, so by hypothesis is :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore, as :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`, so is :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`.

Therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` has the same ratio to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:/ D E / = 0209.g2:/ G E />` to :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DF$ <0209.g2:/ D F />` is also :eq:`equal <0209.g2:/ D F / = 0209.g2:/ G F />` to :g:`$GF$ <0209.g2:/ G F />`.

Since then :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:/ D E / = 0209.g2:/ E G />` to :g:`$EG$ <0209.g2:/ E G />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />` is common,

.. subclause::

   the two sides :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />` are :eq:`equal <0209.g2:/ D E / = 0209.g2:/ G E />; 0209.g2:/ E F / = 0209.g2:/ E F />` to the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. subclause::

   and the base :g:`$DF$ <0209.g2:/ D F />` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:/ D F / = 0209.g2:/ F G />` to the base :g:`$FG$ <0209.g2:/ F G />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< D E F ) = 0209.g2:< G E F )>` to the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>`,

.. subclause::

   and the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F >>` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< D E F > = 0209.g2:< G E F >>` to the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>`, and the remaining angles are :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< D F E ) = 0209.g2:< G F E ); 0209.g2:< E D F ) = 0209.g2:< E G F )>` to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend. :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0209.g2:< D F E )>` is also :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< D F E ) = 0209.g2:< G F E ); 0209.g2:< E D F ) = 0209.g2:< E G F )>` to the angle :g:`$GFE$ <0209.g2:< G F E )>`, and the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0209.g2:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`$EGF$ <0209.g2:< E G F )>`.

And, since the angle :g:`$FED$ <0209.g2:< F E D )>` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< F E D ) = 0209.g2:< G E F )>` to the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>`,

.. subclause::

   while the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0209.g2:< G E F ) = 0209.g1:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0209.g1:< A B C ) = 0209.g2:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F )>`.

.. _folio-ii-0210:

.. folio:: 0210
   :page: 204

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0209.g1:< A C B )>` is also :eq:`equal <0209.g1:< A C B ) = 0209.g2:< D F E ); 0209.g1:< A ) = 0209.g2:< D )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0209.g2:< D F E )>`, and further, the angle at :g:`$A$ <0209.g1:< A )>` to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0209.g2:< D )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0209.g1:< A B C ) = 0209.g2:< D E F ); 0209.g1:< A C B ) = 0209.g2:< D F E ); 0209.g1:< A ) = 0209.g2:< D )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
