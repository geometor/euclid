PROPOSITION 12.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To three given straight lines to find a fourth proportional.

Let :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>` be the three given straight lines;

thus it is required to find a fourth proportional to :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0221.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12

Let two straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0221.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$DF$ <0221.g1:/ D F />` be set out containing any angle :g:`$EDF$ <0221.g1:< E D F )>`; let :g:`$DG$ <0221.g1:/ D G />` be made :eq:`equal <0221.g1:/ D G / = 0221.g1:A>` to :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>`, :g:`$GE$ <0221.g1:/ G E />` :eq:`equal <0221.g1:/ G E / = 0221.g1:B>` to :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, and further :g:`$DH$ <0221.g1:/ D H />` :eq:`equal <0221.g1:/ D H / = 0221.g1:C>` to :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>`; let :g:`$GH$ <0221.g1:/ G H />` be joined, and let :g:`$EF$ <0221.g1:/ E F />` be drawn through :g:`$E$ <0221.g1:E>` parallel to it. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. subclause::

   Since, then, :g:`$GH$ <0221.g1:/ G H />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$EF$ <0221.g1:/ E F />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0221.g1:< D E F >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   therefore, as :g:`$DG$ <0221.g1:/ D G />` is to :g:`$GE$ <0221.g1:/ G E />`, so is :g:`$DH$ <0221.g1:/ D H />` to :g:`$HF$ <0221.g1:/ H F />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DG$ <0221.g1:/ D G />` is :eq:`equal <0221.g1:/ D G / = 0221.g1:A; 0221.g1:/ G E / = 0221.g1:B; 0221.g1:/ D H / = 0221.g1:C>` to :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>`, :g:`$GE$ <0221.g1:/ G E />` to :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, and :g:`$DH$ <0221.g1:/ D H />` to :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>` to :g:`$HF$ <0221.g1:/ H F />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore to the three given straight lines :g:`$A$ <0221.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0221.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0221.g1:C>` a fourth proportional :g:`$HF$ <0221.g1:/ H F />` has been found. :just:`Q. E. F.`
