PROPOSITION 13.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To two given straight lines to find a mean proportional.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0222.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0222.g1:/ B C />` be the two given straight lines;

thus it is required to find a mean proportional to :g:`$AB$ <0222.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0222.g1:/ B C />`.

Let them be placed in a straight line, and let the semicircle :g:`$ADC$ <0222.g1:( A D C )>` be described on :g:`$AC$ <0222.g1:/ A C />`; let :g:`$BD$ <0222.g1:/ B D />` be drawn from the point :g:`$B$ <0222.g1:B>` at right angles to the straight line :g:`$AC$ <0222.g1:/ A C />`, and let :g:`$AD$ <0222.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0222.g1:/ D C />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0222.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 13

.. assertion::

   Since the angle :g:`$ADC$ <0222.g1:< A D C )>` is an angle in a semicircle, it is right. :just:`[III. 31] <III.31>`

.. subclause::

   And, since, in the right-angled triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0222.g1:< A D C >>`, :g:`$DB$ <0222.g1:/ D B />` has been drawn from the right angle perpendicular to the base,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DB$ <0222.g1:/ D B />` is a mean proportional between the segments of the base, :g:`$AB$ <0222.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0222.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[VI. 8, Por.]`

.. qed::

   Therefore to the two given straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0222.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0222.g1:/ B C />` a mean proportional :g:`$DB$ <0222.g1:/ D B />` has been found. :just:`Q. E. F.`
