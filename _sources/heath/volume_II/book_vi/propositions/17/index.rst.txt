PROPOSITION 17.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If three straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the square on the mean; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the square on the mean, the three straight lines will be proportional.

Let the three straight lines :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` be proportional, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`;

I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0234.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 17

Let :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` be made :eq:`equal <0234.g1:D = 0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

Then, since, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`,

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is :eq:`equal <0234.g1:B = 0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

But, if four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained by the means. :just:`[VI. 16] <VI.16>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` is the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, for :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is :eq:`equal <0234.g1:B = 0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

Next, let the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` be equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`;

I say that, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

.. _folio-ii-0235:

.. folio:: 0235
   :page: 229

For, with the same construction, since the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`,

.. subclause::

   while the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`, for :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is :eq:`equal <0234.g1:B = 0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`.

But, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to that contained by the means, the four straight lines are proportional. :just:`[VI. 16] <VI.16>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is :eq:`equal <0234.g1:B = 0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
