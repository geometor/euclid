Commentary on Proposition VI.21
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VI.21 <VI.21>`.

.. folio:: 0246
   :page: 240

It will be observed that the text above omits a step which the editions generally have before the final inference "Therefore :g:`A <0246.g1:A>` is similar to :g:`B <0246.g1:B>`." The words omitted are "so that :g:`A <0246.g1:A>` is also equiangular with :g:`B <0246.g1:B>` and [with :g:`B <0246.g1:B>`] has the sides about the equal angles proportional." :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>` follows $P$ in leaving them out, conjecturing that they may be an addition of Theon's.
