PROPOSITION 30.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To cut a given finite straight line in extreme and mean ratio.

Let :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />` be the given finite straight line ;

thus it is required to cut :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />` in extreme and mean ratio.

.. picture:: 0273.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 30

.. subclause::

   On :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />` let the square :g:`BC <0273.g1:< B C >>` be described ; and let there be applied to :g:`AC <0273.g1:/ A C />` the parallelogram :g:`CD <0273.g1:< C D >>` :eq:`equal <0273.g1:< C D > = 0273.g1:< B C >>` to :g:`BC <0273.g1:< B C >>` and exceeding by the figure :g:`AD <0273.g1:< A D >>` similar to :g:`BC <0273.g1:< B C >>`. :just:`[VI. 29] <VI.29>`

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`BC <0273.g1:< B C >>` is a square ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AD <0273.g1:< A D >>` is also a square.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`BC <0273.g1:< B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0273.g1:< B C > = 0273.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`CD <0273.g1:< C D >>`, let :g:`CE <0273.g1:< C E >>` be subtracted from each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`BF <0273.g1:< B F >>` is :eq:`equal <0273.g1:< B F > = 0273.g1:< A D >>` to the remainder :g:`AD <0273.g1:< A D >>`.

.. _folio-ii-0274:

.. folio:: 0274
   :page: 268

.. subclause::

   But it is also equiangular with it ;

.. assertion::

   therefore in :g:`BF <0273.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`AD <0273.g1:< A D >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional ; :just:`[VI. 14] <VI.14>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`FE <0273.g1:/ F E />` is to :g:`ED <0273.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`EB <0273.g1:/ E B />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`FE <0273.g1:/ F E />` is :eq:`equal <0273.g1:/ F E / = 0273.g1:/ A B />; 0273.g1:/ E D / = 0273.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />`, and :g:`ED <0273.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`BA <0273.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`EB <0273.g1:/ E B />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />` is also greater than :g:`EB <0273.g1:/ E B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the straight line :g:`AB <0273.g1:/ A B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`E <0273.g1:E>`, and the greater segment of it is :g:`AE <0273.g1:/ A E />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. F.`
