Πολλαπλάσιος δὲ ὁ μείζων τοῦ ἐλάσσονος, ὅταν καταμετρῆται ὑπὸ τοῦ ἐλάσσονος.

The definition of a *multiple* is identical with that in V. Def. 2, except that the masculine of the adjectives is used agreeing with :term:`ἀριθμός <number>` understood instead of the neuter agreeing with :term:`μέγεθος <megethos>` understood. :person:`Nicomachus <nicomachus>` (:cite:`I. 18, 1 <nicomachus:intro-arith:i-18-1>`) defines a multiple as being “a species of the greater which is naturally first in order and origin, being the number which, when considered in comparison with another, contains it in itself completely more than once.”
