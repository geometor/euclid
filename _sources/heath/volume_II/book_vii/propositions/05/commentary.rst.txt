Commentary on Proposition VII.5
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VII.5 <VII.5>`.

.. folio:: 0310
   :page: 304

If

$$a = \frac{1}{n}b, \text{ and } c = \frac{1}{n}d, \text{ then}$$

$$a + c = \frac{1}{n}(b + d).$$

The proposition is of course true for any quantity of pairs of numbers similarly related, as is the next proposition also; and both propositions are used in the extended form in VII. 9, 10.
