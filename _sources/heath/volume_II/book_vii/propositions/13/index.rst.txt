PROPOSITION 13.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If four numbers be proportional, they will also be proportional alternately.

Let the four numbers :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>` be proportional, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>`;

I say that they will also be proportional alternately, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>`, so will :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>` be to :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0319.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever part or parts :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is of :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>`, the same part or the same parts is :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>` of :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>` also. :just:`[VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, alternately, whatever part or parts :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is of :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>`, the same part or the same parts is :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>` of :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>` also. :just:`[VII. 10] <VII.10>`

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0319.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0319.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0319.g1:B>` to :g:`$D$ <0319.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
