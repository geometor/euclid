PROPOSITION 19.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If four numbers be proportional, the number produced from the first and fourth will be equal to the number produced from the second and third; and, if the number produced from the first and fourth be equal to that produced from the second and third, the four numbers will be proportional.

Let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` be four numbers in proportion, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`; and let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` make :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` make :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`;

I say that :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` is :eq:`equal <0325.g1:E = 0325.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

For let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` make :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`.

.. _folio-ii-0325:

.. folio:: 0325
   :page: 319

.. subclause::

   Since, then, :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` has made :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`, and by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` has made :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, the number :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying the two numbers :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` has made :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`, :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   Therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`.

.. picture:: 0325.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 19

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` has made :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`, but, further, :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>` has also by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` made :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`, the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>` by multiplying a certain number :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` have made :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`, :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But further, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` also;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` is to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` has to each of the numbers :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>` the same ratio;

.. assertion:: [cf. V. 9]

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` is :eq:`equal <0325.g1:E = 0325.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

Again, let :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` be :eq:`equal <0325.g1:E = 0325.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`;

I say that, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`.

For, with the same construction,

.. subclause::

   since :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` is :eq:`equal <0325.g1:E = 0325.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`,

.. assertion:: [cf. V. 7]

   therefore, as :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` is to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` is to :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`, :just:`[VII. 17] <VII.17>` and, as :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>` is to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`. :just:`[VII. 18] <VII.18>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
