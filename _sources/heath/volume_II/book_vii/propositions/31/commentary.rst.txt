Commentary on Proposition VII.31
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VII.31 <VII.31>`.

.. folio:: 0339

:person:`Heiberg <heiberg>` relegates to the Appendix an alternative proof of this proposition, to the following effect. Since :g:`$A$ <0338.g1:A>` is composite, some number will measure it. Let :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>` be the *least* such number. I say that :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>` is prime. For, if not, :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>` is composite, and some number will measure it, say :g:`$C$ <0338.g1:C>`; so that :g:`$C$ <0338.g1:C>` is less than :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>`. But, since :g:`$C$ <0338.g1:C>` measures :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>`, and :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>` measures :g:`$A$ <0338.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0338.g1:C>` must measure :g:`$A$ <0338.g1:A>`. And :g:`$C$ <0338.g1:C>` is less than :g:`$B$ <0338.g1:B>`: which is contrary to the hypothesis.
