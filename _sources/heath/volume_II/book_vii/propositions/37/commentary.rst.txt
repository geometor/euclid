Commentary on Proposition VII.37
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VII.37 <VII.37>`.

.. folio:: 0348
   :page: 342

If :math:`b` measures :math:`a`, then :math:`\frac{1}{b}\text{th}` of :math:`a` is a whole number.

Let :math:`a = m \cdot b`.

Now :math:`m = m \cdot 1`.

Thus :math:`1, m, b, a` satisfy the enunciation of VII. 15; therefore :math:`m` measures :math:`a` the same number of times that :math:`1` measures :math:`b`.

But :math:`1` is :math:`\frac{1}{b}\text{th}` part of :math:`b`; therefore :math:`m` is :math:`\frac{1}{b}\text{th}` part of :math:`a`.
