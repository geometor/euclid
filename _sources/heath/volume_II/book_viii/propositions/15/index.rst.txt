PROPOSITION 15.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a cube number measure a cube number, the side will also measure the side; and, if the side measure the side, the cube will also measure the cube.

For let the cube number :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` measure the cube :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>`, and let :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` be the side of :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` and :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>` of :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>`;

I say that :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`.

.. _folio-ii-0375:

.. folio:: 0375
   :page: 369

For let :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$E$ <0375.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$G$ <0375.g1:G>`; further, let :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>` make :g:`$F$ <0375.g1:F>`, and let :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0375.g1:F>` make :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>` respectively.

.. picture:: 0375.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 15

.. assertion::

   Now it is manifest that :g:`$E$ <0375.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0375.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0375.g1:G>` and :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>`, :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>`, :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`. :just:`[VIII. 11, 12]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>`, :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>`, :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>` are continuously proportional, and :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>`,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 7] <VIII.7>

   therefore it also measures :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` is to :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`.

Next, let :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` will also measure :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>`.

For, with the same construction, we can prove in a similar manner that :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>`, :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>`, :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`, and, as :g:`$C$ <0375.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0375.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` to :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>`, :just:`[VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>` so that :g:`$A$ <0375.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0375.g1:B>` also.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
