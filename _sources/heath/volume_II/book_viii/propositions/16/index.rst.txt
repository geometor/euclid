PROPOSITION 16.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a square number do not measure a square number, neither will the side measure the side; and, if the side do not measure the side, neither will the square measure the square.

Let :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>` be square numbers, and let :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>` be their sides; and let :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` not measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`;

I say that neither does :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   For, if :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`, :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` will also measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`. :just:`[VIII. 14] <VIII.14>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` does not measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither will :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0376.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 16

Again, let :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`;

I say that neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`.

.. subclause:: [VIII. 14] <VIII.14>

   For, if :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` will also measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` does not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
