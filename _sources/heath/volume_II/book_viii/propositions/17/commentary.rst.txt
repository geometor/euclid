Commentary on Proposition VIII.17
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.17 <VIII.17>`.

.. folio:: 0377
   :page: 371

If $a^3$ does not measure $b^3$, $a$ will not measure $b$; and vice versa.

Proved by :term:`*reductio ad absurdum* <reductio-ad-absurdum>` employing VIII. 15.
