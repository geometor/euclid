PROPOSITION 24.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number, and the first be square, the second will also be square.

For let the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0386.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0386.g1:B>` have to one another the ratio which the square number :g:`$C$ <0386.g1:C>` has to the square number :g:`$D$ <0386.g1:D>`, and let :g:`$A$ <0386.g1:A>` be square;

I say that :g:`$B$ <0386.g1:B>` is also square.

.. picture:: 0386.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 24

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$C$ <0386.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0386.g1:D>` are square,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$C$ <0386.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0386.g1:D>` are similar plane numbers.

.. assertion::

   Therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$C$ <0386.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0386.g1:D>`. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$C$ <0386.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0386.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0386.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0386.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$A$ <0386.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0386.g1:B>` also. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0386.g1:A>` is square;

.. assertion:: [VIII. 22] <VIII.22>

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0386.g1:B>` is also square.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
