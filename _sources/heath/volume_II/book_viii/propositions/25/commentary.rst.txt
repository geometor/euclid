Commentary on Proposition VIII.25
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.25 <VIII.25>`.

.. folio:: 0387
   :page: 381

If $a : b = c^3 : d^3$, and $a$ is a cube, then $b$ is also a cube. For $c^3, d^3$ have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

Therefore $a, b$ also have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And $a$ is a cube: therefore $b$ is a cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
