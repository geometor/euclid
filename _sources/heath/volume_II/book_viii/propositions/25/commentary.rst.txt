Commentary on Proposition VIII.25
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.25 <VIII.25>`.

.. folio:: 0387
   :page: 381

If :math:`a : b = c^3 : d^3`, and :math:`a` is a cube, then :math:`b` is also a cube. For :math:`c^3, d^3` have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

Therefore :math:`a, b` also have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And :math:`a` is a cube: therefore :math:`b` is a cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
