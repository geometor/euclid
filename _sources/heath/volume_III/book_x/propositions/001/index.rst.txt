PROPOSITION 1.
==============

.. enunciation::

   Two unequal magnitudes being set out, if from the greater there be subtracted a magnitude greater than its half, and from that which is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the lesser magnitude set out.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>` be two unequal magnitudes of which :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` is the greater : I say that, if from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` there be subtracted a magnitude greater than its half, and from that which is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the magnitude :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0018.g1.svg
   :alt: Book X, Proposition 1

For :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>` if multiplied will sometime be greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[cf. v. Def. 4]`

Let it be multiplied, and let :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` be a multiple of :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`, and greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`; let :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` be divided into the parts :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0018.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0018.g1:/ G E />` equal to :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`, from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` let there be subtracted :g:`$BH$ <0018.g1:/ B H />` greater than its half, and, from :g:`$AH$ <0018.g1:/ A H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0018.g1:/ H K />` greater than its half, and let this process be repeated continually until the divisions in :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` are equal in multitude with the divisions in :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />`.

Let, then, :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0018.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HB$ <0018.g1:/ H B />` be divisions which are equal in multitude with :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0018.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0018.g1:/ G E />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` is greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`, and from :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` there has been subtracted :g:`$EG$ <0018.g1:/ E G />` less than its half, and, from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0018.g1:/ B H />` greater than its half,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0018.g1:/ G D />` is greater than the remainder :g:`$HA$ <0018.g1:/ H A />`.

.. _folio-iii-0019:

.. folio:: 0019
   :page: 15

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$GD$ <0018.g1:/ G D />` is greater than :g:`$HA$ <0018.g1:/ H A />`, and there has been subtracted, from :g:`$GD$ <0018.g1:/ G D />`, the half :g:`$GF$ <0018.g1:/ G F />`, and, from :g:`$HA$ <0018.g1:/ H A />`, :g:`$HK$ <0018.g1:/ H K />` greater than its half,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />` is greater than the remainder :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />` is equal to :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>` is also greater than :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />` is less than :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore there is left of the magnitude :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` the magnitude :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />` which is less than the lesser magnitude set out, namely :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. assertion::

   And the theorem can be similarly proved even if the parts subtracted be halves.
