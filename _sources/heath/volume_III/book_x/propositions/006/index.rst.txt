PROPOSITION 6.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two magnitudes have to one another the ratio which a number has to a number, the magnitudes will be commensurable.

For let the two magnitudes :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>` have to one another the ratio which the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` has to the number :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`; I say that the magnitudes :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>` are commensurable.

.. picture:: 0030.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 6 diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` be divided into as many equal parts as there are units in :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`, and let :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` be equal to one of them; and let :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>` be made up of as many magnitudes equal to :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` as there are units in :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then there are in :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` as many magnitudes equal to :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` as there are units in :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`, whatever part the unit is of :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`, the same part is :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` of :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` also;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` is to :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, so is the unit to :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But the unit measures the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`.\ [#f1]_

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` is to :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, so is the unit to :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, inversely, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>`, so is the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` to the unit. :just:`[cf. V. 7, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since there are in :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>` as many magnitudes equal to :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` as there are units in :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`, so is the unit to :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved that, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` to the unit;

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`;

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`, so is it to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>` also.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` has the same ratio to each of the magnitudes :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`;

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>` is :eq:`equal <0030.g1:B = 0030.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` measures :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it measures :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>` also.

.. subclause::

   Further it measures :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` also;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` measures :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`.

.. _folio-iii-0031:

.. folio:: 0031
   :page: 27

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is commensurable with :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.

.. assertion::

   PORISM. From this it is manifest that, if there be two numbers, as :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`, and a straight line, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, it is possible to make a straight line [:g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`] such that the given straight line is to it as the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` is to the number :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   And, if a mean proportional be also taken between :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`, :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`, as :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`, so will the square on :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` be to the square on :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`, that is, as the first is to the third, so is the figure on the first to that which is similar and similarly described on the second. :just:`[VI. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` is to :g:`$F$ <0030.g1:F>`, so is the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` to the number :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it has been contrived that, as the number :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>` is to the number :g:`$E$ <0030.g1:E>`, so also is the figure on the straight line :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>` to the figure on the straight line :g:`$B$ <0030.g1:B>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **But the unit measures the number** :g:`$D$ <0030.g1:D>`; **therefore** :g:`$C$ <0030.g1:C>` **also measures** :g:`$A$ <0030.g1:A>`. These words are redundant, though they are apparently found in all the MSS.
