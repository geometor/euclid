Commentary on Proposition X.20
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.20 <X.20>`.

.. folio:: 0053
   :page: 49

The converse of the last. If :math:`\rho` is a rational straight line, any rational area is of the form :math:`k\rho^2`. If this be "applied" to :math:`\rho`, the breadth is :math:`k\rho` commensurable in length with :math:`\rho` and therefore rational. We should reach the same result if we applied the area to *another* rational straight line :math:`\sigma`. The breadth is then

.. math::

   \frac{k\rho^2}{\sigma} = \frac{k\rho^2}{\sigma^2}\cdot \sigma = \frac{m}{n}k\cdot \sigma \text{ or } k'\sigma\text{, say.}
