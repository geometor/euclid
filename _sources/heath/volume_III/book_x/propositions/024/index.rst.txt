PROPOSITION 24.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in length is medial.

.. picture:: 0059.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 24

For let the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0059.g1:< A C >>` be contained by the medial straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0059.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0059.g1:/ B C />` which are commensurable in length; I say that :g:`$AC$ <0059.g1:< A C >>` is medial.

For on :g:`$AB$ <0059.g1:/ A B />` let the square :g:`$AD$ <0059.g1:< A D >>` be described;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0059.g1:< A D >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0059.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0059.g1:/ B C />`, while :g:`$AB$ <0059.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$BD$ <0059.g1:/ B D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DB$ <0059.g1:/ D B />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0059.g1:/ B C />`; so that :g:`$DA$ <0059.g1:< D A >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$AC$ <0059.g1:< A C >>`. :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DA$ <0059.g1:< D A >>` is medial;

.. assertion:: [X. 23, Por.]

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0059.g1:< A C >>` is also medial.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
