PROPOSITION 30.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find two rational straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with the greater.

Let there be set out a rational straight line :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />`, and two square numbers :g:`$CE$ <0072.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0072.g1:/ E D />` such that their sum :g:`$CD$ <0072.g1:/ C D />` is not square; :just:`[Lemma 2]` let there be described on :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` the semicircle :g:`$AFB$ <0072.g1:( A F B )>`, let it be contrived that, as :g:`$DC$ <0072.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CE$ <0072.g1:/ C E />`, so is the square on :g:`$BA$ <0072.g1:/ B A />` to the square on :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />`, :just:`[X. 6, Por.]` and let :g:`$FB$ <0072.g1:/ F B />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 30

.. assertion::

   Then, in a similar manner to the preceding, we can prove that :g:`$BA$ <0072.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only.

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$DC$ <0072.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CE$ <0072.g1:/ C E />`, so is the square on :g:`$BA$ <0072.g1:/ B A />` to the square on :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$CD$ <0072.g1:/ C D />` is to :g:`$DE$ <0072.g1:/ E D />`, so is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0072.g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[V. 19, Por., III. 31, I. 47]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CD$ <0072.g1:/ C D />` has not to :g:`$DE$ <0072.g1:/ E D />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. _folio-iii-0073:

.. folio:: 0073
   :page: 69

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0072.g1:/ B F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number ; therefore :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$BF$ <0072.g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />` by the square on :g:`$FB$ <0072.g1:/ F B />` incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and the square on :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$AF$ <0072.g1:/ A F />` by the square on :g:`$FB$ <0072.g1:/ F B />` incommensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0072.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
