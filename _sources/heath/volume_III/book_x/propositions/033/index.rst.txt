PROPOSITION 33.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational but the rectangle contained by them medial.

Let there be set out two rational straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` commensurable in square only and such that the square on the greater :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on the less :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[X. 30] <X.30>`

.. picture:: 0079.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 diagram

.. _folio-iii-0080:

.. folio:: 0080
   :page: 76

let :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` be :eq:`bisected <0079.g1:/ B D / = 0079.g1:/ D C />` at :g:`$D$ <0079.g1:D>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the square on either of the straight lines :g:`$BD$ <0079.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0079.g1:/ D C />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />` ; :just:`[VI. 28] <VI.28>` let the semicircle :g:`$AFB$ <0079.g1:( A F B )>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, let :g:`$EF$ <0079.g1:/ E F />` be drawn at :eq:`right angles <0079.g1:< A E F ) = 0079.g1:< B E F )>` to :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, and let :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` are unequal straight lines, and the square on :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, while there has been applied to :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />`, that is, to the square on half of it, and deficient by a square figure, making the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 18] <X.18>

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable with :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />`, so is the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0079.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />` to the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BE$ <0079.g1:/ B E />`, while the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0079.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, and the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BE$ <0079.g1:/ B E />` to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0079.g1:/ B F />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable with the square on :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` ; therefore :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` are incommensurable in square.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />` is also rational ; so that the sum of the squares on :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` is also rational. :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. subclause::

   And since, again, the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EF$ <0079.g1:/ E F />`, and, by hypothesis, the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0079.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0079.g1:/ E B />` is also equal to the square on :g:`$BD$ <0079.g1:/ B D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FE$ <0079.g1:/ F E />` is :eq:`equal <0079.g1:/ F E / = 0079.g1:/ B D />` to :g:`$BD$ <0079.g1:/ B D />` ; therefore :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` is double of :g:`$FE$ <0079.g1:/ F E />`, so that the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` is also commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$EF$ <0079.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause:: [X. 21] <X.21>

   But the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0079.g1:/ B C />` is medial ;

.. assertion:: [X. 23, Por.]

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$EF$ <0079.g1:/ E F />` is also medial.

.. _folio-iii-0081:

.. folio:: 0081
   :page: 77

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0079.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$EF$ <0079.g1:/ E F />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` ; :just:`[Lemma]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved that the sum of the squares on these straight lines is rational.

.. qed::

   Therefore two straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0079.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0079.g1:/ F B />` incommensurable in square have been found which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial. :just:`Q. E. D.`
