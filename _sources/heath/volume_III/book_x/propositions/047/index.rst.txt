PROPOSITION 47.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The side of the sum of two medial areas is divided at one point only.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0104.g1:/ A B />` be divided at :g:`$C$ <0104.g1:C>`, so that :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` are incommensurable in square and make the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`,
:g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` medial, and the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them; I say that :g:`$AB$ <0104.g1:/ A B />` is not divided at another point so as to fulfil the given conditions.

.. _folio-iii-0104:

.. folio:: 0104
   :page: 100

.. picture:: 0104.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 47 diagram

For, if possible, let it be divided at :g:`$D$ <0104.g1:D>`, so that again :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />` is of course not the same as :g:`$BD$ <0104.g1:/ B D />`, but :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />` is supposed greater; let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />` be set out, and let there be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />` the rectangle :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`, and the rectangle :g:`$HK$ <0104.g1:< H K >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion:: [II. 4] <II.4>

   therefore the whole :g:`$EK$ <0104.g1:< E K >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0104.g1:/ A B />`.

Again, let :g:`$EL$ <0104.g1:< E L >>`, equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0104.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0104.g1:/ D B />`, be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0104.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0104.g1:/ D B />`, is equal to the remainder :g:`$MK$ <0104.g1:< M K >>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, by hypothesis, the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HE$ <0104.g1:/ H E />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$HN$ <0104.g1:/ H N />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` is incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$GN$ <0104.g1:< G N >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>

   so that :g:`$EH$ <0104.g1:/ E H />` is also incommensurable with :g:`$HN$ <0104.g1:/ H N />`.

.. subclause::

   And they are rational;

.. folio:: 0105
   :page: 101

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0104.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HN$ <0104.g1:/ H N />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;

.. assertion:: [X. 36] <X.36>

   therefore :g:`$EN$ <0104.g1:/ E N />` is a binomial straight line divided at :g:`$H$ <0104.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that it is also divided at :g:`$M$ <0104.g1:M>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EH$ <0104.g1:/ E H />` is not the same with :g:`$MN$ <0104.g1:/ M N />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore a binomial has been divided at different points :

.. assertion:: [X. 42] <X.42>

   which is absurd.

.. qed::

   Therefore a side of the sum of two medial areas is not divided at different points; therefore it is divided at one point only.
