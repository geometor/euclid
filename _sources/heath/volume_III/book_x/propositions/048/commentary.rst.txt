Commentary on Proposition X.48
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.48 <X.48>`.

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 103

Let :math:`kp` be a straight line commensurable in length with :math:`\rho`, a given rational straight line.

The two numbers taken may be written :math:`p(m^2 - n^2)`, :math:`pn^2`, where :math:`(m^2 - n^2)` is not a square.

Take :math:`x` such that

.. math::

   pm^2 : p(m^2 - n^2) = k^2 p^2 : x^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1),

whence

.. math::

   x = kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}.

Then :math:`kp + x`, or :math:`kp + kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}`, is a :term:`*first binomial* <first-binomial>` straight line :math:`\dots\dots(2)`.

.. _folio-iii-0108:

.. folio:: 0108
   :page: 104

To prove this we have, from (1),

.. math::

   x^2 \sim k^2 p^2,

and :math:`x` is rational, but :math:`x \cup kp`; that is, :math:`x` is rational and :math:`\sim kp`, so that :math:`kp + x` is a binomial straight line.

Also, :math:`k^2 p^2` being greater than :math:`x^2`, suppose :math:`k^2 p^2 - x^2 = y^2`.

Then, from (1),

.. math::

   pm^2 : pn^2 = k^2 p^2 : y^2,

whence :math:`y` is rational and :math:`\sim kp`.

Therefore :math:`kp + x` is a :term:`*first binomial* <first-binomial>` straight line [x. Deff. II. 1].

This binomial straight line may be written thus,

.. math::

   kp + kp \sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}.

When we come to x. 85, we shall find that the corresponding straight line with a negative sign is the :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`,

.. math::

   kp - kp \sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}.

Consider now the equation of which these two expressions are the roots. The equation is

.. math::

   x^2 - 2kp \cdot x + \lambda^2 k^2 p^2 = 0.

In other words, the first binomial and the first apotome correspond to the roots of the equation

.. math::

   x^2 - 2ax + \lambda^2 a^2 = 0,

where :math:`a = kp`.
