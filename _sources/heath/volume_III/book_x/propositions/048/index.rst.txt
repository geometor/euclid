PROPOSITION 48.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the first binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0106.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 48 Diagram

Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0106.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that the sum of them :g:`$AB$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0106.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number, but has not to :g:`$CA$ <0106.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; :just:`[Lemma 1 after X. 28]` let any rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0106.g1:D>` be set out, and let :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$D$ <0106.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is also rational.

Let it be contrived that, as the number :g:`$BA$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a number has to a number;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` also has to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` the ratio which a number has to a number, so that the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BA$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. _folio-iii-0107:

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 103

.. assertion::

   neither, therefore, has the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EG$ <0106.g1:/ E G />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

I say that it is also a first binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For since, as the number :g:`$BA$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`, while :g:`$BA$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is also greater than the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`.

Let then the squares on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$H$ <0106.g1:H>` be equal to the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`$BA$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0106.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$AB$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0106.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0106.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0106.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0106.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` also has to the square on :g:`$H$ <0106.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0106.g1:H>`; :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>` therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0106.g1:/ F G />` are rational, and :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$D$ <0106.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0106.g1:/ E F />` is a first binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
