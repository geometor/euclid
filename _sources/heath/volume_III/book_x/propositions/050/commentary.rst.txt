Commentary on Proposition X.50
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.50 <X.50>`.

.. folio:: 0112
   :page: 108

Let :math:`\rho` be a rational straight line.

Take the numbers :math:`q(m^2 - n^2)`, :math:`qn^2`, and let :math:`p` be a third number which is not a square and which has not to :math:`qm^2` or :math:`q(m^2 - n^2)` the ratio of square to square.

Take :math:`x` such that

.. math::

   p : qm^2 = \rho^2 : x^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1).

Thus :math:`x` is rational and :math:`\sim \rho` :math:`\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(2)`.

Next suppose that

.. math::

   qm^2 : q(m^2 - n^2) = x^2 : y^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(3).

It follows that :math:`y` is rational and :math:`\sim x` :math:`\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(4)`.

Thus :math:`(x + y)` is a binomial straight line.

Again, from (1) and (3), :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`,

.. math::

   p : q(m^2 - n^2) = \rho^2 : y^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(5),

whence

.. math::

   y \cup \rho \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(6).

Suppose that

.. math::

   x^2 - y^2 = z^2.

Then, from (3), :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`,

.. math::

   qm^2 : qn^2 = x^2 : z^2,

whence

.. math::

   z \sim x.

Thus

.. math::

   \sqrt{x^2 - y^2} \sim x,

and :math:`x, y` are both :math:`\cup\ \rho`; therefore :math:`x + y` is a :term:`*third binomial* <third-binomial>` straight line.

Now, from (1),

.. math::

   x = \rho \cdot \frac{m\sqrt{q}}{\sqrt{p}},

and, by (5),

.. math::

   y = \rho \cdot \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2} \cdot \sqrt{q}}{\sqrt{p}}.

Thus the :term:`*third binomial* <third-binomial>` is

.. math::

   \sqrt{\frac{q}{p}} \cdot \rho(m + \sqrt{m^2 - n^2}),

which we may write in the form

.. math::

   m\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho + m\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}.

.. _folio-iii-0113:

.. folio:: 0113

The corresponding :term:`*third apotome* <third-apotome>` [x. 87] is

.. math::

   m\sqrt{k}\cdot p - m\sqrt{k}\cdot p\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}.

The two expressions are accordingly the roots of the equation

.. math::

   x^2 - 2m\sqrt{k}\cdot px + \lambda^2 m^2 kp^2 = 0,

or

.. math::

   x^2 - 2ax + \lambda^2 a^2 = 0,

where

.. math::

   a = m\sqrt{k}\cdot p.

See also note on **x. 53** (*ad fin.*).
