PROPOSITION 52.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the fifth binomial straight line.

Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0115.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0115.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />` has not to either of them the ratio which a square number has to a square number; let any rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0115.g1:D>` be set out,
and let :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` be commensurable with :g:`$D$ <0115.g1:D>`;

.. _folio-iii-0115:

.. folio:: 0115
   :page: 111

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` is rational.

.. picture:: 0115.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 52

Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$CA$ <0115.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 6, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CA$ <0115.g1:/ A C />` has not to :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; :just:`[x. 9]` therefore :g:`$EG$ <0115.g1:/ E G />` is binomial. :just:`[x. 36]`

I say next that it is also a fifth binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$CA$ <0115.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />`, inversely, as :g:`$BA$ <0115.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0115.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$FE$ <0115.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$GF$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FE$ <0115.g1:/ E F />`.

Let then the squares on :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$H$ <0115.g1:H>` be equal to the square on :g:`$GF$ <0115.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as the number :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0115.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`$GF$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0115.g1:H>`. :just:`[v. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0115.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$BC$ <0115.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0115.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0115.g1:H>`; :just:`[x. 9]` so that the square on :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FE$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0115.g1:/ F G />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$GF$ <0115.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$FE$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and the lesser term :g:`$EF$ <0115.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0115.g1:D>` set out.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$EG$ <0115.g1:/ E G />` is a fifth binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
