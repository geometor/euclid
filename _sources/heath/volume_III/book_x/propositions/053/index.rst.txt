PROPOSITION 53.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the sixth binomial straight line.

Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0116.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` has not to either of them the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and let there also be another number :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` which is not square and which has not to either of the numbers :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. picture:: 0116.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 53

Let any rational straight line :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` be set out, and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[x. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 6]`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` has not to :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. _folio-iii-0117:

.. folio:: 0117
   :page: 113

.. assertion::

   neither has the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

Again, let it be contrived that, as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 6]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$HG$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is rational; therefore :g:`$HG$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   neither has the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$FH$ <0116.g1:/ F H />` is binomial. :just:`[x. 36]`

It is next to be proved that it is also a sixth binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, and also, as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[v. 22]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved incommensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`.

Let then the squares on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$K$ <0116.g1:K>` be equal to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`;

.. _folio-iii-0118:

.. folio:: 0118
   :page: 114

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0116.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$K$ <0116.g1:K>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$BC$ <0116.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   so that neither has the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$K$ <0116.g1:K>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion:: [X. 9] <X.9>

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$K$ <0116.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and neither of them is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` set out.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$FH$ <0116.g1:/ F H />` is a sixth binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
