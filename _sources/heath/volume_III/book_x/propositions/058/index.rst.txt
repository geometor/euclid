PROPOSITION 58.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fifth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.

For let the area :g:`$AC$ <0132.g1:< A B C D >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AB$ <0132.g1:/ A B />` and the fifth binomial :g:`$AD$ <0132.g1:/ A D />` divided into its terms at :g:`$E$ <0132.g1:E>`, so that :g:`$AE$ <0132.g1:/ A E />` is the greater term; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0132.g1:< A B C D >>` is the irrational straight line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.

For let the same construction be made as before shown; it is then manifest that :g:`$MO$ <0132.g1:/ M O />` is the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0132.g1:< A B C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0132.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 58

It is then to be proved that :g:`$MO$ <0132.g1:/ M O />` is the side of a rational plus a medial area.

.. subclause:: [X. 18] <X.18>

   For, since :g:`$AG$ <0132.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable with :g:`$GE$ <0132.g1:/ G E />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AH$ <0132.g1:< A B H G >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$HE$ <0132.g1:< G H K E >>`, :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>` that is, the square on :g:`$MN$ <0132.g1:/ M N />` with the square on :g:`$NO$ <0132.g1:/ N O />`; therefore :g:`$MN$ <0132.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0132.g1:/ N O />` are incommensurable in square.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AD$ <0132.g1:/ A D />` is a fifth binomial straight line, and :g:`$ED$ <0132.g1:/ E D />` the lesser segment,

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 5]

   therefore :g:`$ED$ <0132.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0132.g1:/ A B />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0132.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable with :g:`$ED$ <0132.g1:/ E D />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0132.g1:/ A B />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$AE$ <0132.g1:/ A E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AK$ <0132.g1:< A B K E >>`, that is, the sum of the squares on :g:`$MN$ <0132.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0132.g1:/ N O />`, is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$DE$ <0132.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0132.g1:/ A B />`, that is, with :g:`$EK$ <0132.g1:/ E K />`, while :g:`$DE$ <0132.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0132.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 12] <X.12>

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0132.g1:/ E F />` is also commensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0132.g1:/ E K />`.

.. _folio-iii-0133:

.. folio:: 0133
   :page: 129

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EK$ <0132.g1:/ E K />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EL$ <0132.g1:< E K L F >>`, that is, :g:`$MR$ <0132.g1:< TL M N R >>`, that is, the rectangle :g:`$MN$ <0132.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0132.g1:/ N O />`, is also rational. :just:`[X. 19] <X.19>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$MN$ <0132.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0132.g1:/ N O />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$MO$ <0132.g1:/ M O />` is the side of a rational plus a medial area :just:`[X. 40] <X.40>` and is the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0132.g1:< A B C D >>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
