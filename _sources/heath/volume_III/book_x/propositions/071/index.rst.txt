PROPOSITION 71.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a rational and a medial area be added together, four irrational straight lines arise, namely a binomial or a first bimedial or a major or a side of a rational plus a medial area.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` be rational, and :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` medial; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is a binomial or a first bimedial or a major or a side of a rational plus a medial area.

.. _folio-iii-0156:

.. folio:: 0156
   :page: 152

For :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is either greater or less than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`.

First, let it be greater; let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` be set out, let there be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` the rectangle :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< E G > = 0156.g1:< A B >>` to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>`, producing :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` as breadth, and let :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`, :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< H I > = 0156.g1:< D C >>` to :g:`$DC$ <0156.g1:< D C >>`, be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` as breadth.

.. picture:: 0156.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 71

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is rational and is :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< A B > = 0156.g1:< E G >>` to :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[x. 20]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` is medial and is :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< C D > = 0156.g1:< H I >>` to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[x. 22]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` is medial, while :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`, so that :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`.

.. subclause:: [vi. 1]

   But, as :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`, so is :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`. :just:`[x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a binomial straight line, divided at :g:`$H$ <0156.g1:H>`. :just:`[x. 36]`

.. _folio-iii-0157:

.. folio:: 0157
   :page: 153

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is greater than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`, while :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< A B > = 0156.g1:< E G >; 0156.g1:< C D > = 0156.g1:< H I >>` to :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` and :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also greater than :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>` ; therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also greater than :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

The square, then, on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is greater than the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable with itself.

.. subclause::

   Now the greater straight line :g:`$HE$ <0156.g1:/ H E />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out ;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. II. 1]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a first binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational ; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the first binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is binomial. :just:`[x. 54]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the "side" of :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is binomial ; so that the "side" of :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also binomial.

Next, let the square on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` be greater than the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   Now the greater straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out ;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. II. 4]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a fourth binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational ; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fourth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called major. :just:`[x. 57]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is major ; so that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also major.

.. subclause::

   Next, let :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` be less than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also less than :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`, so that :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also less than :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

Now the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

.. _folio-iii-0158:

.. folio:: 0158
   :page: 154

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with itself.

.. subclause::

   Now the lesser straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 2]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a second binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational, and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the second binomial, the side of the square equal to it is a first bimedial; :just:`[X. 55] <X.55>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is a first bimedial, so that the "side" of :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also a first bimedial.

Next, let the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` be greater than the square on :g:`$HE$ <0156.g1:/ H E />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

.. subclause::

   Now the lesser straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 5]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a fifth binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fifth binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is a side of a rational plus a medial area. :just:`[X. 58] <X.58>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is a side of a rational plus a medial area, so that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also a side of a rational plus a medial area.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
