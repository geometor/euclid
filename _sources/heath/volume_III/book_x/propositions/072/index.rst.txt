PROPOSITION 72.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two medial areas incommensurable with one another be added together, the remaining two irrational straight lines arise, namely either a second bimedial or a side of the sum of two medial areas.

For let two medial areas :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>` incommensurable with one another be added together; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0159.g1:< A D >>` is either a second bimedial or a side of the sum of two medial areas.

.. picture:: 0159.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 72 diagram

For :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>` is either greater or less than :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>`. First, if it so chance, let :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>` be greater than :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>`. Let the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />` be set out, and to :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />` let there be applied the rectangle :g:`$EG$ <0159.g1:< E G >>` :eq:`equal <0159.g1:< E G > = 0159.g1:< A B >>` to
:g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>` and producing :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` as breadth, and the rectangle :g:`$HI$ <0159.g1:< H I >>` :eq:`equal <0159.g1:< H I > = 0159.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>` and producing :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` as breadth.

.. _folio-iii-0160:

.. folio:: 0160
   :page: 156

.. subclause::

   Now, since each of the areas :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the areas :g:`$EG$ <0159.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HI$ <0159.g1:< H I >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`$FE$ <0159.g1:/ F E />`, producing :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>`, and :g:`$AB$ <0159.g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <0159.g1:< A B > = 0159.g1:< E G >; 0159.g1:< C D > = 0159.g1:< H I >>` to :g:`$EG$ <0159.g1:< E G >>`, and :g:`$CD$ <0159.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$HI$ <0159.g1:< H I >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0159.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$HI$ <0159.g1:< H I >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$EG$ <0159.g1:< E G >>` is to :g:`$HI$ <0159.g1:< H I >>`, so is :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EK$ <0159.g1:/ E K />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` is greater than the square on :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with itself.

.. subclause::

   Now neither of the straight lines :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 3]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0159.g1:/ E K />` is a third binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />` is rational; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the third binomial, the "side" of the area is a second bimedial; :just:`[X. 56] <X.56>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the "side" of :g:`$EI$ <0159.g1:< E I >>`, that is, of :g:`$AD$ <0159.g1:< A D >>`, is a second bimedial.

Next, let the square on :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />` be greater than the square on :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   Now each of the straight lines :g:`$EH$ <0159.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0159.g1:/ H K />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0159.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 6]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0159.g1:/ E K />` is a sixth binomial.

.. subclause::

   But, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and

.. folio:: 0161
   :page: 157

.. subclause::

   the sixth binomial, the "side" of the area is the side of the sum of two medial areas; :just:`[X. 59] <X.59>`

.. assertion::

   so that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0159.g1:< A D >>` is also the side of the sum of two medial areas.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
