Commentary on Proposition X.74
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.74 <X.74>`.

.. folio:: 0164
   :page: 160

The :term:`*first apotome of a medial straight line* <first-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>` is the difference between straight lines of the form :math:`k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho, k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho`, which are medial straight lines commensurable in square only and forming a rational rectangle.

By hypothesis, :math:`x^2, y^2` are medial areas.

And, since :math:`xy` is rational,

.. math::

   (x^2 + y^2) \smile xy

.. math::

   \smile 2xy,

whence

.. math::

   (x - y)^2 \smile 2xy.

But :math:`2xy` is rational;
therefore :math:`(x - y)^2`, and consequently :math:`(x - y)`, is irrational.

This irrational, which is of the form :math:`(k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho \sim k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho)`, is the :term:`*first apotome of a medial straight line* <first-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>`; the term corresponding of course to :term:`*first bimedial* <first-bimedial>`, which applies where the sign is positive.
