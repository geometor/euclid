Commentary on Proposition X.85
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.85 <X.85>`.

.. folio:: 0183
   :page: 179

Take :math:`k\rho` commensurable in length with :math:`\rho`, the given rational straight line.
Let :math:`m^2, n^2` be square numbers such that :math:`(m^2 - n^2)` is not square.
Take :math:`x` such that

.. math::

   m^2 : (m^2 - n^2) = k^2\rho^2 : x^2 \tag{1},

so that

.. math::

   x = k\rho \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}

.. math::

   = k\rho \sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}, \text{ say.}

Then shall :math:`k\rho - x`, or :math:`k\rho - k\rho\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}`, be a :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`.

For (:math:`\alpha`) it follows from (1) that :math:`x` is rational but incommensurable with :math:`k\rho`, whence :math:`k\rho, x` are rational and :math:`\sim`, so that :math:`(k\rho - x)` is an apotome.

(:math:`\beta`) If :math:`y^2 = k^2\rho^2 - x^2`, then, by (1), :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`,

.. math::

   m^2 : n^2 = k^2\rho^2 : y^2,

whence :math:`y`, that is, :math:`\sqrt{k^2\rho^2 - x^2}`, is commensurable in length with :math:`k\rho`.

And :math:`k\rho \frown \rho` ; therefore :math:`k\rho - x` is a :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`.

As explained in the note to x. 48, the first apotome

.. math::

   k\rho - k\rho\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}

is one of the roots of the equation

.. math::

   x^2 - 2k\rho \cdot x + \lambda^2 k^2\rho^2 = 0.
