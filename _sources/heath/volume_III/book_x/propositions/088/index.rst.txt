PROPOSITION 88.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the fourth apotome.

Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0188.g1:A>` be set out, and :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` commensurable in length with it;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is also rational.

.. picture:: 0188.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 88

Let two numbers :g:`$DF$ <0188.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0188.g1:/ F E />` be set out such that the whole :g:`$DE$ <0188.g1:/ D E />` has not to either of the numbers :g:`$DF$ <0188.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$EF$ <0188.g1:/ E F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$DE$ <0188.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0188.g1:/ E F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />`; :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` is also rational ; therefore :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` is rational.

.. _folio-iii-0189:

.. folio:: 0189
   :page: 185

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DE$ <0188.g1:/ D E />` has not to :g:`$EF$ <0188.g1:/ E F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$BC$ <0188.g1:/ B C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

Now let the square on :g:`$H$ <0188.g1:H>` be that by which the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then, as :g:`$DE$ <0188.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0188.g1:/ E F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$ED$ <0188.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0188.g1:/ D F />`, so is the square on :g:`$GB$ <0188.g1:/ G B />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0188.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$ED$ <0188.g1:/ E D />` has not to :g:`$DF$ <0188.g1:/ D F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$GB$ <0188.g1:/ G B />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0188.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0188.g1:H>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` by the square on :g:`$H$ <0188.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0188.g1:/ G C />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   And the whole :g:`$BG$ <0188.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0188.g1:A>` set out.

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 4]

   Therefore :g:`$BC$ <0188.g1:/ B C />` is a fourth apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore the fourth apotome has been found. :just:`Q. E. D.`
