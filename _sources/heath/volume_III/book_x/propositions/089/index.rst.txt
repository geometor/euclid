PROPOSITION 89.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the fifth apotome.

.. picture:: 0190.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 89 Diagram

Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>` be set out, and let :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` is rational.

Let two numbers :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` be set out such that :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` again has not to either of the numbers :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />`, so is the square on :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />` is also rational; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>` therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`, while :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` has not to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. _folio-iii-0191:

.. folio:: 0191
   :page: 187

I say next that it is also a fifth apotome.

For let the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>` be that by which the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then, as the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />` has not to :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />` by the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />`.

.. subclause::

   And the annex :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 5]

   therefore :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` is a fifth apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore the fifth apotome :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` has been found. :just:`Q. E. D.`
