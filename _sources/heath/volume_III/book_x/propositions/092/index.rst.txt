PROPOSITION 92.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a second apotome, the "side" of the area is a first apotome of a medial straight line.

For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0199.g1:/ A C />` and the second apotome :g:`$AD$ <0199.g1:/ A D />`;

.. _folio-iii-0199:

.. folio:: 0199
   :page: 195

I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>` is a first apotome of a medial straight line.

.. picture:: 0199.g1.svg
   :alt: Book X, Proposition 92

For let :g:`$DG$ <0199.g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`$AD$ <0199.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0199.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>` and the annex :g:`$DG$ <0199.g1:/ D G />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0199.g1:/ A C />` set out, while the square on the whole :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on the annex :g:`$GD$ <0199.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />`. :just:`[X. Deff. III. 2]`

.. subclause::

   Since then the square on :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GD$ <0199.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$GD$ <0199.g1:/ G D />` and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. :just:`[X. 17] <X.17>`

Let then :g:`$DG$ <0199.g1:/ D G />` be bisected at :g:`$E$ <0199.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AG$ <0199.g1:/ A G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0199.g1:/ A C />`;

.. _folio-iii-0200:

.. folio:: 0200
   :page: 196

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0199.g1:/ A C />`; :just:`[x. 13]` therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>` is medial. :just:`[x. 21]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DE$ <0199.g1:/ D E />` is commensurable with :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0199.g1:/ D G />` is also commensurable with each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0199.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DG$ <0199.g1:/ D G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0199.g1:/ A C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$DH$ <0199.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>` is rational. :just:`[x. 19]`

Let then the square :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>` be constructed equal to :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>`, and let there be subtracted :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>` equal to :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>` and being about the same angle with :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>`, namely the angle :g:`$LPM$ <0199.g1:< L P M )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>` are about the same diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>`

Let :g:`$PR$ <0199.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>` are medial and are equal to the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />`, the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />` are also medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />` are also medial straight lines commensurable in square only.

.. subclause::

   And, since the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 17] <VI.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />`,

.. subclause::

   while, as :g:`$AF$ <0199.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>`, and, as :g:`$EG$ <0199.g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`$FG$ <0199.g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0199.g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between the squares :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`, and :g:`$AI$ <0199.g1:< A I >>` is equal to :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`$FK$ <0199.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0199.g1:< M N >>` is also equal to :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DH$ <0199.g1:< D H >>` is equal to :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>`, and :g:`$LO$ <0199.g1:< L O >>` equal to :g:`$MN$ <0199.g1:< M N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$DK$ <0199.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0199.g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the whole :g:`$AK$ <0199.g1:< A K >>` is equal to :g:`$LM$ <0199.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`, and, in these, :g:`$DK$ <0199.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0199.g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>` is equal to :g:`$TS$ <0199.g1:< T S >>`.

.. _folio-iii-0201:

.. folio:: 0201
   :page: 197

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$TS$ <0199.g1:< T S >>` is the square on :g:`$LN$ <0199.g1:/ L N />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LN$ <0199.g1:/ L N />` is equal to the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>`; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0199.g1:/ L N />` is the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>`.

I say that :g:`$LN$ <0199.g1:/ L N />` is a first apotome of a medial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$EK$ <0199.g1:< E K >>` is rational and is equal to :g:`$LO$ <0199.g1:< L O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LO$ <0199.g1:< L O >>`, that is, the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />`, is rational.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>` was proved medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LO$ <0199.g1:< L O >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$LO$ <0199.g1:< L O >>` is to :g:`$NO$ <0199.g1:< N O >>`, so is :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />` to :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />` are incommensurable in length.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LP$ <0199.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0199.g1:/ P N />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0199.g1:/ L N />` is a first apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`[x. 74]`

.. subclause::

   And it is the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0199.g1:< A B >>` is a first apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
