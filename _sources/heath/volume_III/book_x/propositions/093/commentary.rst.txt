Commentary on Proposition X.93
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.93 <X.93>`.

.. folio:: 0206
   :page: 202

Here we are to find and classify the irrational straight line

.. math::

   \sqrt{\rho(\sqrt{k}.\rho - \sqrt{k}.\rho\sqrt{1-\lambda^2})}.

Following the same method, we put

.. math::

   \left.\begin{aligned}
   u + v &= \sqrt{k}.\rho \\
   uv &= \frac{1}{4}k\rho^2(1 - \lambda^2)
   \end{aligned}\right\} \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1).

Next, :math:`u, v` being found, let

.. math::

   \left.\begin{aligned}
   x^2 &= \rho u \\
   y^2 &= \rho v
   \end{aligned}\right\} \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(2);

then :math:`(x - y)` is the square root required and is a :term:`*second apotome of a medial* <second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>` *straight line*.

That :math:`(x - y)` is the square root required and that :math:`x^2, y^2` are medial areas, so that :math:`x, y` are medial straight lines, is proved exactly as in the last proposition.

The rectangle :math:`xy`, being equal to :math:`\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k}.\rho^2\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}`, is also medial.

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   &\text{Now, from (1), by x. 17,} & u &\mathbin{\frown} v, \\
   &\text{whence} & u + v &\mathbin{\frown} u. \\
   &\text{But} & (u + v)\text{, or }\sqrt{k}.\rho &\mathbin{\smile} \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k}.\rho\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}; \\
   &\text{therefore} & u &\mathbin{\smile} \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k}.\rho\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}, \\
   &\text{and consequently} & \rho u &\mathbin{\smile} \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k}.\rho^2\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}, \\
   &\text{or} & x^2 &\mathbin{\smile} xy, \\
   &\text{whence} & x &\mathbin{\smile} y. \\
   &\text{And, since } u \mathbin{\frown} v\text{,} & \rho u &\mathbin{\frown} \rho v, \\
   &\text{or} & x^2 &\mathbin{\frown} y^2.
   \end{aligned}

Thus :math:`x, y` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only. And :math:`xy` is a medial area. Therefore :math:`(x - y)` is a :term:`*second apotome of a medial straight line* <second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>`.

Its actual form is found by solving equations (1), (2); thus

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   u &= \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{k}.\rho + \lambda\sqrt{k}.\rho), \\
   v &= \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{k}.\rho - \lambda\sqrt{k}.\rho),
   \end{aligned}

and

.. math::

   x - y = \rho\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{k}}{2}(1+\lambda)} - \rho\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{k}}{2}(1-\lambda)}.

As explained in the note on x. 56, this is the lesser positive root of the equation

.. math::

   x^4 - 2\sqrt{k}.\rho^2 x^2 + \lambda^2 k\rho^4 = 0.
