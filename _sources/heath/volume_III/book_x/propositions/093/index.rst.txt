PROPOSITION 93.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a third apotome, the “side” of the area is a second apotome of a medial straight line.

For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />` and the third apotome :g:`$AD$ <0203.g1:/ A D />`; I say that the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>` is a second apotome of a medial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`$AD$ <0203.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and neither of the straight lines :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />` set out, while the square on the whole :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on the annex :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`. :just:`[x. Deff. III. 3]`

.. picture:: 0203.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 93

.. subclause::

   Since then the square on :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` and deficient by a square figure, it will divide it into commensurable parts. :just:`[x. 17]`

Let then :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <0203.g1:/ D E / = 0203.g1:/ E G />` at :g:`$E$ <0203.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />`.

.. _folio-iii-0204:

.. folio:: 0204
   :page: 200

Let :g:`$EH$ <0203.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$FI$ <0203.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`$GK$ <0203.g1:/ G K />` be drawn through the points :g:`$E$ <0203.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0203.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0203.g1:G>` parallel to :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are commensurable; therefore :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`. :just:`[VI. 1, x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are commensurable in length,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 13]

   so that :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are so also.

.. assertion:: [x. 21]

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`; :just:`[x. 13]` therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$DH$ <0203.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` is medial. :just:`[x. 21]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` are commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, and :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 13]

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`.

Now let the square :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< L M > = 0203.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>`, and let there be subtracted :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>` :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< N O > = 0203.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>` and being about the same angle with :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>`; therefore :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>` are about the same diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>` Let :g:`$PR$ <0203.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 17] <VI.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />`.

.. _folio-iii-0205:

.. folio:: 0205
   :page: 201

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`, and, as :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is to :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>` therefore :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0203.g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between the squares :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>`, and :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< A I > = 0203.g1:< L M >; 0203.g1:< F K > = 0203.g1:< N O >>` to :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` is also :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< E K > = 0203.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`$MN$ <0203.g1:< M N >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0203.g1:< M N >>` is :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< M N > = 0203.g1:< L O >>` to :g:`$LO$ <0203.g1:< L O >>`, and :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< E K > = 0203.g1:< D H >>` to :g:`$DH$ <0203.g1:< D H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$DK$ <0203.g1:< D K >>` is also equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0203.g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AK$ <0203.g1:< A K >>` is also equal to :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0203.g1:< N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <0203.g1:< A B > = 0203.g1:< S T >>` to :g:`$ST$ <0203.g1:< S T >>`, that is, to the square on :g:`$LN$ <0203.g1:/ L N />`; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0203.g1:/ L N />` is the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>`.

I say that :g:`$LN$ <0203.g1:/ L N />` is a second apotome of a medial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>` were proved medial, and are equal to the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />` is also medial; therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />` is medial.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1, x. 11]

   And, since :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is commensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />` is also commensurable with the square on :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` was proved incommensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LM$ <0203.g1:< L M >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$MN$ <0203.g1:< M N >>`, that is, the square on :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />` with the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />`; so that :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />`; :just:`[VI. 1, x. 11]` therefore :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only.

I say next that they also contain a medial rectangle.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` was proved medial, and is equal to the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />` is also medial, so that :g:`$LP$ <0203.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0203.g1:/ P N />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a medial rectangle.

.. _folio-iii-0206:

.. folio:: 0206
   :page: 202

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LN$ <0203.g1:/ L N />` is a second apotome of a medial straight line; :just:`[x. 75]` and it is the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0203.g1:< A B >>` is a second apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
