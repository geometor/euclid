PROPOSITION 97.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The square on an apotome applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a first apotome.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` rational, and to :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CE$ <0216.g1:< C F E D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` as breadth; I say that :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` is a first apotome.

.. picture:: 0216.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 97 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[x. 73]`

To :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BG$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`, and, in these, :g:`$CE$ <0216.g1:< C F E D >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />`; therefore the remainder :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

Let :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` be :eq:`bisected <0216.g1:/ F N / = 0216.g1:/ N M />` at the point :g:`$N$ <0216.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$NO$ <0216.g1:/ N O />` be drawn through :g:`$N$ <0216.g1:N>` parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`; therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0216.g1:< F N O E >>`, :g:`$LN$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are rational, and :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 20]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` is medial, and :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is medial.

.. _folio-iii-0217:

.. folio:: 0217
   :page: 213

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 22]`

.. subclause::

   And, since the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are rational, while twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$CL$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`, and :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is to :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>`, so is :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` to :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` is an apotome. :just:`[x. 73]`

I say next that it is also a first apotome.

.. subclause::

   For, since the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` is a mean proportional between the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`, and :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BG$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`, and :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` is also a mean proportional between :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>`; therefore, as :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>`, so is :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` to :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`$NM$ <0216.g1:/ N M />`, and, as :g:`$NL$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` is to :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>`, so is :g:`$NM$ <0216.g1:/ N M />` to :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$NM$ <0216.g1:/ N M />` :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`, that is, to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />` is commensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` are two unequal straight lines,

.. _folio-iii-0218:

.. folio:: 0218
   :page: 214

.. subclause::

   and to :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` there has been applied the rectangle :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />` equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` and deficient by a square figure, while :g:`$CK$ <0216.g1:/ C K />` is commensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0216.g1:/ K M />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MF$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />`. :just:`[x. 17]`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` set out;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 1]

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` is a first apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
