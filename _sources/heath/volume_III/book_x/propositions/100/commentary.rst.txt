Commentary on Proposition X.100
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.100 <X.100>`.

.. folio:: 0227
   :page: 223

We have to find and classify

.. math::

   \frac{1}{\sigma}\left\{\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}} - \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}\right\}^2.

We will call this, for brevity,

.. math::

   \frac{1}{\sigma}(u-v)^2.

Take :math:`x, y, z` such that

.. math::

   \left.\begin{aligned}
   \sigma x &= u^2 \\
   \sigma y &= v^2 \\
   \sigma \cdot 2z &= 2uv
   \end{aligned}\right\},

where it has to be remembered that :math:`u^2, v^2` are incommensurable, :math:`(u^2+v^2)` is rational, and :math:`2uv` medial.

.. _folio-iii-0228:

.. folio:: 0228
   :page: 224

It follows that :math:`\sigma(x+y)` is rational and :math:`\sigma \cdot 2z` medial, so that :math:`(x+y)` is rational and :math:`\sim \sigma` ....................................(1), while :math:`2z` is rational and :math:`\smile \sigma` ........................................(2), and :math:`\sigma(x+y) \smile \sigma \cdot 2z`, so that :math:`(x+y) \smile 2z` ................................................(3).

Thus [(1), (2), (3)] :math:`(x+y)`, :math:`2z` are rational and :math:`\sim`, so that :math:`(x+y) - 2z` is an apotome.

Next, since

.. math::

   u^2 \smile v^2,

.. math::

   \sigma x \smile \sigma y,

or

.. math::

   x \smile y.

And it is proved, as usual, that

.. math::

   xy = z^2 = \frac{1}{4}(2z)^2.

Therefore [X. 18] :math:`\sqrt{(x+y)^2 - (2z)^2} \smile (x+y)`.

But :math:`(x+y) \sim \sigma`, therefore :math:`x+y-2z` is a :term:`*fourth apotome* <fourth-apotome>`.

Its value is of course :math:`\frac{\rho^2}{\sigma}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}\right)`.
