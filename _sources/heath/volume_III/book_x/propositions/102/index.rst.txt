PROPOSITION 102.
================

.. enunciation::

   The square on the straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a sixth apotome.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0231.g1:/ A B />` be the straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole, and :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line, and to :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />` let :g:`$CE$ <0231.g1:< C E >>` be applied equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0231.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$CF$ <0231.g1:/ C F />` as breadth; I say that :g:`$CF$ <0231.g1:/ C F />` is a sixth apotome.

.. picture:: 0231.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 102 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0231.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0231.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` medial, and the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`. :just:`[X. 78] <X.78>`

Now to :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />` and producing :g:`$CK$ <0231.g1:/ C K />` as breadth, and :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BG$ <0231.g1:/ B G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`; therefore :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Since now :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, and, in these, :g:`$CE$ <0231.g1:< C E >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0231.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. subclause::

   And twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` is medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>` is also medial.

.. _folio-iii-0232:

.. folio:: 0232
   :page: 228

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$FE$ <0231.g1:/ F E />`, producing :g:`$FM$ <0231.g1:/ F M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FM$ <0231.g1:/ F M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, and :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, and :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CL$ <0231.g1:< C L >>` is to :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>`, so is :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />` to :g:`$MF$ <0231.g1:/ M F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MF$ <0231.g1:/ M F />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0231.g1:/ M F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$CF$ <0231.g1:/ C F />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

I say next that it is also a sixth apotome.

For, since :g:`$FL$ <0231.g1:< F L >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, let :g:`$FM$ <0231.g1:/ F M />` be bisected at :g:`$N$ <0231.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$NO$ <0231.g1:/ N O />` be drawn through :g:`$N$ <0231.g1:N>` parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />`; therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0231.g1:< F O >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0231.g1:< N L >>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` are incommensurable in square,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable with the square on :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` is to :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0231.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`$KM$ <0231.g1:/ K M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$CK$ <0231.g1:/ C K />` is incommensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0231.g1:/ K M />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />` is a mean proportional between the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, and :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>` equal to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`, and :g:`$NL$ <0231.g1:< N L >>` equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0231.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0231.g1:/ G B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$NL$ <0231.g1:< N L >>` is also a mean proportional between :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>`; therefore, as :g:`$CH$ <0231.g1:< C H >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0231.g1:< N L >>`, so is :g:`$NL$ <0231.g1:< N L >>` to :g:`$KL$ <0231.g1:< K L >>`.

.. _folio-iii-0233:

.. folio:: 0233
   :page: 229

.. assertion::

   And for the same reason as before the square on :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MF$ <0231.g1:/ M F />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$CM$ <0231.g1:/ C M />`. :just:`[X. 18] <X.18>`

.. subclause::

   And neither of them is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0231.g1:/ C D />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 6]

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0231.g1:/ C F />` is a sixth apotome.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
