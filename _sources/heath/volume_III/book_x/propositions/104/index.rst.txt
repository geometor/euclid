PROPOSITION 104.
================

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with an apotome of a medial straight line is an apotome of a medial straight line and the same in order.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome of a medial straight line, and let :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`;

I say that :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` is also an apotome of a medial straight line and the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0235.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 104 diagram

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` is an apotome of a medial straight line, let :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` be the annex to it.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 74, 75]`

Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$BE$ <0235.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$DF$ <0235.g1:/ D F />`; :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />` is also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$BE$ <0235.g1:/ B E />` with :g:`$DF$ <0235.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[V. 12, X. 11] <V.12, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` are also medial straight lines :just:`[X. 23] <X.23>` commensurable in square only; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>` therefore :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` is an apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`[X. 74, 75]`

.. folio:: 0236
   :page: 232

I say next that it is also the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`.

.. subclause::

   Since, as :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the square on :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />` is to the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />`, so is the square on :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />` to the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` is also commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 16, X. 11] <V.16, X.11>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, if the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` is rational, the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` will also be rational, :just:`[X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>` and if the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` is medial, the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` is also medial. :just:`[X. 23, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` is an apotome of a medial straight line and the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[X. 74, 75]`

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
