PROPOSITION 105.
================

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with a minor straight line is minor.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0236.g1:/ A B />` be a minor straight line, and :g:`$CD$ <0236.g1:/ C D />` commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0236.g1:/ A B />`;

I say that :g:`$CD$ <0236.g1:/ C D />` is also minor.

.. picture:: 0236.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 105 diagram

Let the same construction be made as before;

.. subclause::

   then, since :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` are incommensurable in square, :just:`[X. 76] <X.76>`

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />` are also incommensurable in square.

.. subclause:: [V. 12, V. 16] <V.12, V.16>

   Now since, as :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the square on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />` is to the square on :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />`, so is the square on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />` to the square on :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`, as the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` are to the square on :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />`, so are the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />` to the square on :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 18] <V.18>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$BE$ <0236.g1:/ B E />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$DF$ <0236.g1:/ D F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` is also commensurable with the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 16, X. 11] <V.16, X.11>`

.. subclause:: [X. 76] <X.76>

   But the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />` is also rational. :just:`[X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>`

.. _folio-iii-0237:

.. folio:: 0237
   :page: 233

.. subclause::

   Again, since, as the square on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />` is to the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />`, so is the square on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />` to the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`, while the square on :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` is also commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause:: [x. 76]

   But the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0236.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0236.g1:/ E B />` is medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />` is also medial; :just:`[x. 23, Por.]` therefore :g:`$CF$ <0236.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0236.g1:/ F D />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial.

.. assertion:: [x. 76]

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0236.g1:/ C D />` is minor.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
