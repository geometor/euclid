PROPOSITION 111.
================

.. enunciation::

   The apotome is not the same with the binomial straight line.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0244.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome; I say that :g:`$AB$ <0244.g1:/ A B />` is not the same with the binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0244.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 111

For, if possible, let it be so; let a rational straight line :g:`$DC$ <0244.g1:/ D C />` be set out, and to :g:`$CD$ <0244.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied the rectangle :g:`$CE$ <0244.g1:< C E >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0244.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$DE$ <0244.g1:/ D E />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0244.g1:/ A B />` is an apotome,

.. assertion:: [x. 97]

   :g:`$DE$ <0244.g1:/ D E />` is a first apotome.

Let :g:`$EF$ <0244.g1:/ E F />` be the annex to it;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0244.g1:/ F E />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, the square on :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FE$ <0244.g1:/ F E />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />`, and :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$DC$ <0244.g1:/ D C />` set out. :just:`[x. Deff. III. 1]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AB$ <0244.g1:/ A B />` is binomial,

.. assertion:: [x. 60]

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0244.g1:/ D E />` is a first binomial straight line.

Let it be divided into its terms at :g:`$G$ <0244.g1:G>`, and let :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />` be the greater term;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0244.g1:/ G E />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,

.. _folio-iii-0245:

.. folio:: 0245
   :page: 241

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GE$ <0244.g1:/ G E />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />`, and the greater term :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$DC$ <0244.g1:/ D C />` set out. :just:`[x. Deff. II. 1]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$DG$ <0244.g1:/ D G />`; :just:`[x. 12]` therefore the remainder :g:`$GF$ <0244.g1:/ G F />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DF$ <0244.g1:/ D F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0244.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 13]

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0244.g1:/ F G />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0244.g1:/ E F />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GF$ <0244.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0244.g1:/ F E />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EG$ <0244.g1:/ E G />` is an apotome. :just:`[x. 73]`

.. subclause::

   But it is also rational:

.. assertion::

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the apotome is not the same with the binomial straight line.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
