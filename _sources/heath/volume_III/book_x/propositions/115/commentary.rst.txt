Commentary on Proposition X.115
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.115 <X.115>`.

.. _folio-iii-0259:

.. folio:: 0259
   :page: 255

:person:`Heiberg <heiberg>` is clearly right in holding that this proposition, at all events, is alien to the general scope of Book X, and is therefore probably an interpolation, made however before :person:`Theon <theon-of-alexandria>`'s time. It is of the same character as a scholium at the end of the Book, which is (along with the interpolated proposition proving, in two ways, the incommensurability of the diagonal of a square with its side) relegated by :person:`August <august>` as well as :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>` to an Appendix.

The proposition amounts to this.

The straight line $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ being medial, if $\sigma$ be a rational straight line, $\sqrt{k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho\sigma}$ is a new irrational straight line. So is the mean proportional between this and another rational straight line $\sigma'$, and so on indefinitely.
