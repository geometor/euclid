PROPOSITION 115.
================

.. enunciation::

   From a medial straight line there arise irrational straight lines infinite in number, and none of them is the same as any of the preceding.

Let :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` be a medial straight line; I say that from :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` there arise irrational straight lines infinite in number, and none of them is the same as any of the preceding.

.. picture:: 0258.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 115 diagram

Let a rational straight line :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>` be set out, and let the square on :g:`$C$ <0258.g1:C>` be equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>`, :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>`;

.. assertion:: [X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0258.g1:C>` is irrational;

.. subclause::

   for that which is contained by an irrational and a rational straight line is irrational. :just:`[deduction from X. 20]`

.. assertion::

   And it is not the same with any of the preceding; for the square on none of the preceding, if applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a medial straight line.

Again, let the square on :g:`$D$ <0258.g1:D>` be equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0258.g1:C>`;

.. assertion:: [deduction from X. 20]

   therefore the square on :g:`$D$ <0258.g1:D>` is irrational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$D$ <0258.g1:D>` is irrational; :just:`[X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>` and it is not the same with any of the preceding, for the square on none of the preceding, if applied to a rational straight line, produces :g:`$C$ <0258.g1:C>` as breadth.

.. qed::

   Similarly, if this arrangement proceeds :term:`*ad infinitum* <ad-infinitum>`, it is manifest that from the medial straight line there arise irrational straight lines infinite in number, and none is the same with any of the preceding. :just:`Q. E. D.`
