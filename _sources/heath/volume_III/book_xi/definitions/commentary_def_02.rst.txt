Commentary on Book XI Definition 2
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book XI Definitions <XI.def>`.

Στερεοῦ δὲ πέρας :term:`ἐπιφάνεια <surface>`.

In like manner :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` says (:cite:`*Metaph.* 1066 b 23 <aristotle:met:1066.b.23>`) that the notion (:term:`λόγος <ratio>`) of body is "that which is bounded by surfaces" (ἐπιπέδοις in this case) and (:cite:`*Metaph.* 1060 b 15 <aristotle:met:1060.b.15>`) "surfaces (ἐπιφάνειαι) are divisions of bodies."

So :person:`Heron <heron>` (Def. 11): "Every solid is bounded (περατοῦται) by surfaces, and is produced when a surface is moved from a forward position in a backward direction."
