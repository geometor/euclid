Commentary on Book XI Definition 8
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book XI Definitions <XI.def>`.

Παράλληλα ἐπίπεδά ἐστι τὰ ἀσύμπτωτα.

:person:`Heron <heron>` has the same definition of parallel planes (Def. 115). The Greek word which is translated "which do not meet" is ἀσύμπτωτα, the term which has been adopted for the asymptotes of a curve.
