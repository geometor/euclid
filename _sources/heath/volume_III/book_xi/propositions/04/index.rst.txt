PROPOSITION 4.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be set up at right angles to two straight lines which cut one another, at their common point of section, it will also be at right angles to the plane through them.

.. _folio-iii-0282:

.. folio:: 0282
   :page: 278

For let a straight line :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` be set up at right angles to the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`, which cut one another at the point :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`, from :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`; I say that :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` is also at right angles to the plane through :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

For let :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$CE$ <0282.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0282.g1:/ E D />` be cut off :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A E / = 0282.g1:/ E B / = 0282.g1:/ C E / = 0282.g1:/ E D />` to one another, and let any straight line :g:`$GEH$ <0282.g1:/ G E H />` be drawn across through :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`, at random; let :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />` be joined, and further let :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />`, :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />`, :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />` be joined from the point :g:`$F$ <0282.g1:F>` taken at random &lt;on :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` &gt;.

.. picture:: 0282.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 4

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0282.g1:/ E D />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A E / = 0282.g1:/ C E /; 0282.g1:/ E D / = 0282.g1:/ E B />` to the two straight lines :g:`$CE$ <0282.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, and contain :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< A E D ) = 0282.g1:< C E B )>` angles, :just:`[I. 15] <I.15>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A D / = 0282.g1:/ C B />` to the base :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />`, and the triangle :g:`$AED$ <0282.g1:< A E D >>` will be :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< A E D > = 0282.g1:< C E B >>` to the triangle :g:`$CEB$ <0282.g1:< C E B >>`; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>` so that the angle :g:`$DAE$ <0282.g1:< D A E )>` is also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< D A E ) = 0282.g1:< E B C )>` to the angle :g:`$EBC$ <0282.g1:< E B C )>`.

.. subclause:: [I. 15] <I.15>

   But the angle :g:`$AEG$ <0282.g1:< A E G )>` is also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< A E G ) = 0282.g1:< B E H )>` to the angle :g:`$BEH$ <0282.g1:< B E H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AGE$ <0282.g1:< A G E >>`, :g:`$BEH$ <0282.g1:< B E H >>` are two triangles which have two angles :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< D A E ) = 0282.g1:< E B C ); 0282.g1:< A E G ) = 0282.g1:< B E H )>` to two angles respectively, and one side :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A E / = 0282.g1:/ E B />` to one side, namely that adjacent to the :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< D A E ) = 0282.g1:< E B C ); 0282.g1:< A E G ) = 0282.g1:< B E H )>` angles, that is to say, :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`; therefore they will also have the remaining sides :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ E H /; 0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to the remaining sides. :just:`[I. 26] <I.26>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ E H /; 0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0282.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A E / = 0282.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, while :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B />` to the base :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />` is also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F C / = 0282.g1:/ F D />` to :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A D / = 0282.g1:/ C B />` to :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />`, and :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` is also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B /; 0282.g1:/ A D / = 0282.g1:/ B C />` to the two sides :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0282.g1:/ B C />` respectively; and the base :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F D / = 0282.g1:/ F C />` to the base :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$FAD$ <0282.g1:< F A D )>` is also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< F A D ) = 0282.g1:< F B C )>` to the angle :g:`$FBC$ <0282.g1:< F B C )>`.

.. _folio-iii-0283:

.. folio:: 0283
   :page: 279

.. subclause::

   And since, again, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`, and further :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B /; 0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to the two sides :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`. And the angle :g:`$FAG$ <0282.g1:< F A G )>` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< F A G ) = 0282.g1:< F B H )>` to the angle :g:`$FBH$ <0282.g1:< F B H )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F G / = 0282.g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   Now since, again, :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0282.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` is common, the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ H E />` to the two sides :g:`$HE$ <0282.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />`; and the base :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F G / = 0282.g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>` to the angle :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the angles :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>`, :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>` is :eq:`right <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at :eq:`right angles <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>` to :g:`$GH$ <0282.g1:/ G H />` drawn at random through :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane; :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane through :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
