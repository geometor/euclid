PROPOSITION 4.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be set up at right angles to two straight lines which cut one another, at their common point of section, it will also be at right angles to the plane through them.

.. _folio-iii-0282:

.. folio:: 0282
   :page: 278

For let a straight line :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` be set up at right angles to the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`, which cut one another at the point :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`, from :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`; I say that :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` is also at right angles to the plane through :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

For let :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$CE$ <0282.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0282.g1:/ E D />` be cut off equal to one another, and let any straight line :g:`$GEH$ <0282.g1:/ G E H />` be drawn across through :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`, at random; let :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />` be joined, and further let :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />`, :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />`, :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />` be joined from the point :g:`$F$ <0282.g1:F>` taken at random &lt;on :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` &gt;.

.. picture:: 0282.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 4

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0282.g1:/ E D />` are equal to the two straight lines :g:`$CE$ <0282.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, and contain equal angles, :just:`[I. 15] <I.15>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` is equal to the base :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />`, and the triangle :g:`$AED$ <0282.g1:< A E D >>` will be equal to the triangle :g:`$CEB$ <0282.g1:< C E B >>`; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>` so that the angle :g:`$DAE$ <0282.g1:< D A E )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$EBC$ <0282.g1:< E B C )>`.

.. subclause:: [I. 15] <I.15>

   But the angle :g:`$AEG$ <0282.g1:< A E G )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$BEH$ <0282.g1:< B E H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AGE$ <0282.g1:< A G E >>`, :g:`$BEH$ <0282.g1:< B E H >>` are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely that adjacent to the equal angles, that is to say, :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`; therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides. :just:`[I. 26] <I.26>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />` is equal to :g:`$EH$ <0282.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AE$ <0282.g1:/ A E />` is equal to :g:`$EB$ <0282.g1:/ E B />`, while :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` is equal to the base :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />` is also equal to :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$CB$ <0282.g1:/ C B />`, and :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` is also equal to :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AD$ <0282.g1:/ A D />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0282.g1:/ B C />` respectively; and the base :g:`$FD$ <0282.g1:/ F D />` was proved equal to the base :g:`$FC$ <0282.g1:/ F C />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$FAD$ <0282.g1:< F A D )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FBC$ <0282.g1:< F B C )>`.

.. _folio-iii-0283:

.. folio:: 0283
   :page: 279

.. subclause::

   And since, again, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` was proved equal to :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`, and further :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` also equal to :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`. And the angle :g:`$FAG$ <0282.g1:< F A G )>` was proved equal to the angle :g:`$FBH$ <0282.g1:< F B H )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the base :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   Now since, again, :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />` was proved equal to :g:`$EH$ <0282.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` is common, the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$HE$ <0282.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />`; and the base :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the base :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the angles :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>`, :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>` is right.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to :g:`$GH$ <0282.g1:/ G H />` drawn at random through :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane; :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane through :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
