PROPOSITION 12.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To set up a straight line at right angles to a given plane from a given point in it.

Let the plane of reference be the given plane, and :g:`$A$ <0298.g1:A>` the point in it; thus it is required to set up from the point :g:`$A$ <0298.g1:A>` a straight line at right angles to the plane of reference.

.. picture:: 0298.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12

Let any elevated point :g:`$B$ <0298.g1:B>` be conceived, from :g:`$B$ <0298.g1:B>` let :g:`$BC$ <0298.g1:/ B C />` be drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference, :just:`[XI. 11] <XI.11>` and through the point :g:`$A$ <0298.g1:A>` let :g:`$AD$ <0298.g1:/ A D />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0298.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

Then, since :g:`$AD$ <0298.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$CB$ <0298.g1:/ C B />` are two parallel straight lines, while one of them, :g:`$BC$ <0298.g1:/ B C />`, is at right angles to the plane of reference,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining one, :g:`$AD$ <0298.g1:/ A D />`, is also at right angles to the plane of reference. :just:`[XI. 8] <XI.8>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AD$ <0298.g1:/ A D />` has been set up at right angles to the given plane from the point :g:`$A$ <0298.g1:A>` in it.
