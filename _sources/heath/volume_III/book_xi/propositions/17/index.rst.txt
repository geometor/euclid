PROPOSITION 17.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines be cut by parallel planes, they will be cut in the same ratios.

For let the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0305.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0305.g2:/ C D />` be cut by the parallel planes :g:`$GH$ <0305.g2:< G H >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0305.g2:< K L >>`, :g:`$MN$ <0305.g2:< M N >>` at the points :g:`$A$ <0305.g2:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0305.g2:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0305.g2:B>` and :g:`$C$ <0305.g2:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0305.g2:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0305.g2:D>`; I say that, as the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0305.g2:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0305.g2:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0305.g2:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0305.g2:/ F D />`.

.. picture:: 0305.g2.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 17

For let :g:`$AC$ <0305.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0305.g2:/ B D />`, :g:`$AD$ <0305.g2:/ A D />` be joined, let :g:`$AD$ <0305.g2:/ A D />` meet the plane :g:`$KL$ <0305.g2:< K L >>` at the point :g:`$O$ <0305.g2:O>`, and let :g:`$EO$ <0305.g2:/ E O />`, :g:`$OF$ <0305.g2:/ O F />` be joined.

.. folio:: 0306

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two parallel planes :g:`$KL$ <0305.g2:< K L >>`, :g:`$MN$ <0305.g2:< M N >>` are cut by the plane :g:`$EBDO$ <0305.g2:< E B D O >>`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 16] <XI.16>

   their common sections :g:`$EO$ <0305.g2:/ E O />`, :g:`$BD$ <0305.g2:/ B D />` are parallel.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason, since the two parallel planes :g:`$GH$ <0305.g2:< G H >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0305.g2:< K L >>` are cut by the plane :g:`$AOFC$ <0305.g2:< A O F C >>`,

.. assertion:: [*id.*]

   their common sections :g:`$AC$ <0305.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$OF$ <0305.g2:/ O F />` are parallel.

.. subclause::

   And, since the straight line :g:`$EO$ <0305.g2:/ E O />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$BD$ <0305.g2:/ B D />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0305.g2:< A B D >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$AE$ <0305.g2:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0305.g2:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$AO$ <0305.g2:/ A O />` to :g:`$OD$ <0305.g2:/ O D />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the straight line :g:`$OF$ <0305.g2:/ O F />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$AC$ <0305.g2:/ A C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0305.g2:< A D C >>`,

.. assertion:: [*id.*]

   proportionally, as :g:`$AO$ <0305.g2:/ A O />` is to :g:`$OD$ <0305.g2:/ O D />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0305.g2:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0305.g2:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved that, as :g:`$AO$ <0305.g2:/ A O />` is to :g:`$OD$ <0305.g2:/ O D />`, so is :g:`$AE$ <0305.g2:/ A E />` to :g:`$EB$ <0305.g2:/ E B />`;

.. assertion:: [v. 11]

   therefore also, as :g:`$AE$ <0305.g2:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0305.g2:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0305.g2:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0305.g2:/ F D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
