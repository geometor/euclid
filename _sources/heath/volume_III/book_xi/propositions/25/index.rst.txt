PROPOSITION 25.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane which is parallel to the opposite planes, then, as the base is to the base, so will the solid be to the solid.

For let the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$ABCD$ <0329.g1:< A B C D >>` be cut by the plane :g:`$FG$ <0329.g1:< F G >>` which is parallel to the opposite planes :g:`$RA$ <0329.g1:< R A >>`, :g:`$DH$ <0329.g1:< D H >>`; I say that, as the base :g:`$AEFV$ <0329.g1:< A E F V >>` is to the base :g:`$EHCF$ <0329.g1:< E H C F >>`, so is the solid :g:`$ABFU$ <0329.g1:< A B F U >>` to the solid :g:`$EGCD$ <0329.g1:< E G C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0329.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0329 Figure 1

For let :g:`$AH$ <0329.g1:/ A H />` be produced in each direction, let any number of straight lines whatever, :g:`$AK$ <0329.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0329.g1:/ K L />`, be made :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ A K / = 0329.g1:/ K L / = 0329.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$AE$ <0329.g1:/ A E />`, and any number whatever, :g:`$HM$ <0329.g1:/ H M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0329.g1:/ M N />`, :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ H M / = 0329.g1:/ M N / = 0329.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0329.g1:/ E H />`; and let the parallelograms :g:`$LP$ <0329.g1:< L P >>`, :g:`$KV$ <0329.g1:< K V >>`, :g:`$HW$ <0329.g1:< H W >>`, :g:`$MS$ <0329.g1:< M S >>` and the solids :g:`$LQ$ <0329.g1:< L Q >>`, :g:`$KR$ <0329.g1:< K R >>`, :g:`$DM$ <0329.g1:< D M >>`, :g:`$MT$ <0329.g1:< M T >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the straight lines :g:`$LK$ <0329.g1:/ L K />`, :g:`$KA$ <0329.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0329.g1:/ A E />` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ L K / = 0329.g1:/ K A / = 0329.g1:/ A E />` to one another,

.. assertion::

   the parallelograms :g:`$LP$ <0329.g1:< L P >>`, :g:`$KV$ <0329.g1:< K V >>`, :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>` are also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L P > = 0329.g1:< K V > = 0329.g1:< A F >>` to one another, :g:`$KO$ <0329.g1:< K O >>`, :g:`$KB$ <0329.g1:< K B >>`, :g:`$AG$ <0329.g1:< A G >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< K O > = 0329.g1:< K B > = 0329.g1:< A G >>` to one another, and further :g:`$LX$ <0329.g1:< L X >>`, :g:`$KQ$ <0329.g1:< K Q >>`, :g:`$AR$ <0329.g1:< A R >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L X > = 0329.g1:< K Q > = 0329.g1:< A R >>` to one another, for they are opposite. :just:`[XI. 24] <XI.24>`

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the parallelograms :g:`$EC$ <0329.g1:< E C >>`, :g:`$HW$ <0329.g1:< H W >>`, :g:`$MS$ <0329.g1:< M S >>` are also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< E C > = 0329.g1:< H W > = 0329.g1:< M S >>` to one another, :g:`$HG$ <0329.g1:< H G >>`, :g:`$HI$ <0329.g1:< H I >>`, :g:`$IN$ <0329.g1:< I N >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< H G > = 0329.g1:< H I > = 0329.g1:< I N >>` to one another, and further :g:`$DH$ <0329.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$MY$ <0329.g1:< M Y >>`, :g:`$NT$ <0329.g1:< N T >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< D H > = 0329.g1:< M Y > = 0329.g1:< N T >>` to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the solids :g:`$LQ$ <0329.g1:< L Q >>`, :g:`$KR$ <0329.g1:< K R >>`, :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>` three planes are equal to three planes.

.. _folio-iii-0330:

.. folio:: 0330
   :page: 326

.. subclause::

   But the three planes are equal to the three opposite;

.. assertion::

   therefore the three solids :g:`$LQ$ <0329.g1:< L Q >>`, :g:`$KR$ <0329.g1:< K R >>`, :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L Q > = 0329.g1:< K R > = 0329.g1:< A U >>` to one another.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the three solids :g:`$ED$ <0329.g1:< E D >>`, :g:`$DM$ <0329.g1:< D M >>`, :g:`$MT$ <0329.g1:< M T >>` are also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< E D > = 0329.g1:< D M > = 0329.g1:< M T >>` to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever multiple the base :g:`$LF$ <0329.g1:< L F >>` is of the base :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>`, the same multiple also is the solid :g:`$LU$ <0329.g1:< L U >>` of the solid :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason, whatever multiple the base :g:`$NF$ <0329.g1:< N F >>` is of the base :g:`$FH$ <0329.g1:< F H >>`, the same multiple also is the solid :g:`$NU$ <0329.g1:< N U >>` of the solid :g:`$HU$ <0329.g1:< H U >>`.

.. assertion::

   And, if the base :g:`$LF$ <0329.g1:< L F >>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L F > = 0329.g1:< N F >>` to the base :g:`$NF$ <0329.g1:< N F >>`, the solid :g:`$LU$ <0329.g1:< L U >>` is also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L U > = 0329.g1:< N U >>` to the solid :g:`$NU$ <0329.g1:< N U >>`; if the base :g:`$LF$ <0329.g1:< L F >>` exceeds the base :g:`$NF$ <0329.g1:< N F >>`, the solid :g:`$LU$ <0329.g1:< L U >>` also exceeds the solid :g:`$NU$ <0329.g1:< N U >>`; and, if one falls short, the other falls short.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, there being four magnitudes, the two bases :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>`, :g:`$FH$ <0329.g1:< F H >>`, and the two solids :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>`, :g:`$UH$ <0329.g1:< U H >>`, equimultiples have been taken of the base :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>` and the solid :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>`, namely the base :g:`$LF$ <0329.g1:< L F >>` and the solid :g:`$LU$ <0329.g1:< L U >>`, and equimultiples of the base :g:`$HF$ <0329.g1:< H F >>` and the solid :g:`$HU$ <0329.g1:< H U >>`, namely the base :g:`$NF$ <0329.g1:< N F >>` and the solid :g:`$NU$ <0329.g1:< N U >>`, and it has been proved that, if the base :g:`$LF$ <0329.g1:< L F >>` exceeds the base :g:`$FN$ <0329.g1:< F N >>`, the solid :g:`$LU$ <0329.g1:< L U >>` also exceeds the solid :g:`$NU$ <0329.g1:< N U >>`, if the bases are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L F > = 0329.g1:< F N >>`, the solids are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L U > = 0329.g1:< N U >>`, and if the base falls short, the solid falls short.

.. qed::

   Therefore, as the base :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>` is to the base :g:`$FH$ <0329.g1:< F H >>`, so is the solid :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>` to the solid :g:`$UH$ <0329.g1:< U H >>`. :just:`[v. Def. 5] <V.def.5>` :just:`Q. E. D.`
