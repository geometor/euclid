PROPOSITION 27.
===============

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line to describe a parallelepipedal solid similar and similarly situated to a given parallelepipedal solid.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0333.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line and :g:`$CD$ <0333.g1:< C D >>` the given parallelepipedal solid ; thus it is required to describe on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0333.g1:/ A B />` a parallelepipedal solid similar and similarly situated to the given parallelepipedal solid :g:`$CD$ <0333.g1:< C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0333.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 27

For on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0333.g1:/ A B />` and at the point :g:`$A$ <0333.g1:A>` on it let the solid angle, contained by the angles :g:`$BAH$ <0333.g1:< B A H )>`, :g:`$HAK$ <0333.g1:< H A K )>`, :g:`$KAB$ <0333.g1:< K A B )>`, be constructed equal to the solid angle at :g:`$C$ <0333.g1:C>`, so that the angle :g:`$BAH$ <0333.g1:< B A H )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ECF$ <0333.g1:< E C F )>`, the angle :g:`$BAK$ <0333.g1:< B A K )>` equal to the angle :g:`$ECG$ <0333.g1:< E C G )>`, and the angle :g:`$KAH$ <0333.g1:< K A H )>` to the angle :g:`$GCF$ <0333.g1:< G C F )>` ; and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$EC$ <0333.g1:/ E C />` is to :g:`$CG$ <0333.g1:/ C G />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0333.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$AK$ <0333.g1:/ A K />`, and, as :g:`$GC$ <0333.g1:/ G C />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0333.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$KA$ <0333.g1:/ K A />` to :g:`$AH$ <0333.g1:/ A H />`. :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$EC$ <0333.g1:/ E C />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0333.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0333.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$AH$ <0333.g1:/ A H />`. :just:`[V. 22] <V.22>`

Let the parallelogram :g:`$HB$ <0333.g1:< H B >>` and the solid :g:`$AL$ <0333.g1:< A L >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`$EC$ <0333.g1:/ E C />` is to :g:`$CG$ <0333.g1:/ C G />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0333.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$AK$ <0333.g1:/ A K />`, and the sides about the equal angles :g:`$ECG$ <0333.g1:< E C G )>`, :g:`$BAK$ <0333.g1:< B A K )>` are thus proportional,

.. folio:: 0334
   :page: 330

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$GE$ <0333.g1:< G E >>` is similar to the parallelogram :g:`$KB$ <0333.g1:< K B >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the parallelogram :g:`$KH$ <0333.g1:< K H >>` is also similar to the parallelogram :g:`$GF$ <0333.g1:< G F >>`, and further :g:`$FE$ <0333.g1:< F E >>` to :g:`$HB$ <0333.g1:< H B >>` ; therefore three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$CD$ <0333.g1:< C D >>` are similar to three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$AL$ <0333.g1:< A L >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the former three are both equal and similar to the three opposite parallelograms, and the latter three are both equal and similar to the three opposite parallelograms ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole solid :g:`$CD$ <0333.g1:< C D >>` is similar to the whole solid :g:`$AL$ <0333.g1:< A L >>`. :just:`[XI. Def. 9] <XI.def.9>`

.. qed::

   Therefore on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0333.g1:/ A B />` there has been described :g:`$AL$ <0333.g1:< A L >>` similar and similarly situated to the given parallelepipedal solid :g:`$CD$ <0333.g1:< C D >>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
