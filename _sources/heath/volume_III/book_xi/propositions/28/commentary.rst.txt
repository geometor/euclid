Commentary on Proposition XI.28
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XI.28 <XI.28>`.

.. folio:: 0335
   :page: 331

:person:`Simson <simson>` properly observes that it ought to be proved that the diagonals of two opposite faces are in one plane, before we speak of drawing a plane through them. :person:`Clavius <clavius>` supplied the proof, which is of course simple enough.

Since :g:`$EF$ <0334.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$CD$ <0334.g1:/ C D />` are both parallel to :g:`$AG$ <0334.g1:/ A G />` or :g:`$BH$ <0334.g1:/ B H />`, they are parallel to one another.

Consequently a plane can be drawn through :g:`$CD$ <0334.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0334.g1:/ E F />` and the diagonals :g:`$DE$ <0334.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$CF$ <0334.g1:/ C F />` are in that plane :just:`[XI. 7] <XI.7>`. Moreover :g:`$CD$ <0334.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0334.g1:/ E F />` are equal as well as parallel ; so that :g:`$CF$ <0334.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0334.g1:/ D E />` are also equal and parallel.

:person:`Simson <simson>` does not, however, seem to have noticed a more serious difficulty. The two prisms are shown by Euclid to be contained by equal faces—the faces are in fact equal and similar—and Euclid then infers at once that the prisms are *equal*. But they are not equal in the only sense in which we have, at present, a right to speak of solids being equal, namely in the sense that they can be *applied*, the one to the other. They cannot be so applied because the faces, though equal respectively, are not *similarly arranged* ; consequently the prisms are *symmetrical*, and it ought to be proved that they are, though not equal and similar, equal in content, or *equivalent*, as :person:`Legendre <legendre>` has it.

:person:`Legendre <legendre>` addressed himself to proving that the two prisms are equivalent, and his method has been adopted, though his name is not mentioned, by :person:`Schultze <schultze>` and Sevenoak and by :person:`Holgate <holgate>`. Certain preliminary propositions are necessary.

.. picture:: 0335.g1.svg
   :alt: Prism cut by parallel planes

**1.** *The sections of a prism made by parallel planes cutting all the lateral edges are equal polygons.*

Suppose a prism :g:`$MN$ <0335.g1:< M N >>` cut by parallel planes which make sections :g:`$ABCDE$ <0335.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$A'B'C'D'E'$ <0335.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >>`.

Now :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0335.g1:/ C D />`, ... are respectively parallel to :g:`$A'B'$ <0335.g1:/ A' B' />`, :g:`$B'C'$ <0335.g1:/ B' C' />`, :g:`$C'D'$ <0335.g1:/ C' D' />`, ... :just:`[XI. 16] <XI.16>`

Therefore the angles :g:`$ABC$ <0335.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCD$ <0335.g1:< B C D )>`, ... are equal to the angles :g:`$A'B'C'$ <0335.g1:< A' B' C' )>`, :g:`$B'C'D'$ <0335.g1:< B' C' D' )>`, ... respectively. :just:`[XI. 10] <XI.10>`

Also :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0335.g1:/ C D />`, ... are respectively equal to :g:`$A'B'$ <0335.g1:/ A' B' />`, :g:`$B'C'$ <0335.g1:/ B' C' />`, :g:`$C'D'$ <0335.g1:/ C' D' />`, ... :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

Thus the polygons :g:`$ABCDE$ <0335.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$A'B'C'D'E'$ <0335.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >>` are equilateral and equiangular to one another.

**2.** *Two prisms are equal when they have a solid angle in each contained by three faces equal each to each and similarly arranged.*

Let the faces :g:`$ABCDE$ <0336.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$AG$ <0336.g1:< A G >>`, :g:`$AL$ <0336.g1:< A L >>` be equal and similarly placed to the faces :g:`$A'B'C'D'E'$ <0336.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >>`, :g:`$A'G'$ <0336.g1:< A' G' >>`, :g:`$A'L'$ <0336.g1:< A' L' >>`.

Since the three plane angles at :g:`$A$ <0336.g1:A>`, :g:`$A'$ <0336.g1:A'>` are equal respectively and are similarly placed, the trihedral angle at :g:`$A$ <0336.g1:A>` is equal to the trihedral angle at :g:`$A'$ <0336.g1:A'>`. :just:`[(3) in note to XI. 21]`
Place the trihedral angle at :g:`$A$ <0336.g1:A>` on that at :g:`$A'$ <0336.g1:A'>`. Then the face :g:`$ABCDE$ <0336.g1:< A B C D E >>` coincides with the face :g:`$A'B'C'D'E'$ <0336.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >>`, the face :g:`$AG$ <0336.g1:< A G >>` with the face :g:`$A'G'$ <0336.g1:< A' G' >>`, and the face :g:`$AL$ <0336.g1:< A L >>` with the face :g:`$A'L'$ <0336.g1:< A' L' >>`. The point :g:`$C$ <0336.g1:C>` falls on :g:`$C'$ <0336.g1:C'>` and :g:`$D$ <0336.g1:D>` on :g:`$D'$ <0336.g1:D'>`.

Since the lateral edges of a prism are parallel, :g:`$CH$ <0336.g1:/ C H />` will fall on :g:`$C'H'$ <0336.g1:/ C' H' />`, and :g:`$DK$ <0336.g1:/ D K />` on :g:`$D'K'$ <0336.g1:/ D' K' />`.

And the points :g:`$F$ <0336.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0336.g1:G>`, :g:`$L$ <0336.g1:L>` coincide respectively with :g:`$F'$ <0336.g1:F'>`, :g:`$G'$ <0336.g1:G'>`, :g:`$L'$ <0336.g1:L'>`, so that the planes :g:`$GK$ <0336.g1:< G K >>`, :g:`$G'K'$ <0336.g1:< G' K' >>` coincide.

Hence :g:`$H$ <0336.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0336.g1:K>` coincide with :g:`$H'$ <0336.g1:H'>`, :g:`$K'$ <0336.g1:K'>` respectively. Thus the prisms coincide throughout and are equal.

In the same way we can prove that two *truncated* prisms with three faces forming a solid angle related to one another as in the above proposition are identically equal.

In particular, *Cor.* *Two right prisms having equal bases and equal heights are equal.*

**3.** *An oblique prism is equivalent to a right prism whose base is a right section of the oblique prism and whose height is equal to a lateral edge of the oblique prism.*

Suppose :g:`$GL$ <0336.g2:< G L >>` to be a right section of the oblique prism :g:`$AD'$ <0336.g2:< A D' >>`, and let :g:`$GL'$ <0336.g2:< G L' >>` be a right prism on :g:`$GL$ <0336.g2:< G L >>` as base and with height equal to a lateral edge of :g:`$AD'$ <0336.g2:< A D' >>`.

.. picture:: 0336.g2.svg
   :alt: Oblique prism equivalent to right prism

Now the lateral edges of :g:`$GL'$ <0336.g2:< G L' >>` are equal to the lateral edges of :g:`$AD'$ <0336.g2:< A D' >>`.

Therefore :g:`$AG$ <0336.g2:/ A G />` = :g:`$A'G'$ <0336.g2:/ A' G' />`, :g:`$BH$ <0336.g2:/ B H />` = :g:`$B'H'$ <0336.g2:/ B' H' />`, :g:`$CK$ <0336.g2:/ C K />` = :g:`$C'K'$ <0336.g2:/ C' K' />`, etc.

Thus the faces :g:`$AH$ <0336.g2:< A H >>`, :g:`$BK$ <0336.g2:< B K >>`, :g:`$CL$ <0336.g2:< C L >>` are equal respectively to the faces :g:`$A'H'$ <0336.g2:< A' H' >>`, :g:`$B'K'$ <0336.g2:< B' K' >>`, :g:`$C'L'$ <0336.g2:< C' L' >>`.

Therefore [by the proposition above] (truncated prism :g:`$AL$ <0336.g2:< A L >>`) = (truncated prism :g:`$A'L'$ <0336.g2:< A' L' >>`).

Subtracting each from the whole solid :g:`$AL'$ <0336.g2:< A L' >>`, we see that the prisms :g:`$AD'$ <0336.g2:< A D' >>`, :g:`$GL'$ <0336.g2:< G L' >>` are equivalent.

Now suppose the parallelepiped of Euclid's proposition to be cut by the plane through :g:`$AG$ <0337.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$DF$ <0337.g1:/ D F />`.

Let :g:`$KLMN$ <0337.g1:< K L M N >>` be a right section of the parallelepiped cutting the edges :g:`$AD$ <0337.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$BC$ <0337.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$GF$ <0337.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$HE$ <0337.g1:/ H E />`. Then :g:`$KLMN$ <0337.g1:< K L M N >>` is a parallelogram; and, if the diagonal :g:`$KM$ <0337.g1:/ K M />` be drawn,

.. container:: center

   :g:`$\triangle KLM$ <0337.g1:< K L M >>` = :g:`$\triangle MNK$ <0337.g1:< M N K >>`.

.. picture:: 0337.g1.svg
   :alt: Parallelepiped cut by plane

Now the prism of which the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ABG$ <0337.g1:< A B G >>`, :g:`$DCF$ <0337.g1:< D C F >>` are the bases is equal to the right prism on :g:`$\triangle KLM$ <0337.g1:< K L M >>` as base and of height :g:`$AD$ <0337.g1:/ A D />`.

Similarly the prism of which the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$AGH$ <0337.g1:< A G H >>`, :g:`$DFE$ <0337.g1:< D F E >>` are the bases is equal to the right prism on :g:`$\triangle MNK$ <0337.g1:< M N K >>` as base and with height :g:`$AD$ <0337.g1:/ A D />`. :just:`[(3) above]`

And the right prisms on $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$KLM$ <0337.g1:< K L M >>`, :g:`$MNK$ <0337.g1:< M N K >>` as bases and of equal height :g:`$AD$ <0337.g1:/ A D />` are equal. :just:`[(2), Cor. above]`

Consequently the two prisms into which the parallelepiped is divided are equivalent.
