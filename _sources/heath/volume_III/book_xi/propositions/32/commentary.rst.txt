Commentary on Proposition XI.32
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XI.32 <XI.32>`.

.. folio:: 0345
   :page: 341

As :person:`Clavius <clavius>` observed, Euclid should have said, in applying the parallelogram :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` to :g:`$FG$ <0345.g1:/ F G />`, that it should be applied “in the angle :g:`$FGH$ <0345.g1:< F G H )>` :eq:`equal <0345.g1:< F G H ) = 0345.g1:< L C G )>` to the angle :g:`$LCG$ <0345.g1:< L C G )>`.” :person:`Simson <simson>` is however, I think, hypercritical when he states as regards the completion of the solid :g:`$GK$ <0345.g1:< G K >>` that it ought to be said, “complete
the solid of which the base is :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>`, and one of its insisting straight lines is :g:`$FD$ <0345.g1:/ F D />`." Surely, when we have two faces :g:`$DG$ <0345.g1:< D G >>`, :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` meeting in an edge, to say "complete the solid" is quite sufficient, though the words "on :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` as base" might perhaps as well be left out. The same "completion" of a parallelepipedal solid occurs in XI. 31 and 33.
