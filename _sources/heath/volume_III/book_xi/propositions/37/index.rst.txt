PROPOSITION 37.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described will also be proportional; and, if the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described be proportional, the straight lines will themselves also be proportional.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />` be four straight lines in proportion, so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />`; and let there be described on :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />` the similar and similarly situated parallelepipedal solids :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>`, :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>`, :g:`$ME$ <0362.g1:< M E >>`, :g:`$NG$ <0362.g1:< N G >>`; I say that, as :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>` is to :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`$ME$ <0362.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`$NG$ <0362.g1:< N G >>`.

.. picture:: 0362.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 37 diagram

.. subclause::

   For, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>` is similar to :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>` has to :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[XI. 33] <XI.33>`

.. _folio-iii-0363:

.. folio:: 0363
   :page: 359

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$ME$ <0363.g1:< M E >>` also has to :g:`$NG$ <0363.g1:< N G >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$EF$ <0363.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`$GH$ <0363.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[*id.*]`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$AB$ <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$AK$ <0363.g1:< A K >>` is to :g:`$LC$ <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`$ME$ <0363.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`$NG$ <0363.g1:< N G >>`.

Next, as the solid :g:`$AK$ <0363.g1:< A K >>` is to the solid :g:`$LC$ <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so let the solid :g:`$ME$ <0363.g1:< M E >>` be to the solid :g:`$NG$ <0363.g1:< N G >>`; I say that, as the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. subclause::

   For since, again, :g:`$KA$ <0363.g1:< K A >>` has to :g:`$LC$ <0363.g1:< L C >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$AB$ <0363.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$CD$ <0363.g1:/ C D />`, :just:`[XI. 33] <XI.33>` and :g:`$ME$ <0363.g1:< M E >>` also has to :g:`$NG$ <0363.g1:< N G >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$EF$ <0363.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`$GH$ <0363.g1:/ G H />`, :just:`[*id.*]` and, as :g:`$KA$ <0363.g1:< K A >>` is to :g:`$LC$ <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`$ME$ <0363.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`$NG$ <0363.g1:< N G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$AB$ <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
