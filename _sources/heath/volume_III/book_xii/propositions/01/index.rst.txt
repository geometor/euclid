PROPOSITION 1.
==============

.. enunciation::

   Similar polygons inscribed in circles are to one another as the squares on the diameters.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0373.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$FGH$ <0373.g1:( F G H )>` be circles, let :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>` be similar polygons inscribed in them, and let :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />`, :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />` be diameters of the circles; I say that, as the square on :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to the square on :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`, so is the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`.

.. picture:: 0373.g1.svg
   :alt: Similar polygons inscribed in circles

For let :g:`$BE$ <0373.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$AM$ <0373.g1:/ A M />`, :g:`$GL$ <0373.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`$FN$ <0373.g1:/ F N />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` is similar to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`, the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0373.g1:< B A E )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$GFL$ <0373.g1:< G F L )>`, and, as :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0373.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FL$ <0373.g1:/ F L />`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

Thus :g:`$BAE$ <0373.g1:< B A E >>`, :g:`$GFL$ <0373.g1:< G F L >>` are two triangles which have one angle equal to one angle, namely the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0373.g1:< B A E )>` to the angle :g:`$GFL$ <0373.g1:< G F L )>`, and the sides about the equal angles proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0373.g1:< A B E >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$FGL$ <0373.g1:< F G L >>`. :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0373.g1:< A E B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$FLG$ <0373.g1:< F L G )>`.

.. _folio-iii-0374:

.. folio:: 0374
   :page: 370

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0373.g1:< A E B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>`, for they stand on the same circumference; :just:`[III. 27] <III.27>` and the angle :g:`$FLG$ <0373.g1:< F L G )>` to the angle :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the right angle :g:`$BAM$ <0373.g1:< B A M )>` is also equal to the right angle :g:`$GFN$ <0373.g1:< G F N )>`; :just:`[III. 31] <III.31>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle is equal to the remaining angle. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABM$ <0373.g1:< A B M >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$FGN$ <0373.g1:< F G N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. subclause::

   But the ratio of the square on :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` to the square on :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />` is duplicate of the ratio of :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` to :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`, and the ratio of the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>` is duplicate of the ratio of :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`; :just:`[VI. 20] <VI.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the square on :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to the square on :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`, so is the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
