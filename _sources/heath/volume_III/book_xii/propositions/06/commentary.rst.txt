Commentary on Proposition XII.6
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XII.6 <XII.6>`.

.. folio:: 0397
   :page: 393

It will be seen that, in order to obtain the proportion (base :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>`) $: \triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MABCDE$ <0396.g1:< M A B C D E >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`), Euclid employs V. 18 (:term:`*componendo* <componendo>`) twice over, with an :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>` step :just:`[V. 22] <V.22>` intervening.

We might arrive at it more concisely by using V. 24 extended to any number of antecedents.

Thus

.. assertion::

   $\triangle$ :g:`$ABC$ <0396.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MABC$ <0396.g1:< M A B C >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`), $\triangle$ :g:`$ACD$ <0396.g1:< A C D >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MACD$ <0396.g1:< M A C D >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`),

and lastly

.. assertion::

   $\triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`).

Therefore, adding the antecedents :just:`[V. 24] <V.24>`, we have

.. assertion::

   (polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>`) $: \triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MABCDE$ <0396.g1:< M A B C D E >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`).

Again, since the pyramids :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`, :g:`$NFGH$ <0396.g1:< N F G H >>` are of the same height,

.. assertion::

   $\triangle$ :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$FGH$ <0396.g1:< F G H >>` $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MADE$ <0396.g1:< M A D E >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$NFGH$ <0396.g1:< N F G H >>`).

Lastly, using the same argument for the pyramid :g:`$NFGHKL$ <0396.g1:< N F G H K L >>` as for :g:`$MABCDE$ <0396.g1:< M A B C D E >>`, and inverting, we have

.. assertion::

   $\triangle$ :g:`$FGH$ <0396.g1:< F G H >>` $:$ (polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L >>`) $=$ (pyramid :g:`$NFGH$ <0396.g1:< N F G H >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$NFGHKL$ <0396.g1:< N F G H K L >>`).

Thus from the three proportions, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`,

.. assertion::

   (polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>`) $:$ (polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L >>`) $=$ (pyramid :g:`$MABCDE$ <0396.g1:< M A B C D E >>`) $:$ (pyramid :g:`$NFGHKL$ <0396.g1:< N F G H K L >>`).
