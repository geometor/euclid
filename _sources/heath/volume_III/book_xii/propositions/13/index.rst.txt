PROPOSITION 13.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a cylinder be cut by a plane which is parallel to its opposite planes, then, as the cylinder is to the cylinder, so will the axis be to the axis.

For let the cylinder :g:`$AD$ <0421.g1:< A D >>` be cut by the plane :g:`$GH$ <0421.g1:( G H )>` which is parallel to the opposite planes :g:`$AB$ <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`$CD$ <0421.g1:( C D )>`, and let the plane :g:`$GH$ <0421.g1:( G H )>` meet the axis at the point :g:`$K$ <0421.g1:K>`; I say that, as the cylinder :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` is to the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`, so is the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />`.

.. picture:: 0421.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13

For let the axis :g:`$EF$ <0421.g1:/ E F />` be produced in both directions to the points :g:`$L$ <0421.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0421.g1:M>`, and let there be set out any number whatever of axes :g:`$EN$ <0421.g1:/ E N />`, :g:`$NL$ <0421.g1:/ N L />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ E N / = 0421.g1:/ N L / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />`, and any number whatever :g:`$FO$ <0421.g1:/ F O />`, :g:`$OM$ <0421.g1:/ O M />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ F O / = 0421.g1:/ O M / = 0421.g1:/ F K />` to :g:`$FK$ <0421.g1:/ F K />`; and let the cylinder :g:`$PW$ <0421.g1:< P W >>` on the axis :g:`$LM$ <0421.g1:/ L M />` be conceived of which the circles :g:`$PQ$ <0421.g1:( P Q )>`, :g:`$VW$ <0421.g1:( V W )>` are the bases.

Let planes be carried through the points :g:`$N$ <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0421.g1:O>` parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`$CD$ <0421.g1:( C D )>` and to the bases of the cylinder :g:`$PW$ <0421.g1:< P W >>`, and let them produce the circles :g:`$RS$ <0421.g1:( R S )>`, :g:`$TU$ <0421.g1:( T U )>` about the centres :g:`$N$ <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0421.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the axes :g:`$LN$ <0421.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`$NE$ <0421.g1:/ N E />`, :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ L N / = 0421.g1:/ N E / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to one another,

.. _folio-iii-0422:

.. folio:: 0422
   :page: 418

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`$QR$ <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`$RB$ <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` are to one another as their bases. :just:`[XII. 11] <XII.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the bases are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:( P Q ) = 0421.g1:( R S ) = 0421.g1:( A B ) = 0421.g1:( G H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`$QR$ <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`$RB$ <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q R > = 0421.g1:< R B > = 0421.g1:< B G >>` to one another.

.. subclause::

   Since then the axes :g:`$LN$ <0421.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`$NE$ <0421.g1:/ N E />`, :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ L N / = 0421.g1:/ N E / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to one another, and the cylinders :g:`$QR$ <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`$RB$ <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q R > = 0421.g1:< R B > = 0421.g1:< B G >>` to one another, and the multitude of the former is equal to the multitude of the latter,

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever multiple the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is of the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />`, the same multiple also will the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` be of the cylinder :g:`$GB$ <0421.g1:< G B >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason, whatever multiple the axis :g:`$MK$ <0421.g1:/ M K />` is of the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />`, the same multiple also is the cylinder :g:`$WG$ <0421.g1:< W G >>` of the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`.

.. assertion::

   And, if the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ K L / = 0421.g1:/ K M />` to the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` will also be :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q G > = 0421.g1:< G W >>` to the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if the axis is greater than the axis, the cylinder will also be greater than the cylinder, and if less, less.

.. subclause::

   Thus, there being four magnitudes, the axes :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />` and the cylinders :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>`, :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`, there have been taken equimultiples of the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` and of the cylinder :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>`, namely the axis :g:`$LK$ <0421.g1:/ L K />` and the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>`, and equimultiples of the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />` and of the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`, namely the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />` and the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`; and it has been proved that, if the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is in excess of the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` is also in excess of the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ K L / = 0421.g1:/ K M />`, :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q G > = 0421.g1:< G W >>`, and if less, less.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` is to the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />`, so is the cylinder :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` to the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
