PROPOSITION 18.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Spheres are to one another in the triplicate ratio of their respective diameters.

Let the spheres :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` be conceived, and let :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` be their diameters; I say that the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has to the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   For, if the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has not to the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   then the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` will have either to some less sphere than the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`, or to a greater, the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

First, let it have that ratio to a less sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`, let :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` be conceived about the same centre with :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`, let there be inscribed in the greater sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` a polyhedral solid which does not touch the lesser sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` at its surface, :just:`[XII. 17] <XII.17>`
and let there also be inscribed in the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` a polyhedral solid similar to the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`;

.. _folio-iii-0439:

.. folio:: 0439
   :page: 435

.. assertion::

   therefore the polyhedral solid in :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has to the polyhedral solid in :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[XII. 17, Por.]`

.. picture:: 0439.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XII, Proposition 18

.. subclause::

   But the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` also has to the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is to the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`, so is the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` to the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`; and, alternately, as the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is to the polyhedron in it, so is the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` to the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is greater than the polyhedron in it;

.. assertion::

   therefore the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` is also greater than the polyhedron in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   But it is also less, for it is enclosed by it.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has not to a less sphere than the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. _folio-iii-0440:

.. folio:: 0440
   :page: 436

Similarly we can prove that neither has the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` to a less sphere than the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />`.

I say next that neither has the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` to any greater sphere than the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let it have that ratio to a greater, :g:`$LMN$ <0439.g1:( L M N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, inversely, the sphere :g:`$LMN$ <0439.g1:( L M N )>` has to the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` has to the diameter :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   But, inasmuch as :g:`$LMN$ <0439.g1:( L M N )>` is greater than :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the sphere :g:`$LMN$ <0439.g1:( L M N )>` is to the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>`, so is the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` to some less sphere than the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>`, as was before proved. :just:`[XII. 2, Lemma]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` also has to some less sphere than the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` : which was proved impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has not to any sphere greater than the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But it was proved that neither has it that ratio to a less sphere.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has to the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
