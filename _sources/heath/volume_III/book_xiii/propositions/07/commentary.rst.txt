Commentary on Proposition XIII.7
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XIII.7 <XIII.7>`.

.. folio:: 0457
   :page: 453

This proposition is required in XIII. 17. The steps of the proof may be shown thus.

I. Suppose that the angles at :g:`A <0455.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0455.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0455.g1:C>` are all :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< B A E ) = 0455.g1:< C B A ) = 0455.g1:< B C D )>`. Then the isosceles triangles :g:`BAE <0455.g1:< B A E >>`, :g:`ABC <0455.g1:< A B C >>` are :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< B A E > = 0455.g1:< A B C >>` in all respects; thus :g:`BE <0455.g1:/ B E />` :math:`=` :g:`AC <0455.g1:/ A C />`, :math:`\angle` :g:`BCA <0455.g1:< B C A )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`BEA <0455.g1:< B E A )>`, :math:`\angle` :g:`CAB <0455.g1:< C A B )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`EBA <0455.g1:< E B A )>`. By the last equality, :g:`FA <0455.g1:/ F A />` :math:`=` :g:`FB <0455.g1:/ F B />`, so that, since :g:`BE <0455.g1:/ B E />` :math:`=` :g:`AC <0455.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`FC <0455.g1:/ F C />` :math:`=` :g:`FE <0455.g1:/ F E />`. The :math:`\Delta\text{s}` :g:`FED <0455.g1:< F E D >>`, :g:`FCD <0455.g1:< F C D >>` are now :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< F E D > = 0455.g1:< F C D >>` in all respects, :just:`[I. 8, 4]` and :math:`\angle` :g:`FCD <0455.g1:< F C D )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`FED <0455.g1:< F E D )>`. But :math:`\angle` :g:`ACB <0455.g1:< A C B )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`AEB <0455.g1:< A E B )>`, from above, whence, by addition, :math:`\angle` :g:`BCD <0455.g1:< B C D )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`AED <0455.g1:< A E D )>`. Similarly it may be proved that :math:`\angle` :g:`CDE <0455.g1:< C D E )>` is also :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< C D E ) = 0455.g1:< B A E ) = 0455.g1:< C B A ) = 0455.g1:< B C D )>` to any one of the angles at :g:`A <0455.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0455.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0455.g1:C>`.

II. Suppose the angles at :g:`A <0455.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0455.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0455.g1:D>` to be :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< B A E ) = 0455.g1:< B C D ) = 0455.g1:< C D E )>`. Then the isosceles triangles :g:`ABE <0455.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`CBD <0455.g1:< C B D >>` are :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< A B E > = 0455.g1:< C B D >>` in all respects, and hence :g:`BE <0455.g1:/ B E />` :math:`=` :g:`BD <0455.g1:/ B D />` (so that :math:`\angle` :g:`BDE <0455.g1:< B D E )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`BED <0455.g1:< B E D )>`), and :math:`\angle` :g:`CDB <0455.g1:< C D B )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`AEB <0455.g1:< A E B )>`. By addition of the equal angles, :math:`\angle` :g:`CDE <0455.g1:< C D E )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`DEA <0455.g1:< D E A )>`. Similarly it may be proved that :math:`\angle` :g:`ABC <0455.g1:< A B C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0455.g1:< A B C ) = 0455.g1:< B A E ) = 0455.g1:< B C D ) = 0455.g1:< C D E )>` to each of the angles at :g:`A <0455.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0455.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0455.g1:D>`.
