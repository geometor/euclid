PROPOSITION 10.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an equilateral pentagon be inscribed in a circle, the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the squares on the side of the hexagon and on that of the decagon inscribed in the same circle.

Let :g:`$ABCDE$ <0462.g1:( A B C D E )>` be a circle, and let the equilateral pentagon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0462.g1:< A B C D E >>` be inscribed in the circle :g:`$ABCDE$ <0462.g1:( A B C D E )>`. I say that the square on the side of the pentagon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0462.g1:< A B C D E >>` is equal to the squares on the side of the hexagon and on that of the decagon inscribed in the circle :g:`$ABCDE$ <0462.g1:( A B C D E )>`.

For let the centre of the circle, the point :g:`$F$ <0462.g1:F>`, be taken, let :g:`$AF$ <0462.g1:/ A F />` be joined and carried through to the point :g:`$G$ <0462.g1:G>`, let :g:`$FB$ <0462.g1:/ F B />` be joined, let :g:`$FH$ <0462.g1:/ F H />` be drawn from :g:`$F$ <0462.g1:F>` perpendicular to :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />` and be carried through to :g:`$K$ <0462.g1:K>`,
let :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KB$ <0462.g1:/ K B />` be joined, let :g:`$FL$ <0462.g1:/ F L />` be again drawn from :g:`$F$ <0462.g1:F>` perpendicular to :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, and be carried through to :g:`$M$ <0462.g1:M>`, and let :g:`$KN$ <0462.g1:/ K N />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Since the circumference :g:`$ABCG$ <0462.g1:( A B C G )>` is equal to the circumference :g:`$AEDG$ <0462.g1:( A E D G )>`, and in them :g:`$ABC$ <0462.g1:( A B C )>` is equal to :g:`$AED$ <0462.g1:( A E D )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, the circumference :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>`, is equal to the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0462.g1:( G D )>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:/ C D />` belongs to a pentagon;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` belongs to a decagon.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$FA$ <0462.g1:/ F A />` is equal to :g:`$FB$ <0462.g1:/ F B />`, and :g:`$FH$ <0462.g1:/ F H />` is perpendicular,

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AFK$ <0462.g1:< A F K )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$KFB$ <0462.g1:< K F B )>`. :just:`[I. 5, I. 26] <I.5, I.26>`

.. assertion::

   Hence the circumference :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is also equal to :g:`$KB$ <0462.g1:( K B )>`; :just:`[III. 26] <III.26>` therefore the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>` is double of the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`; therefore the straight line :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />` is a side of a decagon.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is also double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>` is double of the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`, while the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is equal to the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is also double of the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is also double of :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` is equal to the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>` is double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`, since :g:`$KA$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is so also;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` is also double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`.

.. _folio-iii-0463:

.. folio:: 0463
   :page: 459

.. subclause::

   But, further, the circumference :g:`$CB$ <0462.g1:( C B )>` is also double of the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`, for the circumference :g:`$CB$ <0462.g1:( C B )>` is equal to :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:( B A )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole circumference :g:`$GB$ <0462.g1:( G B )>` is also double of :g:`$BM$ <0462.g1:( B M )>`; hence the angle :g:`$GFB$ <0462.g1:< G F B )>` is also double of the angle :g:`$BFM$ <0462.g1:< B F M )>`. :just:`[VI. 33] <VI.33>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$GFB$ <0462.g1:< G F B )>` is also double of the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>`, for the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F >>` and :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$AFB$ <0462.g1:< A F B )>` is equal to the remaining angle :g:`$BNF$ <0462.g1:< B N F )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`, so is :g:`$FB$ <0462.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />`; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AL$ <0462.g1:/ A L />` is equal to :g:`$LK$ <0462.g1:/ L K />`, while :g:`$LN$ <0462.g1:/ L N />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$KN$ <0462.g1:/ K N />` is equal to the base :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />`; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>` therefore the angle :g:`$LKN$ <0462.g1:< L K N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$LAN$ <0462.g1:< L A N )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$LAN$ <0462.g1:< L A N )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$KBN$ <0462.g1:< K B N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$LKN$ <0462.g1:< L K N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$KBN$ <0462.g1:< K B N )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle at :g:`$A$ <0462.g1:A>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$AKB$ <0462.g1:< A K B >>` and :g:`$AKN$ <0462.g1:< A K N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remaining angle :g:`$AKB$ <0462.g1:< A K B )>` is equal to the remaining angle :g:`$KNA$ <0462.g1:< K N A )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$KBA$ <0462.g1:< K B A >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$KNA$ <0462.g1:< K N A >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, so is :g:`$KA$ <0462.g1:/ K A />` to :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />`; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />` was also proved equal to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`;

.. _folio-iii-0464:

.. folio:: 0464
   :page: 460

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />` together with the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />`, that is, the square on :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />` :just:`[II. 2] <II.2>`, is equal to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />` together with the square on :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`.

.. assertion::

   And :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />` is a side of the pentagon, :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />` of the hexagon :just:`[IV. 15, Por.]`, and :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />` of the decagon.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
