Commentary on Proposition XIII.11
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XIII.11 <XIII.11>`.

.. folio:: 0469
   :page: 465

Here we require certain definitions and propositions of Book X. First we require the definition of an *apotome* :just:`[see X. 73]`, which is a straight line of the form $(\rho \sim \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho)$, where $\rho$ is a "rational" straight line and $k$ is any integer or numerical fraction, the square root of which is not *integral* or expressible in integers. The lesser of the straight lines $\rho$, $\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho$ is the *annex*.

Next we require the definition of the :term:`*fourth apotome* <fourth-apotome>` :just:`[X. Deff. III. (after X. 84)]`, which is a straight line of the form $(x - y)$, where $x$, $y$ are (being both rational and commensurable in square only) are also such that $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is incommensurable with $x$, while $x$ is commensurable with a given rational straight line $\rho$. As shown on X. 88 (note), the :term:`*fourth apotome* <fourth-apotome>` is of the form

$$(k\rho - \frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1 + \lambda}}).$$

Lastly the *minor* (straight line) is the irrational straight line defined in

X. 76. It is of the form $(x - y)$, where $x$, $y$ are incommensurable in square, and $(x^2 + y^2)$ is 'rational,' while $xy$ is 'medial.' As shown in the note on

X. 76, the *minor irrational straight line* is of the form

$$\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}} \sqrt{1 + \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}} - \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}.$$

The proposition may be put as follows. :g:`$ABCDE$ <0466.g1:< A B C D E >>` being a regular pentagon inscribed in a circle, :g:`$AG$ <0466.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />` the diameters through :g:`$A$ <0466.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0466.g1:B>` meeting :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` in :g:`$L$ <0466.g1:L>` and :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` in :g:`$M$ <0466.g1:M>` respectively, :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` is made equal to $\frac{1}{4}$ :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />`. Now, the radius :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />` ($r$) being rational, so are :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`, :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`. The arcs :g:`$CG$ <0466.g1:( C G )>`, :g:`$GD$ <0466.g1:( G D )>` are equal; hence $\angle\text{s}$ at :g:`$L$ <0466.g1:L>` are right, and :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` $= 2$ :g:`$CL$ <0466.g1:/ C L />`. Similarly $\angle\text{s}$ at :g:`$M$ <0466.g1:M>` are right, and :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` $= 2$ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`.

We have to prove

(1) that :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is an apotome,

(2) that :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is a fourth apotome,

(3) that :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` is a minor irrational straight line.

Remembering that, if :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` is divided in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is equal to the side of the pentagon :just:`[XIII. 8] <XIII.8>`, and that accordingly :just:`[XIII. 1] <XIII.1>` $($ :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` $+ \frac{1}{2}$ :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` $)^2 = 5(\frac{1}{2}$ :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` $)^2$, we work towards a proportion containing the ratio $($ :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` $+ $ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $)^2 : $ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $^2$, thus.

The $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L >>`, :g:`$AFM$ <0466.g1:< A F M >>` are equiangular and therefore similar. Therefore :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` $:$ :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` $= $ :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` $:$ :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`, and accordingly $2$ :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` $:$ :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` $= $ :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` $: \frac{1}{2}$ :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`; thus $2$ :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` $: \frac{1}{2}$ :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />` $= $ :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` $: \frac{1}{4}$ :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`, or :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` $:$ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $= $ :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` $:$ :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`; whence, :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`, and squaring, $($ :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` $+ $ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $)^2 : $ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $^2 = $ :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` $^2 : $ :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` $^2$. But $($ :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` $+ $ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $)^2 = 5$ :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` $^2$; therefore :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` $^2 = 5$ :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` $^2$.

(1) To prove that :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is an apotome and :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` its annex. We have :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` $= 4$ :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`; therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $= 5$ :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`, :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $^2 = 25$ :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` $^2$ $= 5$ :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` $^2$, from above; therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $^2$ has not to :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` $^2$ the ratio of a square number to a square number; therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` are incommensurable in length. (2) To prove that :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is a fourth apotome. First, since :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />` are commensurable, :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` are commensurable, i.e. :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is commensurable with :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`, a given rational straight line. Secondly, if :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` $^2 = $ :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $^2 - $ :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` $^2$, since :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $^2 : $ :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` $^2 = 5 : 1$, it follows that :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` $^2 : $ :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` $^2 = 5 : 4$, whence :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` are incommensurable. Therefore :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is a fourth apotome.

(3) To prove that :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` is a minor irrational straight line. If a fourth apotome form a rectangle with a rational straight line, the side of the square equivalent to the rectangle is *minor* :just:`[X. 94] <X.94>`. Now :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` $^2 = $ :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />` $\cdot$ :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />`, :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />` is rational, and :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />` is a fourth apotome; therefore :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` is a minor irrational straight line. [:g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` $= r\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{5}{4} - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{4}} = \frac{r}{2}\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}$. If this is separated into the difference between two straight lines, we have :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />` $= \frac{r}{2}\sqrt{5 + 2\sqrt{5}} - \frac{r}{2}\sqrt{5 - 2\sqrt{5}}$.]
