PROPOSITION 12.
===============

.. enunciation::

   *If an equilateral triangle be inscribed in a circle, the square on the side of the triangle is triple of the square on the radius of the circle.*

.. _folio-iii-0471:

.. folio:: 0471
   :page: 467

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:( A B C )>` be a circle, and let the :eq:`equilateral <0471.g1:/ A B / = 0471.g1:/ B C / = 0471.g1:/ C A />` triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:< A B C >>` be inscribed in it; I say that the square on one side of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:< A B C >>` is triple of the square on the radius of the circle.

.. picture:: 0471.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12 Diagram

For let the centre :g:`$D$ <0471.g1:D>` of the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:( A B C )>` be taken, let :g:`$AD$ <0471.g1:/ A D />` be joined and carried through to :g:`$E$ <0471.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equilateral <0471.g1:/ A B / = 0471.g1:/ B C / = 0471.g1:/ C A />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`$BEC$ <0471.g1:( B E C )>` is a third part of the circumference of the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0471.g1:( A B C )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the circumference :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:( B E )>` is a sixth part of the circumference of the circle; therefore the straight line :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />` belongs to a hexagon; therefore it is :eq:`equal <0471.g1:/ B E / = 0471.g1:/ D E />` to the radius :g:`$DE$ <0471.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[IV. 15, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AE$ <0471.g1:/ A E />` is double of :g:`$DE$ <0471.g1:/ D E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$AE$ <0471.g1:/ A E />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$ED$ <0471.g1:/ E D />`, that is, of the square on :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$AE$ <0471.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0471.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />`; :just:`[III. 31, I. 47] <III.31, I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0471.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />` are quadruple of the square on :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, :term:`*separando* <separando>`, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0471.g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BE$ <0471.g1:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <0471.g1:/ B E / = 0471.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$DE$ <0471.g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0471.g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`$DE$ <0471.g1:/ D E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on the side of the triangle is triple of the square on the radius.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
