Commentary on Proposition XIII.15
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XIII.15 <XIII.15>`.

.. folio:: 0483
   :page: 479

:g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` is divided so that :g:`$AC$ <0482.g1:/ A C />` $= 2$ :g:`$CB$ <0482.g1:/ C B />`; :g:`$CD$ <0482.g1:/ C D />` is drawn at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />`, and :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />` is joined.

:g:`$KG$ <0482.g1:< K G >>` is, by construction, a cube of side equal to :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />`.

To prove (1) that it is inscribable in a sphere.

Since :g:`$KE$ <0482.g1:/ K E />` is perpendicular to :g:`$EH$ <0482.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$EF$ <0482.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$KE$ <0482.g1:/ K E />` is perpendicular to :g:`$EG$ <0482.g1:/ E G />`.

.. _folio-iii-0484:

.. folio:: 0484
   :page: 480

Thus, :g:`$KEG$ <0482.g1:< K E G )>` being a right angle, :g:`$E$ <0482.g1:E>` lies on a semicircle with diameter :g:`$KG$ <0482.g1:/ K G />`. The same thing is proved in the same way of the other vertices :g:`$F$ <0482.g1:F>`, :g:`$H$ <0482.g1:H>`, :g:`$L$ <0482.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0482.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0482.g1:N>`.

.. assertion::

   Thus the cube is inscribed in the sphere of which :g:`$KG$ <0482.g1:/ K G />` is a diameter.

(2) :g:`$KG$ <0482.g1:/ K G />` $^2 = $ :g:`$KE$ <0482.g1:/ K E />` $^2 + $ :g:`$EG$ <0482.g1:/ E G />` $^2$ $= $ :g:`$KE$ <0482.g1:/ K E />` $^2 + 2$ :g:`$EF$ <0482.g1:/ E F />` $^2$ $= 3$ :g:`$EK$ <0482.g1:/ E K />` $^2$. Also :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $= 3$ :g:`$BC$ <0482.g1:/ B C />`, while :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $:$ :g:`$BC$ <0482.g1:/ B C />` $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $^2 : $ :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $\cdot$ :g:`$BC$ <0482.g1:/ B C />` $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $^2 : $ :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />` $^2$;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $^2 = 3$ :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />` $^2$.

But :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />` $= $ :g:`$EK$ <0482.g1:/ E K />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KG$ <0482.g1:/ K G />` $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />`.

(3) :g:`$AB$ <0482.g1:/ A B />` $^2 = 3$ :g:`$BD$ <0482.g1:/ B D />` $^2$ $= 3$ :g:`$KE$ <0482.g1:/ K E />` $^2$.

If $r$ be the radius of the circumscribed sphere,

$$\text{(edge of cube)} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot r = \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3} \cdot r.$$

**Pappus' solution.**

In this case too :person:`Pappus <pappus>` (III. pp. 144—148) gives the full analysis and synthesis.

*Analysis.*

Suppose the problem solved, and let the vertices of the cube be :g:`$A$ <0484.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0484.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0484.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0484.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0484.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0484.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0484.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0484.g1:H>`. Draw planes through :g:`$A$ <0484.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0484.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0484.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0484.g1:D>` and :g:`$E$ <0484.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0484.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0484.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0484.g1:H>` respectively; these will produce parallel circular sections, which are also equal since the inscribed squares are equal. And :g:`$CE$ <0484.g1:/ C E />` will be a diameter of the sphere. Join :g:`$EG$ <0484.g1:/ E G />`. Now, since :g:`$EG$ <0484.g1:/ E G />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$EH$ <0484.g1:/ E H />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$GC$ <0484.g1:/ G C />` $^2$, and the angle :g:`$CGE$ <0484.g1:< C G E )>` is right,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$CE$ <0484.g1:/ C E />` $^2 = $ :g:`$GC$ <0484.g1:/ G C />` $^2 + $ :g:`$EG$ <0484.g1:/ E G />` $^2 = \frac{3}{2}$ :g:`$EG$ <0484.g1:/ E G />` $^2$.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CE$ <0484.g1:/ C E />` $^2$ is given;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0484.g1:/ E G />` $^2$ is given, so that the circles :g:`$EFGH$ <0484.g1:( E F G H )>`, :g:`$ABCD$ <0484.g1:( A B C D )>`, and the squares inscribed in them, are given.

.. picture:: 0484.g1.svg
   :alt: Pappus' solution for the cube

*Synthesis.*

Draw two parallel circular sections with equal diameters $d'$, such that

$$d^2 = \frac{3}{2}d'^2,$$

where $d$ is the diameter of the given sphere. Inscribe a square in one of the circles, as :g:`$ABCD$ <0484.g1:< A B C D >>`. In the other circle draw :g:`$FG$ <0484.g1:/ F G />` equal and parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0484.g1:/ B C />`, and complete the square on :g:`$FG$ <0484.g1:/ F G />` inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0484.g1:( E F G H )>`.

.. assertion::

   The eight vertices of the required cube are thus determined.
