Johan Ludvig Heiberg
====================

*Danish Classical Philologist & Critical Editor of Greek Mathematical Texts*

Professor of Classical Philology at the University of Copenhagen. Heiberg established the modern critical Greek text of the ancient mathematicians, discovering the famous Archimedes Palimpsest in Constantinople in 1906. Between 1883 and 1888, he edited the definitive five-volume Greek critical edition of Euclid's *Elements* (*Euclidis Elementa*, Teubner), identifying Codex Vaticanus Graecus 190 (P) as the superior pre-Theonine archetype and purging the accretions introduced by Theon of Alexandria in the fourth century CE. He co-directed the monumental *Euclidis Opera Omnia* series with Heinrich Menge.

* **Primary Contribution**: Critical Greek text of the *Elements* (Vols. I–V of *Euclidis Opera Omnia*)
