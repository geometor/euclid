Robert Simson
=============

*Scottish Mathematician & Professor of Mathematics at the University of Glasgow*

Pioneering restorationist of ancient Greek geometry who dedicated nearly five decades to recovering the original purity of Euclid and Apollonius. His landmark edition, *The Elements of Euclid... Also the Book of Euclid's Data* (Glasgow: Robert & Andrew Foulis, 1756 / 1762), rescued the *Data* from corrupted medieval Latin versions, providing brilliant critical corrections, restored propositions, and annotations that guided British geometry education for over a century and directly informed Heath's and Menge's recensions.

* **Primary Contribution**: Critical reconstruction of *Data* and Latin/English commentaries on *Elements*
