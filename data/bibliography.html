Bibliography & Critical Editions¶

Manuscript archetypes, critical Greek and English editions, ancient commentaries, and modern geometric treatises cited across the Data Book Codex.

Euclidis Data cum commentario Marini et scholiis antiquis (Euclidis Opera Omnia, Vol. VI, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1896) (menge:1896)

  • Author / Editor: Heinrich Menge (ed.)

  • Locus: Vol. VI, pp. 1–256

Commentarius in Euclidis Data (Protheoria, c. 485 CE; ed. Menge 1896, pp. 234–256) (marinus:protheoria)

  • Author / Editor: Marinus of Neapolis

  • Locus: Menge pp. 234–256

Euclid’s Data: In This Edition Several Errors Are Corrected, and Some Propositions Added (2nd ed., Glasgow, 1762; rev. London, 1806 / 1838) (simson:1762)

  • Author / Editor: Robert Simson

  • Locus: pp. 357–468

The Thirteen Books of Euclid’s Elements, 2nd ed. (Cambridge University Press, 1926; Dover, 1956) (heath:1926)

  • Author / Editor: Sir Thomas Little Heath

  • Locus: Vols. I–III

A History of Greek Mathematics, 2 vols. (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1921) (heath:1921)

  • Author / Editor: Sir Thomas Little Heath

  • Locus: Vol. I, pp. 421–425

Euclidis Elementa (Euclidis Opera Omnia, Vols. I–V, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1883–1888) (heiberg:1883)

  • Author / Editor: Johan Ludvig Heiberg (ed.)

  • Locus: Vols. I–V

Synagoge (Mathematical Collection), Book VII: On the Domain of Analysis (ed. F. Hultsch, Berlin, 1877; ed. A. Jones, 1986) (pappus:synagoge)

  • Author / Editor: Pappus of Alexandria

  • Locus: Book VII, pp. 634–640 Hultsch

In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii (ed. G. Friedlein, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1873) (proclus:1873)

  • Author / Editor: Proclus Diadochus

  • Locus: pp. 1–436 Friedlein

Εὐκλείδου Δεδομένα · Euclidis Data, opus ad veterum geometriae autorum Archimedis, Apollonii, Pappi, etc. (Paris: Melchior Mondiere, 1625) (hardy:1625)

  • Author / Editor: Claude Hardy (ed.)

  • Locus: Editio princeps

Εὐκλείδου τὰ σωζόμενα · Euclidis quae supersunt omnia (Oxford: Sheldonian Theatre, 1703) (gregory:1703)

  • Author / Editor: David Gregory (ed.)

  • Locus: pp. 449–530

Euclidis Megarensis philosophi Platonici Mathematicarum disciplinarum Janitoris … Data (Venice: Joannes Tacuinus, 1505) (zamberti:1505)

  • Author / Editor: Bartholomaeus Zamberti

  • Locus: Data translation

Codex Vaticanus Graecus 190 (P, 9th century CE: non-Theonine archetype of Euclid’s Elements and Data) (vat-gr-190)

  • Author / Editor: Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana

  • Locus: Codex P (9th c.)

On Vergings (Περὶ νεύσεων) and General Treatise (Καθόλου πραγματεία, lost; testified in Marinus & Pappus) (apollonius:neuseis)

  • Author / Editor: Apollonius of Perga

  • Locus: ap. Marinus p. 234 & Pappus VII

Mathematike Syntaxis (Almagest, c. 150 CE; Chord Table & Menelaus/Ptolemy theorems) (ptolemy:syntaxis)

  • Author / Editor: Claudius Ptolemy

  • Locus: Book I, Ch. 10

On Spirals (Περὶ ἑλίκων, c. 225 BCE) (archimedes:spirals)

  • Author / Editor: Archimedes of Syracuse

  • Locus: ap. Marinus pp. 240–248

Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica, 2nd ed. (Cambridge, 1713) (newton:1713)

  • Author / Editor: Sir Isaac Newton

  • Locus: Book I, Lemma XVII

Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (Copenhagen / London, 1879) (petersen:1879)

  • Author / Editor: Julius Petersen

  • Locus: Chs. I–III

College Geometry: An Introduction to the Modern Geometry of the Triangle and the Circle, 2nd ed. (Barnes & Noble, 1952; Dover, 2007) (altshiller-court:1952)

  • Author / Editor: Nathan Altshiller-Court

  • Locus: Ch. I, §§ 1–55; Ch. III; Ch. VII

ΔΕΔΟΜΕΝΑ: Euclid’s Data, or The Importance of Being Given (Copenhagen: Museum Tusculanum Press, 2003) (taisbak:2003)

  • Author / Editor: Christian Marinus Taisbak

  • Locus: Historical reference
