Euclid’s Data (Δεδομένα)¶

A Synchronized Four-Track Bilingual Book Codex of the Foundation of the Ancient Treasury of Analysis (:term:`ὁ ἀναλυόμενος τόπος <analyomenos-topos>`*)*

Composed by Euclid of Alexandria alongside the thirteen books of the Elements, the Data (Δεδομένα, Dedomena) is the algorithmic and epistemological grammar of Greek geometric problem-solving. Where the Elements proceeds by synthesis to construct figures and demonstrate static properties, the Data establishes functional determinacy: given certain magnitudes, ratios, angles, or loci in hypothesis, what further geometric entities are thereby uniquely procurable (πόριμον)?

This GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium edition presents the complete non-Theonine Greek text established by Heinrich Menge from the ninth-century Vatican archetype Vat. gr. 190 (Codex P) (Euclidis Opera Omnia, Vol. VI, 1896), prefaced by the fifth-century philosophical commentary (Protheoria) of Marinus of Neapolis, and collated throughout with Robert Simson's landmark critical reconstruction (1762 / 1806) and Sir Thomas Heath's mathematical analysis (1921, 1926).

Each thematic unit is organized into four synchronized tracks selectable from the Book Codex header bar:

  1. Proposition (English) — Complete literal translation of Marinus’s Protheoria (§§ 1–18), Definitions 1–15, and Propositions 1–94 (D.1–D.94), wired with interactive .euclid-graphic SVGs, :g: geometric highlights, and live :just: justification pills linking directly into the Heath Elements.

  2. Greek (Menge 1896) — Complete polytonic Greek text of Marinus and Euclid with marginal .. folio:: milestones keyed to Menge’s 1896 Teubner pagination.

  3. Summary — Functional Given(...) -> Determined(...) signatures, constructive dependency tables, and computational primitives for geometor.model.

  4. Notes — Textual criticism (Vat. gr. 190 vs. Theonine recension vs. Simson), diorismoi, and cross-corpus links to Pappus (Synagoge VII), Julius Petersen (Methods and Theories, 1879), and Nathan Altshiller-Court (College Geometry, 1952).

Functional Architecture of the Data¶

Interactive architecture map: Unit 0 (Protheoria & Defs. 1–15) grounds the modes of givenness alongside Euclid's Elements I–VI. From these rise Unit 1 (Magnitudes & Ratios, D.1–24) and Unit 2 (Position & Parallel Loci, D.25–38), converging in Unit 3 (Triangle & Polygon Species, D.39–55), branching through Unit 4 (Area Application & Cosine Metric, D.56–67) and Unit 5 (Proportional Figures, D.68–83), and culminating in Unit 6 (Quadratic Systems & Circle Loci, D.84–94)—powering Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge VII).

Thematic Units¶

Unit 0: Marinus’s Protheoria & Definitions 1–15

Spaces, lines, and angles are called given in magnitude to which we can procure equals.

Unit 1: Magnitudes & Ratios (Propositions 1–24)

The ratio of given magnitudes to one another is given.

Unit 2: Position, Angles & Parallel Loci (Propositions 25–38)

If two lines given in position cut one another, the point at which they cut one another is given in position.

Unit 3: Triangles & Polygons Given in Species (Propositions 39–55)

If each of the sides of a triangle be given in magnitude, the triangle is given in species.

Unit 4: Application of Areas, Quadratics & Cosine Projections (Propositions 56–67)

If two equiangular parallelograms have a given ratio to one another, as a side of the first is to a side of the second, so is the remaining side of the second to that straight line to which the remaining side of the first has the given ratio that the parallelogram has to the parallelogram.

Unit 5: Proportional Parallelograms, Triangles & Four-Line Relations (Propositions 68–83)

If two equiangular parallelograms have a given ratio to one another, and one side have to one side a given ratio, the remaining side will also have to the remaining side a given ratio.

Unit 6: Hyperbolic/Elliptic Systems, Circles, Chords & Angle Bisectors (Propositions 84–94)

If two straight lines contain a given area in a given angle, and one exceed the other by a given straight line, each of them will also be given.
