Proposition 25 [κε΄ · Prop. XXVIII]¶

If two lines given in position cut one another, the point at which they cut one another is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(L1), Pos(L2), Meet(L1, L2) = E) -> Determined(Pos(E)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 4], [Post. 1], [I Def. 4]

Let two lines $AB$ and $CD$, given in position, intersect one another at the point $E$. I say that the point $E$ is given in position.

For if $E$ is not given in position, it will shift its place (μεταπεσεῖται, [Data Def. 4]), while $AB$ and $CD$ remain fixed in position. Consequently at least one of the lines $AB$ or $CD$ passing through $E$ would also shift its position; yet both were assumed given in position—which is absurd. Therefore the intersection point $E$ does not shift, and so is given in position ([Data Def. 4]).
