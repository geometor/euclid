Proposition 47 [μζ΄ · Prop. Prop. LI] — Philological Notes¶

Cross-Corpus Concordance¶

  • D.47 proves that every species-given $n$-gon triangulates into $(n-2)$ species-given triangles by ear-clipping diagonals (D.41).

Textual & Critical Apparatus¶

  • Critical Edition: Heinrich Menge (Euclidis Opera Omnia, Vol. VI, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1896, pp. 84ff).

  • Critical Reconstruction: Robert Simson (Glasgow 1762 / London 1806, Prop. LI).

  • Elements Causal Flow: Synthesizes 2 foundational theorems from the Elements with internal Data lemmas.
