Proposition 60 [ξ΄ · Prop. LXXXII]¶

If a parallelogram given in species and in magnitude be increased or diminished by a gnomon given in magnitude, the breadths of the gnomon are given.

Functional Signature: Given(Species(P), Mag(Area(P)), Mag(Gnomon G)) -> Determined(Mag(Sides of P +/- G)) · Dependencies: [Data 3], [Data 4], [Data 55], [VI.24]

First, let a parallelogram $ABCD$, given both in species and in magnitude, be increased by a given gnomon $ECBDFG$ so as to form a larger similar parallelogram $AEFG$. I say that the breadths $CE$ and $DF$ of the gnomon are given in magnitude.

Since $ABCD$ is given in magnitude and the gnomon $ECBDFG$ is given in magnitude, the whole parallelogram $AEFG = ABCD + ECBDFG$ is given in magnitude ([Data 3]). Moreover, because a gnomon adjoined around the diagonal preserves similarity ([VI.24]), $AEFG$ is similar to $ABCD$ and hence given in species ([Data Def. 3]). Therefore the side $AE$ of $AEFG$ is given in magnitude ([Data 55]), while the side $AC$ of $ABCD$ is likewise given in magnitude ([Data 55]). Subtracting $AC$ from $AE$ yields the gnomon breadth $CE$ given in magnitude ([Data 4]), and similarly $DF$ is given.

Second, let the species-and-magnitude-given parallelogram $AEFG$ be diminished by a given gnomon $ECBDFG$ leaving $ABCD$: then the remainder $ABCD = AEFG - ECBDFG$ is given in magnitude ([Data 4]) and in species ($\sim AEFG$), so both $AE$ and $AC$ are given in magnitude ([Data 55]), whence their difference $CE$ (and $DF$) is given in magnitude ([Data 4]).
