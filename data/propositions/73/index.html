Proposition 73 [ογ΄ · Prop. LXIV (pt. 2)]¶

If of two parallelograms about equal angles, or about unequal yet given angles, the sides stand so that as a side of the first is to a side of the second, so is the remaining side of the second to some other straight line, and the remaining side of the first have a given ratio to that straight line, the parallelograms themselves will also have a given ratio to one another.

Functional Signature: Given(Angle(P1), Angle(P2), CB:FG = EF:CK, Ratio(AC:CK)) -> Determined(Ratio(P1 : P2)) · Dependencies: [Data 8], [Data 40], [Data 56], [Data 70], [I.23], [I.35], [VI.1], [VI.14]

Let two parallelograms $ABCD$ and $EFGH$ be either equiangular ($\angle B = \angle F$) or in unequal given angles ($\angle B, \angle F$), and as $BC$ is to $FG$, so let $FE$ be to a straight line $K$ to which $BA$ has a given ratio $BA : K$. I say that the area ratio $ABCD : EFGH$ is given.

First, let $ABCD$ and $EFGH$ be equiangular. Along $BA$ (produced if necessary) cut off $BL = K$ ([I.3]), so $BA : BL$ is given, and complete the equiangular parallelogram $BLMC$ on $BL$ and $BC$ ([I.31]). Because $BC : FG = FE : BL$ around equal angles $\angle B = \angle F$, the parallelograms $BLMC$ and $EFGH$ have reciprocally proportional sides and are therefore equal: $BLMC = EFGH$ ([VI.14]). Moreover, on the common base $BC$, $ABCD : BLMC = BA : BL$ ([VI.1]), which is given! Substituting $BLMC = EFGH$ proves that $ABCD : EFGH$ is given.

Second, let $\angle B$ and $\angle F$ be unequal given angles. Shear $ABCD$ on base $BC$ between the same parallels into parallelogram $PBCQ$ having $\angle PBQ = \angle F$ ([I.23], [I.35]). As in [Data 69], $\\triangle ABP$ is given in species ([Data 40]), so $BP : BA$ is given; combining with given $BA : K$ gives $BP : K$ given ([Data 8]). Applying the first case to equiangular $PBCQ$ and $EFGH$ gives $PBCQ : EFGH$ given; since $ABCD = PBCQ$ ([I.35]), $ABCD : EFGH$ is given.
