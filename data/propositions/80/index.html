Proposition 80 [π΄ · Prop. LXXVIII]¶

If a triangle have one angle given, and the rectangle contained by the sides about the given angle have a given ratio to the square on the remaining side, the triangle is given in species.

Functional Signature: Given(Angle A, Ratio((b*c) : a^2)) -> Determined(Species(△ABC)) · Dependencies: [Data 8], [Data 66], [Data 68], [Data 79], [I.12], [I.41], [VI.4], [VI.16]

Let \(\\triangle ABC\) have a given angle \(\angle BAC\), and let the rectangle \(BA \cdot AC\) contained by the sides about the given angle have a given ratio to the square \(BC^2\) on the remaining side. I say that \(\\triangle ABC\) is given in species.

Drop the altitude \(AD \perp BC\) ([I.12]). Because \(\\triangle ABC\) has given angle \(\angle BAC\), the ratio \((BA \cdot AC) : \\triangle ABC\) is given ([Data 66]). Combining this with the given ratio \((BA \cdot AC) : BC^2\) yields \(BC^2 : \\triangle ABC\) given ([Data 8]), and since \(BC \cdot AD = 2\,\\triangle ABC\) ([I.41]), the ratio \(BC^2 : (BC \cdot AD) = BC : AD\) is given ([VI.1]). Now set out a straight line \(FG\) given in position and magnitude, and on \(FG\) describe the circular segment \(FHG\) admitting an angle equal to given \(\angle BAC\) ([III.33]), whose arc is given in position. Take \(L\) so that \(FG : L = BC : AD\) ([Data 2]), erect \(FK = L\) perpendicular to \(FG\), and draw \(KH \parallel FG\) meeting the arc \(FHG\) at \(H\) ([Data 28], [Data 25]). Joining \(FH\) and \(HG\) gives \(\\triangle FHG\) in species ([Data 39]) with \(\angle FHG = \angle BAC\) and base-to-altitude ratio \(FG : L = BC : AD\). By [Data 79], \(\\triangle ABC\) is equiangular with \(\\triangle FHG\), and therefore \(\\triangle ABC\) is given in species ([Data 40]).
