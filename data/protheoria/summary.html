Marinus’s Protheoria — Analytical Summary¶

Marinus’s Fourfold Epistemic Classification (Menge pp. 234–252)¶

Marinus of Neapolis organizes ancient definitions of the given (δεδομένον) across four fundamental binary predicates and three relational standpoints:

Predicate Pair

Standpoint

Ancient Authority

Diagnostic Geometric Criterion & Counterexample

τεταγμένον / ἄτακτον (Ordered / Unordered)

Nature itself (πρὸς τὴν φύσιν)

Apollonius of Perga (On Vergings)

Unique invariance. Line through 2 points or circle through 3 is ordered; circle through 2 points or general scalene triangle is unordered. Quadrature of the circle is ordered yet unprocurable.

γνώριμον / ἄγνωστον (Known / Unknown)

Relative to us (πρὸς ἡμᾶς)

Diodorus of Alexandria

Cognitive grasp (Genus of given). Uniqueness of tangent to Archimedes' spiral is known by reductio before its construction is procurable.

πόριμον / ἄπορον (Procurable / Unprocurable)

Constructive power (ἐν πόρῳ)

Euclid (Data Defs. 1–2)

Constructive exhibibility from elementary postulates (Specific Differentia of given). Converts coextensively with comprehensible (καταληπτόν).

ῥητόν / ἄλογον (Rational / Irrational)

Unit measure (πρὸς τὸ μέτρον)

Claudius Ptolemy (Syntaxis)

Numerical commensurability. Strictly narrower than given: $\sqrt{2}$ and Book X binomials ([X.36]) are irrational yet completely given.

Marinus’s Canon (§ 15): $\text{Δεδομένον} \iff \text{Γνώριμον (Genus)} \land \text{Πόριμον (Differentia)}$.
