Unit 2: Position, Angles & Parallel Loci (Propositions 25–38)¶

Straight Lines Given in Position, Angular Rays, and Parallel Locus Determination — D.25–D.38 (Simson XXVIII–XL) · Menge (1896) pp. 42–66

Note

Edition Track (English): Transition from abstract magnitudes to planar geometry: uniqueness of line-line intersections (D.25), endpoints and directions of segments (D.26–D.30), neusis/circle distance meets (D.31), inclined transversals across parallels (D.32–D.33), and parallel ratio-division loci through a fixed pole (D.34–D.38). Switch tabs above for Greek (Menge 1896), Summary, or Notes.

Interactive locus diagram (Menge pp. 46–66): From given external pole P, secants PF (cutting AB at E and CD at F) and PH (cutting at G and H) are divided in a constant given ratio (D.34), while internal division points M, N, and S trace the parallel locus MN (D.35–D.38). Ray KQ from given point K at given angle ∠BKQ (D.29) meets CD at unique point Q (D.25).

Proposition 25 [κε΄ · Prop. XXVIII]¶

[Folio Menge 46 | p. 46]
If two lines given in position cut one another, the point at which they cut one another is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(L1), Pos(L2), Meet(L1, L2) = E) -> Determined(Pos(E)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 4], [Post. 1], [I Def. 4]

Let two lines $AB$ and $CD$, given in position, intersect one another at the point $E$. I say that the point $E$ is given in position.

For if $E$ is not given in position, it will shift its place (μεταπεσεῖται, [Data Def. 4]), while $AB$ and $CD$ remain fixed in position. Consequently at least one of the lines $AB$ or $CD$ passing through $E$ would also shift its position; yet both were assumed given in position—which is absurd. Therefore the intersection point $E$ does not shift, and so is given in position ([Data Def. 4]).

Proposition 26 [κϛ΄ · Prop. XXIX]¶

If the extremities of a straight line be given in position, the straight line is given in position and in magnitude.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(A), Pos(B)) -> Determined(Pos(AB), Mag(AB)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 1], [Data Def. 4], [Post. 1], [Post. 3]

Let the extremities $A$ and $B$ of a straight line $AB$ be given in position. I say that the straight line $AB$ is given in position and in magnitude.

For if the straight line $AB$ did not retain the same place while $A$ and $B$ remain fixed, or if its length varied, two distinct straight lines could be drawn between the same two fixed points $A$ and $B$, enclosing a space—contrary to [Post. 1]. Therefore $AB$ always occupies the same place and has an invariant equal constructible on it ([I.2]), so $AB$ is given both in position ([Data Def. 4]) and in magnitude ([Data Def. 1]).

Proposition 27 [κζ΄ · Prop. XXX]¶

[Folio Menge 48 | p. 48]
If one extremity of a straight line given in position and in magnitude be given, the other extremity will also be given.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(Ray A->), Mag(AB), Pos(A)) -> Determined(Pos(B)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 1], [Data Def. 4], [Data Def. 6], [Post. 3], [I Def. 15]

Let one extremity $A$ of a straight line $AB$, given in position and in magnitude, be given. I say that the other extremity $B$ is also given.

For if $B$ were not given in position while $A$ remains fixed and the line $AB$ remains fixed in position, then either the straight line $AB$ would shift its position or its length $AB$ would change (falling at some distinct point $C$ on the same ray with $AC \neq AB$). Yet $AB$ is given both in position and in magnitude—or constructively, describing the circle with given centre $A$ and given radius $AB$ ([Post. 3], [Data Def. 6]) cuts the position-given ray at $B$ ([Data 25]). Therefore $B$ is given in position.

Proposition 28 [κη΄ · Prop. XXXI]¶

If through a given point a straight line be drawn parallel to a straight line given in position, the straight line drawn is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(A), Pos(Line BC)) -> Determined(Pos(Parallel(A, BC))) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 4], [I.27], [I.29], [I.30], [I.31]

Through a given point $A$, let the straight line $DAE$ be drawn parallel to a straight line $BC$ given in position ([I.31]). I say that $DAE$ is given in position.

For if $DAE$ were not given in position while passing through the fixed point $A$, it could shift into another straight line $FAE$ through $A$ still parallel to $BC$. Then $BC$ would be parallel simultaneously to two intersecting straight lines $DAE$ and $FAE$—which is impossible by [I.29] and [I.30] (Playfair’s / Euclid’s Fifth Postulate [Post. 5]). Therefore $DAE$ always occupies the same place and is given in position ([Data Def. 4]).

Proposition 29 [κθ΄ · Prop. XXXII]¶

[Folio Menge 50 | p. 50]
If to a straight line given in position, and at a given point upon it, a straight line be drawn making a given angle, the straight line drawn is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(Line AB), Pos(C in AB), Mag(Angle theta)) -> Determined(Pos(Ray CD)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 1], [Data Def. 4], [I.23]

To a straight line $AB$ given in position, and at a given point $C$ on it, let a straight line $CD$ be drawn making a given angle $\angle BCD$ ([I.23]). I say that $CD$ is given in position.

For if $CD$ did not retain the same place (on the assigned side of $AB$) while $AB$ and $C$ remain fixed, it would shift to another ray $CE$ through $C$ making $\angle BCE = \angle BCD$, the lesser equal to the greater—which is impossible ([C.N. 5]). Therefore $CD$ is given in position ([Data Def. 4]).

Proposition 30 [λ΄ · Prop. XXXIII]¶

If from a given point a straight line be drawn onto a straight line given in position making a given angle, the straight line drawn is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(A), Pos(Line BC), Mag(Angle ADC)) -> Determined(Pos(Line AD)) · Dependencies: [Data 28], [Data 29], [I.23], [I.29], [I.31]

From a given point $A$ outside a straight line $BC$ given in position, let a straight line $AD$ be drawn making a given angle $\angle ADC$ with $BC$. I say that $AD$ is given in position.

Through the given point $A$ draw $EAF$ parallel to $BC$ ([I.31]); then $EAF$ is given in position ([Data 28]). Because $EAF \parallel BC$, the alternate angle $\angle EAD$ is equal to $\angle ADC$ ([I.29]). Yet $\angle ADC$ is given; therefore $\angle EAD$ is given. Thus to the position-given line $EAF$ and at the given point $A$ on it, the straight line $AD$ has been drawn making the given angle $\angle EAD$; therefore $AD$ is given in position ([Data 29]).

Proposition 31 [λα΄ · Prop. XXXIV]¶

If from a given point onto a straight line given in position a straight line given in magnitude be drawn, it is also given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(A), Pos(Line BC), Mag(AD >= dist(A, BC))) -> Determined(Pos(AD)) [2-root diorismos] · Dependencies: [Data Def. 4], [Data Def. 6], [Data 25], [Data 26], [I.12], [Post. 3]

From a given point $A$, let a straight line $AB$ of given magnitude be drawn to meet a straight line $CD$ given in position at $B$. I say that $AB$ is given in position (up to the two symmetric intersections on $CD$).

With given centre $A$ and given distance $AB$, describe the circle $EBF$ ([Post. 3]). Since the centre $A$ is given in position and the radius $AB$ is given in magnitude, the circle $EBF$ is given in position ([Data Def. 6]). Moreover, the straight line $CD$ is given in position; therefore their intersection $B$ is given in position ([Data 25]). Since $A$ is also given in position, the straight line $AB$ joining $A$ and $B$ is given in position ([Data 26]).

Proposition 32 [λβ΄ · Prop. XXXV]¶

[Folio Menge 54 | p. 54]
If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn making given angles, the straight line cut off between the parallels is given in magnitude.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(AB || CD), Mag(Angle BEF)) -> Determined(Mag(EF)) · Dependencies: [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 30], [I.29], [I.31], [I.34]

Between two parallel straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ given in position, let a straight line $EF$ be drawn making given angles $\angle BEF$ and $\angle EFD$. I say that $EF$ is given in magnitude.

Take any given point $G$ on $AB$, and through $G$ draw $GH$ parallel to $EF$ meeting $CD$ at $H$ ([I.31]). Because $GH \parallel EF$, the interior angle $\angle AGH$ (or $\angle BGH$) equals the corresponding angle $\angle BEF$ ([I.29]), which is given; hence $GH$ is drawn from the given point $G$ on position-given $AB$ at a given angle, so $GH$ is given in position ([Data 29]). Since $CD$ is also given in position, their intersection $H$ is given ([Data 25]). Because both $G$ and $H$ are given, $GH$ is given in magnitude ([Data 26]). Finally, $GEFH$ is a parallelogram, so $EF = GH$ ([I.34]); therefore $EF$ is given in magnitude ([Data Def. 1]).

Proposition 33 [λγ΄ · Prop. XXXVI]¶

If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line given in magnitude be drawn, it will make given angles with the parallels.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(AB || CD), Mag(EF >= strip_width)) -> Determined(Mag(Angle BEF)) · Dependencies: [Data Def. 1], [Data Def. 4], [Data 28], [Data 31], [I.29], [I.34]

Between two parallel straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ given in position, let a straight line $EF$ of given magnitude be drawn. I say that $EF$ makes given angles $\angle BEF$ and $\angle EFD$ with the parallels.

Take a given point $G$ on $AB$, and through $G$ draw $GH$ parallel to $EF$ meeting $CD$ at $H$ ([I.31]). Then $GEFH$ is a parallelogram, so $GH = EF$ ([I.34]); since $EF$ is given in magnitude, $GH$ is given in magnitude. With given centre $G$ and given distance $GH$, describe the circle $KHL$ ([Post. 3]), which is given in position ([Data Def. 6]). Since $CD$ is given in position, the intersection $H$ is given in position ([Data 25]), whence the straight line $GH$ is given in position ([Data 26]). Because both $AB$ and $GH$ are given in position, the angle $\angle BGH$ is given; and by parallels $\angle BEF = \angle BGH$ and $\angle EFD = 2\text{ rt.} - \angle BEF$ ([I.29]), so $\angle BEF$ and $\angle EFD$ are given ([Data 4]).

Proposition 34 [λδ΄ · Prop. XXXVIII]¶

If to parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn from a given point, it will be cut in a given ratio.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(AB || CD), Pos(Pole E)) -> Determined(Ratio(EF : FG)) · Dependencies: [Data 1], [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 30], [I.12], [VI.2], [VI.4]

From a given point $E$, let a straight line $EFG$ be drawn across two parallel straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ given in position, meeting them at $F$ and $G$. I say that $EFG$ is cut in a given ratio $EF : FG$.

From the given point $E$, draw $EHK$ perpendicular to $AB$ (and hence also to $CD$), meeting $AB$ at $H$ and $CD$ at $K$ ([I.12]). Since $EHK$ makes a given (right) angle with position-given $AB$ from given $E$, $EHK$ is given in position ([Data 30]). Because $AB$ and $CD$ are given in position, the intersections $H$ and $K$ are given in position ([Data 25]). Since $E, H, K$ are all given in position, the segments $EH$ and $HK$ are given in magnitude ([Data 26]), so the ratio $EH : HK$ is given ([Data 1]). Yet in $\triangle EKG$, since $FH \parallel GK$, we have $EF : FG = EH : HK$ ([VI.2]). Therefore the ratio $EF : FG$ is given ([Data Def. 2]).

Proposition 35 [λε΄ · Prop. XXXIX (pt. 1)]¶

If from a given point onto a straight line given in position a straight line be drawn and cut in a given ratio, and through the point of section a parallel be drawn to the straight line given in position, that parallel is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(Pole A), Pos(Line BC), InternalRatio(AE : ED)) -> Determined(Pos(Line FEG || BC)) · Dependencies: [Data 7], [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 27], [Data 28], [Data 30], [VI.2]

From a given point $A$ outside a straight line $BC$ given in position, let a straight line $AD$ be drawn to $BC$ and produced to $E$ so that the ratio $DE : AD$ is given. I say that $E$ lies on a straight line given in position.

From $A$ draw $AF$ perpendicular to $BC$ meeting $BC$ at $F$ ([I.12]), and produce $AF$ to $G$ so that $FG : AF = DE : AD$ ([VI.12]). Because $AF$ is drawn at a right angle from given $A$ to position-given $BC$, $AF$ is given in position ([Data 30]), $F$ is given ([Data 25]), and $AF$ is given in magnitude ([Data 26]). Since $FG : AF = DE : AD$ is given, $FG$ is given in magnitude ([Data 2]), so the extremity $G$ on the position-given ray $AFG$ is given in position ([Data 27]). Through $G$ draw $HGK$ parallel to $BC$ ([I.31]), which is given in position ([Data 28]). Because $AD : DE = AF : FG$, the line through $G$ and $E$ is parallel to $FD$ ([VI.2]) and therefore coincides with $HGK$; hence $E$ lies on a straight line given in position.

Proposition 36 [λϛ΄ · Prop. XXXIX (pt. 2)]¶

If from a given point onto a straight line given in position a straight line be drawn, and there be added to it a straight line having a given ratio to it, and through the extremity a parallel be drawn to the straight line given in position, that parallel is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(Pole A), Pos(Line BC), ExternalRatio(AD : DE)) -> Determined(Pos(Line FEG || BC)) · Dependencies: [Data 2], [Data 6], [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 27], [Data 28], [Data 30], [VI.2]

From a given point $A$ to a straight line $BC$ given in position, let $AD$ be drawn, and on $AD$ (or its opposite ray through $D$) let $DE$ be cut off toward $A$ having a given ratio $DE : AD$. I say that $E$ lies on a straight line given in position.

Draw $AF$ perpendicular to $BC$ ([I.12]), so $AF$ is given in position ([Data 30]), $F$ is given ([Data 25]), and $AF$ is given in magnitude ([Data 26]). Cut off $FG$ toward $A$ (or beyond $A$ if $DE > AD$) such that $FG : FA = DE : DA$ ([Data Def. 2]). Since $FA$ is given and $FG : FA$ is given, $FG$ is given in magnitude ([Data 2]), whence $G$ is given in position ([Data 27]). Because $FD \parallel GE$ follows from $DA : DE = FA : FG$ (and separando $AE : ED = AG : GF$, [V.17], [VI.2]), the straight line $GE$ passes through the given point $G$ parallel to position-given $BC$, and so is given in position ([Data 28]).

Proposition 37 [λζ΄ · Prop. XL (pt. 1)]¶

[Folio Menge 62 | p. 62]
If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn and cut in a given ratio, and through the point of section a parallel be drawn to the parallels given in position, that parallel is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(AB || CD), InternalRatio(EG : GF)) -> Determined(Pos(Line HK || AB)) · Dependencies: [Data 7], [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 27], [Data 28], [Data 30], [I.30], [I.34], [VI.2]

Between two parallel straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ given in position, let any straight line $EF$ be drawn, and let $EF$ be produced to $G$ in a given ratio $EF : FG$. I say that $G$ lies on a straight line given in position parallel to $AB$ and $CD$.

Take a given point $H$ on $AB$, draw $HK$ perpendicular to $CD$ meeting $CD$ at $K$ ([I.12]), and produce $HK$ to $L$ so that $HK : KL = EF : FG$. By [Data 30], [Data 25], and [Data 26], $HK$ is given in position and magnitude; since $HK : KL$ is given, $KL$ is given in magnitude ([Data 2]), so $L$ is given in position ([Data 27]). Through $L$ draw $MLN$ parallel to $CD$ ([I.31]), which is given in position ([Data 28]). Produce $EF$ to meet $MLN$ at $G'$: by parallel transversals ([VI.2] via the auxiliary parallel from $H$ to $EF$), $EF : FG' = HK : KL = EF : FG$, whence $FG' = FG$ ([V.9]) and $G$ coincides with $G'$ on the position-given line $MLN$.

Proposition 38 [λη΄ · Prop. XL (pt. 2)]¶

If onto parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn, and there be added to it a straight line having a given ratio to it, and through the extremity a parallel be drawn to the parallels given in position, that parallel is given in position.

Functional Signature: Given(Pos(AB || CD), ExternalRatio(EF : FG)) -> Determined(Pos(Line HK || AB)) · Dependencies: [Data 2], [Data 25], [Data 26], [Data 27], [Data 28], [Data 30], [I.30], [I.34], [VI.2]

Between two parallel straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ given in position, let a straight line $EF$ be drawn and divided internally at $G$ in a given ratio $EG : GF$. I say that $G$ lies on a straight line given in position.

Take a given point $N$ on $AB$ and draw $NM$ perpendicular to $CD$ meeting $CD$ at $M$ ([I.12]), so $NM$ is given in position and magnitude ([Data 30], [Data 25], [Data 26]). Divide $NM$ at $L$ so that $NL : LM = EG : GF$ ([VI.10]); since $NM$ is given and $NL : LM$ is given, $NL$ is given in magnitude ([Data 7]), so $L$ is given in position ([Data 27]). Draw $KLH$ through $L$ parallel to $CD$, which is given in position ([Data 28]). Since $AB \parallel KLH \parallel CD$ cuts $NM$ and $EF$ proportionally ([VI.2]), $KLH$ divides $EF$ in the ratio $NL : LM = EG : GF$, and therefore passes through $G$.
