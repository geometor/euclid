Unit 2: Position, Angles & Parallel Loci (Propositions 25–38) — Analytical Summary¶

Unit Overview & Role in Greek Geometric Analysis¶

Transition from abstract magnitudes to planar geometry: uniqueness of line-line intersections (D.25), endpoints and directions of segments (D.26–D.30), neusis/circle distance meets (D.31), inclined transversals across parallels (D.32–D.33), and parallel ratio-division loci through a fixed pole (D.34–D.38).

  • Scope: 14 propositions (D.25–D.38 (Simson XXVIII–XL), Menge pp. 42–66).

  • Analytical Function: Each proposition below compiles into a forward-chaining inference rule Given(...) -> Determined(...) within Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge Book VII) and geometor.model symbolic DAGs.

Computational Primitives (geometor.model & SymPy)¶

  • D.25 (Prop. XXVIII): Fundamental model.intersect(L1, L2) primitive in geometor.model.

  • D.26 (Prop. XXIX): Fundamental model.set_line(A, B) and Euclidean metric norm |B - A|.

  • D.27 (Prop. XXX): Line-circle intersection model.intersect(line, Circle(A, r)) fixing endpoint B on a given ray.

  • D.28 (Prop. XXXI): Euclidean parallel constructor through P along direction vector of BC (Elements I.31).

  • D.29 (Prop. XXXII): Angular ray constructor Elements I.23 rotating line direction at vertex C by given angle.

  • D.30 (Prop. XXXIII): Auxiliary angle construction at A on parallel through A (D.28 + D.29), yielding dropped oblique/altitude.

  • D.31 (Prop. XXXIV): Circle-line intersection Circle(A, r) cap Line(BC) (1 tangent or 2 symmetric chords; Simson diorismos).

  • D.32 (Prop. XXXV): Translational invariance of oblique transversal length across a fixed parallel strip (I.34).

  • D.33 (Prop. XXXVI): Converse of D.32 reducing free transversal of given length to pinned circle-line meet (D.31).

  • D.34 (Prop. XXXVIII): Thales projection invariant: collinear segments from fixed pole E cut parallel lines in constant ratio.

  • D.35 (Prop. XXXIX (pt. 1)): Internal homothetic dilation H(A, k) with 0 < k < 1 mapping given line BC to parallel locus FEG.

  • D.36 (Prop. XXXIX (pt. 2)): External homothetic dilation H(A, 1 + r) or H(A, -r) mapping line BC to parallel locus FEG.

  • D.37 (Prop. XL (pt. 1)): Affine convex combination (1 - t)*L1 + t*L2 of two parallel lines given in position.

  • D.38 (Prop. XL (pt. 2)): Extrapolated affine combination (1 + r)*L2 - r*L1 outside the parallel strip.
