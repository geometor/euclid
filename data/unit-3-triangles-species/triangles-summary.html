Unit 3: Triangles & Polygons Given in Species (Propositions 39–55) — Analytical Summary¶

Unit Overview & Role in Greek Geometric Analysis¶

Constructive determination of triangles up to similarity (εἴδει) from SSS-ratios (D.39), AAA (D.40), SAS-ratio (D.41), side-ratios (D.42), right-SSA (D.43), general SSA bifurcation with obtuse/acute diorismos (D.44), sum/difference of sides (D.45–D.46), decomposing rectilinear polygons into triangles (D.47), and quadratic area-scaling of figures given in species (D.48–D.55).

  • Scope: 17 propositions (D.39–D.55 (Simson XLII–LX), Menge pp. 66–98).

  • Analytical Function: Each proposition below compiles into a forward-chaining inference rule Given(...) -> Determined(...) within Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge Book VII) and geometor.model symbolic DAGs.

Computational Primitives (geometor.model & SymPy)¶

  • D.39 (Prop. XLII): Constructive SSS rigidity (Elements I.22 + I.8) determining all angles and side-ratios.

  • D.40 (Prop. XLIII): Prototype unit-base construction (D.27 + D.29) + equiangular similarity (Elements VI.4).

  • D.41 (Prop. XLIV): SAS similarity (Elements VI.6) on an auxiliary model triangle with fixed side D and E = (b/c)*D.

  • D.42 (Prop. XLV): Scale-free SSS similarity (Elements VI.5 + D.39) instantiating segments F, G, H in given ratios.

  • D.43 (Prop. XLVI): Unambiguous right-triangle HL similarity (semicircle intersection fixes unique acute complement).

  • D.44 (Prop. XLVII): Elements VI.7 SSA similarity split into acute and obtuse roots C1, C2 of Circle(B, r) cap Ray(AC).

  • D.45 (Prop. XLVIII): Produce BA to D with AD = AC: angle BDC = A/2 is given, reducing (b+c):a to SSA (D.44) on △DBC!

  • D.46 (Prop. XLIX): Produce BA to D with AD = AC: △DBC has given Angle B and given ratio BD : BC (D.41), then CD bisector fixes A!

  • D.47 (Prop. LI): Inductive diagonal ear-clipping (D.41 peels △ABC, subtracts ∠ACB from ∠BCD via D.4, scales ratio via D.8).

  • D.48 (Prop. LII): Common-base area ratio equals ratio of altitudes h1 : h2, determined by species angles and side ratios.

  • D.49 (Prop. LIII): Triangulate both polygons (D.47), relate sub-triangles via D.48 and VI.20, sum via D.22.

  • D.50 (Prop. LIV): Duplicate-ratio scaling (Elements VI.20 cor.): Area(E) : Area(F) = AB : third_proportional(AB, CD) = r^2.

  • D.51 (Prop. LV): Copy figure G ~ E onto AB or CD (VI.18), bridge Area(E):Area(G) by D.50 and Area(G):Area(F) by D.49, compose via D.8.

  • D.52 (Prop. LVI): Construct square ABFG on given AB (I.46, given in magnitude and species); Area(P) : AB^2 is given by D.49, hence Area(P) by D.2.

  • D.53 (Prop. LVII): Chain internal side ratios from Def. 3 across the cross-figure bridge ratio via D.8.

  • D.54 (Prop. LIX): Reduce Area(A):Area(B) to similar figures via D.49, then extract square-root side ratio from duplicate ratio via D.24!

  • D.55 (Prop. LX): Instantiate unit-side prototype B ~ A (D.52 gives Area(B)); D.1 gives Area(A):Area(B); D.54 + D.2 yield exact sides (Elements VI.25!
