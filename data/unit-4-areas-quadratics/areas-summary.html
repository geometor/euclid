Unit 4: Application of Areas, Quadratics & Cosine Projections (Propositions 56–67) — Analytical Summary¶

Unit Overview & Role in Greek Geometric Analysis¶

Equiangular parallelograms and parabolic division of areas (D.56–D.57), quadratic root extraction via elliptic and hyperbolic application of areas (D.58–D.59) and gnomons (D.60), altitude-to-base ratios (D.61–D.63), generalized Law of Cosines projections in obtuse and acute triangles (D.64–D.65), area-product law BD·BC : △ABC (D.66), and the algebraic identity (a+b)² − c² (D.67).

  • Scope: 12 propositions (D.56–D.67 (Simson LXI–LXXXIV), Menge pp. 98–126).

  • Analytical Function: Each proposition below compiles into a forward-chaining inference rule Given(...) -> Determined(...) within Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge Book VII) and geometor.model symbolic DAGs.

Computational Primitives (geometor.model & SymPy)¶

  • D.56 (Prop. LXIII): Reduction of non-equal equiangular parallelogram areas to reciprocal sides (Elements VI.14) by scaling side BC -> BG.

  • D.57 (Prop. LXI): Linear division of area (parabolē, Elements I.44): x = S / (a * sin(theta)).

  • D.58 (Prop. LXXXIII): Quadratic solver for elliptic application (Elements VI.28 + II.5 gnomon completion) over Q(sqrt((a/2)^2 - S/c)).

  • D.59 (Prop. LXXXIV): Unconditional quadratic solver (Elements VI.29 + II.6) yielding exact radical / Q(phi) positive root.

  • D.60 (Prop. LXXXII): Since P +/- G preserves species (Elements VI.24) and has given area (D.3/D.4), D.55 gives new sides, D.4 gives gnomon widths.

  • D.61 (Prop. LXIX): Compare AFCB with auxiliary parallelogram FB (D.49), shear CD to equal-base KB (I.35, VI.1), then solve △LCK (D.40) for LC : CB.

  • D.62 (Prop. LXX): Describe parallelogram AG ~ DF on AB (VI.18); D.50 + D.8 give Area(AEB) : Area(AG), reducing directly to D.61!

  • D.63 (Omitted (case of Prop. LIII)): Direct specialization of D.49 pairing △ABC with the squares on its sides (omitted as corollarial by Simson).

  • D.64 (Prop. LXXIV): Elements II.12 gives AC^2 - (AB^2 + BC^2) = 2*BC*BD; right △ABD is given in species (D.40), fixing 2*BC*BD : (1/2*BC*AD) = 4*BD : AD!

  • D.65 (Prop. LXXV): Elements II.13 gives (AB^2 + BC^2) - AC^2 = 2*BC*BD; right △ABD has given ratio BD : AD (D.40), fixing 4*BD : AD!

  • D.66 (Prop. LXII Cor.): Drop altitude BD perp AC: right △ABD has given AB : BD (D.40), and Rect(AB, AC) : △ABC = 2*AB : BD.

  • D.67 (Prop. LXXVI): Extend BA to AD = AC and BE || AC: (BA+AC)^2 - BC^2 = Rect(DC, CE) (II.6), whose ratio to Rect(BA, AC) (D.50) and △ABC (D.66) is given!
