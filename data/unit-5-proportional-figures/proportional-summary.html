Unit 5: Proportional Parallelograms, Triangles & Four-Line Relations (Propositions 68–83) — Analytical Summary¶

Unit Overview & Role in Greek Geometric Analysis¶

Compound ratio calculus for equiangular and supplementary-angle parallelograms and triangles (D.68–D.75), dropped-altitude ratios in species-given triangles (D.76), duplicated/corollarial area-species bridges (D.77–D.78), inscribed circle-triangle similarity lemmas and area-product determination (D.79–D.80), and four-line proportional transformations (D.81–D.83).

  • Scope: 16 propositions (D.68–D.83 (Simson L–LXXVIII), Menge pp. 126–164).

  • Analytical Function: Each proposition below compiles into a forward-chaining inference rule Given(...) -> Determined(...) within Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge Book VII) and geometor.model symbolic DAGs.

Computational Primitives (geometor.model & SymPy)¶

  • D.68 (Prop. LXV): Apply auxiliary parallelogram EG on EB equal to CD (VI.1), then cancel shared angle via reciprocal sides (Elements VI.14).

  • D.69 (Prop. LXVI): Shear P1 at constant base & height (Elements I.35) to match Angle(P2); D.40 gives oblique-side ratio, reducing to D.68.

  • D.70 (Props. LXVII–LXVIII): Equiangular compound-ratio case (Elements VI.14/VI.23), generalized to arbitrary given angles by shearing (I.35 + D.40).

  • D.71 (Prop. LXVIII Cor. 1): Complete each triangle into its double parallelogram (I.34, V.15) and invoke D.70.

  • D.72 (Prop. LXVIII Cor. 2): Construct parallelograms along the oblique direction AG/DH equal to twice each triangle (I.34/I.41) and apply D.70.

  • D.73 (Prop. LXIV (pt. 2)): Converse of D.74 linking reciprocal-side fourth proportionals (VI.14) to parallelogram area ratios (text repaired by Simson Prop. LXIV).

  • D.74 (Prop. LXIV (pt. 1; cf. LXIII)): Generalization of D.56 from equiangular parallelograms to arbitrary given angles via equal-area shear (I.35 + D.40).

  • D.75 (Prop. LXIV Cor.): Half-parallelogram specialization of D.74 (Elements I.34, V.15).

  • D.76 (Prop. L): Right △ABD has given Angle B (Def. 3), hence Species(△ABD) by D.40, giving AD : AB; compose with AB : BC via D.8.

  • D.77 (Omitted (dup. of Prop. LIX / D.54)): Textual doublet of D.54 preserved in the Greek archetype (Vat. gr. 190) and omitted by Simson.

  • D.78 (Omitted (case of Prop. LXX / D.62)): Immediate corollary of D.62 (once both side ratios and angle of parallelogram B are given, B is given in species).

  • D.79 (Prop. LXXVII Cor. 1): Pure similarity lemma for D.80: inscribe △EDF in equal-angle segment on EF (III.21), cut at equal altitude, establish congruence/similarity.

  • D.80 (Prop. LXXVIII): Since AB*AC : △ABC is given (D.66) and △ABC = 1/2*BC*h_A, BC^2 : BC*h_A = BC : h_A is given; prototype triangle matches by Lemma D.79!

  • D.81 (Prop. LXXI): Rectangles A*C = B^2 and D*F = E^2 (VI.17): D.70 gives B^2 : E^2, D.54 extracts B : E (and D.50 + D.68 gives converse).

  • D.82 (Prop. LXXII): Projective rescaling of second and fourth proportionals: A/(B/k) = C/(D/k) (Elements V.22).

  • D.83 (Prop. LXXIII): Perturbed four-line proportion (Elements V.23 / VI.16): A*D = B*(C/k) <-> D*k*A… -> D : C = B : (k*A).
