Unit 6: Hyperbolic/Elliptic Systems, Circles, Chords & Angle Bisectors (Propositions 84–94) — Analytical Summary¶

Unit Overview & Role in Greek Geometric Analysis¶

Canonical two-line systems xy = A² with x − y = d (D.84), x + y = s (D.85), and x² − y² = C² (D.86); circles given in magnitude and position, inscribed angle loci, and chord/arc determination (D.87–D.89); secant/chord power of a point (D.90–D.92); the celebrated Prop. 93 interior angle-bisector theorem; and its orthogonal chord-reflection corollary (D.94).

  • Scope: 11 propositions (D.84–D.94 (Simson LXXXV–C), Menge pp. 164–186).

  • Analytical Function: Each proposition below compiles into a forward-chaining inference rule Given(...) -> Determined(...) within Pappus's Treasury of Analysis (Synagoge Book VII) and geometor.model symbolic DAGs.

Computational Primitives (geometor.model & SymPy)¶

  • D.84 (Prop. LXXXV): Canonical difference-and-product solver: x*(x + d)*sin B = S reduces directly to hyperbolic application D.59!

  • D.85 (Prop. LXXXVI): Canonical sum-and-product (Vieta) solver: x*(s - x)*sin B = S reduces directly to elliptic application D.58!

  • D.86 (Prop. LXXXIX (cf. LXXXVII–LXXXVIII)): Biquadratic system x*y = S’, y^2 - k*x^2 = D -> k*x^4 + D*x^2 - S’^2 = 0 solved in radicals via quadratic substitution (D.59/D.58 + D.55)!

  • D.87 (Prop. XCI): Draw diameter BD (Mag(BD) = 2R by Def. 5); join DC (∠BCD = 90° by III.31, ∠BDC = ∠BAC by III.21); right △BCD given by D.40 + D.53!

  • D.88 (Prop. XCII): Converse of D.87: diameter BD = 2R (Def. 5), ratio BC : BD given (D.1), right △BCD given in species by D.43, fixing ∠BDC and supplementary arc angle (III.22)!

  • D.89 (Prop. XCIII): Join centre D to B (D.26); central ∠BDC = 2·∠BAC (Elements III.20) is given, so ray DC (D.29) meets Circle(D, R) (D.25) at given point C!

  • D.90 (Prop. XCIV): Join centre E to D (D.26); semicircle on ED cuts Circle(E, R) at A (∠EAD = 90° by III.31/III.18), fixing Pos(A) and Mag(DA)!

  • D.91 (Prop. XCV): Draw tangent DA (Mag(DA) given by D.90, DA^2 given by D.52); by Elements III.36, Rect(DB, DC) = DA^2 is invariant across all secants!

  • D.92 (Prop. XCVI): Draw diameter FEG through centre E and D: FD = R + ED (D.3) and DG = R - ED (D.4) are given; by Elements III.35, BD*DC = FD*DG!

  • D.93 (Prop. XCVII (cf. Prop. XCVIII [Stanhope])): Masterpiece of Data: chords BC and BD = DC are given (D.87); VI.3 + V.12 + △ACE ~ △ADB ~ △BDE yield (AB+AC):AD = BC:BD and (AB+AC)*ED = BC*BD!

  • D.94 (Prop. XCIX (cf. Prop. C [Stewart])): Produce EF to G on circle: since ∠GAE = ∠DAE = 90° (I.29), GE… wait: ∠AEG = 90° -> AG is a diameter through centre H (III.31), so △AHD ~= △GHF (I.26), fixing HF = HD (Pos(F)) and AD*EF = GF*EF (D.92/D.91)!
