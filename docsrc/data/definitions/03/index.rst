Definition 3 [γ΄ · Def. III]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Rectilineal figures are said to be given in species whose angles are severally given and the ratios of whose sides to one another are given.

*Category*: **Species (εἴδει)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenSpecies(P) := forall i,j: GivenAngle(ang_i(P)) and GivenRatio(s_i(P) : s_j(P))``
