Definition 7 [ζ΄ · Def. VII]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Segments of circles are said to be given in magnitude in which the inscribed angles are given and the bases of the segments are given in magnitude.

*Category*: **Circular Segment in Magnitude** · *Functional Predicate*: ``GivenSegmentMag(S) := GivenAngle(inscribed_angle(S)) and GivenMagnitude(base(S))``
