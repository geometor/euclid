Definition 9 [θ΄ · Def. IX]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. folio:: Menge 4
   :page: 4

.. enunciation::

   A magnitude is greater than a magnitude by a given magnitude when, the given magnitude being subtracted, the remainder is equal to that same magnitude.

*Category*: **Affine Excess (δοθέντι μεῖζον)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``ExceedsByGiven(A, B) := exists GivenMagnitude(D) with A - D = B``
