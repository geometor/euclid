Definition 12 [ιβ΄ · Omitted (expanded verbally in Props. XIV–XXVII)]
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A magnitude is less than a magnitude by a given magnitude than in ratio when, the given magnitude being added, the whole has a given ratio to that same magnitude.

*Category*: **Affine-Ratio Defect (δοθέντι ἔλασσον ἢ ἐν λόγῳ)** · *Functional Predicate*: ``LessThanInRatio(A, B) := exists GivenMagnitude(D), GivenRatio(k) with (A + D) : B = k``
