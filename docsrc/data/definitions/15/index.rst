Definition 15 [ιε΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)] · *[Bracketed Post-Euclidean Scholium]*
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Parallel-to-position (para thesei) is the straight line drawn through a given point parallel to a straight line given in position.]

*Category*: **Parallel to Position [Bracketed Scholium]** · *Functional Predicate*: ``ParaThesei(P, L) := line through GivenPoint(P) parallel to GivenLine(L)``
