Nathan Altshiller-Court
=======================

*Geometer · Author of College Geometry (1952)*

Professor of Mathematics at the University of Oklahoma whose College Geometry systematizes classical locus, similitude, power-of-a-point, and angle-bisector constructions descending directly from Euclid's Data.
