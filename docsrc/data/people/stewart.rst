Matthew Stewart
===============

*Professor of Mathematics at Edinburgh · Pupil of Simson*

Discoverer of Stewart's Theorem (Some General Theorems of Considerable Use in the Higher Parts of Mathematics, 1746) who communicated Proposition C of Simson's Data generalizing Euclid's Data Proposition 94.
