Proposition 1 [α΄ · Prop. I]
----------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 4
   :page: 4

.. enunciation::

   The ratio of given magnitudes to one another is given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(A), Mag(B)) -> Determined(Ratio(A : B))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`, :just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`, :just:`[V.7] <V.7>`, :just:`[V.16] <V.16>`

Let :math:`A` and :math:`B` be given magnitudes. I say that the ratio of :math:`A` to :math:`B` is given.

Since :math:`A` is given, it is possible to procure a magnitude equal to it (:just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`); let it be procured and let it be :math:`C`. Again, since :math:`B` is given, let :math:`D` be procured equal to :math:`B` (:just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`). Because :math:`A = C` and :math:`B = D`, as :math:`A` is to :math:`C`, so is :math:`B` to :math:`D` (:just:`[V.7] <V.7>`); and *alternando*, as :math:`A` is to :math:`B`, so is :math:`C` to :math:`D` (:just:`[V.16] <V.16>`). Therefore the ratio of :math:`A` to :math:`B` is given, for the same ratio :math:`C : D` has been procured for it (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`).
