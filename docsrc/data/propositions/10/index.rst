.. _D.10:

Proposition 10 [ι΄ · Prop. XVI]
-------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 16
   :page: 16

.. enunciation::

   If a magnitude be greater than a magnitude by a given magnitude than in ratio, the compounded magnitude will also be greater than the same by a given magnitude than in ratio; and if the compounded magnitude be greater than the same by a given magnitude than in ratio, either the remainder is also greater than the same by a given magnitude than in ratio, or the remainder together with the following magnitude to which the other has a given ratio is given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given((AB - D) : BC = k) <-> Determined(((AB+BC) - D) : BC = k+1 | (AB + BE = D where BC:BE given))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 9] <D.def.9>`, :just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`, :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`, :just:`[Data 5] <D.5>`, :just:`[Data 6] <D.6>`, :just:`[V.18] <V.18>`, :just:`[V.19] <V.19>`

First, let the magnitude :math:`AB` be :term:`greater by a given magnitude than in ratio <dothenti-meizon-e-en-logo>` to :math:`BC`. I say that the compound :math:`AC` is also greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the same :math:`BC`.

From :math:`AB` let the given magnitude :math:`AD` be subtracted (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`); then the ratio of the remainder :math:`DB` to :math:`BC` is given. *Componendo*, the ratio :math:`DC : BC` is given (:just:`[Data 6] <D.6>`, :just:`[V.18] <V.18>`). And since :math:`AD` is given, the whole :math:`CA` is greater than :math:`DC` by the given magnitude :math:`AD`, and hence :math:`AC` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`BC` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`).

Conversely, let the compound :math:`AC` be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`BC`. I say that the remaining part :math:`AB` is either greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`BC`, or else :math:`AB` together with a magnitude to which :math:`BC` has a given ratio is given.

Subtract the given excess from :math:`AC` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`). This given magnitude is either less than :math:`AB` or greater than :math:`AB` (if equal to :math:`AB`, then :math:`BC` itself is in a given ratio to :math:`BC`, a boundary case). First let it be less than :math:`AB`, and let it be :math:`AD`: then :math:`DC : CB` is given, so *separando* :math:`DB : BC` is given (:just:`[Data 5] <D.5>`, :just:`[V.17] <V.17>`); and :math:`AD` is given, so :math:`AB` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`BC` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`). Next let the given magnitude be greater than :math:`AB`, and let :math:`AE` be set equal to it (with :math:`E` falling between :math:`B` and :math:`C`): then the remainder :math:`EC` has a given ratio to :math:`CB`, so *invertendo* :math:`BC : EC` is given, and *convertendo* :math:`BC : BE` is given (:just:`[Data 5] <D.5>`). Yet :math:`AE = AB + BE` is given; therefore :math:`AB` together with :math:`BE`—to which :math:`BC` has a given ratio—is given.
