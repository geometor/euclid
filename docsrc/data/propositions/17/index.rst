.. _D.17:

Proposition 17 [ιζ΄ · Prop. XXV]
--------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 32
   :page: 32

.. enunciation::

   If there be three magnitudes, and the first be greater than the second by a given magnitude than in ratio, and the third be also greater than the same second by a given magnitude than in ratio, then either the first has a given ratio to the third, or one is greater than the other by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given((A - d1):B = k1, (C - d2):B = k2) -> Determined(Ratio(A:C) | GreaterThanInRatio(A, C))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 14] <D.14>`, :just:`[V.11] <V.11>`

Let there be three magnitudes :math:`AB, CD, E`, and let each of :math:`AB` and :math:`E` be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`CD` (in Menge's order: first and third each greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the second, or first and second to the third). Specifically, let :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` each be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`E`. I say that :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` either have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the other.

From :math:`AB` subtract the given magnitude :math:`AF` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`), so that :math:`FB : E` is given; and from :math:`CD` subtract the given magnitude :math:`CG`, so that :math:`GD : E` is given. Since both :math:`FB` and :math:`GD` have given ratios to :math:`E`, the ratio :math:`FB : GD` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). And to the two magnitudes :math:`FB` and :math:`GD`, having a given ratio to one another, the given magnitudes :math:`AF` and :math:`CG` have been added; therefore the wholes :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` either have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the other (:just:`[Data 14] <D.14>`).
