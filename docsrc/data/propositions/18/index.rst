.. _D.18:

Proposition 18 [ιη΄ · Prop. XXVI]
---------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be three magnitudes, and one of them be greater than each of the remaining two by a given magnitude than in ratio, the remaining two will either have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater than the other by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given((C - d1):A = k1, (C - d2):E = k2) -> Determined(Ratio(A:E) | GreaterThanInRatio(A, E))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 15] <D.15>`, :just:`[V.11] <V.11>`

Let there be three magnitudes :math:`AB, CD, EF`, and let the middle one :math:`CD` be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to each of :math:`AB` and :math:`EF`. I say that :math:`AB` and :math:`EF` either have a given ratio to one another, or one is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to the other.

Since :math:`CD` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`AB`, subtract the given magnitude :math:`CG` from :math:`CD` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`), so that :math:`GD : AB` is given. Again, since :math:`CD` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`EF`, subtract the given magnitude :math:`CH` from :math:`CD`, so that :math:`HD : EF` is given. Now the two given magnitudes :math:`CG` and :math:`CH` are either equal or unequal. First let :math:`CG = CH`: then the remainders :math:`GD` and :math:`HD` are identical, so :math:`GD` has a given ratio both to :math:`AB` and to :math:`EF`; hence :math:`AB : EF` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Next let :math:`CG < CH`: then :math:`H` falls between :math:`G` and :math:`D`, and the difference :math:`GH` is given (:just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`). Since :math:`GD : AB` is given, make :math:`GH : AK = GD : AB` (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`); because :math:`GH` is given, :math:`AK` is given (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`), and the remainder :math:`HD : KB = GD : AB` is given (:just:`[V.19] <V.19>`). Since :math:`HD : EF` is also given, :math:`KB : EF` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`), while :math:`AB = AK + KB` exceeds :math:`KB` by the given magnitude :math:`AK`; therefore :math:`AB` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`EF` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`).
