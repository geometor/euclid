Proposition 19 [ιθ΄ · Prop. XXVII]
----------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 34
   :page: 34

.. enunciation::

   If there be three magnitudes, and the first be greater than the second by a given magnitude than in ratio, and the second be greater than the third by a given magnitude than in ratio, the first will also be greater than the third by a given magnitude than in ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given((A - d1):B = k1, (B - d2):C = k2) -> Determined((A - (d1 + k1*d2)) : C = k1*k2)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 13] <D.13>`, :just:`[V.11] <V.11>`

Let there be three magnitudes :math:`AB, CD, E`; let :math:`AB` be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`CD`, and let :math:`CD` be greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`E`. I say that :math:`AB` is also greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`E`.

Since :math:`AB` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`CD`, subtract the given magnitude :math:`AF` from :math:`AB` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`), so that the remainder :math:`FB` has a given ratio to :math:`CD`. Because :math:`FB : CD` is given and :math:`CD` is greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`E`, :math:`FB` is also greater by a given magnitude than in ratio to :math:`E` (:just:`[Data 13] <D.13>`). Subtract that given magnitude :math:`FG` from :math:`FB`, leaving :math:`GB : E` given. Since both :math:`AF` and :math:`FG` are given, the whole :math:`AG = AF + FG` is given (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`), and the remainder :math:`GB` has a given ratio to :math:`E`. Therefore :math:`AB` is greater by the given magnitude :math:`AG` than in ratio to :math:`E` (:just:`[Data Def. 11] <D.def.11>`).
