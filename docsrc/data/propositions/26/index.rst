Proposition 26 [κϛ΄ · Prop. XXIX]
---------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If the extremities of a straight line be given in position, the straight line is given in position and in magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(A), Pos(B)) -> Determined(Pos(AB), Mag(AB))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`, :just:`[Data Def. 4] <D.def.4>`, :just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`, :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`

Let the extremities :math:`A` and :math:`B` of a straight line :math:`AB` be given in position. I say that the straight line :math:`AB` is given in position and in magnitude.

For if the straight line :math:`AB` did not retain the same place while :math:`A` and :math:`B` remain fixed, or if its length varied, two distinct straight lines could be drawn between the same two fixed points :math:`A` and :math:`B`, enclosing a space—contrary to :just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`. Therefore :math:`AB` always occupies the same place and has an invariant equal constructible on it (:just:`[I.2] <I.2>`), so :math:`AB` is given both in position (:just:`[Data Def. 4] <D.def.4>`) and in magnitude (:just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`).
