Proposition 27 [κζ΄ · Prop. XXX]
--------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 48
   :page: 48

.. enunciation::

   If one extremity of a straight line given in position and in magnitude be given, the other extremity will also be given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(Ray A->), Mag(AB), Pos(A)) -> Determined(Pos(B))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`, :just:`[Data Def. 4] <D.def.4>`, :just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`, :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`, :just:`[I Def. 15] <I.def.15>`

Let one extremity :math:`A` of a straight line :math:`AB`, given in position and in magnitude, be given. I say that the other extremity :math:`B` is also given.

For if :math:`B` were not given in position while :math:`A` remains fixed and the line :math:`AB` remains fixed in position, then either the straight line :math:`AB` would shift its position or its length :math:`AB` would change (falling at some distinct point :math:`C` on the same ray with :math:`AC \neq AB`). Yet :math:`AB` is given both in position and in magnitude—or constructively, describing the circle with given centre :math:`A` and given radius :math:`AB` (:just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`, :just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`) cuts the position-given ray at :math:`B` (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`). Therefore :math:`B` is given in position.
