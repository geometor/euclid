Proposition 31 [λα΄ · Prop. XXXIV]
----------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If from a given point onto a straight line given in position a straight line given in magnitude be drawn, it is also given in position.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(A), Pos(Line BC), Mag(AD >= dist(A, BC))) -> Determined(Pos(AD)) [2-root diorismos]`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 4] <D.def.4>`, :just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[I.12] <I.12>`, :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`

From a given point :math:`A`, let a straight line :math:`AB` of given magnitude be drawn to meet a straight line :math:`CD` given in position at :math:`B`. I say that :math:`AB` is given in position (up to the two symmetric intersections on :math:`CD`).

With given centre :math:`A` and given distance :math:`AB`, describe the circle :math:`EBF` (:just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`). Since the centre :math:`A` is given in position and the radius :math:`AB` is given in magnitude, the circle :math:`EBF` is given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`). Moreover, the straight line :math:`CD` is given in position; therefore their intersection :math:`B` is given in position (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`). Since :math:`A` is also given in position, the straight line :math:`AB` joining :math:`A` and :math:`B` is given in position (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`).
