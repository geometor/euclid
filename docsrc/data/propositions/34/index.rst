Proposition 34 [λδ΄ · Prop. XXXVIII]
------------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If to parallel straight lines given in position a straight line be drawn from a given point, it will be cut in a given ratio.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Pos(AB || CD), Pos(Pole E)) -> Determined(Ratio(EF : FG))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`, :just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`, :just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[I.12] <I.12>`, :just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`, :just:`[VI.4] <VI.4>`

From a given point :math:`E`, let a straight line :math:`EFG` be drawn across two parallel straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` given in position, meeting them at :math:`F` and :math:`G`. I say that :math:`EFG` is cut in a given ratio :math:`EF : FG`.

From the given point :math:`E`, draw :math:`EHK` perpendicular to :math:`AB` (and hence also to :math:`CD`), meeting :math:`AB` at :math:`H` and :math:`CD` at :math:`K` (:just:`[I.12] <I.12>`). Since :math:`EHK` makes a given (right) angle with position-given :math:`AB` from given :math:`E`, :math:`EHK` is given in position (:just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`). Because :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` are given in position, the intersections :math:`H` and :math:`K` are given in position (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`). Since :math:`E, H, K` are all given in position, the segments :math:`EH` and :math:`HK` are given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`), so the ratio :math:`EH : HK` is given (:just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`). Yet in :math:`\triangle EKG`, since :math:`FH \parallel GK`, we have :math:`EF : FG = EH : HK` (:just:`[VI.2] <VI.2>`). Therefore the ratio :math:`EF : FG` is given (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`).
