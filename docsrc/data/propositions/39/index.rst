Proposition 39 [λθ΄ · Prop. XLII]
---------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 66
   :page: 66

.. enunciation::

   If each of the sides of a triangle be given in magnitude, the triangle is given in species.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(a), Mag(b), Mag(c)) -> Determined(Species(Triangle ABC))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 1] <D.def.1>`, :just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`, :just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`, :just:`[I.8] <I.8>`, :just:`[I.22] <I.22>`

Let each of the sides :math:`AB, BC, CA` of :math:`\triangle ABC` be given in magnitude. I say that :math:`\triangle ABC` is :term:`given in species <eidei>`.

Set out a straight line :math:`DM` given in position from a given endpoint :math:`D`, and cut off :math:`DE = AB` (:just:`[I.3] <I.3>`), so :math:`DE` is given in magnitude and :math:`E` is given in position (:just:`[Data 27] <D.27>`). Similarly procure segments equal to :math:`BC` and :math:`CA`, and on :math:`DE` construct :math:`\triangle DEH` with :math:`EH = BC` and :math:`DH = CA` (:just:`[I.22] <I.22>`): the circles :math:`(E, BC)` and :math:`(D, CA)` are both given in position (:just:`[Data Def. 6] <D.def.6>`), so their intersection :math:`H` (on an assigned side of :math:`DE`) is given in position (:just:`[Data 25] <D.25>`), and the sides :math:`DH, EH` are given in position and magnitude (:just:`[Data 26] <D.26>`). Thus every angle and side-ratio of :math:`\triangle DEH` is given (:just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`). By :just:`[I.8] <I.8>` and :just:`[I.4] <I.4>`, :math:`\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEH`, whence all angles and side-ratios of :math:`\triangle ABC` are given; therefore :math:`\triangle ABC` is given in species (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`).
