Proposition 48 [μη΄ · Prop. LII]
--------------------------------

.. enunciation::

   If on the same straight line two triangles given in species be described, they will have a given ratio to one another.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Species(△ABC), Species(△ABD), SharedBase(AB)) -> Determined(Ratio(△ABC : △ABD))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 28] <D.28>`, :just:`[Data 30] <D.30>`, :just:`[I.37] <I.37>`, :just:`[VI.1] <VI.1>`, :just:`[VI.4] <VI.4>`

On the same straight line :math:`AB`, let two triangles :math:`\triangle ABC` and :math:`\triangle ABD` given in species be described. I say that :math:`\triangle ABC` has a given ratio to :math:`\triangle ABD`.

Through :math:`A` draw :math:`AE` perpendicular to :math:`AB` (:just:`[I.11] <I.11>`), and through :math:`C` and :math:`D` draw :math:`CE` and :math:`DF` parallel to :math:`AB` meeting the line :math:`AE` (produced as needed) at :math:`E` and :math:`F` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`). Since :math:`\triangle ABC` is given in species, dropping altitude :math:`CG \perp AB` (with :math:`CG = AE` by :just:`[I.34] <I.34>`) gives right-angled :math:`\triangle AGC` in species (:just:`[Data 40] <D.40>`), so the ratio :math:`AE : AB` (:math:`= CG : AB` via :math:`CG : AC` and :math:`AC : AB`, :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`) is given. For the same reason, since :math:`\triangle ABD` is given in species, the ratio :math:`AB : AF` is given. Combining :math:`AE : AB` and :math:`AB : AF` yields :math:`AE : AF` given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Yet triangles :math:`\triangle ABC` and :math:`\triangle ABD` on the same base :math:`AB` are to one another as their altitudes :math:`AE` and :math:`AF` (specifically, completing parallelograms :math:`ABHE` and :math:`ABKF`, :math:`\triangle ABC : \triangle ABD = \square ABHE : \square ABKF = AE : AF` by :just:`[I.41] <I.41>` and :just:`[VI.1] <VI.1>`). Therefore the ratio :math:`\triangle ABC : \triangle ABD` is given (:just:`[Data Def. 2] <D.def.2>`).
