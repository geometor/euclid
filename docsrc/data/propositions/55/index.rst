Proposition 55 [νε΄ · Prop. LX]
-------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 98
   :page: 98

.. enunciation::

   If an area be given in species and in magnitude, its sides will also be given in magnitude.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Species(A), Mag(Area(A))) -> Determined(Mag(sides(A)))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`, :just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`, :just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`, :just:`[Data 54] <D.54>`, :just:`[I.46] <I.46>`, :just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`

Let a rectilinear figure :math:`A` be given both in species and in magnitude. I say that its sides are also given in magnitude.

Let :math:`BC` be a side of :math:`A`. Set out a straight line :math:`DE` given in magnitude, and on :math:`DE` describe a rectilinear figure :math:`F` similar and similarly situated to :math:`A` (:just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`). Since :math:`A` is given in species, :math:`F` is also given in species (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`); and since :math:`F` is described on the given straight line :math:`DE`, :math:`F` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`). Because both :math:`A` and :math:`F` are given in magnitude, their ratio :math:`A : F` is given (:just:`[Data 1] <D.1>`). Therefore, by :just:`[Data 54] <D.54>`, their homologous sides have a given ratio :math:`BC : DE`. Since :math:`DE` is given in magnitude, :math:`BC` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`), and since :math:`A` is given in species all remaining sides have given ratios to :math:`BC` and so are given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`).
