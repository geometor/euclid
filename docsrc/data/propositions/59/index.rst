Proposition 59 [νθ΄ · Prop. LXXXIV]
-----------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 106
   :page: 106

.. enunciation::

   If a given area be applied to a given straight line, exceeding by a figure given in species, the breadths of the excess are given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Mag(S), Mag(a), Species(Excess)) -> Determined(Positive root of a*x + c*x^2 = S)`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`, :just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`, :just:`[Data 55] <D.55>`, :just:`[II.6] <II.6>`, :just:`[VI.24] <VI.24>`, :just:`[VI.29] <VI.29>`

Let a given area :math:`AB` (i.e., parallelogram :math:`ACDB` applied along :math:`AC` produced to :math:`B`) be applied to a straight line :math:`AC` given in magnitude, :term:`exceeding <hyperbole>` by a parallelogram :math:`CBEF` given in species (:just:`[VI.29] <VI.29>`). I say that the sides of the excess (:math:`CB` and :math:`BE`) are given in magnitude.

Bisect the given straight line :math:`AC` at :math:`D` (:just:`[I.10] <I.10>`), so :math:`DC` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 7] <D.7>`). On :math:`DC` describe the parallelogram :math:`DGHC` similar and similarly situated to the species-given excess :math:`CBEF` (:just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`), so :math:`DGHC` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 52] <D.52>`). Completing the gnomon around the diagonal :math:`BHF` (:just:`[II.6] <II.6>`, :just:`[VI.29] <VI.29>`), the large parallelogram :math:`DBEK` described on :math:`DB = DC + CB` (similar and similarly situated to :math:`CBEF`) is equal to the sum of the given applied area :math:`AB` and the given parallelogram :math:`DGHC`. Therefore :math:`DBEK` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`). Since :math:`DBEK` is also given in species (:math:`\sim CBEF`), its side :math:`DB` is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 55] <D.55>`). Subtracting given :math:`DC` from :math:`DB` leaves :math:`CB` given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 4] <D.4>`), and because :math:`CBEF` is given in species its adjacent side :math:`BE` (and the total base :math:`AB = AC + CB`) is given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 2] <D.2>`).
