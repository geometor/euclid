.. _D.62:

Proposition 62 [ξβ΄ · Prop. LXX]
--------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 112
   :page: 112

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines have a given ratio to one another, and on one of them be described a figure given in species, and on the other a parallelogrammic area in a given angle, and the figure have a given ratio to the parallelogram, the parallelogram is given in species.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Ratio(AB : CD), Species(AEB on AB), Angle(DF), Ratio(AEB : DF)) -> Determined(Species(DF))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`, :just:`[Data 50] <D.50>`, :just:`[Data 61] <D.61>`, :just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`, :just:`[VI.20] <VI.20>`

Let two straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`BC` have a given ratio :math:`AB : BC`; on :math:`AB` let a rectilinear figure :math:`AEB` given in species be described, and on :math:`BC` let a parallelogram :math:`BD` (:math:`= BCGD`) be described in a given angle :math:`\angle GBC`. If the area ratio :math:`AEB : BD` is given, I say that the parallelogram :math:`BD` is given in species.

On :math:`BC` describe a rectilinear figure :math:`BHC` similar and similarly situated to :math:`AEB` (:just:`[VI.18] <VI.18>`), so :math:`BHC` is given in species. Since :math:`AB : BC` is given and :math:`AEB \sim BHC`, the area ratio :math:`AEB : BHC` is given (:just:`[Data 50] <D.50>`). Yet :math:`AEB : BD` is given by hypothesis; therefore the ratio :math:`BHC : BD` is given (:just:`[Data 8] <D.8>`). Because the parallelogram :math:`BD` is applied in the given angle :math:`\angle GBC` to the side :math:`BC` of the species-given figure :math:`BHC` and :math:`BHC : BD` is given, the parallelogram :math:`BD` is given in species (:just:`[Data 61] <D.61>`).
