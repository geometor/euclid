Proposition 84 [πδ΄ · Prop. LXXXV]
----------------------------------

.. folio:: Menge 164
   :page: 164

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines contain a given area in a given angle, and one exceed the other by a given straight line, each of them will also be given.

*Functional Signature*: ``Given(Angle B, Area(x, y) = S, y - x = d) -> Determined(Mag(x), Mag(y))`` · *Dependencies*: :just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`, :just:`[Data 59] <D.59>`, :just:`[I.31] <I.31>`

Let two straight lines :math:`AB` and :math:`BC` contain a given area :math:`AB \cdot BC` in a given angle :math:`\angle ABC`, and let :math:`BC` exceed :math:`AB` by a given straight line. I say that each of :math:`AB` and :math:`BC` is given in magnitude.

On :math:`BC` cut off :math:`BD = AB` (:just:`[I.3] <I.3>`). Since :math:`BC` exceeds :math:`AB` (:math:`= BD`) by a given magnitude, the remainder :math:`DC = BC - BD` is given in magnitude! Complete the parallelogram :math:`ABCE` in the given angle :math:`\angle ABC` (whose area is given by hypothesis), and draw :math:`DF` parallel to :math:`AB` meeting :math:`AE` at :math:`F` (:just:`[I.31] <I.31>`), splitting :math:`ABCE` into :math:`ABDF` and :math:`FDCE`. Because :math:`BD = AB` and :math:`\angle ABD` is given, the rhombus :math:`ABDF` has all sides equal (:math:`1:1`) and all angles given, so :math:`ABDF` is given in species (:just:`[Data Def. 3] <D.def.3>`). Thus the given parallelogram :math:`ABCE` has been applied to the given straight line :math:`DC`, :term:`exceeding <hyperbole>` by the parallelogram :math:`ABDF` given in species! By :just:`[Data 59] <D.59>`, the sides of the excess—namely :math:`BD` (:math:`= AB`)—are given in magnitude; and adding given :math:`DC` to :math:`BD` yields :math:`BC` given in magnitude (:just:`[Data 3] <D.3>`).
