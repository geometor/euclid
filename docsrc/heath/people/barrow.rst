Isaac Barrow
============

*First Lucasian Professor of Mathematics at Cambridge; Teacher of Newton*

Published widely used Latin and English editions of Euclid's Elements (1655, 1660) and defended the philosophical rigor of Euclid's definitions and Book V theory of proportion in his Lectiones Mathematicae.

**Cited Works:** *Euclidis Elementorum Libri XV Breviter Demonstrati (1655)*, *Lectiones Mathematicae (1683)*, :ref:`Lectiones Mathematicae Cantabrigienses (1683) <work-barrow-lectiones-cantabrigienses>`
