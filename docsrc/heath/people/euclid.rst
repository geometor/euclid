Euclid of Alexandria
====================

*Alexandrian Geometer; Author of the Elements*

Compiled and systematized the Thirteen Books of the Elements at Alexandria under Ptolemy I Soter, integrating the discoveries of the Pythagoreans, Hippocrates of Chios, Eudoxus of Cnidus, and Theaetetus of Athens into a rigorous deductive canon.
