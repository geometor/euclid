Eudemus of Rhodes
=================

*Peripatetic Philosopher & First Historian of Mathematics*

Disciple of Aristotle whose History of Geometry (Γεωμετρικὴ Ἱστορία) is the primary source for Proclus's Eudemian Summary and Simplicius's account of Hippocrates' quadrature of lunes.
