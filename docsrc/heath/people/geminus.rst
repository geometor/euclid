Geminus of Rhodes
=================

*Stoic Mathematician & Philosopher of Mathematics*

Author of a comprehensive philosophical encyclopaedia on the classification and foundations of the mathematical sciences, extensively excerpted by Proclus and an-Nairīzī.

**Cited Works:** *Doctrine/Theory of the Mathematical Sciences (Φιλοκαλία)*
