Johan Ludvig Heiberg
====================

*Danish Classical Philologist & Editor of the Critical Greek Text of Euclid*

Edited the definitive Teubner critical Greek edition of Euclid's Elements (Vols. I–V, 1883–1888) based on the non-Theonine Vatican manuscript P, as well as the critical editions of Archimedes, Apollonius, Ptolemy, and Simplicius.
