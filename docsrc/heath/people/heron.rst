Heron of Alexandria
===================

*Alexandrian Geometer, Engineer & Commentator on Euclid*

Wrote an extensive commentary on Euclid's Elements (extant in Proclus and an-Nairīzī's Arabic commentary) introducing alternative direct proofs, semi-algebraical methods for Book II, and the formula for the area of a triangle in terms of its sides.
