David Hilbert
=============

*German Mathematician*

Author of Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899), reorganizing Euclidean geometry into five rigorous axiom groups (incidence, order, congruence, parallels, continuity) and proving the theory of plane area independent of continuity.

**Cited Works:** *Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899)*, :ref:`Grundlagen der Geometrie (Foundations of Geometry, 1899) <work-hilbert-grundlagen>`
