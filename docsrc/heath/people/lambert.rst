Johann Heinrich Lambert
=======================

*Swiss-German Mathematician, Physicist & Philosopher*

Proved the irrationality of π (1761) and wrote Die Theorie der Parallellinien (1766), discovering that under the hypothesis of the acute angle the area of a triangle is proportional to its angular defect and would hold on a sphere of imaginary radius.

**Cited Works:** *Die Theorie der Parallellinien (1766)*
