Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky
=============================

*Co-Founder of Hyperbolic Non-Euclidean Geometry*

Published the first complete, self-consistent system of hyperbolic geometry ('Imaginary' or 'Pangeometry', 1829–1855) replacing Euclid's Fifth Postulate with its negation.

**Cited Works:** *Geometrische Untersuchungen zur Theorie der Parallellinien (1840)*, *Pangeometry (1855)*
