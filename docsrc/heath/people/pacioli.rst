Fra Luca Bartolomeo de Pacioli
==============================

*Renaissance Franciscan Mathematician*

Author of the Summa de arithmetica (1494) and De Divina Proportione (1509, illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci), and editor of a 1509 Venice Latin edition of Euclid's Elements.

**Cited Works:** *Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (1494)*, *De Divina Proportione (1509)*, *Euclidis Opera (1509)*
