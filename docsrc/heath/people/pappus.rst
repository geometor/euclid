Pappus of Alexandria
====================

*Alexandrian Geometer & Commentator*

Author of the Mathematical Collection (Synagoge) and a commentary on Euclid Book X (surviving in Arabic), preserving the Treasury of Analysis and advanced lemmas on projective and solid geometry.
