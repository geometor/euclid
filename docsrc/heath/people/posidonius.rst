Posidonius of Apamea
====================

*Stoic Polymath & Geometer*

Defended geometry against the Epicurean critiques of Zeno of Sidon and proposed defining parallel lines as equidistant coplanar straight lines.

**Cited Works:** *Against Zeno of Sidon*
