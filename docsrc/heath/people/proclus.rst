Proclus Lycius Diadochus
========================

*Neoplatonic Philosopher & Commentator on Euclid Book I*

Head of the Athenian Academy whose Commentary on Book I of Euclid's Elements preserves the Eudemian Summary of early Greek geometry alongside the critiques and alternative proofs of Geminus, Posidonius, Heron, Ptolemy, and Pappus.
