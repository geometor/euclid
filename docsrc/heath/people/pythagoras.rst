Pythagoras of Samos
===================

*Founder of the Pythagorean Brotherhood*

Transformed geometry into a liberal philosophical science; credited with the discovery of the theory of proportions, the application of areas (Book II), the theorem on the right-angled triangle (I.47), and the construction of the cosmic figures.
