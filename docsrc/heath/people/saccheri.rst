Girolamo Saccheri, S.J.
=======================

*Jesuit Logician & Precursor of Non-Euclidean Geometry*

Author of Euclides ab omni naevo vindicatus (1733), investigating the summit angles of the Saccheri quadrilateral under the hypotheses of the obtuse, acute, and right angle, unwittingly establishing dozens of theorems of non-Euclidean geometry.
