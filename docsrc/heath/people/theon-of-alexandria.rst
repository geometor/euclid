Theon of Alexandria
===================

*Alexandrian Editor of Euclid's Elements; Father of Hypatia*

Produced the standard late-antique recension of Euclid's Elements that dominated all Greek manuscripts until Peyrard's discovery of the non-Theonine Vatican manuscript (P = Vat. gr. 190).

**Cited Works:** *Recension of Euclid's Elements*, *Commentary on Ptolemy's Syntaxis*
