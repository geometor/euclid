Theon of Smyrna
===============

*Middle Platonic Mathematician*

Author of an exposition of arithmetic, music, and astronomy useful for reading Plato, preserving the theory of side and diameter numbers (rational approximations to √2).

**Cited Works:** *Expositio rerum mathematicarum ad legendum Platonem utilium*
