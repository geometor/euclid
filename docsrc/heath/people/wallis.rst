John Wallis
===========

*Savilian Professor of Geometry at Oxford*

Pioneered infinitesimal analysis, defended Euclid's treatment of the horn-like angle of contact against Clavius, and showed that the Parallel Postulate is equivalent to the existence of similar non-congruent triangles of arbitrary size.

**Cited Works:** *De Postulato Quinto (1663 / 1693)*, *De Angulo Contactus et Semicirculi (1656)*, *Arithmetica Infinitorum (1656)*, :ref:`De Angulo Contactus et Semicirculi Disquisitio Geometrica (1656) <work-wallis-de-angulo-contactus>`
