DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _I.def.1:

1. A :term:`**point** <point>` is that which has no part.

.. _I.def.2:

2. A :term:`**line** <line>` is breadthless length.

.. _I.def.3:

3. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a :term:`line <line>` are :term:`points <point>`.

.. _I.def.4:

4. A :term:`**straight line** <straight-line>` is a :term:`line <line>` which lies evenly with the :term:`points <point>` on itself.

.. _I.def.5:

5. A :term:`**surface** <surface>` is that which has length and breadth only.

.. _I.def.6:

6. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a :term:`surface <surface>` are :term:`lines <line>`.

.. _I.def.7:

7. A :term:`**plane surface** <plane-surface>` is a :term:`surface <surface>` which lies evenly with the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` on itself.

.. _I.def.8:

8. A :term:`**plane angle** <plane-angle>` is the inclination to one another of two :term:`lines <line>` in a :term:`plane <plane-surface>` which meet one another and do not lie in a :term:`straight line <straight-line>`.

.. _I.def.9:

9. And when the :term:`lines <line>` containing the :term:`angle <plane-angle>` are straight, the angle is called :term:`**rectilineal** <rectilineal-angle>`.

.. _I.def.10:

10. When a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` set up on a straight line makes the adjacent :term:`angles <plane-angle>` equal to one another, each of the equal angles is :term:`**right** <right-angle>`, and the straight line standing on the other is called a :term:`**perpendicular** <perpendicular>` to that on which it stands.

.. _I.def.11:

11. An :term:`**obtuse angle** <obtuse-angle>` is an :term:`angle <plane-angle>` greater than a :term:`right angle <right-angle>`.

.. _I.def.12:

12. An :term:`**acute angle** <acute-angle>` is an :term:`angle <plane-angle>` less than a :term:`right angle <right-angle>`.

.. _I.def.13:

13. A :term:`**boundary** <boundary>` is that which is an :term:`extremity <extremity>` of anything.

.. _I.def.14:

14. A :term:`**figure** <figure>` is that which is contained by any :term:`boundary <boundary>` or boundaries.

.. _I.def.15:

15. A :term:`**circle** <circle>` is a :term:`plane figure <plane-surface>` :term:`contained by one line <extremity>` such that all the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` falling upon it from one :term:`point <point>` among those lying within the :term:`figure <figure>` are equal to one another ;

.. folio:: 0168
   :page: 154

.. _I.def.16:

16. And the :term:`point <point>` is called the :term:`**centre** <centre>` of the :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _I.def.17:

17. A :term:`**diameter** <diameter>` of the :term:`circle <circle>` is any :term:`straight line <straight-line>` drawn through the :term:`centre <centre>` and terminated in both directions by the circumference of the circle, and such a straight line also bisects the circle.

.. _I.def.18:

18. A :term:`**semicircle** <semicircle>` is the :term:`figure <figure>` contained by the :term:`diameter <diameter>` and the circumference cut off by it. And the :term:`centre <centre>` of the semicircle is the same as that of the :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _I.def.19:

19. :term:`**Rectilineal figures** <rectilineal-figure>` are those which are contained by :term:`straight lines <straight-line>`, :term:`**trilateral figures** <trilateral>` being those contained by three, :term:`**quadrilateral** <quadrilateral>` those contained by four, and :term:`**multilateral** <multilateral>` those contained by more than four straight lines.

.. _I.def.20:

20. Of :term:`trilateral figures <trilateral>`, an :term:`**equilateral triangle** <equilateral-triangle>` is that which has its three sides equal, an :term:`**isosceles triangle** <isosceles-triangle>` that which has two of its sides alone equal, and a :term:`**scalene triangle** <scalene-triangle>` that which has its three sides unequal.

.. _I.def.21:

21. Further, of :term:`trilateral figures <trilateral>`, a :term:`**right-angled triangle** <right-angled-triangle>` is that which has a :term:`right angle <right-angle>`, an :term:`**obtuse-angled triangle** <obtuse-angled-triangle>` that which has an :term:`obtuse angle <obtuse-angle>`, and an :term:`**acute-angled triangle** <acute-angled-triangle>` that which has its three :term:`angles <plane-angle>` :term:`acute <acute-angle>`.

.. _I.def.22:

22. Of :term:`quadrilateral figures <quadrilateral>`, a :term:`**square** <square>` is that which is both :term:`equilateral <equilateral-triangle>` and right-angled ; an :term:`**oblong** <oblong>` that which is right-angled but not equilateral ; a :term:`**rhombus** <rhombus>` that which is equilateral but not right-angled ; and a :term:`**rhomboid** <rhomboid>` that which has its opposite sides and :term:`angles <plane-angle>` equal to one another but is neither equilateral nor right-angled. And let quadrilaterals other than these be called :term:`**trapezia** <trapezium>`.

.. _I.def.23:

23. :term:`**Parallel** <parallel-straight-lines>` :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` are straight lines which, being in the same :term:`plane <plane-surface>` and being produced indefinitely in both directions, do not meet one another in either direction.
