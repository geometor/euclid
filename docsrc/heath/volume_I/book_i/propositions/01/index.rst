PROPOSITION 1.
==============

.. enunciation::

   On a given finite straight line to construct an equilateral triangle.

.. picture:: 0255.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 1 figure

Let :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />` be the given finite straight line.

Thus it is required to construct an equilateral triangle on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`.

| With centre :g:`$A$ <0255.g1:A>` and distance :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />` let the circle :g:`$BCD$ <0255.g1:( B C D )>` be described; :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`
| :line:`10` again, with centre :g:`$B$ <0255.g1:B>` and distance :g:`$BA$ <0255.g1:/ B A />` let the circle :g:`$ACE$ <0255.g1:( A C E )>` be described; :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`
| and from the point :g:`$C$ <0255.g1:C>`, in which the circles cut one another, to the points :g:`$A$ <0255.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0255.g1:B>` let the straight lines :g:`$CA$ <0255.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$CB$ <0255.g1:/ C B />` be joined. :just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`

:line:`15` Now, since the point :g:`$A$ <0255.g1:A>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$CDB$ <0255.g1:( C D B )>`,

.. assertion:: [Def. 15] <I.def.15>

   :g:`$AC$ <0255.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`.

Again, since the point :g:`$B$ <0255.g1:B>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$CAE$ <0255.g1:( C A E )>`,

.. assertion:: [Def. 15] <I.def.15>

   :g:`$BC$ <0255.g1:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$BA$ <0255.g1:/ B A />`.

| But :g:`$CA$ <0255.g1:/ C A />` was also proved equal to :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`;
| :line:`20` therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$CA$ <0255.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$CB$ <0255.g1:/ C B />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`.

And things which are equal to the same thing are also equal to one another; :just:`[C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CA$ <0255.g1:/ C A />` is also equal to :g:`$CB$ <0255.g1:/ C B />`.

:line:`25` Therefore the three straight lines :g:`$CA$ <0255.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0255.g1:/ B C />` are equal to one another.

.. folio:: 0256
   :page: 242

Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0255.g1:< A B C >>` is equilateral; and it has been constructed on the given finite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0255.g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   (Being) what it was required to do.
