PROPOSITION 3.
==============

.. enunciation::

   Given two unequal straight lines, to cut off from the greater a straight line equal to the less.

.. picture:: 0260.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 3 figure

Let :g:`$AB$ <0260.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>` be the two given unequal straight lines, and let :g:`$AB$ <0260.g1:/ A B />` be the greater of them.

Thus it is required to cut off from :g:`$AB$ <0260.g1:/ A B />` the greater a straight line equal to :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>` the less.

At the point :g:`$A$ <0260.g1:A>` let :g:`$AD$ <0260.g1:/ A D />` be placed equal to the straight line :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>`; :just:`[I. 2] <I.2>`

and with centre :g:`$A$ <0260.g1:A>` and distance :g:`$AD$ <0260.g1:/ A D />` let the circle :g:`$DEF$ <0260.g1:( D E F )>` be described. :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`
Now, since the point :g:`$A$ <0260.g1:A>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$DEF$ <0260.g1:( D E F )>`,

.. _folio-i-0261:

.. folio:: 0261
   :page: 247

.. assertion:: [Def. 15] <I.def.15>

   :g:`$AE$ <0260.g1:/ A E />` is equal to :g:`$AD$ <0260.g1:/ A D />`.

But :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>` is also equal to :g:`$AD$ <0260.g1:/ A D />`.

Therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0260.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>` is equal to :g:`$AD$ <0260.g1:/ A D />`; so that :g:`$AE$ <0260.g1:/ A E />` is also equal to :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>`. :just:`[C.N. 1] <I.cn.1>`

Therefore, given the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0260.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>`, from :g:`$AB$ <0260.g1:/ A B />` the greater :g:`$AE$ <0260.g1:/ A E />` has been cut off equal to :g:`$C$ <0260.g1:C>` the less.

.. qed::

   (Being) what it was required to do.
