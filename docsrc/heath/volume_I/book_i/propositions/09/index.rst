PROPOSITION 9.
==============

.. enunciation::

   To bisect a given rectilineal angle.

.. picture:: 0278.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 9 figure

| Let the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0278.g2:< B A C )>` be the given rectilineal angle.
| Thus it is required to bisect it.
| Let a point :g:`$D$ <0278.g2:D>` be taken at random on :g:`$AB$ <0278.g2:/ A B />`;
| let :g:`$AE$ <0278.g2:/ A E />` be cut off from :g:`$AC$ <0278.g2:/ A C />` equal to :g:`$AD$ <0278.g2:/ A D />`; :just:`[I. 3] <I.3>`
| :line:`5` let :g:`$DE$ <0278.g2:/ D E />` be joined, and on :g:`$DE$ <0278.g2:/ D E />` let the equilateral triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0278.g2:< D E F >>` be constructed;
| let :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />` be joined.

I say that the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0278.g2:< B A C )>` has been bisected by the straight line :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />`.

| :line:`10` For, since :g:`$AD$ <0278.g2:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$AE$ <0278.g2:/ A E />`,
| and :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />` is common,

.. assertion::

   the two sides :g:`$DA$ <0278.g2:/ D A />`, :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$EA$ <0278.g2:/ E A />`, :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />` respectively.

And the base :g:`$DF$ <0278.g2:/ D F />` is equal to the base :g:`$EF$ <0278.g2:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   :line:`15` therefore the angle :g:`$DAF$ <0278.g2:< D A F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$EAF$ <0278.g2:< E A F )>`.

Therefore the given rectilineal angle :g:`$BAC$ <0278.g2:< B A C )>` has been bisected by the straight line :g:`$AF$ <0278.g2:/ A F />`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
