PROPOSITION 10.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To bisect a given finite straight line.

.. picture:: 0281.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 10 figure

Let :g:`$AB$ <0281.g1:/ A B />` be the given finite straight line. Thus it is required to bisect the finite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0281.g1:/ A B />`.

Let the equilateral triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0281.g1:< A B C >>` be constructed on it, :just:`[I. 1] <I.1>`

and let the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0281.g1:< A C B )>` be bisected by the straight line :g:`$CD$ <0281.g1:/ C D />`; :just:`[I. 9] <I.9>`

I say that the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0281.g1:/ A B />` has been bisected at the point :g:`$D$ <0281.g1:D>`.

For, since :g:`$AC$ <0281.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$CB$ <0281.g1:/ C B />`, and :g:`$CD$ <0281.g1:/ C D />` is common,

.. assertion::

   the two sides :g:`$AC$ <0281.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0281.g1:/ C D />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$BC$ <0281.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0281.g1:/ C D />` respectively;

and the angle :g:`$ACD$ <0281.g1:< A C D )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$BCD$ <0281.g1:< B C D )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$AD$ <0281.g1:/ A D />` is equal to the base :g:`$BD$ <0281.g1:/ B D />`.

Therefore the given finite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0281.g1:/ A B />` has been bisected at :g:`$D$ <0281.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
