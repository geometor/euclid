PROPOSITION 15.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines cut one another, they make the vertical angles equal to one another.

.. picture:: 0291.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 15 figure

| For let the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0291.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0291.g2:/ C D />` cut one another at the point :g:`$E$ <0291.g2:E>`;
| :line:`5` I say that the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0291.g2:< A E C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DEB$ <0291.g2:< D E B )>`,
| and the angle :g:`$CEB$ <0291.g2:< C E B )>` to the angle :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>`.

| For, since the straight line :g:`$AE$ <0291.g2:/ A E />` stands
| :line:`10` on the straight line :g:`$CD$ <0291.g2:/ C D />`, making the angles :g:`$CEA$ <0291.g2:< C E A )>`, :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 13] <I.13>

   the angles :g:`$CEA$ <0291.g2:< C E A )>`, :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>` are equal to two right angles.

.. folio:: 0292
   :page: 278

Again, since the straight line :g:`$DE$ <0291.g2:/ D E />` stands on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0291.g2:/ A B />`, making the angles :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>`, :g:`$DEB$ <0291.g2:< D E B )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 13] <I.13>

   the angles :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>`, :g:`$DEB$ <0291.g2:< D E B )>` are equal to two right angles.

:line:`15` But the angles :g:`$CEA$ <0291.g2:< C E A )>`, :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>` were also proved equal to two right angles;

.. assertion:: [Post. 4 and C. N. 1] <I.post.4, I.cn.1>

   therefore the angles :g:`$CEA$ <0291.g2:< C E A )>`, :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>` are equal to the angles :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>` :g:`$DEB$ <0291.g2:< D E B )>`.

.. subclause::

   Let the angle :g:`$AED$ <0291.g2:< A E D )>` be subtracted from each;

:line:`20` therefore the remaining angle :g:`$CEA$ <0291.g2:< C E A )>` is equal to the remaining angle :g:`$BED$ <0291.g2:< B E D )>`. :just:`[C. N. 3] <I.cn.3>`

Similarly it can be proved that the angles :g:`$CEB$ <0291.g2:< C E B )>`, :g:`$DEA$ <0291.g2:< D E A )>` are also equal.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. porism::

   :line:`25` From this it is manifest that, if two straight lines cut one another, they will make the angles at the point of section equal to four right angles.
