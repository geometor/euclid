PROPOSITION 17.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In any triangle two angles taken together in any manner\ [#f1]_ are less than two right angles.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C >>` be a triangle;

I say that two angles of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C >>` taken together in any manner are less than two right angles.

For let :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />` be produced to :g:`$D$ <0296.g1:D>`. :just:`[Post. 2] <I.post.2>`

Then, since the angle :g:`$ACD$ <0296.g1:< A C D )>` is an exterior angle of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C >>`,

.. assertion::

   it is greater than the interior and opposite angle :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C )>`.

.. _folio-i-0296:

.. folio:: 0296
   :page: 282

.. subclause::

   Let the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0296.g1:< A C B )>` be added to each;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$ACD$ <0296.g1:< A C D )>`, :g:`$ACB$ <0296.g1:< A C B )>` are greater than the angles :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCA$ <0296.g1:< B C A )>`.

.. picture:: 0296.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 17 figure

But the angles :g:`$ACD$ <0296.g1:< A C D )>`, :g:`$ACB$ <0296.g1:< A C B )>` are equal to two right angles. :just:`[I. 13] <I.13>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angles :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCA$ <0296.g1:< B C A )>` are less than two right angles.

Similarly we can prove that the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0296.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`$ACB$ <0296.g1:< A C B )>` are also less than two right angles, and so are the angles :g:`$CAB$ <0296.g1:< C A B )>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0296.g1:< A B C )>` as well.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **taken together in any manner,** πάντη μεταλαμβανόμεναι, i.e. any pair added together.
