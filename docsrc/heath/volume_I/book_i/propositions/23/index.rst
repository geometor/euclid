PROPOSITION 23.
===============

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line and at a point on it to construct a rectilineal angle equal to a given rectilineal angle.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0308.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`$A$ <0308.g1:A>` the point on it, and the angle :g:`$DCE$ <0308.g1:< D C E )>` the given rectilineal angle;
| thus it is required to construct on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0308.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0308.g1:A>` on it, a rectilineal angle equal to the given rectilineal angle :g:`$DCE$ <0308.g1:< D C E )>`.

.. picture:: 0308.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0308 Figure 1 - Proposition 23

| On the straight lines :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:[ C D ]>`, :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:[ C E ]>` respectively let the points :g:`$D$ <0308.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0308.g1:E>` be taken at random;
| let :g:`$DE$ <0308.g1:/ D E />` be joined,
| and out of three straight lines which are equal to the three

.. folio:: 0309
   :page: 295

straight lines :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DE$ <0308.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:/ C E />` let the triangle :g:`$AFG$ <0308.g1:< A F G >>` be constructed in such a way that :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />` is equal to :g:`$AF$ <0308.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:/ C E />` to :g:`$AG$ <0308.g1:/ A G />`, and further :g:`$DE$ <0308.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$FG$ <0308.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[I. 22] <I.22>`

| Then, since the two sides :g:`$DC$ <0308.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:/ C E />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0308.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0308.g1:/ A G />` respectively,
| and the base :g:`$DE$ <0308.g1:/ D E />` is equal to the base :g:`$FG$ <0308.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   the angle :g:`$DCE$ <0308.g1:< D C E )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$FAG$ <0308.g1:< F A G )>`.

Therefore on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0308.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0308.g1:A>` on it, the rectilineal angle :g:`$FAG$ <0308.g1:< F A G )>` has been constructed equal to the given rectilineal angle :g:`$DCE$ <0308.g1:< D C E )>`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
