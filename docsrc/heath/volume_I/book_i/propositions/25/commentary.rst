Commentary on Proposition I.25
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition I.25 <I.25>`.

.. _folio-i-0314:

.. folio:: 0314
   :page: 300

:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` points out that this proposition (as also I. 8) is a purely *logical* consequence of I. 4 and I. 24 in the same way as I. 19 and I. 6 are purely *logical* consequences of I. 18 and I. 5. If :g:`a <0313.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`b <0313.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`c <0313.g1:/ A B />` denote the sides, :g:`A <0313.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`B <0313.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`C <0313.g1:< A C B )>` the angles opposite to them in a triangle :g:`ABC <0313.g1:< A B C >>`, and :g:`a' <0313.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`b' <0313.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`c' <0313.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`A' <0313.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`B' <0313.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`C' <0313.g1:< D F E )>` the sides and opposite angles respectively in a triangle :g:`A'B'C' <0313.g1:< D E F >>`, I. 4 and I. 24 tell us that, :g:`b <0313.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`c <0313.g1:/ A B />` being respectively :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ A C / = 0313.g1:/ D F /; 0313.g1:/ A B / = 0313.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`b' <0313.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`c' <0313.g1:/ D E />`,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{(1) if } A \text{ is equal to } A'\text{, then } a \text{ is equal to } a'\text{,} \\
&\text{(2) if } A \text{ is less than } A'\text{, then } a \text{ is less than } a'\text{,} \\
&\text{(3) if } A \text{ is greater than } A'\text{, then } a \text{ is greater than } a'\text{;}
\end{aligned}
$$

and it follows logically that,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{(1) if } a \text{ is equal to } a'\text{, the angle } A \text{ is equal to the angle } A'\text{,} & [\textbf{I. 8}] \\
&\left.
\begin{aligned}
&\text{(2) if } a \text{ is less than } a'\text{, } A \text{ is less than } A'\text{,} \\
&\text{(3) if } a \text{ is greater than } a'\text{, } A \text{ is greater than } A'\text{.}
\end{aligned}
\right\} & [\textbf{I. 25}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Two alternative proofs of this theorem are given by :person:`Proclus <proclus>` (:cite:`pp. 345–7 <proclus:in-euclid:345-7>`), and they are both interesting. Moreover both are *direct*.

**I. Proof by Menelaus of Alexandria.**

Let :g:`ABC <0314.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0314.g1:< D E F >>` be two triangles having the two sides :g:`BA <0314.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`AC <0314.g1:/ A C />` :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ B A / = 0314.g1:/ E D /; 0314.g1:/ A C / = 0314.g1:/ D F />` to the two sides :g:`ED <0314.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`DF <0314.g1:/ D F />`, but the base :g:`BC <0314.g1:/ B C />` greater than the base :g:`EF <0314.g1:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0314.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0314 Figure 1 - Menelaus Direct Proof of Proposition 25

Then shall the angle at :g:`A <0314.g1:< B A C )>` be greater than the angle at :g:`D <0314.g1:< E D F )>`.

From :g:`BC <0314.g1:/ B C />` cut off :g:`BG <0314.g1:/ B G />` :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ B G / = 0314.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`EF <0314.g1:/ E F />`. At :g:`B <0314.g1:B>`, on the straight line :g:`BC <0314.g1:/ B C />`, make the angle :g:`GBH <0314.g1:< G B H )>` (on the side of :g:`BG <0314.g1:/ B G />` remote from :g:`A <0314.g1:A>`) :eq:`equal <0314.g1:< G B H ) = 0314.g1:< F E D )>` to the angle :g:`FED <0314.g1:< F E D )>`. Make :g:`BH <0314.g1:/ B H />` :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ B H / = 0314.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`DE <0314.g1:/ D E />`; join :g:`HG <0314.g1:/ H G />`, and produce it to meet :g:`AC <0314.g1:/ A C />` in :g:`K <0314.g1:K>`. Join :g:`AH <0314.g1:/ A H />`.

Then, since the two sides :g:`GB <0314.g1:/ G B />`, :g:`BH <0314.g1:/ B H />` are :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ G B / = 0314.g1:/ F E /; 0314.g1:/ B H / = 0314.g1:/ E D />` to the two sides :g:`FE <0314.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`ED <0314.g1:/ E D />` respectively, and the angles contained by them are :eq:`equal <0314.g1:< G B H ) = 0314.g1:< F E D )>`, :g:`HG <0314.g1:/ H G />` is :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ H G / = 0314.g1:/ D F / = 0314.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`DF <0314.g1:/ D F />` or :g:`AC <0314.g1:/ A C />`, and the angle :g:`BHG <0314.g1:< B H G )>` is :eq:`equal <0314.g1:< B H G ) = 0314.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`EDF <0314.g1:< E D F )>`. Now :g:`HK <0314.g1:/ H K />` is greater than :g:`HG <0314.g1:/ H G />` or :g:`AC <0314.g1:/ A C />`, and *a fortiori* greater than :g:`AK <0314.g1:/ A K />`; therefore the angle :g:`KAH <0314.g1:< K A H )>` is greater than the angle :g:`KHA <0314.g1:< K H A )>`. And, since :g:`AB <0314.g1:/ A B />` is :eq:`equal <0314.g1:/ A B / = 0314.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`BH <0314.g1:/ B H />`, the angle :g:`BAH <0314.g1:< B A H )>` is :eq:`equal <0314.g1:< B A H ) = 0314.g1:< B H A )>` to the angle :g:`BHA <0314.g1:< B H A )>`. Therefore, by addition, the whole angle :g:`BAC <0314.g1:< B A C )>` is greater than the whole angle :g:`BHG <0314.g1:< B H G )>`, that is, greater than the angle :g:`EDF <0314.g1:< E D F )>`.

**II. Heron's proof.**

Let the triangles be given as before. Since :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />` is greater than :g:`EF <0315.g1:/ E F />`, produce :g:`EF <0315.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`G <0315.g1:G>` so that :g:`EG <0315.g1:/ E G />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ E G / = 0315.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />`. Produce :g:`ED <0315.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`H <0315.g1:H>` so that :g:`DH <0315.g1:/ D H />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ D H / = 0315.g1:/ D F />` to :g:`DF <0315.g1:/ D F />`. The circle with centre :g:`D <0315.g1:D>` and radius :g:`DF <0315.g1:/ D F />` will then pass through :g:`H <0315.g1:H>`. Let it be described, as :g:`FKH <0315.g1:( F K H )>`.

.. picture:: 0315.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0315 Figure 1 - Heron Direct Proof of Proposition 25

Now, since :g:`BA <0315.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`AC <0315.g1:/ A C />` are together greater than :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`BA <0315.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`AC <0315.g1:/ A C />` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ B A / = 0315.g1:/ E D /; 0315.g1:/ A C / = 0315.g1:/ D H />` to :g:`ED <0315.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`DH <0315.g1:/ D H />` respectively, while :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ B C / = 0315.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`EG <0315.g1:/ E G />`, :g:`EH <0315.g1:/ E H />` is greater than :g:`EG <0315.g1:/ E G />`. Therefore the circle with centre :g:`E <0315.g1:E>` and radius :g:`EG <0315.g1:/ E G />` will cut :g:`EH <0315.g1:/ E H />`, and therefore will cut the circle already drawn. Let it cut that circle in :g:`K <0315.g1:K>`, and join :g:`DK <0315.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`KE <0315.g1:/ K E />`. Then, since :g:`D <0315.g1:D>` is the centre of the circle :g:`FKH <0315.g1:( F K H )>`, :g:`DK <0315.g1:/ D K />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ D K / = 0315.g1:/ D F / = 0315.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`DF <0315.g1:/ D F />` or :g:`AC <0315.g1:/ A C />`. Similarly, since :g:`E <0315.g1:E>` is the centre of the circle :g:`KG <0315.g1:( E G )>`, :g:`EK <0315.g1:/ E K />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ E K / = 0315.g1:/ E G / = 0315.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`EG <0315.g1:/ E G />` or :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />`, And :g:`DE <0315.g1:/ D E />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ D E / = 0315.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`AB <0315.g1:/ A B />`. Therefore the two sides :g:`BA <0315.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`AC <0315.g1:/ A C />` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ B A / = 0315.g1:/ E D /; 0315.g1:/ A C / = 0315.g1:/ D K />` to the two sides :g:`ED <0315.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`DK <0315.g1:/ D K />` respectively; and the base :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ B C / = 0315.g1:/ E K />` to the base :g:`EK <0315.g1:/ E K />`; therefore the angle :g:`BAC <0315.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< B A C ) = 0315.g1:< E D K )>` to the angle :g:`EDK <0315.g1:< E D K )>`. Therefore the angle :g:`BAC <0315.g1:< B A C )>` is greater than the angle :g:`EDF <0315.g1:< E D F )>`.
