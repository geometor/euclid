PROPOSITION 27.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line falling on two straight lines make the alternate angles equal to one another, the straight lines will be parallel to one another.

.. picture:: 0321.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0321 Figure 1 - Proposition 27

| For let the straight line :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />` falling on the two straight
| :line:`5` lines :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` make the alternate angles :g:`$AEF$ <0321.g1:< A E F )>`, :g:`$EFD$ <0321.g1:< E F D )>` equal to one another;
| I say that :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />`.

| For, if not, :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` when produced will meet either in the direction
| :line:`10` of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` or towards :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0321.g1:C>`.
| Let them be produced and meet, in the direction of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`, at :g:`$G$ <0321.g1:G>`.

| Then, in the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0321.g1:< G E F >>`, the exterior angle :g:`$AEF$ <0321.g1:< A E F )>` is equal to the interior and opposite
| :line:`15` angle :g:`$EFG$ <0321.g1:< E F G )>`:

.. assertion:: [I. 16] <I.16>

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` when produced will not meet in the direction of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly it can be proved that neither will they meet
   | :line:`20` towards :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0321.g1:C>`.

.. folio:: 0322
   :page: 308

But straight lines which do not meet in either direction are parallel; :just:`[Def. 23] <I.def.23>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
