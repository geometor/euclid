PROPOSITION 31.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Through a given point to draw a straight line parallel to a given straight line.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0330.g1:A>` be the given point, and :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />` the given straight line;
| thus it is required to draw through the point :g:`$A$ <0330.g1:A>` a straight line parallel to the straight line :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />`.

.. folio:: 0330
   :page: 316

.. picture:: 0330.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0330 Figure 1 - Proposition 31

| Let a point :g:`$D$ <0330.g1:D>` be taken at random on :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />`, and let :g:`$AD$ <0330.g1:/ A D />` be joined;
| on the straight line :g:`$DA$ <0330.g1:/ D A />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0330.g1:A>` on it, let the angle :g:`$DAE$ <0330.g1:< D A E )>` be constructed equal to the angle :g:`$ADC$ <0330.g1:< A D C )>` :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`;
| and let the straight line :g:`$AF$ <0330.g1:/ A F />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`$EA$ <0330.g1:/ E A />`.

Then, since the straight line :g:`$AD$ <0330.g1:/ A D />` falling on the two straight lines :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0330.g1:[ E F ]>` has made the alternate angles :g:`$EAD$ <0330.g1:< E A D )>`, :g:`$ADC$ <0330.g1:< A D C )>` equal to one another,

.. assertion:: [I. 27] <I.27>

   therefore :g:`$EAF$ <0330.g1:/ E A F />` is parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />`.

Therefore through the given point :g:`$A$ <0330.g1:A>` the straight line :g:`$EAF$ <0330.g1:/ E A F />` has been drawn parallel to the given straight line :g:`$BC$ <0330.g1:/ B C />`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
