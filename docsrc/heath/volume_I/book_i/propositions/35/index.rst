PROPOSITION 35.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Parallelograms which are on the same base and in the same parallels are equal to one another.

| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0341.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EBCF$ <0341.g1:< E B C F >>` be parallelograms on the same base :g:`$BC$ <0341.g1:/ B C />` and in the same parallels :g:`$AF$ <0341.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$BC$ <0341.g1:/ B C />`;
| :line:`5` I say that :g:`$ABCD$ <0341.g1:< A B C D >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$EBCF$ <0341.g1:< E B C F >>`.

| For, since :g:`$ABCD$ <0341.g1:< A B C D >>` is a parallelogram,
| :g:`$AD$ <0341.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$BC$ <0341.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. folio:: 0341
   :page: 327

| For the same reason also
| :g:`$EF$ <0341.g1:/ E F />` is equal to :g:`$BC$ <0341.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion:: [C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>

   :line:`10` so that :g:`$AD$ <0341.g1:/ A D />` is also equal to :g:`$EF$ <0341.g1:/ E F />`;

and :g:`$DE$ <0341.g1:/ D E />` is common;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   therefore the whole :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:/ A E />` is equal to the whole :g:`$DF$ <0341.g1:/ D F />`.

| But :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to :g:`$DC$ <0341.g1:/ D C />`; :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`
| therefore the two sides :g:`$EA$ <0341.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:/ A B />` are equal to the two sides
| :line:`15` :g:`$FD$ <0341.g1:/ F D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0341.g1:/ D C />` respectively,

.. picture:: 0341.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0341 Figure 1 - Proposition 35

and the angle :g:`$FDC$ <0341.g1:< F D C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$EAB$ <0341.g1:< E A B )>`, the exterior to the interior; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. subclause::

   | therefore the base :g:`$EB$ <0341.g1:/ E B />` is equal
   | :line:`20` to the base :g:`$FC$ <0341.g1:/ F C />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   and the triangle :g:`$EAB$ <0341.g1:< E A B >>` will be equal to the triangle :g:`$FDC$ <0341.g1:< F D C >>`.

Let :g:`$DGE$ <0341.g1:< D G E >>` be subtracted from each;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 3] <I.cn.3>

   therefore the trapezium :g:`$ABGD$ <0341.g1:< A B G D >>` which remains is equal to the trapezium :g:`$EGCF$ <0341.g1:< E G C F >>` which remains.

:line:`25` Let the triangle :g:`$GBC$ <0341.g1:< G B C >>` be added to each;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   therefore the whole parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0341.g1:< A B C D >>` is equal to the whole parallelogram :g:`$EBCF$ <0341.g1:< E B C F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
