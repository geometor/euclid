PROPOSITION 40.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Equal triangles which are on equal bases and on the same side are also in the same parallels.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0351.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$CDE$ <0351.g1:< C D E >>` be equal triangles on equal bases :g:`$BC$ <0351.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CE$ <0351.g1:/ C E />` and on the same side.

I say that they are also in the same parallels.

| For let :g:`$AD$ <0351.g1:/ A D />` be joined;
| I say that :g:`$AD$ <0351.g1:/ A D />` is parallel to :g:`$BE$ <0351.g1:/ B E />`.

For, if not, let :g:`$AF$ <0351.g1:/ A F />` be drawn through :g:`$A$ <0351.g1:A>` parallel to :g:`$BE$ <0351.g1:/ B E />` :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`, and let :g:`$FE$ <0351.g1:/ F E />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0351.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0351 Figure 1 - Proposition 40

| Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0351.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$FCE$ <0351.g1:< F C E >>`;
| for they are on equal bases :g:`$BC$ <0351.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CE$ <0351.g1:/ C E />` and in the same parallels :g:`$BE$ <0351.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$AF$ <0351.g1:/ A F />`. :just:`[I. 38] <I.38>`

But the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0351.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$DCE$ <0351.g1:< D C E >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the triangle :g:`$DCE$ <0351.g1:< D C E >>` is also equal to the triangle :g:`$FCE$ <0351.g1:< F C E >>`, :just:`[C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>`
   | the greater to the less: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AF$ <0351.g1:/ A F />` is not parallel to :g:`$BE$ <0351.g1:/ B E />`.

.. folio:: 0352
   :page: 338

Similarly we can prove that neither is any other straight line except :g:`$AD$ <0351.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0351.g1:/ A D />` is parallel to :g:`$BE$ <0351.g1:/ B E />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.]`
