PROPOSITION 42.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given triangle.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>` be the given triangle, and :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>` the given rectilineal angle;

thus it is required to construct in the rectilineal angle :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>` a parallelogram :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< F E C G > = 0353.g2:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>`.

Let :g:`$BC$ <0353.g2:/ B C />` be :eq:`bisected <0353.g2:/ B E / = 0353.g2:/ E C />` at :g:`$E$ <0353.g2:E>`, and let :g:`$AE$ <0353.g2:/ A E />` be joined; on the straight line :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, and at the point :g:`$E$ <0353.g2:E>` on it, let the angle :g:`$CEF$ <0353.g2:< C E F )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< C E F ) = 0353.g2:< D )>` to the angle :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>`; :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

through :g:`$A$ <0353.g2:A>` let :g:`$AG$ <0353.g2:/ A G />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, and :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

through :g:`$C$ <0353.g2:C>` let :g:`$CG$ <0353.g2:/ C G />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$EF$ <0353.g2:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0353.g2.svg
   :alt: Folio 0353 Figure 2 - Proposition 42

Then :g:`$FECG$ <0353.g2:< F E C G >>` is a parallelogram.

And, since :g:`$BE$ <0353.g2:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <0353.g2:/ B E / = 0353.g2:/ E C />` to :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0353.g2:< A B E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< A B E > = 0353.g2:< A E C >>` to the triangle :g:`$AEC$ <0353.g2:< A E C >>`, for they are on :eq:`equal <0353.g2:/ B E / = 0353.g2:/ E C />` bases :g:`$BE$ <0353.g2:/ B E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />` and in the same parallels :g:`$BC$ <0353.g2:/ B C />`, :g:`$AG$ <0353.g2:/ A G />`; :just:`[I. 38] <I.38>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>` is double of the triangle :g:`$AEC$ <0353.g2:< A E C >>`.

.. _folio-i-0354:

.. folio:: 0354
   :page: 340

.. subclause:: [I. 41] <I.41>

   But the parallelogram :g:`$FECG$ <0353.g2:< F E C G >>` is also double of the triangle :g:`$AEC$ <0353.g2:< A E C >>`, for it has the same base with it and is in the same parallels with it;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$FECG$ <0353.g2:< F E C G >>` is :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< F E C G > = 0353.g2:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>`.

And it has the angle :g:`$CEF$ <0353.g2:< C E F )>` :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< C E F ) = 0353.g2:< D )>` to the given angle :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the parallelogram :g:`$FECG$ <0353.g2:< F E C G >>` has been constructed :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< F E C G > = 0353.g2:< A B C >>` to the given triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>`, in the angle :g:`$CEF$ <0353.g2:< C E F )>` which is :eq:`equal <0353.g2:< C E F ) = 0353.g2:< D )>` to :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
