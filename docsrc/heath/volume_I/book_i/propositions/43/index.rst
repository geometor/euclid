PROPOSITION 43.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In any parallelogram the complements of the parallelograms about the diameter are equal to one another.

| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0354.g1:< A B C D >>` be a parallelogram, and :g:`$AC$ <0354.g1:/ A C />` its diameter;
| and about :g:`$AC$ <0354.g1:/ A C />` let :g:`$EH$ <0354.g1:< A E K H >>`, :g:`$FG$ <0354.g1:< K F C G >>` be parallelograms, and :g:`$BK$ <0354.g1:< E B F K >>`, :g:`$KD$ <0354.g1:< H K G D >>`
| :line:`5` the so-called complements;
| I say that the complement :g:`$BK$ <0354.g1:< E B F K >>` is equal to the complement :g:`$KD$ <0354.g1:< H K G D >>`.

.. picture:: 0354.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0354 Figure 1 - Proposition 43

| For, since :g:`$ABCD$ <0354.g1:< A B C D >>` is a parallelogram, and :g:`$AC$ <0354.g1:/ A C />` its diameter,
| the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0354.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to
| :line:`10` the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0354.g1:< A C D >>`. :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

| Again, since :g:`$EH$ <0354.g1:< A E K H >>` is a parallelogram, and :g:`$AK$ <0354.g1:/ A K />` is its diameter,
| the triangle :g:`$AEK$ <0354.g1:< A E K >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$AHK$ <0354.g1:< A H K >>`.

| :line:`15` For the same reason
| the triangle :g:`$KFC$ <0354.g1:< K F C >>` is also equal to :g:`$KGC$ <0354.g1:< K G C >>`.

| Now, since the triangle :g:`$AEK$ <0354.g1:< A E K >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$AHK$ <0354.g1:< A H K >>`,
| and :g:`$KFC$ <0354.g1:< K F C >>` to :g:`$KGC$ <0354.g1:< K G C >>`,

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   :line:`20` the triangle :g:`$AEK$ <0354.g1:< A E K >>` together with :g:`$KGC$ <0354.g1:< K G C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$AHK$ <0354.g1:< A H K >>` together with :g:`$KFC$ <0354.g1:< K F C >>`.

And the whole triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0354.g1:< A B C >>` is also equal to the whole :g:`$ADC$ <0354.g1:< A D C >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the complement :g:`$BK$ <0354.g1:< E B F K >>` which remains is equal to the
   | :line:`25` complement :g:`$KD$ <0354.g1:< H K G D >>` which remains. :just:`[C. N. 3] <I.cn.3>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
