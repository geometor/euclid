Commentary on Book II Definition 1
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book II Definitions <II.def>`.

.. folio:: 0384
   :page: 370

Πᾶν :term:`παραλληλόγραμμον <parallelogrammon>` :term:`ὀρθογώνιον <orthogonion>` περιέχεσθαι λέγεται ὑπὸ δύο τῶν τὴν ὀρθὴν :term:`γωνίαν <angle>` περιεχουσῶν :term:`εὐθειῶν <straight-line>`.

As the full expression in Greek for "the angle :math:`BAC`" is "the angle contained by the (straight lines) :math:`BA`, :math:`AC`," ἡ ὑπὸ τῶν ΒΑ, ΑΓ περιεχομένη :term:`γωνία <angle>`, so the full expression for "the rectangle contained by :math:`BA`, :math:`AC`" is τὸ ὑπὸ τῶν ΒΑ, ΑΓ περιεχόμενον :term:`ὀρθογώνιον <orthogonion>`. In this case too ΒΑ, ΑΓ is commonly abbreviated by the Greek geometers into ΒΑΓ. Thus in :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` and :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` τὸ ὑπὸ ΒΑΓ or τὸ ὑπὸ τῶν ΒΑΓ means *the rectangle* :math:`BA`, :math:`AC`, just as ἡ ὑπὸ ΒΑΓ means *the angle* :math:`BAC`; the gender of the article shows which is meant in each case. In the early Books :person:`Euclid <euclid>` uses the full expression τὸ ὑπὸ τῶν ΒΑ, ΑΓ; but the shorter form τὸ ὑπὸ τῶν ΒΑΓ is found from Book X. onwards. Cf. XII. 11, where τὰ (τμήματα) ἐπὶ τῶν ΘΟΕ, ΕΠΖ, ΖΡΗ, ΗΣΘ means the segments on the eight straight lines ΘΟ, ΟΕ, ΕΠ, ΠΖ, ΖΡ, ΡΗ, ΗΣ, ΣΘ.
