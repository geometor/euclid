PROPOSITION 2.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be cut at random, the rectangle contained by the whole and both of the segments is equal to the square on the whole.

For let the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />` be cut at random at the point :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>`;

I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0390.g1:/ B C />` together with the rectangle contained by :g:`$BA$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0390.g1:/ A C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0390.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For let the square :g:`$ADEB$ <0390.g1:< A D E B >>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />` :just:`[I. 46] <I.46>`, and let :g:`$CF$ <0390.g1:/ C F />` be drawn through :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>` parallel to either :g:`$AD$ <0390.g1:/ A D />` or :g:`$BE$ <0390.g1:/ B E />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. assertion::

   Then :g:`$AE$ <0390.g1:< A E >>` is equal to :g:`$AF$ <0390.g1:< A F >>`, :g:`$CE$ <0390.g1:< C E >>`.

Now :g:`$AE$ <0390.g1:< A E >>` is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`;

.. subclause::

   :g:`$AF$ <0390.g1:< A F >>` is the rectangle contained by :g:`$BA$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0390.g1:/ A C />`, for it is contained by :g:`$DA$ <0390.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$AC$ <0390.g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`$AD$ <0390.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`;

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$CE$ <0390.g1:< C E >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0390.g1:/ B C />`, for :g:`$BE$ <0390.g1:/ B E />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0390.g1:/ A C />` together with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0390.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0390.g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
