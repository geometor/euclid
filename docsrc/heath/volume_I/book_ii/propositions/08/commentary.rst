Commentary on Proposition II.8
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition II.8 <II.8>`.

.. folio:: 0405
   :page: 391

This proposition is quoted by :person:`Pappus <pappus>` (p. 428, ed. :person:`Hultsch <hultsch>`) and is used also by :person:`Euclid <euclid>` himself in the *Data*, Prop. 86. Further, it is of decided use in proving the fundamental property of a parabola.

Two alternative proofs are worth giving.

The first is that suggested by the consideration mentioned in the last note, though the proof is old enough, being given by :person:`Clavius <clavius>` and others. It is of the semi-algebraical type.

Produce :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`D <0404.g1:D>` (in the figure of the proposition), so that :g:`BD <0404.g1:/ B D />` is :eq:`equal <0404.g1:/ B D / = 0404.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />`.

By II. 4, the square on :g:`AD <0404.g1:/ A D />` is equal to the squares on :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BD <0404.g1:/ B D />` and twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BD <0404.g1:/ B D />`, i.e. to the squares on :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />` and twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />`.

By II. 7, the squares on :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />` are equal to twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`AC <0404.g1:/ A C />`.

Therefore the square on :g:`AD <0404.g1:/ A D />` is equal to four times the rectangle :g:`AB <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0404.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`AC <0404.g1:/ A C />`.

The second proof is after the manner of :person:`Euclid <euclid>` but with a difference. Produce :g:`BA <0405.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`D <0405.g1:D>` so that :g:`AD <0405.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0405.g1:/ A D / = 0405.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`BC <0405.g1:/ B C />`. On :g:`BD <0405.g1:/ B D />` construct the square :g:`BEFD <0405.g1:< B E F D >>`.

Take :g:`BG <0405.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`EH <0405.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`FK <0405.g1:/ F K />` each :eq:`equal <0405.g1:/ B G / = 0405.g1:/ E H / = 0405.g1:/ F K / = 0405.g1:/ B C / = 0405.g1:/ A D />` to :g:`BC <0405.g1:/ B C />` or :g:`AD <0405.g1:/ A D />`, and draw :g:`ALP <0405.g1:/ A L P />`, :g:`HNM <0405.g1:/ H N M />` parallel to :g:`BE <0405.g1:/ B E />` and :g:`GML <0405.g1:/ G M L />`, :g:`KPN <0405.g1:/ K P N />` parallel to :g:`BD <0405.g1:/ B D />`.

Then it can be shown that each of the rectangles :g:`BL <0405.g1:< B L >>`, :g:`AK <0405.g1:< A K >>`, :g:`FN <0405.g1:< F N >>`, :g:`EM <0405.g1:< E M >>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`AB <0405.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0405.g1:/ B C />`, and that :g:`PM <0405.g1:< P M >>` is equal to the square on :g:`AC <0405.g1:/ A C />`.

Therefore the square on :g:`BD <0405.g1:/ B D />` is equal to four times the rectangle :g:`AB <0405.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0405.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`AC <0405.g1:/ A C />`.
