DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _III.def.1:

1. :term:`**Equal circles** <equal-circles>` are those the :term:`diameters <diameter>` of which are equal, or the radii of which are equal.

.. _III.def.2:

2. A :term:`straight line <straight-line>` is said to :term:`**touch a circle** <tangent-to-circle>` which, meeting the :term:`circle <circle>` and being produced, does not cut the circle.

.. _III.def.3:

3. **Circles** are said to :term:`**touch one another** <circles-touching>` which, meeting one another, do not cut one another.

.. _III.def.4:

4. In a :term:`circle <circle>` :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` are said to be :term:`**equally distant from the centre** <equally-distant-from-centre>` when the :term:`perpendiculars <perpendicular>` drawn to them from the :term:`centre <centre>` are equal.

.. _III.def.5:

5. And that :term:`straight line <straight-line>` is said to be :term:`**at a greater distance** <greater-distance-from-centre>` on which the greater :term:`perpendicular <perpendicular>` falls.

.. _III.def.6:

6. A :term:`**segment of a circle** <segment-of-circle>` is the :term:`figure <figure>` contained by a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` and a circumference of a :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _III.def.7:

7. An :term:`**angle of a segment** <angle-of-segment>` is that contained by a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` and a circumference of a :term:`circle <circle>`.

.. _III.def.8:

8. An :term:`**angle in a segment** <angle-in-segment>` is the :term:`angle <plane-angle>` which, when a :term:`point <point>` is taken on the circumference of the segment and :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` are joined from it to the :term:`extremities <extremity>` of the straight line which is the :term:`**base of the segment** <base-of-segment>`, is contained by the straight lines so joined.

.. _III.def.9:

9. And, when the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` containing the :term:`angle <plane-angle>` cut off a circumference, the angle is said to :term:`**stand upon** <stand-upon-circumference>` that circumference.

.. _folio-ii-0008:

.. folio:: 0008
   :page: 2

.. _III.def.10:

10. A :term:`**sector of a circle** <sector-of-circle>` is the :term:`figure <figure>` which, when an :term:`angle <plane-angle>` is constructed at the :term:`centre <centre>` of the :term:`circle <circle>`, is contained by the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` containing the angle and the circumference cut off by them.

.. _III.def.11:

11. :term:`**Similar segments of circles** <similar-segments-of-circles>` are those which admit equal :term:`angles <plane-angle>`, or in which the angles are equal to one another.
