Commentary on Proposition III.14
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition III.14 <III.14>`.

.. folio:: 0042
   :page: 36

:person:`Heron <heron>` (:person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>`, pp. 125—7) has an elaborate addition to this proposition in which he proves, first by :term:`*reductio ad absurdum* <reductio-ad-absurdum>`, and then directly, that the centre of the circle falls between the two chords.
