PROPOSITION 19.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line touch a circle, and from the point of contact a straight line be drawn at right angles to the tangent, the centre of the circle will be on the straight line so drawn.

For let a straight line :g:`$DE$ <0051.g1:/ D E />` touch the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0051.g1:( A B C )>` at the
point :g:`$C$ <0051.g1:C>`, and from :g:`$C$ <0051.g1:C>` let :g:`$CA$ <0051.g1:/ C A />` be
drawn at right angles to :g:`$DE$ <0051.g1:/ D E />`;
I say that the centre of the circle
is on :g:`$AC$ <0051.g1:/ A C />`.

.. picture:: 0051.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram for Proposition 19

For suppose it is not, but, if
possible, let :g:`$F$ <0051.g1:F>` be the centre,
and let :g:`$CF$ <0051.g1:/ C F />` be joined.

Since a straight line :g:`$DE$ <0051.g1:/ D E />` touches
the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0051.g1:( A B C )>`,
and :g:`$FC$ <0051.g1:/ F C />` has been joined from the
centre to the point of contact,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$FC$ <0051.g1:/ F C />` is perpendicular to :g:`$DE$ <0051.g1:/ D E />`; :just:`[III. 18] <III.18>` therefore the angle :g:`$FCE$ <0051.g1:< F C E )>` is right.

But the angle :g:`$ACE$ <0051.g1:< A C E )>` is also right;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$FCE$ <0051.g1:< F C E )>` is :eq:`equal <0051.g1:< F C E ) = 0051.g1:< A C E )>` to the angle :g:`$ACE$ <0051.g1:< A C E )>`, the less to the greater: which is impossible.

Therefore :g:`$F$ <0051.g1:F>` is not the centre of the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0051.g1:( A B C )>`.

Similarly we can prove that neither is any other point
except a point on :g:`$AC$ <0051.g1:/ A C />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
