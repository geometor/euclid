PROPOSITION 27.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In equal circles angles standing on equal circumferences are equal to one another, whether they stand at the centres or at the circumferences.

For in :eq:`equal <0064.g1:( A B C ) = 0064.g1:( D E F )>` circles :g:`$ABC$ <0064.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0064.g1:( D E F )>`, on :eq:`equal <0064.g1:( B C ) = 0064.g1:( E F )>` circumferences
:g:`$BC$ <0064.g1:( B C )>`, :g:`$EF$ <0064.g1:( E F )>`, let the angles :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>`, :g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>` stand at the centres :g:`$G$ <0064.g1:G>`,
:g:`$H$ <0064.g1:H>`, and the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0064.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0064.g1:< E D F )>` at the circumferences;
I say that the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>` is :eq:`equal <0064.g1:< B G C ) = 0064.g1:< E H F )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>`,
and the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0064.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0064.g1:< B A C ) = 0064.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0064.g1:< E D F )>`.

.. picture:: 0064.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For, if the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>` is unequal to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>`,

.. assertion::

   one of them is greater.

Let the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>` be greater: and on the straight line :g:`$BG$ <0064.g1:/ B G />`,
and at the point :g:`$G$ <0064.g1:G>` on it, let the angle :g:`$BGK$ <0064.g1:< B G K )>` be constructed
:eq:`equal <0064.g1:< B G K ) = 0064.g1:< E H F )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>`. :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

Now equal angles stand on equal circumferences, when
they are at the centres; :just:`[III. 26] <III.26>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0064.g1:( B K )>` is :eq:`equal <0064.g1:( B K ) = 0064.g1:( E F )>` to the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0064.g1:( E F )>`.

But :g:`$EF$ <0064.g1:( E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0064.g1:( E F ) = 0064.g1:( B C )>` to :g:`$BC$ <0064.g1:( B C )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BK$ <0064.g1:( B K )>` is also :eq:`equal <0064.g1:( B K ) = 0064.g1:( B C )>` to :g:`$BC$ <0064.g1:( B C )>`, the less to the greater: which is impossible.

Therefore the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>` is not unequal to the angle
:g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it is :eq:`equal <0064.g1:< B G C ) = 0064.g1:< E H F )>` to it.

.. _folio-ii-0065:

.. folio:: 0065
   :page: 59

And the angle at :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is half of the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0064.g1:< B G C )>`,
and the angle at :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` half of the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0064.g1:< E H F )>`; :just:`[III. 20] <III.20>`
therefore the angle at :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is also :eq:`equal <0064.g1:< B A C ) = 0064.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
