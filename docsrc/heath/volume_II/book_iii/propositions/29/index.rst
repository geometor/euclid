PROPOSITION 29.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In equal circles equal circumferences are subtended by equal straight lines.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0066.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0066.g1:( D E F )>` be :eq:`equal <0066.g1:( A B C ) = 0066.g1:( D E F )>` circles, and in them let :eq:`equal <0066.g1:( B G C ) = 0066.g1:( E H F )>`
circumferences :g:`$BGC$ <0066.g1:( B G C )>`, :g:`$EHF$ <0066.g1:( E H F )>` be cut off; and let the straight
lines :g:`$BC$ <0066.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0066.g1:/ E F />` be joined;
I say that :g:`$BC$ <0066.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0066.g1:/ B C / = 0066.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$EF$ <0066.g1:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0066.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For let the centres of the circles be taken, and let them
be :g:`$K$ <0066.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0066.g1:L>`; let :g:`$BK$ <0066.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$KC$ <0066.g1:/ K C />`, :g:`$EL$ <0066.g1:/ E L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0066.g1:/ L F />` be joined.

Now, since the circumference :g:`$BGC$ <0066.g1:( B G C )>` is :eq:`equal <0066.g1:( B G C ) = 0066.g1:( E H F )>` to the
circumference :g:`$EHF$ <0066.g1:( E H F )>`,

.. assertion:: [III. 27] <III.27>

   the angle :g:`$BKC$ <0066.g1:< B K C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0066.g1:< B K C ) = 0066.g1:< E L F )>` to the angle :g:`$ELF$ <0066.g1:< E L F )>`.

And, since the circles :g:`$ABC$ <0066.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0066.g1:( D E F )>` are :eq:`equal <0066.g1:( A B C ) = 0066.g1:( D E F )>`,

.. assertion::

   the radii are also :eq:`equal <0066.g1:/ B K / = 0066.g1:/ K C / = 0066.g1:/ E L / = 0066.g1:/ L F />`;

therefore the two sides :g:`$BK$ <0066.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$KC$ <0066.g1:/ K C />` are :eq:`equal <0066.g1:/ B K / = 0066.g1:/ E L /; 0066.g1:/ K C / = 0066.g1:/ L F />` to the two sides
:g:`$EL$ <0066.g1:/ E L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0066.g1:/ L F />`; and they contain :eq:`equal <0066.g1:< B K C ) = 0066.g1:< E L F )>` angles;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$BC$ <0066.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0066.g1:/ B C / = 0066.g1:/ E F />` to the base :g:`$EF$ <0066.g1:/ E F />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
