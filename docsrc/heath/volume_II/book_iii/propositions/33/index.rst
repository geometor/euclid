PROPOSITION 33.
===============

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line to describe a segment of a circle admitting an angle equal to a given rectilineal angle.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, and the angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>` the
given rectilineal angle;
thus it is required to describe
on the given straight line
:g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />` a segment of a circle admitting
an angle equal to the
angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>`.

.. picture:: 0073.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 first figure: acute angle case

The angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>` is then
acute, or right, or obtuse.

First let it be acute,
and, as in the first figure, on
the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, let the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0073.g1:< B A D )>`
be constructed :eq:`equal <0073.g1:< B A D ) = 0073.g1:< C )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0073.g1:< B A D )>` is also acute.

Let :g:`$AE$ <0073.g1:/ A E />` be drawn at right angles to :g:`$DA$ <0073.g1:/ D A />`, let :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />` be
bisected at :g:`$F$ <0073.g1:F>`, let :g:`$FG$ <0073.g1:/ F G />` be drawn from the point :g:`$F$ <0073.g1:F>` at right
angles to :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />`, and let :g:`$GB$ <0073.g1:/ G B />` be joined.

.. _folio-ii-0074:

.. folio:: 0074
   :page: 68

Then, since :g:`$AF$ <0073.g1:/ A F />` is :eq:`equal <0073.g1:/ A F / = 0073.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$FB$ <0073.g1:/ F B />`,
and :g:`$FG$ <0073.g1:/ F G />` is common,

.. subclause::

   the two sides :g:`$AF$ <0073.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0073.g1:/ F G />` are :eq:`equal <0073.g1:/ A F / = 0073.g1:/ B F /; 0073.g1:/ F G / = 0073.g1:/ F G />` to the two sides :g:`$BF$ <0073.g1:/ B F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0073.g1:/ F G />`;

and the angle :g:`$AFG$ <0073.g1:< A F G )>` is :eq:`equal <0073.g1:< A F G ) = 0073.g1:< B F G )>` to the angle :g:`$BFG$ <0073.g1:< B F G )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$AG$ <0073.g1:/ A G />` is :eq:`equal <0073.g1:/ A G / = 0073.g1:/ B G />` to the base :g:`$BG$ <0073.g1:/ B G />`.

Therefore the circle described with centre :g:`$G$ <0073.g1:G>` and distance
:g:`$GA$ <0073.g1:/ G A />` will pass through :g:`$B$ <0073.g1:B>` also.

Let it be drawn, and let it be :g:`$ABE$ <0073.g1:( A B E )>`;
let :g:`$EB$ <0073.g1:/ E B />` be joined.

Now, since :g:`$AD$ <0073.g1:/ A D />` is drawn from :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, the extremity of the
diameter :g:`$AE$ <0073.g1:/ A E />`, at right angles to :g:`$AE$ <0073.g1:/ A E />`,

.. assertion:: [III. 16, Por.]

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0073.g1:/ A D />` touches the circle :g:`$ABE$ <0073.g1:( A B E )>`.

Since then a straight line :g:`$AD$ <0073.g1:/ A D />` touches the circle :g:`$ABE$ <0073.g1:( A B E )>`,
and from the point of contact at :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>` a straight line :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />` is
drawn across in the circle :g:`$ABE$ <0073.g1:( A B E )>`,
the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0073.g1:< D A B )>` is :eq:`equal <0073.g1:< D A B ) = 0073.g1:< A E B )>` to the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0073.g1:< A E B )>` in the alternate
segment of the circle. :just:`[III. 32] <III.32>`

But the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0073.g1:< D A B )>` is :eq:`equal <0073.g1:< D A B ) = 0073.g1:< C )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>`;
therefore the angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0073.g1:< C ) = 0073.g1:< A E B )>` to the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0073.g1:< A E B )>`.

Therefore on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0073.g1:/ A B />` the segment
:g:`$AEB$ <0073.g1:( A E B )>` of a circle has been described admitting the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0073.g1:< A E B )>`
:eq:`equal <0073.g1:< A E B ) = 0073.g1:< C )>` to the given angle, the angle at :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:< C )>`.

Next let the angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>` be right;

.. picture:: 0074.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 second figure: right angle case

and let it be again required to describe on :g:`$AB$ <0074.g1:/ A B />` a segment
of a circle admitting an angle equal to the right angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>`.

Let the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0074.g1:< B A D )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0074.g1:< B A D ) = 0074.g1:< C )>` to the right
angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>`, as is the case in the second figure;

.. _folio-ii-0075:

.. folio:: 0075
   :page: 69

let :g:`$AB$ <0074.g1:/ A B />` be bisected at :g:`$F$ <0074.g1:F>`, and with centre :g:`$F$ <0074.g1:F>` and distance
either :g:`$FA$ <0074.g1:/ F A />` or :g:`$FB$ <0074.g1:/ F B />` let the circle :g:`$AEB$ <0074.g1:( A E B )>` be described.

Therefore the straight line :g:`$AD$ <0074.g1:/ A D />` touches the circle :g:`$ABE$ <0074.g1:( A B E )>`,
because the angle at :g:`$A$ <0074.g1:< A )>` is right. :just:`[III. 16, Por.]`

And the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0074.g1:< B A D )>` is equal to the angle in the segment
:g:`$AEB$ <0074.g1:( A E B )>`, for the latter too is itself a right angle, being an
angle in a semicircle. :just:`[III. 31] <III.31>`

But the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0074.g1:< B A D )>` is also :eq:`equal <0074.g1:< B A D ) = 0074.g1:< C )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0074.g1:< A E B )>` is also :eq:`equal <0074.g1:< A E B ) = 0074.g1:< C )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>`.

Therefore again the segment :g:`$AEB$ <0074.g1:( A E B )>` of a circle has been
described on :g:`$AB$ <0074.g1:/ A B />` admitting an angle equal to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0074.g1:< C )>`.

Next, let the angle at :g:`$C$ <0075.g1:< C )>` be obtuse;

.. picture:: 0075.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 third figure: obtuse angle case

and on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0075.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0075.g1:A>`, let the
angle :g:`$BAD$ <0075.g1:< B A D )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0075.g1:< B A D ) = 0075.g1:< C )>` to it, as is the case in the
third figure;
let :g:`$AE$ <0075.g1:/ A E />` be drawn at right angles to :g:`$AD$ <0075.g1:/ A D />`, let :g:`$AB$ <0075.g1:/ A B />` be again
bisected at :g:`$F$ <0075.g1:F>`, let :g:`$FG$ <0075.g1:/ F G />` be drawn at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0075.g1:/ A B />`, and
let :g:`$GB$ <0075.g1:/ G B />` be joined.

Then, since :g:`$AF$ <0075.g1:/ A F />` is again :eq:`equal <0075.g1:/ A F / = 0075.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$FB$ <0075.g1:/ F B />`,
and :g:`$FG$ <0075.g1:/ F G />` is common,

.. subclause::

   the two sides :g:`$AF$ <0075.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0075.g1:/ F G />` are :eq:`equal <0075.g1:/ A F / = 0075.g1:/ B F /; 0075.g1:/ F G / = 0075.g1:/ F G />` to the two sides :g:`$BF$ <0075.g1:/ B F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0075.g1:/ F G />`;

and the angle :g:`$AFG$ <0075.g1:< A F G )>` is :eq:`equal <0075.g1:< A F G ) = 0075.g1:< B F G )>` to the angle :g:`$BFG$ <0075.g1:< B F G )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$AG$ <0075.g1:/ A G />` is :eq:`equal <0075.g1:/ A G / = 0075.g1:/ B G />` to the base :g:`$BG$ <0075.g1:/ B G />`.

Therefore the circle described with centre :g:`$G$ <0075.g1:G>` and distance
:g:`$GA$ <0075.g1:/ G A />` will pass through :g:`$B$ <0075.g1:B>` also; let it so pass, as :g:`$AEB$ <0075.g1:( A E B )>`.

Now, since :g:`$AD$ <0075.g1:/ A D />` is drawn at right angles to the diameter
:g:`$AE$ <0075.g1:/ A E />` from its extremity,

.. assertion:: [III. 16, Por.]

   :g:`$AD$ <0075.g1:/ A D />` touches the circle :g:`$AEB$ <0075.g1:( A E B )>`.

And :g:`$AB$ <0075.g1:/ A B />` has been drawn across from the point of contact
at :g:`$A$ <0075.g1:A>`;
therefore the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0075.g1:< B A D )>` is equal to the angle constructed
in the alternate segment :g:`$AHB$ <0075.g1:( A H B )>` of the circle. :just:`[III. 32] <III.32>`
But the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0075.g1:< B A D )>` is :eq:`equal <0075.g1:< B A D ) = 0075.g1:< C )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0075.g1:< C )>`.

.. _folio-ii-0076:

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: 70

Therefore the angle in the segment :g:`$AHB$ <0075.g1:( A H B )>` is also equal to
the angle at :g:`$C$ <0075.g1:< C )>`.

Therefore on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0075.g1:/ A B />` the segment
:g:`$AHB$ <0075.g1:( A H B )>` of a circle has been described admitting an angle equal
to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0075.g1:< C )>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. F.`
