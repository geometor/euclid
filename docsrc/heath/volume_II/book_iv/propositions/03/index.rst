PROPOSITION 3.
==============

.. enunciation::

   About a given circle to circumscribe a triangle equiangular with a given triangle.

Let :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>` be the given circle, and :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F >>` the given
triangle;
thus it is required to circumscribe about the circle :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>` a
triangle equiangular with the triangle :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. picture:: 0089.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram for Proposition 3

Let :g:`EF <0089.g1:/ E F />` be produced in both directions to the points
:g:`G <0089.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0089.g1:H>`,
let the centre :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` of the circle :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>` be taken :just:`[III. 1] <III.1>`, and let
the straight line :g:`KB <0089.g1:/ K B />` be drawn across at random;\ [#f1]_
on the straight line :g:`KB <0089.g1:/ K B />`, and at the point :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` on it, let the
angle :g:`BKA <0089.g1:< B K A )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< B K A ) = 0089.g1:< D E G )>` to the angle :g:`DEG <0089.g1:< D E G )>`,
and the angle :g:`BKC <0089.g1:< B K C )>` :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< B K C ) = 0089.g1:< D F H )>` to the angle :g:`DFH <0089.g1:< D F H )>`; :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`
and through the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>` let :g:`LAM <0089.g1:/ L A M />`, :g:`MBN <0089.g1:/ M B N />`, :g:`NCL <0089.g1:/ N C L />` be
drawn touching the circle :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>`. :just:`[III. 16, Por.]`

Now, since :g:`LM <0089.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`MN <0089.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`NL <0089.g1:/ N L />` touch the circle :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>` at the
points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>`,
and :g:`KA <0089.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`KB <0089.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`KC <0089.g1:/ K C />` have been joined from the centre :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` to
the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>`,
therefore the angles at the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>` are right. :just:`[III. 18] <III.18>`

.. _folio-ii-0090:

.. folio:: 0090
   :page: 84

And, since the four angles of the quadrilateral :g:`AMBK <0089.g1:< A M B K >>`
are equal to four right angles, inasmuch as :g:`AMBK <0089.g1:< A M B K >>` is in fact
divisible\ [#f2]_ into two triangles,

.. subclause::

   and the angles :g:`KAM <0089.g1:< K A M )>`, :g:`KBM <0089.g1:< K B M )>` are right,

therefore the remaining angles :g:`AKB <0089.g1:< A K B )>`, :g:`AMB <0089.g1:< A M B )>` are equal to two
right angles.

But the angles :g:`DEG <0089.g1:< D E G )>`, :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F )>` are also equal to two right
angles; :just:`[I. 13] <I.13>`
therefore the angles :g:`AKB <0089.g1:< A K B )>`, :g:`AMB <0089.g1:< A M B )>` are equal to the angles
:g:`DEG <0089.g1:< D E G )>`, :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F )>`,
of which the angle :g:`AKB <0089.g1:< A K B )>` is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< A K B ) = 0089.g1:< D E G )>` to the angle :g:`DEG <0089.g1:< D E G )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`AMB <0089.g1:< A M B )>` which remains is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< A M B ) = 0089.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F )>` which remains.

Similarly it can be proved that the angle :g:`LNB <0089.g1:< L N B )>` is also
:eq:`equal <0089.g1:< L N B ) = 0089.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`DFE <0089.g1:< D F E )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`MLN <0089.g1:< M L N )>` is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< M L N ) = 0089.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`EDF <0089.g1:< E D F )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

Therefore the triangle :g:`LMN <0089.g1:< L M N >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0089.g1:< A M B ) = 0089.g1:< D E F ); 0089.g1:< L N B ) = 0089.g1:< D F E ); 0089.g1:< M L N ) = 0089.g1:< E D F )>` with the
triangle :g:`DEF <0089.g1:< D E F >>`; and it has been circumscribed about the
circle :g:`ABC <0089.g1:( A B C )>`.

Therefore about a given circle there has been circumscribed
a triangle equiangular with the given triangle.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. F.`

.. [#f1] **at random**, literally "as it may chance," ὡς ἔτυχεν. The same expression is used in III. 1 and commonly.

.. [#f2] **is in fact divisible**, καὶ διαιρεῖται, literally "is actually divided."
