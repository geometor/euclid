PROPOSITION 1.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two similar plane numbers by multiplying one another make some number, the product will be square.

Let :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>` be two similar plane numbers, and let :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>` make :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>`;

I say that :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>` is square.

.. picture:: 0390.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 1 Diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>` is square.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>` are similar plane numbers,

.. assertion::

   therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$A$ <0390.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0390.g1:B>`. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

.. subclause::

   But, if numbers fall between two numbers in continued proportion, as many as fall between them, so many also fall between those which have the same ratio; :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. assertion::

   so that one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>` also.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$D$ <0390.g1:D>` is square;

.. assertion:: [VIII. 22] <VIII.22>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0390.g1:C>` is also square.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
