Commentary on Proposition IX.2
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition IX.2 <IX.2>`.

.. folio:: 0391
   :page: 385

If :math:`ab` is a square number, :math:`a`, :math:`b` are similar plane numbers. (The converse of IX. 1.) For

.. math::

   a : b = a^2 : ab. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}

And :math:`a^2`, :math:`ab` being square numbers, and therefore similar plane numbers, they have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

Therefore :math:`a`, :math:`b` also have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

whence :math:`a`, :math:`b` are similar plane numbers. :just:`[VIII. 20] <VIII.20>`
