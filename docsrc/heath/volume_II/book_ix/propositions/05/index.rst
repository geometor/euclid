PROPOSITION 5.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a cube number by multiplying any number make a cube number, the multiplied number will also be cube.

For let the cube number :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` by multiplying any number :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` make the cube number :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`;

I say that :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` is cube.

.. picture:: 0393.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 5 Diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>`;

.. assertion:: [IX. 3] <IX.3>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>` is cube.

.. _folio-ii-0394:

.. folio:: 0394
   :page: 388

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`.

.. subclause::

   And since :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>` are cube,

.. assertion::

   they are similar solid numbers.

.. assertion::

   Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>` is to :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` also. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` is cube;

.. assertion:: [VIII. 23] <VIII.23>

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` is also cube.
