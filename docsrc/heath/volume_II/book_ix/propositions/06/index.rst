PROPOSITION 6.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a number by multiplying itself make a cube number, it will itself also be cube.

For let the number :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself make the cube number :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` is also cube.

.. picture:: 0394.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 6 Diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` make :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>` is cube.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` according to the units in itself.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` according to the units in it.

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   Therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` measures :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>` according to the units in :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` according to the units in it.

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   Therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>` are cube,

.. assertion::

   they are similar solid numbers.

.. assertion::

   Therefore there are two mean proportional numbers between :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` is to :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore there are two mean proportional numbers between :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` also. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` is cube;

.. assertion:: [cf. VIII. 23]

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` is also cube.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
