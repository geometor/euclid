PROPOSITION 16.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two :term:`numbers <number>` be :term:`prime to one another <prime-to-one-another>`, the second will not be to any other number as the first is to the second.

For let the two numbers :g:`A <0412.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` be prime to one another ;

I say that :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` is not to any other number as :g:`A <0412.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0412.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0412.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 16

For, if possible, as :g:`A <0412.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0412.g1:B>`, so let :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` be to :g:`C <0412.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`A <0412.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` are :term:`prime <prime-number>`, primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>` and the least numbers :term:`measure <part-aliquot>` those which have the :term:`same ratio <in-the-same-ratio>` the same number of times, the :term:`antecedent <corresponding-magnitudes>` the antecedent and the consequent the consequent ; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`A <0412.g1:A>` measures :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` as antecedent antecedent.

.. subclause::

   But it also measures itself ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`A <0412.g1:A>` measures :g:`A <0412.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` which are prime to one another : which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`B <0412.g1:B>` will not be to :g:`C <0412.g1:C>`, as :g:`A <0412.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0412.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
