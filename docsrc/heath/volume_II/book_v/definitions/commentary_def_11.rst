Commentary on Book V Definition 11
==================================

**Ὁμόλογα μεγέθη λέγεται τὰ μὲν ἡγούμενα τοῖς ἡγουμένοις τὰ δὲ ἑπόμενα τοῖς ἑπομένοις.**

It is difficult to express the meaning of the Greek in as few words. A translation more literal, but conveying less, would be, "Antecedents are called *corresponding magnitudes* to antecedents, and consequents to consequents."

I have preferred to translate :term:`ὁμόλογος <corresponding-magnitudes>` by "corresponding" rather than by "homologous." I do not agree with :person:`Max Simon <simon>` when he says (Euclid, p. 111) that the technical term "homologous" is not the adjective :term:`ὁμόλογος <corresponding-magnitudes>`, and does not mean "corresponding," "agreeing," but "like in respect of the proportion" ("ähnlich in Bezug auf das Verhältniss"). The definition seems to me to be for the purpose of appropriating to a technical use precisely the ordinary adjective :term:`ὁμόλογος <corresponding-magnitudes>`, "agreeing" or "corresponding."

*Antecedents*, :term:`ἡγούμενα <hegoumenon>`, are literally "*leading* (terms)," and *consequents*, :term:`ἑπόμενα <hepomenon>`, "*following* (terms)."
