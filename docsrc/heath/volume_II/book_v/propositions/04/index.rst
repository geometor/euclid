PROPOSITION 4.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third to a fourth, any equimultiples whatever of the first and third will also have the same ratio to any equimultiples whatever of the second and fourth respectively, taken in corresponding order.

For let a first magnitude :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>` have to a second :g:`$B$ <0148.g1:B>` the same ratio as a third :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>` to a fourth :g:`$D$ <0148.g1:D>`; and let equimultiples :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>` be taken of :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>`, and :g:`$G$ <0148.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0148.g1:H>` other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$B$ <0148.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0148.g1:D>`;

I say that, as :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>` is to :g:`$G$ <0148.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>` to :g:`$H$ <0148.g1:H>`.

.. picture:: 0148.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4 diagram

For let equimultiples :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` be taken of :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>`, and other, chance, equimultiples :g:`$M$ <0148.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0148.g1:N>` of :g:`$G$ <0148.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0148.g1:H>`.

Since :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>` is the same multiple of :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>` that :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>` is of :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>`, and equimultiples :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` of :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>` have been taken,

.. assertion:: [V. 3] <V.3>

   therefore :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>` is the same multiple of :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>` that :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` is of :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$M$ <0148.g1:M>` is the same multiple of :g:`$B$ <0148.g1:B>` that :g:`$N$ <0148.g1:N>` is of :g:`$D$ <0148.g1:D>`.

.. _folio-ii-0149:

.. folio:: 0149
   :page: 143

And, since, as :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0148.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0148.g1:D>`, and of :g:`$A$ <0148.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>` equimultiples :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` have been taken, and of :g:`$B$ <0148.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0148.g1:D>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`$M$ <0148.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0148.g1:N>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>` is in excess of :g:`$M$ <0148.g1:M>`, :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` also is in excess of :g:`$N$ <0148.g1:N>`, if it is equal, equal, and if less, less. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`

And :g:`$K$ <0148.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0148.g1:L>` are equimultiples of :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>`, and :g:`$M$ <0148.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0148.g1:N>` other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$G$ <0148.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0148.g1:H>`;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>

   therefore, as :g:`$E$ <0148.g1:E>` is to :g:`$G$ <0148.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0148.g1:F>` to :g:`$H$ <0148.g1:H>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
