PROPOSITION 13.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third to a fourth, and the third have to the fourth a greater ratio than a fifth has to a sixth, the first will also have to the second a greater ratio than the fifth to the sixth.

For let a first magnitude :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>` have to a second :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>` the same ratio as a third :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>` has to a fourth :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`, and let the third :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>` have to the fourth :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>` a greater ratio than a fifth :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>` has to a sixth :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`;

I say that the first :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>` will also have to the second :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>` a greater ratio than the fifth :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>` to the sixth :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0166.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13 diagram

For, since there are some equimultiples of :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>`, and of :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples, such that the multiple of :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>` is in excess of the multiple of :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`,
while the multiple of :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>` is not in excess of the multiple of :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`, :just:`[V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>`

.. folio:: 0167
   :page: 161

let them be taken, and let :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0166.g1:H>` be equimultiples of :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>`, and :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0166.g1:L>` other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`, so that :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>`, but :g:`$H$ <0166.g1:H>` is not in excess of :g:`$L$ <0166.g1:L>`; and, whatever multiple :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>` is of :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>`, let :g:`$M$ <0166.g1:M>` be also that multiple of :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>`, and, whatever multiple :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>` is of :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`, let :g:`$N$ <0166.g1:N>` be also that multiple of :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>`.

Now, since, as :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>`, and of :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0166.g1:C>` equimultiples :g:`$M$ <0166.g1:M>`, :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>` have been taken, and of :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0166.g1:D>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`$N$ <0166.g1:N>`, :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`$M$ <0166.g1:M>` is in excess of :g:`$N$ <0166.g1:N>`, :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>` is also in excess of :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>`, if :eq:`equal <0166.g1:M = 0166.g1:N>`, :eq:`equal <0166.g1:G = 0166.g1:K>`, and if less, less. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$G$ <0166.g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`$K$ <0166.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$M$ <0166.g1:M>` is also in excess of :g:`$N$ <0166.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$H$ <0166.g1:H>` is not in excess of :g:`$L$ <0166.g1:L>`;

and :g:`$M$ <0166.g1:M>`, :g:`$H$ <0166.g1:H>` are equimultiples of :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>`, and :g:`$N$ <0166.g1:N>`, :g:`$L$ <0166.g1:L>` other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0166.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0166.g1:B>` a greater ratio than :g:`$E$ <0166.g1:E>` has to :g:`$F$ <0166.g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
