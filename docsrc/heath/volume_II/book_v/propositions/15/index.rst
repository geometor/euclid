PROPOSITION 15.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Parts have the same ratio as the same multiples of them taken in corresponding order.

For let :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` be the same multiple of :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>` that :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />` is of :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>` ;

I say that, as :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />`.

.. picture:: 0169.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 15 diagram

For, since :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` is the same multiple of :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>` that :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />` is of :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>`, as many magnitudes as there are in :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` equal to :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>`, so many are there also in :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />` equal to :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>`.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` be divided into the magnitudes :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0169.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HB$ <0169.g1:/ H B />` :eq:`equal <0169.g1:/ A G / = 0169.g1:/ G H / = 0169.g1:/ H B / = 0169.g1:C>` to :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>`, and :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />` into the magnitudes :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0169.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0169.g1:/ L E />` :eq:`equal <0169.g1:/ D K / = 0169.g1:/ K L / = 0169.g1:/ L E / = 0169.g1:F>` to :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>` ; then the multitude of the magnitudes :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0169.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HB$ <0169.g1:/ H B />` will be equal to the multitude of the magnitudes :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0169.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0169.g1:/ L E />`.

And, since :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0169.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HB$ <0169.g1:/ H B />` are :eq:`equal <0169.g1:/ A G / = 0169.g1:/ G H / = 0169.g1:/ H B />` to one another, and :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0169.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0169.g1:/ L E />` are also :eq:`equal <0169.g1:/ D K / = 0169.g1:/ K L / = 0169.g1:/ L E />` to one another,

.. assertion:: [V. 7] <V.7>

   therefore, as :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />` is to :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />`, so is :g:`$GH$ <0169.g1:/ G H />` to :g:`$KL$ <0169.g1:/ K L />`, and :g:`$HB$ <0169.g1:/ H B />` to :g:`$LE$ <0169.g1:/ L E />`.

Therefore, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the consequents ; :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />` is to :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />`.

.. _folio-ii-0170:

.. folio:: 0170
   :page: 164

But :g:`$AG$ <0169.g1:/ A G />` is :eq:`equal <0169.g1:/ A G / = 0169.g1:C; 0169.g1:/ D K / = 0169.g1:F>` to :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>` and :g:`$DK$ <0169.g1:/ D K />` to :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0169.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0169.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0169.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$DE$ <0169.g1:/ D E />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
