PROPOSITION 17.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If magnitudes be proportional componendo, they will also be proportional separando.

Let $AB$, :g:`$BE$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0173.g1:/ F D />` be magnitudes proportional *componendo*, so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`;

I say that they will also be proportional :term:`*separando* <separando>`, that is, as :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$DF$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`.

For of :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />` let equimultiples :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0173.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0173.g1:/ M N />` be taken, and of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />` other, chance, equimultiples, :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />`.

.. _folio-ii-0173:

.. folio:: 0173
   :page: 167

Then, since :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$HK$ <0173.g1:/ H K />` is of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 1] <V.1>

   therefore :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` is of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`.

But :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />` that :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is of :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` that :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is of :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />`.

.. picture:: 0173.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 17 diagram of line segments

Again, since :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$MN$ <0173.g1:/ M N />` is of :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 1] <V.1>

   therefore :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />`.

But :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` was the same multiple of :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />` that :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` is of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` that :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` are equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />`.

Again, since :g:`$HK$ <0173.g1:/ H K />` is the same multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />` that :g:`$MN$ <0173.g1:/ M N />` is of :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`, and :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />` is also the same multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />` that :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />` is of :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum :g:`$HO$ <0173.g1:/ H O />` is also the same multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />` that :g:`$MP$ <0173.g1:/ M P />` is of :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 2] <V.2>`

And, since, as :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`, and of :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0173.g1:/ C D />` equimultiples :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` have been taken, and of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />` equimultiples :g:`$HO$ <0173.g1:/ H O />`, :g:`$MP$ <0173.g1:/ M P />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` is in excess of :g:`$HO$ <0173.g1:/ H O />`, :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` is also in excess of :g:`$MP$ <0173.g1:/ M P />`, if equal, equal, and if less, less.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` be in excess of :g:`$HO$ <0173.g1:/ H O />`; then, if :g:`$HK$ <0173.g1:/ H K />` be subtracted from each,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` is also in excess of :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />`.

But we saw that, if :g:`$GK$ <0173.g1:/ G K />` was in excess of :g:`$HO$ <0173.g1:/ H O />`, :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` was also in excess of :g:`$MP$ <0173.g1:/ M P />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LN$ <0173.g1:/ L N />` is also in excess of :g:`$MP$ <0173.g1:/ M P />`,

.. _folio-ii-0174:

.. folio:: 0174
   :page: 168

.. subclause::

   and, if :g:`$MN$ <0173.g1:/ M N />` be subtracted from each,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is also in excess of :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />`;

so that, if :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` is in excess of :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` is also in excess of :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />`.

Similarly we can prove that, if :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />` be equal to :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` will also be equal to :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />`, and if less, less.

And :g:`$GH$ <0173.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$LM$ <0173.g1:/ L M />` are equimultiples of :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />`, while :g:`$KO$ <0173.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`$NP$ <0173.g1:/ N P />` are other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`:

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AE$ <0173.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0173.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0173.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0173.g1:/ F D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
