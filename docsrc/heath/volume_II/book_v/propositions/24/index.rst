PROPOSITION 24.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third has to a fourth, and also a fifth have to the second the same ratio as a sixth to the fourth, the first and fifth added together will have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth have to the fourth.

Let a first magnitude :g:`AB <0189.g2:/ A B />` have to a second :g:`C <0189.g2:C>` the same ratio as a third :g:`DE <0189.g2:/ D E />` has to a fourth :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`; and let also a fifth :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />` have to the second :g:`C <0189.g2:C>` the same ratio as a sixth :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />` has to the fourth :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`;

I say that the first and fifth added together, :g:`AG <0189.g2:/ A G />`, will have to the second :g:`C <0189.g2:C>` the same ratio as the third and sixth, :g:`DH <0189.g2:/ D H />`, has to the fourth :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`.

.. picture:: 0189.g2.svg
   :alt: Book V, Proposition 24, Line Segments

.. _folio-ii-0190:

.. folio:: 0190
   :page: 184

For since, as :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />` is to :g:`C <0189.g2:C>`, so is :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />` to :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`,

.. assertion::

   inversely, as :g:`C <0189.g2:C>` is to :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />`, so is :g:`F <0189.g2:F>` to :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, as :g:`AB <0189.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`C <0189.g2:C>`, so is :g:`DE <0189.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`, and, as :g:`C <0189.g2:C>` is to :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />`, so is :g:`F <0189.g2:F>` to :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`AB <0189.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />`, so is :g:`DE <0189.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />`.

And, since the magnitudes are proportional :term:`*separando* <separando>`, they will also be proportional :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`; :just:`[V. 18] <V.18>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`AG <0189.g2:/ A G />` is to :g:`GB <0189.g2:/ B G />`, so is :g:`DH <0189.g2:/ D H />` to :g:`HE <0189.g2:/ E H />`.

But also, as :g:`BG <0189.g2:/ B G />` is to :g:`C <0189.g2:C>`, so is :g:`EH <0189.g2:/ E H />` to :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`;

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`AG <0189.g2:/ A G />` is to :g:`C <0189.g2:C>`, so is :g:`DH <0189.g2:/ D H />` to :g:`F <0189.g2:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
