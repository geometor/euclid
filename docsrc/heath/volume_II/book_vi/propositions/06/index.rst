PROPOSITION 6.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two triangles have one angle equal to one angle and the sides about the equal angles proportional, the triangles will be equiangular and will have those angles equal which the corresponding sides subtend.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0211.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F >>` be two triangles having one angle :g:`$BAC$ <0211.g1:< B A C )>`
:eq:`equal <0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< E D F )>` to one angle :g:`$EDF$ <0211.g1:< E D F )>` and the sides about the equal angles
proportional, so that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$BA$ <0211.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0211.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`$ED$ <0211.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`;

I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0211.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< E D F ); 0211.g1:< A B C ) = 0211.g1:< D E F ); 0211.g1:< A C B ) = 0211.g1:< D F E )>` with the triangle
:g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F >>`, and will have the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0211.g1:< A B C )>` :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< A B C ) = 0211.g1:< D E F ); 0211.g1:< A C B ) = 0211.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F )>`,
and the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0211.g1:< A C B )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0211.g1:< D F E )>`.

For on the straight line :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`, and at the points :g:`$D$ <0211.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0211.g1:F>` on it,
let there be constructed the angle :g:`$FDG$ <0211.g1:< F D G )>` :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< F D G ) = 0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< E D F )>` to either of the
angles :g:`$BAC$ <0211.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0211.g1:< E D F )>`, and the angle :g:`$DFG$ <0211.g1:< D F G )>` :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< D F G ) = 0211.g1:< A C B )>` to the angle
:g:`$ACB$ <0211.g1:< A C B )>`; :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$B$ <0211.g1:< B )>` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< B ) = 0211.g1:< G )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$G$ <0211.g1:< G )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0211.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< F D G ); 0211.g1:< A C B ) = 0211.g1:< D F G ); 0211.g1:< B ) = 0211.g1:< G )>` with the triangle :g:`$DGF$ <0211.g1:< D G F >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$BA$ <0211.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0211.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`$GD$ <0211.g1:/ G D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

But, by hypothesis, as :g:`$BA$ <0211.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0211.g1:/ A C />`, so also is :g:`$ED$ <0211.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore also, as :g:`$ED$ <0211.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`, so is :g:`$GD$ <0211.g1:/ G D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`.

.. picture:: 0211.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 6 diagram

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   Therefore :g:`$ED$ <0211.g1:/ E D />` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:/ E D / = 0211.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`$DG$ <0211.g1:/ D G />`;

and :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />` is common;

.. subclause::

   therefore the two sides :g:`$ED$ <0211.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />` are :eq:`equal <0211.g1:/ E D / = 0211.g1:/ G D />; 0211.g1:/ D F / = 0211.g1:/ D F />` to the two sides :g:`$GD$ <0211.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0211.g1:/ D F />`; and the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0211.g1:< E D F )>` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< E D F ) = 0211.g1:< G D F )>` to the angle :g:`$GDF$ <0211.g1:< G D F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$EF$ <0211.g1:/ E F />` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:/ E F / = 0211.g1:/ G F />` to the base :g:`$GF$ <0211.g1:/ G F />`,

.. subclause::

   and the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F >>` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< D E F > = 0211.g1:< D G F >>` to the triangle :g:`$DGF$ <0211.g1:< D G F >>`, and the remaining angles will be :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< D F G ) = 0211.g1:< D F E ); 0211.g1:< D G F ) = 0211.g1:< D E F )>` to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend. :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$DFG$ <0211.g1:< D F G )>` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< D F G ) = 0211.g1:< D F E ); 0211.g1:< D G F ) = 0211.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0211.g1:< D F E )>`, and the angle :g:`$DGF$ <0211.g1:< D G F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F )>`.

But the angle :g:`$DFG$ <0211.g1:< D F G )>` is :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< D F G ) = 0211.g1:< A C B )>` to the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0211.g1:< A C B )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0211.g1:< A C B )>` is also :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< A C B ) = 0211.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0211.g1:< D F E )>`.

And, by hypothesis, the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0211.g1:< B A C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0211.g1:< E D F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$B$ <0211.g1:< B )>` is also :eq:`equal <0211.g1:< B ) = 0211.g1:< E )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$E$ <0211.g1:< E )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0211.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0211.g1:< B A C ) = 0211.g1:< E D F ); 0211.g1:< A C B ) = 0211.g1:< D F E ); 0211.g1:< B ) = 0211.g1:< E )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0211.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
