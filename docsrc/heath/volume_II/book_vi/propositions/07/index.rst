PROPOSITION 7.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two triangles have one angle equal to one angle, the sides about other angles proportional, and the remaining angles either both less or both not less than a right angle, the triangles will be equiangular and will have those angles equal, the sides about which are proportional.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F >>` be two triangles having one angle :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< B A C ) = 0212.g1:< E D F )>`
to one angle, the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0212.g1:< B A C )>` to
the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0212.g1:< E D F )>`, the sides about
other angles :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>` proportional
, so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0212.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0212.g1:/ B C />`,
so is :g:`$DE$ <0212.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0212.g1:/ E F />`, and, first, each
of the remaining angles at :g:`$C$ <0212.g1:< C )>`, :g:`$F$ <0212.g1:< F )>`
less than a right angle;
I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C >>` is
:eq:`equiangular <0212.g1:< B A C ) = 0212.g1:< E D F ); 0212.g1:< A B C ) = 0212.g1:< D E F ); 0212.g1:< C ) = 0212.g1:< F )>` with the triangle
:g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F >>`, the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C )>` will be
:eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A B C ) = 0212.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>`, and the remaining angle, namely
the angle at :g:`$C$ <0212.g1:< C )>`, :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< C ) = 0212.g1:< F )>` to the remaining angle, the angle
at :g:`$F$ <0212.g1:< F )>`.

.. picture:: 0212.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 7 diagram, Case 1

For, if the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C )>` is unequal to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>`, one
of them is greater.

Let the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C )>` be greater;
and on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0212.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$B$ <0212.g1:B>` on it, let the
angle :g:`$ABG$ <0212.g1:< A B G )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A B G ) = 0212.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>`. :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

Then, since the angle :g:`$A$ <0212.g1:< A )>` is :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A ) = 0212.g1:< D ); 0212.g1:< A B G ) = 0212.g1:< D E F )>` to :g:`$D$ <0212.g1:< D )>`,
and the angle :g:`$ABG$ <0212.g1:< A B G )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$AGB$ <0212.g1:< A G B )>` is :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A G B ) = 0212.g1:< D F E )>` to the remaining angle :g:`$DFE$ <0212.g1:< D F E )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABG$ <0212.g1:< A B G >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0212.g1:< A ) = 0212.g1:< D ); 0212.g1:< A B G ) = 0212.g1:< D E F ); 0212.g1:< A G B ) = 0212.g1:< D F E )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. assertion:: [VI. 4] <VI.4>

   Therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0212.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BG$ <0212.g1:/ B G />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0212.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0212.g1:/ E F />`.

But, as :g:`$DE$ <0212.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0212.g1:/ E F />`, so by hypothesis is :g:`$AB$ <0212.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$BC$ <0212.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0212.g1:/ A B />` has the same ratio to each of the straight lines :g:`$BC$ <0212.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$BG$ <0212.g1:/ B G />`; :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   therefore :g:`$BC$ <0212.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0212.g1:/ B C / = 0212.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$BG$ <0212.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 5] <I.5>

   so that the angle at :g:`$C$ <0212.g1:< C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< C ) = 0212.g1:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0212.g1:< B G C )>`.

.. _folio-ii-0213:

.. folio:: 0213
   :page: 207

But, by hypothesis, the angle at :g:`$C$ <0212.g1:< B C A )>` is less than a right angle;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0212.g1:< B G C )>` is also less than a right angle;

.. assertion::

   so that the angle :g:`$AGB$ <0212.g1:< A G B )>` adjacent to it is greater than a right angle. :just:`[I. 13] <I.13>`

And it was proved :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A G B ) = 0212.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle at :g:`$F$ <0212.g1:< D F E )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle at :g:`$F$ <0212.g1:< D F E )>` is also greater than a right angle.

But it is by hypothesis less than a right angle: which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C )>` is not unequal to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it is :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< A B C ) = 0212.g1:< D E F )>` to it.

But the angle at :g:`$A$ <0212.g1:< B A C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< B A C ) = 0212.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0212.g1:< E D F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$C$ <0212.g1:< B C A )>` is :eq:`equal <0212.g1:< B C A ) = 0212.g1:< D F E )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$F$ <0212.g1:< D F E )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0212.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0212.g1:< B A C ) = 0212.g1:< E D F ); 0212.g1:< A B C ) = 0212.g1:< D E F ); 0212.g1:< B C A ) = 0212.g1:< D F E )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0212.g1:< D E F >>`.

But, again, let each of the angles at :g:`$C$ <0213.g1:< B C A )>`, :g:`$F$ <0213.g1:< D F E )>` be supposed not less than a right angle; I say again that, in this case too, the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0213.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0213.g1:< B A C ) = 0213.g1:< E D F ); 0213.g1:< A B C ) = 0213.g1:< D E F ); 0213.g1:< B C A ) = 0213.g1:< D F E )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0213.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. picture:: 0213.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 7 diagram, Case 2

For, with the same construction, we can prove similarly that

.. assertion::

   :g:`$BC$ <0213.g1:/ B C />` is :eq:`equal <0213.g1:/ B C / = 0213.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$BG$ <0213.g1:/ B G />`;

.. assertion::

   so that the angle at :g:`$C$ <0213.g1:< B C A )>` is also :eq:`equal <0213.g1:< B C A ) = 0213.g1:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0213.g1:< B G C )>`. :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

But the angle at :g:`$C$ <0213.g1:< B C A )>` is not less than a right angle;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither is the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0213.g1:< B G C )>` less than a right angle.

.. assertion::

   Thus in the triangle :g:`$BGC$ <0213.g1:< B G C >>` the two angles are not less than two right angles: which is impossible. :just:`[I. 17] <I.17>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, once more, the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0213.g1:< A B C )>` is not unequal to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0213.g1:< D E F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it is :eq:`equal <0213.g1:< A B C ) = 0213.g1:< D E F )>` to it.

But the angle at :g:`$A$ <0213.g1:< B A C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0213.g1:< B A C ) = 0213.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle at :g:`$D$ <0213.g1:< E D F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$C$ <0213.g1:< B C A )>` is :eq:`equal <0213.g1:< B C A ) = 0213.g1:< D F E )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$F$ <0213.g1:< D F E )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. _folio-ii-0214:

.. folio:: 0214
   :page: 208

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0213.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0213.g1:< B A C ) = 0213.g1:< E D F ); 0213.g1:< A B C ) = 0213.g1:< D E F ); 0213.g1:< B C A ) = 0213.g1:< D F E )>` with the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0213.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
