PROPOSITION 10.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To cut a given uncut straight line similarly to a given cut straight line.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` be the given uncut straight line, and :g:`$AC$ <0219.g1:/ A C />` the straight line cut at the points :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0219.g1:E>`; and let them be so placed as to contain any angle; let :g:`$CB$ <0219.g1:/ C B />` be joined, and through :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0219.g1:E>` let :g:`$DF$ <0219.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$EG$ <0219.g1:/ E G />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0219.g1:/ B C />`, and through :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>` let :g:`$DHK$ <0219.g1:/ D H K />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. picture:: 0219.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 10

.. subclause::

   Therefore each of the figures :g:`$FH$ <0219.g1:< F H >>`, :g:`$HB$ <0219.g1:< H B >>` is a parallelogram;

.. assertion:: [I. 34] <I.34>

   therefore :g:`$DH$ <0219.g1:/ D H />` is :eq:`equal <0219.g1:/ D H / = 0219.g1:/ F G /; 0219.g1:/ H K / = 0219.g1:/ G B />` to :g:`$FG$ <0219.g1:/ F G />` and :g:`$HK$ <0219.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ G B />`.

Now, since the straight line :g:`$HE$ <0219.g1:/ H E />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$KC$ <0219.g1:/ K C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$DKC$ <0219.g1:< D K C >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`$KH$ <0219.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`$HD$ <0219.g1:/ H D />`. :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`

.. folio:: 0220
   :page: 214

.. assertion::

   But :g:`$KH$ <0219.g1:/ K H />` is :eq:`equal <0219.g1:/ K H / = 0219.g1:/ B G /; 0219.g1:/ H D / = 0219.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, and :g:`$HD$ <0219.g1:/ H D />` to :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />`; therefore, as :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />`.

Again, since :g:`$FD$ <0219.g1:/ F D />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$GE$ <0219.g1:/ G E />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$AGE$ <0219.g1:< A G E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0219.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0219.g1:/ F A />`. :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`

But it was also proved that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />`, and, as :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0219.g1:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0219.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0219.g1:/ F A />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the given uncut straight line :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` has been cut similarly to the given cut straight line :g:`$AC$ <0219.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
