PROPOSITION 15.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In equal triangles which have one angle equal to one angle the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle, and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, are equal.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` be :eq:`equal <0226.g1:< A B C > = 0226.g1:< A D E >>` triangles having one angle :eq:`equal <0226.g1:< B A C ) = 0226.g1:< D A E )>` to one angle, namely the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0226.g1:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$DAE$ <0226.g1:< D A E )>`;

I say that in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`.

.. _folio-ii-0226:

.. folio:: 0226
   :page: 220

For let them be placed so that :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is in a straight line with :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` is also in a straight line with :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[I. 14] <I.14>`

Let :g:`$BD$ <0226.g1:/ B D />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0226.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 15

Since then the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0226.g1:< A B C > = 0226.g1:< A D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>`, and :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>` is another area,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`$CAB$ <0226.g1:< C A B >>` is to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`. :just:`[V. 7] <V.7>`

.. assertion::

   But, as :g:`$CAB$ <0226.g1:< C A B >>` is to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>` and, as :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` is to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[*id.*]`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   Therefore also, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

Next, let the sides of the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` be reciprocally proportional, that is to say, let :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` be to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />` as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`;

I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0226.g1:< A B C > = 0226.g1:< A D E >>` to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>`.

For, if :g:`$BD$ <0226.g1:/ B D />` be again joined, since, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`, while, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, and, as :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`, so is the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. subclause::

   Therefore each of the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` has the same ratio to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0226.g1:< A B C > = 0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>`. :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
