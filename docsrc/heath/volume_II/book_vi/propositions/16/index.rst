PROPOSITION 16.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained by the means; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the rectangle contained by the means, the four straight lines will be proportional.

Let the four straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` be proportional, so that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`;

I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` is equal to the rectangle contained by :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`.

.. picture:: 0227.g2.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 16 (BG and E)

.. picture:: 0227.g3.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 16 (DH and F)

Let :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />`, :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` be drawn from the points :g:`$A$ <0227.g2:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0227.g3:C>` at right angles to the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, and let :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />` be made equal to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`, and :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` equal to :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`.

Let the parallelograms :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>`, :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>` be completed.

Then since, as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`,

.. subclause::

   while :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` is equal to :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />`, and :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` to :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` to :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>`, :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

.. _folio-ii-0228:

.. folio:: 0228
   :page: 222

But those equiangular parallelograms in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional are equal; :just:`[VI. 14] <VI.14>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`, for :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />` is equal to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`; and :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>` is the rectangle :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`, for :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` is equal to :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` is equal to the rectangle contained by :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`.

Next, let the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` be equal to the rectangle contained by :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`;

I say that the four straight lines will be proportional, so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`.

For, with the same construction, since the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`, and the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />` is :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>`, for :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />` is equal to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`, and the rectangle :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` is :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>`, for :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` is equal to :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BG$ <0227.g2:< B G >>` is equal to :g:`$DH$ <0227.g3:< D H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And they are equiangular

But in equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional. :just:`[VI. 14] <VI.14>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` to :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CH$ <0227.g3:/ C H />` is equal to :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />`, and :g:`$AG$ <0227.g2:/ A G />` to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0227.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0227.g3:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$E$ <0227.g2:/ E />` to :g:`$F$ <0227.g3:/ F />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
