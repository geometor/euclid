PROPOSITION 20.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Similar polygons are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes,\ [#f1]_ and the polygon has to the polygon a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

Let :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` be similar polygons, and let :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` correspond to :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`;

I say that the polygons :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes, and the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` has to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` a ratio duplicate of that which :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`.

Let :g:`BE <0241.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`EC <0241.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`GL <0241.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`LH <0241.g1:/ L H />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0241.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 20 Diagram

.. subclause::

   Now, since the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` is similar to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>`,

.. assertion::

   the angle :g:`BAE <0241.g1:< B A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L )>` to the angle :g:`GFL <0241.g1:< G F L )>`;

.. assertion:: [VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>

   and, as :g:`BA <0241.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`AE <0241.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`GF <0241.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`FL <0241.g1:/ F L />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>` are two triangles having one angle :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L )>` to one angle and the sides about the equal angles proportional,

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L ); 0241.g1:< A B E ) = 0241.g1:< F G L )>` with the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`; :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. assertion:: [VI. 4 and Def. 1] <VI.4, VI.def.1>

   so that it is also similar;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A B E ) = 0241.g1:< F G L )>` to the angle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L )>`.

.. _folio-ii-0242:

.. folio:: 0242
   :page: 236

.. subclause::

   But the whole angle :g:`ABC <0241.g1:< A B C )>` is also :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A B C ) = 0241.g1:< F G H )>` to the whole angle :g:`FGH <0241.g1:< F G H )>` because of the similarity of the polygons;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< E B C ) = 0241.g1:< L G H )>` to the angle :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since, because of the similarity of the triangles :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`EB <0241.g1:/ E B />` is to :g:`BA <0241.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`LG <0241.g1:/ L G />` to :g:`GF <0241.g1:/ G F />`,

.. subclause::

   and moreover also, because of the similarity of the polygons,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`BC <0241.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />` to :g:`GH <0241.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`EB <0241.g1:/ E B />` is to :g:`BC <0241.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`LG <0241.g1:/ L G />` to :g:`GH <0241.g1:/ G H />`;

.. assertion::

   that is, the sides about the :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< E B C ) = 0241.g1:< L G H )>` angles :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C )>`, :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H )>` are proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< E B C ) = 0241.g1:< L G H )>` with the triangle :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`, :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. assertion::

   so that the triangle :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`. :just:`[VI. 4 and Def. 1] <VI.4, VI.def.1>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the triangle :g:`ECD <0241.g1:< E C D >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`LHK <0241.g1:< L H K >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the similar polygons :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` have been divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude.

I say that they are also in the same ratio as the wholes, that is, in such manner that the triangles are proportional, and :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>`, :g:`ECD <0241.g1:< E C D >>` are antecedents, while :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`, :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`, :g:`LHK <0241.g1:< L H K >>` are their consequents,\ [#f2]_ and that the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` has to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`.

For let :g:`AC <0241.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`FH <0241.g1:/ F H />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then since, because of the similarity of the polygons, the angle :g:`ABC <0241.g1:< A B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A B C ) = 0241.g1:< F G H )>` to the angle :g:`FGH <0241.g1:< F G H )>`, and, as :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`BC <0241.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />` to :g:`GH <0241.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 6] <VI.6>

   the triangle :g:`ABC <0241.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< A B C ) = 0241.g1:< F G H ); 0241.g1:< B A C ) = 0241.g1:< G F H ); 0241.g1:< B C A ) = 0241.g1:< G H F )>` with the triangle :g:`FGH <0241.g1:< F G H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`BAC <0241.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A C ) = 0241.g1:< G F H ); 0241.g1:< B C A ) = 0241.g1:< G H F )>` to the angle :g:`GFH <0241.g1:< G F H )>`, and the angle :g:`BCA <0241.g1:< B C A )>` to the angle :g:`GHF <0241.g1:< G H F )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the angle :g:`BAM <0241.g1:< B A M )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A M ) = 0241.g1:< G F N )>` to the angle :g:`GFN <0241.g1:< G F N )>`, and the angle :g:`ABM <0241.g1:< A B M )>` is also :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A B M ) = 0241.g1:< F G N )>` to the angle :g:`FGN <0241.g1:< F G N )>`,

.. _folio-ii-0243:

.. folio:: 0243
   :page: 237

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`AMB <0241.g1:< A M B )>` is also :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A M B ) = 0241.g1:< F N G )>` to the remaining angle :g:`FNG <0241.g1:< F N G )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`ABM <0241.g1:< A B M >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< B A M ) = 0241.g1:< G F N ); 0241.g1:< A B M ) = 0241.g1:< F G N ); 0241.g1:< A M B ) = 0241.g1:< F N G )>` with the triangle :g:`FGN <0241.g1:< F G N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that the triangle :g:`BMC <0241.g1:< B M C >>` is also :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< B C A ) = 0241.g1:< G H F ); 0241.g1:< E B C ) = 0241.g1:< L G H )>` with the triangle :g:`GNH <0241.g1:< G N H >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` is to :g:`MB <0241.g1:/ M B />`, so is :g:`FN <0241.g1:/ F N />` to :g:`NG <0241.g1:/ N G />`, and, as :g:`BM <0241.g1:/ B M />` is to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`, so is :g:`GN <0241.g1:/ G N />` to :g:`NH <0241.g1:/ N H />`;

.. assertion::

   so that, in addition, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` is to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`, so is :g:`FN <0241.g1:/ F N />` to :g:`NH <0241.g1:/ N H />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` is to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`, so is the triangle :g:`ABM <0241.g1:< A B M >>` to :g:`MBC <0241.g1:< M B C >>`, and :g:`AME <0241.g1:< A M E >>` to :g:`EMC <0241.g1:< E M C >>`; for they are to one another as their bases. :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. subclause::

   Therefore also, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents; :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`AMB <0241.g1:< A B M >>` is to :g:`BMC <0241.g1:< B M C >>`, so is :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` to :g:`CBE <0241.g1:< C B E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`AMB <0241.g1:< A B M >>` is to :g:`BMC <0241.g1:< B M C >>`, so is :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` is to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`, so is the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` to the triangle :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason also, as :g:`FN <0241.g1:/ F N />` is to :g:`NH <0241.g1:/ N H />`, so is the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>` to the triangle :g:`GLH <0241.g1:< G L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`AM <0241.g1:/ A M />` is to :g:`MC <0241.g1:/ M C />`, so is :g:`FN <0241.g1:/ F N />` to :g:`NH <0241.g1:/ N H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is to the triangle :g:`BEC <0241.g1:< B E C >>`, so is the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>` to the triangle :g:`GLH <0241.g1:< G L H >>`;

.. assertion::

   and, alternately, as the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is to the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`, so is the triangle :g:`BEC <0241.g1:< B E C >>` to the triangle :g:`GLH <0241.g1:< G L H >>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove, if :g:`BD <0241.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`GK <0241.g1:/ G K />` be joined, that, as the triangle :g:`BEC <0241.g1:< B E C >>` is to the triangle :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`, so also is the triangle :g:`ECD <0241.g1:< E C D >>` to the triangle :g:`LHK <0241.g1:< L H K >>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, as the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is to the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`, so is :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>` to :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`, and further :g:`ECD <0241.g1:< E C D >>` to :g:`LHK <0241.g1:< L H K >>`,

.. subclause::

   therefore also, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents; :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is to the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`, so is the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>`.

.. _folio-ii-0244:

.. folio:: 0244
   :page: 238

.. assertion::

   But the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` has to the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>` a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` has to the corresponding side :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`; for similar triangles are in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides. :just:`[VI. 19] <VI.19>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` also has to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` has to the corresponding side :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.

.. enunciation::

   PORISM. Similarly also it can be proved in the case of quadrilaterals that they are in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides. And it was also proved in the case of triangles; therefore also, generally, similar rectilineal figures are to one another in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **in the same ratio as the wholes.** The same word :term:`ὁμόλογος <corresponding-magnitudes>` is used which I have generally translated by "corresponding." But here it is followed by a dative, :term:`ὁμόλογα <corresponding-magnitudes>` τοῖς ὅλοις " *homologous with the wholes*," instead of being used absolutely. The meaning can therefore here be nothing else but "in the same ratio with" or "proportional to the wholes"; and Euclid seems to recognise that he is making a special use of the word, because he explains it lower down (l. 46): "the triangles are homologous to the wholes, that is, in such manner that the triangles are proportional, and :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`EBC <0241.g1:< E B C >>`, :g:`ECD <0241.g1:< E C D >>` are antecedents, while :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`, :g:`LGH <0241.g1:< L G H >>`, :g:`LHK <0241.g1:< L H K >>` are their consequents."

.. [#f2] **ἑπόμενα αὐτῶν, "their consequents,"** is a little awkward, but may be supposed to indicate which triangles correspond to which as consequent to antecedent.
