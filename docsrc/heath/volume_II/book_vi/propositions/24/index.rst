PROPOSITION 24.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In any parallelogram the parallelograms about the diameter are similar both to the whole and to one another.

Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` be a parallelogram, and :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` its diameter, and let :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>` be parallelograms about :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` ;

I say that each of the parallelograms :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>` is similar both to the whole :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` and to the other.

.. picture:: 0257.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 24 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$EF$ <0257.g1:/ E F />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0257.g1:/ B C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0257.g1:< A B C >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   proportionally, as :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$FG$ <0257.g1:/ F G />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0257.g1:/ C D />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0257.g1:< A C D >>`,

.. assertion::

   proportionally, as :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, so is :g:`$DG$ <0257.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`$GA$ <0257.g1:/ G A />`. :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`.

.. subclause::

   But it was proved that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, so also is :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />`, so is :g:`$DG$ <0257.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`$GA$ <0257.g1:/ G A />`,

.. subclause::

   and therefore, :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`,

.. assertion:: [V. 18] <V.18>

   as :g:`$BA$ <0257.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0257.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`$DA$ <0257.g1:/ D A />` to :g:`$AG$ <0257.g1:/ A G />`,

.. subclause::

   and, alternately,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   as :g:`$BA$ <0257.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0257.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AG$ <0257.g1:/ A G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, the sides about the common angle :g:`$BAD$ <0257.g1:< B A D )>` are proportional.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$GF$ <0257.g1:/ G F />` is parallel to :g:`$DC$ <0257.g1:/ D C />`,

.. _folio-ii-0258:

.. folio:: 0258
   :page: 252

.. assertion::

   the angle :g:`$AFG$ <0257.g1:< A F G )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DCA$ <0257.g1:< D C A )>` ;

.. subclause::

   and the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0257.g1:< D A C )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ADC$ <0257.g1:< A D C >>`, :g:`$AGF$ <0257.g1:< A G F >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0257.g1:< A D C >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$AGF$ <0257.g1:< A G F >>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the triangle :g:`$ACB$ <0257.g1:< A C B >>` is also equiangular with the triangle :g:`$AFE$ <0257.g1:< A F E >>`,

.. assertion::

   and the whole parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` is equiangular with the parallelogram :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`.

.. subclause::

   Therefore, proportionally,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$AD$ <0257.g1:/ A D />` is to :g:`$DC$ <0257.g1:/ D C />`, so is :g:`$AG$ <0257.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$GF$ <0257.g1:/ G F />`, as :g:`$DC$ <0257.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0257.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0257.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, as :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$CB$ <0257.g1:/ C B />`, so is :g:`$AF$ <0257.g1:/ A F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0257.g1:/ F E />`, and further, as :g:`$CB$ <0257.g1:/ C B />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0257.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`$FE$ <0257.g1:/ F E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since it was proved that, as :g:`$DC$ <0257.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0257.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0257.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, and, as :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$CB$ <0257.g1:/ C B />`, so is :g:`$AF$ <0257.g1:/ A F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0257.g1:/ F E />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$DC$ <0257.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CB$ <0257.g1:/ C B />`, so is :g:`$GF$ <0257.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0257.g1:/ F E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>` the sides about the equal angles are proportional ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` is similar to the parallelogram :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` is also similar to the parallelogram :g:`$KH$ <0257.g1:< K H >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the parallelograms :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>` is similar to :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   But figures similar to the same rectilineal figure are also similar to one another ; :just:`[VI. 21] <VI.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>` is also similar to the parallelogram :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
