PROPOSITION 26.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If from a parallelogram there be taken away a parallelogram similar and similarly situated to the whole and having a common angle with it, it is about the same diameter with the whole.

For from the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` let there be taken away the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0261.g1:< A E F G >>` similar and similarly situated to :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>`, and having the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0261.g1:< D A B )>` common with it ;

I say that :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` is about the same diameter with :g:`$AF$ <0261.g1:< A E F G >>`.

.. picture:: 0261.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 26

.. subclause::

   For suppose it is not, but, if possible, let :g:`$AHC$ <0261.g1:/ A H C />` be the diameter &lt;*of* :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` &gt;, let :g:`$GF$ <0261.g1:/ G F />` be produced and carried through to :g:`$H$ <0261.g1:H>`, and let :g:`$HK$ <0261.g1:/ K H />` be drawn through :g:`$H$ <0261.g1:H>` parallel to either of the straight lines :g:`$AD$ <0261.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$BC$ <0261.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. subclause::

   Since, then, :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` is about the same diameter with :g:`$KG$ <0261.g1:< A K H G >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 24] <VI.24>

   therefore, as :g:`$DA$ <0261.g1:/ A D />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0261.g1:/ A B />`, so is :g:`$GA$ <0261.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$AK$ <0261.g1:/ A K />`.

.. subclause::

   But also, because of the similarity of :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EG$ <0261.g1:< A E F G >>`,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$DA$ <0261.g1:/ A D />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0261.g1:/ A B />`, so is :g:`$GA$ <0261.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$AE$ <0261.g1:/ A E />` ;

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore also, as :g:`$GA$ <0261.g1:/ A G />` is to :g:`$AK$ <0261.g1:/ A K />`, so is :g:`$GA$ <0261.g1:/ A G />` to :g:`$AE$ <0261.g1:/ A E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GA$ <0261.g1:/ A G />` has the same ratio to each of the straight lines :g:`$AK$ <0261.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$AE$ <0261.g1:/ A E />`.

.. _folio-ii-0262:

.. folio:: 0262
   :page: 256

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AE$ <0261.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0261.g1:/ A E / = 0261.g1:/ A K />` to :g:`$AK$ <0261.g1:/ A K />` :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`, the less to the greater : which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` cannot but be about the same diameter with :g:`$AF$ <0261.g1:< A E F G >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$ABCD$ <0261.g1:< A B C D >>` is about the same diameter with the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0261.g1:< A E F G >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
