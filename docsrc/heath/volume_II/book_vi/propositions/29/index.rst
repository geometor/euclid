PROPOSITION 29.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to a given one.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` the given rectilineal figure to which the figure to be applied to :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` is required to be equal, and :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>` that to which the excess is required to be similar ;

thus it is required to apply to the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>`.

.. picture:: 0271.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 29

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` be :eq:`bisected <0271.g1:/ A E / = 0271.g1:/ E B />` at :g:`$E$ <0271.g1:E>` ; let there be described on :g:`$EB$ <0271.g1:/ E B />` the parallelogram :g:`$BF$ <0271.g1:< B F >>` similar and similarly situated to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>` ; and let :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` be constructed at once equal to the sum of :g:`$BF$ <0271.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` and similar and similarly situated to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>`. :just:`[VI. 25] <VI.25>`

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$KH$ <0271.g1:/ K H />` correspond to :g:`$FL$ <0271.g1:/ F L />` and :g:`$KG$ <0271.g1:/ K G />` to :g:`$FE$ <0271.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` is greater than :g:`$FB$ <0271.g1:< F B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KH$ <0271.g1:/ K H />` is also greater than :g:`$FL$ <0271.g1:/ F L />`, and :g:`$KG$ <0271.g1:/ K G />` than :g:`$FE$ <0271.g1:/ F E />`.

.. folio:: 0272
   :page: 266

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$FL$ <0271.g1:[ F L ]>`, :g:`$FE$ <0271.g1:[ F E ]>` be produced, let :g:`$FLM$ <0271.g1:/ F L M />` be :eq:`equal <0271.g1:/ F L M / = 0271.g1:/ K H />; 0271.g1:/ F E N / = 0271.g1:/ K G />` to :g:`$KH$ <0271.g1:/ K H />`, and :g:`$FEN$ <0271.g1:/ F E N />` to :g:`$KG$ <0271.g1:/ K G />`, and let :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>` be completed ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>` is both :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< M N > = 0271.g1:< G H >>` and similar to :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` is similar to :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>` ;

.. assertion:: [VI. 21] <VI.21>

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>` is also similar to :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>` ;

.. assertion:: [VI. 26] <VI.26>

   therefore :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>` is about the same diameter with :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let their diameter :g:`$FO$ <0271.g1:/ F O />` be drawn, and let the figure be described.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` is equal to :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>`, :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>`, while :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< G H > = 0271.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0271.g1:< M N >>` is also equal to :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>`, :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>` be subtracted from each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, the gnomon :g:`$XWV$ <0271.g1:< X W V >>`, is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< X W V > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AE$ <0271.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:/ A E / = 0271.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$EB$ <0271.g1:/ E B />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AN$ <0271.g1:< A N >>` is also :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A N > = 0271.g1:< N B > = 0271.g1:< L P >>` to :g:`$NB$ <0271.g1:< N B >>` :just:`[I. 36] <I.36>`, that is, to :g:`$LP$ <0271.g1:< L P >>` :just:`[I. 43] <I.43>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$EO$ <0271.g1:< E O >>` be added to each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$AO$ <0271.g1:< A O >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< V W X >>` to the gnomon :g:`$VWX$ <0271.g1:< V W X >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`$VWX$ <0271.g1:< V W X >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< V W X > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AO$ <0271.g1:< A O >>` is also :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore to the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` there has been applied the parallelogram :g:`$AO$ <0271.g1:< A O >>` :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure :g:`$QP$ <0271.g1:< Q P >>` which is similar to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>`, since :g:`$PQ$ <0271.g1:< P Q >>` is also similar to :g:`$EL$ <0271.g1:< E L >>` :just:`[VI. 24] <VI.24>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. F.`
