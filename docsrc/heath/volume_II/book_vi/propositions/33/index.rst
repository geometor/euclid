PROPOSITION 33.
===============

.. enunciation::

   In equal circles angles have the same ratio as the circumferences on which they stand, whether they stand at the centres or at the circumferences.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0279.g2:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0279.g2:( D E F )>` be :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( A B C ) = 0279.g2:( D E F )>` circles, and let the angles :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`, :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>` be angles at their centres :g:`$G$ <0279.g2:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0279.g2:H>`, and the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>` angles at the circumferences;

I say that, as the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` is to the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, so is the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, and the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>`.

.. picture:: 0279.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33

For let any number of consecutive circumferences :g:`$CK$ <0279.g2:( C K )>`, :g:`$KL$ <0279.g2:( K L )>` be made :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( C K ) = 0279.g2:( K L ) = 0279.g2:( B C )>` to the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`, and any number of consecutive circumferences :g:`$FM$ <0279.g2:( F M )>`, :g:`$MN$ <0279.g2:( M N )>` :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( F M ) = 0279.g2:( M N ) = 0279.g2:( E F )>` to the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`; and let :g:`$GK$ <0279.g2:/ G K />`, :g:`$GL$ <0279.g2:/ G L />`, :g:`$HM$ <0279.g2:/ H M />`, :g:`$HN$ <0279.g2:/ H N />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the circumferences :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`, :g:`$CK$ <0279.g2:( C K )>`, :g:`$KL$ <0279.g2:( K L )>` are :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( B C ) = 0279.g2:( C K ) = 0279.g2:( K L )>` to one another,

.. assertion:: [III. 27] <III.27>

   the angles :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`, :g:`$CGK$ <0279.g2:< C G K )>`, :g:`$KGL$ <0279.g2:< K G L )>` are also :eq:`equal <0279.g2:< B G C ) = 0279.g2:< C G K ) = 0279.g2:< K G L )>` to one another;

.. _folio-ii-0280:

.. folio:: 0280
   :page: 274

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever multiple the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is of :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`, that multiple also is the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` of the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion::

   whatever multiple the circumference :g:`$NE$ <0279.g2:( N E )>` is of :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, that multiple also is the angle :g:`$NHE$ <0279.g2:< N H E )>` of the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`.

.. assertion::

   If then the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( B L ) = 0279.g2:( E N )>` to the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`, the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also :eq:`equal <0279.g2:< B G L ) = 0279.g2:< E H N )>` to the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`; :just:`[III. 27] <III.27>` if the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is greater than the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`, the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also greater than the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`; and, if less, less.

.. subclause::

   There being then four magnitudes, two circumferences :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`, :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, and two angles :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`, :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, there have been taken, of the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` and the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` equimultiples, namely the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` and the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>`, and of the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>` and the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>` equimultiples, namely the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>` and the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`.

.. subclause::

   And it has been proved that, if the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is in excess of the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`, the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also in excess of the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`; if :eq:`equal <0279.g2:( B L ) = 0279.g2:( E N )>`, :eq:`equal <0279.g2:< B G L ) = 0279.g2:< E H N )>`; and if less, less.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` is to :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, so is the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` is to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, so is the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>`; for they are doubles respectively.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` is to the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, so is the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, and the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
