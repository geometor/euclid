Commentary on Proposition VII.4
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VII.4 <VII.4>`.

.. folio:: 0309
   :page: 303

The meaning of the enunciation is of course that, if :math:`a`, :math:`b` be two numbers of which :math:`b` is the less, then :math:`b` is either a submultiple or *some proper fraction of* :math:`a`.

(1) If :math:`a`, :math:`b` are prime to one another, divide each into its units; then :math:`b` contains :math:`b` of the same parts of which :math:`a` contains :math:`a`. Therefore :math:`b` is "parts" or *a proper fraction of* :math:`a`.

(2) If :math:`a`, :math:`b` be not prime to one another, either :math:`b` measures :math:`a`, in which case :math:`b` is a submultiple or "part" of :math:`a`, or, if :math:`g` be the greatest common measure of :math:`a`, :math:`b`, we may put :math:`a = mg` and :math:`b = ng`, and :math:`b` will contain :math:`n` of the same parts (:math:`g`) of which :math:`a` contains :math:`m`, so that :math:`b` is again "parts," or *a proper fraction, of* :math:`a`.
