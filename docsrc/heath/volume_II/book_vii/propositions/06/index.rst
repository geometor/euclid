PROPOSITION 6.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a number be parts of a number, and another be the same parts of another, the sum will also be the same parts of the sum that the one is of the one.

For let the number :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` be parts of the number :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, and another, :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />`, the same parts of another, :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`, that :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`;

I say that the sum of :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />` is also the same parts of the sum of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>` that :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0311.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 6

.. subclause::

   For since, whatever parts :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />` is also the same parts of :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as many parts of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>` as there are in :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />`, so many parts of :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>` are there also in :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />`.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` be divided into the parts of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, namely :g:`$AG$ <0311.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0311.g1:/ G B />`, and :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />` into the parts of :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`, namely :g:`$DH$ <0311.g1:/ D H />`, :g:`$HE$ <0311.g1:/ H E />`;

.. assertion::

   thus the multitude of :g:`$AG$ <0311.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0311.g1:/ G B />` will be equal to the multitude of :g:`$DH$ <0311.g1:/ D H />`, :g:`$HE$ <0311.g1:/ H E />`.

.. subclause::

   And since, whatever part :g:`$AG$ <0311.g1:/ A G />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, the same part is :g:`$DH$ <0311.g1:/ D H />` of :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>` also,

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever part :g:`$AG$ <0311.g1:/ A G />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, the same part also is the sum of :g:`$AG$ <0311.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$DH$ <0311.g1:/ D H />` of the sum of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`. :just:`[VII. 5] <VII.5>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason,

.. assertion::

   whatever part :g:`$GB$ <0311.g1:/ G B />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, the same part also is the sum of :g:`$GB$ <0311.g1:/ G B />`, :g:`$HE$ <0311.g1:/ H E />` of the sum of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever parts :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />` is of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, the same parts also is the sum of :g:`$AB$ <0311.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$DE$ <0311.g1:/ D E />` of the sum of :g:`$C$ <0311.g1:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0311.g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
