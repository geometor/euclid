PROPOSITION 8.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a number be the same parts of a number that a number subtracted is of a number subtracted, the remainder will also be the same parts of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.

For let the number :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />` be the same parts of the number :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />` that :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` subtracted is of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />` subtracted;

I say that the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0313.g1:/ E B />` is also the same parts of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0313.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 8

For let :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` be made :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ G H / = 0313.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever parts :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same parts also is :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`.

Let :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` be divided into the parts of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, namely :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />`, and :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` into the parts of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`, namely :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`;

.. assertion::

   thus the multitude of :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` will be equal to the multitude of :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`.

.. _folio-ii-0314:

.. folio:: 0314
   :page: 308

.. subclause::

   Now since, whatever part :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same part also is :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`, while :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />` is greater than :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />` is also greater than :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$GM$ <0313.g1:/ G M />` be made :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ G M / = 0313.g1:/ A L />` to :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever part :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same part also is :g:`$GM$ <0313.g1:/ G M />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also the remainder :g:`$MK$ <0313.g1:/ M K />` is the same part of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[VII. 7] <VII.7>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since, whatever part :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same part also is :g:`$EL$ <0313.g1:/ L E />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`, while :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />` is greater than :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HK$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` is also greater than :g:`$EL$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$KN$ <0313.g1:/ K N />` be made :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ K N / = 0313.g1:/ L E />` to :g:`$EL$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever part :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same part also is :g:`$KN$ <0313.g1:/ K N />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also the remainder :g:`$NH$ <0313.g1:/ N H />` is the same part of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[VII. 7] <VII.7>`

.. subclause::

   But the remainder :g:`$MK$ <0313.g1:/ M K />` was also proved to be the same part of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also the sum of :g:`$MK$ <0313.g1:/ M K />`, :g:`$NH$ <0313.g1:/ N H />` is the same parts of :g:`$DF$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$HG$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   But the sum of :g:`$MK$ <0313.g1:/ M K />`, :g:`$NH$ <0313.g1:/ N H />` is equal to :g:`$EB$ <0313.g1:/ E B />`, and :g:`$HG$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` is :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ G H / = 0313.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$BA$ <0313.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0313.g1:/ E B />` is the same parts of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
