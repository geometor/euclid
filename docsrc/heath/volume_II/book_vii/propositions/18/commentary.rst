Commentary on Proposition VII.18
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VII.18 <VII.18>`.

.. folio:: 0324
   :page: 318

It is here proved that

.. math::

   a : b = ac : bc.

The argument is as follows.

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   ac &= ca. & \text{[VII. 16]} \\
   \text{Similarly}\qquad bc &= cb. \\
   \text{And}\qquad a : b &= ca : cb; & \text{[VII. 17]} \\
   \text{therefore}\qquad a : b &= ac : bc.
   \end{aligned}
