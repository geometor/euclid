PROPOSITION 34.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Given two numbers, to find the least number which they measure.

Let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` be the two given numbers;

thus it is required to find the least number which they measure.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` are either prime to one another or not.

First, let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` be prime to one another, and let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` make :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` has made :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`. :just:`[VII. 16] <VII.16>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measure :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0342.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 34 diagram

I say next that it is also the least number they measure.

.. subclause::

   For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` will measure some number which is less than :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`.

Let them measure :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`.

Then, as many times as :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`, and, as many times as :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` has made :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, and :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>` has made :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`; :just:`[VII. Def. 15] <VII.def.15>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>` to :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` are prime, primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>` and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`, as consequent consequent.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` is to :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, the greater the less: which is impossible.

.. _folio-ii-0343:

.. folio:: 0343
   :page: 337

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` do not measure any number less than :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`; therefore :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` is the least that is measured by :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`.

Next, let :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` not be prime to one another, and let :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33] <VII.33>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`. :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

And let :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` make :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>` has made :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` measure :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0343.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 34 diagram

I say next that it is also the least number that they measure.

.. subclause::

   For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` will measure some number which is less than :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`.

Let them measure :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`.

And, as many times as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`, and, as many times as :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` measures :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>` has made :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, and :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` has made :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` to :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>` to :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>` is to :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` to :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` are least, and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` measures :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>` has made :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` is to :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` measures :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, the greater the less: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` will not measure any number which is less than :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>` is the least that is measured by :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
