PROPOSITION 35.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers measure any number, the least number measured by them will also measure the same.

For let the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0345.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g1:B>` measure any number :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:/ C D />`, and let :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>` be the least that they measure;

I say that :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>` also measures :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0345.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 35

.. subclause::

   For, if :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>` does not measure :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:/ C D />`, let :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>`, measuring :g:`$DF$ <0345.g1:/ F D />`, leave :g:`$CF$ <0345.g1:/ C F />` less than itself.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0345.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g1:B>` measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>`, and :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>` measures :g:`$DF$ <0345.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0345.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g1:B>` will also measure :g:`$DF$ <0345.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But they also measure the whole :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore they will also measure the remainder :g:`$CF$ <0345.g1:/ C F />` which is less than :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>`: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$E$ <0345.g1:E>` cannot fail to measure :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:/ C D />`; therefore it measures it.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
