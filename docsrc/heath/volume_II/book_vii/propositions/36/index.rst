PROPOSITION 36.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Given three numbers, to find the least number which they measure.

Let :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` be the three given numbers;

thus it is required to find the least number which they measure.

.. picture:: 0345.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 36

Let :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`, the least number measured by the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, be taken. :just:`[VII. 34] <VII.34>`

.. subclause::

   Then :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` either measures, or does not measure, :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`.

First, let it measure it.

.. _folio-ii-0346:

.. folio:: 0346
   :page: 340

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` also measure :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`.

I say next that it is also the least that they measure.

For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` will measure some number which is less than :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`.

Let them measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` will also measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` is the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` will measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`, the greater the less: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` will not measure any number which is less than :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` is the least that :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure.

Again, let :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, and let :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`, the least number measured by :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, be taken. :just:`[VII. 34] <VII.34>`

.. picture:: 0346.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 36

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, and :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` measures :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

I say next that it is also the least that they measure.

For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` will measure some number which is less than :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

Let them measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` will also measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` is the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` measures :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`, so that the least number measured by :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` will also measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.

.. _folio-ii-0347:

.. folio:: 0347
   :page: 341

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>` is the least number measured by :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>` measures :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`, the greater the less: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` will not measure any number which is less than :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>` is the least that is measured by :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
