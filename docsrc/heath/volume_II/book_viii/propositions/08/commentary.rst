Commentary on Proposition VIII.8
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.8 <VIII.8>`.

.. folio:: 0364
   :page: 358

If $a : b = e : f$, and between $a$, $b$ there are any number of geometric means $c$, $d$, there will be as many such means between $e$, $f$.

Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \dots, \delta$ be the least possible terms in the same ratio as $a$, $c$, $d, \dots b$. Then $\alpha, \delta$ are prime to one another, :just:`[VIII. 3] <VIII.3>`

and, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`,

$$\begin{aligned}
\alpha : \delta &= a : b\\
&= e : f.
\end{aligned}$$

Therefore $e = m\alpha$, $f = m\delta$, where $m$ is some integer. :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

Take the numbers

$$m\alpha,\quad m\beta,\quad m\gamma,\quad \dots\quad m\delta.$$

This is a series in the given ratio, and we have the same number of geometric means between $m\alpha$, $m\delta$, or $e$, $f$, that there are between $a$, $b$.
