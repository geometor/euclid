Commentary on Proposition VIII.8
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.8 <VIII.8>`.

.. folio:: 0364
   :page: 358

If :math:`a : b = e : f`, and between :math:`a`, :math:`b` there are any number of geometric means :math:`c`, :math:`d`, there will be as many such means between :math:`e`, :math:`f`.

Let :math:`\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \dots, \delta` be the least possible terms in the same ratio as :math:`a`, :math:`c`, :math:`d, \dots b`. Then :math:`\alpha, \delta` are prime to one another, :just:`[VIII. 3] <VIII.3>`

and, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`,

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   \alpha : \delta &= a : b\\
   &= e : f.
   \end{aligned}

Therefore :math:`e = m\alpha`, :math:`f = m\delta`, where :math:`m` is some integer. :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

Take the numbers

.. math::

   m\alpha,\quad m\beta,\quad m\gamma,\quad \dots\quad m\delta.

This is a series in the given ratio, and we have the same number of geometric means between :math:`m\alpha`, :math:`m\delta`, or :math:`e`, :math:`f`, that there are between :math:`a`, :math:`b`.
