Commentary on Proposition VIII.12
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.12 <VIII.12>`.

.. folio:: 0371
   :page: 365

The cube numbers :math:`a^3`, :math:`b^3` being given, Euclid forms the products :math:`a^2b`, :math:`ab^2` and then proves, as usual, by means of VII. 17, 18 that

.. math::

   a^3,\quad a^2b,\quad ab^2,\quad b^3

are in continued proportion.

He assumes that, since :math:`a^3` has to :math:`b^3` the ratio triplicate of :math:`a^3 : a^2b`, the ratio :math:`a^3 : b^3` is triplicate of the ratio :math:`a : b` which is equal to :math:`a^3 : a^2b`. This is again an obvious inference from VII. 14.
