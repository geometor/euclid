PROPOSITION 12.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Between two cube numbers there are two mean proportional numbers, and the cube has to the cube the ratio triplicate of that which the side has to the side.

Let :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` be cube numbers, and let :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` be the side of :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, and :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` of :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>`;

I say that between :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` there are two mean proportional numbers, and :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` has to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0370.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 12

For let :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>`, and by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` let it make :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>`; let :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>`, and let the numbers :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` make :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>` respectively.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` is a cube, and :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` its side, and :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>` and by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>` has made :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>` and by multiplying :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>` has made :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` by multiplying the numbers :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` has made :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>`.

.. _folio-ii-0371:

.. folio:: 0371
   :page: 365

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   as :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` by multiplying the numbers :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` has made :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>` is to :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` to :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$E$ <0370.g1:E>` is to :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` to :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the numbers :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` have made :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>` by multiplying each of the numbers :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>` has made :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>`, :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` is to :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>` to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$F$ <0370.g1:F>` is to :g:`$G$ <0370.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` to :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>`, and :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>` to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>` are two mean proportionals between :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>`.

I say next that :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` also has to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` has to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>`, :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0370.g1:K>`, :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` are four numbers in proportion,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` has to :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. Def. 10] <V.def.10>`

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` is to :g:`$H$ <0370.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0370.g1:A>` also has to :g:`$B$ <0370.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$C$ <0370.g1:C>` has to :g:`$D$ <0370.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
