PROPOSITION 14.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a square measure a square, the side will also measure the side; and, if the side measure the side, the square will also measure the square.

Let :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>` be square numbers, let :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>` be their sides, and let :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>`;

I say that :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`.

For let :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>` make :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`. :just:`[VIII. 11] <VIII.11>`

.. picture:: 0373.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 14

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>` are continuously proportional, and :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>`,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 7] <VIII.7>

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`.

.. _folio-ii-0374:

.. folio:: 0374
   :page: 368

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` is to :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore also :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`.

Again, let :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   For, with the same construction, we can in a similar manner prove that :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` to :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, and :g:`$C$ <0373.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0373.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0373.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>` are continuously proportional ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0373.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$B$ <0373.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
