PROPOSITION 18.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Between two similar plane numbers there is one mean proportional number; and the plane number has to the plane number the ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

Let :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` be two similar plane numbers, and let the numbers :g:`C <0377.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` be the sides of :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, and :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0377.g1:F>` of :g:`B <0377.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0377.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 18

.. subclause::

   Now, since similar plane numbers are those which have their sides proportional, :just:`[VII. Def. 21] <VII.def.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`D <0377.g1:D>`, so is :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`.

I say then that between :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` there is one mean proportional number, and :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` has to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` has to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, or :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`, that is, of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`D <0377.g1:D>`, so is :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   therefore, alternately, as :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, so is :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` is plane, and :g:`C <0377.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` are its sides,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` has made :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`F <0377.g1:F>` has made :g:`B <0377.g1:B>`.

Now let :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` make :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` has made :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, and by multiplying :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` has made :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, so is :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`.

.. _folio-ii-0378:

.. folio:: 0378
   :page: 372

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, so is :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` is to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`, so is :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` has made :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`, and by multiplying :g:`F <0377.g1:F>` has made :g:`B <0377.g1:B>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` is to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`, so is :g:`G <0377.g1:G>` to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved that, as :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` is to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`, so is :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` is to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`, so is :g:`G <0377.g1:G>` to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`, :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` are in continued proportion.

.. assertion::

   Therefore between :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` there is one mean proportional number.

I say next that :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` also has to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is, of that which :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` has to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` or :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`A <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`, :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` are in continued proportion,

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 9] <V.def.9>

   :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` has to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which it has to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` is to :g:`G <0377.g1:G>`, so is :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>`, and so is :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <0377.g1:A>` also has to :g:`B <0377.g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which :g:`C <0377.g1:C>` has to :g:`E <0377.g1:E>` or :g:`D <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`F <0377.g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
