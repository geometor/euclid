PROPOSITION 19.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Between two similar solid numbers there fall two mean proportional numbers; and the solid number has to the similar solid number the ratio triplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

Let :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` be two similar solid numbers, and let :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` be the sides of :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, and :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0379.g1:H>` of :g:`B <0379.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since similar solid numbers are those which have their sides proportional, :just:`[VII. Def. 21] <VII.def.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, so is :g:`F <0379.g1:F>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and, as :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` is to :g:`E <0379.g1:E>`, so is :g:`G <0379.g1:G>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

I say that between :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` there fall two mean proportional numbers, and :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` has to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` has to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. picture:: 0379.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 19

For let :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` make :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, and let :g:`F <0379.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`G <0379.g1:G>` make :g:`L <0379.g1:L>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` are in the same ratio with :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` is the product of :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, and :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` the product of :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` are similar plane numbers; :just:`[VII. Def. 21] <VII.def.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore between :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` there is one mean proportional number. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

Let it be :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` is the product of :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, as was proved in the theorem preceding this. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` has made :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, and by multiplying :g:`F <0379.g1:F>` has made :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, so is :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` to :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` is to :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`, so is :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` to :g:`L <0379.g1:L>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`, :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`.

.. _folio-ii-0380:

.. folio:: 0380
   :page: 374

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, so is :g:`F <0379.g1:F>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   alternately therefore, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, so is :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` is to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, so is :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`, :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`, :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, in the ratio of :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also in the ratio of :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

Next, let :g:`E <0379.g1:E>`, :g:`H <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` make :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0379.g1:O>` respectively.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` is a solid number, and :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` are its sides,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying the product of :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` has made :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But the product of :g:`C <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` is :g:`K <0379.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` has made :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`H <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` has made :g:`B <0379.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` has made :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, and further also by multiplying :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` has made :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` is to :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`, so is :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`K <0379.g1:K>` is to :g:`M <0379.g1:M>`, so is :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`E <0379.g1:E>`, :g:`H <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` have made :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0379.g1:O>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   therefore, as :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` is to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`N <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` is to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>` and :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>` and :g:`N <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`H <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` has made :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`, and further also by multiplying :g:`L <0379.g1:L>` has made :g:`B <0379.g1:B>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` is to :g:`L <0379.g1:L>`, so is :g:`O <0379.g1:O>` to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`M <0379.g1:M>` is to :g:`L <0379.g1:L>`, so is :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`, so not only is :g:`O <0379.g1:O>` to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>`, but also :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>` and :g:`N <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`, :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the aforesaid ratios of the sides.

I say that :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` also has to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is, of the ratio which the number :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` has to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, or :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. folio:: 0381
   :page: 375

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`A <0379.g1:A>`, :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0379.g1:O>`, :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` are four numbers in continued proportion,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` has to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` has to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`. :just:`[v. Def. 10] <V.def.10>`

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` is to :g:`N <0379.g1:N>`, so it was proved that :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <0379.g1:A>` also has to :g:`B <0379.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is, of the ratio which the number :g:`C <0379.g1:C>` has to :g:`F <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`D <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`G <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`E <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`H <0379.g1:H>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
