PROPOSITION 20.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If one mean proportional number fall between two numbers, the numbers will be similar plane numbers.

For let one mean proportional number :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` fall between the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` are similar plane numbers.

Let :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33] <VII.33>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>` the same number of times that :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` measures :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`. :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

Now, as many times as :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` has made :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, so that :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>` is plane, and :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` are its sides.

.. _folio-ii-0382:

.. folio:: 0382
   :page: 376

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` are the least of the numbers which have the same ratio with :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` measures :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` the same number of times that :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` measures :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`. :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. picture:: 0382.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 20

As many times, then, as :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` measures :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` measures :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` according to the units in :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`; therefore :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` has made :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` is plane, and :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>` are its sides.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` are plane numbers.

I say next that they are also similar.

.. subclause::

   For, †\ [#f1]_ since :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` has made :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, and by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>` to :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`, that is, :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` to :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   Again,† since :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>` has made :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` is to :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` to :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` is to :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   And alternately, as :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` is to :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` to :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>` are similar plane numbers; for their sides are proportional.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **For, †since** :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>`......**27**. :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` **to** :g:`$B$ <0382.g1:B>`. The text has clearly suffered corruption here. It is not necessary to infer from other facts that, as :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>` to :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`; for this is part of the hypotheses (ll. 6, 7). Again, there is no explanation of the statement (l. 25) that :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`. It is the statement and explanation of this latter fact which are alone wanted; after which the proof proceeds in l. 28. We might therefore substitute for ll. 25—28 the following. “For, since :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` measures :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>` the same number of times that :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0382.g1:A>` [l. 8], that is, according to the units in :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` [l. 10], therefore :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0382.g1:C>`. And, since :g:`$E$ <0382.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0382.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0382.g1:G>`,” etc. etc.
