PROPOSITION 21.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two mean proportional numbers fall between two numbers, the numbers are similar solid numbers.

For let two mean proportional numbers :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>` fall between the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>` are similar solid numbers.

.. picture:: 0383.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 21

For let three numbers :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>`, the least of those which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the extremes of them :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are prime to one another. :just:`[VIII. 3] <VIII.3>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since one mean proportional number :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>` has fallen between :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 20] <VIII.20>

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are similar plane numbers.

Let, then, :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` be the sides of :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, and :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` of :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore it is manifest from the theorem before this that :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are continuously proportional in the ratio of :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>` to :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>` and that of :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` to :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are the least of the numbers which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`, and the multitude of the numbers :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` is equal to the multitude of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 14] <VII.14>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` is to :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>` to :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are prime, primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>` and the least measure those which have the same ratio with

.. _folio-ii-0384:

.. folio:: 0384
   :page: 378

.. subclause::

   them the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less, that is, the antecedent the antecedent and the consequent the consequent ; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` measures :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>` the same number of times that :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` measures :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`.

Now, as many times as :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` measures :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` has made :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` is the product of :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` by multiplying the product of :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` has made :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>` is solid, and :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>`, :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` are its sides.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` are the least of the numbers which have the same ratio as :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0383.g1:D>`, :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` measures :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>` the same number of times that :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` measures :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`.

Now, as many times as :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` measures :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, so many units let there be in :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` measures :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>` according to the units in :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>` by multiplying :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` has made :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$G$ <0383.g1:G>` is the product of :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>` by multiplying the product of :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` has made :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>` is solid, and :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>`, :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>` are its sides ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>` are solid.

I say that they are also similar.

.. subclause::

   For since :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` have made :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   therefore, as :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` is to :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>` to :g:`$C$ <0383.g1:C>`, that is, :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$E$ <0383.g1:E>` is to :g:`$F$ <0383.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>` to :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>` and :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` to :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>` is to :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, so is :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>` to :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` and :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` to :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$H$ <0383.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0383.g1:K>`, :g:`$N$ <0383.g1:N>` are the sides of :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, and :g:`$O$ <0383.g1:O>`, :g:`$L$ <0383.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0383.g1:M>` the sides of :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0383.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0383.g1:B>` are similar solid numbers.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
