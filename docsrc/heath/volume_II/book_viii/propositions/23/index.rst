PROPOSITION 23.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If four numbers be in continued proportion, and the first be cube, the fourth will also be cube.

Let :g:`$A$ <0385.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0385.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0385.g2:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0385.g2:D>` be four numbers in continued proportion, and let :g:`$A$ <0385.g2:A>` be cube;

I say that :g:`$D$ <0385.g2:D>` is also cube.

.. picture:: 0385.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 23

.. subclause::

   For, since between :g:`$A$ <0385.g2:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0385.g2:D>` there are two mean proportional numbers :g:`$B$ <0385.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0385.g2:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 21] <VIII.21>

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0385.g2:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0385.g2:D>` are similar solid numbers.

.. _folio-ii-0386:

.. folio:: 0386
   :page: 380

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0385.g2:A>` is cube;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0385.g2:D>` is also cube.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
