Commentary on Proposition VIII.24
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.24 <VIII.24>`.

.. folio:: 0386
   :page: 380

If $a : b = c^2 : d^2$, and $a$ is a square, then $b$ is also a square. For $c^2, d^2$ have one mean proportional $cd$. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

Therefore $a, b$, which are in the same ratio, have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And, since $a$ is square, $b$ must also be a square. :just:`[VIII. 22] <VIII.22>`
