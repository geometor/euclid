Commentary on Proposition VIII.24
=================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition VIII.24 <VIII.24>`.

.. folio:: 0386
   :page: 380

If :math:`a : b = c^2 : d^2`, and :math:`a` is a square, then :math:`b` is also a square. For :math:`c^2, d^2` have one mean proportional :math:`cd`. :just:`[VIII. 18] <VIII.18>`

Therefore :math:`a, b`, which are in the same ratio, have one mean proportional. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And, since :math:`a` is square, :math:`b` must also be a square. :just:`[VIII. 22] <VIII.22>`
