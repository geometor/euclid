PROPOSITION 26.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Similar plane numbers have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

Let :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>` be similar plane numbers;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. picture:: 0387.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 26

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>` are similar plane numbers,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 18] <VIII.18>

   therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>`.

.. _folio-ii-0388:

.. folio:: 0388
   :page: 382

Let it so fall, and let it be :g:`$C$ <0387.g2:C>`; and let :g:`$D$ <0387.g2:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0387.g2:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0387.g2:F>`, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0387.g2:C>`, :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>`, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]`

.. assertion:: [VIII. 2, Por.]

   therefore the extremes of them :g:`$D$ <0387.g2:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0387.g2:F>` are square.

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$D$ <0387.g2:D>` is to :g:`$F$ <0387.g2:F>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>`, and :g:`$D$ <0387.g2:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0387.g2:F>` are square,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0387.g2:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0387.g2:B>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
