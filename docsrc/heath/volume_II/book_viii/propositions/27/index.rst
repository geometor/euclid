PROPOSITION 27.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Similar solid numbers have to one another the ratio which a cube number has to a cube number.

Let :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>` be similar solid numbers;

I say that :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>` the ratio which a cube number has to a cube number.

.. picture:: 0388.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 27

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>` are similar solid numbers,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 19] <VIII.19>

   therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>`.

Let :g:`$C$ <0388.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0388.g1:D>` so fall, and let :g:`$E$ <0388.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0388.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0388.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0388.g1:H>`, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0388.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0388.g1:D>`, :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>`, and equal with them in multitude, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]`

.. assertion:: [VIII. 2, Por.]

   therefore the extremes of them :g:`$E$ <0388.g1:E>`, :g:`$H$ <0388.g1:H>` are cube.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$E$ <0388.g1:E>` is to :g:`$H$ <0388.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0388.g1:A>` also has to :g:`$B$ <0388.g1:B>` the ratio which a cube number has to a cube number.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
