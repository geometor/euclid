INDEX OF GREEK WORDS AND FORMS.

**δικόλουρος**, *twice-truncated* (of pyramidal numbers) **291**

**διπλάσιος λόγος**, *double* ratio: **διπλασίων λόγος**, *duplicate* ratio, contrasted with, **133**

**δύναμις**, power: = actual value of a submultiple in units (Nicomachus) **282**: = side of number not a complete square (i.e. *root* or *surd*) in Plato **288**, **290**: = square in Plato **294–5**
