Commentary on Book X Definition 2
=================================

Back to :ref:`Book X Definitions <X.def>`.

.. container:: center

   Εὐθεῖαι :term:`δυνάμει <in-square>` σύμμετροί εἰσιν, ὅταν τὰ ἀπ᾿ αὐτῶν τετράγωνα τῷ αὐτῷ χωρίῳ μετρῆται, ἀσύμμετροι δέ, ὅταν τοῖς ἀπ᾿ αὐτῶν τετραγώνοις μηδὲν ἐνδέχεται χωρίον κοινὸν μέτρον γενέσθαι.

*Commensurable in square* is in the Greek :term:`δυνάμει <in-square>` :term:`σύμμετρος <commensurable>`. In earlier translations (e.g. Williamson's) :term:`δυνάμει <in-square>` has been translated "in power," but, as the particular *power* represented by :term:`δύναμις <dynamis>` in Greek geometry is *square*, I have thought it best to use the latter word throughout. It will be observed that Euclid's expression :term:`*commensurable in square only* <commensurable-in-square-only>` (used in Def. 3 and constantly) corresponds to what :person:`Plato <plato>` makes :person:`Theaetetus <theaetetus>` call a *square root* (:term:`δύναμις <dynamis>`) in the sense of a *surd*. If :math:`a` is any straight line, :math:`a` and :math:`a\sqrt{m}`, or :math:`a\sqrt{m}` and :math:`a\sqrt{n}` (where :math:`m, n` are integers or arithmetical fractions in their lowest terms, proper or improper, but not square) are :term:`*commensurable in square only* <commensurable-in-square-only>`. Of course (as explained in the Porism to x. 10) all straight lines :term:`*commensurable in length* <commensurable-in-length>` (:term:`μήκει <in-length>`), in Euclid's phrase, are commensurable in square also; but not all straight lines which are *commensurable in square* are :term:`*commensurable in length* <commensurable-in-length>` as well. On the other hand, straight lines *incommensurable in square* are necessarily :term:`*incommensurable in length* <incommensurable-in-length>` also; but not all straight lines which are :term:`*incommensurable in length* <incommensurable-in-length>` are incommensurable in square. In fact, straight lines which are :term:`*commensurable in square only* <commensurable-in-square-only>` are incommensurable in length, but obviously not incommensurable in square.
