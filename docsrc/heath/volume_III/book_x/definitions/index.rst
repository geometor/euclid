DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _X.def.1:

1. Those magnitudes are said to be :term:`**commensurable** <commensurable>` which are :term:`measured <part-aliquot>` by the same measure, and those :term:`**incommensurable** <incommensurable>` which cannot have any common measure.

.. _X.def.2:

2. :term:`Straight lines <straight-line>` are :term:`**commensurable in square** <commensurable-in-square>` when the :term:`squares <square>` on them are :term:`measured <part-aliquot>` by the same area, and :term:`**incommensurable in square** <incommensurable-in-square>` when the squares on them cannot possibly have any area as a common measure.

.. _X.def.3:

3. With these hypotheses, it is proved that there exist :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` infinite in multitude which are :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` and incommensurable respectively, some in length only, and others in square also, with an assigned straight line. Let then the assigned straight line be called :term:`**rational** <rational>`, and those straight lines which are commensurable with it, whether in length and in square or in square only, :term:`**rational** <rational>`, but those which are incommensurable with it :term:`**irrational** <irrational>`.

.. _X.def.4:

4. And let the :term:`square <square>` on the assigned :term:`straight line <straight-line>` be called :term:`**rational** <rational>` and those areas which are :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` with it :term:`**rational** <rational>`, but those which are incommensurable with it :term:`**irrational** <irrational>`, and the straight lines which produce them :term:`**irrational** <irrational>`, that is, in case the areas are squares, the sides themselves, but in case they are any other :term:`rectilineal figures <rectilineal-figure>`, the straight lines on which are described squares equal to them.
