DEFINITIONS III.
================

.. _X.def.iii.1:

**1.** Given a :term:`rational <rational>` :term:`straight line <straight-line>` and an apotome, if the :term:`square <square>` on the whole be greater than the square on the annex by the square on a straight line :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` in length with the whole, and the whole be commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, let the apotome be called a :term:`**first apotome** <first-apotome>`.

.. _X.def.iii.2:

**2.** But if the annex be :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` in length with the :term:`rational <rational>` :term:`straight line <straight-line>` set out, and the :term:`square <square>` on the whole be greater than that on the annex by the square on a straight line commensurable with the whole, let the apotome be called a :term:`**second apotome** <second-apotome>`.

.. _X.def.iii.3:

**3.** But if neither be :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` in length with the :term:`rational <rational>` :term:`straight line <straight-line>` set out, and the :term:`square <square>` on the whole be greater than the square on the annex by the square on a straight line commensurable with the whole, let the apotome be called a :term:`**third apotome** <third-apotome>`.

.. _X.def.iii.4:

**4.** Again, if the :term:`square <square>` on the whole be greater than the square on the annex by the square on a :term:`straight line <straight-line>` incommensurable with the whole, then, if the whole be :term:`commensurable <commensurable>` in length with the :term:`rational <rational>` straight line set out, let the apotome be called a :term:`**fourth apotome** <fourth-apotome>` ;

.. _X.def.iii.5:

**5.** if the annex be so :term:`commensurable <commensurable>`, a :term:`**fifth** <fifth-apotome>` ;

.. _X.def.iii.6:

**6.** and, if neither, a :term:`**sixth** <sixth-apotome>`.
