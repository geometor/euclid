PROPOSITION 10.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find two straight lines incommensurable, the one in length only, and the other in square also, with an assigned straight line.

Let :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` be the assigned straight line ; thus it is required to find two straight lines incommensurable, the one in length only, and the other in square also, with :g:`A <0036.g1:A>`.

Let two numbers :g:`B <0036.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0036.g1:C>` be set out which have not to one
another the ratio which a square number has to a square number, that is, which are not similar plane numbers ; and let it be contrived that, as :g:`B <0036.g1:B>` is to :g:`C <0036.g1:C>`, so is the square on :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` to the square on :g:`D <0036.g1:D>` —for we have learnt how to do this— :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. _folio-iii-0036:

.. folio:: 0036
   :page: 32

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is commensurable with the square on :g:`D <0036.g1:D>`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. picture:: 0036.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 10 diagram

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`B <0036.g1:B>` has not to :g:`C <0036.g1:C>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` to the square on :g:`D <0036.g1:D>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number ; therefore :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`D <0036.g1:D>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

Let :g:`E <0036.g1:E>` be taken a mean proportional between :g:`A <0036.g1:A>`, :g:`D <0036.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is to :g:`D <0036.g1:D>`, so is the square on :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` to the square on :g:`E <0036.g1:E>`. :just:`[V. Def. 9] <V.def.9>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`D <0036.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is also incommensurable with the square on :g:`E <0036.g1:E>` ; :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>` therefore :g:`A <0036.g1:A>` is incommensurable in square with :g:`E <0036.g1:E>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore two straight lines :g:`D <0036.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0036.g1:E>` have been found incommensurable, :g:`D <0036.g1:D>` in length only, and :g:`E <0036.g1:E>` in square and of course in length also, with the assigned straight line :g:`A <0036.g1:A>`.]
