Commentary on Proposition X.16
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.16 <X.16>`.

.. folio:: 0045
   :page: 41

If :math:`a` be the given straight line, and :math:`x` the side of the square by which the applied rectangle is to be deficient, the rectangle is equal to :math:`ax - x^2`, which is of course equal to :math:`x(a - x)`. The rectangle may be written :math:`xy`, where :math:`x + y = a`. Given the area :math:`x(a - x)`, or :math:`xy` (where :math:`x + y = a`), two different *applications* will give rectangles equal to this area, the sides of the defect being :math:`x` or :math:`a - x` (:math:`x` or :math:`y`) respectively; but the second mode of expression shows that the rectangles do not differ in form but only in position.
