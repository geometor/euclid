PROPOSITION 25.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in square only is either rational or medial.

.. picture:: 0060.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 25

For let the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` be contained by the medial straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />` which are commensurable in square only; I say that :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` is either rational or medial.

For on :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />` let the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` be described;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` is medial.

Let a rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />` be set out, to :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />` let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` equal to :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, producing :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` as breadth, to :g:`$HM$ <0060.g1:/ H M />` let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram :g:`$MK$ <0060.g1:< M K >>` equal to :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />` as breadth, and further to :g:`$KN$ <0060.g1:/ K N />` let there be similarly applied :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>` equal to :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>`, producing :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` are in a straight line.

.. subclause::

   Since then each of the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` is medial, and :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>` is equal to :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>`, and :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>` is commensurable with :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   And, as :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`, so is :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` to :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` are rational straight lines commensurable in length;

.. assertion:: [X. 19] <X.19>

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$DB$ <0060.g1:/ D B />` is equal to :g:`$BA$ <0060.g1:/ B A />`, and :g:`$OB$ <0060.g1:/ O B />` to :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$DB$ <0060.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$BO$ <0060.g1:/ B O />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$DB$ <0060.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$DA$ <0060.g1:< D A >>` to :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>` and, as :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BO$ <0060.g1:/ B O />`, so is :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` to :g:`$CO$ <0060.g1:< C O >>`; :just:`[id.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$DA$ <0060.g1:< D A >>` is to :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>`, so is :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` to :g:`$CO$ <0060.g1:< C O >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>` is equal to :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>`, :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` to :g:`$MK$ <0060.g1:< M K >>` and :g:`$CO$ <0060.g1:< C O >>` to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` is to :g:`$MK$ <0060.g1:< M K >>`, so is :g:`$MK$ <0060.g1:< M K >>` to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` is to :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />`, so is :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />`; :just:`[VI. 1, V. 11] <VI.1, V.11>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` is equal to the square on :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />` is also rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, if it is commensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 19] <X.19>

   :g:`$HN$ <0060.g1:< H N >>` is rational;

.. subclause::

   but, if it is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$KH$ <0060.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HM$ <0060.g1:/ H M />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and therefore :g:`$HN$ <0060.g1:< H N >>` is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$HN$ <0060.g1:< H N >>` is either rational or medial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$HN$ <0060.g1:< H N >>` is equal to :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` is either rational or medial.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
