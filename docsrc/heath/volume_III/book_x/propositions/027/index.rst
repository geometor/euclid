PROPOSITION 27.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle.

Let two rational straight lines :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` commensurable in square only be set out; let :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` be taken a mean proportional between :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, :just:`[VI. 13] <VI.13>` and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`. :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` are rational and commensurable in square only,

.. assertion::

   the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, that is, the square on :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`, is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. assertion:: [X. 21] <X.21>

   Therefore :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` is medial.

.. picture:: 0064.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 27 diagram

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`, and :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` are also commensurable in square only.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` is medial;

.. assertion:: [X. 23, addition]

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` is also medial.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` are medial and commensurable in square only.

I say that they also contain a rational rectangle.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   therefore, alternately, as :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` to :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0064.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` to :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>` is to :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` to :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>`.

.. _folio-iii-0065:

.. folio:: 0065
   :page: 61

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` is also rational.

.. qed::

   Therefore medial straight lines commensurable in square only have been found which contain a rational rectangle. :just:`Q. E. D.`
