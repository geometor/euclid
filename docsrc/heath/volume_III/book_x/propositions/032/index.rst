PROPOSITION 32.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find two medial straight lines commensurable in square only, containing a medial rectangle, and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable with the greater.

.. _folio-iii-0076:

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: 72

Let there be set out three rational straight lines :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` commensurable in square only, and such that the square on :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is greater than the square on :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`, :just:`[X. 29] <X.29>` and let the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` be equal to the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0076.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 32 diagram

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is medial ; therefore :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is also medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

Let the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` be equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Then since, as the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>` is to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` to :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`, while the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, and the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`, so is the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` to the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is to the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is commensurable with :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` in square only ;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is also commensurable with :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` in square only.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is medial ;

.. assertion:: [X. 23, addition]

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And, since, as :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`, while the square on :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is greater than the square on :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` will be greater than the square on :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`. :just:`[X. 14] <X.14>`

I say next that the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   For, since the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`, while the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0076.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` is medial, :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` is also medial.

.. qed::

   Therefore two medial straight lines :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>`, commensurable in square only, and containing a medial rectangle, have been found such that the square on the greater is greater than the

.. _folio-iii-0077:

.. folio:: 0077
   :page: 73

.. qed::

   square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable with the greater.

.. assertion::

   Similarly again it can be proved that the square on :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>` is greater than the square on :g:`$E$ <0076.g1:E>` by the square on a straight line *incommensurable* with :g:`$D$ <0076.g1:D>`, when the square on :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>` is greater than the square on :g:`$C$ <0076.g1:C>` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$A$ <0076.g1:A>`. :just:`[X. 30] <X.30>`
