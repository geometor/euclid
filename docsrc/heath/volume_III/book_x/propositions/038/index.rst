PROPOSITION 38.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a medial rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational; and let it be called a :term:`**second bimedial straight line** <second-bimedial>`.

For let two medial straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` commensurable in square only and containing a medial rectangle be added together; I say that :g:`$AC$ <0089.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. picture:: 0089.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 38

For let a rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />` be set out, and let the parallelogram :g:`$DF$ <0089.g1:< D F >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0089.g1:/ A C />` be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />`, producing :g:`$DG$ <0089.g1:/ D G />` as breadth. :just:`[I. 44] <I.44>`

.. subclause::

   Then, since the square on :g:`$AC$ <0089.g1:/ A C />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` and twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, :just:`[II. 4] <II.4>` let :g:`$EH$ <0089.g1:< E H >>`, equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />`;

.. _folio-iii-0090:

.. folio:: 0090
   :page: 86

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$HF$ <0089.g1:< H F >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` are also medial.

.. subclause::

   But, by hypothesis, twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EH$ <0089.g1:< E H >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, while :g:`$FH$ <0089.g1:< F H >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$EH$ <0089.g1:< E H >>`, :g:`$HF$ <0089.g1:< H F >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DH$ <0089.g1:/ D H />`, :g:`$HG$ <0089.g1:/ H G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[x. 22]`

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, and, as :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />` to the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />` is incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   But the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[x. 15]` and twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` is commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[x. 6]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />` is incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[x. 13]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EH$ <0089.g1:< E H >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`$HF$ <0089.g1:< H F >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0089.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0089.g1:/ B C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EH$ <0089.g1:< E H >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$HF$ <0089.g1:< H F >>`, so that :g:`$DH$ <0089.g1:/ D H />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$HG$ <0089.g1:/ H G />`. :just:`[VI. 1, x. 11]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$DH$ <0089.g1:/ D H />`, :g:`$HG$ <0089.g1:/ H G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; so that :g:`$DG$ <0089.g1:/ D G />` is irrational. :just:`[x. 36]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DE$ <0089.g1:/ D E />` is rational; and the rectangle contained by an irrational and a rational straight line is irrational; :just:`[cf. x. 20]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the area :g:`$DF$ <0089.g1:< D F >>` is irrational, and the side of the square equal to it is irrational. :just:`[x. Def. 4] <X.def.4>`

.. _folio-iii-0091:

.. folio:: 0091
   :page: 87

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AC$ <0089.g1:/ A C />` is the side of the square equal to :g:`$DF$ <0089.g1:< D F >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0089.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. qed::

   And let it be called a :term:`**second bimedial straight line** <second-bimedial>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
