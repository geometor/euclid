Commentary on Proposition X.39
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.39 <X.39>`.

.. folio:: 0092

Here :math:`x`, :math:`y` are of the form found in x. 33, viz.

.. math::

   \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}},\quad \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}.

By hypothesis, the rectangle :math:`xy` is medial; therefore :math:`2xy` is medial.

Also :math:`(x^2+y^2)` is a rational area.

Therefore

.. math::

   x^2+y^2 \mathbin{\smile} 2xy,

whence

.. math::

   (x+y)^2 \mathbin{\smile} (x^2+y^2),

so that :math:`(x+y)^2`, and therefore :math:`(x+y)`, is irrational.

The irrational straight line :math:`\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}} + \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}` is called a :term:`**major** <major>` (irrational) straight line.

This and the corresponding *minor* irrational found in x. 76 are the positive roots of the equation

.. math::

   x^4 - 2\rho^2 \cdot x^2 + \frac{k^2}{1+k^2}\rho^4 = 0.
