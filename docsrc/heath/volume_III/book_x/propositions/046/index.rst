PROPOSITION 46.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The side of a rational plus a medial area is divided at one point only.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0103.g1:/ A B />` be the side of a rational plus a medial area divided at :g:`$C$ <0103.g1:C>`, so that :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` are incommensurable in square and make the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` medial, but twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` rational; :just:`[X. 40] <X.40>` I say that :g:`$AB$ <0103.g1:/ A B />` is not so divided at another point.

.. picture:: 0103.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 46 diagram

For, if possible, let it be divided at :g:`$D$ <0103.g1:D>` also, so that :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` are also incommensurable in square and make the sum of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` medial, but twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` rational.

.. subclause::

   Since then that by which twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` differs from twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` is also that by which the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` differ from the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />`,

.. subclause::

   while twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` exceeds twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` by a rational area,

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0103.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0103.g1:/ D B />` also exceed the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0103.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0103.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area, though they are medial:

.. assertion:: [X. 26] <X.26>

   which is impossible.

.. qed::

   Therefore the side of a rational plus a medial area is not divided at different points; therefore it is divided at one point only. :just:`Q. E. D.`
