Commentary on Proposition X.49
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.49 <X.49>`.

.. folio:: 0109
   :page: 105

Taking a rational straight line :math:`kp` commensurable in length with :math:`\rho`, and selecting numbers of the same form as before, viz. :math:`p(m^2 - n^2)`, :math:`pn^2`, we put

.. math::

   p(m^2 - n^2) : pm^2 = k^2 p^2 : x^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1),

so that

.. math::

   x = kp \frac{m}{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}

.. math::

   = kp \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}}, \text{ say} \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(2).

Just as before, :math:`x` is rational and :math:`\sim kp`, whence :math:`kp + x` is a binomial straight line.

By (1),

.. math::

   x^2 > k^2 p^2.

.. _folio-iii-0110:

.. folio:: 0110
   :page: 106

Let

.. math::

   x^2 - k^2 p^2 = y^2,

whence, from (1),

.. math::

   pm^2 : pn^2 = x^2 : y^2,

and :math:`y` is therefore rational and :math:`\sim x`.

The greater term of the binomial straight line is :math:`x` and the lesser :math:`kp`, and

.. math::

   \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + kp

satisfies the definition of the second binomial straight line.

The corresponding :term:`*second apotome* <second-apotome>` [x. 86] is

.. math::

   \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} - kp.

The equation of which the two expressions are the roots is

.. math::

   x^2 - \frac{2kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} \cdot x + \frac{\lambda^2}{1 - \lambda^2} k^2 p^2 = 0,

or

.. math::

   x^2 - 2ax + \lambda^2 a^2 = 0,

where

.. math::

   a = \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}}.
