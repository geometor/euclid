PROPOSITION 49.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To find the second binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0108.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 49 Diagram

Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0108.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0108.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that the sum of them :g:`$AB$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0108.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number, but has not to :g:`$AC$ <0108.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; let a rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0108.g1:D>` be set out, and let :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$D$ <0108.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` is rational.

Let it be contrived then that, as the number :g:`$CA$ <0108.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0108.g1:/ A B />`, so also is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the number :g:`$CA$ <0108.g1:/ A C />` has not to :g:`$AB$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number, neither has the

.. _folio-iii-0109:

.. folio:: 0109
   :page: 105

.. subclause::

   square on :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>` therefore :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EG$ <0108.g1:/ E G />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

It is next to be proved that it is also a second binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For since, inversely, as the number :g:`$BA$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0108.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$GF$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$FE$ <0108.g1:/ E F />`, while :g:`$BA$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$AC$ <0108.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$GF$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FE$ <0108.g1:/ E F />`.

Let the squares on :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$H$ <0108.g1:H>` be equal to the square on :g:`$GF$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$AB$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0108.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0108.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0108.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0108.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` also has to the square on :g:`$H$ <0108.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0108.g1:H>`; :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>` so that the square on :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FE$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$FG$ <0108.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$FE$ <0108.g1:/ E F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and :g:`$EF$ <0108.g1:/ E F />`, the lesser term, is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0108.g1:D>` set out.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$EG$ <0108.g1:/ E G />` is a second binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
