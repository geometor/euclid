PROPOSITION 55.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the second binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line which is called a first bimedial.

For let the area :g:`$ABCD$ <0125.g1:< A B C D >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />` and the second binomial :g:`$AD$ <0125.g1:/ A D />` ; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0125.g1:< A C >>` is a first bimedial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AD$ <0125.g1:/ A D />` is a second binomial straight line, let it be divided into its terms at :g:`$E$ <0125.g1:E>`, so that :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is the greater term ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, the square on :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />`, and the lesser term :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 2]`

Let :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` be bisected at :g:`$F$ <0125.g1:F>`,
and let there be applied to :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` equal to the square on :g:`$EF$ <0125.g1:/ E F />` and deficient by a square figure ;

.. _folio-iii-0125:

.. folio:: 0125
   :page: 121

.. assertion:: [X. 17] <X.17>

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />`.

Through :g:`$G$ <0125.g1:G>`, :g:`$E$ <0125.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0125.g1:F>` let :g:`$GH$ <0125.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$EK$ <0125.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$FL$ <0125.g1:/ F L />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0125.g1:/ D C />`, let the square :g:`$SN$ <0125.g1:< S N >>` be constructed equal to the parallelogram :g:`$AH$ <0125.g1:< A H >>`, and the square :g:`$NQ$ <0125.g1:< N Q >>` equal to :g:`$GK$ <0125.g1:< G K >>`, and let them be placed so that :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />` is in a straight line with :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$RN$ <0125.g1:/ R N />` is also in a straight line with :g:`$NP$ <0125.g1:/ N P />`.

.. picture:: 0125.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 55

Let the square :g:`$SQ$ <0125.g1:< S Q >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   It is then manifest from what was proved before that :g:`$MR$ <0125.g1:< M R >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$SN$ <0125.g1:< S N >>`, :g:`$NQ$ <0125.g1:< N Q >>` and is equal to :g:`$EL$ <0125.g1:< E L >>`, and that :g:`$MO$ <0125.g1:/ M O />` is the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0125.g1:< A C >>`.

It is now to be proved that :g:`$MO$ <0125.g1:/ M O />` is a first bimedial straight line.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />`, while :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with :g:`$EG$ <0125.g1:/ G E />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is also commensurable with each of the straight lines :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />` ;

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` are also incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$BA$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` and :g:`$BA$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` are pairs of rational straight lines commensurable in square only\ [#f1]_ ; so that each of the rectangles :g:`$AH$ <0125.g1:< A H >>`, :g:`$GK$ <0125.g1:< G K >>` is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. assertion::

   Hence each of the squares :g:`$SN$ <0125.g1:< S N >>`, :g:`$NQ$ <0125.g1:< N Q >>` is medial.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />` are also medial.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AH$ <0125.g1:< A H >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$GK$ <0125.g1:< G K >>`, :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>` that is, :g:`$SN$ <0125.g1:< S N >>` is commensurable with :g:`$NQ$ <0125.g1:< N Q >>`, that is, the square on :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />` with the square on :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />`.

.. _folio-iii-0126:

.. folio:: 0126
   :page: 122

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />`, while :g:`$AE$ <0125.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$ED$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0125.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0125.g1:/ E F />` ; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>` so that :g:`$AH$ <0125.g1:< A H >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$EL$ <0125.g1:< E L >>`, that is, :g:`$SN$ <0125.g1:< S N >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$MR$ <0125.g1:< M R >>`, that is, :g:`$PN$ <0125.g1:/ N P />` with :g:`$NR$ <0125.g1:/ R N />`, :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>` that is, :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />` were proved to be both medial and commensurable in square ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only.

I say next that they also contain a rational rectangle.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$DE$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` is, by hypothesis, commensurable with each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$EF$ <0125.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 12] <X.12>

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0125.g1:/ E F />` is also commensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0125.g1:/ E K />`.

.. subclause::

   And each of them is rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EL$ <0125.g1:< E L >>`, that is, :g:`$MR$ <0125.g1:< M R >>` is rational, :just:`[X. 19] <X.19>` and :g:`$MR$ <0125.g1:< M R >>` is the rectangle :g:`$MN$ <0125.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0125.g1:/ N O />`.

.. subclause::

   But, if two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a rational rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational and is called a first bimedial straight line. :just:`[X. 37] <X.37>`

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$MO$ <0125.g1:/ M O />` is a first bimedial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`

.. [#f1] **Therefore** :g:`$BA$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />` **and** :g:`$BA$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` **are pairs of rational straight lines commensurable in square only.** The text has "Therefore :g:`$BA$ <0125.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only," which I have altered because it would naturally convey the impression that *any two* of the three straight lines are commensurable in square only, whereas :g:`$AG$ <0125.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0125.g1:/ G E />` are commensurable in length (l. 18), and it is only the other two pairs which are commensurable in square only.
