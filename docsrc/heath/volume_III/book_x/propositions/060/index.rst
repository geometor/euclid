PROPOSITION 60.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The square on the binomial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the first binomial.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0136.g1:/ A B />` be a binomial straight line divided into its terms at :g:`$C$ <0136.g1:C>`, so that :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />` is the greater term ; let a rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` be set out, and let :g:`$DEFG$ <0136.g1:< D E F G >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0136.g1:/ A B />` be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` producing :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` as its breadth ; I say that :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` is a first binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0136.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 60

For let there be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` the rectangle :g:`$DH$ <0136.g1:< D H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0136.g1:< K L >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BC$ <0136.g1:/ B C />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`, is equal to :g:`$MF$ <0136.g1:< M F >>`.

Let :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` be bisected at :g:`$N$ <0136.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$NO$ <0136.g1:/ N O />` be drawn parallel [to :g:`$ML$ <0136.g1:/ M L />` or :g:`$GF$ <0136.g1:/ G F />`].

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$MO$ <0136.g1:< M O >>`, :g:`$NF$ <0136.g1:< N F >>` is equal to once the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AB$ <0136.g1:/ A B />` is a binomial divided into its terms at :g:`$C$ <0136.g1:C>`,

.. folio:: 0137
   :page: 133

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ; :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>` therefore the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational and commensurable with one another, so that the sum of the squares or :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` is also rational. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`$DL$ <0136.g1:< D L >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DL$ <0136.g1:< D L >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,

.. assertion:: [X. 21] <X.21>

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`, that is :g:`$MF$ <0136.g1:< M F >>`, is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$ML$ <0136.g1:/ M L />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$ML$ <0136.g1:/ M L />`, that is, :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MD$ <0136.g1:/ M D />` is also rational and is commensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />`.

.. subclause::

   And they are rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ; therefore :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

It is next to be proved that it is also a first binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   Since the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` is a mean proportional between the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`, :just:`[cf. Lemma after X. 53]`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MO$ <0136.g1:< M O >>` is also a mean proportional between :g:`$DH$ <0136.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0136.g1:< K L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$DH$ <0136.g1:< D H >>` is to :g:`$MO$ <0136.g1:< M O >>`, so is :g:`$MO$ <0136.g1:< M O >>` to :g:`$KL$ <0136.g1:< K L >>`, that is, as :g:`$DK$ <0136.g1:/ D K />` is to :g:`$MN$ <0136.g1:/ M N />`, so is :g:`$MN$ <0136.g1:/ M N />` to :g:`$MK$ <0136.g1:/ M K />` ; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$DK$ <0136.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0136.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$MN$ <0136.g1:/ M N />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DH$ <0136.g1:< D H >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$KL$ <0136.g1:< K L >>`, so that :g:`$DK$ <0136.g1:/ D K />` is also commensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0136.g1:/ K M />`. :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`

.. _folio-iii-0138:

.. folio:: 0138
   :page: 134

.. subclause::

   And, since the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />` are greater than twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`, :just:`[Lemma]`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DL$ <0136.g1:< D L >>` is also greater than :g:`$MF$ <0136.g1:< M F >>`, so that :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />` is also greater than :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />`. :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. subclause::

   And the rectangle :g:`$DK$ <0136.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0136.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$MN$ <0136.g1:/ M N />`, that is, to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />`, and :g:`$DK$ <0136.g1:/ D K />` is commensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0136.g1:/ K M />`.

.. subclause::

   But, if there be two unequal straight lines, and to the greater there be applied a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, and if it divide it into commensurable parts, the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable with the greater; :just:`[X. 17] <X.17>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` are rational, and :g:`$DM$ <0136.g1:/ D M />`, which is the greater term, is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` set out.

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 1]

   Therefore :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` is a first binomial straight line.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
