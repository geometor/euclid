PROPOSITION 61.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The square on the first bimedial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the second binomial.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0139.g1:/ A B />` be a first bimedial straight line divided into its medials at :g:`$C$ <0139.g1:C>`, of which medials :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />` is the greater; let a rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />` be set out, and let there be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />` the parallelogram :g:`$DF$ <0139.g1:< D F >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0139.g1:/ A B />`, producing :g:`$DG$ <0139.g1:/ D G />` as its breadth; I say that :g:`$DG$ <0139.g1:/ D G />` is a second binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0139.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 61

For let the same construction as before be made.

.. _folio-iii-0140:

.. folio:: 0140
   :page: 136

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0139.g1:/ A B />` is a first bimedial divided at :g:`$C$ <0139.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only, and containing a rational rectangle, :just:`[X. 37] <X.37>` so that the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />` are also medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. assertion:: [X. 15 and 23, Por.]

   Therefore :g:`$DL$ <0139.g1:< D L >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MD$ <0139.g1:/ M D />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />` is rational,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$MF$ <0139.g1:< M F >>` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$ML$ <0139.g1:/ M L />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />` is also rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$ML$ <0139.g1:/ M L />`, that is, :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />`; :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>`

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0139.g1:/ D M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />`.

.. subclause::

   And they are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0139.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;

.. assertion:: [X. 36] <X.36>

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0139.g1:/ D G />` is binomial.

It is next to be proved that it is also a second binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For, since the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />` are greater than twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DL$ <0139.g1:< D L >>` is also greater than :g:`$MF$ <0139.g1:< M F >>`, so that :g:`$DM$ <0139.g1:/ D M />` is also greater than :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />`. :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$AC$ <0139.g1:/ A C />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$CB$ <0139.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DH$ <0139.g1:< D H >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$KL$ <0139.g1:< K L >>`, so that :g:`$DK$ <0139.g1:/ D K />` is also commensurable with :g:`$KM$ <0139.g1:/ K M />`. :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And the rectangle :g:`$DK$ <0139.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0139.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$MN$ <0139.g1:/ M N />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$DM$ <0139.g1:/ D M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$DM$ <0139.g1:/ D M />`. :just:`[X. 17] <X.17>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$MG$ <0139.g1:/ M G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0139.g1:/ D E />`.

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 2]

   Therefore :g:`$DG$ <0139.g1:/ D G />` is a second binomial straight line.
