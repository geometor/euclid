PROPOSITION 66.
===============

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable in length with a binomial straight line is itself also binomial and the same in order.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />` be binomial, and let :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`;

.. picture:: 0149.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 66 Diagram

I say that :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` is binomial and the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`.

For, since :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />` is binomial, let it be divided into its terms at :g:`$E$ <0149.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` be the greater term;

.. _folio-iii-0150:

.. folio:: 0150
   :page: 146

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`; :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore also the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` is to the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` as :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[V. 19] <V.19>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` with :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` are also rational.

.. subclause:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   And, as :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, alternately, as :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` are commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` are also commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And they are rational;

.. assertion:: [X. 36] <X.36>

   therefore :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` is binomial.

I say next that it is the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`.

.. subclause::

   For the square on :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

.. subclause::

   If then the square on :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />` will also be greater than the square on :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`. :just:`[X. 14] <X.14>`

.. subclause::

   And, if :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with the rational straight line set out, :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />` will also be commensurable with it, :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>`

.. assertion::

   and for this reason each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` is a first binomial, that is, the same in order. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 1]`

.. subclause::

   But, if :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with the rational straight line set out, :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` is also commensurable with it, :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>`

.. assertion::

   and for this reason again :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` will be the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, for each of them will be a second binomial. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 2]`

.. _folio-iii-0151:

.. folio:: 0151
   :page: 147

.. subclause::

   But, if neither of the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with the rational straight line set out, neither of the straight lines :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` will be commensurable with it, :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. assertion::

   and each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` is a third binomial. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 3]`

.. subclause::

   But, if the square on :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />` is also greater than the square on :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`. :just:`[X. 14] <X.14>`

.. subclause::

   And, if :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with the rational straight line set out, :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />` is also commensurable with it,

.. assertion::

   and each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` is a fourth binomial. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 4]`

.. subclause::

   But, if :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` is so commensurable, so is :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` also,

.. assertion::

   and each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` will be a fifth binomial. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 5]`

.. subclause::

   But, if neither of the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0149.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0149.g1:/ E B />` is so commensurable, neither of the straight lines :g:`$CF$ <0149.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0149.g1:/ F D />` is commensurable with the rational straight line set out,

.. assertion::

   and each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0149.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0149.g1:/ C D />` will be a sixth binomial. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 6]`

.. qed::

   Hence a straight line commensurable in length with a binomial straight line is binomial and the same in order. :just:`Q. E. D.`
