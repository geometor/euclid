PROPOSITION 68.
===============

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with a major straight line is itself also major.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` be major, and let :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />`; I say that :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` is major.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` be divided at :g:`$E$ <0153.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial. :just:`[x. 39]`

.. picture:: 0153.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 68

Let the same construction be made as before.

.. subclause::

   Then since, as :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion:: [v. 11]

   therefore also, as :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` are also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />` respectively. :just:`[x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`, alternately also, as :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`; :just:`[v. 16]`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`, as :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0153.g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0153.g1:/ D F />`; :just:`[v. 18]` therefore also, as the square on :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to the square on :g:`$BE$ <0153.g1:/ B E />`, so is the square on :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` to the square on :g:`$DF$ <0153.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[vi. 20]`

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that, as the square on :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to the square on :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, so also is the square on :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` to the square on :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the square on :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />`, so is the square on :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` to the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`; therefore also, alternately, as the square on :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is to the square on :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />`, so are the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` to the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[v. 16]`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$AB$ <0153.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` are also commensurable with the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   And the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` together are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />` together are rational.

.. assertion::

   Similarly also twice the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   And twice the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0153.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0153.g1:/ E B />` is medial;

.. assertion:: [x. 23, Por.]

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />` is also medial.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CF$ <0153.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0153.g1:/ F D />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make, at the same time, the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial; therefore the whole :g:`$CD$ <0153.g1:/ C D />` is the irrational straight line called major. :just:`[x. 39]`

.. qed::

   Therefore a straight line commensurable with the major straight line is major. :just:`Q. E. D.`
