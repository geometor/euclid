PROPOSITION 69.
===============

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with the side of a rational plus a medial area is itself also the side of a rational plus a medial area.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0154.g1:/ A B />` be the side of a rational plus a medial area, and let :g:`$CD$ <0154.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0154.g1:/ A B />`; it is to be proved that :g:`$CD$ <0154.g1:/ C D />` is also the side of a rational plus a medial area.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0154.g1:/ A B />` be divided into its straight lines at :g:`$E$ <0154.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0154.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0154.g1:/ E B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. :just:`[x. 40]`

.. picture:: 0154.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 69

Let the same construction be made as before.

.. assertion::

   We can then prove similarly that :g:`$CF$ <0154.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0154.g1:/ F D />` are incommensurable in square, and the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0154.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0154.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0154.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0154.g1:/ F D />`, and the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0154.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0154.g1:/ E B />` with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0154.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0154.g1:/ F D />`; so that the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0154.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0154.g1:/ F D />` is also medial, and the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0154.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0154.g1:/ F D />` rational.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0154.g1:/ C D />` is the side of a rational plus a medial area. :just:`Q. E. D.`
