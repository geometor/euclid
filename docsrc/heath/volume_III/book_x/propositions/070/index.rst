PROPOSITION 70.
===============

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with the side of the sum of two medial areas is the side of the sum of two medial areas.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0155.g1:/ A B />` be the side of the sum of two medial areas, and :g:`$CD$ <0155.g1:/ C D />` commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0155.g1:/ A B />`; it is to be proved that :g:`$CD$ <0155.g1:/ C D />` is also the side of the sum of two medial areas.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AB$ <0155.g1:/ A B />` is the side of the sum of two medial areas,

let it be divided into its straight lines at :g:`$E$ <0155.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0155.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0155.g1:/ E B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, the rectangle contained by them medial, and furthermore the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0155.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0155.g1:/ E B />` incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0155.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0155.g1:/ E B />`. :just:`[x. 41]`

.. picture:: 0155.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 70

Let the same construction be made as before.

.. assertion::

   We can then prove similarly that :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />` are also incommensurable in square, the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0155.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0155.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />`, and the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0155.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0155.g1:/ E B />` with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />`; so that the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />` is also medial, the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />` is medial, and moreover the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />` is incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0155.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0155.g1:/ F D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0155.g1:/ C D />` is the side of the sum of two medial areas. :just:`Q. E. D.`
