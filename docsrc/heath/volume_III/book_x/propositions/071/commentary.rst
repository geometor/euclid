Commentary on Proposition X.71
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.71 <X.71>`.

.. folio:: 0158
   :page: 154

A rational area being of the form :math:`k\rho^2`, and a medial area of the form :math:`\sqrt\lambda . \rho^2`, the problem is to classify

.. math::

   \sqrt{k\rho^2 + \sqrt\lambda . \rho^2}

according to the different possible relations between :math:`k, \lambda`.

Put

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   \sigma u &= k\rho^2, \\
   \sigma v &= \sqrt\lambda . \rho^2.
   \end{aligned}

Then, since the former rectangle is rational, the latter medial, :math:`u` is rational and :math:`\frown \sigma`, :math:`v` is rational and :math:`\smile \sigma`.

Also the rectangles are incommensurable; so that

.. math::

   u \smile v.

Hence :math:`u, v` are rational and :math:`\sim`; whence :math:`(u + v)` is a binomial straight line.

.. _folio-iii-0159:

.. folio:: 0159
   :page: 155

The possibilities now are as follows :

I. :math:`u > v`. Then either

(1) :math:`\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \frown u`, or (2) :math:`\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \smile u`, while in both cases :math:`u \frown \sigma`.

In case (1) :math:`(u + v)` is a :term:`*first binomial* <first-binomial>` straight line, and in case (2) :math:`(u + v)` is a :term:`*fourth binomial* <fourth-binomial>` straight line.

Thus :math:`\sqrt{\sigma (u + v)}` is either (1) a *binomial* straight line :just:`[X. 54] <X.54>` or (2) a *major* irrational straight line :just:`[X. 57] <X.57>`.

II. :math:`v > u`. Then either

(1) :math:`\sqrt{v^2 - u^2} \frown v`, or (2) :math:`\sqrt{v^2 - u^2} \smile v`, while in both cases :math:`v \smile \sigma`, but :math:`u \frown \sigma`.

Hence, in case (1), :math:`(v + u)` is a :term:`*second binomial* <second-binomial>` straight line, and, in case (2), :math:`(v + u)` is a :term:`*fifth binomial* <fifth-binomial>` straight line.

Thus :math:`\sqrt{\sigma (v + u)}` is either (1) a :term:`*first bimedial* <first-bimedial>` straight line :just:`[X. 55] <X.55>`, or (2) a *side of a rational plus a medial area* :just:`[X. 58] <X.58>`.
