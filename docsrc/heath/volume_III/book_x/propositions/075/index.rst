PROPOSITION 75.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If from a medial straight line there be subtracted a medial straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called a second apotome of a medial straight line.

For from the medial straight line :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />` let there be subtracted the medial straight line :g:`$CB$ <0165.g1:/ C B />` which is commensurable with the whole :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />` in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`, which it contains with the whole :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, is medial; :just:`[X. 28] <X.28>`

I say that the remainder :g:`$AC$ <0165.g1:/ A C />` is irrational; and let it be called a second apotome of a medial straight line.

.. picture:: 0165.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 75 Diagram

For let a rational straight line :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />` be set out, let :g:`$DE$ <0165.g1:< D E >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` be applied to :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`$DG$ <0165.g1:/ D G />` as breadth, and let :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` be applied to :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$FE$ <0165.g1:< F E >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0165.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` are medial and commensurable,

.. assertion:: [X. 15 and 23, Por.]

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0165.g1:< D E >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`$DG$ <0165.g1:/ D G />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0165.g1:/ D G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` is also medial. :just:`[X. 23, Por.]`

.. _folio-iii-0166:

.. folio:: 0166
   :page: 162

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`; therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />` is also incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` are commensurable with the square on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>` and twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` is commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />` is incommensurable with the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DE$ <0165.g1:< D E >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>` to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0165.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0165.g1:< D E >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$DE$ <0165.g1:< D E >>` is to :g:`$DH$ <0165.g1:< D H >>`, so is :g:`$GD$ <0165.g1:/ G D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$GD$ <0165.g1:/ G D />` is incommensurable with :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GD$ <0165.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0165.g1:/ D F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$FG$ <0165.g1:/ F G />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DI$ <0165.g1:/ D I />` is rational, and the rectangle contained by a rational and an irrational straight line is irrational, :just:`[deduction from X. 20]` and its "side" is irrational.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$AC$ <0165.g1:/ A C />` is the "side" of :g:`$FE$ <0165.g1:< F E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0165.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. qed::

   And let it be called a :term:`**second apotome of a medial straight line** <second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
