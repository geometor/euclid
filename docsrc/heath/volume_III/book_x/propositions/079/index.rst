PROPOSITION 79.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To an apotome only one rational straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and :g:`$BC$ <0171.g1:/ B C />` an annex to it ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. picture:: 0171.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 79 Diagram

I say that no other rational straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let :g:`$BD$ <0171.g1:/ B D />` be so annexed ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` are also rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` is also the excess of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />`, for both exceed by the same, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. _folio-iii-0172:

.. folio:: 0172
   :page: 168

.. assertion::

   therefore, alternately, the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` over the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` is the excess of twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` exceed the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area, for both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` also exceeds twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area : which is impossible, for both are medial :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`, and a medial area does not exceed a medial by a rational area. :just:`[X. 26] <X.26>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore no other rational straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only.

.. qed::

   Therefore only one rational straight line can be annexed to an apotome which is commensurable with the whole in square only. :just:`Q. E. D.`
