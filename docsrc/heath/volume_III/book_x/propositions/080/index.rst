PROPOSITION 80.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To a first apotome of a medial straight line only one medial straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a rational rectangle.

.. _folio-iii-0173:

.. folio:: 0173
   :page: 169

For let :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` be a first apotome of a medial straight line, and let :g:`$BC$ <0173.g1:/ B C />` be an annex to :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` which they contain is rational; :just:`[X. 74] <X.74>`

.. picture:: 0173.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 80 diagram

I say that no other medial straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a rational area.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` also be so annexed;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` which they contain is rational. :just:`[X. 74] <X.74>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` is also the excess of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />`, for they exceed by the same, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, alternately, the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` over the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` is also the excess of twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   But twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` exceeds twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area, for both are rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` also exceed the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area: which is impossible, for both are medial :just:`[X. 15 and 23, Por.]`, and a medial area does not exceed a medial by a rational area. :just:`[X. 26] <X.26>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
