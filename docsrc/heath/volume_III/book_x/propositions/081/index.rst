PROPOSITION 81.
===============

.. enunciation::

   To a second apotome of a medial straight line only one medial straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` be a second apotome of a medial straight line and :g:`$BC$ <0174.g1:/ B C />` an annex to :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` which they contain is medial. :just:`[X. 75] <X.75>`

.. picture:: 0174.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 81 diagram

I say that no other medial straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let :g:`$BD$ <0174.g1:/ B D />` also be so annexed;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` are also medial straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` which they contain is medial. :just:`[X. 75] <X.75>`

Let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />` be set out, let :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` as breadth, and let :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` be subtracted, producing :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>` so that :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` is the “side” of :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>`.

Again, let :g:`$EI$ <0174.g1:< E I >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EN$ <0174.g1:/ E N />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>` is also equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$HI$ <0174.g1:< H I >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight lines,

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are also medial.

.. _folio-iii-0175:

.. folio:: 0175
   :page: 171

.. subclause::

   And they are equal to :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>`;

.. assertion:: [X. 15 and 23, Por.]

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` is medial,

.. assertion:: [X. 23, Por.]

   twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />` to the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />` is incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are commensurable with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, while twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` is commensurable with the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`, while :g:`$GH$ <0174.g1:< G H >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` is to :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>`, so is :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` to :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MH$ <0174.g1:/ M H />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />`, :g:`$MH$ <0174.g1:/ M H />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EH$ <0174.g1:/ E H />` is an apotome, and :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />` an annex to it. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$HN$ <0174.g1:/ H N />` is also an annex to it ;

.. assertion::

   therefore to an apotome different straight lines are annexed which are commensurable with the wholes in square only : which is impossible. :just:`[X. 79] <X.79>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
