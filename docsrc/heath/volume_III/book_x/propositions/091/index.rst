PROPOSITION 91.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a first apotome, the "side" of the area is an apotome.

For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />` and the first apotome :g:`$AD$ <0195.g1:/ A D />`; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>` is an apotome.

.. _folio-iii-0195:

.. folio:: 0195
   :page: 191

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AD$ <0195.g1:/ A D />` is a first apotome, let :g:`$DG$ <0195.g1:/ D G />` be its annex ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0195.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   And the whole :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />` set out, and the square on :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GD$ <0195.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />`; :just:`[X. Deff. III. 1]`

.. assertion::

   if therefore there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$DG$ <0195.g1:/ D G />` and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. :just:`[X. 17] <X.17>`

.. picture:: 0195.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 91 Diagram

Let :g:`$DG$ <0195.g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <0195.g1:/ D E / = 0195.g1:/ E G />` at :g:`$E$ <0195.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />` is commensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`.

And through the points :g:`$E$ <0195.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0195.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0195.g1:G>` let :g:`$EH$ <0195.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$FI$ <0195.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`$GK$ <0195.g1:/ G K />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AG$ <0195.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>`

.. _folio-iii-0196:

.. folio:: 0196
   :page: 192

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />` is also rational, so that each of the rectangles :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0195.g1:< F K >>` is also rational. :just:`[X. 19] <X.19>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DE$ <0195.g1:/ D E />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0195.g1:/ D G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0195.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DG$ <0195.g1:/ D G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0195.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0195.g1:/ A C />` ; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>` therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$DH$ <0195.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>` is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

Now let the square :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>` be made :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< L M > = 0195.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>`, and let there be subtracted the square :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>` having a common angle with it, the angle :g:`$LPM$ <0195.g1:< L P M )>`, and :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< N O > = 0195.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0195.g1:< F K >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>` are about the same diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>`

Let :g:`$PR$ <0195.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   Since then the rectangle contained by :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 17] <VI.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$AF$ <0195.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>`, and, as :g:`$EG$ <0195.g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`$FG$ <0195.g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$KF$ <0195.g1:< K F >>` ; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$KF$ <0195.g1:< K F >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0195.g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`, as was before proved, :just:`[Lemma after X. 53]` and :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>` is :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< A I > = 0195.g1:< L M >; 0195.g1:< K F > = 0195.g1:< N O >>` to the square :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`$KF$ <0195.g1:< K F >>` to :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$MN$ <0195.g1:< M N >>` is also :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< M N > = 0195.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EK$ <0195.g1:< E K >>` is :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< E K > = 0195.g1:< D H >; 0195.g1:< M N > = 0195.g1:< L O >>` to :g:`$DH$ <0195.g1:< D H >>`, and :g:`$MN$ <0195.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DK$ <0195.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0195.g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AK$ <0195.g1:< A K >>` is also equal to the squares :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< A B > = 0195.g1:< S T >>` to :g:`$ST$ <0195.g1:< S T >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$ST$ <0195.g1:< S T >>` is the square on :g:`$LN$ <0195.g1:/ L N />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LN$ <0195.g1:/ L N />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>` ; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0195.g1:/ L N />` is the "side" of :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>`.

   :page: 193

I say next that :g:`$LN$ <0195.g1:/ L N />` is an apotome.

.. subclause::

   For, since each of the rectangles :g:`$AI$ <0195.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0195.g1:< F K >>` is rational, and they are :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< A I > = 0195.g1:< L M >; 0195.g1:< F K > = 0195.g1:< N O >>` to :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the squares :g:`$LM$ <0195.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`, that is, the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0195.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0195.g1:/ P N />` respectively, is also rational; therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$LP$ <0195.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0195.g1:/ P N />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DH$ <0195.g1:< D H >>` is medial and is :eq:`equal <0195.g1:< D H > = 0195.g1:< L O >>` to :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>` is medial, while :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>` is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$LO$ <0195.g1:< L O >>` is to :g:`$NO$ <0195.g1:< N O >>`, so is :g:`$LP$ <0195.g1:/ L P />` to :g:`$PN$ <0195.g1:/ P N />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$LP$ <0195.g1:/ L P />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$PN$ <0195.g1:/ P N />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LP$ <0195.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0195.g1:/ P N />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0195.g1:/ L N />` is an apotome. :just:`[x. 73]`

.. subclause::

   And it is the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0195.g1:< A B >>` is an apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
