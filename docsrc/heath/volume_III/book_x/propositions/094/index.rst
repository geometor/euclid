PROPOSITION 94.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a fourth apotome, the “side” of the area is minor.

For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />` and the fourth apotome :g:`$AD$ <0207.g1:/ A D />`; I say that the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` is minor.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`$AD$ <0207.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />` set out, and the square on the whole :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on the annex :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`, :just:`[x. Deff. III. 4]`

.. picture:: 0207.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 94

.. subclause::

   Since then the square on :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` and deficient by a square figure, it will divide it into incommensurable parts. :just:`[x. 18]`

Let then :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <0207.g1:/ D E / = 0207.g1:/ E G />` at :g:`$E$ <0207.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`.

.. folio:: 0208
   :page: 204

Let :g:`$EH$ <0207.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$FI$ <0207.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`$GK$ <0207.g1:/ G K />` be drawn through :g:`$E$ <0207.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0207.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0207.g1:G>` parallel to :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0207.g1:/ B D />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion:: [x. 19]

   therefore the whole :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`, and both are rational,

.. assertion:: [x. 21]

   therefore :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1, x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`.

Now let the square :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< L M > = 0207.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>`, and let there be subtracted :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>` :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< N O > = 0207.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>` and about the same angle, the angle :g:`$LPM$ <0207.g1:< L P M )>`. Therefore the squares :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>` are about the same diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>` Let :g:`$PR$ <0207.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   Since then the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>`, and, as :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion:: [v. 11]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MN$ <0207.g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between the squares :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`, and :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` is :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< A I > = 0207.g1:< L M >; 0207.g1:< F K > = 0207.g1:< N O >>` to :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>` is also :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< E K > = 0207.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`$MN$ <0207.g1:< M N >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DH$ <0207.g1:< D H >>` is :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< D H > = 0207.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>`, and :g:`$LO$ <0207.g1:< L O >>` is :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< L O > = 0207.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`$MN$ <0207.g1:< M N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0207.g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, the whole :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is equal to the squares :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`, and, in these, :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0207.g1:< U V W >>` and the square :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <0207.g1:< A B > = 0207.g1:< S T >>` to :g:`$ST$ <0207.g1:< S T >>`, that is, to the square on :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />`; therefore :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />` is the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>`.

I say that :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />` is the irrational straight line called minor.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is rational and is equal to the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is medial, and :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` was proved incommensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />` is also incommensurable with the square on :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but twice the rectangle contained by them medial.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />` is the irrational straight line called minor; :just:`[x. 76]` and it is the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` is minor. :just:`Q. E. D.`
