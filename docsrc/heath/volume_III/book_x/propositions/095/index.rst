PROPOSITION 95.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a fifth apotome, the “side” of the area is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0210.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0210.g1:/ A C />` and the fifth apotome :g:`$AD$ <0210.g1:/ A D />`; I say that the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0210.g1:< A B >>` is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$DG$ <0210.g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`$AD$ <0210.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0210.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,

.. picture:: 0210.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 95

.. assertion::

   the annex :g:`$GD$ <0210.g1:/ G D />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$AC$ <0210.g1:/ A C />` set out, and the square on the whole :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square

.. _folio-iii-0211:

.. folio:: 0211
   :page: 207

.. assertion::

   on the annex :g:`$DG$ <0210.g1:/ D G />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />`. :just:`[x. Deff. III. 5]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, if there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$DG$ <0210.g1:/ D G />` and deficient by a square figure, it will divide it into incommensurable parts. :just:`[x. 18]`

Let then :g:`$DG$ <0210.g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <0210.g1:/ D E / = 0210.g1:/ E G />` at the point :g:`$E$ <0210.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0210.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0210.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0210.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0210.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0210.g1:/ F G />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AG$ <0210.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$CA$ <0210.g1:/ C A />`, and both are rational,

.. assertion:: [x. 21]

   therefore :g:`$AK$ <0210.g1:< A K >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DG$ <0210.g1:/ D G />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0210.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion:: [x. 19]

   :g:`$DK$ <0210.g1:< D K >>` is rational.

Now let the square :g:`$LM$ <0210.g1:< L M >>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0210.g1:< L M > = 0210.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$AI$ <0210.g1:< A I >>`, and let the square :g:`$NO$ <0210.g1:< N O >>` :eq:`equal <0210.g1:< N O > = 0210.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0210.g1:< F K >>` and about the same angle, the angle :g:`$LPM$ <0210.g1:< L P M )>`, be subtracted; therefore the squares :g:`$LM$ <0210.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0210.g1:< N O >>` are about the same diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>`

Let :g:`$PR$ <0210.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn. Similarly then we can prove that :g:`$LN$ <0210.g1:/ L N />` is the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0210.g1:< A B >>`.

I say that :g:`$LN$ <0210.g1:/ L N />` is the straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AK$ <0210.g1:< A K >>` was proved medial and is equal to the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0210.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0210.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0210.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0210.g1:/ P N />` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DK$ <0210.g1:< D K >>` is rational and is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0210.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0210.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   the latter is itself also rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AI$ <0210.g1:< A I >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0210.g1:< F K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LP$ <0210.g1:/ L P />` is also incommensurable with the square on :g:`$PN$ <0210.g1:/ P N />`; therefore :g:`$LP$ <0210.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0210.g1:/ P N />` are straight lines incommensurable in

.. _folio-iii-0212:

.. folio:: 0212
   :page: 208

square which make the sum of the squares on them medial but twice the rectangle contained by them rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remainder :g:`$LN$ <0210.g1:/ L N />` is the irrational straight line called that which produces with a rational area a medial whole ; :just:`[X. 77] <X.77>` and it is the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0210.g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$AB$ <0210.g1:< A B >>` is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole. :just:`Q. E. D.`
