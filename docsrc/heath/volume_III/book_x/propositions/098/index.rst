PROPOSITION 98.
===============

.. enunciation::

   The square on a first apotome of a medial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a second apotome.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` be a first apotome of a medial straight line and :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line, and to :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:< C F E D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`, producing :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` as breadth; I say that :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` is a second apotome.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle. :just:`[x. 74]`

.. picture:: 0219.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 98

To :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, producing :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />` as breadth, and :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, producing :g:`$KM$ <0219.g1:/ K M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`; therefore :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is also medial. :just:`[x. 15 and 23, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 22]`

.. _folio-iii-0220:

.. folio:: 0220
   :page: 216

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, and, in these, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:< C F E D >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, is equal to :g:`$FL$ <0219.g1:< F M L E >>`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. subclause::

   But twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0219.g1:< F M L E >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$FE$ <0219.g1:/ F E />`, producing :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` is also rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 20]`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, that is, :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>`, is medial, while twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, that is, :g:`$FL$ <0219.g1:< F M L E >>`, is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FL$ <0219.g1:< F M L E >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is to :g:`$FL$ <0219.g1:< F M L E >>`, so is :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` to :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` is an apotome. :just:`[x. 73]`

I say next that it is also a second apotome.

For let :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` be bisected at :g:`$N$ <0219.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$NO$ <0219.g1:/ N O />` be drawn through :g:`$N$ <0219.g1:N>` parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0219.g1:< F N O E >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` is a mean proportional between the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, and the square on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />` is equal to :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>`, the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>`, and the square on :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>` is also a mean proportional between :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>`, :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>`; therefore, as :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>`, so is :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>` to :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`$NM$ <0219.g1:/ N M />`, and, as :g:`$NL$ <0219.g1:< N M L O >>` is to :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>`, so is :g:`$NM$ <0219.g1:/ N M />` to :g:`$MK$ <0219.g1:/ K M />`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />` is to :g:`$NM$ <0219.g1:/ N M />`, so is :g:`$NM$ <0219.g1:/ N M />` to :g:`$KM$ <0219.g1:/ K M />`; :just:`[v. 11]` therefore the rectangle :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0219.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$NM$ <0219.g1:/ N M />` :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`, that is, to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />`.

   :page: 217

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` are two unequal straight lines, and the rectangle :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0219.g1:/ K M />` equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$MF$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` and deficient by a square figure has been applied to the greater, :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />`, and divides it into commensurable parts,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MF$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />`. :just:`[x. 17]`

.. subclause::

   And the annex :g:`$FM$ <0219.g1:/ F M />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` set out;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 2]

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` is a second apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
