PROPOSITION 101.
================

.. enunciation::

   The square on the straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a fifth apotome.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />` be the straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole, and :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line, and to :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` let :g:`$CE$ <0228.g1:< C F E D >>` be applied equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` as breadth; I say that :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` is a fifth apotome.

.. picture:: 0228.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 101 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial but twice the rectangle contained by them rational. :just:`[X. 77] <X.77>`

To :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0228.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0228.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`.

.. _folio-iii-0229:

.. folio:: 0229
   :page: 225

.. subclause::

   But the sum of the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` together is medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion:: [X. 22] <X.22>

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` is rational and incommensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the whole :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`, and, in these, :g:`$CE$ <0228.g1:< C F E D >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

Let then :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` be bisected at :g:`$N$ <0228.g1:N>`, and through :g:`$N$ <0228.g1:N>` let :g:`$NO$ <0228.g1:/ N O />` be drawn parallel to either of the straight lines :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$ML$ <0228.g1:/ M L />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0228.g1:< F N O E >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0228.g1:< N M L O >>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` is rational and equal to :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0228.g1:/ F E />`, producing :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is medial, and :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is to :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`, so is :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` to :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

I say next that it is also a fifth apotome.

.. assertion::

   For we can prove similarly that the rectangle :g:`$CK$ <0228.g1:/ C K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0228.g1:/ K M />` is equal to the square on :g:`$NM$ <0228.g1:/ N M />`, that is, to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable with the square on :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`,

.. folio:: 0230
   :page: 226

.. subclause::

   while the square on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />` is equal to :g:`$CH$ <0228.g1:< C K H D >>`, and the square on :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` to :g:`$KL$ <0228.g1:< K M L H >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CH$ <0228.g1:< C K H D >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$KL$ <0228.g1:< K M L H >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CH$ <0228.g1:< C K H D >>` is to :g:`$KL$ <0228.g1:< K M L H >>`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0228.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`$KM$ <0228.g1:/ K M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$CK$ <0228.g1:/ C K />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$KM$ <0228.g1:/ K M />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` are two unequal straight lines, and a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` and deficient by a square figure has been applied to :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />`, and divides it into incommensurable parts,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` is greater than the square on :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />`. :just:`[X. 18] <X.18>`

.. subclause::

   And the annex :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 5]

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` is a fifth apotome.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
