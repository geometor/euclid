PROPOSITION 103.
================

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable in length with an apotome is an apotome and the same in order.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and let :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />`;

I say that :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />` is also an apotome and the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />`.

   :alt: Proposition 103 Diagram

.. _folio-iii-0234:

.. folio:: 0234
   :page: 230

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />` is an apotome, let :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />` be the annex to it;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

Let it be contrived that the ratio of :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$DF$ <0233.g1:/ D F />` is the same as the ratio of :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />`; :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as one is to one, so are all to all; :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>` therefore also, as the whole :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is to the whole :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />` with :g:`$DF$ <0233.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />` are also rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, so is :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$DF$ <0233.g1:/ D F />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   alternately therefore, as :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />`.

.. assertion::

   And the square on :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

.. subclause::

   If then the square on :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />` will also be greater than the square on :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`. :just:`[X. 14] <X.14>`

.. assertion::

   And, if :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />` is so also, :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>` if :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />`, then :g:`$DF$ <0233.g1:/ D F />` also, :just:`[id.]` and, if neither of the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />`, then neither of the straight lines :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   But, if the square on :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />` will also be greater than the square on :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`. :just:`[X. 14] <X.14>`

.. _folio-iii-0235:

.. folio:: 0235
   :page: 231

.. assertion::

   And, if :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />` is so also, if :g:`$BE$ <0233.g1:/ B E />`, then :g:`$DF$ <0233.g1:/ D F />` also, :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>` and, if neither of the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0233.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0233.g1:/ E B />`, then neither of the straight lines :g:`$CF$ <0233.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0233.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0233.g1:/ C D />` is an apotome and the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0233.g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
