PROPOSITION 106.
================

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with that which produces with a rational area a medial whole is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0237.g1:/ A B />` be a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole, and :g:`$CD$ <0237.g1:/ C D />` commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0237.g1:/ A B />`; I say that :g:`$CD$ <0237.g1:/ C D />` is also a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

.. picture:: 0237.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 106 diagram

For let :g:`$BE$ <0237.g1:/ B E />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0237.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0237.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0237.g1:/ E B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0237.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0237.g1:/ E B />` medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. :just:`[x. 77]`

Let the same construction be made.

.. assertion::

   Then we can prove, in manner similar to the foregoing, that :g:`$CF$ <0237.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0237.g1:/ F D />` are in the same ratio as :g:`$AE$ <0237.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0237.g1:/ E B />`, the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0237.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0237.g1:/ E B />` is commensurable with the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0237.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0237.g1:/ F D />`, and the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0237.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0237.g1:/ E B />` with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0237.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0237.g1:/ F D />`; so that :g:`$CF$ <0237.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0237.g1:/ F D />` are also straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on :g:`$CF$ <0237.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0237.g1:/ F D />` medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0237.g1:/ C D />` is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole. :just:`[x. 77]`
