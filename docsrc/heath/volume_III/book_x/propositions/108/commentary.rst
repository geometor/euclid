Commentary on Proposition X.108
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition X.108 <X.108>`.

.. folio:: 0240
   :page: 236

A rational area being of the form :math:`k\rho^2`, and a medial area of the form :math:`\sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2`, the problem is to classify

.. math::

   \sqrt{k\rho^2 - \sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2}

according to the different possible relations between :math:`k`, :math:`\lambda`.

Suppose that

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
   \sigma u &= k\rho^2, \\
   \sigma v &= \sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2.
   \end{aligned}

Since :math:`\sigma u` is rational and :math:`\sigma v` medial, :math:`u` is rational and :math:`\frown \sigma`, while :math:`v` is rational and :math:`\smile \sigma`.

Therefore

.. math::

   u \smile v;

thus :math:`u`, :math:`v` are rational and :math:`\sim`, whence :math:`(u - v)` is an apotome.

The possibilities are now as follows.

(1) :math:`\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \frown u`,

(2) :math:`\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \smile u`.

In both cases :math:`u \frown \sigma`, so that :math:`(u - v)` is either (1) a :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`, or (2) a :term:`*fourth apotome* <fourth-apotome>`.

In case (1) :math:`\sqrt{\sigma(u-v)}` is an *apotome* [x. 91], but in case (2) :math:`\sqrt{\sigma(u-v)}` is a *minor irrational straight line* [x. 94].
