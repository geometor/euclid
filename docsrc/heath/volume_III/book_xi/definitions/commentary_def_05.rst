Commentary on Book XI Definition 5
==================================

Back to :ref:`Book XI Definitions <XI.def>`.

Εὐθείας πρὸς :term:`ἐπίπεδον <plane>` :term:`κλίσις <inclination>` ἐστίν, ὅταν ἀπὸ τοῦ μετεώρου πέρατος τῆς εὐθείας ἐπὶ τὸ :term:`ἐπίπεδον <plane>` :term:`κάθετος <perpendicular>` ἀχθῇ, καὶ ἀπὸ τοῦ γενομένου σημείου ἐπὶ τὸ ἐν τῷ ἐπιπέδῳ πέρας τῆς εὐθείας :term:`εὐθεῖα <straight-line>` ἐπιζευχθῇ, ἡ περιεχομένη :term:`γωνία <angle>` ὑπὸ τῆς ἀχθείσης καὶ τῆς ἐφεστώσης.

.. _folio-iii-0268:

.. folio:: 0268
   :page: 264

In other words, the inclination of a straight line to a plane is the angle between the straight line and its *projection* on the plane. This angle is of course less than the angle between the straight line and any other straight line in the plane through the intersection of the straight line and plane; and the fact is sometimes made the subject of a proposition in modern text-books. It is easily proved by means of the propositions XI. 4, I. 19 and 18.
