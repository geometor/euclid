Commentary on Book XI Definition 15
===================================

Back to :ref:`Book XI Definitions <XI.def>`.

Ἄξων δὲ τῆς σφαίρας ἐστὶν ἡ μένουσα :term:`εὐθεῖα <straight-line>`, περὶ ἣν τὸ ἡμικύκλιον στρέφεται.

That *any* diameter of a sphere may be called an axis is made clear by :person:`Heron <heron>` (Def. 78). *The diameter of the sphere is called an axis, and is any straight line drawn through the centre and bounded in both directions by the sphere, immovable, about which the sphere is moved and turned.* Cf. Euclid's Def. 17.
