DEFINITIONS.
============

.. _XI.def.1:

1. A :term:`**solid** <solid>` is that which has length, breadth, and depth.

.. _XI.def.2:

2. An :term:`extremity <extremity>` of a :term:`solid <solid>` is a :term:`**surface** <surface>`.

.. _XI.def.3:

3. A :term:`straight line <straight-line>` is :term:`**at right angles to a plane** <right-angles-to-plane>`, when it makes :term:`right angles <right-angle>` with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the :term:`plane <plane-surface>`.

.. _XI.def.4:

4. A :term:`**plane is at right angles to a plane** <plane-at-right-angles-to-plane>` when the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` drawn, in one of the :term:`planes <plane-surface>`, :term:`at right angles <right-angle>` to the common section of the planes are at right angles to the remaining plane.

.. _XI.def.5:

5. The :term:`**inclination of a straight line to a plane** <inclination-of-line-to-plane>` is, assuming a :term:`perpendicular <perpendicular>` drawn from the :term:`extremity <extremity>` of the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which is elevated above the :term:`plane <plane-surface>` to the plane, and a straight line joined from the :term:`point <point>` thus arising to the extremity of the straight line which is in the plane, the :term:`angle <plane-angle>` contained by the straight line so drawn and the straight line standing up.

.. _XI.def.6:

6. The :term:`**inclination of a plane to a plane** <inclination-of-plane-to-plane>` is the :term:`acute angle <acute-angle>` contained by the :term:`straight lines <straight-line>` drawn :term:`at right angles <right-angle>` to the common section at the same :term:`point <point>`, one in each of the :term:`planes <plane-surface>`.

.. _XI.def.7:

7. A :term:`plane <plane-surface>` is said to be :term:`**similarly inclined** <similarly-inclined-planes>` to a plane as another is to another when the said :term:`angles <plane-angle>` of the inclinations are equal to one another.

.. _XI.def.8:

8. :term:`**Parallel planes** <parallel-planes>` are those which do not meet.

.. _folio-iii-0265:

.. folio:: 0265
   :page: 261

.. _XI.def.9:

9. :term:`**Similar solid figures** <similar-solid-figures>` are those :term:`contained by similar planes <surface>` equal in multitude.

.. _XI.def.10:

10. :term:`**Equal and similar solid figures** <equal-and-similar-solid-figures>` are those :term:`contained by similar planes <surface>` equal in multitude and in magnitude.

.. _XI.def.11:

11. A :term:`**solid angle** <solid-angle>` is the inclination constituted by more than two :term:`lines <line>` which meet one another and are not in the same :term:`surface <surface>`, towards all the lines.

Otherwise : A :term:`**solid angle** <solid-angle>` is that which is contained by more than two :term:`plane angles <plane-angle>` which are not in the same :term:`plane <plane-surface>` and are constructed to one :term:`point <point>`.

.. _XI.def.12:

12. A :term:`**pyramid** <pyramid>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>`, contained by :term:`planes <plane-surface>`, which is constructed from one plane to one :term:`point <point>`.

.. _XI.def.13:

13. A :term:`**prism** <prism>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>` contained by :term:`planes <plane-surface>` two of which, namely those which are opposite, are equal, similar and :term:`parallel <parallel-straight-lines>`, while the rest are parallelograms.

.. _XI.def.14:

14. When, the :term:`diameter <diameter>` of a :term:`semicircle <semicircle>` remaining fixed, the semicircle is carried round and restored again to the same position from which it began to be moved, the :term:`figure <figure>` so comprehended is a :term:`**sphere** <sphere>`.

.. _XI.def.15:

15. The :term:`**axis of the sphere** <axis-of-sphere>` is the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which remains fixed and about which the :term:`semicircle <semicircle>` is turned.

.. _XI.def.16:

16. The :term:`**centre of the sphere** <centre-of-sphere>` is the same as that of the :term:`semicircle <semicircle>`.

.. _XI.def.17:

17. A :term:`**diameter of the sphere** <diameter-of-sphere>` is any :term:`straight line <straight-line>` drawn through the :term:`centre <centre>` and terminated in both directions by the :term:`surface <surface>` of the :term:`sphere <sphere>`.

.. _XI.def.18:

18. When, one side of those about the :term:`right angle <right-angle>` in a :term:`right-angled triangle <right-angled-triangle>` remaining fixed, the :term:`triangle <trilateral>` is carried round and restored again to the same position from which it began to be moved, the :term:`figure <figure>` so comprehended is a :term:`**cone** <cone>`.

And, if the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which remains fixed be equal to the remaining side about the right angle which is carried round, the cone will be :term:`**right-angled** <right-angled-cone>`; if less, :term:`**obtuse-angled** <obtuse-angled-cone>`; and if greater, :term:`**acute-angled** <acute-angled-cone>`.

.. _XI.def.19:

19. The :term:`**axis of the cone** <axis-of-cone>` is the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which remains fixed and about which the :term:`triangle <trilateral>` is turned.

.. _folio-iii-0266:

   :page: 262

.. _XI.def.20:

20. And the :term:`**base** <base-of-cone>` is the :term:`circle <circle>` described by the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which is carried round.

.. _XI.def.21:

21. When, one side of those about the :term:`right angle <right-angle>` in a :term:`rectangular parallelogram <contained-rectangle>` remaining fixed, the parallelogram is carried round and restored again to the same position from which it began to be moved, the :term:`figure <figure>` so comprehended is a :term:`**cylinder** <cylinder>`.

.. _XI.def.22:

22. The :term:`**axis of the cylinder** <axis-of-cylinder>` is the :term:`straight line <straight-line>` which remains fixed and about which the parallelogram is turned.

.. _XI.def.23:

23. And the :term:`**bases** <bases-of-cylinder>` are the :term:`circles <circle>` described by the two sides opposite to one another which are carried round.

.. _XI.def.24:

24. :term:`**Similar cones and cylinders** <similar-cones-and-cylinders>` are those in which the :term:`axes <axis-of-cone>` and the :term:`diameters of the bases <base-of-cone>` are :term:`proportional <proportional>`.

.. _XI.def.25:

25. A :term:`**cube** <cube>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>` contained by six equal :term:`squares <square>`.

.. _XI.def.26:

26. An :term:`**octahedron** <octahedron>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>` contained by eight equal and :term:`equilateral triangles <equilateral-triangle>`.

.. _XI.def.27:

27. An :term:`**icosahedron** <icosahedron>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>` contained by twenty equal and :term:`equilateral triangles <equilateral-triangle>`.

.. _XI.def.28:

28. A :term:`**dodecahedron** <dodecahedron>` is a :term:`solid figure <solid>` contained by twelve equal, :term:`equilateral <equilateral-triangle>`, and equiangular pentagons.
