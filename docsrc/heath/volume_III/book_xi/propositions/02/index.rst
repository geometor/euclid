PROPOSITION 2.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines cut one another, they are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

For let the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0278.g1:/ C D />` cut one another at the point :g:`$E$ <0278.g1:E>`; I say that :g:`$AB$ <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0278.g1:/ C D />` are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

.. picture:: 0278.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 2

For let points :g:`$F$ <0278.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0278.g1:G>` be taken at random on :g:`$EC$ <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$EB$ <0278.g1:/ E B />`, let :g:`$CB$ <0278.g1:/ C B />`, :g:`$FG$ <0278.g1:/ F G />` be joined, and let :g:`$FH$ <0278.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$GK$ <0278.g1:/ G K />` be drawn across; I say first that the triangle :g:`$ECB$ <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is in one plane.

.. subclause::

   For, if part of the triangle :g:`$ECB$ <0278.g1:< E C B >>`, either :g:`$FHC$ <0278.g1:< F H C >>` or :g:`$GBK$ <0278.g1:< G B K >>`, is in the plane of reference, and the rest in another,

.. assertion::

   a part also of one of the straight lines :g:`$EC$ <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$EB$ <0278.g1:/ E B />` will be in the plane of reference, and a part in another.

.. subclause::

   But, if the part :g:`$FCBG$ <0278.g1:< F C B G >>` of the triangle :g:`$ECB$ <0278.g1:< E C B >>` be in the plane of reference, and the rest in another,

.. assertion::

   a part also of both the straight lines :g:`$EC$ <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$EB$ <0278.g1:/ E B />` will be in the plane of reference and a part in another:

.. assertion:: [XI. 1] <XI.1>

   which was proved absurd.

.. _folio-iii-0279:

.. folio:: 0279
   :page: 275

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ECB$ <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is in one plane.

.. subclause::

   But, in whatever plane the triangle :g:`$ECB$ <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is, in that plane also is each of the straight lines :g:`$EC$ <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$EB$ <0278.g1:/ E B />`, and, in whatever plane each of the straight lines :g:`$EC$ <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$EB$ <0278.g1:/ E B />` is, in that plane are :g:`$AB$ <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0278.g1:/ C D />` also. :just:`[XI. 1] <XI.1>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0278.g1:/ C D />` are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
