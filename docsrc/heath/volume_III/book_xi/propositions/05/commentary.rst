Commentary on Proposition XI.5
==============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XI.5 <XI.5>`.

.. folio:: 0286
   :page: 282

It follows that, *if a right angle be turned about one of the straight lines containing it the other will describe a plane.*

At any point in a straight line it is possible to draw *only one plane which is at right angles to the straight line.*

One such plane can be found by taking any two planes through the given straight line, drawing perpendiculars to the straight line in the respective planes, e.g. :g:`BO <0286.g1:/ B O />`, :g:`CO <0286.g1:/ C O />` in the planes :g:`AOB <0286.g1:< A O B >>`, :g:`AOC <0286.g1:< A O C >>`, each perpendicular to :g:`AO <0286.g1:/ A O />`, and then drawing a plane (:g:`BOC <0286.g1:< B O C >>`) through the perpendiculars.

.. picture:: 0286.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI - Proposition 5, uniqueness of perpendicular plane at a point

If there were another plane through :g:`O <0286.g1:O>` perpendicular to :g:`AO <0286.g1:/ A O />`, it must meet the plane through :g:`AO <0286.g1:/ A O />` and some perpendicular to it as :g:`OC <0286.g1:/ O C />` in a straight line :g:`OC' <0286.g1:/ O C' />` different from :g:`OC <0286.g1:/ O C />`.

Then, by XI. 4, :g:`AOC' <0286.g1:< A O C' )>` is a right angle, and in the same plane with the right angle :g:`AOC <0286.g1:< A O C )>`: which is impossible.

Next, *one plane and only one can be drawn through a point outside a straight line at right angles to that line.*

Let :g:`P <0286.g2:P>` be the given point, :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />` the given straight line.

In the plane through :g:`P <0286.g2:P>` and :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />`, draw :g:`PO <0286.g2:/ P O />` perpendicular to :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />`, and through :g:`O <0286.g2:O>` draw another straight line :g:`OQ <0286.g2:/ O Q />` at right angles to :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0286.g2.svg
   :alt: Book XI - Proposition 5, perpendicular plane from an external point

Then the plane through :g:`OP <0286.g2:/ O P />`, :g:`OQ <0286.g2:/ O Q />` is perpendicular to :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />`.

If there were another plane through :g:`P <0286.g2:P>` perpendicular to :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />`, either

(1) it would intersect :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />` at :g:`O <0286.g2:O>` but not pass through :g:`OQ <0286.g2:/ O Q />`, or

(2) it would intersect :g:`AB <0286.g2:/ A B />` at a point different from :g:`O <0286.g2:O>`. In either case, an absurdity would result.
