PROPOSITION 13.
===============

.. enunciation::

   From the same point two straight lines cannot be set up at right angles to the same plane on the same side.

For, if possible, from the same point :g:`$A$ <0299.g1:A>` let the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0299.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0299.g1:/ A C />` be set up at right angles to the plane of reference and on the same side, and let a plane be drawn through :g:`$BA$ <0299.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0299.g1:/ A C />`; it will then make, as section through :g:`$A$ <0299.g1:A>` in the plane of reference, a straight line. :just:`[XI. 3] <XI.3>`

.. picture:: 0299.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13

Let it make :g:`$DAE$ <0299.g1:/ D A E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0299.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0299.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$DAE$ <0299.g1:/ D A E />` are in one plane.

And, since :g:`$CA$ <0299.g1:/ C A />` is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference. :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DAE$ <0299.g1:/ D A E />` meets it and is in the plane of reference;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$CAE$ <0299.g1:< C A E )>` is right.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0299.g1:< B A E )>` is also right;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$CAE$ <0299.g1:< C A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0299.g1:< C A E ) = 0299.g1:< B A E )>` to the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0299.g1:< B A E )>`.

.. assertion::

   And they are in one plane: which is impossible.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
