Commentary on Proposition XI.16
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XI.16 <XI.16>`.

.. _folio-iii-0305:

.. folio:: 0305
   :page: 301

:person:`Simson <simson>` points out that, in here quoting I. Def. 23, Euclid should have said “But straight lines *in one plane* which do not meet in either direction are parallel.”

From this proposition is deduced the converse of XI. 14.

*If a straight line is perpendicular to one of two parallel planes, it is perpendicular to the other also.*

For suppose that :g:`$MN$ <0305.g1:< M N >>`, :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>` are two parallel planes, and that :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to :g:`$MN$ <0305.g1:< M N >>`. Through :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` draw any plane, and let it intersect the planes :g:`$MN$ <0305.g1:< M N >>`, :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>` in :g:`$AC$ <0305.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0305.g1:/ B D />` respectively. Therefore :g:`$AC$ <0305.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0305.g1:/ B D />` are parallel. [XI. 16] But :g:`$AC$ <0305.g1:/ A C />` is perpendicular to :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` ; therefore :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` is also perpendicular to :g:`$BD$ <0305.g1:/ B D />`. That is, :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to any line in :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>` passing through :g:`$B$ <0305.g1:B>` ; therefore :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>`.

.. picture:: 0305.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 16 Commentary

It follows as a corollary that

*Through a given point one plane, and only one, can be drawn parallel to a given plane.*

In the above figure let :g:`$A$ <0305.g1:A>` be the given point and :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>` the given plane. Draw :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` perpendicular to :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>`. Through :g:`$A$ <0305.g1:A>` draw a plane :g:`$MN$ <0305.g1:< M N >>` at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` (see note on XI. 5 above). Then :g:`$MN$ <0305.g1:< M N >>` is parallel to :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>`. [XI. 14] If there could pass through :g:`$A$ <0305.g1:A>` a second plane parallel to :g:`$PQ$ <0305.g1:< P Q >>`, :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` would also be perpendicular to it. That is, :g:`$AB$ <0305.g1:/ A B />` would be perpendicular to two different planes through :g:`$A$ <0305.g1:A>` : which is impossible (see the same note).

Also it is readily proved that,

*If two planes are parallel to a third plane, they are parallel to one another.*
