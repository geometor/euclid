PROPOSITION 20.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a solid angle be contained by three plane angles, any two, taken together in any manner, are greater than the remaining one.

For let the solid angle at :g:`$A$ <0311.g3:A>` be contained by the three plane angles :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`, :g:`$CAD$ <0311.g3:< C A D )>`, :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`; I say that any two of the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`, :g:`$CAD$ <0311.g3:< C A D )>`, :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`, taken together in any manner, are greater than the remaining one.

.. picture:: 0311.g3.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 20

.. subclause::

   If now the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`, :g:`$CAD$ <0311.g3:< C A D )>`, :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>` are :eq:`equal <0311.g3:< B A C ) = 0311.g3:< C A D ) = 0311.g3:< D A B )>` to one another,

.. assertion::

   it is manifest that any two are greater than the remaining one.

.. subclause::

   But, if not, let :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>` be greater,

and on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0311.g3:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0311.g3:A>` on it, let the
angle :g:`$BAE$ <0311.g3:< B A E )>` be constructed, in the plane through :g:`$BA$ <0311.g3:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0311.g3:/ A C />`, :eq:`equal <0311.g3:< B A E ) = 0311.g3:< D A B )>` to the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`; let :g:`$AE$ <0311.g3:/ A E />` be made :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ A E / = 0311.g3:/ A D />` to :g:`$AD$ <0311.g3:/ A D />`, and let :g:`$BEC$ <0311.g3:/ B E C />`, drawn across through the point :g:`$E$ <0311.g3:E>`, cut the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0311.g3:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0311.g3:/ A C />` at the points :g:`$B$ <0311.g3:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0311.g3:C>`; let :g:`$DB$ <0311.g3:/ D B />`, :g:`$DC$ <0311.g3:/ D C />` be joined.

.. _folio-iii-0312:

.. folio:: 0312
   :page: 308

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DA$ <0311.g3:/ D A />` is :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ D A / = 0311.g3:/ A E />` to :g:`$AE$ <0311.g3:/ A E />`, and :g:`$AB$ <0311.g3:/ A B />` is common, two sides are :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ D A / = 0311.g3:/ A E />` to two sides ; and the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>` is :eq:`equal <0311.g3:< D A B ) = 0311.g3:< B A E )>` to the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0311.g3:< B A E )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$DB$ <0311.g3:/ D B />` is :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ D B / = 0311.g3:/ B E />` to the base :g:`$BE$ <0311.g3:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two sides :g:`$BD$ <0311.g3:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0311.g3:/ D C />` are greater than :g:`$BC$ <0311.g3:/ B C />`, :just:`[I. 20] <I.20>` and of these :g:`$DB$ <0311.g3:/ D B />` was proved :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ D B / = 0311.g3:/ B E />` to :g:`$BE$ <0311.g3:/ B E />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$DC$ <0311.g3:/ D C />` is greater than the remainder :g:`$EC$ <0311.g3:/ E C />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DA$ <0311.g3:/ D A />` is :eq:`equal <0311.g3:/ D A / = 0311.g3:/ A E />` to :g:`$AE$ <0311.g3:/ A E />`, and :g:`$AC$ <0311.g3:/ A C />` is common, and the base :g:`$DC$ <0311.g3:/ D C />` is greater than the base :g:`$EC$ <0311.g3:/ E C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0311.g3:< D A C )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$EAC$ <0311.g3:< E A C )>`. :just:`[I. 25] <I.25>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>` was also proved :eq:`equal <0311.g3:< D A B ) = 0311.g3:< B A E )>` to the angle :g:`$BAE$ <0311.g3:< B A E )>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`, :g:`$DAC$ <0311.g3:< D A C )>` are greater than the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`.

Similarly we can prove that the remaining angles also, taken together two and two, are greater than the remaining one.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
