Commentary on Proposition XI.23
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XI.23 <XI.23>`.

.. folio:: 0322
   :page: 318

The whole difficulty in this proposition is the proof of a fact which makes the construction *possible*, viz. the fact that, if :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>` be a triangle with sides
respectively equal to the bases of the isosceles triangles which have the given angles as vertical angles and the equal sides all of the same length, then one of these equal sides, as :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, is greater than the radius :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` of the circle circumscribing the triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>`.

.. _folio-iii-0323:

.. folio:: 0323
   :page: 319

Assuming that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, we have only to draw from :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>` a perpendicular :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` to the plane of the triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>`, to make :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` of such a length that the sum of the squares on :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, and to join :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />`. (The manner of finding :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` such that the square on it is equal to the difference between the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` and :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` is shown in the Lemma at the end of the text of the proposition. We have already had the same construction in the Lemma after x. 13.)

Then clearly :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />` are :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ R L / = 0319.g2:/ R M / = 0319.g2:/ R N / = 0319.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` and to one another [I. 4 and I. 47].

Therefore the triangles :g:`$LRM$ <0319.g2:< L R M >>`, :g:`$MRN$ <0319.g2:< M R N >>`, :g:`$NRL$ <0319.g2:< N R L >>` have their three sides respectively :eq:`equal <0319.g2:< L R M > = 0319.g1:< A B C >; 0319.g2:< M R N > = 0319.g1:< D E F >; 0319.g2:< N R L > = 0319.g1:< G H K >>` to those of the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K >>` respectively.

Hence their vertical angles are :eq:`equal <0319.g2:< L R M ) = 0319.g1:< A B C ); 0319.g2:< M R N ) = 0319.g1:< D E F ); 0319.g2:< L R N ) = 0319.g1:< G H K )>` to the three given angles respectively; and the required solid angle is constructed.

We return now to the proposition to be proved as a preliminary to the construction, viz. that, in the figures, :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

It will be observed that Euclid, as his manner is, proves it for one case only, that, namely, in which :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>`, the centre of the circle circumscribing the triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>`, falls *within* the triangle, leaving the other cases for the reader to prove. As usual, however, the two other cases are found in the Greek text, after the formal conclusion of the proposition, as above, ending with the words ὅπερ ἔδει ποιῆσαι. This position for the proofs itself suggests that they are not Euclid’s but are interpolated; and this is rendered certain by the fact that words distinguishing three cases at the point where the centre :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>` of the circumscribing circle is found, “It [the centre] will then be either within the triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>` or on one of its sides or without. First let it be within,” are found in the MSS. B and V only and are manifestly interpolated. Nevertheless the additional two cases must have been inserted very early, as they are found in all the best MSS.

In order to give a clear view of the proof of all three cases as given in the text, we will reproduce all three (Euclid’s as well as the others) with abbreviations to make them catch the eye better.

In all three cases the proof is by :term:`*reductio ad absurdum* <reductio-ad-absurdum>`, and it is proved first that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` cannot be *equal* to :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, and secondly that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` cannot be *less* than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

Case I.
~~~~~~~

(1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`. Then :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />` are respectively :eq:`equal <0319.g1:/ A B / = 0319.g2:/ L O /; 0319.g1:/ B C / = 0319.g2:/ O M />` to :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$OM$ <0319.g2:/ O M />`; and :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` $=$ :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` (by construction). Therefore :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`. Similarly :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`, Adding, we have :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>` $=$ four right angles: which contradicts the hypothesis. Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $\ne$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

.. folio:: 0324
   :page: 320

(2) Suppose that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $<$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`. Make :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />`, :g:`$OQ$ <0319.g2:/ O Q />` (measured along :g:`$OL$ <0319.g2:/ O L />`, :g:`$OM$ <0319.g2:/ O M />`) each :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ O P / = 0319.g2:/ O Q / = 0319.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`. Thus, :g:`$OL$ <0319.g2:/ O L />`, :g:`$OM$ <0319.g2:/ O M />` being :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ O L / = 0319.g2:/ O M />` also, it follows that :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />` is $\parallel$ to :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />`. Hence :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` $:$ :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` $:$ :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />`; and, since :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` $>$ :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />`, :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />`, i.e. :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />`, $>$ :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`. Thus, in $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$POQ$ <0319.g2:< P O Q >>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, two sides are equal to two sides, and base :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` $>$ base :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`; therefore :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle POQ$ <0319.g2:< P O Q )>`, i.e. :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`. Similarly :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`, and it follows by addition that :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $>$ (four right angles): which again contradicts the hypothesis.

Case II.
~~~~~~~~

(1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Then (:g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $+$ :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`), or (:g:`$DE$ <0319.g1:/ D E />` $+$ :g:`$EF$ <0319.g1:/ E F />`) $=$ :g:`$MO$ <0324.g1:/ M O />` $+$ :g:`$OL$ <0324.g1:/ O L />` $=$ :g:`$MN$ <0324.g1:/ M N />` $=$ :g:`$DF$ <0319.g1:/ D F />`; which contradicts the hypothesis.

(2) The supposition that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $<$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />` is even more impossible; for in this case it would result that :g:`$DE$ <0319.g1:/ D E />` $+$ :g:`$EF$ <0319.g1:/ E F />` $<$ :g:`$DF$ <0319.g1:/ D F />`.

Case III.
~~~~~~~~~

(1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Then, in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$LOM$ <0324.g1:< L O M >>`, two sides :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />` are respectively :eq:`equal <0319.g1:/ A B / = 0324.g1:/ L O /; 0319.g1:/ B C / = 0324.g1:/ O M />` to two sides :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`, :g:`$OM$ <0324.g1:/ O M />`, and the bases :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />` are :eq:`equal <0319.g1:/ A C / = 0324.g1:/ L M />`; therefore :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0324.g1:< L O M )>`. Similarly :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0324.g1:< N O L )>`. Therefore, by addition, :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` (by hypothesis). But, in the triangles :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N >>`, which are :eq:`equal <0319.g1:< D E F > = 0324.g1:< M O N >>` in all respects, :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`. But it was proved that :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`: which is impossible.

(2) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $<$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Along :g:`$OL$ <0324.g1:/ O L />`, :g:`$OM$ <0324.g1:/ O M />` measure :g:`$OP$ <0324.g1:/ O P />`, :g:`$OQ$ <0324.g1:/ O Q />` each :eq:`equal <0324.g1:/ O P / = 0324.g1:/ O Q / = 0319.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`.

   :alt: Folio 0324 Figure 1

Then :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$PQ$ <0324.g1:/ P Q />` are parallel, and :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />` $:$ :g:`$PQ$ <0324.g1:/ P Q />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />` $:$ :g:`$OP$ <0324.g1:/ O P />`, whence, since :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />` $>$ :g:`$OP$ <0324.g1:/ O P />`, :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />`, or :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />`, $>$ :g:`$PQ$ <0324.g1:/ P Q />`. Thus, in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$POQ$ <0324.g1:< P O Q >>`, :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle POQ$ <0324.g1:< P O Q )>`, i.e. :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0324.g1:< L O M )>`. Similarly, by taking :g:`$OR$ <0324.g1:/ O R />` along :g:`$ON$ <0324.g1:/ O N />` :eq:`equal <0324.g1:/ O R / = 0319.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, we prove that :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle LON$ <0324.g1:< L O N )>`. Now, at :g:`$O$ <0324.g1:O>`, make :g:`$\angle POS$ <0324.g1:< P O S )>` :eq:`equal <0324.g1:< P O S ) = 0319.g1:< A B C )>` to :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>`, and :g:`$\angle POT$ <0324.g1:< P O T )>` :eq:`equal <0324.g1:< P O T ) = 0319.g1:< G H K )>` to :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>`. Make :g:`$OS$ <0324.g1:/ O S />`, :g:`$OT$ <0324.g1:/ O T />` each :eq:`equal <0324.g1:/ O S / = 0324.g1:/ O T / = 0324.g1:/ O P />` to :g:`$OP$ <0324.g1:/ O P />`, and join :g:`$ST$ <0324.g1:/ S T />`, :g:`$SP$ <0324.g1:/ S P />`, :g:`$TP$ <0324.g1:/ T P />`. Then, in the :eq:`equal <0319.g1:< A B C > = 0324.g1:< P O S >>` triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$POS$ <0324.g1:< P O S >>`, :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` $=$ :g:`$PS$ <0324.g1:/ P S />`, so that :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />` $=$ :g:`$PS$ <0324.g1:/ P S />`. Similarly :g:`$LN$ <0324.g1:/ L N />` $=$ :g:`$PT$ <0324.g1:/ P T />`. Therefore in the triangles :g:`$MLN$ <0324.g1:< M L N >>`, :g:`$SPT$ <0324.g1:< S P T >>`, since :g:`$\angle MLN$ <0324.g1:< M L N )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle SPT$ <0324.g1:< S P T )>` [this is assumed, but should have been explained], :g:`$MN$ <0324.g1:/ M N />` $>$ :g:`$ST$ <0324.g1:/ S T />`, or :g:`$DF$ <0319.g1:/ D F />` $>$ :g:`$ST$ <0324.g1:/ S T />`. Lastly, in $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$SOT$ <0324.g1:< S O T >>`, which have two sides equal to two sides, since :g:`$DF$ <0319.g1:/ D F />` $>$ :g:`$ST$ <0324.g1:/ S T />`, :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle SOT$ <0324.g1:< S O T )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` (by construction): which contradicts the hypothesis.

:person:`Simson <simson>` gives rather different proofs for all three cases; but the essence of them can be put, I think, a little more shortly than in his text, as well as more clearly

Case I. (:g:`$O$ <0325.g1:O>` within :g:`$\Delta LMN$ <0325.g1:< L M N >>`.)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) Let :g:`$AB$ <0325.g1:/ A B />` be, if possible, :eq:`equal <0325.g1:/ A B / = 0325.g1:/ L O />` to :g:`$LO$ <0325.g1:/ L O />`. Then the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ABC$ <0325.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0325.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0325.g1:< G H K >>` must be identically :eq:`equal <0325.g1:< A B C > = 0325.g1:< L O M >; 0325.g1:< D E F > = 0325.g1:< M O N >; 0325.g1:< G H K > = 0325.g1:< N O L >>` to the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$LOM$ <0325.g1:< L O M >>`, :g:`$MON$ <0325.g1:< M O N >>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0325.g1:< N O L >>` respectively.

.. picture:: 0325.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0325 Figure 1

Therefore the vertical angles at :g:`$O$ <0325.g1:O>` in the latter triangles are equal respectively to the angles at :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, :g:`$H$ <0325.g1:H>`. The latter are therefore together equal to four right angles: which is impossible.

(2) If :g:`$AB$ <0325.g1:/ A B />` be less than :g:`$LO$ <0325.g1:/ L O />`, construct on the bases :g:`$LM$ <0325.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0325.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NL$ <0325.g1:/ N L />` triangles with vertices :g:`$P$ <0325.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0325.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0325.g1:R>` and identically equal to the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ABC$ <0325.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0325.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0325.g1:< G H K >>` respectively.

   :page: 322

Then :g:`$P$ <0325.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0325.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0325.g1:R>` will fall within the respective angles at :g:`$O$ <0325.g1:O>`, since :g:`$PL$ <0325.g1:/ P L />` = :g:`$PM$ <0325.g1:/ P M />` and < :g:`$LO$ <0325.g1:/ L O />`, and similarly in the other cases. Thus [I. 21] the angles at :g:`$P$ <0325.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0325.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0325.g1:R>` are respectively greater than the angles at :g:`$O$ <0325.g1:O>` in which they lie. Therefore the sum of the angles at :g:`$P$ <0325.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0325.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0325.g1:R>`, i.e. the sum of the angles at :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, :g:`$H$ <0325.g1:H>`, is greater than four right angles: which again contradicts the hypothesis.

Case II. (:g:`$O$ <0326.g1:O>` lying on :g:`$MN$ <0326.g1:/ M N />`.)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In this case, whether (1) :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` = :g:`$LO$ <0326.g1:/ L O />`, or (2) :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` < :g:`$LO$ <0326.g1:/ L O />`, a triangle cannot be formed with :g:`$MN$ <0326.g1:/ M N />` as base and each of the other sides equal to :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />`. In other words, the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>` either reduces to a straight line or is impossible.

Case III. (:g:`$O$ <0327.g1:O>` lying outside the :g:`$\Delta LMN$ <0327.g1:< L M N >>`.)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) *Suppose, if possible, that* :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` *=* :g:`$LO$ <0327.g1:/ L O />`.

Then the triangles :g:`$LOM$ <0327.g1:< L O M >>`, :g:`$MON$ <0327.g1:< M O N >>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0327.g1:< N O L >>` are identically :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< L O M > = 0326.g1:< A B C >; 0327.g1:< M O N > = 0326.g1:< D E F >; 0327.g1:< N O L > = 0327.g1:< G H K >>` to the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K >>`. Since :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0327.g1:< L O M )>` + :g:`$\angle LON$ <0327.g1:< L O N )>` = :g:`$\angle MON$ <0327.g1:< M O N )>`, :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0327.g1:< A B C )>` + :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K )>` = :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0327.g1:< D E F )>`: which contradicts the hypothesis.

(2) *Suppose that* :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` *<* :g:`$OL$ <0327.g1:/ O L />`.

Draw, as before, on :g:`$LM$ <0327.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0327.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NL$ <0327.g1:/ N L />` as bases triangles with vertices :g:`$P$ <0327.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0327.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0327.g1:R>` and identically equal to the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K >>`. Next, at :g:`$N$ <0327.g1:N>` on the straight line :g:`$NR$ <0327.g1:/ N R />`, make :g:`$\angle RNS$ <0327.g1:< R N S )>` :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< R N S ) = 0327.g1:< P L M )>` to the angle :g:`$PLM$ <0327.g1:< P L M )>`, cut off :g:`$NS$ <0327.g1:/ N S />` :eq:`equal <0327.g1:/ N S / = 0327.g1:/ L M />` to :g:`$LM$ <0327.g1:/ L M />` and join :g:`$RS$ <0327.g1:/ R S />`, :g:`$LS$ <0327.g1:/ L S />`. Then :g:`$\Delta NRS$ <0327.g1:< N R S >>` is identically :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< N R S > = 0327.g1:< L P M > = 0326.g1:< A B C >>` to :g:`$\Delta LPM$ <0327.g1:< L P M >>` or :g:`$\Delta ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`. Now (:g:`$\angle LNR$ <0327.g1:< L N R )>` + :g:`$\angle RNS$ <0327.g1:< R N S )>`) < (:g:`$\angle NLO$ <0327.g1:< N L O )>` + :g:`$\angle OLM$ <0327.g1:< O L M )>`), that is, :g:`$\angle LNS$ <0327.g1:< L N S )>` < :g:`$\angle NLM$ <0327.g1:< N L M )>`. Thus, in $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$LNS$ <0327.g1:< L N S >>`, :g:`$NLM$ <0327.g1:< N L M >>`, two sides are equal to two sides, and the included angle in the former is less than the included angle in the other. Therefore :g:`$LS$ <0327.g1:/ L S />` < :g:`$MN$ <0327.g1:/ M N />`.
