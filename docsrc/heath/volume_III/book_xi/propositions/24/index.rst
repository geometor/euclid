PROPOSITION 24.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.

.. picture:: 0327.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 24 figure

For let the solid :g:`$CDHG$ <0327.g1:< C D H G >>` be contained by the parallel planes :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$GF$ <0327.g1:< G F >>`, :g:`$AH$ <0327.g1:< A H >>`, :g:`$DF$ <0327.g1:< D F >>`, :g:`$BF$ <0327.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`$AE$ <0327.g1:< A E >>`; I say that the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.

.. subclause::

   For, since the two parallel planes :g:`$BG$ <0327.g1:< B G >>`, :g:`$CE$ <0327.g1:< C E >>` are cut by the plane :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>`,

.. assertion::

   their common sections are parallel. :just:`[XI. 16] <XI.16>`

.. _folio-iii-0328:

.. folio:: 0328
   :page: 324

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0327.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$DC$ <0327.g1:/ D C />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the two parallel planes :g:`$BF$ <0327.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`$AE$ <0327.g1:< A E >>` are cut by the plane :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 16] <XI.16>

   their common sections are parallel.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$BC$ <0327.g1:/ B C />` is parallel to :g:`$AD$ <0327.g1:/ A D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0327.g1:/ A B />` was also proved parallel to :g:`$DC$ <0327.g1:/ D C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>` is a parallelogram.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that each of the planes :g:`$DF$ <0327.g1:< D F >>`, :g:`$FG$ <0327.g1:< F G >>`, :g:`$GB$ <0327.g1:< G B >>`, :g:`$BF$ <0327.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`$AE$ <0327.g1:< A E >>` is a parallelogram.

Let :g:`$AH$ <0327.g1:/ A H />`, :g:`$DF$ <0327.g1:/ D F />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0327.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$DC$ <0327.g1:/ D C />`, and :g:`$BH$ <0327.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`$CF$ <0327.g1:/ C F />`, the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0327.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0327.g1:/ B H />` which meet one another are parallel to the two straight lines :g:`$DC$ <0327.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CF$ <0327.g1:/ C F />` which meet one another, not in the same plane;

.. assertion::

   therefore they will contain equal angles; :just:`[XI. 10] <XI.10>` therefore the angle :g:`$ABH$ <0327.g1:< A B H )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DCF$ <0327.g1:< D C F )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two sides :g:`$AB$ <0327.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0327.g1:/ B H />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$DC$ <0327.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CF$ <0327.g1:/ C F />`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>` and the angle :g:`$ABH$ <0327.g1:< A B H )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DCF$ <0327.g1:< D C F )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$AH$ <0327.g1:/ A H />` is equal to the base :g:`$DF$ <0327.g1:/ D F />`, and the triangle :g:`$ABH$ <0327.g1:< A B H >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$DCF$ <0327.g1:< D C F >>`. :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`

.. subclause::

   And the parallelogram :g:`$BG$ <0327.g1:< B G >>` is double of the triangle :g:`$ABH$ <0327.g1:< A B H >>`, and the parallelogram :g:`$CE$ <0327.g1:< C E >>` double of the triangle :g:`$DCF$ <0327.g1:< D C F >>`; :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$BG$ <0327.g1:< B G >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$CE$ <0327.g1:< C E >>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>` is also equal to :g:`$GF$ <0327.g1:< G F >>`, and :g:`$AE$ <0327.g1:< A E >>` to :g:`$BF$ <0327.g1:< B F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
