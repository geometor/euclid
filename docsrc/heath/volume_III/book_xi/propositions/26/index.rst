PROPOSITION 26.
===============

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line, and at a given point on it, to construct a solid angle equal to a given solid angle.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` the given point on it, and the angle at :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`, contained by the angles :g:`$EDC$ <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0331.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`$FDC$ <0331.g1:< F D C )>`, the given solid angle ; thus it is required to construct on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` on it, a solid angle equal to the solid angle at :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0331.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 26

For let a point :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>` be taken at random on :g:`$DF$ <0331.g1:/ D F />`, let :g:`$FG$ <0331.g1:/ F G />` be drawn from :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>` perpendicular to the plane through :g:`$ED$ <0331.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0331.g1:/ D C />`, and let it meet the plane at :g:`$G$ <0331.g1:G>`, :just:`[XI. 11] <XI.11>` let :g:`$DG$ <0331.g1:/ D G />` be joined, let there be constructed on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />` and at the point :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` on it the angle :g:`$BAL$ <0331.g1:< B A L )>` equal to the angle :g:`$EDC$ <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, and the angle :g:`$BAK$ <0331.g1:< B A K )>` equal to the angle :g:`$EDG$ <0331.g1:< E D G )>`, :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>` let :g:`$AK$ <0331.g1:/ A K />` be made equal to :g:`$DG$ <0331.g1:/ D G />`,
let :g:`$KH$ <0331.g1:/ K H />` be set up from the point :g:`$K$ <0331.g1:K>` at right angles to the plane through :g:`$BA$ <0331.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AL$ <0331.g1:/ A L />`, :just:`[XI. 12] <XI.12>` let :g:`$KH$ <0331.g1:/ K H />` be made equal to :g:`$GF$ <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and let :g:`$HA$ <0331.g1:/ H A />` be joined ; I say that the solid angle at :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, contained by the angles :g:`$BAL$ <0331.g1:< B A L )>`, :g:`$BAH$ <0331.g1:< B A H )>`, :g:`$HAL$ <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is equal to the solid angle at :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>` contained by the angles :g:`$EDC$ <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0331.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`$FDC$ <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

.. folio:: 0332

For let :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$DE$ <0331.g1:/ D E />` be cut off equal to one another, and let :g:`$HB$ <0331.g1:/ H B />`, :g:`$KB$ <0331.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`$FE$ <0331.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`$GE$ <0331.g1:/ G E />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$FG$ <0331.g1:/ F G />` is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference ; :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the angles :g:`$FGD$ <0331.g1:< F G D )>`, :g:`$FGE$ <0331.g1:< F G E )>` is right.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason each of the angles :g:`$HKA$ <0331.g1:< H K A )>`, :g:`$HKB$ <0331.g1:< H K B )>` is also right.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two sides :g:`$KA$ <0331.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$GD$ <0331.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`$DE$ <0331.g1:/ D E />` respectively, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$KB$ <0331.g1:/ K B />` is equal to the base :g:`$GE$ <0331.g1:/ G E />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$KH$ <0331.g1:/ K H />` is also equal to :g:`$GF$ <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles ;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore :g:`$HB$ <0331.g1:/ H B />` is also equal to :g:`$FE$ <0331.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the two sides :g:`$AK$ <0331.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0331.g1:/ K H />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$DG$ <0331.g1:/ D G />`, :g:`$GF$ <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$AH$ <0331.g1:/ A H />` is equal to the base :g:`$FD$ <0331.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to :g:`$DE$ <0331.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the two sides :g:`$HA$ <0331.g1:/ H A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$DF$ <0331.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0331.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   And the base :g:`$HB$ <0331.g1:/ H B />` is equal to the base :g:`$FE$ <0331.g1:/ F E />` ;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$BAH$ <0331.g1:< B A H )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0331.g1:< E D F )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`$HAL$ <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FDC$ <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle :g:`$BAL$ <0331.g1:< B A L )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$EDC$ <0331.g1:< E D C )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0331.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` on it, a solid angle has been constructed equal to the given solid angle at :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
