PROPOSITION 28.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane through the diagonals of the opposite planes, the solid will be bisected by the plane.

For let the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$AB$ <0334.g1:< A B >>` be cut by the plane :g:`$CDEF$ <0334.g1:< C D E F >>` through the diagonals :g:`$CF$ <0334.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0334.g1:/ D E />` of opposite planes ; I say that the solid :g:`$AB$ <0334.g1:< A B >>` will be bisected by the plane :g:`$CDEF$ <0334.g1:< C D E F >>`.

.. picture:: 0334.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 28

.. subclause::

   For, since the triangle :g:`$CGF$ <0334.g1:< C G F >>` is :eq:`equal <0334.g1:< C G F > = 0334.g1:< C F B >; 0334.g1:< A D E > = 0334.g1:< D E H >>` to the triangle :g:`$CFB$ <0334.g1:< C F B >>`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>` and :g:`$ADE$ <0334.g1:< A D E >>` to :g:`$DEH$ <0334.g1:< D E H >>`, while the parallelogram :g:`$CA$ <0334.g1:< C A >>` is also :eq:`equal <0334.g1:< C A > = 0334.g1:< E B >; 0334.g1:< G E > = 0334.g1:< C H >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EB$ <0334.g1:< E B >>`, for they are opposite, and :g:`$GE$ <0334.g1:< G E >>` to :g:`$CH$ <0334.g1:< C H >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the prism contained by the two triangles :g:`$CGF$ <0334.g1:< C G F >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0334.g1:< A D E >>` and the three parallelograms :g:`$GE$ <0334.g1:< G E >>`, :g:`$AC$ <0334.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$CE$ <0334.g1:< C E >>` is also equal to the prism contained by the two triangles :g:`$CFB$ <0334.g1:< C F B >>`, :g:`$DEH$ <0334.g1:< D E H >>` and the three parallelograms :g:`$CH$ <0334.g1:< C H >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0334.g1:< B E >>`, :g:`$CE$ <0334.g1:< C E >>` ;

.. _folio-iii-0335:

.. folio:: 0335
   :page: 331

.. subclause::

   for they are contained by planes equal both in multitude and in magnitude. :just:`[XI. Def. 10] <XI.def.10>`

.. assertion:: Q. E. D.

   Hence the whole solid :g:`$AB$ <0334.g1:< A B >>` is bisected by the plane :g:`$CDEF$ <0334.g1:< C D E F >>`.
