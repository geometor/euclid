PROPOSITION 31.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another.

Let the parallelepipedal solids :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>`, :g:`$CF$ <0341.g1:< C F >>`, of the same height, be on :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A B > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` bases :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

I say that the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A E > = 0341.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`$CF$ <0341.g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: 0341.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31

First, let the sides which stand up, :g:`$HK$ <0341.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$BE$ <0341.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$AG$ <0341.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$LM$ <0341.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$PQ$ <0341.g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`$DF$ <0341.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$CO$ <0341.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$RS$ <0341.g1:/ R S />`, be at :eq:`right angles <0341.g1:/ H K / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ B E / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ A G / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ L M / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ P Q / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ D F / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ C O / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ R S / = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to the bases :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`; let the straight line :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`$CR$ <0341.g1:/ C R />`; on the straight line :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />`, and at the point :g:`$R$ <0341.g1:R>` on it, let the angle :g:`$TRU$ <0341.g1:< T R U )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< T R U ) = 0341.g1:< A L B )>` to the angle :g:`$ALB$ <0341.g1:< A L B )>`, :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>` let :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` be made :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ R T / = 0341.g1:/ A L />` to :g:`$AL$ <0341.g1:/ A L />`, and :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />` :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ R U / = 0341.g1:/ L B />` to :g:`$LB$ <0341.g1:/ L B />`, and let the base :g:`$RW$ <0341.g1:< R W >>` and the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two sides :g:`$TR$ <0341.g1:/ T R />`, :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />` are :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ T R / = 0341.g1:/ A L /; 0341.g1:/ R U / = 0341.g1:/ L B />` to the two sides :g:`$AL$ <0341.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LB$ <0341.g1:/ L B />`, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$RW$ <0341.g1:< R W >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R W > = 0341.g1:< H L >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`$HL$ <0341.g1:< H L >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since again :g:`$AL$ <0341.g1:/ A L />` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ A L / = 0341.g1:/ R T /; 0341.g1:/ L M / = 0341.g1:/ R S />` to :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />`, and :g:`$LM$ <0341.g1:/ L M />` to :g:`$RS$ <0341.g1:/ R S />`, and they contain right angles,

.. _folio-iii-0342:

.. folio:: 0342
   :page: 338

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R X > = 0341.g1:< A M >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`$AM$ <0341.g1:< A M >>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`$LE$ <0341.g1:< L E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< L E > = 0341.g1:< S U >>` and similar to :g:`$SU$ <0341.g1:< S U >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` are equal and similar to three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the former three are equal and similar to the three opposite, and the latter three to the three opposite; :just:`[XI. 24] <XI.24>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A E > = 0341.g1:< X U >>` to the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>`. :just:`[XI. Def. 10] <XI.def.10>`

Let :g:`$DR$ <0341.g1:/ D R />`, :g:`$WU$ <0341.g1:/ W U />` be drawn through and meet one another at :g:`$Y$ <0341.g1:Y>`, let :g:`$aTb$ <0341.g1:/ a T b />` be drawn through :g:`$T$ <0341.g1:T>` parallel to :g:`$DY$ <0341.g1:/ D Y />`, let :g:`$PD$ <0341.g1:/ P D />` be produced to :g:`$a$ <0341.g1:a>`, and let the solids :g:`$YX$ <0341.g1:< Y X >>`, :g:`$RI$ <0341.g1:< R I >>` be completed.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`$XY$ <0341.g1:< X Y >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`$Yc$ <0341.g1:< Y c >>` its opposite, is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X Y > = 0341.g1:< X U >>` to the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` of which the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`$UV$ <0341.g1:< U V >>` its opposite, for they are on the same base :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` and of the same height, and the extremities of their sides which stand up, namely :g:`$RY$ <0341.g1:/ R Y />`, :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />`, :g:`$Tb$ <0341.g1:/ T b />`, :g:`$TW$ <0341.g1:/ T W />`, :g:`$Se$ <0341.g1:/ S e />`, :g:`$Sd$ <0341.g1:/ S d />`, :g:`$Xc$ <0341.g1:/ X c />`, :g:`$XV$ <0341.g1:/ X V />`, are on the same straight lines :g:`$YW$ <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :g:`$eV$ <0341.g1:/ e V />`. :just:`[XI. 29] <XI.29>`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X U > = 0341.g1:< A E >>` to :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` :

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`$XY$ <0341.g1:< X Y >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X Y > = 0341.g1:< A E >>` to the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelogram :g:`$RUWT$ <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< Y T >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$YT$ <0341.g1:< Y T >>` for they are on the same base :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` and in the same parallels :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />`, :g:`$YW$ <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :just:`[I. 35] <I.35>` while :g:`$RUWT$ <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`, since it is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< A B >>` to :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$YT$ <0341.g1:< Y T >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< Y T > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DT$ <0341.g1:< D T >>` is another parallelogram;

.. assertion:: [V. 7] <V.7>

   therefore, as the base :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`$DT$ <0341.g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`$YT$ <0341.g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`$DT$ <0341.g1:< D T >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$CI$ <0341.g1:< C I >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`$RF$ <0341.g1:< R F >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>` is to the base :g:`$DT$ <0341.g1:< D T >>`, so is the solid :g:`$CF$ <0341.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`$RI$ <0341.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[XI. 25] <XI.25>`

.. _folio-iii-0343:

.. folio:: 0343
   :page: 339

.. subclause::

   For the same reason, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$YI$ <0343.g1:< Y I >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`$RX$ <0343.g1:< R X >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`$YT$ <0343.g1:< Y T >>` is to the base :g:`$TD$ <0343.g1:< T D >>`, so is the solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[XI. 25] <XI.25>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`$CD$ <0343.g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`$DT$ <0343.g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`$YT$ <0343.g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`$DT$ <0343.g1:< D T >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>` is to the solid :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`, so is the solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the solids :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`, :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` has to :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>` the same ratio; therefore the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< C F > = 0343.g1:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>`. :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` was proved :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< Y X > = 0343.g1:< A E >>` to :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

Next, let the sides standing up, :g:`$AG$ <0343.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$HK$ <0343.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$BE$ <0343.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$LM$ <0343.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$CN$ <0343.g1:/ C N />`, :g:`$PQ$ <0343.g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`$DF$ <0343.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$RS$ <0343.g1:/ R S />`, not be at right angles to the bases :g:`$AB$ <0343.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0343.g1:< C D >>`; I say again that the solid :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: 0343.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31, Case 2

For from the points :g:`$K$ <0343.g1:K>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0343.g1:M>`, :g:`$Q$ <0343.g1:Q>`, :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`$N$ <0343.g1:N>`, :g:`$S$ <0343.g1:S>` let :g:`$KO$ <0343.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`$ET$ <0343.g1:/ E T />`, :g:`$GU$ <0343.g1:/ G U />`, :g:`$MV$ <0343.g1:/ M V />`, :g:`$QW$ <0343.g1:/ Q W />`, :g:`$FX$ <0343.g1:/ F X />`, :g:`$NY$ <0343.g1:/ N Y />`, :g:`$SI$ <0343.g1:/ S I />` be drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference, and let them meet the plane at the points :g:`$O$ <0343.g1:O>`, :g:`$T$ <0343.g1:T>`, :g:`$U$ <0343.g1:U>`, :g:`$V$ <0343.g1:V>`, :g:`$W$ <0343.g1:W>`, :g:`$X$ <0343.g1:X>`, :g:`$Y$ <0343.g1:Y>`, :g:`$I$ <0343.g1:I>`, and let :g:`$OT$ <0343.g1:/ O T />`, :g:`$OU$ <0343.g1:/ O U />`, :g:`$UV$ <0343.g1:/ U V />`, :g:`$TV$ <0343.g1:/ T V />`, :g:`$WX$ <0343.g1:/ W X />`, :g:`$WY$ <0343.g1:/ W Y />`, :g:`$YI$ <0343.g1:/ Y I />`, :g:`$IX$ <0343.g1:/ I X />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`$KV$ <0343.g1:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K V > = 0343.g1:< Q I >>` to the solid :g:`$QI$ <0343.g1:< Q I >>`, for they are on the :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K M > = 0343.g1:< Q S >>` bases :g:`$KM$ <0343.g1:< K M >>`, :g:`$QS$ <0343.g1:< Q S >>` and of the same height, and their sides which stand up are at right angles to their bases. :just:`[First part of this Prop.]`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`$KV$ <0343.g1:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K V > = 0343.g1:< A E >; 0343.g1:< Q I > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>`, and :g:`$QI$ <0343.g1:< Q I >>` to :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`; for they are on the same base and of the same height, while the extremities of their sides which stand up are not on the same straight lines. :just:`[XI. 30] <XI.30>`

.. _folio-iii-0344:

.. folio:: 0344
   :page: 340

.. assertion::

   Therefore the solid :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
