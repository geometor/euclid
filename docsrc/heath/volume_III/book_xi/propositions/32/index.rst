PROPOSITION 32.
===============

.. enunciation::

   Parallelepipedal solids which are of the same height are to one another as their bases.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>` be parallelepipedal solids of the same height; I say that the parallelepipedal solids :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>` are to one another as their bases, that is, that, as the base :g:`$AE$ <0345.g1:< A E >>` is to the base :g:`$CF$ <0345.g1:< C F >>`, so is the solid :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>` to the solid :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0345.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 32

For let :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` equal to :g:`$AE$ <0345.g1:< A E >>` be applied to :g:`$FG$ <0345.g1:/ F G />`, :just:`[I. 45] <I.45>` and on :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` as base, and with the same height as that of :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>`, let the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$GK$ <0345.g1:< G K >>` be completed.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>` is equal to the solid :g:`$GK$ <0345.g1:< G K >>`; for they are on equal bases :g:`$AE$ <0345.g1:< A E >>`, :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` and of the same height. :just:`[XI. 31] <XI.31>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$CK$ <0345.g1:< C K >>` is cut by the plane :g:`$DG$ <0345.g1:< D G >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$CF$ <0345.g1:< C F >>` is to the base :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>`, so is the solid :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>` to the solid :g:`$DH$ <0345.g1:< D H >>`. :just:`[XI. 25] <XI.25>`

.. subclause::

   But the base :g:`$FH$ <0345.g1:< F H >>` is equal to the base :g:`$AE$ <0345.g1:< A E >>`, and the solid :g:`$GK$ <0345.g1:< G K >>` to the solid :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the base :g:`$AE$ <0345.g1:< A E >>` is to the base :g:`$CF$ <0345.g1:< C F >>`, so is the solid :g:`$AB$ <0345.g1:< A B >>` to the solid :g:`$CD$ <0345.g1:< C D >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
