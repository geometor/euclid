PROPOSITION 36.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If three straight lines be proportional, the parallelepipedal solid formed out of the three is equal to the parallelepipedal solid on the mean which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

Let :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` be three straight lines in proportion, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />` is to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, so is :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` to :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />`; I say that the solid formed out of :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` is equal to the solid on :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

Let there be set out the solid angle at :g:`$E$ <0361.g1:E>` contained by the angles :g:`$DEG$ <0361.g1:< D E G )>`, :g:`$GEF$ <0361.g1:< G E F )>`, :g:`$FED$ <0361.g1:< F E D )>`,
let each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0361.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$GE$ <0361.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0361.g1:/ E F />` be made :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ D E / = 0361.g1:/ G E / = 0361.g1:/ E F / = 0361.g1:/ B />` to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, and let the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$EK$ <0361.g1:< E K >>` be completed, let :g:`$LM$ <0361.g1:/ L M />` be made :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ L M / = 0361.g1:/ A />` to :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, and on the straight line :g:`$LM$ <0361.g1:/ L M />`, and at the point :g:`$L$ <0361.g1:L>` on it, let there be constructed a solid angle equal to the solid angle at :g:`$E$ <0361.g1:E>`, namely that contained by :g:`$NLO$ <0361.g1:< N L O )>`, :g:`$OLM$ <0361.g1:< O L M )>`, :g:`$MLN$ <0361.g1:< M L N )>`; let :g:`$LO$ <0361.g1:/ L O />` be made :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ L O / = 0361.g1:/ B />` to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, and :g:`$LN$ <0361.g1:/ L N />` :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ L N / = 0361.g1:/ C />` to :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since, as :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />` is to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, so is :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` to :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />`, while :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />` is :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ A / = 0361.g1:/ L M /; 0361.g1:/ B / = 0361.g1:/ L O / = 0361.g1:/ E D /; 0361.g1:/ C / = 0361.g1:/ L N />` to :g:`$LM$ <0361.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` to each of the straight lines :g:`$LO$ <0361.g1:/ L O />`, :g:`$ED$ <0361.g1:/ E D />`, and :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` to :g:`$LN$ <0361.g1:/ L N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$LM$ <0361.g1:/ L M />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0361.g1:/ E F />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0361.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$LN$ <0361.g1:/ L N />`.

.. subclause::

   Thus the sides about the :eq:`equal <0361.g1:< N L M ) = 0361.g1:< D E F )>` angles :g:`$NLM$ <0361.g1:< N L M )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0361.g1:< D E F )>` are reciprocally proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$MN$ <0361.g1:< M N >>` is :eq:`equal <0361.g1:< M N > = 0361.g1:< D F >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$DF$ <0361.g1:< D F >>`. :just:`[VI. 14] <VI.14>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the angles :g:`$DEF$ <0361.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$NLM$ <0361.g1:< N L M )>` are two plane rectilineal angles, and on them the elevated straight lines :g:`$LO$ <0361.g1:/ L O />`, :g:`$EG$ <0361.g1:/ E G />` are set up which are :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ L O / = 0361.g1:/ E G />` to one another and contain equal angles with the original straight lines respectively,

.. assertion::

   therefore the perpendiculars drawn from the points :g:`$G$ <0361.g1:G>`, :g:`$O$ <0361.g1:O>` to the planes through :g:`$NL$ <0361.g1:/ N L />`, :g:`$LM$ <0361.g1:/ L M />` and :g:`$DE$ <0361.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0361.g1:/ E F />` are equal to one another; :just:`[XI. 35, Por.]` hence the solids :g:`$LH$ <0361.g1:< L H >>`, :g:`$EK$ <0361.g1:< E K >>` are of the same height.

.. subclause::

   But parallelepipedal solids on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another; :just:`[XI. 31] <XI.31>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`$HL$ <0361.g1:< H L >>` is :eq:`equal <0361.g1:< H L > = 0361.g1:< E K >>` to the solid :g:`$EK$ <0361.g1:< E K >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$LH$ <0361.g1:< L H >>` is the solid formed out of :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />`, and :g:`$EK$ <0361.g1:< E K >>` the solid on :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`;

.. folio:: 0362
   :page: 358

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelepipedal solid formed out of :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` is equal to the solid on :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
