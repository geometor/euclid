Commentary on Proposition XII.1
===============================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XII.1 <XII.1>`.

.. folio:: 0374
   :page: 370

As, from this point onward, the text of each proposition usually occupies considerable space, I shall generally give in the notes a summary of the argument, to enable it to be followed more easily.

Here we have to prove that a pair of corresponding sides are in the ratio of the corresponding diameters.

Since $\angle\text{s}$ :g:`$BAE$ <0373.g1:< B A E )>`, :g:`$GFL$ <0373.g1:< G F L )>` are equal, and the sides about those angles proportional,

.. assertion::

   $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$ABE$ <0373.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`$FGL$ <0373.g1:< F G L >>` are equiangular,

so that

.. assertion::

   $\angle$ :g:`$AEB$ <0373.g1:< A E B )>` $= \angle$ :g:`$FLG$ <0373.g1:< F L G )>`

Hence their equals in the same segments, $\angle\text{s}$ :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>`, :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`, are equal. And the right angles :g:`$BAM$ <0373.g1:< B A M )>`, :g:`$GFN$ <0373.g1:< G F N )>` are equal. Therefore $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$ABM$ <0373.g1:< A B M >>`, :g:`$FGN$ <0373.g1:< F G N >>` are equiangular, so that

.. assertion::

   :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $:$ :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />` $=$ :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` $:$ :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`.

The duplicates of these ratios are therefore equal, whence

.. assertion::

   $(\text{polygon }$ :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` $) : (\text{polygon }$ :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>` $)$ $= \text{duplicate ratio of }$ :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` $\text{ to }$ :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />` $= \text{duplicate ratio of }$ :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $\text{ to }$ :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />` $=$ :g:`$BM^2$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $:$ :g:`$GN^2$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`.
