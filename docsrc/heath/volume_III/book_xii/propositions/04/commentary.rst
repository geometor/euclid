LEMMA.
~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   But that, as the triangle :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the triangle :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, so is the prism in which the triangle :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite, we must prove as follows.

For in the same figure let perpendiculars be conceived drawn from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0387.g1:H>` to the planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`; these are of course equal because, by hypothesis, the pyramids are of equal height.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`GC <0387.g1:/ G C />` and the perpendicular from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>` are cut by the parallel planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 17] <XI.17>

   they will be cut in the same ratios.

.. _folio-iii-0389:

.. folio:: 0389
   :page: 385

.. subclause::

   And :g:`GC <0387.g1:/ G C />` is bisected by the plane :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` at :g:`N <0387.g1:N>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the perpendicular from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>` to the plane :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` will also be bisected by the plane :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the perpendicular from :g:`H <0387.g1:H>` to the plane :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` will also be bisected by the plane :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>`.

.. subclause::

   And the perpendiculars from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0387.g1:H>` to the planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are equal;

.. assertion::

   therefore the perpendiculars from the triangles :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` to the planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are also equal.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the prisms in which the triangles :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` are bases, and :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` their opposites, are of equal height.

.. assertion::

   Hence also the parallelepipedal solids described from the said prisms are of equal height and are to one another as their bases; :just:`[XI. 32] <XI.32>` therefore their halves, namely the said prisms, are to one another as the base :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the base :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

We can incorporate the lemma at the end of the proposition and summarise the proof thus.

Since :g:`LO <0387.g1:/ L O />` is parallel to :g:`AB <0387.g1:/ A B />`, $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` are similar. In like manner $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` are similar. And, since :g:`BC <0387.g1:/ B C />` $:$ :g:`CO <0387.g1:/ C O />` $=$ :g:`EF <0387.g1:/ E F />` $:$ :g:`FV <0387.g1:/ F V />`, $\triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>` and, alternately, $\triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>`.

Now the prisms :g:`(LOC, PMN) <0387.g1:< L O C P M N >>` and :g:`(RVF, STU) <0387.g1:< R V F S T U >>` are equal in height: for the perpendiculars from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0387.g1:H>` on the bases :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are divided by the planes :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` (parallel to the bases) in the same proportion as :g:`GC <0387.g1:/ G C />`, :g:`HF <0387.g1:/ H F />` are divided by those planes :just:`[XI. 17] <XI.17>`, i.e. they are bisected; hence the heights of the prisms, being half the equal heights of the pyramids, are equal.

And the prisms are the halves respectively of parallelepipeds of the same height on parallelogrammic bases double of the $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` respectively; :just:`[XI. 28 and note]` hence they are in the same ratio as those parallelepipeds, and therefore as their bases :just:`[XI. 32] <XI.32>`.

Therefore $(\text{prism }$ :g:`LOC, PMN <0387.g1:< L O C P M N >>` $) : (\text{prism }$ :g:`RVF, STU <0387.g1:< R V F S T U >>` $) = \triangle$ :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`.

And since the other prisms in the pyramids are equal to these prisms respectively, $(\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`GABC <0387.g1:< G A B C >>` $) : (\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`HDEF <0387.g1:< H D E F >>` $) = \triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`.

Similarly, if the pyramids :g:`GPMN <0387.g1:< G P M N >>`, :g:`HSTU <0387.g1:< H S T U >>` be divided in like manner, and also the pyramids :g:`PAKL <0387.g1:< P A K L >>`, :g:`SDQR <0387.g1:< S D Q R >>`, we shall have e.g. $(\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`GPMN <0387.g1:< G P M N >>` $) : (\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`HSTU <0387.g1:< H S T U >>` $) = \triangle$ :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, and similarly for the second pair of pyramids.

The process may be continued indefinitely, and we shall always have $(\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`GABC <0387.g1:< G A B C >>` $) : (\text{sum of prisms in }$ :g:`HDEF <0387.g1:< H D E F >>` $) = \triangle$ :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`.
