PROPOSITION 4.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If there be two pyramids of the same height which have triangular bases, and each of them be divided into two pyramids equal to one another and similar to the whole, and into two equal prisms, then, as the base of the one pyramid is to the base of the other pyramid, so will all the prisms in the one pyramid be to all the prisms, being equal in multitude, in the other pyramid.

Let there be two pyramids of the same height which have the triangular bases :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, and vertices the points :g:`$G$ <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0387.g1:H>`, and let each of them be divided into two pyramids equal to one another and similar to the whole and into two equal prisms; :just:`[XII. 3] <XII.3>`

I say that, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so are all the prisms in the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0387.g1:< A B C G >>` to all the prisms, being equal in multitude, in the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0387.g1:< D E F H >>`,

.. _folio-iii-0387:

.. folio:: 0387
   :page: 383

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$BO$ <0387.g1:/ B O />` is equal to :g:`$OC$ <0387.g1:/ O C />`, and :g:`$AL$ <0387.g1:/ A L />` to :g:`$LC$ <0387.g1:/ L C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LO$ <0387.g1:/ L O />` is parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0387.g1:/ A B />`, and the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`.

.. picture:: 0387.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />` is double of :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />` of :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />`.

.. subclause::

   And on :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />` are described the similar and similarly situated rectilineal figures :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, and on :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />` the similar and similarly situated figures :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`; :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>` therefore, alternately, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, so is the prism in which the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite; :just:`[Lemma following]`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the prism in which the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite.

.. assertion::

   But, as the said prisms are to one another, so is the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`$KBOL$ <0387.g1:< K B O L >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`$PM$ <0387.g1:/ P M />` its opposite to the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`$QEVR$ <0387.g1:< Q E V R >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`$ST$ <0387.g1:/ S T />` its opposite. :just:`[XI. 39; cf. XII. 3]`

.. _folio-iii-0388:

.. folio:: 0388
   :page: 384

.. assertion::

   Therefore also the two prisms, that in which the parallelogram :g:`$KBOL$ <0387.g1:< K B O L >>` is the base and :g:`$PM$ <0387.g1:/ P M />` its opposite, and that in which the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite, are to the prisms in which :g:`$QEVR$ <0387.g1:< Q E V R >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`$ST$ <0387.g1:/ S T />` its opposite and in which the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite in the same ratio. :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so are the said two prisms to the said two prisms.

.. subclause::

   And similarly, if the pyramids :g:`$PMNG$ <0387.g1:< P M N G >>`, :g:`$STUH$ <0387.g1:< S T U H >>` be divided into two prisms and two pyramids,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` is to the base :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>`, so will the two prisms in the pyramid :g:`$PMNG$ <0387.g1:< P M N G >>` be to the two prisms in the pyramid :g:`$STUH$ <0387.g1:< S T U H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` is to the base :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>`, so is the base :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`; for the triangles :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` are equal to the triangles :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` respectively.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so are the four prisms to the four prisms.

.. qed::

   And similarly also, if we divide the remaining pyramids into two pyramids and into two prisms, then, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so will all the prisms in the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0387.g1:< A B C G >>` be to all the prisms, being equal in multitude, in the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0387.g1:< D E F H >>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
