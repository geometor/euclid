PROPOSITION 7.
==============

.. enunciation::

   Any prism which has a triangular base is divided into three pyramids equal to one another which have triangular bases.

Let there be a prism in which the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0398.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and :g:`$DEF$ <0398.g1:< D E F >>` its opposite; I say that the prism :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0398.g1:< A B C D E F >>` is divided into three pyramids equal to one another, which have triangular bases.

.. picture:: 0398.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XII, Proposition 7

For let :g:`$BD$ <0398.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$EC$ <0398.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0398.g1:/ C D />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$ABED$ <0398.g1:< A B E D >>` is a parallelogram, and :g:`$BD$ <0398.g1:/ B D />` is its diameter,

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$EBD$ <0398.g1:< E B D >>`; :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>` therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex is equal to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$DEB$ <0398.g1:< D E B >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex. :just:`[XII. 5] <XII.5>`

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$DEB$ <0398.g1:< D E B >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex is the same with the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$EBC$ <0398.g1:< E B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex; for they are contained by the same planes.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex is also equal to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$EBC$ <0398.g1:< E B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$FCBE$ <0398.g1:< F C B E >>` is a parallelogram, and :g:`$CE$ <0398.g1:/ C E />` is its diameter,

.. assertion:: [I. 34] <I.34>

   the triangle :g:`$CEF$ <0398.g1:< C E F >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$CBE$ <0398.g1:< C B E >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$BCE$ <0398.g1:< B C E >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex is equal to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ECF$ <0398.g1:< E C F >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex. :just:`[XII. 5] <XII.5>`

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$BCE$ <0398.g1:< B C E >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex was proved equal to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex;

.. _folio-iii-0399:

.. folio:: 0399
   :page: 395

.. assertion::

   therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$CEF$ <0398.g1:< C E F >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex is equal to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex; therefore the prism :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0398.g1:< A B C D E F >>` has been divided into three pyramids equal to one another which have triangular bases.

.. subclause::

   And, since the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex is the same with the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$CAB$ <0398.g1:< C A B >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex, for they are contained by the same planes, while the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0398.g1:< A B D >>` is the base and the point :g:`$C$ <0398.g1:C>` the vertex was proved to be a third of the prism in which the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0398.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and :g:`$DEF$ <0398.g1:< D E F >>` its opposite,

.. assertion::

   therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0398.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0398.g1:D>` the vertex is a third of the prism which has the same base, the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0398.g1:< A B C >>`, and :g:`$DEF$ <0398.g1:< D E F >>` as its opposite.

.. assertion::

   PORISM. *From this it is manifest that any pyramid is a third part of the prism which has the same base with it and equal height.* :just:`Q. E. D.`
