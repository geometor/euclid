PROPOSITION 9.
==============

.. enunciation::

   In equal pyramids which have triangular bases the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those pyramids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.

For let there be :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< A B C G > = 0402.g1:< D E F H >>` pyramids which have the triangular bases :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>` and vertices the points :g:`$G$ <0402.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0402.g1:H>`; I say that in the pyramids :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights, that is, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`.

.. picture:: 0402.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XII, Proposition 9

For let the parallelepipedal solids :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`, :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< A B C G > = 0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`, and the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is six times the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, and the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` six times the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< B G M L > = 0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>`.

.. subclause::

   But in equal parallelepipedal solids the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; :just:`[XI. 34] <XI.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to the base :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the height of the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the height of the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`. :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the height of the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. folio:: 0403

.. subclause::

   But the height of the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` is the same with the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`, and the height of the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is the same with the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the pyramids :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights.

Next, in the pyramids :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` let the bases be reciprocally proportional to the heights ; that is, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so let the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` be to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` ; I say that the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< A B C G > = 0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`.

.. subclause::

   For, with the same construction, since, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, while, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the parallelogram :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the parallelogram :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to the parallelogram :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` is the same with the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>`, and the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is the same with the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to the base :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`.

.. subclause::

   But those parallelepipedal solids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal ; :just:`[XI. 34] <XI.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< B G M L > = 0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>`.

.. subclause::

   And the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is a sixth part of :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`, and the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` a sixth part of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` ;

   therefore the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is :eq:`equal <0402.g1:< A B C G > = 0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`.
