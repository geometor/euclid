PROPOSITION 1.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, the square on the greater segment added to the half of the whole is five times the square on the half.

For let the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />` be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the point :g:`$C$ <0444.g1:C>`, and let :g:`$AC$ <0444.g1:/ A C />` be the greater segment; let the straight line :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`$CA$ <0444.g1:/ C A />`, and let :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />` be made half of :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`; I say that the square on :g:`$CD$ <0444.g1:/ C D />` is five times the square on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`.

For let the squares :g:`$AE$ <0444.g1:< A E >>`, :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$DC$ <0444.g1:/ D C />`, and let the figure in :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` be drawn; let :g:`$FC$ <0444.g1:/ F C />` be carried through to :g:`$G$ <0444.g1:G>`.

.. picture:: 0444.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 1

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`$C$ <0444.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0444.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0444.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[VI. Def. 3, VI. 17] <VI.def.3, VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$CE$ <0444.g1:< C E >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0444.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`$FH$ <0444.g1:< F H >>` the square on :g:`$AC$ <0444.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CE$ <0444.g1:< C E >>` is :eq:`equal <0444.g1:< C E > = 0444.g1:< F H >>` to :g:`$FH$ <0444.g1:< F H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BA$ <0444.g1:/ B A />` is double of :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`, while :g:`$BA$ <0444.g1:/ B A />` is :eq:`equal <0444.g1:/ B A / = 0444.g1:/ K A /; 0444.g1:/ A D / = 0444.g1:/ A H />` to :g:`$KA$ <0444.g1:/ K A />`, and :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />` to :g:`$AH$ <0444.g1:/ A H />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KA$ <0444.g1:/ K A />` is also double of :g:`$AH$ <0444.g1:/ A H />`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$KA$ <0444.g1:/ K A />` is to :g:`$AH$ <0444.g1:/ A H />`, so is :g:`$CK$ <0444.g1:< C K >>` to :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CK$ <0444.g1:< C K >>` is double of :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$LH$ <0444.g1:< L H >>`, :g:`$HC$ <0444.g1:< H C >>` are also double of :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$KC$ <0444.g1:< K C >>` is equal to :g:`$LH$ <0444.g1:< L H >>`, :g:`$HC$ <0444.g1:< H C >>`.

.. _folio-iii-0445:

.. folio:: 0445
   :page: 441

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CE$ <0444.g1:< C E >>` was also proved :eq:`equal <0444.g1:< C E > = 0444.g1:< H F >>` to :g:`$HF$ <0444.g1:< H F >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole square :g:`$AE$ <0444.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0444.g1:< A E > = 0444.g1:< M N O >>` to the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0444.g1:< M N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BA$ <0444.g1:/ B A />` is double of :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$BA$ <0444.g1:/ B A />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`, that is, :g:`$AE$ <0444.g1:< A E >>` is quadruple of :g:`$DH$ <0444.g1:< D H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0444.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0444.g1:< A E > = 0444.g1:< M N O >>` to the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0444.g1:< M N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0444.g1:< M N O >>` is also quadruple of :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>`; therefore the whole :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` is five times :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` is the square on :g:`$DC$ <0444.g1:/ D C />`, and :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>` the square on :g:`$DA$ <0444.g1:/ D A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0444.g1:/ C D />` is five times the square on :g:`$DA$ <0444.g1:/ D A />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
