PROPOSITION 2.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If the square on a straight line be five times the square on a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original straight line.

For let the square on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0447.g1:/ A B />` be five times the square on the segment :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />` of it, and let :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` be double of :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />`; I say that, when :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`.

Let the squares :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>`, :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0447.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` respectively, let the figure in :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>` be drawn, and let :g:`$BE$ <0447.g1:/ B E />` be drawn through.

.. picture:: 0447.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 2

.. subclause::

   Now, since the square on :g:`$BA$ <0447.g1:/ B A />` is five times the square on :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>` is five times :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` is quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`, that is, :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>` is quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` was also proved quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` is equal to :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`, while :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is equal to :g:`$CK$ <0447.g1:/ C K />`, and :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$CH$ <0447.g1:/ C H />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   therefore :g:`$KB$ <0447.g1:< K B >>` is also double of :g:`$BH$ <0447.g1:< B H >>`.

.. _folio-iii-0448:

.. folio:: 0448
   :page: 444

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$LH$ <0447.g1:< L H >>`, :g:`$HB$ <0447.g1:< H B >>` are also double of :g:`$HB$ <0447.g1:< H B >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KB$ <0447.g1:< K B >>` is equal to :g:`$LH$ <0447.g1:< L H >>`, :g:`$HB$ <0447.g1:< H B >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the whole gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` was also proved equal to the whole :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$HF$ <0447.g1:< H F >>` is equal to :g:`$BG$ <0447.g1:< B G >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BG$ <0447.g1:< B G >>` is the rectangle :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0447.g1:/ D B />`, for :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` is equal to :g:`$DG$ <0447.g1:/ D G />`; and :g:`$HF$ <0447.g1:< H F >>` is the square on :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0447.g1:/ D B />` is equal to the square on :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`, so is :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />` to :g:`$BD$ <0447.g1:/ B D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is greater than :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />` is also greater than :g:`$BD$ <0447.g1:/ B D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, when the straight line :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` is cut in extreme and mean ratio, :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />` is the greater segment.

.. assertion::

   Therefore etc.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
