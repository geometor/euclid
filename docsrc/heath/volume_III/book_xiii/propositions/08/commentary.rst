Commentary on Proposition XIII.8
================================

Back to :ref:`Proposition XIII.8 <XIII.8>`.

In order to prove this theorem we have to show (1) that the :math:`\Delta\text{s}` :g:`AEB <0457.g1:< A E B >>`, :g:`HAB <0457.g1:< H A B >>` are similar, and (2) that :g:`EH <0457.g1:/ E H />` :math:`=` :g:`EA <0457.g1:/ E A />` (:math:`=` :g:`AB <0457.g1:/ A B />`).

To prove (2) we have

.. assertion::

   :math:`\Delta\text{s}` :g:`AEB <0457.g1:< A E B >>`, :g:`BAC <0457.g1:< B A C >>` :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< A E B > = 0457.g1:< B A C >>` in all respects,

whence

.. assertion::

   :g:`EB <0457.g1:/ E B />` :math:`=` :g:`AC <0457.g1:/ A C />`,

and

.. assertion::

   :math:`\angle` :g:`BAC <0457.g1:< B A C )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`ABE <0457.g1:< A B E )>`.

Therefore

.. assertion::

   :math:`\angle` :g:`AHE <0457.g1:< A H E )>` :math:`= 2\angle` :g:`BAC <0457.g1:< B A C )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`EAC <0457.g1:< E A C )>`,

so that

.. assertion::

   :g:`EH <0457.g1:/ E H />` :math:`=` :g:`EA <0457.g1:/ E A />` :math:`=` :g:`AB <0457.g1:/ A B />`.

To prove (1) we have, in the :math:`\Delta\text{s}` :g:`AEB <0457.g1:< A E B >>`, :g:`HAB <0457.g1:< H A B >>`,

.. assertion::

   :math:`\angle` :g:`BAH <0457.g1:< B A H )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`EBA <0457.g1:< E B A )>` :math:`= \angle` :g:`AEB <0457.g1:< A E B )>`,

and

.. subclause::

   :math:`\angle` :g:`ABE <0457.g1:< A B E )>` is common,

therefore the third :math:`\angle\text{s}` :g:`AHB <0457.g1:< A H B )>`, :g:`EAB <0457.g1:< E A B )>` are :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< A H B ) = 0457.g1:< E A B )>`,
and

.. assertion::

   :math:`\Delta\text{s}` :g:`AEB <0457.g1:< A E B >>`, :g:`HAB <0457.g1:< H A B >>` are similar.

Now, since these triangles are similar,

.. assertion::

   :g:`EB <0457.g1:/ E B />` :math:`:` :g:`BA <0457.g1:/ B A />` :math:`=` :g:`BA <0457.g1:/ B A />` :math:`:` :g:`BH <0457.g1:/ B H />`,

or

.. assertion::

   :math:`(\text{rect. }` :g:`EB <0457.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`BH <0457.g1:/ B H />` :math:`) = (\text{sq. on }` :g:`BA <0457.g1:/ B A />` :math:`)` :math:`= (\text{sq. on }` :g:`EH <0457.g1:/ E H />` :math:`)`,

so that :g:`EB <0457.g1:/ E B />` is divided in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`H <0457.g1:H>`.

Similarly its equal, :g:`CA <0457.g1:/ C A />`, is divided in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`H <0457.g1:H>`.
