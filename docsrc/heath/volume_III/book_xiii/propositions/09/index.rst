PROPOSITION 9.
==============

.. enunciation::

   If the side of the hexagon and that of the decagon inscribed in the same circle be added together, the whole straight line has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and its greater segment is the side of the hexagon.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0459.g1:( A B C )>` be a circle; of the figures inscribed in the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0459.g1:( A B C )>` let :g:`$BC$ <0459.g1:/ B C />` be the side of a decagon, :g:`$CD$ <0459.g1:/ C D />` that of a hexagon, and let them be in a straight line; I say that the whole straight line :g:`$BD$ <0459.g1:/ B D />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and :g:`$CD$ <0459.g1:/ C D />` is its greater segment.

.. picture:: 0459.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 9

For let the centre of the circle, the point :g:`$E$ <0459.g1:E>`, be taken, let :g:`$EB$ <0459.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$EC$ <0459.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$ED$ <0459.g1:/ E D />` be joined, and let :g:`$BE$ <0459.g1:/ B E />` be carried through to :g:`$A$ <0459.g1:A>`.

Since :g:`$BC$ <0459.g1:/ B C />` is the side of an equilateral decagon,

.. folio:: 0460
   :page: 456

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`$ACB$ <0459.g1:( A C B )>` is five times the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0459.g1:( B C )>`; therefore the circumference :g:`$AC$ <0459.g1:( A C )>` is quadruple of :g:`$CB$ <0459.g1:( C B )>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the circumference :g:`$AC$ <0459.g1:( A C )>` is to :g:`$CB$ <0459.g1:( C B )>`, so is the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` to the angle :g:`$CEB$ <0459.g1:< C E B )>`; :just:`[VI. 33] <VI.33>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` is quadruple of the angle :g:`$CEB$ <0459.g1:< C E B )>`.

.. subclause:: [I. 5] <I.5>

   And, since the angle :g:`$EBC$ <0459.g1:< E B C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ECB$ <0459.g1:< E C B )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` is double of the angle :g:`$ECB$ <0459.g1:< E C B )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the straight line :g:`$EC$ <0459.g1:/ E C />` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0459.g1:/ C D />`, for each of them is equal to the side of the hexagon inscribed in the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0459.g1:( A B C )>`, :just:`[IV. 15, Por.]` the angle :g:`$CED$ <0459.g1:< C E D )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$CDE$ <0459.g1:< C D E )>`; :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

.. assertion:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore the angle :g:`$ECB$ <0459.g1:< E C B )>` is double of the angle :g:`$EDC$ <0459.g1:< E D C )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` was proved double of the angle :g:`$ECB$ <0459.g1:< E C B )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` is quadruple of the angle :g:`$EDC$ <0459.g1:< E D C )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0459.g1:< A E C )>` was also proved quadruple of the angle :g:`$BEC$ <0459.g1:< B E C )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$EDC$ <0459.g1:< E D C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$BEC$ <0459.g1:< B E C )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$EBD$ <0459.g1:< E B D )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$BEC$ <0459.g1:< B E C >>` and :g:`$BED$ <0459.g1:< B E D >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$BED$ <0459.g1:< B E D )>` is also equal to the remaining angle :g:`$ECB$ <0459.g1:< E C B )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$EBD$ <0459.g1:< E B D >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$EBC$ <0459.g1:< E B C >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$DB$ <0459.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0459.g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`$EB$ <0459.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`$BC$ <0459.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EB$ <0459.g1:/ E B />` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0459.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$BD$ <0459.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$DC$ <0459.g1:/ D C />`, so is :g:`$DC$ <0459.g1:/ D C />` to :g:`$CB$ <0459.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BD$ <0459.g1:/ B D />` is greater than :g:`$DC$ <0459.g1:/ D C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DC$ <0459.g1:/ D C />` is also greater than :g:`$CB$ <0459.g1:/ C B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the straight line :g:`$BD$ <0459.g1:/ B D />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and :g:`$DC$ <0459.g1:/ D C />` is its greater segment.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
