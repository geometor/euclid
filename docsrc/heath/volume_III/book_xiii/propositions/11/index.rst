PROPOSITION 11.
===============

.. enunciation::

   If in a circle which has its diameter rational an equilateral pentagon be inscribed, the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight line called minor.

For in the circle :g:`$ABCDE$ <0466.g1:( A B C D E )>` which has its diameter rational let the :eq:`equilateral <0466.g1:/ A B / = 0466.g1:/ B C / = 0466.g1:/ C D / = 0466.g1:/ D E / = 0466.g1:/ E A />` pentagon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0466.g1:< A B C D E >>` be inscribed; I say that the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight line called minor.

For let the centre of the circle, the point :g:`$F$ <0466.g1:F>`, be taken, let :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />` be joined and carried through to the points, :g:`$G$ <0466.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0466.g1:H>`, let :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` be joined, and let :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` be made a fourth part of :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is also rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since the circumference :g:`$ACG$ <0466.g1:( A C G )>` is :eq:`equal <0466.g1:( A C G ) = 0466.g1:( A D G )>` to the circumference :g:`$ADG$ <0466.g1:( A D G )>`, and in them :g:`$ABC$ <0466.g1:( A B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0466.g1:( A B C ) = 0466.g1:( A E D )>` to :g:`$AED$ <0466.g1:( A E D )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$CG$ <0466.g1:( C G )>` is :eq:`equal <0466.g1:( C G ) = 0466.g1:( G D )>` to the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0466.g1:( G D )>`.

.. _folio-iii-0466:

.. folio:: 0466
   :page: 462

.. assertion::

   And, if we join :g:`$AD$ <0466.g1:/ A D />`, we conclude that the angles at :g:`$L$ <0466.g1:L>` are right, and :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` is double of :g:`$CL$ <0466.g1:/ C L />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angles at :g:`$M$ <0466.g1:M>` are also right, and :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`.

.. picture:: 0466.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 11

.. subclause::

   Since then the angle :g:`$ALC$ <0466.g1:< A L C )>` is :eq:`equal <0466.g1:< A L C ) = 0466.g1:< A M F )>` to the angle :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F )>`, and the angle :g:`$LAC$ <0466.g1:< L A C )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L >>` and :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L )>` is :eq:`equal <0466.g1:< A C L ) = 0466.g1:< M F A )>` to the remaining angle :g:`$MFA$ <0466.g1:< M F A )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F >>`; therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   And the doubles of the antecedents may be taken;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is the double of :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the double of :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to the half of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to the half of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   And the halves of the consequents may be taken;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to the half of :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to the fourth of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. _folio-iii-0467:

.. folio:: 0467
   :page: 463

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is half of :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, and :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` a fourth part of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`.

.. subclause::

   :term:`*Componendo* <componendo>` also, as the sum of :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is to :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`; :just:`[v. 18]`

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the square on the sum of :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is to the square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   And since, when the straight line subtending two sides of the pentagon, as :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />`, is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is equal to the side of the pentagon, that is, to :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :just:`[xiii. 8]` while the square on the greater segment added to the half of the whole is five times the square on the half of the whole, :just:`[xiii. 1]` and :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is half of the whole :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` taken as one straight line is five times the square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`.

.. subclause::

   But it was proved that, as the square on :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` taken as one straight line is to the square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` is rational, for the diameter is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is also rational; therefore :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is quadruple of :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`; therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is twenty-five times the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`; therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has not to the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. subclause::

   And each of them is rational.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only.

.. _folio-iii-0468:

.. folio:: 0468
   :page: 464

.. subclause::

   But, if from a rational straight line there be subtracted a rational straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, the remainder is irrational, namely an apotome;

.. assertion:: [x. 73]

   therefore :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is an apotome and :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` the annex to it.

I say next that :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is also a fourth apotome.

Let the square on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` be equal to that by which the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`; therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` by the square on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` is commensurable with :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />`,

.. assertion:: [x. 15]

   :term:`*componendo* <componendo>` also, :g:`$KB$ <0466.g1:/ K B />` is commensurable with :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is commensurable with :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 12]

   therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is also commensurable with :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has to the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` the ratio which $5$ has to $1$.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has to the square on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` the ratio which $5$ has to $4$ :just:`[v. 19, Por.]`, and this is not the ratio which a square number has to a square number; therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is incommensurable with :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />`; :just:`[x. 9]` therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the square on the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on the annex :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, and the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is commensurable with the rational straight line, :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`, set out,

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 4]

   therefore :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is a fourth apotome.

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and a fourth apotome is irrational, and its square root is irrational, and is called minor. :just:`[x. 94]`

.. assertion::

   But the square on :g:`$AB$ <0466.g1:/ A B />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />`, :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />`, because, when :g:`$AH$ <0466.g1:/ A H />` is joined, the triangle :g:`$ABH$ <0466.g1:< A B H >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$ABM$ <0466.g1:< A B M >>`, and, as :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0466.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />`.

.. _folio-iii-0469:

.. folio:: 0469
   :page: 465

.. assertion::

   Therefore the side :g:`$AB$ <0466.g1:/ A B />` of the pentagon is the irrational straight line called minor.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
