**Digital reconstruction, semantic discovery, and critical Book Codices of Euclid's *Elements* and *Data* (*Δεδομένα*).**

``geometor.euclid`` is the primary textual and visual source of truth for :person:`Euclid of Alexandria <euclid>` in the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium. It unites the three volumes of Sir Thomas L. Heath's 1926 Cambridge/Dover critical edition of *The Thirteen Books of the Elements* (1,416 archival folios and 831 recalibrated geometric SVGs) with a synchronized four-track bilingual **Book Codex** of **Euclid's *Data* (*Δεδομένα*)** based on Heinrich Menge's 1896 Teubner Greek text (`Vat. gr. 190`) and Robert Simson's critical edition, powered by ``photon-platform/publish``.

----

Codices & Edition Highlights
----------------------------

* :doc:`Elements (Books I–XIII, Heath 1926) <heath/index>` — Complete de-paginated text of Books I–XIII with marginal ``.. folio::`` markers, interactive SVG diagrams, and synchronized **Proposition** and **Commentary** tracks.
* :doc:`Data (Protheoria & Propositions 1–94, Menge 1896) <data/index>` — Complete four-track bilingual codex (**Proposition** · **Greek (Menge 1896)** · **Summary** · **Notes**) covering Marinus of Neapolis's *Protheoria* (§§ 1–18), Definitions 1–15, and Propositions 1–94 (`D.1`–`D.94`), with :doc:`Master Concordance <data/concordance>` and interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVGs.
* :doc:`People <people/index>` & :doc:`Bibliography <bibliography>` — Unified biographical register (:doc:`Euclid <people/index>`, :doc:`Heath <people/index>`, :doc:`Heiberg <people/index>`, :doc:`Menge <people/index>`, :doc:`Simson <people/index>`, :doc:`Marinus <people/index>`) and critical bibliography, backed by individual codex indices (:doc:`Elements People <heath/people/index>` · :doc:`Data People <data/people/index>` · :doc:`Elements Bibliography <heath/bibliography>` · :doc:`Data Bibliography <data/bibliography>`).
* :doc:`Using This Edition (Reader's Guide) <usage/index>` — Comprehensive guide to synchronized tracks, 1,416 archival folios, interactive SVG highlights, and search tools.
* **Interactive Geometry & Justifications** — Bidirectional ``:g:`` and ``:eq:`` highlighting between proof text and SVG primitives, plus ``:just:`` justification pills linking *Data* proofs directly to the *Elements* (`I.1`–`XIII.18`).
* **Downstream Symbolic Codices** — Feeds compiled ``objects.inv`` inventories and functional dependency DAGs into `GEOMETOR • elements <https://geometor.github.io/elements>`_ (**G-Index** ``G.1``–``G.525``) and `GEOMETOR • pappus <https://geometor.github.io/pappus>`_ (*Treasury of Analysis*).
