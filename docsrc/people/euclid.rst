Euclid of Alexandria
====================

*Geometer · Author of Elements, Data, Phaenomena & Optics*

Alexandrian mathematician called ὁ Στοιχειωτής ("the Writer of Elements") by Proclus and Marinus of Neapolis. Active during the reign of Ptolemy I Soter (323–285 BCE), Euclid founded the mathematical school of Alexandria. His monumental *Elements* (Στοιχεῖα) unified centuries of Hellenistic mathematical discovery—from the Pythagorean theory of numbers and Eudoxus's theory of proportion to Theaetetus's classification of incommensurable lines and regular polyhedra—into an axiomatic-deductive system that became the paradigm of rigorous demonstration for two millennia. Alongside the synthetic *Elements*, Euclid composed the *Data* (Δεδομένα) as the foundational manual of geometric analysis and functional determinacy, establishing what magnitudes, ratios, and figures are uniquely determined when others are given.
