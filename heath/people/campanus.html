Campanus of Novara¶

Medieval Italian Mathematician & Editor of the Latin Euclid

Prepared the authoritative medieval Latin edition and commentary of the Elements (based on Adelard of Bath and Arabic sources), which was the first printed edition of Euclid (Ratdolt, Venice, 1482) and the basis of Pacioli’s 1509 edition.

Cited Works: Elementa Geometriae (printed Venice, 1482)
