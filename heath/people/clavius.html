Christopher Clavius, S.J.¶

Jesuit Mathematician & Architect of the Gregorian Calendar

Published an encyclopedic Latin edition and commentary on Euclid’s Elements (1574) containing extensive scholia, critiques of Peletier on the angle of contact, and an attempted proof of the Parallel Postulate.

Cited Works: Euclidis Elementorum Libri XV (Rome, 1574)
