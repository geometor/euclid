Augustus De Morgan¶

British Mathematician & Logician

Wrote penetrating critical analyses of Euclid’s logic, Book V theory of proportion, and Book X theory of irrationals in the Companion to the Almanac and the Penny Cyclopaedia.

Cited Works: Study and Difficulties of Mathematics (1831), Connexion of Number and Magnitude (1836), Formal Logic (1847), Articles in The Penny Cyclopaedia
