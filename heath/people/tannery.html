Paul Tannery¶

French Historian of Ancient Science & Mathematics

Pioneered the critical reconstruction of pre-Euclidean Greek geometry, the history of the terms axiom and common notion, and the edition of Diophantus.

Cited Works: La Géométrie grecque (1887), Pour l’histoire de la science hellène (1887), Memoires Bordeaux
