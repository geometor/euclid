Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- Continues the discussion of W. Bolyai and Lobachewsky's construction of the plane from Folio ``0188`` (page 174), beginning mid-sentence (``before on the curve of intersection (a circle)...``).
- Presents Rausenberger's critique and proves Simson's plane property via five successive congruent triangle pairs ($ACM, BCM$; $ACN, BCN$; $AMN, BMN$; $AMP, BMP$; $ACP, BCP$).
- Summarizes "Other views" on the plane definition/postulate: Enriques and Amaldi (*Elementi di geometria*, 1905), Veronese, Hilbert, and G. Ingrami (*Elementi di geometria*, 1904, *trilatero* / three-side postulate).
- Concludes at the bottom of page 175 with the locus definition of a plane (``*the locus of all points equidistant from two fixed points in space*.``), which continues on Folio ``0190`` (page 176) with Leibniz and Beeckman.
