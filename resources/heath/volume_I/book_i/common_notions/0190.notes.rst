Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Terminology**:

  - ``Ἐπίπεδος δὲ γωνία`` — plane angle (Def. 8).
  - ``εὐθύγραμμος καλεῖται ἡ γωνία`` — the angle is called rectilineal (Def. 9).
  - ``συνεχής`` / ``οὐ συνεχεῖς`` — continuous / not continuous (Heron's gloss on Euclid's ``μὴ ἐπ’ εὐθείας κειμένων``).
  - ``κλίσις`` vs. ``κλάσις`` (``κεκλάσθαι``, ``κλᾶν``, ``τὴν κεκαμμένην καὶ ἔχουσαν γωνίαν``) — Euclid's *inclination* of two meeting lines versus the pre-Euclidean/Aristotelian conception of an angle as a *breaking* or *bending* of a single line.
  - ``ὁ ἡμέτερος καθηγεμών`` — "our master/professor", Proclus's standard designation for Syrianus.
  - ``συναγωγὴ ἐπιφανείας ἢ στερεοῦ πρὸς ἑνὶ σημείῳ ὑπὸ κεκλασμένῃ γραμμῇ ἢ ἐπιφανείᾳ`` — Apollonius's definition of a plane or solid angle as the contraction of a surface or solid at one point under a broken line or surface.

- **Typographic Note**: In the translation of Definition 9 (``And when the lines containing the angle are straight, the angle is called rectilineal.``), the tail of the comma after ``straight`` is faintly printed on the scan, appearing almost like a period.
