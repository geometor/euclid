## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terms & Phrases**:
  - `τὸ πρῶτον διάστημα ὑπὸ τὸ σημεῖον` — "the first distance under the point" (split across folios `0190` and `0191`).
  - `ἐπ’ ἄπειρον` — without limit (*ad infinitum*).
  - `ἐφ’ ἓν διεστώς` / `τὸ ἐφ’ ἓν διαστατόν` — extended in one sense / that which is extended in one dimension.
  - `πάντων παραδοξότατον` — the greatest possible paradox.
  - `ποσόν`, `ποιόν`, `πρός τι` — the three Aristotelian categories under debate: *quantum* (quantity), *quale* (quality), and *relation*.
  - `κερατοειδής` — the "horn-like" (cornicular/contingent) angle between a circular arc and its tangent, which violates the Archimedean property (Eucl. V. Def. 4) relative to rectilineal angles as proved in Eucl. III. 16.
- **Historical & Arabic Transmission**: Notes "Aganis" (`socius` of Simplicius in Gerard of Cremona's Latin translation of an-Nairīzī, p. 13), who adapted Apollonius's definition explicitly into the category of quantity.
