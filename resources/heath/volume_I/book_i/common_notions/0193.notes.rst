Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Critique of "Direction"**: Heath cites Schotten and O. Th. Bürklen to show why defining an angle as "the difference of direction between two straight lines" is logically circular: direction is neither an extensive nor an intensive magnitude, so "difference of direction" covertly presupposes the concept of angle.
- **Dimensionality of the Angle (Veronese & Amaldi)**: Contrasts the 2D planar region (*angular sector*, a two-dimensional aggregate of points) with the 1D pencil/cluster segment (a one-dimensional aggregate of *rays* issuing from the vertex).
