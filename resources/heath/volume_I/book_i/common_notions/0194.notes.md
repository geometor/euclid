## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Typographic Emphasis**: In paragraph (2), within the italicized definition `*the aggregate of the rays issuing from* O *and belonging to the angular sector which has* a *and* b *for sides*`, the symbols `O`, `a`, and `b` are printed in upright roman type to contrast with the surrounding italic text.
- **Veronese's Point-Reflection Definition of Parallels**: By defining two lines as parallel when two points of one are the central reflection ("opposite" or "reflex") of two points of the other across the midpoint of a common transversal, Veronese introduces parallelism in 1D/point-reflection terms before defining the 2D plane $Pr$, enabling him to include the limiting line $r'$ through $P$ that fails to meet $r$.
