Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Two-Line Bounded Figures (Proclus pp. 159, 14—160, 9)**:

  - Summarizes five classes of two-line figures:

    1. Circumference + circumference: ($a$) *lune* (``τὸ μηνοειδές``) and bi-convex lens; ($b$) *angle-less* (``ἀγώνιον``), i.e., the *coronal* region between concentric circles.
    2. Circumference + straight line: semicircle or smaller segments (``ἁψῖδες``).
    3. Mixed line + mixed line (e.g., intersecting ellipses).
    4. Mixed line + circumference (e.g., intersecting ellipse and circle).
    5. Mixed line + straight line (e.g., half an ellipse).

- **Interpolated Definition after Def. 18**:

  - Notes that MSS. interpolate a definition of a *segment of a circle* from Book III Def. 6, omitted by Proclus, Martianus Capella, and Boethius.

- **Definitions 19–21 (Greek & English)**:

  - **Def. 19**: Rectilineal figures (``Σχήματα εὐθύγραμμά``), subdivided into trilateral (``τρίπλευρα``), quadrilateral (``τετράπλευρα``), and multilateral (``πολύπλευρα``). Heath observes that these ``-πλευρον`` terms are likely Euclid's own coinage to disambiguate ``τετράγωνον`` (restricting it to the square).
  - **Def. 20**: Classification of triangles by sides: equilateral (``ἰσόπλευρον``), isosceles (``ἰσοσκελὲς``, "with equal legs"), and scalene (``σκαληνὸν``, variant ``σκαληνής``).
  - **Def. 21**: Classification of triangles by angles: right-angled (``ὀρθογώνιον``), obtuse-angled (``ἀμβλυγώνιον``), and acute-angled (``ὀξυγώνιον``).
