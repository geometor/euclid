## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Group (1) — Non-secant & Projective Definitions**:
  - Examines Schotten's critique of projective formulations—lines meeting at infinity or sharing a point at infinity—attributed to **Kepler** (1604, as a *"façon de parler"*) and **Desargues** (1639).
  - Quotes **Gauss** (letter to Schumacher) on the epistemological limits of finite observation regarding the infinite, and **Jacob Steiner** (*"nach einer und derselben Richtung hin"*) on a rotating ray becoming parallel when *"there is no real cutting"*.
- **Modern Italian & German Foundations**:
  - Cites **Ingrami** (*Elementi di geometria*, 1904), **Enriques and Amaldi** (*Elementi di geometria*, 1905), and **Hilbert** as retaining Euclid's non-secant definition.
  - Contrasts **Giuseppe Veronese** (*Fondamenti di geometria*, 1891 vs. *Elementi*), who defines parallels via central symmetry ("figures opposite to one another with respect to a point $O$", or *reflex* figures) to avoid explicit appeal to the plane or infinite production.
