--------------------------

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Saccheri's Proof of Postulate 4**: Superimposes perpendicular $LH$ (standing on $FHM$) onto $DA$ (standing on $BAC$) with $DA = HL$. By Saccheri's third lemma, $FHM$ cannot merely touch $BC$ at $A$; if it cuts $BC$ with $F$ above and $M$ below, then $\angle LHF = \angle DAF < \angle DAB = \angle DAC < \angle DAM = \angle LHM$, contradicting $\angle LHF = \angle LHM$.
- **Pappus' Lunular Angle**: Constructs equal semicircles $AEB$ and $BDC$ on perpendicular equal segments $BA, BC$. Since mixed angles $\angle EBA = \angle DBC$, adding the common mixed angle $\angle ABD$ yields the curvilineal ("lunular") angle $\angle EBD$ equal in area/superposition to the rectilineal right angle $\angle ABC$, showing why the converse of Postulate 4 ("an angle equal to a right angle is right") requires restricting to *rectilineal* angles.
- **Modern Axiom Systems & Structural Placement**:

  - Veronese, Ingrami, and Enriques & Amaldi deduce the equality of right angles from the equality of flat angles.
  - Hilbert deduces Postulate 4 from his Group III axioms of congruence.
  - Within Euclid's architecture, Postulate 4 is an indispensable prerequisite to Postulate 5, since "less than two right angles" requires a right angle to be a determinate and invariable standard.
