## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Postulate 5**:
  - Greek text: `Καὶ ἐὰν εἰς δύο εὐθείας εὐθεῖα ἐμπίπτουσα τὰς ἐντὸς καὶ ἐπὶ τὰ αὐτὰ μέρη γωνίας δύο ὀρθῶν ἐλάσσονας ποιῇ, ἐκβαλλομένας τὰς δύο εὐθείας ἐπ' ἄπειρον συμπίπτειν, ἐφ' ἃ μέρη εἰσὶν αἱ τῶν δύο ὀρθῶν ἐλάσσονες.`
  - Translation: *That, if a straight line falling on two straight lines make the interior angles on the same side less than two right angles, the two straight lines, if produced indefinitely, meet on that side on which are the angles less than the two right angles.*
- **Originality of Euclid's Formulation**: Heath connects Aristotle's remark in *Analytica Priora* II. 16, 65 a 4 (that contemporary theories of parallels involved a *petitio principii*) with Euclid's formulation of Postulate 5 as an explicit, indemonstrable hypothesis.
- **Bibliographical References**:
  - Pietro Riccardi, *Saggio di una bibliografia Euclidea*, Part IV (Bologna, 1890): 20 quarto pages of monographs on Postulate 5 from 1607 to 1887.
  - Max Simon, *Ueber die Entwicklung der Elementar-geometrie im XIX. Jahrhundert* (1906), pp. 53–61.
  - Heinrich Schotten, *Inhalt und Methode des planimetrischen Unterrichts*, II, pp. 183–332.
  - Roberto Bonola, Article 8, *Sulla teoria delle parallele e sulle geometrie non-euclidee*, in Federigo Enriques (ed.), *Questioni riguardanti le matematiche elementari*, I, pp. 247–363.
