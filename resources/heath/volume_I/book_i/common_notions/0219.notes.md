# Transcription Notes: Folio 0219 (Page 205)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio**: `0219` (Page 205)
- **Section**: Book I, Note on Postulate 5 (continued from Folio `0218` / Page 204 and continuing onto Folio `0220` / Page 206).
- **Content**:
  1. Conclusion of Heath's bracketed remark on the symmetry argument for common perpendiculars and its interpretation under the Riemann (elliptic) hypothesis, where two straight lines meet at two antipodal points $K, L$ (or a single point in the single-elliptic / projective plane).
  2. **Part II of Ptolemy's attempt**: Ptolemy's attempt to prove Proposition I.29 without Postulate 5 (preserved in Proclus, *In Eucl.* pp. 365, 14–367, 27), arguing by symmetry that $AF, CG$ are no more parallel than $FB, GD$.
  3. **Part III of Ptolemy's attempt**: Ptolemy's deduction of Postulate 5 from I.29 by *reductio ad absurdum*.
