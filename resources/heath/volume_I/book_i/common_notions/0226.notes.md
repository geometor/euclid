## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Imperfection in Saccheri's Refutation of the Obtuse Angle Hypothesis**: Heath notes that Saccheri's proofs of Prop. XII. and Prop. XIII. rely on Eucl. I. 18 and I. 16 (the exterior angle theorem), which implicitly assumes that straight lines are infinite in length—an assumption that fails in elliptic/Riemannian geometry.
- **Asymptotic Parallels (Props. XXIII, XXX–XXXII)**: Summarizes Saccheri's anticipation of Lobachevsky and Bolyai regarding ultra-parallel lines (having a common perpendicular) and limiting/asymptotic parallels in a pencil of rays.
- **G. S. Klügel (1763) & J. H. Lambert (1728–1777)**: Discusses Klügel's *Conatuum praecipuorum theoriam parallelarum demonstrandi recensio* (1763) and Lambert's *Theorie der Parallellinien* (written 1766, published posthumously by G. Bernoulli and C. F. Hindenburg). Introduces the "Lambert quadrilateral" (a trirectangular quadrilateral, half of Saccheri's quadrilateral divided by its median) and Lambert's theorem that angular defect/excess is proportional to triangle area.
