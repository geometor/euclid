Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Legendre's Twelfth Edition Construction (1823)**: Starting with $\triangle ABC$, Legendre draws the median $AD$ to the midpoint $D$ of $BC$, lays off $AC' = AB$ along $AD$ produced, and $AB' = 2AD$ along $AB$ produced. By decomposing $\triangle AB'C'$ via $K$ (where $AK = AD$) into $\triangle AC'K \cong \triangle ABD$ and $\triangle B'C'K \cong \triangle ACD$, the angle sum of $\triangle AB'C'$ equals that of $\triangle ABC$, while $\angle C'AB' < \frac{1}{2}\angle A$.
- **Relation to Euclid I. 16**: Heath notes in brackets that $\triangle AB'C'$ is congruent to the triangle $\triangle AEB$ formed by the standard Euclid I. 16 construction (shown by the dotted lines $C'E$ and $BE$ in the diagram), merely rotated so that its side $AB'$ lies along the baseline $AB$.
