## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Subtlety in the Proof of Theorem VII**: In the Lemma, marking off $RR' = PR$ along $r$ guarantees $\angle RR'P \le \omega/2$ unconditionally (by Eucl. I. 16 / Theorem I), so rays from $P$ meeting $r$ make arbitrarily small angles $\le \omega/2^n$ with $r$. Only in the final step—identifying the angle made with $r$ with the alternate interior angle made with $s \perp PQ$—is the hypothesis that $\triangle PQR$ has angle sum $2R$ invoked (whereas in hyperbolic geometry $\angle QPR + \angle QRP < \pi/2$, so $\angle(PR_n, s)$ stays bounded above the complement of the angle of parallelism).
- **Bibliographical Sources**: Heath acknowledges his heavy reliance on Roberto Bonola's *Sulla teoria delle parallele e sulle geometrie non-euclidee* (1900) and *La geometria non-euclidea* (1906), as well as Stäckel and Engel's *Die Theorie der Parallellinien von Euklid bis auf Gauss* (1895).
