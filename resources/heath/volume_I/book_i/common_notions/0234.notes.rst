Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Catalogue of 9 Equivalents to Postulate 5**:

  1. **Playfair's Axiom** (uniqueness of parallels, traced back to Proclus on I. 31), along with its equivalents **(1** *a*) (transversal intersecting one parallel intersects the other, Proclus) and **(1** *b*) (transitivity of parallelism, Eucl. I. 30).
  2. **Equidistance** (Posidonius and Geminus) and bounded distance between parallels (Proclus).
  3. **Existence of a single triangle with angle sum $2R$** (Legendre, assuming Archimedes' Axiom).
  4. **Existence of similar unequal figures** (Wallis, Carnot, Laplace), refined by Saccheri to two unequal triangles with equal angles.
  5. **Crossbar through an interior point of an angle $< \frac{2}{3}R$** (Legendre), compared with Lorenz's axiom (1791).
  6. **Circumcircle through any three non-collinear points** (Legendre, Wolfgang Bolyai).
  7. **Triangles of arbitrarily large area** (Gauss to W. Bolyai, December 1799), compared with Legendre's Prop. IV and Worpitzky's axiom excluding triangles with arbitrarily small angles.
  8. **Clairaut's rectangle axiom** (1741: a Lambert quadrilateral has a fourth right angle).
  9. **Point-reflection symmetry of parallels across midpoints of transversal segments** (Veronese, *Elementi*, 1904; Ingrami, *Elementi*, 1904).
