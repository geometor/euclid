## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heron's Three Axioms**: Proclus (p. 196, 15) reports that Heron reduced the axioms to the first three, whereas Proclus himself defended retaining five (including *the whole is greater than the part* and *things which coincide are equal*).
- **Common Notion 1 Greek Text**: `Τὰ τῷ αὐτῷ ἴσα καὶ ἀλλήλοις ἐστὶν ἴσα.` ("Things which are equal to the same thing are also equal to one another.")
- **Apollonius's Proof of C.N. 1**: Quoted from Proclus (p. 194, 25), reducing equality to occupying the same space (`τόπον ἴσον κατέχει` / `occupies the same space with it`), illustrated by three vertical segments $A$, $B$, $C$.
