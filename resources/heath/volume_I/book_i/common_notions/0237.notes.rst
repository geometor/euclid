Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Terms & Citations**:

  - ``γνωριμώτερον`` ("better known / more intelligible"): Aristotelian epistemological criterion for demonstration (*Posterior Analytics* I. 2, 71b21).
  - ``τόπος`` ("space / place"): Cited from Proclus (p. 195, 6) regarding Apollonius's middle term of occupying the same space.
  - ``καθόλου`` ("general / universal"): Aristotelian requirement that axioms apply universally across categories (numbers, speeds, periods of time) rather than merely spatial magnitudes.
  - **Common Notion 2**: ``Καὶ ἐὰν ἴσοις ἴσα προστεθῇ, τὰ ὅλα ἐστὶν ἴσα.`` (*If equals be added to equals, the wholes are equal.*)
  - **Common Notion 3**: ``Καὶ ἐὰν ἀπὸ ἴσων ἴσα ἀφαιρεθῇ, τὰ καταλειπόμενά ἐστιν ἴσα.`` (*If equals be subtracted from equals, the remainders are equal.*)

- **Interpolated Axioms** (*a*–*d*):

  - Heiberg brackets (*a*), (*b*), (*c*) in the Teubner text and omits (*d*).
  - An-Nairīzī records (*a*), (*d*), (*b*), (*c*) along with Simplicius's testimony that only three axioms (``sententiae acceptae``) stood in the ancient manuscripts.
  - Heath critiques (*a*) by reference to I. 17, noting the logical indeterminacy of "unequal" vs. "greater" and "less".
