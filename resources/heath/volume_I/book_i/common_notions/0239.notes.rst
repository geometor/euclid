Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Terms & Syntax**:

  - ``ἐπί`` with the accusative (Euclid, Archimedes) vs. dative (Pappus) after ``ἐφαρμόζειν``.
  - ``ἐφαρμοζομένου`` ("being applied", passive participle in I. 4) and ``τιθεμένου`` ("being placed", passive participle in I. 4).
  - ``παραλλάξουσιν`` ("fall beside them / take a different position", future active in I. 8).

- **Authenticity & Role of Superposition**:

  - Heath compares two unquoted assumptions in I. 4: (1) that coinciding bases are equal (C. N. 4), and (2) that if they do not coincide, two straight lines will enclose a space. Because neither is cited as an explicit axiom in I. 4 and Heron recognized only three Common Notions, Heath inclines toward regarding C. N. 4 as an early interpolation.
  - Despite Euclid's reluctance to use superposition where avoidable (e.g., proving I. 26 by *reductio ad absurdum* rather than superposition), Heath stresses that I. 4 makes superposition fundamental to the architecture of the *Elements*.
