Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Philosophical & Foundational Citations**:

  - **Veronese's** *petitio principii* **argument**: To say figure $A$ moves from $A_1$ to $A_2$ "without deformation" means the mutual relations among points in $A_2$ are *equal* to those in $A_1$, which presupposes the concept of equality of distinct figures in space prior to defining rigid motion.
  - **Schopenhauer** (*Die Welt als Wille***, 2nd ed. 1844, II. p. 130**): Critiques the eighth axiom (Common Notion 4 in older editions numbering the postulates and axioms together): ``'Figures which coincide (sich decken) are equal to one another'``, contrasting ``das Sichdecken`` (coincidence) and external empirical mobility of matter with ``Anschauung`` (pure intuition of space).
  - **Bertrand Russell (1902)**: Explains geometrical "motion" as the transference of attention from an original figure to a new figure defined by corresponding elements and shared properties.
