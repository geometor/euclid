Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Univocal Point Correspondence (Ingrami)**:

  - Defines equality (isometry) of arbitrary figures via a bijection ("correspond *univocally*") preserving congruence of all pairwise rectilineal segments $AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD, \dots$.

- **Comparative Axiomatics of Congruence**:

  - **Pasch (1882)**: Takes congruence of *finite point-configurations* as primitive; derives congruent segments and angles.
  - **Veronese (1891, 1904)**: Takes congruence of *rectilineal segments* as primitive; bridges to angles/triangles via a postulate on intersecting line pairs $(AB, AC)$ and $(A'B', A'C')$ where $AB \cong A'B'$, $AC \cong A'C'$, and $BC \cong B'C'$.
  - **Hilbert (1903)**: Takes congruence of *both segments and angles* as primitive, trading minimal theoretical primitives for didactic clarity.
