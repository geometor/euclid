Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Congruence of Supplementary Angles**: Proves that supplements of congruent angles are congruent (analogous to part of Euclid I. 13) using only segment additivity (9) and SAS congruence (11), without assuming that a straight angle is divisible or measurable.
- **Interior Half-Ray Proposition**: Demonstrates that an interior ray $l$ dividing angle $(h, k)$ has a corresponding interior ray $l'$ in any congruent angle $(h', k')$ such that both sub-angles are congruent—a lemma needed to establish the congruence of all right angles (Euclid Postulate 4).
