## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proof of Euclid's Postulate 4 ("All right angles are congruent with one another")**: Hilbert treats Euclid's Postulate 4 as a theorem deduced from the congruence axioms, supplementary angle congruence, and the interior half-ray theorem.
- **Printed Notation `a half-ray CAD'''`**: In the sixth paragraph, Heath prints "we can find within the angle $CAD$ a half-ray $CAD'''$ such that..." (writing $CAD'''$ instead of $AD'''$ for the half-ray, influenced by the adjacent angle notation $CAD'''$). This has been preserved verbatim.
- **Missing Period After $C'A'D'$**: At the end of the third paragraph ("...with its adjacent angle $C'A'D'$  All four angles are then right angles."), the period after $C'A'D'$ is faintly inked or dropped in the scan next to the prime; supplied in transcription for clarity.
- **Pappus's Proof of Euclid I. 5 & Philo's Proof of Euclid I. 8**: Hilbert's deduction of Euclid I. 5 by applying SAS to $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle ACB$ directly echoes Pappus's well-known proof, and the proof of Euclid I. 8 via juxtaposition along $BC$ and joining $AA''$ is the classic Philo of Byzantium / textbook alternative proof avoiding direct superposition.
