Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Missing Intersection Postulates in Euclid**: Heath analyses Euclid's tacit assumptions regarding the existence of intersections of a circle with a straight line (e.g., in I. 12) and of a circle with a circle (e.g., in I. 1 and I. 22), observing that even Book III lacks a formal discussion of the conditions under which two circles share two, one, or no common points.
- **Principle of Continuity**: Cites Wilhelm Killing (*Einführung in die Grundlagen der Geometrie*, Vol. II, p. 43) for two geometric formulations (*a* static, *b* kinematic) of the Principle of Continuity.
- **Introduction to Dedekind's Postulate**: Draws on Giuseppe Vitali's article in Federigo Enriques' *Questioni riguardanti le matematiche elementari* (Vol. I, Art. 5, pp. 123–143) to motivate Dedekind's cut on a line segment $AB$ divided by a point $C$ into two parts satisfying three order properties.
