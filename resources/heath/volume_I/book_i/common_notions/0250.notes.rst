--------------------------------------------

- **Dedekind's Postulate**: Contrasts the segment formulation (following Vitali in Enriques' *Questioni*) with Richard Dedekind's original 1872 formulation in *Stetigkeit und irrationale Zahlen* (new ed. 1905, p. 11).
