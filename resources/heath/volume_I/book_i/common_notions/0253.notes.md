## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Monotonicity of Distance on the Semicircle**: By Euclid I. 24 (comparing triangles $OO'P$ and $OO'Q$ with $OO'$ common, $OP = OQ$, and $\angle POO' < \angle QOO'$), the distance $O'P$ strictly increases as $P$ moves along the semicircle from $A$ (inside $C'$) to $B$ (outside $C'$), enabling Dedekind's Postulate on circular arcs.
- **Verbatim Typographical Note (`R` for `R'`)**: Near the bottom of page 239, Heath initially writes "I say that $O'M = R'$." and "For, if not, suppose $O'M < R'$.", but in the concluding lines of the proof prints:
  - "Similarly it may be proved that $O'M$ is not greater than $R$."
  - "Hence $O'M = R$."
  Here $R$ is a typographical slip in the printed edition for $R'$ (the radius of circle $C'$ with centre $O'$); the printed text is preserved verbatim in `0253.md`.
