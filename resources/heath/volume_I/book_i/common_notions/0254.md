Join <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ O P /">$OP$</span>, and draw another radius <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ O Q /">$OQ$</span> such that the angle <span data-ref="0254.g1:< P O Q )">$POQ$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0254.g1:< M O P )">$MOP$</span>.

![Construction of chord MM' not greater than sigma](0254.g1.svg)

The intersection, <span data-ref="0254.g1:M'">$M'$</span>, of <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ O Q /">$OQ$</span> with the circle satisfies the required condition.

For <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M M' /">$MM'$</span> meets <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ O P /">$OP$</span> at right angles in <span data-ref="0254.g1:S">$S$</span>.

Therefore, in the right-angled triangle <span data-ref="0254.g1:< M S P >">$MSP$</span>, <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M S /">$MS$</span> is not greater than <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span> (it is less, unless <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span> coincides with <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M S /">$MS$</span>, when it is equal).

Therefore <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M S /">$MS$</span> is not greater than <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M P /">$\sigma/2$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M M' /">$MM'$</span> is not greater than <span data-ref="0254.g1:/ M M' /">$\sigma$</span>.]
