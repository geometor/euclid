BOOK I.
=======

DEFINITIONS.
------------

1. A :term:`**point** <point>` is that which has no part.

2. A :term:`**line** <line>` is breadthless length.

3. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a line are points.

4. A :term:`**straight line** <straight-line>` is a line which lies evenly with the points on itself.

5. A :term:`**surface** <surface>` is that which has length and breadth only.

6. The :term:`**extremities** <extremity>` of a surface are lines.

7. A :term:`**plane surface** <plane-surface>` is a surface which lies evenly with the straight lines on itself.

8. A :term:`**plane angle** <plane-angle>` is the inclination to one another of two lines in a plane which meet one another and do not lie in a straight line.

9. And when the lines containing the angle are straight, the angle is called :term:`**rectilineal** <rectilineal-angle>`.

10. When a straight line set up on a straight line makes the adjacent angles equal to one another, each of the equal angles is :term:`**right** <right-angle>`, and the straight line standing on the other is called a :term:`**perpendicular** <perpendicular>` to that on which it stands.

11. An :term:`**obtuse angle** <obtuse-angle>` is an angle greater than a right angle.

12. An :term:`**acute angle** <acute-angle>` is an angle less than a right angle.

13. A :term:`**boundary** <boundary>` is that which is an extremity of anything.

14. A :term:`**figure** <figure>` is that which is contained by any boundary or boundaries.

15. A :term:`**circle** <circle>` is a plane figure contained by one line such that all the straight lines falling upon it from one point among those lying within the figure are equal to one another ;
