# BOOK I. PROPOSITIONS.

### PROPOSITION 1.

<p class="enunciation">On a given finite straight line to construct an equilateral triangle.</p>

![Proposition 1 figure](0255.g1.svg)

Let $AB$ be the given finite straight line.

Thus it is required to construct an equilateral triangle on the straight line $AB$.

With centre $A$ and distance $AB$ let the circle $BCD$ be described; <span class="justification">[Post. 3]</span>  
<sup>10</sup> again, with centre $B$ and distance $BA$ let the circle $ACE$ be described; <span class="justification">[Post. 3]</span>  
and from the point $C$, in which the circles cut one another, to the points $A$, $B$ let the straight lines $CA$, $CB$ be joined. <span class="justification">[Post. 1]</span>

<sup>15</sup> Now, since the point $A$ is the centre of the circle $CDB$,
<p class="assertion"><span class="justification">[Def. 15]</span>$AC$ is equal to $AB$.</p>

Again, since the point $B$ is the centre of the circle $CAE$,
<p class="assertion"><span class="justification">[Def. 15]</span>$BC$ is equal to $BA$.</p>

But $CA$ was also proved equal to $AB$;  
<sup>20</sup> therefore each of the straight lines $CA$, $CB$ is equal to $AB$.

And things which are equal to the same thing are also equal to one another; <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore $CA$ is also equal to $CB$.</p>

<sup>25</sup> Therefore the three straight lines $CA$, $AB$, $BC$ are equal to one another.
