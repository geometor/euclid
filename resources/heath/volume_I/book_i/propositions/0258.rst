PROPOSITION 2.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To place at a given point (as an extremity) a straight line equal to a given straight line.

.. picture:: 0258.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 2 figure

Let :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` be the given point, and :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />` the given straight line.

Thus it is required to place at the point :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` (as an extremity) a straight line equal to the given straight line :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />`.

| From the point :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` to the point :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>` let the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0258.g1:/ A B />` be joined; :just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`
| and on it let the equilateral triangle :g:`$DAB$ <0258.g1:< D A B >>` be constructed. :just:`[I. 1] <I.1>`

| Let the straight lines :g:`$AE$ <0258.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$BF$ <0258.g1:/ B F />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`$DA$ <0258.g1:/ D A />`, :g:`$DB$ <0258.g1:/ D B />`; :just:`[Post. 2] <I.post.2>`
| with centre :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>` and distance :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />` let the circle :g:`$CGH$ <0258.g1:( B C )>` be described; :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`
| and again, with centre :g:`$D$ <0258.g1:D>` and distance :g:`$DG$ <0258.g1:/ D G />` let the circle :g:`$GKL$ <0258.g1:( D G )>` be described. :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`

Then, since the point :g:`$B$ <0258.g1:B>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$CGH$ <0258.g1:( B C )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$BG$ <0258.g1:/ B G />`.

:line:`20` Again, since the point :g:`$D$ <0258.g1:D>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$GKL$ <0258.g1:( D G )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DL$ <0258.g1:/ D L />` is equal to :g:`$DG$ <0258.g1:/ D G />`.

| And in these :g:`$DA$ <0258.g1:/ D A />` is equal to :g:`$DB$ <0258.g1:/ D B />`;
| therefore the remainder :g:`$AL$ <0258.g1:/ A L />` is equal to the remainder :g:`$BG$ <0258.g1:/ B G />`. :just:`[C.N. 3] <I.cn.3>`

| :line:`25` But :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />` was also proved equal to :g:`$BG$ <0258.g1:/ B G />`;
| therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AL$ <0258.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$BG$ <0258.g1:/ B G />`.

And things which are equal to the same thing are also equal to one another; :just:`[C.N. 1] <I.cn.1>`

.. assertion::

   :line:`30` therefore :g:`$AL$ <0258.g1:/ A L />` is also equal to :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />`.

Therefore at the given point :g:`$A$ <0258.g1:A>` the straight line :g:`$AL$ <0258.g1:/ A L />` is placed equal to the given straight line :g:`$BC$ <0258.g1:/ B C />`.

.. qed::

   (Being) what it was required to do.

**1. (as an extremity).** I have inserted these words because “to place a straight line at a given point” (πρὸς τῷ δοθέντι σημείῳ) is not quite clear enough, at least in English.
