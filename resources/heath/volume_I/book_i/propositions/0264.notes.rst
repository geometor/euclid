Summary
-------

- **Content**: Completion of commentary on Book I, Proposition 4. Continues congruence-axiom requirements (items 2 and 3), followed by comparisons with David Hilbert's axioms and Moritz Pasch's "Grundsatz" (*Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie*, p. 109). Concludes with an in-depth analysis of two subtle points in Euclid's proof:

  1. The coincident bases relying on the impossibility of two straight lines enclosing a space.
  2. The unstated converse of *Common Notion 4* for straight lines, Proclus' restriction to things "of the same form" (ὁμοειδῆ), and Sir Henry Savile's / Dionysius Lardner's arguments requiring both Postulates 1 and 2 (lines cannot enclose a space and cannot have a common segment).

- **Folio Continuity**: Follows directly from Folio 0263 (page 249) item (1), opening with item (2). Concludes Proposition 4 commentary in full; Proposition 5 begins on Folio 0265 (page 251).
