Summary
-------

- **Content**:

  - Conclusion of commentary Note ``1—6``: Thomas Taylor's impassioned retort defending Proclus against Robert Simson's dismissal.
  - Commentary Note ``**20.**``: Suggesting the natural insertion of the tacit intermediate step ``“but the angle $ACD$ is greater than the angle $BCD$. [C. N. 5.]”``.
  - Commentary Note ``**21. much greater,**``: Philological discussion of πολλῷ μείζων (“greater by much” vs. Simson’s “much more then is... greater”), noting that πολλῷ μᾶλλον is used by Apollonius but not by Euclid or Archimedes.
  - Axiomatic analysis of betweenness and ordering: Highlighting the tacit need for postulates of relative position (foreshadowing modern axioms of order like Pasch's Axiom) to justify that $CD$ falls within angle $ACB$, etc.
  - Augustus De Morgan’s proposed common notion on inequality transitivity ($A > B$ implies $A$ is greater than any magnitude $\le B$).
  - Proclus’ second case of Proposition 7:

    - Addressing the objection that Euclid's proof only considered the case where $D$ is outside triangle $ABC$.
    - Refuting the objection for an interior point $D$ using the second part of I. 5 (angles under the base of an isosceles triangle).
    - Embedded figure ``0274.g1.svg``.
    - Formal demonstration of the second case.
    - Concluding remark on the degenerate case where $D$ falls on $AC$ or $BC$.

Editorial & Typographical Findings
----------------------------------

- **Discrepancies with Perseus TEI XML** (``elem.1.7``):

    - In Note 21, Perseus TEI XML reads: ``πολλῷ (or πολὺ[ρπαρ ] μᾶλλόν ἐστι...μείζων.``
    - Heath's printed text clearly and cleanly reads: ``πολλῷ (or πολὺ) μᾶλλόν ἐστι...μείζων.``. The Perseus XML artifact ``[ρπαρ ]`` was an erroneous OCR token for the closing parenthesis ``)``.

  - **Greek Accents**:

    - Perseus XML prints ``μεἰζων`` with an acute accent over iota instead of the diphthong acute ``μείζων``. Heath's printed edition is correctly accented.

  - **Quotation Marks**:

    - Perseus XML omits opening and closing quotation marks on the translated clauses: ``“greater by much”`` and ``“much more then is the angle BDC greater than the angle BCD,”``.

  - ``**21. much greater,**``: Heath bolds the numeral and the lemma catchwords from Euclid's proof text.
