# Transcription Notes: Folio 0276 (Page 262)

## Summary
- **Content**:
  - Completion of the proof of **Book I, Proposition 8** (lines 18–38: indirect argument via I. 7, congruence of angle $BAC$ and angle $EDF$, "If therefore etc.", and "Q. E. D.").
  - Textual and philological notes on Proposition 8:
    - **Note 19**: Textual variant where the Greek manuscript tradition reads $BA$, $CA$ rather than $BA$, $AC$.
    - **Note 21**: Etymology of "fall beside them" (Greek future παραλλάξουσι from παραλλάττω, to pass by without touching, miss, or deviate).
  - General commentary on Proposition 8:
    - Characterization of I. 8 as a *partial* converse of I. 4 (referencing discussion on p. 257).
    - Explanation (following Proclus and Sir Henry Savile) of why Euclid proves only the equality of vertical angles in I. 8 rather than the equality of the triangles and remaining angles (which already follows from I. 4).
    - Aristotle's allusion to Proposition 8 in *Meteorologica* III. 3, 373 a 5—16 regarding rainbow reflection and congruence of triangles on base $AEB$.
    - Citation of J. L. Heiberg (*Mathematisches zu Aristoteles*, p. 18) analyzing Aristotle's pre-Euclidean phrasing.
- **Folio Continuity**:
  - Preceding: Connects directly from Folio 0275 (page 261), which ends mid-proof: `because BC is equal to EF.`. Folio 0276 opens with: `Then, BC coinciding with EF, / BA, AC will also coincide with ED, DF;...`.
  - Succeeding: Ends mid-sentence at the bottom of page 262: `...recut for the rigid structure of his`, continuing onto Folio 0277 (page 263): `Elements.” I do not, however, think that this inference...`.
