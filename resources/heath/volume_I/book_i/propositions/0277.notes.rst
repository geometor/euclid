Content Analysis
----------------

1. **Continuation of Aristotle Commentary**:

   - Concludes Heath's discussion of Heiberg's conjecture (*Mathematisches zu Aristoteles*, p. 18) regarding the pre-Euclidean formulation of Proposition 8.
   - Text picks up from Folio 0276 (page 262): ``...recut for the rigid structure of his`` -> ``*Elements*.”``.
   - Heath disputes Heiberg's deduction from Aristotle's *Meteorologica* III. 3, 373 a 5—16, noting that modern readers would likewise infer triangle congruence from I. 8 alone and pointing out Aristotle's loose geometric terminology regarding perpendiculars $CE, FE, DE$ dropped onto line $AEB$.
   - Illustrated by Graphic ``0277.g1.svg``.

2. **Philo's Proof of Proposition 8**:

   - Preserved in Proclus (*In primum Euclidis Elementorum librum commentarii*, ed. Friedlein, pp. 266, 15—268, 14).
   - Proves Proposition 8 directly without appealing to Proposition 7 by superposing triangle $ABC$ onto triangle $DEF$ on the opposite side of base $EF$, forming a quadrilateral $DEGF$.
   - Case I: $DFG$ forms a single straight line, establishing $\triangle DEG$ is isosceles and $\angle D = \angle G$ by I. 5. Illustrated by Graphic ``0277.g2.svg``.
   - Case II: $DF, FG$ form an angle interior (κατὰ τὸ ἐντός) to the figure; auxiliary segment $DG$ is joined, yielding equality of the full angles by addition of base angles ($\angle EDG + \angle FDG = \angle EGD + \angle FGD$). Illustrated by Graphic ``0277.g3.svg``.
   - Case III continues onto Folio 0278 (page 264).
