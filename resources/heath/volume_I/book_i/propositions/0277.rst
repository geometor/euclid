This of course follows from their being “in equal triangles” (by means of Eucl. Philo's proof of I. 8.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

This alternative proof avoids the use of I. 7, and it is elegant; but it is inconvenient in one respect, since three cases have to be distinguished. :person:`Proclus <proclus>` gives the proof in the following order (pp. 266, 15—268, 14).

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0277.g2:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0277.g2:< D E F >>` be two triangles having the sides :g:`$AB$ <0277.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0277.g2:/ A C />` equal to the sides :g:`$DE$ <0277.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$DF$ <0277.g2:/ D F />` respectively, and the base :g:`$BC$ <0277.g2:/ B C />` equal to the base :g:`$EF$ <0277.g2:/ E F />`.

Let the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0277.g2:< A B C >>` be applied to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0277.g2:< D E F >>`, so that :g:`$B$ <0277.g2:B>` is placed on :g:`$E$ <0277.g2:E>` and :g:`$BC$ <0277.g2:/ B C />` on :g:`$EF$ <0277.g2:/ E F />`, but so that :g:`$A$ <0277.g2:A>` falls on the opposite side of :g:`$EF$ <0277.g2:/ E F />` from :g:`$D$ <0277.g2:D>`, taking the position :g:`$G$ <0277.g2:G>`. Then :g:`$C$ <0277.g2:C>` will coincide with :g:`$F$ <0277.g2:F>`, since :g:`$BC$ <0277.g2:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$EF$ <0277.g2:/ E F />`.

Now :g:`$FG$ <0277.g2:/ F G />` will either be in a straight line with :g:`$DF$ <0277.g2:/ D F />`, or make an angle with it, and in the latter case the angle will either be *interior* (κατὰ τὸ ἐντός) to the figure or *exterior* (κατὰ τὸ ἐκτός).

.. picture:: 0277.g2.svg
   :alt: Philo Proof of I. 8 Case I

I. Let :g:`$FG$ <0277.g2:/ F G />` be in a straight line with :g:`$DF$ <0277.g2:/ D F />`. Then, since :g:`$DE$ <0277.g2:/ D E />` is equal to :g:`$EG$ <0277.g2:/ E G />`, and :g:`$DFG$ <0277.g2:/ D F G />` is a straight line, :g:`$DEG$ <0277.g2:< D E G >>` is an isosceles triangle, and the angle at :g:`$D$ <0277.g2:< E D G )>` is equal to the angle at :g:`$G$ <0277.g2:< E G D )>`. [I. 5].

.. picture:: 0277.g3.svg
   :alt: Philo Proof of I. 8 Case II

II. Let :g:`$DF$ <0277.g3:/ D F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0277.g3:/ F G />` form an angle *interior* to the figure. Let :g:`$DG$ <0277.g3:/ D G />` be joined. Then, since :g:`$DE$ <0277.g3:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0277.g3:/ E G />` are equal, the angle :g:`$EDG$ <0277.g3:< E D G )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$EGD$ <0277.g3:< E G D )>`. Again, since :g:`$DF$ <0277.g3:/ D F />` is equal to :g:`$FG$ <0277.g3:/ F G />`, the angle :g:`$FDG$ <0277.g3:< F D G )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$FGD$ <0277.g3:< F G D )>`. Therefore, by addition, the whole angle :g:`$EDF$ <0277.g3:< E D F )>` is equal to the whole angle :g:`$EGF$ <0277.g3:< E G F )>`.
