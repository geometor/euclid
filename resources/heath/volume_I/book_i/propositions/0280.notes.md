# Transcription Notes: Folio 0280 (Page 266)

## Summary
- **Content**:
  - Subsection `#### (a) Trisection by means of the conchoid.`
    - Reference back to Heath's earlier commentary on the conchoid of Nicomedes (note on Definition 2, pp. 160—1).
    - Pappus’ construction (*Collection* iv. pp. 274—5) for trisecting an acute angle $ABC$ by drawing $AC \perp BC$, completing parallelogram $FBCA$, and drawing line $BE$ through $B$ such that the intercept $DE$ between $AC$ and $FA$ produced equals $2 AB$.
    - Step-by-step proof that $\angle EBC = \frac{1}{3} \angle ABC$, using the midpoint $G$ of $DE$ where $AG = DG = EG = AB$.
    - Realization of the construction via the conchoid of Nicomedes having pole $B$, directrix $AC$, and distance $2 AB$.
    - Figure `0280.g1.png` illustrating the conchoid trisection construction.
  - Subsection `#### (b) Use of the quadratrix.`
    - Philological note on the plural *quadratrices* as a Hellenism for the singular *quadratrix* (Greek τετραγωνίζουσα).
    - Historical provenance: invented by Hippias of Elis (c. 420 B.C.) according to Proclus (p. 356, 11); used by Nicomedes for trisection, and applied to the quadrature of the circle by Dinostratus and Nicomedes as recounted by Pappus (iv. pp. 250, 33—252, 4).
    - Kinematic generation of the curve: in square $ABCD$ with quadrant $BED$ of center $A$, radius $AB$ rotates uniformly to $AD$ while side $BC$ moves uniformly parallel to itself from $BC$ to $AD$ in the same time; the intersection $F$ traces the quadratrix.
    - Figure `0280.g2.png` displaying the quadratrix curve in square $ABCD$.
- **Folio Continuity**:
  - Continues directly from the introduction to trisection on Folio 0279 (page 265).
  - Concludes at the definition of the quadratrix locus; the mathematical property $(\rho \sin \phi)/a = \phi/(\frac{1}{2}\pi)$, division of angles in any ratio, and subsection `(c) Use of the spiral of Archimedes` follow on Folio 0281 (page 267).
