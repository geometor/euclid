## Content Analysis
1. **Commentary on Proposition 9 (continued)**:
   - **Quadratrix property & equation**: If $F$ is any point on the curve, $\text{arc } BED : \text{arc } ED = AB : FH$. In polar coordinates, with $\phi = \angle FAD$, $\rho = AF$, and side of square $a$:
     $$(\rho \sin \phi)/a = \phi/\frac{1}{2}\pi.$$
   - **Division in any given ratio**: Dividing $FH$ at $K$ in the given ratio, drawing $KL \parallel AD$ meeting the curve in $L$, and joining $AL$ produces ray $AN$ dividing quadrant arc in the same ratio (Pappus iv. p. 286).
   - **(c) Use of the spiral of Archimedes**: Bisection/division in any given ratio of $\angle AOB$ between radii vectores $OA, OB$ of Archimedes' spiral. Mark $OC = OA$ along $OB$, cut $CB$ at $D$ in the given ratio, draw circle centre $O$ radius $OD$ cutting spiral in $E$; ray $OE$ divides $\angle AOB$ in the required ratio.

2. **Proposition 10**:
   - **Enunciation**: *To bisect a given finite straight line.*
   - **Setting-out & Specification**: Given finite straight line $AB$. Required to bisect $AB$.
   - **Construction**: Construct equilateral triangle $ABC$ on $AB$ [I. 1]; bisect $\angle ACB$ by $CD$ [I. 9].
   - **Proof**: In triangles $ACD, BCD$, since $AC = CB$, $CD$ is common, and $\angle ACD = \angle BCD$, base $AD = BD$ by [I. 4].
   - **Conclusion**: Q. E. F. (*quod erat faciendum*), as this is a problem/construction.
   - Illustrated by Graphic `0281.g1.png`.
