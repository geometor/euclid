## Content Analysis
1. **Completion of De Morgan Remark on I. 11**:

2. **Apollonius' Construction for Proposition 11**:
   - **Source**: Proclus, p. 282, 8.
   - **Method**: Instead of invoking Proposition 1 to construct an equilateral triangle on $DE$, Apollonius takes $D, E$ equidistant from $C$ and explicitly describes intersecting circles from $D$ and $E$ meeting at $F$.
   - **Heath's Critique**: While this requires reproving $DF = FE$, Euclid’s modular citation of I. 1 dispenses with repeated circle constructions. Apollonius’ method reflects *practical geometry* rather than theoretical exposition.
   - Illustrated by Graphic `0284.g1.svg`.

3. **Heron's Construction at an Extremity (via an-Nairīzī)**:
   - **Problem**: Erect a perpendicular at the extremity of a given straight line $AB$ at point $A$ without being permitted to *produce* the line beyond $A$.
   - **Attribution**: Mentioned by Proclus and explicitly attributed to Heron in an-Nairīzī's commentary (*Codex Leidensis* 399, 1, ed. Besthorn-Heiberg, pp. 73—4; ed. Curtze, pp. 54—5).
   - **Construction**: Take any point $C$ on $AB$; erect $CE \perp AB$; cut off $CD = AC$; bisect $\angle ACE$ with $CF$ [I. 9]; draw $DF \perp CE$ meeting $CF$ at $F$; join $FA$.
   - **Proof**: Side-Angle-Side congruence of $\triangle ACF$ and $\triangle DCF$ [I. 4] ($AC = CD$, $CF$ common, $\angle ACF = \angle DCF$), yielding $\angle FAC = \angle FDC = \text{right angle}$.
   - Illustrated by Graphic `0284.g2.svg`.

4. **Proposition 12**:
   - **Heading**: `### PROPOSITION 12.`
   - **Enunciation**: *To a given infinite straight line, from a given point which is not on it, to draw a perpendicular straight line.*
   - **Setting-out**: `Let $AB$ be the given infinite straight line, and $C$ the given point which is not on it;`
   - Page ends at semicolon `;`. Specification, construction, and proof continue on Folio 0285 (page 271).
