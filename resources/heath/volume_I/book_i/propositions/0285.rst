:line:`5` thus it is required to draw to the given infinite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>`, from the given point :g:`$C$ <0285.g1:C>` which is not on it, a perpendicular straight line.

| For let a point :g:`$D$ <0285.g1:D>` be taken
| :line:`10` at random on the other side of the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>`, and with centre :g:`$C$ <0285.g1:C>` and distance :g:`$CD$ <0285.g1:/ C D />` let the circle :g:`$EFG$ <0285.g1:( C D )>` be described; :just:`[Post. 3] <I.post.3>`
| let the straight line :g:`$EG$ <0285.g1:/ E G />`
| :line:`15` be bisected at :g:`$H$ <0285.g1:H>`, :just:`[I. 10] <I.10>`
| and let the straight lines :g:`$CG$ <0285.g1:/ C G />`, :g:`$CH$ <0285.g1:/ C H />`, :g:`$CE$ <0285.g1:/ C E />` be joined. :just:`[Post. 1] <I.post.1>`

I say that :g:`$CH$ <0285.g1:/ C H />` has been drawn perpendicular to the given infinite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>` from the given point :g:`$C$ <0285.g1:C>` which is not on it.

| :line:`20` For, since :g:`$GH$ <0285.g1:/ G H />` is equal to :g:`$HE$ <0285.g1:/ H E />`,
| and :g:`$HC$ <0285.g1:/ H C />` is common,

.. assertion::

   the two sides :g:`$GH$ <0285.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0285.g1:/ H C />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$EH$ <0285.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0285.g1:/ H C />` respectively;

and the base :g:`$CG$ <0285.g1:/ C G />` is equal to the base :g:`$CE$ <0285.g1:/ C E />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   :line:`25` therefore the angle :g:`$CHG$ <0285.g1:< C H G )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$EHC$ <0285.g1:< E H C )>`.

And they are adjacent angles.

| But, when a straight line set up on a straight line makes the adjacent angles equal to one another, each of the equal angles is right, and the straight line standing on the other is
| :line:`30` called a perpendicular to that on which it stands. :just:`[Def. 10] <I.def.10>`

Therefore :g:`$CH$ <0285.g1:/ C H />` has been drawn perpendicular to the given infinite straight line :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>` from the given point :g:`$C$ <0285.g1:C>` which is not on it.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.

**2. a perpendicular straight line**, κάθετον εὐθεῖαν γραμμήν. This is the full expression for a perpendicular, κάθετος meaning *let fall* or *let down*, so that the expression corresponds to our *plumb-line*. ἡ κάθετος is however constantly used alone for a perpendicular, γραμμή being understood.

**10. on the other side of the straight line** :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>`, literally “towards the other parts of the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>`,” ἐπὶ τὰ ἕτερα μέρη τῆς :g:`$AB$ <0285.g1:[ A B ]>`.
