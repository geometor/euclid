### PROPOSITION 13.

<p class="enunciation">If a straight line set up on a straight line make angles, it will make either two right angles or angles equal to two right angles.</p>

![Proposition 13 figure](0289.g1.svg)

For let any straight line <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> set up on the straight line  
<sup>5</sup> <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> make the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B A )">$CBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span>;  
I say that the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B A )">$CBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span> are either two right angles or equal to two right angles.

Now, if the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B A )">$CBA$</span> is equal to  
<sup>10</sup> the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">they are two right angles. <span class="justification">[Def. 10]</span></p>

But, if not, let <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0289.g1:B">$B$</span> at right  
angles to <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 11]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B E )">$CBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span> are two right angles.</p>

<sup>15</sup> Then, since the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B E )">$CBE$</span> is equal to the two angles  
<span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B A )">$CBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span>,  
<p class="sub-clause">let the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span> be added to each;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B E )">$CBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span> are equal to the three angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B A )">$CBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span></p>

<sup>20</sup> Again, since the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< D B A )">$DBA$</span> is equal to the two angles  
<span data-ref="0289.g1:< D B E )">$DBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B A )">$EBA$</span>,  
<p class="sub-clause">let the angle <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> be added to each;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< D B A )">$DBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> are equal to the three angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< D B E )">$DBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B A )">$EBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span></p>

<sup>25</sup> But the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B E )">$CBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span> were also proved equal to  
the same three angles;  
and things which are equal to the same thing are also  
equal to one another; <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< C B E )">$CBE$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< E B D )">$EBD$</span> are also equal to the <sup>30</sup> angles <span data-ref="0289.g1:< D B A )">$DBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>.</p>
