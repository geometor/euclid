PROPOSITION 13.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line set up on a straight line make angles, it will make either two right angles or angles equal to two right angles.

.. picture:: 0289.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13 figure

| For let any straight line :g:`$AB$ <0289.g1:/ A B />` set up on the straight line
| :line:`5` :g:`$CD$ <0289.g1:/ C D />` make the angles :g:`$CBA$ <0289.g1:< C B A )>`, :g:`$ABD$ <0289.g1:< A B D )>`;
| I say that the angles :g:`$CBA$ <0289.g1:< C B A )>`, :g:`$ABD$ <0289.g1:< A B D )>` are either two right angles or equal to two right angles.

| Now, if the angle :g:`$CBA$ <0289.g1:< C B A )>` is equal to
| :line:`10` the angle :g:`$ABD$ <0289.g1:< A B D )>`,

.. assertion:: [Def. 10] <I.def.10>

   they are two right angles.

| But, if not, let :g:`$BE$ <0289.g1:/ B E />` be drawn from the point :g:`$B$ <0289.g1:B>` at right
| angles to :g:`$CD$ <0289.g1:/ C D />`; :just:`[I. 11] <I.11>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$CBE$ <0289.g1:< C B E )>`, :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>` are two right angles.

| :line:`15` Then, since the angle :g:`$CBE$ <0289.g1:< C B E )>` is equal to the two angles
| :g:`$CBA$ <0289.g1:< C B A )>`, :g:`$ABE$ <0289.g1:< A B E )>`,

.. subclause::

   let the angle :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>` be added to each;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   therefore the angles :g:`$CBE$ <0289.g1:< C B E )>`, :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>` are equal to the three angles :g:`$CBA$ <0289.g1:< C B A )>`, :g:`$ABE$ <0289.g1:< A B E )>`, :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>`.

| :line:`20` Again, since the angle :g:`$DBA$ <0289.g1:< D B A )>` is equal to the two angles
| :g:`$DBE$ <0289.g1:< D B E )>`, :g:`$EBA$ <0289.g1:< E B A )>`,

.. subclause::

   let the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0289.g1:< A B C )>` be added to each;

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   therefore the angles :g:`$DBA$ <0289.g1:< D B A )>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0289.g1:< A B C )>` are equal to the three angles :g:`$DBE$ <0289.g1:< D B E )>`, :g:`$EBA$ <0289.g1:< E B A )>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0289.g1:< A B C )>`.

| :line:`25` But the angles :g:`$CBE$ <0289.g1:< C B E )>`, :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>` were also proved equal to
| the same three angles;
| and things which are equal to the same thing are also
| equal to one another; :just:`[C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$CBE$ <0289.g1:< C B E )>`, :g:`$EBD$ <0289.g1:< E B D )>` are also equal to the :line:`30` angles :g:`$DBA$ <0289.g1:< D B A )>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0289.g1:< A B C )>`.
