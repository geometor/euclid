- **Folio**: `0297` (Page 283), Volume I, Book I, Section: Propositions.

    - Citation of Proclus (pp. 315, 11—316, 13) recording Porphyry's proof of I. 18 where $CD$ (rather than $AD$) is cut off equal to $AB$, $BE$ is taken equal to $AD$ on $AB$ produced, establishing $AE = AC$. Proof continues on folio 0298.

## 4. Greek Typography & Perseus TEI XML Comparison
- **Greek Text & Diacritics**:
  - `ὑποτείνειν`: Rough breathing on initial upsilon (`ὑ`), acute on diphthong `ει` (`εί`).
  - `ὑπό` / `ὑπὸ`: Rough breathing on initial upsilon (`ὑ`), acute/grave on omicron (`ό` / `ὸ`).
  - `παντὸς τριγώνου ἡ μείζων πλευρὰ τὴν μείζονα γωνίαν ὑποτείνει` (I. 18 enunciation).
  - `παντὸς τριγώνου ὑπὸ τὴν μείζονα γωνίαν ἡ μείζων πλευρὰ ὑποτείνει` (I. 19 enunciation).
  - `ὑπὸ γὰρ τὴν μείζω γωνίαν ὑποτείνει` (Aristotle *Meteorologica* III. 5, 376 a 12).
- **Comparison with Perseus TEI XML (`elem.1.18`)**:
  - The enunciation and demonstration text match the XML ground truth exactly (noting that Perseus XML has the minor typographical artifact `bejoined` for `be joined`).
  - In the XML commentary note, Perseus has `In the enunciation of this number we have...`, whereas Heath's printed text clearly has `In the enunciation of this proposition we have...`.
