**Proof of I. 19.**

| Let :g:`$ABC$ <0300.g1:< A B C >>` be a triangle in which the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0300.g1:< A B C )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0300.g1:< A C B )>`.
| Bisect :g:`$BC$ <0300.g1:/ B C />` at :g:`$D$ <0300.g1:D>`, join :g:`$AD$ <0300.g1:/ A D />`, and produce it to :g:`$E$ <0300.g1:E>` so that :g:`$DE$ <0300.g1:/ D E />` is equal to :g:`$AD$ <0300.g1:/ A D />`. Join :g:`$BE$ <0300.g1:/ B E />`.

.. picture:: 0300.g1.svg
   :alt: Heron/an-Nairizi Proof of I. 19 Figure

| Then the two sides :g:`$BD$ <0300.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DE$ <0300.g1:/ D E />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$CD$ <0300.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0300.g1:/ D A />`, and the vertical angles at :g:`$D$ <0300.g1:D>` are equal;
| therefore :g:`$BE$ <0300.g1:/ B E />` is equal to :g:`$AC$ <0300.g1:/ A C />`,
| and the angle :g:`$DBE$ <0300.g1:< D B E )>` to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0300.g1:< A C B )>`.
| But the angle at :g:`$C$ <0300.g1:< A C B )>` is less than the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0300.g1:< A B C )>`;
| therefore the angle :g:`$DBE$ <0300.g1:< D B E )>` is less than the angle :g:`$ABD$ <0300.g1:< A B D )>`.
| Hence, if :g:`$BF$ <0300.g1:/ B F />` bisect the angle :g:`$ABE$ <0300.g1:< A B E )>`, :g:`$BF$ <0300.g1:/ B F />` meets :g:`$AE$ <0300.g1:/ A E />` between :g:`$A$ <0300.g1:A>` and :g:`$D$ <0300.g1:D>`. Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0300.g1:/ E F />` is greater than :g:`$FA$ <0300.g1:/ F A />`.
| It follows, by the lemma, that :g:`$BE$ <0300.g1:/ B E />` is greater than :g:`$BA$ <0300.g1:/ B A />`,
| that is, :g:`$AC$ <0300.g1:/ A C />` is greater than :g:`$AB$ <0300.g1:/ A B />`.

PROPOSITION 20.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   In any triangle two sides taken together in any manner are greater than the remaining one.

| For let :g:`$ABC$ <0300.g2:< A B C >>` be a triangle;
| I say that in the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0300.g2:< A B C >>` two sides taken together in any manner are greater than the remaining one, namely

.. subclause::

   :g:`$BA$ <0300.g2:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0300.g2:/ A C />` greater than :g:`$BC$ <0300.g2:/ B C />`,

.. subclause::

   :g:`$AB$ <0300.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0300.g2:/ B C />` greater than :g:`$AC$ <0300.g2:/ A C />`,

.. subclause::

   :g:`$BC$ <0300.g2:/ B C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0300.g2:/ C A />` greater than :g:`$AB$ <0300.g2:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0300.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 20 figure

For let :g:`$BA$ <0300.g2:[ B A ]>` be drawn through to the point :g:`$D$ <0300.g2:D>`, let :g:`$DA$ <0300.g2:/ D A />` be made equal to :g:`$CA$ <0300.g2:/ C A />`, and let :g:`$DC$ <0300.g2:/ D C />` be joined.

| Then, since :g:`$DA$ <0300.g2:/ D A />` is equal to :g:`$AC$ <0300.g2:/ A C />`,
| the angle :g:`$ADC$ <0300.g2:< A D C )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$ACD$ <0300.g2:< A C D )>`; :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

.. assertion:: [C. N. 5] <I.cn.5>

   therefore the angle :g:`$BCD$ <0300.g2:< B C D )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$ADC$ <0300.g2:< A D C )>`.

| And, since :g:`$DCB$ <0300.g2:< D C B >>` is a triangle having the angle :g:`$BCD$ <0300.g2:< B C D )>` greater than the angle :g:`$BDC$ <0300.g2:< B D C )>`,
| and the greater angle is subtended by the greater side, :just:`[I. 19] <I.19>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DB$ <0300.g2:/ D B />` is greater than :g:`$BC$ <0300.g2:/ B C />`.
