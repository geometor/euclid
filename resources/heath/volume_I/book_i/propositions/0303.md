### PROPOSITION 21.

<p class="enunciation">If on one of the sides of a triangle, from its extremities, there be constructed two straight lines meeting within the triangle, the straight lines so constructed will be less than the remaining two sides of the triangle, but will contain a greater <sup>5</sup> angle.</p>

On <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, one of the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, from its extremities <span data-ref="0303.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:C">$C$</span>, let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be constructed meeting within the triangle;  
I say that <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> are less than the remaining two sides <sup>10</sup> of the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, but contain an angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B D C )">$BDC$</span> greater than the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span>.

![Folio 0303 Figure 1 - Proposition 21](0303.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0303.g1:[ B D ]">$BD$</span> be drawn through to <span data-ref="0303.g1:E">$E$</span>.  
Then, since in any triangle two sides are greater than the remaining <sup>15</sup> one, <span class="justification">[I. 20]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore, in the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span>, the two sides <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> be added to each;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>.</p>

<sup>20</sup> Again, since, in the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< C E D >">$CED$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">the two sides <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">let <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be added to each;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>.</p>

But <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> were proved greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>;  
<p class="assertion"><sup>25</sup> therefore <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> are much greater than <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.</p>

Again, since in any triangle the exterior angle is greater than the interior and opposite angle, <span class="justification">[I. 16]</span>  
therefore, in the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< C D E >">$CDE$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">the exterior angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B D C )">$BDC$</span> is greater than the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< C E D )">$CED$</span>.</p>

<sup>30</sup> For the same reason, moreover, in the triangle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span> also,  
<p class="assertion">the exterior angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< C E B )">$CEB$</span> is greater than the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span>.</p>

But the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B D C )">$BDC$</span> was proved greater than the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< C E B )">$CEB$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B D C )">$BDC$</span> is much greater than the angle <span data-ref="0303.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed"><sup>35</sup> Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

**2. be constructed...meeting within the triangle.** The word “meeting” is not in the Greek, where the words are ἐντὸς συσταθῶσιν. συνίστασθαι is the word used of constructing two straight lines to a point (cf. I. 7) or so as to form a triangle; but it is necessary in English to indicate that they meet.

**3. the straight lines so constructed.** Observe the elegant brevity of the Greek αἱ συσταθεῖσαι.
