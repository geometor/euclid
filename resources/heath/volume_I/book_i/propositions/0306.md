### PROPOSITION 22.

<p class="enunciation">Out of three straight lines, which are equal to three given straight lines, to construct a triangle : thus it is necessary that two of the straight lines taken together in any manner should be greater than the remaining one. <span class="justification">[I. 20]</span></p>

Let the three given straight lines be <span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span>, and of these let two taken together in any manner be greater than the remaining one,  
namely  
<p class="sub-clause"><span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span> greater than <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause"><span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span> greater than <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span> greater than <span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>;</p>
thus it is required to construct a triangle out of straight lines equal to <span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span>.

Let there be set out a straight line <span data-ref="0306.g1:[ D E ]">$DE$</span>, terminated at <span data-ref="0306.g1:D">$D$</span> but of infinite length in the direction of <span data-ref="0306.g1:E">$E$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0306.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0306.g1:B">$B$</span>, and <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0306.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 3]</span>  
With centre <span data-ref="0306.g1:F">$F$</span> and distance <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> let the circle <span data-ref="0306.g1:( D K L )">$DKL$</span> be described;
