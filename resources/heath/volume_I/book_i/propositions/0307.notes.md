- **Book / Section**: Book I, Propositions.

- **Contents**:
  - **Proposition 22 (Conclusion)**:
    - Completion of proof: lines establishing $FD = FK = A$, $GH = GK = C$, and $FG = B$, concluding that triangle $KFG$ is constructed out of straight lines equal to $A, B, C$.
    - Concluding formula: `Q. E. F.` (quod erat faciendum / ὅπερ ἔδει ποιῆσαι).
  - **Notes on the Enunciation of Proposition 22**:
    - Note **2–4**: On the first occurrence of a διορισμός (*diorismos*) to a problem in the *Elements*.
    - Note **2**: Discussion of the Greek phrase δεῖ δή (*dei dē*) vs δεῖ δέ (*dei de*) and its proper translation as "thus it is necessary" rather than "But it is necessary".
    - Note **4**: On the textual status of the explanatory clause added in all MSS. and Boethius after the διορισμός ("because in any triangle two sides..."), omitted by Proclus and Campanus and regarded as a gloss.
  - **Commentary on Circle Intersection Assumptions**:
    - Historical criticism of Euclid's assumption that circles described in the construction necessarily intersect.
    - Discussion of Proclus (*In Primum Euclidis*, p. 331, 8 sqq.) using *reductio ad absurdum* with three cases: cutting, touching, standing apart (διεστάναι).
    - Camerer and Todhunter's supplementation of internal cases.
    - Quotation from Robert Simson's commentary defending Euclid, continuing across the page boundary onto Folio 0308.
