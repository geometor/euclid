title: "Proposition 26"

Let $ABC$, $DEF$ be two triangles having the two angles $ABC$, $BCA$ equal to the two angles $DEF$, $EFD$ respectively, namely the angle $ABC$ to the angle $DEF$, and the angle <sup>10</sup>$BCA$ to the angle $EFD$; and let them also have one side equal to one side, first that adjoining the equal angles, namely $BC$ to $EF$;

I say that they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively, namely $AB$ to $DE$ and <sup>15</sup>$AC$ to $DF$, and the remaining angle to the remaining angle, namely the angle $BAC$ to the angle $EDF$.

![Folio 0316 Figure 1 - Proposition 26](0316.g1.svg)

For, if $AB$ is unequal to $DE$, one of them is greater.  
Let $AB$ be greater, and let $BG$ be made equal to $DE$;  
and let $GC$ be joined.  
<sup>20</sup>Then, since $BG$ is equal to $DE$, and $BC$ to $EF$,  
the two sides $GB$, $BC$ are equal to the two sides $DE$, $EF$ respectively;  
and the angle $GBC$ is equal to the angle $DEF$;  
<p class="sub-clause">therefore the base $GC$ is equal to the base $DF$,</p>
<p class="sub-clause"><sup>25</sup>and the triangle $GBC$ is equal to the triangle $DEF$,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend; <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle $GCB$ is equal to the angle $DFE$.</p>

But the angle $DFE$ is by hypothesis equal to the angle $BCA$;  
<p class="assertion"><sup>30</sup>therefore the angle $BCG$ is equal to the angle $BCA$,</p>
<p class="assertion">the less to the greater: which is impossible.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore $AB$ is not unequal to $DE$,</p>
<p class="assertion">and is therefore equal to it.</p>

But $BC$ is also equal to $EF$;  
<sup>35</sup>therefore the two sides $AB$, $BC$ are equal to the two sides $DE$, $EF$ respectively,  
and the angle $ABC$ is equal to the angle $DEF$;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the base $AC$ is equal to the base $DF$,</p>
<p class="assertion">and the remaining angle $BAC$ is equal to the remaining <sup>40</sup>angle $EDF$. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
