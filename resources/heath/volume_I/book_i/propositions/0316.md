Let <span data-ref="0316.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> be two triangles having the two angles <span data-ref="0316.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B C A )">$BCA$</span> equal to the two angles <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:< E F D )">$EFD$</span> respectively, namely the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>, and the angle <sup>10</sup><span data-ref="0316.g1:< B C A )">$BCA$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< E F D )">$EFD$</span>; and let them also have one side equal to one side, first that adjoining the equal angles, namely <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;

I say that they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively, namely <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> and <sup>15</sup><span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, and the remaining angle to the remaining angle, namely the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>.

![Folio 0316 Figure 1 - Proposition 26](0316.g1.svg)

For, if <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is unequal to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, one of them is greater.  
Let <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be greater, and let <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;  
and let <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> be joined.  
<sup>20</sup>Then, since <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,  
the two sides <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> respectively;  
and the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< G B C )">$GBC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>;  
<p class="sub-clause">therefore the base <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause"><sup>25</sup>and the triangle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< G B C >">$GBC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend; <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< G C B )">$GCB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D F E )">$DFE$</span>.</p>

But the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D F E )">$DFE$</span> is by hypothesis equal to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B C A )">$BCA$</span>;  
<p class="assertion"><sup>30</sup>therefore the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B C G )">$BCG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B C A )">$BCA$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the less to the greater: which is impossible.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is not unequal to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">and is therefore equal to it.</p>

But <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;  
<sup>35</sup>therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> respectively,  
and the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">and the remaining angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span> is equal to the remaining <sup>40</sup>angle <span data-ref="0316.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
