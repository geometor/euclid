:title: Proposition 26

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0316.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F >>` be two triangles having the two angles :g:`$ABC$ <0316.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCA$ <0316.g1:< B C A )>` :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< A B C ) = 0316.g1:< D E F ); 0316.g1:< B C A ) = 0316.g1:< E F D )>` to the two angles :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$EFD$ <0316.g1:< E F D )>` respectively, namely the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0316.g1:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F )>`, and the angle :g:`$BCA$ <0316.g1:< B C A )>` to the angle :g:`$EFD$ <0316.g1:< E F D )>`; and let them also have one side :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ B C / = 0316.g1:/ E F />` to one side, first that adjoining the :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< A B C ) = 0316.g1:< D E F ); 0316.g1:< B C A ) = 0316.g1:< E F D )>` angles, namely :g:`$BC$ <0316.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`$EF$ <0316.g1:/ E F />`;

I say that they will also have the remaining sides :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ A B / = 0316.g1:/ D E /; 0316.g1:/ A C / = 0316.g1:/ D F /; 0316.g1:< B A C ) = 0316.g1:< E D F )>` to the remaining sides respectively, namely :g:`$AB$ <0316.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />` and :g:`$AC$ <0316.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$DF$ <0316.g1:/ D F />`, and the remaining angle to the remaining angle, namely the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0316.g1:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0316.g1:< E D F )>`.

.. picture:: 0316.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0316 Figure 1 - Proposition 26

For, if :g:`$AB$ <0316.g1:/ A B />` is unequal to :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`, one of them is greater.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0316.g1:/ A B />` be greater, and let :g:`$BG$ <0316.g1:/ B G />` be made :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ B G / = 0316.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`;

and let :g:`$GC$ <0316.g1:/ G C />` be joined. Then, since :g:`$BG$ <0316.g1:/ B G />` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ B G / = 0316.g1:/ D E /; 0316.g1:/ B C / = 0316.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`, and :g:`$BC$ <0316.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`$EF$ <0316.g1:/ E F />`, the two sides :g:`$GB$ <0316.g1:/ G B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0316.g1:/ B C />` are :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ G B / = 0316.g1:/ D E /; 0316.g1:/ B C / = 0316.g1:/ E F />` to the two sides :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0316.g1:/ E F />` respectively; and the angle :g:`$GBC$ <0316.g1:< G B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< G B C ) = 0316.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F )>`;

.. subclause::

   therefore the base :g:`$GC$ <0316.g1:/ G C />` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ G C / = 0316.g1:/ D F />` to the base :g:`$DF$ <0316.g1:/ D F />`,

.. subclause::

   and the triangle :g:`$GBC$ <0316.g1:< G B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< G B C > = 0316.g1:< D E F >>` to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F >>`,

.. subclause:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$GCB$ <0316.g1:< G C B )>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< G C B ) = 0316.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0316.g1:< D F E )>`.

But the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0316.g1:< D F E )>` is by hypothesis :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< D F E ) = 0316.g1:< B C A )>`\ [#f2]_ to the angle :g:`$BCA$ <0316.g1:< B C A )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$BCG$ <0316.g1:< B C G )>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< B C G ) = 0316.g1:< B C A )>` to the angle :g:`$BCA$ <0316.g1:< B C A )>`,

.. assertion::

   the less to the greater: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0316.g1:/ A B />` is not unequal to :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`,

.. assertion::

   and is therefore :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ A B / = 0316.g1:/ D E />` to it.

But :g:`$BC$ <0316.g1:/ B C />` is also :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ B C / = 0316.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$EF$ <0316.g1:/ E F />`; therefore the two sides :g:`$AB$ <0316.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0316.g1:/ B C />` are :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ A B / = 0316.g1:/ D E /; 0316.g1:/ B C / = 0316.g1:/ E F />` to the two sides :g:`$DE$ <0316.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0316.g1:/ E F />` respectively, and the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0316.g1:< A B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< A B C ) = 0316.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0316.g1:< D E F )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$AC$ <0316.g1:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:/ A C / = 0316.g1:/ D F />` to the base :g:`$DF$ <0316.g1:/ D F />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   and the remaining angle :g:`$BAC$ <0316.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0316.g1:< B A C ) = 0316.g1:< E D F )>` to the remaining angle :g:`$EDF$ <0316.g1:< E D F )>`.
