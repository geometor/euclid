1. Overview
-----------

- **Book / Section**: Book I, Propositions.
- **Folio**: ``0320`` (Page 306).
- **Running Header**: ``306 BOOK I [I. 26``.
- **Contents**:

  - **Recapitulation of Congruence Theorems (Conclusion)**:

    - Continuation from page 305 of Proclus's classification of hypotheses of class (3) (sides and angles combined):

      - 1 side and 1 angle in each triangle: not enough.
      - ($a$) 1 side and all 3 angles: more than enough.
      - ($b$) 2 sides and 2 or 3 angles: more than enough.
      - ($c$) 3 sides and 1 or 2 angles: more than enough.

    - Summary of the three essential hypotheses to be examined:

      - (a) three sides [Euclid I. 8];
      - (β) two sides and one angle [Euclid I. 4 proves the included angle case];
      - (γ) one side and two angles [Euclid I. 26 covers all cases].

    - Discussion of Proclus's omission of the *ambiguous case*:

      - Camerer's attribution to Proclus (p. 351) shown to be based on a misunderstanding of Proclus's figure, which merely illustrated that in I. 26 the equal sides must be *corresponding* sides.

  - **The Ambiguous Case (Beginning)**:

    - Section heading: ``### The ambiguous case.``
    - Enunciation: *If two triangles have two sides equal to two sides respectively, and if the angles opposite to one pair of equal sides be also equal, then will the angles opposite the other pair of equal sides be either equal or supplementary ; and, in the former case, the triangles will be equal in all respects.*
    - Setting-out and Specification: triangles $ABC, DEF$ with $AB = DE$, $AC = DF$, and $\angle ABC = \angle DEF$; to prove $\angle ACB, \angle DFE$ are either equal or supplementary.
    - Diagram: ``0320.g1.svg`` (``.. picture:: 0320.g1.svg``).
    - Case (1): $\angle BAC = \angle EDF \implies \triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$ in all respects [I. 4], and $\angle ACB = \angle DFE$.
    - Case (2): $\angle BAC \neq \angle EDF \implies$ construction of $\angle EDG = \angle BAC$ with $EF$ produced to meet $DG$ in $G$; proof continues on page 307.
