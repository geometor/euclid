If it is desired to avoid the ambiguity and secure that the triangles may be congruent, we can introduce the necessary conditions into the enunciation, on the analogy of Eucl. **VI.** 7.

*If two triangles have two sides of the one equal to two sides of the other respectively, and the angles opposite to a pair of equal sides equal, then, if the angles opposite to the other pair of equal sides are both acute, or both obtuse, or if one of them is a right angle, the two triangles are equal in all respects.*

The proof of the three cases (by *reductio ad absurdum*) was given by :person:`Todhunter <todhunter>`.

PROPOSITION 27.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line falling on two straight lines\ [#f1]_ make the alternate angles\ [#f2]_ equal to one another, the straight lines will be parallel to one another.

.. picture:: 0321.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0321 Figure 1 - Proposition 27

For let the straight line :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />` falling on the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` make the alternate angles :g:`$AEF$ <0321.g1:< A E F )>`, :g:`$EFD$ <0321.g1:< E F D )>` equal to one another;

I say that :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />`.

For, if not, :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` when produced will meet either in the direction of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` or towards :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0321.g1:C>`.\ [#f3]_ Let them be produced and meet, in the direction of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`, at :g:`$G$ <0321.g1:G>`.

Then, in the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0321.g1:< G E F >>`, the exterior angle :g:`$AEF$ <0321.g1:< A E F )>` is equal to the interior and opposite angle :g:`$EFG$ <0321.g1:< E F G )>`:

.. assertion:: [I. 16] <I.16>

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0321.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0321.g1:/ C D />` when produced will not meet in the direction of :g:`$B$ <0321.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly it can be proved that neither will they meet towards :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0321.g1:C>`.
