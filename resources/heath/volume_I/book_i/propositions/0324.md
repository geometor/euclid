For let the straight line <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> falling on the two straight lines <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> make the exterior angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< E G B )">$EGB$</span> equal to the interior and opposite angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>, or the interior angles on the same side, namely <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>, equal to two right angles;  
I say that <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Folio 0324 Figure 1 - Proposition 28](0324.g1.svg)

For, since the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< E G B )">$EGB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>,  
while the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< E G B )">$EGB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< A G H )">$AGH$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span>  
<p class="assertion">the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< A G H )">$AGH$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and they are alternate;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 27]</span></p>

Again, since the angles <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span> are equal to two right angles,  
and the angles <span data-ref="0324.g1:< A G H )">$AGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> are also equal to two right angles, <span class="justification">[I. 13]</span>  
<p class="assertion">the angles <span data-ref="0324.g1:< A G H )">$AGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> are equal to the angles <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>.</p>
Let the angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> be subtracted from each;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< A G H )">$AGH$</span> is equal to the remaining angle <span data-ref="0324.g1:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and they are alternate;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0324.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 27]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

One criterion of parallelism, the equality of alternate angles, is given in **I.** 27; here we have two more, each of which is easily reducible, and is actually reduced, to the other.

Proclus observes (pp. 358—9) that Euclid could have stated six criteria as well as three, by using, in addition, other pairs of angles in the figure (not adjacent) of which it could be predicated that the two angles are equal or that their sum is equal to two right angles. A natural division is to consider, first the pairs which are on the *same* side of the transversal, and secondly the pairs which are on *different* sides of it.

![Folio 0324 Figure 2 - Proclus Criteria for Parallelism](0324.g2.svg)

Taking (1) the possible pairs on the *same* side, we may have a pair consisting of

*(a)* two internal angles, viz. the pairs (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>) and (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< A G H )">$AGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< G H C )">$GHC$</span>);

*(b)* two external angles, viz. the pairs (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< E G B )">$EGB$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< D H F )">$DHF$</span>) and (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< E G A )">$EGA$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< C H F )">$CHF$</span>);

*(c)* one external and one internal angle, viz. the pairs (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< E G B )">$EGB$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< G H D )">$GHD$</span>), (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< F H D )">$FHD$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< H G B )">$HGB$</span>), (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< E G A )">$EGA$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< G H C )">$GHC$</span>) and (<span data-ref="0324.g2:< F H C )">$FHC$</span>, <span data-ref="0324.g2:< H G A )">$HGA$</span>).
