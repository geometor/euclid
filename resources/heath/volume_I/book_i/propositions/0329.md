De Morgan adds that a proposition is much wanted about parallels (or perpendiculars) to two straight lines respectively making the same angles with one another as the latter do. The proposition may be enunciated thus:

*If the sides of one angle be respectively (1) parallel or (2) perpendicular to the sides of another angle, the two angles are either equal or supplementary.*

(1) Let <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be parallel to <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ G E F /">$GEF$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

To prove that the angles <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> are equal and the angles <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> supplementary.

![Folio 0329 Figure 1 - Angles with Parallel Sides (Case 1)](0329.g1.svg)

Produce <span data-ref="0329.g1:[ D H ]">$DE$</span> to meet <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> in <span data-ref="0329.g1:H">$H$</span>.

Then [I. 29] the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D H C )">$DHC$</span>,  
and the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D H C )">$DHC$</span>.

Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>; whence also the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> is supplementary to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>.

(2) Let <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E D /">$ED$</span> be perpendicular to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ G E F /">$GEF$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

To prove that the angles <span data-ref="0329.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> are equal, and the angles <span data-ref="0329.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> supplementary.

![Folio 0329 Figure 2 - Angles with Perpendicular Sides (Case 2)](0329.g2.svg)

Draw <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E D' /">$ED'$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E D /">$ED$</span> on the side of it opposite to <span data-ref="0329.g2:B">$B$</span>, and draw <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E G' /">$EG'$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> on the side of it opposite to <span data-ref="0329.g2:B">$B$</span>.

Then, since the angles <span data-ref="0329.g2:< B D E )">$BDE$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E D' )">$DED'$</span>, being right angles, are equal,  
<span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E D' /">$ED'$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ B A /">$BA$</span>. [I. 27]

Similarly <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ E G' /">$EG'$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0329.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

Therefore [Part (1)] the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D' E G' )">$D'EG'$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>.

But, the right angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E D' )">$DED'$</span> being equal to the right angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< G E G' )">$GEG'$</span>, if the common angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< G E D' )">$GED'$</span> be subtracted,  
the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D' E G' )">$D'EG'$</span>.

Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E G )">$DEG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>; and hence the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> is supplementary to the angle <span data-ref="0329.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>.

### PROPOSITION 31.

<p class="enunciation">
Through a given point to draw a straight line parallel to a given straight line.
</p>

Let <span data-ref="0330.g1:A">$A$</span> be the given point, and <span data-ref="0330.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> the given straight line;  
thus it is required to draw through the point <span data-ref="0330.g1:A">$A$</span> a straight line parallel to the straight line <span data-ref="0330.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.
