:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` adds that a proposition is much wanted about parallels (or perpendiculars) to two straight lines respectively making the same angles with one another as the latter do. The proposition may be enunciated thus:

*If the sides of one angle be respectively (1) parallel or (2) perpendicular to the sides of another angle, the two angles are either equal or supplementary.*

(1) Let :g:`DE <0329.g1:/ D E />` be parallel to :g:`AB <0329.g1:/ A B />` and :g:`GEF <0329.g1:/ G E F />` parallel to :g:`BC <0329.g1:/ B C />`.

To prove that the angles :g:`ABC <0329.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`DEG <0329.g1:< D E G )>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< A B C ) = 0329.g1:< D E G )>` and the angles :g:`ABC <0329.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`DEF <0329.g1:< D E F )>` supplementary.

.. picture:: 0329.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0329 Figure 1 - Angles with Parallel Sides (Case 1)

Produce :g:`DE <0329.g1:[ D H ]>` to meet :g:`BC <0329.g1:/ B C />` in :g:`H <0329.g1:H>`.

Then [I. 29] the angle :g:`DEG <0329.g1:< D E G )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< D E G ) = 0329.g1:< D H C )>` to the angle :g:`DHC <0329.g1:< D H C )>`, and the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g1:< A B C )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< A B C ) = 0329.g1:< D H C )>` to the angle :g:`DHC <0329.g1:< D H C )>`.

Therefore the angle :g:`DEG <0329.g1:< D E G )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< D E G ) = 0329.g1:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g1:< A B C )>`; whence also the angle :g:`DEF <0329.g1:< D E F )>` is supplementary to the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g1:< A B C )>`.

(2) Let :g:`ED <0329.g2:/ E D />` be perpendicular to :g:`AB <0329.g2:/ A B />`, and :g:`GEF <0329.g2:/ G E F />` perpendicular to :g:`BC <0329.g2:/ B C />`.

To prove that the angles :g:`ABC <0329.g2:< A B C )>`, :g:`DEG <0329.g2:< D E G )>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< A B C ) = 0329.g2:< D E G )>`, and the angles :g:`ABC <0329.g2:< A B C )>`, :g:`DEF <0329.g2:< D E F )>` supplementary.

.. picture:: 0329.g2.svg
   :alt: Folio 0329 Figure 2 - Angles with Perpendicular Sides (Case 2)

Draw :g:`ED' <0329.g2:/ E D' />` at right angles to :g:`ED <0329.g2:/ E D />` on the side of it opposite to :g:`B <0329.g2:B>`, and draw :g:`EG' <0329.g2:/ E G' />` at right angles to :g:`EF <0329.g2:/ E F />` on the side of it opposite to :g:`B <0329.g2:B>`.

Then, since the angles :g:`BDE <0329.g2:< B D E )>`, :g:`DED' <0329.g2:< D E D' )>`, being right angles, are :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< B D E ) = 0329.g2:< D E D' )>`, :g:`ED' <0329.g2:/ E D' />` is parallel to :g:`BA <0329.g2:/ B A />`. [I. 27]

Similarly :g:`EG' <0329.g2:/ E G' />` is parallel to :g:`BC <0329.g2:/ B C />`.

Therefore [Part (1)] the angle :g:`D'EG' <0329.g2:< D' E G' )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< D' E G' ) = 0329.g2:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g2:< A B C )>`.

But, the right angle :g:`DED' <0329.g2:< D E D' )>` being :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< D E D' ) = 0329.g2:< G E G' )>` to the right angle :g:`GEG' <0329.g2:< G E G' )>`, if the common angle :g:`GED' <0329.g2:< G E D' )>` be subtracted, the angle :g:`DEG <0329.g2:< D E G )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< D E G ) = 0329.g2:< D' E G' )>` to the angle :g:`D'EG' <0329.g2:< D' E G' )>`.

Therefore the angle :g:`DEG <0329.g2:< D E G )>` is :eq:`equal <0329.g2:< D E G ) = 0329.g2:< A B C )>` to the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g2:< A B C )>`; and hence the angle :g:`DEF <0329.g2:< D E F )>` is supplementary to the angle :g:`ABC <0329.g2:< A B C )>`.

PROPOSITION 31.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Through a given point to draw a straight line parallel to a given straight line.

Let :g:`A <0330.g1:A>` be the given point, and :g:`BC <0330.g1:/ B C />` the given straight line;

thus it is required to draw through the point :g:`A <0330.g1:A>` a straight line parallel to the straight line :g:`BC <0330.g1:/ B C />`.
