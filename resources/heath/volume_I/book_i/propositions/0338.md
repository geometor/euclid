<sup>5</sup> I say that the opposite sides and angles of the parallelogram $ACDB$ are equal to one another, and the diameter $BC$ bisects it.

![Folio 0338 Figure 1 - Proposition 34](0338.g1.png)

For, since $AB$ is parallel to $CD$,  
and the straight line $BC$ has fallen  
<sup>10</sup> upon them,  
<p class="assertion">the alternate angles $ABC$, $BCD$ are equal to one another. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

Again, since $AC$ is parallel to $BD$,  
and $BC$ has fallen upon them,  
<p class="assertion"><sup>15</sup> the alternate angles $ACB$, $CBD$ are equal to one another. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

Therefore $ABC$, $DCB$ are two triangles having the two angles $ABC$, $BCA$ equal to the two angles $DCB$, $CBD$ respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely that  
<sup>20</sup> adjoining the equal angles and common to both of them, $BC$;  
<p class="sub-clause">therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively, and the remaining angle to the remaining angle; <span class="justification">[I. 26]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the side $AB$ is equal to $CD$,</p>
<p class="assertion"><sup>25</sup> and $AC$ to $BD$,</p>
<p class="assertion">and further the angle $BAC$ is equal to the angle $CDB$.</p>

And, since the angle $ABC$ is equal to the angle $BCD$,  
and the angle $CBD$ to the angle $ACB$,  
<p class="assertion">the whole angle $ABD$ is equal to the whole angle $ACD$. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause"><sup>30</sup> And the angle $BAC$ was also proved equal to the angle $CDB$.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore in parallelogrammic areas the opposite sides and angles are equal to one another.</p>

I say, next, that the diameter also bisects the areas.

For, since $AB$ is equal to $CD$,  
<sup>35</sup> and $BC$ is common,  
the two sides $AB$, $BC$ are equal to the two sides $DC$, $CB$ respectively;  
and the angle $ABC$ is equal to the angle $BCD$;  
<p class="sub-clause">therefore the base $AC$ is also equal to $DB$,</p>
<p class="assertion"><sup>40</sup> and the triangle $ABC$ is equal to the triangle $DCB$. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore the diameter $BC$ bisects the parallelogram $ACDB$.</p>

<p class="qed">Q. E. D.</p>
