:title: Proposition 34

:line:`5` I say that the opposite sides and angles of the parallelogram :g:`$ACDB$ <0338.g1:< A C D B >>` are equal to one another, and the diameter :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` bisects it.

.. picture:: 0338.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0338 Figure 1 - Proposition 34

| For, since :g:`$AB$ <0338.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0338.g1:/ C D />`,
| and the straight line :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` has fallen
| :line:`10` upon them,

.. assertion:: [I. 29] <I.29>

   the alternate angles :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCD$ <0338.g1:< B C D )>` are equal to one another.

| Again, since :g:`$AC$ <0338.g1:/ A C />` is parallel to :g:`$BD$ <0338.g1:/ B D />`,
| and :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` has fallen upon them,

.. assertion:: [I. 29] <I.29>

   :line:`15` the alternate angles :g:`$ACB$ <0338.g1:< A C B )>`, :g:`$CBD$ <0338.g1:< C B D )>` are equal to one another.

| Therefore :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DCB$ <0338.g1:< D C B >>` are two triangles having the two angles :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$BCA$ <0338.g1:< B C A )>` equal to the two angles :g:`$DCB$ <0338.g1:< D C B )>`, :g:`$CBD$ <0338.g1:< C B D )>` respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely that
| :line:`20` adjoining the equal angles and common to both of them, :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />`;

.. subclause:: [I. 26] <I.26>

   therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively, and the remaining angle to the remaining angle;

.. assertion::

   therefore the side :g:`$AB$ <0338.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0338.g1:/ C D />`,

.. assertion::

   :line:`25` and :g:`$AC$ <0338.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$BD$ <0338.g1:/ B D />`,

.. assertion::

   and further the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0338.g1:< B A C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$CDB$ <0338.g1:< C D B )>`.

| And, since the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$BCD$ <0338.g1:< B C D )>`,
| and the angle :g:`$CBD$ <0338.g1:< C B D )>` to the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0338.g1:< A C B )>`,

.. assertion:: [C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>

   the whole angle :g:`$ABD$ <0338.g1:< A B D )>` is equal to the whole angle :g:`$ACD$ <0338.g1:< A C D )>`.

.. subclause::

   :line:`30` And the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0338.g1:< B A C )>` was also proved equal to the angle :g:`$CDB$ <0338.g1:< C D B )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in parallelogrammic areas the opposite sides and angles are equal to one another.

I say, next, that the diameter also bisects the areas.

| For, since :g:`$AB$ <0338.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0338.g1:/ C D />`,
| :line:`35` and :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` is common,
| the two sides :g:`$AB$ <0338.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$DC$ <0338.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0338.g1:/ C B />` respectively;
| and the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$BCD$ <0338.g1:< B C D )>`;

.. subclause::

   therefore the base :g:`$AC$ <0338.g1:/ A C />` is also equal to :g:`$DB$ <0338.g1:/ D B />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   :line:`40` and the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0338.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$DCB$ <0338.g1:< D C B >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the diameter :g:`$BC$ <0338.g1:/ B C />` bisects the parallelogram :g:`$ACDB$ <0338.g1:< A C D B >>`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
