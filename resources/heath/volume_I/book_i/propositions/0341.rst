Equality in a new sense.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

It is important to observe that we are in this proposition introduced for the first time to a new conception of equality between figures. Hitherto we have had equality in the sense of *congruence* only, as applied to straight lines, angles, and even triangles (cf. I. 4). Now, without any explicit reference to any change in the meaning of the term, figures are inferred to be *equal* which are equal in *area* or in *content* but need not be of the *same form*.
