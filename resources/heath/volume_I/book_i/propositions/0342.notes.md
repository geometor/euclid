# Transcription Notes: Folio 0342 (Page 328)

## Summary
- **Content**:
  - **Commentary on Equality of Area (continued)**:
    - Continuation from Folio 0341 (page 327): adding common triangle $\triangle GBC$ to trapezia $ABGD$ and $EGCF$ to establish equality of parallelograms $ABCD$ and $EBCF$.
    - **Simson's (after Clairaut) Single-Step Proof**:
      - Replaces Euclid's two-step procedure (subtract $\triangle DGE$, then add $\triangle GBC$) with a single step: subtract congruent triangles $\triangle AEB$, $\triangle DFC$ from the larger trapezium $ABCF$.
      - This formulation applies universally to all three possible configurations without requiring separate case proofs.
    - **Tacit Assumptions & De Morgan's Analysis**:
      - Augustus De Morgan observes the tacit postulate: “an area taken from an area leaves the same area from whatever part it may be taken.”
      - Essential foundation for establishing the equality of non-rectilineal areas that cannot be brought into geometric coincidence.
    - **Legendre's Terminological Distinction**:
      - A. M. Legendre introduced *equivalent* for equality of area/content, reserving *equal* for congruent figures.
    - **Modern Theories of Equivalence**:
      - Mentions nineteenth-century researches of W. Bolyai, J. M. C. Duhamel, A. De Zolt, O. Stolz, F. Schur, G. Veronese, D. Hilbert, U. Amaldi, and M. Simon.
      - Citations:
        - Ugo Amaldi, *Sulla teoria dell’ equivalenza* in *Questioni riguardanti le matematiche elementari*, I. (Bologna, 1912), pp. 145—198.
        - Max Simon, *Über die Entwicklung der Elementar-geometrie im XIX. Jahrhundert* (Leipzig, 1906), pp. 115—120.
    - **Hilbert's Formalization (*Grundlagen der Geometrie*, pp. 39, 40)**:
      - (1) **Divisibly-equal** (*zerlegungsgleich*): two polygons partitionable into a finite number of pairwise congruent triangles (Amaldi: *equivalent by sum*).
      - (2) **Equal in content** (*inhaltsgleich* / *of equal content*): polygons to which divisibly-equal polygons can be added to make the unions divisibly-equal (Amaldi: *equivalent by difference*).
      - Theorems on closure and transitivity of divisible equality and equality in content.
  - **Section: The different cases**:
    - Proclus (pp. 399—400) supplies separate proofs for the other two cases omitted by Euclid.
    - For the case where $E$ falls between $A$ and $D$, Proclus adds congruent triangles $\triangle ABE$, $\triangle DCF$ to the smaller trapezium $EBCD$ (as opposed to Simson's subtraction from the larger trapezium $ABCF$).
