Transcription Notes: Folio 0343 (Page 329)
==========================================

Summary
-------

- **Content**:

  - **An ancient “Budget of Paradoxes”**:

    - Proclus observes (p. 396, 12 sqq.) that Euclid I. 35 and the corresponding theorem for triangles (I. 37) were classed among the "paradoxical theorems" of mathematics, because the uninstructed would find it paradoxical that parallelograms remain equal in area while their non-base sides increase without limit.
    - Mathematicians compiled a collection of such paradoxes called the *treasury of paradoxes* (ὁ παράδοξος τόπος), comparable to the *treasury of analysis* (τόπος ἀναλυόμενος) and the *treasury of astronomy* (τόπος ἀστρονομούμενος), and analogous to the Stoics' collections of illustrative examples (ὥσπερ οἱ ἀπὸ τῆς Στοᾶς ἐπὶ τῶν δειγμάτων).
    - Heath conjectures that this treasury may have been the work of Erycinus cited by Pappus (III. p. 107, 8; cf. note on I. 21, p. 290).

  - **Locus-theorems and loci in Greek geometry**:

    - Proclus (pp. 394–6) identifies Euclid I. 35 as the first *locus-theorem* (τοπικὸν θεώρημα) in the *Elements*.
    - Proclus, along with Pappus and Eutocius, provides our primary ancient testimony on Greek locus theory.
    - Proclus' definitions (pp. 394, 15—395, 2):

      - *Locus-theorems* (τοπικά): theorems where the same property holds over the whole of some locus (πρὸς ὅλῳ τινὶ τόπῳ).
      - *Locus* (τόπος): a position of a line or surface yielding one and the same property (γραμμῆς ἢ ἐπιφανείας θέσιν ποιοῦσαν ἓν καὶ ταὐτὸν σύμπτωμα).
      - Division into loci on lines (πρὸς γραμμαῖς συνιστάμενα) and loci on surfaces (πρὸς ἐπιφανείαις).
      - Lines divided into *plane* (ἐπίπεδοι, conceived simply in a plane: ὧν ἐν ἐπιπέδῳ ἁπλῆ ἡ νόησις) and *solid* (στερεαί, generated from a section of a solid, such as the cylindrical helix and conic sections: ὡς τῆς κυλινδρικῆς ἕλικος καὶ τῶν κωνικῶν γραμμῶν).
      - Locus-theorems on lines correspondingly give *plane loci* or *solid loci*.

  - **Ancient Conception of Plane and Solid Loci**:

    - Eutocius (*Apollonius*, ed. Heiberg, II. p. 184): solid loci are named because lines used in solving them originate in sections of solids (cones and others).
    - Pappus: plane loci are straight lines and circles; solid loci are conics.
    - Aristaeus wrote five books of *Solid Loci* (Pappus VII. p. 672, 20) "supplementary to the conics".
    - Pappus VII. p. 662, 10—15 (bracketed by Hultsch) and III. p. 54, 7—16: plane problems solved by straight lines and circles; solid problems solved by conic sections.

Editorial & Philological Details
--------------------------------

- **Polytonic Greek Orthography**:

  - ``ὁ παράδοξος τόπος`` (*treasury of paradoxes*): omicron with dasia; παράδοξος with oxia on alpha; τόπος with oxia on omicron.
  - ``τόπος ἀναλυόμενος`` (*treasury of analysis*): ἀναλυόμενος with psili on initial alpha and oxia on omicron.
  - ``τόπος ἀστρονομούμενος`` (*treasury of astronomy*): psili on alpha, oxia on upsilon (contract verb ἀστρονομέω).
  - ``ὥσπερ οἱ ἀπὸ τῆς Στοᾶς ἐπὶ τῶν δειγμάτων``: ὥσπερ (dasia + oxia on omega), οἱ (dasia on iota), ἀπὸ (psili + varia), Στοᾶς (perispomeni on alpha), ἐπὶ (psili + varia), δειγμάτων (oxia on alpha).
  - ``τοπικὸν θεώρημα``: oxia on omega of θεώρημα; varia on omicron of oxytone τοπικὸν before noun.
  - ``πρὸς ὅλῳ τινὶ τόπῳ``: ὅλῳ with dasia and oxia on omicron, iota subscript on omega; τινὶ with varia on iota; τόπῳ with oxia on omicron, iota subscript on omega.
  - ``γραμμῆς ἢ ἐπιφανείας θέσιν ποιοῦσαν ἓν καὶ ταὐτὸν σύμπτωμα``:

    - ``γραμμῆς``: perispomeni on eta.
    - ``ἢ``: psili and varia on eta.
    - ``ἐπιφανείας``: psili on epsilon, oxia on diphthong ει.
    - ``θέσιν``: oxia on epsilon.
    - ``ποιοῦσαν``: perispomeni on diphthong ου.
    - ``ἓν``: dasia and varia on epsilon.
    - ``καὶ``: varia on iota.
    - ``ταὐτὸν``: crasis (τὸ αὐτό) with psili (coronis) on upsilon, varia on omicron.
    - ``σύμπτωμα``: oxia on upsilon.

  - ``τῶν γὰρ τοπικῶν τὰ μέν ἐστι πρὸς γραμμαῖς συνιστάμενα, τὰ δὲ πρὸς ἐπιφανείαις``:

    - ``μέν ἐστι``: μέν retains acute before enclitic ἐστι.
    - ``γραμμαῖς``: perispomeni on diphthong αις.
    - ``ἐπιφανείαις``: psili on epsilon, oxia on diphthong ει.

  - ``ἐπίπεδοι`` / ``στερεαί``: plane lines / solid lines.
  - ``ὧν ἐν ἐπιπέδῳ ἁπλῆ ἡ νόησις``: ὧν (dasia + perispomeni), ἐπιπέδῳ (psili on epsilon, oxia on second epsilon, iota subscript on omega), ἁπλῆ (dasia on alpha, perispomeni on eta), ἡ (dasia on eta), νόησις (oxia on omicron).
  - ``ὡς τῆς κυλινδρικῆς ἕλικος καὶ τῶν κωνικῶν γραμμῶν``:

    - ``κυλινδρικῆς``: perispomeni on eta.
    - ``ἕλικος``: dasia and oxia on epsilon.
    - ``κωνικῶν``: perispomeni on omega.
    - ``γραμμῶν``: perispomeni on omega.

  - ``φαίην ἄν``: optative φαίην (oxia on diphthong αι); particle ἄν (psili + oxia).
