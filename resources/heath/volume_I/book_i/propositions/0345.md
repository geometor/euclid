### PROPOSITION 36.

<p class="enunciation">Parallelograms which are on equal bases and in the same<br>
parallels are equal to one another.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E F G H &gt;">$EFGH$</span> be parallelograms which are on equal bases <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> and in the same parallels <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span>;

![Folio 0345 Figure 1 - Proposition 36](0345.g1.svg)

I say that the parallelogram <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E F G H &gt;">$EFGH$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ C H /">$CH$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>  
while <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>,
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But they are also parallel.<br>
And <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span> join them;<br>
but straight lines joining equal and parallel straight lines (at the extremities which are) in the same directions (respectively) are equal and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E B C H &gt;">$EBCH$</span> is a parallelogram. <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>;<br>
for it has the same base <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> with it, and is in the same parallels <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0345.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> with it. <span class="justification">[I. 35]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason also <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E F G H &gt;">$EFGH$</span> is equal to the same <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E B C H &gt;">$EBCH$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 35]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that the parallelogram <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0345.g1:&lt; E F G H &gt;">$EFGH$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
