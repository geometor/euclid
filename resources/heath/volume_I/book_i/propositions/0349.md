Then, since the angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span> are equal to two right angles, the angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D H )">$EDH$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span> are equal to two right angles.  
But so are the angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D H )">$EDH$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span>.  
Therefore the angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span> are equal.  
And the alternate angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D F H )">$DFH$</span> are equal. [I. 29]  
Therefore the angles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D F H )">$DFH$</span> are equal,  
and <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>. [I. 6]  
Hence the two sides <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; and the included angles are equal.  
Therefore the triangles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D E H &gt;">$DEH$</span> are equal in all respects.  
And the triangles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D E F &gt;">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D E H &gt;">$DEH$</span> between the same parallels are equal. [I. 37]  
Therefore the triangles <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:&lt; D E F &gt;">$DEF$</span> are equal.  
[Proclus takes the construction of Eucl. I. 24, i.e., he makes <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> and then proves that <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0348.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> are parallel.]

(2) Suppose the angles <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span> together less than two right angles.  
As before, make the angle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D G )">$EDG$</span> equal to the angle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span>, draw <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, and join <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>.

![Folio 0349 Figure 1 - Proposition 38 Commentary (Heron, Case 2)](0349.g1.svg)

In this case the angles <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D H )">$EDH$</span>, <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span> are together less than two right angles, while the angles <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D H )">$EDH$</span>, <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span> are equal to two right angles. [I. 29]  
Hence the angle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span>, and therefore the angle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D F H )">$DFH$</span>, is less than the angle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span>.  
Therefore <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> is less than <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [I. 19]

Produce <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> to <span data-ref="0349.g1:G">$G$</span> so that <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> or <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, and join <span data-ref="0349.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.

Then the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D E G &gt;">$DEG$</span>, which is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, is greater than the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D E H &gt;">$DEH$</span>, and therefore greater than the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g1:&lt; D E F &gt;">$DEF$</span>.

(3) Suppose the angles <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; E D F )">$EDF$</span> together greater than two right angles.

![Folio 0349 Figure 2 - Proposition 38 Commentary (Heron, Case 3)](0349.g2.svg)

We make the same construction in this case, and we prove in like manner that the angle <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; D H F )">$DHF$</span> is less than the angle <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; D F H )">$DFH$</span>,  
whence <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ D H /">$DH$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> or <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

Make <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ D G /">$DG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, and join <span data-ref="0349.g2:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.

It then follows that the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; D E F &gt;">$DEF$</span> is greater than the triangle <span data-ref="0349.g2:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>.
