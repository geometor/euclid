## Textual Observations & Editorial Decisions

1. **Top Commentary on Proposition 39 (Note on Line 5)**:
   - Line note `5. [I say that they are also in the same parallels.]` comments on the specification statement of Proposition 39 (transcribed on the preceding folio 0350).
   - Cites J. L. Heiberg's article in *Hermes* (**xxxviii.**, 1903, p. 50), utilizing the papyrus discovery from Grenfell, Hunt, and Hogarth (*Fayūm Towns and Their Papyri*, 1900, p. 96, No. ix.).
   - Heiberg demonstrated that the words were interpolated by an ancient reader who mistook the setting-out clause ("And let $AD$ be joined") for part of the construction, mistakenly believing that a specification (*diorismos*) should precede it, subsequently altering "And" to "For".
   - Greek terms transcribed with polytonic accents:
     - `ἔκθεσις` (*ekthesis*, setting-out)
     - `κατασκευή` (*kataskeuē*, construction)
     - `διορισμός` (*diorismos*, definition/specification of the goal)
   - Orthography: Preserved macron in `Fayūm`.

2. **Heath's Commentary on Converses (I. 37–40)**:

   - Highlights the necessity of the explicit clause *on the same side* of the base, which is inherently satisfied in I. 37 but must be stated for the converse.
