Let <span data-ref="0353.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be a trapezium in which <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are the parallel sides, and <span data-ref="0353.g1:E">$E$</span> the middle point of one of the non-parallel sides, say <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.  
Join <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>, <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> and produce <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to meet <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> produced in <span data-ref="0353.g1:F">$F$</span>.

![Folio 0353 Figure 1 - Proclus Lemma on Trapezium and Triangle Area](0353.g1.svg)

Then the triangles <span data-ref="0353.g1:< B E C >">$BEC$</span>, <span data-ref="0353.g1:< F E D >">$FED$</span> have two angles equal respectively, and one side <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> equal to one side <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;  
therefore the triangles are equal in all respects. [I. 26]

Add to each the quadrilateral <span data-ref="0353.g1:< A B E D >">$ABED$</span>;  
therefore the trapezium <span data-ref="0353.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g1:< A B F >">$ABF$</span>,  
that is, to twice the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g1:< A E B >">$AEB$</span>, since <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0353.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [I. 38]

The three properties proved by Proclus may be combined in one enunciation thus:

*If a triangle be formed by joining the middle point of one side of a trapezium to the extremities of the opposite side, the area of the trapezium is (1) greater than, (2) equal to, or (3) less than, double the area of the triangle according as the side the middle point of which is taken is (1) the greater of the parallel sides, (2) either of the non-parallel sides, or (3) the lesser of the parallel sides.*

### PROPOSITION 42.

<p class="enunciation">To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram<br>
equal to a given triangle.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> be the given triangle, and <span data-ref="0353.g2:< D )">$D$</span> the given rectilineal angle;  
thus it is required to construct in the rectilineal angle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< D )">$D$</span> a parallelogram equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0353.g2:E">$E$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be joined;  
on the straight line <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, and at the point <span data-ref="0353.g2:E">$E$</span> on it, let the angle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< C E F )">$CEF$</span> be constructed equal to the angle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< D )">$D$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 23]</span>  
through <span data-ref="0353.g2:A">$A$</span> let <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, and <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>  
through <span data-ref="0353.g2:C">$C$</span> let <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ C G /">$CG$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

![Folio 0353 Figure 2 - Proposition 42](0353.g2.svg)

Then <span data-ref="0353.g2:< F E C G >">$FECG$</span> is a parallelogram.

And, since <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A B E >">$ABE$</span> is also equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A E C >">$AEC$</span>, for they are on equal bases <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span> and in the same parallels <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0353.g2:/ A G /">$AG$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 38]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is double of the triangle <span data-ref="0353.g2:< A E C >">$AEC$</span>.</p>
