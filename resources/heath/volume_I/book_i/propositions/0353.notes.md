1. **Conclusion of Proclus' Trapezium Lemma (Commentary on I. 41)**:
   - Continuation from folio 0352 (p. 338), which enunciated Proclus' theorem that a triangle formed by joining the midpoint of either non-parallel side of a trapezium to the opposite vertices is half the trapezium in area.
   - Folio 0353 gives the demonstration:
     - Midpoint $E$ of side $DC$, line $BE$ produced to meet $AD$ produced in $F$.
     - Triangles $BEC, FED$ are congruent via [I. 26] (two angles and one side equal).
     - Adding quadrilateral $ABED$ yields trapezium $ABCD = \triangle ABF = 2 \times \triangle AEB$ by [I. 38] ($BE = EF$).

     - Area of trapezium is (1) greater than, (2) equal to, or (3) less than double the triangle according as the midpoint is on (1) the greater parallel side, (2) either non-parallel side, or (3) the lesser parallel side.
