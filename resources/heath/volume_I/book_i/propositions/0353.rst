| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0353.g1:< A B C D >>` be a trapezium in which :g:`$AD$ <0353.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$BC$ <0353.g1:/ B C />` are the parallel sides, and :g:`$E$ <0353.g1:E>` the middle point of one of the non-parallel sides, say :g:`$DC$ <0353.g1:/ D C />`.
| Join :g:`$EA$ <0353.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`$EB$ <0353.g1:/ E B />` and produce :g:`$BE$ <0353.g1:/ B E />` to meet :g:`$AD$ <0353.g1:/ A D />` produced in :g:`$F$ <0353.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0353.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0353 Figure 1 - Proclus Lemma on Trapezium and Triangle Area

| Then the triangles :g:`$BEC$ <0353.g1:< B E C >>`, :g:`$FED$ <0353.g1:< F E D >>` have two angles equal respectively, and one side :g:`$CE$ <0353.g1:/ C E />` equal to one side :g:`$DE$ <0353.g1:/ D E />`;
| therefore the triangles are equal in all respects. [I. 26]

| Add to each the quadrilateral :g:`$ABED$ <0353.g1:< A B E D >>`;
| therefore the trapezium :g:`$ABCD$ <0353.g1:< A B C D >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$ABF$ <0353.g1:< A B F >>`,
| that is, to twice the triangle :g:`$AEB$ <0353.g1:< A E B >>`, since :g:`$BE$ <0353.g1:/ B E />` is equal to :g:`$EF$ <0353.g1:/ E F />`. [I. 38]

The three properties proved by :person:`Proclus <proclus>` may be combined in one enunciation thus:

*If a triangle be formed by joining the middle point of one side of a trapezium to the extremities of the opposite side, the area of the trapezium is (1) greater than, (2) equal to, or (3) less than, double the area of the triangle according as the side the middle point of which is taken is (1) the greater of the parallel sides, (2) either of the non-parallel sides, or (3) the lesser of the parallel sides.*

PROPOSITION 42.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given triangle.

| Let :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>` be the given triangle, and :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>` the given rectilineal angle;
| thus it is required to construct in the rectilineal angle :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>` a parallelogram equal to the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>`.

| Let :g:`$BC$ <0353.g2:/ B C />` be bisected at :g:`$E$ <0353.g2:E>`, and let :g:`$AE$ <0353.g2:/ A E />` be joined;
| on the straight line :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, and at the point :g:`$E$ <0353.g2:E>` on it, let the angle :g:`$CEF$ <0353.g2:< C E F )>` be constructed equal to the angle :g:`$D$ <0353.g2:< D )>`; :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`
| through :g:`$A$ <0353.g2:A>` let :g:`$AG$ <0353.g2:/ A G />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, and :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`
| through :g:`$C$ <0353.g2:C>` let :g:`$CG$ <0353.g2:/ C G />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$EF$ <0353.g2:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0353.g2.svg
   :alt: Folio 0353 Figure 2 - Proposition 42

Then :g:`$FECG$ <0353.g2:< F E C G >>` is a parallelogram.

And, since :g:`$BE$ <0353.g2:/ B E />` is equal to :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />`, the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0353.g2:< A B E >>` is also equal to the triangle :g:`$AEC$ <0353.g2:< A E C >>`, for they are on equal bases :g:`$BE$ <0353.g2:/ B E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0353.g2:/ E C />` and in the same parallels :g:`$BC$ <0353.g2:/ B C />`, :g:`$AG$ <0353.g2:/ A G />`; :just:`[I. 38] <I.38>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0353.g2:< A B C >>` is double of the triangle :g:`$AEC$ <0353.g2:< A E C >>`.
