### PROPOSITION 43.

<p class="enunciation">In any parallelogram the complements of the parallelograms<br>
about the diameter are equal to one another.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be a parallelogram, and <span data-ref="0354.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> its diameter;  
and about <span data-ref="0354.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> let <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A E K H >">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K F C G >">$FG$</span> be parallelograms, and <span data-ref="0354.g1:< E B F K >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0354.g1:< H K G D >">$KD$</span>  
<sup>5</sup> the so-called complements;  
I say that the complement <span data-ref="0354.g1:< E B F K >">$BK$</span> is equal to the complement <span data-ref="0354.g1:< H K G D >">$KD$</span>.

![Folio 0354 Figure 1 - Proposition 43](0354.g1.svg)

For, since <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> is a parallelogram, and <span data-ref="0354.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> its diameter,  
the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is equal to  
<sup>10</sup> the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A C D >">$ACD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span>

Again, since <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A E K H >">$EH$</span> is a parallelogram, and <span data-ref="0354.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is its diameter,  
the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A E K >">$AEK$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A H K >">$AHK$</span>.

<sup>15</sup> For the same reason  
the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K F C >">$KFC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K G C >">$KGC$</span>.

Now, since the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A E K >">$AEK$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A H K >">$AHK$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K F C >">$KFC$</span> to <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K G C >">$KGC$</span>,
<p class="assertion"><sup>20</sup> the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A E K >">$AEK$</span> together with <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K G C >">$KGC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A H K >">$AHK$</span> together with <span data-ref="0354.g1:< K F C >">$KFC$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span></p>

And the whole triangle <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is also equal to the whole <span data-ref="0354.g1:< A D C >">$ADC$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the complement <span data-ref="0354.g1:< E B F K >">$BK$</span> which remains is equal to the<br>
<sup>25</sup> complement <span data-ref="0354.g1:< H K G D >">$KD$</span> which remains. <span class="justification">[C. N. 3]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
