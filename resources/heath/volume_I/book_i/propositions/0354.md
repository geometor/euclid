### PROPOSITION 43.

<p class="enunciation">In any parallelogram the complements of the parallelograms<br>
about the diameter are equal to one another.</p>

Let $ABCD$ be a parallelogram, and $AC$ its diameter;  
and about $AC$ let $EH$, $FG$ be parallelograms, and $BK$, $KD$  
<sup>5</sup> the so-called complements;  
I say that the complement $BK$ is equal to the complement $KD$.

![Folio 0354 Figure 1 - Proposition 43](0354.g1.svg)

For, since $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, and $AC$ its diameter,  
the triangle $ABC$ is equal to  
<sup>10</sup> the triangle $ACD$. <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span>

Again, since $EH$ is a parallelogram, and $AK$ is its diameter,  
the triangle $AEK$ is equal to the triangle $AHK$.

<sup>15</sup> For the same reason  
the triangle $KFC$ is also equal to $KGC$.

Now, since the triangle $AEK$ is equal to the triangle $AHK$,  
and $KFC$ to $KGC$,
<p class="assertion"><sup>20</sup> the triangle $AEK$ together with $KGC$ is equal to the triangle $AHK$ together with $KFC$. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span></p>

And the whole triangle $ABC$ is also equal to the whole $ADC$;
<p class="assertion">therefore the complement $BK$ which remains is equal to the<br>
<sup>25</sup> complement $KD$ which remains. <span class="justification">[C. N. 3]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
