2. **Proposition 43 Ground-Truth Cross-Check (`elem.1.43`)**:
   - Enunciation: *"In any parallelogram the complements of the parallelograms about the diameter are equal to one another."*
     - Greek: Παντὸς παραλληλογράμμου τῶν περὶ τὴν διάμετρον παραλληλογράμμων τὰ παραπληρώματα ἴσα ἀλλήλοις ἐστίν.
   - Setting-out: Parallelogram $ABCD$, diameter $AC$, parallelograms $EH, FG$ about $AC$, complements $BK, KD$.
   - Specification: `I say that the complement BK is equal to the complement KD.`
   - Proof:
     - Parallelogram $ABCD$, diameter $AC \implies \triangle ABC = \triangle ACD$ [I. 34].
     - Parallelogram $EH$, diameter $AK \implies \triangle AEK = \triangle AHK$.
     - Similarly $\triangle KFC = \triangle KGC$.
     - By Common Notion 2 [C. N. 2]: $\triangle AEK + \triangle KGC = \triangle AHK + \triangle KFC$.
     - By Common Notion 3 [C. N. 3]: Subtracting these sums from whole triangles $\triangle ABC = \triangle ADC$ leaves complement $BK = \text{complement } KD$.
     - Conclusion: `Therefore etc. Q. E. D.`
   - Cross-check against Perseus TEI XML confirms exact correspondence of wording. Note that Heath's print formats Common Notions as `[C. N. 2]` and `[C. N. 3]` with internal spacing, preserved in markdown.
