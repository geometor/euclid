## Textual Observations & Editorial Decisions

1. **Notes on Proposition 43 (Completion from Page 340)**:
   - Proposition 43 was demonstrated on folio 0354 (page 340). Folio 0355 opens directly with Heath's textual and philological notes keyed to the proposition lines:
     - **Note 1**: Lemma `complements`, Greek `παραπληρώματα` (*parapleromata*, literally "fillings-in", from *para* + *pleroo*), glossed as "the figures put in to fill up (interstices)."
     - **Note 4**: Lemma `and about $AC$....`. Heath notes Euclid's terminology regarding the complements and parallelograms being "about" the diagonal.
     - **Greek citation**:
       `περὶ δὲ τὴν ΑΓ παραλληλόγραμμα μὲν ἔστω τὰ ΕΘ, ΖΗ, τὰ δὲ λεγόμενα παραπληρώματα τὰ ΒΚ, ΚΔ.`
       - All accents and breathings verified: grave on `περὶ`, `δὲ`, `τὴν`, `μὲν`, `τὰ` (thrice); smooth breathing and acute on `ἔστω`; acute on `παραλληλόγραμμα`, `λεγόμενα`, and `παραπληρώματα`.
       - Geometric letter mapping: In Greek, Euclid uses $\text{E}\Theta$ and $\text{Z}\text{H}$ for the parallelograms about the diameter, where Greek $\Theta$ corresponds to Heath's English label $H$, and Greek $\text{Z}$ corresponds to Heath's $F$.
       - The phrase `τὰ δὲ λεγόμενα παραπληρώματα` ("the so-called complements") reflects established technical vocabulary prior to Euclid.

2. **Proclus Commentary & Lacuna Notice**:
   - Heath observes a textual defect in Proclus' commentary as transmitted:
     *"In the text of Proclus' commentary as we have it, the end of the note on I. 41, the whole of that on I. 42, and the beginning of that on I. 43 are missing."*
   - Proclus discussion (Friedlein ed., pp. 417, 1 sqq. and 418, 15—20):
     - Euclid gave no formal definition of "complement" because the geometric reality made it obvious.
     - When the two parallelograms about the diameter are not formed through a single common point on the diagonal, the complements need not be parallelograms:
       1. Non-overlapping: The complements are 5-sided figures.
       2. Overlapping: Proclus loosely designates $FG, EH$ as the complements, but Heath critically notes that strictly the complements are $FG - \triangle LMN$ and $EH - \triangle KMN$ respectively.
     - In all cases, the complements remain equal.
