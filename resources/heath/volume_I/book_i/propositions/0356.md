<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be drawn through to <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> be drawn through  
<span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> parallel to either <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> or <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>  
Let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> be joined.

![Folio 0356 Figure 1 - Proposition 44](0356.g1.svg)

Then, since the straight line <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> falls upon the parallels  
<sup>15</sup> <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
<p class="assertion">the angles <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A H F )">$AHF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; H F E )">$HFE$</span> are equal to two right angles. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

Therefore the angles <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; B H G )">$BHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; G F E )">$GFE$</span> are less than two right angles;  
and straight lines produced indefinitely from angles less than  
<sup>20</sup> two right angles meet; <span class="justification">[Post. 5]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, when produced, will meet.</p>

Let them be produced and meet at <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>; through the point  
<span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span> let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> be drawn parallel to either <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> or <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>  
and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> be produced to the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:M">$M$</span>.

<sup>25</sup> Then <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; H L K F &gt;">$HLKF$</span> is a parallelogram,  
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is its diameter, and <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; H A B G &gt;">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; B M K E &gt;">$ME$</span> are parallelograms, and  
<span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M L &gt;">$LB$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; B E F G &gt;">$BF$</span> the so-called complements, about <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M L &gt;">$LB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; B E F G &gt;">$BF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span></p>

But <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; B E F G &gt;">$BF$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>;
<p class="assertion"><sup>30</sup> therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M L &gt;">$LB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span></p>

And, since the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; G B E )">$GBE$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M )">$ABM$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span>  
while the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; G B E )">$GBE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; D )">$D$</span>,
<p class="assertion">the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M )">$ABM$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; D )">$D$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M L &gt;">$LB$</span> equal to the given triangle  
<sup>35</sup> <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span> has been applied to the given straight line <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, in the angle  
<span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; A B M )">$ABM$</span> which is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:&lt; D )">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>
