Then, since the straight line $HF$ falls upon the parallels  
<sup>15</sup> $AH$, $EF$,
<p class="assertion">the angles $AHF$, $HFE$ are equal to two right angles. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

Therefore the angles $BHG$, $GFE$ are less than two right angles;  
and straight lines produced indefinitely from angles less than  
<sup>20</sup> two right angles meet; <span class="justification">[Post. 5]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore $HB$, $FE$, when produced, will meet.</p>

Let them be produced and meet at $K$; through the point  
$K$ let $KL$ be drawn parallel to either $EA$ or $FH$, <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>  
and let $HA$, $GB$ be produced to the points $L$, $M$.

<sup>25</sup> Then $HLKF$ is a parallelogram,  
$HK$ is its diameter, and $AG$, $ME$ are parallelograms, and  
$LB$, $BF$ the so-called complements, about $HK$;
<p class="assertion">therefore $LB$ is equal to $BF$. <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span></p>

But $BF$ is equal to the triangle $C$;
<p class="assertion"><sup>30</sup> therefore $LB$ is also equal to $C$. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span></p>

And, since the angle $GBE$ is equal to the angle $ABM$, <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span>  
while the angle $GBE$ is equal to $D$,
<p class="assertion">the angle $ABM$ is also equal to the angle $D$.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore the parallelogram $LB$ equal to the given triangle  
<sup>35</sup> $C$ has been applied to the given straight line $AB$, in the angle  
$ABM$ which is equal to $D$. <span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>
