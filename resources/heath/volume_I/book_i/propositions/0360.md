Let the angle $KHG$ be added to each;  
therefore the angles $FKH$, $KHG$ are equal to the angles  
$KHG$, $GHM$.

But the angles $FKH$, $KHG$ are equal to two right angles; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
<sup>20</sup> therefore the angles $KHG$, $GHM$ are also equal to two right  
angles.

Thus, with a straight line $GH$, and at the point $H$ on it,  
two straight lines $KH$, $HM$ not lying on the same side make  
the adjacent angles equal to two right angles;
<p class="assertion"><sup>25</sup> therefore $KH$ is in a straight line with $HM$. <span class="justification">[I. 14]</span></p>

And, since the straight line $HG$ falls upon the parallels  
$KM$, $FG$, the alternate angles $MHG$, $HGF$ are equal to one  
another. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>

Let the angle $HGL$ be added to each;  
<sup>30</sup> therefore the angles $MHG$, $HGL$ are equal to the angles  
$HGF$, $HGL$. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span>

But the angles $MHG$, $HGL$ are equal to two right angles; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
therefore the angles $HGF$, $HGL$ are also equal to two right  
angles. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span>

<p class="assertion"><sup>35</sup> Therefore $FG$ is in a straight line with $GL$. <span class="justification">[I. 14]</span></p>

And, since $FK$ is equal and parallel to $HG$, <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span>  
and $HG$ to $ML$ also,  
$KF$ is also equal and parallel to $ML$; <span class="justification">[C. N. 1 ; I. 30]</span>  
and the straight lines $KM$, $FL$ join them (at their extremities);  
<sup>40</sup> therefore $KM$, $FL$ are also equal and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span>
<p class="assertion">Therefore $KFLM$ is a parallelogram.</p>

And, since the triangle $ABD$ is equal to the parallelogram  
$FH$,  
and $DBC$ to $GM$,
<p class="assertion"><sup>45</sup> the whole rectilineal figure $ABCD$ is equal to the whole  
parallelogram $KFLM$.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore the parallelogram $KFLM$ has been constructed  
equal to the given rectilineal figure $ABCD$, in the angle $FKM$  
which is equal to the given angle $E$. <span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

**2, 3, 6, 45, 48. rectilineal figure**, in the Greek “rectilineal” simply, without “figure,” εὐθύγραμμον being here used as a substantive, like the similarly formed παραλληλόγραμμον.
