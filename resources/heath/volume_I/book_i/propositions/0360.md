Let the angle <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K H G )">$KHG$</span> be added to each;  
therefore the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; F K H )">$FKH$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K H G )">$KHG$</span> are equal to the angles  
<span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K H G )">$KHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; G H M )">$GHM$</span>.

But the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; F K H )">$FKH$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K H G )">$KHG$</span> are equal to two right angles; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
<sup>20</sup> therefore the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K H G )">$KHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; G H M )">$GHM$</span> are also equal to two right  
angles.

Thus, with a straight line <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, and at the point <span data-ref="0359.g1:H">$H$</span> on it,  
two straight lines <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> not lying on the same side make  
the adjacent angles equal to two right angles;
<p class="assertion"><sup>25</sup> therefore <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is in a straight line with <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 14]</span></p>

And, since the straight line <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> falls upon the parallels  
<span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, the alternate angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; M H G )">$MHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G F )">$HGF$</span> are equal to one  
another. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>

Let the angle <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G L )">$HGL$</span> be added to each;  
<sup>30</sup> therefore the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; M H G )">$MHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G L )">$HGL$</span> are equal to the angles  
<span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G F )">$HGF$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G L )">$HGL$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 2]</span>

But the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; M H G )">$MHG$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G L )">$HGL$</span> are equal to two right angles; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
therefore the angles <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G F )">$HGF$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; H G L )">$HGL$</span> are also equal to two right  
angles. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span>

<p class="assertion"><sup>35</sup> Therefore <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is in a straight line with <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ G L /">$GL$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 14]</span></p>

And, since <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span>  
and <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> to <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span> also,  
<span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>; <span class="justification">[C. N. 1 ; I. 30]</span>  
and the straight lines <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> join them (at their extremities);  
<sup>40</sup> therefore <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0359.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> are also equal and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K F L M &gt;">$KFLM$</span> is a parallelogram.</p>

And, since the triangle <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; A B D &gt;">$ABD$</span> is equal to the parallelogram  
<span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; F K H G &gt;">$FH$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; D B C &gt;">$DBC$</span> to <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; G H M L &gt;">$GM$</span>,
<p class="assertion"><sup>45</sup> the whole rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> is equal to the whole  
parallelogram <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K F L M &gt;">$KFLM$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; K F L M &gt;">$KFLM$</span> has been constructed  
equal to the given rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>, in the angle <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; F K M )">$FKM$</span>  
which is equal to the given angle <span data-ref="0359.g1:&lt; E )">$E$</span>. <span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>
