Let the angle :g:`$KHG$ <0359.g1:< K H G )>` be added to each; therefore the angles :g:`$FKH$ <0359.g1:< F K H )>`, :g:`$KHG$ <0359.g1:< K H G )>` are equal to the angles :g:`$KHG$ <0359.g1:< K H G )>`, :g:`$GHM$ <0359.g1:< G H M )>`.

But the angles :g:`$FKH$ <0359.g1:< F K H )>`, :g:`$KHG$ <0359.g1:< K H G )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

therefore the angles :g:`$KHG$ <0359.g1:< K H G )>`, :g:`$GHM$ <0359.g1:< G H M )>` are also equal to two right angles.

Thus, with a straight line :g:`$GH$ <0359.g1:/ G H />`, and at the point :g:`$H$ <0359.g1:H>` on it, two straight lines :g:`$KH$ <0359.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HM$ <0359.g1:/ H M />` not lying on the same side make the adjacent angles equal to two right angles;

.. assertion:: [I. 14] <I.14>

   therefore :g:`$KH$ <0359.g1:/ K H />` is in a straight line with :g:`$HM$ <0359.g1:/ H M />`.

And, since the straight line :g:`$HG$ <0359.g1:/ H G />` falls upon the parallels :g:`$KM$ <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`$FG$ <0359.g1:/ F G />`, the alternate angles :g:`$MHG$ <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`$HGF$ <0359.g1:< H G F )>` are :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< M H G ) = 0359.g1:< H G F )>` to one another. :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

Let the angle :g:`$HGL$ <0359.g1:< H G L )>` be added to each; therefore the angles :g:`$MHG$ <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`$HGL$ <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are equal to the angles :g:`$HGF$ <0359.g1:< H G F )>`, :g:`$HGL$ <0359.g1:< H G L )>`. :just:`[C. N. 2] <I.cn.2>`

But the angles :g:`$MHG$ <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`$HGL$ <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

therefore the angles :g:`$HGF$ <0359.g1:< H G F )>`, :g:`$HGL$ <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are also equal to two right angles. :just:`[C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>`

.. assertion:: [I. 14] <I.14>

   Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0359.g1:/ F G />` is in a straight line with :g:`$GL$ <0359.g1:/ G L />`.

And, since :g:`$FK$ <0359.g1:/ F K />` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ F K / = 0359.g1:/ H G / = 0359.g1:/ M L />` and parallel to :g:`$HG$ <0359.g1:/ H G />`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

and :g:`$HG$ <0359.g1:/ H G />` to :g:`$ML$ <0359.g1:/ M L />` also, :g:`$KF$ <0359.g1:/ K F />` is also :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ K F / = 0359.g1:/ M L />` and parallel to :g:`$ML$ <0359.g1:/ M L />`; :just:`[C. N. 1 ; I. 30] <I.cn.1, I.30>`

and the straight lines :g:`$KM$ <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`$FL$ <0359.g1:/ F L />` join them (at their extremities); therefore :g:`$KM$ <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`$FL$ <0359.g1:/ F L />` are also :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ K M / = 0359.g1:/ F L />` and parallel. :just:`[I. 33] <I.33>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$KFLM$ <0359.g1:< K F L M >>` is a parallelogram.

And, since the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0359.g1:< A B D >>` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< A B D > = 0359.g1:< F K H G >; 0359.g1:< D B C > = 0359.g1:< G H M L >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$FH$ <0359.g1:< F K H G >>`, and :g:`$DBC$ <0359.g1:< D B C >>` to :g:`$GM$ <0359.g1:< G H M L >>`,

.. assertion::

   the whole rectilineal figure :g:`$ABCD$ <0359.g1:< A B C D >>` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< A B C D > = 0359.g1:< K F L M >>` to the whole parallelogram :g:`$KFLM$ <0359.g1:< K F L M >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the parallelogram :g:`$KFLM$ <0359.g1:< K F L M >>` has been constructed :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< K F L M > = 0359.g1:< A B C D >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`$ABCD$ <0359.g1:< A B C D >>`, in the angle :g:`$FKM$ <0359.g1:< F K M )>` which is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< F K M ) = 0359.g1:< E )>` to the given angle :g:`$E$ <0359.g1:< E )>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
