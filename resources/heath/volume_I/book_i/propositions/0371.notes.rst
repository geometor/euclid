Transcription Notes — Folio 0371 (Page 357)
===========================================

Summary
-------

Folio 0371 continues Heath's commentary on Book I, Proposition 47, discussing the Pythagorean and Platonic formulas for generating right-angled triangles in rational numbers, Cantor and Röth's conjecture based on *similar numbers* (ὅμοιοι ἀριθμοί) as defined in Theon of Smyrna, and the connection to Euclid X, Lemma 1 after Prop. 28.

Observations & Details
----------------------

- **Greek text**: ``ὅμοιοι ἀριθμοί`` ("similar numbers") is clearly printed with rough breathing on ``ὅμοιοι`` and smooth breathing on ``ἀριθμοί``.
- **Minor print artifacts**:

  - In the sentence "Since therefore 6 is to 3 as 4 is to 2, the numbers 6 and 24 are similar", the ``m`` in ``similar`` has a faint middle stroke making it look slightly like ``siinilar`` in the scan; transcribed as ``similar``.
  - In "As Cantor remarks, the form in which $c$ and $b$ appear correspond sufficiently closely...", Heath prints plural ``correspond`` (attracted to ``$c$ and $b$``); preserved verbatim as printed.

- **Page break**: The page ends mid-sentence with ``*to find two square numbers such that their*``, continuing on folio 0372 (page 358) with ``*sum is also a square.*``
