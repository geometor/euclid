Transcription Notes for Folio 0374 (Page 360)
=============================================

Overview
--------

- **Folio**: 0374 (Page 360)
- **Content**: Continuation of Heath's commentary on Book I, Proposition 47 (Euclid II. 8 confirmation of the Platonic formula; beginning of the section ``The “theorem of Pythagoras” in India.``).

Key Details & Observations
--------------------------

1. **Sanskrit Diacritics**:

   - Transcribed with full diacritics as printed in Heath's text: ``*Das Āpastamba-Śulba-Sūtra*``, ``*The Śulvasūtras*``, ``Śulvasūtras``, ``Bāudhāyana``, ``Āpastamba``, ``Kātyāyana``, and ``Āpastamba-Ś.-S.``.
   - Note that in the first occurrence of ``*Das Āpastamba-Śulba-Sūtra*`` (lines 20–21), the acute accent on ``Ś`` in ``Sulba`` is very faint/absent in the italic font across the line break, whereas in ``Āpastamba-Ś.-S.`` (line 28) and ``*The Śulvasūtras*`` / ``Śulvasūtras`` (lines 25–26) the ``Ś`` is clearly marked.

2. **Typographical Note (Unclosed Parenthesis)**:

   - At the bottom of the page, the parenthetical citation for Heinrich Vogt opens at ``(*Haben die alten Inder...`` and is never closed with a ``)`` in the original print (``...in the *Bibliotheca Mathematica*, VII_3, 1906, pp. 6—23. See also Cantor's *Geschichte der Mathematik*, I_3, pp. 635—645.``). This has been preserved verbatim as printed.

3. **Edition Subscripts**:

   - ``$\text{VII}_3$`` (3rd series of *Bibliotheca Mathematica*, Vol. VII) and ``$\text{I}_3$`` (3rd edition of Cantor's *Geschichte der Mathematik*, Vol. I) are formatted with subscripts matching the printed typography.

4. **Figures**:

   - ``0374.g1.svg``: Placed adjacent to the paragraph discussing Euclid II. 8, illustrating a square with both an outer and an inner single-row gnomon marked off.
