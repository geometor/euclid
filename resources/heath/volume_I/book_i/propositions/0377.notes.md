# Transcription Notes: Folio 0377 (Page 363)

## Overview
Folio 0377 (Page 363) continues Heath's commentary on Book I, Proposition 47, discussing Indian geometry in the *Śulvasūtras* (Āpastamba and Bāudhāyana), "rational rectangles" (Pythagorean triples), and Thibaut's explanation of the Indian approximation for $\sqrt{2}$.

## Typographical & Paleographical Observations
1. **Diacritics on Āpastamba and Bāudhāyana**:
   - Line 3 prints `Apastamba` without a macron over the initial `A`, whereas all subsequent occurrences on this page (lines 11, 13, and 30) print `Āpastamba`. Normalized to `Āpastamba` for consistency.
   - Line 12 prints `Bāudhāyana-Ś.-S.` with macrons on both `ā`s and an acute accent on `Ś`.
2. **Faint Print (`that`)**:
   - On line 22 ("there is nothing to show that the assumption"), the final `t` of `that` is faintly inked in the scan (`tha`), clearly reading `that`.
3. **Mathematical Notation**:
   - Thibaut's derivation of the *Śulvasūtra* approximation for $\sqrt{2}$ from $17^2 = 289 = 2 \cdot 12^2 + 1$ by subtracting a gnomon of breadth $\frac{1}{34}$ is rendered in LaTeX display math:
     $$\sqrt{2} = 1 + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3 \cdot 4} - \frac{1}{3 \cdot 4 \cdot 34}\text{, approximately.}$$
