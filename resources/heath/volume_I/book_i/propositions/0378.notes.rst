Transcription Notes: Folio 0378 (Page 364)
==========================================

Overview
--------

Folio 0378 (Page 364) concludes Heath's discussion of Indian geometry in the *Śulvasūtras* (Thibaut on Bāudhāyana's units of length, Indian circle-squaring approximations, Vogt's three stages for the discovery of the irrational, and the net conclusions regarding Bürk's papers) and begins the section **Alternative proofs** of Book I, Proposition 47 with the dissection proof of Thābit b. Qurra preserved by an-Nairīzī.

Typographical & Paleographical Observations
-------------------------------------------

1. **Decimal Point Convention**:

   - Heath's Cambridge University Press typography uses the British raised dot for decimal points in ``3·09`` and ``3·004``; these have been transcribed using standard decimal points (``\pi = 3.09`` and ``\pi = 3.004``).

2. **Diacritics**:

   - ``Bāudhāyana``, ``Āpastamba``, ``Bürk``, ``an-Nairīzī``, and ``Thābit b. Qurra`` are transcribed with their full diacritical marks as printed.

3. **Line Breaks in Thābit b. Qurra's Construction**:

   - At the bottom of page 364, the construction steps are set as short indented lines leading into the figure and continuation on page 365 (folio 0379).
