Transcription Notes for Folio 0379 (Page 365)
=============================================

Overview
--------

Folio 0379 continues Heath's commentary on Book I, Proposition 47:

1. Completion of the second proof by Thābit b. Qurra (9th century), accompanied by Figure 1 (``0379.g1.svg``).
2. Section ``II.``: Remark on deriving a proof of I. 47 as a special case of Pappus' extension.
3. Section ``III.``: Proof published by J. W. Müller (1819) and Ign. Hoffmann (1821), attributed to Leonardo da Vinci ("Lionardo da Vinci", 1452–1519), accompanied by Figure 2 (``0379.g2.svg``).

Editorial & Orthographic Details
--------------------------------

- **Lionardo da Vinci**: Heath's spelling ``Lionardo da Vinci (1452—1519)`` is preserved verbatim from the scan.
- **German Bibliographic Citations**: Preserved nineteenth-century German spelling (``theils bekannten theils neuen Beweisen``) and italics as printed.
- **Thābit b. Qurra**: Macron on ``ā`` preserved (``Thābit's``).
