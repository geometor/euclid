Transcription Notes for Folio 0380 (Page 366)
=============================================

Overview
--------

Folio 0380 continues Heath's commentary on Book I, Proposition 47:

1. Conclusion of Leonardo da Vinci's proof (``Hence the hexagons ADEFGC, ABCHLK are equal...``).
2. Section ``Pappus' extension of I. 47.``: Enunciation and proof from Pappus (*Collection* IV, p. 177), accompanied by Figure 1 (``0380.g1.svg``).
3. Section ``Heron's proof that AL, BK, CF in Euclid's figure meet in a point.``: Opening paragraph quoting Proclus (p. 429, 9–15), ending mid-sentence (``These words cannot``) at the bottom of page 366.

Editorial & Typographical Details
---------------------------------

- In Pappus' enunciation, Heath prints the geometric labels ($ABC$, $ABED$, etc.) in upright roman type inside the italicized enunciation block; these are rendered in standard math mode ``$ABC$`` within the italicized statement.
- Notice that in the enunciation Heath writes ``sum of the angles BAC, DHB`` while near the end of the proof he writes ``sum of the angles BAC, BHD``; both are preserved exactly as printed.
