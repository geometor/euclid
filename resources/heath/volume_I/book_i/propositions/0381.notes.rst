Transcription Notes: Folio 0381 (Page 367)
==========================================

Summary
-------

- Transcribed page 367 of Heath's Volume I, continuing the commentary on Book I, Proposition 47.
- Covers Heron's three lemmas preserved in the commentary of an-Nairīzī (pp. 175–185, ed. Besthorn-Heiberg; pp. 78–84, ed. Curtze) used to prove that $AL, FC, BK$ in Euclid's diagram for I. 47 are concurrent, using only the principles of Book I:

  1. *First lemma*: A line from the vertex of a triangle to the midpoint of the base bisects any line parallel to the base (``0381.g1.svg``).
  2. *Second lemma*: Extension of the first lemma to the case where $DE$ meets $BA, CA$ produced beyond $A$ (shown in ``0381.g1.svg`` with $D', G', E'$).
  3. *Third lemma*: Converse of I. 43 (``0381.g2.svg``).
