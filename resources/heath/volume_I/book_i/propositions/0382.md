Heron now proceeds to prove the proposition that, in the accompanying figure, if <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A K L /">$AKL$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> meet <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> in <span data-ref="0382.g1:M">$M$</span>, and if <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> be joined,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are in one straight line.

![Folio 0382 Figure 1 - Proposition 47 Commentary (Heron Concurrency Proof)](0382.g1.svg)

Parallelograms are completed as shown in the figure, and the diagonals <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ O A /">$OA$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> of the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F H &gt;">$FH$</span> are drawn.

Then the triangles <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F A H &gt;">$FAH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; B A C &gt;">$BAC$</span> are clearly equal in all respects;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; H F A )">$HFA$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; A B C )">$ABC$</span>, and therefore to the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; C A K )">$CAK$</span> (since <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is perpendicular to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>).

But, the diagonals of the rectangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F H &gt;">$FH$</span> cutting one another in <span data-ref="0382.g1:Y">$Y$</span>,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ F Y /">$FY$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ Y A /">$YA$</span>,
and the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; H F A )">$HFA$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; O A F )">$OAF$</span>.

Therefore the angles <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; O A F )">$OAF$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; C A K )">$CAK$</span> are equal, and accordingly
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ O A /">$OA$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> are in a straight line.

Hence <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ O M /">$OM$</span> is the diagonal of <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; S Q &gt;">$SQ$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; A S &gt;">$AS$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; A Q &gt;">$AQ$</span>,
and, if we add <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; A M &gt;">$AM$</span> to each,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F M &gt;">$FM$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; M H &gt;">$MH$</span>.

But, since <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> is the diagonal of the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F N &gt;">$FN$</span>,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; F M &gt;">$FM$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; M N &gt;">$MN$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; M H &gt;">$MH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:&lt; M N &gt;">$MN$</span>;
and, by the third lemma, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are in a straight line.

### PROPOSITION 48.

<p class="enunciation">If in a triangle the square on one of the sides be equal to<br>
the squares on the remaining two sides of the triangle, the<br>
angle contained by the remaining two sides of the triangle is<br>
right.</p>

For in the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g2:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> let the square on one side <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be equal to the squares on the sides <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;

I say that the angle <span data-ref="0382.g2:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span> is right.

![Folio 0382 Figure 2 - Proposition 48](0382.g2.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0382.g2:A">$A$</span> at right angles to the straight line <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, let <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be joined.

Since <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,  
the square on <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0382.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.
