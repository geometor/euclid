## Commentary Highlights
- **Connection to I. 47**: Heath points out that the identity $BC^2 = BL + CL = BC \cdot BD + BC \cdot DC$ was already used geometrically in the shearing proof of Pythagoras (I. 47), but Book II gives it its independent algebraic status.
