### PROPOSITION 4.

<p class="enunciation">If a straight line be cut at random, the square on the whole<br />
is equal to the squares on the segments and twice the rectangle<br />
contained by the segments.</p>

For let the straight line <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut at random at <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>;  
<sup>5</sup> I say that the square on <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B C /">$CB$</span> and twice the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B C /">$CB$</span>.

![Diagram](0393.g1.svg)

For let the square <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; A D E B &gt;">$ADEB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 46]</span>  
<sup>10</sup> let <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined;  
through <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> let <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> be drawn parallel to either <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> or <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B E /">$EB$</span>,  
and through <span data-ref="0393.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be drawn parallel to either <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> or <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>

<sup>15</sup> Then, since <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> has fallen on them,  
the exterior angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; C G B )">$CGB$</span> is equal to the interior and opposite angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; A D B )">$ADB$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>

But the angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; A D B )">$ADB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; A B D )">$ABD$</span>,  
<sup>20</sup> since the side <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 5]</span>  
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; C G B )">$CGB$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0393.g1:&lt; G B C )">$GBC$</span>,  
so that the side <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also equal to the side <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 6]</span>
