# Transcription Notes: Folio 0395 (Page 381)

## Summary
- **Content**: Heath's commentary on Proposition II. 4 (complete on this folio).
- **Folio Continuity**: Folio is entirely dedicated to commentary on II. 4, analyzing Theon's alternate proof (ἄλλως), the spurious interpolated Porism on squares about the diameter, the semi-algebraical proof via II. 2 and II. 3, and the geometric visual demonstration of $(a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2$.

## Commentary Highlights
- **Theon's Abbreviation**: Heath notes Theon's shorter proof that $CGKB$ is a square by deducing it directly from one right angle ($\angle CBK$) rather than proving all four angles right as Euclid does in I. 46.
- **Interpolated Porism**: Rejects the Greek text's Porism (*"in square areas the parallelograms about the diameter are squares"*) based on papyrus evidence (Heiberg, *Hermes* 1903) and Proclus' testimony.
- **Semi-Algebraic Derivation**: Traces the deduction of II. 4 from II. 2 and II. 3 found in Clavius: $(x+y)^2 = (x+y)x + (x+y)y = (x^2 + xy) + (y^2 + xy) = x^2 + y^2 + 2xy$.
