For let [the straight line] <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be produced in a straight line [with <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>], and let <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;  
let the square <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A E F D &gt;">$AEFD$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, and let the figure be drawn double.

![Diagram](0404.g1.svg)

Then, since <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, while <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span>,  
therefore <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span>.

For the same reason  
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0404.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span>,  
therefore <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; K D &gt;">$KD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; G R &gt;">$GR$</span> to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R N &gt;">$RN$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 36]</span>

But <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R N &gt;">$RN$</span>, for they are complements of the parallelogram <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C P &gt;">$CP$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span>  
therefore <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; K D &gt;">$KD$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; G R &gt;">$GR$</span>;  
therefore the four areas <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; D K &gt;">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; G R &gt;">$GR$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R N &gt;">$RN$</span> are equal to one another.

<p class="assertion">Therefore the four are quadruple of <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span>.</p>

Again, since <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,  
while <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, that is <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>, that is <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G Q /">$GQ$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G Q /">$GQ$</span>.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ G Q /">$GQ$</span>, and <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span> to <span data-ref="0404.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>,  
<span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; M Q &gt;">$MQ$</span>, and <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; Q L &gt;">$QL$</span> to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R F &gt;">$RF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 36]</span>

But <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; M Q &gt;">$MQ$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; Q L &gt;">$QL$</span>, for they are complements of the parallelogram <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; M L &gt;">$ML$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span>  
therefore <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R F &gt;">$RF$</span>;  
therefore the four areas <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; M Q &gt;">$MQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; Q L &gt;">$QL$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R F &gt;">$RF$</span> are equal to one another.

<p class="assertion">Therefore the four are quadruple of <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span>.</p>

But the four areas <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; K D &gt;">$KD$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; G R &gt;">$GR$</span>, <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; R N &gt;">$RN$</span> were proved to be quadruple of <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; C K &gt;">$CK$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the eight areas, which contain the gnomon <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; S T U &gt;">$STU$</span>, are quadruple of <span data-ref="0404.g1:&lt; A K &gt;">$AK$</span>.</p>
