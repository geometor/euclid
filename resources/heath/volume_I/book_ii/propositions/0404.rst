For let [the straight line] :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` be produced in a straight line [with :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`], and let :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` be made equal to :g:`$CB$ <0404.g1:/ C B />`; let the square :g:`$AEFD$ <0404.g1:< A E F D >>` be described on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />`, and let the figure be drawn double.

.. picture:: 0404.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

Then, since :g:`$CB$ <0404.g1:/ C B />` is equal to :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />`, while :g:`$CB$ <0404.g1:/ C B />` is equal to :g:`$GK$ <0404.g1:/ G K />`, and :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` to :g:`$KN$ <0404.g1:/ K N />`, therefore :g:`$GK$ <0404.g1:/ G K />` is also equal to :g:`$KN$ <0404.g1:/ K N />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$QR$ <0404.g1:/ Q R />` is also equal to :g:`$RP$ <0404.g1:/ R P />`.

And, since :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` is equal to :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />`, and :g:`$GK$ <0404.g1:/ G K />` to :g:`$KN$ <0404.g1:/ K N />`, therefore :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>` is also equal to :g:`$KD$ <0404.g1:< K D >>`, and :g:`$GR$ <0404.g1:< G R >>` to :g:`$RN$ <0404.g1:< R N >>`. :just:`[I. 36] <I.36>`

But :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>` is equal to :g:`$RN$ <0404.g1:< R N >>`, for they are complements of the parallelogram :g:`$CP$ <0404.g1:< C P >>`; :just:`[I. 43] <I.43>`

therefore :g:`$KD$ <0404.g1:< K D >>` is also equal to :g:`$GR$ <0404.g1:< G R >>`; therefore the four areas :g:`$DK$ <0404.g1:< D K >>`, :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>`, :g:`$GR$ <0404.g1:< G R >>`, :g:`$RN$ <0404.g1:< R N >>` are equal to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the four are quadruple of :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>`.

Again, since :g:`$CB$ <0404.g1:/ C B />` is equal to :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />`, while :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` is equal to :g:`$BK$ <0404.g1:/ B K />`, that is :g:`$CG$ <0404.g1:/ C G />`, and :g:`$CB$ <0404.g1:/ C B />` is equal to :g:`$GK$ <0404.g1:/ G K />`, that is :g:`$GQ$ <0404.g1:/ G Q />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0404.g1:/ C G />` is also equal to :g:`$GQ$ <0404.g1:/ G Q />`.

And, since :g:`$CG$ <0404.g1:/ C G />` is equal to :g:`$GQ$ <0404.g1:/ G Q />`, and :g:`$QR$ <0404.g1:/ Q R />` to :g:`$RP$ <0404.g1:/ R P />`, :g:`$AG$ <0404.g1:< A G >>` is also equal to :g:`$MQ$ <0404.g1:< M Q >>`, and :g:`$QL$ <0404.g1:< Q L >>` to :g:`$RF$ <0404.g1:< R F >>`. :just:`[I. 36] <I.36>`

But :g:`$MQ$ <0404.g1:< M Q >>` is equal to :g:`$QL$ <0404.g1:< Q L >>`, for they are complements of the parallelogram :g:`$ML$ <0404.g1:< M L >>`; :just:`[I. 43] <I.43>`

therefore :g:`$AG$ <0404.g1:< A G >>` is also equal to :g:`$RF$ <0404.g1:< R F >>`; therefore the four areas :g:`$AG$ <0404.g1:< A G >>`, :g:`$MQ$ <0404.g1:< M Q >>`, :g:`$QL$ <0404.g1:< Q L >>`, :g:`$RF$ <0404.g1:< R F >>` are equal to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the four are quadruple of :g:`$AG$ <0404.g1:< A G >>`.

But the four areas :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>`, :g:`$KD$ <0404.g1:< K D >>`, :g:`$GR$ <0404.g1:< G R >>`, :g:`$RN$ <0404.g1:< R N >>` were proved to be quadruple of :g:`$CK$ <0404.g1:< C K >>`;

.. assertion::
