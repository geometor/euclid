.. assertion::

   therefore four times the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$STU$ <0404.g1:< S T U >>`.

Let :g:`$OH$ <0404.g1:< O H >>`, which is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />`, be added to each;

.. assertion::

   therefore four times the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` together with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$STU$ <0404.g1:< S T U >>` and :g:`$OH$ <0404.g1:< O H >>`.

But the gnomon :g:`$STU$ <0404.g1:< S T U >>` and :g:`$OH$ <0404.g1:< O H >>` are the whole square :g:`$AEFD$ <0404.g1:< A E F D >>`, which is described on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore four times the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` together with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />`.

But :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` is equal to :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore four times the rectangle contained by :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />`, that is to the square described on :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />` and :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` as on one straight line.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

This proposition is quoted by :person:`Pappus <pappus>` (p. 428, ed. :person:`Hultsch <hultsch>`) and is used also by :person:`Euclid <euclid>` himself in the *Data*, Prop. 86. Further, it is of decided use in proving the fundamental property of a parabola.

Two alternative proofs are worth giving.

The first is that suggested by the consideration mentioned in the last note, though the proof is old enough, being given by :person:`Clavius <clavius>` and others. It is of the semi-algebraical type.

Produce :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$D$ <0404.g1:D>` (in the figure of the proposition), so that :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` is equal to :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />`.

By II. 4, the square on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />` and twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BD$ <0404.g1:/ B D />`, i.e. to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` and twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />`.

By II. 7, the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` are equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />`.

Therefore the square on :g:`$AD$ <0404.g1:/ A D />` is equal to four times the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0404.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0404.g1:/ B C />` together with the square on :g:`$AC$ <0404.g1:/ A C />`.

The second proof is after the manner of :person:`Euclid <euclid>` but with a difference. Produce :g:`$BA$ <0405.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>` so that :g:`$AD$ <0405.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$BC$ <0405.g1:/ B C />`. On :g:`$BD$ <0405.g1:/ B D />` construct the square :g:`$BEFD$ <0405.g1:< B E F D >>`.
