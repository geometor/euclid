### PROPOSITION 9.

<p class="enunciation">If a straight line be cut into equal and unequal segments,<br />
the squares on the unequal segments of the whole are double<br />
of the square on the half and of the square on the straight line<br />
between the points of section.</p>

For let a straight line <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut into equal segments at <span data-ref="0406.g1:C">$C$</span>, and into unequal segments at <span data-ref="0406.g1:D">$D$</span>;  
I say that the squares on <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> are double of the squares on <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0406.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, and let it be made equal to either <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> or <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;  
let <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> be joined,  
let <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0406.g1:D">$D$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> through <span data-ref="0406.g1:F">$F$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,  
and let <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> be joined.

![Diagram](0406.g1.svg)

Then, since <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0406.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>,  
the angle <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; E A C )">$EAC$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; A E C )">$AEC$</span>.

And, since the angle at <span data-ref="0406.g1:C">$C$</span> is right,  
the remaining angles <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; E A C )">$EAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; A E C )">$AEC$</span> are equal to one right angle. <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span>

And they are equal;  
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; C E A )">$CEA$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; C A E )">$CAE$</span> is half a right angle.</p>

For the same reason  
each of the angles <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; C E B )">$CEB$</span>, <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; E B C )">$EBC$</span> is also half a right angle;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole angle <span data-ref="0406.g1:&lt; A E B )">$AEB$</span> is right.</p>
