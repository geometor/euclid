### PROPOSITION 10.

<p class="enunciation">If a straight line be bisected, and a straight line be added<br />
to it in a straight line, the square on the whole with the added<br />
straight line and the square on the added straight line both<br />
together are double of the square on the half and of the square<br />
described on the straight line made up of the half and the<br />
added straight line as on one straight line.</p>
