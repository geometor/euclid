the angles <span data-ref="0409.g1:< C E F )">$CEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E F D )">$EFD$</span> are equal to two right angles; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
therefore the angles <span data-ref="0409.g1:< F E B )">$FEB$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E F D )">$EFD$</span> are less than two right angles.

But straight lines produced from angles less than two right angles meet; <span class="justification">[I. Post. 5]</span>  
therefore <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, if produced in the direction <span data-ref="0409.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:D">$D$</span>, will meet.

Let them be produced and meet at <span data-ref="0409.g1:G">$G$</span>,  
and let <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>,  
the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E A C )">$EAC$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< A E C )">$AEC$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 5]</span>  
and the angle at <span data-ref="0409.g1:< A C E )">$C$</span> is right;  
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E A C )">$EAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:< A E C )">$AEC$</span> is half a right angle. <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>

For the same reason  
each of the angles <span data-ref="0409.g1:< C E B )">$CEB$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span> is also half a right angle;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< A E B )">$AEB$</span> is right.</p>

And, since the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span> is half a right angle,  
the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D B G )">$DBG$</span> is also half a right angle. <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span>

But the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< B D G )">$BDG$</span> is also right,  
for it is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D C E )">$DCE$</span>, they being alternate; <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span>  
<p class="sub-clause">therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D G B )">$DGB$</span> is half a right angle; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D G B )">$DGB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D B G )">$DBG$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">so that the side <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is also equal to the side <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 6]</span></p>

Again, since the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E G F )">$EGF$</span> is half a right angle,  
and the angle at <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E F G )">$F$</span> is right, for it is equal to the opposite angle, the angle at <span data-ref="0409.g1:< D C E )">$C$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span>  
<p class="sub-clause">the remaining angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< F E G )">$FEG$</span> is half a right angle; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< E G F )">$EGF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0409.g1:< F E G )">$FEG$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">so that the side <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is also equal to the side <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 6]</span></p>

Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>,  
the squares on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> are double of the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0409.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. <span class="justification">[C. N. 1]</span></p>
