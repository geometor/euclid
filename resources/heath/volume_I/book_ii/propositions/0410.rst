:title: Proposition 10 (continued)

the angles :g:`$CEF$ <0409.g1:< C E F )>`, :g:`$EFD$ <0409.g1:< E F D )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

therefore the angles :g:`$FEB$ <0409.g1:< F E B )>`, :g:`$EFD$ <0409.g1:< E F D )>` are less than two right angles.

But straight lines produced from angles less than two right angles meet; :just:`[I. Post. 5] <I.post.5>`

therefore :g:`$EB$ <0409.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$FD$ <0409.g1:/ F D />`, if produced in the direction :g:`$B$ <0409.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0409.g1:D>`, will meet.

Let them be produced and meet at :g:`$G$ <0409.g1:G>`, and let :g:`$AG$ <0409.g1:/ A G />` be joined.

Then, since :g:`$AC$ <0409.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$CE$ <0409.g1:/ C E />`, the angle :g:`$EAC$ <0409.g1:< E A C )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$AEC$ <0409.g1:< A E C )>`; :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

and the angle at :g:`$C$ <0409.g1:< A C E )>` is right;

.. assertion:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore each of the angles :g:`$EAC$ <0409.g1:< E A C )>`, :g:`$AEC$ <0409.g1:< A E C )>` is half a right angle.

For the same reason each of the angles :g:`$CEB$ <0409.g1:< C E B )>`, :g:`$EBC$ <0409.g1:< E B C )>` is also half a right angle;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0409.g1:< A E B )>` is right.

And, since the angle :g:`$EBC$ <0409.g1:< E B C )>` is half a right angle, the angle :g:`$DBG$ <0409.g1:< D B G )>` is also half a right angle. :just:`[I. 15] <I.15>`

But the angle :g:`$BDG$ <0409.g1:< B D G )>` is also right, for it is equal to the angle :g:`$DCE$ <0409.g1:< D C E )>`, they being alternate; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. subclause:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$DGB$ <0409.g1:< D G B )>` is half a right angle;

therefore the angle :g:`$DGB$ <0409.g1:< D G B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DBG$ <0409.g1:< D B G )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 6] <I.6>

   so that the side :g:`$BD$ <0409.g1:/ B D />` is also equal to the side :g:`$GD$ <0409.g1:/ G D />`.

Again, since the angle :g:`$EGF$ <0409.g1:< E G F )>` is half a right angle, and the angle at :g:`$F$ <0409.g1:< E F G )>` is right, for it is equal to the opposite angle, the angle at :g:`$C$ <0409.g1:< D C E )>`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. subclause:: [I. 32] <I.32>

   the remaining angle :g:`$FEG$ <0409.g1:< F E G )>` is half a right angle;

therefore the angle :g:`$EGF$ <0409.g1:< E G F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$FEG$ <0409.g1:< F E G )>`,

.. assertion:: [I. 6] <I.6>

   so that the side :g:`$GF$ <0409.g1:/ G F />` is also equal to the side :g:`$EF$ <0409.g1:/ E F />`.

Now, since the square on :g:`$EC$ <0409.g1:/ E C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$CA$ <0409.g1:/ C A />`, the squares on :g:`$EC$ <0409.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0409.g1:/ C A />` are double of the square on :g:`$CA$ <0409.g1:/ C A />`.

But the square on :g:`$EA$ <0409.g1:/ E A />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$EC$ <0409.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0409.g1:/ C A />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion:: [C. N. 1] <I.cn.1>

   therefore the square on :g:`$EA$ <0409.g1:/ E A />` is double of the square on :g:`$AC$ <0409.g1:/ A C />`.
