## Summary

- **Side and Diagonal Numbers**: Connects Euclid II. 9 ($AD^2 + DB^2 = 2AC^2 + 2CD^2$) with $CD = x, DB = y$ to the algebraic identity $(2x+y)^2 - 2(x+y)^2 = 2x^2 - y^2$, showing that if $(x, y)$ satisfies $2x^2 - y^2 = \pm 1$, then $(x+y, 2x+y)$ generates the next solution with alternating sign.
