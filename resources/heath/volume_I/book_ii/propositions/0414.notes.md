## Commentary Highlights
- **Proclus' Graphic Proof via II. 10**: Uses II. 10 ($AD^2 + DC^2 = 2AB^2 + 2BD^2$) with $DC^2 = 2AB^2$ to prove $AD^2 = 2BD^2$, showing that $AD = 2a + d$ is the exact diagonal for side $BD = a + d$.
- **Editorial Analysis**: Discusses Proclus' phrasing *ἀπ’ ἐκείνου* ("by him" / "in the second Book of the Elements") and affirms that the substance of Book II is Pythagorean in origin.
