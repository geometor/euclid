# Transcription Notes: Folio 0415 (Page 401)

## Summary
- **Content**: Heath's commentary on Proposition II. 10 (concluded), containing P. Bergh's geometric figure (`0415.g1.png`) and the important deductions from II. 9 and II. 10: Apollonius' Theorem on the median of a triangle (proved via Pappus with diagrams `0415.g2.png` and `0415.g3.png`) and the Parallelogram Law.
- **Folio Continuity**: Concludes the commentary essay on II. 9 and II. 10. Precedes Proposition 11 (the Golden Section), which opens on Folio 0416 (page 402).

## Commentary Highlights
- **Bergh's Geometric Generation**: Illustrates with `0415.g1.png` how right isosceles triangle expansion yields the recurrence $a_n = a_{n-1} + d_{n-1}, d_n = 2a_{n-1} + d_{n-1}$.
- **Apollonius' Median Theorem**: Quotes Pappus' proof that in any $\triangle ABC$ with median $AD$, $AB^2 + AC^2 = 2(BD^2 + AD^2)$ directly using II. 9 and II. 10 on the base segments $BE, EC$.
- **Parallelogram Law**: Records the early derivation by Gregory of St. Vincent (1657) and Viviani (1692) that the sum of the squares on the diagonals of a parallelogram equals the sum of the squares on the sides ($d_1^2 + d_2^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2)$).
