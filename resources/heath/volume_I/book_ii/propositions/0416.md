### PROPOSITION 11.

<p class="enunciation">To cut a given straight line so that the rectangle contained<br />
by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the square on<br />
the remaining segment.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the given straight line;  
thus it is required to cut <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> so that the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the square on the remaining segment.

![0416.g1](0416.g1.svg)

For let the square <span data-ref="0416.g1:&lt; A B D C &gt;">$ABDC$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 46]</span>  
let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be bisected at the point <span data-ref="0416.g1:E">$E$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be joined;  
let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> be drawn through to <span data-ref="0416.g1:F">$F$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>;  
let the square <span data-ref="0416.g1:&lt; F H &gt;">$FH$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be drawn through to <span data-ref="0416.g1:K">$K$</span>.

I say that <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has been cut at <span data-ref="0416.g1:H">$H$</span> so as to make the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>.

For, since the straight line <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> has been bisected at <span data-ref="0416.g1:E">$E$</span>, and <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is added to it,  
<p class="assertion">the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 6]</span></p>

But <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>.</p>

But the squares on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, for the angle at <span data-ref="0416.g1:&lt; B A E )">$A$</span> is right: <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>.</p>

Let the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be subtracted from each;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> which remains is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0416.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.</p>
