Transcription Notes: Folio 0419 (Page 405)
==========================================

Summary
-------

- **Content**: Heath's commentary on Proposition II. 12 (continued), presenting Gregory of St. Vincent's windmill proof of II. 12 (``0419.g1.svg``) and Heron of Alexandria's converse proof (``0419.g2.svg``), which concludes on Folio 0420.
- **Folio Continuity**: Carries over commentary from Folio 0418. Establishes the obtuse triangle Law of Cosines via sheared triangles as in I. 47, introduces Heron's converse theorem (*"if $a^2 > b^2 + c^2$, the angle is obtuse"*), and breaks mid-proof into Folio 0420.

Commentary Highlights
---------------------

- **Gregory of St. Vincent's Proof (1647)**: Employs the windmill figure of I. 47 on obtuse triangle $\triangle ABC$, proving rectangle $BP = BR$ and $CP = CQ$, while $AQ = AR$, yielding $BC^2 = BA^2 + AC^2 + 2(BA \cdot AN)$.
- **Anticipation of III. 35**: Observes that $BA \cdot AN = CA \cdot AM$ anticipates the intersecting chords theorem of Book III.
- **Heron's Converse**: Proves that if $BC^2 > BA^2 + AC^2$, then $\angle BAC > 90^\circ$ by constructing auxiliary right triangle $\triangle DAC$ with $DA = BA$ and applying I. 25.
