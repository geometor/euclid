## Commentary Highlights
- **Generalization of II. 13**: Cites Greek scholiast, Campanus, and Clavius showing that II. 13 applies to any acute angle in any triangle, not merely triangles all of whose angles are acute.
- **Heron's Converse**: Notes Heron's formulation: *"If $a^2 < b^2 + c^2$, the angle contained by $b$ and $c$ is acute."*
- **Gregory of St. Vincent's Proof**: Extends the windmill figure of I. 47 to acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ (`0421.g2.png`), drawing perpendiculars $ALP, BMQ, CNR$ across the three squares.
