Commentary Highlights
---------------------

- **Windmill Proof for Obtuse Triangle with Acute Projection**: Demonstrates that when $\triangle ABC$ is obtuse at $A$, the acute projection on side $BC$ yields $CA^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 - 2(CB \cdot BL)$.
- **Heron's Converse Proof**: Constructs right triangle $\triangle DBC$ with leg $DB = AB$ and leg $BC$ common. By I. 47, $DC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 > AC^2 \implies DC > AC \implies \angle DBC > \angle ABC \implies \angle ABC < 90^\circ$ (acute).
