### PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">To construct a square equal to a given rectilineal figure.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> be the given rectilineal figure;  
thus it is required to construct a square equal to the rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span>.

![Diagram](0423.g1.svg)

<sup>5</sup> For let there be constructed the rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0423.g1:< B D E C >">$BD$</span> equal to the rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 45]</span>

Then, if <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, that which was enjoined will have been done; for a square <span data-ref="0423.g1:< B D E C >">$BD$</span> has been constructed equal to the rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span>.

<sup>10</sup> But, if not, one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is greater.  
Let <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be greater, and let it be produced to <span data-ref="0423.g1:F">$F$</span>;  
let <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0423.g1:G">$G$</span>.

With centre <span data-ref="0423.g1:G">$G$</span> and distance one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> let the semicircle <span data-ref="0423.g1:( B H F )">$BHF$</span> be described; let <span data-ref="0423.g1:[ D E ]">$DE$</span> be produced <sup>15</sup> to <span data-ref="0423.g1:H">$H$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be joined.

Then, since the straight line <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> has been cut into equal segments at <span data-ref="0423.g1:G">$G$</span>, and into unequal segments at <span data-ref="0423.g1:E">$E$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 5]</span></p>

<sup>20</sup> But <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.</p>

But the squares on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>  
<sup>25</sup> <p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.</p>
