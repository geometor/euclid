PROPOSITION 14.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To construct a square equal to a given rectilineal figure.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0423.g1:A>` be the given rectilineal figure;
| thus it is required to construct a square equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`$A$ <0423.g1:A>`.

.. picture:: 0423.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

:line:`5` For let there be constructed the rectangular parallelogram :g:`$BD$ <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`$A$ <0423.g1:A>`. :just:`[I. 45] <I.45>`

Then, if :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />` is equal to :g:`$ED$ <0423.g1:/ E D />`, that which was enjoined will have been done; for a square :g:`$BD$ <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` has been constructed equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`$A$ <0423.g1:A>`.

| :line:`10` But, if not, one of the straight lines :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0423.g1:/ E D />` is greater.
| Let :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />` be greater, and let it be produced to :g:`$F$ <0423.g1:F>`;
| let :g:`$EF$ <0423.g1:/ E F />` be made equal to :g:`$ED$ <0423.g1:/ E D />`, and let :g:`$BF$ <0423.g1:/ B F />` be bisected at :g:`$G$ <0423.g1:G>`.

With centre :g:`$G$ <0423.g1:G>` and distance one of the straight lines :g:`$GB$ <0423.g1:/ G B />`, :g:`$GF$ <0423.g1:/ G F />` let the semicircle :g:`$BHF$ <0423.g1:( B H F )>` be described; let :g:`$DE$ <0423.g1:[ D E ]>` be produced :line:`15` to :g:`$H$ <0423.g1:H>`, and let :g:`$GH$ <0423.g1:/ G H />` be joined.

Then, since the straight line :g:`$BF$ <0423.g1:/ B F />` has been cut into equal segments at :g:`$G$ <0423.g1:G>`, and into unequal segments at :g:`$E$ <0423.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [II. 5] <II.5>

   the rectangle contained by :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`$EG$ <0423.g1:/ E G />` is equal to the square on :g:`$GF$ <0423.g1:/ G F />`.

:line:`20` But :g:`$GF$ <0423.g1:/ G F />` is equal to :g:`$GH$ <0423.g1:/ G H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`$GE$ <0423.g1:/ G E />` is equal to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0423.g1:/ G H />`.

| But the squares on :g:`$HE$ <0423.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0423.g1:/ E G />` are equal to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0423.g1:/ G H />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`
| :line:`25`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$BE$ <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`$GE$ <0423.g1:/ G E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$HE$ <0423.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0423.g1:/ E G />`.
