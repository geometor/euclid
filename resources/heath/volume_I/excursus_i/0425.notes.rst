Textual, Lexical & Historical Observations
------------------------------------------

- **αὐτὸς ἔφα** (*Ipse dixit*): Heath opens Excursus I by noting the historiographical difficulty of separating Pythagoras's personal discoveries from those of his school, citing the classic Pythagorean formula of appeal to the Master's authority.
- **Hippasus & Oral Tradition**: Summarizes the two competing traditions regarding Hippasus's transgression (publishing the inscription of the dodecahedron in the sphere vs. disclosing incommensurability/irrationality) and argues that the lack of written treatises prior to Philolaus reflects oral instruction rather than a formal oath of secrecy regarding mathematics.
- **Second Edition Addition (Vogt & Junge)**: This Excursus was added for the second edition to engage with Heinrich Vogt's two landmark studies in *Bibliotheca Mathematica* ($\text{IX}_3$, 1908/9, pp. 15–54; $\text{X}_3$, 1910, pp. 97–155) following G. Junge's 1907 paper (previously cited on p. 351).
