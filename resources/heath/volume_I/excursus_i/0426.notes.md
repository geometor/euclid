## Textual, Lexical & Historical Observations
- **Syntax of Proclus p. 426, 6–9**: Heath refutes Vogt's rendering of Proclus's testimony on I. 47 by pointing out the Greek participial syntax: without the article τῶν before ἀναπεμπόντων, the genitive participles ἀναπεμπόντων and λεγόντων cannot denote an earlier generation cited by τῶν...βουλομένων, but are attracted into the genitive from an understood accusative τινὰς (object of εὑρεῖν), confirming Paul Tannery's translation (*La Géométrie grecque*, p. 103).
- **Diels' Emendation of Proclus p. 65, 19**: Heath accepts Hermann Diels' emendation of τῶν ἀλόγων πραγματεία ("treatise on irrationals") to τῶν ἀνὰ λόγον πραγματεία ("theory of proportionals") in the Eudemian summary.
- **Plato's *Theaetetus* 147 D**: Heath notes that Theodorus's demonstration of irrationality starts at $\sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{5}$ up to $\sqrt{17}$, omitting $\sqrt{2}$ because the case of $\sqrt{2}$ was already familiar prior to Theodorus.
