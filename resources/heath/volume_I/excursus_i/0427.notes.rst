Textual, Lexical & Historical Observations
------------------------------------------

- **ἄρρητος καὶ ῥητὴ διάμετρος τῆς πεμπάδος**: Refers to Plato's *Republic* VIII, 546 C, where 7 is the "rational diameter of 5" (since $7^2 = 49 = 2 \cdot 5^2 - 1$) as a rational approximation to $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$, the "irrational diameter of 5".
- **Democritus's περὶ ἀλόγων γραμμῶν καὶ ναστῶν**: Heath critiques Vogt's claim that ἀλόγων in Democritus's title meant "without ratio" ("verhältnislos", $\infty/\infty$) due to atomic lines, citing the scholium to Aristotle's *De caelo* (p. 469 b 14, Brandis) and Simplicius to show that Democritus distinguished physical atoms from mathematical divisibility *ad infinitum* (unlike Xenocrates' indivisible lines).
- **ἡ τῶν κοσμικῶν σχημάτων σύστασις (Proclus p. 65, 20)**: Distinguishes between the full stereometric constructions and proofs of Book XIII (attributable to Theaetetus, cf. Suidas: πρῶτος δὲ τὰ πέντε καλούμενα στερεὰ ἔγραψε) and the earlier Pythagorean "putting together" (σύστασις) of regular plane polygons around a solid angle as in Plato's *Timaeus*.
