- **Dulcarnon (I. 47)**: Etymology traced via Skeat to Persian/Arabic *du 'lkarnayn* ("two-horned"), quoted in Chaucer's *Troilus and Criseyde* (III, ll. 930–3) and Billingsley's 1570 Euclid.
