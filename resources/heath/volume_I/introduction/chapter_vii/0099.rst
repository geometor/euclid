found fully noticed by :person:`Steinschneider <steinschneider>`\ [#f85_1]_. I shall mention here the commentators etc. referred to in the :cite:`*Fihrist* <ibn-al-nadim:fihrist>`, with a few others.

1. Abū 'l 'Abbās al-Faḍl b. Ḥātim :person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>` (born at Nairīz, died about 922) has already been mentioned\ [#f85_2]_. His commentary survives, as regards Books I.—VI., in the Codex Leidensis 399, 1, now edited, as to four Books, by :person:`Besthorn <besthorn>` and :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>`, and as regards Books I.—X. in the Latin translation made by :person:`Gherard of Cremona <gherard-of-cremona>` in the 12th c. and now published by :person:`Curtze <curtze>` from a Cracow MS.\ [#f85_3]_ Its importance lies mainly in the quotations from :person:`Heron <heron>` and :person:`Simplicius <simplicius>`.

2. Aḥmad b. 'Umar al-Karābīsī (date uncertain, probably 9th—10th c.), "who was among the most distinguished geometers and arithmeticians\ [#f85_4]_."

3. Al-'Abbās b. Sa'īd al-Jauharī (fl. 830) was one of the astronomical observers under al-Ma'mūn, but devoted himself mostly to geometry. He wrote a commentary to the whole of the *Elements*, from the beginning to the end; also the "Book of the propositions which he added to the first book of :person:`Euclid <euclid>`\ [#f85_5]_."

4. Muḥ. b. 'Īsā Abū 'Abdallāh al-Māhānī (d. between 874 and 884) wrote, according to the :cite:`*Fihrist* <ibn-al-nadim:fihrist>`, (1) a commentary on Eucl. Book V., (2) "On proportion," (3) "On the 26 propositions of the first Book of :person:`Euclid <euclid>` which are proved without *reductio ad absurdum*\ [#f85_6]_." The work "On proportion" survives and is probably identical with, or part of, the commentary on Book V.\ [#f85_7]_ He also wrote, what is not mentioned by the :cite:`*Fihrist* <ibn-al-nadim:fihrist>`, a commentary on Eucl. Book X., a fragment of which survives in a Paris MS.\ [#f85_8]_

5. Abū Ja'far al-Khāzin (i.e. "the treasurer" or "librarian"), one of the first mathematicians and astronomers of his time, was born in Khurāsān and died between the years 961 and 971. The :cite:`*Fihrist* <ibn-al-nadim:fihrist>` speaks of him as having written a commentary on the whole of the *Elements*\ [#f85_9]_, but only the commentary on the beginning of Book X. survives (in Leiden, Berlin and Paris); therefore either the notes on the rest of the Books have perished, or the :cite:`*Fihrist* <ibn-al-nadim:fihrist>` is in error\ [#f85_10]_. The latter would seem more probable, for, at the end of his commentary, al-Khāzin remarks that the rest had already been commented on by Sulaimān b. 'Uṣma (Leiden MS.)\ [#f85_11]_ or 'Oqba (:person:`Suter <suter>`), to be mentioned below. Al-Khāzin's method is criticised unfavourably in the preface to the Oxford MS. quoted by Nicoll (see p. 77 above).
