# Transcription Notes: Folio 0007 (Page 1)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio**: `0007` (Page 1)
- **Section**: Book III, Definitions 1–9
- **Continuity**: Opens Book III with the English text of Definitions 1–9. Definitions 10–11 follow at the top of Folio `0008` (Page 2).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Defined Terms (Boldface)**: Heath sets the key defined terms in boldface: **Equal circles** (Def. 1), **touch a circle** (Def. 2), **Circles ... touch one another** (Def. 3), **equally distant from the centre** (Def. 4), **at a greater distance** (Def. 5), **segment of a circle** (Def. 6), **angle of a segment** (Def. 7), **angle in a segment** and **base of the segment** (Def. 8), and **stand upon** (Def. 9).
- **Def. 7 vs. Def. 8**: Note the nearly identical wording of Def. 6 ("A **segment of a circle** is the figure contained by a straight line and a circumference of a circle") and Def. 7 ("An **angle of a segment** is that contained by a straight line and a circumference of a circle"), a mixed curvilineal angle contrasted with the rectilineal **angle in a segment** of Def. 8.
