Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Def. 1 (Ἴσοι κύκλοι)**: Heath defends Euclid's classification of equal circles as a genuine definition (against Tartaglia, Borelli, Playfair, Simson, and Pfleiderer), noting that it avoids superposition and satisfies Aristotle's criterion for a ὁριστικὸς λόγος (*De anima* II. 2, 413 a 13).
- **Greek Idiom for Radius**: Draws attention to the Greek periphrasis ἡ ἐκ τοῦ κέντρου (εὐθεῖα) for *radius* and its predicative use without the article (ἐκ τοῦ κέντρου / ἐκ κέντρου γάρ).
- **Def. 2 (ἅπτεσθαι vs. ἐφάπτεσθαι)**: Contrasts the simplex verb ἅπτεσθαι ("to meet") with the compound ἐφάπτεσθαι ("to touch" / be tangent to).
