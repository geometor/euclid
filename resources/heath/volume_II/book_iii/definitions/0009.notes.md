- **Locus Terminology**: Quotes Aristotle (*Meteorologica* III. 5, 376 b 9 and 376 a 6) and Pappus (ἅψεται τὸ σημεῖον θέσει δεδομένης εὐθείας) on the use of ἅπτεσθαι / ἐφάπτεσθαι to express that a point lies on a given locus.
- **Def. 3 (Contact of Circles)**: Discusses Todhunter's view on whether "do not cut one another" is local (at the point of contact) or global, arguing for the local reading and explaining the presence of III. 7, 8 as providing tools to answer objections regarding unproved assumptions in III. 11–13. Also clarifies the minimum topological assumptions required for *internal* vs. *external* contact in III. 6, 11, 12.
