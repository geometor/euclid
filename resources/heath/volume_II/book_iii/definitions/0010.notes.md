## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Def. 7 (Τμήματος γωνία)**: Heath explains the "angle of a segment" as a historical survival of pre-Euclidean "mixed" (curvilineal-rectilineal) angles, alongside the "angle of a semicircle" and the "horn-like angle" (κερατοειδής) appearing in III. 16. References Aristotle (*Anal. prior.* I. 24, 41 b 13 sqq.) to show that pre-Euclidean geometers proved I. 5 using the equality of the two angles of a segment, a symmetry implicitly assumed in Euclid's singular phrasing "*the* angle contained by a straight line and a circumference of a circle."
