Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Def. 10 (Τομεὺς κύκλου)**: Discusses the etymology of τομεύς ("sector") from σκυτοτομικὸς τομεύς ("shoemaker's knife", parallel to Archimedes' ἄρβηλος), and cites the wider classification found in the Greek scholia and an-Nairīzī (likely from Heron) distinguishing sectors from "sector-like figures" (τομοειδῆ σχήματα).
- **Euclid's** *On Divisions* **(περὶ διαιρέσεων, Prop. 28**): Presents the construction (via Woepcke and Cantor, *Gesch. d. Math.* $\text{I}_3$, pp. 287–8) for bisecting a sector-like figure $ABCD$ by a single straight line $CF$, replacing the bisecting broken line $AEC$ via the parallel $EF \parallel AC$ (producing equal triangles $\triangle AFC = \triangle AEC$ on the same base $AC$).
- **Def. 11 (Ὅμοια τμήματα κύκλων)**: Cites De Morgan's observation that "similar" here anticipates the proportion theory of Book VI.
