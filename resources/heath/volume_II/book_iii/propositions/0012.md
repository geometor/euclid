# BOOK III. PROPOSITIONS.

## PROPOSITION 1.

*To find the centre of a given circle.*

Let <span data-ref="0012.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> be the given circle;
thus it is required to find the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0012.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span>.

Let a straight line <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be drawn
<sup>5</sup> through it at random, and let it be bisected
at the point <span data-ref="0012.g1:D">$D$</span>;
from <span data-ref="0012.g1:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be drawn at right angles
to <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and let it be drawn through to <span data-ref="0012.g1:E">$E$</span>;
let <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0012.g1:F">$F$</span>;
<sup>10</sup> I say that <span data-ref="0012.g1:F">$F$</span> is the centre of the circle
<span data-ref="0012.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span>.

![Proposition 1](0012.g1.svg)

For suppose it is not,[^7_1] but, if possible,
let <span data-ref="0012.g1:G">$G$</span> be the centre,
and let <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ G A /">$GA$</span>, <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> be joined.

<sup>15</sup> Then, since <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is common,
the two sides <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> are equal to the two sides
<span data-ref="0012.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> respectively;[^7_2]
and the base <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ G A /">$GA$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0012.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>, for they are
<sup>20</sup> radii;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0012.g1:< A D G )">$ADG$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0012.g1:< G D B )">$GDB$</span>. [I. 8]

But, when a straight line set up on a straight line makes
the adjacent angles equal to one another, each of the equal
angles is right; [I. Def. 10]
<sup>25</sup> therefore the angle <span data-ref="0012.g1:< G D B )">$GDB$</span> is right.
