## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Philology**:
  - Line 12 (`[^7_1]`): $\text{Μὴ γάρ}$ ("For suppose it is not"), a characteristic Euclidean elliptical phrase introducing a *reductio ad absurdum*.
  - Todhunter's critique: Distinction between $\delta\iota\acute{\eta}\chi\theta\omega$ ("let it be *drawn through*") and $\dot{\varepsilon}\kappa\beta\varepsilon\beta\lambda\acute{\eta}\sigma\theta\omega$ ("let it be *produced*").
- **De Morgan's Alternative Construction**: Uses the intersection $O$ of perpendicular bisectors $DO$, $EO$ of two chords $AB$, $AC$, unifying III. 1, III. 25, and IV. 5 and showing that three non-collinear points uniquely determine a circle.
