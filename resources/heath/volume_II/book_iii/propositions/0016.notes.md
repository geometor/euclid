## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Intersection of a Line and a Circle**: Heath notes that from Proclus's lemma on I. 16 (or from I. 7, as in the note on I. 12), at most two equal straight lines can be drawn from the centre to a line, whence *A straight line cannot cut a circle in more than two points*.
- **Proposition 3 First Part**: Proves that the diameter $CD$ through the centre $E$ bisecting chord $AB$ at $F$ cuts $AB$ at right angles via triangle congruence $\triangle AFE \cong \triangle BFE$ [I. 8] and [I. Def. 10], and sets up the converse in lines 24–25.
