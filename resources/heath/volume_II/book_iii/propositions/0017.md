## PROPOSITION 4.

*If in a circle two straight lines cut one another which are not through the centre, they do not bisect one another.*

Let <span data-ref="0017.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> be a circle, and in it let the two straight lines
<span data-ref="0017.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, which are not through the
centre, cut one another at <span data-ref="0017.g1:E">$E$</span>;
I say that they do not bisect one
another.

![Proposition 4](0017.g1.svg)

For, if possible, let them bisect one
another, so that <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>;
let the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0017.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> be
taken [III. 1], and let it be <span data-ref="0017.g1:F">$F$</span>; let <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> be
joined.

Then, since a straight line <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> through the centre bisects
a straight line <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> not through the centre,
it also cuts it at right angles; [III. 3]
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0017.g1:< F E A )">$FEA$</span> is right.

Again, since a straight line <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> bisects a straight line <span data-ref="0017.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,
it also cuts it at right angles; [III. 3]
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0017.g1:< F E B )">$FEB$</span> is right.
