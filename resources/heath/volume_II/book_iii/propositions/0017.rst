PROPOSITION 4.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If in a circle two straight lines cut one another which are not through the centre, they do not bisect one another.

| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0017.g1:( A B C D )>` be a circle, and in it let the two straight lines
| :g:`$AC$ <0017.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0017.g1:/ B D />`, which are not through the
| centre, cut one another at :g:`$E$ <0017.g1:E>`;
| I say that they do not bisect one
| another.

.. picture:: 0017.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4

| For, if possible, let them bisect one
| another, so that :g:`$AE$ <0017.g1:/ A E />` is equal to :g:`$EC$ <0017.g1:/ E C />`,
| and :g:`$BE$ <0017.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$ED$ <0017.g1:/ E D />`;
| let the centre of the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0017.g1:( A B C D )>` be
| taken :just:`[III. 1] <III.1>`, and let it be :g:`$F$ <0017.g1:F>`; let :g:`$FE$ <0017.g1:/ F E />` be
| joined.

| Then, since a straight line :g:`$FE$ <0017.g1:/ F E />` through the centre bisects
| a straight line :g:`$AC$ <0017.g1:/ A C />` not through the centre,

.. subclause:: [III. 3] <III.3>

   it also cuts it at right angles;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$FEA$ <0017.g1:< F E A )>` is right.

Again, since a straight line :g:`$FE$ <0017.g1:/ F E />` bisects a straight line :g:`$BD$ <0017.g1:/ B D />`,

.. subclause:: [III. 3] <III.3>

   it also cuts it at right angles;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$FEB$ <0017.g1:< F E B )>` is right.
