Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 4 Conclusion**: Completes the *reductio ad absurdum* showing that two chords not both passing through the centre cannot bisect one another ($\angle FEA = \angle FEB$, the less to the greater).
- **Proposition 5** (*reductio ad absurdum*): Demonstrates that two intersecting circles $ABC, CDG$ cannot share the same centre $E$ by joining $EC$ to an intersection point $C$ and drawing a secant $EFG$ at random, yielding $EF = EC = EG$, which is impossible.
- **Unified Enunciation (III. 5 & 6)**: Heath observes that III. 5 (intersecting circles) and III. 6 (tangent circles) can be unified into the single statement: *If the circumferences of two circles meet at a point they cannot have the same centre* (unless they coincide altogether).
