## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Interpolation of ἐντός ("internally")**: Heath notes that English editions traditionally restricted the enunciation of III. 6 to circles touching *internally*, translating the interpolated Greek word ἐντός. Euclid illustrates only internal contact as the more difficult case, though the proof applies equally to external contact.
- **Camerer's Objection on Tacit Assumption**: Camerer observed that assuming $FEB$ meets the two circles at distinct points $E$ and $B$ appears to anticipate III. 13 (that two circles cannot touch at more than one point). Heath replies that the proof only requires *one* ray from $F$ to meet the circles in distinct points, which follows directly from the definition of internal contact.
