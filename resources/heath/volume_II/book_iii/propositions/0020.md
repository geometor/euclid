### PROPOSITION 7.

<p class="enunciation">If on the diameter of a circle a point be taken which is not<br />
the centre of the circle, and from the point straight lines fall<br />
upon the circle, that will be greatest on which the centre is, the<br />
remainder of the same diameter will be least, and of the rest<br />
<sup>5</sup> the nearer to the straight line through the centre is always<br />
greater than the more remote, and only two equal straight<br />
lines will fall from the point on the circle, one on each side<br />
of the least straight line.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0020.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> be a circle, and let <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be a diameter of it; <sup>10</sup> on <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> let a point <span data-ref="0020.g1:F">$F$</span> be taken which is not the centre of the circle, let <span data-ref="0020.g1:E">$E$</span> be the centre of the circle, and from <span data-ref="0020.g1:F">$F$</span> let straight lines <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> fall upon the circle <span data-ref="0020.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is greatest, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is least, and of the rest <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> is <sup>15</sup> greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.

![Proposition 7 Diagram](0020.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> be joined.

Then, since in any triangle two sides are greater than the remaining one, [I. 20]
<sup>20</sup> <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span>.

Again, since <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is common,
<sup>25</sup> the two sides <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

But the angle <span data-ref="0020.g1:< B E F )">$BEF$</span> is also greater than the angle <span data-ref="0020.g1:< C E F )">$CEF$</span>;
therefore the base <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is greater than the base <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>. [I. 24]

For the same reason
<span data-ref="0020.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.

<sup>30</sup> Again, since <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, and <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,
<span data-ref="0020.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be subtracted from each;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is greater than the remainder <sup>35</sup> <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is greatest, <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is least, and <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> than <span data-ref="0020.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.
