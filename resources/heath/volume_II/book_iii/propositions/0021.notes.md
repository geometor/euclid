## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Philological Notes**:
  - Line 4: ἐλαχίστη δὲ ἡ λοιπή ("and the remaining [straight line] least"), expanded by Heath with "of the same diameter".
  - Lines 7, 39: ἐφ' ἑκάτερα τῆς ἐλαχίστης ("on both sides, or on each of the two sides, of the least"), rendered by Heath as "one on each side of the least".
- **De Morgan's Observation**: Euclid defines "nearer to the straight line through the centre" at the eccentric point $F$ ($\angle DFB > \angle DFC$), but applies I. 24 at the centre $E$ assuming $\angle DEB > \angle DEC$. Heath supplies a proof using the intersection $M$ of $FB$ and $EC$, showing $EM < EC = EB$.
