I say also that from the point :g:`$F$ <0020.g1:F>` only two equal straight lines will fall on the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0020.g1:( A B C D )>`, one on each side\ [#f2]_ of the least :g:`$FD$ <0020.g1:/ F D />`.

For on the straight line :g:`$EF$ <0020.g1:/ E F />`, and at the point :g:`$E$ <0020.g1:E>` on it, let the angle :g:`$FEH$ <0020.g1:< F E H )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0020.g1:< F E H ) = 0020.g1:< G E F )>` to the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0020.g1:< G E F )>` :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`, and let :g:`$FH$ <0020.g1:/ F H />` be joined.

Then, since :g:`$GE$ <0020.g1:/ G E />` is :eq:`equal <0020.g1:/ G E / = 0020.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0020.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0020.g1:/ E F />` is common,

.. subclause::

   the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0020.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0020.g1:/ E F />` are :eq:`equal <0020.g1:/ G E / = 0020.g1:/ H E />` to the two sides :g:`$HE$ <0020.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0020.g1:/ E F />`;

and the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0020.g1:< G E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0020.g1:< G E F ) = 0020.g1:< H E F )>` to the angle :g:`$HEF$ <0020.g1:< H E F )>`;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$FG$ <0020.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0020.g1:/ F G / = 0020.g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`$FH$ <0020.g1:/ F H />`.

I say again that another straight line equal to :g:`$FG$ <0020.g1:/ F G />` will not fall on the circle from the point :g:`$F$ <0020.g1:F>`.

For, if possible, let :g:`$FK$ <0020.g1:/ F K />` so fall.

Then, since :g:`$FK$ <0020.g1:/ F K />` is :eq:`equal <0020.g1:/ F K / = 0020.g1:/ F G /; 0020.g1:/ F H / = 0020.g1:/ F G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0020.g1:/ F G />`, and :g:`$FH$ <0020.g1:/ F H />` to :g:`$FG$ <0020.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$FK$ <0020.g1:/ F K />` is also :eq:`equal <0020.g1:/ F K / = 0020.g1:/ F H />` to :g:`$FH$ <0020.g1:/ F H />`,

.. subclause::

   the nearer to the straight line through the centre being thus equal to the more remote: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore another straight line equal to :g:`$GF$ <0020.g1:/ G F />` will not fall from the point :g:`$F$ <0020.g1:F>` upon the circle;

.. assertion::

   therefore only one straight line will so fall.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

.. [#f1] **of the same diameter.** I have inserted these words for clearness' sake. The text has simply ἐλαχίστη δὲ ἡ λοιπή, "and the remaining (straight line) least."

.. [#f2] **one on each side.** The word "one" is not in the Greek, but is necessary to give the force of ἐφ' ἑκάτερα τῆς ἐλαχίστης, literally "on both sides," or "on each of the two sides, of the least."

:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` points out that there is an unproved assumption in this demonstration. We draw straight lines *from* :g:`$F$ <0021.g1:F>`, as :g:`$FB$ <0021.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$FC$ <0021.g1:/ F C />`, such that the angle :g:`$DFB$ <0021.g1:< D F B )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$DFC$ <0021.g1:< D F C )>` and then assume, with respect to the straight lines drawn *from the centre* :g:`$E$ <0021.g1:E>` to :g:`$B$ <0021.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0021.g1:C>`, that the angle :g:`$DEB$ <0021.g1:< D E B )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$DEC$ <0021.g1:< D E C )>`. This is most easily proved, I think, by means of the converse of part of the theorem about the lengths of different straight lines drawn to a given straight line from an external point which was mentioned above in the note on III. 2. This converse would be to the effect that, *If two unequal straight lines be drawn from a point to a given straight line which are not perpendicular to the straight line, the greater of the two is the further from the perpendicular from the point to the given straight line.* This can either be proved from its converse by :term:`*reductio ad absurdum* <reductio-ad-absurdum>`, or established directly by means of I. 47. Thus, in the accompanying figure, :g:`$FB$ <0021.g1:/ F B />` must cut :g:`$EC$ <0021.g1:/ E C />` in some point :g:`$M$ <0021.g1:M>`, since the angle :g:`$BFE$ <0021.g1:< B F E )>` is less than the angle :g:`$CFE$ <0021.g1:< C F E )>`.

Therefore :g:`$EM$ <0021.g1:/ E M />` is less than :g:`$EC$ <0021.g1:/ E C />`, and therefore than :g:`$EB$ <0021.g1:/ E B />`.
