Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Alternative Enunciations (Taylor, Nixon, Smith & Bryant)**: Modern editors often substitute "subtends the greater angle at the centre" for Euclid's "nearer to the straight line through the centre", avoiding the unproved step noted by De Morgan. However, Heath defends Euclid's criterion (comparing angles at the eccentric point $F$) as more directly useful in subsequent propositions.
- **Heron's Reinterpretation via Perpendicular Distance**: Heron (via an-Nairīzī) interprets "nearer" as having a smaller perpendicular distance from the centre $D$ ($DG < DH$, per III. Deff. 4, 5) and compares segments $AE, AF$ on opposite sides of the diameter using the Pythagorean theorem (I. 47) on right triangles $DGE, DGA$ and $DHF, DHA$.
