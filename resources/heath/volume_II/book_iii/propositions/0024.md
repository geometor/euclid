title: "Book III, Proposition 8 (Enunciation cont. and Proof)"

<p class="enunciation">the nearer to that through the centre is always greater than<br />
the more remote, but, of the straight lines falling on the convex<br />
circumference, that between the point and the diameter is least,<br />
while of the rest the nearer to the least is always less than the<br />
more remote, and only two equal straight lines will fall on the<br />
circle from the point, one on each side of the least.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0024.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> be a circle, and let a point <span data-ref="0024.g1:D">$D$</span> be taken outside <span data-ref="0024.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span>;  
let there be drawn through from it straight lines <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> be through the centre;  
I say that, of the straight lines falling on the concave circumference <span data-ref="0024.g1:( A E F C )">$AEFC$</span>, the straight line <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> through the centre is greatest,  
while <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>;  
but, of the straight lines falling on the convex circumference <span data-ref="0024.g1:( H L K G )">$HLKG$</span>, the straight line <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> between the point and the diameter <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is least;  
and the nearer to the least <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is always less than the more remote, namely <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span> than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>.

![Diagram for Proposition 8](0024.g1.svg)

For let the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0024.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> be taken <span class="justification">[III. 1]</span>, and let it be <span data-ref="0024.g1:M">$M$</span>;  
let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M E /">$ME$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M C /">$MC$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A M /">$AM$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>,  
let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> be added to each;  
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>.</p>

But <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 20]</span>  
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>.</p>

Again, since <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M E /">$ME$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> is common,  
therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> are equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>;  
and the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< E M D )">$EMD$</span> is greater than the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< F M D )">$FMD$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is greater than the base <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 24]</span></p>

Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is greatest, while <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.</p>
