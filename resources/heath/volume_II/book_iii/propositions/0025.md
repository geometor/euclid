Next, since <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span> are greater than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 20]</span>  
and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>,  
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span> is greater than the remainder <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>,  
<p class="assertion">so that <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is less than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span>.</p>

And, since on <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>, one of the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< M L D >">$MLD$</span>, two straight lines <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span> were constructed meeting within the triangle,  
therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span> are less than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ L D /">$LD$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 21]</span>  
and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is less than the remainder <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span>.</p>

Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span> is also less than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is least, while <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is less than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span>, and<br />
<span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span> than <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>.</p>

I say also that only two equal straight lines will fall from the point <span data-ref="0024.g1:D">$D$</span> on the circle, one on each side of the least <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span>.

On the straight line <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>, and at the point <span data-ref="0024.g1:M">$M$</span> on it, let the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< D M B )">$DMB$</span> be constructed equal to the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< K M D )">$KMD$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span>,  
and <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> is common,  
<p class="sub-clause">the two sides <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> respectively;</p>
and the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< K M D )">$KMD$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0024.g1:< B M D )">$BMD$</span>;  
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

I say that no other straight line equal to the straight line <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> will fall on the circle from the point <span data-ref="0024.g1:D">$D$</span>.

For, if possible, let a straight line so fall, and let it be <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>.  
Then, since <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>,  
while <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,  
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>,</p>
that is, the nearer to the least <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> equal to the more remote: which was proved impossible.

<p class="assertion">Therefore no more than two equal straight lines will fall<br />
on the circle <span data-ref="0024.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> from the point <span data-ref="0024.g1:D">$D$</span>, one on each side of<br />
<span data-ref="0024.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> the least.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.</p>
