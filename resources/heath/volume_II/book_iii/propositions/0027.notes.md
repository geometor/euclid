## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Genuine Proof of III. 9 (Perpendicular Bisectors)**: As Heath explains on the next page (`0028`), the proof given here in the main text—using the perpendicular bisectors $GK$ and $HL$ of chords $AB$ and $BC$ and invoking [III. 1, Por.]—is regarded by August and Heiberg as the genuine Euclidean proof, whereas Simson printed the alternative proof (by *reductio ad absurdum* from III. 7) which Heiberg relegated to the Appendix.
