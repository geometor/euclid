## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Aristotle Reference**: Quotes *Meteorologica* III. 3, 373a 13–16 (where Aristotle uses the property that only from the centre can three equal lines be drawn to the circle in his theory of the halo).
- **Contrapositive Logic (De Morgan)**: Highlights a foundational characteristic of Greek synthetic geometry: Euclid does not treat contrapositives ("every not-$A$ is not-$B$" $\iff$ "every $B$ is $A$") as automatic logical corollaries, but instead provides a separate geometric demonstration (and in the genuine proof, an independent construction via [III. 1, Por.] rather than *reductio* from III. 7).
- **Todhunter's Refinement of the Appendix Proof**: If the hypothetical centre $E$ lies inside $\angle ADC$ so that the diameter $FG$ separates $DA, DB, DC$, the monotonic ordering $DC > DB > DA$ does not hold on a single side of the diameter, though at least one of $DC, DA$ must still be unequal to $DB$.
