Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Line Note** ``1.``: Note ``1.`` is keyed to line 1 of the proposition enunciation (*A circle does not cut a circle at more points than two*), explaining that $\kappa\acute{\upsilon}\kappa\lambda\omicron\varsigma$ here denotes the *circumference* ($\pi\epsilon\rho\iota\phi\acute{\epsilon}\rho\epsilon\iota\alpha$) rather than the bounded plane figure (cf. I. Def. 15).
- **Point Naming in Proof vs. Diagram**: In the setting-out, Euclid writes ``namely B, C, F, H``, whereas the chords joined are $BH$ and $BG$ (using the fourth intersection point $G$, while $C$ is the extremity of the perpendicular bisector $AC$). In the alternative proof at the bottom of the page, the four intersection points are explicitly listed as $B, G, H, F$.
- **Parallel Structure with III. 9**: Just as in III. 9, the genuine proof uses perpendicular bisectors of chords ([III. 1, Por.]) and reduces to [III. 5] (intersecting circles cannot be concentric), whereas the Appendix proof adopted by Simson uses radii from the centre of one circle and reduces to III. 9.
