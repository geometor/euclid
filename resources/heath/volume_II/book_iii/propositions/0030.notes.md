## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heron's Alternative Proof of III. 10**: Heath notes that this second proof in the Greek text is claimed by Heron (as preserved in an-Nairīzī, ed. Curtze, pp. 120–121). Because it uses III. 9, it implicitly assumes $K$ lies *within* circle $DEF$, requiring supplementation via III. 8 (and its boundary analogue) for the cases where $K$ lies *on* or *outside* $DEF$.
- **Intersection of Perpendicular Bisectors**: Heath highlights Euclid's tacit assumption in the primary proof of III. 10 that the perpendicular bisectors of chords $BG$ and $BH$ meet at a point $P$, cross-referencing his note on IV. 5.
- **Cross-Page Footnotes (`[^25_2]`, `[^25_3]`)**: The footnotes referring to lines 2 and 3 of the enunciation of III. 11 on Page 24 ("the straight line joining their centres" and "point of contact") are printed at the end of the proposition on Page 25 (`0031.md`).
