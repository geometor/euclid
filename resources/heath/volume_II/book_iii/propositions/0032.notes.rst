Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Heath's Shortened Validation of Euclid's Proof**: Rather than first proving global non-intersection of the two circles as Camerer does, Heath observes that Euclid's inequality ($AG + GF > FA = FH \implies AG > GH$, contradicting $AG = GD$ if $GD \le GH$) requires only that $GD \le GH$ when $FG$ is produced beyond $G$. If $GD > GH$, since points near $A$ on the inner circle lie inside the outer circle while $D$ lies outside it, the circles must intersect at some point $X$ between $A$ and $D$ on one side of the line of centres $FD$. By symmetry (III. 7, 8), they must also intersect at a corresponding point $Y$ on the opposite side of $FD$, giving three common points ($A, X, Y$) in contradiction to III. 10.
- **Degenerate Collinear Case**: When $A$ lies on $GF$ produced beyond $F$ (``0032.g1.svg``), $GD \le GH < FH = FA$, whereas $GD = GA > FA$, an immediate contradiction.
