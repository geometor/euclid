## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Pappus's Lemmas to Apollonius's *Tactiones***: Heath cites Pappus, *Collection* VII, pp. 824, 826 (Lemmas 5 and 6 to Apollonius's *Tactiones* Book I), which provide partial converses to III. 11 using the common tangent at $A$ and III. 32.
- **Bracketed Heading `[PROPOSITION 12.`**: Heath encloses Proposition 12 in square brackets (`[PROPOSITION 12.` on Page 27 through `Q. E. D.]` on Page 28) because, as explained in his note on Page 28, neither Heron nor an-Nairīzī had III. 12 in Euclid's text; Heron supplied it as an addition to III. 11, which Theon or another editor subsequently incorporated as a separate proposition.
- **Line Numbering**: Marginal line number `5` (`<sup>5</sup>`) appears on the penultimate line of Page 27 ("externally at the point $A$, and let the centre $F$ of $ABC$, and").
