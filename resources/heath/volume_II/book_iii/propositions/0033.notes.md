## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Pappus's Lemmas to Apollonius's *Tactiones***:
  - Lemma 6 (Pappus VII, p. 826): Circles on collinear segments $AB, AC$ on the same side of $A$ as diameters touch internally at $A$.
  - Lemma 5 (Pappus VII, p. 824): If a secant $ABC$ through the point of internal contact $A$ contains the centre of the outer circle, it also contains the centre of the inner circle.
- **Square Bracket before `PROPOSITION 12`**: Heiberg and Heath enclose Proposition 12 in square brackets (`[PROPOSITION 12.` ... `Q. E. D.]`) because, as Heath explains on Page 28, Heron expressly states in his commentary on III. 11 that Euclid only treated internal contact and that Heron himself added the case of external contact.
