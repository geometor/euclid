## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Double Negative (`[^28_23]`)**: In line 23, Euclid's standard *reductio* conclusion uses the Greek double negative οὐκ ἄρα ἡ...εὐθεῖα...οὐκ ἐλεύσεται ("will not fail to pass", literally "will not *not*-pass").
- **Interpolation of Proposition 12 & Renumbering of Book III**: Heath documents that Heron explicitly states on III. 11 that Euclid only treated internal contact and that Heron himself is supplying the proof for external contact. Consequently, an-Nairīzī, Campanus, and Naṣīraddīn aṭ-Ṭūsī lack III. 12 as an independent proposition (giving only a brief corollary to III. 11) and number Heiberg's Prop. 13 as Prop. 12, and so on throughout Book III. Heath attributes the insertion of Heron's proof as Prop. 12 to Theon or another late editor.
