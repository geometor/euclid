Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Double Negative** (``23.``): οὐκ ἄρα ἡ...εὐθεῖα...οὐκ ἐλεύσεται ("Therefore the straight line...will not fail to pass...").
- **Heron's Authorship of III. 12**: Heron (in an-Nairīzī) explicitly states that Euclid only proved internal contact (III. 11) and supplies III. 12 himself; Campanus and Arabic editions consequently number III. 13 as Proposition 12.
