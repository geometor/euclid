I say that the straight line joined from :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0034.g1:G>` will pass through the point of contact at :g:`$A$ <0034.g1:A>`.

.. picture:: 0034.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram for Proposition 12 showing two circles ABC and ADE touching externally at A

For suppose it does not, but, if possible, let it pass as :g:`$FCDG$ <0034.g1:/ F C D G />`, and let :g:`$AF$ <0034.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$AG$ <0034.g1:/ A G />` be joined.

Then, since the point :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0034.g1:( A B C )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$FA$ <0034.g1:/ F A />` is equal to :g:`$FC$ <0034.g1:/ F C />`.

Again, since the point :g:`$G$ <0034.g1:G>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$ADE$ <0034.g1:( A D E )>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$GA$ <0034.g1:/ G A />` is equal to :g:`$GD$ <0034.g1:/ G D />`.

But :g:`$FA$ <0034.g1:/ F A />` was also proved equal to :g:`$FC$ <0034.g1:/ F C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FA$ <0034.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0034.g1:/ A G />` are equal to :g:`$FC$ <0034.g1:/ F C />`, :g:`$GD$ <0034.g1:/ G D />`,

.. assertion::

   so that the whole :g:`$FG$ <0034.g1:/ F G />` is greater than :g:`$FA$ <0034.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0034.g1:/ A G />`;

but it is also less :just:`[I. 20] <I.20>`: which is impossible.

Therefore the straight line joined from :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0034.g1:G>` will not fail to pass\ [#f1]_ through the point of contact at :g:`$A$ <0034.g1:A>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it will pass through it.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.]`

----

.. [#f1] **will not fail to pass.** The Greek has the double negative, οὐκ ἄρα ἡ...εὐθεῖα...οὐκ ἐλεύσεται, literally "the straight line...will not *not*-pass...."

:person:`Heron <heron>` says on III. 11: "Euclid in proposition 11 has supposed the two circles to touch internally, made his proposition deal with this case and proved what was sought in it. *But I will show how it is to be proved if the contact is external.*" He then gives substantially the proof and figure of III. 12. It seems clear that neither :person:`Heron <heron>` nor :person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>` had III. 12 in this place.

:person:`Campanus <campanus>` and the Arabic edition of :person:`Naṣīraddīn aṭ-Ṭūsī <nasiraddin>` have nothing more of III. 12 than the following addition to III. 11. "In the case of external contact the two lines :g:`$ae$ <0034.g1:/ A G />` and :g:`$eb$ <0034.g1:/ A F />` will be greater than :g:`$ab$ <0034.g1:/ F G />`, whence :g:`$ad$ <0034.g1:/ G D />` and :g:`$cb$ <0034.g1:/ F C />` will be greater than the whole :g:`$ab$ <0034.g1:/ F G />`, which is false." (The points :g:`$a$ <0034.g1:G>`, :g:`$b$ <0034.g1:F>`, :g:`$c$ <0034.g1:C>`, :g:`$d$ <0034.g1:D>`, :g:`$e$ <0034.g1:A>` correspond respectively to :g:`$G$ <0034.g1:G>`, :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>`, :g:`$C$ <0034.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0034.g1:D>`, :g:`$A$ <0034.g1:A>` in the above figure.) It is most probable that Theon or some other editor added :person:`Heron <heron>`'s proof in his edition and made Prop. 12 out of it (:person:`an-Nairīzī <an-nairizi>`, ed. Curtze, pp. 121—2). An-Nairīzī and :person:`Campanus <campanus>`, conformably with what has been said, number Prop. 13 of :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>`'s text Prop. 12, and so on through the Book.

What was said in the note on the last proposition applies, :term:`*mutatis mutandis* <mutatis-mutandis>`, to this. :person:`Camerer <camerer>` proceeds in the same manner as before; and we may use the same alternative argument in this case also.

Euclid's proof is valid provided only that, if :g:`$FG$ <0034.g1:/ F G />`, joining the assumed centres, meets the circle with centre :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>` in :g:`$C$ <0034.g1:C>` and the other circle in :g:`$D$ <0034.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0034.g1:C>` is not within the circle :g:`$ADE$ <0034.g1:( A D E )>` and :g:`$D$ <0034.g1:D>` is not within the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0034.g1:( A B C )>`. (The proof is equally valid whether :g:`$C$ <0034.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0034.g1:D>` coincide or the successive points are, as drawn in the figure, in the order :g:`$F$ <0034.g1:F>`, :g:`$C$ <0034.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0034.g1:D>`, :g:`$G$ <0034.g1:G>`.)
