Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Degenerate External Contact Configuration** (``0035.g1.svg``): To rule out the collinear case where $A$ lies on $FG$ produced (so that $F, C, D, G, A$ lie in order along the line), Heath notes that if the circles do not intersect elsewhere, $C$ must either coincide with $D$ or lie nearer to $F$ than $D$ does, which forces the outer curve with centre $F$ to loop around the circle with centre $G$ to reach $A$, making $FC \le FD < FA$ in contradiction to $FC = FA$.
- **Contraposition in Greek Geometry (De Morgan & Keynes)**: Heath contrasts H. M. Taylor's derivation of III. 11–12 (by contraposing the theorem that circles meeting off the line of centres intersect) with Euclid's practice, quoting Augustus De Morgan (via J. N. Keynes, *Formal Logic*, p. 81): *"Euclid may have been ignorant of the identity of 'Every $X$ is $Y$' and 'Every not-$Y$ is not-$X$,' for anything that appears in his writings; he makes the one follow from the other by a new proof each time."*
