## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Continental Treatment of Two Circles**: Heath summarizes the five-case classification of the relative position of two circles by the distance $OO'$ between their centres and their radii $r, r'$, citing Legendre, Baltzer, Henrici and Treutlein, Veronese, Ingrami, and Enriques and Amaldi.
- **Typographical Slip in Print**: In Case 1, near the bottom of the proof ("therefore $O'C$ is greater than $r'$, or $C$ is external to $O$."), the printed text reads `to O.` instead of `to O'.` (since $C$ was assumed internal to circle $O$ and proved external to circle $O'$). Preserved verbatim as printed.
