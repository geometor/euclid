Then, since a straight line <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> through
the centre cuts a straight line <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> not through
the centre at right angles, it also bisects it. <span class="justification">[III. 3]</span>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>.</p>

For the same reason

<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>;</p>

and <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>,

<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>.</p>

But the squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
for the angle at <span data-ref="0041.g1:< E F A )">$F$</span> is right;
and the squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>,
for the angle at <span data-ref="0041.g1:< E G C )">$G$</span> is right; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> are equal to the<br />
squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>,</p>

of which the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>,
for <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> which remains is equal to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.</p>

But in a circle straight lines are said to be equally distant
from the centre when the perpendiculars drawn to them from
the centre are equal; <span class="justification">[III. Def. 4]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> are equally distant from the centre.</p>

Next, let the straight lines <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be equally distant
from the centre; that is, let <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.

I say that <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

For, with the same construction, we can prove, similarly,
that <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> of <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>,

<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>.</p>

But the squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
and the squares on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0041.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>
