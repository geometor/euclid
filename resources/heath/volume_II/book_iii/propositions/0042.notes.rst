Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 14 Conclusion**: Completes the converse proof that chords equally distant from the centre are equal, using the equality of squares on $EF, FA$ and $EG, GC$ and subtracting equals ($EF^2 = EG^2$) to deduce $AF = CG$, whence $AB = CD$.
- **Heron's Addition (an-Nairīzī, pp. 125–7)**: Heath notes Heron's supplementary proof showing that the centre of the circle lies between the two chords.
- **Proposition 15**: Enunciates that the diameter is the greatest chord in a circle, and chords nearer to the centre are greater than those more remote.
- **Cross-Page Apparatus**: Heath's line-numbered notes (``1. Of straight lines`` and ``5. Nearer to the diameter AD``) referring to lines 1 and 5 of Proposition 15 on this page are printed on Page 37 (``0043.png``) immediately after the ``Q. E. D.`` of Proposition 15.
