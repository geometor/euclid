# Transcription Notes: Folio 0043 (Page 37)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio**: `0043` (Page 37)
- **Content**: Conclusion of Book III, Proposition 15 (proof and Heath's commentary comparing Euclid's proof with Simson's and Theodosius's) and the beginning of Book III, Proposition 16 (enunciation and first line of setting out).
- **Continuity**: Continues the proof of Proposition 15 from Folio `0042` (Page 36) ("But $ME$, $EN$ are greater than $MN$...") and continues Proposition 16 onto Folio `0044` (Page 38) ("Let $ABC$ be a circle about $D$ as centre and $AB$ as diameter;").

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Footnotes `[^37_1]` and `[^37_5]`**: Line-referenced notes for Proposition 15 (whose enunciation and setting out began on Page 36). Note 5 points out the slight inaccuracy in Euclid's phrase "nearer to the diameter $AD$" instead of "nearer to the centre" when $BC, FG$ are not parallel to $AD$.
- **Euclid vs. Simson and Theodosius (*Sphaerica* I. 6)**: Heath contrasts Euclid's auxiliary chord $MN$ (drawn perpendicular to $EK$ so that $MN$ is parallel to $FG$ and equal to $BC$, reducing the comparison of chords to I. 24) with Simson's algebraic/Pythagorean comparison of $EH^2 + HB^2 = EK^2 + KF^2$ following Theodosius.
- **Proposition 16 Enunciation**: Introduces the celebrated theorem on the tangent at the extremity of a diameter, the non-interposability of any straight line between the tangent and circumference, the "angle of the semicircle", and the "remaining" (cornicular/horn-like) angle.
