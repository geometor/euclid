- **Apparatus Line Notes (`1. Of straight lines.` and `5. Nearer to the diameter AD.`)**: Line-referenced notes for Proposition 15 (whose enunciation and setting out began on Page 36). Note 5 points out the slight inaccuracy in Euclid's phrase "nearer to the diameter $AD$" instead of "nearer to the centre" when $BC, FG$ are not parallel to $AD$. Rendered directly as bold-headed line commentary paragraphs rather than synthetic footnote tags.
- **Euclid vs. Simson and Theodosius (*Sphaerica* I. 6)**: Heath contrasts Euclid's auxiliary chord $MN$ (drawn perpendicular to $EK$ so that $MN$ is parallel to $FG$ and equal to $BC$, reducing the comparison of chords to I. 24) with Simson's algebraic/Pythagorean comparison of $EH^2 + HB^2 = EK^2 + KF^2$ following Theodosius.
- **Proposition 16 Enunciation**: Introduces the celebrated theorem on the tangent at the extremity of a diameter, the non-interposability of any straight line between the tangent and circumference, the "angle of the semicircle", and the "remaining" (cornicular/horn-like) angle.
