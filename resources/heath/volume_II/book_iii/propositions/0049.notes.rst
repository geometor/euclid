Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Theonine and Simsonian Additions to III. 16 Porism**: Heath notes that Heiberg brackets the clause asserting that a tangent touches a circle at one point only as an interpolation by Theon of Alexandria, and that Robert Simson added the further remark on the uniqueness of the tangent at a given point.
- **Proposition 17 Construction (Concentric Auxiliary Circle)**: Rather than using the semicircle on $AE$ (which requires III. 31, not yet proved), Euclid constructs a concentric circle $AFG$ of radius $EA$, erects $DF \perp EA$ at $D$ (where $EA$ meets the given circle $BCD$), joins $EF$ meeting $BCD$ at $B$, and proves $\triangle EBA \cong \triangle EDF$ by I. 4 so that $\angle EBA = \angle EDF = \text{right angle}$.
- **Absence of Q.E.F.**: In Heath's printing of Proposition 17 at the bottom of p. 43, the concluding formula ``Q. E. F.`` is omitted after the final sentence.
