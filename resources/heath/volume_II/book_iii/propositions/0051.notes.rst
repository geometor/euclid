Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Terminology**: ``ἡ ἐφαπτομένη`` (sc. ``εὐθεῖα``), the participial substantive from ``ἐφάπτομαι`` ("to touch"), is Euclid's technical term for "the tangent."
- **Logical Triads of Partial Converses**: Heath analyzes the logical symmetry between III. 16 Por., III. 18, and III. 19 using a triad of conditions (analogous to his analysis of III. 1 Por. and III. 3):

  - First triad: (1) tangent at a point, (2) line from centre to point of contact, (3) right angles at point of contact. Here ``(2) & (3) => (1)`` is III. 16 Por., ``(1) & (2) => (3)`` is III. 18, and ``(1) & (3) => (2)`` is III. 19.
  - Second triad (bottom of p. 45, continuing onto p. 46): (1) line through centre, (2) line through point of contact, (3) perpendicular to the tangent.
