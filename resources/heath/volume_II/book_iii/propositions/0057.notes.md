## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Extremal Perimeter Property (Serenus)**:
  - Since $BF = BC + CF = AC + CB$ is a diameter of the outer circle $AHB$, and $BE = AD + DB$ is a non-diameter chord of the same circle, $AC + CB > AD + DB$ (by III. 15). Thus among all triangles on base $AB$ with given vertical angle, the isosceles triangle $ACB$ maximizes the perimeter $AB + AD + DB$, and the perimeter decreases monotonically as $D$ moves away from $C$.
- **Diorismos (*Determined Limits*) in Todhunter's Problem**:
  - To construct $D$ on segment $ACB$ such that $AD + DB = L$ (a given length), the necessary and sufficient condition (diorismos) for real solutions is $AB < L \le 2 AC$:
    - For $AB < L < 2 AC$, there are two symmetric solutions on either side of $C$.
    - For $L = 2 AC$, the two solutions coalesce at the apex $C$.
    - For $L > 2 AC$, no solution exists.
- **Proposition 22 Enunciation**:
  - Euclid's classic theorem on cyclic quadrilaterals: $\angle ABC + \angle ADC = 2\text{ right angles}$ and $\angle BAD + \angle DCB = 2\text{ right angles}$.
