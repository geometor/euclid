## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Typographical Observation on Page 52**:
  - In the first line of the proof of Proposition 23 (`For, if possible...`), the scan shows a slight typographical imperfection in the second `l` of `possible` (`possibie`), which is rendered as `possible` in standard text and noted here.
- **Proof Structure of III. 22**:
  - Uses the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ to split the angle $\angle ADC$ into $\angle ADB + \angle BDC$, which by III. 21 are equal to $\angle ACB + \angle CAB$, reducing the sum of opposite angles $\angle ABC + \angle ADC$ to the angle-sum of $\triangle ABC$ (I. 32).
- **Converse of III. 22**:
  - Heath cites Todhunter's observation on the converse: if $\angle B + \angle D = 2\text{ right angles}$, the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ must pass through $D$ by *reductio ad absurdum* (analogous to the converse of III. 21 proved on p. 50).
