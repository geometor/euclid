Therefore, given a segment of a circle, the complete circle has been described.
And it is manifest that the segment <span data-ref="0061.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is less than a semicircle, because the centre <span data-ref="0061.g1:E">$E$</span> happens to be outside it.
