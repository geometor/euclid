## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio**: `0064` (Page 58)

- **Continuity**:

  - Ends after establishing that the central angles $\angle BGC$ and $\angle EHF$ are equal; the deduction that the inscribed angles $\angle BAC$ and $\angle EDF$ are also equal continues at the top of Folio `0065` (Page 59).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Lardner's Method (III. 26)**: By bisecting a right or obtuse angle at the circumference, each half is acute, allowing direct application of Euclid's acute-angle proof in III. 26 without appealing to reflex central angles.
- **Method of Exhaustion / Reductio ad Absurdum in III. 27**: Euclid proves the converse of III. 26 by *reductio ad absurdum*: assuming $\angle BGC > \angle EHF$, he constructs $\angle BGK = \angle EHF$ at the centre $G$ (`[I. 23]`), applies `[III. 26]` to infer $\text{arc } BK = \text{arc } EF = \text{arc } BC$, contradicting Common Notion 5 ("the less to the greater: which is impossible").
