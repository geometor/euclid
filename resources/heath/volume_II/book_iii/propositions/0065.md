### PROPOSITION 28.

*In equal circles equal straight lines cut off equal circumferences, the greater equal to the greater and the less to the less.*

Let <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span> be equal circles, and in the circles let <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be equal straight lines cutting off <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A C B )">$ACB$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D F E )">$DFE$</span> as greater circumferences and <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A G B )">$AGB$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D H E )">$DHE$</span> as lesser;
I say that the greater circumference <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A C B )">$ACB$</span> is equal to the greater circumference <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D F E )">$DFE$</span>, and the less circumference <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A G B )">$AGB$</span> to <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D H E )">$DHE$</span>.

![Diagram](0065.g1.svg)

For let the centres <span data-ref="0065.g1:K">$K$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:L">$L$</span> of the circles be taken, and let <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> be joined.

Now, since the circles are equal,
the radii are also equal;
therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ D L /">$DL$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>;
and the base <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0065.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0065.g1:< A K B )">$AKB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0065.g1:< D L E )">$DLE$</span>. [I. 8]

But equal angles stand on equal circumferences, when they are at the centres; [III. 26]
therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0065.g1:( A G B )">$AGB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0065.g1:( D H E )">$DHE$</span>.
