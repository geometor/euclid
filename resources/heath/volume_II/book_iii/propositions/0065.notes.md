# Transcription Notes: Folio 0065 (Page 59)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio**: `0065` (Page 59)
- **Content**: Conclusion of Book III, Proposition 27 and Heath's brief note on it, followed by the enunciation, setting-out, diagram, and first part of the proof of Book III, Proposition 28.
- **Continuity**:
  - Continues III. 27 from Folio `0064` (Page 58), applying `[III. 20]` to deduce that the angles at the circumference ($\angle A$ and $\angle D$) are equal because they are halves of the equal central angles $\angle BGC$ and $\angle EHF$.
  - Ends after proving that the lesser circumferences $AGB$ and $DHE$ in III. 28 are equal; the deduction that the greater circumferences $ACB$ and $DFE$ are also equal continues at the top of Folio `0066` (Page 60).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 28 Proof**: Given equal chords $AB = DE$ in equal circles $ABC, DEF$ with centres $K, L$, SSS triangle congruence (`[I. 8]`) yields $\angle AKB = \angle DLE$, whence `[III. 26]` gives $\text{arc } AGB = \text{arc } DHE$.
