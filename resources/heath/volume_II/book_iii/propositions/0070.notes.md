## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Alternative Proof in Greek Texts**: Discusses the alternative proof that the angle in a semicircle is right (relegated to an Appendix by August and Heiberg) using exterior angles of isosceles triangles $\triangle EAB$ and $\triangle EAC$.
- **Lardner's Proof**: Presents Dionysius Lardner's simplified proof that the angle in a segment greater than a semicircle is acute, using the diameter $AD$ and III. 21.
- **Angle *of* a Segment**: Notes Euclid's mention of the mixed-line "angle of a segment" as a vestige of pre-Euclidean controversies (cf. III. Def. 7 and III. 16).
- **Interpolated Porism**: Examines the traditional Porism/Corollary attached to III. 31, omitted by Heiberg as post-Theonic because it follows $\text{ὅπερ ἔδει δεῖξαι}$ ($\text{Q.E.D.}$).
- **Pappus's Lemma on Apollonius's $\text{νεύσεις}$**: Introduces the lemma from Pappus VII, p. 812 on Book II of Apollonius's *Neuseis*.
