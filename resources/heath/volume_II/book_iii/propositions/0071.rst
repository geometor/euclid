For, if :g:`$DE$ <0070.g2:/ D E />` be joined, the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0070.g2:< D E F )>` (in a semicircle) is a right angle [III. 31]; and :g:`$DE$ <0070.g2:/ D E />`, being at right angles to the chord :g:`$BG$ <0070.g2:/ B G />` of the circle :g:`$ABC$ <0070.g2:( A B C )>`, also bisects it [III. 3].

The second is a proposition in the :cite:`*Liber Assumptorum* <archimedes:liber-assumptorum>`, attributed (no doubt erroneously as regards much of it) to :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, which has reached us through the Arabic (:person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, ed. :person:`Heiberg <heiberg>`, II. pp. 520—521).

*If two chords* :g:`$AB$ <0071.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0071.g1:/ C D />` *in a circle intersect at right angles in a point* :g:`$O$ <0071.g1:O>`, *then the sum of the squares on* :g:`$AO$ <0071.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$BO$ <0071.g1:/ B O />`, :g:`$CO$ <0071.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$DO$ <0071.g1:/ D O />` *is equal to the square on the diameter*.

For draw the diameter :g:`$CE$ <0071.g1:/ C E />`, and join :g:`$AC$ <0071.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0071.g1:/ C B />`, :g:`$AD$ <0071.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$BE$ <0071.g1:/ B E />`.

Then the angle :g:`$CAO$ <0071.g1:< C A O )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$CEB$ <0071.g1:< C E B )>`. (This follows, in the first figure, from III. 21 and, in the second, from I. 13 and III. 22.) Also the angle :g:`$COA$ <0071.g1:< C O A )>`, being right, is equal to the angle :g:`$CBE$ <0071.g1:< C B E )>` which, being the angle in a semicircle, is also right [III. 31].

Therefore the triangles :g:`$AOC$ <0071.g1:< A O C >>`, :g:`$EBC$ <0071.g1:< E B C >>` have two angles equal respectively; whence the third angles :g:`$ACO$ <0071.g1:< A C O )>`, :g:`$ECB$ <0071.g1:< E C B )>` are equal. (In the second figure the angle :g:`$ACO$ <0071.g1:< A C O )>` is, by I. 13 and III. 22, equal to the angle :g:`$ABD$ <0071.g1:< A B D )>`, and therefore the angles :g:`$ABD$ <0071.g1:< A B D )>`, :g:`$ECB$ <0071.g1:< E C B )>` are equal.)

Therefore, in both figures, the arcs :g:`$AD$ <0071.g1:( A D )>`, :g:`$BE$ <0071.g1:( B E )>`, and consequently the chords :g:`$AD$ <0071.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$BE$ <0071.g1:/ B E />` subtended by them, are equal. :just:`[III. 26, 29]`

Now the squares on :g:`$AO$ <0071.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$DO$ <0071.g1:/ D O />` are equal to the square on :g:`$AD$ <0071.g1:/ A D />` [I. 47], that is, to the square on :g:`$BE$ <0071.g1:/ B E />`.

And the squares on :g:`$CO$ <0071.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$BO$ <0071.g1:/ B O />` are equal to the square on :g:`$BC$ <0071.g1:/ B C />`.

Therefore, by addition, the squares on :g:`$AO$ <0071.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$BO$ <0071.g1:/ B O />`, :g:`$CO$ <0071.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$DO$ <0071.g1:/ D O />` are equal to the squares on :g:`$EB$ <0071.g1:/ E B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0071.g1:/ B C />`, i.e. to the square on :g:`$CE$ <0071.g1:/ C E />`. :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`
