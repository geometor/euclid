### PROPOSITION 35.

<p class="enunciation">If in a circle two straight lines cut one another, the<br />
rectangle contained by the segments of the one is equal to the<br />
rectangle contained by the segments of the other.</p>

For in the circle <span data-ref="0077.g2:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> cut one another at the point <span data-ref="0077.g2:E">$E$</span>;
I say that the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span> is equal to the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E B /">$EB$</span>.

![Proposition 35 Case 1 diagram](0077.g2.svg)

If now <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are through the centre, so that <span data-ref="0077.g2:E">$E$</span> is the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0077.g2:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>,
it is manifest that, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E B /">$EB$</span> being equal,
the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E C /">$EC$</span> is also equal to the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0077.g2:/ E B /">$EB$</span>.
