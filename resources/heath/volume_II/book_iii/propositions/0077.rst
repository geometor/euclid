PROPOSITION 35.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If in a circle two straight lines cut one another, the rectangle contained by the segments of the one is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments of the other.

For in the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0077.g2:( A B C D )>` let the two straight lines :g:`$AC$ <0077.g2:/ A C />`,
:g:`$BD$ <0077.g2:/ B D />` cut one another at the point :g:`$E$ <0077.g2:E>`;
I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`$AE$ <0077.g2:/ A E />`,
:g:`$EC$ <0077.g2:/ E C />` is equal to the rectangle contained by
:g:`$DE$ <0077.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0077.g2:/ E B />`.

.. picture:: 0077.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 35 Case 1 diagram

If now :g:`$AC$ <0077.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0077.g2:/ B D />` are through the centre,
so that :g:`$E$ <0077.g2:E>` is the centre of the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0077.g2:( A B C D )>`,
it is manifest that, :g:`$AE$ <0077.g2:/ A E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0077.g2:/ E C />`, :g:`$DE$ <0077.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0077.g2:/ E B />`
being equal,
the rectangle contained by :g:`$AE$ <0077.g2:/ A E />`, :g:`$EC$ <0077.g2:/ E C />` is also equal to the
rectangle contained by :g:`$DE$ <0077.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0077.g2:/ E B />`.
