Next let <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> not be through the centre;
let the centre of <span data-ref="0078.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> be taken, and
let it be <span data-ref="0078.g1:F">$F$</span>;
from <span data-ref="0078.g1:F">$F$</span> let <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> be drawn perpen-
dicular to the straight lines <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 35 Case 2 diagram](0078.g1.svg)

Then, since a straight line <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>
through the centre cuts a straight line
<span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> not through the centre at right
angles,
<p class="assertion">it also bisects it; <span class="justification">[III. 3]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>.</p>

Since, then, the straight line <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> has been cut into equal
parts at <span data-ref="0078.g1:G">$G$</span> and into unequal parts at <span data-ref="0078.g1:E">$E$</span>,
the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the square
on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>; <span class="justification">[II. 5]</span>

Let the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> be added;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the squares
on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,
and the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ C G /">$CG$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>;
<span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the square
on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the square on
<span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>.

For the same reason, also,
the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is
equal to the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>.

But the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the square on
<span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> was also proved equal to the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> together with the square on
<span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> together with the
square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.

Let the square on <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> be subtracted from each;
therefore the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> which remains
is equal to the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0078.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>.

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.</p>
