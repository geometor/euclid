- **Geometric Application of Book II (`[II. 5]`)**: By dropping perpendiculars $FG, FH$ from the centre $F$ to the chords $AC, DB$, Euclid bisects $AC$ at $G$ (`[III. 3]`) and applies II. 5 to obtain $\text{rect.}(AE, EC) + EG^2 = GC^2$.
