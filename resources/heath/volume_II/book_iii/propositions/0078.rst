Next let :g:`AC <0078.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`DB <0078.g1:/ D B />` not be through the centre;
let the centre of :g:`ABCD <0078.g1:( A B C D )>` be taken, and
let it be :g:`F <0078.g1:F>`;
from :g:`F <0078.g1:F>` let :g:`FG <0078.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`FH <0078.g1:/ F H />` be drawn perpendicular
to the straight lines :g:`AC <0078.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`DB <0078.g1:/ D B />`,
and let :g:`FB <0078.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`FC <0078.g1:/ F C />`, :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0078.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 35 Case 2 diagram

Then, since a straight line :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />`
through the centre cuts a straight line
:g:`AC <0078.g1:/ A C />` not through the centre at right
angles,

.. assertion:: [III. 3] <III.3>

   it also bisects it;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AG <0078.g1:/ A G />` is :eq:`equal <0078.g1:/ A G / = 0078.g1:/ G C />` to :g:`GC <0078.g1:/ G C />`.

Since, then, the straight line :g:`AC <0078.g1:/ A C />` has been cut into equal
parts at :g:`G <0078.g1:G>` and into unequal parts at :g:`E <0078.g1:E>`,
the rectangle contained by :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the square
on :g:`EG <0078.g1:/ E G />` is equal to the square on :g:`GC <0078.g1:/ G C />`; :just:`[II. 5] <II.5>`

Let the square on :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />` be added;
therefore the rectangle :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the squares
on :g:`GE <0078.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />` is equal to the squares on :g:`CG <0078.g1:/ C G />`, :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />`.

But the square on :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`EG <0078.g1:/ E G />`, :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />`,
and the square on :g:`FC <0078.g1:/ F C />` is equal to the squares on :g:`CG <0078.g1:/ C G />`, :g:`GF <0078.g1:/ G F />`;
:just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`
therefore the rectangle :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the square
on :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` is equal to the square on :g:`FC <0078.g1:/ F C />`.

And :g:`FC <0078.g1:/ F C />` is :eq:`equal <0078.g1:/ F C / = 0078.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`FB <0078.g1:/ F B />`;
therefore the rectangle :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the square on
:g:`EF <0078.g1:/ E F />` is equal to the square on :g:`FB <0078.g1:/ F B />`.

For the same reason, also,
the rectangle :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`EB <0078.g1:/ E B />` together with the square on :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` is
equal to the square on :g:`FB <0078.g1:/ F B />`.

But the rectangle :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the square on
:g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` was also proved equal to the square on :g:`FB <0078.g1:/ F B />`;
therefore the rectangle :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` together with the square on
:g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`EB <0078.g1:/ E B />` together with the
square on :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />`.

Let the square on :g:`FE <0078.g1:/ F E />` be subtracted from each;
therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`AE <0078.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`EC <0078.g1:/ E C />` which remains
is equal to the rectangle contained by :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`EB <0078.g1:/ E B />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
