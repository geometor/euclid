BOOK IV.
========

DEFINITIONS.
------------

1. A rectilineal figure is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a rectilineal figure** <inscribed-in-rectilineal-figure>` when the respective angles of the inscribed figure lie on the respective sides of that in which it is inscribed.

2. Similarly a figure is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a figure** <circumscribed-about-figure>` when the respective sides of the circumscribed figure pass through the respective angles of that about which it is circumscribed.

3. A rectilineal figure is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a circle** <inscribed-in-circle>` when each angle of the inscribed figure lies on the circumference of the circle.

4. A rectilineal figure is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a circle** <circumscribed-about-circle>`, when each side of the circumscribed figure touches the circumference of the circle.

5. Similarly a circle is said to be :term:`**inscribed in a figure** <circle-inscribed-in-figure>` when the circumference of the circle touches each side of the figure in which it is inscribed.

6. A circle is said to be :term:`**circumscribed about a figure** <circumscribed-about-figure>` when the circumference of the circle passes through each angle of the figure about which it is circumscribed.

7. A straight line is said to be :term:`**fitted into a circle** <fitted-into-circle>` when its extremities are on the circumference of the circle.
