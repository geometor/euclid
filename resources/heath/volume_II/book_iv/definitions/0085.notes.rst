Transcription Notes: Folio 0085 (Page 79)
=========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio**: ``0085`` (Page 79, Volume II)
- **Content**: Continuation and conclusion of Heath's commentary on Book IV Definitions 1–7, presenting the Greek text of Definitions 2–7 and discussing the translation of $\dot{\varepsilon}\kappa\dot{\alpha}\sigma\tau\eta\ \pi\lambda\varepsilon\upsilon\rho\dot{\alpha}$, $\dot{\varepsilon}\kappa\dot{\alpha}\sigma\tau\eta\ \gamma\omega\nu\dot{\iota}\alpha$ ("respective sides", "respective angles") and the polysemy of $\ddot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$ vs. $\dot{\varepsilon}\phi\dot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$.
- **Continuity**: Continues from Folio ``0084`` (Page 78) and concludes the ``definitions`` section of Book IV. Book IV Propositions begin on Folio ``0086`` (Page 80).

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **$\ddot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$ vs. $\dot{\varepsilon}\phi\dot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$**: Heath notes that whereas Euclid normally distinguishes $\ddot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$ (*to meet*) from $\dot{\varepsilon}\phi\dot{\alpha}\pi\tau\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$ (*to touch*, used in Def. 4), in Definitions 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6 the simple verb $\ddot{\alpha}\pi\tau\eta\tau\alpha\iota$ bears three distinct senses: *to lie on* (Deff. 1, 3), *to pass through* (Deff. 2, 6), and *to touch* (Def. 5, where Simson proposed reading $\dot{\varepsilon}\phi\dot{\alpha}\pi\tau\eta\tau\alpha\iota$).
- **$\dot{\varepsilon}\nu\alpha\rho\mu\dot{o}\zeta\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$**: In Def. 7, "to be fitted into a circle" translates $\varepsilon\dot{\iota}\varsigma\ \kappa\dot{\upsilon}\kappa\lambda\mathrm{o}\nu\ \dot{\varepsilon}\nu\alpha\rho\mu\dot{o}\zeta\varepsilon\sigma\theta\alpha\iota$.
