## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Apollonius's *Neuseis* ($\nu\varepsilon\dot{\upsilon}\sigma\varepsilon\iota\varsigma$)**: Heath explains how fitting a chord of given length into a circle so that it verges ($\nu\varepsilon\dot{\upsilon}\mathrm{o}\upsilon\sigma\alpha\nu$) towards a given point $C$ reduces to drawing a tangent from $C$ to the concentric circle of radius $OL$ (where $L$ is the midpoint of any trial chord $HK$ of the given length). This yields the full $\delta\iota\mathrm{o}\rho\iota\sigma\mu\dot{o}\varsigma$ when $C$ lies inside the given circle.
- **Proposition 2 Construction**: Begins by drawing the tangent $GH$ to circle $ABC$ at $A$ using [III. 16, Por.].
