## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **De Morgan on Cyclotomy**: Heath cites Augustus De Morgan's observation that Book IV is fundamentally concerned with dividing a revolution (the circumference of a circle) into equal parts; Euclid omits a separate proposition for inscribing an equilateral triangle (used in IV. 16) because IV. 2 solves the general triangle just as easily as the equilateral case.
- **Marginal Line Numbers**: Marginal line numbers `5`, `10`, and `15` appear on this page for Proposition 3; note that the apparatus note for line 10 ("**at random**, literally 'as it may chance,' $\dot{\omega}\varsigma\ \ddot{\varepsilon}\tau\upsilon\chi\varepsilon\nu$...") is printed at the end of Proposition 3 on Folio `0090` (Page 84).
