### PROPOSITION 4.

<p class="enunciation">In a given triangle to inscribe a circle.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0091.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> be the given triangle;
thus it is required to inscribe a circle in the triangle <span data-ref="0091.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>.

Let the angles <span data-ref="0091.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:< A C B )">$ACB$</span>
<sup>5</sup> be bisected by the straight lines
<span data-ref="0091.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> <span class="justification">[I. 9]</span>, and let these meet
one another at the point <span data-ref="0091.g1:D">$D$</span>;
from <span data-ref="0091.g1:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be
drawn perpendicular to the straight
<sup>10</sup> lines <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.

is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0091.g1:< C B D )">$CBD$</span>,

<span data-ref="0091.g1:< B F D )">$BFD$</span>,
<sup>15</sup> <span data-ref="0091.g1:< E B D >">$EBD$</span>, <span data-ref="0091.g1:< F B D >">$FBD$</span> are two triangles having two angles equal to two
angles and one side equal to one side, namely that subtending

triangles;
therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to
<sup>20</sup> the remaining sides; <span class="justification">[I. 26]</span>
