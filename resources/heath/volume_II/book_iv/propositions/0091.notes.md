## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Escribed Circles (IV. 3 Commentary)**: Heath connects the construction of IV. 3 (circumscribing a triangle about a circle) with escribed circles by noting that drawing tangents parallel to the sides of $\triangle DEF$ on both sides of the circle yields 6 tangents forming 8 triangles: 2 circumscribed and 6 having the circle as an escribed circle touching one side and two sides produced.
- **Proposition 4 Construction**: Uses angle bisectors $BD$, $CD$ [I. 9] meeting at $D$, drops perpendiculars $DE, DF, DG$ to the sides, and applies AAS triangle congruence [I. 26] to prove $DE = DF = DG$.
- **Indirect Tangency Proof**: Employs *reductio ad absurdum* via III. 16 to prove that the circle with centre $D$ and radius $DE$ touches $AB, BC, CA$.
