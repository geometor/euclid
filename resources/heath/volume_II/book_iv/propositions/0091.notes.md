- **Proposition 4 Construction**: Uses angle bisectors $BD$, $CD$ [I. 9] meeting at $D$, drops perpendiculars $DE, DF, DG$ to the sides, and applies AAS triangle congruence [I. 26] to prove $DE = DF = DG$.
