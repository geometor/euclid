## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heron's Formula via the Incircle**: Because Heron's proof depends directly on inscribing the circle $DEF$ with incentre $G$ inside $\triangle ABC$ and decomposing $\triangle ABC$ into $\triangle BGC + \triangle ACG + \triangle ABG$, Heath places it as an excursus to IV. 4.
- **Ungeometrical Expressions in Heron**: Heath remarks on Heron's arithmetized geometric language—multiplying an area by an area ($CH^2 \cdot EG^2$) and taking the "side" ($\pi\lambda\varepsilon\upsilon\rho\acute{\alpha}$, i.e., square root) of a four-dimensional product.
