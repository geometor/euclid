## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Indeterminacy of Inscribing Equiangular Quadrilaterals**: Heath demonstrates why inscribing a quadrilateral equiangular to a given cyclic quadrilateral is indeterminate unless the quadrilateral is a square: rotating the chord $AC$'s subtending vertices from $B, D$ to $B', D'$ by equal arcs ($\angle BAB' = \angle DAD'$) preserves all four interior angles of $AB'CD'$ while altering the side ratios (*form*).
