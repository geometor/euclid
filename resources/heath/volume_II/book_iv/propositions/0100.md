### PROPOSITION 8.

*In a given square to inscribe a circle.*

Let <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be the given square;
thus it is required to inscribe a circle in the given square <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span>.

Let the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be bisected at the points <span data-ref="0100.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:F">$F$</span> respectively [I. 10],
through <span data-ref="0100.g1:E">$E$</span> let <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> be drawn parallel to either <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> or <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, and through <span data-ref="0100.g1:F">$F$</span> let <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> be drawn parallel to either <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> or <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>; [I. 31]

![Proposition IV. 8 Diagram](0100.g1.svg)

therefore each of the figures <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A F K D >">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< F B C K >">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A B H E >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< E H C D >">$HD$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A F G E >">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< G H C K >">$GC$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< F B H G >">$BG$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:< E G K D >">$GD$</span> is a parallelogram, and their opposite sides are evidently equal. [I. 34]

Now, since <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is half of <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> half of <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>,
so that the opposite sides are also equal;
therefore <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>.

Similarly we can prove that each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>;
therefore the four straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> are equal to one another.

Therefore the circle described with centre <span data-ref="0100.g1:G">$G$</span> and distance one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> will pass also through the remaining points.

And it will touch the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, because the angles at <span data-ref="0100.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:K">$K$</span> are right.

For, if the circle cuts <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, the straight line drawn at right angles to the diameter of the circle from its extremity will fall within the circle: which was proved absurd; [III. 16]
therefore the circle described with centre <span data-ref="0100.g1:G">$G$</span> and distance one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> will not cut the straight lines <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0100.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>.

Therefore it will touch them, and will have been inscribed in the square <span data-ref="0100.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span>.

Therefore in the given square a circle has been inscribed.
