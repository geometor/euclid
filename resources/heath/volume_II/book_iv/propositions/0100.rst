PROPOSITION 8.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   In a given square to inscribe a circle.

Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0100.g1:< A B C D >>` be the given square;
thus it is required to inscribe a circle in the given square
:g:`$ABCD$ <0100.g1:< A B C D >>`.

Let the straight lines :g:`$AD$ <0100.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />` be
bisected at the points :g:`$E$ <0100.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0100.g1:F>` respectively
:just:`[I. 10] <I.10>`,
through :g:`$E$ <0100.g1:E>` let :g:`$EH$ <0100.g1:/ E H />` be drawn parallel
to either :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />` or :g:`$CD$ <0100.g1:/ C D />`, and through
:g:`$F$ <0100.g1:F>` let :g:`$FK$ <0100.g1:/ F K />` be drawn parallel to either
:g:`$AD$ <0100.g1:/ A D />` or :g:`$BC$ <0100.g1:/ B C />`; :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`
therefore each of the figures :g:`$AK$ <0100.g1:< A F K D >>`, :g:`$KB$ <0100.g1:< F B C K >>`,
:g:`$AH$ <0100.g1:< A B H E >>`, :g:`$HD$ <0100.g1:< E H C D >>`, :g:`$AG$ <0100.g1:< A F G E >>`, :g:`$GC$ <0100.g1:< G H C K >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0100.g1:< F B H G >>`, :g:`$GD$ <0100.g1:< E G K D >>` is a parallelogram,
and their opposite sides are evidently equal. :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. picture:: 0100.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition IV. 8 Diagram

Now, since :g:`$AD$ <0100.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ A D / = 0100.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />`,
and :g:`$AE$ <0100.g1:/ A E />` is half of :g:`$AD$ <0100.g1:/ A D />`, and :g:`$AF$ <0100.g1:/ A F />` half of :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0100.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ A E / = 0100.g1:/ A F />` to :g:`$AF$ <0100.g1:/ A F />`,

so that the opposite sides are also equal;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0100.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ F G / = 0100.g1:/ G E />` to :g:`$GE$ <0100.g1:/ G E />`.

Similarly we can prove that each of the straight lines :g:`$GH$ <0100.g1:/ G H />`,
:g:`$GK$ <0100.g1:/ G K />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ G H / = 0100.g1:/ G K / = 0100.g1:/ F G / = 0100.g1:/ G E />` to each of the straight lines :g:`$FG$ <0100.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0100.g1:/ G E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the four straight lines :g:`$GE$ <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$GF$ <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$GK$ <0100.g1:/ G K />` are :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ G E / = 0100.g1:/ G F / = 0100.g1:/ G H / = 0100.g1:/ G K />` to one another.

Therefore the circle described with centre :g:`$G$ <0100.g1:G>` and distance
one of the straight lines :g:`$GE$ <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$GF$ <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$GK$ <0100.g1:/ G K />` will pass also
through the remaining points.

And it will touch the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0100.g1:/ D A />`,
because the angles at :g:`$E$ <0100.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0100.g1:F>`, :g:`$H$ <0100.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0100.g1:K>` are right.

For, if the circle cuts :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0100.g1:/ D A />`, the straight
line drawn at right angles to the diameter of the circle from
its extremity will fall within the circle: which was proved
absurd; :just:`[III. 16] <III.16>`
therefore the circle described with centre :g:`$G$ <0100.g1:G>` and distance
one of the straight lines :g:`$GE$ <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$GF$ <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$GK$ <0100.g1:/ G K />` will not cut
the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0100.g1:/ D A />`.

Therefore it will touch them, and will have been inscribed
in the square :g:`$ABCD$ <0100.g1:< A B C D >>`.

Therefore in the given square a circle has been inscribed.
