PROPOSITION 10.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To construct an isosceles triangle having each of the angles at the base double of the remaining one.

Let any straight line :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />` be set out, and let it be cut at
the point :g:`$C$ <0102.g1:C>` so that the rectangle
contained by :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0102.g1:/ B C />` is equal to
the square on :g:`$CA$ <0102.g1:/ C A />`; :just:`[II. 11] <II.11>`
with centre :g:`$A$ <0102.g1:A>` and distance :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />` let
the circle :g:`$BDE$ <0102.g1:( B D E )>` be described,
and let there be fitted in the circle
:g:`$BDE$ <0102.g1:( B D E )>` the straight line :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />` equal to
the straight line :g:`$AC$ <0102.g1:/ A C />` which is not
greater than the diameter of the
circle :g:`$BDE$ <0102.g1:( B D E )>`. :just:`[IV. 1] <IV.1>`

.. picture:: 0102.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition IV. 10 Diagram

Let :g:`$AD$ <0102.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0102.g1:/ D C />` be joined, and let
the circle :g:`$ACD$ <0102.g1:( A C D )>` be circumscribed about the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0102.g1:< A C D >>`.
:just:`[IV. 5] <IV.5>`

Then, since the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0102.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square
on :g:`$AC$ <0102.g1:/ A C />`,
and :g:`$AC$ <0102.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0102.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />`.

And, since a point :g:`$B$ <0102.g1:B>` has been taken outside the circle
:g:`$ACD$ <0102.g1:( A C D )>`,
and from :g:`$B$ <0102.g1:B>` the two straight lines :g:`$BA$ <0102.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />` have fallen on
the circle :g:`$ACD$ <0102.g1:( A C D )>`, and one of them cuts it, while the other falls
on it,
and the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0102.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0102.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />`,

.. assertion:: [III. 37] <III.37>

   therefore :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />` touches the circle :g:`$ACD$ <0102.g1:( A C D )>`.

Since, then, :g:`$BD$ <0102.g1:/ B D />` touches it, and :g:`$DC$ <0102.g1:/ D C />` is drawn across
from the point of contact at :g:`$D$ <0102.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the angle :g:`$BDC$ <0102.g1:< B D C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0102.g1:< D A C )>` in the
   | alternate segment of the circle. :just:`[III. 32] <III.32>`

Since, then, the angle :g:`$BDC$ <0102.g1:< B D C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0102.g1:< D A C )>`,

.. subclause::

   let the angle :g:`$CDA$ <0102.g1:< C D A )>` be added to each;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the whole angle :g:`$BDA$ <0102.g1:< B D A )>` is equal to the two angles
   | :g:`$CDA$ <0102.g1:< C D A )>`, :g:`$DAC$ <0102.g1:< D A C )>`.
