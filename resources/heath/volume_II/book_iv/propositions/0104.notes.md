## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Platonic Elementary Triangles (*Timaeus* 54 A–E)**:
  - The square face of the cube is composed of 4 isosceles right-angled ($45^\circ\text{–}45^\circ\text{–}90^\circ$) triangles (`0104.g1.svg`).
  - The equilateral triangular face of the tetrahedron, octahedron, and icosahedron is composed of 6 scalene right-angled ($30^\circ\text{–}60^\circ\text{–}90^\circ$) triangles meeting at the centroid (`0104.g2.svg`).
- **Dodecahedral Face Subdivision (Plutarch & Alcinous)**:
  - Each of the 12 pentagonal faces of the dodecahedron is divided into 5 isosceles triangles, and each isosceles triangle into 6 scalene right-angled triangles, yielding $12 \times 30 = 360$ elementary triangles (`0104.g3.svg`), whose internal diagonals form the Pythagorean star-pentagon or pentagram (`0104.g4.svg`).
