## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Pythagorean Pentagram**: Heath cites Lucian and the scholiast to Aristophanes' *Clouds* (via Bretschneider, pp. 85–86) on the triple interwoven triangle (`τὸ τριπλοῦν τρίγωνον, τὸ δι' ἀλλήλων, τὸ πεντάγραμμον`) used by the Pythagoreans as a token of recognition (`συμβόλῳ πρὸς τοὺς ὁμοδόξους ἐχρῶντο`) and called "Health" (*Hygieia*).
- **Golden Section & Application of Areas**: Connects IV. 10 with II. 11 and VI. 30 (*extreme and mean ratio*), and quotes Proclus (p. 67, 6 on Eudoxus and Plato regarding "the section"; p. 419, 15 quoting Eudemus on the Pythagorean discovery of the application of areas, their exceeding and falling short).
- **Geometrical Analysis of IV. 10**: Reconstructs the classical analysis assuming the isosceles triangle $ABD$ ($\angle B = \angle D = 2 \angle A$) is found, bisecting $\angle ADB$ by $DC$, showing $BD$ is tangent to the circumcircle of $\triangle ACD$ by the converse of III. 32, and applying III. 36 to obtain $AB \cdot BC = BD^2 = AC^2$, reducing the construction to II. 11.
