## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Trisection/Quinquesection of a Right Angle**: Heath notes that since $\angle BAD = \frac{2}{5}R$, bisecting $\angle BAD$ divides a right angle into five equal parts ($18^\circ$), and $BD$ is the side of a regular decagon inscribed in the circle of radius $AB$.
- **Formal Divisions of a Proposition (Proclus)**: Refers back to Vol. I, p. 130 regarding the omission of *ekthesis* (*setting-out*) and *diorismos* ("*definition*" or specification) when no datum appears in the *protasis* (enunciation), while noting that Euclid practically supplies a datum by setting out "any straight line $AB$".
- **Proposition 11 Construction**: Uses IV. 10 to construct the auxiliary isosceles triangle $FGH$ and IV. 2 to inscribe an equiangular triangle $ACD$ in the given circle $ABCDE$.
