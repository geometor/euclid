- **Proposition 12 Construction**: Notably does not require actually drawing the sides of the inscribed pentagon $ABCDE$, but only conceiving (`νενοήσθω`) $A, B, C, D, E$ as the angular points of the inscribed pentagon from IV. 11 so that the five arcs are equal, and drawing tangents $GH, HK, KL, LM, MG$ at those points via III. 16, Por.
