Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **H. M. Taylor's Construction**: Combines II. 11 and IV. 1 directly inside the circle: with perpendicular diameters $AOC, BOD$ and $E$ the midpoint of $OD$, cutting off $EF = OE$ along $AE$ makes $AF$ equal to the golden-section segment of the radius $OA$, which is the side of the inscribed regular decagon.
- **Proposition 12 Construction**: Notably does not require actually drawing the sides of the inscribed pentagon $ABCDE$, but only conceiving (``νενοήσθω``) $A, B, C, D, E$ as the angular points of the inscribed pentagon from IV. 11 so that the five arcs are equal, and drawing tangents $GH, HK, KL, LM, MG$ at those points via III. 16, Por.
