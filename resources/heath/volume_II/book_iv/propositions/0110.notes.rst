2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Generalizability of IV. 12–14**: Heath quotes De Morgan's observation that the methods of IV. 12, 13, and 14 (circumscribing a regular polygon about a circle given the inscribed one, and inscribing/circumscribing a circle in/about a given regular polygon) apply generally to any regular $n$-gon, as Euclid himself remarks in IV. 15 Porism (hexagon) and IV. 16 (15-gon).
- **Manuscript Omission of Conclusion**: Heath notes that the formal *sumperasma* ("therefore about the given circle an equilateral and equiangular pentagon has been circumscribed") is omitted in the Greek MSS.
