## PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">About a given pentagon, which is equilateral and equi-<br />
angular, to circumscribe a circle.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0112.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span> be the given pentagon, which is equilateral
and equiangular;
thus it is required to circumscribe a circle
about the pentagon <span data-ref="0112.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span>.

Let the angles <span data-ref="0112.g1:< B C D )">$BCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0112.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span> be bisected
by the straight lines <span data-ref="0112.g1:/ F C /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0112.g1:/ F D /">$DF$</span> respectively,
and from the point <span data-ref="0112.g1:F">$F$</span>, at which the straight
lines meet, let the straight lines <span data-ref="0112.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0112.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>,
<span data-ref="0112.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> be joined to the points <span data-ref="0112.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0112.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0112.g1:E">$E$</span>.
