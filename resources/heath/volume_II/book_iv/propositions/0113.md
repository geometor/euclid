## PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">In a given circle to inscribe an equilateral and equiangular<br />
hexagon.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0113.g1:( A B C D E F )">$ABCDEF$</span> be the given circle;
thus it is required to inscribe an equilateral and equiangular hexagon in the circle <span data-ref="0113.g1:( A B C D E F )">$ABCDEF$</span>.

Let the diameter <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> of the circle <span data-ref="0113.g1:( A B C D E F )">$ABCDEF$</span> be drawn;
let the centre <span data-ref="0113.g1:G">$G$</span> of the circle be taken, and with centre <span data-ref="0113.g1:D">$D$</span> and distance <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ G D /">$DG$</span> let the circle <span data-ref="0113.g1:( E G C H )">$EGCH$</span> be described;
let <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ G E /">$EG$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ G C /">$CG$</span> be joined and carried through to the points <span data-ref="0113.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:F">$F$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> be joined.

I say that the hexagon <span data-ref="0113.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is equilateral and equiangular.

For, since the point <span data-ref="0113.g1:G">$G$</span> is the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0113.g1:( A B C D E F )">$ABCDEF$</span>,
<span data-ref="0113.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>.
