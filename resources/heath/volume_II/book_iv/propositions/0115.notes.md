## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Porism to IV. 15 & Proclus's Reference**: Heath endorses Heiberg's emendation of Proclus (p. 304, 2), reading ἐν τῷ δʹ βιβλίῳ ("in the fourth Book") instead of ἐν τῷ δευτέρῳ βιβλίῳ ("in the second Book") for Proclus's example of a porism attached to a *problem* (since II. 4 is a theorem, whereas IV. 15 is a problem, and Proclus's singular article τὸ... κείμενον confirms that IV. 15 had the only genuine porism in Book IV, excluding the addition to IV. 5).
- **Theon's Alteration**: Heath notes that Theon replaced ὁμοίως δὲ τοῖς ἐπὶ τοῦ πενταγώνου ("And, in like manner as in the case of the pentagon") at the start of the second paragraph of the Porism with the simple conjunction καί, missing the distinction between drawing tangents *as in the pentagon* and proving the resulting figure regular *in conformity with what was explained in the case of the pentagon* (reducing IV. 12–14 for the hexagon to a brief corollary rather than three separate propositions).
