## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heading Typography**: In the scan, Proposition 1 is titled `PROPOSITION I.` using Roman numeral `I`, while later propositions use Arabic numerals (`PROPOSITION 2.`).
- **Mathematical Structure**: The proof shows that if $A, B$ are similar plane numbers, their product $C = AB$ is square. By taking $D = A^2$ (square), Euclid establishes $A : B = D : C$ via VII. 17. By VIII. 18, one mean proportional falls between $A, B$. By VIII. 8, one mean proportional falls between $D, C$. By VIII. 22, since $D$ is square, $C$ must also be square.
- **Heath's Commentary**: Heath summarizes the proof algebraically with $a : b = a^2 : ab$, highlighting the chain of Book VII and Book VIII citations.
