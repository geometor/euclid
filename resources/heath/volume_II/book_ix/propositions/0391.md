### PROPOSITION 2.

<p class="enunciation">If two numbers by multiplying one another make a square<br />
number, they are similar plane numbers.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> be two numbers, and let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span><br />
make the square number <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar plane<br />
numbers.

![Proposition 2 Diagram](0391.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself<br />
make <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is square.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, and by<br />
multiplying <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 17]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is square, and <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span> is so also,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span> are similar plane numbers.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore one mean proportional number falls between<br />
<span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is to <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore one mean proportional number falls between <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span><br />
also. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if one mean proportional number fall between two<br />
numbers, they are similar plane numbers; <span class="justification">[VIII. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar plane numbers.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

If $ab$ is a square number, $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. (The converse<br />
of IX. 1.)<br />
For
$$a : b = a^2 : ab. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$
And $a^2$, $ab$ being square numbers, and therefore similar plane numbers,<br />
they have one mean proportional. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span><br />
Therefore $a$, $b$ also have one mean proportional. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span><br />
whence $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. <span class="justification">[VIII. 20]</span>

### PROPOSITION 3.

<p class="enunciation">If a cube number by multiplying itself make some number,<br />
the product will be cube.</p>

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0392.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0392.g1:B">$B$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0392.g1:B">$B$</span> is cube.
