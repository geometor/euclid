### PROPOSITION 2.

*If two numbers by multiplying one another make a square number, they are similar plane numbers.*

Let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> be two numbers, and let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> make the square number <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>;  
I say that <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar plane numbers.

![Proposition 2 Diagram](0391.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is square.

Now, since <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>, therefore, as <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>. [VII. 17]

And, since <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is square, and <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span> is so also, therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span> are similar plane numbers.

Therefore one mean proportional number falls between <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>. [VIII. 18]

And, as <span data-ref="0391.g1:D">$D$</span> is to <span data-ref="0391.g1:C">$C$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span>; therefore one mean proportional number falls between <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> also. [VIII. 8]

But, if one mean proportional number fall between two numbers, they are similar plane numbers; [VIII. 20]  
therefore <span data-ref="0391.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0391.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar plane numbers.

**Q. E. D.**

---

If $ab$ is a square number, $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. (The converse of IX. 1.)  
For
$$a : b = a^2 : ab. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$
And $a^2$, $ab$ being square numbers, and therefore similar plane numbers, they have one mean proportional. [VIII. 18]  
Therefore $a$, $b$ also have one mean proportional. [VIII. 8]  
whence $a$, $b$ are similar plane numbers. [VIII. 20]

### PROPOSITION 3.

*If a cube number by multiplying itself make some number, the product will be cube.*

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0392.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0392.g1:B">$B$</span>;  
I say that <span data-ref="0392.g1:B">$B$</span> is cube.
