## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Converse Relationship**: Heath explicitly notes that Proposition 2 is "(The converse of IX. 1.)".
- **Mathematical Logic**: If $C = AB$ is square, setting $D = A^2$ yields $A : B = D : C$ (VII. 17). Since $D$ and $C$ are squares, they are similar plane numbers (VIII. 18 implies a mean proportional falls between them). By VIII. 8, a mean proportional also falls between $A$ and $B$, which by VIII. 20 implies $A, B$ are similar plane numbers.
- **Proposition 3 Introduction**: Proposition 3 introduces the multiplication of cube numbers: if $A = a^3$, $A^2 = a^6 = (a^2)^3$ is cube.
