## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Use of Unity in Proportions**: In the proof, Euclid establishes continued proportions from the unit: $1 : C = C : D = D : A$, showing that between 1 and $A$ there are two mean proportionals ($C, D$).
- **Application of VIII. 8 with Unity**: Heath remarks that while VIII. 8 was enunciated for two pairs of numbers, the argument holds equally when one number of a pair is unity ($1 : A = A : B$ implies two mean proportionals fall between $A$ and $B$).
- **Citing VII. Def. 20**: The definition of proportional numbers is applied directly from unit measurement relations.
