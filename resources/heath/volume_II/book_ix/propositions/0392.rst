:title: Proposition 3

| For let :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, the side of :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`, be taken, and let :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` by multiplying
| itself make :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | It is then manifest that :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` by multiplying
   | :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` has made :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`.

.. picture:: 0392.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 3 Diagram

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` by multiplying itself has
   | made :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` according to the units in itself.

.. subclause::

   | But further the unit also measures :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` according to the units
   | in it;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` has made :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` according to the units in :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` according to the units in it;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` to :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the unit is to :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the unit is to :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>`, and :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` to :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore between the unit and the number :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` two mean
   | proportional numbers :g:`$C$ <0392.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0392.g1:D>` have fallen in continued proportion.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$B$ <0392.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0392.g1:B>` according to the units in itself.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` according to the units in it;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0392.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   | But between the unit and :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` two mean proportional numbers
   | have fallen;

.. assertion::

   | therefore two mean proportional numbers will also fall between
   | :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0392.g1:B>`. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   | But, if two mean proportional numbers fall between two
   | numbers, and the first be cube, the second will also be cube.
   | :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0392.g1:A>` is cube;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0392.g1:B>` is also cube.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| The product of $a^3$ into itself, or $a^3 \cdot a^3$, is a cube.
| For

$$1 : a = a : a^2 = a^2 : a^3.$$

| Therefore between $1$ and $a^3$ there are two mean proportionals.
| Also

$$1 : a^3 = a^3 : a^3 \cdot a^3.$$

| Therefore two mean proportionals fall between $a^3$ and $a^3 \cdot a^3$. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`
| (It is true that VIII. 8 is only enunciated of two pairs of numbers, but the
| proof is equally valid if one number of one pair is unity.)
| And $a^3$ is a cube number:
| therefore $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is also cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`
