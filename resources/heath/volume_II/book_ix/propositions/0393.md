### PROPOSITION 4.

<p class="enunciation">If a cube number by multiplying a cube number make some<br />
number, the product will be cube.</p>

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying the cube number<br />
<span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> make <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> is cube.

![Proposition 4 Diagram](0393.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying<br />
itself make <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> is cube. <span class="justification">[IX. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiply-<br />
ing itself has made <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 17]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> are cube numbers,</p>

<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar solid numbers.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between<br />
<span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span>; <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">so that two mean proportional numbers will fall between <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> also. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> is cube;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> is also cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

The product of two cubes, say $a^3 \cdot b^3$, is a cube.<br />
For
$$a^3 : b^3 = a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3 \cdot b^3. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$
And two mean proportionals fall between $a^3$, $b^3$ which are similar solid<br />
numbers. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span><br />
Therefore two mean proportionals fall between $a^3 \cdot a^3$, $a^3 \cdot b^3$. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span><br />
But $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is a cube: <span class="justification">[IX. 3]</span><br />
therefore $a^3 \cdot b^3$ is a cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span>

### PROPOSITION 5.

<p class="enunciation">If a cube number by multiplying any number make a cube<br />
number, the multiplied number will also be cube.</p>

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying any number <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span><br />
make the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span> is cube.

itself make <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span> is cube. <span class="justification">[IX. 3]</span></p>
