### PROPOSITION 4.

*If a cube number by multiplying a cube number make some number, the product will be cube.*

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> make <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>;  
I say that <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> is cube.

![Proposition 4 Diagram](0393.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> is cube. [IX. 3]

And, since <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>, therefore, as <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span>. [VII. 17]

And, since <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> are cube numbers, <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar solid numbers.

Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between <span data-ref="0393.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:B">$B$</span>; [VIII. 19]  
so that two mean proportional numbers will fall between <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> also. [VIII. 8]

And <span data-ref="0393.g1:D">$D$</span> is cube;  
therefore <span data-ref="0393.g1:C">$C$</span> is also cube. [VIII. 23]

**Q. E. D.**

---

The product of two cubes, say $a^3 \cdot b^3$, is a cube.  
For
$$a^3 : b^3 = a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3 \cdot b^3. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$
And two mean proportionals fall between $a^3$, $b^3$ which are similar solid numbers. [VIII. 19]  
Therefore two mean proportionals fall between $a^3 \cdot a^3$, $a^3 \cdot b^3$. [VIII. 8]  
But $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is a cube: [IX. 3]  
therefore $a^3 \cdot b^3$ is a cube. [VIII. 23]

### PROPOSITION 5.

*If a cube number by multiplying any number make a cube number, the multiplied number will also be cube.*

For let the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying any number <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span> make the cube number <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>;  
I say that <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span> is cube.

![Proposition 5 Diagram](0393.g2.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span> is cube. [IX. 3]
