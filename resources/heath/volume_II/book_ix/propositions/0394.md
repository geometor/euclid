<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span>, and by<br />
multiplying <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 17]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span> are cube,</p>
<p class="assertion">they are similar solid numbers.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between<br />
<span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0393.g2:D">$D$</span> is to <span data-ref="0393.g2:C">$C$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span><br />
also. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0393.g2:A">$A$</span> is cube;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0393.g2:B">$B$</span> is also cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span></p>

---

If the product $a^3b$ is a cube number, $b$ is cube.<br />
By IX. 3, the product $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is a cube.<br />
And
$$a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3b = a^3 : b. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$
The first two terms are cubes, and therefore "similar solids"; therefore<br />
there are two mean proportionals between them. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span><br />
Therefore there are two mean proportionals between $a^3$, $b$. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span><br />
And $a^3$ is a cube:<br />
therefore $b$ is a cube number. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span>

### PROPOSITION 6.

<p class="enunciation">If a number by multiplying itself make a cube number, it<br />
will itself also be cube.</p>

For let the number <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make the cube<br />
number <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> is also cube.

![Proposition 6 Diagram](0394.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span> make <span data-ref="0394.g1:C">$C$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since, then, <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself<br />
has made <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span> has<br />
made <span data-ref="0394.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0394.g1:C">$C$</span> is cube.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span> according to the units in itself.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the unit also measures <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> according to the units in it.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore, as the unit is to <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 20]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span> has made <span data-ref="0394.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0394.g1:B">$B$</span> measures <span data-ref="0394.g1:C">$C$</span> according to the units in <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the unit also measures <span data-ref="0394.g1:A">$A$</span> according to the units in it.</p>
