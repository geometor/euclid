.. assertion::

   they are similar solid numbers.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between
   | :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$D$ <0393.g2:D>` is to :g:`$C$ <0393.g2:C>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>`
   | also. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0393.g2:A>` is cube;

.. assertion:: [VIII. 23] <VIII.23>

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0393.g2:B>` is also cube.

----

| If the product $a^3b$ is a cube number, $b$ is cube.
| By IX. 3, the product $a^3 \cdot a^3$ is a cube.
| And

$$a^3 \cdot a^3 : a^3b = a^3 : b. \hfill \text{[VII. 17]}$$

| The first two terms are cubes, and therefore "similar solids"; therefore
| there are two mean proportionals between them. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`
| Therefore there are two mean proportionals between $a^3$, $b$. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`
| And $a^3$ is a cube:
| therefore $b$ is a cube number. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`

----

PROPOSITION 6.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If a number by multiplying itself make a cube number, it will itself also be cube.

| For let the number :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself make the cube
| number :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`;
| I say that :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` is also cube.

.. picture:: 0394.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 6 Diagram

For let :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` make :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   | Since, then, :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself
   | has made :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` has
   | made :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>` is cube.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` according to the units in itself.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` according to the units in it.

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   Therefore, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0394.g1:B>` measures :g:`$C$ <0394.g1:C>` according to the units in :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But the unit also measures :g:`$A$ <0394.g1:A>` according to the units in it.
