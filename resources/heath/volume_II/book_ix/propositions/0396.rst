   | And, since :g:`$A$ <0395.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0395.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0395.g1:C>`,
   | and :g:`$A$ <0395.g1:A>` is the product of :g:`$D$ <0395.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0395.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$D$ <0395.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0395.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0395.g1:B>` has made :g:`$C$ <0395.g1:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$C$ <0395.g1:C>` is solid, and :g:`$D$ <0395.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0395.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0395.g1:B>` are its sides.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| Since a composite number is the product of two factors, the result of
| multiplying it by another number is to produce a number which is the
| product of three factors, i.e. a "solid number."

PROPOSITION 8.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be in continued proportion, the third from the unit will be square, as will also those which successively leave out one; the fourth will be cube, as will also all those which leave out two; and the seventh will be at once cube and square, as will also those which leave out five.

| Let there be as many numbers as we please, :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0396.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0396.g1:D>`,
| :g:`$E$ <0396.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0396.g1:F>`, beginning from an unit and in con-
| tinued proportion;
| I say that :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`, the third from the unit, is
| square, as are also all those which leave
| out one; :g:`$C$ <0396.g1:C>`, the fourth, is cube, as are
| also all those which leave out two; and
| :g:`$F$ <0396.g1:F>`, the seventh, is at once cube and
| square, as are also all those which leave out five.

.. subclause::

   For since, as the unit is to :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the unit measures the number :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` the same number
   | of times that :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`. :just:`[VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>`

.. subclause::

   | But the unit measures the number :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` according to the
   | units in it;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` also measures :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>` according to the units in :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0396.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>` is square.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0396.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0396.g1:D>` are in continued proportion, and :g:`$B$ <0396.g1:B>` is
   | square,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 22] <VIII.22>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0396.g1:D>` is also square.
