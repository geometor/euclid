### PROPOSITION 9.

<p class="enunciation">If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be<br />
in continued proportion, and the number after the unit be square,<br />
all the rest will also be square. And, if the number after the<br />
unit be cube, all the rest will also be cube.</p>

Let there be as many numbers as we please, <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0398.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:F">$F$</span>, beginning from an unit and in con-<br />
tinued proportion, and let <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span>, the number<br />
after the unit, be square ;<br />
I say that all the rest will also be square.

![Proposition 9 Diagram](0398.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Now it has been proved that <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>, the<br />
third from the unit, is square, as are also<br />
all those which leave out one ; <span class="justification">[ix. 8]</span></p>

I say that all the rest are also square.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:C">$C$</span> are in continued proportion,<br />
and <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> is square,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0398.g1:C">$C$</span> is also square. <span class="justification">[VIII. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0398.g1:D">$D$</span> are in continued proportion,<br />
and <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span> is square,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0398.g1:D">$D$</span> is also square. <span class="justification">[VIII. 22]</span></p>

Similarly we can prove that all the rest are also square.

Next, let <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> be cube ;<br />
I say that all the rest are also cube.

<p class="sub-clause">Now it has been proved that <span data-ref="0398.g1:C">$C$</span>, the fourth from the unit,<br />
is cube, as also are all those which leave out two ; <span class="justification">[ix. 8]</span></p>

I say that all the rest are also cube.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since, as the unit is to <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the unit measures <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> the same number of times as <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span><br />
measures <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the unit measures <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> according to the units in it ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> also measures <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span> according to the units in itself ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0398.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0398.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>
