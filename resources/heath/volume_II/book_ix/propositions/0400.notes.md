# Transcription Notes: Folio 0400 (Page 394)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 394 continues Book IX, Proposition 10 from page 393 (folio 0399).
- **Euclidean Text**: Contains the reduction-ad-absurdum proofs for both the square case (showing that if $C$ were square, $A$ would be square) and the cube case (showing that if $D$ were cube, $B$ would be cube, whence by IX. 6 $A$ would be cube).
- **Continuity**: The conclusion of the cube case carries over to page 395 (folio 0401).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Brackets**: Angle brackets `<and those which leave out one>` appear in line 2 of the page, restoring words omitted in certain manuscripts. Square brackets `[...]` are used by Heath around text he regards as likely spurious (notably referring to VIII. 26 converse).
- **Cross-References**: Lowercase references `[ix. 8]` and `[ix. 6]` are used by Heath for Book IX propositions, alongside uppercase `[VIII. 26, converse]` and `[VIII. 25]`.
