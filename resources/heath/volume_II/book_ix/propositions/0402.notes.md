# Transcription Notes: Folio 0402 (Page 396)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 396 contains the setting-out, proof, diagram, and Porism for Book IX, Proposition 11 (whose enunciation was given on page 395, folio 0401), followed by Heath's commentary.
- **Euclidean Proof**: Proves using VII. 15 (alternate proportion) that in $1, B, C, D, E$, $B$ measures $E$ according to $D$.
- **Porism**: Generalizes the reciprocal indexing: if the measuring number is the $r$-th from the unit, it measures the product according to the $r$-th number reckoned backward from the measured number.
- **Heath Commentary**: Notes that Euclid explicitly writes out only the base step $a_n = a \cdot a_{n-1}$, and supplies the full proof of the porism via *ex aequali* (VII. 14) to establish $a_n = a_r \cdot a_{n-r}$, connecting it to the law of indices $a^{m+n} = a^m \cdot a^n$.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Propositions Cited**: VII. 15 (alternando for numbers), VII. 14 (*ex aequali* in continued proportion).
- **Punctuation**: Preserves the em-dash punctuation after `proportional numbers.—` and `number before it.—`.
