## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 397 begins Book IX, Proposition 12.
- **Euclidean Text**: Contains the enunciation, setting-out, diagram, and the initial stages of the proof of Proposition 12: if $1, A, B, C, D$ are in continued proportion, any prime $E$ measuring $D$ must also measure $A$.
- **Proof Structure**: Assumes $E$ does not measure $A$, so $E, A$ are prime to one another (VII. 29). Writing $D = E \cdot F = A \cdot C$ (using IX. 11 and Porism), $A : E = F : C$ (VII. 19). Since $A, E$ are prime, they are least in that ratio (VII. 21), so $E$ measures $C$ (VII. 20). Setting $C = E \cdot G$ and noting $C = A \cdot B$ by IX. 11, the induction/reduction continues onto page 398.
