<p class="assertion">Therefore the product of <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to the product of<br />
<span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:G">$G$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0403.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0403.g1:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 19]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> are prime,<br />
primes are also least, <span class="justification">[VII. 21]</span><br />
and the least numbers measure those which have the same<br />
ratio with them the same number of times, the antecedent the<br />
antecedent and the consequent the consequent : <span class="justification">[VII. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

Let it measure it according to <span data-ref="0403.g1:H">$H$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0403.g1:H">$H$</span> has made <span data-ref="0403.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But further <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span> has also by multiplying itself made <span data-ref="0403.g1:B">$B$</span>; <span class="justification">[IX. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the product of <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:H">$H$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> is to <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0403.g1:H">$H$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 19]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> are prime,<br />
primes are also least, <span class="justification">[VII. 21]</span><br />
and the least measure those which have the same ratio the<br />
same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent and the<br />
consequent the consequent ; <span class="justification">[VII. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, as antecedent antecedent.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, again, it also does not measure it:</p>

<p class="assertion">which is impossible.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span> are not prime to one another.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore they are composite to one another.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But numbers composite to one another are measured by<br />
some number. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 14]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> is by hypothesis prime,<br />
and the prime is not measured by any number other than itself,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">so that <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">[But it also measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:D">$D$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0403.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0403.g1:D">$D$</span>.]</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that, by however many prime<br />
numbers <span data-ref="0403.g1:D">$D$</span> is measured, <span data-ref="0403.g1:A">$A$</span> will also be measured by the same.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

If $1, a, a_2, \dots a_n$ be a geometrical progression, and $a_n$ be measured by any<br />
prime number $p$, $a$ will also be measured by $p$.
