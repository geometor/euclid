:title: Proposition 12 (concluded)

.. assertion::

   Therefore the product of :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0403.g1:B>` is equal to the product of :g:`E <0403.g1:E>`, :g:`G <0403.g1:G>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`A <0403.g1:A>` is to :g:`E <0403.g1:E>`, so is :g:`G <0403.g1:G>` to :g:`B <0403.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` are prime, primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>` and the least numbers measure those which have the same ratio with them the same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent and the consequent the consequent : :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` measures :g:`B <0403.g1:B>`.

Let it measure it according to :g:`H <0403.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`H <0403.g1:H>` has made :g:`B <0403.g1:B>`.

.. subclause:: [IX. 8] <IX.8>

   But further :g:`A <0403.g1:A>` has also by multiplying itself made :g:`B <0403.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`E <0403.g1:E>`, :g:`H <0403.g1:H>` is equal to the square on :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` is to :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, so is :g:`A <0403.g1:A>` to :g:`H <0403.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` are prime, primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>` and the least measure those which have the same ratio the same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent and the consequent the consequent ; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` measures :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, as antecedent antecedent.

.. subclause::

   But, again, it also does not measure it:

.. assertion::

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>`, :g:`A <0403.g1:A>` are not prime to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore they are composite to one another.

.. subclause::

   But numbers composite to one another are measured by some number. :just:`[VII. Def. 14] <VII.def.14>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` is by hypothesis prime, and the prime is not measured by any number other than itself,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` measures :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, :g:`E <0403.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   so that :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` measures :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   [But it also measures :g:`D <0403.g1:D>`; therefore :g:`E <0403.g1:E>` measures :g:`A <0403.g1:A>`, :g:`D <0403.g1:D>`.]

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that, by however many prime numbers :g:`D <0403.g1:D>` is measured, :g:`A <0403.g1:A>` will also be measured by the same.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

If $1, a, a_2, \dots a_n$ be a geometrical progression, and $a_n$ be measured by any prime number $p$, $a$ will also be measured by $p$.
