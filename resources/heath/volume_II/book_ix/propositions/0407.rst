:title: Proposition 13 (continued)

| I say next that it will not be measured by any other prime
| except :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   | For, if any other prime number measures :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`,
   | and :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` measures :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   | that other will also measure :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>` ;
   | so that it will also measure :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` :just:`[IX. 12] <IX.12>`, which is prime, though it
   | is not the same with it :
   | which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` measures :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$E$ <0405.g1:E>` measures :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>` according to :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0405.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` has made :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>`.

.. subclause:: [IX. 11] <IX.11>

   But, further, :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` has also by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>` made :g:`$D$ <0405.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>` is equal to the product of :g:`$E$ <0405.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` is to :g:`$E$ <0405.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` to :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` measures :g:`$E$ <0405.g1:E>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` also measures :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>`.

Let it measure it according to :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly, then, we can prove that :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` is not the same with
   | any of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>`, and that it is measured by :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` measures :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>` according to :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` by multiplying :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` has made :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>`.

.. subclause:: [IX. 11] <IX.11>

   But, further, :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` has also by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>` made :g:`$C$ <0405.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>` is equal to the product of :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` is to :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` to :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` measures :g:`$F$ <0405.g1:F>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` also measures :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>`.

Let it measure it according to :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly then we can prove that :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>` is not the same
   | with :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` measures :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>` according to :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` by multiplying :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>` has made :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>`.

.. subclause:: [IX. 8] <IX.8>

   But further :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` has also by multiplying itself made :g:`$B$ <0405.g1:B>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>`, :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>` is equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`$H$ <0405.g1:H>` is to :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0405.g1:A>` to :g:`$G$ <0405.g1:G>`.
