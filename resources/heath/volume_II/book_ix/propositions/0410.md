### PROPOSITION 15.

*If three numbers in continued proportion be the least of those which have the same ratio with them, any two whatever added together will be prime to the remaining number.*

Let <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>, three numbers in continued proportion, be the least of those which have the same ratio with them;  
I say that any two of the numbers <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> whatever added together are prime to the remaining number, namely <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>; <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>; and further <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>.

![Proposition 15 diagram](0410.g1.svg)

For let two numbers <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, the least of those which have the same ratio with <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>, be taken. [^404_1]

It is then manifest that <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> has made <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, and, further, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> by multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>. [^404_2]

Now, since <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are least,  
they are prime to one another. [^404_3]

But, if two numbers be prime to one another,  
their sum is also prime to each; [^404_4]  
therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is also prime to each of the numbers <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

But further <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is also prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> are prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

But, if two numbers be prime to any number,  
their product is also prime to the other; [^404_5]  
so that the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;  
hence the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is also prime to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [^404_6]

But the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> together with the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>; [^404_7]  
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> together with the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is prime to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

And the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>,  
the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>,  
and the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span> added together are prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>.
