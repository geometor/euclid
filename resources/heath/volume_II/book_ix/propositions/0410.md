### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">If three numbers in continued proportion be the least of<br />
those which have the same ratio with them, any two whatever<br />
added together will be prime to the remaining number.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>, three numbers in continued proportion, be<br />
the least of those which have the same<br />
ratio with them ;<br />
I say that any two of the numbers<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> whatever added together are<br />
prime to the remaining number, namely<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> ; <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span> ; and further <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>.

![Proposition 15 diagram](0410.g1.svg)

For let two numbers <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, the least of those which<br />
have the same ratio with <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>, be taken. <span class="justification">[VIII. 2]</span>

<p class="assertion">It is then manifest that <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> by multiplying itself has made<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, and by multiplying <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> has made <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>, and, further, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> by<br />
multiplying itself has made <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 2]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are least,</p>
<p class="assertion">they are prime to one another. <span class="justification">[VII. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if two numbers be prime to one another,<br />
their sum is also prime to each ; <span class="justification">[VII. 28]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is also prime to each of the numbers <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But further <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is also prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> are prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if two numbers be prime to any number,<br />
their product is also prime to the other ; <span class="justification">[VII. 24]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> ;<br />
hence the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is also prime to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 25]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D F /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> together<br />
with the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> ; <span class="justification">[II. 3]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> together with the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is prime to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>,<br />
the product of <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
and the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0410.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:B">$B$</span> added together are prime to <span data-ref="0410.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>
