PROPOSITION 15.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If three numbers in continued proportion be the least of those which have the same ratio with them, any two whatever added together will be prime to the remaining number.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>`, three numbers in continued proportion, be
| the least of those which have the same
| ratio with them ;
| I say that any two of the numbers
| :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>` whatever added together are
| prime to the remaining number, namely
| :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>` ; :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>` to :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>` ; and further :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>` to :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0410.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 15 diagram

| For let two numbers :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />`, the least of those which
| have the same ratio with :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>`, be taken. :just:`[VIII. 2] <VIII.2>`

.. assertion::

   | It is then manifest that :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` by multiplying itself has made
   | :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, and by multiplying :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` has made :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`, and, further, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` by
   | multiplying itself has made :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>`. :just:`[VIII. 2] <VIII.2>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` are least,

.. assertion:: [VII. 22] <VII.22>

   they are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | But, if two numbers be prime to one another,
   | their sum is also prime to each ; :just:`[VII. 28] <VII.28>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DF$ <0410.g1:/ D F />` is also prime to each of the numbers :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But further :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` is also prime to :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DF$ <0410.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` are prime to :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | But, if two numbers be prime to any number,
   | their product is also prime to the other ; :just:`[VII. 24] <VII.24>`

.. assertion::

   | so that the product of :g:`$FD$ <0410.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` is prime to :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` ;
   | hence the product of :g:`$FD$ <0410.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` is also prime to the square
   | on :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[VII. 25] <VII.25>`

.. subclause::

   | But the product of :g:`$FD$ <0410.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` is the square on :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` together
   | with the product of :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` ; :just:`[II. 3] <II.3>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` together with the product of :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`,
   | :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` is prime to the square on :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | And the square on :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />` is :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`,
   | the product of :g:`$DE$ <0410.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` is :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>`,
   | and the square on :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:/ E F />` is :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0410.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0410.g1:B>` added together are prime to :g:`$C$ <0410.g1:C>`.
