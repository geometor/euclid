# Transcription Notes: Folio 0413 (Page 407)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Folio 0413 corresponds to page 407 of Heath's *Elements*, Volume II, Book IX.
- **Content**:
  - Concludes the Greek proof of **Proposition 17** (from folio 0412). By chained divisibility, $A$ measures each intermediate term and ultimately $D$, contradicting the premise that $A, D$ are coprime.
  - Heath's commentary on Proposition 17: generalizes to progression $a, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$ where $a, a_n$ coprime implies no integral fourth proportional $x$ exists for $a : a_2 = a_n : x$.
  - Begins **Proposition 18**: problem enunciation, setting out (*ekthesis*), and first case ($A, B$ coprime, impossible by IX. 16). Proof continues onto folio 0414.
- **Continuity**:
  - Follows folio 0412 (page 406).
  - Precedes folio 0414 (page 408).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Subscripts in Heath's Commentary**:
  - Heath uses numerical subscripts $a, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$ to represent general continued proportion.
- **Citations in Margins**:
  - `[VII. 21]`, `[VII. 20]`: coprimes are least, and least measure terms in same ratio.
  - `[VII. Def. 20]`: definition of proportional numbers (divisibility preserved across equal ratios).
  - `[IX. 16]`: cited at bottom right to dispatch case 1 of Proposition 18.
- **Problem Formulation**:
  - "to investigate whether it is possible to find..." (*σκοπεῖν, εἰ δυνατόν ἐστιν...*), characterizing an investigation / decision problem (*problema* with condition of possibility).
