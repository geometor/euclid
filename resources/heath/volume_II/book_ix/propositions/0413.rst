.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| If $a, a_2, a_3, \dots a_n$ be a geometrical progression, and $a, a_n$ are prime to one
| another, then $a, a_2, a_n$ can have no integral fourth proportional.

For, if possible, let

$$a : a_2 = a_n : x.$$

Therefore

$$a : a_n = a_2 : x,$$

and hence :just:`[VII. 20, 21]` *a* measures $a_2$.

| Therefore $a_2$ measures $a_3$, :just:`[VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>`
| and hence *a* measures $a_3$, and therefore also ultimately $a_n$.

Thus $a, a_n$ are both measured by *a* : which is contrary to the hypothesis.

----

PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Given two numbers, to investigate whether it is possible to find a third proportional to them.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0414.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0414.g1:B>` be the given two numbers, and let it be required
| to investigate whether it is possible to find a third proportional
| to them.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$A$ <0414.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0414.g1:B>` are either prime to one another or not.

.. assertion::

   | And, if they are prime to one another, it has been proved
   | that it is impossible to find a third proportional to them. :just:`[IX. 16] <IX.16>`
