# Transcription Notes: Folio 0414 (Page 408)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Folio 0414 corresponds to page 408 of Heath's *Elements*, Volume II, Book IX.
- **Content**:
  - Concludes **Proposition 18** (from folio 0413).
  - Handles the case where $A, B$ are not prime to one another. Sets $C = B^2$.
  - Subcase 1: $A$ measures $C$ according to $D \implies A \cdot D = C = B^2 \implies A : B = B : D$ (third proportional found).
  - Subcase 2: $A$ does not measure $C \implies$ impossible for third proportional to exist (by *reductio ad absurdum*).
  - Concludes with Q. E. D.
  - Followed by Heath's commentary: criterion for integral third proportional, showing condition (1) is subsumed by condition (2) ($a \mid b^2$).
- **Continuity**:
  - Follows folio 0413 (page 407).
  - Precedes folio 0415 (page 409), where Proposition 19 begins.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Arithmetic Equivalence**:
  - VII. 19: Four numbers are proportional iff product of extremes equals product of means ($A : B = B : D \iff A \cdot D = B^2$).
- **Commentary Insights**:
  - Heath demonstrates that the condition $a \mid b^2$ is necessary and sufficient, making the explicit coprimality test from IX. 16 mathematically redundant though historically didactic.
  - Progression terms written as $a, a(b/a), a(b/a)^2$.
