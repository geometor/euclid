# Transcription Notes: Folio 0415 (Page 409)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Folio 0415 corresponds to page 409 of Heath's *Elements*, Volume II, Book IX.
- **Content**:
  - Begins **Proposition 19**: Problem to investigate when three given numbers $A, B, C$ admit an integral fourth proportional.
  - Diagram (`0415.g1.png`) embedded between the setting out (*ekthesis*) and specification of cases.
  - Fourfold case enumeration:
    1. Not in continued proportion, extremes prime to one another.
    2. In continued proportion, extremes not prime to one another.
    3. Not in continued proportion, extremes not prime to one another.
    4. In continued proportion, extremes prime to one another.
  - Case 4 dispatched by IX. 17.
  - Case 1 begins with a dagger `†` designating a known interpolation / corrupt proof in the Greek transmission. The proof continues onto folio 0416.
- **Continuity**:
  - Follows folio 0414 (page 408).
  - Precedes folio 0416 (page 410), where the proof of Proposition 19 concludes.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Critical Signs**:
  - Dagger `†`: Inserted by Heath before "Next, let _A_, _B_, _C_ not be in continued proportion..." to bracket the corrupt text that Theon later excised.
- **Citations in Margins**:
  - `[IX. 17]`: Extreme terms coprime in continued proportion cannot have fourth proportional.
  - `[VII. 14]`: *Ex aequali* in numerical proportion ($A : B = C : D$ and $B : C = D : E \implies A : C = C : E$).
  - `[VII. 21]`, `[VII. 20]`: Least terms measure equimultiples.
