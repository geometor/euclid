:title: Proposition 19 (conclusion)

.. subclause::

   But it also measures itself ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` measures :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` which are prime to one another :
   | which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore it is not possible to find a fourth proportional
   | to :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>`.†

| Next, let :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` be again in continued proportion,
| but let :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` not be prime to one another.

| I say that it is possible to find a fourth proportional to
| them.

For let :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` make :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` either measures :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` or does not measure it.

First, let it measure it according to :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` has made :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But, further, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>` has also by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` made :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` is equal to the product of
   | :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` ;
   | therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` to :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` ; :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`
   | therefore :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` has been found a fourth proportional to :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>`.

Next, let :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` ;

| I say that it is impossible to find a fourth proportional number
| to :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>`.

For, if possible, let :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` have been found ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>`.
   | :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

.. subclause::

   But the product of :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` is :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` is also equal to :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>` has made :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` ;
   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>` according to :g:`$E$ <0415.g1:E>`,
   | so that :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But it also does not measure it :

.. assertion::

   which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore it is not possible to find a fourth proportional
   | number to :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` when :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` does not measure :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`.

| Next, let :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` not be in continued proportion, nor the
| extremes prime to one another.

And let :g:`$B$ <0415.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0415.g1:C>` make :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly then it can be proved that, if :g:`$A$ <0415.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0415.g1:D>`,
   | it is possible to find a fourth proportional to them, but, if it
   | does not measure it, impossible.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
