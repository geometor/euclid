### PROPOSITION 20.

<p class="enunciation">Prime numbers are more than any assigned multitude of<br />
prime numbers.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span> be the assigned prime numbers ;<br />
I say that there are more<br />
prime numbers than <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>.

For let the least number<br />
measured by <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span> be<br />
taken,<br />
and let it be <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> ;<br />
let the unit <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be added to <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

![Book IX, Proposition 20](0418.g1.svg)

Then <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is either prime or not.

First, let it be prime ;
<p class="assertion">then the prime numbers <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> have been found which<br />
are more than <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>

Next, let <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> not be prime ;
<p class="assertion">therefore it is measured by some prime number. <span class="justification">[VII. 31]</span></p>

Let it be measured by the prime number <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span>.

I say that <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span> is not the same with any of the numbers<br />
<span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>.

For, if possible, let it be so.

<p class="sub-clause">Now <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span> measure <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span> also will measure <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it also measures <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span>, being a number, will measure the remainder,<br />
the unit <span data-ref="0418.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> :<br />
which is absurd.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span> is not the same with any one of the numbers<br />
<span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And by hypothesis it is prime.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the prime numbers <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:G">$G$</span> have been found<br />
which are more than the assigned multitude of <span data-ref="0418.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0418.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
