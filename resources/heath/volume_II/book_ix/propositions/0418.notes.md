- **Textual Authority**: Heiberg maintains ms. P's uncorrected text over Theon's editorial excisions.
- **Mathematical Significance**: Proposition 20 proves the infinitude of primes without modern proof by contradiction (reductio *ad absurdum* on the entirety of all primes). Instead, it constructs for *any* finite set of primes an additional prime outside the set.
- **Propositions Cited**: Book VII, Proposition 31.
