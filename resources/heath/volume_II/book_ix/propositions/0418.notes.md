## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Text Structure**: The folio opens with the final two lines of Heath's commentary on Proposition 19 regarding text critical authority (manuscript P vs. Theon's recension). It is followed by the complete enunciation, setting-out, construction, and two-case proof of Proposition 20.
- **Mathematical Significance**: Proposition 20 is Euclid's celebrated proof of the infinitude of prime numbers. Rather than assuming the existence of all primes and deriving a contradiction, Euclid proves constructively that given any finite collection of primes $\{A, B, C\}$, a new prime (either $EF = \text{LCM}(A, B, C) + 1$ or a prime factor $G$ dividing $EF$) can always be found outside the given set.
