<p class="sub-clause">For, since each of the numbers <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is odd,<br />

### PROPOSITION 23.

<p class="enunciation">If as many odd numbers as we please be added together,<br />
and their multitude be odd, the whole will also be odd.</p>

For let as many odd numbers as we please, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,<br />
the multitude of which is odd,[^414_1]<br />
be added together ;<br />
I say that the whole <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is<br />
also odd.

![Book IX, Proposition 23](0420.g2.svg)

Let the unit <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be subtracted from <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span> ;

<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C E /">$CE$</span> is even. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 7]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is also even ; <span class="justification">[IX. 22]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also even. <span class="justification">[IX. 21]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is an unit.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is odd. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 7]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

[^414_1]: 3. Literally “let there be as many numbers as we please, of which *let the multitude be odd*.” This form, natural in Greek, is awkward in English.

### PROPOSITION 24.

<p class="enunciation">If from an even number an even number be subtracted, the<br />
remainder will be even.</p>

For from the even number <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let the even number <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ B C /">$BC$</span><br />
be subtracted :<br />
I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is even.

![Book IX, Proposition 24](0420.g3.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is even, it has a half<br />
part. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 6]</span></p>
