For, since each of the numbers <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is odd, if an unit be subtracted from each, each of the remainders will be even; [VII. Def. 7]  
so that the sum of them will be even. [IX. 21]

![Book IX, Proposition 22](0420.g1.svg)

But the multitude of the units is also even.  
Therefore the whole <span data-ref="0420.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also even. [IX. 21]

<p align="right">Q. E. D.</p>

### PROPOSITION 23.

*If as many odd numbers as we please be added together, and their multitude be odd, the whole will also be odd.*

For let as many odd numbers as we please, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the multitude of which is odd,[^414_1] be added together;  
I say that the whole <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also odd.

![Book IX, Proposition 23](0420.g2.svg)

Let the unit <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be subtracted from <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;  
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ C E /">$CE$</span> is even. [VII. Def. 7]  
But <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is also even; [IX. 22]  
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also even. [IX. 21]  
And <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is an unit.  
Therefore <span data-ref="0420.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is odd. [VII. Def. 7]

<p align="right">Q. E. D.</p>

[^414_1]: 3. Literally “let there be as many numbers as we please, of which *let the multitude be odd*.” This form, natural in Greek, is awkward in English.

### PROPOSITION 24.

*If from an even number an even number be subtracted, the remainder will be even.*

For from the even number <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let the even number <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be subtracted:  
I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is even.

![Book IX, Proposition 24](0420.g3.svg)

For, since <span data-ref="0420.g3:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is even, it has a half part. [VII. Def. 6]
