### PROPOSITION 27.

<p class="enunciation">If from an odd number an even number be subtracted, the<br />
remainder will be odd.</p>

For from the odd number <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let the even number <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be<br />
subtracted ;<br />
I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is odd.

![Book IX, Proposition 27](0422.g1.svg)

Let the unit <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be subtracted ;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ B D /">$DB$</span> is even. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 7]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also even ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is even. <span class="justification">[IX. 24]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0422.g1:/ A C /">$CA$</span> is odd. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 7]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

### PROPOSITION 28.

<p class="enunciation">If an odd number by multiplying an even number make<br />
some number, the product will be even.</p>

For let the odd number <span data-ref="0422.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying the even number<br />
<span data-ref="0422.g2:B">$B$</span> make <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span> ;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span> is even.

![Book IX, Proposition 28](0422.g2.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0422.g2:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0422.g2:B">$B$</span> has<br />
made <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span> is made up of as many numbers equal to <span data-ref="0422.g2:B">$B$</span> as<br />
there are units in <span data-ref="0422.g2:A">$A$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0422.g2:B">$B$</span> is even ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span> is made up of even numbers.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if as many even numbers as we please be added<br />
together, the whole is even. <span class="justification">[IX. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0422.g2:C">$C$</span> is even.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
