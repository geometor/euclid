## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Text Structure**: Contains Proposition 27 (the fourth of the subtraction-parity theorems), Proposition 28 (odd $\times$ even), and the opening of Proposition 29 (odd $\times$ odd).
- **Mathematical Significance**: 
  - IX. 27: $\text{odd} - \text{even} = \text{odd}$. Proven by taking a unit $AD$ from $AB$, showing $DB$ is even, $CD = DB - BC$ is even by IX. 24, hence $CA = CD + AD$ is odd (VII. Def. 7).
  - IX. 28: $\text{odd} \times \text{even} = \text{even}$. Connects multiplication (defined as repeated addition in VII. Def. 15) with the sum of even numbers (IX. 21).
  - IX. 29: $\text{odd} \times \text{odd} = \text{odd}$. Begun on this page, analyzing $C$ as an odd multitude of odd numbers.
- **Cross-References**: VII. Def. 7, VII. Def. 15, IX. 21, IX. 24.
