### PROPOSITION 30.

<p class="enunciation">If an odd number measure an even number, it will also<br />
measure the half of it.</p>

For let the odd number <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> measure the even number <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span>;<br />
I say that it will also measure the half<br />
of it.

![Book IX, Proposition 30](0423.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
let it measure it according to <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span>;</p>

I say that <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span> is not odd.

For, if possible, let it be so.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span> according to <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span> has made <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span> is made up of odd numbers the multitude<br />
of which is odd.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span> is odd: <span class="justification">[IX. 23]</span><br />
which is absurd, for by hypothesis it is even.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span> is not odd;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0423.g1:C">$C$</span> is even.</p>

<p class="assertion">Thus <span data-ref="0423.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0423.g1:B">$B$</span> an even number of times.</p>

<p class="assertion">For this reason then it also measures the half of it.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

### PROPOSITION 31.

<p class="enunciation">If an odd number be prime to any number, it will also be<br />
prime to the double of it.</p>

For let the odd number <span data-ref="0423.g2:A">$A$</span> be prime to any number <span data-ref="0423.g2:B">$B$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0423.g2:C">$C$</span> be double of <span data-ref="0423.g2:B">$B$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0423.g2:A">$A$</span> is prime to <span data-ref="0423.g2:C">$C$</span>.

![Book IX, Proposition 31](0423.g2.svg)

For, if they are not prime<br />
to one another, some number<br />
will measure them.
