## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Text Structure**: Concludes Proposition 29 by application of IX. 23; provides Proposition 30 in full via reductio ad absurdum; opens Proposition 31 with its indirect proof setup.
- **Mathematical Significance**: 
  - IX. 29: Product of two odd numbers is odd, since it is an odd multitude of odd numbers (IX. 23).
  - IX. 30: If an odd number divides an even number, it divides its half. Proof: if $A \times C = B$, $C$ cannot be odd (for then $B$ would be odd by IX. 29/IX. 23), so $C$ is even, say $C = 2K$, whence $B = 2(AK)$ and $A$ measures $B/2$.
  - IX. 31: If an odd number is coprime to $B$, it is coprime to $2B$.
- **Cross-References**: VII. Def. 15, IX. 23.
