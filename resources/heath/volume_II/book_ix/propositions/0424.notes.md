## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Text Structure**: Concludes Proposition 31 using Proposition 30 to derive a contradiction with the coprimality of $A$ and $B$. Contains Proposition 32 in full.
- **Mathematical Significance**: 
  - IX. 31: Completes the proof that if $D$ divides $A$ (odd) and $C = 2B$ (even), $D$ must be odd, hence by IX. 30 it divides $B$, contradicting $\gcd(A, B) = 1$.
  - IX. 32: Classifies powers of two ($2^n$, where $A=2, B=4, C=8, D=16\dots$). Citing IX. 13, because the series starts with prime 2 after unity, no prime or other divisor exists other than the preceding powers of 2. Since all factors are even, the number is "even-times even only" (*ἀρτιάκις ἄρτιος μόνον*), having no odd factors (VII. Def. 8).
- **Cross-References**: VII. Def. 8, IX. 13, IX. 30.
