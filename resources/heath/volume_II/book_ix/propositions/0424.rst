Let a number measure them, and let it be :g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$A$ <0423.g2:A>` is odd;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>` is also odd.

.. subclause::

   | And since :g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>` which is odd measures :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>`,
   | and :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>` is even,

.. assertion:: [IX. 30] <IX.30>

   therefore [:g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>`] will measure the half of :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>` also.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$B$ <0423.g2:B>` is half of :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>` measures :g:`$B$ <0423.g2:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But it also measures :g:`$A$ <0423.g2:A>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0423.g2:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0423.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0423.g2:B>` which are prime to one another:
   | which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0423.g2:A>` cannot but be prime to :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0423.g2:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0423.g2:C>` are prime to one another.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

PROPOSITION 32.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Each of the numbers which are continually doubled beginning from a dyad is even-times even only.

| For let as many numbers as we please, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, have been
| continually doubled beginning
| from the dyad :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`;
| I say that :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` are even-
| times even only.

.. picture:: 0424.g1.svg
   :alt: Book IX, Proposition 32

.. assertion::

   | Now that each of the
   | numbers :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` is even-times even is manifest; for it is
   | doubled from a dyad.

I say that it is also even-times even only.

For let an unit be set out.

.. subclause::

   | Since then as many numbers as we please beginning from
   | an unit are in continued proportion,
   | and the number :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>` after the unit is prime,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, the greatest of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>`, will not
   | be measured by any other number except :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>`. :just:`[IX. 13] <IX.13>`

.. subclause::

   And each of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0424.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>` is even;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 8] <VII.def.8>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0424.g1:D>` is even-times even only.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly we can prove that each of the numbers :g:`$B$ <0424.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0424.g1:C>` is
   | even-times even only.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
