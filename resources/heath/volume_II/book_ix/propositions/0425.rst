:title: Proposition 33, Proposition 34

| See the notes on VII. Deff. 8 to 11 for a discussion of the difficulties
| shown by :person:`Iamblichus <iamblichus>` to be involved by the Euclidean definitions of “even-
| times even,” “even-times odd” and “odd-times even.”

----

PROPOSITION 33.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a number have its half odd, it is even-times odd only.

| For let the number :g:`$A$ <0425.g1:A>` have its half odd;
| I say that :g:`$A$ <0425.g1:A>` is even-times odd only.

.. picture:: 0425.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 Diagram

.. assertion::

   | Now that it is even-times odd is
   | manifest; for the half of it, being odd,
   | measures it an even number of times. :just:`[VII. Def. 9] <VII.def.9>`

I say next that it is also even-times odd only.

.. subclause::

   For, if :g:`$A$ <0425.g1:A>` is even-times even also,

.. assertion::

   | it will be measured by an even number according to an even
   | number; :just:`[VII. Def. 8] <VII.def.8>`

.. assertion::

   | so that the half of it will also be measured by an even number
   | though it is odd:
   | which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0425.g1:A>` is even-times odd only.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

PROPOSITION 34.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a number neither be one of those which are continually doubled from a dyad, nor have its half odd, it is both even- times even and even-times odd.

| For let the number :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>` neither be one of those doubled
| from a dyad, nor have its half odd;
| I say that :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>` is both even-times even
| and even-times odd.

.. picture:: 0425.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 34 Diagram

.. assertion::

   | Now that :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>` is even-times even is manifest;
   | for it has not its half odd. :just:`[VII. Def. 8] <VII.def.8>`

I say next that it is also even-times odd.

.. subclause::

   | For, if we bisect :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>`, then bisect its half, and do this con-
   | tinually, we shall come upon some odd number which will
   | measure :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>` according to an even number.

.. subclause::

   | For, if not, we shall come upon a dyad,
   | and :g:`$A$ <0425.g2:A>` will be among those which are doubled from a dyad:
   | which is contrary to the hypothesis.
