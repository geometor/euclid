:title: Proposition 36 (cont.)

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the product of :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` is equal to the product of
   | :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`. :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

.. subclause::

   And the product of :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` is :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>` is also :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>` has made :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` ;
   | therefore :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>` measures :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` according to the units in :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>` is a dyad ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` is double of :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`.

.. picture:: 0428.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 36 Diagram

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` are continuously double of each other ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`, :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` are continuously proportional in
   | double proportion.

| Now let there be subtracted from the second :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />` and the
| last :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` the numbers :g:`$HN$ <0428.g1:/ H N />`, :g:`$FO$ <0428.g1:/ F O />`, each equal to the first :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the excess of the second is to the first, so is the
   | excess of the last to all those before it. :just:`[IX. 35] <IX.35>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$NK$ <0428.g1:/ K N />` is to :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$OG$ <0428.g1:/ G O />` to :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$KH$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$NK$ <0428.g1:/ K N />` is equal to :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$OG$ <0428.g1:/ G O />` is also equal to :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$FO$ <0428.g1:/ F O />` is also equal to :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`,
   | and :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` is equal to :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` and the unit.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the whole :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` is equal to :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>` and
   | :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` and the unit ;
   | and it is measured by them.

| I say also that :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />` will not be measured by any other
| number except :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>` and the unit.

| For, if possible, let some number :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` measure :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />`,
| and let :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` not be the same with any of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`,
| :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0428.g1:M>`.

.. subclause::

   | And, as many times as :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` measures :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />`, so many units let
   | there be in :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>` by multiplying :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` has made :g:`$FG$ <0428.g1:/ F G />`.
