.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` are continuously proportional
   | beginning from an unit,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` will not be measured by any other number except
   | :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`. :just:`[IX. 13] <IX.13>`

.. subclause::

   | And, by hypothesis, :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` is not the same with any of the
   | numbers :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` will not measure :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` is to :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` to :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>` ;

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20] <VII.def.20>

   therefore neither does :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` measure :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>`.

.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` is prime ;
   | and any prime number is prime to any number which it does
   | not measure. :just:`[VII. 29] <VII.29>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | But primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>`
   | and the least numbers measure those which have the same
   | ratio the same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent
   | and the consequent the consequent ; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`
   | and, as :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` is to :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>`, so is :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` to :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` measures :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` the same number of times that :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>`
   | measures :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   | :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>` is the same with one of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0428.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`.

| :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>` be taken beginning from :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`, :g:`$HK$ <0428.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>` are in the same ratio with :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0428.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion:: [VII. 14] <VII.14>

   therefore, *ex aequali*, as :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>` is to :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>` to :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the product of :g:`$B$ <0428.g1:B>`, :g:`$L$ <0428.g1:L>` is equal to the product of
   | :g:`$D$ <0428.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0428.g1:E>`. :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

.. subclause::

   | therefore the product of :g:`$Q$ <0428.g1:Q>`, :g:`$P$ <0428.g1:P>` is also equal to the product of

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>
