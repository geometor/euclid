Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Key Definitions on this Folio**:

  - **Def. 3**: **ratio** (λόγος) as a relation in respect of size (κατὰ πηλικότητα ποιὰ σχέσις) between two homogeneous magnitudes.
  - **Def. 4**: **have a ratio** (λόγον ἔχειν), often associated with the Archimedean axiom of comparability.
  - **Def. 5**: **be in the same ratio** (ἐν τῷ αὐτῷ λόγῳ), Eudoxus's celebrated equimultiple criterion applicable to commensurable and incommensurable magnitudes alike.
  - **Def. 6**: **proportional** (ἀνάλογον).
  - **Def. 7**: **have a greater ratio** (μείζονα λόγον ἔχειν).

  - **Def. 10**: **triplicate ratio** (τριπλασίων λόγος); note Heath's angle-bracketed editorial insertion ``< continuously >`` rendering the Greek ``<ἑξῆς>`` / implied continuous proportion.

  - **Def. 12**: **Alternate ratio** (ἐναλλὰξ λόγος, *alternando*).
  - **Def. 13**: **Inverse ratio** (ἀνάπαλιν λόγος, *invertendo*).
