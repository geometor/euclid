## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Definitions 14–18**:
  - **Def. 14**: **Composition of a ratio** (σύνθεσις λόγου, *componendo*): $(a+b):b$.
  - **Def. 15**: **Separation of a ratio** (διαίρεσις λόγου, *separando* / *dividendo*): $(a-b):b$.
  - **Def. 16**: **Conversion of a ratio** (ἀναστροφὴ λόγου, *convertendo*): $a:(a-b)$.
  - **Def. 17**: Ratio **ex aequali** (δι' ἴσου λόγος).
  - **Def. 18**: **Perturbed proportion** (τεταραγμένη ἀναλογία).
- **Greek Text of Definition 1**:
  - `Μέρος ἐστὶ μέγεθος μεγέθους τὸ ἔλασσον τοῦ μείζονος, ὅταν καταμετρῇ τὸ μεῖζον.`
- **Lexical Distinction**:
  - Heath contrasts the restricted sense of `μέρος` (*submultiple* or *aliquot part*, as here and in VII. Def. 3, vs. plural *parts* in VII. Def. 4) with the general sense in Common Notion 5 ("the whole is greater than the part"), citing Aristotle, *Metaphysics* Δ. 25, 1023 b 12 (`τὸ ποσόν`, `τὰ καταμετροῦντα`).
