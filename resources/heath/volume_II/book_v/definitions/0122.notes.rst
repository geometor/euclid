Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Text of Definitions 2 & 3**:

  - **Def. 2**: ``Πολλαπλάσιον δὲ τὸ μεῖζον τοῦ ἐλάττονος, ὅταν καταμετρῆται ὑπὸ τοῦ ἐλάττονος.``
  - **Def. 3**: ``Λόγος ἐστὶ δύο μεγεθῶν ὁμογενῶν ἡ κατὰ πηλικότητα ποιὰ σχέσις.``

- **Lexical Analysis of** ``πηλικότης``:

  - De Morgan (following Wallis and Gregory) rendered ``πηλικότης`` as *quantuplicity*, relying on the interpolated Book VI Def. 5 and Eutocius (Archimedes, ed. Heiberg, III. p. 120).
  - Heath refutes this by examining classical usage of ``πηλίκος`` (*how great*) in Aristotle (*Eth. Nic.* V. 10, 1134 b 11: ``ἕως ἂν ᾖ πηλίκον``), Nicomachus (*Introd. Arith.* I. 2, 5, p. 5, ed. Hoche), and Iamblichus (*in Nicom.* p. 8, 3–5), where ``πηλίκος``/``πηλίκον`` refers to continuous *magnitude* (the subject of geometry) as opposed to ``ποσός``/``ποσόν`` referring to discrete *multitude* (the subject of arithmetic).
