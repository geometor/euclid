Transcription Notes: Folio 0123 (Page 117)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio**: ``0123`` (Page 117)
- **Section**: Book V, Note on Definition 3 (continued)
- **Content**: Concludes the philological argument for translating ``πηλικότης`` as *size* (the attribute possessed by a concrete *magnitude*, ``μέγεθος``), evaluates the authenticity and metaphysical purpose of Definition 3 (citing Simson, Hankel, Heiberg, Isaac Barrow, and De Morgan), and begins tracing the historical development of ``λόγος`` (*ratio*) from commensurable ratios (Eucl. X. 5) to incommensurables (``ἄλογος``, vs. ``ῥητός``).
- **Continuity**: Continues from the hyphenated word ``*con-*`` on Folio ``0122`` (Page 116) (``*tinuous*, and ποσόν...``) and continues onto Folio ``0124`` (Page 118) with *"Euclid himself shows us how we are to set about finding the ratio..."*.

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Citations & Terms**:

  - Ptolemy, *Syntaxis*, ed. Heiberg, p. 31: ``περὶ τῆς πηλικότητος τῶν ἐν τῷ κύκλῳ εὐθειῶν`` ("concerning the size of chords in a circle").
  - Manuscript variant in V. Def. 3: ``πρὸς ἄλληλα`` ("to one another"), rejected by Heiberg as a Theonic interpolation.
  - Parallel with Book I Def. 4: ``εὐθεῖα γραμμή`` (straight line).
  - ``λόγος`` (expressible ratio), ``ἄλογος`` (irrational / having no ratio in the primitive sense), ``ῥητός`` (rational).

- **Scholarly Citations**:

  - Isaac Barrow, *Lectiones Cantabrigienses* (London, 1684, Lect. III of 1666), defending V. Def. 3 as a "metaphysical, rather than a mathematical definition" inserted for completeness and ornament.
