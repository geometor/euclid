Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio**: ``0124`` (Page 118)
- **Section**: Book V, Note on Definition 3 (continued, touching also upon Definition 4)
- **Content**: Explains the Euclidean algorithm (``ἀνθυφαίρεσις``, Eucl. X. 3) for finding the greatest common measure of two commensurable magnitudes $A, B$ via remainders $R_1, R_2, \dots$; describes how the non-termination of this process for incommensurables motivates the broader Book V conception of ``λόγος`` (*relative magnitude*, supported by V.
