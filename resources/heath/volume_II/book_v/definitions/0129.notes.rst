Transcription Notes: Folio 0129 (Page 123)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio 0129 (Page 123)** continues Heath's exposition of Augustus De Morgan's analysis of **Book V, Definition 5**, establishing both the necessity and sufficiency of Euclid's equimultiple criterion via the scale-model colonnade/railing analogy.
- **Preceding Folio Continuity** (``0128.rst``, **p. 122**): Continues mid-sentence from ``"...the second railing is pushed forward by $\frac{2}{1000}C$, the third by $\frac{3}{1000}C$, and so on, so that"`` at the bottom of page 122 with ``"the railings after the thousandth are pushed forward by more than $C$; i.e. the order with respect to the columns is disarranged."``
- **Following Folio Continuity** (``0130.rst``, **p. 124**): Ends mid-sentence at the bottom of page 123 (``"Suppose that the distribution of the railings among the"``), continuing on page 124 with ``"columns should be found to agree in the model and the original as far as the millionth railing."``

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Equimultiple Distribution Formula**:

  - Reformulates V. Def. 5 in terms of interval distribution: for any positive integers $m, n$, if $nB < mA < (n+1)B$, then $nD < mC < (n+1)D$ (along with exact coincidence when $mA = nB$).

- **Asymptotic Sufficiency of the Criterion**:

  - Notes that if the equimultiple condition holds for all multiples from and after some threshold (e.g., $100A$ and $100C$), it automatically holds for all smaller multiples (illustrated by scaling $5A, 5C$ by $50$ to $250A, 250C$ lying between $678B, 679B$ and $678D, 679D$, which upon division by $50$ places both $5A$ and $5C$ between $13\frac{28}{50}$ and $13\frac{29}{50}$ of $B$ and $D$, and *a fortiori* between $13$ and $14$ of $B$ and $D$).
