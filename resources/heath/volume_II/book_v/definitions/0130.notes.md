## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **De Morgan's Proof of the Parallel Intercept Theorem (cf. Eucl. VI. 2)**:
  - Demonstrates how universal quantification over an infinite family of equimultiples ($m \cdot OA$ vs. $n \cdot Oa$ and $m \cdot OB$ vs. $n \cdot Ob$) is established in a single stroke by the non-intersection property of parallel lines ($A_m B_m$ cannot cross $a_n b_n$ or $a_{n+1} b_{n+1}$).
- **Comparison with Weierstrass and Dedekind**:
  - Cites Max Simon (*Euclid und die sechs planimetrischen Bücher*, p. 110) and H. G. Zeuthen on the equivalence between V. Def. 5 and Weierstrass's definition of equal real numbers.
  - Introduces the correspondence between Euclid's definition of equal ratios and Richard Dedekind's theory of irrational numbers (*Stetigkeit und irrationale Zahlen*, 1872).
