Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Definition 6** (``ἀνάλογον``): Heath analyzes the grammar and etymology of ``ἀνάλογον`` (from ``ἀνὰ λόγον``, "in proportion"), noting its usage as an indeclinable adjective (``αἱ τέσσαρες εὐθεῖαι ἀνάλογον ἔσονται``, ``τρίγωνα τὰς πλευρὰς ἀνάλογον ἔχοντα``) and as an adverb (``ἀνάλογον ἄρα ἐστὶν...``, ``ἡ μέση ἀνάλογον (εὐθεῖα)``), and defends "proportional" against Max Simon's criticism. He also notes that the imperative ``καλείσθω`` ("let ... be called") suggests the definition was formulated by Euclid himself.
- **Definition 7 (Greater Ratio)**: Gives the Greek text of Euclid V. Def. 7 (``Ὅταν δὲ τῶν ἰσάκις πολλαπλασίων...``) and introduces De Morgan's observation on the practical test of disproportion versus proportion.
