## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Order Assumptions on Equimultiples**: Heath explicitly isolates the two order axioms needed to complete the proof that $A:B > C:D$ cannot simultaneously satisfy two incompatible equimultiple criteria:
  1. According as $X > = < Y$, $rX > = < rY$ (Simson's Axioms 1 and 3).
  2. According as $rX > = < sX$, $rY > = < sY$ (used by Simson in his proof of V. 18).
- **Definition 8 Greek Text & Variant**:
  - **Ἀναλογία δὲ ἐν τρισὶν ὅροις ἐλαχίστη ἐστίν** (Peyrard's MS., followed by Heiberg and Camerer) vs. the variant reading **ἐλαχίστοις** ("consists in three terms *at least*").
  - Heath defends the genuineness of Def. 8 against Hankel's suspicion of interpolation by citing Aristotle's *Nicomachean Ethics* (V. 6, 7, 1131 b 5, 9 and 1131 a 31) on **ὅρος** ("term") and the distinction between **διῃρημένη** (*discrete*) and **συνεχής** (*continuous*, or **συνημμένη** in Nicomachus) proportions, as well as the opening words of Definition 9.
