## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Interpolated Definition of "Ordered Proportion" (τεταγμένη ἀναλογία)**:
  - Post-Theonic interpolation between Def. 17 and Def. 18 intended to contrast the ordinary case of *ex aequali* with *perturbed proportion* (**τεταραγμένη ἀναλογία**), though redundant after Def. 17 and never used.
- **Simson's Four Axioms for Book V**:
  1. $X = Y \implies mX = mY$
  2. $mX = mY \implies X = Y$
  3. $X > Y \implies mX > mY$
  4. $mX > mY \implies X > Y$
  - These four explicit axioms supply the foundational properties of multiples of magnitudes used tacitly by Euclid throughout Book V (and noted by Heath on p. 131).
