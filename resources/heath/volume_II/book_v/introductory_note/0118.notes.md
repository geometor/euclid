## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terminology**:
  - `ἀναλογία` (*analogia*): proportion *par excellence*, initially associated by the Pythagoreans with the geometric mean.
- **Mathematical Formula**:
  - The "most perfect proportion" (*harmonic* proportion of four terms) cited from Iamblichus (*Comm. on Nicomachus*, p. 118):
    $$a : \frac{a+b}{2} = \frac{2ab}{a+b} : b$$
    relating the extreme terms $a, b$ with their arithmetic mean $\frac{a+b}{2}$ and harmonic mean $\frac{2ab}{a+b}$.
- **Historical & Scholarly Citations**:
  - Anonymous scholiast to Book V (Euclid, ed. Heiberg, Vol. V, p. 280).
  - Iamblichus, *Comm. on Nicomachus*, p. 118 (attributing the four-term harmonic proportion to the Babylonians and its introduction into Greece to Pythagoras).
  - Paul Tannery, *La Géométrie grecque*, p. 98 (on the "veritable logical scandal" caused by the discovery of incommensurability).
