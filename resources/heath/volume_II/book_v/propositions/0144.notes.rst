Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Distributivity of Multiplication over Addition of Magnitudes**: Proposition 1 establishes $ma + mb + mc + \dots = m(a + b + c + \dots)$ by partitioning each multiple $AB = m \cdot E$ and $CD = m \cdot F$ into unit-multiple components ($AG, GB = E$ and $CH, HD = F$) and pairing them one-to-one.
- **Idiom of Addition by Juxtaposition**: Note Euclid's characteristic phrasing: "since $AG$ is equal to $E$, and $CH$ to $F$, therefore $AG$ is equal to $E$, and $AG$, $CH$ to $E$, $F$." Rather than invoking Common Notion 2 directly with "let $CH, F$ be added to each," Euclid states the component equalities and combines the magnitudes by comma-separated juxtaposition ($AG, CH$ and $E, F$).
