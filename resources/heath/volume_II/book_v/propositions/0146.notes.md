## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Distributivity of Scalar Multiplication over Integer Addition**: Heath points out that while Proposition 2 formally states that $ma + na$ is the same multiple of $a$ that $mb + nb$ is of $b$, the proof's unit-by-unit decomposition practically establishes $ma + na = (m + n)a$.
- **Simson's Corollary to V. 2**: Extends Proposition 2 by finite induction to any finite sum of multiples $ma + na + pa + \dots = (m + n + p + \dots)a$, which is frequently needed when applying Proposition 2 to multipliers $m > 2$ (as in Proposition 3 when $EF$ is divided into more than two parts equal to $A$).
- **Use of *ex aequali* ($\delta\iota\text{'} \text{ }\ἴ\sigma\omicron\upsilon$) in Proposition 3**: Appears italicized in the enunciation before any reference to ratios of four magnitudes in Def. 17; Heath discusses Heiberg's remark on this terminology on Page 141.
