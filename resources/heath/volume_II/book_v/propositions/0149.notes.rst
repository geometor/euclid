2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Logical Structure of the Def. 5 Invocations**:

  1. *Direct application*: From the hypothesis $A : B = C : D$, any equimultiples $K, L$ of $A, C$ and $M, N$ of $B, D$ satisfy $K \gtreqqless M \iff L \gtreqqless N$. Here $K, L$ and $M, N$ are particular instances of equimultiples ($pm \cdot A, pm \cdot C$ and $qn \cdot B, qn \cdot D$).
  2. *Converse application*: Since $p$ and $q$ were chosen arbitrarily, $K, L$ and $M, N$ represent *any equimultiples whatever* of $E, F$ and $G, H$. Because $K \gtreqqless M \iff L \gtreqqless N$ holds for all such multipliers, V. Def. 5 establishes that $E : G = F : H$ ($mA : nB = mC : nD$).

- **Greek Textual Articulation (Simson, Briggs, and Gregory on ἃ ἔτυχεν)**: Simson objected to calling $M, N = qnB, qnD$ "chance equimultiples" of $B, D$ because they are multiples of $nB, nD$.
