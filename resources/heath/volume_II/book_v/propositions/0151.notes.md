## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **De Morgan on Unity as a Multiple**: Heath highlights De Morgan's critique of Robert Simson's grammatical purism: because Greek $\pi\omicron\lambda\lambda\alpha\pi\lambda\\sigma\iota\omicron\nu$ and Latin *multiplex* etymologically imply "many-fold" ($m \ge 2$), Simson added special corollaries for the case $m = 1$ or $n = 1$, whereas mathematically $1 \cdot M$ belongs naturally to the sequence of multiples.
- **Distributive Law for Subtraction of Magnitudes**: Proposition 5 establishes $ma - mb = m(a - b)$, the subtractive counterpart to Proposition 1 ($ma + mb = m(a + b)$).
