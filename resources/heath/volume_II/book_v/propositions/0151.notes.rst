2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **De Morgan on Unity as a Multiple**: Heath highlights De Morgan's critique of Robert Simson's grammatical purism: because Greek $\pi\omicron\lambda\lambda\alpha\pi\lambda\alpha\sigma\iota\omicron\nu$ and Latin *multiplex* etymologically imply "many-fold" ($m \ge 2$), Simson added special corollaries for the case $m = 1$ or $n = 1$, whereas mathematically $1 \cdot M$ belongs naturally to the sequence of multiples.
- **Distributive Law for Subtraction of Magnitudes**: Proposition 5 establishes $ma - mb = m(a - b)$, the subtractive counterpart to Proposition 1 ($ma + mb = m(a + b)$).
- **Hypothetical Construction in Line 10**: Euclid's construction ("let $EB$ be made that multiple of $CG$") requires constructing $CG$ as a submultiple of $EB$ such that $EB = m \cdot CG$ where $m = AE / CF$. As Peletarius and Heath observe, dividing an arbitrary magnitude into $m$ equal parts is not demonstrated until Book VI, Proposition 9; Simson remedies this by taking a multiple of $FD$ instead.
- **Apparatus Note Removal**: The pseudo-footnote call ``[#f146_1]_`` from line 10 has been removed from ``0151.rst`` as the actual apparatus note is printed by Heath at the foot of page 146 (``0152.rst``).
