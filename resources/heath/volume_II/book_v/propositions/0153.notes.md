## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Algebraic Formulation of V. 5**: Heath contrasts Euclid's implicit division by $m$ ($ma - mb = mx \implies m(x+b) = ma \implies x+b = a$) with Simson's direct application of V. 1 to $x = m(a - b)$ and $mb$.
- **Proposition 6**: Proves that if $AB = mE, CD = mF$ and $AG = nE, CH = nF$ ($m > n$), then $GB = (m-n)E$ and $HD = (m-n)F$ are either equal to $E, F$ (when $m - n = 1$) or equimultiples of $E, F$ (when $m - n > 1$).
