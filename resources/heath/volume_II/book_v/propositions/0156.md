Let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> be unequal magnitudes, and let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be greater ;
let <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> be another, chance,
magnitude ;
I say that <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> a
greater ratio than <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> has to
<span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, and <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> a greater
ratio than it has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

![Proposition 8 Diagram (Case 1)](0156.g1.svg)

For, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater
than <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span>, let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span> be made equal
to <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
then the less of the magni-
tudes <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, if multiplied,
will sometime be greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 4]</span>

[Case 1.]

First, let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> ;
let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be multiplied, and let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be a multiple of it which is
greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
then, whatever multiple <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, let <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be made the
same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> and <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> of <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
and let <span data-ref="0156.g1:L">$L$</span> be taken double of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span> triple of it, and successive
multiples increasing by one, until what is taken is a multiple
of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> and the first that is greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span>. Let it be taken,
and let it be <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> which is quadruple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> and the first
multiple of it that is greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span>.

Then, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> first,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is not less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span>.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> that <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is of
<span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> that <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 1]</span></p>

But <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> that <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is of <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> that <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is of <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> are equimultiples of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>

Again, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> that <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is
of <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span>,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span>.</p>
