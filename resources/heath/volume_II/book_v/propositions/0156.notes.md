## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Archimedean Axiom (`[V. Def. 4]`)**: Proposition 8 is the first proposition in Book V to invoke Definition 4 explicitly as an axiom of comparability/Archimedean property: since $AB > C$, the excess $AE = AB - C$ and $EB = C$ are two magnitudes of the same kind as $D$, so the lesser of $AE, EB$, when multiplied sufficiently, will exceed $D$.
- **Bracketed Editorial Subheading**: `[Case 1.]` is printed in square brackets by Heath to mark the division of cases ($AE < EB$ vs. $AE > EB$), whichSimson later simplified into a single case by multiplying $AE$ directly regardless of whether $AE < EB$ or $AE \ge EB$.
