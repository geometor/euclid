But <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is not less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore neither is <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span>.</p>

And <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span> together.</p>

But <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span> together are equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span>, inasmuch as <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span> is
triple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, and <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> together are quadruple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>, while
<span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> is also quadruple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> ; whence <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> together are equal
to <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0156.g1:M">$M$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is in excess of <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span>,</p>
while <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> is not in excess of <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> are equimultiples of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span>, while <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> is
another, chance, multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> a greater ratio than <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 7]</span></p>

I say next, that <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> also has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> a greater ratio than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>
has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

For, with the same construction, we can prove similarly
that <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> is in excess of <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span>, while <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> is not in excess of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0156.g1:N">$N$</span> is a multiple of <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:K">$K$</span> are other, chance, equimultiples of <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:C">$C$</span> a greater ratio than <span data-ref="0156.g1:D">$D$</span> has to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 7]</span></p>

[Case 2.]

Again, let <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be greater than <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>.

Then the less, <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, if multiplied, will sometime be greater
than <span data-ref="0157.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 4]</span>

Let it be multiplied, and
let <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be a multiple of <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>
and greater than <span data-ref="0157.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
and, whatever multiple <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is
of <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, let <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be made the
same multiple of <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0157.g1:K">$K$</span>
of <span data-ref="0157.g1:C">$C$</span>.

![Proposition 8 Diagram (Case 2)](0157.g1.svg)

Then we can prove simi-
larly that <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0157.g1:K">$K$</span> are equi-
multiples of <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0157.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
and, similarly, let <span data-ref="0157.g1:N">$N$</span> be taken a multiple of <span data-ref="0157.g1:D">$D$</span> but the first
that is greater than <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,
so that <span data-ref="0157.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is again not less than <span data-ref="0157.g1:M">$M$</span>.
