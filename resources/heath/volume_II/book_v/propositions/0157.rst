But :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is not less than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither is :g:`$GH$ <0156.g1:/ G H />` less than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`.

And :g:`$FG$ <0156.g1:/ F G />` is greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is greater than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` together.

But :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` together are equal to :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`, inasmuch as :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>` is
triple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, and :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` together are quadruple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`, while
:g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is also quadruple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ; whence :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` together are equal
to :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`.

But :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is greater than :g:`$M$ <0156.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />` is in excess of :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`,

while :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` is not in excess of :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>`.

And :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>`, while :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is
another, chance, multiple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` a greater ratio than :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` has to :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`.

I say next, that :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` also has to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` a greater ratio than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`
has to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

For, with the same construction, we can prove similarly
that :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is in excess of :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>`, while :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is not in excess of :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`.

And :g:`$N$ <0156.g1:N>` is a multiple of :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>`,
while :g:`$FH$ <0156.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0156.g1:K>` are other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 7] <V.def.7>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` has to :g:`$C$ <0156.g1:C>` a greater ratio than :g:`$D$ <0156.g1:D>` has to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:/ A B />`.

[Case 2.]

Again, let :g:`$AE$ <0157.g1:/ A E />` be greater than :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`.

Then the less, :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`, if multiplied, will sometime be greater
than :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>`. :just:`[V. Def. 4] <V.def.4>`

Let it be multiplied, and
let :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />` be a multiple of :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`
and greater than :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>` ;
and, whatever multiple :g:`$GH$ <0157.g1:/ G H />` is
of :g:`$EB$ <0157.g1:/ E B />`, let :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />` be made the
same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0157.g1:/ A E />`, and :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>`
of :g:`$C$ <0157.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0157.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 8 Diagram (Case 2)

Then we can prove similarly
that :g:`$FH$ <0157.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$K$ <0157.g1:K>` are equimultiples
of :g:`$AB$ <0157.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0157.g1:C>` ;
and, similarly, let :g:`$N$ <0157.g1:N>` be taken a multiple of :g:`$D$ <0157.g1:D>` but the first
that is greater than :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />`,
so that :g:`$FG$ <0157.g1:/ F G />` is again not less than :g:`$M$ <0157.g1:M>`.
