## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Symbolic Formulation of V. 8**: Heath translates the Eudoxian archimedean step into modern algebraic notation: choosing $m$ such that $mC > D$ and $m(AB - C) = m(AE) > D$, and letting $pD$ be the first multiple of $D$ greater than $mC$ (with $nD = (p-1)D \le mC$). Then $m(AB) = mC + m(AE) > nD + D = pD > mC$, immediately satisfying V. Def. 7 for both $(AB : D) > (C : D)$ and $(D : C) > (D : AB)$.
