## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Direct Appeal to V. Def. 5 vs. V. 8**: Euclid's brief *reductio* proof invokes Proposition 8 ("would not have had the same ratio to $C$"). However, as Heath explains (anticipating the famous logical critique of V. 10), "not having the same ratio" (negation of Def. 5) and "having a greater/less ratio" (Def. 7) require careful bridge-building; Simson therefore uses the construction of Prop. 8 to exhibit explicit equimultiples $D, E$ of $A, B$ and $F$ of $C$ such that $D > F$ and $E \le F$, directly contradicting Def. 5.
