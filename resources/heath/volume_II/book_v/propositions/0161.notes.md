## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 10 Enunciation & Proof**: Proves the converse of Proposition 8: if $(A : C) > (B : C)$, then $A > B$; and if $(C : B) > (C : A)$, then $B < A$.
- **Logical Structure**: The Greek proof proceeds by trichotomy ($A = B$, $A < B$, or $A > B$), ruling out $A = B$ via V. 7 and $A < B$ via V. 8. On the next two pages (156–157), Heath and Simson analyze why this trichotomy argument implicitly assumes Trichotomy of Ratios without proof from Def. 7.
