Therefore, as was shown in Prop. 8, there is some multiple $F$ of :g:`$C$ <0160.g1:C>`, and
some equimultiples $E$ and $D$ of :g:`$B$ <0160.g1:B>` and :g:`$A$ <0160.g1:A>`, such that $F$ is greater than $E$ and
not greater than $D$.

But, because :g:`$C$ <0160.g1:C>` is to :g:`$B$ <0160.g1:B>` as :g:`$C$ <0160.g1:C>` is to :g:`$A$ <0160.g1:A>`,
and $F$ the multiple of the first is greater than $E$ the multiple of the second,

.. assertion:: [V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>

   $F$ the multiple of the third is greater than $D$ the multiple of the fourth.

But $F$ is not greater than $D$ : which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0160.g1:A>` is equal to :g:`$B$ <0160.g1:B>`."

PROPOSITION 10.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Of magnitudes which have a ratio to the same, that which has a greater ratio is greater ; and that to which the same has a greater ratio is less.

For let :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` have to :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` a greater ratio than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` has to :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` ;
I say that :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is greater than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0161.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0161 Figure 1

For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is either equal to :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` or less.

Now :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is not equal to :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` ;
for in that case each of the magnitudes :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` would have
had the same ratio to :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` ; :just:`[V. 7] <V.7>`
but they have not ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is not equal to :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`.

Nor again is :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` less than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` ;
for in that case :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` would have had to :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` a less ratio than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`
has to :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` ; :just:`[V. 8] <V.8>`
but it has not ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is not less than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`.

But it was proved not to be equal either ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is greater than :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`.

Again, let :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` have to :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` a greater ratio than :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` has to :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` ;
I say that :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` is less than :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>`.

For, if not, it is either equal or greater.

Now :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` is not equal to :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` ;
for in that case :g:`$C$ <0161.g1:C>` would have had the same ratio to each of
the magnitudes :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>` ; :just:`[V. 7] <V.7>`
but it has not ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0161.g1:A>` is not equal to :g:`$B$ <0161.g1:B>`.
