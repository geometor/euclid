2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Ratios vs. Magnitudes**: Heath highlights that Euclid never assumes Common Notions (such as transitivity of equality, C.N. 1) for ratios without proof—indeed, Proposition 11 is dedicated to proving that ratios equal to the same ratio are equal to one another. Consequently, one cannot assume without proof that $(A:C) > (B:C)$ and $(A:C) < (B:C)$ are mutually exclusive on the basis of V. Def. 7 alone.
- **Validity of the First Step ($A \ne B$)**: Heath shows that ruling out $A = B$ *is* valid under Def. 5 and Def. 7, because universal quantification over all multiples (``Def. 5``) is directly contradicted by the existential witness (``Def. 7``) where $mA > nC$ and $mB \le nC$.
