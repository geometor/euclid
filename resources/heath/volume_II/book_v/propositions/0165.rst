Algebraically, if

$$\begin{aligned}
a : b &= c : d, \\
\text{and}\qquad c : d &= e : f, \\
\text{then}\qquad a : b &= e : f.
\end{aligned}$$

The idiomatic use of the imperfect in quoting a result previously obtained is noteworthy. Instead of saying "But *it was proved that*, if :g:`H <0164.g1:H>` is in excess of :g:`M <0164.g1:M>`, :g:`G <0164.g1:G>` is also in excess of :g:`L <0164.g1:L>`," the Greek text has "But if :g:`H <0164.g1:H>` *was* in excess of :g:`M <0164.g1:M>`, :g:`G <0164.g1:G>` *was* also in excess of :g:`L <0164.g1:L>`," ἀλλὰ εἰ ὑπερεῖχε τὸ Θ τοῦ Μ, ὑπερεῖχε καὶ τὸ Η τοῦ Λ.

This proposition is tacitly used in combination with v. 16 and v. 24 in the geometrical passage in :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>`, $Meteorologica$ III. 5, 376 a 22—26.

PROPOSITION 12.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If any number of magnitudes be proportional, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the consequents.

Let any number of magnitudes :g:`A <0165.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0165.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0165.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0165.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0165.g1:F>` be proportional, so that, as :g:`A <0165.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, so is :g:`C <0165.g1:C>` to :g:`D <0165.g1:D>` and :g:`E <0165.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0165.g1:F>`;

I say that, as :g:`A <0165.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, so are :g:`A <0165.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0165.g1:C>`, :g:`E <0165.g1:E>` to :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0165.g1:D>`, :g:`F <0165.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0165.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12 diagram

For of :g:`A <0165.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0165.g1:C>`, :g:`E <0165.g1:E>` let equimultiples :g:`G <0165.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0165.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0165.g1:K>` be taken, and of :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0165.g1:D>`, :g:`F <0165.g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`L <0165.g1:L>`, :g:`M <0165.g1:M>`, :g:`N <0165.g1:N>`.

Then since, as :g:`A <0165.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, so is :g:`C <0165.g1:C>` to :g:`D <0165.g1:D>`, and :g:`E <0165.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0165.g1:F>`, and of :g:`A <0165.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0165.g1:C>`, :g:`E <0165.g1:E>` equimultiples :g:`G <0165.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0165.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0165.g1:K>` have been taken, and of :g:`B <0165.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0165.g1:D>`, :g:`F <0165.g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`L <0165.g1:L>`, :g:`M <0165.g1:M>`, :g:`N <0165.g1:N>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`G <0165.g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`L <0165.g1:L>`, :g:`H <0165.g1:H>` is also in excess of :g:`M <0165.g1:M>`, and :g:`K <0165.g1:K>` of :g:`N <0165.g1:N>`, if :eq:`equal <0165.g1:G = 0165.g1:L>`, :eq:`equal <0165.g1:H = 0165.g1:M; 0165.g1:K = 0165.g1:N>`, and if less, less;

.. assertion::

   so that, in addition, if :g:`G <0165.g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`L <0165.g1:L>`, then :g:`G <0165.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0165.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0165.g1:K>` are in excess of :g:`L <0165.g1:L>`, :g:`M <0165.g1:M>`, :g:`N <0165.g1:N>`, if :eq:`equal <0165.g1:G = 0165.g1:L>`, equal, and if less, less.
