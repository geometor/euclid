2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Role of V. 1 in V. 12**: Euclid invokes V. 1 verbatim to show that the sum of equimultiples $G + H + K$ is the same multiple of the sum $A + C + E$ as $G$ is of $A$, and similarly $L + M + N$ is the same multiple of $B + D + F$ as $L$ is of $B$.
- **Aristotelian Distributive Justice Citation**: Heath notes Aristotle's quotation of V. 12 in *Nicomachean Ethics* V. 7, 1131b14 ("the whole is to the whole what each part is to each part").
- **Proposition 13**: Substitutivity of equal ratios into an inequality of ratios: if $A : B = C : D$ and $C : D > E : F$, then $A : B > E : F$.
