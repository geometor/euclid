2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Simson's Corollary to V. 13**: Simson added a corollary for the case $A : B > C : D$ and $C : D = E : F \implies A : B > E : F$, which Heath notes is merely a permutation of the two hypotheses of V. 13.
- **Proposition 14**: Proves that in a proportion $A : B = C : D$ (where $A, C$ and $B, D$ are comparable), $A > = < C \implies B > = < D$. Euclid proves the first case ($A > C \implies B > D$) using V. 8, V. 13, and V. 10, and dispatches the remaining two cases ($A = C$ and $A < C$) with "Similarly we can prove that..." (ὁμοίως δὴ δείξομεν ὅτι...).
- **Implicit Homogeneity Requirement**: As Heath notes later on page 165, Proposition 14 requires $A$ and $C$ (and $B$ and $D$) to be magnitudes of the same kind so that $A > = < C$ is defined, even though $A, B$ need not be of the same kind as $C, D$ in general proportions until V. 16 (*alternando*).
