Algebraically, if $a : b = c : d$,<br />
then, according as $a > = < c$, $b > = < d$.

Simson adds the specific proof of the second and third parts of this<br />
proposition, which Euclid dismisses with "Similarly we can prove...."

"Secondly, if <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0168.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span> ; for <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span> as <span data-ref="0168.g1:C">$C$</span>, that<br />
is <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span>, is to <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 9]</span></p>

Thirdly, if <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span> be less than <span data-ref="0168.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span> shall be less than <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span>.<br />
For <span data-ref="0168.g1:C">$C$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span> ;<br />
and, because <span data-ref="0168.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span> as <span data-ref="0168.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span>,
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span>, by the first case.</p>
Wherefore <span data-ref="0168.g1:B">$B$</span> is less than <span data-ref="0168.g1:D">$D$</span>."

Aristotle, *Meteorol.* III. 5, 376 a 11—14, quotes the equivalent proposition<br />
that, if $a > b$, $c > d$.

### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">Parts have the same ratio as the same multiples of them<br />
taken in corresponding order.</p>

For let <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the same multiple of <span data-ref="0169.g1:C">$C$</span> that <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0169.g1:F">$F$</span> ;<br />
I say that, as <span data-ref="0169.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0169.g1:F">$F$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

![Proposition 15 diagram](0169.g1.svg)

For, since <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the same multiple of <span data-ref="0169.g1:C">$C$</span> that <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0169.g1:F">$F$</span>,<br />
as many magnitudes as there are in <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:C">$C$</span>, so many<br />
are there also in <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:F">$F$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be divided into the magnitudes <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:C">$C$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> into the magnitudes <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:F">$F$</span> ;<br />
then the multitude of the magnitudes <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> will be<br />
equal to the multitude of the magnitudes <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> are equal to one another,<br />
and <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> are also equal to one another,<br />
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 7]</span></p>

Therefore, as one of the antecedents is to one of the<br />
consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the<br />
consequents ; <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span><br />
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>
