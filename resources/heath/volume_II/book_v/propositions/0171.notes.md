## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Brachylogy in Proportional Ectheses**: Heath explains his translation policy for the terse Greek formula Ἔστω τέσσαρα μεγέθη ἀνάλογον τὰ Α, Β, Γ, Δ, ὡς τὸ Α πρὸς τὸ Β, οὕτως τὸ Γ πρὸς τὸ Δ, where English requires interpolating "so that, as $A$ is to $B$, so is $C$ to $D$" (and similarly in V. 17, 18, 20–23). Note that the line reference `3.` refers to line 3 of Proposition 16 on the preceding page (page 164).
- **Homogeneity Condition**: All four magnitudes $A, B, C, D$ in V. 16 must be of the same kind (ὁμογενῆ) so that the alternate ratios $A : C$ and $B : D$ exist (and so that $E = mA$ can be compared in magnitude with $G = nC$ in V. 14).
- **Smith and Bryant's Proof via VI. 1**: Begins the exposition of Smith and Bryant's (*Euclid's Elements of Geometry*, 1901, pp. 298 sqq.) geometric proof of V. 16 for straight lines and rectilineal areas using application of areas and VI. 1.
