Secondly, let the magnitudes be straight lines $AB, BC, CD, DE$.<br />
Construct the rectangles $Ab, Bc, Cd, De$ with the same height.

![Commentary diagram of four adjacent rectangles of equal height](0172.g2.svg)

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Then} \qquad Ab : Bc &= AB : BC, \\
\text{and} \qquad Cd : De &= CD : DE. && [\text{VI. 1}] \\
\text{But} \qquad AB : BC &= CD : DE. \\
\text{Therefore} \qquad Ab : Bc &= Cd : De. && [\text{V. 11}]
\end{aligned}$$

Hence, by the first case,

$$Ab : Cd = Bc : De,$$

and, since these rectangles have the same height,

$$AB : CD = BC : DE.$$

### PROPOSITION 17.

<p class="enunciation">If magnitudes be proportional componendo, they will also<br />
be proportional separando.</p>

Let $AB, BE, CD, DF$ be magnitudes proportional *com-<br />
ponendo*, so that, as $AB$ is to $BE$, so is $CD$ to $DF$;<br />
I say that they will also be proportional *separando*, that is,<br />
as $AE$ is to $EB$, so is $CF$ to $DF$.

For of $AE, EB, CF, FD$ let equimultiples $GH, HK$,<br />
$LM, MN$ be taken,<br />
and of $EB, FD$ other, chance, equimultiples, $KO, NP$.
