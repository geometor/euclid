## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Smith and Bryant's Geometric Proof of V. 16 (*Alternando*)**:
  - Uses VI. 1 (rectangles of the same height are to one another as their bases) and its converse to show that if four rectilineal areas $P, Q, R, S$ are proportional ($P : Q = R : S$), they can be represented as four rectangles $ac, be, ag, bk$ arranged in a $2 \times 2$ grid sharing common heights ($ab, bf$) and widths ($bc, bg$), immediately yielding $P : R = Q : S$.
  - The second case reduces proportional straight lines $AB, BC, CD, DE$ to the area case by constructing rectangles $Ab, Bc, Cd, De$ of equal height on those segments as bases.
- **Proposition 17 (*Separando*)**:
  - Introduces the Latin technical terms *componendo* ($\sigma\upsilon\gamma\kappa\epsilon\acute{\iota}\mu\epsilon\nu\alpha$) and *separando* ($\delta\iota\alpha\iota\rho\epsilon\theta\acute{\epsilon}\nu\tau\alpha$) italicized inside the English translation of the enunciation and setting-out.
