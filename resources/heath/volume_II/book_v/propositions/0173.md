Again, since $LM$ is the same multiple of $CF$ that $MN$<br />
is of $FD$,
<p class="assertion">therefore $LM$ is the same multiple of $CF$ that $LN$ is of $CD$. <span class="justification">[V. 1]</span></p>

But $LM$ was the same multiple of $CF$ that $GK$ is of $AB$;
<p class="assertion">therefore $GK$ is the same multiple of $AB$ that $LN$ is of $CD$.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore $GK, LN$ are equimultiples of $AB, CD$.</p>

Again, since $HK$ is the same multiple of $EB$ that $MN$ is<br />
of $FD$,<br />
and $KO$ is also the same multiple of $EB$ that $NP$ is of $FD$,
<p class="assertion">therefore the sum $HO$ is also the same multiple of $EB$ that<br />
$MP$ is of $FD$. <span class="justification">[V. 2]</span></p>

And, since, as $AB$ is to $BE$, so is $CD$ to $DF$,<br />
and of $AB, CD$ equimultiples $GK, LN$ have been taken,<br />
and of $EB, FD$ equimultiples $HO, MP$,
<p class="assertion">therefore, if $GK$ is in excess of $HO$, $LN$ is also in excess of<br />
$MP$,<br />
