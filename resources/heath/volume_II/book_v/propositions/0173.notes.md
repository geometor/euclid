## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Structure of the Equimultiple Proof**:
  - First applies V. 1 twice to show that $GK (= GH + HK)$ and $LN (= LM + MN)$ are equimultiples of the wholes $AB$ and $CD$.
  - Next applies V. 2 to show that $HO (= HK + KO)$ and $MP (= MN + NP)$ are equimultiples of the parts $EB$ and $FD$.
  - Then invokes V. Def. 5 on the hypothesis $AB : BE = CD : DF$ and subtracts the common multiples $HK$ and $MN$ to compare $GH$ with $KO$ and $LM$ with $NP$.
