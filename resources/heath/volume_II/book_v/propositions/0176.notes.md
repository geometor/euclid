- **Existence of the Fourth Proportional (Saccheri, Clavius, Simson)**:
  - Euclid's proof of V. 18 assumes that if $AB : BE \neq CD : FD$, there exists some magnitude $DG$ (less or greater than $FD$) such that $AB : BE = CD : DG$.
  - As Saccheri and Simson observed, the existence of a fourth proportional to three magnitudes is an unproved continuity/completeness assumption (equivalent to Dedekind completeness for general magnitudes, or constructible via VI. 1, 2, 12 for straight lines and polygons).
