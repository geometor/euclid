2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Limitation of Austin's Proof via V. 16**:

  - Heath points out that Austin's direct proof of V. 18 relies on alternation (V. 16) and addition of antecedents/consequents (V. 12), which restricts the proof to four magnitudes all of the same species, whereas Euclid's generality in Book V requires only that $A, B$ be of one kind and $C, D$ be of another (or the same) kind.

- **Anachronistic Use of VI. 1 in Smith & Bryant's Proof**:

  - Smith and Bryant's proof uses the application of rectangles (``rect. ac``, ``rect. be``, ``rect. ag``, ``rect. bk``) and Book VI, Proposition 1 (rectangles of equal height are to one another as their bases) to bypass the fourth-proportional existence postulate, at the cost of restricting magnitudes to straight lines and rectilineal areas and relying on Book VI results.
