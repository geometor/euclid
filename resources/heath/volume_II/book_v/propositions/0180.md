### PROPOSITION 19.

<p class="enunciation">If, as a whole is to a whole, so is a part subtracted to a<br />
part subtracted, the remainder will also be to the remainder<br />
as whole to whole.</p>

For, as the whole $AB$ is to the whole $CD$, so let the<br />
part $AE$ subtracted be to the part $CF$<br />
subtracted ;<br />
I say that the remainder $EB$ will also be<br />
to the remainder $FD$ as the whole $AB$ to<br />
the whole $CD$.

![Proposition 19](0180.g1.svg)

For since, as $AB$ is to $CD$, so is $AE$<br />
to $CF$,
<p class="assertion">alternately also, as $BA$ is to $AE$, so is $DC$ to $CF$. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

And, since the magnitudes are proportional *componendo*,<br />
they will also be proportional *separando*, <span class="justification">[V. 17]</span><br />
that is, as $BE$ is to $EA$, so is $DF$ to $CF$,<br />
and, alternately,
<p class="assertion">as $BE$ is to $DF$, so is $EA$ to $FC$. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

But, as $AE$ is to $CF$, so by hypothesis is the whole $AB$<br />
to the whole $CD$.

<p class="assertion">Therefore also the remainder $EB$ will be to the remainder<br />
$FD$ as the whole $AB$ is to the whole $CD$. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

Therefore etc.

[PORISM. From this it is manifest that, if magnitudes be<br />
proportional *componendo*, they will also be proportional<br />
*convertendo*.]

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
