$$P : Q = C : D. \quad [\text{VI. } 1]$$

### PROPOSITION 19.

<p class="enunciation">If, as a whole is to a whole, so is a part subtracted to a<br />
part subtracted, the remainder will also be to the remainder<br />
as whole to whole.</p>

For, as the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so let the<br />
part <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> subtracted be to the part <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span><br />
subtracted ;<br />
I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> will also be<br />
to the remainder <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> as the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to<br />
the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Proposition 19](0180.g1.svg)

For since, as <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,
<p class="assertion">alternately also, as <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$DC$</span> to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

And, since the magnitudes are proportional *componendo*,<br />
they will also be proportional *separando*, <span class="justification">[V. 17]</span><br />
that is, as <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$EA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ F D /">$DF$</span> to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,<br />
and, alternately,
<p class="assertion">as <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ F D /">$DF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$FC$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

But, as <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so by hypothesis is the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
to the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

<p class="assertion">Therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> will be to the remainder<br />
<span data-ref="0180.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> as the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0180.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

Therefore etc.

[PORISM. From this it is manifest that, if magnitudes be<br />
proportional *componendo*, they will also be proportional<br />
*convertendo*.]

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
