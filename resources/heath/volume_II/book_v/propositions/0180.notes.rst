2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 19 & Alternation**: Because Euclid uses V. 16 (*alternando*) twice in the proof of V. 19 (switching $AB : CD = AE : CF$ to $BA : AE = DC : CF$ and back from $BE : EA = DF : CF$ to $BE : DF = EA : FC$), all four magnitudes in V. 19 must be of the same kind—as is already implied by subtracting $AE$ from $AB$ and $CF$ from $CD$ while comparing $AB$ to $CD$.
- **The Bracketed Porism on** *Convertendo*: Heiberg brackets the transitional explanation in the Greek manuscripts leading up to the Porism, while Heath retains the Porism itself in square brackets ``[PORISM. ...]`` and argues on the following page that both the transitional lines and the Porism are pre-Theonic interpolations.
