PROPOSITION 19.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If, as a whole is to a whole, so is a part subtracted to a part subtracted, the remainder will also be to the remainder as whole to whole.

For, as the whole :g:`$AB$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` is to the whole :g:`$CD$ <0180.g1:/ C D />`, so let the part :g:`$AE$ <0180.g1:/ A E />` subtracted be to the part :g:`$CF$ <0180.g1:/ C F />` subtracted ;

I say that the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0180.g1:/ E B />` will also be to the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0180.g1:/ F D />` as the whole :g:`$AB$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` to the whole :g:`$CD$ <0180.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0180.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 19

For since, as :g:`$AB$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0180.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$AE$ <0180.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$CF$ <0180.g1:/ C F />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   alternately also, as :g:`$BA$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0180.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`$DC$ <0180.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$CF$ <0180.g1:/ C F />`.

And, since the magnitudes are proportional :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`, they will also be proportional :term:`*separando* <separando>`, :just:`[V. 17] <V.17>`

that is, as :g:`$BE$ <0180.g1:/ E B />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0180.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`$DF$ <0180.g1:/ F D />` to :g:`$CF$ <0180.g1:/ C F />`, and, alternately,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   as :g:`$BE$ <0180.g1:/ E B />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0180.g1:/ F D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0180.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$FC$ <0180.g1:/ C F />`.

But, as :g:`$AE$ <0180.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0180.g1:/ C F />`, so by hypothesis is the whole :g:`$AB$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` to the whole :g:`$CD$ <0180.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0180.g1:/ E B />` will be to the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0180.g1:/ F D />` as the whole :g:`$AB$ <0180.g1:/ A B />` is to the whole :g:`$CD$ <0180.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

Therefore etc.

[PORISM. From this it is manifest that, if magnitudes be proportional :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`, they will also be proportional :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`.]

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

$$(a - c) : (b - d) = a : b.$$
