2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Equimultiples in Proportion** (*ex aequali*): The proof constructs equimultiples $m A = G, m D = H$; $n B = K, n E = L$; $p C = M, p F = N$. By V. 4, $G : K = H : L$ and $K : M = L : N$. Applying V. 20 (*ex aequali* for three terms in order) yields the condition of V. Def. 5: $G > = < M \implies H > = < N$.
- **Simson's Extension**: Simson formalizes the inductive chain: $(A : C = E : G) \land (C : D = G : H) \implies A : D = E : H$.
