### PROPOSITION 23.

<p class="enunciation">If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in<br />
multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same<br />
ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, they will also<br />
be in the same ratio ex aequali.</p>

Let there be three magnitudes $A$, $B$, $C$, and others equal<br />
to them in multitude, which, taken two and two together, are<br />
in the same proportion, namely $D$, $E$, $F$; and let the propor-<br />
tion of them be perturbed, so that,
<p class="assertion">as $A$ is to $B$, so is $E$ to $F$,</p>
<p class="assertion">and, as $B$ is to $C$, so is $D$ to $E$;</p>
I say that, as $A$ is to $C$, so is $D$ to $F$.

![Book V, Proposition 23, Line Segments](0187.g1.svg)

Of $A$, $B$, $D$ let equimultiples $G$, $H$, $K$ be taken,<br />
and of $C$, $E$, $F$ other, chance, equimultiples $L$, $M$, $N$.

Then, since $G$, $H$ are equimultiples of $A$, $B$,<br />
and parts have the same ratio as the same multiples of<br />
them, <span class="justification">[V. 15]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as $A$ is to $B$, so is $G$ to $H$.</p>

For the same reason also,
<p class="assertion">as $E$ is to $F$, so is $M$ to $N$.</p>

And, as $A$ is to $B$, so is $E$ to $F$;<br />
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as $G$ is to $H$, so is $M$ to $N$. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

Next, since, as $B$ is to $C$, so is $D$ to $E$,<br />
<p class="assertion">alternately, also, as $B$ is to $D$, so is $C$ to $E$. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

And, since $H$, $K$ are equimultiples of $B$, $D$,<br />
and parts have the same ratio as their equimultiples,
<p class="assertion">therefore, as $B$ is to $D$, so is $H$ to $K$. <span class="justification">[V. 15]</span></p>
