## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Recensions (Heiberg vs. Simson)**: The step proving $H : L = K : M$ ($mb : nc = md : ne$) is deduced directly by Simson using V. 4 ($B : C = D : E \implies mb : nc = md : ne$). In Heiberg's text, this is proved through an elaborate detour using V. 11, V. 15, and V. 16 twice over.
- **Homogeneity Constraint**: Heath criticizes Heiberg's text because V. 16 (*alternando*) requires all four magnitudes to be of the same kind, whereas V. 23 should apply generally to pairs of magnitudes of different kinds.
