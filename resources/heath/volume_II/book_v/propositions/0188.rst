   therefore also, as :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>` is to :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>` to :g:`$E$ <0187.g1:E>`.

Again, since :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>` are equimultiples of :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0187.g1:E>`, 

.. assertion:: [V. 15] <V.15>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>` is to :g:`$E$ <0187.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>` to :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>`.

But, as :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>` is to :g:`$E$ <0187.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>`; 

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>` is to :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>`, so is :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>` to :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>`, :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`
   | and, alternately, as :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>` is to :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>`, so is :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>` to :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

But it was also proved that, 

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$G$ <0187.g1:G>` is to :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>` to :g:`$N$ <0187.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   | Since, then, there are three magnitudes :g:`$G$ <0187.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0187.g1:H>`, :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>`, and
   | others equal to them in multitude :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>`, :g:`$M$ <0187.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0187.g1:N>`, which taken two
   | and two together are in the same ratio,
   | and the proportion of them is perturbed,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, *ex aequali*, if :g:`$G$ <0187.g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>`, :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>` is also in excess
   | of :g:`$N$ <0187.g1:N>`;
   | if equal, equal; and if less, less. :just:`[V. 21] <V.21>`

| And :g:`$G$ <0187.g1:G>`, :g:`$K$ <0187.g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`$A$ <0187.g1:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0187.g1:D>`,
| and :g:`$L$ <0187.g1:L>`, :g:`$N$ <0187.g1:N>` of :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>`, :g:`$F$ <0187.g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0187.g1:A>` is to :g:`$C$ <0187.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0187.g1:D>` to :g:`$F$ <0187.g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
