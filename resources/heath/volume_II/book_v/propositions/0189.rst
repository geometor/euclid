PROPOSITION 24.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third has to a fourth, and also a fifth have to the second the same ratio as a sixth to the fourth, the first and fifth added together will have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth have to the fourth.

| Let a first magnitude :g:`$AB$ <0189.g2:/ A B />` have to a second :g:`$C$ <0189.g2:C>` the same
| ratio as a third :g:`$DE$ <0189.g2:/ D E />` has to a
| fourth :g:`$F$ <0189.g2:F>`;
| and let also a fifth :g:`$BG$ <0189.g2:/ B G />` have to
| the second :g:`$C$ <0189.g2:C>` the same ratio as
| a sixth :g:`$EH$ <0189.g2:/ E H />` has to the fourth
| :g:`$F$ <0189.g2:F>`;
| I say that the first and fifth added together, :g:`$AG$ <0189.g2:/ A G />`, will have
| to the second :g:`$C$ <0189.g2:C>` the same ratio as the third and sixth, :g:`$DH$ <0189.g2:/ D H />`,
| has to the fourth :g:`$F$ <0189.g2:F>`.
