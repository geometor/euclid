2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Division of Ratios**: Heath highlights that the proof essentially computes $(a / c) / (b / c) = a / b$, which when composed with $b$ yields $(a+b)/b$, and then by recombining with $b/c$ produces $(a+b)/c = (d+e)/f$.
- **Aristotelian Reference**: Aristotle's use of Proposition 24 in *Meteorologica* III. 5 (376 a 22–26) in his theory of the rainbow demonstrates that this proposition was part of the early pre-Euclidean stock of Eudoxian proportion theory.
