2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Extremes and Means Inequality**: If $a : b = c : d$ with $a$ greatest, then $d$ must be least, and $a + d > b + c$. Euclid proves this by subtraction ($a - c$ and $b - d$) using V. 19.
- **Simson's Clarification**: Euclid's enunciation says "let $AB$ be the greatest of them and $F$ the least." Simson observes that if $AB$ is the greatest in a proportion of four magnitudes, $F$ must necessarily be the least, which Euclid takes as given without separate proof.
