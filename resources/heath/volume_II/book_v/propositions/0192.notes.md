## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Arithmetic vs. Geometric Mean**: When $a : b = b : d$, $a + d > 2b$, meaning the arithmetic mean of two magnitudes is strictly greater than their geometric mean. Heath notes this is established geometrically in VI. 27.
- **Barrow & Simson on Book V**: Heath preserves Isaac Barrow's tribute to Eudoxus/Euclid: "nothing in the whole body of the elements of a more subtile invention, nothing more solidly established, and more accurately handled than the doctrine of proportionals."
