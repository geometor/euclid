Transcription Notes: Folio 0196 (Page 190)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio Range**: Folio 0196 (Page 190).
- **Contents**: Continuation and conclusion of Heath's commentary on the spurious Definition 5 (composition of ratios). The remainder of the page is unprinted blank space before the Propositions section commences on page 191.
- **Continuity**: Seamlessly completes the sentence from page 189 ("from the Arabic; and the other mss...."). Concludes the entire Definitions section of Book VI.

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Textual Evidence against Definition 5**:

  - Absent from Campanus' Arabic-Latin tradition.
  - Present only in the margin of Codex Vaticanus Graecus 190 (P).
  - Unstable placement across later manuscripts.
  - Never cited or used in VI. 23 or elsewhere in Euclid, Archimedes, or Apollonius.
  - Attested only in Eutocius (Archimedes commentary) and Theon (commentary on Ptolemy's *Syntaxis*).

- **Mathematical Incoherence**: The multiplication of ratio "sizes" (``πηλικότητες``) is alien to Greek synthetic geometry. Commentators were forced into numerical interpretations:

  - Eutocius: numerical index of ratio.
  - Commandinus: *quantitas*.
  - Wallis: *quantuplicity* / *rationis exponens*.
  - Peletarius: *denominatio ipsae proportionis*.
  - Clavius: *denominator*.
  - Sir Henry Savile: famously regarded Definition 5 as one of the two great blemishes on geometry (alongside Postulate 5).

- **Hultsch's Counter-thesis**: Friedrich Hultsch argued for its authenticity based on its presence in the ancient recension and need for preparation for VI. 23. Heath refutes this by noting that if genuine, it represents an obsolete pre-Eudoxian relic limited to commensurables.
