## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Eudoxian Elegance vs Modern Dichotomy**: Heath highlights how Euclid's Book V definition of proportion handles commensurable and incommensurable magnitudes simultaneously with a single, uniform demonstration. In contrast, modern textbooks relying on numerical ratios ($a/b$) must bifurcate into commensurable cases and cumbersome limit or exhaustion arguments for incommensurables.
- **Archimedean Technique in Legendre**: Legendre adapts Archimedes' technique from *Equilibrium of Planes* I. 7, using sub-segment division of size $< EO$ to force a division point $I$ between $E$ and $O$, setting up a contradiction.
