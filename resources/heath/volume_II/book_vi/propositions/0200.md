### PROPOSITION 2.

*If a straight line be drawn parallel to one of the sides of a triangle, it will cut the sides of the triangle proportionally; and, if the sides of the triangle be cut proportionally, the line joining the points of section will be parallel to the remaining side of the triangle.*

For let <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, one of the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>;
I say that, as <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>.

![Book VI Proposition 2](0200.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be joined.

Therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< B D E >">$BDE$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< C D E >">$CDE$</span>;
for they are on the same base <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> and in the same parallels <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. [I. 38]

And the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span> is another area.

But equals have the same ratio to the same; [v. 7]
therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< B D E >">$BDE$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< C D E >">$CDE$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>.

But, as the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< B D E >">$BDE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>;
for, being under the same height, the perpendicular drawn from <span data-ref="0200.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, they are to one another as their bases. [VI. 1]

For the same reason also,
as the triangle <span data-ref="0200.g1:< C D E >">$CDE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0200.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>.

Therefore also, as <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> to <span data-ref="0200.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>. [v. 11]
