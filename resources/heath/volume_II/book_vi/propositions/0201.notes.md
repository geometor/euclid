# Transcription Notes: Folio 0201 (Page 195)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Concludes the converse proof of Proposition 2, contains Heath's commentary on Proposition 2 (criticisms and emendations by Robert Simson and I. Todhunter), and introduces Proposition 3 with the first part of its enunciation.
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0200 (Page 194) establishes the direct part of Proposition 2 and the construction for the converse.
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0202 (Page 196) completes the enunciation of Proposition 3, provides its figure, and gives the direct proof and setup for the converse proof.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 2 (Converse)**: The proof uses VI. 1 (triangles under the same height are to one another as their bases), V. 11 (equality of ratios), V. 9 (equality of magnitudes having the same ratio to the same magnitude), and I. 39 (equal triangles on the same base are in the same parallels).
- **Heath's Commentary on Prop. 2**:
  - Notes Euclid's omission of cases where the transversal cuts the sides produced beyond the vertex or beyond the base, citing Robert Simson's three figures and modified enunciation.
  - Cites I. Todhunter regarding ambiguity in the converse proportion: segments adjacent to the base must correspond to ensure parallelism.
- **Proposition 3 Enunciation**: Heath translates the classic angle bisector theorem. The enunciation carries over across the folio boundary mid-sentence ("...and, if the segments of the base have the same ratio as the").
