Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Harmonic Range**:

  - $BE : EC = BD : DC = (BE - ED) : (ED - EC)$, defining $BE, ED, EC$ as in harmonic progression, $DE$ as the harmonic mean, and $(B, D; C, E)$ as a harmonic range.
  - Since $AD \perp AE$ ($\angle DAE = 90^\circ$), the circle on diameter $DE$ passes through the vertex $A$.

- **Circle of Apollonius**:

  - Locus of points whose distances from two fixed points $B, C$ have a constant ratio $h : k \ne 1$.
  - Historical attribution: Discussed in Apollonius' lost treatise *Plane Loci* (Book II), recorded by Pappus (VII, p. 666) and preserved with proof by Eutocius (ed. Heiberg, vol. II, pp. 180–184).
  - Apollonius determines the centre $O$ and radius $r$ directly from $BC$ and $h : k$ without using $D$ and $E$.
