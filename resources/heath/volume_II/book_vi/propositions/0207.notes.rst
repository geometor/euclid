Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 4 (Similar Triangles)**:

  - Configuration: Triangles $ABC$ and $DCE$ placed with bases $BC$ and $CE$ contiguous in a single straight line.
  - Intersection of $BA$ and $ED$: Proved rigorously using I. 17 ($\angle ABC + \angle ACB < 2\text{ right angles}$) and substitution ($\angle ACB = \angle DEC$), hence $\angle ABC + \angle DEC < 2\text{ right angles}$, so the lines meet at $F$ by Postulate 5.
  - Parallelogram: $FACD$ is formed because $BF \parallel CD$ (equal alternate/corresponding angles [I. 28]) and $AC \parallel FE$ [I. 28]. Opposites are equal: $FA = DC$ and $AC = FD$ [I. 34].
  - Application of VI. 2:

    - $AC \parallel FE \implies BA : AF = BC : CE$. Substituting $AF = CD \implies BA : CD = BC : CE \implies AB : BC = DC : CE$ [V. 16].
    - $CD \parallel BF \implies BC : CE = FD : DE$. Substituting $FD = AC \implies BC : CE = AC : DE \implies BC : CA = CE : ED$ [V. 16].

  - Note: Bottom of page leaves off after the second alternation, carrying over to page 202 for the final combination and "Q. E. D.".
