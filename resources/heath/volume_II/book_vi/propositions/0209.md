<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle at <span data-ref="0209.g1:< A )">$A$</span> is equal to the remaining<br />
angle at <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G )">$G$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0209.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is equiangular with the<br />
triangle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F >">$GEF$</span>.</p>

Therefore in the triangles <span data-ref="0209.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F >">$GEF$</span> the sides about<br />
the equal angles are proportional, and those are corresponding<br />
sides which subtend the equal angles; <span class="justification">[VI. 4]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0209.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0209.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G E /">$GE$</span> to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>

But, as <span data-ref="0209.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0209.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so by hypothesis is <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G E /">$GE$</span> to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

Therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G E /">$GE$</span> has the<br />
same ratio to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 9]</span></p>

For the same reason
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G F /">$GF$</span>.</p>

Since then <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is common,
<p class="sub-clause">the two sides <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the base <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0209.g2:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F )">$GEF$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the triangle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F >">$GEF$</span>,<br />
and the remaining angles are equal to the remaining angles,<br />
namely those which the equal sides subtend. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< D F E )">$DFE$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G F E )">$GFE$</span>,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< E D F )">$EDF$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< E G F )">$EGF$</span>.</p>

And, since the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< F E D )">$FED$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F )">$GEF$</span>,
<p class="sub-clause">while the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< G E F )">$GEF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0209.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0209.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>.</p>
