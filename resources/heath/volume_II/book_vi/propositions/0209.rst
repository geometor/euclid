.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>`.

Therefore in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>` the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and those are corresponding sides which subtend the equal angles; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0209.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`.

But, as :g:`$AB$ <0209.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0209.g1:/ B C />`, so by hypothesis is :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore, as :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`, so is :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`.

Therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />` has the same ratio to :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is equal to :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DF$ <0209.g2:/ D F />` is also equal to :g:`$GF$ <0209.g2:/ G F />`.

Since then :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />` is equal to :g:`$EG$ <0209.g2:/ E G />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />` is common,

.. subclause::

   the two sides :g:`$DE$ <0209.g2:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0209.g2:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0209.g2:/ E F />`;

.. subclause::

   and the base :g:`$DF$ <0209.g2:/ D F />` is equal to the base :g:`$FG$ <0209.g2:/ F G />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>`,

.. subclause::

   and the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F >>`, and the remaining angles are equal to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend. :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0209.g2:< D F E )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$GFE$ <0209.g2:< G F E )>`, and the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0209.g2:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`$EGF$ <0209.g2:< E G F )>`.

And, since the angle :g:`$FED$ <0209.g2:< F E D )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>`,

.. subclause::

   while the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0209.g2:< G E F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0209.g1:< A B C )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0209.g2:< D E F )>`.
