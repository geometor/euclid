## Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 7 (Simson's Case 3)**: If $\angle C$ is right, then if $\triangle ABC$ were not equiangular to $\triangle DEF$, constructing $\angle ABG = \angle DEF$ yields $BG = BC$. But $\angle BCG = 90^\circ$, requiring $\angle BGC = 90^\circ$, making the sum of two angles of $\triangle BGC$ not less than two right angles, which contradicts I. 17.
- **Proposition 8 Foundations**: Sets up the classic right-triangle altitude theorem, which establishes the fundamental similarity $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DBA \sim \triangle DAC$, giving rise to the geometric mean relations and the Pythagorean theorem proof in Book VI.
