For, since the angle $BAC$ is equal to the angle $ADB$,<br />
for each is right,<br />
and the angle at $B$ is common to the<br />
two triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$,

![Proposition 8](0216.g1.svg)

<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle $ACB$<br />
is equal to the remaining angle<br />
$BAD$; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle $ABC$ is equi-<br />
angular with the triangle $ABD$.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as $BC$ which subtends the right angle in the<br />
triangle $ABC$ is to $BA$ which subtends the right angle in<br />
the triangle $ABD$, so is $AB$ itself which subtends the angle<br />
at $C$ in the triangle $ABC$ to $BD$ which subtends the equal<br />
angle $BAD$ in the triangle $ABD$, and so also is $AC$ to $AD$<br />
which subtends the angle at $B$ common to the two triangles. <span class="justification">[VI. 4]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the triangle $ABC$ is both equiangular to the<br />
triangle $ABD$ and has the sides about the equal angles<br />
proportional.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the triangle $ABC$ is similar to the triangle<br />
$ABD$. <span class="justification">[VI. Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Similarly we can prove that the triangle $ABC$ is also<br />
similar to the triangle $ADC$;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the triangles $ABD, ADC$ is similar to the<br />
whole $ABC$.</p>

I say next that the triangles $ABD, ADC$ are also similar<br />
to one another.

For, since the right angle $BDA$ is equal to the right angle<br />
$ADC$,<br />
and moreover the angle $BAD$ was also proved equal to the<br />
angle at $C$,
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle at $B$ is also equal to the<br />
remaining angle $DAC$; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle $ABD$ is equiangular with the triangle<br />
$ADC$.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as $BD$ which subtends the angle $BAD$ in the<br />
triangle $ABD$ is to $DA$ which subtends the angle at $C$ in the<br />
triangle $ADC$ equal to the angle $BAD$, so is $AD$ itself<br />
which subtends the angle at $B$ in the triangle $ABD$ to $DC$<br />
which subtends the angle $DAC$ in the triangle $ADC$ equal</p>
