## Mathematical Observations
- **Altitude on Hypotenuse**: In right triangle $ABC$ with altitude $AD$ to hypotenuse $BC$:
  1. $\angle BAC = \angle ADB = 90^\circ$, $\angle B$ common $\implies \angle ACB = \angle BAD$ by I. 32.
  2. $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DBA$ by VI. 4 and VI. Def. 1.
  3. Analogously, $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DAC$.
  4. Between the two smaller triangles: $\angle BDA = \angle ADC = 90^\circ$, $\angle BAD = \angle C \implies \angle B = \angle DAC$. Thus $BD : DA = AD : DC = BA : AC$.
