:title: Proposition 8 (cont.)

| For, since the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0216.g1:< B A C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ADB$ <0216.g1:< A D B )>`,
| for each is right,
| and the angle at :g:`$B$ <0216.g1:< A B C )>` is common to the
| two triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` and :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`,

.. picture:: 0216.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 8

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remaining angle :g:`$ACB$ <0216.g1:< A C B )>`
   | is equal to the remaining angle
   | :g:`$BAD$ <0216.g1:< B A D )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` is equi-
   | angular with the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, as :g:`$BC$ <0216.g1:/ B C />` which subtends the right angle in the
   | triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` is to :g:`$BA$ <0216.g1:/ B A />` which subtends the right angle in
   | the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />` itself which subtends the angle
   | at :g:`$C$ <0216.g1:< A C B )>` in the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` to :g:`$BD$ <0216.g1:/ B D />` which subtends the equal
   | angle :g:`$BAD$ <0216.g1:< B A D )>` in the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`, and so also is :g:`$AC$ <0216.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$AD$ <0216.g1:/ A D />`
   | which subtends the angle at :g:`$B$ <0216.g1:< A B C )>` common to the two triangles. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` is both equiangular to the
   | triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>` and has the sides about the equal angles
   | proportional.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` is similar to the triangle
   | :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

.. subclause::

   | Similarly we can prove that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>` is also
   | similar to the triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the triangles :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`, :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>` is similar to the
   | whole :g:`$ABC$ <0216.g1:< A B C >>`.

| I say next that the triangles :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>`, :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>` are also similar
| to one another.

| For, since the right angle :g:`$BDA$ <0216.g1:< B D A )>` is equal to the right angle
| :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C )>`,
| and moreover the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0216.g1:< B A D )>` was also proved equal to the
| angle at :g:`$C$ <0216.g1:< A C B )>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$B$ <0216.g1:< A B D )>` is also equal to the
   | remaining angle :g:`$DAC$ <0216.g1:< D A C )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>` is equiangular with the triangle
   | :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, as :g:`$BD$ <0216.g1:/ B D />` which subtends the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0216.g1:< B A D )>` in the
   | triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>` is to :g:`$DA$ <0216.g1:/ D A />` which subtends the angle at :g:`$C$ <0216.g1:< A C D )>` in the
   | triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>` equal to the angle :g:`$BAD$ <0216.g1:< B A D )>`, so is :g:`$AD$ <0216.g1:/ A D />` itself
   | which subtends the angle at :g:`$B$ <0216.g1:< A B D )>` in the triangle :g:`$ABD$ <0216.g1:< A B D >>` to :g:`$DC$ <0216.g1:/ D C />`
   | which subtends the angle :g:`$DAC$ <0216.g1:< D A C )>` in the triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0216.g1:< A D C >>` equal
