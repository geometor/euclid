## 2. Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Expressions**: Heath notes τυχοῦσα γωνία (*tychousa gōnia*, "any angle") versus τυχὸν σημεῖον (*tychon sēmeion*, "a point taken at random").
- **Simson's Critique**: Simson criticizes Euclid's demonstration for only proving a particular fraction (1/3) and for relying on an unproved property of proportionals (which Simson supplied as Prop. D in Book V).
- **Propositions Cited**:
  - Euclid I. 3 (cutting off equal segment from greater).
  - Euclid I. 31 (drawing parallel line through given point).
  - Euclid VI. 2 (line parallel to side cuts sides proportionally).
- **Mathematical Significance (Euclid's 1/3 Case)**:
  - From $A$, draw ray $AC$ at any angle to $AB$.
  - Choose $D$ on $AC$, make $DE = EC = AD$, so $CD = 2 AD$ and $AC = 3 AD$.
  - Join $BC$, draw $DF \parallel BC$.
  - By VI. 2, $CD : DA = BF : FA \implies BF = 2 FA \implies BA = 3 AF$.
