## Mathematical Observations
- **Alternative to Simson's Prop. D**:
  - $CA : AD = BA : AE \implies CA : BA = AD : AE$ (alternando, V. 16).
  - Since $AD : AE = n \cdot AD : n \cdot AE$ (V. 15) and $n \cdot AD = AC$, substitution and V. 11 yield $AC : AB = AC : n \cdot AE \implies AB = n \cdot AE$ by V. 14.
- **Proposition 10 Construction**:
  - Uncut line $AB$ and cut line $AC$ (cut at $D, E$) meet at $A$.
  - Connect $CB$, draw $DF, EG \parallel BC$.
  - Draw $DHK \parallel AB$ through $D$.
  - $DFGH$ and $EGKB$ (or $FH, HB$) form parallelograms, translating segment lengths: $DH = FG$ and $HK = GB$.
  - In $\triangle DKC$, $HE \parallel KC \implies CE : ED = KH : HD = BG : GF$.
