Because :g:`$ED$ <0218.g2:/ E D />` is parallel to one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0218.g2:< A B C >>`, viz. to :g:`$BC$ <0218.g2:/ B C />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   as :g:`$CD$ <0218.g2:/ C D />` is to :g:`$DA$ <0218.g2:/ D A />`, so is :g:`$BE$ <0218.g2:/ B E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0218.g2:/ E A />`,

and, :term:`*componendo* <componendo>`,

.. assertion:: [V. 18] <V.18>

   :g:`$CA$ <0218.g2:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />`, as :g:`$BA$ <0218.g2:/ B A />` to :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

But :g:`$CA$ <0218.g2:/ C A />` is a multiple of :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />`;

.. assertion:: [Prop. D]

   therefore :g:`$BA$ <0218.g2:/ B A />` is the same multiple of :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

.. assertion::

   | Whatever part therefore :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` is of :g:`$AC$ <0218.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />` is the same part of :g:`$AB$ <0218.g2:/ A B />`;
   | wherefore from the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0218.g2:/ A B />` the part required is cut off."

| The use of :person:`Simson <simson>`'s Prop. D can be avoided, as noted by :person:`Camerer <camerer>` after
| Baermann, in the following way. We first prove, as above, that

.. assertion::

   :g:`$CA$ <0218.g2:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` as :g:`$BA$ <0218.g2:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

Then we infer that, alternately,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   :g:`$CA$ <0218.g2:/ C A />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0218.g2:/ B A />` as :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` to :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

| But :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />` as n · :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` to n · :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`
| (where $n$ is the number of times that :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` is contained in :g:`$AC$ <0218.g2:/ A C />`); :just:`[V. 15] <V.15>`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   whence :g:`$AC$ <0218.g2:/ A C />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0218.g2:/ A B />` as n · :g:`$AD$ <0218.g2:/ A D />` is to n · :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

| In this proportion the first term is equal to the third; therefore :just:`[V. 14] <V.14>`
| the second is equal to the fourth,

.. assertion::

   so that :g:`$AB$ <0218.g2:/ A B />` is equal to $n$ times :g:`$AE$ <0218.g2:/ A E />`.

Prop. 9 is of course only a particular case of Prop. 10.

----

PROPOSITION 10.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To cut a given uncut straight line similarly to a given cut straight line.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` be the given uncut straight line, and :g:`$AC$ <0219.g1:/ A C />` the
| straight line cut at the points :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>`,
| :g:`$E$ <0219.g1:E>`; and let them be so placed as
| to contain any angle;
| let :g:`$CB$ <0219.g1:/ C B />` be joined, and through :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>`,
| :g:`$E$ <0219.g1:E>` let :g:`$DF$ <0219.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$EG$ <0219.g1:/ E G />` be drawn parallel
| to :g:`$BC$ <0219.g1:/ B C />`, and through :g:`$D$ <0219.g1:D>` let :g:`$DHK$ <0219.g1:/ D H K />`
| be drawn parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. picture:: 0219.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 10

.. subclause::

   | Therefore each of the figures
   | :g:`$FH$ <0219.g1:< F H >>`, :g:`$HB$ <0219.g1:< H B >>` is a parallelogram;

.. assertion:: [I. 34] <I.34>

   therefore :g:`$DH$ <0219.g1:/ D H />` is equal to :g:`$FG$ <0219.g1:/ F G />` and :g:`$HK$ <0219.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ G B />`.

| Now, since the straight line :g:`$HE$ <0219.g1:/ H E />` has been drawn parallel
| to :g:`$KC$ <0219.g1:/ K C />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$DKC$ <0219.g1:< D K C >>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:/ C E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0219.g1:/ E D />`, so is :g:`$KH$ <0219.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`$HD$ <0219.g1:/ H D />`.
   | :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`
