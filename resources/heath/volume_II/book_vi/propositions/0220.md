### PROPOSITION 11.

*To two given straight lines to find a third proportional.*

Let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the two given straight lines, and let them be placed so as to contain any angle;
thus it is required to find a third proportional to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

For let them be produced to the points <span data-ref="0220.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:E">$E$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; [I. 3]
let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be joined, and through <span data-ref="0220.g1:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn parallel to it. [I. 31]

![Proposition 11](0220.g1.svg)

Since, then, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has been drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, one of the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0220.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span>,
proportionally, as <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>. [VI. 2]

But <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>.

Therefore to two given straight lines <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> a third proportional to them, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, has been found.

Q. E. F.

---

**1. to find.** The Greek word, here and in the next two propositions, is προσευρεῖν, literally "to find *in addition*."

This proposition is again a particular case of the succeeding Prop. 12.
Given a ratio between straight lines, VI. 11 enables us to find the ratio which is its duplicate.
