### PROPOSITION 11.

<p class="enunciation">To two given straight lines to find a third proportional.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the two given straight lines, and let<br />
them be placed so as to contain any<br />
angle;<br />
thus it is required to find a third pro-<br />
portional to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

For let them be produced to the<br />
points <span data-ref="0220.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:E">$E$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be made equal<br />
to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 3]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be joined, and through <span data-ref="0220.g1:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span><br />
be drawn parallel to it. <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>

![Proposition 11](0220.g1.svg)

Since, then, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has been drawn<br />
parallel to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, one of the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0220.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>,
<p class="assertion">proportionally, as <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 2]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">But <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;<br />
therefore, as <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore to two given straight lines <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> a third<br />
proportional to them, <span data-ref="0220.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, has been found.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

**1. to find.** The Greek word, here and in the next two propositions, is προσευρεῖν,<br />
literally "to find *in addition*."

This proposition is again a particular case of the succeeding Prop. 12.

Given a ratio between straight lines, VI. 11 enables us to find the ratio<br />
which is its duplicate.
