## Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 10 Resolution**: Segments of uncut line $AB$ ($AF, FG, GB$) are proportional to segments of cut line $AC$ ($AD, DE, EC$).
- **Proposition 11 (Third Proportional)**:
  - Given segments $BA$ and $AC$, we seek $x$ such that $BA : AC = AC : x$.
  - Place $BA, AC$ at any angle with common vertex $A$. Produce $AB$ to $D$ such that $BD = AC$. Produce $AC$ to $E$.
  - Draw $DE \parallel BC$.
  - By VI. 2, $AB : BD = AC : CE$. Substituting $BD = AC$ yields $AB : AC = AC : CE$, so $CE$ is the third proportional.
- **Connection to Duplicate Ratio**: If $a : b = b : c$, then $a : c$ is the duplicate ratio of $a : b$ (by Book V, Def. 9).
