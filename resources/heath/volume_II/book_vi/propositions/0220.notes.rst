- **Proposition 10 Resolution**: Segments of uncut line $AB$ ($AF, FG, GB$) are proportional to segments of cut line $AC$ ($AD, DE, EC$), fulfilling the definition of similar division.
- **Proposition 11 (Third Proportional)**:

  - Given segments $BA$ and $AC$, construct $CE$ such that $BA : AC = AC : CE$.
  - Place $BA, AC$ at vertex $A$. Produce $AB$ to $D$ with $BD = AC$; produce $AC$ to $E$.
  - Join $BC$, draw $DE \parallel BC$.
  - By VI. 2, $AB : BD = AC : CE$. Substituting $BD = AC$ yields $AB : AC = AC : CE$.
