PROPOSITION 11.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To two given straight lines to find\ [#f1]_ a third proportional.

Let :g:`$BA$ <0220.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />` be the two given straight lines, and let them be placed so as to contain any angle;

thus it is required to find a third proportional to :g:`$BA$ <0220.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />`.

For let them be produced to the points :g:`$D$ <0220.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0220.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$BD$ <0220.g1:/ B D />` be made equal to :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />`; :just:`[I. 3] <I.3>`

let :g:`$BC$ <0220.g1:/ B C />` be joined, and through :g:`$D$ <0220.g1:D>` let :g:`$DE$ <0220.g1:/ D E />` be drawn parallel to it. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. picture:: 0220.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 11

Since, then, :g:`$BC$ <0220.g1:/ B C />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$DE$ <0220.g1:/ D E />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0220.g1:< A D E >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   proportionally, as :g:`$AB$ <0220.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0220.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$CE$ <0220.g1:/ C E />`.

.. assertion::

   But :g:`$BD$ <0220.g1:/ B D />` is equal to :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />`; therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0220.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$CE$ <0220.g1:/ C E />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore to two given straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0220.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0220.g1:/ A C />` a third proportional to them, :g:`$CE$ <0220.g1:/ C E />`, has been found. :just:`Q. E. F.`

----

.. [#f1] **to find.** The Greek word, here and in the next two propositions, is προσευρεῖν, literally "to find *in addition*."

This proposition is again a particular case of the succeeding Prop. 12.

Given a ratio between straight lines, VI. 11 enables us to find the ratio which is its duplicate.
