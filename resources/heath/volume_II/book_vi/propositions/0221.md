### PROPOSITION 12.

<p class="enunciation">To three given straight lines to find a fourth proportional.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span> be the three given straight lines;<br />
thus it is required to find a fourth proportional to <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span>.

![Proposition 12](0221.g1.svg)

Let two straight lines <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be set out containing any<br />
angle <span data-ref="0221.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>;<br />
let <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, and further <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
let <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be joined, and let <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0221.g1:E">$E$</span> parallel<br />
to it. <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>

<p class="sub-clause">Since, then, <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> has been drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, one of<br />
the sides of the triangle <span data-ref="0221.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> to <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 2]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> to <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, and <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span> to <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore to the three given straight lines <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span> a fourth<br />
proportional <span data-ref="0221.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> has been found.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

---

We have here the geometrical equivalent of the "rule of three."

It is of course immaterial whether, as in Euclid's proof, the first and<br />
second straight lines are measured on one of the lines forming the angle and<br />
the third on the other, or the first and third are measured on one and the<br />
second on the other.

If it should be desired that the first and the required fourth be measured<br />
on one of the lines, and the second and third on<br />
the other, we can use the following construction.<br />
Measure <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> on one straight line equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:A">$A$</span>, and<br />
on any other straight line making an angle with<br />
the first at the point <span data-ref="0221.g2:D">$D$</span> measure <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ D G /">$DG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0221.g1:C">$C$</span>. Join <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and through <span data-ref="0221.g2:G">$G$</span><br />
draw <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ G H /">$GH$</span> anti-parallel to <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, i.e. make the angle<br />
<span data-ref="0221.g2:< D G H )">$DGH$</span> equal to the angle <span data-ref="0221.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>; let <span data-ref="0221.g2:/ G H /">$GH$</span> meet<br />
<span data-ref="0221.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> (produced if necessary) in <span data-ref="0221.g2:H">$H$</span>.
