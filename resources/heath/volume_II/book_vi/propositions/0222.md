### PROPOSITION 13.

*To two given straight lines to find a mean proportional.*

Let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be the two given straight lines;
thus it is required to find a mean proportional to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

Let them be placed in a straight line, and let the semicircle <span data-ref="0222.g1:( A D C )">$ADC$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0222.g1:B">$B$</span> at right angles to the straight line <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be joined.

![Book VI Proposition 13](0222.g1.svg)

Since the angle <span data-ref="0222.g1:< A D C )">$ADC$</span> is an angle in a semicircle, it is right. [III. 31]

And, since, in the right-angled triangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:< A D C >">$ADC$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> has been drawn from the right angle perpendicular to the base, therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is a mean proportional between the segments of the base, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. [VI. 8, Por.]

Therefore to the two given straight lines <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> a mean proportional <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> has been found.

$$\text{Q. E. F.}$$

---

This proposition, the Book VI. version of II. 14, is equivalent to the extraction of the square root. It further enables us, given a ratio between straight lines, to find the ratio which is its *sub-duplicate*, or the ratio of which it is *duplicate*.

### PROPOSITION 14.

*In equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and equiangular parallelograms in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional are equal.*
