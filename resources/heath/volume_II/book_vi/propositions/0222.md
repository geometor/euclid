### PROPOSITION 13.

<p class="enunciation">To two given straight lines to find a mean proportional.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be the two given straight lines;<br />
thus it is required to find a mean<br />
proportional to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

Let them be placed in a straight<br />
line, and let the semicircle <span data-ref="0222.g1:( A D C )">$ADC$</span> be<br />
described on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;<br />
let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0222.g1:B">$B$</span> at<br />
right angles to the straight line <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be joined.

![Book VI Proposition 13](0222.g1.svg)

<p class="assertion">Since the angle <span data-ref="0222.g1:< A D C )">$ADC$</span> is an angle in a semicircle, it is<br />
right. <span class="justification">[III. 31]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since, in the right-angled triangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:< A D C >">$ADC$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> has<br />
been drawn from the right angle perpendicular to the base,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is a mean proportional between the segments of<br />
the base, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 8, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore to the two given straight lines <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> a mean<br />
proportional <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> has been found.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

---

This proposition, the Book VI. version of II. 14, is equivalent to the<br />
extraction of the square root. It further enables us, given a ratio between<br />
straight lines, to find the ratio which is its *sub-duplicate*, or the ratio of which<br />
it is *duplicate*.

### PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">In equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about<br />
the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and equiangular<br />
parallelograms in which the sides about the equal angles are<br />
reciprocally proportional are equal.</p>
