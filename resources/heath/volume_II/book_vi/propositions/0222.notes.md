# Transcription Notes: Folio 0222 (Page 216)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page 216** contains three distinct textual components:

  2. The complete text of **Proposition 13** ("To two given straight lines to find a mean proportional"), including enunciation, setting-out, construction, proof, concluding formula (Q.E.F.), and Heath's introductory commentary comparing it to II. 14 and square root extraction.
  3. The enunciation of **Proposition 14** ("In equal and equiangular parallelograms...").

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Cross References**: Euclid cites Book III. 31 (angle in a semicircle is right) and Book VI. 8, Porism (perpendicular from right angle to base is mean proportional between base segments).
- **Mathematical Significance**: Heath points out that VI. 13 is the geometric equivalent of square root extraction ($\sqrt{ab}$) and provides the construction of sub-duplicate ratios ($a : \sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a} : \sqrt{b}$).
- **Italics & Typography**: Heath italicizes mathematical terms *sub-duplicate* and *duplicate*.
