## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Reciprocal Proportion**: Defined geometrically by Euclid: sides about equal angles are reciprocally proportional when antecedent is to consequent in one figure as the other consequent is to the other antecedent ($DB : BE = GB : BF$).
- **Citations**: Euclid relies heavily on Book V (V. 7, V. 11, V. 9) and Book VI. 1 (parallelograms with same height are to one another as their bases).
- **De Morgan Quote**: Discusses compound ratio and argues VI. 14 is a special case of VI. 23 where the compounded ratio is equality ($1:1$).
