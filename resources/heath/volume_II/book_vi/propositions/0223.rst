:title: Proposition 14

Let :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` be equal and equiangular parallelograms having the angles at :g:`$B$ <0223.g1:< D B F )>` equal, and let :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />`, :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />` be placed in a straight line;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FB$ <0223.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BG$ <0223.g1:/ B G />` are also in a straight line. :just:`[I. 14] <I.14>`

I say that, in :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>`, the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that, as :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />`.

.. picture:: 0223.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 14

For let the parallelogram :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, the parallelogram :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>`,

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>` is another area,

.. assertion:: [V. 7] <V.7>

   therefore, as :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>` and, as :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` is to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[*id.*]`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore also, as :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

Next, let :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` be to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />` as :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`;

I say that the parallelogram :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>`.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />`, while, as :g:`$DB$ <0223.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BE$ <0223.g1:/ B E />`, so is the parallelogram :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>` and, as :g:`$GB$ <0223.g1:/ G B />` is to :g:`$BF$ <0223.g1:/ B F />`, so is the parallelogram :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   therefore also, as :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>` to :g:`$FE$ <0223.g1:< F E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$AB$ <0223.g1:< A B >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$BC$ <0223.g1:< B C >>`. :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` says upon this proposition: “Owing to the disjointed manner in which Euclid treats compound ratio, this proposition is strangely out of place. It is a particular case of VI. 23, being that in which the ratio of the sides, compounded, gives a ratio of equality. The proper definition of four magnitudes being reciprocally proportional is that the ratio compounded of their ratios is that of equality.”
