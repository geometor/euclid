# Transcription Notes: Folio 0224 (Page 218)

## 1. Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page 218** contains extensive scholarly commentary by Heath on Proposition 14:
  1. A reasoned defense of Euclid's pedagogical arrangement against Augustus De Morgan's critique of compound ratio.
  2. A step-by-step rigorous deduction justifying the collinearity of $FB, BG$ from I. 14 using Common Notions 1 and 2 and I. 13 (as well as Proclus's converse of I. 15).
  3. Enunciation and diagram for the *other partial converse* of VI. 14 and VI. 15 (where the included angles may be either equal or supplementary).
- **Antecedent Continuity**: Follows De Morgan's quote from the bottom of Folio 0223 (Page 217).
- **Subsequent Continuity**: The proof of this general partial converse carries over to the top of Folio 0225 (Page 219).

## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Printer Omission / Typo**: In paragraph 1, line 4, the original text reads: `"VI. 14 and 15. But is perfectly consistent with Euclid's manner..."`, omitting the pronoun `"it"`. Transcribed verbatim as printed.
- **Pedagogy of Compound Ratio**: Heath makes a profound pedagogical point: explaining inverse ratios by compound ratio replaces a simple intuitive concept with a more sophisticated, abstract one.
- **Common Notions Cited**: Common Notion 2 (equals added to equals are equal) and Common Notion 1 (things equal to the same thing are equal).
