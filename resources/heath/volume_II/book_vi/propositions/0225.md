### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">In equal triangles which have one angle equal to one angle<br />
the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional;<br />
and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle,<br />
and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally<br />
proportional, are equal.</p>

Let *ABC*, *ADE* be equal triangles having one angle<br />
equal to one angle, namely the angle *BAC* to the angle<br />
*DAE*;

I say that in the triangles *ABC*, *ADE* the sides about the<br />
equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that,
<p class="assertion">as *CA* is to *AD*, so is *EA* to *AB*.</p>
