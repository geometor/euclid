### PROPOSITION 15.

*In equal triangles which have one angle equal to one angle the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle, and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, are equal.*

Let <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span> be equal triangles having one angle equal to one angle, namely the angle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A C )">$BAC$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; D A E )">$DAE$</span>;

I say that in the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span> the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.
