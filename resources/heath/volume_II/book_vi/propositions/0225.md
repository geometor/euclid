### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">In equal triangles which have one angle equal to one angle<br />
the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional;<br />
and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle,<br />
and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally<br />
proportional, are equal.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span> be equal triangles having one angle<br />
equal to one angle, namely the angle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span> to the angle<br />
<span data-ref="0226.g1:< D A E )">$DAE$</span>;

I say that in the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span> the sides about the<br />
equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that,
<p class="assertion">as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.</p>
