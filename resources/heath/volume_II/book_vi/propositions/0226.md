For let them be placed so that <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is in a straight<br />
line with <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is also in a straight line with<br />
<span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 14]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined.

![Book VI Proposition 15](0226.g1.svg)

Since then the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is equal to<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span> is another<br />
area,
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< C A B >">$CAB$</span> is to the<br />
triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span> to<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 7]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">But, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:< C A B >">$CAB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span><br />
and, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[*id.*]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore in the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span> the sides about<br />
the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.</p>

Next, let the sides of the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span> be reci-<br />
procally proportional, that is to say, let <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> be to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
I say that the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>.

For, if <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be again joined,<br />
since, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
while, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> to the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span> to the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>, so is<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Therefore each of the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span> has the same<br />
ratio to <span data-ref="0226.g1:< B A D >">$BAD$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:< E A D >">$EAD$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[V. 9]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

As indicated in the partial converse given in the last note, this proposition<br />
is equally true if the angle included by the two sides in one triangle is<br />
supplementary, instead of being equal, to the angle included by the two sides<br />
in the other.
