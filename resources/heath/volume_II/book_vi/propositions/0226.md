For let them be placed so that <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is in a straight line with <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is also in a straight line with <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [I. 14]

Let <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined.

![Book VI Proposition 15](0226.g1.svg)

Since then the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span> is another area, therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; C A B &gt;">$CAB$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>. [V. 7]

But, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; C A B &gt;">$CAB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, [VI. 1]
and, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [*id.*]
Therefore also, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [V. 11]

Therefore in the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span> the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

Next, let the sides of the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span> be reciprocally proportional, that is to say, let <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> be to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
I say that the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A D E &gt;">$ADE$</span>.

For, if <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be again joined,
since, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
while, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>,
and, as <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0226.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>, [VI. 1]
therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>. [V. 11]

Therefore each of the triangles <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span> has the same ratio to <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; B A D &gt;">$BAD$</span>.

Therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0226.g1:&lt; E A D &gt;">$EAD$</span>. [V. 9]

Therefore etc.

$$\text{Q. E. D.}$$

---

As indicated in the partial converse given in the last note, this proposition is equally true if the angle included by the two sides in one triangle is supplementary, instead of being equal, to the angle included by the two sides in the other.
