:title: Proposition 15

| For let them be placed so that :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is in a straight
| line with :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` is also in a straight line with
   | :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[I. 14] <I.14>`

Let :g:`$BD$ <0226.g1:/ B D />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0226.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 15

| Since then the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to
| the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>`, and :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>` is another
| area,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the triangle :g:`$CAB$ <0226.g1:< C A B >>` is to the
   | triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to
   | the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`. :just:`[V. 7] <V.7>`

.. assertion::

   | But, as :g:`$CAB$ <0226.g1:< C A B >>` is to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`
   | and, as :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` is to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[*id.*]`

.. assertion:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   Therefore also, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` the sides about
   | the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

| Next, let the sides of the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>` be reci-
| procally proportional, that is to say, let :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` be to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />` as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />`
| to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`;
| I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$ADE$ <0226.g1:< A D E >>`.

| For, if :g:`$BD$ <0226.g1:/ B D />` be again joined,
| since, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`,
| while, as :g:`$CA$ <0226.g1:/ C A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0226.g1:/ A D />`, so is the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` to the triangle
| :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`,
| and, as :g:`$EA$ <0226.g1:/ E A />` is to :g:`$AB$ <0226.g1:/ A B />`, so is the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle
| :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`, so is
   | the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` to the triangle :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. subclause::

   | Therefore each of the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>` has the same
   | ratio to :g:`$BAD$ <0226.g1:< B A D >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0226.g1:< A B C >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$EAD$ <0226.g1:< E A D >>`.
   | :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| As indicated in the partial converse given in the last note, this proposition
| is equally true if the angle included by the two sides in one triangle is
| supplementary, instead of being equal, to the angle included by the two sides
| in the other.
