This proposition is a particular case of VI. 14, but one which is on all<br />
accounts worth separate statement. It may also be enunciated in the follow-<br />
ing form:

*Rectangles which have their bases reciprocally proportional to their heights<br />
are equal in area; and equal rectangles have their bases reciprocally proportional<br />
to their heights.*

Since any parallelogram is equal to a rectangle of the same height and<br />
on the same base, and any triangle with the same height and on the same<br />
base is equal to half the parallelogram or rectangle, it follows that *Equal<br />
parallelograms or triangles have their bases reciprocally proportional to their<br />
heights and vice versa.*
