# Transcription Notes: Folio 0230 (Page 224)

- **Proposition**: Book VI, Proposition 16 (commentary continued; Simson's Prop. B converse concluded) and Proposition C (Simson's addition to Book VI).

  - Transcribes the converse of Simson's Prop. B: if $AD^2 + BD \cdot DC = BA \cdot AC$, then $AD$ bisects $\angle BAC$ unless $AB = AC$.

  - Introduces Simson's Proposition C: for triangle $ABC$ with altitude $AD$ and circumdiameter $AE$, $BA \cdot AC = EA \cdot AD$.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Simson's Proposition B Converse**: Uses III. 35, II. 3, VI. 16, III. 21, and VI. 7 to show that $\angle BDA$ and $\angle ECA$ are either equal or supplementary. In the supplementary case, it reduces to $AB = AC$.
- **Reference to *Data***: Mentions Euclid, *Data*, Prop. 67: if $BA = AC$, for any point $D$ on $BC$, $BD \cdot DC + AD^2 = AB^2$.
- **Proposition C Text**: "Let *ABC* be a triangle and *AD* the perpendicular on *BC*."
