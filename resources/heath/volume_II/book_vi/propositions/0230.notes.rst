2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Simson's Proposition B Converse**: Uses [III. 35], [hyp. and II. 3], [VI. 16], [III. 21], and [VI. 7 and note] to show that $\angle BDA$ and $\angle ECA$ are either equal or supplementary. In the supplementary case, angle arithmetic on $\triangle ABD$ and cyclic quad $ABEC$ demonstrates $AB = AC$.
- **Reference to** *Data*: Mentions Euclid, *Data*, Prop. 67: if $BA = AC$, for any point $D$ on $BC$, $BD \cdot DC + AD^2 = AB^2$.
- **Heath Misprint**: In the setting-out for Proposition C, Heath's original printed text has "Let *ABC* be a triangle and *AD* the perpendicular on *AB*." instead of "on *BC*". This typographical error is corrected to *BC* in the transcription.
