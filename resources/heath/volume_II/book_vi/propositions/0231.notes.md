## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition**: Book VI, Proposition 16 (commentary continued; Proposition C concluded, Proposition D introduced).

- **Current Folio**:
  - Completes the proof of Proposition C: $\triangle ABD \sim \triangle AEC \implies BA : AD = EA : AC \implies BA \cdot AC = EA \cdot AD$.
  - Notes the connection to the circumradius formula $R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta}$.
  - Introduces Proposition D (Ptolemy's Theorem): lemma from the *Syntaxis* (Almagest, ed. Heiberg I, 36–37) for the chord table: περὶ τῆς πηλικότητος τῶν ἐν τῷ κύκλῳ εὐθειῶν.

  - Begins Ptolemy's proof: $\angle ABE = \angle DBC$; establishes $\triangle ABD \sim \triangle EBC \implies BC \cdot AD = BD \cdot CE$; sets up second similarity $\triangle ABE \sim \triangle DBC$.
