- **Proposition**: Book VI, Proposition 16 (commentary continued; Proposition D completed and extended).

  - Completes Ptolemy's proof: $BA \cdot DC = BD \cdot AE$; combined with $BC \cdot AD = BD \cdot CE$, yielding $AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot DC + AD \cdot BC$.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Dr. Lachlan's Simson-Line Lemma**: Explicitly references the pedal line theorem (commonly known as Simson's line) and its converse: pedal points $X, Y, Z$ are collinear if and only if $D$ lies on the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$.
- **Ptolemy's Inequality**: Derived directly from the triangle inequality applied to the pedal triangle $XYZ$: $XZ \le XY + YZ \implies BD \cdot AC \le AD \cdot BC + AB \cdot CD$.
- **British Typography**: "Dr Lachlan" printed without period.
