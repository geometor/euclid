### PROPOSITION 17.

<p class="enunciation">If three straight lines be proportional, the rectangle con-<br />
tained by the extremes is equal to the square on the mean;<br />
and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the<br />
square on the mean, the three straight lines will be proportional.</p>

Let the three straight lines <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> be proportional, so<br />
that, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
I say that the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.

![Proposition 17](0234.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.

Then, since, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>,<br />
<p class="sub-clause">and <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>

But, if four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle<br />
contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained<br />
by the means. <span class="justification">[VI. 16]</span>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, for <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> is<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

Next, let the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> be equal to the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>;<br />
I say that, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>.
