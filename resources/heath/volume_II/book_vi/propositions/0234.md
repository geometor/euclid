### PROPOSITION 17.

*If three straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the square on the mean; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the square on the mean, the three straight lines will be proportional.*

Let the three straight lines <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> be proportional, so that, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>;
I say that the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.

![Proposition 17](0234.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.

Then, since, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>.

But, if four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained by the means. [VI. 16]

Therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>.

But the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, for <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0234.g1:D">$D$</span>;
therefore the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>.

Next, let the rectangle <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span> be equal to the square on <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>;
I say that, as <span data-ref="0234.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0234.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0234.g1:C">$C$</span>.
