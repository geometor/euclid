PROPOSITION 17.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If three straight lines be proportional, the rectangle con- tained by the extremes is equal to the square on the mean; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the square on the mean, the three straight lines will be proportional.

| Let the three straight lines :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` be proportional, so
| that, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`;
| I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the
| square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0234.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 17

Let :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` be made equal to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

Then, since, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`, 

.. subclause::

   and :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is equal to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

| But, if four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle
| contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained
| by the means. :just:`[VI. 16] <VI.16>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the rectangle
   | :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   | But the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` is the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, for :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is
   | equal to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the
   | square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`.

| Next, let the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` be equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`;
| I say that, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.
