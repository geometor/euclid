For, with the same construction,<br />
since the rectangle *A*, *C* is equal to the square on *B*,
<p class="sub-clause">while the square on *B* is the rectangle *B*, *D*, for *B* is equal<br />
to *D*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle *A*, *C* is equal to the rectangle *B*, *D*.</p>

But, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal<br />
to that contained by the means, the four straight lines are<br />
proportional. <span class="justification">[VI. 16]</span>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as *A* is to *B*, so is *D* to *C*.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But *B* is equal to *D*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as *A* is to *B*, so is *B* to *C*.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

### PROPOSITION 18.

<p class="enunciation">On a given straight line to describe a rectilinear figure<br />
similar and similarly situated to a given rectilinear figure.</p>

Let *AB* be the given straight line and *CE* the given<br />
rectilinear figure;<br />
thus it is required to describe on the straight line *AB* a<br />
rectilinear figure similar and similarly situated to the recti-<br />
lineal figure *CE*.

![Proposition 18, given figure CE](0235.g1.svg)
![Proposition 18, constructed figure AH](0235.g2.svg)

Let *DF* be joined, and on the straight line *AB*, and at<br />
the points *A*, *B* on it, let the angle *GAB* be constructed<br />
equal to the angle at *C*, and the angle *ABG* equal to the<br />
angle *CDF*. <span class="justification">[I. 23]</span>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the remaining angle *CFD* is equal to the angle<br />
*AGB*; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle *FCD* is equiangular with the triangle<br />
*GAB*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, proportionally, as *FD* is to *GB*, so is *FC* to<br />
*GA*, and *CD* to *AB*.</p>
