For, with the same construction, since the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`,

.. subclause::

   while the square on :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`, for :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is equal to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`.

But, if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to that contained by the means, the four straight lines are proportional. :just:`[VI. 16] <VI.16>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` is equal to :g:`$D$ <0234.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0234.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0234.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0234.g1:C>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line to describe a rectilinear figure similar and similarly situated to a given rectilinear figure.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />` be the given straight line and :g:`$CE$ <0235.g1:< C E >>` the given rectilinear figure;

thus it is required to describe on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />` a rectilinear figure similar and similarly situated to the rectilineal figure :g:`$CE$ <0235.g1:< C E >>`.

.. picture:: 0235.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 18 (figure CE)

.. picture:: 0235.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 18 (figure AH)

Let :g:`$DF$ <0235.g1:/ D F />` be joined, and on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />`, and at the points :g:`$A$ <0235.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0235.g2:B>` on it, let the angle :g:`$GAB$ <0235.g2:< G A B )>` be constructed equal to the angle at :g:`$C$ <0235.g1:< F C D )>`, and the angle :g:`$ABG$ <0235.g2:< A B G )>` equal to the angle :g:`$CDF$ <0235.g1:< C D F )>`. :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remaining angle :g:`$CFD$ <0235.g1:< C F D )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$AGB$ <0235.g2:< A G B )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$FCD$ <0235.g1:< F C D >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$GAB$ <0235.g2:< G A B >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` is to :g:`$GB$ <0235.g2:/ G B />`, so is :g:`$FC$ <0235.g1:/ F C />` to :g:`$GA$ <0235.g2:/ G A />`, and :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />`.
