Again, on the straight line *BG*, and at the points *B*, *G* on<br />
it, let the angle *BGH* be constructed equal to the angle *DFE*,<br />
and the angle *GBH* equal to the angle *FDE*. <span class="justification">[I. 23]</span>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the remaining angle at *E* is equal to the re-<br />
maining angle at *H*; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle *FDE* is equiangular with the triangle<br />
*GBH*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, proportionally, as *FD* is to *GB*, so is *FE* to<br />
*GH*, and *ED* to *HB*. <span class="justification">[VI. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it was also proved that, as *FD* is to *GB*, so is *FC* to<br />
*GA*, and *CD* to *AB*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as *FC* is to *AG*, so is *CD* to *AB*, and *FE*<br />
to *GH*, and further *ED* to *HB*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the angle *CFD* is equal to the angle *AGB*,<br />
and the angle *DFE* to the angle *BGH*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole angle *CFE* is equal to the whole angle<br />
*AGH*.</p>

For the same reason
<p class="assertion">the angle *CDE* is also equal to the angle *ABH*.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">And the angle at *C* is also equal to the angle at *A*,<br />
and the angle at *E* to the angle at *H*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore *AH* is equiangular with *CE*;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and they have the sides about their equal angles proportional;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectilinear figure *AH* is similar to the<br />
rectilineal figure *CE*. <span class="justification">[VI. Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore on the given straight line *AB* the rectilinear<br />
figure *AH* has been described similar and similarly situated<br />
to the given rectilinear figure *CE*.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>
