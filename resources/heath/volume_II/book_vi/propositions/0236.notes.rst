2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Simson's Criticisms**: Simson objected that (1) Euclid only proves for four sides; (2) Euclid uses the permuted form $a : a' = b : b'$ directly from VI. 4 instead of stating $a : b = a' : b'$ and applying *alternando* (V. 16).
- **Heath's Defense**: Heath points out that the ratio of corresponding sides between the two figures ($a : a' = b : b'$) is the primary form established in the proof of VI. 4.
- **Q. E. F. Formulation**: As a problem of geometric construction, Proposition 18 terminates with *quod erat faciendum* (``Q. E. F.``).
