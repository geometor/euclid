:title: Proposition 18

Again, on the straight line :g:`$BG$ <0235.g2:/ B G />`, and at the points :g:`$B$ <0235.g2:B>`, :g:`$G$ <0235.g2:G>` on it, let the angle :g:`$BGH$ <0235.g2:< B G H )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0235.g2:< B G H ) = 0235.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0235.g1:< D F E )>`, and the angle :g:`$GBH$ <0235.g2:< G B H )>` :eq:`equal <0235.g2:< G B H ) = 0235.g1:< F D E )>` to the angle :g:`$FDE$ <0235.g1:< F D E )>`. :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remaining angle at :g:`$E$ <0235.g1:< F E D )>` is :eq:`equal <0235.g1:< F E D ) = 0235.g2:< G H B )>` to the remaining angle at :g:`$H$ <0235.g2:< G H B )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$FDE$ <0235.g1:< F D E >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0235.g1:< D F E ) = 0235.g2:< B G H ); 0235.g1:< F D E ) = 0235.g2:< G B H ); 0235.g1:< F E D ) = 0235.g2:< G H B )>` with the triangle :g:`$GBH$ <0235.g2:< G B H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` is to :g:`$GB$ <0235.g2:/ G B />`, so is :g:`$FE$ <0235.g1:/ F E />` to :g:`$GH$ <0235.g2:/ G H />`, and :g:`$ED$ <0235.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$HB$ <0235.g2:/ H B />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved that, as :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` is to :g:`$GB$ <0235.g2:/ G B />`, so is :g:`$FC$ <0235.g1:/ F C />` to :g:`$GA$ <0235.g2:/ G A />`, and :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$FC$ <0235.g1:/ F C />` is to :g:`$AG$ <0235.g2:/ A G />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />`, and :g:`$FE$ <0235.g1:/ F E />` to :g:`$GH$ <0235.g2:/ G H />`, and further :g:`$ED$ <0235.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$HB$ <0235.g2:/ H B />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the angle :g:`$CFD$ <0235.g1:< C F D )>` is :eq:`equal <0235.g1:< C F D ) = 0235.g2:< A G B ); 0235.g1:< D F E ) = 0235.g2:< B G H )>` to the angle :g:`$AGB$ <0235.g2:< A G B )>`, and the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0235.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`$BGH$ <0235.g2:< B G H )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole angle :g:`$CFE$ <0235.g1:< C F E )>` is :eq:`equal <0235.g1:< C F E ) = 0235.g2:< A G H )>` to the whole angle :g:`$AGH$ <0235.g2:< A G H )>`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the angle :g:`$CDE$ <0235.g1:< C D E )>` is also :eq:`equal <0235.g1:< C D E ) = 0235.g2:< A B H )>` to the angle :g:`$ABH$ <0235.g2:< A B H )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle at :g:`$C$ <0235.g1:< F C D )>` is also :eq:`equal <0235.g1:< F C D ) = 0235.g2:< G A B ); 0235.g1:< F E D ) = 0235.g2:< G H B )>` to the angle at :g:`$A$ <0235.g2:< G A B )>`, and the angle at :g:`$E$ <0235.g1:< F E D )>` to the angle at :g:`$H$ <0235.g2:< G H B )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AH$ <0235.g2:< A H >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0235.g1:< C F E ) = 0235.g2:< A G H ); 0235.g1:< C D E ) = 0235.g2:< A B H ); 0235.g1:< F C D ) = 0235.g2:< G A B ); 0235.g1:< F E D ) = 0235.g2:< G H B )>` with :g:`$CE$ <0235.g1:< C E >>`;

.. subclause::

   and they have the sides about their equal angles proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectilinear figure :g:`$AH$ <0235.g2:< A H >>` is similar to the rectilineal figure :g:`$CE$ <0235.g1:< C E >>`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

.. qed::

   Therefore on the given straight line :g:`$AB$ <0235.g2:/ A B />` the rectilinear figure :g:`$AH$ <0235.g2:< A H >>` has been described similar and similarly situated to the given rectilinear figure :g:`$CE$ <0235.g1:< C E >>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`

:person:`Simson <simson>` thinks the proof of this proposition has been vitiated, his grounds for this view being (1) that it is demonstrated only with reference to quadrilaterals, and does not show how it may be extended to figures of five or more sides, (2) that Euclid infers, from the fact of two triangles being equiangular, that a side of the one is to the corresponding side of the other as another side of the first is to the side corresponding to it in the other, *i.e.* he permutes, without mentioning the fact that he does so, the proportions obtained in VI. 4, whereas the proof of the very next proposition gives, in a similar case, the intermediate step of permutation.
