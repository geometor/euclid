## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Significance of Clavius's Method**: Clavius relies on rigid body translation ("moving a figure bodily from one position to any other"), which goes beyond Euclid's strict ruler-and-compass postulate restrictions, but provides a much more direct affine/homothetic construction.
- **Central Projection / Homothety**: The second alternative construction anticipates projective geometry and dilatation with center $O$.
- **Reference to Subjoined Figure**: The text refers to "the subjoined figure", which appears at the top of page 232 (`0238.png`).
