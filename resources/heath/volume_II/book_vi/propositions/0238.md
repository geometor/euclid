De Morgan would rearrange Props. 18 and 20 in the following manner.<br />
He would combine Prop. 18 and the first part of Prop. 20 into one, with the<br />
enunciation :

![General construction for similar polygons](0238.g1.svg)

*Pairs of similar triangles, similarly put together, give similar figures ; and<br />
every pair of similar figures is composed of pairs of similar triangles similarly<br />
put together.*

He would then make the *problem* of VI. 18 an application of the first part.<br />
In form this would certainly appear to be an improvement; but, provided that<br />
the relation of the propositions is understood, the matter of form is perhaps<br />
not of great importance.

### PROPOSITION 19.

<p class="enunciation">Similar triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio<br />
of the corresponding sides.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> be similar triangles having the angle at<br />
<span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B C )">$B$</span> equal to the angle at <span data-ref="0238.g2:< D E F )">$E$</span>, and such that, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so<br />
<sup>5</sup> is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> corresponds to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>; <span class="justification">[v. Def. 11]</span>

I say that the triangle <span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> has to the triangle <span data-ref="0238.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> a ratio<br />
duplicate of that which <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

![Book VI Proposition 19 Diagram](0238.g2.svg)

For let a third proportional <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be taken to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so<br />
that, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 11]</span><br />
<sup>10</sup> and let <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, alternately, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[v. 16]</span></p>
