:person:`De Morgan <de-morgan>` would rearrange Props. 18 and 20 in the following manner. He would combine Prop. 18 and the first part of Prop. 20 into one, with the enunciation :

.. picture:: 0238.g1.svg
   :alt: General construction for similar polygons

*Pairs of similar triangles, similarly put together, give similar figures ; and every pair of similar figures is composed of pairs of similar triangles similarly put together.*

He would then make the *problem* of VI. 18 an application of the first part. In form this would certainly appear to be an improvement; but, provided that the relation of the propositions is understood, the matter of form is perhaps not of great importance.

----

PROPOSITION 19.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Similar triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides.

Let :g:`$ABC$ <0238.g2:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0238.g2:< D E F >>` be similar triangles having the angle at :g:`$B$ <0238.g2:< A B C )>` :eq:`equal <0238.g2:< A B C ) = 0238.g2:< D E F )>` to the angle at :g:`$E$ <0238.g2:< D E F )>`, and such that, as :g:`$AB$ <0238.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0238.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`,\ [#f1]_ so that :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />` corresponds to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`; :just:`[v. Def. 11] <V.def.11>`

I say that the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0238.g2:< A B C >>` has to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0238.g2:< D E F >>` a ratio duplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0238.g2.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 19 Diagram

For let a third proportional :g:`$BG$ <0238.g2:/ B G />` be taken to :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`, so that, as :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />` to :g:`$BG$ <0238.g2:/ B G />`; :just:`[VI. 11] <VI.11>`

and let :g:`$AG$ <0238.g2:/ A G />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Since then, as :g:`$AB$ <0238.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0238.g2:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [v. 16]

   therefore, alternately, as :g:`$AB$ <0238.g2:/ A B />` is to :g:`$DE$ <0238.g2:/ D E />`, so is :g:`$BC$ <0238.g2:/ B C />` to :g:`$EF$ <0238.g2:/ E F />`.
