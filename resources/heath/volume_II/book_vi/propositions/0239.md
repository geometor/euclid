De Morgan would tack on to Prop. 19 the second part of Prop. 20, which<br />
asserts that, if similar polygons be divided into the same number of similar<br />
triangles, the triangles are "homologous to the wholes" (in the sense that the<br />
polygons have the same ratio as the corresponding triangles have), and that<br />
the polygons are to one another in the duplicate ratio of corresponding sides.<br />
