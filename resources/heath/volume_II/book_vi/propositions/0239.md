<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span>. <span class="justification">[v. 11]</span></p>

<p class="assertion"><sup>15</sup> Therefore in the triangles <span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B G >">$ABG$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> the sides about<br />

<p class="sub-clause">But those triangles which have one angle equal to one<br />
angle, and in which the sides about the equal angles are<br />

<p class="assertion"><sup>20</sup> therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B G >">$ABG$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0238.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span>,<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span> a ratio duplicate of that which <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ C B /">$CB$</span><br />

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0238.g2:/ B G /">$BG$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0238.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> to the<br />
