# Transcription Notes: Folio 0239 (Page 233)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0238 (page 232) established the initial proportion $AB : DE = BC : EF$.
- **Folio Contents**:
  - Completes the proof of Proposition 19:
    - Combines proportions via V. 11 to get $AB : DE = EF : BG$.
    - Applies VI. 15 (triangles with reciprocally proportional sides about equal angles are equal) to show $\triangle ABG = \triangle DEF$.
