# Transcription Notes: Folio 0240 (Page 234)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0239 (page 233) opened discussion on De Morgan's suggestion to prove Prop. 19 via Prop. 23 (parallelograms and compound ratio).
- **Folio Contents**:
  - Heath defends Euclid's arrangement against De Morgan: compounding ratios directly vs. the geometric construction of a third proportional.
  - Scholarly discussion of Greek ratio manipulation (composition of ratios in Archimedes and Apollonius).
  - In-depth textual and philological analysis of the Porism to VI. 19:
    - The textual problem of εἶδος (*figure*) vs. τρίγωνον (*triangle*) or τετράγωνον (*square*).
    - Examination of manuscript witnesses: P (Vaticanus 190), Campanus, Philoponus, Psellus, and Theon's recension.
    - Heiberg's conclusions regarding Theon's alterations and the second Porism to VI. 20.
    - Heath's hypothesis regarding how Euclid may have relocated the Porism from after VI. 20 to VI. 19.
  - Begins note on Heiberg's bracketing of the final explanatory clause in the Porism.
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0241 (page 235) completes this note and begins Proposition 20.
