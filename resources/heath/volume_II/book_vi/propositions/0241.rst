PROPOSITION 20.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Similar polygons are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes,\ [#f1]_ and the polygon has to the polygon a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

Let :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` be similar polygons, and let :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` correspond to :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`;

I say that the polygons :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes, and the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` has to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>` a ratio duplicate of that which :g:`AB <0241.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`FG <0241.g1:/ F G />`.

Let :g:`BE <0241.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`EC <0241.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`GL <0241.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`LH <0241.g1:/ L H />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0241.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 20 Diagram

.. subclause::

   Now, since the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0241.g1:< A B C D E >>` is similar to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0241.g1:< F G H K L >>`,

.. assertion::

   the angle :g:`BAE <0241.g1:< B A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L )>` to the angle :g:`GFL <0241.g1:< G F L )>`;

.. assertion:: [VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>

   and, as :g:`BA <0241.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`AE <0241.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`GF <0241.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`FL <0241.g1:/ F L />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>` are two triangles having one angle :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L )>` to one angle and the sides about the equal angles proportional,

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0241.g1:< B A E ) = 0241.g1:< G F L ); 0241.g1:< A B E ) = 0241.g1:< F G L )>` with the triangle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L >>`; :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. assertion:: [VI. 4 and Def. 1] <VI.4, VI.def.1>

   so that it is also similar;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`ABE <0241.g1:< A B E )>` is :eq:`equal <0241.g1:< A B E ) = 0241.g1:< F G L )>` to the angle :g:`FGL <0241.g1:< F G L )>`.
