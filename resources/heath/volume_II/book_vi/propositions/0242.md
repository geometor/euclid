<p class="sub-clause"><sup>25</sup> But the whole angle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is also equal to the whole angle<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:< F G H )">$FGH$</span> because of the similarity of the polygons;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span> is equal to the angle<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:< L G H )">$LGH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since, because of the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0241.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span>,<br />
<sup>30</sup><span data-ref="0241.g1:< F G L >">$FGL$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">as <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ L G /">$LG$</span> to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and moreover also, because of the similarity of the polygons,</p>
<p class="assertion">as <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, *ex aequali*, as <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ L G /">$LG$</span> to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>; <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>
<p class="assertion"><sup>35</sup> that is, the sides about the equal angles <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:< L G H )">$LGH$</span> are<br />
proportional;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E B C >">$EBC$</span> is equiangular with the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:< L G H >">$LGH$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that the triangle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E B C >">$EBC$</span> is also similar to the triangle<br />
<sup>40</sup><span data-ref="0241.g1:< L G H >">$LGH$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 4 and Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason</p>
<p class="assertion">the triangle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C D >">$ECD$</span> is also similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0241.g1:< L H K >">$LHK$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the similar polygons <span data-ref="0241.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:< F G H K L >">$FGHKL$</span> have<br />

<sup>45</sup>multitude.</p>

I say that they are also in the same ratio as the wholes,<br />
that is, in such manner that the triangles are proportional,<br />
and <span data-ref="0241.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E B C >">$EBC$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C D >">$ECD$</span> are antecedents, while <span data-ref="0241.g1:< F G L >">$FGL$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:< L G H >">$LGH$</span>,<br />

<sup>50</sup>has to the polygon <span data-ref="0241.g1:< F G H K L >">$FGHKL$</span> a ratio duplicate of that which<br />
the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.
