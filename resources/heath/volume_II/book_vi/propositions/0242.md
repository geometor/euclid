<sup>25</sup> But the whole angle _ABC_ is also equal to the whole angle _FGH_ because of the similarity of the polygons;
therefore the remaining angle _EBC_ is equal to the angle _LGH_.

And, since, because of the similarity of the triangles _ABE_,
<sup>30</sup>_FGL_,
as _EB_ is to _BA_, so is _LG_ to _GF_,
and moreover also, because of the similarity of the polygons,
as _AB_ is to _BC_, so is _FG_ to _GH_,
therefore, _ex aequali_, as _EB_ is to _BC_, so is _LG_ to _GH_; [v. 22]
<sup>35</sup>that is, the sides about the equal angles _EBC_, _LGH_ are proportional;
therefore the triangle _EBC_ is equiangular with the triangle _LGH_, [VI. 6]
so that the triangle _EBC_ is also similar to the triangle
<sup>40</sup>_LGH_. [VI. 4 and Def. 1]

For the same reason
the triangle _ECD_ is also similar to the triangle _LHK_.

Therefore the similar polygons _ABCDE_, _FGHKL_ have been divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in
<sup>45</sup>multitude.

I say that they are also in the same ratio as the wholes, that is, in such manner that the triangles are proportional, and _ABE_, _EBC_, _ECD_ are antecedents, while _FGL_, _LGH_, _LHK_ are their consequents, and that the polygon _ABCDE_
<sup>50</sup>has to the polygon _FGHKL_ a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is _AB_ to _FG_.

For let _AC_, _FH_ be joined.

Then since, because of the similarity of the polygons,
<sup>55</sup>the angle _ABC_ is equal to the angle _FGH_,
and, as _AB_ is to _BC_, so is _FG_ to _GH_,
the triangle _ABC_ is equiangular with the triangle _FGH_; [VI. 6]
therefore the angle _BAC_ is equal to the angle _GFH_,
and the angle _BCA_ to the angle _GHF_.
