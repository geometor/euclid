<p class="sub-clause"><sup>25</sup> But the whole angle *ABC* is also equal to the whole angle<br />
*FGH* because of the similarity of the polygons;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle *EBC* is equal to the angle<br />
*LGH*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since, because of the similarity of the triangles *ABE*,<br />
<sup>30</sup>*FGL*,</p>
<p class="assertion">as *EB* is to *BA*, so is *LG* to *GF*,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and moreover also, because of the similarity of the polygons,</p>
<p class="assertion">as *AB* is to *BC*, so is *FG* to *GH*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, *ex aequali*, as *EB* is to *BC*, so is *LG* to *GH*; <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>
<p class="assertion"><sup>35</sup> that is, the sides about the equal angles *EBC*, *LGH* are<br />
proportional;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle *EBC* is equiangular with the triangle<br />
*LGH*, <span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that the triangle *EBC* is also similar to the triangle<br />
<sup>40</sup>*LGH*. <span class="justification">[VI. 4 and Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason</p>
<p class="assertion">the triangle *ECD* is also similar to the triangle *LHK*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the similar polygons *ABCDE*, *FGHKL* have<br />
been divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in<br />
<sup>45</sup>multitude.</p>

I say that they are also in the same ratio as the wholes,<br />
that is, in such manner that the triangles are proportional,<br />
and *ABE*, *EBC*, *ECD* are antecedents, while *FGL*, *LGH*,<br />
*LHK* are their consequents, and that the polygon *ABCDE*<br />
<sup>50</sup>has to the polygon *FGHKL* a ratio duplicate of that which<br />
the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is<br />
*AB* to *FG*.

For let *AC*, *FH* be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then since, because of the similarity of the polygons,<br />
<sup>55</sup>the angle *ABC* is equal to the angle *FGH*,<br />
and, as *AB* is to *BC*, so is *FG* to *GH*,</p>
<p class="assertion">the triangle *ABC* is equiangular with the triangle *FGH*; <span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle *BAC* is equal to the angle *GFH*,<br />
and the angle *BCA* to the angle *GHF*.</p>
