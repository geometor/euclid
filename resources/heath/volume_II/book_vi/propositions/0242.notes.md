# Transcription Notes: Folio 0242 (Page 236)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0241 (page 235) proved $\triangle ABE \sim \triangle FGL$ and $\angle ABE = \angle FGL$.
- **Folio Contents**:
  - Continues the proof of Proposition 20:
    - Subtracts equal angles from equal whole angles to show $\angle EBC = \angle LGH$.
    - Applies V. 22 (*ex aequali*) to show $EB : BC = LG : GH$.
    - Uses VI. 6 and VI. 4 to deduce $\triangle EBC \sim \triangle LGH$.
    - Extends the result inductively to $\triangle ECD \sim \triangle LHK$, completing Part 1.
    - Sets out Part 2 and Part 3: triangles are proportional to the wholes, and polygons are in duplicate ratio of corresponding sides.
    - Joins auxiliary lines $AC$ and $FH$.
    - Proves $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle FGH$, yielding $\angle BAC = \angle GFH$ and $\angle BCA = \angle GHF$.
    - Establishes angle equalities for $\triangle ABM$ and $\triangle FGN$.
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0243 (page 237) completes the proportionality of the triangles.
