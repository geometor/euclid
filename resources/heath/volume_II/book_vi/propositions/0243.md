<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle *AMB* is also equal to the<br />
remaining angle *FNG*; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle *ABM* is equiangular with the triangle<br />
<sup>65</sup>*FGN*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that<br />
the triangle *BMC* is also equiangular with the triangle *GNH*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, proportionally, as *AM* is to *MB*, so is *FN* to<br />
*NG*,<br />
<sup>70</sup>and, as *BM* is to *MC*, so is *GN* to *NH*;</p>
<p class="assertion">so that, in addition, *ex aequali*,<br />
as *AM* is to *MC*, so is *FN* to *NH*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as *AM* is to *MC*, so is the triangle *ABM* to *MBC*,<br />
and *AME* to *EMC*; for they are to one another as their<br />
<sup>75</sup>bases. <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Therefore also, as one of the antecedents is to one of the<br />
consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents; <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the triangle *AMB* is to *BMC*, so is *ABE* to<br />
*CBE*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause"><sup>80</sup> But, as *AMB* is to *BMC*, so is *AM* to *MC*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as *AM* is to *MC*, so is the triangle *ABE* to<br />
the triangle *EBC*.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason also,<br />
as *FN* is to *NH*, so is the triangle *FGL* to the triangle<br />
<sup>85</sup>*GLH*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as *AM* is to *MC*, so is *FN* to *NH*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the triangle *ABE* is to the triangle *BEC*,<br />
so is the triangle *FGL* to the triangle *GLH*;</p>
<p class="assertion">and, alternately, as the triangle *ABE* is to the triangle *FGL*,<br />
<sup>90</sup>so is the triangle *BEC* to the triangle *GLH*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove, if *BD*, *GK* be joined, that, as the<br />
triangle *BEC* is to the triangle *LGH*, so also is the triangle<br />
*ECD* to the triangle *LHK*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, as the triangle *ABE* is to the triangle *FGL*,<br />
<sup>95</sup>so is *EBC* to *LGH*, and further *ECD* to *LHK*,</p>
<p class="sub-clause">therefore also, as one of the antecedents is to one of the<br />
consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents; <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the triangle *ABE* is to the triangle *FGL*,<br />
so is the polygon *ABCDE* to the polygon *FGHKL*.</p>
