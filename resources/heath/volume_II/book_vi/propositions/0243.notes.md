# Transcription Notes: Folio 0243 (Page 237)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0242 (page 236) established equiangularity of two angles in $\triangle ABM$ and $\triangle FGN$.
- **Folio Contents**:
  - Proves $\triangle ABM \sim \triangle FGN$ (via I. 32).
  - Proves $\triangle BMC \sim \triangle GNH$ similarly.
  - Derives $AM : MC = FN : NH$ (*ex aequali*).
  - Uses VI. 1 (triangles under same height as bases) and V. 12 (sum of antecedents to sum of consequents) to establish:
    $\triangle ABE : \triangle EBC = AM : MC$ and $\triangle FGL : \triangle GLH = FN : NH$.
  - Deduces $\triangle ABE : \triangle FGL = \triangle BEC : \triangle GLH = \triangle ECD : \triangle LHK$ (alternando).
  - Applies V. 12 again to prove $\triangle ABE : \triangle FGL = \text{polygon } ABCDE : \text{polygon } FGHKL$, establishing Part 2 ("homologous to the wholes").
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0244 (page 238) establishes Part 3 (duplicate ratio), concludes Proposition 20, and begins commentary.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Euclidean Elegance vs. Complexity**: This folio exhibits Euclid's classic synthetic proof technique. Instead of using the area relations from VI. 19 directly, Euclid proves the proportionality of the triangular components using base ratios (VI. 1) and proportion manipulation (V. 12).
- **Line Numbers**: Marginal markers at 65, 70, 75, 80, 85, 90, and 95.
- **Cross References**:
  - `[I. 32]`: sum of angles of a triangle equals two right angles.
  - `[VI. 1]`: ratio of triangles with equal height is that of their bases.
  - `[v. 12]`: in a series of equal ratios, antecedent sum is to consequent sum as one antecedent is to one consequent. Printed as lowercase "v. 12".
