# Transcription Notes: Folio 0244 (Page 238)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0243 (page 237) proved that $\triangle ABE : \triangle FGL = \text{polygon } ABCDE : \text{polygon } FGHKL$.
- **Folio Contents**:
  - Completes Proposition 20:
    - By VI. 19, $\triangle ABE : \triangle FGL$ is duplicate ratio of $AB : FG$.
    - Therefore polygon $ABCDE$ to polygon $FGHKL$ is in duplicate ratio of $AB : FG$.
  - Porism extending duplicate ratio theorem to quadrilaterals and all rectilineal figures.
  - Q. E. D.
  - Heath's commentary:
    - Note on line 2: grammatical and semantic analysis of ὁμόλογος followed by dative (ὁμόλογα τοῖς ὅλοις).
    - Note on line 49: Greek phrase ἑπόμενα αὐτῶν ("their consequents").
    - Discussion of the interpolated alternative proof for Part 2 relegated to Appendix by August and Heiberg, but adopted by Clavius, Billingsley, Barrow, Simson.
    - Embedded diagram for the alternative proof (`0244.g1.png`).
    - Opening lines of the alternative proof.
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0245 (page 239) completes the alternative proof and introduces Proposition 21.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terms**:
  - ὁμόλογος (*homologos*, corresponding / homologous)
  - ὁμόλογα τοῖς ὅλοις (*homologa tois holois*, homologous with the wholes)
  - ἑπόμενα αὐτῶν (*hepomena auton*, their consequents)
- **Line Numbers**: Marginal markers at 100, 105, and 110.
- **Historical Textual Transmission**: Heath notes how earlier editors preferred the alternative proof because of its brevity, but August and Heiberg identified it as an ancient interpolation.
