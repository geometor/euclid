- **Textual Variant**: Heath notes that older editions insert a transitional step before the final conclusion of VI. 21 ("so that *A* is also equiangular with *B*..."). Heiberg rejects this step following MS P (Vaticanus 190), regarding it as an editorial addition by Theon of Alexandria.
- **Line Numbers**: No marginal line numbering is printed on this page in Heath's edition.
- **Mathematical Form**: The enunciation of Proposition 22 covers both the direct and converse cases for four proportional straight lines and similar figures described upon them.
