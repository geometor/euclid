.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0246.g1:A>` is similar to :g:`$C$ <0246.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   | it is equiangular with it and has the sides about the equal
   | angles proportional. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

   :alt: Book VI, Proposition 21

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$B$ <0246.g1:B>` is similar to :g:`$C$ <0246.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   | it is equiangular with it and has the sides about the equal
   | angles proportional.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore each of the figures :g:`$A$ <0246.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0246.g1:B>` is equiangular with :g:`$C$ <0246.g1:C>`
   | and with :g:`$C$ <0246.g1:C>` has the sides about the equal angles proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0246.g1:A>` is similar to :g:`$B$ <0246.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

PROPOSITION 22.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, the rectilineal figures similar and similarly described upon them will also be pro- portional; and, if the rectilineal figures similar and similarly described upon them be proportional, the straight lines will themselves also be proportional.

| Let the four straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0247.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0247.g1:/ G H />` be pro-
| portional,
| so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0247.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0247.g1:/ G H />`,
| and let there be described on :g:`$AB$ <0247.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />` the similar and similarly
| situated rectilineal figures :g:`$KAB$ <0247.g1:< K A B >>`, :g:`$LCD$ <0247.g1:< L C D >>`,
| and on :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0247.g1:/ G H />` the similar and similarly situated rectilineal
| figures :g:`$MF$ <0247.g1:< M F >>`, :g:`$NH$ <0247.g1:< N H >>`;
| I say that, as :g:`$KAB$ <0247.g1:< K A B >>` is to :g:`$LCD$ <0247.g1:< L C D >>`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0247.g1:< M F >>` to :g:`$NH$ <0247.g1:< N H >>`.

| For let there be taken a third proportional :g:`$O$ <0247.g1:O>` to :g:`$AB$ <0247.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />`,
| and a third proportional :g:`$P$ <0247.g1:P>` to :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0247.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[VI. 11] <VI.11>`
