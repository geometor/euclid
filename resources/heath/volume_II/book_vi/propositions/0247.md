<p class="sub-clause">Then since, as *AB* is to *CD*, so is *EF* to *GH*,<br />
and, as *CD* is to *O*, so is *GH* to *P*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, *ex aequali*, as *AB* is to *O*, so is *EF* to *P*. <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">But, as *AB* is to *O*, so is *KAB* to *LCD*,<br />
and, as *EF* is to *P*, so is *MF* to *NH*; <span class="justification">[VI. 19, Por.]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as *KAB* is to *LCD*, so is *MF* to *NH*. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

![Book VI, Proposition 22](0247.g1.svg)

Next, let *MF* be to *NH* as *KAB* is to *LCD*;<br />
I say also that, as *AB* is to *CD*, so is *EF* to *GH*.

<p class="sub-clause">For, if *EF* is not to *GH* as *AB* to *CD*,</p>
<p class="assertion">let *EF* be to *QR* as *AB* to *CD*, <span class="justification">[VI. 12]</span><br />
and on *QR* let the rectilineal figure *SR* be described similar<br />
and similarly situated to either of the two *MF*, *NH*. <span class="justification">[VI. 18]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then, as *AB* is to *CD*, so is *EF* to *QR*,<br />
and there have been described on *AB*, *CD* the similar and<br />
similarly situated figures *KAB*, *LCD*,<br />
and on *EF*, *QR* the similar and similarly situated figures<br />
*MF*, *SR*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as *KAB* is to *LCD*, so is *MF* to *SR*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But also, by hypothesis,<br />
as *KAB* is to *LCD*, so is *MF* to *NH*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as *MF* is to *SR*, so is *MF* to *NH*. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore *MF* has the same ratio to each of the figures<br />
*NH*, *SR*;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *NH* is equal to *SR*. <span class="justification">[V. 9]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it is also similar and similarly situated to it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *GH* is equal to *QR*.</p>
