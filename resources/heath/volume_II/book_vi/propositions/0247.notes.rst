2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Logical Structure**: Part 1 utilizes duplicate ratio expressed through third proportionals ($O, P$) and *ex aequali* proportion theory. Part 2 uses a constructive indirect method introducing an auxiliary fourth proportional segment $QR$ and similar figure $SR$.
- **Crux of the Demonstration**: Euclid concludes $GH = QR$ from $NH = SR$ on the basis that the figures are similar and similarly situated. As Heath details in subsequent commentary, this step assumes without prior demonstration that equality of similar rectilineal figures entails equality of corresponding sides.
