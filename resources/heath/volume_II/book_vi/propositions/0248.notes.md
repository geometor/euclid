# Transcription Notes: Folio 0248 (Page 242)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 242, concluding the formal proof of Proposition 22.
- Transition to Heath's extended mathematical commentary discussing the famous historical defect in Euclid's proof: assuming without proof that equality of similar figures implies equality of corresponding sides ($NH = SR \implies GH = QR$).
- Heath outlines two approaches to rectify the defect:
  1. The "minimum lemma": equal similar figures have equal corresponding sides (interpolated lemma found in the Greek text).
  2. The general theorem on duplicate ratios: equal duplicate ratios imply equal ratios.
- Transcribes the first part of the ancient interpolated lemma.

## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Scholarly Analysis**: Heiberg identifies the lemma following VI. 22 in Greek manuscripts as an interpolation, though pre-Theonine.
- **Cross References**: Mentions connections to VI. 28, VI. 29, and Book V proportion theory.
