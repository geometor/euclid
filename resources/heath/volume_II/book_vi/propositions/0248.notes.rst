2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Logical Defect**: Euclid assumes without lemma that if similar figures are equal, their corresponding sides are equal ($NH = SR \implies GH = QR$). Heath provides the historical context of how commentators (including Austin, Simson, and Heiberg) treated this omission.
- **Scholarly Analysis**: Heiberg identifies the lemma appearing in Greek manuscripts after VI. 22 as an interpolation because Euclid never places lemmas after the propositions in which they are needed; however, the lemma predates Theon of Alexandria.
- **Mathematical Cross-References**: Mentions VI. 28, VI. 29, and Book V proportion theory (V. 11, V. 25).
- **Authority Citations**: ``[by V. 11]`` cited in commentary.
