## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **De Morgan's Lemma**: Heath notes Euclid assumes without proof that if $D, F$ are given and rect. $D, F = E^2$, then $E$ is given. Heath provides three alternative proofs: De Morgan (p. 245), the "minimum lemma" (pp. 242—3), and Proclus on I. 46.
- **Line Numbers**: Line numbers `5` and `10` appear in the margin of Proposition 23, transcribed as `<sup>5</sup>` and `<sup>10</sup>`.
- **Fourth Proportionals**: Euclid cites VI. 12 for finding straight lines $L$ and $M$ such that $BC : CG = K : L$ and $DC : CE = L : M$.
