| Since each parallelogram in the figure of the proposition can be divided
| into pairs of equal triangles, and all the triangles which are the halves of either
| parallelogram have two sides respectively equal and the angles included by
| them equal or supplementary, it can be at once deduced from VI. 23 (or it
| can be independently proved by the same method) that *triangles which have
| one angle of the one equal or supplementary to one angle of the other are in the
| ratio compounded of the ratios of the sides about the equal or supplementary
| angles*. Cf. Pappus VII. pp. 894—6.

| VI. 23 also shows that *rectangles, and therefore parallelograms or triangles,
| are to one another in the ratio compounded of the ratios of their bases and
| heights*.

| The converse of VI. 23 is also true, as is easily proved by *reductio ad
| absurdum*. More generally, *if two parallelograms or triangles are in the ratio
| compounded of the ratios of two adjacent sides, the angles included by those sides
| are either equal or supplementary*.

PROPOSITION 24.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   In any parallelogram the parallelograms about the diameter are similar both to the whole and to one another.

| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` be a parallelogram, and :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` its diameter,
| and let :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>` be parallelograms
| about :g:`$AC$ <0257.g1:/ A C />` ;
| I say that each of the parallelograms
| :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, :g:`$HK$ <0257.g1:< H K >>` is similar both to the whole
| :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>` and to the other.

   :alt: Proposition 24 Diagram

.. subclause::

   | For, since :g:`$EF$ <0257.g1:/ E F />` has been drawn
   | parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0257.g1:/ B C />`, one of the sides of the
   | triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0257.g1:< A B C >>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 2] <VI.2>

   proportionally, as :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   | Again, since :g:`$FG$ <0257.g1:/ F G />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0257.g1:/ C D />`, one of
   | the sides of the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0257.g1:< A C D >>`,

.. assertion::

   proportionally, as :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, so is :g:`$DG$ <0257.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`$GA$ <0257.g1:/ G A />`. :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`.

.. subclause::

   But it was proved that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$CF$ <0257.g1:/ C F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0257.g1:/ F A />`, so also is :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$BE$ <0257.g1:/ B E />` is to :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />`, so is :g:`$DG$ <0257.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`$GA$ <0257.g1:/ G A />`,

.. subclause::

   and therefore, *componendo*,

.. assertion:: [V. 18] <V.18>

   as :g:`$BA$ <0257.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AE$ <0257.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`$DA$ <0257.g1:/ D A />` to :g:`$AG$ <0257.g1:/ A G />`,

.. subclause::

   and, alternately,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   as :g:`$BA$ <0257.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AD$ <0257.g1:/ A D />`, so is :g:`$EA$ <0257.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`$AG$ <0257.g1:/ A G />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`$ABCD$ <0257.g1:< A B C D >>`, :g:`$EG$ <0257.g1:< E G >>`, the sides
   | about the common angle :g:`$BAD$ <0257.g1:< B A D )>` are proportional.
