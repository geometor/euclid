<p class="assertion">the angle *AFG* is equal to the angle *DCA* ;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the angle *DAC* is common to the two triangles *ADC*,<br />
*AGF* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle *ADC* is equiangular with the triangle<br />
*AGF*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason</p>
<p class="assertion">the triangle *ACB* is also equiangular with the triangle<br />
*AFE*,</p>
<p class="assertion">and the whole parallelogram *ABCD* is equiangular with the<br />
parallelogram *EG*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Therefore, proportionally,</p>
<p class="assertion">as *AD* is to *DC*, so is *AG* to *GF*,<br />
as *DC* is to *CA*, so is *GF* to *FA*,<br />
as *AC* is to *CB*, so is *AF* to *FE*,<br />
and further, as *CB* is to *BA*, so is *FE* to *EA*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since it was proved that,<br />
as *DC* is to *CA*, so is *GF* to *FA*,<br />
and, as *AC* is to *CB*, so is *AF* to *FE*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, *ex aequali*, as *DC* is to *CB*, so is *GF* to *FE*. <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore in the parallelograms *ABCD*, *EG* the sides<br />
about the equal angles are proportional ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram *ABCD* is similar to the parallelo-<br />
gram *EG*. <span class="justification">[VI. Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason</p>
<p class="assertion">the parallelogram *ABCD* is also similar to the parallelogram<br />
*KH* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the parallelograms *EG*, *HK* is similar to<br />
*ABCD*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But figures similar to the same rectilineal figure are also<br />
similar to one another ; <span class="justification">[VI. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram *EG* is also similar to the parallelo-<br />
gram *HK*.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
