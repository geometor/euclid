### PROPOSITION 25.

<p class="enunciation">To construct one and the same figure similar to a given<br />
rectilineal figure and equal to another given rectilineal figure.</p>

Let *ABC* be the given rectilineal figure to which the<br />
figure to be constructed must be similar, and *D* that to which<br />
it must be equal ;<br />
thus it is required to construct one and the same figure similar<br />
to *ABC* and equal to *D*.

![Proposition 25 Diagram](0259.g1.svg)

Let there be applied to *BC* the parallelogram *BE* equal<br />
to the triangle *ABC* <span class="justification">[I. 44]</span>, and to *CE* the parallelogram *CM*<br />
equal to *D* in the angle *FCE* which is equal to the angle<br />
*CBL*. <span class="justification">[I. 45]</span>
