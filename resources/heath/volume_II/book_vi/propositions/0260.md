**3. to which the figure to be constructed must be similar,** literally "to which it<br />
is required to construct (one) similar," ᾧ δεῖ ὅμοιον συστήσασθαι.

This is the highly important problem which Pythagoras is credited with<br />
having solved. Compare the passage from Plutarch (*Symp.* VIII. 2, 4) quoted<br />
in the note on I. 44 above, Vol. I. pp. 343—4.

one and the *size* of another rectilineal figure. The corresponding proposition<br />
of the $Data$, Prop. 55, asserts that, "if an area (χωρίον) be given in form<br />
(εἴδει) and in magnitude, its sides will also be given in magnitude."
