Then, since, as <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> to <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and, if three straight lines be proportional, as the first is to the third, so is the figure on the first to the similar and similarly situated figure described on the second, [VI. 19, Por.] therefore, as <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0259.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< K G H >">$KGH$</span>.

Therefore also, as the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< K G H >">$KGH$</span>, so is the parallelogram <span data-ref="0259.g1:< B L E C >">$BE$</span> to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0259.g1:< C E M F >">$EF$</span>; therefore, alternately, as the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0259.g1:< B L E C >">$BE$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0259.g1:< K G H >">$KGH$</span> to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0259.g1:< C E M F >">$EF$</span>.
<span style="float: right;">[V. 16]</span>

But the parallelogram <span data-ref="0259.g1:< C E M F >">$EF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0259.g1:< D >">$D$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0259.g1:< K G H >">$KGH$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0259.g1:< D >">$D$</span>.

[^254_3]**3. to which the figure to be constructed must be similar,** literally "to which it is required to construct (one) similar," ᾧ δεῖ ὅμοιον συστήσασθαι.

This is the highly important problem which Pythagoras is credited with having solved. Compare the passage from Plutarch (*Symp.* VIII. 2, 4) quoted in the note on I. 44 above, Vol. I. pp. 343—4.

We are bidden to construct a rectilineal figure which shall have the *form* of one and the *size* of another rectilineal figure. The corresponding proposition of the *Data*, Prop. 55, asserts that, "if an area (χωρίον) be given in form (εἴδει) and in magnitude, its sides will also be given in magnitude."
