.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$BC$ <0259.g1:/ B C />` is in a straight line with :g:`$CF$ <0259.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$LE$ <0259.g1:/ L E />` with :g:`$EM$ <0259.g1:/ E M />`.

.. subclause::

   Now let :g:`$GH$ <0259.g1:/ G H />` be taken a mean proportional to :g:`$BC$ <0259.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CF$ <0259.g1:/ C F />` :just:`[VI. 13] <VI.13>`, and on :g:`$GH$ <0259.g1:/ G H />` let :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` be described similar and similarly situated to :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>`. :just:`[VI. 18] <VI.18>`

.. subclause::

   Then, since, as :g:`$BC$ <0259.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$GH$ <0259.g1:/ G H />`, so is :g:`$GH$ <0259.g1:/ G H />` to :g:`$CF$ <0259.g1:/ C F />`, and, if three straight lines be proportional, as the first is to the third, so is the figure on the first to the similar and similarly situated figure described on the second, :just:`[VI. 19, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$BC$ <0259.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0259.g1:/ C F />`, so is the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$BC$ <0259.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CF$ <0259.g1:/ C F />`, so also is the parallelogram :g:`$BE$ <0259.g1:< B L E C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EF$ <0259.g1:< C E M F >>`. :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>`, so is the parallelogram :g:`$BE$ <0259.g1:< B L E C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EF$ <0259.g1:< C E M F >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore, alternately, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>` is to the parallelogram :g:`$BE$ <0259.g1:< B L E C >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EF$ <0259.g1:< C E M F >>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0259.g1:< A B C > = 0259.g1:< B L E C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$BE$ <0259.g1:< B L E C >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` is also :eq:`equal <0259.g1:< K G H > = 0259.g1:< C E M F >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EF$ <0259.g1:< C E M F >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the parallelogram :g:`$EF$ <0259.g1:< C E M F >>` is :eq:`equal <0259.g1:< C E M F > = 0259.g1:< D >>` to :g:`$D$ <0259.g1:< D >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` is also :eq:`equal <0259.g1:< K G H > = 0259.g1:< D >>` to :g:`$D$ <0259.g1:< D >>`.

.. assertion::

   And :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` is also similar to :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore one and the same figure :g:`$KGH$ <0259.g1:< K G H >>` has been constructed similar to the given rectilineal figure :g:`$ABC$ <0259.g1:< A B C >>` and :eq:`equal <0259.g1:< K G H > = 0259.g1:< D >>` to the other given figure :g:`$D$ <0259.g1:< D >>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`

.. [#f1] **to which the figure to be constructed must be similar,** literally "to which it is required to construct (one) similar," ᾧ δεῖ ὅμοιον συστήσασθαι.

This is the highly important problem which :person:`Pythagoras <pythagoras>` is credited with having solved. Compare the passage from :person:`Plutarch <plutarch>` (*Symp.* VIII. 2, 4) quoted in the note on I. 44 above, Vol. I. pp. 343—4.

We are bidden to construct a rectilineal figure which shall have the *form* of one and the *size* of another rectilineal figure. The corresponding proposition of the $Data$, Prop. 55, asserts that, "if an area (χωρίον) be given in form (εἴδει) and in magnitude, its sides will also be given in magnitude."
