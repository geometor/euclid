The other observation is more important. After Euclid has proved that
$$\text{(fig. } <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">ABC</span>\text{) : (fig. } <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; K G H &gt;">KGH</span>\text{)} = (<span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; B L E C &gt;">BE</span>) : (<span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; C E M F &gt;">EF</span>),$$
he might have inferred *directly* from V. 14 that, since <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">$ABC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; B L E C &gt;">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; K G H &gt;">$KGH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; C E M F &gt;">$EF$</span>. For V. 14 includes the proof of the fact that, if $A$ is to $B$ as $C$ is to $D$, and $A$ is equal to $C$, then $B$ is equal to $D$, or that of four proportional magnitudes, if the first is equal to the third, the second is equal to the fourth. Instead of proceeding in this way, Euclid first permutes the proportion by V. 16 into
$$\text{(fig. } <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; A B C &gt;">ABC</span>\text{) : }(<span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; B L E C &gt;">BE</span>) = \text{(fig. } <span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; K G H &gt;">KGH</span>\text{) : }(<span data-ref="0259.g1:&lt; C E M F &gt;">EF</span>),$$
and then infers, as if the inference were easier in this form, that, since the *first* is equal to the *second*, the *third* is equal to the *fourth*. Yet there is no proposition to this effect in Euclid. The same unnecessary step of permutation is also found in the Greek text of XI. 23 and XII. 2, 5, 11, 12 and 18. In reproducing the proofs we may simply leave out the steps and refer to V. 14.

### PROPOSITION 26.

*If from a parallelogram there be taken away a parallelogram similar and similarly situated to the whole and having a common angle with it, it is about the same diameter with the whole.*

For from the parallelogram <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> let there be taken away the parallelogram <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A E F G &gt;">$AF$</span> similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>, and having the angle <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; D A B )">$DAB$</span> common with it;

I say that <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> is about the same diameter with <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A E F G &gt;">$AF$</span>.

![Proposition 26](0261.g1.svg)

For suppose it is not, but, if possible, let <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A H C /">$AHC$</span> be the diameter <*of* <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>>, let <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> be produced and carried through to <span data-ref="0261.g1:H">$H$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ K H /">$HK$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0261.g1:H">$H$</span> parallel to either of the straight lines <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.
<span style="float: right;">[I. 31]</span>

Since, then, <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span> is about the same diameter with <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A K H G &gt;">$KG$</span>, therefore, as <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A D /">$DA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>.
<span style="float: right;">[VI. 24]</span>

But also, because of the similarity of <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A B C D &gt;">$ABCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0261.g1:&lt; A E F G &gt;">$EG$</span>, as <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A D /">$DA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>; therefore also, as <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> to <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>.
<span style="float: right;">[V. 11]</span>

Therefore <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> has the same ratio to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0261.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>.
