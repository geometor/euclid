# Transcription Notes: Folio 0262 (Page 256)

- **Proposition**: VI. 26 (concluded); Heath Commentary on VI. 26.

- **Continuity Forward**: Page completes the discussion and extension of VI. 26. Folio 0263 (Page 257) begins Proposition 27.

## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Greek Text & Manuscript Variants**:
  - `ἔστω αὐτῶν διάμετρος ἡ ΑΘΓ`: Heath critiques `αὐτῶν` ("of them"), noting manuscripts F and V omit it, whereas Peyrard proposed `αὐτοῦ`. Heiberg followed F and V.
  - `(καὶ ἐκβληθεῖσα ἡ ΗΖ διήχθω ἐπὶ τὸ Θ)`: Clause omitted by Theon due to MS figure label confusion between E, Z and K, Θ. August and Heiberg restored the reading and corrected the diagram labels.
- **Direct Proof Alternative**:
  - Heath cites Dr. Lachlan's direct proof using VI. Def. 1 and VI. 6 (similar triangles $\triangle AEF \sim \triangle ABC \implies \angle FAE = \angle CAB$), avoiding the reductio.

## Mathematical & Historical Commentary
- **Extension to Vertically Opposite Parallelograms**:
  - Heath explains that VI. 26 applies equally when the similar parallelogram is placed vertically opposite at angle $A$ rather than "taken away" from within. In that case, the diagonals are collinear (form one continuous straight line).
  - This geometric lemma is fundamental to Euclid's second solution of VI. 28.
