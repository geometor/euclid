2. Textual & Lexical Observations
---------------------------------

- **Greek Text & Manuscript Variants**:

  - ``ἔστω αὐτῶν διάμετρος ἡ ΑΘΓ``: Heath critiques ``αὐτῶν`` ("of them"), noting manuscripts F and V omit it, whereas Peyrard proposed ``αὐτοῦ``. Heiberg followed F and V. Heath inserts ``<of ABCD>`` in English translation.
  - ``(καὶ ἐκβληθεῖσα ἡ ΗΖ διήχθω ἐπὶ τὸ Θ)``: Clause omitted by Theon due to MS figure label confusion between E, Z and K, Θ. August and Heiberg restored the reading and corrected the diagram labels.

- **Direct Proof Alternative**:

  - Heath cites Dr. Lachlan's direct proof using VI. Def. 1 and VI. 6 (similar triangles $\triangle AEF \sim \triangle ABC \implies \angle FAE = \angle CAB$), avoiding the reductio.

- **Cross References**:

  - ``[V. 9]``: Magnitudes which have the same ratio to the same are equal to one another ($AE = AK$, yielding the contradiction).
  - ``[VI. Def. 1]``: Definition of similar rectilineal figures.
  - ``[VI. 6]``: Equiangular triangles with proportional sides about equal angles.
