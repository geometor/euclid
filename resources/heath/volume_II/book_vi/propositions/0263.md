### PROPOSITION 27.

<p class="enunciation">Of all the parallelograms applied to the same straight line<br />
and deficient by parallelogrammic figures similar and similarly<br />
situated to that described on the half of the straight line, that<br />
parallelogram is greatest which is applied to the half of the<br />
straight line and is similar to the defect.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a straight line and let it be bisected at <span data-ref="0263.g1:C">$C$</span> ;<br />
let there be applied to the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> the parallelogram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span><br />
deficient by the parallelogrammic<br />
figure <span data-ref="0263.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span> described on the half of<br />
<span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> ;

I say that, of all the parallelograms<br />
applied to <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and deficient by<br />
parallelogrammic figures similar and<br />
similarly situated to <span data-ref="0263.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span>, <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is greatest.

![Proposition 27](0263.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For let there be applied to the straight line <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> the<br />
parallelogram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> deficient by the parallelogrammic figure<br />
<span data-ref="0263.g1:< F B >">$FB$</span> similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0263.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span> ;</p>

I say that <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the parallelogram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span> is similar to the parallelo-<br />
gram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< F B >">$FB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">they are about the same diameter. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let their diameter <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be drawn, and let the figure be<br />
described.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0263.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0263.g1:< F B >">$FB$</span> is common,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span> is equal to the whole <span data-ref="0263.g1:< K E >">$KE$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">But <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span>, since <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0263.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 36]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C G >">$GC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0263.g1:< K E >">$EK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0263.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span> be added to each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0263.g1:< L M N >">$LMN$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">so that the parallelogram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, is greater than<br />
the parallelogram <span data-ref="0263.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
