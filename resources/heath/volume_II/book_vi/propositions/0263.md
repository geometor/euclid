### PROPOSITION 27.

<p class="enunciation">Of all the parallelograms applied to the same straight line<br />
and deficient by parallelogrammic figures similar and similarly<br />
situated to that described on the half of the straight line, that<br />
parallelogram is greatest which is applied to the half of the<br />
straight line and is similar to the defect.</p>

Let *AB* be a straight line and let it be bisected at *C* ;<br />
let there be applied to the straight<br />
line *AB* the parallelogram *AD*<br />
deficient by the parallelogrammic<br />
figure *DB* described on the half of<br />
*AB*, that is, *CB* ;

I say that, of all the parallelograms<br />
applied to *AB* and deficient by<br />
parallelogrammic figures similar and<br />
similarly situated to *DB*, *AD* is greatest.

![Proposition 27](0263.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For let there be applied to the straight line *AB* the<br />
parallelogram *AF* deficient by the parallelogrammic figure<br />
*FB* similar and similarly situated to *DB* ;</p>

I say that *AD* is greater than *AF*.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the parallelogram *DB* is similar to the parallelo-<br />
gram *FB*,</p>
<p class="assertion">they are about the same diameter. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let their diameter *DB* be drawn, and let the figure be<br />
described.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since *CF* is equal to *FE*, <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span><br />
and *FB* is common,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole *CH* is equal to the whole *KE*.</p>

<p class="assertion">But *CH* is equal to *CG*, since *AC* is also equal to *CB*.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 36]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore *GC* is also equal to *EK*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *CF* be added to each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole *AF* is equal to the gnomon *LMN* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">so that the parallelogram *DB*, that is, *AD*, is greater than<br />
the parallelogram *AF*.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
