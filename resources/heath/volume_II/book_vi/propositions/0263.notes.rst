2. Textual & Lexical Observations
---------------------------------

- **Technical Vocabulary**:

  - "applied to the same straight line" (παραβαλλόμενα παρὰ τὴν αὐτὴν εὐθεῖαν).
  - "deficient by parallelogrammic figures" (ἐλλείποντα εἴδεσι παραλληλογράμμοις).
  - "gnomon *LMN*": The complementary area resulting from subtracting sub-parallelogram $FB$ from $DB$ is denoted as gnomon $LMN$.

- **Proof Structure**:

  - Bisect $AB$ at $C$, apply parallelogram $AD$ on $AC$ deficient by $DB$ on $CB$.
  - For any other applied parallelogram $AF$ on $AK$ deficient by $FB$ on $KB$, by VI. 26 $DB$ and $FB$ share common diameter $DB$.
  - Using complements $CF = FE$ (I. 43) and equal bases $AC = CB$ (I. 36), the area of $AF$ equals gnomon $LMN < DB = AD$.

- **Cross References**:

  - ``[VI. 26]``: Parallelogram taken away similar and similarly situated to the whole shares the same diameter.
  - ``[I. 43]``: Complements of parallelograms about the diameter are equal ($CF = FE$).
  - ``[I. 36]``: Parallelograms on equal bases and in the same parallels are equal ($CH = CG$).
