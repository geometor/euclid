## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Critical Commentary on Modern Editors**:
  - Heath sharply critiques Todhunter, Austin, Walker, and Lardner for excising VI. 27–29 as "useless".
- **Greek Terms**:
  - `διορισμός` (*diorismos*): condition of possibility / determination.
  - `τὸ ἀπὸ τῆς ἡμισείας ἀναγραφόμενον` (*to apo tēs hēmiseias anagraphomenon*): the figure described on half the line.
- **Mathematical Symbolism**:
  - Standard Heath mathematical notation for non-exceeding: $S \ngtr \frac{c}{b}\cdot\frac{a^2}{4}$.

## Mathematical & Historical Commentary
- **Algebraic Derivation of the Diorismos**:
  - Heath formulates the algebraic equivalent: $ax - \frac{b}{c}x^2 = S$.
  - Setting $CB = \frac{a}{2}$, the maximum rectangle $CE$ has sides $\frac{a}{2}$ and $\frac{c}{b}\frac{a}{2}$, giving maximum area $S_{\max} = \frac{c}{b}\frac{a^2}{4}$.
  - Special case when defect is a square ($b = c$): reduces to Euclid II. 5, stating that the rectangle contained by the segments of a divided line is maximized when the line is bisected.
- **Introduction of the Interpolated Second "Case"**:
  - Takes point $F$ on $BD$ produced beyond $D$ with $DF < BD$, leading to the discussion on page 260.
