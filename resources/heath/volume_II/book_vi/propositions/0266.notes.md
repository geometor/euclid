## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Textual Criticism of the Second "Case"**:
  - Found in all manuscripts, but relegated by Heiberg to Appendix II as a post-Euclidean interpolation.
  - Reasons:
    1. Un-Euclidean structure: appended after the formal conclusion ("Q. E. D.") rather than integrated before.
    2. Lack of preliminary announcement in the protasis/enunciation.
    3. Easy mathematical reduction to the primary case by reflecting $F$ to $F'$ across $D$ ($FD = F'D$).
- **Proposition 28 Protasis**:
  - Enunciation incorporates the diorismos established in VI. 27: "thus the given rectilinear figure must not be greater than the parallelogram described on the half of the straight line and similar to the defect."

## Mathematical & Historical Commentary
- **Geometric Quadratic Solution**:
  - Proposition VI. 28 provides the full geometric construction solving $ax - px^2 = C$ subject to $C \le \text{Max}$.
- **Excess/Defect Area Visualization**:
  - Heath highlights the geometric elegance: the difference between any applied area $AF$ and the maximum area $AD$ is precisely represented by the small parallelogram $DF$.
