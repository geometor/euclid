## 2. Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Textual Criticism of the Second "Case"**:
  - Found in all manuscripts ("mss."), but relegated by Heiberg to Appendix II as a post-Euclidean interpolation.
  - Reasons:
    1. Un-Euclidean structure: appended after the formal *conclusion* rather than integrated before it.
    2. Lack of preliminary announcement in the protasis/enunciation.
    3. Easy mathematical reduction to the primary case by reflecting $F$ to $F'$ across $D$ ($FD = F'D$).
- **Proposition 28 Protasis**:
  - Standardized heading `### PROPOSITION 28.`.
  - Enunciation incorporates the diorismos established in VI. 27: "thus the given rectilineal figure must not be greater than the parallelogram described on the half of the straight line and similar to the defect."
  - Heath's translation uses "rectilineal" (matching the Cambridge letterpress).
