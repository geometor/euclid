<p class="sub-clause">But, if not, let *HE* be greater than *C*.</p>
<p class="assertion">Now *HE* is equal to *GB* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *GB* is also greater than *C*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *KLMN* be constructed at once equal to the excess<br />
by which *GB* is greater than *C* and similar and similarly<br />
situated to *D*. <span class="justification">[VI. 25]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But *D* is similar to *GB* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *KM* is also similar to *GB*. <span class="justification">[VI. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let, then, *KL* correspond to *GE*, and *LM* to *GF*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since *GB* is equal to *C*, *KM*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *GB* is greater than *KM* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also *GE* is greater than *KL*, and *GF* than *LM*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *GO* be made equal to *KL*, and *GP* equal to *LM* ;<br />
and let the parallelogram *OGPQ* be completed ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore it is equal and similar to *KM*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore *GQ* is also similar to *GB* ; <span class="justification">[VI. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *GQ* is about the same diameter with *GB*. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>
