Let <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be bisected at the point <span data-ref="0267.g1:E">$E$</span>, and on <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> let <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; E B F G &gt;">$EBFG$</span> be described similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; D &gt;">$D$</span>; [VI. 18]

If then <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>, that which was enjoined will have been done;
for there has been applied to the given straight line <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> the parallelogram <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; A G &gt;">$AG$</span> equal to the given rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span> and deficient by a parallelogrammic figure <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span> which is similar to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; D &gt;">$D$</span>.

But, if not, let <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; H E &gt;">$HE$</span> be greater than <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>.
Now <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; H E &gt;">$HE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>.
Let <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; K L M N &gt;">$KLMN$</span> be constructed at once equal to the excess by which <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span> and similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; D &gt;">$D$</span>. [VI. 25]
But <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; D &gt;">$D$</span> is similar to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; K M &gt;">$KM$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span>. [VI. 21]
Let, then, <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> correspond to <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> to <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>.
Now, since <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; C &gt;">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; K M &gt;">$KM$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; K M &gt;">$KM$</span>;
therefore also <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> than <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>.
Let <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G O /">$GO$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ G P /">$GP$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0267.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>;
and let the parallelogram <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; O G P Q &gt;">$OGPQ$</span> be completed;
therefore it is equal and similar to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; K M &gt;">$KM$</span>.
Therefore <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G Q &gt;">$GQ$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span>; [VI. 21]
therefore <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G Q &gt;">$GQ$</span> is about the same diameter with <span data-ref="0267.g1:&lt; G B &gt;">$GB$</span>. [VI. 26]
