# Transcription Notes: Folio 0268 (Page 262)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- Page 262 concludes the Euclidean proof of Proposition 28 (Q.E.F.) and begins Heath's commentary.
- Euclid completes the gnomon argument: gnomon $UWV = C$; complements $PR = OS$, so $PB = OB = TE$; adding $OS$ gives $TS = \text{gnomon } VWU = C$. Thus parallelogram $ST$ is applied to $AB$, equal to $C$, with defect $QB \sim D$.
- Heath's commentary critiques Theon's expanded reading of the διορισμός (diorismos) compared to MS. P (Vat. gr. 190), and highlights the tacit assumption that for two similar parallelograms, if one is greater than the other, its sides are correspondingly greater.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- Greek transcriptions verified:
  - `διορισμός` (determination / condition of possibility).
  - `ᾧ δεῖ ἴσον παραβαλεῖν` (to which it is required to apply [a figure] equal).
  - `παραβαλλομένου instead of ἀναγραφομένου` (applied instead of described).
  - `ὁμοίων ὄντων τῶν ἐλλειμμάτων τοῦ τε ἀπὸ τῆς ἡμισείας καὶ ᾧ δεῖ ὅμοιον ἐλλείπειν` (the defects being similar...).
- Reference to MS. P: Heiberg's primary manuscript authority for Euclid's original text over Theonine recensions.
- Reference to I. 36 (parallelograms on equal bases and in same parallels are equal).
