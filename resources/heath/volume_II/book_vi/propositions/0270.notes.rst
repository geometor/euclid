2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Derivation of the Roots**:

  - The Euclidean construction yields the negative root: $x = \frac{c}{b}\frac{a}{2} - \sqrt{\frac{c}{b}\left(\frac{c}{b}\frac{a^2}{4} - S\right)}$ by taking $x = QS = GE - GO$.
  - Two geometrical constructions for the positive root ($x = EG + GO$):

    1. Placing $GOQP$ in the vertically opposite angle to $FGE$ along $BG$ produced, giving parallelogram $AQ'$.

- **Euclid's Data Prop. 85**:

  - Special case where defect is a rhombus with a given angle (sides of defect equal).
  - Given area $AC$ in angle $ABC$, and given sum of containing sides $CD = CB + BD = CB + AB$.
  - Leads to application with rhombus defect on base $CD$.
