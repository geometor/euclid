### PROPOSITION 29.

<p class="enunciation">To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to<br />
a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic<br />
figure similar to a given one.</p>

Let *AB* be the given straight line, *C* the given rectilineal<br />
figure to which the figure to be applied to *AB* is required to<br />
be equal, and *D* that to which the excess is required to be<br />
similar ;<br />
thus it is required to apply to the straight line *AB* a parallelo-<br />
gram equal to the rectilineal figure *C* and exceeding by a<br />
parallelogrammic figure similar to *D*.

![Book VI Proposition 29](0271.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Let *AB* be bisected at *E* ;<br />
let there be described on *EB* the parallelogram *BF* similar<br />
and similarly situated to *D* ;<br />
and let *GH* be constructed at once equal to the sum of *BF*,<br />
*C* and similar and similarly situated to *D*. <span class="justification">[VI. 25]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *KH* correspond to *FL* and *KG* to *FE*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since *GH* is greater than *FB*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *KH* is also greater than *FL*, and *KG* than *FE*.</p>
