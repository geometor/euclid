### PROPOSITION 29.

<p class="enunciation">To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to<br />
a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic<br />
figure similar to a given one.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the given straight line, <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> the given rectilineal<br />
figure to which the figure to be applied to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is required to<br />
be equal, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span> that to which the excess is required to be<br />
similar ;<br />
thus it is required to apply to the straight line <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelo-<br />
gram equal to the rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> and exceeding by a<br />
parallelogrammic figure similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>.

![Book VI Proposition 29](0271.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0271.g1:E">$E$</span> ;<br />
let there be described on <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> the parallelogram <span data-ref="0271.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span> similar<br />
and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span> ;<br />
and let <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> be constructed at once equal to the sum of <span data-ref="0271.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> and similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 25]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> correspond to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> and <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0271.g1:< F B >">$FB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> than <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.</p>
