### PROPOSITION 29.

*To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to a given one.*

Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the given straight line, <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> the given rectilineal figure to which the figure to be applied to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is required to be equal, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span> that to which the excess is required to be similar;
thus it is required to apply to the straight line <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelogram equal to the rectilineal figure <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>.

![Book VI Proposition 29](0271.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0271.g1:E">$E$</span>;
let there be described on <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> the parallelogram <span data-ref="0271.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span> similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>;
and let <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> be constructed at once equal to the sum of <span data-ref="0271.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>, <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> and similar and similarly situated to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>. [VI. 25]
Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> correspond to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> and <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.
Now, since <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0271.g1:< F B >">$FB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> than <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.
