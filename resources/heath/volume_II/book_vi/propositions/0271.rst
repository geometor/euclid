PROPOSITION 29.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to a given one.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` the given rectilineal
| figure to which the figure to be applied to :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` is required to
| be equal, and :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>` that to which the excess is required to be
| similar ;
| thus it is required to apply to the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` a parallelo-
| gram equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` and exceeding by a
| parallelogrammic figure similar to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>`.

.. picture:: 0271.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 29

.. subclause::

   | Let :g:`$AB$ <0271.g1:/ A B />` be bisected at :g:`$E$ <0271.g1:E>` ;
   | let there be described on :g:`$EB$ <0271.g1:/ E B />` the parallelogram :g:`$BF$ <0271.g1:< B F >>` similar
   | and similarly situated to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>` ;
   | and let :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` be constructed at once equal to the sum of :g:`$BF$ <0271.g1:< B F >>`,
   | :g:`$C$ <0271.g1:< C >>` and similar and similarly situated to :g:`$D$ <0271.g1:< D >>`. :just:`[VI. 25] <VI.25>`

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$KH$ <0271.g1:/ K H />` correspond to :g:`$FL$ <0271.g1:/ F L />` and :g:`$KG$ <0271.g1:/ K G />` to :g:`$FE$ <0271.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$GH$ <0271.g1:< G H >>` is greater than :g:`$FB$ <0271.g1:< F B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KH$ <0271.g1:/ K H />` is also greater than :g:`$FL$ <0271.g1:/ F L />`, and :g:`$KG$ <0271.g1:/ K G />` than :g:`$FE$ <0271.g1:/ F E />`.
