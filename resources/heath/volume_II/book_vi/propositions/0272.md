<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:[ F L ]">$FL$</span>, <span data-ref="0271.g1:[ F E ]">$FE$</span> be produced,<br />
let <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F L M /">$FLM$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E N /">$FEN$</span> to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> be completed ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is both equal and similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> ; <span class="justification">[VI. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is about the same diameter with <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let their diameter <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F O /">$FO$</span> be drawn, and let the figure be<br />
described.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0271.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> be subtracted from each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the gnomon <span data-ref="0271.g1:< X W V >">$XWV$</span>, is equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0271.g1:< A N >">$AN$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< N B >">$NB$</span> <span class="justification">[I. 36]</span>, that is, to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< L P >">$LP$</span> <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E O >">$EO$</span> be added to each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0271.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0271.g1:< V W X >">$VWX$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the gnomon <span data-ref="0271.g1:< V W X >">$VWX$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0271.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore to the given straight line <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> there has been<br />
applied the parallelogram <span data-ref="0271.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> equal to the given rectilineal<br />
figure <span data-ref="0271.g1:< C >">$C$</span> and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure <span data-ref="0271.g1:< Q P >">$QP$</span><br />
which is similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span>, since <span data-ref="0271.g1:< P Q >">$PQ$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> <span class="justification">[VI. 24]</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

The corresponding proposition in the $Data$ is (Prop. 59), *If a given (area)<br />
be applied (i.e. in the form of a parallelogram) to a given straight line exceeding<br />
by a figure given in species, the breadths of the excess are given.*

The problem of VI. 29 corresponds of course to the solution of the<br />
quadratic equation
$$ax + \frac{b}{c} x^2 = S.$$

The algebraical solution of this equation gives
$$x = - \frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a}{2} \pm \sqrt{\frac{c}{b} \left(\frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a^2}{4} + S\right)}.$$

The exact correspondence of Euclid's method to the algebraical solution<br />
may be seen, as in the case of VI. 28, by supposing the parallelograms to be<br />
*rectangles*. In this case Euclid's construction on <span data-ref="0271.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> similar to <span data-ref="0271.g1:< D >">$D$</span> is equivalent to finding that
<p align="center"><span data-ref="0271.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> = $\frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a}{2}$, and <span data-ref="0271.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> = $\frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a^2}{4}$.</p>
