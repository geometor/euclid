<p class="sub-clause">Let *FL*, *FE* be produced,<br />
let *FLM* be equal to *KH*, and *FEN* to *KG*,<br />
and let *MN* be completed ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *MN* is both equal and similar to *GH*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But *GH* is similar to *EL* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *MN* is also similar to *EL* ; <span class="justification">[VI. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *EL* is about the same diameter with *MN*. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let their diameter *FO* be drawn, and let the figure be<br />
described.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since *GH* is equal to *EL*, *C*,<br />
while *GH* is equal to *MN*,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *MN* is also equal to *EL*, *C*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *EL* be subtracted from each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the gnomon *XWV*, is equal to *C*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since *AE* is equal to *EB*,</p>
<p class="assertion">*AN* is also equal to *NB* <span class="justification">[I. 36]</span>, that is, to *LP* <span class="justification">[I. 43]</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let *EO* be added to each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole *AO* is equal to the gnomon *VWX*.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the gnomon *VWX* is equal to *C* ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore *AO* is also equal to *C*.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore to the given straight line *AB* there has been<br />
applied the parallelogram *AO* equal to the given rectilineal<br />
figure *C* and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure *QP*<br />
which is similar to *D*, since *PQ* is also similar to *EL* <span class="justification">[VI. 24]</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

The corresponding proposition in the *Data* is (Prop. 59), *If a given (area)<br />
be applied (i.e. in the form of a parallelogram) to a given straight line exceeding<br />
by a figure given in species, the breadths of the excess are given.*

The problem of VI. 29 corresponds of course to the solution of the<br />
quadratic equation
$$ax + \frac{b}{c} x^2 = S.$$

The algebraical solution of this equation gives
$$x = - \frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a}{2} \pm \sqrt{\frac{c}{b} \left(\frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a^2}{4} + S\right)}.$$

The exact correspondence of Euclid's method to the algebraical solution<br />
may be seen, as in the case of VI. 28, by supposing the parallelograms to be<br />
*rectangles*. In this case Euclid's construction on *EB* of the parallelogram<br />
*EL* similar to *D* is equivalent to finding that
$$FE = \frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a}{2}, \quad \text{and } EL = \frac{c}{b} \cdot \frac{a^2}{4}.$$
