# Transcription Notes: Folio 0273 (Page 267)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- Page 267 concludes Heath's commentary on Proposition 29 (determining $FN$, absence of a diorismos, connection to *Data* Prop. 84, and Book II. 6 for the square excess) and begins Proposition 30.
- Proposition 30 addresses cutting a given finite straight line in extreme and mean ratio ($\alpha\kappa\rho\omicron\varsigma \; \kappa\alpha\iota \; \mu\acute{\varepsilon}\sigma\omicron\varsigma \; \lambda\acute{\omicron}\gamma\omicron\varsigma$ — golden ratio / section).
- Euclid's construction uses VI. 29 directly: describe square $BC$ on $AB$, apply to $AC$ parallelogram $CD = BC$ exceeding by $AD \sim BC$ (a square). Subtracting common area $CE$ yields $BF = AD$.
- The proof continues on Folio 0274 (Page 268) showing $BA : AE = AE : EB$.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- Greek term: `διορισμός` (condition of possibility; none required here as $S > 0$ always gives a real positive root).
- Elegant reuse of Proposition 29 to solve the extreme and mean ratio problem, contrasting with the Book II. 11 construction.
- Note the quadratic equation connection: cutting $a$ in extreme and mean ratio corresponds geometrically to $ax + x^2 = a^2$.
