It will be observed that the construction in the text is a direct application of the preceding Prop. 29 in the particular case where the *excess* of the parallelogram which is applied is a *square*. This fact coupled with the position of VI. 30 is a sufficient indication that the construction is Euclid's.

In one place Theon appears to have amplified the argument. The text above says "But <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>," while the MSS. B, F, V and p have "But <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, that is, to <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>."

The MSS. give after ὅπερ ἔδει ποιῆσαι an alternative construction which Heiberg relegates to the Appendix. The text-books give this construction alone and leave out the other. It will be remembered that the alternative proof does no more than refer to the equivalent construction in II. 11.

"Let <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut at <span data-ref="0273.g1:C">$C$</span> so that the rectangle <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>. [II. 11]

Since then the rectangle <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>. [VI. 17]

Therefore <span data-ref="0273.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0273.g1:C">$C$</span>."

It is intrinsically improbable that this alternative construction was added to the other by Euclid himself. It is however just the kind of interpolation that might be expected from an editor. If Euclid had preferred the alternative construction, he would have been more likely to give it alone.
