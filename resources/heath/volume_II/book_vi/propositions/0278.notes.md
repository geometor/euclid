## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 272 contains extensive commentary on Book VI, Proposition 32, analyzing its geometric formulation, criticisms by modern geometers, and alternative theorems.
- **Continuity**: Connects backward to folio 0277 (page 271) and forward to folio 0279 (page 273), where Simson's theory connecting VI. 32 to VI. 26 concludes.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Critical Ambiguity in VI. 32**: Heath illustrates that Euclid's enunciation does not forbid the included angles from being supplementary rather than equal, which would invalidate collinearity of the third sides.
- **Defence of Utility**: Refutes Todhunter's claim of uselessness by citing Euclid's explicit deployment of VI. 32 in Book XIII, Proposition 17 (construction of the regular dodecahedron).
- **Substitutes Analyzed**:
  - De Morgan: Similar triangles with parallel bases.
  - Lachlan: Similar triangles with two pairs of parallel sides implying third sides parallel.
  - H. M. Taylor: Homothetic triangles whose vertices connect through a centre of perspective or are parallel.
- **Simson's Hypothesis**: Simson suggested VI. 32 was intended for a direct proof of VI. 26 for vertically opposite similar parallelograms.
