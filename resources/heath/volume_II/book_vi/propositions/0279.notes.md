## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 33 Scope**: Proposition 33 is the final proposition of Book VI in the Euclidean text. It establishes that in equal circles, angles at the centres or circumferences are proportional to their intercepted arcs.
- **Methodology**: Operates strictly under the Eudoxian proportion theory of Book V (Def. 5), taking equimultiples of arcs ($BC, EF$) by successive additions ($CK, KL$ and $FM, MN$) and showing that the corresponding central angles ($BGL, NHE$) are equimultiples of the base angles ($BGC, EHF$) via III. 27.
