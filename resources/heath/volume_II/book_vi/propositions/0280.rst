.. assertion::

   | therefore, whatever multiple the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is of :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`,
   | that multiple also is the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` of the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion::

   | whatever multiple the circumference :g:`$NE$ <0279.g2:( N E )>` is of :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, that
   | multiple also is the angle :g:`$NHE$ <0279.g2:< N H E )>` of the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`.

.. assertion::

   | If then the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is equal to the circumference
   | :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`, the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`; :just:`[III. 27] <III.27>`
   | if the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is greater than the circumference
   | :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`, the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also greater than the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`;
   | and, if less, less.

.. subclause::

   | There being then four magnitudes, two circumferences
   | :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>`, :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, and two angles :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>`, :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`,
   | there have been taken, of the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` and the angle
   | :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` equimultiples, namely the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` and the
   | angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>`,
   | and of the circumference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>` and the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>` equi-
   | multiples, namely the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>` and the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`.

.. subclause::

   | And it has been proved that,
   | if the circumference :g:`$BL$ <0279.g2:( B L )>` is in excess of the circumference :g:`$EN$ <0279.g2:( E N )>`,
   | the angle :g:`$BGL$ <0279.g2:< B G L )>` is also in excess of the angle :g:`$EHN$ <0279.g2:< E H N )>`;
   | if equal, equal;
   | and if less, less.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, as the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` is to :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, so is the
   | angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`

.. subclause::

   | But, as the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` is to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, so is the
   | angle :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>`; for they are doubles respec-
   | tively.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore also, as the circumference :g:`$BC$ <0279.g2:( B C )>` is to the circum-
   | ference :g:`$EF$ <0279.g2:( E F )>`, so is the angle :g:`$BGC$ <0279.g2:< B G C )>` to the angle :g:`$EHF$ <0279.g2:< E H F )>`, and
   | the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0279.g2:< B A C )>` to the angle :g:`$EDF$ <0279.g2:< E D F )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

This proposition as generally given includes a second part relating to *sectors* of circles, corresponding to the following words added to the enunciation: "and further the sectors, as constructed at the centres" (ἔτι δὲ καὶ οἱ τομεῖς ἅτε [or οἵτε] πρὸς τοῖς κέντροις συνιστάμενοι).
