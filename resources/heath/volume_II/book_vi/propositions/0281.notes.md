# Transcription Notes: Folio 0281 (Page 275)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 275 continues Heath's commentary on Proposition 33, analyzing manuscript evidence, Theon's sector proof, and the foundational nature of angular magnitudes in Euclid.
- **Continuity**: Connects backward to folio 0280 (page 274) and forward to folio 0282 (page 276) where the commentary and Book VI conclude.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Manuscript Evidence**: Campanus omits Theon's sector addition; Vatican MS. 190 (P) has it only in a later hand in the margin or between lines.
- **Theon's Sector Deduction**: Theon equates sectors $GBC, GCK$ by decomposing them into triangles ($GBC, GCK$, equal by I. 8, 4) and circular segments ($BOC, CPK$, similar and equal by III. Def. 11, III. 24).
- **"The Angle Breaks Prison"**: De Morgan's famous phrase concerning equimultiples exceeding two right angles.
- **Dodgson vs. Euclid**: Analysis of C. L. Dodgson's (*Euclid and his Modern Rivals*) interpretation that multiples of angles were aggregates rather than single magnitudes. Heath points out that Euclid's explicit Greek phrasing treats them as single angles:
  $$\text{ἡ ὑπὸ ΒΗΛ γωνία, ἡ ὑπὸ ΕΘΝ γωνία}$$
  (noting the correspondence between English $G, H, L$ and Greek $H, \Theta, \Lambda$).
