Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Opening Format**: Page 277 lacks a running header and page number in the original print, following the standard book opening layout of Cambridge University Press.
- **Foundational Arithmetic Definitions**:

  - **Def. 1 (Unit / Monad)**: "An unit is that by virtue of which each of the things that exist is called one."
  - **Def. 2 (Number)**: "A number is a multitude composed of units." Euclid defines number strictly as a discrete collection of units ($\ge 2$).
  - **Def. 3–5 (Part, Parts, Multiple)**: Introduces the distinction between a submultiple ("part", $\mu\acute{\epsilon}\rho o\varsigma$) where the unit fraction measures the greater, and general proper fractions ("parts", $\mu\acute{\epsilon}\rho\eta$).
  - **Def. 6–10 (Parity and Products)**: Definitions of even, odd, even-times even, even-times odd, and odd-times odd numbers.
