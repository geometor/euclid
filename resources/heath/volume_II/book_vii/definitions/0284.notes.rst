Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Def. 11–14 (Prime and Composite Numbers)**: Definitions of prime, relatively prime (coprime), composite, and relatively composite numbers.
- **Def. 15 (Multiplication)**: Defined constructively as repeated addition of the multiplicand as many times as there are units in the multiplier.
- **Def. 16–19 (Figurate Numbers)**: Plane and solid numbers, their "sides", square numbers, and cubes.
- **Def. 20 (Proportion of Numbers)**: The arithmetic definition of proportion: four numbers are proportional if the first is the same multiple, part, or parts of the second as the third is of the fourth. This stands alongside Book V's Eudoxian definition for continuous magnitudes.
- **Def. 21–22 (Similarity and Perfect Numbers)**: Similar plane/solid numbers (proportional sides) and perfect numbers (a number equal to the sum of its proper divisors/parts).
