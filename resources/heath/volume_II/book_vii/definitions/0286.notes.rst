Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Greek Enunciations**:

  - Def. 2: ``᾿Αριθμὸς δὲ τὸ ἐκ μονάδων συγκείμενον πλῆθος.``
  - Def. 3: ``Μέρος ἐστὶν ἀριθμὸς ἀριθμοῦ ὁ ἐλάσσων τοῦ μείζονος, ὅταν καταμετρῇ τὸν μείζονα.``
  - Def. 4: ``Μέρη δέ, ὅταν μὴ καταμετρῇ.``

- **Scholarly & Historical Context**:

  - **Def. 2**: Heath contrasts definitions of number across ancient philosophy: Thales's and Moderatus's "collection of units" (μονάδων σύστημα), Eudoxus's "defined multitude" (πλῆθος ὡρισμένον), Nicomachus's synthesis, and Aristotle's diverse formulations (limited multitude, several ones / ἕνα πλείω, multitude measurable by one).
  - **Def. 3 & 4 (Part vs. Parts)**: Euclid's precise terminology distinguishes an aliquot part or unit fraction ($1/n$, μέρος) from a non-aliquot proper fraction ($m/n$ with $1 < m < n$, μέρη). Heath notes that Euclid's Def. 3 directly parallels Book V Def. 1, substituting ἀριθμός for μέγεθος.
