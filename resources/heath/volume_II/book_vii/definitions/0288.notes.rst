Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Euclidean vs. Neo-Pythagorean Taxonomy**:

  - Heath demonstrates that Euclid's definition of even-times even ($4km$) is intentional and consistent with Propositions IX. 32 and IX. 34, refuting Iamblichus's claim that Euclid erred. The word μόνως ("only") in some late manuscripts is identified as an interpolation attempting to force agreement with the Pythagorean classification ($2^n$).
  - Mathematical notation: exponential form $2^n$, and submultiples $\frac{1}{2^m}\text{th}$ part with value $2^{n-m}$.
  - Greek terms: δύναμις used by Nicomachus in the sense of actual value in units; ἀρτιάκις ἀρτιοδύναμον.

- **Def. 9 (Even-times Odd)**:

  - Greek text: ``᾿Αρτιάκις δὲ περισσός ἐστιν ὁ ὑπὸ ἀρτίου ἀριθμοῦ μετρούμενος κατὰ περισσὸν ἀριθμόν.``
  - Contrasted with Nicomachus's compound ἀρτιοπέριττος (even-odd) of form $2(2m+1)$: $6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30,\text{ etc.}$
