- **Textual Problem of Def. 10 (Odd-times Even)**:

  - Heath analyzes whether Definition 10 is an interpolation or original to Euclid. If an even-times odd number is defined as $2k \times (2m+1)$, commutativity makes it also odd-times even $((2m+1) \times 2k)$. Heath notes the resulting tension with Proposition IX. 33 ("If a number have its half odd, it is even-times odd only").
