## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Euclidean Text**: Definitions 10 and 11 are presented with Greek enunciation followed directly by Heath's critical commentary.
- **Greek Terms**:
  - περισσάκις ἄρτιος (*odd-times even*)
  - περισσάρτιος (*odd-even*, Iamblichus' term)
  - Περισσάκις δὲ περισσὸς ἀριθμός (*odd-times odd number*)
  - Πρῶτος ἀριθμός (*prime number*)
  - ἀσύνθετος (*incomposite*)
- **Mathematical Notes**: Heath analyzes the conflict between the Euclidean definitions and the later Neo-Pythagorean classifications (Nicomachus, Iamblichus) where numbers are partitioned strictly into odd and even branches with differing sub-species.
