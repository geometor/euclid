- **Folio**: 0293 (Page 287)
- **Book / Section**: Book VII, Definitions (Deff. 14—16)

- **Core Content**:
  - Detailed critique of Nicomachus' 3-fold classification of odd numbers (prime and incomposite, secondary and composite, secondary/composite in itself but prime/incomposite to another) and Nesselmann's critique of overlapping classes.

  - Commentary on Definition 15 defining multiplication as repeated addition.
  - Definition 16 (Greek text): `Ὅταν δὲ δύο ἀριθμοὶ πολλαπλασιάσαντες ἀλλήλους ποιῶσί τινα, ὁ γενόμενος ἐπίπεδος καλεῖται, πλευραὶ δὲ αὐτοῦ οἱ πολλαπλασιάσαντες ἀλλήλους ἀριθμοί.`
  - Opening of commentary on Definition 16 (*plane numbers* and *sides*).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terms & Orthography**:
  - ἀρχοειδές (*nature of a first principle*)
  - Ἀριθμὸς ἀριθμὸν πολλαπλασιάζειν λέγεται (*A number is said to multiply a number...*)
  - ἐπίπεδος (*plane*)
  - πλευραί (*sides*)
  - γραμμικός (*linear*)
- **Mathematical Notes**: Euclid's definition of multiplication is purely constructive and arithmetical: multiplying $A$ by $B$ means adding $A$ together as many times as there are units in $B$. Definition 16 introduces the geometric nomenclature for numbers (*plane* and *sides*).
