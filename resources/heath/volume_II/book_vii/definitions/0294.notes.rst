  - Detailed philological and mathematical note on *plane numbers* (ἐπίπεδος ἀριθμός) versus geometric planes.
  - Exegesis of the famous passage in Plato's *Theaetetus* (147 E—148 B) on square numbers, oblong numbers (προμήκης, ἑτερομήκης), lengths (μήκη), and roots/powers (δυνάμεις).
  - Comparison of line-representation of numbers (Euclidean/Platonic) versus discrete point-representation (psephoi / pebble arithmetic of Eurytus, Nicomachus, Theon of Smyrna).

- **Mathematical Notes**: Heath illuminates the historical transition between early Pythagorean pebble arithmetic (representing numbers by dot patterns forming triangles, squares, pentagons) and Euclid's rigorous representation of numbers by line segments whose lengths are proportional to the numbers.
