# Transcription Notes: Folio 0302 (Page 296)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Book VII, Proposition 1**: Enunciation and beginning of proof.
- This folio opens the proposition section of Book VII (number theory).
- It introduces the Euclidean subtractive algorithm (anthyphairesis) applied as a test for relative primeness (coprimality).
- Text flows seamlessly into Folio 0303 (Page 297), which begins with `"But it also measures the whole DC;"`.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heading**: Centered header `BOOK VII. PROPOSITIONS.` above `PROPOSITION 1.`.
- **Enunciation**: Set in italics. Uses the phrasing `"an unit"` characteristic of Heath's style.
- **Representation of Numbers**: Numbers are represented geometrically by straight-line segments (*AB*, *CD*, *E*), following Heiberg's text based on the MSS.
- **Marginal Line Numbering**: Heath's English edition does not include 5-line marginal markers in Book VII propositions.
