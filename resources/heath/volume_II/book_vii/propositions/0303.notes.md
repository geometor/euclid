## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Def. 12 Citation**: Right-aligned `[VII. Def. 12]` at the close of the proof references the definition of numbers prime to one another.
- **Editorial Commentary**: Heath notes Heiberg's choice of straight lines over points from the manuscript tradition.
- **Division Schema**: Heath represents anthyphairesis via a long division cascade ending in unity:
  `b) a ( p ... c) b ( q ... d) c ( r ... 1`.
- **Axiomatic Assumptions**: Heath points out Euclid's implicit assumptions: if $c \mid a$ and $c \mid b$, then $c \mid (a - pb)$ and $c \mid (a + pb)$.
