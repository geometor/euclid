# Transcription Notes: Folio 0304 (Page 298)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Book VII, Proposition 2**: Enunciation and first part of the proof (finding a common measure).
- Solves the problem of finding the greatest common measure (G.C.M. / gcd) of two non-coprime numbers via the Euclidean algorithm.
- Establishes that *CF* is a common measure of *AB* and *CD*.
- Carries over directly to Folio 0305 (Page 299), which begins `"I say next that it is also the greatest."`.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Two Cases**: Euclid splits the problem into: (1) *CD* measures *AB* immediately, and (2) *CD* does not measure *AB*.
- **Punctuation**: Em-dashes in `"If now CD measures AB—and it also measures itself—CD is a common measure..."`.
- **Cross References**: Citation `[VII. 1]` directly embedded in the text for the non-unity remainder.
