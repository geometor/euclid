## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Part vs. Parts (μέρος vs. μέρη)**: Essential Euclidean distinction defined in Book VII, Definitions 3 and 4.
  - "Part": an aliquot part / submultiple ($1/m$).
  - "Parts": multiple parts, i.e. a proper fraction ($n/m$ with $n < m$).
- **Euclid VII. 33 Reference**: Heath notes Euclid's later tacit use of finding the G.C.M. of an arbitrary finite set of numbers.
- **Quotations and Typography**: Exact preservation of quotes and italics: `"parts"` vs `"part"`, `*a proper fraction of a*`.
