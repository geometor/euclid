PROPOSITION 4.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Any number is either a part or parts of any number, the less of the greater.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` be two numbers, and let :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` be the less ;
| I say that :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` is either a part, or parts, of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   | For :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` are either prime to one another
   | or not.

First, let :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` be prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | Then, if :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` be divided into the units in it,
   | each unit of those in :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` will be some part of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>` ;

.. assertion::

   so that :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` is parts of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

| Next let :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` not be prime to one another ;
| then :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` either measures, or does not measure, :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   If now :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` measures :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` is a part of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

.. picture:: 0309.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4 Diagram

| But, if not, let the greatest common measure :g:`$D$ <0309.g1:D>` of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />`
| be taken ; :just:`[VII. 2] <VII.2>`
| and let :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` be divided into the numbers equal to :g:`$D$ <0309.g1:D>`, namely
| :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0309.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FC$ <0309.g1:/ F C />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$D$ <0309.g1:D>` measures :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0309.g1:D>` is a part of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0309.g1:D>` is equal to each of the numbers :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0309.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FC$ <0309.g1:/ F C />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the numbers :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0309.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FC$ <0309.g1:/ F C />` is also a part of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>` ;

.. assertion::

   so that :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` is parts of :g:`$A$ <0309.g1:A>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| The meaning of the enunciation is of course that, if $a$, $b$ be two numbers
| of which $b$ is the less, then $b$ is either a submultiple or *some proper fraction of $a$*.

| (1) If $a$, $b$ are prime to one another, divide each into its units; then $b$
| contains $b$ of the same parts of which $a$ contains $a$. Therefore $b$ is "parts" or
| *a proper fraction of $a$*.

| (2) If $a$, $b$ be not prime to one another, either $b$ measures $a$, in which
| case $b$ is a submultiple or "part" of $a$, or, if $g$ be the greatest common
| measure of $a$, $b$, we may put $a = mg$ and $b = ng$, and $b$ will contain $n$ of the
| same parts ($g$) of which $a$ contains $m$, so that $b$ is again "parts," or *a proper
| fraction, of $a$*.
