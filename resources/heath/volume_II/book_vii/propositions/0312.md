### PROPOSITION 7.

*If a number be that part of a number, which a number
subtracted is of a number subtracted, the remainder will also
be the same part of the remainder that the whole is of the
whole.*

For let the number <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be that part of the number <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>
which <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> subtracted is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> subtracted;
I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also the same part of the
remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Book VII, Proposition 7](0312.g1.svg)

For, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also let
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> be of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$CG$</span>.

Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part
also is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$CG$</span>,
therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also is
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>. [VII. 5]

But, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also, by
hypothesis, is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, the same part is it of
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> also;
therefore <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> be subtracted from each;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.

Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part
also is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also is
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.
