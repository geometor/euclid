### PROPOSITION 7.

<p class="enunciation">If a number be that part of a number, which a number<br />
subtracted is of a number subtracted, the remainder will also<br />
be the same part of the remainder that the whole is of the<br />
whole.</p>

For let the number <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be that part of the number <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span><br />
which <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> subtracted is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> subtracted;

I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also the same part of the<br />
remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Book VII, Proposition 7](0312.g1.svg)

For, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also let<br />
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> be of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$CG$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part<br />
also is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$CG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also is<br />
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 5]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also, by<br />
hypothesis, is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, the same part is it of<br />
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> also;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> be subtracted from each;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part<br />
also is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also is<br />
<span data-ref="0312.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, whatever part <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, the same part also is <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
of <span data-ref="0312.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;</p>
