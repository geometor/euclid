.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

If $a = \frac{1}{n}b$ and $c = \frac{1}{n}d$, we are to prove that

$$a - c = \frac{1}{n}(b - d),$$

a result differing from that of VII. 5 in that *minus* is substituted for *plus*.
Euclid's method is as follows.

Suppose that $e$ is taken such that

$$a - c = \frac{1}{n}e. \tag{1}$$

Now

$$c = \frac{1}{n}d.$$

Therefore

$$a = \frac{1}{n}(d + e), \tag{VII. 5}$$

whence, from the hypothesis, $d + e = b$, so that $e = b - d$, and, substituting this value of $e$ in (1), we have

$$a - c = \frac{1}{n}(b - d).$$

PROPOSITION 8.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If a number be the same parts of a number that a number subtracted is of a number subtracted, the remainder will also be the same parts of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.

For let the number :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />` be the same parts of the number :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />` that :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` subtracted is of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />` subtracted;

I say that the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0313.g1:/ E B />` is also the same parts of the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0313.g1:/ F D />` that the whole :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />` is of the whole :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0313.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 8

For let :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` be made :eq:`equal <0313.g1:/ G H / = 0313.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0313.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, whatever parts :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` is of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, the same parts also is :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`.

Let :g:`$GH$ <0313.g1:/ G H />` be divided into the parts of :g:`$CD$ <0313.g1:/ C D />`, namely :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />`, and :g:`$AE$ <0313.g1:/ A E />` into the parts of :g:`$CF$ <0313.g1:/ C F />`, namely :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`;

.. assertion::

   thus the multitude of :g:`$GK$ <0313.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0313.g1:/ K H />` will be equal to the multitude of :g:`$AL$ <0313.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LE$ <0313.g1:/ L E />`.
