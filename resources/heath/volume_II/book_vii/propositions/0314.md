<p class="sub-clause">Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part also<br />

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part<br />

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ L E /">$EL$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is the same part of the<br />

<p class="sub-clause">But the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> was also proved to be the same<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore also the sum of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is the same parts of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$DF$</span><br />

<p class="sub-clause">But the sum of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,<br />

If
$$a = \frac{m}{n}b \text{ and } c = \frac{m}{n}d, \quad (m < n)$$
then
$$a - c = \frac{m}{n}(b - d).$$

Euclid's proof amounts to the following.

Take $e$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}b$, and $f$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}d$.

Then since, by hypothesis, $b > d$,
$$e > f,$$
and, by VII. 7,
$$e - f = \frac{1}{n}(b - d).$$
