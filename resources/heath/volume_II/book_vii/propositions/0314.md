Now since, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part
also is <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span> of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span>.

Therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part also
is <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span> of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;
therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is the same part of the
remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [VII. 7]

Again, since, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part
also is <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ L E /">$EL$</span> of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$HK$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ L E /">$EL$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ L E /">$EL$</span>.

Therefore, whatever part <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part
also is <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;
therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is the same part of the
remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [VII. 7]

But the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> was also proved to be the same
part of the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is of the whole
<span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore also the sum of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is the same parts of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$DF$</span>
that the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G H /">$HG$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

But the sum of <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span>, <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ N H /">$NH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ G H /">$HG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span>;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is the same parts of the remainder
<span data-ref="0313.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of the whole <span data-ref="0313.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

If
$$a = \frac{m}{n}b \text{ and } c = \frac{m}{n}d, \quad (m < n)$$
then
$$a - c = \frac{m}{n}(b - d).$$

Euclid's proof amounts to the following.

Take $e$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}b$, and $f$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}d$.

Then since, by hypothesis, $b > d$,
$$e > f,$$
and, by VII. 7,
$$e - f = \frac{1}{n}(b - d).$$
