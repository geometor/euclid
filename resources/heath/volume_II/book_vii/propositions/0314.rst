If

$$a = \frac{m}{n}b \text{ and } c = \frac{m}{n}d, \quad (m < n)$$

then

$$a - c = \frac{m}{n}(b - d).$$

Euclid's proof amounts to the following.

Take $e$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}b$, and $f$ equal to $\frac{1}{n}d$.

Then since, by hypothesis, $b > d$,

$$e > f,$$

and, by VII. 7,

$$e - f = \frac{1}{n}(b - d).$$
