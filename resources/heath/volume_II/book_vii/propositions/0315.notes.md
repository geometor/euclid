## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Alternation ($\acute{\epsilon}\nu\alpha\lambda\lambda\acute{\alpha}\xi$, alternately)**: Establishes permutation of inner terms: if $A : BC = D : EF$ (both being $1/n$), then $A : D = BC : EF$.
- **"Part or parts"**: Notice the phrasing "whatever part or parts the first is of the third, the same part, or the same parts, will the second also be of the fourth." Euclid explicitly accommodates both unit fractions and general proper/improper fractions in the ratio of $A$ to $D$.
