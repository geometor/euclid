## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Alternando with Fractions**: While Proposition 9 handled unit parts ($A = \frac{1}{n}BC$), Proposition 10 extends alternando to arbitrary fractional parts ($AB = \frac{m}{n}C$).
- **Reduction Strategy**: Euclid divides $AB$ into $m$ parts equal to $\frac{1}{n}C$ and $DE$ into $m$ parts equal to $\frac{1}{n}F$. Applying VII. 9 to each individual pair $AG, DH$ gives $C : F = AG : DH = GB : HE$, which are then synthesized.
- **Order of Words**: Note the phrase "whatever parts or part the first is of the third, the same parts or the same part will the second also be of the fourth" — here "parts" precedes "part", reflecting that the ratio of $AB$ to $DE$ is typically a general fraction rather than a unit fraction.
- **Citation**: Marginal reference `[VII. 9]`.
