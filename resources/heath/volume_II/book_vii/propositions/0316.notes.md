## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Alternando with Fractions**: While Proposition 9 handled unit parts ($A = \frac{1}{n}BC$), Proposition 10 extends alternando to arbitrary fractional parts ($AB = \frac{m}{n}C$).
