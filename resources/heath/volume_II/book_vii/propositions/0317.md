If $a = \frac{m}{n} b$ and $c = \frac{m}{n} d$, then, whatever fraction $a$ is of $c$, the same fraction<br />
is $b$ of $d$.

To prove this, $a$ is divided into its $m$ parts equal to $b/n$, and $c$ into its<br />
$m$ parts equal to $d/n$.

Then, by VII. 9, whatever fraction one of the $m$ parts of $a$ is of one of the<br />
$m$ parts of $c$, the same fraction is $b$ of $d$.

And, by VII. 5, 6, whatever fraction one of the $m$ parts of $a$ is of one of<br />
the $m$ parts of $c$, the same fraction is the sum of the parts of $a$ (that is, $a$) of<br />
the sum of the parts of $c$ (that is, $c$).

Whence the result follows.

In the Greek text, after the words "so that, in addition" in the last line<br />
but one, is an additional explanation making the reference to VII. 5, 6 clearer,<br />
as follows: "whatever part or parts <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is of <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>, the same part or the<br />
same parts is <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> of <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> also;

therefore also, whatever part or parts <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is of <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>, the same part or the same<br />
parts is <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> also. <span class="justification">[VII. 5, 6]</span>

But it was proved that, whatever part or parts <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is of <span data-ref="0316.g1:/ D H /">$DH$</span>, the same<br />
part or the same parts is <span data-ref="0316.g1:C">$C$</span> of <span data-ref="0316.g1:F">$F$</span> also;

therefore also" etc. as in the last two lines of the text.

Heiberg concludes, on the authority of P, which only has the words in<br />

### PROPOSITION 11.

<p class="enunciation">If, as whole is to whole, so is a number subtracted to a<br />
number subtracted, the remainder will also be to the remainder<br />
as whole to whole.</p>

As the whole <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so let <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> subtracted<br />
be to <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> subtracted;

I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also to the remainder<br />
<span data-ref="0317.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> as the whole <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the whole <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since, as <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">whatever part or parts <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, the same part<br />
or the same parts is <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> of <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> also; <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is the same<br />
part or parts of <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> that <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is of <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 7, 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0317.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 20]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
