# Transcription Notes: Folio 0317 (Page 311)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 10 (concluded)**: Contains the concluding step and Q.E.D. continuing from Page 310 (Folio 0316).
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 10**: Algebraic explanation using fractions ($a = \frac{m}{n}b, c = \frac{m}{n}d \implies a/c = b/d$) based on VII. 9 and VII. 5, 6. Notes an explanatory gloss in the Greek text attributed by Heiberg to Theon due to its appearance only in the margin of manuscript P in a later hand.
- **Proposition 11 (complete)**: Establishes that if whole is to whole as subtracted number to subtracted number, the remainder is to the remainder as whole to whole ($a : b = c : d \implies (a - c) : (b - d) = a : b$).
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 11**: Begins at the foot of Page 311, discussing Euclid's convention of contemplating only cases where the first term is "a part" or "parts" of the second, continuing onto Page 312 (Folio 0318).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Proportional Subtraction**: Proposition 11 is the numerical counterpart of Book V, Proposition 19 for general magnitudes.
- **Justifications cited**: VII. Def. 20 (definition of proportional numbers), VII. 7, 8 (subtraction of part/parts from part/parts).
- **Mathematical Scope**: Heath highlights that Euclid's definition of proportion (VII. Def. 20) strictly assumes the first term is smaller than the second ("a part or parts"), necessitating ratio reduction/inversion when working with numbers where the antecedent exceeds the consequent.
