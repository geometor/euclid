# Transcription Notes: Folio 0318 (Page 312)

## 1. Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 11 (cont.)**: Carries over from Page 311 (Folio 0317). Examines the tension between Euclid's formal definition of proportion (requiring $a < b$) and the figures drawn in Props 11 and 13. Heath details the algebraic interpretation $(a - c) : (b - d) = a : b$ and grammatical agreement (masculine ἀριθμός vs. neuter μέγεθος in Book V, Prop 19).
- **Proposition 12 (complete)**: Proves that in a series of proportional numbers, one antecedent is to its consequent as the sum of all antecedents is to the sum of all consequents ($a : b = c : d \implies a : b = (a + c) : (b + d)$). Note: Euclid's text on this page ends directly with `[VII. Def. 20]` without a formal `Q. E. D.`
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 12**: Generalizes to $n$ terms: $a : a' = b : b' = c : c' = \dots = (a + b + c + \dots) : (a' + b' + c' + \dots)$. Compares with Book V, Proposition 12.

## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Lexical Terms**: Heath highlights the exact verbal correspondence between Book VII arithmetical enunciations and Book V magnitude enunciations, with the masculine noun ἀριθμός replacing the neuter μέγεθος.
- **Omission of Q.E.D.**: Proposition 12 concludes without the standard concluding formula "Q. E. D.", transitioning straight to Heath's commentary.
- **Quotation of VII. 5, 6**: Euclid quotes VII. 5, 6 as valid for any number of terms, though originally enunciated only for two numbers.
