# Transcription Notes: Folio 0321 (Page 315)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 15 (demonstration & conclusion)**: Demonstrates the alternando property for unit measurements: if $1$ measures $m$ as $a$ measures $ma$, then alternately $1$ measures $a$ as $m$ measures $ma$. Concludes with Q.E.D.
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 15**: Notes that Proposition 15 is mathematically identical to VII. 9, with the only distinction being that the first number is unity and the term "measure" is used rather than "a part". It represents a special case of VII. 9 expressed in the language of multiplication.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Use of VII. 12**: Euclid decomposes the numbers into their constituent parts and applies VII. 12 (sum of antecedents to sum of consequents) to establish the proportional relationship before converting back into measurement language.
- **Role in Commutativity**: Proposition 15 serves as the key lemma required to prove the commutativity of multiplication in Proposition 16.
