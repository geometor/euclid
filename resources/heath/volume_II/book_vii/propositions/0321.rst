:title: Proposition 15

| For let the unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>` measure any number :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />`,
| and let another number :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`
| measure any other number :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`
| the same number of times;

| I say that, alternately also, the
| unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>` measures the number
| :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` the same number of times that :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` measures :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`.

.. picture:: 0321.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 15 Diagram

.. subclause::

   | For, since the unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>` measures the number :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` the same
   | number of times that :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` measures :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as many units as there are in :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />`, so many numbers
   | equal to :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` are there in :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />` also.

| Let :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` be divided into the units in it, :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0321.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0321.g1:/ H C />`,
| and :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />` into the numbers :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0321.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0321.g1:/ L F />` equal to :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Thus the multitude of :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0321.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0321.g1:/ H C />` will be equal to the
   | multitude of :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0321.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0321.g1:/ L F />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the units :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0321.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0321.g1:/ H C />` are equal to one another,
   | and the numbers :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0321.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0321.g1:/ L F />` are also equal to one another,
   | while the multitude of the units :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0321.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HC$ <0321.g1:/ H C />` is equal to the
   | multitude of the numbers :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0321.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LF$ <0321.g1:/ L F />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the unit :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />` is to the number :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, so will the
   | unit :g:`$GH$ <0321.g1:/ G H />` be to the number :g:`$KL$ <0321.g1:/ K L />`, and the unit :g:`$HC$ <0321.g1:/ H C />` to the
   | number :g:`$LF$ <0321.g1:/ L F />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore also, as one of the antecedents is to one of
   | the consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the
   | consequents; :just:`[VII. 12] <VII.12>`
   | therefore, as the unit :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />` is to the number :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />`, so is :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` to
   | :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | But the unit :g:`$BG$ <0321.g1:/ B G />` is equal to the unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>`,
   | and the number :g:`$EK$ <0321.g1:/ E K />` to the number :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, as the unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>` is to the number :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` to
   | :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the unit :g:`$A$ <0321.g1:A>` measures the number :g:`$D$ <0321.g1:D>` the same
   | number of times that :g:`$BC$ <0321.g1:/ B C />` measures :g:`$EF$ <0321.g1:/ E F />`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| If there be four numbers $1, m, a, ma$ (such that $1$ measures $m$ the same
| number of times that $a$ measures $ma$), $1$ measures $a$ the same number of
| times that $m$ measures $ma$.

| Except that the first number is unity and the numbers are said to *measure*
| instead of being a *part* of others, this proposition and its proof do not differ
| from VII. 9; in fact this proposition is a particular case of the other.
