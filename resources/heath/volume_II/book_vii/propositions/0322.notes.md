# Transcription Notes: Folio 0322 (Page 316)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 16 (complete)**: The fundamental theorem of commutativity of multiplication of integers ($A \times B = B \times A$). Proof proceeds by applying Proposition 15 to the unit $E$ and terms $A, B$, showing that $A$ measures both products $C$ and $D$ the same number of times, hence $C = D$. Concludes with Q.E.D.
- **Textual Footnote [^316_1]**: Heath comments on the Greek phrase οἱ γενόμενοι ἐξ αὐτῶν ("the numbers produced from them"), discussing Euclid's meaning and justifying the omission of words in English translation to prevent ambiguity.
- **Heath Commentary on Prop 16**: Introduces the modern algebraic interpretation $ab = ba$, analyzes VII. Def. 15, and initiates the proportion-based representation of the proof, which continues onto Page 317 (Folio 0323).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Commutative Law**: Proposition 16 is one of the pillars of Greek arithmetic. Heath notes that Euclid does not take commutativity as a definition or axiom, but meticulously proves it from the definition of multiplication (VII. Def. 15) and alternate measurement (VII. 15).
