## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Preservation of Ratio under Scaling**: Proposition 17 establishes the fundamental property of fractions/ratios that multiplying both terms by a common factor leaves the ratio unaltered ($b/c = ab/ac$).
- **Methodological Transition**: Euclid here transitions directly from the language of units measuring numbers to the formal proportion relation by citing VII. Def. 20 and applying alternate proportion (VII. 13).
