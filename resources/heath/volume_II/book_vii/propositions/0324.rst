PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers by multiplying any number make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will have the same ratio as the multipliers.

For let two numbers :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0324.g1:B>` by multiplying any number :g:`$C$ <0324.g1:C>` make :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0324.g1:E>`;

I say that, as :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0324.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0324.g1:E>`.

.. picture:: 0324.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 18

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0324.g1:C>` has made :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 16] <VII.16>

   therefore also :g:`$C$ <0324.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>` has made :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also :g:`$C$ <0324.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0324.g1:B>` has made :g:`$E$ <0324.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the number :g:`$C$ <0324.g1:C>` by multiplying the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0324.g1:B>` has made :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0324.g1:E>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0324.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0324.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0324.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0324.g1:E>`.

----

It is here proved that

$$a : b = ac : bc.$$

The argument is as follows.

$$\begin{aligned}
ac &= ca. & \text{[VII. 16]} \\
\text{Similarly}\qquad bc &= cb. \\
\text{And}\qquad a : b &= ca : cb; & \text{[VII. 17]} \\
\text{therefore}\qquad a : b &= ac : bc.
\end{aligned}$$

PROPOSITION 19.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If four numbers be proportional, the number produced from the first and fourth will be equal to the number produced from the second and third; and, if the number produced from the first and fourth be equal to that produced from the second and third, the four numbers will be proportional.

Let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` be four numbers in proportion, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>`; and let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0325.g1:D>` make :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>`, and let :g:`$B$ <0325.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` make :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`;

I say that :g:`$E$ <0325.g1:E>` is equal to :g:`$F$ <0325.g1:F>`.

For let :g:`$A$ <0325.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0325.g1:C>` make :g:`$G$ <0325.g1:G>`.
