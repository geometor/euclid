### Proposition 24.

*If two numbers be prime to any number, their product also will be prime to the same.*

![Book VII, Proposition 24](0331.g1.svg)

For let the two numbers <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:B">$B$</span> be prime to any number <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0331.g1:B">$B$</span> make <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span> are prime to one another.

For, if <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span> are not prime to one another, some number will measure <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span>.

Let a number measure them, and let it be <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span>.

Now, since <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span> are prime to one another, and a certain number <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0331.g1:C">$C$</span>, therefore <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span> are prime to one another. [VII. 23]

As many times, then, as <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span>, so many units let there be in <span data-ref="0331.g1:F">$F$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0331.g1:F">$F$</span> also measures <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span> according to the units in <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span>. [VII. 16]

Therefore <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0331.g1:F">$F$</span> has made <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span>. [VII. Def. 15]

But, further, <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0331.g1:B">$B$</span> has also made <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span>; therefore the product of <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:F">$F$</span> is equal to the product of <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:B">$B$</span>.

But, if the product of the extremes be equal to that of the means, the four numbers are proportional; [VII. 19] therefore, as <span data-ref="0331.g1:E">$E$</span> is to <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0331.g1:B">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0331.g1:F">$F$</span>.
