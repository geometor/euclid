PROPOSITION 24.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers be prime to any number, their product also will be prime to the same.

| For let the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0331.g1:B>` be prime to any number :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`,
| and let :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0331.g1:B>` make :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`;

I say that :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>` are prime to one another.

.. picture:: 0331.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 24

.. subclause::

   | For, if :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>` are not prime to one another,
   | some number will measure :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`.

| Let a number measure them, and let it
| be :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`, :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` are prime to one
   | another,
   | and a certain number :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` measures :g:`$C$ <0331.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 23] <VII.23>

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` are prime to one another.

| As many times, then, as :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` measures :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`, so many units let
| there be in :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. 16] <VII.16>

   therefore :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>` according to the units in :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 15] <VII.def.15>

   Therefore :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>` has made :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But, further, :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0331.g1:B>` has also made :g:`$D$ <0331.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>` is equal to the product of :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0331.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   | But, if the product of the extremes be equal to that of the
   | means, the four numbers are proportional ; :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` is to :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0331.g1:B>` to :g:`$F$ <0331.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$A$ <0331.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0331.g1:E>` are prime to one another,
   | numbers which are prime to one another are also the least of
   | those which have the same ratio, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>`
   | and the least numbers of those which have the same ratio
   | with them measure those which have the same ratio the same
