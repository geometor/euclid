If $a$, $b$ are both prime to $c$, then $ab$, $c$ are prime to one another.<br />
The proof is again by reductio ad absurdum.<br />
If $ab$, $c$ are not prime to one another, let them be measured by $d$ and be<br />
equal to $md$, $nd$, say, respectively.<br />
Now, since $a$, $c$ are prime to one another and $d$ measures $c$,
$$a,\ d\text{ are prime to one another. \qquad [VII. 23]}$$
But, since $ab = md$,
$$d : a = b : m. \qquad\text{[VII. 19]}$$
Therefore [VII. 20] $d$ measures $b$,<br />
or $b = pd$, say.<br />
But $c = nd$.<br />
Therefore $d$ measures both $b$ and $c$, which are therefore not prime to one<br />
another : which is impossible.<br />
Therefore etc.

### PROPOSITION 25.

<p class="enunciation">If two numbers be prime to one another, the product of one<br />
of them into itself will be prime to the remaining one.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span> be two numbers prime to one another,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span> by multiplying itself make <span data-ref="0332.g1:C">$C$</span>:

I say that <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:C">$C$</span> are prime to one another.

![Book VII, Proposition 25](0332.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0332.g1:D">$D$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span> are prime to one another,<br />
and <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0332.g1:D">$D$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0332.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span> are also prime to one another.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the two numbers <span data-ref="0332.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span> is<br />
prime to <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the product of <span data-ref="0332.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0332.g1:A">$A$</span> will also be prime to <span data-ref="0332.g1:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 24]</span></p>
