PROPOSITION 27.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers be prime to one another, and each by multiplying itself make a certain number, the products will be prime to one another ; and, if the original numbers by multi- plying the products make certain numbers, the latter will also be prime to one another [and this is always the case with the extremes].

| Let :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` be two numbers prime to one another,
| let :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`, and by
| multiplying :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>` make :g:`$D$ <0334.g1:D>`,
| and let :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` by multiplying itself make :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`, and
| by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>` make :g:`$F$ <0334.g1:F>`;

| I say that both :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>` and :g:`$D$ <0334.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0334.g1:F>` are prime
| to one another.

.. picture:: 0334.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 27

.. subclause::

   | For, since :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` are prime to one another,
   | and :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 25] <VII.25>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`, :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | Since then :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`, :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` are prime to one another,
   | and :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [id.]

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | Again, since :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` are prime to one another,
   | and :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [id.]

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | Since then the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>` are prime to the two
   | numbers :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`, both to each,

.. assertion::

   | therefore also the product of :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>` is prime to the product of
   | :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`. :just:`[VII. 26] <VII.26>`

.. subclause::

   | And the product of :g:`$A$ <0334.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0334.g1:C>` is :g:`$D$ <0334.g1:D>`, and the product of :g:`$B$ <0334.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0334.g1:E>`
   | is :g:`$F$ <0334.g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$D$ <0334.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0334.g1:F>` are prime to one another.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| If $a$, $b$ are prime to one another, so are $a^2$, $b^2$ and so are $a^3$, $b^3$ ; and,
| generally, $a^n$, $b^n$ are prime to one another.
