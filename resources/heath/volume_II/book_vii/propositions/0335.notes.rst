Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Textual Transmission**: Heiberg dates the interpolation of the generalisation in VII.27 to before Theon of Alexandria, as it is found across all manuscript traditions.
- **Proposition VII.28 Mathematical Content**:

  - Direct: $\gcd(a, b) = 1 \implies \gcd(a+b, a) = 1$ and $\gcd(a+b, b) = 1$.
  - Converse: $\gcd(a+b, a) = 1 \implies \gcd(a, b) = 1$.

- **Euclidean Segment Representation**: Euclid represents additive arithmetic with contiguous line segments: line $AC$ is composed of $AB$ and $BC$. If a common measure $D$ measures the whole $AC$ and the part $AB$, it must measure the remainder $BC$.
