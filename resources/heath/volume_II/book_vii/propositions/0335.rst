PROPOSITION 28.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers be prime to one another, the sum will also be prime to each of them ; and, if the sum of two numbers be prime to any one of them, the original numbers will also be prime to one another.

| For let two numbers :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />` prime to one another be
| added ;
| I say that the sum :g:`$AC$ <0335.g1:/ A C />` is also prime
| to each of the numbers :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`.

.. picture:: 0335.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 28

.. subclause::

   | For, if :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />` are not prime to
   | one another,
   | some number will measure :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`.

Let a number measure them, and let it be :g:`$D$ <0335.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$D$ <0335.g1:D>` measures :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore it will also measure the remainder :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   But it also measures :g:`$BA$ <0335.g1:/ A B />` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0335.g1:D>` measures :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />` which are prime to one another :
   | which is impossible. :just:`[VII. Def. 12] <VII.def.12>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore no number will measure the numbers :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />` ;
   | therefore :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AC$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0335.g1:/ B C />` are also prime to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />` is prime to each of the numbers :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`.

| Again, let :g:`$CA$ <0335.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />` be prime to one another ;
| I say that :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />` are also prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | For, if :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />` are not prime to one another,
   | some number will measure :g:`$AB$ <0335.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0335.g1:/ B C />`.
