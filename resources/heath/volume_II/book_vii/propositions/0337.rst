| If $a$ is prime and does not measure $b$, then $a$, $b$ are prime to one another.
| The proof is self-evident.

PROPOSITION 30.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If two numbers by multiplying one another make some number, and any prime number measure the product, it will also measure one of the original numbers.

| For let the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>` by multiplying one another
| make :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>`, and let any prime number
| :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measure :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>`;

| I say that :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measures one of the
| numbers :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0337.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VII, Proposition 30

For let it not measure :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` is prime ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` are prime to one
   | another. :just:`[VII. 29] <VII.29>`

| And, as many times as :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measures :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>`, so many units let
| there be in :g:`$E$ <0337.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measures :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>` according to the units in :g:`$E$ <0337.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 15] <VII.def.15>

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0337.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Further, :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>` has also made :g:`$C$ <0337.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the product of :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0337.g1:E>` is equal to the product of
   | :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` is to :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>` to :g:`$E$ <0337.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>`, :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>` are prime to one another,
   | primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>`
   | and the least measure the numbers which have the same
   | ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and
   | the less the less, that is, the antecedent the antecedent and
   | the consequent the consequent ; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measures :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly we can also show that, if :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` do not measure :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>`,
   | it will measure :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$D$ <0337.g1:D>` measures one of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0337.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0337.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
