## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heath's Notes on VII. 31**:
  - Note 8 clarifies the Greek idiom "what was enjoined will have been done" (*i.e.* the implied construction problem).
  - Note 18 discusses a textual omission in manuscript P attributed by Heiberg to *homoioteleuton* (`ὁμοιοτέλευτον`).
  - Alternative proof: Select $B$ as the *least* divisor of $A$ ($>1$); if $B$ were composite, its divisor $C < B$ would measure $A$, contradicting minimality.
- **Proposition 32**: Immediate corollary combining the definition of prime and Proposition 31. Concludes with `Q. E. D.`
- **Proposition 33**: Fundamental reduction of a ratio of integers to lowest terms: given numbers $A, B, C$, find the least numbers in the same ratio. Case 1 handles coprime numbers via VII. 21; Case 2 begins by taking the G.C.M. $D$ via VII. 3.
- **Line Numbering**: In Proposition 33, marginal line markers appear at lines 5 and 10 (`<sup>5</sup>`, `<sup>10</sup>`).
