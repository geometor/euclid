# Transcription Notes: Folio 0342 (Page 336)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Header**: `336 BOOK VII [VII. 34`
- **Continuity**: Folio 0342 presents the enunciation and Case 1 (when the two given numbers $A, B$ are coprime) of **Proposition 34** (construction of the Least Common Multiple of two numbers). Case 1 concludes at the bottom of the page; Case 2 (when $A, B$ are not coprime) begins at the top of folio 0343 (page 337).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Enunciation**: "Given two numbers, to find the least number which they measure" — Euclid's formulation of finding the least common multiple ($\text{lcm}(A, B)$).
- **Case 1 Proof ($A, B$ Coprime)**:
  - Construction: $C = A \cdot B = B \cdot A$ (VII. 16).
  - Minimality by contradiction: Suppose a smaller common multiple $D < C$. Let $D = A \cdot E = B \cdot F$.
  - Then $A : B = F : E$ (VII. 19).
  - Since $A, B$ are prime to one another, they are the least numbers in that ratio (VII. 21).
  - By VII. 20, $B$ measures $E$ ("as consequent consequent").
  - But $A \cdot B = C$ and $A \cdot E = D$, so $B : E = C : D$ (VII. 17).
  - Since $B \mid E$, it follows that $C \mid D$, meaning the greater measures the less, which is absurd.
- **Line Numbering**: Unusually for Book VII propositions, page 336 has no marginal line numbers printed in the scan.
