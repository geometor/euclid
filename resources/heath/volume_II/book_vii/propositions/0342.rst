PROPOSITION 34.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Given two numbers, to find the least number which they measure.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` be the two given numbers;
| thus it is required to find the least number which they
| measure.

.. subclause::

   | Now :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` are either prime to one
   | another or not.

| First, let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` be prime to one
| another, and let :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`
| make :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` has
   | made :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`. :just:`[VII. 16] <VII.16>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measure :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0342.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 34 diagram

I say next that it is also the least number they measure.

.. subclause::

   | For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` will measure some number which is less
   | than :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`.

Let them measure :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`.

| Then, as many times as :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, so many units let
| there be in :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`,
| and, as many times as :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, so many units let
| there be in :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` has made :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`,
   | and :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>` has made :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`; :just:`[VII. Def. 15] <VII.def.15>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>`.

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0342.g1:F>` to :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` are prime,
   | primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>`
   | and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio
   | the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less
   | the less; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`, as consequent consequent.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`, :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>` has made :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` is to :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` measures :g:`$E$ <0342.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$D$ <0342.g1:D>`, the greater the less:
   | which is impossible.
