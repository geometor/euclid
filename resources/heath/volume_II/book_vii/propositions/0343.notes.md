## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Case 2 Proof ($A, B$ Not Coprime)**:
  - Construction: Use VII. 33 to find $F, E$, the least numbers in the ratio $A : B$. Then $A \cdot E = B \cdot F$ (VII. 19). Set $C = A \cdot E = B \cdot F$.
  - Minimality proof: Assume $D < C$ is measured by $A, B$. Let $D = A \cdot G = B \cdot H$.
  - Then $A : B = H : G$ (VII. 19).
  - But $A : B = F : E$, so $F : E = H : G$.
  - Since $F, E$ are the least numbers in that ratio, $E$ measures $G$ (VII. 20).
  - Since $A \cdot E = C$ and $A \cdot G = D$, we have $E : G = C : D$ (VII. 17).
  - Since $E \mid G$, $C$ measures $D$, the greater the less, an impossibility.
