VII. 34] PROPOSITION 34 337

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>` do not measure any number less than :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>`;
   | therefore :g:`$C$ <0342.g1:C>` is the least that is measured by :g:`$A$ <0342.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0342.g1:B>`.

| Next, let :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` not be prime to one another,
| and let :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, the least numbers of those which have the same
| ratio with :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, be taken; :just:`[VII. 33] <VII.33>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` is equal to the product of :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`.
   | :just:`[VII. 19] <VII.19>`

| And let :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`
| make :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`
   | has made :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` measure :g:`$C$ <0343.g1:C>`.

Let them measure :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`.

| And, as many times as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` measures :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, so many units let
| there be in :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`,
| and, as many times as :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` measures :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`, so many units let there
| be in :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>` has made :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`,
   | and :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` has made :g:`$D$ <0343.g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the product of :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>`, :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>` is equal to the product of
   | :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. 19] <VII.19>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` to :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0343.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0343.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>` to :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>` is to :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0343.g1:H>` to :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$F$ <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` are least,
   | and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio
   | the same number of times, the greater the greater and the
   | less the less; :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0343.g1:E>` measures :g:`$G$ <0343.g1:G>`.
