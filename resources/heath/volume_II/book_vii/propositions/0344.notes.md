## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heath's Commentary**:
  - Part I (Coprime case): If $\gcd(a, b) = 1$, then $\text{lcm}(a, b) = ab$. Direct proof by contradiction using VII. 19, VII. 20, 21, and VII. 17.
  - Part II (General case): If $\gcd(a, b) = g > 1$, let $m, n$ be the least numbers in ratio $a : b$ (found via VII. 33). Then $m = a/g$ and $n = b/g$. The L.C.M. is $c = an = bm = ab/g$.
  - Fundamental identity: Establishes the classical theorem relating G.C.D. and L.C.M.:
    $$\text{lcm}(a, b) = \frac{ab}{\gcd(a, b)} \iff \gcd(a, b) \cdot \text{lcm}(a, b) = ab.$$
