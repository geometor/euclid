:title: PROPOSITION 36

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` also measure :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`.

I say next that it is also the least that they measure.

| For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` will measure some number which is
| less than :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`.

Let them measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>` will also
   | measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` is the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` will measure :g:`$E$ <0345.g2:E>`, the greater the less:
   | which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` will not measure any number which is
   | less than :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0345.g2:D>` is the least that :g:`$A$ <0345.g2:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0345.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0345.g2:C>` measure.

| Again, let :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`,
| and let :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`, the least number measured by
| :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, be taken. :just:`[VII. 34] <VII.34>`

.. subclause::

   | Since :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`,
   | and :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` measures :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

I say next that it is also the least that they measure.

| For, if not, :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` will measure some number which
| is less than :g:`$E$ <0346.g1:E>`.

Let them measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>` will also
   | measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` is the least number measured by :g:`$A$ <0346.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0346.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>` measures :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` also measures :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`,
   | so that the least number measured by :g:`$D$ <0346.g1:D>`, :g:`$C$ <0346.g1:C>` will also measure :g:`$F$ <0346.g1:F>`.
