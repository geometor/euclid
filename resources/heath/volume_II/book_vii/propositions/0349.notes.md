## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Headings & Running Title**: Left reference `VII. 38, 39]`, centered title `PROPOSITIONS 37—39`, page number `343`.
- **Mathematical Significance**: Proposition 39 is the capstone problem of Book VII: finding the least number having given aliquot parts (which translates in modern arithmetic to finding the least common multiple of the denominators of given unit fractions).
- **Citations**: Cites `[VII. 15]` in VII. 38; cites `[VII. 36]` (LCM of three numbers) and `[VII. 37]` in VII. 39.
