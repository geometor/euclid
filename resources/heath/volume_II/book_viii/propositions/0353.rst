.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, and by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` has made :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` has made :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, and :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the numbers :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` have made the numbers :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>` respectively.

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   Therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>` has made :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, so was :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>` has made :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>` to :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>` to :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>` have made :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`, and :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>` to :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>` is to :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>` to :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`, and :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>`; therefore :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`, and :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0352.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>` are proportional in the ratio of :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`.

I say next that they are the least numbers that are so.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` are the least of those which have the same ratio with them, and the least of those which have the same ratio are prime to one another, :just:`[VII. 22] <VII.22>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   And the numbers :g:`$A$ <0352.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>` by multiplying themselves respectively have made the numbers :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>`, and by multiplying the numbers :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>` respectively have made the numbers :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>`, :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>`;

.. assertion:: [VII. 27] <VII.27>

   therefore :g:`$C$ <0352.g1:C>`, :g:`$E$ <0352.g1:E>` and :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>`, :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>` are prime to one another respectively.
