# BOOK VIII.

## PROPOSITION 4.

*Given as many ratios as we please in least numbers, to find numbers in continued proportion which are the least in the given ratios.*

Let the given ratios in least numbers be that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>,  
<sup>5</sup> that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>;  
thus it is required to find numbers in continued proportion which are the least that are in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>.

![Proposition 4 Diagram 1](0356.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, the least number measured by <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span>, be taken.
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 34]</div>
<sup>10</sup> And, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, so many times also let <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span>,  
and, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, so many times also let <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>.  
Now <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> either measures or does not measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>.  
<sup>15</sup> First, let it measure it.  
And, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>, so many times let <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span> also.  
Now, since <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span> the same number of times that <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>,  
<sup>20</sup> therefore, as <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>.
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. Def. 20, VII. 13]</div>
For the same reason also,
<div style="text-align: center;">as <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>,</div>
and further, as <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span> are continuously proportional in the  
<sup>25</sup> ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>.  
I say next that they are also the least that have this property.
