# BOOK VIII.

## PROPOSITION 4.

<p class="enunciation">Given as many ratios as we please in least numbers, to find<br />
numbers in continued proportion which are the least in the<br />
given ratios.</p>

Let the given ratios in least numbers be that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
<sup>5</sup> that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and that of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>;<br />
thus it is required to find numbers in continued proportion<br />
which are the least that are in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, in the<br />
ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>.

![Proposition 4 Diagram 1](0356.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, the least number measured by <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span>, be taken. <span class="justification">[VII. 34]</span>

<sup>10</sup> And, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, so many times also<br />
let <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span>,<br />
and, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, so many times also let<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> either measures or does not measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>.</p>

<sup>15</sup> First, let it measure it.

And, as many times as <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>, so many times let<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span> measure <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span> also.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span> the same number of times that<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span> measures <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><sup>20</sup> therefore, as <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. Def. 20, VII. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason also,<br />
<span style="display: block; text-align: center;">as <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>,</span>
and further, as <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span> is to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span> are continuously proportional in the<br />
<sup>25</sup> ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:B">$B$</span>, in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span>, and in the ratio of <span data-ref="0356.g1:E">$E$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0356.g1:F">$F$</span>.</p>

I say next that they are also the least that have this<br />
property.
