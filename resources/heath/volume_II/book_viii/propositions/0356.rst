BOOK VIII.
==========

PROPOSITION 4.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Given as many ratios as we please in least numbers, to find numbers in continued proportion which are the least in the given ratios.

| Let the given ratios in least numbers be that of :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>`,
| :line:`5` that of :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>`, and that of :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0356.g1:F>`;
| thus it is required to find numbers in continued proportion
| which are the least that are in the ratio of :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>`, in the
| ratio of :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>`, and in the ratio of :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0356.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0356.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4 Diagram 1

Let :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, the least number measured by :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>`, be taken. :just:`[VII. 34] <VII.34>`

| :line:`10` And, as many times as :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>` measures :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, so many times also
| let :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` measure :g:`$H$ <0356.g1:H>`,
| and, as many times as :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>` measures :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, so many times also let
| :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>` measure :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>` either measures or does not measure :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`.

:line:`15` First, let it measure it.

| And, as many times as :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>` measures :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`, so many times let
| :g:`$F$ <0356.g1:F>` measure :g:`$L$ <0356.g1:L>` also.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` measures :g:`$H$ <0356.g1:H>` the same number of times that
   | :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>` measures :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. Def. 20, VII. 13] <VII.def.20, VII.13>

   :line:`20` therefore, as :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0356.g1:H>` to :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason also,
   | as :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>` to :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`,
   | and further, as :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>` is to :g:`$F$ <0356.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>` to :g:`$L$ <0356.g1:L>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$H$ <0356.g1:H>`, :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0356.g1:L>` are continuously proportional in the
   | :line:`25` ratio of :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0356.g1:B>`, in the ratio of :g:`$C$ <0356.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>`, and in the ratio of :g:`$E$ <0356.g1:E>`
   | to :g:`$F$ <0356.g1:F>`.

| I say next that they are also the least that have this
| property.
