But, as *H* is to *G*, so is *A* to *B*;  
<sup>60</sup> therefore also, as *A* is to *B*, so is *N* to *O*.  
For the same reason also,
$$\text{as } C \text{ is to } D\text{, so is } O \text{ to } M\text{.}$$
Again, since *E* measures *M* the same number of times that *F* measures *P*,  
<sup>65</sup> therefore, as *E* is to *F*, so is *M* to *P*;
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 13 and Def. 20]</div>
therefore *N*, *O*, *M*, *P* are continuously proportional in the ratios of *A* to *B*, of *C* to *D*, and of *E* to *F*.  
I say next that they are also the least that are in the ratios *A* : *B*, *C* : *D*, *E* : *F*.  
<sup>70</sup> For, if not, there will be some numbers less than *N*, *O*, *M*, *P* continuously proportional in the ratios *A* : *B*, *C* : *D*, *E* : *F*.  
Let them be *Q*, *R*, *S*, *T*.  
Now since, as *Q* is to *R*, so is *A* to *B*,  
<sup>75</sup> while *A*, *B* are least,  
and the least numbers measure those which have the same ratio with them the same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent and the consequent the consequent,
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 20]</div>
therefore *B* measures *R*.  
<sup>80</sup> For the same reason *C* also measures *R*;  
therefore *B*, *C* measure *R*.  
Therefore the least number measured by *B*, *C* will also measure *R*.
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 35]</div>
But *G* is the least number measured by *B*, *C*;  
<sup>85</sup> therefore *G* measures *R*.  
And, as *G* is to *R*, so is *K* to *S*:
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 13]</div>
therefore *K* also measures *S*.  
But *E* also measures *S*;  
therefore *E*, *K* measure *S*.  
<sup>90</sup> Therefore the least number measured by *E*, *K* will also measure *S*.
<div style="text-align: right;">[VII. 35]</div>
But *M* is the least number measured by *E*, *K*;  
therefore *M* measures *S*, the greater the less :  
which is impossible.  
<sup>95</sup> Therefore there will not be any numbers less than *N*, *O*, *M*, *P* continuously proportional in the ratios of *A* to *B*, of *C* to *D*, and of *E* to *F*;
