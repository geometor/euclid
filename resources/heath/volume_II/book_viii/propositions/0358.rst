.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`H <0357.g1:H>` is to :g:`G <0357.g1:G>`, so is :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` to :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` is to :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, so is :g:`N <0357.g1:N>` to :g:`O <0357.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also, as :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` is to :g:`D <0357.g1:D>`, so is :g:`O <0357.g1:O>` to :g:`M <0357.g1:M>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` measures :g:`M <0357.g1:M>` the same number of times that :g:`F <0357.g1:F>` measures :g:`P <0357.g1:P>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 13 and Def. 20] <VII.13, VIII.def.20>

   therefore, as :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` is to :g:`F <0357.g1:F>`, so is :g:`M <0357.g1:M>` to :g:`P <0357.g1:P>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`N <0357.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0357.g1:O>`, :g:`M <0357.g1:M>`, :g:`P <0357.g1:P>` are continuously proportional in the ratios of :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` to :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, of :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` to :g:`D <0357.g1:D>`, and of :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0357.g1:F>`.

I say next that they are also the least that are in the ratios :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` : :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` : :g:`D <0357.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` : :g:`F <0357.g1:F>`.\ [#f1]_

.. subclause::

   For, if not, there will be some numbers less than :g:`N <0357.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0357.g1:O>`, :g:`M <0357.g1:M>`, :g:`P <0357.g1:P>` continuously proportional in the ratios :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` : :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` : :g:`D <0357.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` : :g:`F <0357.g1:F>`.

Let them be :g:`Q <0357.g1:Q>`, :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`, :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`, :g:`T <0357.g1:T>`.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`Q <0357.g1:Q>` is to :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`, so is :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` to :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, while :g:`A <0357.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0357.g1:B>` are least, and the least numbers measure those which have the same ratio with them the same number of times, the antecedent the antecedent and the consequent the consequent, :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`B <0357.g1:B>` measures :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` also measures :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` measure :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the least number measured by :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` will also measure :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`G <0357.g1:G>` is the least number measured by :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0357.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`G <0357.g1:G>` measures :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`.

.. subclause:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   And, as :g:`G <0357.g1:G>` is to :g:`R <0357.g1:R>`, so is :g:`K <0357.g1:K>` to :g:`S <0357.g1:S>` :

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`K <0357.g1:K>` also measures :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` also measures :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`E <0357.g1:E>`, :g:`K <0357.g1:K>` measure :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the least number measured by :g:`E <0357.g1:E>`, :g:`K <0357.g1:K>` will also measure :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`. :just:`[VII. 35] <VII.35>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`M <0357.g1:M>` is the least number measured by :g:`E <0357.g1:E>`, :g:`K <0357.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`M <0357.g1:M>` measures :g:`S <0357.g1:S>`, the greater the less : which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore there will not be any numbers less than :g:`N <0357.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0357.g1:O>`, :g:`M <0357.g1:M>`, :g:`P <0357.g1:P>` continuously proportional in the ratios of :g:`A <0357.g1:A>` to :g:`B <0357.g1:B>`, of :g:`C <0357.g1:C>` to :g:`D <0357.g1:D>`, and of :g:`E <0357.g1:E>` to :g:`F <0357.g1:F>`;
