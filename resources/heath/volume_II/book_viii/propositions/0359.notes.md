# Transcription Notes: Folio 0359 (Page 353)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Header**: `VIII. 4] PROPOSITION 4 353`
- **Content**: Concludes Proposition 4 with the final determination of $N, O, M, P$ as the least numbers continuously proportional in the given ratios $A : B, C : D, E : F$, followed by Q. E. D.
- **Commentary**: Extensive commentary by Sir Thomas L. Heath analyzing:
  - Greek shorthand idiom `οἱ ΑΒ, ΓΔ, ΕΖ λόγοι` (lines 69, 71, 99).
  - Clarification that "in continued proportion" here denotes a series of given ratios rather than a single uniform geometrical progression.
  - A complete modern symbolic reconstruction using L.C.M. notation ($l_1, l_2$) showing why Euclid's construction yields the least integers in the given ratios.
  - Cross-reference to Book VI Proposition 23 on compounding ratios.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
