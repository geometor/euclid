# Transcription Notes: Folio 0360 (Page 354)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Header**: `354 BOOK VIII [VIII. 5`
- **Content**: Complete presentation of Proposition 5:
  - Enunciation: Plane numbers have to one another the ratio compounded of the ratios of their sides.
  - Setting-out, specification, and complete demonstration using auxiliary product $L = D \times E$ and citations of VIII. 4, VII. 17, and VII. 14 (*ex aequali*).
  - Q. E. D.
- **Commentary**: Heath's textual note on lines 1, 5, 29, 31 pointing out that the Greek text uses the phrase "compounded of their sides" (λόγος ἐκ τῶν πλευρῶν συγκείμενος), exactly analogous to VI. 23. Algebraic formulation begins at the bottom and continues onto Folio 0361.
