PROPOSITION 8.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If between two numbers there fall numbers in continued proportion with them, then, however many numbers fall between them in continued proportion, so many will also fall in con- tinued proportion between the numbers which have the same ratio with the original numbers.

| Let the numbers :g:`$C$ <0363.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0363.g1:D>` fall between the two numbers :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`,
| :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>` in continued proportion with them, and let :g:`$E$ <0363.g1:E>` be made in
| the same ratio to :g:`$F$ <0363.g1:F>` as :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>`;

| I say that, as many numbers as have fallen between :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>` in
| continued proportion, so many will also fall between :g:`$E$ <0363.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0363.g1:F>` in
| continued proportion.

.. picture:: 0363.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII Proposition 8

| For, as many as :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0363.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0363.g1:D>` are in multitude, let so many
| numbers :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0363.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0363.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>`, the least of those which have the same
| ratio with :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0363.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0363.g1:D>`, :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>`, be taken ; :just:`[VII. 33] <VII.33>`

.. assertion:: [VIII. 3] <VIII.3>

   therefore the extremes of them :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>`, :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>` are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0363.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0363.g1:D>`, :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>` are in the same ratio with :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0363.g1:H>`,
   | :g:`$K$ <0363.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>`,
   | and the multitude of the numbers :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>`, :g:`$C$ <0363.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0363.g1:D>`, :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>` is equal to the
   | multitude of the numbers :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0363.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0363.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 14] <VII.14>

   therefore, *ex aequali*, as :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>` to :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$A$ <0363.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0363.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0363.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0363.g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>` is to :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0363.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0363.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$G$ <0363.g1:G>`, :g:`$L$ <0363.g1:L>` are prime,
   | primes are also least, :just:`[VII. 21] <VII.21>`
   | and the least numbers measure those which have the same
   | ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and
   | the less the less, that is, the antecedent the antecedent and the
   | consequent the consequent. :just:`[VII. 20] <VII.20>`
