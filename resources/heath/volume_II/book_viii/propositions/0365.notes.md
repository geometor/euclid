# Transcription Notes: Folio 0365 (Page 359)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Header**: `VIII. 9] PROPOSITIONS 8, 9 359`
- **Content**:
  - Completes the enunciation/specification begun on Folio 0364 ("tinued proportion...").
  - Construction of least sets in ratio: $F, G$ (two), $H, K, L$ (three), up to $M, N, O, P$ (four) via VIII. 2.

- **Mathematical Significance**: Fundamental theorem linking continuous proportion between coprime integers to power sequences from unity.
