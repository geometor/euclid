## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 361 continues the proof of **Book VIII, Proposition 10** ("If numbers fall between two numbers and a unit in continued proportion, however many numbers fall between each of them and a unit in continued proportion, so many also will fall between the numbers themselves in continued proportion").
- **Antecedent**: Folio 0366 (page 360) contains the enunciation and setting out of Proposition 10.
- **Folio Scope**: Euclid forms the cross-product $H = D \times F$ and the secondary products $K = D \times H$ and $L = F \times H$. He demonstrates that $E = D^2$, $A = D \times E = D^3$, $G = F^2$, and $B = F \times G = F^3$, and concludes with the initial ratio relation $E : H = D : F$ via VII. 17.
- **Consequent**: Folio 0368 (page 362) continues and completes the proof of Proposition 10.
