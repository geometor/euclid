# Transcription Notes: Folio 0369 (Page 363)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 363 opens with the final sentence of Heath's commentary on Proposition 10, presents the full enunciation, proof, and diagram of **Book VIII, Proposition 11**, and begins Heath's commentary on Proposition 11.
- **Antecedent**: Folio 0368 (page 362) concluded the proof of Proposition 10 and opened Heath's commentary.
- **Folio Scope**: Euclid proves that between two squares $A = C^2$ and $B = D^2$, the product $E = CD$ is the unique mean proportional ($A : E = E : B = C : D$), and that $A : B$ is duplicate of $C : D$. Heath's commentary introduces the historical connection to Nicomachus (*Introductio Arithmetica* II. 24. 1) and Plato's *Timaeus* 31 C–32 B.
- **Consequent**: Folio 0370 (page 364) continues Heath's commentary on Proposition 11 and introduces Proposition 12.
