| Let there be as many numbers as we please, :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>`, in
| continued proportion, so that, as :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>`;
| let :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>` by multiplying themselves make :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>`, and by
| multiplying :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>` let them make :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>`;
| I say that :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>` and :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>` are in continued proportion.

| For let :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>` by multiplying :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` make :g:`$L$ <0372.g1:L>`,
| and let the numbers :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$L$ <0372.g1:L>` make :g:`$M$ <0372.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0372.g1:N>`
| respectively.

| And again let :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>` make :g:`$O$ <0372.g1:O>`,
| and let the numbers :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>` by multiplying :g:`$O$ <0372.g1:O>` make :g:`$P$ <0372.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0372.g1:Q>`
| respectively.

.. assertion::

   | Then, in manner similar to the foregoing, we can prove
   | that
   | :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$L$ <0372.g1:L>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>` and :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0372.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0372.g1:N>`, :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>` are continuously proportional in the
   | ratio of :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>` to :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`,
   | and further :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$O$ <0372.g1:O>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>` and :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, :g:`$P$ <0372.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0372.g1:Q>`, :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>` are continuously propor-
   | tional in the ratio of :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, as :g:`$A$ <0372.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$B$ <0372.g1:B>` to :g:`$C$ <0372.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$L$ <0372.g1:L>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>` are also in the same ratio with :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$O$ <0372.g1:O>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>`,
   | and further :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0372.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0372.g1:N>`, :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>` in the same ratio with :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, :g:`$P$ <0372.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0372.g1:Q>`, :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>`.

.. subclause::

   | And the multitude of :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>`, :g:`$L$ <0372.g1:L>`, :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>` is equal to the multitude of
   | :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, :g:`$O$ <0372.g1:O>`, :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>`, and that of :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0372.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0372.g1:N>`, :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>` to that of :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, :g:`$P$ <0372.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0372.g1:Q>`, :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, *ex aequali*,
   | as :g:`$D$ <0372.g1:D>` is to :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0372.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0372.g1:F>`,
   | and, as :g:`$G$ <0372.g1:G>` is to :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$H$ <0372.g1:H>` to :g:`$K$ <0372.g1:K>`. :just:`[VII. 14] <VII.14>`
