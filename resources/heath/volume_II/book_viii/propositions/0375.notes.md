- **Euclidean Citations**:
  - `[VIII. 11, 12]`: Between two square numbers there is one mean proportional, and between two cube numbers two mean proportionals.
  - `[VIII. 7]`: If there are terms in continued proportion and the first measures the last, it also measures the second.
  - `[VII. Def. 20]`: Proportionality definition in terms of parts/measures.
- **Commentary**: Heath uses the shorthand *mutatis mutandis* comparing this cubic proposition to the quadratic analogue (Proposition 14).
