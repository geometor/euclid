PROPOSITION 16.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a square number do not measure a square number, neither will the side measure the side; and, if the side do not measure the side, neither will the square measure the square.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>` be square numbers, and let :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>` be their sides;
| and let :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` not measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`;
| I say that neither does :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   | For, if :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`, :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` will also
   | measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`. :just:`[VIII. 14] <VIII.14>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` does not measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither will :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0376.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 16

| Again, let :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`;
| I say that neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`.

.. subclause:: [VIII. 14] <VIII.14>

   For, if :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` will also measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0376.g1:C>` does not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g1:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| If $a^2$ does not measure $b^2$, $a$ will not measure $b$; and, if $a$ does not
| measure $b$, $a^2$ will not measure $b^2$.

The proof is a mere *reductio ad absurdum* using VIII. 14.

----

PROPOSITION 17.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a cube number do not measure a cube number, neither will the side measure the side; and, if the side do not measure the side, neither will the cube measure the cube.

| For let the cube number :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` not measure the cube
| number :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`,
| and let :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` be the side of :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>`, and :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`
| of :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`;
| I say that :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` will not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`.

.. subclause::

   | For if :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` measures :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`, :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` will
   | also measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`. :just:`[VIII. 15] <VIII.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` does not measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither does :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`.

.. picture:: 0376.g2.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 17

| Again, let :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`;
| I say that neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`.
