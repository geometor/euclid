## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 18 Structure**:
  - Defines sides: $A = C \times D$, $B = E \times F$.
  - Proportionality of sides: $C : D = E : F$ (VII. Def. 21), hence alternately $C : E = D : F$ (VII. 13).
  - Construction of mean: $G = D \times E$.
  - Ratio theorem: Since $D$ multiplies $C$ to make $A$ and $E$ to make $G$, $C : E = A : G$ (VII. 17).
