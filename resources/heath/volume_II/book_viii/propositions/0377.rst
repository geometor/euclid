.. subclause:: [VIII. 15] <VIII.15>

   For, if :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` measures :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` will also measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$C$ <0376.g2:C>` does not measure :g:`$D$ <0376.g2:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither will :g:`$A$ <0376.g2:A>` measure :g:`$B$ <0376.g2:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

If $a^3$ does not measure $b^3$, $a$ will not measure $b$; and vice versa.

Proved by *reductio ad absurdum* employing VIII. 15.

----

PROPOSITION 18.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Between two similar plane numbers there is one mean proportional number; and the plane number has to the plane number the ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0377.g1:B>` be two similar plane numbers, and let the numbers
| :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` be the sides of :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>`, and :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>` of :g:`$B$ <0377.g1:B>`.

.. picture:: 0377.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 18

.. subclause::

   | Now, since similar plane numbers are those which have
   | their sides proportional, :just:`[VII. Def. 21] <VII.def.21>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>`.

| I say then that between :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0377.g1:B>` there is one mean propor-
| tional number, and :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0377.g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that
| which :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` has to :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>`, or :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>`, that is, of that which the corre-
| sponding side has to the corresponding side.

.. subclause::

   Now since, as :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>` to :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   therefore, alternately, as :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` to :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>` is plane, and :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` are its sides,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` has made :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$F$ <0377.g1:F>` has made :g:`$B$ <0377.g1:B>`.

Now let :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>` make :g:`$G$ <0377.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   | Then, since :g:`$D$ <0377.g1:D>` by multiplying :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` has made :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>`, and by
   | multiplying :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>` has made :g:`$G$ <0377.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0377.g1:C>` is to :g:`$E$ <0377.g1:E>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0377.g1:A>` to :g:`$G$ <0377.g1:G>`.
