But, as <span data-ref="0377.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0377.g1:E">$E$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>; therefore also, as <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> is to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:G">$G$</span>.

But it was also proved that, as <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> is to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:G">$G$</span>; therefore also, as <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0377.g1:G">$G$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:B">$B$</span>.

Therefore between <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0377.g1:B">$B$</span> there is one mean proportional number.

I say next that <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> also has to <span data-ref="0377.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is, of that which <span data-ref="0377.g1:C">$C$</span> has to <span data-ref="0377.g1:E">$E$</span> or <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>.

[V. Def. 9]

And, as <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0377.g1:G">$G$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:E">$E$</span>, and so is <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0377.g1:A">$A$</span> also has to <span data-ref="0377.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio duplicate of that which <span data-ref="0377.g1:C">$C$</span> has to <span data-ref="0377.g1:E">$E$</span> or <span data-ref="0377.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0377.g1:F">$F$</span>.

If $ab$, $cd$ be "similar plane numbers," i.e. products of factors such that
$$a : b = c : d,$$
there is one mean proportional between $ab$ and $cd$; and $ab$ is to $cd$ in the duplicate ratio of $a$ to $c$ or of $b$ to $d$.

Form the product $bc$ (or $ad$, which is equal to it, by VII. 19).

Then
$$\left.\begin{array}{r} ab,\ bc \\ = ad \end{array}\right\},\ cd$$
is a series of terms in geometrical progression.

For
$$a : b = c : d.$$

Therefore
$$a : c = b : d. \qquad \text{[VII. 13]}$$

Therefore
$$ab : bc = bc : cd. \qquad \text{[VII. 17 and 16]}$$

Thus $bc$ (or $ad$) is a geometric mean between $ab$, $cd$.

And $ab$ is to $cd$ in the duplicate ratio of $ab$ to $bc$ or of $bc$ to $cd$, that is, of $a$ to $c$ or of $b$ to $d$.
