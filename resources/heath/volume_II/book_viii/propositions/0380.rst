:title: Proposition 19 (continued)

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>`, so is :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 13] <VII.13>

   alternately therefore, as :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` is to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, so is :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>`, :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>`, :g:`$L$ <0379.g1:L>` are continuously proportional in the
   | ratio of :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, in the ratio of :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and also in the ratio
   | of :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`.

Next, let :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>`, :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` make :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>` respectively.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` is a solid number, and :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` are its sides,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying the product of :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` has made :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   But the product of :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` is :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>` has made :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`$L$ <0379.g1:L>` has made :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` by multiplying :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>` has made :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>`, and further
   | also by multiplying :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` has made :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>` is to :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$K$ <0379.g1:K>` is to :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and also :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   | Again, since :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>`, :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` have made :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`
   | respectively,

.. assertion:: [VII. 18] <VII.18>

   therefore, as :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` is to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` is to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>` and :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` to
   | :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>` and :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   | Again, since :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>` by multiplying :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` has made :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`, and further
   | also by multiplying :g:`$L$ <0379.g1:L>` has made :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>`,

.. assertion:: [VII. 17] <VII.17>

   therefore, as :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` is to :g:`$L$ <0379.g1:L>`, so is :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>` to :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$M$ <0379.g1:M>` is to :g:`$L$ <0379.g1:L>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore also, as :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` is to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`, so not
   | only is :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>` to :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>`, but also :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` to :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>` and :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>` to :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>`, :g:`$N$ <0379.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0379.g1:O>`, :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>` are continuously proportional in the
   | aforesaid ratios of the sides.

| I say that :g:`$A$ <0379.g1:A>` also has to :g:`$B$ <0379.g1:B>` the ratio triplicate of that which
| the corresponding side has to the corresponding side, that is,
| of the ratio which the number :g:`$C$ <0379.g1:C>` has to :g:`$F$ <0379.g1:F>`, or :g:`$D$ <0379.g1:D>` to :g:`$G$ <0379.g1:G>`, and
| also :g:`$E$ <0379.g1:E>` to :g:`$H$ <0379.g1:H>`.
