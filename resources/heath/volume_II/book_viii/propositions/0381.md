In other words, if $a : b : c = d : e : f$, then there are two geometric means<br />
between $abc$, $def$; and $abc$ is to $def$ in the triplicate ratio of $a$ to $d$, or $b$ to $e$,<br />
or $c$ to $f$.

Euclid first takes the plane numbers $ab$, $de$ (leaving out $c$, $f$) and forms<br />
the product $bd$. Thus, as in VIII. 18,
$$\left.\begin{array}{r} ab,\ bd \\ = ea \end{array}\right\},\ de$$
are three terms in geometrical progression in the ratio of $a$ to $d$, or of $b$ to $e$.

He next forms the products of $c$, $f$ respectively into the mean $bd$.

Then
$$abc,\ cbd,\ fbd,\ def$$
are in geometrical progression in the ratio of $a$ to $d$ etc.

For
$$\left.\begin{aligned} abc : cbd &= ab : bd = a : d \\ bd : fbd &= c : f \\ fbd : def &= bd : de = b : e \end{aligned}\right\} \qquad \text{[VII. 17]}$$

And
$$a : d = b : e = c : f.$$

The ratio of $abc$ to $def$ is the ratio triplicate of that of $abc$ to $cbd$, i.e. of<br />
that of $a$ to $d$ etc.

### PROPOSITION 20.

<p class="enunciation">If one mean proportional number fall between two numbers,<br />
the numbers will be similar plane numbers.</p>

For let one mean proportional number <span data-ref="0382.g1:C">$C$</span> fall between the<br />
two numbers <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:B">$B$</span>;

<sup>5</sup> I say that <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar plane numbers.

Let <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:E">$E$</span>, the least numbers of those which have the same<br />
ratio with <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:C">$C$</span>, be taken; <span class="justification">[VII. 33]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> measures <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span> the same number of times that <span data-ref="0382.g1:E">$E$</span><br />
measures <span data-ref="0382.g1:C">$C$</span>. <span class="justification">[VII. 20]</span></p>

<sup>10</sup> Now, as many times as <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> measures <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span>, so many units let<br />
there be in <span data-ref="0382.g1:F">$F$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0382.g1:F">$F$</span> by multiplying <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> has made <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0382.g1:A">$A$</span> is plane, and <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:F">$F$</span> are its sides.</p>
