This proposition is the converse of VIII. 18. If $a, c, b$ are in geometrical<br />
progression, $a, b$ are "similar plane numbers."

Let $\alpha : \beta$ be the ratio $a : c$ (and therefore also the ratio $c : b$) in its lowest<br />
terms.

Then <span class="justification">[VII. 20]</span>
$$\begin{aligned}
a &= m\alpha, & c &= m\beta, && \text{where } m \text{ is some integer,} \\
c &= n\alpha, & b &= n\beta, && \text{where } n \text{ is some integer.}
\end{aligned}$$
