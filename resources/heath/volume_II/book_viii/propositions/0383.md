### PROPOSITION 21.

*If two mean proportional numbers fall between two numbers, the numbers are similar solid numbers.*

For let two mean proportional numbers <span data-ref="0383.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:D">$D$</span> fall between the two numbers <span data-ref="0383.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:B">$B$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0383.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar solid numbers.

![Proposition 21](0383.g1.svg)

For let three numbers <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span>, the least of those which have the same ratio with <span data-ref="0383.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:D">$D$</span>, be taken; [VII. 33 or VIII. 2]
therefore the extremes of them <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span> are prime to one another. [VIII. 3]

Now, since one mean proportional number <span data-ref="0383.g1:F">$F$</span> has fallen between <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span> are similar plane numbers. [VIII. 20]

Let, then, <span data-ref="0383.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:K">$K$</span> be the sides of <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, and <span data-ref="0383.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:M">$M$</span> of <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span>.

Now, since <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span> are the least of the numbers which have the same ratio with <span data-ref="0383.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:D">$D$</span>,
and the multitude of the numbers <span data-ref="0383.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:G">$G$</span> is equal to the multitude of the numbers <span data-ref="0383.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0383.g1:D">$D$</span>,
