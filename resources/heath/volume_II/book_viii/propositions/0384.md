The converse of VIII. 19. If $a, c, d, b$ are in geometrical progression, $a, b$ are "similar solid numbers."
Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ be the least numbers in the ratio of $a, c, d$ (and therefore also of $c, d, b$). [VII. 33 or VIII. 2]
Therefore $\alpha, \gamma$ are prime to one another. [VIII. 3]
They are also "similar plane numbers." [VIII. 20]
Let
$$\alpha = mn, \quad \gamma = pq,$$
where
$$m : n = p : q.$$
