The converse of VIII. 19. If $a, c, d, b$ are in geometrical progression, $a, b$<br />
are "similar solid numbers."

Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ be the least numbers in the ratio of $a, c, d$ (and therefore also<br />
of $c, d, b$). <span class="justification">[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]</span>

Therefore $\alpha, \gamma$ are prime to one another. <span class="justification">[VIII. 3]</span>

They are also "similar plane numbers." <span class="justification">[VIII. 20]</span>

Let
$$\alpha = mn, \quad \gamma = pq,$$
where
$$m : n = p : q.$$
