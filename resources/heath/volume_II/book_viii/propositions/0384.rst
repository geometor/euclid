| The converse of VIII. 19. If $a, c, d, b$ are in geometrical progression, $a, b$
| are "similar solid numbers."

| Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ be the least numbers in the ratio of $a, c, d$ (and therefore also
| of $c, d, b$). :just:`[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]`

Therefore $\alpha, \gamma$ are prime to one another. :just:`[VIII. 3] <VIII.3>`

They are also "similar plane numbers." :just:`[VIII. 20] <VIII.20>`

Let

$$\alpha = mn, \quad \gamma = pq,$$

where

$$m : n = p : q.$$
