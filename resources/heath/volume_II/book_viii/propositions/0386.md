### PROPOSITION 24.

<p class="enunciation">If two numbers have to one another the ratio which a square<br />
number has to a square number, and the first be square, the<br />
second will also be square.</p>

For let the two numbers <span data-ref="0386.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0386.g1:B">$B$</span> have to one another the<br />
ratio which the square number <span data-ref="0386.g1:C">$C$</span> has<br />
to the square number <span data-ref="0386.g1:D">$D$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0386.g1:A">$A$</span> be<br />
square;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0386.g1:B">$B$</span> is also square.

![Proposition 24](0386.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0386.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0386.g1:D">$D$</span> are square,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0386.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0386.g1:D">$D$</span> are similar plane numbers.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore one mean proportional number falls between<br />
<span data-ref="0386.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0386.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0386.g1:C">$C$</span> is to <span data-ref="0386.g1:D">$D$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0386.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0386.g1:B">$B$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore one mean proportional number falls between <span data-ref="0386.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0386.g1:B">$B$</span><br />
also. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0386.g1:A">$A$</span> is square;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0386.g1:B">$B$</span> is also square. <span class="justification">[VIII. 22]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

If $a : b = c^2 : d^2$, and $a$ is a square, then $b$ is also a square.
For $c^2, d^2$ have one mean proportional $cd$. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span>
Therefore $a, b$, which are in the same ratio, have one mean proportional.<br />
<span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span>
And, since $a$ is square, $b$ must also be a square. <span class="justification">[VIII. 22]</span>
