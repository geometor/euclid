<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0387.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:D">$D$</span> are cube,<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:D">$D$</span> are similar solid numbers.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:D">$D$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span></p>

![Proposition 25](0387.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">And, as many numbers as fall between <span data-ref="0387.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:D">$D$</span> in continued<br />
proportion, so many will also fall between those which have<br />
the same ratio with them; <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">so that two mean proportional numbers fall between <span data-ref="0387.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:B">$B$</span><br />
also.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0387.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:F">$F$</span> so fall.

<p class="sub-clause">Since, then, the four numbers <span data-ref="0387.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:B">$B$</span> are in continued<br />
proportion,<br />
and <span data-ref="0387.g1:A">$A$</span> is cube,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0387.g1:B">$B$</span> is also cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

If $a : b = c^3 : d^3$, and $a$ is a cube, then $b$ is also a cube.<br />
For $c^3, d^3$ have two mean proportionals. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span><br />
Therefore $a, b$ also have two mean proportionals. <span class="justification">[VIII. 8]</span><br />
And $a$ is a cube:<br />
therefore $b$ is a cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 23]</span>

### PROPOSITION 26.

<p class="enunciation">Similar plane numbers have to one another the ratio which<br />
a square number has to a square number.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0387.g2:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g2:B">$B$</span> be similar plane numbers;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0387.g2:A">$A$</span> has to <span data-ref="0387.g2:B">$B$</span> the ratio which a square number has<br />
to a square number.

![Proposition 26](0387.g2.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0387.g2:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g2:B">$B$</span> are similar plane numbers,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore one mean proportional number falls between <span data-ref="0387.g2:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g2:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span></p>
