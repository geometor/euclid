.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`C <0387.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0387.g1:D>` are cube, :g:`C <0387.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0387.g1:D>` are similar solid numbers.

.. assertion::

   Therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`C <0387.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0387.g1:D>`. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

.. picture:: 0387.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 25

.. subclause::

   And, as many numbers as fall between :g:`C <0387.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0387.g1:D>` in continued proportion, so many will also fall between those which have the same ratio with them; :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

.. assertion::

   so that two mean proportional numbers fall between :g:`A <0387.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0387.g1:B>` also.

Let :g:`E <0387.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0387.g1:F>` so fall.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, the four numbers :g:`A <0387.g1:A>`, :g:`E <0387.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0387.g1:F>`, :g:`B <0387.g1:B>` are in continued proportion, and :g:`A <0387.g1:A>` is cube,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 23] <VIII.23>

   therefore :g:`B <0387.g1:B>` is also cube.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

If $a : b = c^3 : d^3$, and $a$ is a cube, then $b$ is also a cube. For $c^3, d^3$ have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 19] <VIII.19>`

Therefore $a, b$ also have two mean proportionals. :just:`[VIII. 8] <VIII.8>`

And $a$ is a cube: therefore $b$ is a cube. :just:`[VIII. 23] <VIII.23>`

----

PROPOSITION 26.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Similar plane numbers have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

Let :g:`A <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0387.g2:B>` be similar plane numbers;

I say that :g:`A <0387.g2:A>` has to :g:`B <0387.g2:B>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. picture:: 0387.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 26

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`A <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0387.g2:B>` are similar plane numbers,

.. assertion:: [VIII. 18] <VIII.18>

   therefore one mean proportional number falls between :g:`A <0387.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0387.g2:B>`.
