If $a, b$ are similar “plane numbers,” let $c$ be the mean proportional<br />
between them. <span class="justification">[VIII. 18]</span><br />
Take $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ the smallest numbers in the ratio of $a, c, b$. <span class="justification">[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]</span><br />
Then $\alpha, \gamma$ are squares. <span class="justification">[VIII. 2, Por.]</span><br />
Therefore $a, b$ are in the ratio of a square to a square.

### PROPOSITION 27.

<p class="enunciation">Similar solid numbers have to one another the ratio which<br />
a cube number has to a cube number.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span> be similar solid numbers;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span> has to <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio which a cube number has to<br />
a cube number.

![Proposition 27](0388.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span> are similar solid numbers,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore two mean proportional numbers fall between <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[VIII. 19]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0388.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:D">$D$</span> so fall,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0388.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:H">$H$</span>, the least numbers of those which have<br />
the same ratio with <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span>, and equal with them in<br />
multitude, be taken; <span class="justification">[VII. 33 or VIII. 2]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore the extremes of them <span data-ref="0388.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0388.g1:H">$H$</span> are cube. <span class="justification">[VIII. 2, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0388.g1:E">$E$</span> is to <span data-ref="0388.g1:H">$H$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0388.g1:A">$A$</span> also has to <span data-ref="0388.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio which a cube number has<br />
to a cube number.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
