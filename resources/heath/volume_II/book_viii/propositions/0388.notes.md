## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 26 Proof Completion**: Takes least numbers $D, E, F$ in the ratio $A, C, B$ (using VII. 33 or VIII. 2); by VIII. 2 Porism, the extremes $D, F$ are square; hence $A : B = D : F$ is a ratio of square to square.
- **Commentary on Prop 26**: Expresses the argument algebraically using $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$.
- **Proposition 27**: The three-dimensional analogue for similar solid numbers, using two mean proportionals $C, D$ (by VIII. 19), least numbers $E, F, G, H$, and VIII. 2 Porism to show extremes $E, H$ are cube numbers. Concludes with Q. E. D.
