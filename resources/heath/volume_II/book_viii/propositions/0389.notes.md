## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **Commentary on Proposition 27**:
  - Highlights Heron's additions preserved in al-Nayrīzī's commentary (ed. Curtze, pp. 194–195).
  - Transcribes Heron's two propositions (the converses of VIII. 26 and VIII. 27):
    1. If $a : b = c^2 : d^2$, then $a, b$ are similar plane numbers;
    2. If $a : b = c^3 : d^3$, then $a, b$ are similar solid numbers.
  - Outlines proofs via mean proportionals ($cd$ between $c^2, d^2$ gives a mean proportional between $a, b$ by VIII. 8, implying similar plane numbers by VIII. 20).
  - Discusses textual implications for IX. 10 and references Heron's extension of VII. 3 tacitly assumed in VII. 33.
- **Book VIII Conclusion**: Marks the completion of Euclid's Elements, Book VIII.
