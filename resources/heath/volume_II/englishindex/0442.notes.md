## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terms**: Includes *διαίρεσις λόγου* (separation of ratio), *διελόντι* (separando), *πηλικότης* (translated as size), and *μονάς* with its ancient etymological connection to *μόνος* (solitary) or *μονή* (rest).
- **Mathematical Formulae**: Restatement of Heron's formula for the triangle area: $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$.
- **Scholarly Citations**: Extensive citation list for *Simson, R.* spanning over thirty pages of Volume II, along with *Theon of Alexandria* and *Theon of Smyrna*. Cross-reference for *aṭ-Ṭūsī* directing to *Naṣīraddin*.
