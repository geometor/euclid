Transcription Notes: Folio 0516 (Page 512)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio Range**: Folio 0516 corresponds to Page 512 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section I: "The Contents of the So-Called Book XIV. By Hypsicles."
- **Continuity**: This page begins the Appendix to Volume III, introducing Hypsicles' supplement to Book XIII on the comparison of the dodecahedron and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere. It includes Heath's editorial introduction, Hypsicles' preface addressed to Protarchus detailing Apollonius' work and Basilides of Tyre, and the enunciation of Proposition 1.
- **Folio Transition**: The enunciation of Prop. 1 concludes page 512; its proof and figure follow on page 513 (Folio 0517).

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Hypsicles' Preface**: Contains essential historical testimony regarding the transmission and correction of Apollonius' treatise comparing the dodecahedron and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere.
- **Translation Style**: Heath notes that literal translations from the Greek text are enclosed in inverted commas ("..."), while other portions are reproduced in briefer mathematical form.
- **Proposition 1 Enunciation**: Establishes that the apothem of a regular pentagon in a circle equals half the sum of the side of the regular hexagon and the side of the regular decagon inscribed in that circle ($r_{\text{pent-perp}} = \frac{1}{2}(s_6 + s_{10})$).
