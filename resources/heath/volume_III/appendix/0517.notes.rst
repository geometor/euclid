Transcription Notes: Folio 0517 (Page 513)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio Range**: Folio 0517 corresponds to Page 513 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section I: "The So-Called 'Book XIV'".
- **Continuity**: Carries forward the proof of Proposition 1 from page 512. It then quotes the corollary regarding the perpendicular to the side of an equilateral triangle (from Book XIII. 12), states Proposition 2, provides Hypsicles' historical remarks regarding Aristaeus' *Comparison of the five figures* and Apollonius' second edition, and introduces the Lemma required for Proposition 2.
- **Folio Transition**: The proof of the Lemma carries forward to page 514 (Folio 0518).

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Aristaeus & Apollonius**: Hypsicles cites Aristaeus' lost work *Comparison of the five figures* ($\Sigma\acute{\upsilon}\gamma\kappa\rho\iota\sigma\iota\varsigma\ \tau\tilde{\omega}\nu\ \pi\acute{\epsilon}\nu\tau\epsilon\ \sigma\chi\eta\mu\acute{\alpha}\tau\omega\nu$) and Apollonius' second edition proving that the ratio of surfaces equals the ratio of volumes because the insphere radii to the pentagonal and triangular faces are equal.
- **Mathematical Relations**:

  - In Prop. 1: $2 DE = DF + FC$, where $DF = r = s_6$ and $FC = s_{10}$, proving $DE = \frac{1}{2}(s_6 + s_{10})$.
  - In the Lemma: For a regular pentagon in a circle of radius $r$, $d^2 + s^2 = 5 r^2$ (where $d$ is the diagonal subtending two sides and $s$ is the side).
