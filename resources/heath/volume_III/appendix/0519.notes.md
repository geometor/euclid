# Transcription Notes: Folio 0519 (Page 515)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Folio 0519 corresponds to Page 515 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section I: "The So-Called 'Book XIV'".
- **Continuity**: Concludes the proof of Proposition 2 with Q. E. D., then presents Propositions 3, 4, 5, and the enunciation of Proposition 6.
- **Folio Transition**: The proof of Proposition 6 continues on page 516 (Folio 0520).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Conclusion of Prop. 2**: Uses XIII. 12 ($3 \cdot \text{side}^2 = 9 r^2 \implies 5 KL^2 = 15 r_{KLH}^2$) and the Lemma ($DG^2 + CG^2 = 5 r_{CDEFG}^2 \implies 3 DG^2 + 3 CG^2 = 15 r_{CDEFG}^2$) to establish that the circumscribing radii are identical.
- **Propositions 3 & 4**: Express the total surface of the dodecahedron (12 pentagonal faces) and icosahedron (20 triangular faces) in terms of 30 times the rectangle contained by a face-side and face-apothem.
- **Proposition 5**: Derives the surface ratio as $(s_{\text{pent}} \cdot a_{\text{pent}}) : (s_{\text{tri}} \cdot a_{\text{tri}})$.
- **Proposition 6 Enunciation**: Sets out to prove that $\text{Surface}_{\text{dod}} : \text{Surface}_{\text{icos}} = \text{Side}_{\text{cube}} : \text{Side}_{\text{icos}}$.
