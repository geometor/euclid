# Transcription Notes: Folio 0520 (Page 516)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Folio 0520 corresponds to Page 516 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section I: "The Contents of the So-Called Book XIV. By Hypsicles."
- **Continuity**: Carries forward Proposition 6 from page 515, completing its first proof, and then begins an alternative proof of the same theorem with a preliminary lemma regarding the area of a regular pentagon ($\text{rect. } AF, BH = \text{area of pentagon}$).
- **Folio Transition**: The preliminary lemma and alternative proof conclude on page 517 (Folio 0521).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **First Proof of Prop. 6**:
  - Uses the extreme and mean ratio properties from XIII. 9, Prop. 1, and XIII. 17 Porisma.
  - Since $EB + BC$ is divided at $B$, its half $EG$ is divided into greater segment $\frac{1}{2}EB = EF$.
  - Since cube edge $H$ divided in extreme and mean ratio has greater segment $CA$ ($s_{\text{pent}}$), it follows that $H : CA = EG : EF$, leading directly to $H : CD = (CA \cdot EG) : (FE \cdot CD) = \text{Surface}_{\text{dod}} : \text{Surface}_{\text{icos}}$.
- **Alternative Proof Preliminary**:
  - Sets $DF = \frac{1}{2}AD \implies AF = \frac{3}{2}AD$.
  - Sets $CH = \frac{1}{3}CG \implies GC = \frac{3}{2}GH$.
  - Yields $AF \cdot GH = AD \cdot CG = 2 (\triangle ABD)$.
