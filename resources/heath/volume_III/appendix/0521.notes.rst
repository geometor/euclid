Summary & Folio Continuity

- **Continuity**: Carries forward from page 516 the conclusion of the preliminary lemma ($AF \cdot BH = \text{area of pentagon}$) and the alternative proof of Proposition 6. It then introduces Proposition 7 and begins its setup and construction.
- **Folio Transition**: The mathematical deduction of Proposition 7 continues onto page 518 (Folio 0522).

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Alternative Proof of Prop. 6**:

  - Combines $AG \cdot BH = \text{area of pentagon}$ and $AG \cdot GD = \text{area of triangle}$ to find $BH : GD = \text{area of pentagon} : \text{area of triangle}$.
  - Then $12 BH : 20 GD = \text{Surface}_{\text{dod}} : \text{Surface}_{\text{icos}}$.
  - Since $BH = 5 HC$ and $BC = 6 HC$, $12 BH = 60 HC = 10 BC$. Also $20 GD = 10 (2 GD) = 10 DM$.
  - Therefore $\text{Surface}_{\text{dod}} : \text{Surface}_{\text{icos}} = 10 BC : 10 DM = BC : DM = \text{side of cube} : \text{side of icosahedron}$.

- **Proposition 7 Setup**:

  - Radius $CB$ divided at $D$ in extreme and mean ratio, $CD$ being the greater segment (side of decagon).
  - Solids inscribed in sphere: $E = \text{side of icosahedron}$, $F = \text{side of dodecahedron}$, $G = \text{side of cube}$.
