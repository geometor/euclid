# Transcription Notes: Folio 0522 (Page 518)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Folio 0522 corresponds to Page 518 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section I: "The Contents of the So-Called Book XIV. By Hypsicles."
- **Continuity**: Carries forward from page 517 the completion of Proposition 7, presents Proposition 8 (showing that the ratio of volumes of dodecahedron and icosahedron equals the ratio of cube side to icosahedron side), and introduces the Lemma on extreme and mean ratio (referred to on page 514).
- **Folio Transition**: The proof of the Lemma continues onto page 519 (Folio 0523).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 7 Conclusion**:
  - $E^2 = 3 CB^2$ (XIII. 12); $CB^2 + BD^2 = 3 CD^2$ (XIII. 4).
  - Shows $G^2 : E^2 = (BC^2 + CD^2) : (CB^2 + BD^2)$, fulfilling the enunciation.
- **Proposition 8**:
  - Proves that the pyramids from the centre of the sphere to the faces have equal altitude because the face circumcircles are equal (Prop. 2), hence are equally distant from the centre of the sphere.
  - Thus volume ratio = surface ratio = side of cube : side of icosahedron (by Prop. 6).
- **Lemma on Extreme and Mean Ratio**:
  - Statement: If two lines $AB, DE$ are cut in extreme and mean ratio at $C, F$, then $AB : AC = DE : DF$.
  - Begins by setting up $4 AB \cdot BC : AC^2 = 4 DE \cdot EF : DF^2$.
