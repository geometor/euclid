Transcription Notes: Folio 0523 (Page 519)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio Range**: Folio 0523 corresponds to Page 519 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, transition from Section I ("Book XIV") to Section II ("Note on the So-Called 'Book XV'").
- **Continuity**: Completes the algebraic/geometric proof of the extreme and mean ratio Lemma using *componendo* and II. 8, provides Heath's concise four-part "Summary of results" for Book XIV, and begins Section II with an introductory assessment and division of Book XV.
- **Folio Transition**: The discussion of Isidorus of Miletus carries over mid-sentence into page 520 (Folio 0524): "attributed to 'Isidorus / our great teacher.'"

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Lemma Completion**:

  - Uses Euclid II. 8 ($4 AB \cdot BC + AC^2 = (AB + BC)^2$).
  - Successive *componendo* steps lead from $(AB + BC) : AC = (DE + EF) : DF$ to $(AB + BC + AC) : AC = 2 AB : AC = 2 DE : DF$, proving $AB : AC = DE : DF$.

- **Summary of Results**:

  - Encapsulates the core theorems of Book XIV relating the edge, surface, and volume ratios of the cube, dodecahedron, and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere.

- **Book XV Introduction**:

  - Characterizes Book XV as inferior, obscure, and in parts inaccurate.
  - Divides it into three distinct parts: (1) inscriptions of regular solids in one another (pp. 40–48); (2) counting edges and solid angles (pp. 48–50); (3) dihedral angle determinations via isosceles triangles (pp. 50–66).
  - Mentions the attribution of part 3 to Isidorus.
