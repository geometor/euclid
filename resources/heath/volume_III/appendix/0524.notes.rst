Transcription Notes: Folio 0524 (Page 520)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio Range**: Folio 0524 corresponds to Page 520 of Heath's *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III.
- **Section**: Appendix, Section II: "Note on the So-Called 'Book XV.'"
- **Continuity**: Completes the sentence from page 519 ("...attributed to 'Isidorus / our great teacher.'"). Heath provides historical and philological analysis of Isidorus of Miletus, Eutocius, Kluge's dissertation, and Hultsch's Pauly-Wissowa article. It then tabulates Isidorus' geometric construction for determining the dihedral angles (face inclinations) of the regular solids.
- **Folio Transition**: Concludes page 520; subsequent pages continue additional notes or general index.

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Isidorus of Miletus**: Co-architect of Hagia Sophia (St. Sophia) with Anthemius of Tralles, c. 532 A.D. Heath quotes Eutocius' Greek formula: $\text{ὁ Μιλήσιος μηχανικὸς Ἰσίδωρος ἡμέτερος διδάσκαλος}$.
- **Authorship of Book XV**: Mentions Kluge (1891), proposing that Part 1 dates from the 3rd century (time of Pappus) and Part 3 from the 6th century (a pupil of Isidorus), while Hultsch (1907) remains unconvinced.
- **Dihedral Angle Table**:

  - Models the angle between adjacent faces by constructing an isosceles triangle whose vertical angle equals the angle of inclination.
  - Tabulates the base and equal sides for tetrahedron, octahedron, icosahedron, and dodecahedron (cube having a right-angled isosceles triangle). Cross-references figures in Eucl. XIII. 17 ($BC$ and $HX$).
