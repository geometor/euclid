# BOOK X. PROPOSITIONS.

### PROPOSITION 1.

*Two unequal magnitudes being set out, if from the greater there be subtracted a magnitude greater than its half, and from that which is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the lesser magnitude set out.*

Let <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:C">$C$</span> be two unequal magnitudes of which <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the greater :
I say that, if from <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> there be subtracted a magnitude greater than its half, and from that which is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the magnitude <span data-ref="0018.g1:C">$C$</span>.

![Book X, Proposition 1](0018.g1.svg)

For <span data-ref="0018.g1:C">$C$</span> if multiplied will sometime be greater than <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [cf. v. Def. 4]
Let it be multiplied, and let <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be a multiple of <span data-ref="0018.g1:C">$C$</span>, and greater than <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
let <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be divided into the parts <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0018.g1:C">$C$</span>,
from <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let there be subtracted <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span> greater than its half,
and, from <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> greater than its half,
and let this process be repeated continually until the divisions in <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal in multitude with the divisions in <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

Let, then, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> be divisions which are equal in multitude with <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>.

Now, since <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and from <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> there has been subtracted <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> less than its half,
and, from <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span> greater than its half,
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is greater than the remainder <span data-ref="0018.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>.
