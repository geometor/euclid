BOOK X. PROPOSITIONS.
=====================

PROPOSITION 1.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Two unequal magnitudes being set out, if from the greater there be subtracted a magnitude greater than its half, and from that which is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the lesser magnitude set out.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>` be two unequal magnitudes of which :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` is
| the greater :
| I say that, if from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` there be
| subtracted a magnitude greater
| than its half, and from that which
| is left a magnitude greater than its half, and if this process be
| repeated continually, there will be left some magnitude which
| will be less than the magnitude :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0018.g1.svg
   :alt: Book X, Proposition 1

| For :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>` if multiplied will sometime be greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`.
| :just:`[cf. v. Def. 4]`

| Let it be multiplied, and let :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` be a multiple of :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`, and
| greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`;
| let :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` be divided into the parts :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0018.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0018.g1:/ G E />` equal to :g:`$C$ <0018.g1:C>`,
| from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` let there be subtracted :g:`$BH$ <0018.g1:/ B H />` greater than its half,
| and, from :g:`$AH$ <0018.g1:/ A H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0018.g1:/ H K />` greater than its half,
| and let this process be repeated continually until the divisions
| in :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />` are equal in multitude with the divisions in :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />`.

| Let, then, :g:`$AK$ <0018.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KH$ <0018.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HB$ <0018.g1:/ H B />` be divisions which are equal in
| multitude with :g:`$DF$ <0018.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0018.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0018.g1:/ G E />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` is greater than :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`,
   | and from :g:`$DE$ <0018.g1:/ D E />` there has been subtracted :g:`$EG$ <0018.g1:/ E G />` less than its
   | half,
   | and, from :g:`$AB$ <0018.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0018.g1:/ B H />` greater than its half,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0018.g1:/ G D />` is greater than the remainder :g:`$HA$ <0018.g1:/ H A />`.
