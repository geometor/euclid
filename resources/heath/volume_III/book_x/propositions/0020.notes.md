## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Classical Sources**: Aristotle *Physics* VIII. 10 (266 b 2) and III. 7 (207 b 10). Heath notes that calling it "Archimedes' Axiom" is historically misleading as Archimedes never claimed discovery and the principle was pre-Eudoxian.
- **Modern Foundations**: Otto Stolz's proof (cited via Giuseppe Vitali in Federigo Enriques' *Questioni riguardanti le matematiche elementari*, I, pp. 129–130) showing that the Archimedean property is a logical consequence of Dedekind completeness.
