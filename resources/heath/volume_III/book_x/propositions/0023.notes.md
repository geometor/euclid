## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Historiography of Incommensurables**: Heath challenges Allman's view that the golden section preceded $\sqrt{2}$ as the original discovery of incommensurability, asserting that the side and diagonal of a square was the primary discovery.
- **Theaetetus & XIII. 6**: Connects the segments of a rational line cut in extreme and mean ratio to *apotomes* ($\alpha\pi\mathrm{o}\tau\mathrm{o}\mu\alpha\acute{\iota}$).
- **Chrystal's Geometric Anthyphairesis**: Geometric infinite descent showing $a_{k+1} = d_k - a_k$ and $d_{k+1} = 2a_k - d_k$, constructing successively smaller squares whose sides and diagonals would all be integers of the same unit.
