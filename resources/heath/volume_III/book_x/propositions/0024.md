### PROPOSITION 3.

<p class="enunciation">Given two commensurable magnitudes, to find their greatest<br />
common measure.</p>

Let the two given commensurable magnitudes be <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span><br />
of which <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the less ;<br />
thus it is required to find the greatest common measure of<br />
<span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Now the magnitude <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> either measures <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> or it does<br />
not.

<p class="assertion">If then it measures it—and it measures itself also—<span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is<br />
a common measure of <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">And it is manifest that it is also the greatest ;<br />
for a greater magnitude than the magnitude <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> will not<br />
measure <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.</p>

![Book X, Proposition 3](0024.g1.svg)

Next, let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> not measure <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.<br />
Then, if the less be continually subtracted in turn from<br />
the greater, that which is left over will sometime measure<br />
the one before it, because <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> are not incommensurable ; <span class="justification">[cf. x. 2]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, measuring <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, leave <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span> less than itself,<br />
let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ E C /">$EC$</span>, measuring <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, leave <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> less than itself,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> measure <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since, then, <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> measures <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> measures <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> will also measure <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it measures itself also ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> will also measure the whole <span data-ref="0024.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.</p>
