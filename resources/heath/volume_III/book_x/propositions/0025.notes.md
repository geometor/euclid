## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Reductio phraseology**: "measure the remainder AF, the greater the less: which is impossible" corresponds to the classical Greek formulation τὸ μεῖζον τὸ ἔλασσον, an axiomatic impossibility in Euclidean magnitude theory where a part cannot measure a whole that is smaller than it.
- **Structural role of the Porism**: The Porism ("if a magnitude measure two magnitudes, it will also measure their greatest common measure") is fundamental for the associative extension to three or more magnitudes in Proposition 4.
- **Heath's Division Display**: The division schema is set in the 19th/early-20th century British long division layout $b) a (p$, displaying successive quotients $p, q, r$ and remainders $c, d$, terminating when $rd = c$.
