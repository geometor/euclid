----

PROPOSITION 4.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Given three commensurable magnitudes, to find their greatest common measure.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>` be the three given commensurable magnitudes ;
| thus it is required to find the greatest
| common measure of :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`.

.. picture:: 0026.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4 diagram

| Let the greatest common measure
| of the two magnitudes :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>` be taken,
| and let it be :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` ; :just:`[X. 3] <X.3>`
| then :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` either measures :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`, or does
| not measure it.

First, let it measure it.

.. subclause::

   | Since then :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` measures :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`,
   | while it also measures :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` is a common measure of :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`.

.. assertion::

   | And it is manifest that it is also the greatest ;
   | for a greater magnitude than the magnitude :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` does not
   | measure :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`.

Next, let :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` not measure :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`.

I say first that :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` are commensurable.

.. subclause::

   | For, since :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>` are commensurable,
   | some magnitude will measure them,
   | and this will of course measure :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>` also ;

.. assertion::

   | so that it will also measure the greatest common measure of
   | :g:`$A$ <0026.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0026.g1:B>`, namely :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>`. :just:`[X. 3, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But it also measures :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   | so that the said magnitude will measure :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` ;
   | therefore :g:`$C$ <0026.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0026.g1:D>` are commensurable.
