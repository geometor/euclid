## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Foundational Hiatus**: Heath underscores that Euclid treats a mixed proportion (magnitude to magnitude as number to number) without proving that numerical equimultiples satisfy Eudoxus's definition of proportion (V. Def. 5). Heath reproduces Simson's proof from Book V.
- **Identified Erratum in Print**: In line 38 of the page, the text reads:
  `Therefore, according as pa >=< qb, pa >=< qd.`
  This is an indisputable misprint in Heath's edition for `pc >=< qd`, since the equations immediately above define $pa = pmb$ and $pc = pmd$, and the concluding sentence immediately below refers to $pa, pc$ and $qb, qd$. Transcribed faithfully with footnote `[^25_1]`.
- **Trichotomy Notation**: The stacked comparison symbols `> = <` in Heath represent trichotomy ($\gtreqless$), which we format via `\gtreqless`.
