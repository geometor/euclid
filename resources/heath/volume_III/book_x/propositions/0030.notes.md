- **Proof Structure**: Construct $C = A/D$ and $F = E \cdot C$. By proportion, $A : F = D : E = A : B$. Hence by Book V Proposition 9, $B = F$. Since $C$ measures $F$, $C$ measures $B$, so $C$ is a common measure of $A$ and $B$.
