## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Marginal Line Numbering**: Numbering continues from page 26 with markers `35`, `40`, `45`.
- **Porism Importance in Book X**: The Porism establishes that given any straight line $a$ and numbers $m, n$:
  1. A straight line $x$ can be constructed with $a : x = m : n$.
  2. Taking $y$ as the mean proportional between $a$ and $x$, $a^2 : y^2 = a : x = m : n$ (by VI. 19 Porism and V. Def. 9). This enables the geometric construction of lines whose squares have any rational ratio, a fundamental tool throughout the classification of irrationals in Book X.
