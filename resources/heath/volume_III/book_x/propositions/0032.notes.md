# Transcription Notes: Folio 0032 (Page 28)

## Summary & Folio Continuity

- **Source**: Heath, *Euclid's Elements*, Volume III, Book X, Page 28.
- **Propositions**:
  - **Proposition 7** (complete): Proof by reductio using Proposition 6 that incommensurable magnitudes cannot have the ratio of a number to a number.
  - **Proposition 8** (complete): Converse of Proposition 7, proof by reductio using Proposition 5.
  - **Proposition 9** (enunciation): Full statement of the fundamental theorem of Theaetetus relating commensurability/incommensurability in length of straight lines to the ratio of their squares being that of square numbers.
- **Continuity**:
  - **Preceding**: Folio 0031 (Page 27) concludes Proposition 6 with commentary.
  - **Succeeding**: Folio 0033 (Page 29) opens with the setting out and proof of Proposition 9 ("For let $A$, $B$ be commensurable in length;").

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Structure**: Both Propositions 7 and 8 are concise negative contrapositives / converses of Propositions 5 and 6, using indirect proofs (*reductio ad absurdum*).
- **Enunciation of Proposition 9**: Formulated in four distinct clauses:
  1. $A \commensurablesymbol B \implies A^2 : B^2 = m^2 : n^2$.
  2. $A^2 : B^2 = m^2 : n^2 \implies A \commensurablesymbol B$.
  3. $A \incommensurablesymbol B \implies A^2 : B^2 \neq m^2 : n^2$.
  4. $A^2 : B^2 \neq m^2 : n^2 \implies A \incommensurablesymbol B$.
- **Line Numbers**: No marginal line numbering is present in Heath's printed text for these propositions.
