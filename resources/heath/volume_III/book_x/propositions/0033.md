For let <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> be commensurable in length ;<br />
I say that the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span><br />
has to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> the<br />
ratio which a square number<br />
has to a square number.

![Proposition 9 diagram](0033.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> has to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio which a number has to a<br />
number. <span class="justification">[X. 5]</span></p>

Let it have to it the ratio which <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> has to <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then, as <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
while the ratio of the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> is<br />
duplicate of the ratio of <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
for similar figures are in the duplicate ratio of their corre-<br />
sponding sides ; <span class="justification">[VI. 20, Por.]</span><br />
and the ratio of the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span> is duplicate<br />
of the ratio of <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
for between two square numbers there is one mean proportional<br />
number, and the square number has to the square number the<br />
ratio duplicate of that which the side has to the side ; <span class="justification">[VIII. 11]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>, so<br />
is the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>.</p>

Next, as the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>, so let<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> be to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span> ;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For since, as the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
while the ratio of the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> is<br />
duplicate of the ratio of <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>,<br />
and the ratio of the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span> is duplicate<br />
of the ratio of <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> has to <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> the ratio which the number <span data-ref="0033.g1:C">$C$</span> has<br />
to the number <span data-ref="0033.g1:D">$D$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 6]</span></p>

Next, let <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> be incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> ;<br />
I say that the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:A">$A$</span> has not to the square on <span data-ref="0033.g1:B">$B$</span> the<br />
ratio which a square number has to a square number.
