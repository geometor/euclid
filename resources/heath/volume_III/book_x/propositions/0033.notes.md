## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Key Theorems Invoked**:
  - Proposition X. 5: straight lines commensurable in length have to one another the ratio of a number to a number.
  - Proposition VI. 20, Porism: similar figures are in the duplicate ratio of corresponding sides ($A^2 : B^2$ is duplicate of $A : B$).
  - Proposition VIII. 11: between two square numbers there is one mean proportional number, and the ratio of square numbers is duplicate of the ratio of their sides ($C^2 : D^2$ is duplicate of $C : D$).
  - Proposition X. 6: straight lines having the ratio of a number to a number are commensurable in length.
- **Mathematical Significance**: Bridges geometry (ratios of squares on straight lines) with arithmetic (Book VIII theory of square numbers and duplicate ratios).
