## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Theaetetus Attribution**: Heath cites *Schol. x. No. 62* confirming that Proposition 9 was the discovery of Theaetetus of Athens, matching Plato's account in the *Theaetetus* (147 d–148 b).
- **The Porism**: Notes that lines commensurable in length are always commensurable in square, but lines commensurable in square are not always commensurable in length.
- **The Lemma**: Bracketed by Heiberg as an interpolation meant to serve as a lemma for the spurious Proposition 10; invokes Book VIII Proposition 26 and its converse regarding similar plane numbers.
- **Typography**: The text of the Lemma is set in roman with standard bracketed citations. Heath's commentary transitions below the horizontal rule.
