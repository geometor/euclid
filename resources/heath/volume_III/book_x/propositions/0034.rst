| A scholium to this proposition (Schol. x. No. 62) says categorically that
| the theorem proved in it was the discovery of Theaetetus.

If $a$, $b$ be straight lines, and

$$a : b = m : n,$$

where $m$, $n$ are numbers,
then

$$a^2 : b^2 = m^2 : n^2;$$

and conversely.
