## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Critical Analysis of Euclid's Foundations**:
  - Heath highlights Euclid's tacit assumption: $A : B = C : D \implies duplicate(A : B) = duplicate(C : D)$, and the much harder converse: $duplicate(A : B) = duplicate(C : D) \implies A : B = C : D$. Neither is formally proved as a general proposition in Book V.
  - References note on VI. 22 for rigorous proofs of both lemmas using V. 22.
- **Similar Plane Numbers**: General algebraic forms $pm \cdot pn$ and $qm \cdot qn$, or $mnp^2$ and $mnq^2$.
- **Spurious Nature of Proposition 10**: Marked by opening bracket `[` before `PROPOSITION 10.` indicating it is rejected by Heiberg.
