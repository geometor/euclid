## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Philological & Logical Arguments against Proposition 10**:
  - Circular / Forward Dependency: The proof of X. 10 relies directly on X. 11 ("therefore the square on $A$ is also incommensurable with the square on $E$; [x. 11]"). Euclid never uses a later proposition to prove an earlier one.
  - David Gregory attempted to place X. 10 after X. 11, but this leaves the preceding Lemma disconnected.
  - Linguistic Marker: The phrase ἐμάθομεν γάρ (*emáthomen gár*, "for we have learnt") is uncharacteristic of Euclid's austere style, indicating an interpolator/commentator.
