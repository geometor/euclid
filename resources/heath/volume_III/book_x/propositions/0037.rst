| The argument of X. 10 is simple. If $a$ be a given straight line and $m$, $n$
| numbers which have not to one another the ratio of square to square, take $x$
| such that

$$a^2 : x^2 = m : n, \qquad \text{[X. 6, Por.]}$$

whence $a$, $x$ are incommensurable in length. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

Then take $y$ a mean proportional between $a$, $x$, whence

$$a^2 : y^2 = a : x \qquad \text{[V. Def. 9]}$$

$$[= \sqrt{m} : \sqrt{n}],$$

| and $x$ is incommensurable in length only, while $y$ is incommensurable in
| square as well as in length, with $a$.

PROPOSITION 11.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If four magnitudes be proportional, and the first be com- mensurable with the second, the third will also be commensurable with the fourth ; and, if the first be incommensurable with the second, the third will also be incommensurable with the fourth.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>` be four magnitudes in proportion, so
| that, as :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>`
| to :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>`,
| and let :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` be commensurable
| with :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>` ;
| I say that :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>` will also be commensurable with :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0037.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 11 diagram

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` is commensurable with :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` has to :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>` the ratio which a number has to a
   | number. :just:`[X. 5] <X.5>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>` also has to :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>` the ratio which a number has to a
   | number ;
   | therefore :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>` is commensurable with :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

| Next, let :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` be incommensurable with :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>` ;
| I say that :g:`$C$ <0037.g1:C>` will also be incommensurable with :g:`$D$ <0037.g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` is incommensurable with :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0037.g1:A>` has not to :g:`$B$ <0037.g1:B>` the ratio which a number has to a
   | number. :just:`[X. 7] <X.7>`
