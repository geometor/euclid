| Let :g:`$AD$ <0040.g2:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0040.g2:/ D B />` be the given two straight lines, and let it be
| required to find the straight line the square on which is equal

| Let them be placed so as to contain a right angle, that
| formed by :g:`$AD$ <0040.g2:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0040.g2:/ D B />`;
| and let :g:`$AB$ <0040.g2:/ A B />` be joined.

   | It is again manifest that the straight line the square on
   | which is equal to the sum of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0040.g2:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0040.g2:/ D B />` is :g:`$AB$ <0040.g2:/ A B />`.

PROPOSITION 14.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, and the square on the first be greater than the square on the second by the square on a straight line commensurable with the first, the square on the third will also be greater than the square on the fourth by :line:`5` the square on a straight line commensurable with the third.

.. enunciation::

   And, if the square on the first be greater than the square on the second by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the first, the square on the third will also be greater than the square on the fourth by the square on a straight line in- :line:`10` commensurable with the third.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>` be four straight lines in proportion, so
| that, as :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>`;
| and let the square on :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` be greater than
| the square on :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>` by the square on :g:`$E$ <0041.g1:E>`, and
| :line:`15` let the square on :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` be greater than the
| square on :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>` by the square on :g:`$F$ <0041.g1:F>`;
| I say that, if :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` is commensurable with :g:`$E$ <0041.g1:E>`,
| :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` is also commensurable with :g:`$F$ <0041.g1:F>`,
| and, if :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` is incommensurable with :g:`$E$ <0041.g1:E>`, :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` is
| :line:`20` also incommensurable with :g:`$F$ <0041.g1:F>`.

.. picture:: 0041.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 14 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` is to :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>`, so is :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` to :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the square on :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>` is to the square on :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>`, so is
   | the square on :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>` to the square on :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>`. :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>`

.. subclause::

   | But the squares on :g:`$E$ <0041.g1:E>`, :g:`$B$ <0041.g1:B>` are equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0041.g1:A>`,
   | :line:`25` and the squares on :g:`$D$ <0041.g1:D>`, :g:`$F$ <0041.g1:F>` are equal to the square on :g:`$C$ <0041.g1:C>`.
