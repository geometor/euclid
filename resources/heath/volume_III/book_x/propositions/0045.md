### LEMMA.

<p class="enunciation">If to any straight line there be applied a parallelogram<br />
deficient by a square figure, the applied parallelogram is equal<br />
to the rectangle contained by the segments of the straight line<br />
resulting from the application.</p>

For let there be applied to the straight line <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> the<br />
parallelogram <span data-ref="0045.g1:< A C D E >">$AD$</span> deficient by the<br />
square figure <span data-ref="0045.g1:< C B F D >">$DB$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0045.g1:< A C D E >">$AD$</span> is equal to the rectangle<br />
contained by <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

![Lemma Diagram](0045.g1.svg)

This is indeed at once manifest;

<p class="sub-clause">for, since <span data-ref="0045.g1:< C B F D >">$DB$</span> is a square,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0045.g1:/ C D /">$DC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">and <span data-ref="0045.g1:< A C D E >">$AD$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, that is, the rectangle <span data-ref="0045.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0045.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.</p>

If $a$ be the given straight line, and $x$ the side of the square by which the<br />
applied rectangle is to be deficient, the rectangle is equal to $ax - x^2$, which is<br />
of course equal to $x(a - x)$. The rectangle may be written $xy$, where<br />
$x + y = a$. Given the area $x(a - x)$, or $xy$ (where $x + y = a$), two different<br />
*applications* will give rectangles equal to this area, the sides of the defect<br />
being $x$ or $a - x$ ($x$ or $y$) respectively; but the second mode of expression<br />
shows that the rectangles do not differ in form but only in position.
