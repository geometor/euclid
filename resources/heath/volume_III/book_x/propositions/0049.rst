| It is often more convenient to use the symmetrical form of equation in
| this and similar cases, viz.

$$\left.\begin{aligned} xy &= \frac{b^2}{4} \\ x + y &= a \end{aligned}\right\}$$

| The result with this mode of expression is that
| (1) if $x \mathrel{⌢} y$, then $a \mathrel{⌢} \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$; and
| (2) if $a \mathrel{⌢} \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$, then $x \mathrel{⌢} y$.
| The truth of the proposition is even easier to see in this case, since
| $(x - y)^2 = (a^2 - b^2)$.

PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be two unequal straight lines, and to the greater there be applied a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, and if it divide it into parts which are incommensurable, the square :line:`5` on the greater will be greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the greater.

.. enunciation::

   And, if the square on the greater be greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the greater, and if there be applied to the greater a :line:`10` parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into parts which are incommensurable.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0049.g1:A>`, :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` be two unequal straight lines, of which :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` is
| the greater,
| and to :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` let there be applied a parallelogram equal
| to the fourth part of the square on the less, :g:`$A$ <0049.g1:A>`, and
| deficient by a square figure. Let this be the rect-
| angle :g:`$BD$ <0049.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DC$ <0049.g1:/ D C />`, :just:`[cf. Lemma before X. 17]`
| and let :g:`$BD$ <0049.g1:/ B D />` be incommensurable in length with :g:`$DC$ <0049.g1:/ D C />`;
| I say that the square on :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` is greater than the
| square on :g:`$A$ <0049.g1:A>` by the square on a straight line incom-
| mensurable with :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />`.

.. picture:: 0049.g1.svg

| For, with the same construction as before, we can prove
| similarly that the square on :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` is greater than the square on
| :g:`$A$ <0049.g1:A>` by the square on :g:`$FD$ <0049.g1:/ F D />`.

| It is to be proved that :g:`$BC$ <0049.g1:/ B C />` is incommensurable in length
| with :g:`$FD$ <0049.g1:/ F D />`.
