[Lemma.
Since it has been proved that straight lines commen-<br />
surable in length are always commensurable in square also,<br />
while those commensurable in square are not always com-<br />
mensurable in length also, but can of course be either<br />
commensurable or incommensurable in length, it is manifest<br />
that, if any straight line be commensurable in length with a<br />
given rational straight line, it is called rational and commen-<br />
surable with the other not only in length but in square also,<br />
since straight lines commensurable in length are always<br />
commensurable in square also.

But, if any straight line be commensurable in square with<br />
a given rational straight line, then, if it is also commensurable<br />
in length with it, it is called in this case also rational and<br />
commensurable with it both in length and in square; but, if<br />
again any straight line, being commensurable in square with a<br />
given rational straight line, be incommensurable in length<br />
with it, it is called in this case also rational but commensurable<br />
in square only.]

### PROPOSITION 19.

<p class="enunciation">The rectangle contained by rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in length is rational.</p>

For let the rectangle <span data-ref="0051.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be contained by the rational<br />
straight lines <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> commensurable in<br />
length;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0051.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is rational.

![Proposition 19](0051.g1.svg)

For on <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let the square <span data-ref="0051.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> be de-<br />
scribed;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0051.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is rational. <span class="justification">[X. Def. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable in<br />
length with <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0051.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.</p>
