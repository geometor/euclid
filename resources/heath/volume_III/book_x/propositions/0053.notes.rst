2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 20**: The converse of Proposition 19—applying a rational area to a rational straight line produces a breadth commensurable in length with the base.

  - Text preserves the phrase "once more rational in any of the aforesaid ways" in the setting-out, which Heath discussed on Page 48.

- **Commentary on Proposition 20**: Expresses the division algebraically: $k\rho^2 / \sigma = (k\rho^2/\sigma^2)\sigma = (m/n)k\sigma = k'\sigma$.
- **Proposition 21 (Introduction of the Medial)**: Defines the *medial* (*μέση*) straight line and medial area: the rectangle contained by rational lines commensurable in square only is irrational, and its side ($\sqrt{ab}$) is called medial.
