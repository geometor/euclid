### PROPOSITION 22.

<p class="enunciation">The square on a medial straight line, if applied to a<br />
rational straight line, produces as breadth a straight line<br />
rational and incommensurable in length with that to which it<br />
is applied.</p>

![Proposition 22](0055.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0055.g1:A">$A$</span> be medial and <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> rational,<br />
and let a rectangular area <span data-ref="0055.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0055.g1:A">$A$</span> be<br />
applied to <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> as<br />
breadth;

I say that <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is rational and incom-<br />
mensurable in length with <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0055.g1:A">$A$</span> is medial, the square<br />
on it is equal to a rectangular area<br />
contained by rational straight lines<br />
commensurable in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

Let the square on it be equal to <span data-ref="0055.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on it is also equal to <span data-ref="0055.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0055.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0055.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it is also equiangular with it;<br />
and in equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about<br />
the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; <span class="justification">[VI. 14]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, proportionally, as <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0055.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0055.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 22]</span></p>
