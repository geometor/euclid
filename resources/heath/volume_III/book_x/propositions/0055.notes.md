## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proportional Application**: Proposition 22 applies the square on a medial line $A^2$ to a rational straight line $CB$ to produce breadth $CD$. Since $A^2 = \sqrt{k}\rho^2$ and $CB = \sigma$, breadth $CD = \frac{\sqrt{k}\rho^2}{\sigma}$, which is proved to be rational and incommensurable in length with $\sigma$.
