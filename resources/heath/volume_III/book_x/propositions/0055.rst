PROPOSITION 22.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   The square on a medial straight line, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a straight line rational and incommensurable in length with that to which it is applied.

.. picture:: 0055.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 22

| Let :g:`$A$ <0055.g1:A>` be medial and :g:`$CB$ <0055.g1:/ C B />` rational,
| and let a rectangular area :g:`$BD$ <0055.g1:< B D >>` equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0055.g1:A>` be
| applied to :g:`$BC$ <0055.g1:/ B C />`, producing :g:`$CD$ <0055.g1:/ C D />` as
| breadth;

| I say that :g:`$CD$ <0055.g1:/ C D />` is rational and incom-
| mensurable in length with :g:`$CB$ <0055.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   | For, since :g:`$A$ <0055.g1:A>` is medial, the square
   | on it is equal to a rectangular area
   | contained by rational straight lines
   | commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

Let the square on it be equal to :g:`$GF$ <0055.g1:< G F >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on it is also equal to :g:`$BD$ <0055.g1:< B D >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BD$ <0055.g1:< B D >>` is equal to :g:`$GF$ <0055.g1:< G F >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But it is also equiangular with it;
   | and in equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about
   | the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; :just:`[VI. 14] <VI.14>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$BC$ <0055.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0055.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0055.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$CD$ <0055.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore also, as the square on :g:`$BC$ <0055.g1:/ B C />` is to the square on
   | :g:`$EG$ <0055.g1:/ E G />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0055.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$CD$ <0055.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>`
