## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Notation for Commensurability and Incommensurability**:
  - Heath uses $\sim$ for commensurable and $\smile$ for incommensurable:
    - $\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho \sim \rho$ denotes commensurability in square.
    - $\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho \smile \rho$ denotes incommensurability in length.
    - $k\rho^2 \smile \sqrt{k}\cdot\rho^2$ shows area incommensurability.
    - $x \smile \sigma$ concludes incommensurability in length.
- **Proposition 23 Deduction**:
  - Applies $A^2$ to rational $CD$ giving breadth $ED$ (rational and incommensurable with $CD$ by X. 22).
  - Applies $B^2$ to $CD$ giving breadth $DF$.
  - Uses proportionality of areas to breadths on the same base $CD$ (VI. 1) and transitivity of commensurability (X. 11, X. 13).
