(2) Since $\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho \sim \rho$, $\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho \smile \rho$.

But [Lemma]

$$\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho : \rho = k\rho^2 : \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2,$$

whence

$$k\rho^2 \smile \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2. \qquad \text{[x. 11]}$$

But $\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2 = \sigma x$, and $k\rho^2 \sim x^2$ (from above);
therefore

$$x^2 \smile \sigma x; \qquad \text{[x. 13]}$$

and, since $x^2 : \sigma x = x : \sigma$, [Lemma]

$$x \smile \sigma.$$

PROPOSITION 23.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with a medial straight line is medial.

.. picture:: 0057.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 23

| Let :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>` be medial, and let :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>` be commensurable with :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>`;
| I say that :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>` is also medial.

| For let a rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0057.g1:/ C D />`
| be set out,
| and to :g:`$CD$ <0057.g1:/ C D />` let the rectangular area :g:`$CE$ <0057.g1:< C E >>`
| equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>` be applied,
| producing :g:`$ED$ <0057.g1:/ E D />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$ED$ <0057.g1:/ E D />` is rational and incommen-
   | surable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0057.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

| And let the rectangular area :g:`$CF$ <0057.g1:< C F >>`
| equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>` be applied to
| :g:`$CD$ <0057.g1:/ C D />`, producing :g:`$DF$ <0057.g1:/ D F />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>` is commensurable with :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>` is also commensurable with the square on :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$EC$ <0057.g1:< E C >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0057.g1:A>`,
   | and :g:`$CF$ <0057.g1:< C F >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$B$ <0057.g1:B>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EC$ <0057.g1:< E C >>` is commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0057.g1:< C F >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   And, as :g:`$EC$ <0057.g1:< E C >>` is to :g:`$CF$ <0057.g1:< C F >>`, so is :g:`$ED$ <0057.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0057.g1:/ D F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$ED$ <0057.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$DF$ <0057.g1:/ D F />`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$ED$ <0057.g1:/ E D />` is rational and incommensurable in length with
   | :g:`$DC$ <0057.g1:/ D C />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$DF$ <0057.g1:/ D F />` is also rational :just:`[X. Def. 3] <X.def.3>` and incommensurable
   | in length with :g:`$DC$ <0057.g1:/ D C />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0057.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0057.g1:/ D F />` are rational and commensurable in
   | square only.
