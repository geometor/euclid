But $x \sim \lambda x$, so that $x^2 \sim x \cdot \lambda x$. [x. 11]
Now $x^2$ is medial [x. 21];

We may of course write two medial straight lines commensurable in length in the forms $m k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$, $n k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$; and these may either be $m \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, $n \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, or $m \sqrt[4]{AB}$, $n \sqrt[4]{AB}$.

### Proposition 25.

*The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in square only is either rational or medial.*

![Proposition 25](0060.g1.svg)

For let the rectangle <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be contained by the medial straight lines <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> which are commensurable in square only;
I say that <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is either rational or medial.

For on <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> let the squares <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> be described;
therefore each of the squares <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> is medial.
Let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out,
to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> as breadth,
to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0060.g1:< M K >">$MK$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> as breadth,
and further to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> let there be similarly applied <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> are in a straight line.
Since then each of the squares <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> is medial,
and <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>,
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span> is also medial.
And they are applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [x. 22]
And, since <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>.
And, as <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>. [x. 11]
