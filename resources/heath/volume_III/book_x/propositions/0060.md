But $x \frown \lambda x$, so that $x^2 \frown x \cdot \lambda x$. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span><br />
Now $x^2$ is medial <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span>;<br />
therefore $x \cdot \lambda x$ is also medial. <span class="justification">[X. 23, Por.]</span>

We may of course write two medial straight lines commensurable in length<br />
in the forms $m k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$, $n k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$; and these may either be $m \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, $n \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, or<br />
$m \sqrt[4]{AB}$, $n \sqrt[4]{AB}$.

### Proposition 25.

<p class="enunciation">The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only is either rational or medial.</p>

![Proposition 25](0060.g1.svg)

For let the rectangle <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be contained by the medial<br />
straight lines <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> which are<br />
commensurable in square only;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is either rational<br />
or medial.

For on <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> let the<br />
squares <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> be described;
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the squares<br />
<span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> is medial.</p>

Let a rational straight line<br />
<span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out,<br />
to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> as breadth,<br />
to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0060.g1:< M K >">$MK$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> as breadth,<br />
and further to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> let there be similarly applied <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span> equal to<br />
<span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> as breadth;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> are in a straight line.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then each of the squares <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> is medial,<br />
and <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is rational and<br />
incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0060.g1:< A D >">$AD$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0060.g1:< B E >">$BE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0060.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0060.g1:< N L >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> to <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0060.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>
