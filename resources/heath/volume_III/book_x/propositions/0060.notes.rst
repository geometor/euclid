Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Proposition 25 Fundamental Result**: Shows that the product of two medial straight lines commensurable in square only can be *either rational or medial*.
- **Application of Areas Technique**:

  - Construction of three consecutive parallelograms on common breadth $FG$:

    - $GH = AD = AB^2$ with breadth $FH$.
    - $MK = AC = AB \cdot BC$ with breadth $HK$.

  - By X. 22, $FH$ and $KL$ are rational and incommensurable in length with $FG$.
  - By VI. 1 and X. 11, $FH$ is commensurable in length with $KL$.
