But $x \frown \lambda x$, so that $x^2 \frown x \cdot \lambda x$. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>` Now $x^2$ is medial :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`; therefore $x \cdot \lambda x$ is also medial. :just:`[X. 23, Por.]`

We may of course write two medial straight lines commensurable in length in the forms $m k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$, $n k^{\frac{1}{4}} \rho$; and these may either be $m \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, $n \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}$, or $m \sqrt[4]{AB}$, $n \sqrt[4]{AB}$.

PROPOSITION 25.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in square only is either rational or medial.

.. picture:: 0060.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 25

For let the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` be contained by the medial straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />` which are commensurable in square only; I say that :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>` is either rational or medial.

For on :g:`$AB$ <0060.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0060.g1:/ B C />` let the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` be described;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` is medial.

Let a rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />` be set out, to :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />` let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` :eq:`equal <0060.g1:< G H > = 0060.g1:< A D >>` to :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, producing :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` as breadth, to :g:`$HM$ <0060.g1:/ H M />` let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram :g:`$MK$ <0060.g1:< M K >>` :eq:`equal <0060.g1:< M K > = 0060.g1:< A C >>` to :g:`$AC$ <0060.g1:< A C >>`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />` as breadth, and further to :g:`$KN$ <0060.g1:/ K N />` let there be similarly applied :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>` :eq:`equal <0060.g1:< N L > = 0060.g1:< B E >>` to :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>`, producing :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0060.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` are in a straight line.

.. subclause::

   Since then each of the squares :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>`, :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` is medial, and :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>` is :eq:`equal <0060.g1:< A D > = 0060.g1:< G H >; 0060.g1:< B E > = 0060.g1:< N L >>` to :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>`, and :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>` to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0060.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AD$ <0060.g1:< A D >>` is commensurable with :g:`$BE$ <0060.g1:< B E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   And, as :g:`$GH$ <0060.g1:< G H >>` is to :g:`$NL$ <0060.g1:< N L >>`, so is :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` to :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0060.g1:/ F H />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$KL$ <0060.g1:/ K L />`.
