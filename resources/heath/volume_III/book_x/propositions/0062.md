### PROPOSITION 26.

*A medial area does not exceed a medial area by a rational
area.*

For, if possible, let the medial area <span data-ref="0062.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> exceed the medial
area <span data-ref="0062.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> by the rational area
<span data-ref="0062.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span>,
and let a rational straight line
<span data-ref="0062.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set out;
to <span data-ref="0062.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> let there be applied the
rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0062.g1:< F H >">$FH$</span>
equal to <span data-ref="0062.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0062.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> as
breadth,
and let the rectangle <span data-ref="0062.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0062.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be subtracted;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0062.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0062.g1:< K H >">$KH$</span>.
