<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0064.g1:B">$B$</span> is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0064.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0064.g1:D">$D$</span> is also rational.</p>

---

Euclid takes two rational straight lines commensurable in square only, say<br />
$\rho$, $k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$.<br />
Find the mean proportional, i.e. $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$.<br />
Take $x$ such that
$$\rho : k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho = k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho : x \ \text{.........................................(1).}$$
This gives $x = k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho$,<br />
and the lines required are $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$, $k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho$.<br />
For ($\alpha$) $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ is medial.<br />
And ($\beta$), by (1), since $\rho \sim k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$,
$$k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho \sim k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho,$$
whence [addition to X. 23], since $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ is medial,
$$k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho \text{ is also medial.}$$

The medial straight lines thus found may take either of the forms
$$(1) \quad \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}, \quad \sqrt{\frac{B\sqrt{B}}{a}} \quad \text{or} \quad (2) \quad \sqrt[4]{AB}, \quad \sqrt{B\sqrt{\frac{B}{A}}}.$$

### PROPOSITION 28.

<p class="enunciation">To find medial straight lines commensurable in square only<br />
which contain a medial rectangle.</p>

Let the rational straight lines <span data-ref="0065.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:C">$C$</span> commensurable in<br />
square only be set out;<br />
let <span data-ref="0065.g1:D">$D$</span> be taken a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0065.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 13]</span><br />
and let it be contrived that,<br />
as <span data-ref="0065.g1:B">$B$</span> is to <span data-ref="0065.g1:C">$C$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0065.g1:D">$D$</span> to <span data-ref="0065.g1:E">$E$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 12]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0065.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0065.g1:B">$B$</span>, that is, the square on <span data-ref="0065.g1:D">$D$</span> <span class="justification">[VI. 17]</span>,<br />
