## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 27 Conclusion**: Concludes that rectangle $C, D$ is equal to $B^2$, which is rational.
- **Commentary on Proposition 27**: Heath shows that with base lines $\rho, k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$, the mean proportional is $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ and the fourth proportional is $k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho$. Their product is $k\rho^2$ (rational), and both are medial lines commensurable in square only. He details algebraic expressions for cases when the given lines are $a, \sqrt{B}$ or $\sqrt{A}, \sqrt{B}$.
- **Proposition 28 Enunciation**: "To find medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a medial rectangle." Uses three rational lines $A, B, C$ commensurable in square only.
