.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$B$ <0064.g1:B>` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$C$ <0064.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0064.g1:D>` is also rational.

.. qed::

   | Therefore medial straight lines commensurable in square
   | only have been found which contain a rational rectangle. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| Euclid takes two rational straight lines commensurable in square only, say
| $\rho$, $k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$.
| Find the mean proportional, i.e. $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$.
| Take $x$ such that

$$\rho : k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho = k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho : x \ \text{.........................................(1).}$$

| This gives $x = k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho$,
| and the lines required are $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$, $k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho$.
| For ($\alpha$) $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ is medial.
| And ($\beta$), by (1), since $\rho \sim k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$,

$$k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho \sim k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho,$$

whence [addition to X. 23], since $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ is medial,

$$k^{\frac{3}{4}}\rho \text{ is also medial.}$$

The medial straight lines thus found may take either of the forms

$$(1) \quad \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}, \quad \sqrt{\frac{B\sqrt{B}}{a}} \quad \text{or} \quad (2) \quad \sqrt[4]{AB}, \quad \sqrt{B\sqrt{\frac{B}{A}}}.$$

----

PROPOSITION 28.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To find medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a medial rectangle.

| Let the rational straight lines :g:`$A$ <0065.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0065.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0065.g1:C>` commensurable in
| square only be set out;
| let :g:`$D$ <0065.g1:D>` be taken a mean proportional between :g:`$A$ <0065.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0065.g1:B>`, :just:`[VI. 13] <VI.13>`
| and let it be contrived that,
| as :g:`$B$ <0065.g1:B>` is to :g:`$C$ <0065.g1:C>`, so is :g:`$D$ <0065.g1:D>` to :g:`$E$ <0065.g1:E>`. :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. picture:: 0065.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 28 diagram

.. subclause::

   | Since :g:`$A$ <0065.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0065.g1:B>` are rational straight lines commensurable in
   | square only,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0065.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0065.g1:B>`, that is, the square on :g:`$D$ <0065.g1:D>` :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`,
   | is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`
