## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Euclidean Proof**: Proves that rectangle $D, E = A, C$ using proportion alternation and mean proportionality ($B : D = D : A \implies D : A = C : E$). Since $A, C$ are rational lines commensurable in square only, their rectangle is medial by X. 21, hence $D, E$ is medial.
- **Cross References**: `[X. 21]`, `[X. 11]`, `[X. 23, addition]`, `[V. 16]`, `[VI. 16]`.
- **Heath's Algebraic Analysis**: Identifies the three initial lines as $\rho, k^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho, \lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho$. The lines obtained are $k^{\frac{1}{4}}\rho$ and $\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho / k^{\frac{1}{4}}$. Their product is $\lambda^{\frac{1}{2}}\rho^2$, which is medial.
