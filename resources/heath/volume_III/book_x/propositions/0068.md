*To find two square numbers such that their sum is not square.*

Let the unit <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be subtracted; therefore the product of <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> is less than the square on <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

I say then that the square product of <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> together with the square on <span data-ref="0068.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> will not be square.
