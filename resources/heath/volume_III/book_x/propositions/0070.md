The number is clearly less than $mp^2 \cdot mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2}\right)^2$, i.e. less than<br />
$\left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2}\right)^2$.

If then the number is square, its side must be greater than, equal to, or<br />
less than $\left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - 1\right)$, the number next less than $\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2}$.

But (1) the side cannot be $> \left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - 1\right)$ without being equal to<br />
$\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2}$, since they are consecutive numbers.

(2) $(mp^2 - 2) mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2 = \left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2$. <span class="justification">[II. 6]</span>

If then $mp^2 \cdot mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2$ is also equal to $\left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2$,<br />
we must have
$$(mp^2 - 2) mq^2 = mp^2 \cdot mq^2,$$
or
$$mp^2 - 2 = mp^2 :$$
which is impossible.

(3) If $mp^2 \cdot mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2 < \left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2$,<br />
suppose it equal to $\left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - r\right)^2$.

But <span class="justification">[II. 6]</span> $(mp^2 - 2r) mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - r\right)^2 = \left(\frac{mp^2 + mq^2}{2} - r\right)^2$.

Therefore
$$(mp^2 - 2r) mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - r\right)^2 = mp^2 \cdot mq^2 + \left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2 :$$
which is impossible.

Hence all three hypotheses are false, and the sum of the squares<br />
$mp^2 \cdot mq^2$ and $\left(\frac{mp^2 - mq^2}{2} - 1\right)^2$ is *not* square.
