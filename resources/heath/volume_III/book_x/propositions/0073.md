In this case we take $m^2$, $n^2$ such that $m^2 + n^2$ is not square.

Find $x$ such that
$$m^2 + n^2 : m^2 = \rho^2 : x^2,$$
whence
$$x^2 = \frac{m^2}{m^2 + n^2} \rho^2,$$
or
$$x = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}, \quad \text{where } k = \frac{n}{m}.$$

Then $\rho$, $\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}$ satisfy the condition.

The proof is after the manner of the proof of the preceding proposition<br />
and need not be repeated.

According as $\rho$ is of the form $a$ or $\sqrt{A}$, the straight lines take the<br />
form (1) $a$, $\sqrt{a^2 - \frac{k^2 a^2}{1 + k^2}}$, that is, $a$, $\sqrt{a^2 - B}$, or (2) $\sqrt{A}$, $\sqrt{A - B}$ and<br />
$\sqrt{A}$, $\sqrt{A - b^2}$.

### PROPOSITION 31.

<p class="enunciation">To find two medial straight lines commensurable in square<br />
only, containing a rational rectangle, and such that the square<br />
on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the<br />
square on a straight line commensurable in length with the<br />
greater.</p>

Let there be set out two rational straight lines <span data-ref="0073.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0073.g1:B">$B$</span><br />

the square on <span data-ref="0073.g1:B">$B$</span> the less by the square on a<br />
