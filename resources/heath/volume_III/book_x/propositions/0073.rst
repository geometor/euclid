In this case we take $m^2$, $n^2$ such that $m^2 + n^2$ is not square.

Find $x$ such that

$$m^2 + n^2 : m^2 = \rho^2 : x^2,$$

whence

$$x^2 = \frac{m^2}{m^2 + n^2} \rho^2,$$

or

$$x = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}, \quad \text{where } k = \frac{n}{m}.$$

Then $\rho$, $\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}$ satisfy the condition.

| The proof is after the manner of the proof of the preceding proposition
| and need not be repeated.

| According as $\rho$ is of the form $a$ or $\sqrt{A}$, the straight lines take the
| form (1) $a$, $\sqrt{a^2 - \frac{k^2 a^2}{1 + k^2}}$, that is, $a$, $\sqrt{a^2 - B}$, or (2) $\sqrt{A}$, $\sqrt{A - B}$ and
| $\sqrt{A}$, $\sqrt{A - b^2}$.

PROPOSITION 31.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To find two medial straight lines commensurable in square only, containing a rational rectangle, and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with the greater.

| Let there be set out two rational straight lines :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0073.g1:B>`
| commensurable in square only and such that the
| square on :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, being the greater, is greater than
| the square on :g:`$B$ <0073.g1:B>` the less by the square on a
| straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`.
| :just:`[X. 29] <X.29>`

.. picture:: 0073.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 31

| And let the square on :g:`$C$ <0073.g1:C>` be equal to the
| rectangle :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0073.g1:B>`.

.. subclause:: [X. 21] <X.21>

   Now the rectangle :g:`$A$ <0073.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0073.g1:B>` is medial;
