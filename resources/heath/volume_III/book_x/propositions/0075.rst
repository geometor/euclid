| For ($\alpha$) $\rho^2\sqrt{1-k^2}$ is a medial area, and therefore $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}$ is a medial
| straight line .................................................(1);
| and $x\ .\ \rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}} = \rho^2(1-k^2)$ and is therefore a rational area.

| ($\beta$) $\rho$, $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}$, $\rho\sqrt{1-k^2}$, $x$ are straight lines in continued proportion, by
| construction.

Therefore

$$\rho : \rho\sqrt{1-k^2} = \rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}} : x \text{ ....................(2).}$$

| (This Euclid has to prove in a somewhat roundabout way by means of the
| lemma after x. 21 to the effect that $a : b = ab : b^2$.)

| From (2) it follows [x. 11] that $x \mathbin{\sim} \rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}$; whence, since $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}$ is
| medial, $x$ or $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{3}{4}}$ is medial also.

| ($\gamma$) From (2), since $\rho$, $\rho\sqrt{1-k^2}$ satisfy the remaining condition of the
| problem, $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}$, $\rho(1-k^2)^{\frac{3}{4}}$ do so also [x. 14].

According as $\rho$ is of the form $a$ or $\sqrt{A}$, the straight lines take the forms

$$\begin{array}{lcc}
(1) & \sqrt{a\sqrt{a^2-b^2}}, & \dfrac{a^2-b^2}{\sqrt{a\sqrt{a^2-b^2}}}, \\
\text{or}\quad (2) & \sqrt[4]{A(A-k^2A)}, & \dfrac{A-k^2A}{\sqrt[4]{A(A-k^2A)}}.
\end{array}$$

| II. To find medial straight lines *commensurable in square only* contain-
| ing a rational rectangle, and such that the square on one exceeds the square
| on the other by the square on a straight line *incommensurable* with the former,
| we simply begin with the rational straight lines having the corresponding
| property [x. 30], viz. $\rho$, $\dfrac{\rho}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}$, and we arrive at the straight lines

$$\dfrac{\rho}{(1+k^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}},\quad \dfrac{\rho}{(1+k^2)^{\frac{3}{4}}}.$$

| According as $\rho$ is of the form $a$ or $\sqrt{A}$, these (if we use the same
| transformation as at the end of the note on x. 30) may take any of the forms

$$\begin{array}{lcc}
(1) & \sqrt{a\sqrt{a^2-B}}, & \dfrac{a^2-B}{\sqrt{a\sqrt{a^2-B}}}, \\
\text{or}\quad (2) & \sqrt[4]{A(A-B)}, & \dfrac{A-B}{\sqrt[4]{A(A-B)}}, \\
\text{or} & \sqrt[4]{A(A-b^2)}, & \dfrac{A-b^2}{\sqrt[4]{A(A-b^2)}}.
\end{array}$$

PROPOSITION 32.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To find two medial straight lines commensurable in square only, containing a medial rectangle, and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable with the greater.
