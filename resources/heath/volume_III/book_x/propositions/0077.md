I. Euclid takes three straight lines of the form $\rho$, $\rho\sqrt{\lambda}$, $\rho\sqrt{1-k^2}$,<br />
takes the mean proportional $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$ between the first two ....................(1),<br />
and then finds $x$ such that
$$\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}} : \rho\sqrt{\lambda} = \rho\sqrt{1-k^2} : x \text{ .........................................(2),}$$
whence $x = \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\sqrt{1-k^2}$,<br />
and the straight lines $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$, $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\sqrt{1-k^2}$ satisfy the given conditions.

Now ($\alpha$) $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$ is medial.

($\beta$) We have, from (1) and (2),
$$\rho : \rho\sqrt{1-k^2} = \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}} : x \text{ .........................................(3),}$$
whence $x \mathbin{\sim} \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$ ; and $x$ is therefore medial and $\mathbin{\sim} \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$.

($\gamma$) $x\ .\ \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}} = \rho\sqrt{\lambda}\ .\ \rho\sqrt{1-k^2}$.<br />
But the latter is medial ; [x. 21]<br />
therefore $x\ .\ \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$, or $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\ .\ \rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\sqrt{1-k^2}$, is medial.

Lastly ($\delta$) $\rho$, $\rho\sqrt{1-k^2}$ have the remaining property in the enunciation ;<br />
therefore $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$, $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\sqrt{1-k^2}$ have it also. [x. 14]

(Euclid has not the assistance of symbols to prove the proportion (3) above.<br />
He therefore uses the lemmas $ab : bc = a : c$ and $d^2 : de = d : e$ to deduce from<br />
the relations
$$\left.\begin{array}{r} ab = d^2 \\ d : b = c : e \end{array}\right\}$$
and that $a : c = d : e$.)

The straight lines $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}$, $\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}\sqrt{1-k^2}$ may take any of the following forms<br />
according as the straight lines first taken are<br />
(1) $a$, $\sqrt{B}$, $\sqrt{a^2-c^2}$, (2) $\sqrt{A}$, $\sqrt{B}$, $\sqrt{A-k^2A}$, (3) $\sqrt{A}$, $b$, $\sqrt{A-k^2A}$.
$$\begin{array}{lcc}
(1) & \sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}, & \dfrac{\sqrt{B(a^2-c^2)}}{\sqrt{a\sqrt{B}}} ; \\
(2) & \sqrt[4]{AB}, & \dfrac{\sqrt{B(A-k^2A)}}{\sqrt[4]{AB}} ; \\
(3) & \sqrt{b\sqrt{A}}, & \dfrac{b\sqrt{A-k^2A}}{\sqrt{b\sqrt{A}}}.
\end{array}$$
