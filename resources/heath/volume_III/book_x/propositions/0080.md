let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0079.g1:D">$D$</span>,
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square on either of the straight lines <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> ; [vi. 28]
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0079.g1:( A F B )">$AFB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be drawn at right angles to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are unequal straight lines,
and the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
while there has been applied to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, that is, to the square on half of it, and deficient by a square figure, making the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>. [x. 18]

And, as <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>,
while the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>,
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> ;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> ;
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> are incommensurable in square.

And, since <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is rational,
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also rational ;
so that the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> is also rational. [i. 47]

And since, again, the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
and, by hypothesis, the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> ;
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>,
so that the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also commensurable with the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

But the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial ; [x. 21]
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also medial. [x. 23, Por.]
