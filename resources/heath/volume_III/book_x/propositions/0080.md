let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0079.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square<br />
on either of the straight lines <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> and deficient by a<br />
square figure, and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> ; <span class="justification">[VI. 28]</span><br />
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0079.g1:( A F B )">$AFB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be drawn at right angles to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are unequal straight lines,<br />
and the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> by<br />
the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
while there has been applied to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> a parallelogram equal to<br />
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, that is, to the square on<br />
half of it, and deficient by a square figure, making the rect-<br />
angle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 18]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>,<br />
while the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>,<br />
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> are incommensurable in square.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is rational,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also rational ;<br />
so that the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> is also rational.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, again, the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is equal to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
and, by hypothesis, the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also equal to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>,<br />
so that the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also commensurable with the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial ; <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0079.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also medial. <span class="justification">[X. 23, Por.]</span></p>
