PROPOSITION 34.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial but the rectangle contained by them rational.

Let there be set out two medial straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0082.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0082.g1:/ B C />`, commensurable in square only, such that the rectangle which they contain is rational, and the square on :g:`$AB$ <0082.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$BC$ <0082.g1:/ B C />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0082.g1:/ A B />` ; :just:`[X. 31, *ad fin.*]`

.. picture:: 0082.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 34 diagram

let the semicircle :g:`$ADB$ <0082.g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0082.g1:/ A B />`, let :g:`$BC$ <0082.g1:/ B C />` be bisected at :g:`$E$ <0082.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`$AB$ <0082.g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`$BE$ <0082.g1:/ B E />` and deficient by a square figure, namely the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0082.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0082.g1:/ F B />` ; :just:`[VI. 28] <VI.28>`
