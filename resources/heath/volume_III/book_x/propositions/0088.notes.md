# Transcription Notes: Folio 0088 (Page 84)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Page 84 contains Heath's important programmatic commentary on the "first hexad" of compound irrational straight lines (Props. 36-41), followed by the enunciation and initial proof of Proposition 37.
- **Folio Continuity**: Preceded by folio 0087 (Page 83, Proposition 36). Continued by folio 0089 (Page 85, Proposition 37 conclusion and Proposition 38).
- **Propositions Covered**: Proposition 36 (commentary); Proposition 37 (enunciation, setting-out, and initial proof step).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heath Commentary on the Hexad**: Explains the structural architecture of Book X: the 6 compound irrationals formed by addition (Props. 36-41), their connection to the 6 apotomes formed by subtraction (Props. 73-78), and their relationship as square roots of the six binomials in Definitions II / Props. 48-53.
- **Algebraic Shorthand**: Introduces $x, y$ as segments with $x > y$. For Prop. 36: $x = \rho, y = \sqrt{k}\rho$. Commensurable in square only indicated by $\simeq$, length incommensurability by $\smile$, and commensurability by $\sim$. Connects binomial to roots of the biquadratic equation $x^4 - 2(1+k)\rho^2 x^2 + (1-k)^2\rho^4 = 0$.
- **Proposition 37**: Enunciation and first steps defining the **first bimedial** line. References previous proof via `[cf. x. 36, ll. 9—20]`.
