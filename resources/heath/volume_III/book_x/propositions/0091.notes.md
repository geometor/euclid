# Transcription Notes: Folio 0091 (Page 87)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 38 (Conclusion):** Completes the proof establishing that $AC$ is irrational and names it a **second bimedial straight line**, terminating with line 55 and `Q. E. D.`.
- **Heath's Commentary on Prop. 38:** Explains Euclid's implicit deduction that $EH$ is a medial area via x. 15 and x. 23, Por. Heath gives the algebraic formulation where $x, y$ are $k^{1/4}\rho, \lambda^{1/2}\rho/k^{1/4}$, shows $(x+y)$ is irrational via application of areas to a rational line $\sigma$, and relates the second bimedial and second apotome of a medial (x. 75) to the biquadratic equation $x^4 - 2\frac{k+\lambda}{\sqrt{k}}\rho^2 x^2 + \frac{(k-\lambda)^2}{k}\rho^4 = 0$.
- **Proposition 39 (Enunciation):** Introduces the *major* irrational straight line, which continues on Folio 0092 (Page 88).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations

- **Typographical Features:** Bold terms indicate defined irrational types: "second bimedial straight line", "major".
