# Transcription Notes: Folio 0092 (Page 88)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 39 (Proof & Conclusion):** Carries over from Folio 0091 (Page 87). Proves that $AC = AB + BC$ is irrational and names it **major**, ending with `Q. E. D.`.
- **Heath's Commentary on Prop. 39:** Formulates $x, y$ in terms of radicals from x. 33, deduces $(x+y)$ irrationality from $(x+y)^2 \cup (x^2+y^2)$, and connects the major straight line and the *minor* (x. 76) to the biquadratic equation $x^4 - 2\rho^2 x^2 + \frac{k^2}{1+k^2}\rho^4 = 0$.
- **Proposition 40 (Enunciation):** Enunciates the definition and irrationality of the **side of a rational plus a medial area**, with proof continuing on Folio 0093 (Page 89).
