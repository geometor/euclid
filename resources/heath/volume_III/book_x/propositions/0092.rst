.. subclause::

   For, since the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />` is medial,

.. assertion:: [x. 6 and 23, Por.]

   twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   But the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   | therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />` is incommensurable
   | with the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />`,
   | so that the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />` together with twice the rect-
   | angle :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />`, that is, the square on :g:`$AC$ <0092.g1:/ A C />`, is also incommen-
   | surable with the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0092.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0092.g1:/ B C />`; :just:`[x. 16]`
   | therefore the square on :g:`$AC$ <0092.g1:/ A C />` is irrational,
   | so that :g:`$AC$ <0092.g1:/ A C />` is also irrational. :just:`[x. Def. 4] <X.def.4>`

.. qed::

   And let it be called **major**. :just:`Q. E. D.`

Here $x$, $y$ are of the form found in x. 33, viz.

$$\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}},\quad \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}.$$

| By hypothesis, the rectangle $xy$ is medial;
| therefore $2xy$ is medial.

Also $(x^2+y^2)$ is a rational area.

Therefore

$$x^2+y^2 \mathbin{\smile} 2xy,$$

whence

$$(x+y)^2 \mathbin{\smile} (x^2+y^2),$$

so that $(x+y)^2$, and therefore $(x+y)$, is irrational.

| The irrational straight line $\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}} + \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}$ is
| called a **major** (irrational) straight line.

| This and the corresponding *minor* irrational found in x. 76 are the
| positive roots of the equation

$$x^4 - 2\rho^2 \cdot x^2 + \frac{k^2}{1+k^2}\rho^4 = 0.$$

PROPOSITION 40.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the **side of a rational plus a medial area**.
