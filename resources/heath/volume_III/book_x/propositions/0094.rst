:title: Proposition 41 (cont.)

For let two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />` incommensurable in square and satisfying the given conditions :just:`[X. 35] <X.35>` be added together; I say that :g:`$AC$ <0094.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. picture:: 0094.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 41 diagram

Let a rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />` be set out, and let there be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />` the rectangle :g:`$DF$ <0094.g1:< D F >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />`, and the rectangle :g:`$GH$ <0094.g1:< G H >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$DH$ <0094.g1:< D H >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0094.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[II. 4] <II.4>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />` is medial, and is equal to :g:`$DF$ <0094.g1:< D F >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DF$ <0094.g1:< D F >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0094.g1:/ D G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`$GK$ <0094.g1:/ G K />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$GF$ <0094.g1:/ G F />`, that is, :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the squares on :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0094.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0094.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$DF$ <0094.g1:< D F >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$GH$ <0094.g1:< G H >>`; so that :g:`$DG$ <0094.g1:/ D G />` is also incommensurable with :g:`$GK$ <0094.g1:/ G K />`. :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And they are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DG$ <0094.g1:/ D G />`, :g:`$GK$ <0094.g1:/ G K />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$DK$ <0094.g1:/ D K />` is irrational and what is called binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DE$ <0094.g1:/ D E />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DH$ <0094.g1:< D H >>` is irrational, and the side of the square which is equal to it is irrational. :just:`[X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AC$ <0094.g1:/ A C />` is the side of the square equal to :g:`$HD$ <0094.g1:< H D >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0094.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. assertion::

   And let it be called the :term:`**side of the sum of two medial areas** <side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
