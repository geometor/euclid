PROPOSITION 42.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A binomial straight line is divided into its terms at one point only.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0096.g1:/ A B />` be a binomial straight line divided into its terms
| at :g:`$C$ <0096.g1:C>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AC$ <0096.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0096.g1:/ C B />` are rational
   | straight lines commensurable in
   | square only. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

.. picture:: 0096.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 42 diagram

| I say that :g:`$AB$ <0096.g1:/ A B />` is not divided at another point into two
| rational straight lines commensurable in square only.
