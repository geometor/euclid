# Transcription Notes: Folio 0098 (Page 94)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 42 (Commentary Conclusion):** Completes Heath's commentary from Folio 0097 (Page 93) demonstrating algebraically that $(x^2+y^2) - (x'^2+y'^2) = 2x'y' - 2xy$ equates a rational area with the difference of two medial areas, impossible by x. 26.
- **Proposition 43 (Enunciation & Proof):** Proves that a first bimedial straight line is divided into its terms at one point only, using the contradiction that the difference of two medial squares would equal a rational area.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terminology:** Heath introduces the technical Greek term for terms: *terms* (ὀνόματα).
- **Ending Formula:** Folio ends with `therefore it is so divided at one point only.` without `Q. E. D.` printed on this page.
