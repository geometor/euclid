:title: Proposition 42 (note concl.), Proposition 43

| The proposition states that a binomial straight line cannot be split up into
| *terms* (ὀνόματα) in two ways. For, if possible, let

$$x + y = x' + y',$$

| where $x$, $y$, and also $x'$, $y'$, are the *terms* of a binomial straight line, $x'$, $y'$
| being different from $x$, $y$ (or $y$, $x$).

| One pair is necessarily more nearly equal than the other. Let $x'$, $y'$ be
| more nearly equal than $x$, $y$.

Then

$$(x^2 + y^2) - (x'^2 + y'^2) = 2x'y' - 2xy.$$

| Now by hypothesis $(x^2 + y^2)$, $(x'^2 + y'^2)$ are rational areas, being of the form
| $\rho^2 + k\rho^2$;
| but $2x'y'$, $2xy$ are medial areas, being of the form $\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho^2$;
| therefore the difference of two medial areas is rational:
| which is impossible. [x. 26]

Therefore $x'$, $y'$ cannot be different from $x$, $y$ (or $y$, $x$).

PROPOSITION 43.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A first bimedial straight line is divided at one point only.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0098.g1:/ A B />` be a first bimedial straight line divided at :g:`$C$ <0098.g1:C>`, so
| that :g:`$AC$ <0098.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0098.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight
| lines commensurable in square
| only and containing a rational
| rectangle; :just:`[X. 37] <X.37>`

.. picture:: 0098.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 43 diagram

I say that :g:`$AB$ <0098.g1:/ A B />` is not so divided at another point.

| For, if possible, let it be divided at :g:`$D$ <0098.g1:D>` also, so that :g:`$AD$ <0098.g1:/ A D />`,
| :g:`$DB$ <0098.g1:/ D B />` are also medial straight lines commensurable in square
| only and containing a rational rectangle.

.. subclause::

   | Since, then, that by which twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0098.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0098.g1:/ D B />`
   | differs from twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0098.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0098.g1:/ C B />` is that by which the
   | squares on :g:`$AC$ <0098.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0098.g1:/ C B />` differ from the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0098.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0098.g1:/ D B />`,
   | while twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0098.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0098.g1:/ D B />` differs from twice the
   | rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0098.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0098.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area—for both are rational—

.. assertion::

   | therefore the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0098.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0098.g1:/ C B />` also differ from the squares
   | on :g:`$AD$ <0098.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0098.g1:/ D B />` by a rational area, though they are medial :
   | which is absurd. :just:`[X. 26] <X.26>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore a first bimedial straight line is not divided into
   | its terms at different points;
   | therefore it is so divided at one point only.
