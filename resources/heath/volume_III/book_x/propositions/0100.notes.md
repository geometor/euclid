## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 44 (Continued)**: Carries forward the reduction of Proposition 44. By applying the areas $EG$ ($= AC^2 + CB^2$) and $HK$ ($= 2 AC \cdot CB$) to the rational segment $EF$, Euclid demonstrates that their breadths $EH$ and $HN$ are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, so that $EN$ is a binomial straight line divided at $H$. By identical reasoning with $D$, $EN$ is shown to be a binomial straight line divided at $M$.
- **Continuity**: Follows folio 0099 (page 95) directly and concludes on folio 0101 (page 97) with the demonstration that $EH \neq MN$.
