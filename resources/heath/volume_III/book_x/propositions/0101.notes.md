# Transcription Notes: Folio 0101 (Page 97)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Proposition 44 (Conclusion)**: Completes the proof of Proposition 44 by demonstrating that $EH > MN$ (and therefore $EH \neq MN$), completing the contradiction against Proposition X. 42 (uniqueness of division of a binomial straight line).
- **Commentary on Proposition 44**: Heath details the algebraic structure of the proof, using standard notation with $\sim$ for commensurability and $\smile$ for incommensurability.
- **Continuity**: Follows folio 0100 (page 96) and precedes Proposition 45 on folio 0102 (page 98).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Symbols**:
  - `\smile`: Incommensurable (e.g., $u, v$ both rational and $\smile \sigma$, $x \smile y$, $u \smile v$).
  - `\sim`: Commensurable (e.g., $x^2 \sim (x^2 + y^2)$, $xy \sim 2xy$).
- **Scholarly Note**: Heath notes Euclid's tacit assumption $x^2 + y^2 > 2xy$ (an immediate consequence of II. 7) and points out that this makes the separate Lemma placed after X. 59 likely an interpolation.
