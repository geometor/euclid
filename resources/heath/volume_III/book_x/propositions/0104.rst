| :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` medial, and the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` medial and also in-
| commensurable with the sum of the squares on them;
| I say that :g:`$AB$ <0104.g1:/ A B />` is not divided at another point so as to fulfil
| the given conditions.

.. picture:: 0104.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 47 diagram

| For, if possible, let it be divided at :g:`$D$ <0104.g1:D>`, so that again :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`
| is of course not the same as :g:`$BD$ <0104.g1:/ B D />`, but :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />` is supposed greater;
| let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />` be set out,
| and let there be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />` the rectangle :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` equal to the
| squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`,
| and the rectangle :g:`$HK$ <0104.g1:< H K >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion:: [II. 4] <II.4>

   therefore the whole :g:`$EK$ <0104.g1:< E K >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0104.g1:/ A B />`.

| Again, let :g:`$EL$ <0104.g1:< E L >>`, equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0104.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0104.g1:/ D B />`, be applied
| to :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0104.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0104.g1:/ D B />`, is equal
   | to the remainder :g:`$MK$ <0104.g1:< M K >>`.

.. subclause::

   | And since, by hypothesis, the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`,
   | :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` is medial,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$HE$ <0104.g1:/ H E />` is rational and incommensurable in length with
   | :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`$HN$ <0104.g1:/ H N />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0104.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />` is incom-
   | mensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0104.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0104.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0104.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$GN$ <0104.g1:< G N >>`,
