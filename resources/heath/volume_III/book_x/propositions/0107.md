Let $kp$ be a straight line commensurable in length with $\rho$, a given rational<br />
straight line.

The two numbers taken may be written $p(m^2 - n^2)$, $pn^2$, where $(m^2 - n^2)$ is<br />
not a square.

Take $x$ such that
$$pm^2 : p(m^2 - n^2) = k^2 p^2 : x^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1),$$
whence
$$x = kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}.$$

Then $kp + x$, or $kp + kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}$, is a *first binomial* straight line $\dots\dots(2)$.
