therefore the square on $EF$ also has to the square on $H$ the ratio which a square number has to a square number.
    Therefore $EF$ is commensurable in length with $H$; [x. 9]
therefore the square on $EF$ is greater than the square on $FG$ by the square on a straight line commensurable with $EF$.

    Therefore $EF$ is a first binomial straight line.

Let $kp$ be a straight line commensurable in length with $\rho$, a given rational straight line.
    The two numbers taken may be written $p(m^2 - n^2)$, $pn^2$, where $(m^2 - n^2)$ is not a square.
    Take $x$ such that
$$pm^2 : p(m^2 - n^2) = k^2 p^2 : x^2 \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1),$$
whence
$$x = kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}.$$
    Then $kp + x$, or $kp + kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}$, is a *first binomial* straight line $\dots\dots(2)$.
