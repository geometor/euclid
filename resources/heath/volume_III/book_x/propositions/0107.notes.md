## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Misprint in Euclid's Text**:
  - The printed conclusion in the text reads: "Therefore EF is a first binomial straight line."
  - Mathematically, this is an obvious lapsus calami/typographical error in the printed edition for $EG$, as $EG = EF + FG$ is the binomial straight line formed by the two rational terms $EF$ and $FG$, while $EF$ is merely the greater term.
- **Heath's Commentary**:
  - Sets up the algebraic notation: $\rho$ is the unit rational straight line, $kp$ is the term commensurable with $\rho$, numbers are $p(m^2 - n^2)$ and $pn^2$ with $m^2 - n^2$ non-square.
  - Derives $x = kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}$, giving the binomial $kp + kp \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}$.
