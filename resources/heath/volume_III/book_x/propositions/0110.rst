:title: Proposition 49 (commentary cont.), Proposition 50

Let

$$x^2 - k^2 p^2 = y^2,$$

whence, from (1),

$$pm^2 : pn^2 = x^2 : y^2,$$

and $y$ is therefore rational and $\sim x$.

The greater term of the binomial straight line is $x$ and the lesser $kp$, and

$$\frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + kp$$

satisfies the definition of the second binomial straight line.

The corresponding :term:`*second apotome* <second-apotome>` [x. 86] is

$$\frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} - kp.$$

The equation of which the two expressions are the roots is

$$x^2 - \frac{2kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} \cdot x + \frac{\lambda^2}{1 - \lambda^2} k^2 p^2 = 0,$$

or

$$x^2 - 2ax + \lambda^2 a^2 = 0,$$

where

$$a = \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}}.$$

PROPOSITION 50.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To find the third binomial straight line.

| Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0110.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0110.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that the sum
| of them :g:`$AB$ <0110.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`$BC$ <0110.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has
| to a square number, but has not to :g:`$AC$ <0110.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square
| number has to a square number.

.. picture:: 0110.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 50 Diagram

| Let any other number :g:`$D$ <0110.g1:D>`, not square, be set out also, and
| let it not have to either of the numbers :g:`$BA$ <0110.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AC$ <0110.g1:/ A C />` the ratio
| which a square number has to a square number.

| Let any rational straight line :g:`$E$ <0110.g1:E>` be set out,
| and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$D$ <0110.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AB$ <0110.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square
| on :g:`$E$ <0110.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0110.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$E$ <0110.g1:E>` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$FG$ <0110.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$E$ <0110.g1:E>` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FG$ <0110.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.
