### PROPOSITION 51.

<p class="enunciation">To find the fourth binomial straight line.</p>

Let two numbers <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> be set out such that <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
neither has to <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span>, nor yet to <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, the ratio<br />
which a square number has to a square number.

Let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0113.g1:D">$D$</span> be set out,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0113.g1:D">$D$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also rational.</p>

Let it be contrived that, as the number <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span><br />
is to <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>; <span class="justification">[x. 6, Por.]</span>

<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is commensurable<br />
with the square on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>; <span class="justification">[x. 6]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> has not to <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> the ratio which a square<br />
number has to a square number,</p>

<p class="assertion">neither has the square on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> the ratio<br />
which a square number has to a square number;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 9]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only;<br />
so that <span data-ref="0113.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is binomial.</p>

I say next that it is also a fourth binomial straight line.
