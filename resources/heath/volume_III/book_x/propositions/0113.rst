The corresponding :term:`*third apotome* <third-apotome>` [x. 87] is

$$m\sqrt{k}\cdot p - m\sqrt{k}\cdot p\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}.$$

The two expressions are accordingly the roots of the equation

$$x^2 - 2m\sqrt{k}\cdot px + \lambda^2 m^2 kp^2 = 0,$$

or

$$x^2 - 2ax + \lambda^2 a^2 = 0,$$

where

$$a = m\sqrt{k}\cdot p.$$

See also note on **x. 53** (*ad fin.*).

PROPOSITION 51.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To find the fourth binomial straight line.

| Let two numbers :g:`$AC$ <0113.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0113.g1:/ C B />` be set out such that :g:`$AB$ <0113.g1:/ A B />`
| neither has to :g:`$BC$ <0113.g1:/ C B />`, nor yet to :g:`$AC$ <0113.g1:/ A C />`, the ratio
| which a square number has to a square number.

.. picture:: 0113.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 51

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$D$ <0113.g1:D>` be set out,
| and let :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$D$ <0113.g1:D>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` is also rational.

| Let it be contrived that, as the number :g:`$BA$ <0113.g1:/ A B />`
| is to :g:`$AC$ <0113.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` to the square
| on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[x. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable
   | with the square on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[x. 6]`
   | therefore :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$BA$ <0113.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0113.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square
   | number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   | neither has the square on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />` the ratio
   | which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />` are rational straight lines commen-
   | surable in square only;
   | so that :g:`$EG$ <0113.g1:/ E G />` is binomial.

I say next that it is also a fourth binomial straight line.

.. subclause::

   | For since, as :g:`$BA$ <0113.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0113.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` to the
   | square on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`.

| Let then the squares on :g:`$FG$ <0113.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$H$ <0113.g1:H>` be equal to the square
| on :g:`$EF$ <0113.g1:/ E F />`;
