   therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as the number :g:`AB <0113.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`BC <0113.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`H <0113.g1:H>`. :just:`[v. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`AB <0113.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`BC <0113.g1:/ C B />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   therefore neither has the square on :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />` to the square on :g:`H <0113.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`H <0113.g1:H>`; :just:`[x. 9]` therefore the square on :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />` is greater than the square on :g:`GF <0113.g1:/ F G />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`FG <0113.g1:/ F G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, and :g:`EF <0113.g1:/ E F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`D <0113.g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`EG <0113.g1:/ E G />` is a fourth binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

Take numbers $m$, $n$ such that $(m + n)$ has not to either $m$ or $n$ the ratio of square to square.

Take $x$ such that

$$(m + n) : m = k^2 p^2 : x^2,$$

whence

$$x = kp \sqrt{\frac{m}{m + n}} = \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 + \lambda}}\text{, say.}$$

Then $kp + x$, or $kp + \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 + \lambda}}$, is a :term:`*fourth binomial* <fourth-binomial>` straight line.

For $\sqrt{k^2 p^2 - x^2}$ is incommensurable in length with $kp$, and $kp$ is commensurable in length with $p$.

The corresponding :term:`*fourth apotome* <fourth-apotome>` [x. 88] is

$$kp - \frac{kp}{\sqrt{1 + \lambda}}.$$

The equation of which the two expressions are the roots is

$$x^2 - 2kp \cdot x + \frac{\lambda}{1 + \lambda} k^2 p^2 = 0,$$

or

$$x^2 - 2ax + \frac{\lambda}{1 + \lambda} a^2 = 0,$$

where

$$a = kp.$$
