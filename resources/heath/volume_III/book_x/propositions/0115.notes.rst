2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
-----------------------------------------------

- **Fifth Binomial Distinction**: In the fifth binomial, the square on the greater term ($GF$) exceeds the square on the lesser ($FE$) by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the greater, and the *lesser* term ($EF$) is commensurable in length with the given rational straight line $D$ (contrasting with the fourth binomial where the *greater* term is commensurable with $D$).
