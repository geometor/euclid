- **Folio**: 0117

- **Content**: Pure proposition folio continuing the proof of Proposition 53 ("To find the sixth binomial straight line").
- **Key Developments**:
  - Establishes that $FG$ and $GH$ are rational straight lines commensurable in square only, so $FH$ is binomial [x. 36].
  - Uses *ex aequali* proportion [v. 22] ($D : AC = \text{sq}(E) : \text{sq}(GH)$) to prove $E$ is incommensurable in length with $GH$ [x. 9].
  - Concludes that neither $FG$ nor $GH$ is commensurable in length with $E$.
  - Sets up squares on $GH, K$ equal to the square on $FG$.
- **Continuity**: Proof concludes on Folio 0118 (Page 114).
