.. assertion::

   | neither has the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` the ratio
   | which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

| Again, let it be contrived that, as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the
| square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable with the
   | square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 6]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the square on :g:`$HG$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is rational;
   | therefore :g:`$HG$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square
   | number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   | neither has the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` the ratio
   | which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` are rational straight lines commen-
   | surable in square only;
   | therefore :g:`$FH$ <0116.g1:/ F H />` is binomial. :just:`[x. 36]`

| It is next to be proved that it is also a sixth binomial
| straight line.

.. subclause::

   | For since, as :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AB$ <0116.g1:/ A B />`, so is the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the
   | square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`,
   | and also, as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to the
   | square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, *ex aequali*, as :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>`
   | to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[v. 22]`

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$D$ <0116.g1:D>` has not to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />` the ratio which a square number
   | has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has the square on :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` to the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved incommensurable with :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />` is incommen-
   | surable in length with :g:`$E$ <0116.g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since as :g:`$BA$ <0116.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0116.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` to
   | the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0116.g1:/ F G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GH$ <0116.g1:/ G H />`.
