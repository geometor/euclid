### PROPOSITION 54.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the<br />
first binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight<br />
line which is called binomial.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the first binomial <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;<br />
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight<br />
line which is called binomial.
