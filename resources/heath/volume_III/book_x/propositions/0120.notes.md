## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Lemma Conclusion**: Establishes that $DG$ is a mean proportional between $AB, BC$, and $DC$ between $AC, CB$. Heath renders these algebraically as $x^2 : xy = xy : y^2$ and $(x+y)^2 : (x+y)y = (x+y)y : y^2$.
- **Genuineness Remark**: Heath observes that the first proportion was already proved in X. 25, suggesting this lemma may be an interpolation.
- **Proposition 54**: The first of six major conversion theorems (Props. 54–59) finding the "side" ($\sqrt{\cdot}$) of an area formed by a rational straight line and one of the six binomial straight lines. For the first binomial, the side is the *binomial* ($x+y$).
