It is here proved that

$$x^2 : xy = xy : y^2,$$

and

$$(x + y)^2 : (x + y)y = (x + y)y : y^2.$$

The first of the two results is proved in the course of X. 25 (lines 6—8 on p. 57 above). This fact may, I think, suggest doubt as to the genuineness of this Lemma.

PROPOSITION 54.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the first binomial, the "side"\ [#f1]_ of the area is the irrational straight line which is called binomial.

For let the area :g:`AC <0121.g1:< A C >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`AB <0121.g1:/ A B />` and the first binomial :g:`AD <0121.g1:/ A D />`; I say that the "side" of the area :g:`AC <0121.g1:< A C >>` is the irrational straight line which is called binomial.

For, since :g:`AD <0121.g1:/ A D />` is a first binomial straight line, let it be divided into its terms at :g:`E <0121.g1:E>`, and let :g:`AE <0121.g1:/ A E />` be the greater term.
