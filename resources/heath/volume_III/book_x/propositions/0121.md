It is then manifest that <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are rational straight lines<br />
commensurable in square only,<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the<br />
square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> set out. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 1]</span>

Let <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> be bisected at the point <span data-ref="0121.g1:F">$F$</span>.

![Proposition 54](0121.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, if there be applied to the greater <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> a parallelogram<br />
equal to the fourth part of the square on the less, that is, to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and deficient by a square figure, it divides<br />
it into commensurable parts. <span class="justification">[X. 17]</span></p>

Let then the rectangle <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$EG$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0121.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:F">$F$</span> parallel to<br />
either of the straight lines <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ D C /">$CD$</span>;<br />
let the square <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> be constructed equal to the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, and the square <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>, <span class="justification">[II. 14]</span><br />
and let them be placed so that <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is in a straight line with<br />
<span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$RN$</span> is also in a straight line with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>.</p>

And let the parallelogram <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S Q >">$SQ$</span> be completed;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S Q >">$SQ$</span> is a square. <span class="justification">[Lemma]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$FE$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$EG$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 17]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$KG$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>.</p>
