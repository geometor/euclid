But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span> is also a mean proportional between the same <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>; [Lemma]
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>,
so that it is also equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< P O >">$PO$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> are also equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>;
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is equal to the whole <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S Q >">$SQ$</span>, that is, to the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>.
I say next that <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is binomial.
For, since <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. [x. 15]
But <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also, by hypothesis, commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> are also commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [x. 12]
And <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is rational;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is also rational;
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> is rational, [x. 19]
and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>, that is, the squares on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, are rational and commensurable.
And, since <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, [x. 13]
so that <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>. [VI. 1, x. 11]
But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>.
But, as <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> is to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span>. [x. 11]
But <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.
And the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>,
and each is rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only.
