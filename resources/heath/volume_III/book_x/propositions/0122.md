<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span> is also a mean proportional between the same<br />
<span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>; <span class="justification">[Lemma]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">so that it is also equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< P O >">$PO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> are also equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is equal to the whole <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S Q >">$SQ$</span>, that is, to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>.</p>

I say next that <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is binomial.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with each of the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also, by hypothesis, commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> are also commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is also rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> is rational, <span class="justification">[X. 19]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>, that is, the squares on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, are<br />
rational and commensurable.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commen-<br />
surable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0121.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> is to <span data-ref="0121.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span> to <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the square on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is commensurable with the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>,<br />
and each is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0121.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only.</p>
