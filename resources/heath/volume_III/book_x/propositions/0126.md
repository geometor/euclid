<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<sup>50</sup> and <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> ; <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span><br />
so that <span data-ref="0125.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0125.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ N P /">$PN$</span> with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ R N /">$NR$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span><br />
<sup>55</sup> that is, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> were proved to be both medial and com-<br />
mensurable in square ;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only.</p>

<sup>60</sup> I say next that they also contain a rational rectangle.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is, by hypothesis, commensurable with each<br />
of the straight lines <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And each of them is rational ;</p>

<p class="assertion"><sup>65</sup> therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0125.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span> is rational, <span class="justification">[X. 19]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0125.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if two medial straight lines commensurable in square<br />
only and containing a rational rectangle be added together, the<br />
whole is irrational and is called a first bimedial straight line.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 37]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><sup>70</sup> Therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is a first bimedial straight line.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

39. **Therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> and <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> are pairs of rational straight lines com-<br />
mensurable in square only.** The text has "Therefore <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> are rational straight<br />
lines commensurable in square only," which I have altered because it would naturally convey<br />
the impression that *any two* of the three straight lines are commensurable in square only,<br />
whereas <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0125.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> are commensurable in length (l. 18), and it is only the other two pairs<br />
which are commensurable in square only.

A *second binomial* straight line being [x. 49] of the form
$$\frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + k\rho,$$
the present proposition is equivalent to finding the *square root of the expression*
$$\rho \left(\frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + k\rho\right).$$
