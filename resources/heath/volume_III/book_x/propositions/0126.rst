   | in square only.

:line:`60` I say next that they also contain a rational rectangle.

   | For, since :g:`$DE$ <0125.g1:/ E D />` is, by hypothesis, commensurable with each

.. subclause::

   | But, if two medial straight lines commensurable in square

   | whole is irrational and is called a first bimedial straight line.

A :term:`*second binomial* <second-binomial>` straight line being [x. 49] of the form

$$\frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + k\rho,$$

the present proposition is equivalent to finding the *square root of the expression*

$$\rho \left(\frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}} + k\rho\right).$$
