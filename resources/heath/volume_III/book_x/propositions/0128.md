### PROPOSITION 56.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the<br />
third binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight<br />
line called a second bimedial.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be contained by the rational<br />
straight line <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the third binomial <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> divided into its<br />
terms at <span data-ref="0128.g1:E">$E$</span>, of which terms <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater;<br />
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight<br />
line called a second bimedial.

For let the same construction be made as before.

![Proposition 56](0128.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a third binomial straight line,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only,<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the<br />
square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,<br />
and neither of the terms <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is commensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 3]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Then, in manner similar to the foregoing, we shall prove<br />
that <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only;<br />
so that <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is bimedial.</p>

It is next to be proved that it is also a second bimedial<br />
straight line.

<p class="sub-clause">Since <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that is,<br />
with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are rational;</p>
