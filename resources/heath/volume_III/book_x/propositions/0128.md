### PROPOSITION 56.

*If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the third binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called a second bimedial.*

For let the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be contained by the rational straight line <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the third binomial <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> divided into its terms at <span data-ref="0128.g1:E">$E$</span>, of which terms <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater;
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight line called a second bimedial.

For let the same construction be made as before.

![Proposition 56](0128.g1.svg)

Now, since <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a third binomial straight line,
therefore <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,
the square on <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
and neither of the terms <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [x. Deff. II. 3]

Then, in manner similar to the foregoing, we shall prove that <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0128.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in square only;
so that <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is bimedial.

It is next to be proved that it is also a second bimedial straight line.

Since <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that is, with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0128.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. [x. 13]

And they are rational;
