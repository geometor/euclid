PROPOSITION 56.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the third binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called a second bimedial.

| For let the area :g:`$ABCD$ <0128.g1:< A B C D >>` be contained by the rational
| straight line :g:`$AB$ <0128.g1:/ A B />` and the third binomial :g:`$AD$ <0128.g1:/ A D />` divided into its
| terms at :g:`$E$ <0128.g1:E>`, of which terms :g:`$AE$ <0128.g1:/ A E />` is the greater;
| I say that the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0128.g1:< A C >>` is the irrational straight
| line called a second bimedial.

For let the same construction be made as before.

.. picture:: 0128.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 56

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AD$ <0128.g1:/ A D />` is a third binomial straight line,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AE$ <0128.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0128.g1:/ E D />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only,
   | the square on :g:`$AE$ <0128.g1:/ A E />` is greater than the square on :g:`$ED$ <0128.g1:/ E D />` by the
   | square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$AE$ <0128.g1:/ A E />`,
   | and neither of the terms :g:`$AE$ <0128.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0128.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable in length
   | with :g:`$AB$ <0128.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[X. Deff. II. 3]`

.. assertion::

   | Then, in manner similar to the foregoing, we shall prove
   | that :g:`$MO$ <0128.g1:/ M O />` is the "side" of the area :g:`$AC$ <0128.g1:< A C >>`,
   | and :g:`$MN$ <0128.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0128.g1:/ N O />` are medial straight lines commensurable in
   | square only;
   | so that :g:`$MO$ <0128.g1:/ M O />` is bimedial.

| It is next to be proved that it is also a second bimedial
| straight line.

.. subclause::

   | Since :g:`$DE$ <0128.g1:/ E D />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0128.g1:/ A B />`, that is,
   | with :g:`$EK$ <0128.g1:/ E K />`,
   | and :g:`$DE$ <0128.g1:/ E D />` is commensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0128.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`$EF$ <0128.g1:/ E F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$EK$ <0128.g1:/ E K />`.

.. subclause::

   And they are rational;
