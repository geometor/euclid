This proposition in like manner is the equivalent of finding the square<br />
root of the product of $\rho$ and the *third binomial* [X. 50], i.e. of the expression
$$\rho(\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho + \sqrt{k}\cdot\rho\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}).$$
As before, put
$$\left.\begin{aligned} u+v &= \sqrt{k}\cdot\rho \\ uv &= \frac{1}{4}k\rho^2(1-\lambda^2) \end{aligned}\right\}\quad\quad\quad\quad\dots\dots(1).$$
Next, $u$, $v$ being found, let
$$\left.\begin{aligned} x^2 &= \rho u, \\ y^2 &= \rho v; \end{aligned}\right\}$$
then $(x+y)$ is the square root required and is a *second bimedial* straight line.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 38]</span>

For, as in the last proposition, it is proved that $(x+y)$ is the square root,<br />
and $x$, $y$ are medial and $\sim$.

Again, $xy = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho^2\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}$, which is *medial*.

Hence $(x+y)$ is a *second bimedial* straight line.

By solving equations (1), we find
$$\begin{aligned} u &= \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho + \lambda\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho), \\ v &= \frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho - \lambda\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho), \end{aligned}$$
and
$$x+y = \rho\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{k}}{2}(1+\lambda)} + \rho\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{k}}{2}(1-\lambda)}.$$

The corresponding *second apotome of a medial* found in X. 93 is the same<br />
thing with a minus sign between the terms, and the two are the roots (cf. note<br />
on X. 50) of the biquadratic equation
$$x^4 - 2\sqrt{k}\cdot\rho^2 x^2 + \lambda^2 k\rho^4 = 0.$$

### PROPOSITION 57.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the<br />
fourth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight<br />
line called major.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0130.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the fourth binomial <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> divided into its terms<br />
at <span data-ref="0130.g1:E">$E$</span>, of which terms let <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be the greater;<br />
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0130.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight<br />
line called major.
