126 BOOK X [x. 57

For, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a fourth binomial straight line,
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,
the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [x. Deff. II. 4]

Let <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0130.g1:F">$F$</span>,
and let there be applied to <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> a parallelogram, the rectangle <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, equal to the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. [x. 18]

Let <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let the rest of the construction be as before;
it is then manifest that <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0130.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>.

![Proposition 57](0130.g1.svg)

It is next to be proved that <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the irrational straight line called major.

Since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$EG$</span>,
<span data-ref="0130.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0130.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> with <span data-ref="0130.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>; [VI. 1, x. 11]
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incommensurable in square.

And, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
<span data-ref="0130.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is rational; [x. 19]
and it is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is also rational.

And, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that is, with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. [x. 13]

Therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:< E L >">$LE$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0130.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, is medial. [x. 21]

And it is contained by <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is medial.
