<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a fourth binomial straight line,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only,<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> by the<br />
square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 4]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0130.g1:F">$F$</span>,<br />
and let there be applied to <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> a parallelogram, the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, equal to the square on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 18]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let the rest of the construction be as before;
<p class="assertion">it is then manifest that <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0130.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>.</p>

![Proposition 57](0130.g1.svg)

It is next to be proved that <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the irrational straight<br />
line called major.

<p class="sub-clause">Since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ G E /">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0130.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0130.g1:< S N >">$SN$</span> with <span data-ref="0130.g1:< N Q >">$NQ$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incommensurable in square.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0130.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is rational; <span class="justification">[X. 19]</span><br />
and it is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;<br />
therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
that is, with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0130.g1:< E L >">$LE$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0130.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, is medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is contained by <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0130.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is medial.</p>
