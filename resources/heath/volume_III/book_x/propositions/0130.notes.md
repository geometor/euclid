# Transcription Notes: Folio 0130 (Page 126)

## Summary & Folio Continuity

- **Content**: Proof of Proposition 57 (fourth binomial straight line application yields the *major* irrational straight line). Includes construction, diagram, proof that $MN, NO$ are incommensurable in square, that the sum of the squares on $MN, NO$ is rational, and that the rectangle $MN, NO$ is medial.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Structure**: Corresponds to $\sqrt{\rho(k\rho + \frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}})}$.
  - $AE, ED$ commensurable in square only, $\sqrt{AE^2 - ED^2}$ incommensurable with $AE$, $AE$ commensurable in length with $AB$ [x. Deff. II. 4].
  - By application of areas [x. 18], $AG$ is incommensurable in length with $GE$.
  - Hence $SN$ is incommensurable with $NQ$, proving $MN, NO$ incommensurable in square.
  - $AK$ is rational [x. 19], giving sum of squares rational.
  - $LE = MR$ is medial [x. 21], giving rectangle medial.
- **Cross References**: [x. Deff. II. 4], [x. 18], [VI. 1, x. 11], [x. 19], [x. 13], [x. 21].
