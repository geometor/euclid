## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Insertion**: "And the [sum] of the squares..." contains "[sum]" in brackets, inserted by Heath to remedy Euclid's concise Greek text.
- **Notation for Incommensurability**:
  - Heath uses $\smile$ for incommensurable in length ($u \smile v$, $\rho u \smile \rho v$, $x^2 \smile y^2$).
  - Heath uses $\bumpeq$ for incommensurable in square ($x \bumpeq y$).
  - Heath uses $\sim$ for commensurable in length ($(u+v) \sim \rho$).
