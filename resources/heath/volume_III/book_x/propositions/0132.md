128 BOOK X [x. 58

### PROPOSITION 58.

*If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fifth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.*

For let the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span> be contained by the rational straight line <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the fifth binomial <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> divided into its terms at <span data-ref="0132.g1:E">$E$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater term;
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.

For let the same construction be made as before shown;
it is then manifest that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span>.

![Proposition 58](0132.g1.svg)

It is then to be proved that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the side of a rational plus a medial area.

For, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, [x. 18]
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B H G >">$AH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:< G H K E >">$HE$</span>, [VI. 1, x. 11]
that is, the square on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> with the square on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incommensurable in square.

And, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a fifth binomial straight line, and <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> the lesser segment,
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [x. Deff. II. 5]

But <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>. [x. 13]

Therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B K E >">$AK$</span>, that is, the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, is medial. [x. 21]

And, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that is, with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. [x. 12]
