### PROPOSITION 58.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the<br />
fifth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight<br />
line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the fifth binomial <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> divided into its terms at<br />
<span data-ref="0132.g1:E">$E$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater term;<br />
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span> is the irrational straight<br />
line called the side of a rational plus a medial area.

For let the same construction be made as before shown;<br />
it is then manifest that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B C D >">$AC$</span>.

![Proposition 58](0132.g1.svg)

It is then to be proved that <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the side of a rational<br />
plus a medial area.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span class="justification">[X. 18]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B H G >">$AH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:< G H K E >">$HE$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span><br />
that is, the square on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> with the square on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incommensurable in square.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a fifth binomial straight line, and <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span><br />
the lesser segment,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 5]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:< A B K E >">$AK$</span>, that is, the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0132.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, is medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, that<br />
is, with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E D /">$DE$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0132.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span></p>
