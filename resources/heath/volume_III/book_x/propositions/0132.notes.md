## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Classification**: Deals with the fifth binomial straight line ($k\rho\sqrt{1+\lambda} + k\rho$), whose side on a rational rectangle produces the *side of a rational plus a medial area*.
- **Textual Quirks & Misprints**:
  - In line 19 of the page, the printed text reads: "therefore $AH$ is also commensurable with $HE$," which is a clear typographical error in Heath's print for "incommensurable" (since $AG$ is incommensurable with $GE$, and $AH:HE = AG:GE$ by VI. 1, $AH$ must be incommensurable with $HE$, as correctly concluded in the following line: "therefore $MN, NO$ are incommensurable in square").
