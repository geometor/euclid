130 BOOK X [x. 59

### PROPOSITION 59.

*If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the sixth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called the side of the sum of two medial areas.*

For let the area <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be contained by the rational straight line <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the sixth binomial <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, divided into its terms at <span data-ref="0134.g1:E">$E$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater term;
I say that the "side" of <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the side of the sum of two medial areas.

Let the same construction be made as before shown.

![Proposition 59](0134.g1.svg)

It is then manifest that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, and that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is incommensurable in square with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.

Now, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span>, that is, the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, is medial. [x. 21]

Again, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>; [x. 13]
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0134.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, that is, the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, is medial. [x. 21]

And, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
<span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>. [VI. 1, x. 11]

But <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, and <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>;
therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is incommensurable with the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.

And each of them is medial, and <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incommensurable in square.
