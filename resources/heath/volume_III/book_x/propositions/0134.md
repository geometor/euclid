### PROPOSITION 59.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the<br />
sixth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight<br />
line called the side of the sum of two medial areas.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be contained by the rational<br />
straight line <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the sixth binomial <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, divided into its<br />
terms at <span data-ref="0134.g1:E">$E$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is the greater term ;<br />
I say that the "side" of <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span> is the side of the sum of two<br />
medial areas.

Let the same construction be made as before shown.

![Proposition 59](0134.g1.svg)

<p class="assertion">It is then manifest that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M O /">$MO$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, and<br />
that <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> is incommensurable in square with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span>, that is, the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>,<br />
is medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> ; <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0134.g1:< M R >">$MR$</span>, that is, the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, is<br />
medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0134.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0134.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is incommen-<br />
surable with the rectangle <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">And each of them is medial, and <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0134.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> are incom-<br />
mensurable in square.</p>
