PROPOSITION 59.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and the sixth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight line called the side of the sum of two medial areas.

| For let the area :g:`$ABCD$ <0134.g1:< A B C D >>` be contained by the rational
| straight line :g:`$AB$ <0134.g1:/ A B />` and the sixth binomial :g:`$AD$ <0134.g1:/ A D />`, divided into its
| terms at :g:`$E$ <0134.g1:E>`, so that :g:`$AE$ <0134.g1:/ A E />` is the greater term ;
| I say that the "side" of :g:`$AC$ <0134.g1:< A C >>` is the side of the sum of two
| medial areas.

Let the same construction be made as before shown.

.. picture:: 0134.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 59

.. assertion::

   | It is then manifest that :g:`$MO$ <0134.g1:/ M O />` is the "side" of :g:`$AC$ <0134.g1:< A C >>`, and
   | that :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />` is incommensurable in square with :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$EA$ <0134.g1:/ E A />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0134.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EA$ <0134.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0134.g1:/ A B />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only ;
   | therefore :g:`$AK$ <0134.g1:< A K >>`, that is, the sum of the squares on :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />`,
   | is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$ED$ <0134.g1:/ E D />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AB$ <0134.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$FE$ <0134.g1:/ F E />` is also incommensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0134.g1:/ E K />` ; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`
   | therefore :g:`$FE$ <0134.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`$EK$ <0134.g1:/ E K />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only ;
   | therefore :g:`$EL$ <0134.g1:< E L >>`, that is, :g:`$MR$ <0134.g1:< M R >>`, that is, the rectangle :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />`, is
   | medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AE$ <0134.g1:/ A E />` is incommensurable with :g:`$EF$ <0134.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>

   :g:`$AK$ <0134.g1:< A K >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$EL$ <0134.g1:< E L >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$AK$ <0134.g1:< A K >>` is the sum of the squares on :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />`,
   | and :g:`$EL$ <0134.g1:< E L >>` is the rectangle :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />` is incommen-
   | surable with the rectangle :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />`.

.. assertion::

   | And each of them is medial, and :g:`$MN$ <0134.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0134.g1:/ N O />` are incom-
   | mensurable in square.
