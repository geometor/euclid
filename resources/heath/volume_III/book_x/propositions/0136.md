### PROPOSITION 60.

*The square on the binomial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the first binomial.*

Let <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a binomial straight line divided into its terms at <span data-ref="0136.g1:C">$C$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the greater term; let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be set out, and let <span data-ref="0136.g1:< D E F G >">$DEFG$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> producing <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as its breadth;
I say that <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a first binomial straight line.
