### PROPOSITION 60.

<p class="enunciation">The square on the binomial straight line applied to a<br />
rational straight line produces as breadth the first binomial.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a binomial straight line divided into its terms<br />
at <span data-ref="0136.g1:C">$C$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the greater term ;<br />
let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be<br />
set out,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0136.g1:< D E F G >">$DEFG$</span> equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> producing<br />
<span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as its breadth ;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a first binomial<br />
straight line.

![Proposition 60](0136.g1.svg)

For let there be applied to <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> the rectangle <span data-ref="0136.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span> equal<br />
to the square on <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0136.g1:< K L >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> ;<br />
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, is equal<br />
to <span data-ref="0136.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0136.g1:N">$N$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be drawn parallel<br />
[to <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span> or <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>].

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0136.g1:< M O >">$MO$</span>, <span data-ref="0136.g1:< N F >">$NF$</span> is equal to<br />
once the rectangle <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0136.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.</p>
