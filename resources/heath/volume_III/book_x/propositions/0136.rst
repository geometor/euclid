PROPOSITION 60.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   The square on the binomial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the first binomial.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0136.g1:/ A B />` be a binomial straight line divided into its terms at :g:`$C$ <0136.g1:C>`, so that :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />` is the greater term ; let a rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` be set out, and let :g:`$DEFG$ <0136.g1:< D E F G >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0136.g1:/ A B />` be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` producing :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` as its breadth ; I say that :g:`$DG$ <0136.g1:/ D G />` is a first binomial straight line.

.. picture:: 0136.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 60

For let there be applied to :g:`$DE$ <0136.g1:/ D E />` the rectangle :g:`$DH$ <0136.g1:< D H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0136.g1:< K L >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BC$ <0136.g1:/ B C />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`, is equal to :g:`$MF$ <0136.g1:< M F >>`.

Let :g:`$MG$ <0136.g1:/ M G />` be :eq:`bisected <0136.g1:/ M N / = 0136.g1:/ N G />` at :g:`$N$ <0136.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$NO$ <0136.g1:/ N O />` be drawn parallel [to :g:`$ML$ <0136.g1:/ M L />` or :g:`$GF$ <0136.g1:/ G F />`].

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$MO$ <0136.g1:< M O >>`, :g:`$NF$ <0136.g1:< N F >>` is equal to once the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0136.g1:/ C B />`.
