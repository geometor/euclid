.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AC <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ; :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>` therefore the squares on :g:`AC <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational and commensurable with one another, so that the sum of the squares or :g:`AC <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0136.g1:/ C B />` is also rational. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`DL <0136.g1:< D L >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DL <0136.g1:< D L >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`DE <0136.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DM <0136.g1:/ D M />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`DE <0136.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`AC <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0136.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,

.. assertion:: [X. 21] <X.21>

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`AC <0136.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0136.g1:/ C B />`, that is :g:`MF <0136.g1:< M F >>`, is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`ML <0136.g1:/ M L />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`MG <0136.g1:/ M G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`ML <0136.g1:/ M L />`, that is, :g:`DE <0136.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`MD <0136.g1:/ M D />` is also rational and is commensurable in length with :g:`DE <0136.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion:: [X. 13] <X.13>

   therefore :g:`DM <0136.g1:/ D M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`MG <0136.g1:/ M G />`.

.. subclause::

   And they are rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DM <0136.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`MG <0136.g1:/ M G />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ; therefore :g:`DG <0136.g1:/ D G />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

It is next to be proved that it is also a first binomial straight line.
