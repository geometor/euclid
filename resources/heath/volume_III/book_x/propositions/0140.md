<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is a first bimedial divided at <span data-ref="0139.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only, and containing a rational rectangle, <span class="justification">[X. 37]</span><br />
so that the squares on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are also medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is medial. <span class="justification">[X. 15 and 23, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it has been applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is rational,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0139.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is also rational and commensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. 20]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is binomial. <span class="justification">[X. 36]</span></p>

It is next to be proved that it is also a second binomial<br />
straight line.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the squares on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are greater than twice<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0139.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the square on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0139.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0139.g1:< K L >">$KL$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the rectangle <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 17]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0139.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a second binomial straight line. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 2]</span></p>

straight line, as found in X. 37,
