<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is a second bimedial divided at <span data-ref="0141.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only and containing a medial rectangle, <span class="justification">[X. 38]</span><br />
so that the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is also medial.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 15 and 23 Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is equal to <span data-ref="0141.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0141.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason,<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>,<br />
that is, with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is rational and<br />
incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also incommensurable with the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Hence the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is incommen-<br />
surable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, <span class="justification">[X. 12, 13]</span><br />
that is, <span data-ref="0141.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0141.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is binomial. <span class="justification">[X. 36]</span></p>

It is to be proved that it is also a third binomial straight line.

In manner similar to the foregoing we may conclude that<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>,<br />
and that <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">And the rectangle <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> is equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is commen-<br />
surable in length with <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0141.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a third binomial straight line. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. II. 3]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
