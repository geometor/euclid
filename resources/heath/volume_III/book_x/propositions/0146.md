### PROPOSITION 64.

*The square on the side of a rational plus a medial area applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the fifth binomial.*

   Let <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the side of a rational plus a medial area, divided into its straight lines at <span data-ref="0146.g1:C">$C$</span>, so that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the greater ;
let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be set out,
and let there be applied to <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> the parallelogram <span data-ref="0146.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as its breadth ;
I say that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a fifth binomial straight line.

![Proposition 64 Diagram](0146.g1.svg)

   Let the same construction as before be made.
   Since then <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the side of a rational plus a medial area, divided at <span data-ref="0146.g1:C">$C$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. [x. 40]
   Since then the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial,
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is medial,
so that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. [x. 22]
   Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0146.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>, is rational,
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is rational and commensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. [x. 20]
   Therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> ; [x. 13]
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ;
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is binomial. [x. 36]
   I say next that it is also a fifth binomial straight line.
   For it can be proved similarly that the rectangle <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,
and that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> ;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>. [x. 18]
