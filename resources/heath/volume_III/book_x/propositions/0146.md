### PROPOSITION 64.

<p class="enunciation">The square on the side of a rational plus a medial area<br />
applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth the fifth<br />
binomial.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the side of a rational plus a medial area,<br />
divided into its straight lines at <span data-ref="0146.g1:C">$C$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the greater ;<br />
let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be set<br />
out,<br />
and let there be applied to <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> the<br />
parallelogram <span data-ref="0146.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span> equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as its breadth;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a fifth binomial<br />
straight line.

![Proposition 64 Diagram](0146.g1.svg)

Let the same construction as before be made.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the side of a rational plus a medial<br />
area, divided at <span data-ref="0146.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square<br />
which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the<br />
rectangle contained by them rational. <span class="justification">[x. 40]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is medial,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0146.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>, is<br />
rational,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is rational and commensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> ; <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is binomial. <span class="justification">[x. 36]</span></p>

I say next that it is also a fifth binomial straight line.

<p class="sub-clause">For it can be proved similarly that the rectangle <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span><br />
is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,<br />
and that <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0146.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[x. 18]</span></p>
