## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Definition referenced:** Side of a rational plus a medial area (X. 40).
- **Breadth components:**
  - $DL = AC^2 + CB^2$ is medial $\implies DM$ is rational and incommensurable with $DE$ (X. 22).
  - $MF = 2 \cdot AC \cdot CB$ is rational $\implies MG$ is rational and commensurable with $DE$ (X. 20).
  - Here the *lesser* term $MG$ is commensurable with $DE$, characterizing the fifth binomial straight line.
