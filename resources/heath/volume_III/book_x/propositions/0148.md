I say that <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a sixth binomial straight line.

For let the same construction be made as before.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the side of<br />
the sum of two medial areas, divided<br />
at <span data-ref="0148.g1:C">$C$</span>,</p>

![Proposition 65 Diagram](0148.g1.svg)

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are straight lines<br />
incommensurable in square which<br />
make the sum of the squares on<br />
them medial, the rectangle contained<br />
by them medial, and moreover the<br />
sum of the squares on them incom-<br />
mensurable with the rectangle contained by them, <span class="justification">[X. 41]</span><br />
so that, in accordance with what was before proved, each of<br />
the rectangles <span data-ref="0148.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span> is medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is rational and<br />
incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is incom-<br />
mensurable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:< D L >">$DL$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0148.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is binomial. <span class="justification">[X. 36]</span></p>

I say next that it is also a sixth binomial straight line.

<p class="assertion">Similarly again we can prove that the rectangle <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span><br />
is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,<br />
and that <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>;<br />
and, for the same reason, the square on <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> is greater than<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> by the square on a straight line incom-<br />
mensurable in length with <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span> is commen-<br />
surable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> set out.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore <span data-ref="0148.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is a sixth binomial straight line.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
