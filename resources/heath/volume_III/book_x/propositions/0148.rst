:title: Proposition 65 (concluded)

I say that :g:`$DG$ <0148.g1:/ D G />` is a sixth binomial straight line.

For let the same construction be made as before.

.. subclause::

   | Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0148.g1:/ A B />` is the side of
   | the sum of two medial areas, divided
   | at :g:`$C$ <0148.g1:C>`,

.. picture:: 0148.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 65 Diagram

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AC$ <0148.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0148.g1:/ C B />` are straight lines
   | incommensurable in square which
   | make the sum of the squares on
   | them medial, the rectangle contained
   | by them medial, and moreover the
   | sum of the squares on them incom-
   | mensurable with the rectangle contained by them, :just:`[X. 41] <X.41>`
   | so that, in accordance with what was before proved, each of
   | the rectangles :g:`$DL$ <0148.g1:< D L >>`, :g:`$MF$ <0148.g1:< M F >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0148.g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0148.g1:/ M G />` is rational and
   | incommensurable in length with :g:`$DE$ <0148.g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   | And, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0148.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0148.g1:/ C B />` is incom-
   | mensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0148.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0148.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DL$ <0148.g1:< D L >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$MF$ <0148.g1:< M F >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />` is also incommensurable with :g:`$MG$ <0148.g1:/ M G />`;
   | :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`
   | therefore :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0148.g1:/ M G />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only;
   | therefore :g:`$DG$ <0148.g1:/ D G />` is binomial. :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

I say next that it is also a sixth binomial straight line.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly again we can prove that the rectangle :g:`$DK$ <0148.g1:/ D K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0148.g1:/ K M />`
   | is equal to the square on :g:`$MN$ <0148.g1:/ M N />`,
   | and that :g:`$DK$ <0148.g1:/ D K />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$KM$ <0148.g1:/ K M />`;
   | and, for the same reason, the square on :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />` is greater than
   | the square on :g:`$MG$ <0148.g1:/ M G />` by the square on a straight line incom-
   | mensurable in length with :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />`.

.. subclause::

   | And neither of the straight lines :g:`$DM$ <0148.g1:/ D M />`, :g:`$MG$ <0148.g1:/ M G />` is commen-
   | surable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$DE$ <0148.g1:/ D E />` set out.

.. qed::

   Therefore :g:`$DG$ <0148.g1:/ D G />` is a sixth binomial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
