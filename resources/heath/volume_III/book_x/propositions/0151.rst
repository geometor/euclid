PROPOSITION 67.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable in length with a bimedial straight line is itself also bimedial and the same in order.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` be bimedial, and let :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in
| length with :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />`;

.. picture:: 0151.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 67

| I say that :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` is bimedial and the same
| in order with :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />`.
