<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 37, 38]</span></p>

And let it be contrived that,<br />
as <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore also the remainder <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is to the remainder <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> as<br />
<span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 19]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are also commensurable with <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span><br />
respectively. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also medial. <span class="justification">[X. 23]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, as <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are commensurable in square only,</p>

<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also commensurable in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But they were also proved medial;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is bimedial.</p>

I say next that it is also the same in order with <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For since, as <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;<br />
therefore, alternately,<br />
as the square on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is the rect-<br />
angle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also commensurable with<br />
the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">If therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is rational,</p>

<p class="assertion">the rectangle <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is also rational,<br />
[and for this reason <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is a first bimedial]; <span class="justification">[X. 37]</span><br />
but if medial, medial, <span class="justification">[X. 23, Por.]</span><br />
and each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is a second bimedial.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 38]</span></p>

<p class="qed">And for this reason <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> will be the same in order with <span data-ref="0151.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
