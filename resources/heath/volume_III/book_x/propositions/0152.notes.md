# Transcription Notes: Folio 0152 (Page 148)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Header**: `148 BOOK X [x. 67`
- **Content**: Complete demonstration and conclusion of Proposition 67, establishing that any straight line commensurable in length with a bimedial straight line is itself bimedial and of the same order (first or second bimedial).
- **Continuity**:
  - Preceding: Begun on folio 0151 (page 147) with division of $AB$ at $E$.
  - Succeeding: Folio 0153 (page 149) begins Proposition 68.
