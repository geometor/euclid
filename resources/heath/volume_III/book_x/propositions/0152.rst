:title: Proposition 67 (conclusion)

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` are medial straight lines commensurable
   | in square only. :just:`[X. 37, 38]`

| And let it be contrived that,
| as :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also the remainder :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is to the remainder :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` as
   | :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[V. 19] <V.19>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` are also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`
   | respectively. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` are medial;

.. assertion:: [X. 23] <X.23>

   therefore :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` are also medial.

.. subclause::

   | And since, as :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`, :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`
   | and :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` are also commensurable in square only.

.. subclause::

   But they were also proved medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` is bimedial.

I say next that it is also the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />`.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is to :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />`, so is :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` to :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the square on :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is to the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`,
   | :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />`, so is the square on :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />` to the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`;
   | therefore, alternately,
   | as the square on :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is to the square on :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, so is the rect-
   | angle :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` to the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   | But the square on :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is also commensurable with
   | the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   If therefore the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0151.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0151.g1:/ E B />` is rational,

.. assertion::

   | the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0151.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0151.g1:/ F D />` is also rational,
   | [and for this reason :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` is a first bimedial]; :just:`[X. 37] <X.37>`
   | but if medial, medial, :just:`[X. 23, Por.]`
   | and each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` is a second bimedial.
   | :just:`[X. 38] <X.38>`

.. qed::

   And for this reason :g:`$CD$ <0151.g1:/ C D />` will be the same in order with :g:`$AB$ <0151.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
