### PROPOSITION 68.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with a major straight<br />
line is itself also major.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be major, and let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is major.

Let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be divided at <span data-ref="0153.g1:E">$E$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensur-<br />
able in square which make the sum of the squares<br />
on them rational, but the rectangle contained by<br />
them medial. <span class="justification">[x. 39]</span></p>

![Proposition 68](0153.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made as before.

<p class="sub-clause">Then since, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[v. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are also commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span><br />
respectively. <span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,<br />
alternately also,<br />
as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>; <span class="justification">[v. 16]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore also, *componendo*,<br />
as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>; <span class="justification">[v. 18]</span><br />
therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>,<br />
so is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[vi. 20]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, so also is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the squares on<br />
<span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;<br />
therefore also, alternately,<br />
as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so are the<br />
squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[v. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable with the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are also commensurable<br />
with the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.</p>
