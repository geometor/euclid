### PROPOSITION 68.

*A straight line commensurable with a major straight line is itself also major.*

Let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be major, and let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is major.

Let <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be divided at <span data-ref="0153.g1:E">$E$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial. [x. 39]

![Proposition 68](0153.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made as before.

Then since, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
therefore also, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 11]

But <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are also commensurable with <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> respectively. [x. 11]

And since, as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
alternately also,
as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>; [v. 16]
therefore also, *componendo*,
as <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>; [v. 18]
therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [vi. 20]

Similarly we can prove that, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, so also is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.

Therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;
therefore also, alternately,
as the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so are the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> to the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 16]

But the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are also commensurable with the squares on <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.
