### PROPOSITION 69.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with the side of a rational<br />
plus a medial area is itself also the side of a rational plus a<br />
medial area.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the side of a rational plus a medial area,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;<br />
it is to be proved that <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also the side of a<br />
rational plus a medial area.

Let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be divided into its straight lines at <span data-ref="0154.g1:E">$E$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensur-<br />
able in square which make the sum of the squares<br />
on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them<br />
rational. <span class="justification">[x. 40]</span></p>

![Proposition 69](0154.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made as before.

<p class="assertion">We can then prove similarly that<br />
<span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are incommensurable in square,<br />
and the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is commensurable<br />
with the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,<br />
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;<br />
so that the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is also medial, and<br />
the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> rational.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is the side of a rational plus a medial area.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
