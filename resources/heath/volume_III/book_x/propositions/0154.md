Therefore <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0153.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square which make, at the same time, the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial;

Therefore a straight line commensurable with the major straight line is major.

### PROPOSITION 69.

*A straight line commensurable with the side of a rational plus a medial area is itself also the side of a rational plus a medial area.*

Let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the side of a rational plus a medial area, and let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
it is to be proved that <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also the side of a rational plus a medial area.

Let <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be divided into its straight lines at <span data-ref="0154.g1:E">$E$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. [x. 40]

![Proposition 69](0154.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made as before.

We can then prove similarly that
<span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are incommensurable in square,
and the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is commensurable with the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;
so that the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is also medial, and the rectangle <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> rational.

Therefore <span data-ref="0154.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is the side of a rational plus a medial area.
<p align="right">Q. E. D.</p>
