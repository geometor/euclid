For <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is either greater or less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>.

First, let it be greater;
let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set out,
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> the rectangle <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> as breadth,
and let <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>, equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< D C >">$DC$</span>, be applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> as breadth.

![Proposition 71](0156.g1.svg)

Then, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is rational and is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also rational.

And it has been applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [x. 20]

Again, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> is medial and is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span> is also medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [x. 22]

And, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> is medial,
while <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is rational,
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>,
so that <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>; [vi. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>. [x. 11]

And both are rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> is a binomial straight line, divided at <span data-ref="0156.g1:H">$H$</span>. [x. 36]
