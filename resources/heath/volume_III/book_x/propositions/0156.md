For <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is either greater or less than <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>.

First, let it be greater;<br />
let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set out,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> the rectangle <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>,<br />
producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> as breadth,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>, equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< D C >">$DC$</span>, be applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span><br />
as breadth.

![Proposition 71](0156.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is rational and is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it has been applied to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> as breadth;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 20]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> is medial and is equal to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, pro-<br />
ducing <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> as breadth;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> is medial,<br />
while <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is rational,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0156.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0156.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0156.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> to <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>; <span class="justification">[vi. 1]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0156.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> is a binomial straight line, divided at <span data-ref="0156.g1:H">$H$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 36]</span></p>
