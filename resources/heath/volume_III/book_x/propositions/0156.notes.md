## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Application of Areas (*Parabole*)**: Essential Book X technique using Book I. 44 / VI. 1 to convert areas ($AB$ rational, $CD$ medial) into rectangles with a common rational height $EF$, transforming area relationships into linear breadths $EH, HK$.
- **Linear Breadths Properties**:
  - $EH$ is rational and commensurable in length with $EF$ (X. 20).
  - $HK$ is rational and incommensurable in length with $EF$ (X. 22).
  - Therefore $EH, HK$ are rational commensurable in square only, making $EK = EH + HK$ a binomial straight line (X. 36).
