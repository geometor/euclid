:title: Proposition 71 (cont.)

For :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is either greater or less than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`.

First, let it be greater; let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` be set out, let there be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` the rectangle :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< E G > = 0156.g1:< A B >>` to :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>`, producing :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` as breadth, and let :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`, :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< H I > = 0156.g1:< D C >>` to :g:`$DC$ <0156.g1:< D C >>`, be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is rational and is :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< A B > = 0156.g1:< E G >>` to :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[x. 20]`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` is medial and is :eq:`equal <0156.g1:< C D > = 0156.g1:< H I >>` to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[x. 22]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` is medial, while :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`, so that :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`.

.. subclause:: [vi. 1]

   But, as :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`, so is :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`. :just:`[x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a binomial straight line, divided at :g:`$H$ <0156.g1:H>`. :just:`[x. 36]`
