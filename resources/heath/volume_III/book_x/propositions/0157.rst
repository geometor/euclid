.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is greater than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>`,
   | while :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` is equal to :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` and :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also greater than :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>` ;
   | therefore :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also greater than :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

| The square, then, on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is greater than the square on
| :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` either by the square on a straight line commensurable
| in length with :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` or by the square on a straight line in-
| commensurable with it.

| First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a
| straight line commensurable with itself.

.. subclause::

   | Now the greater straight line :g:`$HE$ <0156.g1:/ H E />` is commensurable in
   | length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out ;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. II. 1]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a first binomial.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational ;
   | and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the
   | first binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is
   | binomial. :just:`[x. 54]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the "side" of :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is binomial ;
   | so that the "side" of :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also binomial.

| Next, let the square on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` be greater than the square
| on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable
| with :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now the greater straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable in
   | length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out ;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. II. 4]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a fourth binomial.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational ;
   | and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the
   | fourth binomial, the "side" of the area is the irrational straight
   | line called major. :just:`[x. 57]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is major ;
   | so that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also major.

.. subclause::

   Next, let :g:`$AB$ <0156.g1:< A B >>` be less than :g:`$CD$ <0156.g1:< C D >>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EG$ <0156.g1:< E G >>` is also less than :g:`$HI$ <0156.g1:< H I >>`,
   | so that :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is also less than :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

| Now the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />`
| either by the square on a straight line commensurable with
| :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable
| with it.
