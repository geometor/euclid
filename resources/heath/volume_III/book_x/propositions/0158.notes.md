# Transcription Notes: Folio 0158 (Page 154)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 154 continues and concludes Euclid's proof of Book X, Proposition 71 (the second case, where the square on $HK$ exceeds that on $HE$ by a straight line incommensurable with $HK$, establishing that the "side" of $AD$ is a side of a rational plus a medial area).
- **Q.E.D.**: Formal conclusion of Proposition 71.
- **Heath Commentary**: Begins Heath's analytical commentary on Proposition 71, translating the problem into modern algebraic terms ($k\rho^2$ and $\sqrt{\lambda}\rho^2$, applied to width $\sigma$, yielding segments $u, v$).

- **Propositions Cited**:
  - [X. Deff. II. 2]: Definition of second binomial straight line.

  - [X. Deff. II. 5]: Definition of fifth binomial straight line.
