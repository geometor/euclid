:title: Proposition 71 (conclusion)

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with itself.

.. subclause::

   Now the lesser straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 2]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a second binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational, and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the second binomial, the side of the square equal to it is a first bimedial; :just:`[X. 55] <X.55>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is a first bimedial, so that the "side" of :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also a first bimedial.

Next, let the square on :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />` be greater than the square on :g:`$HE$ <0156.g1:/ H E />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$HK$ <0156.g1:/ H K />`.

.. subclause::

   Now the lesser straight line :g:`$EH$ <0156.g1:/ E H />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. II. 5]

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0156.g1:/ E K />` is a fifth binomial.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EF$ <0156.g1:/ E F />` is rational; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fifth binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is a side of a rational plus a medial area. :just:`[X. 58] <X.58>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`$EI$ <0156.g1:< E I >>` is a side of a rational plus a medial area, so that the "side" of the area :g:`$AD$ <0156.g1:< A D >>` is also a side of a rational plus a medial area.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

A rational area being of the form $k\rho^2$, and a medial area of the form $\sqrt\lambda . \rho^2$, the problem is to classify

$$\sqrt{k\rho^2 + \sqrt\lambda . \rho^2}$$

according to the different possible relations between $k, \lambda$.

Put

$$\begin{aligned}
\sigma u &= k\rho^2, \\
\sigma v &= \sqrt\lambda . \rho^2.
\end{aligned}$$

Then, since the former rectangle is rational, the latter medial, $u$ is rational and $\frown \sigma$, $v$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$.

Also the rectangles are incommensurable; so that

$$u \smile v.$$

Hence $u, v$ are rational and $\sim$; whence $(u + v)$ is a binomial straight line.
