Now, since each of the areas *AB*, *CD* is medial,

But, as *EG* is to *HI*, so is *EH* to *HK*; [VI. 1]
therefore *EH* is incommensurable in length with *HK*. [X. 11]

Therefore *EH*, *HK* are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore *EK* is binomial. [X. 36]

But the square on *EH* is greater than the square on *HK* either by the square on a straight line commensurable with *EH* or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

therefore *EK* is a third binomial. [X. Deff. II. 3]
