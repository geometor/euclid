# Transcription Notes: Folio 0164 (Page 160)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 160 contains the proof, diagram, and Q.E.D. of Proposition 74, establishing the *first apotome of a medial straight line*.
- **Heath Commentary**: Analyzes the first apotome of a medial straight line algebraically as $k^{1/4}\rho \sim k^{3/4}\rho$, the direct subtraction counterpart to the first bimedial straight line ($k^{1/4}\rho + k^{3/4}\rho$).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Running Head**: `160 BOOK X [X. 74`
- **Propositions Cited**:
  - [cf. II. 7]: Square on whole plus square on subtracted part equals twice rectangle contained by whole and subtracted part together with square on remainder.
  - [X. 16]: Incommensurability of sum and constituent magnitudes.
  - [X. Def. 4]: Definition of irrational straight line.
- **Algebraic Structure**:
  - $(x^2 + y^2)$ is medial.
  - $2xy$ is rational.
  - $(x - y)^2 = (x^2 + y^2) - 2xy$ is incommensurable with rational $2xy$, hence $(x - y)^2$ is irrational, making $(x - y)$ irrational.
