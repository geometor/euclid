therefore <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;  
therefore <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is an apotome. [x. 73]

But <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span> is rational,  
and the rectangle contained by a rational and an irrational straight line is irrational, [deduction from x. 20]  
and its "side" is irrational.

And <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0165.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0165.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is irrational.

And let it be called a **second apotome of a medial straight line.**
