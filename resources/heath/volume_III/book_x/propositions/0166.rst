.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`DH <0165.g1:< D H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DH <0165.g1:< D H >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it has been applied to the rational straight line :g:`DI <0165.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`DF <0165.g1:/ D F />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DF <0165.g1:/ D F />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`DI <0165.g1:/ D I />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`; therefore the square on :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />` is also incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />` are commensurable with the square on :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>` and twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />` is commensurable with the rectangle :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />` is incommensurable with the squares on :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`DE <0165.g1:< D E >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`DH <0165.g1:< D H >>` to twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0165.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0165.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DE <0165.g1:< D E >>` is incommensurable with :g:`DH <0165.g1:< D H >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`DE <0165.g1:< D E >>` is to :g:`DH <0165.g1:< D H >>`, so is :g:`GD <0165.g1:/ G D />` to :g:`DF <0165.g1:/ D F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`GD <0165.g1:/ G D />` is incommensurable with :g:`DF <0165.g1:/ D F />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`GD <0165.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`DF <0165.g1:/ D F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`FG <0165.g1:/ F G />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`DI <0165.g1:/ D I />` is rational, and the rectangle contained by a rational and an irrational straight line is irrational, :just:`[deduction from X. 20]` and its "side" is irrational.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`AC <0165.g1:/ A C />` is the "side" of :g:`FE <0165.g1:< F E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AC <0165.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. qed::

   And let it be called a :term:`**second apotome of a medial straight line** <second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

$$\begin{aligned}
x^2 + y^2 &= \sigma u, \\
2xy &= \sigma v.
\end{aligned}$$
