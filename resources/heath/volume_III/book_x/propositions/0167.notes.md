## 2. Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 76 Enunciation**: Defines the *minor* (ἐλάσσων) straight line: $x - y$, where $x \smile_2 y$, $x^2 + y^2$ is rational, and $2xy$ is medial.
- **Proof Structure**: Short and elegant proof: since $x^2 + y^2$ is rational and $2xy$ is medial, $(x^2 + y^2) \smile 2xy$. By *convertendo* (Euclid II. 7 and X. 16), $(x^2 + y^2) \smile (x - y)^2$. Since $x^2 + y^2$ is rational, $(x - y)^2$ is irrational, and thus $x - y$ is irrational.
- **Symbolism**: Heath uses $\smile$ for incommensurable in length/area, and $\sim$ for commensurable in square only.
