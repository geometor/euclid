Here $x, y$ are of the form [cf. x. 34]
$$\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2(1 + k^2)}} \sqrt{\sqrt{1 + k^2} + k}, \quad \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2(1 + k^2)}} \sqrt{\sqrt{1 + k^2} - k}.$$

By hypothesis, $(x^2 + y^2)$ is a medial, $xy$ a rational, area;  
thus
$$(x^2 + y^2) \smile 2xy,$$
and therefore
$$(x - y)^2 \smile 2xy,$$
whence $(x - y)^2$, and consequently $(x - y)$, is irrational.

The irrational straight line
$$\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2(1 + k^2)}} \sqrt{\sqrt{1 + k^2} + k} - \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2(1 + k^2)}} \sqrt{\sqrt{1 + k^2} - k}$$
is called *that which produces with a rational area a medial whole* or more<br />
literally *that which with a rational area makes the whole medial* (ἡ μετὰ ῥητοῦ<br />
μέσον τὸ ὅλον ποιοῦσα). Here "produces" means "produces when a square<br />
is described on it." A clearer way of expressing the meaning would be to call<br />
this straight line the "*side*" of a medial minus a rational area corresponding<br />
to the "*side*" of a rational plus a medial area [x. 40].

### PROPOSITION 78.

<p class="enunciation">If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line<br />
which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which<br />
with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial,<br />
twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the<br />
squares on them incommensurable with twice the rectangle<br />
contained by them, the remainder is irrational; and let it be<br />
called <strong>that which produces with a medial area a<br />
medial whole.</strong></p>

For from the straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> let there be subtracted the<br />
straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> incommensurable in<br />
square with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and fulfilling the<br />
given conditions ; <span class="justification">[X. 35]</span>

I say that the remainder <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the<br />
irrational straight line called <strong>that<br />
which produces with a medial<br />
area a medial whole.</strong>
