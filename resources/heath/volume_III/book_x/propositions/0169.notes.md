## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek Terminology**: ἡ μετὰ ῥητοῦ μέσον τὸ ὅλον ποιοῦσα (literally "that which with a rational area makes the whole medial"). Heath explains that "produces" translates ποιοῦσα, meaning "produces when a square is described on it" (i.e. $\sqrt{M - R}$).
- **Proposition 78**: The sixth irrational difference: $x - y$ where $x \smile_2 y$, $x^2 + y^2$ is medial, $2xy$ is medial, and $(x^2 + y^2) \smile 2xy$.
