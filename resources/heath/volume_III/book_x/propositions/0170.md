Therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, [II. 7]  
so that <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>.

Now, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial and is equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span>,  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as breadth;  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>. [x. 22]

Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial and is equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>,  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span> is medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> as breadth;  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>. [x. 22]

And, since the squares on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are incommensurable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>,  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, so also is <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>; [VI. 1]  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [x. 11]

And both are rational;  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only.  
Therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is an apotome. [x. 73]

And <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span> is rational;  
but the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and an apotome is irrational, [deduction from x. 20]  
and its "side" is irrational.

And <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>;  
therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is irrational.

And let it be called **that which produces with a medial area a medial whole.**

<p align="right">Q. E. D.</p>
