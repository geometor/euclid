<p class="assertion">Therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span> is equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span><br />
so that <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial<br />
and is equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> as breadth ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is medial and is<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span> is medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> as breadth ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the squares on <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are incommensurable<br />
with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0169.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, so also is <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> to <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> ; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only.<br />
Therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ D I /">$DI$</span> is rational ;<br />
but the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and an<br />
apotome is irrational, <span class="justification">[deduction from X. 20]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">and its "side" is irrational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0169.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0169.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is irrational.</p>

<p class="qed">And let it be called <strong>that which produces with a<br />
medial area a medial whole.</strong><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
