## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proof Structure**: Uses the geometric algebra of Book II. 7 ($FE = AC^2$) and the application of areas to a rational line $DI$. $DE$ and $DH$ are both medial and incommensurable, which gives $DG$ and $DF$ rational, incommensurable with $DI$, and incommensurable with each other in length, so $GD, DF$ are commensurable in square only. Thus $FG = DG - DF$ is an apotome (X. 73). Rectangle $FE = DI \cdot FG$ is irrational by deduction from X. 20, hence its side $AC$ is irrational.
