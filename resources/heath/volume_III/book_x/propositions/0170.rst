.. assertion::

   Therefore the remainder :g:`FE <0169.g1:< F E >>` is equal to the square on :g:`AC <0169.g1:/ A C />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>` so that :g:`AC <0169.g1:/ A C />` is the "side" of :g:`FE <0169.g1:< F E >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the sum of the squares on :g:`AB <0169.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0169.g1:/ B C />` is medial and is equal to :g:`DE <0169.g1:< D E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DE <0169.g1:< D E >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`DI <0169.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`DG <0169.g1:/ D G />` as breadth ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DG <0169.g1:/ D G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`DI <0169.g1:/ D I />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0169.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0169.g1:/ B C />` is medial and is equal to :g:`DH <0169.g1:< D H >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DH <0169.g1:< D H >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`DI <0169.g1:/ D I />`, producing :g:`DF <0169.g1:/ D F />` as breadth ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DF <0169.g1:/ D F />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`DI <0169.g1:/ D I />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the squares on :g:`AB <0169.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0169.g1:/ B C />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`AB <0169.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`BC <0169.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`DE <0169.g1:< D E >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`DH <0169.g1:< D H >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`DE <0169.g1:< D E >>` is to :g:`DH <0169.g1:< D H >>`, so also is :g:`DG <0169.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`DF <0169.g1:/ D F />` ;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`DG <0169.g1:/ D G />` is incommensurable with :g:`DF <0169.g1:/ D F />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`GD <0169.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`DF <0169.g1:/ D F />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. Therefore :g:`FG <0169.g1:/ F G />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`DI <0169.g1:/ D I />` is rational ; but the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and an apotome is irrational, :just:`[deduction from X. 20]`

.. assertion::

   and its "side" is irrational.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`AC <0169.g1:/ A C />` is the "side" of :g:`FE <0169.g1:< F E >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AC <0169.g1:/ A C />` is irrational.

.. qed::

   And let it be called :term:`**that which produces with a medial area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`
