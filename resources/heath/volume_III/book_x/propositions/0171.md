In this case $x, y$ have respectively the forms [cf. x. 35]
$$\frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 + \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}, \quad \frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}.$$

Suppose that
$$\begin{aligned}
x^2 + y^2 &= \sigma u, \\
2xy &= \sigma v.
\end{aligned}$$

By hypothesis, the areas $\sigma u, \sigma v$ are medial;  
therefore $u, v$ are both rational and $\smile \sigma$ .........................................(1).

Further
$$\sigma u \smile \sigma v,$$
so that
$$u \smile v$$ .................................................(2).

Hence [(1), (2)] $u, v$ are rational and $\sim$,  
so that $(u - v)$ is the irrational straight line called apotome [x. 73].  
Thus $\sigma (u - v)$ is an irrational area,  
so that $(x - y)^2$, and consequently $(x - y)$, is irrational.

The irrational straight line
$$\frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 + \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}} - \frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}$$
is called *that which produces* [i.e. when a square is described on it] *with a*<br />
*medial area a medial whole*, more literally *that which with a medial area makes*<br />
*the whole medial* (ἡ μετὰ μέσου μέσον τὸ ὅλον ποιοῦσα). A clearer phrase (to<br />
us) would be the "*side*" of the difference between two medial areas, correspond-<br />
ing to the "*side*" of (the sum of) two medial areas [x. 41].

### PROPOSITION 79.

<p class="enunciation">To an apotome only one rational straight line can be<br />
annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0171.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be an apotome, and <span data-ref="0171.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> an annex to it ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0171.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0171.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are rational<br />
straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>
