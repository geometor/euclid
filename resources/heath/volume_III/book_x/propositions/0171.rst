In this case $x, y$ have respectively the forms [cf. x. 35]

$$\frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 + \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}, \quad \frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}.$$

Suppose that

$$\begin{aligned}
x^2 + y^2 &= \sigma u, \\
2xy &= \sigma v.
\end{aligned}$$

By hypothesis, the areas $\sigma u, \sigma v$ are medial; therefore $u, v$ are both rational and $\smile \sigma$ .........................................(1).

Further

$$\sigma u \smile \sigma v,$$

so that

$$u \smile v$$

.................................................(2).

Hence [(1), (2)] $u, v$ are rational and $\sim$, so that $(u - v)$ is the irrational straight line called apotome [x. 73]. Thus $\sigma (u - v)$ is an irrational area, so that $(x - y)^2$, and consequently $(x - y)$, is irrational.

The irrational straight line

$$\frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 + \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}} - \frac{\rho \lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt 2} \sqrt{1 - \frac{k}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}}$$

is called *that which produces* [i.e. when a square is described on it] *with a medial area a medial whole*, more literally *that which with a medial area makes the whole medial* (:term:`ἡ μετὰ μέσου μέσον τὸ ὅλον ποιοῦσα <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area>`). A clearer phrase (to us) would be the "*side*" of the difference between two medial areas, corresponding to the "*side*" of (the sum of) two medial areas [x. 41].

PROPOSITION 79.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To an apotome only one rational straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and :g:`$BC$ <0171.g1:/ B C />` an annex to it ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. picture:: 0171.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 79 Diagram

I say that no other rational straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let :g:`$BD$ <0171.g1:/ B D />` be so annexed ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` are also rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0171.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0171.g1:/ D B />` is also the excess of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0171.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0171.g1:/ C B />`, for both exceed by the same, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0171.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`
