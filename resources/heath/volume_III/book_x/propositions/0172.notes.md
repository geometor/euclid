## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Theorem of Surds**: Heath explains that Proposition 79 is equivalent to: if $a - \sqrt{b} = x - \sqrt{y}$ (or $\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{x} - \sqrt{y}$), then $a = x$ and $b = y$.
- **Proof Structure**: Uses the impossibility of a medial area exceeding another medial area by a rational area (X. 26), exactly dual to Proposition 42 for binomial straight lines.
- **Proposition 80 Enunciation**: Begins the series of uniqueness theorems for the six apotomes of medial straight lines and higher irrationals (Definitions IV / Propositions 80–84).
