# Transcription Notes: Folio 0173 (Page 169)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- Folio 0173 (page 169) completes Book X, Proposition 80 (uniqueness of the annex to a first apotome of a medial straight line), which began on page 168 (folio 0172).
- Contains the remainder of the proof of Proposition 80, its Q.E.D., and Heath's algebraic commentary illustrating the contradiction of Proposition 26 when $x - y = x' - y'$.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Internal References**: Proposition 74 (definition of first apotome of a medial), Book II. 7 (difference identity for squares and rectangles), Proposition 15 and Proposition 23 Porism (addition of commensurable medial areas), and Proposition 26 (a medial area cannot exceed a medial area by a rational area).
- **Heath's Commentary**: Translates the Euclidean argument into algebraic form: equating $x^2 + y^2 - (x'^2 + y'^2) = 2xy - 2x'y'$ shows that the difference between two medial areas would be equal to the difference of two rational areas (a rational area), contradicting X. 26.
