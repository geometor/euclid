:title: Proposition 80 (continued)

| For let :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` be a first apotome of a medial straight line,
| and let :g:`$BC$ <0173.g1:/ B C />` be an annex to :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` are medial
   | straight lines commensurable in
   | square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` which they
   | contain is rational; :just:`[X. 74] <X.74>`

.. picture:: 0173.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 80 diagram

| I say that no other medial straight line can be annexed to
| :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />` which is commensurable with the whole in square only
| and which contains with the whole a rational area.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` also be so annexed;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` are medial straight lines commensurable
   | in square only and such that the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` which
   | they contain is rational. :just:`[X. 74] <X.74>`

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` over
   | twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` is also the excess of the squares
   | on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />`,
   | for they exceed by the same, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0173.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore, alternately, the excess of the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />`
   | over the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` is also the excess of twice the
   | rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` over twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   | But twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` exceeds twice the rect-
   | angle :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area,
   | for both are rational.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0173.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0173.g1:/ D B />` also exceed the squares
   | on :g:`$AC$ <0173.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0173.g1:/ C B />` by a rational area:
   | which is impossible,
   | for both are medial :just:`[X. 15 and 23, Por.]`, and a medial area does
   | not exceed a medial by a rational area. :just:`[X. 26] <X.26>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

| Suppose, if possible, that the same :term:`*first apotome of a medial* <first-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line>` straight line
| can be expressed in terms of the required character in two ways, so that

$$x - y = x' - y',$$

and suppose that $x > x'$.

| In this case $x^2 + y^2$, $(x'^2 + y'^2)$ are both medial areas, and $2xy$, $2x'y'$ are both
| rational areas;
| and

$$x^2 + y^2 - (x'^2 + y'^2) = 2xy - 2x'y'.$$

| Hence x. 26 is contradicted again;
| therefore etc.
