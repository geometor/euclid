# Transcription Notes: Folio 0174 (Page 170)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- Folio 0174 (page 170) opens Book X, Proposition 81 (uniqueness of the annex to a second apotome of a medial straight line).
- Contains the enunciation, setting out, and initial geometrical construction applying areas to a rational straight line $EF$.
- The proof continues on folio 0175 (page 171).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Internal References**: Proposition 75 (definition and properties of second apotome of a medial), Book II. 7.
- **Terminology**: The term "side" ($\pi\lambda\epsilon\upsilon\rho\acute{\alpha}$) is placed in quotation marks by Heath to indicate that $AB$ is the side of the square area $EL$.
- **Geometrical Construction**: Rather than direct algebraic difference, Euclid applies the medial sum of squares and twice the medial rectangle to a rational line $EF$, converting areas into linear breadths ($EM, HM, EN$).
