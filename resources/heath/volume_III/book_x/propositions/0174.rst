PROPOSITION 81.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To a second apotome of a medial straight line only one medial straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` be a second apotome of a medial straight line
| and :g:`$BC$ <0174.g1:/ B C />` an annex to :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight
   | lines commensurable in square only and
   | such that the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` which
   | they contain is medial. :just:`[X. 75] <X.75>`

.. picture:: 0174.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 81 diagram

| I say that no other medial straight line
| can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` which is commen-
| surable with the whole in square only and
| which contains with the whole a medial
| rectangle.

.. subclause::

   | For, if possible, let :g:`$BD$ <0174.g1:/ B D />` also be so
   | annexed;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` are also medial straight
   | lines commensurable in square only and
   | such that the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` which
   | they contain is medial. :just:`[X. 75] <X.75>`

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />` be set out,
| let :g:`$EG$ <0174.g1:< E G >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`,
| producing :g:`$EM$ <0174.g1:/ E M />` as breadth,
| and let :g:`$HG$ <0174.g1:< H G >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` be sub-
| tracted, producing :g:`$HM$ <0174.g1:/ H M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`, :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`
   | so that :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />` is the “side” of :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>`.

| Again, let :g:`$EI$ <0174.g1:< E I >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />` be applied
| to :g:`$EF$ <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EN$ <0174.g1:/ E N />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EL$ <0174.g1:< E L >>` is also equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0174.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder :g:`$HI$ <0174.g1:< H I >>` is equal to twice the rectangle
   | :g:`$AD$ <0174.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0174.g1:/ D B />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are medial straight lines,

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0174.g1:/ C B />` are also medial.
