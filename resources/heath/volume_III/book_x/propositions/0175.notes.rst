- **Chained Deductions**:

  - Application of medial area to rational straight line $EF$ yields rational breadth incommensurable in length with $EF$ (X. 22).

  - Hence $EG \smile HG$, which by VI. 1 implies $EM \smile HM$ in length.
  - Since both $EM, HM$ are rational, they are commensurable in square only, so $EH$ is an apotome with annex $HM$ (X. 73).
