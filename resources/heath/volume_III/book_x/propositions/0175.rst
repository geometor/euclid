.. subclause::

   And they are equal to :g:`EG <0174.g1:< E G >>`;

.. assertion:: [X. 15 and 23, Por.]

   therefore :g:`EG <0174.g1:< E G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`EF <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`EM <0174.g1:/ E M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`EM <0174.g1:/ E M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`EF <0174.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` is medial,

.. assertion:: [X. 23, Por.]

   twice the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is equal to :g:`HG <0174.g1:< H G >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`HG <0174.g1:< H G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`EF <0174.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`HM <0174.g1:/ H M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`HM <0174.g1:/ H M />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`EF <0174.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` are commensurable in square only,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />` to the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />` is incommensurable with the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`. :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` are commensurable with the square on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, while twice the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` is commensurable with the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />` are incommensurable with twice the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`. :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`EG <0174.g1:< E G >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`, while :g:`GH <0174.g1:< G H >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`AC <0174.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`CB <0174.g1:/ C B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`EG <0174.g1:< E G >>` is incommensurable with :g:`HG <0174.g1:< H G >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`EG <0174.g1:< E G >>` is to :g:`HG <0174.g1:< H G >>`, so is :g:`EM <0174.g1:/ E M />` to :g:`HM <0174.g1:/ H M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`EM <0174.g1:/ E M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`MH <0174.g1:/ M H />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`EM <0174.g1:/ E M />`, :g:`MH <0174.g1:/ M H />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`EH <0174.g1:/ E H />` is an apotome, and :g:`HM <0174.g1:/ H M />` an annex to it. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`
