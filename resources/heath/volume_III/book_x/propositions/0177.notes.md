## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Logical Flow of Proposition 82**:
  - By II. 7, $(AD^2 + DB^2) - (AC^2 + CB^2) = 2(AD \cdot DB) - 2(AC \cdot CB)$.
  - The left-hand side is the difference of two rational areas, hence rational.
  - The right-hand side is the difference of two medial areas.
  - By X. 26, a medial area cannot exceed a medial area by a rational area; contradiction.
- **Heath's Commentary**: Expresses the contradiction in algebraic form: $(x^2 + y^2) - (x'^2 + y'^2) = 2xy - 2x'y'$.
- **Proposition 83 Enunciation**: Introduces the fifth species of irrational straight line formed by subtraction: "that which produces with a rational area a medial whole" ($\dot{\eta}\ \mu\epsilon\tau\grave{\alpha}\ \dot{\rho}\eta\tau\mathrm{o}\tilde{\upsilon}\ \mu\acute{\epsilon}\sigma\mathrm{o}\nu\ \tau\grave{\mathrm{o}}\ \dot{\mathrm{o}}\lambda\mathrm{o}\nu\ \pi\mathrm{o}\iota\mathrm{o}\tilde{\upsilon}\sigma\alpha$).
