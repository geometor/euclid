<p class="enunciation">on them medial and twice the rectangle contained by them both<br />
medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares<br />
on them.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the straight line which produces with a medial<br />
area a medial whole,<br />
and <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> an annex to it;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square<br />
which fulfil the aforesaid conditions. <span class="justification">[X. 78]</span></p>

![Proposition 84](0179.g1.svg)

I say that no other straight line can be annexed to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
which fulfils the aforesaid conditions.

For, if possible, let <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be so annexed,
<p class="assertion">so that <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> are also straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> added together<br />
medial, twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> medial, and also the<br />
squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> incommensurable with twice the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 78]</span></p>

Let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set out,<br />
let <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
producing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> as breadth,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span> equal to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> be applied<br />
to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> as breadth;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the square on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span>, is equal<br />
to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>.</p>

Again, let <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E I >">$EI$</span> equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be applied<br />
to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E N /">$EN$</span> as breadth.
<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E L >">$EL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span>,<br />
is equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>.</p>
