:title: Proposition 84

.. enunciation::

   on them medial and twice the rectangle contained by them both medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />` be the straight line which produces with a medial
| area a medial whole,
| and :g:`$BC$ <0179.g1:/ B C />` an annex to it;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` are straight lines incommensurable in square
   | which fulfil the aforesaid conditions. :just:`[X. 78] <X.78>`

.. picture:: 0179.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 84

| I say that no other straight line can be annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />`
| which fulfils the aforesaid conditions.

For, if possible, let :g:`$BD$ <0179.g1:/ B D />` be so annexed,

.. assertion::

   | so that :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` are also straight lines incommensurable in
   | square which make the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` added together
   | medial, twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` medial, and also the
   | squares on :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` incommensurable with twice the rectangle
   | :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />`. :just:`[X. 78] <X.78>`

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />` be set out,
| let :g:`$EG$ <0179.g1:< E G >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` be applied to :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`,
| producing :g:`$EM$ <0179.g1:/ E M />` as breadth,
| and let :g:`$HG$ <0179.g1:< H G >>` equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` be applied
| to :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$HM$ <0179.g1:/ H M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, the square on :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />` :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`, is equal
   | to :g:`$EL$ <0179.g1:< E L >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />` is the "side" of :g:`$EL$ <0179.g1:< E L >>`.

| Again, let :g:`$EI$ <0179.g1:< E I >>` equal to the squares on :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` be applied
| to :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`, producing :g:`$EN$ <0179.g1:/ E N />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to :g:`$EL$ <0179.g1:< E L >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle :g:`$AD$ <0179.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$DB$ <0179.g1:/ D B />` :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`,
   | is equal to :g:`$HI$ <0179.g1:< H I >>`.
