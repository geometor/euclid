<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial<br />
and is equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, pro-<br />
ducing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> as breadth ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial and is<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, pro-<br />
ducing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> as breadth ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are incommensurable<br />
with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 1, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is an apotome, and <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> an annex to it. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is again an apotome and<br />
<span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span> an annex to it.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore to an apotome different rational straight lines<br />
are annexed which are commensurable with the wholes in<br />
square only :<br />
which was proved impossible. <span class="justification">[X. 79]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore no other straight line can be so annexed to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> only one straight line can be annexed<br />
which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which<br />
with the whole makes the squares on them added together<br />
medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and<br />
also the squares on them incommensurable with twice the<br />
rectangle contained by them.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
