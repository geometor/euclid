Now, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial
and is equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, pro-
ducing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length
with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. \[[x. 22](x.22.md)\]

Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is medial and is
equal to <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span> is also medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, pro-
ducing <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length
with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. \[[x. 22](x.22.md)\]

And, since the squares on <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> are incommensurable
with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,
<span data-ref="0179.g1:< E G >">$EG$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0179.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span>.
\[[VI. 1, x. 11](VI.1.md)\]

And both are rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>, <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable
in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is an apotome, and <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span> an annex to it. \[[x. 73](x.73.md)\]

Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is again an apotome and
<span data-ref="0179.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span> an annex to it.

Therefore to an apotome different rational straight lines
are annexed which are commensurable with the wholes in
square only:
which was proved impossible. \[[x. 79](x.79.md)\]

Therefore no other straight line can be so annexed to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

Therefore to <span data-ref="0179.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> only one straight line can be annexed
which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which
with the whole makes the squares on them added together
medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and
also the squares on them incommensurable with twice the
rectangle contained by them.

$$\text{Q. E. D.}$$
