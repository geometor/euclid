:title: Proposition 84 (concluded)

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the sum of the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` is medial
   | and is equal to :g:`$EG$ <0179.g1:< E G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EG$ <0179.g1:< E G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`, pro-
   | ducing :g:`$EM$ <0179.g1:/ E M />` as breadth ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EM$ <0179.g1:/ E M />` is rational and incommensurable in length
   | with :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   | Again, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` is medial and is
   | equal to :g:`$HG$ <0179.g1:< H G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HG$ <0179.g1:< H G >>` is also medial.

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`, pro-
   | ducing :g:`$HM$ <0179.g1:/ H M />` as breadth ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$HM$ <0179.g1:/ H M />` is rational and incommensurable in length
   | with :g:`$EF$ <0179.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[X. 22] <X.22>`

.. subclause::

   | And, since the squares on :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />` are incommensurable
   | with twice the rectangle :g:`$AC$ <0179.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0179.g1:/ C B />`,
   | :g:`$EG$ <0179.g1:< E G >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$HG$ <0179.g1:< H G >>` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EM$ <0179.g1:/ E M />` is also incommensurable in length with :g:`$MH$ <0179.g1:/ M H />`.
   | :just:`[VI. 1, X. 11] <VI.1, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$EM$ <0179.g1:/ E M />`, :g:`$MH$ <0179.g1:/ M H />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only ;
   | therefore :g:`$EH$ <0179.g1:/ E H />` is an apotome, and :g:`$HM$ <0179.g1:/ H M />` an annex to it. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   | Similarly we can prove that :g:`$EH$ <0179.g1:/ E H />` is again an apotome and
   | :g:`$HN$ <0179.g1:/ H N />` an annex to it.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore to an apotome different rational straight lines
   | are annexed which are commensurable with the wholes in
   | square only :
   | which was proved impossible. :just:`[X. 79] <X.79>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore no other straight line can be so annexed to :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   | Therefore to :g:`$AB$ <0179.g1:/ A B />` only one straight line can be annexed
   | which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which
   | with the whole makes the squares on them added together
   | medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and
   | also the squares on them incommensurable with twice the
   | rectangle contained by them. :just:`Q. E. D.`
