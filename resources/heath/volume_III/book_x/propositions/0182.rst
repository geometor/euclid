PROPOSITION 85.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To find the first apotome.

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0182.g1:A>` be set out,
| and let :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` be commensurable in length with :g:`$A$ <0182.g1:A>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is also rational.

.. picture:: 0182.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 85

| Let two square numbers :g:`$DE$ <0182.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0182.g1:/ E F />` be set out, and let their
| difference :g:`$FD$ <0182.g1:/ F D />` not be square ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has :g:`$ED$ <0182.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`$DF$ <0182.g1:/ D F />` the ratio which a square
   | number has to a square number.

| Let it be contrived that,
| as :g:`$ED$ <0182.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0182.g1:/ D F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` ;
| :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` is also rational ;
   | therefore :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$ED$ <0182.g1:/ E D />` has not to :g:`$DF$ <0182.g1:/ D F />` the ratio which a square
   | number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number ;
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only ;
   | therefore :g:`$BC$ <0182.g1:/ B C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

I say next that it is also a first apotome.

| For let the square on :g:`$H$ <0182.g1:H>` be that by which the square on
| :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now since, as :g:`$ED$ <0182.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$FD$ <0182.g1:/ F D />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` to
   | the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, *convertendo*, :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`
   | as :g:`$DE$ <0182.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0182.g1:/ E F />`, so is the square on :g:`$GB$ <0182.g1:/ G B />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0182.g1:H>`.
