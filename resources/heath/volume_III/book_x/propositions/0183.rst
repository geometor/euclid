:title: Proposition 85 (concluded)

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DE$ <0182.g1:/ D E />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0182.g1:/ E F />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number, for each is square ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0182.g1:/ G B />` also has to the square on :g:`$H$ <0182.g1:H>` the ratio which a square number has to a square number ; therefore :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0182.g1:H>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` by the square on :g:`$H$ <0182.g1:H>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0182.g1:/ G C />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   And the whole :g:`$BG$ <0182.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0182.g1:A>` set out.

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 1]

   Therefore :g:`$BC$ <0182.g1:/ B C />` is a first apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore the first apotome :g:`$BC$ <0182.g1:/ B C />` has been found. (Being) that which it was required to find.

----

Take $k\rho$ commensurable in length with $\rho$, the given rational straight line.
Let $m^2, n^2$ be square numbers such that $(m^2 - n^2)$ is not square.
Take $x$ such that

$$m^2 : (m^2 - n^2) = k^2\rho^2 : x^2 \tag{1},$$

so that

$$x = k\rho \frac{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}}{m}$$

$$= k\rho \sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}, \text{ say.}$$

Then shall $k\rho - x$, or $k\rho - k\rho\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}$, be a :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`.

For ($\alpha$) it follows from (1) that $x$ is rational but incommensurable with $k\rho$, whence $k\rho, x$ are rational and $\sim$, so that $(k\rho - x)$ is an apotome.

($\beta$) If $y^2 = k^2\rho^2 - x^2$, then, by (1), :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`,

$$m^2 : n^2 = k^2\rho^2 : y^2,$$

whence $y$, that is, $\sqrt{k^2\rho^2 - x^2}$, is commensurable in length with $k\rho$.

And $k\rho \frown \rho$ ; therefore $k\rho - x$ is a :term:`*first apotome* <first-apotome>`.

As explained in the note to x. 48, the first apotome

$$k\rho - k\rho\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2}$$

is one of the roots of the equation

$$x^2 - 2k\rho \cdot x + \lambda^2 k^2\rho^2 = 0.$$
