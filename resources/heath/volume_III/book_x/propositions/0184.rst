PROPOSITION 86.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To find the second apotome.

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0184.g1:A>` be set out, and :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />` com-
| mensurable in length with :g:`$A$ <0184.g1:A>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />` is rational.

.. picture:: 0184.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 86

| Let two square numbers :g:`$DE$ <0184.g1:/ D E />`,
| :g:`$EF$ <0184.g1:/ E F />` be set out, and let their
| difference :g:`$DF$ <0184.g1:/ D F />` not be square.

| Now let it be contrived that,
| as :g:`$FD$ <0184.g1:/ F D />` is to :g:`$DE$ <0184.g1:/ D E />`, so is the square
| on :g:`$CG$ <0184.g1:/ C G />` to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />`. :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the square on :g:`$CG$ <0184.g1:/ C G />` is commensurable with the
   | square on :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$CG$ <0184.g1:/ C G />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />` is also rational ;
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the square on :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />` has not to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion:: [X. 9] <X.9>

   :g:`$CG$ <0184.g1:/ C G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CG$ <0184.g1:/ C G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0184.g1:/ G B />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only ;
   | therefore :g:`$BC$ <0184.g1:/ B C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

I say next that it is also a second apotome.

| For let the square on :g:`$H$ <0184.g1:H>` be that by which the square on
| :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />`.

.. subclause::

   | Since then, as the square on :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />`,
   | so is the number :g:`$ED$ <0184.g1:/ E D />` to the number :g:`$DF$ <0184.g1:/ D F />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, *convertendo*,
   | as the square on :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is to the square on :g:`$H$ <0184.g1:H>`, so is :g:`$DE$ <0184.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0184.g1:/ E F />`.
   | :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   And each of the numbers :g:`$DE$ <0184.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0184.g1:/ E F />` is square ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` has to the square on :g:`$H$ <0184.g1:H>` the ratio
   | which a square number has to a square number ;
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0184.g1:H>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   | And the square on :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />`
   | by the square on :g:`$H$ <0184.g1:H>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$BG$ <0184.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0184.g1:/ G C />`
