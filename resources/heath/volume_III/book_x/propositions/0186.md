therefore <span data-ref="0185.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0185.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0185.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is an apotome. \[[x. 73](x.73.md)\]

I say next that it is also a third apotome.
