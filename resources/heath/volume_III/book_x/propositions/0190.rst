:title: Proposition 88 (continued), Proposition 89

For, by (1), $x$ is rational and $\sim k\rho$.
Also $\sqrt{k^2\rho^2 - x^2}$ is incommensurable with $k\rho$, since

$$(m+n) : m = k^2\rho^2 : (k^2\rho^2 - x^2)\text{,}$$

and the ratio $(m+n) : m$ is not that of a square number to a square number.
And $k\rho \sim \rho$.
As explained in the note on x. 51, the *fourth apotome*

$$k\rho - \frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}$$

is the lesser root of the quadratic equation

$$x^2 - 2k\rho \cdot x + \frac{\lambda}{1+\lambda} k^2\rho^2 = 0.$$

PROPOSITION 89.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To find the fifth apotome.

.. picture:: 0190.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 89 Diagram

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>` be set out,
| and let :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` be commensurable in length
| with :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` is rational.

| Let two numbers :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` be set out
| such that :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` again has not to either of the
| numbers :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` the ratio which a square
| number has to a square number;
| and let it be contrived that, as :g:`$FE$ <0190.g1:/ F E />` is to :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />`,
| so is the square on :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />` is also
   | rational; :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | Now since, as :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` is to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />`, so is the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to
   | the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`,
   | while :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` has not to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />` the ratio which a square number
   | has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   And both are rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />`, :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only;
   | therefore :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`
