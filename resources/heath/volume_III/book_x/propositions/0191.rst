:title: Proposition 89 (continued)

I say next that it is also a fifth apotome.

| For let the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>` be that by which the square on
| :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`.

.. subclause::

   | Since then, as the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is to the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`,
   | so is :g:`$DE$ <0190.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0190.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, as :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />` is to :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />`, so is the square on
   | :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`. :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$ED$ <0190.g1:/ E D />` has not to :g:`$DF$ <0190.g1:/ D F />` the ratio which a square number
   | has to a square number;

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` to the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   | And the square on :g:`$BG$ <0190.g1:/ B G />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`
   | by the square on :g:`$H$ <0190.g1:H>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$GC$ <0190.g1:/ G C />`
   | by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length
   | with :g:`$GB$ <0190.g1:/ G B />`.

.. subclause::

   | And the annex :g:`$CG$ <0190.g1:/ C G />` is commensurable in length with the
   | rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0190.g1:A>` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 5]

   therefore :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` is a fifth apotome.

.. qed::

   Therefore the fifth apotome :g:`$BC$ <0190.g1:/ B C />` has been found. :just:`Q. E. D.`

| Let $\rho$, $k\rho$ and the numbers $m$, $n$ of the last proposition be taken.
| Take $x$ such that

$$n : (m+n) = k^2\rho^2 : x^2 \text{ ..............................(1).}$$

In this case $x > k\rho$, and

$$x = k\rho \sqrt{\frac{m+n}{n}}$$

$$= k\rho \sqrt{1+\lambda}\text{, say.}$$

| Then shall $(x - k\rho)$, or $(k\rho\sqrt{1+\lambda} - k\rho)$, be a :term:`*fifth apotome* <fifth-apotome>`.
| For, by (1), $x$ is rational and $\sim k\rho$.
| And since, by (1), $(m+n) : m = x^2 : (x^2 - k^2\rho^2)$,
| $\sqrt{x^2 - k^2\rho^2}$ is incommensurable with $x$.

| Also $k\rho \sim \rho$.
| As explained in the note on x. 52, the :term:`*fifth apotome* <fifth-apotome>`

$$k\rho\sqrt{1+\lambda} - k\rho$$

is the lesser root of the quadratic

$$x^2 - 2k\rho\sqrt{1+\lambda}\cdot x + \lambda k^2\rho^2 = 0.$$
