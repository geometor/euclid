PROPOSITION 90.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To find the sixth apotome.

.. picture:: 0192.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 90

| Let a rational straight line :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` be set out, and three
| numbers :g:`$E$ <0192.g1:E>`, :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0192.g1:/ C D />` not having
| to one another the ratio which
| a square number has to a square
| number ;
| and further let :g:`$CB$ <0192.g1:/ C B />` also not have
| to :g:`$BD$ <0192.g1:/ B D />` the ratio which a square
| number has to a square number.

| Let it be contrived that, as
| :g:`$E$ <0192.g1:E>` is to :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />`,
| and, as :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0192.g1:/ C D />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` to the square
| on :g:`$GH$ <0192.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[X. 6, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   | Now since, as :g:`$E$ <0192.g1:E>` is to :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` to the
   | square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` is also rational ;
   | therefore :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$E$ <0192.g1:E>` has not to :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />` the ratio which a square
   | number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   | therefore neither has the square on :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` to the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number ;
   | therefore :g:`$A$ <0192.g1:A>` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>`

.. subclause::

   | Again, since, as :g:`$BC$ <0192.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0192.g1:/ C D />`, so is the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` to
   | the square on :g:`$GH$ <0192.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$GH$ <0192.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$FG$ <0192.g1:/ F G />` is rational ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$GH$ <0192.g1:/ G H />` is also rational ;
   | therefore :g:`$GH$ <0192.g1:/ G H />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | number has to a square number,
