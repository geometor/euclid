<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a first apotome, let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be its annex ;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the whole <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with the rational<br />
straight line <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,<br />
and the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line commensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. Deff. III. 1]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">if therefore there be applied to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to<br />
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> and deficient by a square<br />
figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. <span class="justification">[X. 17]</span></p>

![Proposition 91 Diagram](0195.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0195.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,<br />
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.</p>

And through the points <span data-ref="0195.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F I /">$FI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be drawn<br />
parallel to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is commensurable<br />
in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span></p>
