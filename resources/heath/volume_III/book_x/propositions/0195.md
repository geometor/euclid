For, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is a first apotome, let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be its annex ;
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable
in square only. [x. 73]
And the whole <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with the rational
straight line <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,
and the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>
by the square on a straight line commensurable in length
with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>; [x. Deff. III. 1]
if therefore there be applied to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> and deficient by a square
figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. [x. 17]

Let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0195.g1:E">$E$</span>,
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square
on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.
And through the points <span data-ref="0195.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F I /">$FI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be drawn
parallel to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

Now, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of
the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [x. 15]
But <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is commensurable
in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. [x. 12]
