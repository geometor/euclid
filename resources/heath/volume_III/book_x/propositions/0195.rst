.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`AD <0195.g1:/ A D />` is a first apotome, let :g:`DG <0195.g1:/ D G />` be its annex ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`GD <0195.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   And the whole :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />` set out, and the square on :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`GD <0195.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />`; :just:`[X. Deff. III. 1]`

.. assertion::

   if therefore there be applied to :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on :g:`DG <0195.g1:/ D G />` and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. :just:`[X. 17] <X.17>`

Let :g:`DG <0195.g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <0195.g1:/ D E / = 0195.g1:/ E G />` at :g:`E <0195.g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`EG <0195.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the rectangle :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />` is commensurable with :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />`.

And through the points :g:`E <0195.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0195.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0195.g1:G>` let :g:`EH <0195.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`FI <0195.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`GK <0195.g1:/ G K />` be drawn parallel to :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`AG <0195.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable with :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>`
