<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is rational ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is also rational,<br />
so that each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> is also rational. <span class="justification">[X. 19]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is also rational<br />
and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> ; <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span><br />
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is medial. <span class="justification">[X. 21]</span></p>

Now let the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, and let<br />
there be subtracted the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> having a common angle<br />
with it, the angle <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L P M )">$LPM$</span>, and equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> are about the same diameter.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 17]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span> ; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,<br />
as was before proved, <span class="justification">[Lemma after X. 53]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is equal to the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L O >">$LO$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0195.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span> and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also equal to the squares <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.</p>
