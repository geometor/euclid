And <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is rational;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is also rational,
so that each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> is also rational. [x. 19]

Now, since <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of
the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. [x. 15]

But <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length
with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is also rational
and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; [x. 13]
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is medial. [x. 21]

Now let the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, and let
there be subtracted the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> having a common angle
with it, the angle <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L P M )">$LPM$</span>, and equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>;
therefore the squares <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> are about the same diameter.
[VI. 26]

Let <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.
Since then the rectangle contained by <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to
the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [VI. 17]

But, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,
and, as <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span>. [v. 11]

But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,
as was before proved, [Lemma after x. 53]
and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is equal to the square <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< K F >">$KF$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L O >">$LO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0195.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span> and <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also equal to the squares <span data-ref="0195.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0195.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0195.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0195.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is the "side" of <span data-ref="0195.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.
