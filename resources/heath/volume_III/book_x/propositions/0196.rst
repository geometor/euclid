.. subclause::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`AF <0195.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`FG <0195.g1:/ F G />` is also rational, so that each of the rectangles :g:`AI <0195.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`FK <0195.g1:< F K >>` is also rational. :just:`[X. 19] <X.19>`

   But :g:`DG <0195.g1:/ D G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />` ;

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`DE <0195.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`EG <0195.g1:/ E G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`AC <0195.g1:/ A C />` ; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>` therefore each of the rectangles :g:`DH <0195.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`EK <0195.g1:< E K >>` is medial. :just:`[X. 21] <X.21>`
