I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0199.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a first apotome of a<br />
medial straight line.

![Book X, Proposition 92](0199.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only, <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span><br />
and the annex <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is commensurable with the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,<br />
while the square on the whole <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on the annex <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commen-<br />
surable in length with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. Deff. III. 2]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the square on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable<br />
with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore, if there be applied to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to<br />
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> and deficient by a square<br />
figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. <span class="justification">[X. 17]</span></p>

Let then <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0199.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,<br />
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each<br />
of the straight lines <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;</p>
