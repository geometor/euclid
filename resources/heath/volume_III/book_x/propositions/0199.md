I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0199.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a first apotome of a
medial straight line.

![Book X, Proposition 92](0199.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable
in square only, [x. 73]
and the annex <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is commensurable with the rational straight
line <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,
while the square on the whole <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square
on the annex <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commen-
surable in length with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>. [x. Deff. III. 2]

Since then the square on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square
on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable
with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,
therefore, if there be applied to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> and deficient by a square
figure, it divides it into commensurable parts. [x. 17]

Let then <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0199.g1:E">$E$</span>,
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square
on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each
of the straight lines <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [x. 15]
