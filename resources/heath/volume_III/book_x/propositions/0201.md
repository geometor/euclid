But <span data-ref="0199.g1:< T S >">$TS$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>;

But <span data-ref="0199.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> was proved medial;

There is an evident flaw in the text in the place (Heiberg, p. 282,
ll. 17—20: translation p. 196 above) where it is said that "since then <span data-ref="0199.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>
are medial and are equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>, the squares on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>
are also medial; *therefore <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are also medial straight lines commensurable
in square only.*" It is not till the last lines of the proposition (Heiberg, p. 284,
ll. 17, 18) that it is proved that <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are *incommensurable in length*. What
should have been proved in the former passage is that the squares on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>
are commensurable, so that <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are commensurable in square (not
commensurable in square *only*). I have supplied the step in the note below:
"Also $x^2 \mathbin{\frown} y^2$, since $u \mathbin{\frown} v$." Theon seems to have observed the omission and
to have put "and commensurable with one another" after "medial" in the
passage quoted, though even this does not show *why* the squares on <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0199.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>
are commensurable. One MS. (V) also has "only" ($\mu\acute{o}\nu\omicron\nu$) erased after
"commensurable in square."
