### PROPOSITION 93.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a<br />
third apotome, the “side” of the area is a second apotome of a<br />
medial straight line.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and the third apotome <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;<br />
I say that the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a second apotome of<br />
a medial straight line.

<p class="sub-clause">For let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only,<br />
and neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is commensurable<br />
in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,<br />
while the square on the whole <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on the annex <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> by the square on a straight line commen-<br />
surable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 3]</span></p>

![Proposition 93](0203.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable<br />
with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, if there be applied to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to<br />
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> and deficient by a square<br />
figure, it will divide it into commensurable parts. <span class="justification">[x. 17]</span></p>

Let then <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0203.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,<br />
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.
