### PROPOSITION 93.

*If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a third apotome, the “side” of the area is a second apotome of a medial straight line.*

For let the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be contained by the rational straight line <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and the third apotome <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
I say that the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a second apotome of a medial straight line.

For let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,
and neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,
while the square on the whole <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square on the annex <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>. [x. Deff. III. 3]

![Proposition 93](0203.g1.svg)

Since then the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,
therefore, if there be applied to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> and deficient by a square figure, it will divide it into commensurable parts. [x. 17]
Let then <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0203.g1:E">$E$</span>,
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.
