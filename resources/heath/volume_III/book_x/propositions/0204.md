Let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F I /">$FI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be drawn through the points <span data-ref="0203.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:G">$G$</span><br />
parallel to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are commensurable;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are commensurable in length,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are so also. <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> is medial. <span class="justification">[x. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is also rational<br />
and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span><br />
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is medial. <span class="justification">[x. 21]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are commensurable in square only;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span><br />
with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>

Now let the square <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> be constructed equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>,<br />
and let there be subtracted <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> and being about<br />
the same angle with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> are about the same diameter. <span class="justification">[VI. 26]</span><br />
Let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 17]</span></p>
