Let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F I /">$FI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be drawn through the points <span data-ref="0203.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:G">$G$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.
Therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are commensurable;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>. [vi. 1, x. 11]
And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are commensurable in length,
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [x. 15]
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> are so also. [x. 13]
Therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> is medial. [x. 21]

Again, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. [x. 15]
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; [x. 13]
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is medial. [x. 21]
And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are commensurable in square only,
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>. [x. 13]
But, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>; [vi. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>. [x. 11]

Now let the square <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> be constructed equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>,
and let there be subtracted <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> and being about the same angle with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> are about the same diameter. [vi. 26]
Let <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.
Now, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [vi. 17]
