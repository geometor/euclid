| Let :g:`$EH$ <0203.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$FI$ <0203.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`$GK$ <0203.g1:/ G K />` be drawn through the points :g:`$E$ <0203.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0203.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0203.g1:G>`
| parallel to :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are commensurable;
   | therefore :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is also commensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>`. :just:`[VI. 1, x. 11]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are commensurable in length,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of
   | the straight lines :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is rational and incommensurable in length
   | with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 13]

   so that :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0203.g1:/ F G />` are so also.

.. assertion:: [x. 21]

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0203.g1:< F K >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />` is also commensurable in length with each of
   | the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`. :just:`[x. 15]`

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` is rational and incommensurable in length
   | with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0203.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />` is also rational
   | and incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0203.g1:/ A C />`; :just:`[x. 13]`
   | therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$DH$ <0203.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>` is medial. :just:`[x. 21]`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />` are commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$GD$ <0203.g1:/ G D />`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$AG$ <0203.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />`, and :g:`$DG$ <0203.g1:/ D G />`
   | with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 13]

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$AF$ <0203.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`;

.. assertion:: [x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$AI$ <0203.g1:< A I >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$EK$ <0203.g1:< E K >>`.

   | on :g:`$EG$ <0203.g1:/ E G />`,
