But, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,
and, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>; [vi. 1]
therefore also, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>; [v. 11]
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also a mean proportional between the squares <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L O >">$LO$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>;
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is also equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0203.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span> and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span>, that is, to the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.

I say that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is a second apotome of a medial straight line.
For, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> were proved medial, and are equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>,
therefore each of the squares on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is also medial;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is medial.
And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>, [vi. 1, x. 11]
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> is also commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>.
Again, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> was proved incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>,
that is, the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>;
so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>; [vi. 1, x. 11]
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in square only.

I say next that they also contain a medial rectangle.
For, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> was proved medial, and is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>,
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is also medial,
so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a medial rectangle.
