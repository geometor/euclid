<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>; <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also a mean proportional between the squares<br />
<span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L O >">$LO$</span>, and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0203.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is also equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0203.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0203.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span>, that is, to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.</p>

I say that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is a second apotome of a medial straight<br />
line.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> were proved medial, and are equal to the<br />
squares on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the squares on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is also medial;<br />
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> is also commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> was proved incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0203.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>,<br />
that is, the square on <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>;<br />
so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> is also incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only.</p>

I say next that they also contain a medial rectangle.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0203.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> was proved medial, and is equal to the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is also medial,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0203.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only which contain a medial rectangle.</p>
