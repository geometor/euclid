### PROPOSITION 94.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a<br />
fourth apotome, the “side” of the area is minor.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and the fourth apotome <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;<br />
I say that the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is minor.

<p class="sub-clause">For let <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only,<br />
<span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line<br />
<span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,<br />
and the square on the whole <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on the annex <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> by the square on a straight line incommen-<br />
surable in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 4]</span></p>

![Proposition 94](0207.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the square on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable<br />
in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, if there be applied to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to<br />
the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> and deficient by a square<br />
figure, it will divide it into incommensurable parts. <span class="justification">[x. 18]</span></p>

Let then <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0207.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
let there be applied to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> a parallelogram equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> and deficient by a square figure,<br />
and let it be the rectangle <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>.</p>
