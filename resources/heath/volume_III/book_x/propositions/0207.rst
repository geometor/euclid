PROPOSITION 94.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a fourth apotome, the “side” of the area is minor.

| For let the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight
| line :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />` and the fourth apotome :g:`$AD$ <0207.g1:/ A D />`;
| I say that the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` is minor.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`$AD$ <0207.g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only,
   | :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line
   | :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />` set out,
   | and the square on the whole :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square
   | on the annex :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` by the square on a straight line incommen-
   | surable in length with :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`, :just:`[x. Deff. III. 4]`

.. picture:: 0207.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 94

.. subclause::

   | Since then the square on :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square
   | on :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable
   | in length with :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, if there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to
   | the fourth part of the square on :g:`$GD$ <0207.g1:/ G D />` and deficient by a square
   | figure, it will divide it into incommensurable parts. :just:`[x. 18]`

| Let then :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` be bisected at :g:`$E$ <0207.g1:E>`,
| let there be applied to :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square
| on :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure,
| and let it be the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`.
