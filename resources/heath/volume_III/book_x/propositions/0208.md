Let <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F I /">$FI$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0207.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:G">$G$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

Since then <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is rational. [x. 19]
Again, since <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,
and both are rational,
therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is medial. [x. 21]
Again, since <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0207.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>. [vi. 1, x. 11]
Now let the square <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span> be constructed equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>,
and let there be subtracted <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> and about the same angle, the angle <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L P M )">$LPM$</span>.
Therefore the squares <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span> are about the same diameter. [vi. 26]
Let <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

Since then the rectangle <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>,
therefore, proportionally, as <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [vi. 17]
But, as <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>,
and, as <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> is to <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0207.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>; [vi. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span>. [v. 11]
But <span data-ref="0207.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is also a mean proportional between the squares <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A I >">$AI$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, and <span data-ref="0207.g1:< F K >">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0207.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< E K >">$EK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L O >">$LO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>;
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0207.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0207.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span> and <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>.

Since, then, the whole <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A K >">$AK$</span> is equal to the squares <span data-ref="0207.g1:< L M >">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,
and, in these, <span data-ref="0207.g1:< D K >">$DK$</span> is equal to the gnomon <span data-ref="0207.g1:< U V W >">$UVW$</span> and the square <span data-ref="0207.g1:< N O >">$NO$</span>,
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0207.g1:< S T >">$ST$</span>, that is, to the square on <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0207.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0207.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.
