| Let :g:`$EH$ <0207.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$FI$ <0207.g1:/ F I />`, :g:`$GK$ <0207.g1:/ G K />` be drawn through :g:`$E$ <0207.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0207.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0207.g1:G>` parallel to
| :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0207.g1:/ B D />`.

.. subclause::

   | Since then :g:`$AG$ <0207.g1:/ A G />` is rational and commensurable in length
   | with :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion:: [x. 19]

   therefore the whole :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | Again, since :g:`$DG$ <0207.g1:/ D G />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$AC$ <0207.g1:/ A C />`,
   | and both are rational,

.. assertion:: [x. 21]

   therefore :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1, x. 11]

   therefore :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` is also incommensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`.

| Now let the square :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>` be constructed equal to :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>`,
| and let there be subtracted :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>` equal to :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>` and about the
| same angle, the angle :g:`$LPM$ <0207.g1:< L P M )>`.
| Therefore the squares :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>` are about the same
| diameter. :just:`[VI. 26] <VI.26>`
| Let :g:`$PR$ <0207.g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   | Since then the rectangle :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square
   | on :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` to :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`.
   | :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   | But, as :g:`$AF$ <0207.g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` to :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>`,
   | and, as :g:`$EG$ <0207.g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`$FG$ <0207.g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>` to :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[VI. 1] <VI.1>`

   therefore :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>` is a mean proportional between :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>`, :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$MN$ <0207.g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between the squares
   | :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`,
   | and :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` is equal to :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>` to :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`;

   But :g:`$DH$ <0207.g1:< D H >>` is equal to :g:`$EK$ <0207.g1:< E K >>`, and :g:`$LO$ <0207.g1:< L O >>` is equal to :g:`$MN$ <0207.g1:< M N >>`;

   | and :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`.

   | Since, then, the whole :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is equal to the squares
   | :g:`$LM$ <0207.g1:< L M >>`, :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`,
   | and, in these, :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`$UVW$ <0207.g1:< U V W >>` and the
   | square :g:`$NO$ <0207.g1:< N O >>`,
