I say that *LN* is the irrational straight line called minor.
For, since *AK* is rational and is equal to the squares on *LP*, *PN*,
therefore the sum of the squares on *LP*, *PN* is rational.
Again, since *DK* is medial,
and *DK* is equal to twice the rectangle *LP*, *PN*,
therefore twice the rectangle *LP*, *PN* is medial.
And, since *AI* was proved incommensurable with *FK*,
therefore the square on *LP* is also incommensurable with the square on *PN*.
Therefore *LP*, *PN* are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but twice the rectangle contained by them medial.
Therefore *LN* is the irrational straight line called minor; [x. 76]
and it is the “side” of the area *AB*.
Therefore the “side” of the area *AB* is minor.

Q. E. D.

We have here to find and classify the straight line
$$\sqrt{\rho\left(k\rho - \frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}\right)}.$$
As usual, we find $u$, $v$ from the equations
$$\left.\begin{aligned}
u + v &= k\rho \\
uv &= \frac{1}{4}\frac{k^2\rho^2}{1+\lambda}
\end{aligned}\right\} \tag{1},$$
and then, giving $u$, $v$ their values, we put
$$\left.\begin{aligned}
x^2 &= \rho u \\
y^2 &= \rho v
\end{aligned}\right\} \tag{2}.$$
Then $(x - y)$ is the required square root.
This is proved in the same way as before, and, as before, it is proved that
$$xy = \frac{1}{2}\frac{k\rho^2}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}.$$
Now, from (1), by x. 18,
$$u \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} v;$$
therefore
$$\rho u \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} \rho v,$$
or
$$x^2 \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} y^2,$$
so that $x$, $y$ are incommensurable in square.
And $x^2 + y^2$, or $\rho(u + v)$, is a rational area ($k\rho^2$).
But $2xy = \frac{k\rho^2}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}$, which is a medial area.
Hence [x. 76] $(x - y)$ is the irrational straight line called *minor*.
