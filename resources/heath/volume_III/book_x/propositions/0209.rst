:title: Proposition 94 (conclusion and commentary)

I say that :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />` is the irrational straight line called minor.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AK$ <0207.g1:< A K >>` is rational and is equal to the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the sum of the squares on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is medial, and :g:`$DK$ <0207.g1:< D K >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore twice the rectangle :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` is medial.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AI$ <0207.g1:< A I >>` was proved incommensurable with :g:`$FK$ <0207.g1:< F K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />` is also incommensurable with the square on :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LP$ <0207.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$PN$ <0207.g1:/ P N />` are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but twice the rectangle contained by them medial.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LN$ <0207.g1:/ L N />` is the irrational straight line called minor; :just:`[x. 76]` and it is the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the “side” of the area :g:`$AB$ <0207.g1:< A B >>` is minor. :just:`Q. E. D.`

We have here to find and classify the straight line

$$\sqrt{\rho\left(k\rho - \frac{k\rho}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}\right)}.$$

As usual, we find $u$, $v$ from the equations

$$\left.\begin{aligned}
u + v &= k\rho \\
uv &= \frac{1}{4}\frac{k^2\rho^2}{1+\lambda}
\end{aligned}\right\} \tag{1},$$

and then, giving $u$, $v$ their values, we put

$$\left.\begin{aligned}
x^2 &= \rho u \\
y^2 &= \rho v
\end{aligned}\right\} \tag{2}.$$

Then $(x - y)$ is the required square root.
This is proved in the same way as before, and, as before, it is proved that

$$xy = \frac{1}{2}\frac{k\rho^2}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}.$$

Now, from (1), by x. 18,

$$u \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} v;$$

therefore

$$\rho u \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} \rho v,$$

or

$$x^2 \mathbin{\sim\kern-0.6em\raisebox{0.1ex}{/}} y^2,$$

so that $x$, $y$ are incommensurable in square.
And $x^2 + y^2$, or $\rho(u + v)$, is a rational area ($k\rho^2$).
But $2xy = \frac{k\rho^2}{\sqrt{1+\lambda}}$, which is a medial area.
Hence [x. 76] $(x - y)$ is the irrational straight line called *minor*.
