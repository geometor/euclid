### PROPOSITION 96.

*If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a sixth apotome, the "side" of the area is a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.*

For let the area <span data-ref="0213.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be contained by the rational straight line <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and the sixth apotome <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
I say that the "side" of the area <span data-ref="0213.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.

![Proposition 96 Diagram](0213.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only,
neither of them is commensurable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> set out,
and the square on the whole <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is greater than the square on the annex <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>. \hfill [x. Deff. III. 6]
