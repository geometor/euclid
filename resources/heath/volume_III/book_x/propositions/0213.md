### PROPOSITION 96.

<p class="enunciation">If an area be contained by a rational straight line and a<br />
sixth apotome, the “side” of the area is a straight line which<br />
produces with a medial area a medial whole.</p>

For let the area <span data-ref="0213.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be contained by the rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and the sixth apotome <span data-ref="0213.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>;<br />
I say that the “side” of the area <span data-ref="0213.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> is a straight line which<br />
produces with a medial area a medial whole.
