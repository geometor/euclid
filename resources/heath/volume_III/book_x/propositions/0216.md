### PROPOSITION 97.

<p class="enunciation">The square on an apotome applied to a rational straight<br />
line produces as breadth a first apotome.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be an apotome, and <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> rational,<br />
and to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and producing <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> as breadth;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is a first apotome.

![Proposition 97 Diagram](0216.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For let <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

To <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, and <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span>.

<p class="assertion">Therefore the whole <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and, in these, <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;<br />
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> be bisected at the point <span data-ref="0216.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0216.g1:N">$N$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;<br />
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F N O E >">$FO$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$LN$</span> is equal to the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are rational,<br />
and <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$DM$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$DM$</span> is rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it has been applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,<br />
producing <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 20]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is medial, and<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is medial.</p>
