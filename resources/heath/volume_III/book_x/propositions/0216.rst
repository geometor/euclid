PROPOSITION 97.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   The square on an apotome applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a first apotome.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` rational,
| and to :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CE$ <0216.g1:< C F E D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` as breadth;
| I say that :g:`$CF$ <0216.g1:/ C F />` is a first apotome.

.. picture:: 0216.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 97 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only. :just:`[x. 73]`

| To :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0216.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0216.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BG$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,
   | and, in these, :g:`$CE$ <0216.g1:< C F E D >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0216.g1:/ A B />`;
   | therefore the remainder :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle
   | :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

| Let :g:`$FM$ <0216.g1:/ F M />` be bisected at the point :g:`$N$ <0216.g1:N>`,
| and let :g:`$NO$ <0216.g1:/ N O />` be drawn through :g:`$N$ <0216.g1:N>` parallel to :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`;
| therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0216.g1:< F N O E >>`, :g:`$LN$ <0216.g1:< N M L O >>` is equal to the
| rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` are rational,
   | and :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DM$ <0216.g1:< C M L D >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | And it has been applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`,
   | producing :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CM$ <0216.g1:/ C M />` is rational and commensurable in length with
   | :g:`$CD$ <0216.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 20]`

.. subclause::

   | Again, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />` is medial, and
   | :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0216.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0216.g1:/ B G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0216.g1:< F M L E >>` is medial.
