<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are rational,<br />
while twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is medial,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are incommensurable with<br />
twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$DM$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C M L D >">$DM$</span> is to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

I say next that it is also a first apotome.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is a mean proportional<br />
between the squares on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>;<br />
therefore, as <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0216.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>, <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> is equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0216.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> <span class="justification">[VI. 17]</span>, that is, to the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0216.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0216.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> to <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0216.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>
