### PROPOSITION 98.

*The square on a first apotome of a medial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a second apotome.*

Let <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a first apotome of a medial straight line and <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> a rational straight line,
and to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> as breadth;
I say that <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is a second apotome.

For let <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle. [x. 74]

![Proposition 98](0219.g1.svg)

To <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> as breadth, and <span data-ref="0219.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> as breadth;
therefore the whole <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is also medial. [x. 15 and 23, Por.]

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [x. 22]
