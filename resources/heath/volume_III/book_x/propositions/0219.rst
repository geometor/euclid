And, since $\rho^2 \sim k\rho^2$,

\sigma x &\sim \sigma y, \\

Hence [(5), (6)], by x. 17,

$$\sqrt{(x + y)^2 - (2z)^2} \sim (x + y).$$

| And [(4)] $(x + y)$, $2z$ are rational and $\sim$,
| while [(2)] $(x + y) \sim \sigma$;

PROPOSITION 98.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   The square on a first apotome of a medial straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a second apotome.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />` be a first apotome of a medial straight line and
| :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line,
| and to :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CE$ <0219.g1:< C F E D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`, producing :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` as breadth;
| I say that :g:`$CF$ <0219.g1:/ C F />` is a second apotome.

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0219.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />` are medial straight lines commensurable in
   | square only which contain a rational rectangle. :just:`[x. 74]`

.. picture:: 0219.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 98

| To :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0219.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, producing :g:`$CK$ <0219.g1:/ C K />` as breadth, and :g:`$KL$ <0219.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square
| on :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`, producing :g:`$KM$ <0219.g1:/ K M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0219.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0219.g1:/ B G />`;
   | therefore :g:`$CL$ <0219.g1:< C M L D >>` is also medial. :just:`[x. 15 and 23, Por.]`

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`, pro-
   | ducing :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CM$ <0219.g1:/ C M />` is rational and incommensurable in length with
   | :g:`$CD$ <0219.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 22]`
