Now, since <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
and, in these, the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span>,
therefore the remainder, twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>, is equal to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>. [II. 7]

But twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is rational.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> is also rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [x. 20]

Now, since the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span>, is medial, while twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, is rational,
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>. [x. 11]

And both are rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is an apotome. [x. 73]

I say next that it is also a second apotome.

For let <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0219.g1:N">$N$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0219.g1:N">$N$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0219.g1:< F N O E >">$FO$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>.

Now, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is a mean proportional between the squares on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
and the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span>,
the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>,
and the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0219.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>,
and, as <span data-ref="0219.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0219.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ K M /">$MK$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore, as <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> to <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>; [v. 11]
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>, <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> [VI. 17], that is, to the fourth part of the square on <span data-ref="0219.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>.
