### PROPOSITION 99.

<p class="enunciation">The square on a second apotome of a medial straight line<br />
applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a third<br />
apotome.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a second apotome of a medial straight line,<br />
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> rational,<br />
and to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> equal to the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> as breadth;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is a third apotome.

![Proposition 99](0222.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only which contain a medial rectangle. <span class="justification">[x. 75]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,<br />
producing <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> as breadth,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be applied to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>,<br />
producing <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> as breadth;

<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is also medial. <span class="justification">[x. 15 and 23, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the whole <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>, and, in these, <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$LF$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span></p>

Let then <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> be bisected at the point <span data-ref="0222.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F N O E >">$FO$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is equal to the rect-<br />
angle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>.</p>
