<p class="sub-clause">But the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is medial;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F E /">$EF$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are commensurable in square only,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also incommensurable with the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
and twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are incommensurable with<br />
twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

I say next that it is also a third apotome.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is a mean proportional<br />
between the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>;<br />
therefore, as <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>.</p>
