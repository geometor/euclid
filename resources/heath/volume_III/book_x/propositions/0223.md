But the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is medial;
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is also medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F E /">$EF$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> as breadth;
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> is also rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [x. 22]

And, since <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are commensurable in square only,
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is also incommensurable with the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. [VI. 1, x. 11]

But the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,
and twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> are incommensurable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. [x. 13]

But <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> to <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>; [VI. 1]
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>. [x. 11]

And both are rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is an apotome. [x. 73]

I say next that it is also a third apotome.

For, since the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> is commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>,
so that <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. [VI. 1, x. 11]

And, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is a mean proportional between the squares on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,
<span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is also a mean proportional between <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span>, <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0222.g1:< C K H D >">$CH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0222.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> to <span data-ref="0222.g1:< K M L H >">$KL$</span>.
