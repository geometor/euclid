| Thus [(1), (2), (3)] $(x + y)$, $2z$ are rational and $\sim$,
| so that $(x + y) - 2z$ is an *apotome*.

$(\beta)$ $\sigma x \sim \sigma y$, so that $x \sim y$.

And, as before,

$$xy = \frac{1}{4}(2z)^2.$$

Therefore [x. 17]

$$\sqrt{(x + y)^2 - (2z)^2} \sim (x + y).$$

And neither $(x + y)$ nor $2z$ is $\sim \sigma$.

Therefore $(x + y) - 2z$ is a :term:`*third apotome* <third-apotome>`.

It is of course equal to

$$\frac{\rho^2}{\sigma}\left\{\frac{k + \lambda}{\sqrt{k}} - 2\sqrt{\lambda}\right\}.$$

PROPOSITION 100.
----------------

.. enunciation::

   The square on a minor straight line applied to a rational straight line produces as breadth a fourth apotome.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0225.g1:/ A B />` be a minor and :g:`$CD$ <0225.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line, and
| to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0225.g1:/ C D />` let :g:`$CE$ <0225.g1:< C F E D >>` be applied equal to the
| square on :g:`$AB$ <0225.g1:/ A B />` and producing :g:`$CF$ <0225.g1:/ C F />` as breadth;
| I say that :g:`$CF$ <0225.g1:/ C F />` is a fourth apotome.

.. picture:: 0225.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 100

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0225.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0225.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0225.g1:/ B G />` are straight lines incommensurable in
   | square which make the sum of the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0225.g1:/ B G />`
   | rational, but twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0225.g1:/ B G />` medial. :just:`[x. 76]`

| To :g:`$CD$ <0225.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0225.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />` and producing :g:`$CK$ <0225.g1:/ C K />` as breadth,
| and :g:`$KL$ <0225.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$BG$ <0225.g1:/ B G />`, producing :g:`$KM$ <0225.g1:/ K M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0225.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0225.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   And the sum of the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0225.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0225.g1:/ B G />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0225.g1:< C M L D >>` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0225.g1:/ C D />`, producing
   | :g:`$CM$ <0225.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CM$ <0225.g1:/ C M />` is also rational and commensurable in length
   | with :g:`$CD$ <0225.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[x. 20]`
