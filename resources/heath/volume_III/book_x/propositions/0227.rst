We have to find and classify

$$\frac{1}{\sigma}\left\{\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}} - \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}\right\}^2.$$

We will call this, for brevity,

$$\frac{1}{\sigma}(u-v)^2.$$

Take $x, y, z$ such that

$$\left.\begin{aligned}
\sigma x &= u^2 \\
\sigma y &= v^2 \\
\sigma \cdot 2z &= 2uv
\end{aligned}\right\},$$

| where it has to be remembered that $u^2, v^2$ are incommensurable, $(u^2+v^2)$ is
| rational, and $2uv$ medial.
