| It follows that $\sigma(x+y)$ is rational and $\sigma \cdot 2z$ medial,
| so that $(x+y)$ is rational and $\sim \sigma$ ....................................(1),
| while $2z$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$ ........................................(2),
| and $\sigma(x+y) \smile \sigma \cdot 2z$,
| so that $(x+y) \smile 2z$ ................................................(3).

| Thus [(1), (2), (3)] $(x+y)$, $2z$ are rational and $\sim$,
| so that $(x+y) - 2z$ is an apotome.

Next, since

$$u^2 \smile v^2,$$

$$\sigma x \smile \sigma y,$$

or

$$x \smile y.$$

And it is proved, as usual, that

$$xy = z^2 = \frac{1}{4}(2z)^2.$$

Therefore [X. 18] $\sqrt{(x+y)^2 - (2z)^2} \smile (x+y)$.

| But $(x+y) \sim \sigma$,
| therefore $x+y-2z$ is a _fourth apotome_.

Its value is of course $\frac{\rho^2}{\sigma}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}\right)$.

PROPOSITION 101.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   The square on the straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a fifth apotome.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />` be the straight line which produces with a
| rational area a medial whole, and :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` a rational straight line,
| and to :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` let :g:`$CE$ <0228.g1:< C F E D >>` be applied equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />` and
| producing :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` as breadth;
| I say that :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` is a fifth apotome.

.. picture:: 0228.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 101 Diagram

.. subclause::

   For let :g:`$BG$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` are straight lines incommensurable in
   | square which make the sum of the squares on them medial
   | but twice the rectangle contained by them rational. :just:`[X. 77] <X.77>`

| To :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied :g:`$CH$ <0228.g1:< C K H D >>` equal to the square on
| :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, and :g:`$KL$ <0228.g1:< K M L H >>` equal to the square on :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`.
