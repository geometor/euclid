<p class="sub-clause">But the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> together is<br />
medial;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and incommensurable with <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the whole <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and, in these, <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C F E D >">$CE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span></p>

Let then <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0228.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
and through <span data-ref="0228.g1:N">$N$</span> let <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be drawn parallel to either of the<br />
straight lines <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F N O E >">$FO$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:< N M L O >">$NL$</span> is equal to the rect-<br />
angle <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> is rational and<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> is rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F E /">$EF$</span>, producing<br />
<span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 20]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is medial, and <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span> rational,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is to <span data-ref="0228.g1:< F M L E >">$FL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> to <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And both are rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ F M /">$MF$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>
