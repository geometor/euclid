But the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0228.g1:/ B G /">$GB$</span> together is medial; therefore <span data-ref="0228.g1:< C M L D >">$CL$</span> is medial.
