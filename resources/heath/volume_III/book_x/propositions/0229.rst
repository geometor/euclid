.. subclause::

   | But the sum of the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` together is
   | medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is medial.

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`, producing
   | :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` as breadth;

.. assertion:: [X. 22] <X.22>

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` is rational and incommensurable with :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the whole :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is equal to the squares on :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`,
   | and, in these, :g:`$CE$ <0228.g1:< C F E D >>` is equal to the square on :g:`$AB$ <0228.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` is equal to twice the rectangle
   | :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`. :just:`[II. 7] <II.7>`

| Let then :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` be bisected at :g:`$N$ <0228.g1:N>`,
| and through :g:`$N$ <0228.g1:N>` let :g:`$NO$ <0228.g1:/ N O />` be drawn parallel to either of the
| straight lines :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$ML$ <0228.g1:/ M L />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the rectangles :g:`$FO$ <0228.g1:< F N O E >>`, :g:`$NL$ <0228.g1:< N M L O >>` is equal to the rect-
   | angle :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since twice the rectangle :g:`$AG$ <0228.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$GB$ <0228.g1:/ B G />` is rational and
   | equal to :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$EF$ <0228.g1:/ F E />`, producing
   | :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` as breadth;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$FM$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` is rational and commensurable in length with
   | :g:`$CD$ <0228.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is medial, and :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>` rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is incommensurable with :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`.

.. subclause:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   But, as :g:`$CL$ <0228.g1:< C M L D >>` is to :g:`$FL$ <0228.g1:< F M L E >>`, so is :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` to :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />`.

.. subclause::

   And both are rational;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CM$ <0228.g1:/ C M />`, :g:`$MF$ <0228.g1:/ F M />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only;
   | therefore :g:`$CF$ <0228.g1:/ C F />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`
