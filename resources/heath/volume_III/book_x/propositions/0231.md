### PROPOSITION 102.

_The square on the straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a sixth apotome._

Let <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole, and <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> a rational straight line, and to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> be applied equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and producing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> as breadth;
I say that <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is a sixth apotome.

![Proposition 102 Diagram](0231.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> medial, and the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> incommensurable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>. [X. 78]

Now to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> and producing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> as breadth, and <span data-ref="0231.g1:< K L >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span>; therefore the whole <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is also medial.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, producing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth; therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. [X. 22]

Since now <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>, and, in these, <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0231.g1:< F L >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>. [II. 7]

And twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is medial; therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:< F L >">$FL$</span> is also medial.
