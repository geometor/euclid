### PROPOSITION 102.

<p class="enunciation">The square on the straight line which produces with a<br />
medial area a medial whole, if applied to a rational straight<br />
line, produces as breadth a sixth apotome.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be the straight line which produces with a medial<br />
area a medial whole, and <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> a rational straight line,<br />
and to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> be applied equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and<br />
producing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> as breadth;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is a sixth apotome.

![Proposition 102 Diagram](0231.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For let <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,<br />
twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> medial, and the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> incommensurable with twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 78]</span></p>

Now to <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span> equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> and producing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> as breadth,<br />
and <span data-ref="0231.g1:< K L >">$KL$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is also medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, produc-<br />
ing <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> as breadth;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since now <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C L >">$CL$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>,<br />
and, in these, <span data-ref="0231.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0231.g1:< F L >">$FL$</span> is equal to twice the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span>. <span class="justification">[II. 7]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And twice the rectangle <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0231.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is medial;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0231.g1:< F L >">$FL$</span> is also medial.</p>
