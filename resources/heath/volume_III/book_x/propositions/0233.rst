.. assertion::

   And for the same reason as before the square on :g:`CM <0231.g1:/ C M />` is greater than the square on :g:`MF <0231.g1:/ M F />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`CM <0231.g1:/ C M />`. :just:`[X. 18] <X.18>`

.. subclause::

   And neither of them is commensurable with the rational straight line :g:`CD <0231.g1:/ C D />` set out;

.. assertion:: [X. Deff. III. 6]

   therefore :g:`CF <0231.g1:/ C F />` is a sixth apotome.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

We have to find and classify

$$\frac{1}{\sigma}\left\{\frac{\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1+\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}} - \frac{\rho\lambda^{\frac{1}{4}}}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{k}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}}\right\}^2.$$

Call this $\frac{1}{\sigma}(u-v)^2$, and put

$$\left.\begin{aligned}
\sigma x &= u^2, \\
\sigma y &= v^2, \\
\sigma \cdot 2z &= 2uv.
\end{aligned}\right.$$

Here $u^2, v^2$ are incommensurable, $(u^2+v^2), 2uv$ are both medial areas, and

$$(u^2+v^2) \smile 2uv.$$

Since $\sigma(x+y), \sigma \cdot 2z$ are medial and incommensurable, $(x+y)$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$, $2z$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$, and

$$(x+y) \smile 2z.$$

Hence $(x+y), 2z$ are rational and $\sim$, so that $(x+y) - 2z$ is an apotome.

Again, since $u^2, v^2$, or $\sigma x, \sigma y$, are incommensurable,

$$x \smile y.$$

And, as before,

$$xy = z^2 = \frac{1}{4}(2z)^2.$$

Therefore [X. 18]

$$\sqrt{(x+y)^2 - (2z)^2} \smile (x+y).$$

And neither $(x+y)$ nor $2z$ is $\frown \sigma$; therefore $(x+y) - 2z$ is a :term:`*sixth apotome* <sixth-apotome>`.

It is of course

$$\frac{\rho^2}{\sigma}\left(\sqrt{\lambda} - \frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}{\sqrt{1+k^2}}\right).$$

PROPOSITION 103.
----------------

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable in length with an apotome is an apotome and the same in order.

Let :g:`AB <0233.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome, and let :g:`CD <0233.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in length with :g:`AB <0233.g1:/ A B />`;

I say that :g:`CD <0233.g1:/ C D />` is also an apotome and the same in order with :g:`AB <0233.g1:/ A B />`.
