For, since <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is an apotome, let <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to it ;
therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable
in square only. [x. 73]

Let it be contrived that the ratio of <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is the same
as the ratio of <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> ; [VI. 12]
therefore also, as one is to one, so are all to all ; [v. 12]
therefore also, as the whole <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>
to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>
with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [x. 11]

And <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in
square only ;
therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also rational straight lines commensurable in square only. [x. 13]

Now since, as <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,
alternately therefore, as <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 16]

And the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>
either by the square on a straight line commensurable with
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable
with it.

If then the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> will also be greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>
by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.
[x. 14]

And, if <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational
straight line set out,
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is so also, [x. 12]
if <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, then <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> also, [id.]
and, if neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, then neither of
the straight lines <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [x. 13]

But, if the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> will also be greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.
[x. 14]
