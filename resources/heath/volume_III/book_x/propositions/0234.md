<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is an apotome, let <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>

Let it be contrived that the ratio of <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> is the same<br />
as the ratio of <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 12]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as one is to one, so are all to all; <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span><br />
therefore also, as the whole <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span><br />
with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also rational straight lines commensur-<br />
able in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, as <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">alternately therefore, as <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">And the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span><br />
either by the square on a straight line commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable<br />
with it.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">If then the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> will also be greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 14]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">And, if <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational<br />
straight line set out,<br />
<span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is so also, <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span><br />
if <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, then <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> also, <span class="justification">[id.]</span><br />
and, if neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, then neither of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> will also be greater than the square on <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0233.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 14]</span></p>
