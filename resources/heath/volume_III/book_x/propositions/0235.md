### PROPOSITION 104.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with an apotome of a<br />
medial straight line is an apotome of a medial straight line<br />
and the same in order.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be an apotome of a medial straight line,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable in<br />
length with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;

I say that <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also an apotome of a<br />
medial straight line and the same in<br />
order with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

![Proposition 104 diagram](0235.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is an apotome of a medial straight line, let<br />
<span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> be the annex to it.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial straight lines commensur-<br />
able in square only. <span class="justification">[X. 74, 75]</span></p>

Let it be contrived that, as <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 12]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span><br />
with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 12, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in<br />
square only;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also medial straight lines <span class="justification">[X. 23]</span> com-<br />
mensurable in square only; <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is an apotome of a medial straight line. <span class="justification">[X. 74, 75]</span></p>
