This and the following propositions to 107 inclusive (like the corresponding theorems x. 66 to 70) are easy and require no elucidation. They are
equivalent to saying that, if in any of the preceding irrational straight lines
$\frac{m}{n}\rho$ is substituted for $\rho$, the resulting irrational is of the same kind and order
as that from which it is altered.

### PROPOSITION 104.

*A straight line commensurable with an apotome of a
medial straight line is an apotome of a medial straight line
and the same in order.*

Let <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be an apotome of a medial straight line,
and let <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable in
length with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also an apotome of a
medial straight line and the same in
order with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

![Proposition 104 diagram](0235.g1.svg)

For, since <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is an apotome of a medial straight line, let
<span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> be the annex to it.

Therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in square only. [x. 74, 75]

Let it be contrived that, as <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>;
[VI. 12]
therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>
with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [v. 12, x. 11]

But <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are medial straight lines commensurable in
square only ;
therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also medial straight lines [x. 23] commensurable in square only ; [x. 13]
therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is an apotome of a medial straight line. [x. 74, 75]
