PROPOSITION 104.
----------------

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with an apotome of a medial straight line is an apotome of a medial straight line and the same in order.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` be an apotome of a medial straight line,
| and let :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable in
| length with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`;

| I say that :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` is also an apotome of a
| medial straight line and the same in
| order with :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: 0235.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 104 diagram

.. subclause::

   | For, since :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` is an apotome of a medial straight line, let
   | :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` be the annex to it.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` are medial straight lines commensur-
   | able in square only. :just:`[X. 74, 75]`

| Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$AB$ <0235.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$BE$ <0235.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`$DF$ <0235.g1:/ D F />`;
| :just:`[VI. 12] <VI.12>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />` is also commensurable with :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, and :g:`$BE$ <0235.g1:/ B E />`
   | with :g:`$DF$ <0235.g1:/ D F />`. :just:`[V. 12, X. 11] <V.12, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$AE$ <0235.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0235.g1:/ E B />` are medial straight lines commensurable in
   | square only;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CF$ <0235.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0235.g1:/ F D />` are also medial straight lines :just:`[X. 23] <X.23>` com-
   | mensurable in square only; :just:`[X. 13] <X.13>`
   | therefore :g:`$CD$ <0235.g1:/ C D />` is an apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`[X. 74, 75]`
