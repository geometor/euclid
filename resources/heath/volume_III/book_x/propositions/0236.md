### PROPOSITION 105.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with a minor straight line<br />
is minor.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a minor straight line, and <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> commensurable<br />
with <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;

I say that <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also minor.

![Proposition 105 diagram](0236.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made<br />
as before;

<p class="sub-clause">then, since <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are incommensur-<br />
able in square, <span class="justification">[X. 76]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also incommensurable in square. <span class="justification">[X. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, as <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span class="justification">[V. 12, V. 16]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,<br />
so is the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 22]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, *componendo*, as the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are to<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so are the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 18]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is commensurable with the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also commen-<br />
surable with the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is rational; <span class="justification">[X. 76]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is also rational.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. Def. 4]</span></p>
