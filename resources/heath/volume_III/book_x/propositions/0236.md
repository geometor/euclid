I say next that it is also the same in order with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

Since, as <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,
<span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is commensurable with the square
on <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also commensurable with
the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 16, x. 11]

Therefore, if the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is rational, the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> will also be rational, [x. Def. 4]
and if the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is medial, the rectangle <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>
is also medial. [x. 23, Por.]

Therefore <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is an apotome of a medial straight line and
the same in order with <span data-ref="0235.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>. [x. 74, 75]

Q. E. D.

### PROPOSITION 105.

*A straight line commensurable with a minor straight line
is minor.*

Let <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a minor straight line, and <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> commensurable
with <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also minor.

![Proposition 105 diagram](0236.g1.svg)

Let the same construction be made
as before;
then, since <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are incommensurable in square, [x. 76]
therefore <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also incommensurable in square. [x. 13]

Now since, as <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, [v. 12, v. 16]
therefore also, as the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,
so is the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [VI. 22]

Therefore, *componendo*, as the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are to
the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, so are the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> to the
square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 18]

But the square on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is commensurable with the square
on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>;
therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is also commensurable with the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [v. 16, x. 11]

But the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is rational; [x. 76]
therefore the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0236.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is also rational.
[x. Def. 4]
