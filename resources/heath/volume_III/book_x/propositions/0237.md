### PROPOSITION 106.

*A straight line commensurable with that which produces
with a rational area a medial whole is a straight line which
produces with a rational area a medial whole.*

Let <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a straight line which produces with a rational
area a medial whole,
and <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> commensurable with <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also a straight line
which produces with a rational area a
medial whole.

![Proposition 106 diagram](0237.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in
square which make the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>
medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. [x. 77]

Let the same construction be made.

Then we can prove, in manner similar to the foregoing,
that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are in the same ratio as <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is commensurable with
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;
so that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also straight lines incommensurable in
square which make the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> medial,
but the rectangle contained by them rational.
