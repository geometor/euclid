### PROPOSITION 106.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with that which produces<br />
with a rational area a medial whole is a straight line which<br />
produces with a rational area a medial whole.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a straight line which produces with a rational<br />
area a medial whole,<br />
and <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> commensurable with <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also a straight line<br />
which produces with a rational area a<br />
medial whole.

![Proposition 106 diagram](0237.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span><br />
medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational. <span class="justification">[x. 77]</span></p>

Let the same construction be made.

<p class="assertion">Then we can prove, in manner similar to the foregoing,<br />
that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are in the same ratio as <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,<br />
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> is commensurable with<br />
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,<br />
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;<br />
so that <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0237.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> medial,<br />
but the rectangle contained by them rational.</p>
