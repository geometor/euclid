### PROPOSITION 107.

<p class="enunciation">A straight line commensurable with that which produces<br />
with a medial area a medial whole is itself also a straight line<br />
which produces with a medial area a medial whole.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a straight line which produces with a medial<br />
area a medial whole,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also a straight line<br />
which produces with a medial area a<br />
medial whole.

![Proposition 107 diagram](0238.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let the same construction be made;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,<br />
the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum<br />
of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle<br />
contained by them. <span class="justification">[x. 78]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, as was proved, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,<br />
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the sum of the<br />
squares on <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,<br />
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also straight lines incommensurable in<br />
square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,<br />
the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum<br />
of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle<br />
contained by them.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is a straight line which produces with a<br />
medial area a medial whole. <span class="justification">[x. 78]</span></p>
