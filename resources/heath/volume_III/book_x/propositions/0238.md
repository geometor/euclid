### PROPOSITION 107.

*A straight line commensurable with that which produces
with a medial area a medial whole is itself also a straight line
which produces with a medial area a medial whole.*

Let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be a straight line which produces with a medial
area a medial whole,
and let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be commensurable with <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also a straight line
which produces with a medial area a
medial whole.

![Proposition 107 diagram](0238.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let the same construction be made;
therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are straight lines incommensurable in
square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,
the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum
of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle
contained by them. [x. 78]

Now, as was proved, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> are commensurable with
<span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
the sum of the squares on <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the sum of the
squares on <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>,
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> with the rectangle <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also straight lines incommensurable in
square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,
the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum
of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle
contained by them.

Therefore <span data-ref="0238.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is a straight line which produces with a
medial area a medial whole. [x. 78]
