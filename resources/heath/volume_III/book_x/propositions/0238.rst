PROPOSITION 107.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   A straight line commensurable with that which produces with a medial area a medial whole is itself also a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0238.g1:/ A B />` be a straight line which produces with a medial
| area a medial whole,
| and let :g:`$CD$ <0238.g1:/ C D />` be commensurable with :g:`$AB$ <0238.g1:/ A B />`;
| I say that :g:`$CD$ <0238.g1:/ C D />` is also a straight line
| which produces with a medial area a
| medial whole.

.. picture:: 0238.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 107 diagram

| For let :g:`$BE$ <0238.g1:/ B E />` be the annex to :g:`$AB$ <0238.g1:/ A B />`,
| and let the same construction be made;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$AE$ <0238.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0238.g1:/ E B />` are straight lines incommensurable in
   | square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,
   | the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum
   | of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle
   | contained by them. :just:`[x. 78]`

.. subclause::

   | Now, as was proved, :g:`$AE$ <0238.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0238.g1:/ E B />` are commensurable with
   | :g:`$CF$ <0238.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0238.g1:/ F D />`,
   | the sum of the squares on :g:`$AE$ <0238.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0238.g1:/ E B />` with the sum of the
   | squares on :g:`$CF$ <0238.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0238.g1:/ F D />`,
   | and the rectangle :g:`$AE$ <0238.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$EB$ <0238.g1:/ E B />` with the rectangle :g:`$CF$ <0238.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0238.g1:/ F D />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CF$ <0238.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$FD$ <0238.g1:/ F D />` are also straight lines incommensurable in
   | square which make the sum of the squares on them medial,
   | the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the sum
   | of the squares on them incommensurable with the rectangle
   | contained by them.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$CD$ <0238.g1:/ C D />` is a straight line which produces with a
   | medial area a medial whole. :just:`[x. 78]`
