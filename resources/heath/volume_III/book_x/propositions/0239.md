### PROPOSITION 108.

*If from a rational area a medial area be subtracted, the
"side" of the remaining area becomes one of two irrational
straight lines, either an apotome or a minor straight line.*

For from the rational area <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> let the medial area <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> be
subtracted;
I say that the "side" of the
remainder <span data-ref="0239.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> becomes one
of two irrational straight lines,
either an apotome or a minor
straight line.

![Proposition 108 diagrams](0239.g1.svg)

For let a rational straight
line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out,
to <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> let there be applied the
rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>
equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span> be subtracted;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0239.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>.

Since then <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is rational, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> medial,
while <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is rational, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> medial.

And they are applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with
<span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, [x. 20]
while <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
[x. 22]
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>. [x. 13]

Therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome [x. 73], and <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> the annex to it.

Now the square on <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>
by the square on a straight line either commensurable with
<span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> or not commensurable.

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a
straight line commensurable with it.

Now the whole <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is commensurable in length with the
rational straight line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out;
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a first apotome. [x. Deff. III. 1]
