### PROPOSITION 108.

<p class="enunciation">If from a rational area a medial area be subtracted, the<br />
“side” of the remaining area becomes one of two irrational<br />
straight lines, either an apotome or a minor straight line.</p>

For from the rational area <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> let the medial area <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> be<br />
subtracted;<br />
I say that the “side” of the<br />
remainder <span data-ref="0239.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> becomes one<br />
of two irrational straight lines,<br />
either an apotome or a minor<br />
straight line.

![Proposition 108 diagrams](0239.g1.svg)

For let a rational straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out,<br />
to <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> let there be applied the<br />
rectangular parallelogram <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< D B >">$DB$</span> be subtracted;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0239.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is rational, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> medial,<br />
while <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> to <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> is rational, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span> medial.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And they are applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with<br />
<span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span class="justification">[x. 20]</span><br />
while <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[x. 22]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span>, and <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> the annex to it.</p>

Now the square on <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line either commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> or not commensurable.

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a<br />
straight line commensurable with it.

<p class="sub-clause">Now the whole <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is commensurable in length with the<br />
rational straight line <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0239.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a first apotome. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 1]</span></p>
