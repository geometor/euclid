PROPOSITION 108.
----------------

.. enunciation::

   If from a rational area a medial area be subtracted, the “side” of the remaining area becomes one of two irrational straight lines, either an apotome or a minor straight line.

For from the rational area :g:`BC <0239.g1:< B C >>` let the medial area :g:`BD <0239.g1:< B D >>` be subtracted; I say that the “side” of the remainder :g:`EC <0239.g1:< E C >>` becomes one of two irrational straight lines, either an apotome or a minor straight line.

.. picture:: 0239.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 108 diagrams

For let a rational straight line :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />` be set out, to :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />` let there be applied the rectangular parallelogram :g:`GH <0239.g1:< G H >>` :eq:`equal <0239.g1:< G H > = 0239.g1:< B C >>` to :g:`BC <0239.g1:< B C >>`, and let :g:`GK <0239.g1:< G K >>` :eq:`equal <0239.g1:< G K > = 0239.g1:< D B >>` to :g:`DB <0239.g1:< D B >>` be subtracted;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`EC <0239.g1:< E C >>` is :eq:`equal <0239.g1:< E C > = 0239.g1:< L H >>` to :g:`LH <0239.g1:< L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`BC <0239.g1:< B C >>` is rational, and :g:`BD <0239.g1:< B D >>` medial, while :g:`BC <0239.g1:< B C >>` is :eq:`equal <0239.g1:< B C > = 0239.g1:< G H >; 0239.g1:< B D > = 0239.g1:< G K >>` to :g:`GH <0239.g1:< G H >>`, and :g:`BD <0239.g1:< B D >>` to :g:`GK <0239.g1:< G K >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`GH <0239.g1:< G H >>` is rational, and :g:`GK <0239.g1:< G K >>` medial.

.. subclause::

   And they are applied to the rational straight line :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`FH <0239.g1:/ F H />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />`, :just:`[x. 20]` while :g:`FK <0239.g1:/ F K />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />`; :just:`[x. 22]` therefore :g:`FH <0239.g1:/ F H />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`FK <0239.g1:/ F K />`. :just:`[x. 13]`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`FH <0239.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`FK <0239.g1:/ F K />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`KH <0239.g1:/ K H />` is an apotome :just:`[x. 73]`, and :g:`KF <0239.g1:/ K F />` the annex to it.

Now the square on :g:`HF <0239.g1:/ H F />` is greater than the square on :g:`FK <0239.g1:/ F K />` by the square on a straight line either commensurable with :g:`HF <0239.g1:/ H F />` or not commensurable.

First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable with it.

.. subclause::

   Now the whole :g:`HF <0239.g1:/ H F />` is commensurable in length with the rational straight line :g:`FG <0239.g1:/ F G />` set out;

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 1]

   therefore :g:`KH <0239.g1:/ K H />` is a first apotome.
