A rational area being of the form $k\rho^2$, and a medial area of the form<br />
$\sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2$, the problem is to classify
$$\sqrt{k\rho^2 - \sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2}$$
according to the different possible relations between $k$, $\lambda$.

Suppose that
$$\begin{aligned}
\sigma u &= k\rho^2, \\
\sigma v &= \sqrt{\lambda}.\rho^2.
\end{aligned}$$

Since $\sigma u$ is rational and $\sigma v$ medial,<br />
$u$ is rational and $\frown \sigma$,<br />
while $v$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$.

Therefore
$$u \smile v;$$
thus $u$, $v$ are rational and $\sim$,<br />
whence $(u - v)$ is an apotome.

The possibilities are now as follows.<br />
(1) $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \frown u$,<br />
(2) $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \smile u$.

In both cases $u \frown \sigma$,<br />
so that $(u - v)$ is either (1) a *first apotome*,<br />
or (2) a *fourth apotome*.

In case (1) $\sqrt{\sigma(u-v)}$ is an *apotome* [x. 91],<br />
but in case (2) $\sqrt{\sigma(u-v)}$ is a *minor irrational straight line* [x. 94].
