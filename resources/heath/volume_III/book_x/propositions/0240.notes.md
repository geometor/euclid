## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heath's Symbolic Notation**:
  - $\frown$ represents commensurable in length ($\sim$ in length).
  - $\smile$ represents incommensurable in length ($\smile$ in length).
  - $\sim$ represents commensurable in square only.
  - Setting $\sigma u = k\rho^2$ and $\sigma v = \sqrt{\lambda}\rho^2$ shows that $u$ is commensurable in length with $\sigma$ ($u \frown \sigma$), while $v$ is incommensurable in length with $\sigma$ ($v \smile \sigma$).
  - Hence $u \smile v$, and $u, v$ are rational straight lines commensurable in square only ($u, v \text{ rational and } \sim$), making $u - v$ an apotome.
  - Case (1): $\sqrt{u^2-v^2} \frown u \implies$ first apotome $\implies$ "side" is apotome (X. 91).
  - Case (2): $\sqrt{u^2-v^2} \smile u \implies$ fourth apotome $\implies$ "side" is minor (X. 94).
