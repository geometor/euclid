I say that the "side" of the remainder <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> becomes one of two irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

![Propositions 108, 109](0241.g1.svg)

For let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out, and let the areas be similarly applied.

It follows then that <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; [x. 13]
therefore <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome, and <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> the annex to it. [x. 73]

Now the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> either by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

If then the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, while the annex <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a second apotome. [x. Deff. III. 2]

But <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is rational;
so that the "side" of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a first apotome of a medial straight line. [x. 92]

But, if the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, while the annex <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a fifth apotome; [x. Deff. III. 5]
so that the "side" of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole. [x. 95]
