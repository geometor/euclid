I say that the “side” of the remainder <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> becomes one<br />
of two irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a<br />
medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a<br />
rational area a medial whole.

![Propositions 108, 109](0241.g1.svg)

For let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be set out,<br />
and let the areas be similarly applied.

<p class="sub-clause">It follows then that <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is rational and incommensurable<br />
in length with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only; <span class="justification">[x. 13]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome, and <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> the annex to it. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

Now the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span><br />
either by the square on a straight line commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable<br />
with it.

<p class="sub-clause">If then the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>,<br />
while the annex <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with the<br />
rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a second apotome. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 2]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">so that the “side” of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a first apotome of<br />
a medial straight line. <span class="justification">[x. 92]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if the square on <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>,<br />
while the annex <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with the<br />
rational straight line <span data-ref="0241.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0241.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a fifth apotome; <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 5]</span><br />
so that the “side” of <span data-ref="0241.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span> is a straight line which produces<br />
with a rational area a medial whole. <span class="justification">[x. 95]</span></p>
