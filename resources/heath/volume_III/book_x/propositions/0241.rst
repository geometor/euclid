:title: Proposition 109 (continued)

| I say that the “side” of the remainder :g:`$EC$ <0241.g1:< E C >>` becomes one
| of two irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a
| medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a
| rational area a medial whole.

.. picture:: 0241.g1.svg
   :alt: Propositions 108, 109

| For let a rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />` be set out,
| and let the areas be similarly applied.

.. subclause::

   | It follows then that :g:`$FH$ <0241.g1:/ F H />` is rational and incommensurable
   | in length with :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />`,
   | while :g:`$KF$ <0241.g1:/ K F />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$FH$ <0241.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` are rational straight lines commensurable
   | in square only; :just:`[x. 13]`
   | therefore :g:`$KH$ <0241.g1:/ K H />` is an apotome, and :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` the annex to it. :just:`[x. 73]`

| Now the square on :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />` is greater than the square on :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />`
| either by the square on a straight line commensurable with
| :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />` or by the square on a straight line incommensurable
| with it.

.. subclause::

   | If then the square on :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />` is greater than the square on
   | :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />`,
   | while the annex :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` is commensurable in length with the
   | rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />` set out,

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 2]

   :g:`$KH$ <0241.g1:/ K H />` is a second apotome.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   | so that the “side” of :g:`$LH$ <0241.g1:< L H >>`, that is, of :g:`$EC$ <0241.g1:< E C >>`, is a first apotome of
   | a medial straight line. :just:`[x. 92]`

.. subclause::

   | But, if the square on :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />` is greater than the square on
   | :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$HF$ <0241.g1:/ H F />`,
   | while the annex :g:`$FK$ <0241.g1:/ F K />` is commensurable in length with the
   | rational straight line :g:`$FG$ <0241.g1:/ F G />` set out,

.. assertion::

   | :g:`$KH$ <0241.g1:/ K H />` is a fifth apotome; :just:`[x. Deff. III. 5]`
   | so that the “side” of :g:`$EC$ <0241.g1:< E C >>` is a straight line which produces
   | with a rational area a medial whole. :just:`[x. 95]`
