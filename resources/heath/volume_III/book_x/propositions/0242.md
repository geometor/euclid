In this case we have to classify
$$\sqrt{\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2 - \lambda\rho^2}.$$

Suppose that
$$\sigma u = \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2,$$
$$\sigma v = \lambda\rho^2.$$

Thus, $\sigma u$ being medial and $\sigma v$ rational,<br />
$u$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$,<br />
while $v$ is rational and $\mathbin{\Cap} \sigma$.

Thus, as before, $u, v$ are rational and $\sim$,<br />
so that $(u - v)$ is an apotome.

Now either<br />
(1) $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \mathbin{\Cap} u$,<br />
or (2) $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \smile u$,<br />
while in both cases $v$ is commensurable with $\sigma$.

Therefore $(u - v)$ is either (1) a second apotome,<br />
or (2) a fifth apotome,<br />
and hence in case (1) $\sqrt{\sigma (u - v)}$ is the *first apotome of a medial straight line*, <span class="justification">[x. 92]</span><br />
and in case (2) $\sqrt{\sigma (u - v)}$ is the "*side" of a medial, minus a rational, area*. <span class="justification">[x. 95]</span>
