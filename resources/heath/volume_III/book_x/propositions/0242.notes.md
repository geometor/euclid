## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heath's Symbolic Notation**:
  - $\mathbin{\Cap}$: Commensurable in length.
  - $\smile$: Incommensurable in length.
  - $\sim$: Commensurable in square only.
- **Algebraic Reductions**: Expressing areas applied to rational $\sigma$: $\sigma u = \sqrt{k}\rho^2$, $\sigma v = \lambda\rho^2$. Leads to $(u - v)$ as 2nd or 5th apotome, yielding either $\sqrt{\sigma(u-v)}$ as first apotome of a medial straight line [X. 92] or side of a medial minus a rational area [X. 95].
- **Proposition 110 Enunciation**: Distinguishes the two remaining classes of the 6 apotomic irrationals: the second apotome of a medial straight line and the straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.
