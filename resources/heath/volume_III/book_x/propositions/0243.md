For, since each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> is medial,
and <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>,
it follows that each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> will be rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. [x. 22]

And, since <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>,
that is, <span data-ref="0242.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>,
<span data-ref="0242.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>; [VI. 1, x. 11]
therefore <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome. [x. 73]

If then the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>,
while neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,
<span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a third apotome. [x. Deff. III. 3]

But <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is rational,
and the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and a third apotome is irrational,
and the "side" of it is irrational, and is called a second apotome of a medial straight line; [x. 93]
so that the "side" of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a second apotome of a medial straight line.

But, if the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>,
while neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,
<span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a sixth apotome. [x. Deff. III. 6]

But the "side" of the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and a sixth apotome is a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole. [x. 96]

Therefore the "side" of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.

Q. E. D.
