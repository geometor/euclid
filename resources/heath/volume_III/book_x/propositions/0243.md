<p class="sub-clause">For, since each of the rectangles <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span> is medial,<br />
and <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">it follows that each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> will be<br />
rational and incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B C >">$BC$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0242.g1:< G H >">$GH$</span> with <span data-ref="0242.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0242.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is also incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 1, x. 11]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable<br />
in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">If then the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>,<br />
while neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable<br />
in length with the rational straight line <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> set out,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a third apotome. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is rational,<br />
and the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and a<br />
third apotome is irrational,<br />
and the "side" of it is irrational, and is called a second<br />
apotome of a medial straight line; <span class="justification">[x. 93]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">so that the "side" of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a second apotome<br />
of a medial straight line.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if the square on <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>,<br />
while neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is commensurable<br />
in length with <span data-ref="0242.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0242.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a sixth apotome. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 6]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the "side" of the rectangle contained by a rational<br />
straight line and a sixth apotome is a straight line which<br />
produces with a medial area a medial whole. <span class="justification">[x. 96]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore the "side" of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0242.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, is a straight<br />
line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.</p>

<p class="qed">Q. E. D.</p>
