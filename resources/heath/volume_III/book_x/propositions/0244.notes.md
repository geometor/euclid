## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Significance of Proposition 111**: Establishes that an apotome cannot be identical to a binomial. This is the crucial barrier separating the additive and subtractive classes of Euclid's 25 irrational straight lines.
- **Proof Mechanism**: Reductio ad absurdum. If $AB$ were both apotome and binomial, applying $AB^2$ to rational $DC$ makes breadth $DE$ simultaneously a first apotome [X. 97] (with annex $EF$) and a first binomial [X. 60] (with terms $DG, GE$).
