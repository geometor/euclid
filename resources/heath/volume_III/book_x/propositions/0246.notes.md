  - Current: Connects Proposition 111 to the grand classification. Begins the famous unnumbered Euclidean Recapitulation theorem: "The apotome and the irrational straight lines following it are neither the same with the medial straight line nor with one another."
  - Subsequent: The Recapitulation concludes on Folio 0247 (Page 243) with the full catalogue of the 13 irrationals.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **The Recapitulation**: In Heiberg's edition and Heath's translation, this text is treated as a major systematic theorem synthesizing the entire machinery of Book X.
- **Application of Areas to Classify**: The 6 apotomic lines are mapped 1-to-1 to the 6 orders of apotomes via application of their squares to a rational line:
  - Apotome $\to$ 1st apotome [X. 97]
  - First apotome of a medial $\to$ 2nd apotome [X. 98]
  - Second apotome of a medial $\to$ 3rd apotome [X. 99]
  - Minor $\to$ 4th apotome [X. 100]
  - Producing with rational area medial whole $\to$ 5th apotome [X. 101]
  - Producing with medial area medial whole $\to$ 6th apotome [X. 102]
- **Contrast with Medial**: The medial square produces a rational breadth incommensurable in length [X. 22], whereas all the others produce irrational breadths.
