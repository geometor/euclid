### PROPOSITION 112.

<p class="enunciation">The square on a rational straight line applied to the<br />
binomial straight line produces as breadth an apotome the<br />
terms of which are commensurable with the terms of the bi-<br />
nomial and moreover in the same ratio; and further the<br />
apotome so arising will have the same order as the binomial<br />
straight line.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span> be a rational straight line,<br />
let <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be a binomial, and let <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be its greater term;<br />
let the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be equal to the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span>;
