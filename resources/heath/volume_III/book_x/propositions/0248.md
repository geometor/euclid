For again let the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> be equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is equal to the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16, V. 14]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;<br />
therefore, *separando*, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 17]</span></p>

Let it be contrived that, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>;

<p class="assertion">therefore also the whole <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span><br />
is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>;<br />
for, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so<br />
are all the antecedents to all the consequents. <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>; <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>. <span class="justification">[*id*.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is commensurable with the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. 36]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is also commensurable with the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 22, X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, so is<br />
<span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, since the three straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are<br />
proportional. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 9]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> is also commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span> is equal to the rectangle<br />
<span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,<br />
while the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span> is rational,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length<br />
with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. 20]</span><br />
so that <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, being commensurable with it, is also rational and<br />
commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.</p>
