For again let the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> be equal to the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span>.
Since then the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [VI. 16]

But <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [V. 16, V. 14]

Let <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0247.g1:G">$G$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
therefore, *separando*, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>. [V. 17]

Let it be contrived that, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>;
therefore also the whole <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to the whole <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>;
for, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents. [V. 12]

But, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>; [V. 11]
therefore also, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>. [*id*.]

But the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>; [X. 36]
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is also commensurable with the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>. [VI. 22, X. 11]

And, as the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, since the three straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are proportional. [V. Def. 9]
Therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>,
so that <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> is also commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>. [X. 15]

Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,
while the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:A">$A$</span> is rational,
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is also rational.

And it is applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>; [X. 20]
so that <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, being commensurable with it, is also rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.
