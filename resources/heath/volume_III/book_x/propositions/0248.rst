:title: Proposition 112 (continued)

For again let the rectangle :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$G$ <0247.g1:G>` be equal to the square on :g:`$A$ <0247.g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the rectangle :g:`$BC$ <0247.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$G$ <0247.g1:G>`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 16] <VI.16>

   therefore, as :g:`$CB$ <0247.g1:/ C B />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$G$ <0247.g1:G>` to :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CB$ <0247.g1:/ C B />` is greater than :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 16, V. 14] <V.16, V.14>

   therefore :g:`$G$ <0247.g1:G>` is also greater than :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`.

Let :g:`$EH$ <0247.g1:/ E H />` be equal to :g:`$G$ <0247.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$CB$ <0247.g1:/ C B />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$HE$ <0247.g1:/ H E />` to :g:`$EF$ <0247.g1:/ E F />`; therefore, :term:`*separando* <separando>`, as :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$HF$ <0247.g1:/ H F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0247.g1:/ F E />`. :just:`[V. 17] <V.17>`

Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$HF$ <0247.g1:/ H F />` is to :g:`$FE$ <0247.g1:/ F E />`, so is :g:`$FK$ <0247.g1:/ F K />` to :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also the whole :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` is to the whole :g:`$KF$ <0247.g1:/ K F />` as :g:`$FK$ <0247.g1:/ F K />` is to :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />`; for, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents. :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. subclause:: [V. 11] <V.11>

   But, as :g:`$FK$ <0247.g1:/ F K />` is to :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$DB$ <0247.g1:/ D B />`;

.. assertion:: [*id*.]

   therefore also, as :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` is to :g:`$KF$ <0247.g1:/ K F />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$DB$ <0247.g1:/ D B />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$CD$ <0247.g1:/ C D />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$DB$ <0247.g1:/ D B />`; :just:`[X. 36] <X.36>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` is also commensurable with the square on :g:`$KF$ <0247.g1:/ K F />`. :just:`[VI. 22, X. 11] <VI.22, X.11>`

.. subclause::

   And, as the square on :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` is to the square on :g:`$KF$ <0247.g1:/ K F />`, so is :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />`, since the three straight lines :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KF$ <0247.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />` are proportional. :just:`[V. Def. 9] <V.def.9>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$HK$ <0247.g1:/ H K />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$KE$ <0247.g1:/ K E />`, so that :g:`$HE$ <0247.g1:/ H E />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$EK$ <0247.g1:/ E K />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since the square on :g:`$A$ <0247.g1:A>` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$EH$ <0247.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`, while the square on :g:`$A$ <0247.g1:A>` is rational,

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$EH$ <0247.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And it is applied to the rational straight line :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0247.g1:/ E H />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`; :just:`[X. 20] <X.20>` so that :g:`$EK$ <0247.g1:/ E K />`, being commensurable with it, is also rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$BD$ <0247.g1:/ B D />`.
