<p class="sub-clause">Since, then, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> are straight lines commensurable in square<br />
only,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are also commensurable in square only.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also rational.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is an apotome. <span class="justification">[X. 73]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span><br />
either by the square on a straight line commensurable with<br />
<span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable<br />
with it.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">If then the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> by<br />
the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 14]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, if <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational<br />
straight line set out,</p>
<p class="assertion">so also is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. 11, 12]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">if <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is so commensurable,</p>
<p class="assertion">so also is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>; <span class="justification">[X. 12]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">but, if neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is so commensur-<br />
able,</p>
<p class="assertion">neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is so.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on<br />

<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> by<br />
