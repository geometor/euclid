Since, then, as <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> to <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> are straight lines commensurable in square only,
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are also commensurable in square only. [X. 11]

But <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also rational.

Therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only;
therefore <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is an apotome. [X. 73]

Now the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> either by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.

If then the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,
the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> by the square on a straight line commensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>. [X. 14]

And, if <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out,
so also is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>; [X. 11, 12]
if <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is so commensurable,
so also is <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>; [X. 12]
but, if neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is so commensurable,
neither of the straight lines <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is so.

But, if the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,
the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also greater than the square on <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0247.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>. [X. 14]
