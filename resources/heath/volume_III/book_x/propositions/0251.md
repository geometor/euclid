the binomial straight line $\rho + \sqrt k \cdot \rho$. If $u$ be the straight line required,<br />
$(u + w) - w$ is shown to be an apotome of the kind described, where $w$ is<br />
determined in the following manner.

We have
$$\left.\begin{aligned}
(\rho + \sqrt k \cdot \rho)\, u &= \sigma^2 = \sqrt k \cdot \rho \cdot x\text{, say,} \\
\text{whence}\quad x &> u.
\end{aligned}\right\} \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(1).$$

Let
$$x = u + v.$$

Then
$$(\rho + \sqrt k \cdot \rho) : \sqrt k \cdot \rho = (u + v) : u,$$
and hence
$$\rho : \sqrt k \cdot \rho = v : u \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(2).$$

Let $w$ be taken such that
$$v : u = (u + w) : w \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(3).$$

Thus
$$v : u = (u + v + w) : (u + w) \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(4),$$
and therefore
$$\rho : \sqrt k \cdot \rho = (u + v + w) : (u + w).$$

From the last proportion,
$$(u + v + w)^2 \mathbin{\cap} (u + w)^2,$$
and, from the two preceding, $(u + w)$ is a mean proportional between<br />
$(u + v + w)$, $w$, so that
$$(u + v + w)^2 : (u + w)^2 = (u + v + w) : w.$$

Therefore
$$(u + v + w) \mathbin{\cap} w,$$
whence
$$(u + v) \mathbin{\cap} w.$$

Now $\sqrt k \cdot \rho\, (u + v) = \sigma^2$, which is rational;<br />
therefore $(u + v)$ is rational and $\mathbin{\cap} \sqrt k \cdot \rho$;<br />
hence
$$w \text{ is also rational and } \mathbin{\cap} \sqrt k \cdot \rho \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(5).$$

Next, by (2), (3), since $\rho$, $\sqrt k \cdot \rho$ are $\mathbin{\sim}$,
$$(u + w) \mathbin{\sim} w,$$
and $w$ is rational;<br />
therefore $(u + w)$ is rational,<br />
and $(u + w)$, $w$ are rational and $\mathbin{\sim}$.<br />
Hence $(u + w) - w$ is an apotome.

Now either
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{(I)} &\quad \sqrt{\rho^2 - k\rho^2} \mathbin{\cap} \rho, \\
\text{or (II)} &\quad \sqrt{\rho^2 - k\rho^2} \mathbin{\cup} \rho.
\end{aligned}$$

In case (I)
$$\sqrt{(u + w)^2 - w^2} \mathbin{\cap} (u + w), \qquad \text{[(2), (3) and X. 14]}$$
and in case (II)
$$\sqrt{(u + w)^2 - w^2} \mathbin{\cup} (u + w). \qquad \text{[*id*.]}$$

Then, since [(5)] $w \mathbin{\cap} \sqrt k \cdot \rho$,<br />
by X. 11 and (2), (3),
$$(u + w) \mathbin{\cap} \rho \dots\dots\dots\dots\dots\dots(6).$$
[This step is omitted in Euclid, but the result is assumed.]

If therefore $\rho \mathbin{\cap} \sigma$, $(u + w) \mathbin{\cap} \sigma$;<br />
if $\sqrt k \cdot \rho \mathbin{\cap} \sigma$, $w \mathbin{\cap} \sigma$; [(5)]<br />
and, if neither $\rho$ nor $\sqrt k \cdot \rho$ is $\mathbin{\cap} \sigma$, neither $(u + w)$ nor $w$ will be $\mathbin{\cap} \sigma$.

Thus the order of the apotome $(u + w) - w$ is the same as that of the<br />
binomial straight line $\rho + \sqrt k \cdot \rho$; while [(2), (3)] the terms are proportional<br />
and [(5), (6)] commensurable respectively.
