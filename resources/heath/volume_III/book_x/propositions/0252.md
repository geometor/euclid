We find $(u + w)$, $w$ algebraically thus.

By (1),
$$u = \frac{\sigma^2}{\rho + \sqrt k \cdot \rho};$$
and, by (2), (3),
$$\frac{u + w}{w} = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt k \cdot \rho},$$
whence
$$\begin{aligned}
w &= \frac{u \cdot \sqrt k \cdot \rho}{\rho - \sqrt k \cdot \rho} \\
&= \frac{\sigma^2 \cdot \sqrt k \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.
\end{aligned}$$

Thus
$$u + w = w \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt k} = \frac{\sigma^2 \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$

Therefore
$$(u + w) - w = \sigma^2 \cdot \frac{\rho - \sqrt k \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$

### PROPOSITION 113.

*The square on a rational straight line, if applied to an apotome, produces as breadth the binomial straight line the terms of which are commensurable with the terms of the apotome and in the same ratio; and further the binomial so arising has the same order as the apotome.*

![Proposition 113](0252.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0252.g1:A">$A$</span> be a rational straight line and <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> an apotome,
and let the rectangle <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> be equal to the square on <span data-ref="0252.g1:A">$A$</span>, so that the square on the rational straight line <span data-ref="0252.g1:A">$A$</span> when applied to the apotome <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> produces <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> as breadth;
I say that <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is a binomial straight line the terms of which are commensurable with the terms of <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> and in the same ratio; and further <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> has the same order as <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be the annex to <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> are rational straight lines commensurable in square only. [X. 73]

Let the rectangle <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0252.g1:G">$G$</span> be also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0252.g1:A">$A$</span>.
But the square on <span data-ref="0252.g1:A">$A$</span> is rational;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0252.g1:G">$G$</span> is also rational.

And it has been applied to the rational straight line <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0252.g1:G">$G$</span> is rational and commensurable in length with <span data-ref="0252.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. [X. 20]
