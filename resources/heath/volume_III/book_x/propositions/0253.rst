:title: Proposition 113 (continued)

.. subclause::

   | Since now the rectangle :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$G$ <0252.g1:G>` is equal to the rectangle
   | :g:`$BD$ <0252.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$CB$ <0252.g1:/ C B />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0252.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`$G$ <0252.g1:G>`.
   | :just:`[VI. 16] <VI.16>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is greater than :g:`$BD$ <0252.g1:/ B D />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 16, V. 14] <V.16, V.14>

   therefore :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` is also greater than :g:`$G$ <0252.g1:G>`.

Let :g:`$KE$ <0252.g1:/ K E />` be made equal to :g:`$G$ <0252.g1:G>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KE$ <0252.g1:/ K E />` is commensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   And since, as :g:`$CB$ <0252.g1:/ C B />` is to :g:`$BD$ <0252.g1:/ B D />`, so is :g:`$HK$ <0252.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`$KE$ <0252.g1:/ K E />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, *convertendo*, as :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0252.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`$HE$ <0252.g1:/ H E />`.
   | :just:`[V. 19, Por.]`

| Let it be contrived that, as :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`$HE$ <0252.g1:/ H E />`, so is :g:`$HF$ <0252.g1:/ H F />`
| to :g:`$FE$ <0252.g1:/ F E />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also the remainder :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is to :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />` as :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`$HE$ <0252.g1:/ H E />`,
   | that is, as :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0252.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[V. 19] <V.19>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0252.g1:/ C D />` are commensurable in square only;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />` are also commensurable in square only.
   | :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`

.. subclause::

   | And since, as :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`$HE$ <0252.g1:/ H E />`, so is :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` to :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`,
   | while, as :g:`$KH$ <0252.g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`$HE$ <0252.g1:/ H E />`, so is :g:`$HF$ <0252.g1:/ H F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0252.g1:/ F E />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is to :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`, so is :g:`$HF$ <0252.g1:/ H F />` to :g:`$FE$ <0252.g1:/ F E />`, :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`
   | so that also, as the first is to the third, so is the square on the
   | first to the square on the second; :just:`[V. Def. 9] <V.def.9>`
   | therefore also, as :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is to :g:`$FE$ <0252.g1:/ F E />`, so is the square on :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` to the
   | square on :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   | But the square on :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is commensurable with the square
   | on :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`,
   | for :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />` are commensurable in square;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$FE$ <0252.g1:/ F E />`, :just:`[X. 11] <X.11>`
   | so that :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$KE$ <0252.g1:/ K E />`. :just:`[X. 15] <X.15>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$KE$ <0252.g1:/ K E />` is rational and commensurable in length with :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` is also rational and commensurable in length
   | with :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[X. 12] <X.12>`

.. subclause::

   And, since, as :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0252.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />` to :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 16] <V.16>

   alternately, as :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />`, so is :g:`$DC$ <0252.g1:/ D C />` to :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BC$ <0252.g1:/ B C />` is commensurable with :g:`$KF$ <0252.g1:/ K F />`;

.. assertion:: [X. 11] <X.11>

   therefore :g:`$FH$ <0252.g1:/ F H />` is also commensurable in length with :g:`$CD$ <0252.g1:/ C D />`.
