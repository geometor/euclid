To find $(u - w)$, $w$ algebraically we have
$$u = \frac{\sigma^2}{\rho - \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho},$$
$$\frac{u - w}{w} = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}.$$
From the latter
$$w = \frac{u \cdot \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho + \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}$$
$$= \frac{\sigma^2 \cdot \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$
Thus
$$u - w = w \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{k}} = \frac{\sigma^2\rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$
Therefore
$$(u - w) + w = \sigma^2 \cdot \frac{\rho + \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$

### PROPOSITION 114.

*If an area be contained by an apotome and the binomial straight line the terms of which are commensurable with the terms of the apotome and in the same ratio, the "side" of the area is rational.*

For let an area, the rectangle <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, be contained by the apotome <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and the binomial straight line <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> be the greater term of the latter;
let the terms <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> of the binomial straight line be commensurable with the terms <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> of the apotome and in the same ratio;
and let the "side" of the rectangle <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be <span data-ref="0256.g1:G">$G$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0256.g1:G">$G$</span> is rational.

![Proposition 114 diagram](0256.g1.svg)

For let a rational straight line <span data-ref="0256.g1:H">$H$</span> be set out, and to <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> let there be applied a rectangle equal to the square on <span data-ref="0256.g1:H">$H$</span> and producing <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> as breadth.
Therefore <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is an apotome.
Let its terms be <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ M L /">$ML$</span> commensurable with the terms <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0256.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> of the binomial straight line and in the same ratio. [x. 112]
