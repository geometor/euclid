:title: Proposition 114

To find $(u - w)$, $w$ algebraically we have

$$u = \frac{\sigma^2}{\rho - \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho},$$

$$\frac{u - w}{w} = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}.$$

From the latter

$$\begin{aligned}
w &= \frac{u \cdot \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho + \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho} \\
&= \frac{\sigma^2 \cdot \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.
\end{aligned}$$

Thus

$$u - w = w \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{k}} = \frac{\sigma^2\rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$

Therefore

$$(u - w) + w = \sigma^2 \cdot \frac{\rho + \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho}{\rho^2 - k\rho^2}.$$

PROPOSITION 114.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by an apotome and the binomial straight line the terms of which are commensurable with the terms of the apotome and in the same ratio, the “side” of the area is rational.

| For let an area, the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0256.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0256.g1:/ C D />`, be contained by
| the apotome :g:`$AB$ <0256.g1:/ A B />` and the binomial
| straight line :g:`$CD$ <0256.g1:/ C D />`,
| and let :g:`$CE$ <0256.g1:/ C E />` be the greater term of
| the latter;
| let the terms :g:`$CE$ <0256.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0256.g1:/ E D />` of the
| binomial straight line be commen-
| surable with the terms :g:`$AF$ <0256.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0256.g1:/ F B />` of
| the apotome and in the same ratio;
| and let the “side” of the rectangle
| :g:`$AB$ <0256.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0256.g1:/ C D />` be :g:`$G$ <0256.g1:G>`;

.. picture:: 0256.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 114 diagram

I say that :g:`$G$ <0256.g1:G>` is rational.

| For let a rational straight line :g:`$H$ <0256.g1:H>` be set out,
| and to :g:`$CD$ <0256.g1:/ C D />` let there be applied a rectangle equal to the square
| on :g:`$H$ <0256.g1:H>` and producing :g:`$KL$ <0256.g1:/ K L />` as breadth.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$KL$ <0256.g1:/ K L />` is an apotome.

| Let its terms be :g:`$KM$ <0256.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`$ML$ <0256.g1:/ M L />` commensurable with the terms
| :g:`$CE$ <0256.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$ED$ <0256.g1:/ E D />` of the binomial straight line and in the same ratio.
