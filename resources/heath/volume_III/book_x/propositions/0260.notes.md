## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Reconstruction**: Heath points out a major logical lacuna in the Arabic commentator's proof that the trinomial $x + y + z$ is irrational. Heath supplies the missing proof using the incompatibility between bimedial types (X. 72) and apotome types (X. 108), established in X. 111.
- **Relational Notation**: Uses `\sim` for commensurability and `\cup` for incommensurability: $(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) \cup (2yz + 2zx + 2xy)$.
