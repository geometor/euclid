## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Typographical Error in Print**: In line 21, the printed text reads "I presume that the metnod would be like that of x. 38" with "metnod" for "method". Transcribed verbatim as printed.
- **Incompatible Conditions Critique**: Heath critiques the Arabic text regarding the first trimedial line, proving algebraically that if $xy$ and $xz$ are both rational, then $y : z = m : n$, so $y$ and $z$ would be commensurable in length, contradicting the hypothesis that they are commensurable in square only. He adopts Woepcke's emended interpretation.
- **Display Structure**: At the foot of the page, the relation $(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) \sim x^2$ with $\cup xy$ aligned below it demonstrates that $\sigma t \sim x^2 \cup xy \sim \sigma w$, deducing that $t$ is incommensurable with $w$.
