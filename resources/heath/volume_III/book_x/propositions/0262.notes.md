## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Quadrinomial Reduction**: Heath shows that $(x + y + z)^2 = \sigma(t + u + v + w)$ where $t + u + v + w$ is a quadrinomial, thereby proving $x + y + z$ is irrational.
- **Greek Philological Term**: Heath notes the term *annex* with Greek gloss: $(\pi\rho\omicron\sigma\alpha\rho\mu\acute{o}\zeta\omicron\upsilon\sigma\alpha)$, the standard Euclidean term for the added rational segment in apotome theory.
- **Textual Critique of the Commentary**: Heath highlights Woepcke's struggle to make geometric sense of the Arabic text's definition of the three-term major irrational, noting that requiring both $x^2 + y^2$ and $x^2 + z^2$ to be rational is geometrically difficult and exceeds Euclid's construction in X. 33.
