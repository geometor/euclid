Transcription Notes: Folio 0264 (Page 260)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Page Position**: Page 260 is the opening page of Book XI of the *Elements*, initiating Euclid's treatment of Solid Geometry.
- **Content**: Contains the half-title ``BOOK XI.``, the heading ``DEFINITIONS.``, and Definitions 1 through 8.
- **Continuity**: Definitions continue on Page 261 (Folio 0265) with Definitions 9 through 19.

Textual & Lexical Observations
------------------------------

- **Def. 1 (Solid)**: Defines solid by three dimensions (length, breadth, depth).
- **Def. 2 (Surface)**: Extremity of a solid is a surface, extending the dimensional hierarchy from Book I (point $\to$ line $\to$ surface $\to$ solid).
- **Def. 3 (Perpendicular line to plane)**: Requires perpendicularity to *all* straight lines meeting it in the plane.
- **Def. 4 (Perpendicular planes)**: Defined via straight lines drawn perpendicular to the common section.
- **Def. 5 (Inclination of line to plane)**: Defined via projection of the inclined line onto the plane.
- **Def. 6 (Inclination of plane to plane)**: Plane angle of dihedral angle, restricted to the *acute* angle.
- **Def. 7 (Similarly inclined planes)**: Equivalence of inclination angles.
- **Def. 8 (Parallel planes)**: Non-meeting planes (ἀσύμπτωτα).
