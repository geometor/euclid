# Transcription Notes: Folio 0266 (Page 262)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Page 262 carries running header `262` / `BOOK XI` / `[XI. DEFF.`
- **Content**: Concludes Euclid's Book XI Definitions with Definitions 20 through 28, followed by Heath's detailed scholarly commentary commencing on Definition 1.
- **Continuity**: Follows Definitions 9–19 from Folio 0265 (Page 261). Heath's commentary on Definition 1 continues uninterrupted across the page break to Folio 0267 (Page 263).

## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Def. 20–24 (Cone Base & Cylinder)**: Complete the kinematic definitions of solids of revolution (cone and cylinder) and similar cones/cylinders.
- **Def. 25–28 (Regular Polyhedra)**: Definitions of four regular solids: cube, octahedron, icosahedron, dodecahedron (the fifth, tetrahedron, was defined as a pyramid in Def. 12).
- **Greek Text of Def. 1**: `Στερεόν ἐστι τὸ μῆκος καὶ πλάτος καὶ βάθος ἔχον.`
- **Heath's Commentary**: Exhaustively traces the conception of dimension and solid body in Plato (*Sophist*, *Laws*, *Republic*) and Aristotle (*Metaphysics*, *Topics*, *De Caelo*). Includes Greek terms: παράδειγμα, μαθήματα, διαστάσεις, τὸ πάντῃ διάστασιν ἔχον.
