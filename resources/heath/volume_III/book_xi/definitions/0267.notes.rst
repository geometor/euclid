Textual & Lexical Observations
------------------------------

- **Def. 1 (cont.)**: Aristotle's six dimensional directions (*Physics* IV. 1), followed by Heron (*Def.* 11) and Theon of Smyrna (ed. Hiller).
- **Def. 2 (Greek & Commentary)**: ``Στερεοῦ δὲ πέρας ἐπιφάνεια.`` Parallels in Aristotle (*Metaph.* 1066 b 23, 1060 b 15) and Heron's kinematic definition of surface as boundary and path of motion.
- **Def. 3 (Greek & Commentary)**: Perpendicular line to a plane. Heath discusses Fourier's definition of a plane (via Crelle) as the totality of perpendiculars to a line at a point, noting the circularity regarding angle measurement.
- **Def. 4 (Greek & Commentary)**: Perpendicular planes. Notes that the common section is not formally proved to be a straight line until XI. 3, and compares Legendre's reduction to Def. 6.
- **Def. 5 (Greek)**: Greek text defining the inclination of a straight line to a plane.
