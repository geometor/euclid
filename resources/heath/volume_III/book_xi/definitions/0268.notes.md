# Transcription Notes: Folio 0268 (Page 264)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Position**: Page 264 carries running header `264` / `BOOK XI` / `[XI. DEFF. 5, 6`.
- **Content**: Concludes commentary on Definition 5, gives Greek text of Definition 6, and provides Heath's extensive commentary on dihedral angles and Legendre's proof that the plane angle is a proper measure of the dihedral angle (part 1).
- **Continuity**: Follows from Folio 0267 (Page 263). Part (2) of Legendre's proof continues directly onto Folio 0269 (Page 265).

## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Def. 5 (cont.)**: Characterizes inclination as the angle between the straight line and its projection; compares angle magnitudes via XI. 4, I. 19, 18.
- **Def. 6 (Greek & Commentary)**: `Ἐπιπέδου πρὸς ἐπίπεδον κλίσις ἐστὶν ἡ περιεχομένη ὀξεῖα γωνία ὑπὸ τῶν πρὸς ὀρθὰς τῇ κοινῇ toμῇ ἀγομένων πρὸς τῷ αὐτῷ σημείῳ ἐν ἑκατέρῳ τῶν ἐπιπέδων.`
- **Dihedral Angle Analysis**: Traces terminology ("dihedral angle", "angular opening", Apollonius' broken surface via Proclus p. 123, 16, Legendre's provisional term "corner").
- **Legendre's Two-Part Theorem**: Formulates the requirements for plane angle to serve as measure: (1) invariance along common edge $MA$, and (2) proportionality to dihedral angle.
