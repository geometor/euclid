Transcription Notes: Folio 0269 (Page 265)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Page Position**: Page 265 carries running header ``XI. DEFF. 6—10]`` / ``NOTES ON DEFINITIONS 5—10`` / ``265``.
- **Content**: Completes Legendre's dihedral angle proof part (2) from Def. 6; gives Greek texts of Definitions 7, 8, 9, 10; provides commentary on Def. 8 (Heron / asymptotes) and initiates the major critique of Definitions 9 and 10 by Robert Simson.
- **Continuity**: Follows from Folio 0268 (Page 264). Simson's counterexample and Legendre's subsequent analysis continue seamlessly onto Folio 0270 (Page 266).

Textual & Lexical Observations
------------------------------

- **Legendre Proof Part (2)**: Establishes proportionality for commensurable angles, notes Legendre's reduction for incommensurables (analogous to VI. 1), and modern treatments using limits.
- **Def. 7 (Greek)**: ``Ἐπίπεδον πρὸς ἐπίπεδον ὁμοίως κεκλίσθαι λέγεται καὶ ἕτερον πρὸς ἕτερον, ὅταν αἱ εἰρημέναι τῶν κλίσεων γωνίαι ἴσαι ἀλλήλαις ὦσιν.``
- **Def. 8 (Greek & Commentary)**: ``Παράλληλα ἐπίπεδά ἐστι τὰ ἀσύμπτωτα.`` Heath highlights the term ἀσύμπτωτα ("non-falling together") and its connection to geometric asymptotes.
- **Def. 9 & 10 (Greek & Commentary)**:

  - Def. 9: ``Ὅμοια στερεὰ σχήματά ἐστι τὰ ὑπὸ ὁμοίων ἐπιπέδων περιεχόμενα ἴσων τὸ πλῆθος.``
  - Def. 10: ``Ἴσα δὲ καὶ ὅμοια στερεὰ σχήματά ἐστι τὰ ὑπὸ ὁμοίων ἐπιπέδων περιεχόμενα ἴσων τῷ πλήθει καὶ τῷ μεγέθει.``
  - Simson's critique: Equality of solids is a theorem, not a definition; counterexample constructed by adding and subtracting smaller pyramids to/from a base pyramid.
