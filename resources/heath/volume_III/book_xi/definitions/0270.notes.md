## Textual & Lexical Observations
- **Simson's Critique & Revision**:
  - Simson asserts Def. 10 is an interpolation by "an unskilful hand."
  - Emphasizes the re-entrant angle in Simson's counterexample.
  - Simson's amended definition: equality of all solid angles, each to each, along with equal number of similar planes.
- **Legendre's Note XII**:
  - Highlights that equality by equality of faces alone requires proof; otherwise Def. 9 leads to contradictory similarity to two dissimilar solids.
- **Euclid's Defence**:
  - Heath demonstrates Euclid only applies Deff. 9 & 10 to figures whose solid angles consist of at most three plane angles (trihedral angles).
  - Euclid implicitly restricted scope to *convex* polyhedra, rendering Simson's re-entrant counterexample inapplicable.
- **Legendre's Resolution**:
  - Defines similar triangular pyramids first: pairs of faces similar, similarly placed, and equally inclined to one another.
