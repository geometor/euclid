## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Heron's Prismoid**: Heron's broad definition of prism encompasses prismoids having unequal polygonal bases connected by triangles/quadrilaterals.
- **Kinematic Definition of Sphere**: Heath explains why Euclid defined the sphere by revolution of a semicircle: Book XIII requires this conception to circumscribe the five regular polyhedra by showing vertices lie on rotating semicircles.
- **Aristotelian Definition**: Aristotle's static equidistant definition (*De Caelo* II. 14, 297a24) quoted in Greek: τὸ ἴσον ἀπέχειν τοῦ μέσου τὸ ἔσχατον.
