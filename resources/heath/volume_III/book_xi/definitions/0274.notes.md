- **Transitions**: Concludes sphere definitions and opens cone definitions, providing Heath's historical excursus on the pre-Apollonian theory of conic sections. Connects to folio 0275 which gives Definitions 19–28.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Pre-Apollonian Conics**: The second sentence of Euclid's Def. 18 classifies right cones based on the ratio of the fixed side to the rotating side (equal = right-angled cone; less = obtuse-angled cone; greater = acute-angled cone). Menaechmus and Archimedes used these three cone types cut perpendicularly to a generator to obtain the parabola (orthotome), hyperbola (amblytome), and ellipse (oxytome).
- **Apollonius's Double Cone**: Heath details Apollonius's definition in the *Conics*, generating a oblique circular double cone by a line pivoted at a vertex passing through a circle.
- **Archimedes' "Isosceles Cone"**: Archimedes' standard term for a right cone (κῶνος ἰσοσκελής).
