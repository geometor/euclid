# Transcription Notes: Folio 0276 (Page 272)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 272 of Volume III. Opens the Propositions section of Book XI (`BOOK XI. PROPOSITIONS.`). Contains Proposition 1 enunciation, proof, diagram, lexical notes on Greek phrases, and the opening paragraph of Heath's commentary.
- **Transitions**: Transition from Definitions to Propositions. Heath's commentary at the bottom flows directly across to folio 0277 ("which / has commended itself to many writers...").

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Lexical Notes**: Heath provides Greek equivalents for key technical terminology at the base of the proof:
  - `1.` "the plane of reference" = τὸ ὑποκείμενον ἐπίπεδον (the plane laid down or assumed).
  - `2.` "more elevated" = μετεωροτέρῳ.
- **Critique of Euclid's Solid Axioms**: Heath begins his critique noting that Euclid cannot use Def. 7 (plane surface) in these proofs, which fundamentally rely on unstated geometric axioms.
- **Line Continuity**: The proof uses the circular argument of describing a circle around B with radius AB to show the absurdity of a common segment, which Heath critiques on the subsequent page.
