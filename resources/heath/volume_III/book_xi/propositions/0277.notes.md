## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Axiom of the Plane**: Attributed to Theon of Smyrna (p. 112, 5): a straight line meeting a surface in two points lies wholly in it (ὅλη αὐτῷ ἐφαρμόζεται).
- **Clavius's Construction**: Clavius draws CG ⟂ AC and CF ⟂ CG in plane DE to invoke I. 14, but tacitly presumes the very plane containment property being investigated.
- **Circularity Critique**: Heath highlights that Euclid's circle construction in Prop. 1 presumes BA, BC, and BD lie in one plane, thus anticipating Proposition 2.
