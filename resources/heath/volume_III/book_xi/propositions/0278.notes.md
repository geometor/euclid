# Transcription Notes: Folio 0278 (Page 274)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 274 of Volume III. Concludes Simson's altered proof of Proposition 1 and Heath's commentary regarding Theon's interpolation and Thomas Simpson's critique. Opens Proposition 2 enunciation and the first portion of the proof with diagram.
- **Transitions**: Follows directly from folio 0277. The proof of Proposition 2 continues onto page 275 (folio 0279).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Common Segment Interpolation**: Analysis of Theon's interpolation: "for a straight line does not meet a straight line in more points than one; otherwise the straight lines will coincide." Heath defends this argument against Robert Simson's criticism.
- **Proposition 2 Enunciation & Structure**: Deals with two straight lines cutting one another, showing they lie in one plane, and every triangle lies in one plane.
- **Proof Technique**: Heath uses the method of exhaustively considering partial containment of the triangle (sub-triangles FHC, GBK, or trapezoidal quadrilateral FCBG) and reducing them to Proposition 1 [XI. 1].
