## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Four Conditions of Determination**:
  1. Three straight lines meeting two and two.
  2. Three non-collinear points.
  3. Two intersecting straight lines.
  4. A straight line and an external point.
- **Axiomatic Grounding**: Proposition 3 relies on the fundamental property of straight lines that two straight lines cannot enclose a space (Book I, Postulate 2 / common notion).
- **Simson's Critique**: Heath endorses Simson's view that once an absurdity is deduced from assuming $DEB$ and $DFB$ are straight lines differing from $DB$, one immediately concludes $DB$ is a straight line without redundant intermediate steps.
