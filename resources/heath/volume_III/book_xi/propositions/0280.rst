:title: Proposition 3

It follows from the above that

| *A plane is determined (i.e. uniquely determined) by any of the following data:*
| (1) *by three straight lines meeting one another two and two,*
| (2) *by three points not in a straight line,*
| (3) *by two straight lines meeting one another,*
| (4) *by a straight line and a point without it.*

PROPOSITION 3.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If two planes cut one another, their common section is a straight line.

| For let the two planes :g:`$AB$ <0280.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0280.g1:< B C >>` cut one another,
| and let the line :g:`$DB$ <0280.g1:/ D B />` be their common
| section;
| I say that the line :g:`$DB$ <0280.g1:/ D B />` is a straight line.

.. picture:: 0280.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 3

| For, if not, from :g:`$D$ <0280.g1:D>` to :g:`$B$ <0280.g1:B>` let the straight
| line :g:`$DEB$ <0280.g1:/ D E B />` be joined in the plane :g:`$AB$ <0280.g1:< A B >>`, and
| in the plane :g:`$BC$ <0280.g1:< B C >>` the straight line :g:`$DFB$ <0280.g1:/ D F B />`.

.. assertion::

   | Then the two straight lines :g:`$DEB$ <0280.g1:/ D E B />`, :g:`$DFB$ <0280.g1:/ D F B />`
   | will have the same extremities, and will
   | clearly enclose an area:

.. assertion::

   which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$DEB$ <0280.g1:/ D E B />`, :g:`$DFB$ <0280.g1:/ D F B />` are not straight lines.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly we can prove that neither will there be any
   | other straight line joined from :g:`$D$ <0280.g1:D>` to :g:`$B$ <0280.g1:B>` except :g:`$DB$ <0280.g1:/ D B />` the common
   | section of the planes :g:`$AB$ <0280.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0280.g1:< B C >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
