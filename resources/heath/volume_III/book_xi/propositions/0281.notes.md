# Transcription Notes: Folio 0281 (Page 277)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Folio 0281 represents page 277 of Volume III, Book XI.
- **Preceding Context**: Folio 0280 ended with Heath introducing von Staudt's proposition.
- **Current Content**:
  - Full enunciation, proof, and diagram of von Staudt's proposition: "If two planes meet in a point, they meet in a straight line."
  - Complementary modern theorem: "If three planes intersect two and two, their lines of intersection either meet in a point or are parallel two and two." Heath provides the complete proof for both cases: (1) intersecting at concurrency point $O$; (2) mutual parallelism.
  - Enunciation of Proposition 4: erecting a straight line at right angles to two intersecting straight lines at their intersection point makes it perpendicular to their plane.
- **Succeeding Context**: Folio 0282 (page 278) commences the formal setting-out, construction, and proof of Proposition 4.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Modern Solid Geometry Additions**: These two theorems inserted by Heath bridging Props 3 and 4 form standard staples of modern synthetic 3D geometry (often used to define bundles of planes and prismatic configurations).
- **Simson's Definition**: Cited as authority for line $AG$ lying in both planes once two common points $A, G$ are found.
