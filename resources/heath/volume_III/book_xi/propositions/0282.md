For let a straight line <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set up at right angles to the<br />
two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, which<br />
cut one another at the point <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
from <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also at right<br />
angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> be<br />
cut off equal to one another,<br />
and let any straight line <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E H /">$GEH$</span> be drawn across through <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>,<br />
at random;<br />
let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> be joined,<br />
and further let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> be joined from<br />
the point <span data-ref="0282.g1:F">$F$</span> taken at random &lt;on <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>&gt;.

![Book XI, Proposition 4](0282.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are equal to<br />
the two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, and contain equal angles, <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
and the triangle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A E D >">$AED$</span> will be equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< C E B >">$CEB$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span><br />
so that the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< D A E )">$DAE$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A E G )">$AEG$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< B E H )">$BEH$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 15]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A G E >">$AGE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:< B E H >">$BEH$</span> are two triangles which have two<br />
angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal<br />
to one side, namely that adjacent to the equal angles, that<br />
is to say, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>;<br />
therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the<br />
remaining sides. <span class="justification">[I. 26]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is common and at right angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span><br />
respectively;<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> was proved equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F A D )">$FAD$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F B C )">$FBC$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>
