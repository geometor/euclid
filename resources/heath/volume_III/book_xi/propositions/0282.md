For let a straight line <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be set up at right angles to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, which cut one another at the point <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>, from <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>;
I say that <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> be cut off equal to one another, and let any straight line <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E H /">$GEH$</span> be drawn across through <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>, at random; let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> be joined, and further let <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> be joined from the point <span data-ref="0282.g1:F">$F$</span> taken at random &lt;on <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>&gt;.

![Book XI, Proposition 4](0282.g1.svg)

Now, since the two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> are equal to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, and contain equal angles, [I. 15]
therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, and the triangle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A E D >">$AED$</span> will be equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< C E B >">$CEB$</span>; [I. 4]
so that the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< D A E )">$DAE$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< E B C )">$EBC$</span>.

But the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A E G )">$AEG$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< B E H )">$BEH$</span>; [I. 15]
therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:< A G E >">$AGE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:< B E H >">$BEH$</span> are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely that adjacent to the equal angles, that is to say, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>;
therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides.        [I. 26]

Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, while <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is common and at right angles, therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>.        [I. 4]

For the same reason <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> respectively;
and the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> was proved equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F A D )">$FAD$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F B C )">$FBC$</span>. [I. 8]
