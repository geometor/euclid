<p class="sub-clause">And since, again, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> was proved equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>,<br />
and further <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>.<br />
And the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F A G )">$FAG$</span> was proved equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F B H )">$FBH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now since, again, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> was proved equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is common,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< G E F )">$GEF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0282.g1:< G E F )">$GEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> drawn at random<br />
through <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> will also make right<br />
angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the<br />
plane of reference.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it<br />
makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it<br />
and are in that same plane; <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc. <span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
