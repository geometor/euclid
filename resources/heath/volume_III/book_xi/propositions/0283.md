And since, again, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> was proved equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>, and further <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> also equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>.

And the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F A G )">$FAG$</span> was proved equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F B H )">$FBH$</span>;
therefore the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>.        [I. 4]

Now since, again, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> was proved equal to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, and <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is common, the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
and the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< G E F )">$GEF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0282.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span>.        [I. 8]

Therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0282.g1:< G E F )">$GEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span> is right.

Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> drawn at random through <span data-ref="0282.g1:E">$E$</span>.

Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.

But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane;        [XI. Def. 3]
therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference.

But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight lines <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Therefore etc.

                                                                        Q. E. D.
