## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **XI. Def. 3 Invocation**: The critical definition that a straight line perpendicular to a plane must be perpendicular to *all* straight lines meeting it in that plane is formally satisfied by proving it for the transversal $GH$ drawn "at random" (*ἔτυχεν*).
- **Heath's Didactic Synthesis**: Heath numbers the sequence of 7 triangular congruences explicitly to give the student an immediate structural roadmap of Euclid's elaborate proof.
