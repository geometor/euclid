.. subclause::

   And since, again, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`, and further :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />` also :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`$FA$ <0282.g1:/ F A />`, :g:`$AG$ <0282.g1:/ A G />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F A / = 0282.g1:/ F B /; 0282.g1:/ A G / = 0282.g1:/ B H />` to the two sides :g:`$FB$ <0282.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$BH$ <0282.g1:/ B H />`. .. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

.. subclause::

   Now since, again, :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />` was proved :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0282.g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` is common, the two sides :g:`$GE$ <0282.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />` are :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ G E / = 0282.g1:/ H E />` to the two sides :g:`$HE$ <0282.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0282.g1:/ E F />`; and the base :g:`$FG$ <0282.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:/ F G / = 0282.g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`$FH$ <0282.g1:/ F H />`;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>` is :eq:`equal <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>` to the angle :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the angles :g:`$GEF$ <0282.g1:< G E F )>`, :g:`$HEF$ <0282.g1:< H E F )>` is :eq:`right <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at :eq:`right angles <0282.g1:< G E F ) = 0282.g1:< H E F )>` to :g:`$GH$ <0282.g1:/ G H />` drawn at random through :g:`$E$ <0282.g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane; :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane through :g:`$AB$ <0282.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0282.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
