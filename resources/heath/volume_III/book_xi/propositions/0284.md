(7) triangles <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F E G >">$FEG$</span>, <span data-ref="0282.g1:< F E H >">$FEH$</span> equal in all respects,        [by (2), (6) and I. 8]

and therefore <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0282.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.

In consequence of the length of the above proof others have been suggested, and the proof which now finds most general acceptance is that of Cauchy, which is as follows.

Let <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be perpendicular to two straight lines <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> in the plane <span data-ref="0284.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> at their point of intersection <span data-ref="0284.g1:B">$B$</span>.

In the plane <span data-ref="0284.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> draw <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, any straight line through <span data-ref="0284.g1:B">$B$</span>.

Join <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> meet <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> in <span data-ref="0284.g1:E">$E$</span>.

Produce <span data-ref="0284.g1:[ A B ]">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0284.g1:F">$F$</span> so that <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

Join <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>.

Since <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is perpendicular to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> at its middle point <span data-ref="0284.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.

Similarly <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>.

Since in the triangles <span data-ref="0284.g1:< A C D >">$ACD$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:< F C D >">$FCD$</span> the two sides <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> are respectively equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, and the third sides <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> are also equal,
the angles <span data-ref="0284.g1:< A C D )">$ACD$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:< F C D )">$FCD$</span> are equal.        [I. 8]

The triangles <span data-ref="0284.g1:< A C E >">$ACE$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:< F C E >">$FCE$</span> thus have two sides and the included angle equal, whence
<span data-ref="0284.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.        [I. 4]

The triangles <span data-ref="0284.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:< F B E >">$FBE$</span> have now all their sides equal respectively;
therefore the angles <span data-ref="0284.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span>, <span data-ref="0284.g1:< F B E )">$FBE$</span> are equal,        [I. 8]
and <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is perpendicular to <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0284.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is in any straight line through <span data-ref="0284.g1:B">$B$</span> in the plane <span data-ref="0284.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>.

Legendre's proof is not so easy, but it is interesting.  We are first required to draw through any point <span data-ref="0284.g2:E">$E$</span> within the angle <span data-ref="0284.g2:< C B D )">$CBD$</span> a straight line <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span> bisected at <span data-ref="0284.g2:E">$E$</span>.

To do this we draw <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ E K /">$EK$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ D B /">$DB$</span> meeting <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span> in <span data-ref="0284.g2:K">$K$</span>, and then mark off <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ K C /">$KC$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0284.g2:/ B K /">$BK$</span>.
