    - Uses point reflection across the base plane ($BF = AB$).
    - Congruence sequence: $ACD \cong FCD$ (I. 8) $\implies ACE \cong FCE$ (I. 4) $\implies EA = EF \implies ABE \cong FBE$ (I. 8) $\implies AB \perp BE$.

      $AC^2 + AD^2 = 2AE^2 + 2ED^2$
      $BC^2 + BD^2 = 2BE^2 + 2ED^2$

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Cauchy's Method**: Avoids the complex transversal $GH$ of Euclid by reflecting $A$ across the plane to $F$, making the plane a plane of mirror symmetry.
- **Legendre's Algebraic Method**: Uses metric relations ($a^2+b^2=2m^2+2d^2$) and Pythagorean reduction, showing the 19th-century transition toward metric Euclidean geometry.
