| (7) triangles :g:`$FEG$ <0282.g1:< F E G >>`, :g:`$FEH$ <0282.g1:< F E H >>` equal in all respects, :just:`[by (2), (6) and I. 8]`
| so that the angles :g:`$FEG$ <0282.g1:< F E G )>`, :g:`$FEH$ <0282.g1:< F E H )>` are equal,
| and therefore :g:`$FE$ <0282.g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to :g:`$GH$ <0282.g1:/ G H />`.

| In consequence of the length of the above proof others have been
| suggested, and the proof which now finds most general acceptance is that of
| :person:`Cauchy <cauchy>`, which is as follows.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0284.g1:/ A B />` be perpendicular to two straight lines :g:`$BC$ <0284.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0284.g1:/ B D />` in the plane :g:`$MN$ <0284.g1:< M N >>`
| at their point of intersection :g:`$B$ <0284.g1:B>`.
| In the plane :g:`$MN$ <0284.g1:< M N >>` draw :g:`$BE$ <0284.g1:/ B E />`, any straight line
| through :g:`$B$ <0284.g1:B>`.
| Join :g:`$CD$ <0284.g1:/ C D />`, and let :g:`$CD$ <0284.g1:/ C D />` meet :g:`$BE$ <0284.g1:/ B E />` in :g:`$E$ <0284.g1:E>`.
| Produce :g:`$AB$ <0284.g1:[ A B ]>` to :g:`$F$ <0284.g1:F>` so that :g:`$BF$ <0284.g1:/ B F />` is equal to :g:`$AB$ <0284.g1:/ A B />`.
| Join :g:`$AC$ <0284.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AE$ <0284.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$AD$ <0284.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$CF$ <0284.g1:/ C F />`, :g:`$EF$ <0284.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$DF$ <0284.g1:/ D F />`.

.. picture:: 0284.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 4, Cauchy's Proof

| Since :g:`$BC$ <0284.g1:/ B C />` is perpendicular to :g:`$AF$ <0284.g1:/ A F />` at its
| middle point :g:`$B$ <0284.g1:B>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AC$ <0284.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$CF$ <0284.g1:/ C F />`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly :g:`$AD$ <0284.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$DF$ <0284.g1:/ D F />`.

.. subclause::

   | Since in the triangles :g:`$ACD$ <0284.g1:< A C D >>`, :g:`$FCD$ <0284.g1:< F C D >>` the two
   | sides :g:`$AC$ <0284.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0284.g1:/ C D />` are respectively equal to the two
   | sides :g:`$FC$ <0284.g1:/ F C />`, :g:`$CD$ <0284.g1:/ C D />`, and the third sides :g:`$AD$ <0284.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$FD$ <0284.g1:/ F D />` are
   | also equal,

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   the angles :g:`$ACD$ <0284.g1:< A C D )>`, :g:`$FCD$ <0284.g1:< F C D )>` are equal.

| The triangles :g:`$ACE$ <0284.g1:< A C E >>`, :g:`$FCE$ <0284.g1:< F C E >>` thus have two sides and the included angle
| equal, whence

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   :g:`$EA$ <0284.g1:/ E A />` is equal to :g:`$EF$ <0284.g1:/ E F />`.

The triangles :g:`$ABE$ <0284.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`$FBE$ <0284.g1:< F B E >>` have now all their sides equal respectively; 

.. assertion::

   | therefore the angles :g:`$ABE$ <0284.g1:< A B E )>`, :g:`$FBE$ <0284.g1:< F B E )>` are equal, :just:`[I. 8] <I.8>`
   | and :g:`$AB$ <0284.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to :g:`$BE$ <0284.g1:/ B E />`.

And :g:`$BE$ <0284.g1:/ B E />` is in any straight line through :g:`$B$ <0284.g1:B>` in the plane :g:`$MN$ <0284.g1:< M N >>`.

| :person:`Legendre <legendre>`'s proof is not so easy, but it is interesting.  We are first required
| to draw through any point :g:`$E$ <0284.g2:E>` within the angle
| :g:`$CBD$ <0284.g2:< C B D )>` a straight line :g:`$CD$ <0284.g2:/ C D />` bisected at :g:`$E$ <0284.g2:E>`.
| To do this we draw :g:`$EK$ <0284.g2:/ E K />` parallel to :g:`$DB$ <0284.g2:/ D B />`
| meeting :g:`$BC$ <0284.g2:/ B C />` in :g:`$K$ <0284.g2:K>`, and then mark off :g:`$KC$ <0284.g2:/ K C />` equal
| to :g:`$BK$ <0284.g2:/ B K />`.
| :g:`$CE$ <0284.g2:/ C E />` is then joined and produced to :g:`$D$ <0284.g2:D>`; and
| :g:`$CD$ <0284.g2:/ C D />` is the straight line required.

| Now, joining :g:`$AC$ <0284.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AE$ <0284.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$AD$ <0284.g1:/ A D />` in the figure
| above, we have, since :g:`$CD$ <0284.g1:/ C D />` is bisected at :g:`$E$ <0284.g1:E>`,
| (1) in the triangle :g:`$ACD$ <0284.g1:< A C D >>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AC^2$ <0284.g1:/ A C />` $+$ :g:`$AD^2$ <0284.g1:/ A D />` $= 2$ :g:`$AE^2$ <0284.g1:/ A E />` $+ 2$ :g:`$ED^2$ <0284.g1:/ E D />`,

and also (2) in the triangle :g:`$BCD$ <0284.g1:< B C D >>`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$BC^2$ <0284.g1:/ B C />` $+$ :g:`$BD^2$ <0284.g1:/ B D />` $= 2$ :g:`$BE^2$ <0284.g1:/ B E />` $+ 2$ :g:`$ED^2$ <0284.g1:/ E D />`.

| Subtracting, and remembering that the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0284.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$ABD$ <0284.g1:< A B D >>` are right-
| angled, so that

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AC^2$ <0284.g1:/ A C />` $-$ :g:`$BC^2$ <0284.g1:/ B C />` $=$ :g:`$AB^2$ <0284.g1:/ A B />`,

and

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AD^2$ <0284.g1:/ A D />` $-$ :g:`$BD^2$ <0284.g1:/ B D />` $=$ :g:`$AB^2$ <0284.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   $2$ :g:`$AB^2$ <0284.g1:/ A B />` $= 2$ :g:`$AE^2$ <0284.g1:/ A E />` $- 2$ :g:`$BE^2$ <0284.g1:/ B E />`,

or

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AE^2$ <0284.g1:/ A E />` $=$ :g:`$AB^2$ <0284.g1:/ A B />` $+$ :g:`$BE^2$ <0284.g1:/ B E />`,
