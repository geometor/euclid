### PROPOSITION 6.

*If two straight lines be at right angles to the same plane, the straight lines will be parallel.*

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be at right angles to the plane of reference;
I say that <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Book XI - Proposition 6](0287.g1.svg)

For let them meet the plane of reference at the points <span data-ref="0287.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:D">$D$</span>,
let the straight line <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined,
let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn, in the plane of reference, at right angles to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be joined.

Now, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference,
it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.            [XI. Def. 3]

But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is in the plane of reference and meets <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is right.

For the same reason
each of the angles <span data-ref="0287.g1:< C D B )">$CDB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span> is also right.

And, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is common,
the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>;
and they include right angles;
therefore the base <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.            [I. 4]

And, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>,
the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>;
and <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is their common base;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0287.g1:< E D A )">$EDA$</span>.            [I. 8]
