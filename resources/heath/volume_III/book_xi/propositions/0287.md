### PROPOSITION 6.

<p class="enunciation">If two straight lines be at right angles to the same plane,<br />
the straight lines will be parallel.</p>

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be at right angles<br />
to the plane of reference;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Book XI - Proposition 6](0287.g1.svg)

For let them meet the plane of<br />
reference at the points <span data-ref="0287.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:D">$D$</span>,<br />
let the straight line <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined,<br />
let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn, in the plane of<br />
reference, at right angles to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference,<br />
it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which<br />
meet it and are in the plane of reference. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is in the plane of<br />
reference and meets <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
each of the angles <span data-ref="0287.g1:< C D B )">$CDB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span> is also right.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is common,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>;<br />
and they include right angles;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>;<br />
and <span data-ref="0287.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is their common base;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0287.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0287.g1:< E D A )">$EDA$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>
