# Transcription Notes: Folio 0287 (Page 283)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page & Book**: Volume III, Book XI, Page 283.
- **Top of Page**: Conclusion of Heath's commentary on Proposition 5 from folio 0286, detailing the two contradictory cases of assuming a second perpendicular plane from point $P$ to line $AB$.
- **Middle to Bottom of Page**: Proposition 6 begins with enunciation: "If two straight lines be at right angles to the same plane, the straight lines will be parallel."
- **Euclidean Proof**: Setting out and the first half of Euclid's proof for Proposition 6. Includes the primary construction ($BD$ joined, $DE \perp BD$ in the plane, $DE = AB$, and $BE, AE, AD$ joined) and pairwise triangle congruences by I. 4 and I. 8.
- **Continuity**: Ends at the deduction $\angle ABE = \angle EDA$ [I. 8]; the proof continues directly on folio 0288 (page 284) with "But the angle $ABE$ is right...".

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 6 Enunciation**: Crucial theorem in stereometry establishing parallelism from common perpendicularity to a plane.
- **Citations in Margins**: [XI. Def. 3] for right angles made with lines in the plane; [I. 4] (SAS congruence) proving $AD = BE$; [I. 8] (SSS congruence) proving $\angle ABE = \angle EDA$.
