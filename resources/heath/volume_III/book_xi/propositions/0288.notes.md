# Transcription Notes: Folio 0288 (Page 284)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page & Book**: Volume III, Book XI, Page 284.
- **Top of Page**: Conclusion of Euclid's proof of Proposition 6. Uses XI. 5 to show that $BD, DA, DC$ are coplanar, XI. 2 to show $AB, BD, DC$ are coplanar, and I. 28 to deduce $AB \parallel CD$. Concludes with Q. E. D.
- **Middle & Bottom of Page**: Heath's commentary examining the logical ordering of Proposition 6 and its converse, Proposition 8, in historical and modern textbooks (Legendre, Mehler, Lardner, Holgate, Schultze & Sevenoak, Smith & Bryant).
- **Legendre's Alternative Proof**: Heath begins presenting Legendre's proof for XI. 6, setting up plane $MN$, perpendiculars $AB, CD$, joining $BD$, and constructing symmetric transversal $EDF \perp BD$ with $ED = DF$.
- **Continuity**: The construction at the bottom continues on folio 0289 (page 285) with "Join $BE$, $BF$, $AE$, $AD$, $AF$."

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Coplanarity Deductions**: Euclid applies XI. 5 (three lines perpendicular to a common line are coplanar) and XI. 2 (triangle $ABD$ coplanar) to demonstrate that the lines lie in one plane before applying plane theorem I. 28.
- **Editorial Survey**: Heath notes how divergence in foundational definitions led various editors into circular reasoning or awkward ordering of XI. 6 and XI. 8.
