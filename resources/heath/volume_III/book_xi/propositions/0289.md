### PROPOSITION 7.

<p class="enunciation">If two straight lines be parallel and points be taken at<br />
random on each of them, the straight line joining the points is<br />
in the same plane with the parallel straight lines.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be two parallel straight lines,<br />
and let points <span data-ref="0289.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:F">$F$</span> be taken at random<br />
on them respectively;<br />
I say that the straight line joining the<br />
points <span data-ref="0289.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:F">$F$</span> is in the same plane with<br />
the parallel straight lines.

![Book XI - Proposition 7](0289.g1.svg)

For suppose it is not, but, if possible,<br />
let it be in a more elevated plane as<br />
<span data-ref="0289.g1:( E G F )">$EGF$</span>,<br />
and let a plane be drawn through <span data-ref="0289.g1:( E G F )">$EGF$</span>;
<p class="assertion">it will then make, as section in the plane of reference, a<br />
straight line. <span class="justification">[XI. 3]</span></p>
