### PROPOSITION 7.

*If two straight lines be parallel and points be taken at random on each of them, the straight line joining the points is in the same plane with the parallel straight lines.*

Let <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be two parallel straight lines,
and let points <span data-ref="0289.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:F">$F$</span> be taken at random on them respectively;
I say that the straight line joining the points <span data-ref="0289.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0289.g1:F">$F$</span> is in the same plane with the parallel straight lines.

![Book XI - Proposition 7](0289.g1.svg)

For suppose it is not, but, if possible, let it be in a more elevated plane as <span data-ref="0289.g1:( E G F )">$EGF$</span>,
and let a plane be drawn through <span data-ref="0289.g1:( E G F )">$EGF$</span>;
it will then make, as section in the plane of reference, a straight line.            [XI. 3]
