# Transcription Notes: Folio 0289 (Page 285)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page & Book**: Volume III, Book XI, Page 285.
- **Top of Page**: Conclusion of Heath's commentary on Proposition 6. Completes Legendre's and Mehler's proofs using triangle congruences ($BE = BF$, $AE = AF$, $CE = CF$) and either the locus of equidistant points or XI. 5 and XI. 2 to prove coplanarity and parallelism of $AB$ and $CD$.
- **Middle to Bottom of Page**: Proposition 7 begins with enunciation: "If two straight lines be parallel and points be taken at random on each of them, the straight line joining the points is in the same plane with the parallel straight lines."
- **Euclidean Proof**: Setting out and initiation of the indirect proof (*reductio ad absurdum*). Hypothesizes that the transversal lies in a more elevated plane $EGF$ and introduces the planar section through $EGF$ (XI. 3).
- **Continuity**: Breaks off at the citation "[XI. 3]"; continues on folio 0290 (page 286) with "Let it make it, as $EF$; / therefore the two straight lines $EGF$, $EF$ will enclose an area...".

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mehler's Locus Argument**: Points $A, B, C, D$ all lie on the planar locus of points equidistant from $E$ and $F$.
- **Proposition 7 Enunciation**: Foundational property ensuring that a plane containing parallel lines also contains all transversals between them.
- **Euclidean Reductio**: Uses XI. 3 (intersection of two planes is a straight line) to set up the contradiction of two straight lines enclosing an area.
