But in Book XI. it becomes a question whether there can be *more than* one plane through parallel straight lines.

:person:`Simson <simson>` goes on to say (2) “Besides, this 7th Proposition is demonstrated by the preceding 3rd; in which the very same thing which is proposed to be demonstrated in the 7th is twice assumed, viz., that the straight line drawn from one point to another in a plane is in that plane.” But there is nothing in Prop. 3 about a plane in which two parallel straight lines are; therefore there is no assumption of the result of Prop. 7. What is assumed is that, given two points in *a* plane, they can be joined by a straight line in the plane: a legitimate assumption.

Lastly, says :person:`Simson <simson>`, “And the same thing is assumed in the preceding 6th Prop. in which the straight line which joins the points :g:`$B$ <0287.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0287.g1:D>` that are in the plane to which :g:`$AB$ <0287.g1:/ A B />` and :g:`$CD$ <0287.g1:/ C D />` are at right angles is supposed to be in that plane.” Here again there is no question of *a plane in which two parallels are*; so that the criticism here, as with reference to Prop. 3, appears to rest on a misapprehension.

----

PROPOSITION 8.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines be parallel, and one of them be at right angles to any plane, the remaining one will also be at right angles to the same plane.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0291.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0291.g1:/ C D />` be two parallel straight lines, and let one of them, :g:`$AB$ <0291.g1:/ A B />`, be at right angles to the plane of reference; I say that the remaining one, :g:`$CD$ <0291.g1:/ C D />`, will also be at right angles to the same plane.

.. picture:: 0291.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI - Proposition 8

For let :g:`$AB$ <0291.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0291.g1:/ C D />` meet the plane of reference at the points :g:`$B$ <0291.g1:B>`, :g:`$D$ <0291.g1:D>`, and let :g:`$BD$ <0291.g1:/ B D />` be joined;

.. assertion:: [XI. 7] <XI.7>

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0291.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0291.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$BD$ <0291.g1:/ B D />` are in one plane.

Let :g:`$DE$ <0291.g1:/ D E />` be drawn, in the plane of reference, at right angles to :g:`$BD$ <0291.g1:/ B D />`, let :g:`$DE$ <0291.g1:/ D E />` be made :eq:`equal <0291.g1:/ D E / = 0291.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0291.g1:/ A B />`, and let :g:`$BE$ <0291.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$AE$ <0291.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`$AD$ <0291.g1:/ A D />` be joined.
