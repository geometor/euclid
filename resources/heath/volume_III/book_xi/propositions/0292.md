<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0291.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:< C D B )">$CDB$</span> are equal to two right<br />
angles. <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span> is right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< C D B )">$CDB$</span> is also right;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is common,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>;<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< A B D )">$ABD$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< E D B )">$EDB$</span>,<br />
for each is right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span><br />
respectively,<br />
and <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> is their common base;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< E D A )">$EDA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span> is right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0291.g1:< E D A )">$EDA$</span> is also right;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 4]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> will also make right angles with all the<br />
straight lines which meet it and are in the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is in the plane through <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, inasmuch as<br />
<span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are in the plane through <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, <span class="justification">[XI. 2]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is also in the plane in which <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> are.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0291.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is set up at right angles to the two straight<br />
