### PROPOSITION 9.

*Straight lines which are parallel to the same straight line and are not in the same plane with it are also parallel to one another.*

For let each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, not being in the same plane with it;
I say that <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Proposition 9](0294.g1.svg)

    For let a point <span data-ref="0294.g1:G">$G$</span> be taken at random on <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and from it let there be drawn <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, in the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, at right angles to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> in the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> again at right angles to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.
    Now, since <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>. [XI. 4]
    And <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>. [XI. 8]
    For the same reason
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>.
    But, if two straight lines be at right angles to the same plane, the straight lines are parallel; [XI. 6]
therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.
