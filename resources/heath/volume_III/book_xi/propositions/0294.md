### PROPOSITION 9.

<p class="enunciation">Straight lines which are parallel to the same straight line<br />
and are not in the same plane with it are also parallel to one<br />
another.</p>

For let each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be parallel to<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, not being in the same plane<br />
with it;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

![Proposition 9](0294.g1.svg)

For let a point <span data-ref="0294.g1:G">$G$</span> be taken at<br />
random on <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
and from it let there be drawn<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, in the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, at right angles to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> in the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> again at right angles to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is at right angles<br />
to the plane through <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, if two straight lines be at right angles to the same<br />
plane, the straight lines are parallel; <span class="justification">[XI. 6]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0294.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>
