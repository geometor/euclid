### PROPOSITION 10.

*If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two straight lines meeting one another not in the same plane, they will contain equal angles.*

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> meeting one another be parallel to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> meeting one another, not in the same plane;
I say that the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>.

![Proposition 10](0295.g1.svg)

    For let <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be cut off equal to one another, and let <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be joined.
    Now, since <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>. [I. 33]
    For the same reason
<span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.
    Therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.
    But straight lines which are parallel to the same straight line and are not in the same plane with it are parallel to one another; [XI. 9]
therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is parallel and equal to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.
    And <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> join them;
therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. [I. 33]
    Now, since the two sides <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
and the base <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>. [I. 8]
    Therefore etc.

<p align="right">Q. E. D.</p>
