### PROPOSITION 10.

<p class="enunciation">If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to<br />
two straight lines meeting one another not in the same plane,<br />
they will contain equal angles.</p>

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> meeting one<br />
another be parallel to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> meeting<br />
one another, not in the same plane;<br />
I say that the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>.

![Proposition 10](0295.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be cut off equal to one another,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is also equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.</p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span> is equal and<br />
parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But straight lines which are parallel to the same straight<br />
line and are not in the same plane with it are parallel to one<br />
another; <span class="justification">[XI. 9]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is parallel and equal to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> join them;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also equal and, parallel to <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the two sides <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0295.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0295.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.</p>
