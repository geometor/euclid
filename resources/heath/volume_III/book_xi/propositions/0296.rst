:title: Proposition 11

The result of this proposition does not appear to be quoted in Euclid until XII. 3; but Euclid no doubt inserted it here advisedly, because it has the effect of incidentally proving that the "inclination of two planes to one another," as defined in XI. Def. 6, is one and the same angle at whatever point of the common section the plane angle measuring it is drawn.

----

PROPOSITION 11.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   From a given elevated point to draw a straight line perpen- dicular to a given plane.

| Let :g:`$A$ <0296.g1:A>` be the given elevated point, and the plane of
| reference the given plane;
| thus it is required to draw from the
| point :g:`$A$ <0296.g1:A>` a straight line perpendicular to
| the plane of reference.

.. picture:: 0296.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 11

| Let any straight line :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />` be drawn,
| at random, in the plane of reference,
| and let :g:`$AD$ <0296.g1:/ A D />` be drawn from the point :g:`$A$ <0296.g1:A>`
| perpendicular to :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[I. 12] <I.12>`

| If then :g:`$AD$ <0296.g1:/ A D />` is also perpendicular to
| the plane of reference, that which was
| enjoined will have been done.

| But, if not, let :g:`$DE$ <0296.g1:/ D E />` be drawn from the point :g:`$D$ <0296.g1:D>` at right
| angles to :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />` and in the plane of reference, :just:`[I. 11] <I.11>`
| let :g:`$AF$ <0296.g1:/ A F />` be drawn from :g:`$A$ <0296.g1:A>` perpendicular to :g:`$DE$ <0296.g1:/ D E />`, :just:`[I. 12] <I.12>`
| and let :g:`$GH$ <0296.g1:/ G H />` be drawn through the point :g:`$F$ <0296.g1:F>` parallel to :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />`.
| :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

| Now, since :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />` is at right angles to each of the straight
| lines :g:`$DA$ <0296.g1:/ D A />`, :g:`$DE$ <0296.g1:/ D E />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$BC$ <0296.g1:/ B C />` is also at right angles to the plane through
   | :g:`$ED$ <0296.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0296.g1:/ D A />`. :just:`[XI. 4] <XI.4>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$GH$ <0296.g1:/ G H />` is parallel to it;

| but, if two straight lines be parallel, and one of them be at
| right angles to any plane, the remaining one will also be at
| right angles to the same plane; :just:`[XI. 8] <XI.8>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$GH$ <0296.g1:/ G H />` is also at right angles to the plane through
   | :g:`$ED$ <0296.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0296.g1:/ D A />`.
