<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is also at right angles to all the straight<br />
lines which meet it and are in the plane through <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> meets it and is in the plane through <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>,<br />
so that <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

But, if a straight line be set up at right angles to two<br />
straight lines which cut one another, at the point of section,<br />
it will also be at right angles to the plane through them; <span class="justification">[XI. 4]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the plane through <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is the plane of reference;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore from the given elevated point <span data-ref="0296.g1:A">$A$</span> the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0296.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> has been drawn perpendicular to the plane of<br />
reference.</p>

<p class="qed">Q. E. F.</p>

---

The text-books differ in the *form* which they give to this proposition rather than in substance. They commonly assume the construction of a *plane* through the point <span data-ref="0297.g1:A">$A$</span> at right angles to any straight line <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> in the given plane (the construction being effected in the manner shown at the end of the note on XI. 5 above). The advantage of this method is that it enables a perpendicular to be drawn from a point *in* the plane also, by the same construction. (Where the letters for the two figures differ, those referring to the second figure are put in brackets.)

![Proposition 11, Alternative Method](0297.g1.svg)

We can include the construction of the plane through <span data-ref="0297.g1:A">$A$</span> perpendicular to<br />
<span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, and make the whole into one proposition, thus.

<span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> being any straight line in the given plane <span data-ref="0297.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>, draw <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> perpendicu-<br />
lar to <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.<br />
In any plane passing through <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> but not through <span data-ref="0297.g1:A">$A$</span> draw <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> at right<br />
angles to <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.<br />
Through <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> draw a plane; this will intersect the given plane <span data-ref="0297.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span><br />
in a straight line, as <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> (<span data-ref="0297.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>).<br />
In the plane <span data-ref="0297.g1:< A G >">$AG$</span> draw <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> (<span data-ref="0297.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>).<br />
Then <span data-ref="0297.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is the perpendicular required.
