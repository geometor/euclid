### PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">Planes to which the same straight line is at right angles<br />
will be parallel.</p>

For let any straight line <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be at right angles to each of<br />
the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span>;<br />
I say that the planes are<br />
parallel.

![Book XI, Proposition 14](0300.g1.svg)

For, if not, they will meet<br />
when produced.<br />
Let them meet;<br />
they will then make, as<br />
common section, a straight line. <span class="justification">[XI. 3]</span><br />
Let them make <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>;<br />
let a point <span data-ref="0300.g1:K">$K$</span> be taken at random on <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> be joined.

Now, since <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span>,<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> which is a straight<br />
line in the plane <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> produced; <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K )">$ABK$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< B A K )">$BAK$</span> is also right.</p>

Thus, in the triangle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K >">$ABK$</span>, the two angles <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K )">$ABK$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< B A K )">$BAK$</span><br />
are equal to two right angles:
<p class="assertion">which is impossible. <span class="justification">[I. 17]</span></p>

Therefore the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> will not meet when<br />
produced;
<p class="assertion">therefore the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> are parallel. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore planes to which the same straight line is at right<br />
angles are parallel.</p>

<p class="qed">Q. E. D.</p>
