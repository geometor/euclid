### PROPOSITION 14.

*Planes to which the same straight line is at right angles will be parallel.*

For let any straight line <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be at right angles to each of the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span>;
I say that the planes are parallel.

![Book XI, Proposition 14](0300.g1.svg)

For, if not, they will meet when produced.
Let them meet;
they will then make, as common section, a straight line. [XI. 3]
Let them make <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>;
let a point <span data-ref="0300.g1:K">$K$</span> be taken at random on <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> be joined.

Now, since <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0300.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> which is a straight line in the plane <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> produced; [XI. Def. 3]
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K )">$ABK$</span> is right.

For the same reason
the angle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< B A K )">$BAK$</span> is also right.

Thus, in the triangle <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K >">$ABK$</span>, the two angles <span data-ref="0300.g1:< A B K )">$ABK$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< B A K )">$BAK$</span> are equal to two right angles:
which is impossible. [I. 17]

Therefore the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> will not meet when produced;
therefore the planes <span data-ref="0300.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0300.g1:< E F >">$EF$</span> are parallel. [XI. Def. 8]

Therefore planes to which the same straight line is at right angles are parallel.

Q. E. D.
