### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two<br />
straight lines meeting one another, not being in the same plane,<br />
the planes through them are parallel.</p>

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> meeting one another<br />
be parallel to the two straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> meeting one another, not<br />
being in the same plane ;<br />
I say that the planes produced<br />
through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> and <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> will<br />
not meet one another.

![Book XI, Proposition 15](0301.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be drawn from the<br />
point <span data-ref="0301.g1:B">$B$</span> perpendicular to the plane<br />
through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> <span class="justification">[XI. 11]</span>, and let it<br />
meet the plane at the point <span data-ref="0301.g1:G">$G$</span>;<br />
through <span data-ref="0301.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be drawn<br />
parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, and <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 31]</span>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> is at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight<br />
lines which meet it and are in the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> meets it and is in<br />
the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G K )">$BGK$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span class="justification">[XI. 9]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angles <span data-ref="0301.g1:< G B A )">$GBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> are equal to two right angles.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 29]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> is right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0301.g1:< G B A )">$GBA$</span> is also right;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the straight line <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is set up at right angles<br />
to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> which cut one another,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 4]</span></p>
