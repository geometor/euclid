### PROPOSITION 15.

*If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two straight lines meeting one another, not being in the same plane, the planes through them are parallel.*

For let the two straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> meeting one another be parallel to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> meeting one another, not being in the same plane;
I say that the planes produced through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> and <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> will not meet one another.

![Book XI, Proposition 15](0301.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0301.g1:B">$B$</span> perpendicular to the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> [XI. 11], and let it meet the plane at the point <span data-ref="0301.g1:G">$G$</span>;
through <span data-ref="0301.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be drawn parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, and <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [I. 31]

Now, since <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> is at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,
therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [XI. Def. 3]

But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> meets it and is in the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G K )">$BGK$</span> is right.

And, since <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, [XI. 9]
therefore the angles <span data-ref="0301.g1:< G B A )">$GBA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> are equal to two right angles. [I. 29]

But the angle <span data-ref="0301.g1:< B G H )">$BGH$</span> is right;
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0301.g1:< G B A )">$GBA$</span> is also right;
therefore <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is at right angles to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>.

For the same reason
<span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.

Since then the straight line <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is set up at right angles to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> which cut one another,
therefore <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ G B /">$GB$</span> is also at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0301.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>. [XI. 4]
