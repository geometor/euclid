Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Enunciation**: *If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two straight lines meeting one another, not being in the same plane, the planes through them are parallel.*
- **Mathematical Construction**: From $B$, perpendicular $BG$ is dropped to the lower plane containing $DE, EF$ (by XI. 11). Parallels $GH \parallel ED$ and $GK \parallel EF$ are constructed through $G$ (I. 31).
- **Deductive Flow**:

  - $BG \perp$ lower plane $\implies BG \perp GH$ and $BG \perp GK$ (XI. Def. 3).
  - $BA \parallel ED$ and $GH \parallel ED \implies BA \parallel GH$ by transitivity of parallels in space (XI. 9).
  - Interior angles on same side: $\angle GBA + \angle BGH = 2\text{ right angles}$ (I. 29), giving $\angle GBA = 90^\circ$.
  - Similarly, $GB \perp BC$.
  - Since $GB$ is perpendicular to two intersecting lines $BA, BC$, $GB \perp$ the plane of $BA, BC$ (XI. 4).
