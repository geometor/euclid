PROPOSITION 15.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two straight lines meeting one another, not being in the same plane, the planes through them are parallel.

For let the two straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0301.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0301.g1:/ B C />` meeting one another be parallel to the two straight lines :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />` meeting one another, not being in the same plane ; I say that the planes produced through :g:`$AB$ <0301.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0301.g1:/ B C />` and :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />` will not meet one another.

.. picture:: 0301.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 15

For let :g:`$BG$ <0301.g1:/ B G />` be drawn from the point :g:`$B$ <0301.g1:B>` perpendicular to the plane through :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />` :just:`[XI. 11] <XI.11>`, and let it meet the plane at the point :g:`$G$ <0301.g1:G>`; through :g:`$G$ <0301.g1:G>` let :g:`$GH$ <0301.g1:/ G H />` be drawn parallel to :g:`$ED$ <0301.g1:/ E D />`, and :g:`$GK$ <0301.g1:/ G K />` parallel to :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[I. 31] <I.31>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$BG$ <0301.g1:/ B G />` is at right angles to the plane through :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane through :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. subclause::

   But each of the straight lines :g:`$GH$ <0301.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$GK$ <0301.g1:/ G K />` meets it and is in the plane through :g:`$DE$ <0301.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0301.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the angles :g:`$BGH$ <0301.g1:< B G H )>`, :g:`$BGK$ <0301.g1:< B G K )>` is :eq:`right <0301.g1:< B G H ) = 0301.g1:< B G K )>`.

.. subclause:: [XI. 9] <XI.9>

   And, since :g:`$BA$ <0301.g1:/ B A />` is parallel to :g:`$GH$ <0301.g1:/ G H />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$GBA$ <0301.g1:< G B A )>`, :g:`$BGH$ <0301.g1:< B G H )>` are equal to two right angles. :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$BGH$ <0301.g1:< B G H )>` is right;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$GBA$ <0301.g1:< G B A )>` is also right; therefore :g:`$GB$ <0301.g1:/ G B />` is at right angles to :g:`$BA$ <0301.g1:/ B A />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`$GB$ <0301.g1:/ G B />` is also at right angles to :g:`$BC$ <0301.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the straight line :g:`$GB$ <0301.g1:/ G B />` is set up at right angles to the two straight lines :g:`$BA$ <0301.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$BC$ <0301.g1:/ B C />` which cut one another,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$GB$ <0301.g1:/ G B />` is also at right angles to the plane through :g:`$BA$ <0301.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$BC$ <0301.g1:/ B C />`. :just:`[XI. 4] <XI.4>`
