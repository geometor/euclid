5. *If each of two intersecting straight lines is parallel to a given plane,*<br />
*the plane containing them is parallel to the given*<br />
*plane.*

Let <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be parallel to the plane<br />
<span data-ref="0303.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>.<br />
Then, if the plane <span data-ref="0303.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> were to meet the<br />
plane <span data-ref="0303.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>, the intersection would be parallel<br />
both to <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> and to <span data-ref="0303.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> : which is impossible.

![Book XI, Proposition 15 Commentary 3](0303.g1.svg)

Lastly, we have Euclid's proposition.

6. *If two straight lines forming an angle are respectively parallel to two*<br />
*other straight lines forming an angle, the plane of*<br />
*the first angle is parallel to the plane of the second.*

Let <span data-ref="0303.g2:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g2:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> be the angles formed by<br />
straight lines parallel to one another respectively.<br />
Then, since <span data-ref="0303.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0303.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
the plane of <span data-ref="0303.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0303.g2:/ A B /">$AB$</span> [(1) above].<br />
Similarly the plane of <span data-ref="0303.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is parallel to<br />
<span data-ref="0303.g2:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.<br />
Hence the plane of <span data-ref="0303.g2:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is parallel to the<br />
plane of <span data-ref="0303.g2:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> [(5)].

![Book XI, Proposition 15 Commentary 4](0303.g2.svg)

Legendre arrives at the result by yet another method. He first proves<br />
Eucl. XI. 16 to the effect that, *if two parallel planes are cut by a third, the lines*<br />
*of intersection are parallel*, and then deduces from this that, *if two parallel*<br />
*straight lines are terminated by two parallel planes, the straight lines are equal*<br />
*in length.*

(The latter inference is obvious because the plane through the parallels<br />
cuts the parallel planes in parallel lines ; which<br />
therefore, with the given parallel lines, form a<br />
parallelogram.)

Legendre is now in a position to prove<br />
Euclid's proposition XI. 15.<br />
If <span data-ref="0303.g3:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> be the angles, make <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
equal to <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ B C /">$BC$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and join<br />
<span data-ref="0303.g3:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.<br />
Then, as in Eucl. XI. 10, the triangles<br />
<span data-ref="0303.g3:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are equal in all respects ;<br />
and <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ C F /">$CF$</span> are all equal.<br />
It is now proved that the planes are<br />
parallel by *reductio ad absurdum* from the<br />
last preceding result. For, if the plane <span data-ref="0303.g3:< A B C >">$ABC$</span><br />
is not parallel to the plane <span data-ref="0303.g3:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, let the plane drawn through <span data-ref="0303.g3:B">$B$</span> parallel to the<br />
plane <span data-ref="0303.g3:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> meet <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ A D /">$AD$</span> in <span data-ref="0303.g3:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:G">$G$</span> respectively.<br />
Then, by the last result <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ G D /">$GD$</span> will all be equal.<br />
But <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ B E /">$BE$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0303.g3:/ A D /">$AD$</span> are all equal :<br />
which is impossible.<br />
Therefore etc.
