Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Item 5**: Proves that two intersecting lines parallel to a plane determine a plane parallel to that plane. Proof uses impossibility of an intersection line being parallel to two intersecting lines simultaneously.
- **Item 6**: Deduces Euclid XI. 15 as an immediate consequence of (1) and (5).
- **Legendre's Sequence & Proof**:

  - Legendre reverses Euclid's order: he proves XI. 16 first, derives that parallel segments between parallel planes are equal (forming parallelograms), then proves XI. 15 by cutting equal segments $AB = DE, BC = EF$, invoking XI. 10 to establish congruence $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$, and then applying *reductio ad absurdum* with an auxiliary plane through $B$ parallel to $DEF$ meeting $CF, AD$ at $H, G$.
