### PROPOSITION 16.

<p class="enunciation">If two parallel planes be cut by any plane, their common<br />
sections are parallel.</p>

For let the two parallel planes <span data-ref="0304.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> be cut by the<br />
plane <span data-ref="0304.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> be their common sections ;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.

![Book XI, Proposition 16](0304.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, if not, <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> will, when produced, meet either in<br />
the direction of <span data-ref="0304.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:H">$H$</span> or of <span data-ref="0304.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:G">$G$</span>.<br />
Let them be produced, as in the direction of <span data-ref="0304.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:H">$H$</span>, and<br />
let them, first, meet at <span data-ref="0304.g1:K">$K$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F K /">$EFK$</span> is in the plane <span data-ref="0304.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore all the points on <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F K /">$EFK$</span> are also in the plane <span data-ref="0304.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0304.g1:K">$K$</span> is one of the points on the straight line <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F K /">$EFK$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0304.g1:K">$K$</span> is in the plane <span data-ref="0304.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0304.g1:K">$K$</span> is also in the plane <span data-ref="0304.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the planes <span data-ref="0304.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> will meet when produced.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But they do not meet, because they are, by hypothesis,<br />
parallel;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the straight lines <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> will not meet when<br />
produced in the direction of <span data-ref="0304.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:H">$H$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that neither will the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> meet when produced in the direction of <span data-ref="0304.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0304.g1:G">$G$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But straight lines which do not meet in either direction<br />
are parallel. <span class="justification">[I. Def. 23]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0304.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore etc.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
