### PROPOSITION 18.

<p class="enunciation">If a straight line be at right angles to any plane, all the<br />
planes through it will also be at right angles to the same plane.</p>

For let any straight line <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be at right angles to the<br />
plane of reference;<br />
I say that all the planes through<br />
<span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are also at right angles to the<br />
plane of reference.

![Book XI, Proposition 18](0306.g1.svg)

For let the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> be drawn<br />
through <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> be the common section of<br />
the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> and the plane of<br />

and from <span data-ref="0306.g1:F">$F$</span> let <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be drawn in the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> at right<br />
angles to <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 11]</span>
