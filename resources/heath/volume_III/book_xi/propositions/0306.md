### PROPOSITION 18.

*If a straight line be at right angles to any plane, all the planes through it will also be at right angles to the same plane.*

For let any straight line $AB$ be at right angles to the plane of reference;
I say that all the planes through $AB$ are also at right angles to the plane of reference.

For let the plane $DE$ be drawn through $AB$,
let $CE$ be the common section of the plane $DE$ and the plane of reference,
let a point $F$ be taken at random on $CE$,
and from $F$ let $FG$ be drawn in the plane $DE$ at right angles to $CE$. [I. 11]
