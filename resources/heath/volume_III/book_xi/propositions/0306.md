### PROPOSITION 18.

*If a straight line be at right angles to any plane, all the planes through it will also be at right angles to the same plane.*

For let any straight line <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be at right angles to the plane of reference;
I say that all the planes through <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are also at right angles to the plane of reference.

For let the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> be the common section of the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> and the plane of reference,
