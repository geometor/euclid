<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also at right angles to all the straight lines which meet<br />
it and are in the plane of reference; <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span><br />
so that it is also at right angles to <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>;<br />
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0306.g1:< A B F )">$ABF$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0306.g1:< G F B )">$GFB$</span> is also right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 28]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is also at right angles to the plane of reference. <span class="justification">[XI. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now a plane is at right angles to a plane, when the<br />
straight lines drawn, in one of the planes, at right angles to<br />
the common section of the planes are at right angles to the<br />
remaining plane. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, drawn in one of the planes <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> at right angles<br />
to <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, the common section of the planes, was proved to be<br />
at right angles to the plane of reference;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the plane <span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> is at right angles to the plane of<br />
reference.</p>

Similarly also it can be proved that all the planes through<br />
<span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are at right angles to the plane of reference.

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

Starting as Euclid does from the definition of perpendicular planes as<br />
planes such that all straight lines drawn in one of the planes at right angles to<br />
the common section are at right angles to the other plane, it is necessary for<br />
him to show that, if <span data-ref="0306.g1:F">$F$</span> be *any* point in <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> be drawn in the plane<br />
<span data-ref="0306.g1:< D E >">$DE$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>, <span data-ref="0306.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> will be perpendicular to the plane to which<br />
<span data-ref="0306.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is perpendicular.

It is perhaps more scientific to make the definition, as Legendre makes it,<br />
a particular case of the definition of the *inclination of planes*. Perpendicular<br />
planes would thus be planes such that the angle which (when it is acute)<br />
Euclid calls the inclination of a plane to a plane is a right angle. When to this<br />
is added the fact incidentally proved in XI. 10 that the “inclination of a plane to<br />
a plane” is the same at whatever point in their common section it is drawn, it<br />
is sufficient to prove the perpendicularity of two planes if *one* straight line<br />
drawn, in one of them, perpendicular to their common section is perpendicular<br />
to the other.

If this point of view is taken, Props. 18, 19 are much simplified (cf.<br />
Legendre, H. M. Taylor, Smith and Bryant, Rausenberger, Schultze and<br />
Sevenoak, Holgate). The alternative proof is as follows.

Let <span data-ref="0308.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be perpendicular to the plane <span data-ref="0308.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0308.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> any plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0308.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, meeting the plane <span data-ref="0308.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> in the straight line <span data-ref="0308.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.<br />
In the plane <span data-ref="0308.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> draw <span data-ref="0308.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0308.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.
