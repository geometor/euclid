### PROPOSITION 19.

<p class="enunciation">If two planes which cut one another be at right angles to<br />
any plane, their common section will also be at right angles to<br />
the same plane.</p>

For let the two planes <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0308.g2:< B C >">$BC$</span> be at right angles to the<br />
plane of reference,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be their common section;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is at right angles to the<br />
plane of reference.

![Book XI, Proposition 19](0308.g2.svg)

For suppose it is not, and from the<br />
point <span data-ref="0308.g2:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn in the plane<br />
<span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> at right angles to the straight line<br />
<span data-ref="0308.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> in the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< B C >">$BC$</span> at right<br />
angles to <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> is at right<br />
angles to the plane of reference,<br />
and <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> has been drawn in the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> at right angles to<br />
<span data-ref="0308.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, their common section,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 4]</span></p>
