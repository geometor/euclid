### PROPOSITION 19.

*If two planes which cut one another be at right angles to any plane, their common section will also be at right angles to the same plane.*

For let the two planes <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0308.g2:< B C >">$BC$</span> be at right angles to the plane of reference,
and let <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be their common section;
I say that <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference.

![Book XI, Proposition 19](0308.g2.svg)

For suppose it is not, and from the point <span data-ref="0308.g2:D">$D$</span> let <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be drawn in the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> at right angles to the straight line <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, and <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D F /">$DF$</span> in the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< B C >">$BC$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.

Now, since the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference,
and <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> has been drawn in the plane <span data-ref="0308.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, their common section,
therefore <span data-ref="0308.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference.
