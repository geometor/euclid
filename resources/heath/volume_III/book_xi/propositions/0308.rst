PROPOSITION 19.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If two planes which cut one another be at right angles to any plane, their common section will also be at right angles to the same plane.

| For let the two planes :g:`$AB$ <0308.g2:< A B >>`, :g:`$BC$ <0308.g2:< B C >>` be at right angles to the
| plane of reference,
| and let :g:`$BD$ <0308.g2:/ B D />` be their common section;
| I say that :g:`$BD$ <0308.g2:/ B D />` is at right angles to the
| plane of reference.

.. picture:: 0308.g2.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 19

| For suppose it is not, and from the
| point :g:`$D$ <0308.g2:D>` let :g:`$DE$ <0308.g2:/ D E />` be drawn in the plane
| :g:`$AB$ <0308.g2:< A B >>` at right angles to the straight line
| :g:`$AD$ <0308.g2:/ A D />`, and :g:`$DF$ <0308.g2:/ D F />` in the plane :g:`$BC$ <0308.g2:< B C >>` at right
| angles to :g:`$CD$ <0308.g2:/ C D />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the plane :g:`$AB$ <0308.g2:< A B >>` is at right
   | angles to the plane of reference,
   | and :g:`$DE$ <0308.g2:/ D E />` has been drawn in the plane :g:`$AB$ <0308.g2:< A B >>` at right angles to
   | :g:`$AD$ <0308.g2:/ A D />`, their common section,

.. assertion:: [XI. Def. 4] <XI.def.4>

   therefore :g:`$DE$ <0308.g2:/ D E />` is at right angles to the plane of reference.
