| :person:`Legendre <legendre>`, followed by other writers already quoted, uses a preliminary
| proposition equivalent to Euclid's definition of planes at right angles to one
| another.

| *If two planes are perpendicular to one another, a straight line drawn in one
| of them perpendicular to their common section will be perpendicular to the other.*

| Let the perpendicular planes :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:< C E >>`, :g:`$MN$ <0308.g1:< M N >>` (figure of last note) intersect in
| :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />`, and let :g:`$AB$ <0308.g1:/ A B />` be drawn in :g:`$CE$ <0308.g1:< C E >>` perpendicular to :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />`.

In the plane :g:`$MN$ <0308.g1:< M N >>` draw :g:`$BF$ <0308.g1:/ B F />` at right angles to :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />`.

| Then, since the planes are perpendicular, the angle :g:`$ABF$ <0308.g1:< A B F )>` (their *inclination*)
| is a right angle.

| Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0308.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to both :g:`$CD$ <0308.g1:/ C D />` and :g:`$BF$ <0308.g1:/ B F />`, and therefore to the
| plane :g:`$MN$ <0308.g1:< M N >>`.

| We are now in a position to prove XI. 19, viz. *If two planes be perpendicular
| to a third, their intersection is also perpen-
| dicular to that third plane.*

| Let each of the two planes :g:`$AC$ <0309.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$AD$ <0309.g1:< A D >>`
| intersecting in :g:`$AB$ <0309.g1:/ A B />` be perpendicular to the
| plane :g:`$MN$ <0309.g1:< M N >>`.
| Let :g:`$AC$ <0309.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$AD$ <0309.g1:< A D >>` intersect :g:`$MN$ <0309.g1:< M N >>` in :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$BD$ <0309.g1:/ B D />`
| respectively.
| In the plane :g:`$MN$ <0309.g1:< M N >>` draw :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />` at right
| angles to :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />` and :g:`$BF$ <0309.g1:/ B F />` at right angles to
| :g:`$BD$ <0309.g1:/ B D />`.

.. picture:: 0309.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 19, Heath Commentary Diagram

| Now, since the planes :g:`$AC$ <0309.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$MN$ <0309.g1:< M N >>` are at
| right angles, and :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />` is drawn in the latter perpendicular to :g:`$BC$ <0309.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />` is
| perpendicular to the plane :g:`$AC$ <0309.g1:< A C >>`.
| Hence :g:`$AB$ <0309.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />`. :just:`[XI. 4] <XI.4>`
| Similarly :g:`$AB$ <0309.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to :g:`$BF$ <0309.g1:/ B F />`.
| Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0309.g1:/ A B />` is perpendicular to the plane through :g:`$BE$ <0309.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$BF$ <0309.g1:/ B F />`, i.e. to the
| plane :g:`$MN$ <0309.g1:< M N >>`.
