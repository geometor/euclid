:title: Proposition 19 (commentary continued)

| *Given a plane and a straight line not perpendicular to it, one plane, and only
| one, can be drawn through the straight line perpen-
| dicular to the plane.*

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0310.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>` the
| given plane.
| From any point :g:`$C$ <0310.g1:C>` in :g:`$AB$ <0310.g1:/ A B />` draw :g:`$CD$ <0310.g1:/ C D />` perpen-
| dicular to the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>`.
| Through :g:`$AB$ <0310.g1:/ A B />` and :g:`$CD$ <0310.g1:/ C D />` draw a plane :g:`$AE$ <0310.g1:< A E >>`.
| Then the plane :g:`$AE$ <0310.g1:< A E >>` is perpendicular to the
| plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>`. :just:`[XI. 18] <XI.18>`

.. picture:: 0310.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 19, Lemma 1 Diagram

| If any other plane could be drawn through
| :g:`$AB$ <0310.g1:/ A B />` perpendicular to :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>`, the intersection :g:`$AB$ <0310.g1:/ A B />` of
| the two planes perpendicular to :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>` would itself
| be perpendicular to :g:`$MN$ <0310.g1:< M N >>`: :just:`[XI. 19] <XI.19>`
| which contradicts the hypothesis.

*To draw a common perpendicular to two straight lines not in the same plane.*

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />` be the given straight lines.
| Through :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />` draw the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>` parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` (Prop. 2 in note
| to XI. 15).
| Through :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` draw the plane :g:`$AF$ <0310.g2:< A F >>` perpendicular to the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>` (see the
| last preceding proposition).

.. picture:: 0310.g2.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 19, Lemma 2 Diagram

| Let the planes :g:`$AF$ <0310.g2:< A F >>`, :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>` intersect in :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />`, and let :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />` meet :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />` in :g:`$G$ <0310.g2:G>`.
| From :g:`$G$ <0310.g2:G>`, in the plane :g:`$AF$ <0310.g2:< A F >>`, draw :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />` at right angles to :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />`, meeting :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` in :g:`$H$ <0310.g2:H>`.
| :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />` is then the required perpendicular.
| For :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />` (Prop. 4 in note to XI. 15); therefore :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />`,
| being perpendicular to :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />`, is also perpendicular to :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />`.
| But, the plane :g:`$AF$ <0310.g2:< A F >>` being perpendicular to the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>`, and :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />` being
| perpendicular to :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />`, their intersection,
| :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />` is perpendicular to the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>`, and therefore to :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />`.
| Therefore :g:`$GH$ <0310.g2:/ G H />` is perpendicular to both :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` and :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />`.

| *Only one common perpendicular can be drawn to two straight lines not in
| one plane.*
| For, if possible, let :g:`$KL$ <0310.g2:/ K L />` also be perpendicular to both :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` and :g:`$CD$ <0310.g2:/ C D />`.
| Let the plane through :g:`$KL$ <0310.g2:/ K L />`, :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` meet the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>` in :g:`$LQ$ <0310.g2:/ L Q />`.
| Then :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`$LQ$ <0310.g2:/ L Q />` (Prop. 4 in note to XI. 15), so that :g:`$KL$ <0310.g2:/ K L />`, being
| perpendicular to :g:`$AB$ <0310.g2:/ A B />`, is also perpendicular to :g:`$LQ$ <0310.g2:/ L Q />`.
| Therefore :g:`$KL$ <0310.g2:/ K L />` is perpendicular to both :g:`$CL$ <0310.g2:/ C L />` and :g:`$LQ$ <0310.g2:/ L Q />`, and consequently to
| the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>`.
| But, if :g:`$KP$ <0310.g2:/ K P />` be drawn in the plane :g:`$AF$ <0310.g2:< A F >>` perpendicular to :g:`$EF$ <0310.g2:/ E F />`, :g:`$KP$ <0310.g2:/ K P />` is also
| perpendicular to the plane :g:`$MN$ <0310.g2:< M N >>`.
