Let :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0311.g2:/ C D />` be the two given straight lines.

Through any point :g:`$E$ <0311.g2:E>` in :g:`$CD$ <0311.g2:/ C D />` draw :g:`$EF$ <0311.g2:/ E F />` parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />`.

| From any point :g:`$G$ <0311.g2:G>` in :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />` draw :g:`$GH$ <0311.g2:/ G H />` perpendicular to the plane :g:`$CDF$ <0311.g2:< C D F >>`,
| meeting the plane in :g:`$H$ <0311.g2:H>`.

| Through :g:`$H$ <0311.g2:H>` in the plane :g:`$CDF$ <0311.g2:< C D F >>` draw
| :g:`$HK$ <0311.g2:/ H K />` parallel to :g:`$FE$ <0311.g2:/ F E />` or :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />`, to cut :g:`$CD$ <0311.g2:/ C D />`
| in :g:`$K$ <0311.g2:K>`.

| Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />`, :g:`$HK$ <0311.g2:/ H K />` are parallel,
| :g:`$AGHK$ <0311.g2:< A G H K >>` is a plane.

Complete the parallelogram :g:`$GHKL$ <0311.g2:< G H K L >>`.

| Now, since :g:`$LK$ <0311.g2:/ L K />`, :g:`$GH$ <0311.g2:/ G H />` are parallel, and
| :g:`$GH$ <0311.g2:/ G H />` is perpendicular to the plane :g:`$CDF$ <0311.g2:< C D F >>`,
| :g:`$LK$ <0311.g2:/ L K />` is perpendicular to the plane
| :g:`$CDF$ <0311.g2:< C D F >>`.

| Therefore :g:`$LK$ <0311.g2:/ L K />` is perpendicular to :g:`$CD$ <0311.g2:/ C D />` and :g:`$KH$ <0311.g2:/ K H />`, and therefore to :g:`$AB$ <0311.g2:/ A B />` which
| is parallel to :g:`$KH$ <0311.g2:/ K H />`.

PROPOSITION 20.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a solid angle be contained by three plane angles, any two, taken together in any manner, are greater than the remaining one.

| For let the solid angle at :g:`$A$ <0311.g3:A>` be contained by the three
| plane angles :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`, :g:`$CAD$ <0311.g3:< C A D )>`, :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`;
| I say that any two of the angles
| :g:`$BAC$ <0311.g3:< B A C )>`, :g:`$CAD$ <0311.g3:< C A D )>`, :g:`$DAB$ <0311.g3:< D A B )>`, taken to-
| gether in any manner, are greater
| than the remaining one.
