## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Logical Simplification**: Legendre and Rausenberger observed that if angle $A \le B$, then $A + C \le B + C$, and since $B + C > B \ge A$, the inequality is trivially true unless $A$ is strictly greater than both other angles.
- **Application of XI. 20**: In Proposition 21, the triangle inequality for trihedral angles (XI. 20) is applied to each of the three trihedral angles at the base vertices *B, C, D*.
- **Application of I. 32**: Sum of angles of base triangle *BCD* equals two right angles ($180^\circ$).
