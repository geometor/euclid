## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Convexity Requirement**: Heath explicitly restricts the proposition to *convex* polyhedral angles, noting that re-entrant/concave solid angles could exceed four right angles.
- **Trihedral Congruence Criteria**:
  1. Two face angles and included dihedral angle (SAS equivalent).
  2. Two dihedral angles and included face angle (ASA equivalent).
  3. Three face angles arranged in same order (SSS equivalent).
- **Sense/Enantiomorphism**: Heath emphasizes "the equal parts being arranged in the same order" to distinguish congruent from symmetrical (enantiomorphic) trihedral angles.
