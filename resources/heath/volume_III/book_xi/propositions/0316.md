Now, by means of the hypothesis and construction, it appears that the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V A B >">$VAB$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V' A' B' >">$V'A'B'$</span> are equal in all respects.
So are the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V A C >">$VAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V' A' C' >">$V'A'C'$</span>, and the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V B C >">$VBC$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< V' B' C' >">$V'B'C'$</span>.
Thus <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are respectively equal to <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ B' C' /">$B'C'$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ C' A' /">$C'A'$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A' B' /">$A'B'$</span>, and the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' B' C' >">$A'B'C'$</span> are equal in all respects.

Now, in the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' D' E' >">$A'D'E'$</span>,
the angles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A D E )">$ADE$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< D A E )">$DAE$</span> are equal to the angles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' D' E' )">$A'D'E'$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< D' A' E' )">$D'A'E'$</span> respectively,
and <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A' D' /">$A'D'$</span>.
Therefore the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A D E >">$ADE$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' D' E' >">$A'D'E'$</span> are equal in all respects.
Similarly the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A D F >">$ADF$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' D' F' >">$A'D'F'$</span> are equal in all respects.

Thus, in the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A E F >">$AEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' E' F' >">$A'E'F'$</span>,
<span data-ref="0315.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> are respectively equal to <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ E' A' /">$E'A'$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:/ A' F' /">$A'F'$</span>,
and the angle <span data-ref="0315.g1:< E A F )">$EAF$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0315.g1:< E' A' F' )">$E'A'F'$</span> (from above);
therefore the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A E F >">$AEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' E' F' >">$A'E'F'$</span> are equal in all respects.

Lastly, in the triangles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< D' E' F' >">$D'E'F'$</span>, the three sides are respectively equal to the three sides;
therefore the triangles are equal in all respects.
Therefore the angles <span data-ref="0315.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< E' D' F' )">$E'D'F'$</span> are equal.

But these angles are the measures of the dihedral angles formed by the planes <span data-ref="0315.g1:< C V A >">$CVA$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A V B >">$AVB$</span> and by the planes <span data-ref="0315.g1:< C' V' A' >">$C'V'A'$</span>, <span data-ref="0315.g1:< A' V' B' >">$A'V'B'$</span> respectively.
Therefore these dihedral angles are equal.
Similarly for the other two dihedral angles.
Hence the trihedral angles coincide if one is applied to the other; that is, they are equal.

To understand what is implied by "taken in the same order" we may suppose ourselves to be placed at the vertices, and to take the faces in clockwise direction, or the reverse, for *both* angles.
If the face angles and dihedral angles are *taken in reverse directions*, i.e. in clockwise direction in one and in counterclockwise direction in the other, then, if the other conditions in the above three propositions are fulfilled, the trihedral angles are not equal but *symmetrical*.
If the faces of a trihedral angle be produced beyond the vertex, they form another trihedral angle. It is easily seen that these *vertical trihedral angles* are *symmetrical*.
