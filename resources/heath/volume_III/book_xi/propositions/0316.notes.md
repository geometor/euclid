## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **Trihedral Congruence**: Proves that equality of corresponding face angles implies equality of corresponding dihedral angles by sectioning at equal distances along the edges and using planar triangle congruence steps ($ADE \cong A'D'E'$, $ADF \cong A'D'F'$, $AEF \cong A'E'F'$, and finally $DEF \cong D'E'F'$).
- **Chirality & Symmetry**: Heath provides a lucid modern exposition of enantiomorphism in solid angles: trihedral angles with face and dihedral angles equal in reverse cyclic order are *symmetrical* rather than congruent by simple translation/rotation. Vertical trihedral angles formed by producing edges past the vertex are likewise noted to be symmetrical.
- **Proposition 22 Enunciation**: State the fundamental existence condition for constructing a triangle whose sides are the chords subtending three plane angles forming a solid angle (anticipating Proposition 23).
