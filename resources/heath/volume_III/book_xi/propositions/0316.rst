Now, by means of the hypothesis and construction, it appears that the triangles :g:`VAB <0315.g1:< V A B >>`, :g:`V'A'B' <0315.g1:< V' A' B' >>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< V A B > = 0315.g1:< V' A' B' >>` in all respects. So are the triangles :g:`VAC <0315.g1:< V A C >>`, :g:`V'A'C' <0315.g1:< V' A' C' >>`, and the triangles :g:`VBC <0315.g1:< V B C >>`, :g:`V'B'C' <0315.g1:< V' B' C' >>`. Thus :g:`BC <0315.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`CA <0315.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`AB <0315.g1:/ A B />` are respectively :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ B C / = 0315.g1:/ B' C' /; 0315.g1:/ C A / = 0315.g1:/ C' A' /; 0315.g1:/ A B / = 0315.g1:/ A' B' />` to :g:`B'C' <0315.g1:/ B' C' />`, :g:`C'A' <0315.g1:/ C' A' />`, :g:`A'B' <0315.g1:/ A' B' />`, and the triangles :g:`ABC <0315.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`A'B'C' <0315.g1:< A' B' C' >>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< A B C > = 0315.g1:< A' B' C' >>` in all respects.

Now, in the triangles :g:`ADE <0315.g1:< A D E >>`, :g:`A'D'E' <0315.g1:< A' D' E' >>`, the angles :g:`ADE <0315.g1:< A D E )>`, :g:`DAE <0315.g1:< D A E )>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< A D E ) = 0315.g1:< A' D' E' ); 0315.g1:< D A E ) = 0315.g1:< D' A' E' )>` to the angles :g:`A'D'E' <0315.g1:< A' D' E' )>`, :g:`D'A'E' <0315.g1:< D' A' E' )>` respectively, and :g:`AD <0315.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ A D / = 0315.g1:/ A' D' />` to :g:`A'D' <0315.g1:/ A' D' />`. Therefore the triangles :g:`ADE <0315.g1:< A D E >>`, :g:`A'D'E' <0315.g1:< A' D' E' >>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< A D E > = 0315.g1:< A' D' E' >>` in all respects. Similarly the triangles :g:`ADF <0315.g1:< A D F >>`, :g:`A'D'F' <0315.g1:< A' D' F' >>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< A D F > = 0315.g1:< A' D' F' >>` in all respects.

Thus, in the triangles :g:`AEF <0315.g1:< A E F >>`, :g:`A'E'F' <0315.g1:< A' E' F' >>`, :g:`EA <0315.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`AF <0315.g1:/ A F />` are respectively :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ E A / = 0315.g1:/ E' A' /; 0315.g1:/ A F / = 0315.g1:/ A' F' />` to :g:`E'A' <0315.g1:/ E' A' />`, :g:`A'F' <0315.g1:/ A' F' />`, and the angle :g:`EAF <0315.g1:< E A F )>` is :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< E A F ) = 0315.g1:< E' A' F' )>` to the angle :g:`E'A'F' <0315.g1:< E' A' F' )>` (from above); therefore the triangles :g:`AEF <0315.g1:< A E F >>`, :g:`A'E'F' <0315.g1:< A' E' F' >>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< A E F > = 0315.g1:< A' E' F' >>` in all respects.

Lastly, in the triangles :g:`DEF <0315.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`D'E'F' <0315.g1:< D' E' F' >>`, the three sides are respectively :eq:`equal <0315.g1:/ D E / = 0315.g1:/ D' E' /; 0315.g1:/ E F / = 0315.g1:/ E' F' /; 0315.g1:/ D F / = 0315.g1:/ D' F' />` to the three sides; therefore the triangles are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< D E F > = 0315.g1:< D' E' F' >>` in all respects. Therefore the angles :g:`EDF <0315.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`E'D'F' <0315.g1:< E' D' F' )>` are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< E D F ) = 0315.g1:< E' D' F' )>`.

But these angles are the measures of the dihedral angles formed by the planes :g:`CVA <0315.g1:< C V A >>`, :g:`AVB <0315.g1:< A V B >>` and by the planes :g:`C'V'A' <0315.g1:< C' V' A' >>`, :g:`A'V'B' <0315.g1:< A' V' B' >>` respectively. Therefore these dihedral angles are equal. Similarly for the other two dihedral angles. Hence the trihedral angles coincide if one is applied to the other; that is, they are :eq:`equal <0315.g1:< V A B C > = 0315.g1:< V' A' B' C' >>`.

To understand what is implied by “taken in the same order” we may suppose ourselves to be placed at the vertices, and to take the faces in clockwise direction, or the reverse, for *both* angles.

If the face angles and dihedral angles are *taken in reverse directions*, i.e. in clockwise direction in one and in counterclockwise direction in the other, then, if the other conditions in the above three propositions are fulfilled, the trihedral angles are not equal but *symmetrical*.

If the faces of a trihedral angle be produced beyond the vertex, they form another trihedral angle. It is easily seen that these *vertical trihedral angles* are *symmetrical*.
