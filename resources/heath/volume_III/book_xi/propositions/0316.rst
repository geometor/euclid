| Now, by means of the hypothesis and construction, it appears that the
| triangles :g:`$VAB$ <0315.g1:< V A B >>`, :g:`$V'A'B'$ <0315.g1:< V' A' B' >>` are equal in all respects.
| So are the triangles :g:`$VAC$ <0315.g1:< V A C >>`, :g:`$V'A'C'$ <0315.g1:< V' A' C' >>`, and the triangles :g:`$VBC$ <0315.g1:< V B C >>`, :g:`$V'B'C'$ <0315.g1:< V' B' C' >>`.
| Thus :g:`$BC$ <0315.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0315.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0315.g1:/ A B />` are respectively equal to :g:`$B'C'$ <0315.g1:/ B' C' />`, :g:`$C'A'$ <0315.g1:/ C' A' />`, :g:`$A'B'$ <0315.g1:/ A' B' />`, and the
| triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0315.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$A'B'C'$ <0315.g1:< A' B' C' >>` are equal in all respects.

| Now, in the triangles :g:`$ADE$ <0315.g1:< A D E >>`, :g:`$A'D'E'$ <0315.g1:< A' D' E' >>`,
| the angles :g:`$ADE$ <0315.g1:< A D E )>`, :g:`$DAE$ <0315.g1:< D A E )>` are equal to the angles :g:`$A'D'E'$ <0315.g1:< A' D' E' )>`, :g:`$D'A'E'$ <0315.g1:< D' A' E' )>` respectively,
| and :g:`$AD$ <0315.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$A'D'$ <0315.g1:/ A' D' />`.
| Therefore the triangles :g:`$ADE$ <0315.g1:< A D E >>`, :g:`$A'D'E'$ <0315.g1:< A' D' E' >>` are equal in all respects.
| Similarly the triangles :g:`$ADF$ <0315.g1:< A D F >>`, :g:`$A'D'F'$ <0315.g1:< A' D' F' >>` are equal in all respects.

| Thus, in the triangles :g:`$AEF$ <0315.g1:< A E F >>`, :g:`$A'E'F'$ <0315.g1:< A' E' F' >>`,
| :g:`$EA$ <0315.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`$AF$ <0315.g1:/ A F />` are respectively equal to :g:`$E'A'$ <0315.g1:/ E' A' />`, :g:`$A'F'$ <0315.g1:/ A' F' />`,
| and the angle :g:`$EAF$ <0315.g1:< E A F )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$E'A'F'$ <0315.g1:< E' A' F' )>` (from above);
| therefore the triangles :g:`$AEF$ <0315.g1:< A E F >>`, :g:`$A'E'F'$ <0315.g1:< A' E' F' >>` are equal in all respects.

| Lastly, in the triangles :g:`$DEF$ <0315.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$D'E'F'$ <0315.g1:< D' E' F' >>`, the three sides are respectively
| equal to the three sides;
| therefore the triangles are equal in all respects.
| Therefore the angles :g:`$EDF$ <0315.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`$E'D'F'$ <0315.g1:< E' D' F' )>` are equal.

| But these angles are the measures of the dihedral angles formed by the
| planes :g:`$CVA$ <0315.g1:< C V A >>`, :g:`$AVB$ <0315.g1:< A V B >>` and by the planes :g:`$C'V'A'$ <0315.g1:< C' V' A' >>`, :g:`$A'V'B'$ <0315.g1:< A' V' B' >>` respectively.
| Therefore these dihedral angles are equal.
| Similarly for the other two dihedral angles.
| Hence the trihedral angles coincide if one is applied to the other;
| that is, they are equal.

| To understand what is implied by “taken in the same order” we may
| suppose ourselves to be placed at the vertices, and to take the faces in clock-
| wise direction, or the reverse, for *both* angles.

| If the face angles and dihedral angles are *taken in reverse directions*, i.e.
| in clockwise direction in one and in counterclockwise direction in the other,
| then, if the other conditions in the above three propositions are fulfilled, the
| trihedral angles are not equal but *symmetrical*.

| If the faces of a trihedral angle be produced beyond the vertex, they form
| another trihedral angle. It is easily seen that these *vertical trihedral angles*
| are *symmetrical*.
