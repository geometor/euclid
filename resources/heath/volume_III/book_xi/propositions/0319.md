Let the angles <span data-ref="0319.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:< G H K )">$GHK$</span> be the three given<br />
plane angles, and let two of these, taken together in any<br />
manner, be greater than the remaining one, while, further,<br />
the three are less than four right angles;

thus it is required to construct a solid angle out of angles<br />
equal to the angles <span data-ref="0319.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:< G H K )">$GHK$</span>.

![Proposition 23, Figure 1](0319.g1.svg)

Let <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be cut off equal to one<br />
another,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> be joined;<br />
it is therefore possible to construct a triangle out of straight<br />
lines equal to <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 22]</span>

Let <span data-ref="0319.g2:< L M N >">$LMN$</span> be so constructed that<br />
<span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span> to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, and<br />
further <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ N L /">$NL$</span>,<br />
let the circle <span data-ref="0319.g2:( L M N )">$LMN$</span> be described about<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0319.g2:< L M N >">$LMN$</span>,<br />
let its centre be taken, and let it be <span data-ref="0319.g2:O">$O$</span>;<br />
let <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ M O /">$MO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be joined;

I say that <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>.

![Proposition 23, Figure 2](0319.g2.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, if not, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is either equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, or less.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">First, let it be equal.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O L /">$OL$</span> to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O M /">$OM$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O M /">$OM$</span><br />
respectively;<br />
and, by hypothesis, the base <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L M /">$LM$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0319.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0319.g2:< L O M )">$LOM$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0319.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0319.g2:< M O N )">$MON$</span>,<br />
and further the angle <span data-ref="0319.g1:< G H K )">$GHK$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0319.g2:< N O L )">$NOL$</span>;</p>
