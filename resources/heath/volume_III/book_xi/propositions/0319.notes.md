## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **Construction of Triangle and Circumcircle**: Using Proposition 22, chords $AC, DF, GK$ form the sides of $\triangle LMN$. The circumscribing circle of $\triangle LMN$ is constructed with center $O$.
- **Crucial Lemma / Preliminary Inequality**: To erect a perpendicular $OR$ such that $OR^2 = AB^2 - LO^2$, Euclid must prove $AB > LO$.
- **Reductio ad Absurdum (Case 1: $AB = LO$)**: If $AB = LO$, the triangles $ABC, DEF, GHK$ become congruent to $\triangle LOM, \triangle MON, \triangle NOL$ by I. 8 (SSS), which would force the sum of the three given face angles to equal the sum of angles around point $O$ ($360^\circ$ / four right angles), contradicting the hypothesis that the angles are less than four right angles.
