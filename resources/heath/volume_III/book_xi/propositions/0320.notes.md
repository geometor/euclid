- **Series of Justifications**:

  - Alternating and corresponding angles show $\triangle LMO \sim \triangle PQO$ (I. 29, VI. 4).
  - Alternate proportion (V. 16): $LO : OP = LM : PQ$.
  - Since $LO > OP$, $LM > PQ$, and since $LM = AC$, $AC > PQ$.
  - Triangle hinge converse (I. 25) applied to $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle POQ$ gives $\angle ABC > \angle POQ$.
  - Summing the three inequalities gives $\angle ABC + \angle DEF + \angle GHK > \angle LOM + \angle MON + \angle NOL$.
  - Since the given angles sum to less than four right angles, the angles around $O$ must be "much less than four right angles."
