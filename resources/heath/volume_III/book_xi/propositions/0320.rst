.. assertion::

   therefore the three angles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` are :eq:`equal <0319.g1:< A B C ) = 0319.g2:< L O M ); 0319.g1:< D E F ) = 0319.g2:< M O N ); 0319.g1:< G H K ) = 0319.g2:< N O L )>` to the three angles :g:`$LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`, :g:`$MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the three angles :g:`$LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`, :g:`$MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>` are equal to four right angles;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` are equal to four right angles.

.. assertion::

   But they are also, by hypothesis, less than four right angles: which is absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is not equal to :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

I say next that neither is :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` less than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

For, if possible, let it be so, and let :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />` be made :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ O P / = 0319.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, and :g:`$OQ$ <0319.g2:/ O Q />` :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ O Q / = 0319.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`, and let :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is :eq:`equal <0319.g1:/ A B / = 0319.g1:/ B C />` to :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />` is also :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ O P / = 0319.g2:/ O Q />` to :g:`$OQ$ <0319.g2:/ O Q />`, so that the remainder :g:`$LP$ <0319.g2:/ L P />` is :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ L P / = 0319.g2:/ Q M />` to :g:`$QM$ <0319.g2:/ Q M />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` is parallel to :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`, :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>` and :g:`$LMO$ <0319.g2:< L M O >>` is equiangular with :g:`$PQO$ <0319.g2:< P Q O >>`; :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>` therefore, as :g:`$OL$ <0319.g2:/ O L />` is to :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />`, so is :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />` to :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>` and alternately, as :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` is to :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />`, so is :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` to :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` is greater than :g:`$OP$ <0319.g2:/ O P />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` is also greater than :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` was made :eq:`equal <0319.g2:/ L M / = 0319.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` is also greater than :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, the two sides :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />` are :eq:`equal <0319.g1:/ A B / = 0319.g2:/ P O /; 0319.g1:/ B C / = 0319.g2:/ O Q />` to the two sides :g:`$PO$ <0319.g2:/ P O />`, :g:`$OQ$ <0319.g2:/ O Q />`, and the base :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` is greater than the base :g:`$PQ$ <0319.g2:/ P Q />`,

.. assertion:: [I. 25] <I.25>

   therefore the angle :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` is greater than the angle :g:`$POQ$ <0319.g2:< P O Q )>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that the angle :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` is also greater than the angle :g:`$MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, and the angle :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` greater than the angle :g:`$NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the three angles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` are greater than the three angles :g:`$LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`, :g:`$MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`.

.. subclause::

   But, by hypothesis, the angles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` are less than four right angles;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angles :g:`$LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`, :g:`$MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>` are much less than four right angles.
