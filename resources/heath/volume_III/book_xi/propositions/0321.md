<p class="sub-clause">But they are also equal to four right angles:</p>
<p class="assertion">which is absurd.<br />
Therefore <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is not less than <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And it was proved that neither is it equal;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>.</p>

Let then <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span> be set up from the point <span data-ref="0319.g2:O">$O$</span> at right angles<br />
to the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0319.g2:( L M N )">$LMN$</span>, <span class="justification">[XI. 12]</span><br />
and let the square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span> be equal to that area by which<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>; <span class="justification">[Lemma]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R N /">$RN$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R O /">$RO$</span> is at right angles to the plane of the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0319.g2:( L M N )">$LMN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R O /">$RO$</span> is also at right angles to each of the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ M O /">$MO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ N O /">$NO$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O M /">$OM$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span> is common and at right angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R N /">$RN$</span> is also equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the three straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R N /">$RN$</span> are equal to<br />
one another.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Next, since by hypothesis the square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span> is equal to<br />
that area by which the square on <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is greater than the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L R /">$LR$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ L O /">$LO$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ O R /">$OR$</span>,<br />
for the angle <span data-ref="0319.g2:< L O R )">$LOR$</span> is right; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
while each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R N /">$RN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0319.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R L /">$RL$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0319.g2:/ R N /">$RN$</span>.</p>
