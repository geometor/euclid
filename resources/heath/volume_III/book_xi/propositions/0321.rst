.. subclause::

   But they are also equal to four right angles:

.. assertion::

   | which is absurd.
   | Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is not less than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

.. subclause::

   And it was proved that neither is it equal;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

| Let then :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` be set up from the point :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>` at right angles
| to the plane of the circle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:( L M N )>`, :just:`[XI. 12] <XI.12>`
| and let the square on :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` be equal to that area by which
| the square on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the square on :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`; :just:`[Lemma]`
| let :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   | Then, since :g:`$RO$ <0319.g2:/ R O />` is at right angles to the plane of the circle
   | :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:( L M N )>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$RO$ <0319.g2:/ R O />` is also at right angles to each of the straight
   | lines :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$MO$ <0319.g2:/ M O />`, :g:`$NO$ <0319.g2:/ N O />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />` is equal to :g:`$OM$ <0319.g2:/ O M />`,
   | while :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />` is equal to the base :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />` is also equal to each of the straight lines :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the three straight lines :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />` are equal to
   | one another.

.. subclause::

   | Next, since by hypothesis the square on :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />` is equal to
   | that area by which the square on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than the
   | square on :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />`.

.. subclause::

   | But the square on :g:`$LR$ <0319.g2:/ L R />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$OR$ <0319.g2:/ O R />`,
   | for the angle :g:`$LOR$ <0319.g2:< L O R )>` is right; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is equal to the square on :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`;
   | therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`.

.. subclause::

   | But each of the straight lines :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$DE$ <0319.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0319.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0319.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HK$ <0319.g1:/ H K />` is
   | equal to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`,
   | while each of the straight lines :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />` is equal to :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$DE$ <0319.g1:/ D E />`, :g:`$EF$ <0319.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0319.g1:/ G H />`,
   | :g:`$HK$ <0319.g1:/ H K />` is equal to each of the straight lines :g:`$RL$ <0319.g2:/ R L />`, :g:`$RM$ <0319.g2:/ R M />`, :g:`$RN$ <0319.g2:/ R N />`.
