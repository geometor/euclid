| Hence their vertical angles are equal to the three given angles respectively;
| and the required solid angle is constructed.

| We return now to the proposition to be proved as a preliminary to the
| construction, viz. that, in the figures, :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` is greater than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

| It will be observed that Euclid, as his manner is, proves it for one case
| only, that, namely, in which :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>`, the centre of the circle circumscribing the
| triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>`, falls *within* the triangle, leaving the other cases for the reader
| to prove. As usual, however, the two other cases are found in the Greek text,
| after the formal conclusion of the proposition, as above, ending with the words
| ὅπερ ἔδει ποιῆσαι. This position for the proofs itself suggests that they are not
| Euclid’s but are interpolated; and this is rendered certain by the fact that
| words distinguishing three cases at the point where the centre :g:`$O$ <0319.g2:O>` of the
| circumscribing circle is found, “It [the centre] will then be either within the
| triangle :g:`$LMN$ <0319.g2:< L M N >>` or on one of its sides or without. First let it be within,” are
| found in the MSS. B and V only and are manifestly interpolated. Nevertheless
| the additional two cases must have been inserted very early, as they are found
| in all the best MSS.

| In order to give a clear view of the proof of all three cases as given in the
| text, we will reproduce all three (Euclid’s as well as the others) with abbrevia-
| tions to make them catch the eye better.

| In all three cases the proof is by *reductio ad absurdum*, and it is proved
| first that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` cannot be *equal* to :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, and secondly that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` cannot be *less*
| than :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.

Case I.
~~~~~~~

| (1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.
| Then :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />` are respectively equal to :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`, :g:`$OM$ <0319.g2:/ O M />`;
| and :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />` $=$ :g:`$LM$ <0319.g2:/ L M />` (by construction).
| Therefore :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>`.
| Similarly :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>`,
| :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`,
| Adding, we have
| :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0319.g2:< L O M )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle MON$ <0319.g2:< M O N )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0319.g2:< N O L )>`
| $=$ four right angles:
| which contradicts the hypothesis.
| Therefore :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $\ne$ :g:`$LO$ <0319.g2:/ L O />`.
