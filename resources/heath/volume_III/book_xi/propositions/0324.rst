Case II.
~~~~~~~~

(1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Then (:g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $+$ :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />`), or (:g:`$DE$ <0319.g1:/ D E />` $+$ :g:`$EF$ <0319.g1:/ E F />`) $=$ :g:`$MO$ <0324.g1:/ M O />` $+$ :g:`$OL$ <0324.g1:/ O L />` $=$ :g:`$MN$ <0324.g1:/ M N />` $=$ :g:`$DF$ <0319.g1:/ D F />`; which contradicts the hypothesis.

Case III.
~~~~~~~~~

(1) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $=$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Then, in the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$LOM$ <0324.g1:< L O M >>`, two sides :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0319.g1:/ B C />` are respectively equal to two sides :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`, :g:`$OM$ <0324.g1:/ O M />`, and the bases :g:`$AC$ <0319.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$LM$ <0324.g1:/ L M />` are equal; therefore :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0324.g1:< L O M )>`. Similarly :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle NOL$ <0324.g1:< N O L )>`. Therefore, by addition, :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0319.g1:< A B C )>` $+$ :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0319.g1:< G H K )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>` (by hypothesis). But, in the triangles :g:`$DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N >>`, which are equal in all respects, :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $=$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`. But it was proved that :g:`$\angle MON$ <0324.g1:< M O N )>` $>$ :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0319.g1:< D E F )>`: which is impossible.

(2) Suppose, if possible, that :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />` $<$ :g:`$LO$ <0324.g1:/ L O />`. Along :g:`$OL$ <0324.g1:/ O L />`, :g:`$OM$ <0324.g1:/ O M />` measure :g:`$OP$ <0324.g1:/ O P />`, :g:`$OQ$ <0324.g1:/ O Q />` each equal to :g:`$AB$ <0319.g1:/ A B />`.
