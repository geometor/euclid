### Case I. (<span data-ref="0325.g1:O">$O$</span> within <span data-ref="0325.g1:< L M N >">$\Delta LMN$</span>.)

(1) Let <span data-ref="0325.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be, if possible, equal to <span data-ref="0325.g1:/ L O /">$LO$</span>.  
Then the $\Delta\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0325.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0325.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0325.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span> must be identically equal to the $\Delta\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0325.g1:< L O M >">$LOM$</span>, <span data-ref="0325.g1:< M O N >">$MON$</span>, <span data-ref="0325.g1:< N O L >">$NOL$</span> respectively.
