Case II. (:g:`$O$ <0326.g1:O>` lying on :g:`$MN$ <0326.g1:/ M N />`.)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In this case, whether (1) :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` = :g:`$LO$ <0326.g1:/ L O />`, or (2) :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` < :g:`$LO$ <0326.g1:/ L O />`, a triangle cannot be formed with :g:`$MN$ <0326.g1:/ M N />` as base and each of the other sides equal to :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />`. In other words, the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>` either reduces to a straight line or is impossible.

Case III. (:g:`$O$ <0327.g1:O>` lying outside the :g:`$\Delta LMN$ <0327.g1:< L M N >>`.)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

(1) *Suppose, if possible, that* :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` *=* :g:`$LO$ <0327.g1:/ L O />`.

Then the triangles :g:`$LOM$ <0327.g1:< L O M >>`, :g:`$MON$ <0327.g1:< M O N >>`, :g:`$NOL$ <0327.g1:< N O L >>` are identically :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< L O M > = 0326.g1:< A B C >; 0327.g1:< M O N > = 0326.g1:< D E F >; 0327.g1:< N O L > = 0327.g1:< G H K >>` to the triangles :g:`$ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K >>`. Since :g:`$\angle LOM$ <0327.g1:< L O M )>` + :g:`$\angle LON$ <0327.g1:< L O N )>` = :g:`$\angle MON$ <0327.g1:< M O N )>`, :g:`$\angle ABC$ <0327.g1:< A B C )>` + :g:`$\angle GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K )>` = :g:`$\angle DEF$ <0327.g1:< D E F )>`: which contradicts the hypothesis.

(2) *Suppose that* :g:`$AB$ <0326.g1:/ A B />` *<* :g:`$OL$ <0327.g1:/ O L />`.

Draw, as before, on :g:`$LM$ <0327.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0327.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NL$ <0327.g1:/ N L />` as bases triangles with vertices :g:`$P$ <0327.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0327.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0327.g1:R>` and identically equal to the $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0326.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$GHK$ <0327.g1:< G H K >>`. Next, at :g:`$N$ <0327.g1:N>` on the straight line :g:`$NR$ <0327.g1:/ N R />`, make :g:`$\angle RNS$ <0327.g1:< R N S )>` :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< R N S ) = 0327.g1:< P L M )>` to the angle :g:`$PLM$ <0327.g1:< P L M )>`, cut off :g:`$NS$ <0327.g1:/ N S />` :eq:`equal <0327.g1:/ N S / = 0327.g1:/ L M />` to :g:`$LM$ <0327.g1:/ L M />` and join :g:`$RS$ <0327.g1:/ R S />`, :g:`$LS$ <0327.g1:/ L S />`. Then :g:`$\Delta NRS$ <0327.g1:< N R S >>` is identically :eq:`equal <0327.g1:< N R S > = 0327.g1:< L P M > = 0326.g1:< A B C >>` to :g:`$\Delta LPM$ <0327.g1:< L P M >>` or :g:`$\Delta ABC$ <0326.g1:< A B C >>`. Now (:g:`$\angle LNR$ <0327.g1:< L N R )>` + :g:`$\angle RNS$ <0327.g1:< R N S )>`) < (:g:`$\angle NLO$ <0327.g1:< N L O )>` + :g:`$\angle OLM$ <0327.g1:< O L M )>`), that is, :g:`$\angle LNS$ <0327.g1:< L N S )>` < :g:`$\angle NLM$ <0327.g1:< N L M )>`. Thus, in $\Delta\text{s}$ :g:`$LNS$ <0327.g1:< L N S >>`, :g:`$NLM$ <0327.g1:< N L M >>`, two sides are equal to two sides, and the included angle in the former is less than the included angle in the other. Therefore :g:`$LS$ <0327.g1:/ L S />` < :g:`$MN$ <0327.g1:/ M N />`.
