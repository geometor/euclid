### PROPOSITION 24.

*If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.*

For let the solid <span data-ref="0327.g1:< C D H G >">$CDHG$</span> be contained by the parallel planes <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>;  
I say that the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.  
For, since the two parallel planes <span data-ref="0327.g1:< B G >">$BG$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> are cut by the plane <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>,  
their common sections are parallel. [XI. 16]
