### PROPOSITION 24.

<p class="enunciation">If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes<br />
in it are equal and parallelogrammic.</p>

For let the solid <span data-ref="0327.g1:< C D H G >">$CDHG$</span> be contained by the parallel planes<br />
<span data-ref="0327.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>;<br />
I say that the opposite planes<br />
in it are equal and parallelo-<br />
grammic.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the two parallel<br />
planes <span data-ref="0327.g1:< B G >">$BG$</span>, <span data-ref="0327.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> are cut by the<br />
plane <span data-ref="0327.g1:< A C >">$AC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">their common sections are<br />
parallel. <span class="justification">[XI. 16]</span></p>
