## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Structural transition**: This folio marks the transition from Proposition 23 (culmination of extensive commentary on trihedral angle construction) to Proposition 24 (the fundamental theorem on parallelepipeds).
- **Enunciation of XI. 24**: The Greek enunciation is translated verbatim: *"If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic."*
- **Solid nomenclature**: Euclid names the parallelepiped by two diagonally opposite vertices or representative letters: *the solid $CDHG$*, contained by six parallel faces arranged two and two.
