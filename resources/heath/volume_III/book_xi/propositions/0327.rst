PROPOSITION 24.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.

| For let the solid :g:`$CDHG$ <0327.g1:< C D H G >>` be contained by the parallel planes
| :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>`, :g:`$GF$ <0327.g1:< G F >>`, :g:`$AH$ <0327.g1:< A H >>`, :g:`$DF$ <0327.g1:< D F >>`, :g:`$BF$ <0327.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`$AE$ <0327.g1:< A E >>`;
| I say that the opposite planes
| in it are equal and parallelo-
| grammic.

.. subclause::

   | For, since the two parallel
   | planes :g:`$BG$ <0327.g1:< B G >>`, :g:`$CE$ <0327.g1:< C E >>` are cut by the
   | plane :g:`$AC$ <0327.g1:< A C >>`,

.. assertion::

   | their common sections are
   | parallel. :just:`[XI. 16] <XI.16>`
