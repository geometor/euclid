## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual criticism of enunciation**: Heath quotes Heiberg's assessment that XI. 24 is carelessly enunciated because Euclid omitted specifying:
  1. Exactly *six* planes (not more).
  2. The planes are parallel *two and two*.
  3. Opposite faces are *equal and similar* (congruent and similarly situated), which is essential for applying XI. Def. 10 in Proposition 25.
- **Application of previous propositions**:
  - `[XI. 16]`: Common sections of parallel planes with a third plane are parallel.
  - `[XI. 10]`: Angles whose sides are parallel and in the same direction, not in the same plane, are equal.
  - `[I. 34]`: Opposite sides and angles of a parallelogram are equal, and the diameter bisects it.
  - `[I. 4]`: SAS triangle congruence.
