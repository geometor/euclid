### PROPOSITION 25.

<p class="enunciation">If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane which is<br />
parallel to the opposite planes, then, as the base is to the base, so<br />
will the solid be to the solid.</p>

For let the parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span> be cut by the<br />
plane <span data-ref="0329.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span> which is parallel to the opposite planes <span data-ref="0329.g1:< R A >">$RA$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>;<br />
I say that, as the base <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A E F V >">$AEFV$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0329.g1:< E H C F >">$EHCF$</span>, so is the<br />
solid <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A B F U >">$ABFU$</span> to the solid <span data-ref="0329.g1:< E G C D >">$EGCD$</span>.

![Folio 0329 Figure 1](0329.g1.svg)

For let <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> be produced in each direction,<br />
let any number of straight lines whatever, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, be made<br />
equal to <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span>,<br />
and any number whatever, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ H M /">$HM$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, equal to <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>;<br />
and let the parallelograms <span data-ref="0329.g1:< L P >">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K V >">$KV$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< H W >">$HW$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< M S >">$MS$</span> and the solids<br />
<span data-ref="0329.g1:< L Q >">$LQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K R >">$KR$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D M >">$DM$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< M T >">$MT$</span> be completed.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since the straight lines <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ L K /">$LK$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> are equal to<br />
one another,</p>
<p class="assertion">the parallelograms <span data-ref="0329.g1:< L P >">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K V >">$KV$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> are also equal to one another,<br />
<span data-ref="0329.g1:< K O >">$KO$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K B >">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A G >">$AG$</span> are equal to one another,<br />
and further <span data-ref="0329.g1:< L X >">$LX$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K Q >">$KQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A R >">$AR$</span> are equal to one another, for they<br />
are opposite. <span class="justification">[XI. 24]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the parallelograms <span data-ref="0329.g1:< E C >">$EC$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< H W >">$HW$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< M S >">$MS$</span> are also equal to one another,<br />
<span data-ref="0329.g1:< H G >">$HG$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< H I >">$HI$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< I N >">$IN$</span> are equal to one another,<br />
and further <span data-ref="0329.g1:< D H >">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< M Y >">$MY$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< N T >">$NT$</span> are equal to one another.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore in the solids <span data-ref="0329.g1:< L Q >">$LQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< K R >">$KR$</span>, <span data-ref="0329.g1:< A U >">$AU$</span> three planes are<br />
equal to three planes.</p>
