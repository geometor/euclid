# Transcription Notes: Folio 0329 (Page 325)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Previous folio**: Folio 0328 (Page 324) concluded Proposition 24 and its commentary.
- **Current folio**: Introduces Proposition 25 (the solid analog of VI. 1 for parallelepipeds sharing a height). Contains the enunciation, setting-out, construction of equimultiples by extending the base line $AH$, and the initial step proving that repeated slices produce mutually equal solids.
- **Next folio**: Folio 0330 (Page 326) continues and finishes the proof of Proposition 25 via the Eudoxan definition of proportion (V. Def. 5), followed by Heath's commentary on the term *parallelepipedal*.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Analog to VI. 1**: Proposition 25 extends the foundational ratio theory of Book VI (where triangles and parallelograms under the same height are as their bases) into solid geometry for parallelepipeds.
- **Eudoxan equimultiples**: The construction proceeds strictly according to Book V: any number of segments equal to $AE$ are laid off along $AH$ produced to the left ($AK, KL$), and any number equal to $EH$ to the right ($HM, MN$).
- **Application of XI. 24**: The equality of opposite faces proved in Proposition 24 is used directly to establish the equality of corresponding faces in each solid unit.
