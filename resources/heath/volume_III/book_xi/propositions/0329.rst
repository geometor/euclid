PROPOSITION 25.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane which is parallel to the opposite planes, then, as the base is to the base, so will the solid be to the solid.

For let the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$ABCD$ <0329.g1:< A B C D >>` be cut by the plane :g:`$FG$ <0329.g1:< F G >>` which is parallel to the opposite planes :g:`$RA$ <0329.g1:< R A >>`, :g:`$DH$ <0329.g1:< D H >>`; I say that, as the base :g:`$AEFV$ <0329.g1:< A E F V >>` is to the base :g:`$EHCF$ <0329.g1:< E H C F >>`, so is the solid :g:`$ABFU$ <0329.g1:< A B F U >>` to the solid :g:`$EGCD$ <0329.g1:< E G C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0329.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0329 Figure 1

For let :g:`$AH$ <0329.g1:/ A H />` be produced in each direction, let any number of straight lines whatever, :g:`$AK$ <0329.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KL$ <0329.g1:/ K L />`, be made :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ A K / = 0329.g1:/ K L / = 0329.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$AE$ <0329.g1:/ A E />`, and any number whatever, :g:`$HM$ <0329.g1:/ H M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0329.g1:/ M N />`, :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ H M / = 0329.g1:/ M N / = 0329.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0329.g1:/ E H />`; and let the parallelograms :g:`$LP$ <0329.g1:< L P >>`, :g:`$KV$ <0329.g1:< K V >>`, :g:`$HW$ <0329.g1:< H W >>`, :g:`$MS$ <0329.g1:< M S >>` and the solids :g:`$LQ$ <0329.g1:< L Q >>`, :g:`$KR$ <0329.g1:< K R >>`, :g:`$DM$ <0329.g1:< D M >>`, :g:`$MT$ <0329.g1:< M T >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the straight lines :g:`$LK$ <0329.g1:/ L K />`, :g:`$KA$ <0329.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`$AE$ <0329.g1:/ A E />` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:/ L K / = 0329.g1:/ K A / = 0329.g1:/ A E />` to one another,

.. assertion::

   the parallelograms :g:`$LP$ <0329.g1:< L P >>`, :g:`$KV$ <0329.g1:< K V >>`, :g:`$AF$ <0329.g1:< A F >>` are also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L P > = 0329.g1:< K V > = 0329.g1:< A F >>` to one another, :g:`$KO$ <0329.g1:< K O >>`, :g:`$KB$ <0329.g1:< K B >>`, :g:`$AG$ <0329.g1:< A G >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< K O > = 0329.g1:< K B > = 0329.g1:< A G >>` to one another, and further :g:`$LX$ <0329.g1:< L X >>`, :g:`$KQ$ <0329.g1:< K Q >>`, :g:`$AR$ <0329.g1:< A R >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< L X > = 0329.g1:< K Q > = 0329.g1:< A R >>` to one another, for they are opposite. :just:`[XI. 24] <XI.24>`

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the parallelograms :g:`$EC$ <0329.g1:< E C >>`, :g:`$HW$ <0329.g1:< H W >>`, :g:`$MS$ <0329.g1:< M S >>` are also :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< E C > = 0329.g1:< H W > = 0329.g1:< M S >>` to one another, :g:`$HG$ <0329.g1:< H G >>`, :g:`$HI$ <0329.g1:< H I >>`, :g:`$IN$ <0329.g1:< I N >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< H G > = 0329.g1:< H I > = 0329.g1:< I N >>` to one another, and further :g:`$DH$ <0329.g1:< D H >>`, :g:`$MY$ <0329.g1:< M Y >>`, :g:`$NT$ <0329.g1:< N T >>` are :eq:`equal <0329.g1:< D H > = 0329.g1:< M Y > = 0329.g1:< N T >>` to one another.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the solids :g:`$LQ$ <0329.g1:< L Q >>`, :g:`$KR$ <0329.g1:< K R >>`, :g:`$AU$ <0329.g1:< A U >>` three planes are equal to three planes.
