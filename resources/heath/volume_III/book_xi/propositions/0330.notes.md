## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Greek terminology**:
  - `παραλληλεπίπεδος` (*parallelepipedal*): compound adjective meaning "having parallel planes/surfaces".
  - `στερεὸν παραλληλεπίπεδον` (*parallelepipedal solid*): noun phrase literally translating to "parallelepipedal solid".
  - Heath warns against the common misspelling/misnomer *parallelopiped*.
- **Application of Book V, Definition 5**: Euclid explicitly rehearses the full four-magnitude equimultiple criterion, marking a textbook application of Eudoxus' proportion theory to 3D geometry.
- **Foundational critique of XI. Def. 10**: Heath highlights Euclid's assumption that solids contained by equal and similar faces are equal, pointing forward to the requirement of Simson's Proposition C.
