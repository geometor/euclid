let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> be set up from the point <span data-ref="0331.g1:K">$K$</span> at right angles to the plane through <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span>, [XI. 12]  
let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,  
and let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> be joined;  
I say that the solid angle at <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, contained by the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A L )">$BAL$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A H )">$BAH$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H A L )">$HAL$</span> is equal to the solid angle at <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span> contained by the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D C )">$EDC$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F D C )">$FDC$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be cut off equal to one another, and let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference; [XI. Def. 3]  
therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F G D )">$FGD$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F G E )">$FGE$</span> is right.

For the same reason each of the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H K A )">$HKA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H K B )">$HKB$</span> is also right.

And, since the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> respectively, and they contain equal angles, therefore the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. [I. 4]

But <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, and they contain right angles; therefore <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>. [I. 4]

Again, since the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, and they contain right angles, therefore the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. [I. 4]

But <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>; therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

And the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>; therefore the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A H )">$BAH$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>. [I. 8]

For the same reason the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H A L )">$HAL$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F D C )">$FDC$</span>.

And the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A L )">$BAL$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D C )">$EDC$</span>.
