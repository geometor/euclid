let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> be set up from the point <span data-ref="0331.g1:K">$K$</span> at right angles to the<br />
plane through <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span>, <span class="justification">[XI. 12]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> be joined ;<br />
I say that the solid angle at <span data-ref="0331.g1:A">$A$</span>, contained by the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A L )">$BAL$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A H )">$BAH$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H A L )">$HAL$</span> is equal to the solid angle at <span data-ref="0331.g1:D">$D$</span> contained by<br />
the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D C )">$EDC$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F D C )">$FDC$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> be cut off equal to one another,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> is at right angles to the plane of reference,<br />
it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which<br />
meet it and are in the plane of reference ; <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F G D )">$FGD$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F G E )">$FGE$</span> is right.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
each of the angles <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H K A )">$HKA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H K B )">$HKB$</span> is also right.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G D /">$GD$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> respectively,<br />
and they contain equal angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,<br />
and they contain right angles ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>,<br />
and they contain right angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two sides<br />
<span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0331.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A H )">$BAH$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< H A L )">$HAL$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< F D C )">$FDC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< B A L )">$BAL$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0331.g1:< E D C )">$EDC$</span>.</p>
