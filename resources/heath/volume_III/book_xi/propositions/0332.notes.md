## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Structure of Proof**: Euclid constructs the third edge $AH$ of the trihedral solid angle at $A$ by setting up $KH$ perpendicular to plane $BA, AL$ at $K$, with $KH = GF$. By applying I. 4 three times and I. 8 once, he proves pairwise equality of all three face plane angles:
  - $\angle BAH = \angle EDF$
  - $\angle HAL = \angle FDC$
  - $\angle BAL = \angle EDC$
- **Assumed Principle**: As noted by Heath on the following page, Euclid infers the equality of the solid angles directly from the equality of the three plane angles arranged in the same order, without an explicit definition or postulate of solid angle superposition.
- **Propositions Cited**:
  - XI. 12 (to erect a perpendicular to a plane from a point in it)
  - XI. Def. 3 (definition of straight line perpendicular to a plane)
  - I. 4 (SAS triangle congruence, applied thrice)
  - I. 8 (SSS triangle congruence)
