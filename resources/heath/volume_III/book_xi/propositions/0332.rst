:title: Proposition 26

let :g:`KH <0331.g1:/ K H />` be set up from the point :g:`K <0331.g1:K>` at right angles to the plane through :g:`BA <0331.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`AL <0331.g1:/ A L />`, :just:`[XI. 12] <XI.12>` let :g:`KH <0331.g1:/ K H />` be made :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`GF <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and let :g:`HA <0331.g1:/ H A />` be joined ; I say that the solid angle at :g:`A <0331.g1:A>`, contained by the angles :g:`BAL <0331.g1:< B A L )>`, :g:`BAH <0331.g1:< B A H )>`, :g:`HAL <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A L ) = 0331.g1:< E D C ); 0331.g1:< B A H ) = 0331.g1:< E D F ); 0331.g1:< H A L ) = 0331.g1:< F D C )>` to the solid angle at :g:`D <0331.g1:D>` contained by the angles :g:`EDC <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, :g:`EDF <0331.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`FDC <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

For let :g:`AB <0331.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`DE <0331.g1:/ D E />` be cut off :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to one another, and let :g:`HB <0331.g1:/ H B />`, :g:`KB <0331.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`FE <0331.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`GE <0331.g1:/ G E />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`FG <0331.g1:/ F G />` is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference ; :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the angles :g:`FGD <0331.g1:< F G D )>`, :g:`FGE <0331.g1:< F G E )>` is :eq:`right <0331.g1:< F G D ) = 0331.g1:< F G E )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason each of the angles :g:`HKA <0331.g1:< H K A )>`, :g:`HKB <0331.g1:< H K B )>` is also :eq:`right <0331.g1:< H K A ) = 0331.g1:< H K B )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two sides :g:`KA <0331.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`AB <0331.g1:/ A B />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K A / = 0331.g1:/ G D /; 0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to the two sides :g:`GD <0331.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`DE <0331.g1:/ D E />` respectively, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`KB <0331.g1:/ K B />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K B / = 0331.g1:/ G E />` to the base :g:`GE <0331.g1:/ G E />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`KH <0331.g1:/ K H />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`GF <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles ;

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore :g:`HB <0331.g1:/ H B />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H B / = 0331.g1:/ F E />` to :g:`FE <0331.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the two sides :g:`AK <0331.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`KH <0331.g1:/ K H />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A K / = 0331.g1:/ D G /; 0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to the two sides :g:`DG <0331.g1:/ D G />`, :g:`GF <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`AH <0331.g1:/ A H />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A H / = 0331.g1:/ F D />` to the base :g:`FD <0331.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`AB <0331.g1:/ A B />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`DE <0331.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the two sides :g:`HA <0331.g1:/ H A />`, :g:`AB <0331.g1:/ A B />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H A / = 0331.g1:/ D F /; 0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to the two sides :g:`DF <0331.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`DE <0331.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   And the base :g:`HB <0331.g1:/ H B />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H B / = 0331.g1:/ F E />` to the base :g:`FE <0331.g1:/ F E />` ;

.. assertion:: [I. 8] <I.8>

   therefore the angle :g:`BAH <0331.g1:< B A H )>` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A H ) = 0331.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`EDF <0331.g1:< E D F )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`HAL <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< H A L ) = 0331.g1:< F D C )>` to the angle :g:`FDC <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle :g:`BAL <0331.g1:< B A L )>` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A L ) = 0331.g1:< E D C )>` to the angle :g:`EDC <0331.g1:< E D C )>`.
