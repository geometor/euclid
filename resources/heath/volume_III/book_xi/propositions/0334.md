### PROPOSITION 28.

<p class="enunciation">If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane through the<br />
diagonals of the opposite planes, the solid will be bisected by the<br />
plane.</p>

For let the parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0334.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> be cut by the plane<br />
<span data-ref="0334.g1:< C D E F >">$CDEF$</span> through the diagonals <span data-ref="0334.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>, <span data-ref="0334.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> of<br />
opposite planes ;<br />
I say that the solid <span data-ref="0334.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> will be bisected by<br />
the plane <span data-ref="0334.g1:< C D E F >">$CDEF$</span>.
