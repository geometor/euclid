## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Heath's Critique of Euclid's XI. 28**:
  - Highlights a foundational flaw: Euclid infers solid equality from face equality/similarity without noting that the two triangular prisms are *symmetrical* (enantiomorphic), not directly superposable.
  - Cites Legendre's rigorous reformulation using the notion of *equivalence* (equal volume/content) rather than congruence.
- **Preliminary Lemmas for Legendre's Proof**:
  - Lemma 1: Parallel sections of a prism cutting all lateral edges are congruent polygons.
  - Lemma 2: Equality of prisms with congruent, similarly arranged trihedral base elements.
