Place the trihedral angle at <span data-ref="0336.g1:A">$A$</span> on that at <span data-ref="0336.g1:A'">$A'$</span>.<br />
Then the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span> coincides with the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >">$A'B'C'D'E'$</span>, the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A G >">$AG$</span><br />
with the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A' G' >">$A'G'$</span>, and the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> with the face <span data-ref="0336.g1:< A' L' >">$A'L'$</span>.<br />
The point <span data-ref="0336.g1:C">$C$</span> falls on <span data-ref="0336.g1:C'">$C'$</span> and <span data-ref="0336.g1:D">$D$</span> on <span data-ref="0336.g1:D'">$D'$</span>.

![Equal prisms](0336.g1.svg)

Since the lateral edges of a prism are parallel, <span data-ref="0336.g1:/ C H /">$CH$</span> will fall on <span data-ref="0336.g1:/ C' H' /">$C'H'$</span>, and<br />
<span data-ref="0336.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span> on <span data-ref="0336.g1:/ D' K' /">$D'K'$</span>.

And the points <span data-ref="0336.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:L">$L$</span> coincide respectively with <span data-ref="0336.g1:F'">$F'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:G'">$G'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:L'">$L'$</span>, so that<br />
the planes <span data-ref="0336.g1:< G K >">$GK$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:< G' K' >">$G'K'$</span> coincide.

Hence <span data-ref="0336.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:K">$K$</span> coincide with <span data-ref="0336.g1:H'">$H'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g1:K'">$K'$</span> respectively.<br />
Thus the prisms coincide throughout and are equal.

In the same way we can prove that two *truncated* prisms with three faces<br />
forming a solid angle related to one another as in the above proposition are<br />
identically equal.

In particular,<br />
*Cor.* *Two right prisms having equal bases and equal heights are equal.*

**3.** *An oblique prism is equivalent to a right prism whose base is a right<br />
section of the oblique prism and whose<br />
height is equal to a lateral edge of the<br />
oblique prism.*

Suppose <span data-ref="0336.g2:< G L >">$GL$</span> to be a right section of<br />
the oblique prism <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A D' >">$AD'$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0336.g2:< G L' >">$GL'$</span> be<br />
a right prism on <span data-ref="0336.g2:< G L >">$GL$</span> as base and with<br />
height equal to a lateral edge of <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A D' >">$AD'$</span>.

![Oblique prism equivalent to right prism](0336.g2.svg)

Now the lateral edges of <span data-ref="0336.g2:< G L' >">$GL'$</span> are<br />
equal to the lateral edges of <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A D' >">$AD'$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0336.g2:/ A G /">$AG$</span> = <span data-ref="0336.g2:/ A' G' /">$A'G'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g2:/ B H /">$BH$</span> = <span data-ref="0336.g2:/ B' H' /">$B'H'$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0336.g2:/ C K /">$CK$</span> = <span data-ref="0336.g2:/ C' K' /">$C'K'$</span>, etc.

Thus the faces <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A H >">$AH$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g2:< B K >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g2:< C L >">$CL$</span> are<br />
equal respectively to the faces <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A' H' >">$A'H'$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0336.g2:< B' K' >">$B'K'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g2:< C' L' >">$C'L'$</span>.

Therefore [by the proposition<br />
above]<br />
(truncated prism <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A L >">$AL$</span>) = (truncated<br />
prism <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A' L' >">$A'L'$</span>).

Subtracting each from the whole solid <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A L' >">$AL'$</span>, we see that<br />
the prisms <span data-ref="0336.g2:< A D' >">$AD'$</span>, <span data-ref="0336.g2:< G L' >">$GL'$</span> are equivalent.
