Place the trihedral angle at :g:`A <0336.g1:A>` on that at :g:`A' <0336.g1:A'>`. Then the face :g:`ABCDE <0336.g1:< A B C D E >>` coincides with the face :g:`A'B'C'D'E' <0336.g1:< A' B' C' D' E' >>`, the face :g:`AG <0336.g1:< A G >>` with the face :g:`A'G' <0336.g1:< A' G' >>`, and the face :g:`AL <0336.g1:< A L >>` with the face :g:`A'L' <0336.g1:< A' L' >>`. The point :g:`C <0336.g1:C>` falls on :g:`C' <0336.g1:C'>` and :g:`D <0336.g1:D>` on :g:`D' <0336.g1:D'>`.

.. picture:: 0336.g1.svg
   :alt: Equal prisms

Since the lateral edges of a prism are parallel, :g:`CH <0336.g1:/ C H />` will fall on :g:`C'H' <0336.g1:/ C' H' />`, and :g:`DK <0336.g1:/ D K />` on :g:`D'K' <0336.g1:/ D' K' />`.

And the points :g:`F <0336.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0336.g1:G>`, :g:`L <0336.g1:L>` coincide respectively with :g:`F' <0336.g1:F'>`, :g:`G' <0336.g1:G'>`, :g:`L' <0336.g1:L'>`, so that the planes :g:`GK <0336.g1:< G K >>`, :g:`G'K' <0336.g1:< G' K' >>` coincide.

Hence :g:`H <0336.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0336.g1:K>` coincide with :g:`H' <0336.g1:H'>`, :g:`K' <0336.g1:K'>` respectively. Thus the prisms coincide throughout and are equal.

In the same way we can prove that two *truncated* prisms with three faces forming a solid angle related to one another as in the above proposition are identically equal.

In particular, *Cor.* *Two right prisms having equal bases and equal heights are equal.*

**3.** *An oblique prism is equivalent to a right prism whose base is a right section of the oblique prism and whose height is equal to a lateral edge of the oblique prism.*

Suppose :g:`GL <0336.g2:< G L >>` to be a right section of the oblique prism :g:`AD' <0336.g2:< A D' >>`, and let :g:`GL' <0336.g2:< G L' >>` be a right prism on :g:`GL <0336.g2:< G L >>` as base and with height equal to a lateral edge of :g:`AD' <0336.g2:< A D' >>`.

.. picture:: 0336.g2.svg
   :alt: Oblique prism equivalent to right prism

Now the lateral edges of :g:`GL' <0336.g2:< G L' >>` are equal to the lateral edges of :g:`AD' <0336.g2:< A D' >>`.

Therefore :g:`AG <0336.g2:/ A G />` = :g:`A'G' <0336.g2:/ A' G' />`, :g:`BH <0336.g2:/ B H />` = :g:`B'H' <0336.g2:/ B' H' />`, :g:`CK <0336.g2:/ C K />` = :g:`C'K' <0336.g2:/ C' K' />`, etc.

Thus the faces :g:`AH <0336.g2:< A H >>`, :g:`BK <0336.g2:< B K >>`, :g:`CL <0336.g2:< C L >>` are :eq:`equal <0336.g2:< A H > = 0336.g2:< A' H' >; 0336.g2:< B K > = 0336.g2:< B' K' >; 0336.g2:< C L > = 0336.g2:< C' L' >>` respectively to the faces :g:`A'H' <0336.g2:< A' H' >>`, :g:`B'K' <0336.g2:< B' K' >>`, :g:`C'L' <0336.g2:< C' L' >>`.

Therefore [by the proposition above] (truncated prism :g:`AL <0336.g2:< A L >>`) = (truncated prism :g:`A'L' <0336.g2:< A' L' >>`).

Subtracting each from the whole solid :g:`AL' <0336.g2:< A L' >>`, we see that the prisms :g:`AD' <0336.g2:< A D' >>`, :g:`GL' <0336.g2:< G L' >>` are equivalent.
