Now suppose the parallelepiped of Euclid's proposition to be cut by the<br />
plane through <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0337.g1:< K L M N >">$KLMN$</span> be a right section of the parallelepiped<br />
cutting the edges <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ G F /">$GF$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>.<br />
Then <span data-ref="0337.g1:< K L M N >">$KLMN$</span> is a parallelogram; and, if the<br />
diagonal <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> be drawn,
<p align="center"><span data-ref="0337.g1:< K L M >">$\triangle KLM$</span> = <span data-ref="0337.g1:< M N K >">$\triangle MNK$</span>.</p>

![Parallelepiped cut by plane](0337.g1.svg)

Now the prism of which the $\Delta\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0337.g1:< A B G >">$ABG$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:< D C F >">$DCF$</span> are<br />
the bases is equal to the right prism on <span data-ref="0337.g1:< K L M >">$\triangle KLM$</span> as<br />
base and of height <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.

Similarly the prism of which the $\Delta\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0337.g1:< A G H >">$AGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:< D F E >">$DFE$</span><br />
are the bases is equal to the right prism on <span data-ref="0337.g1:< M N K >">$\triangle MNK$</span><br />
as base and with height <span data-ref="0337.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>. <span class="justification">[(3) above]</span>

And the right prisms on $\Delta\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0337.g1:< K L M >">$KLM$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g1:< M N K >">$MNK$</span> as bases and of equal height<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> are equal. <span class="justification">[(2), Cor. above]</span>

Consequently the two prisms into which the parallelepiped is divided are<br />
equivalent.

### PROPOSITION 29.

<p class="enunciation">Parallelepipedal solids which are on the same base and of<br />
the same height, and in which the extremities of the sides which<br />
stand up are on the same straight lines, are equal to one<br />
another.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0337.g2:< C M >">$CM$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:< C N >">$CN$</span> be parallelepipedal solids on the same base<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g2:< A B >">$AB$</span> and of the same height,<br />
and let the extremities of their<br />
sides which stand up, namely<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g2:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ L N /">$LN$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ C E /">$CE$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g2:/ B H /">$BH$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, be on the same straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ F N /">$FN$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ D K /">$DK$</span>;<br />
I say that the solid <span data-ref="0337.g2:< C M >">$CM$</span> is equal<br />
to the solid <span data-ref="0337.g2:< C N >">$CN$</span>.

![Proposition 29](0337.g2.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, since each of the figures<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g2:< C H >">$CH$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:< C K >">$CK$</span> is a parallelogram, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ C B /">$CB$</span><br />
is equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ D H /">$DH$</span>, <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">hence <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ D H /">$DH$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ E K /">$EK$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ E H /">$EH$</span> be subtracted from each;

<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ D E /">$DE$</span> is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0337.g2:/ H K /">$HK$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Hence the triangle <span data-ref="0337.g2:< D C E >">$DCE$</span> is also equal to the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0337.g2:< H B K >">$HBK$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 8, 4]</span><br />
and the parallelogram <span data-ref="0337.g2:< D G >">$DG$</span> to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0337.g2:< H N >">$HN$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 36]</span></p>
