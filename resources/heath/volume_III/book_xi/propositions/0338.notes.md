## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Method of Proof**: Equality of the two solids is proved by showing that each is composed of a common central solid plus one of two equal lateral prisms (or minus equal prisms, depending on the overlap case).
- **Heath's Commentary on Cases**:
  - Euclid provides only the case where the top opposite faces overlap partially without sharing an edge ($DH > DE$).
  - Heath illustrates the two alternative configurations:
    1. Case 2: Where the top faces overlap such that an intermediate prism on base $\triangle GAL, \triangle ECB$ is shared.
    2. Case 3: Where the top faces do not overlap, requiring subtraction from the overarching solid on base $AB$ and top $FDKN$.
  - Refutes Simson's suggestion that XI. 28 was intended as an auxiliary lemma specifically for XI. 29.
