## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations

- **Proposition 30 (General Shear in Space)**: The generalisation of Book I, Proposition 36 in three dimensions, where the standing edges are not constrained to lie in the same vertical planes.
- **Proof Mechanism**: Euclid constructs an intermediate parallelepipedal solid $CP$ on the same base $ACBL$ whose top edges lie on the intersections of the produced planes ($DR$ and $FP$). Then by two successive applications of Proposition 29:
  - $\text{Solid } CM = \text{Solid } CP$ (by XI. 29 along lines $FP, DR$)
  - $\text{Solid } CP = \text{Solid } CN$ (by XI. 29 along lines $GQ, NR$)
  - Therefore $\text{Solid } CM = \text{Solid } CN$.
- **Legendre's Corollary**: Heath introduces Legendre's theorem converting any parallelepiped into an equivalent rectangular parallelepiped of the same height.
