| Let :g:`$CM$ <0339.g1:< C M >>`, :g:`$CN$ <0339.g1:< C N >>` be parallelepipedal solids on the same base
| :g:`$AB$ <0339.g1:< A B >>` and of the same height,
| and let the extremities of their
| sides which stand up, namely
| :g:`$AF$ <0339.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$AG$ <0339.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$LM$ <0339.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$LN$ <0339.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`$CD$ <0339.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$CE$ <0339.g1:/ C E />`,
| :g:`$BH$ <0339.g1:/ B H />`, :g:`$BK$ <0339.g1:/ B K />`, not be on the same
| straight lines ;
| I say that the solid :g:`$CM$ <0339.g1:< C M >>` is
| equal to the solid :g:`$CN$ <0339.g1:< C N >>`.

| For let :g:`$NK$ <0339.g1:/ N K />`, :g:`$DH$ <0339.g1:/ D H />` be pro-
| duced and meet one another
| at :g:`$R$ <0339.g1:R>`,
| and further let :g:`$FM$ <0339.g1:/ F M />`, :g:`$GE$ <0339.g1:/ G E />` be
| produced to :g:`$P$ <0339.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0339.g1:Q>`;
| let :g:`$AO$ <0339.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$LP$ <0339.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$CQ$ <0339.g1:/ C Q />`, :g:`$BR$ <0339.g1:/ B R />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0339.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 30

.. assertion::

   | Then the solid :g:`$CM$ <0339.g1:< C M >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` is
   | the base, and :g:`$FDHM$ <0339.g1:< F D H M >>` its opposite, is equal to the solid :g:`$CP$ <0339.g1:< C P >>`,
   | of which the parallelogram :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` is the base, and :g:`$OQRP$ <0339.g1:< O Q R P >>` its
   | opposite;

.. subclause::

   | for they are on the same base :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` and of the same height,
   | and the extremities of their sides which stand up, namely :g:`$AF$ <0339.g1:/ A F />`,
   | :g:`$AO$ <0339.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$LM$ <0339.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$LP$ <0339.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$CD$ <0339.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$CQ$ <0339.g1:/ C Q />`, :g:`$BH$ <0339.g1:/ B H />`, :g:`$BR$ <0339.g1:/ B R />`, are on the same straight
   | lines :g:`$FP$ <0339.g1:/ F P />`, :g:`$DR$ <0339.g1:/ D R />`. :just:`[XI. 29] <XI.29>`

.. assertion::

   | But the solid :g:`$CP$ <0339.g1:< C P >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` is
   | the base, and :g:`$OQRP$ <0339.g1:< O Q R P >>` its opposite, is equal to the solid :g:`$CN$ <0339.g1:< C N >>`,
   | of which the parallelogram :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` is the base and :g:`$GEKN$ <0339.g1:< G E K N >>` its
   | opposite;

.. subclause::

   | for they are again on the same base :g:`$ACBL$ <0339.g1:< A C B L >>` and of the same
   | height, and the extremities of their sides which stand up,
   | namely :g:`$AG$ <0339.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$AO$ <0339.g1:/ A O />`, :g:`$CE$ <0339.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$CQ$ <0339.g1:/ C Q />`, :g:`$LN$ <0339.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`$LP$ <0339.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$BK$ <0339.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$BR$ <0339.g1:/ B R />`, are on the
   | same straight lines :g:`$GQ$ <0339.g1:/ G Q />`, :g:`$NR$ <0339.g1:/ N R />`.

.. assertion::

   Hence the solid :g:`$CM$ <0339.g1:< C M >>` is also equal to the solid :g:`$CN$ <0339.g1:< C N >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| This proposition completes the proof of the theorem that
| *Two parallelepipeds on the same base and of the same height are equivalent.*

| :person:`Legendre <legendre>` deduced the useful theorem that
| *Every parallelepiped can be changed into an equivalent rectangular parallele-
| piped having the same height and an equivalent base.*

| For suppose we have a parallelepiped on the base :g:`$ABCD$ <0340.g1:< A B C D >>` with :g:`$EFGH$ <0340.g1:< E F G H >>` for
| the opposite face.
