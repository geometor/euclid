### PROPOSITION 31.

*Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another.*

Let the parallelepipedal solids <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span>, of the same height, be on equal bases <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>.

I say that the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> is equal to the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span>.
