### PROPOSITION 31.

<p class="enunciation">Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the<br />
same height are equal to one another.</p>

Let the parallelepipedal solids <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span>, of the same height,<br />
be on equal bases <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>.

I say that the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> is equal to the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C F >">$CF$</span>.
