PROPOSITION 31.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another.

| Let the parallelepipedal solids :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>`, :g:`$CF$ <0341.g1:< C F >>`, of the same height,
| be on equal bases :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

I say that the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` is equal to the solid :g:`$CF$ <0341.g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: 0341.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31

| First, let the sides which stand up, :g:`$HK$ <0341.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$BE$ <0341.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$AG$ <0341.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$LM$ <0341.g1:/ L M />`,
| :g:`$PQ$ <0341.g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`$DF$ <0341.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$CO$ <0341.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$RS$ <0341.g1:/ R S />`, be at right angles to the bases :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`;
| let the straight line :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` be produced in a straight line
| with :g:`$CR$ <0341.g1:/ C R />`;
| on the straight line :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />`, and at the point :g:`$R$ <0341.g1:R>` on it, let the
| angle :g:`$TRU$ <0341.g1:< T R U )>` be constructed equal to the angle :g:`$ALB$ <0341.g1:< A L B )>`, :just:`[I. 23] <I.23>`
| let :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` be made equal to :g:`$AL$ <0341.g1:/ A L />`, and :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />` equal to :g:`$LB$ <0341.g1:/ L B />`,
| and let the base :g:`$RW$ <0341.g1:< R W >>` and the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the two sides :g:`$TR$ <0341.g1:/ T R />`, :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />` are equal to the two
   | sides :g:`$AL$ <0341.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`$LB$ <0341.g1:/ L B />`,
   | and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the parallelogram :g:`$RW$ <0341.g1:< R W >>` is equal and similar to the
   | parallelogram :g:`$HL$ <0341.g1:< H L >>`.
