<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R X >">$RX$</span> is equal and similar to the<br />

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0341.g1:< L E >">$LE$</span> is also equal and similar to <span data-ref="0341.g1:< S U >">$SU$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore three parallelograms of the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> are equal and<br />
similar to three parallelograms of the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X U >">$XU$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the former three are equal and similar to the three<br />
opposite, and the latter three to the three opposite; <span class="justification">[XI. 24]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> is equal to the<br />
whole parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X U >">$XU$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 10]</span></p>

Let <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ D R /">$DR$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ W U /">$WU$</span> be drawn through and meet one another<br />
at <span data-ref="0341.g1:Y">$Y$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ a T b /">$aTb$</span> be drawn through <span data-ref="0341.g1:T">$T$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ D Y /">$DY$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ P D /">$PD$</span> be produced to <span data-ref="0341.g1:a">$a$</span>,<br />
and let the solids <span data-ref="0341.g1:< Y X >">$YX$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R I >">$RI$</span> be completed.

<p class="assertion">Then the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X Y >">$XY$</span>, of which the parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R X >">$RX$</span> is the<br />
base and <span data-ref="0341.g1:< Y c >">$Yc$</span> its opposite, is equal to the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X U >">$XU$</span> of which<br />
the parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R X >">$RX$</span> is the base and <span data-ref="0341.g1:< U V >">$UV$</span> its opposite,<br />
for they are on the same base <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R X >">$RX$</span> and of the same height, and<br />
the extremities of their sides which stand up, namely <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ R Y /">$RY$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0341.g1:/ T b /">$Tb$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ S e /">$Se$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ S d /">$Sd$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ X c /">$Xc$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ X V /">$XV$</span>, are on the same straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0341.g1:/ Y W /">$YW$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ e V /">$eV$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 29]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X U >">$XU$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> :</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< X Y >">$XY$</span> is also equal to the solid <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R U W T >">$RUWT$</span> is equal to the<br />
parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< Y T >">$YT$</span><br />
for they are on the same base <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ R T /">$RT$</span> and in the same parallels<br />
<span data-ref="0341.g1:/ R T /">$RT$</span>, <span data-ref="0341.g1:/ Y W /">$YW$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 35]</span><br />
while <span data-ref="0341.g1:< R U W T >">$RUWT$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>, since it is also equal to <span data-ref="0341.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0341.g1:< Y T >">$YT$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0341.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0341.g1:< D T >">$DT$</span> is another parallelogram;</p>
