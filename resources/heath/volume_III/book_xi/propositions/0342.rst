.. assertion::

   | therefore the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is equal and similar to the
   | parallelogram :g:`$AM$ <0341.g1:< A M >>`.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | :g:`$LE$ <0341.g1:< L E >>` is also equal and similar to :g:`$SU$ <0341.g1:< S U >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` are equal and
   | similar to three parallelograms of the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But the former three are equal and similar to the three
   | opposite, and the latter three to the three opposite; :just:`[XI. 24] <XI.24>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` is equal to the
   | whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>`. :just:`[XI. Def. 10] <XI.def.10>`

| Let :g:`$DR$ <0341.g1:/ D R />`, :g:`$WU$ <0341.g1:/ W U />` be drawn through and meet one another
| at :g:`$Y$ <0341.g1:Y>`,
| let :g:`$aTb$ <0341.g1:/ a T b />` be drawn through :g:`$T$ <0341.g1:T>` parallel to :g:`$DY$ <0341.g1:/ D Y />`,
| let :g:`$PD$ <0341.g1:/ P D />` be produced to :g:`$a$ <0341.g1:a>`,
| and let the solids :g:`$YX$ <0341.g1:< Y X >>`, :g:`$RI$ <0341.g1:< R I >>` be completed.

.. assertion::

   | Then the solid :g:`$XY$ <0341.g1:< X Y >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the
   | base and :g:`$Yc$ <0341.g1:< Y c >>` its opposite, is equal to the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` of which
   | the parallelogram :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`$UV$ <0341.g1:< U V >>` its opposite,
   | for they are on the same base :g:`$RX$ <0341.g1:< R X >>` and of the same height, and
   | the extremities of their sides which stand up, namely :g:`$RY$ <0341.g1:/ R Y />`, :g:`$RU$ <0341.g1:/ R U />`,
   | :g:`$Tb$ <0341.g1:/ T b />`, :g:`$TW$ <0341.g1:/ T W />`, :g:`$Se$ <0341.g1:/ S e />`, :g:`$Sd$ <0341.g1:/ S d />`, :g:`$Xc$ <0341.g1:/ X c />`, :g:`$XV$ <0341.g1:/ X V />`, are on the same straight lines
   | :g:`$YW$ <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :g:`$eV$ <0341.g1:/ e V />`. :just:`[XI. 29] <XI.29>`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`$XU$ <0341.g1:< X U >>` is equal to :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>` :

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`$XY$ <0341.g1:< X Y >>` is also equal to the solid :g:`$AE$ <0341.g1:< A E >>`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the parallelogram :g:`$RUWT$ <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is equal to the
   | parallelogram :g:`$YT$ <0341.g1:< Y T >>`
   | for they are on the same base :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />` and in the same parallels
   | :g:`$RT$ <0341.g1:/ R T />`, :g:`$YW$ <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :just:`[I. 35] <I.35>`
   | while :g:`$RUWT$ <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is equal to :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`, since it is also equal to :g:`$AB$ <0341.g1:< A B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$YT$ <0341.g1:< Y T >>` is also equal to :g:`$CD$ <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$DT$ <0341.g1:< D T >>` is another parallelogram;

.. subclause::

   | And, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$CI$ <0341.g1:< C I >>` has been cut by

   | solid :g:`$RI$ <0341.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[XI. 25] <XI.25>`
