.. subclause::

   | For the same reason,
   | since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$YI$ <0343.g1:< Y I >>` has been cut by the plane
   | :g:`$RX$ <0343.g1:< R X >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   | as the base :g:`$YT$ <0343.g1:< Y T >>` is to the base :g:`$TD$ <0343.g1:< T D >>`, so is the solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to the
   | solid :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[XI. 25] <XI.25>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`$CD$ <0343.g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`$DT$ <0343.g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`$YT$ <0343.g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`$DT$ <0343.g1:< D T >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>` is to the solid :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`, so is the
   | solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore each of the solids :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`, :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` has to :g:`$RI$ <0343.g1:< R I >>` the same
   | ratio;
   | therefore the solid :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>` is equal to the solid :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>`. :just:`[V. 9] <V.9>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$YX$ <0343.g1:< Y X >>` was proved equal to :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AE$ <0343.g1:< A E >>` is also equal to :g:`$CF$ <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

| Next, let the sides standing up, :g:`$AG$ <0343.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$HK$ <0343.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$BE$ <0343.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`$LM$ <0343.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$CN$ <0343.g1:/ C N />`,
