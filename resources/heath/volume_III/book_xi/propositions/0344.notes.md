# Transcription Notes: Folio 0344 (Page 340)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0344 contains page 340 of Volume III. The top concludes Proposition 31 with Q.E.D. The remainder is Heath's extensive commentary summarizing the ingenious structure of the proof (connecting it with I. 44, VI. 14, and VI. 23) and discussing Robert Simson's critiques regarding equiangular bases and parallelepipedal verification. The commentary continues onto page 341 (folio 0345).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Manuscript Reference**: Heath notes that the diagram follows manuscript P (Codex Vaticanus Graecus 190), where bases lie in the plane of the paper and the third dimension stands up.
- **Mathematical Structure**: Heath provides a clear algebraic / symbolic breakdown of Part I and Part II.
- **Simson's Additions**: Discussion of Simson prefixing an equiangular case to Part I.
- **Parallelogram Symbol**: Printed as an open rectangle/square $\Box$ preceding letter pairs $CD, DT, YT, UT, AB$.
