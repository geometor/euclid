### PROPOSITION 33.

<p class="enunciation">Similar parallelepipedal solids are to one another in the<br />
triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0346.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0346.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> be similar parallelepipedal solids,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be the side corresponding to <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;<br />
I say that the solid <span data-ref="0346.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> has to the solid <span data-ref="0346.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> the ratio triplicate<br />
of that which <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> has to <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.
