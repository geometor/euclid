### PROPOSITION 33.

*Similar parallelepipedal solids are to one another in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.*

Let <span data-ref="0346.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0346.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> be similar parallelepipedal solids,
and let <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> be the side corresponding to <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>;
I say that the solid <span data-ref="0346.g1:< A B >">$AB$</span> has to the solid <span data-ref="0346.g1:< C D >">$CD$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> has to <span data-ref="0346.g1:/ C F /">$CF$</span>.
