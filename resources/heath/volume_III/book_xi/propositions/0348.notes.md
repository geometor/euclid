# Transcription Notes: Folio 0348 (Page 344)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page & Folio**: Folio 0348 corresponds to Page 344 of Volume III, Book XI.
- **Content**: 
  - Concluding lines of the proof of Book XI, Proposition 33 ("Similar parallelepipedal solids are to one another in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides").
  - "Therefore etc." and conclusion formula `Q. E. D.`.
  - **Porism**: On four continuous proportionals and the triplicate ratio of similar parallelepipedal solids.
  - Heath's commentary summarizing the synthetic structure of Euclid's proof using modern fractional/ratio notation.
- **Continuity**: Continues directly from folio 0347 (Page 343), where the construction and previous steps were set out, and transitions on folio 0349 to Simson's Proposition D.

## Textual & Lexical Observations
- In the Porism, the word `<continuously>` appears enclosed in angle brackets: Heath uses angle brackets to indicate editorial or manuscript clarification of the Greek text (translating continuous proportionals, *κατὰ τὸ συνεχές*).
- Square symbols ($\square$) are used systematically in the commentary to denote parallelograms ($\square AG$, $\square GK$, etc.).

## Mathematical & Editorial Analysis
- Heath summarizes the triple proportion that establishes the triplicate ratio:
  $$(\text{solid } AB) : (\text{solid } EO) = (\text{solid } EO) : (\text{solid } QL) = (\text{solid } QL) : (\text{solid } KP)$$
  showing how each step links a ratio of edges to a ratio of parallelograms (by VI. 1) and thence to a ratio of solids (by XI. 25 or 32).
- Editorial note: Heath notes that Heiberg doubts the authenticity of the Porism appended to Proposition 33.
