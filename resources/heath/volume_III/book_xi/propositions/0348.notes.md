## Mathematical & Editorial Analysis
- Heath summarizes the triple proportion that establishes the triplicate ratio:
  $$(\text{solid } AB) : (\text{solid } EO) = (\text{solid } EO) : (\text{solid } QL) = (\text{solid } QL) : (\text{solid } KP)$$
  showing how each step links a ratio of edges to a ratio of parallelograms (by VI. 1) and thence to a ratio of solids (by XI. 25 or 32).
- Editorial note: Heath notes that Heiberg doubts the authenticity of the Porism appended to Proposition 33.
