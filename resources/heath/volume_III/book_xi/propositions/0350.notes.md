## Mathematical & Editorial Analysis
- Euclid's method: To compare two solids $AB$ and $CD$ of unequal heights and bases, he constructs an intermediate solid $VC$ having the base of $CD$ ($NQ$) and the height of $AB$ ($CT = AG$).
- He then uses:
  - $(AB : CV) = (EH : NQ)$ because they have equal height (XI. 32).
  - $(CD : CV) = (MQ : TQ) = CM : CT$ because they are on the same base / parallelepipeds on the same base cut by planes parallel to the opposite planes (XI. 25, VI. 1).
  - Since $AB = CD$, $(AB : CV) = (CD : CV)$ by V. 7, hence $EH : NQ = MC : CT = MC : AG$.
