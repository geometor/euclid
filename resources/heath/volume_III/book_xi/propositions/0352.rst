.. assertion:: [V. 9] <V.9>

   Therefore the solid :g:`$AB$ <0352.g1:< A B >>` is equal to the solid :g:`$CD$ <0352.g1:< C D >>`.

| Now let the sides which stand up, :g:`$FE$ <0352.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`$BL$ <0352.g1:/ B L />`, :g:`$GA$ <0352.g1:/ G A />`, :g:`$HK$ <0352.g1:/ H K />`,
| :g:`$ON$ <0352.g1:/ O N />`, :g:`$DP$ <0352.g1:/ D P />`, :g:`$MC$ <0352.g1:/ M C />`, :g:`$RQ$ <0352.g1:/ R Q />`, not be at right angles to their bases;
| let perpendiculars be drawn from the points :g:`$F$ <0352.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0352.g1:G>`, :g:`$B$ <0352.g1:B>`, :g:`$K$ <0352.g1:K>`, :g:`$O$ <0352.g1:O>`,
| :g:`$M$ <0352.g1:M>`, :g:`$D$ <0352.g1:D>`, :g:`$R$ <0352.g1:R>` to the planes through :g:`$EH$ <0352.g1:< E H >>`, :g:`$NQ$ <0352.g1:< N Q >>`, and let them meet
| the planes at :g:`$S$ <0352.g1:S>`, :g:`$T$ <0352.g1:T>`, :g:`$U$ <0352.g1:U>`, :g:`$V$ <0352.g1:V>`, :g:`$W$ <0352.g1:W>`, :g:`$X$ <0352.g1:X>`, :g:`$Y$ <0352.g1:Y>`, :g:`$a$ <0352.g1:a>`,
| and let the solids :g:`$FV$ <0352.g1:< F V >>`, :g:`$Oa$ <0352.g1:< O a >>` be completed;
| I say that, in this case too, if the solids :g:`$AB$ <0352.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`$CD$ <0352.g1:< C D >>` are equal,
| the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights, that is,
| as the base :g:`$EH$ <0352.g1:< E H >>` is to the base :g:`$NQ$ <0352.g1:< N Q >>`, so is the height of the
| solid :g:`$CD$ <0352.g1:< C D >>` to the height of the solid :g:`$AB$ <0352.g1:< A B >>`.
