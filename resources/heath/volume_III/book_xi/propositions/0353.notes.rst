Mathematical & Editorial Analysis
---------------------------------

- Converse for oblique parallelepipeds:

  - Bases reciprocally proportional to heights $\implies FK : OR = \text{height } DX : \text{height } BT$.
  - By Part 1, right solid $BT =$ right solid $DX$.
  - By XI. 29, 30, $BT = BA$ and $DX = DC$.
  - Hence $AB = CD$.

- Heath's commentary identifies:

  1. If two equal parallelepipeds have equal bases, their heights are equal.
  2. If the bases are unequal, the lesser base has the greater height.
  - Heath begins discussing how ancient and modern editors (including Simson and Heiberg) handled the justification of these two lemmas.
