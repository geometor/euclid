### PROPOSITION 35.

*If there be two equal plane angles, and on their vertices there be set up elevated straight lines containing equal angles with the original straight lines respectively, if on the elevated straight lines points be taken at random and perpendiculars be drawn from them to the planes in which the original angles are, and if from the points so arising in the planes straight lines be joined to the vertices of the original angles, they will contain, with the elevated straight lines, equal angles.*

Let the angles <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span> be two equal rectilineal angles, and from the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span> let the elevated straight lines <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> be set up containing, with the original straight lines, equal angles respectively, namely, the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D E )">$MDE$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A B )">$GAB$</span> and the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F )">$MDF$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A C )">$GAC$</span>, let points <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:M">$M$</span> be taken at random on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ G L /">$GL$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> be drawn from the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:M">$M$</span> perpendicular to the planes through <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, and let them meet the planes at <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:N">$N$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ L A /">$LA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N D /">$ND$</span> be joined;
I say that the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A L )">$GAL$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D N )">$MDN$</span>.
