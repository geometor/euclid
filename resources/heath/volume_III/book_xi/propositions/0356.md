### PROPOSITION 35.

<p class="enunciation">If there be two equal plane angles, and on their vertices<br />
there be set up elevated straight lines containing equal angles<br />
with the original straight lines respectively, if on the elevated<br />
straight lines points be taken at random and perpendiculars be<br />
drawn from them to the planes in which the original angles<br />
are, and if from the points so arising in the planes straight<br />
lines be joined to the vertices of the original angles, they will<br />
contain, with the elevated straight lines, equal angles.</p>

Let the angles <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E D F )">$EDF$</span> be two equal rectilineal angles,<br />
and from the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:D">$D$</span> let the elevated straight lines <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span> be set up containing, with the original straight lines,<br />
equal angles respectively, namely, the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D E )">$MDE$</span> to the<br />
angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A B )">$GAB$</span> and the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F )">$MDF$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A C )">$GAC$</span>,<br />
let points <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:M">$M$</span> be taken at random on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ G L /">$GL$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> be drawn from the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:M">$M$</span> perpendicular to<br />
the planes through <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> and <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, and let them meet<br />
the planes at <span data-ref="0356.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ L A /">$LA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N D /">$ND$</span> be joined ;<br />
I say that the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< G A L )">$GAL$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D N )">$MDN$</span>.

Let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be drawn through the point <span data-ref="0356.g1:H">$H$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ G L /">$GL$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ G L /">$GL$</span> is perpendicular to the plane through <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is also perpendicular to the plane through<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 8]</span></p>

From the points <span data-ref="0356.g1:K">$K$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:N">$N$</span> let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N F /">$NF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N E /">$NE$</span> be drawn<br />
perpendicular to the straight lines <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> be joined.
