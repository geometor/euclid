PROPOSITION 35.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be two equal plane angles, and on their vertices there be set up elevated straight lines containing equal angles with the original straight lines respectively, if on the elevated straight lines points be taken at random and perpendiculars be drawn from them to the planes in which the original angles are, and if from the points so arising in the planes straight lines be joined to the vertices of the original angles, they will contain, with the elevated straight lines, equal angles.

Let the angles :g:`$BAC$ <0356.g1:< B A C )>`, :g:`$EDF$ <0356.g1:< E D F )>` be two equal rectilineal angles, and from the points :g:`$A$ <0356.g1:A>`, :g:`$D$ <0356.g1:D>` let the elevated straight lines :g:`$AG$ <0356.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$DM$ <0356.g1:/ D M />` be set up containing, with the original straight lines, equal angles respectively, namely, the angle :g:`$MDE$ <0356.g1:< M D E )>` to the angle :g:`$GAB$ <0356.g1:< G A B )>` and the angle :g:`$MDF$ <0356.g1:< M D F )>` to the angle :g:`$GAC$ <0356.g1:< G A C )>`, let points :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0356.g1:M>` be taken at random on :g:`$AG$ <0356.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`$DM$ <0356.g1:/ D M />`, let :g:`$GL$ <0356.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`$MN$ <0356.g1:/ M N />` be drawn from the points :g:`$G$ <0356.g1:G>`, :g:`$M$ <0356.g1:M>` perpendicular to the planes through :g:`$BA$ <0356.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />` and :g:`$ED$ <0356.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`$DF$ <0356.g1:/ D F />`, and let them meet the planes at :g:`$L$ <0356.g1:L>`, :g:`$N$ <0356.g1:N>`, and let :g:`$LA$ <0356.g1:/ L A />`, :g:`$ND$ <0356.g1:/ N D />` be joined ; I say that the angle :g:`$GAL$ <0356.g1:< G A L )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$MDN$ <0356.g1:< M D N )>`.

   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 35

Let :g:`$AH$ <0356.g1:/ A H />` be made equal to :g:`$DM$ <0356.g1:/ D M />`, and let :g:`$HK$ <0356.g1:/ H K />` be drawn through the point :g:`$H$ <0356.g1:H>` parallel to :g:`$GL$ <0356.g1:/ G L />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$GL$ <0356.g1:/ G L />` is perpendicular to the plane through :g:`$BA$ <0356.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$HK$ <0356.g1:/ H K />` is also perpendicular to the plane through :g:`$BA$ <0356.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />`. :just:`[XI. 8] <XI.8>`

From the points :g:`$K$ <0356.g1:K>`, :g:`$N$ <0356.g1:N>` let :g:`$KC$ <0356.g1:/ K C />`, :g:`$NF$ <0356.g1:/ N F />`, :g:`$KB$ <0356.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`$NE$ <0356.g1:/ N E />` be drawn perpendicular to the straight lines :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$DF$ <0356.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`$AB$ <0356.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$DE$ <0356.g1:/ D E />`, and let :g:`$HC$ <0356.g1:/ H C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0356.g1:/ C B />`, :g:`$MF$ <0356.g1:/ M F />`, :g:`$FE$ <0356.g1:/ F E />` be joined.
