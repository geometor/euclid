<p class="sub-clause">Since the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>,<br />
and the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is also equal to the squares on<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span> is equal to the squares on<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is equal to the squares on<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H C A )">$HCA$</span> is right. <span class="justification">[I. 48]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F M )">$DFM$</span> is also right.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C H )">$ACH$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F M )">$DFM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A C )">$HAC$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F )">$MDF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F >">$MDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A C >">$HAC$</span> are two triangles which have two<br />
angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to<br />
one side, namely, that subtending one of the equal angles,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>;<br />
therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the<br />
remaining sides respectively. <span class="justification">[I. 26]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Since then <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>,<br />
the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< C A B )">$CAB$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< F D E )">$FDE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, the triangle to<br />
the triangle, and the remaining angles to the remaining<br />
angles; <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span><br />
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C B )">$ACB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F E )">$DFE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the right angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C K )">$ACK$</span> is also equal to the right angle<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F N )">$DFN$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B C K )">$BCK$</span> is also equal to the<br />
remaining angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E F N )">$EFN$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< C B K )">$CBK$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< F E N )">$FEN$</span>.</p>
