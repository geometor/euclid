Since the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, and the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, [I. 47] therefore the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is also equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K C /">$KC$</span>; [I. 47] therefore the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>.

Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H C A )">$HCA$</span> is right. [I. 48]

For the same reason the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F M )">$DFM$</span> is also right.

Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C H )">$ACH$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F M )">$DFM$</span>.

But the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A C )">$HAC$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F )">$MDF$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D F >">$MDF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A C >">$HAC$</span> are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely, that subtending one of the equal angles, that is, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>; therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively. [I. 26]

Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>.

Similarly we can prove that <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

Since then <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.

But the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< C A B )">$CAB$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< F D E )">$FDE$</span>; therefore the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, the triangle to the triangle, and the remaining angles to the remaining angles; [I. 4] therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C B )">$ACB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F E )">$DFE$</span>.

But the right angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A C K )">$ACK$</span> is also equal to the right angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D F N )">$DFN$</span>; therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B C K )">$BCK$</span> is also equal to the remaining angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E F N )">$EFN$</span>.

For the same reason the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< C B K )">$CBK$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< F E N )">$FEN$</span>.
