## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Chained Pythagorean Argument**:
  1. $HA^2 = HK^2 + KA^2 = HK^2 + KC^2 + CA^2$ by I. 47.
  2. $HC^2 = HK^2 + KC^2$ by I. 47.
  3. Consequently $HA^2 = HC^2 + CA^2$, whence $\angle HCA = 90^\circ$ by I. 48.
- **Sequence of Congruence Deductions**:
  - Triangles $HAC$ and $MDF$ are congruent by I. 26 (AAS: right angles at $C$ and $F$, equal given angles $\angle HAC = \angle MDF$, and equal opposite sides $HA = MD$), giving $AC = DF$.
  - Similarly $AB = DE$.
  - Base triangles $CAB$ and $FDE$ are congruent by I. 4 (SAS), giving base $BC = EF$ and base angle $\angle ACB = \angle DFE$.
  - Angle subtraction: $\angle ACB - \angle ACK = \angle DFE - \angle DFN \implies \angle BCK = \angle EFN$.
