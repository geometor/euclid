:title: Proposition 35 (continued)

.. subclause::

   Since the square on :g:`$HA$ <0356.g1:/ H A />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$HK$ <0356.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KA$ <0356.g1:/ K A />`, and the squares on :g:`$KC$ <0356.g1:/ K C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0356.g1:/ C A />` are equal to the square on :g:`$KA$ <0356.g1:/ K A />`, :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$HA$ <0356.g1:/ H A />` is also equal to the squares on :g:`$HK$ <0356.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KC$ <0356.g1:/ K C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0356.g1:/ C A />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$HC$ <0356.g1:/ H C />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$HK$ <0356.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`$KC$ <0356.g1:/ K C />`; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$HA$ <0356.g1:/ H A />` is equal to the squares on :g:`$HC$ <0356.g1:/ H C />`, :g:`$CA$ <0356.g1:/ C A />`.

.. assertion:: [I. 48] <I.48>

   Therefore the angle :g:`$HCA$ <0356.g1:< H C A )>` is right.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`$DFM$ <0356.g1:< D F M )>` is also right.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$ACH$ <0356.g1:< A C H )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DFM$ <0356.g1:< D F M )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$HAC$ <0356.g1:< H A C )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$MDF$ <0356.g1:< M D F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$MDF$ <0356.g1:< M D F >>`, :g:`$HAC$ <0356.g1:< H A C >>` are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely, that subtending one of the equal angles, that is, :g:`$HA$ <0356.g1:/ H A />` equal to :g:`$MD$ <0356.g1:/ M D />`; therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides respectively. :just:`[I. 26] <I.26>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$DF$ <0356.g1:/ D F />`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$AB$ <0356.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to :g:`$DE$ <0356.g1:/ D E />`.

.. assertion::

   Since then :g:`$AC$ <0356.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$DF$ <0356.g1:/ D F />`, and :g:`$AB$ <0356.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$DE$ <0356.g1:/ D E />`, the two sides :g:`$CA$ <0356.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0356.g1:/ A B />` are equal to the two sides :g:`$FD$ <0356.g1:/ F D />`, :g:`$DE$ <0356.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$CAB$ <0356.g1:< C A B )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FDE$ <0356.g1:< F D E )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$BC$ <0356.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the base :g:`$EF$ <0356.g1:/ E F />`, the triangle to the triangle, and the remaining angles to the remaining angles; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>` therefore the angle :g:`$ACB$ <0356.g1:< A C B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$DFE$ <0356.g1:< D F E )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the right angle :g:`$ACK$ <0356.g1:< A C K )>` is also equal to the right angle :g:`$DFN$ <0356.g1:< D F N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$BCK$ <0356.g1:< B C K )>` is also equal to the remaining angle :g:`$EFN$ <0356.g1:< E F N )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`$CBK$ <0356.g1:< C B K )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FEN$ <0356.g1:< F E N )>`.
