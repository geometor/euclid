Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B C K >">$BCK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E F N >">$EFN$</span> are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely, that adjacent to the equal angles, that is, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>; therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides. [I. 26]

Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F N /">$FN$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>; therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F N /">$FN$</span>; and they contain right angles.

Therefore the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>. [I. 4]

And, since <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.

But the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, for the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A K H )">$AKH$</span> is right; [I. 47] and the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>, for the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D N M )">$DNM$</span> is right; [I. 47] therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>; and of these the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>; therefore the remaining square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.

And, since the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> are equal to the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span> respectively, and the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> was proved equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A K )">$HAK$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D N )">$MDN$</span>. [I. 8]

Therefore etc.

**PORISM.** *From this it is manifest that, if there be two equal plane angles, and if there be set up on them elevated straight lines which are equal and contain equal angles with the original straight lines respectively, the perpendiculars drawn from their extremities to the planes in which are the original angles are equal to one another.*
