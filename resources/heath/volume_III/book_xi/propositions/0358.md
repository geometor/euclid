<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:< B C K >">$BCK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:< E F N >">$EFN$</span> are two triangles which have two<br />
angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to<br />
one side, namely, that adjacent to the equal angles, that is,<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;<br />
therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the<br />
remaining sides. <span class="justification">[I. 26]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F N /">$FN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> are equal to the two sides<br />
<span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D F /">$DF$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ F N /">$FN$</span>;<br />
and they contain right angles.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>,<br />
for the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< A K H )">$AKH$</span> is right; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span><br />
and the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span> are equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D M /">$DM$</span>,<br />
for the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< D N M )">$DNM$</span> is right; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are equal to the squares<br />
on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>;<br />
and of these the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>;<br />
therefore the remaining square on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ N M /">$NM$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the two sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H A /">$HA$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M D /">$MD$</span>, <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span> respectively,<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> was proved equal to the base <span data-ref="0356.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< H A K )">$HAK$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0356.g1:< M D N )">$MDN$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore etc.</p>

<p class="qed">**PORISM.** From this it is manifest that, if there be two<br />
equal plane angles, and if there be set up on them elevated<br />
straight lines which are equal and contain equal angles with<br />
the original straight lines respectively, the perpendiculars<br />
drawn from their extremities to the planes in which are<br />
the original angles are equal to one another.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
