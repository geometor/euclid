## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Purpose of Proposition 35**: Heath explains that this proposition establishes that in equiangular parallelepipeds, equal corresponding lateral edges determine equal heights.
- **Modern Restatement**: Heath notes that using the concept of inclination of a straight line to a plane, the proposition simply asserts: "If there be two trihedral angles identically equal to one another, corresponding edges in each are equally inclined to the planes through the other two edges respectively."
- **Summary Notation**: Heath adopts concise symbolic notations ($\Delta$ for triangle, $\equiv$ for congruent, $\angle$ for angle, exponents for squares):
  - (1) Pythagorean chain yielding $\angle HCA = 90^\circ$ and $\angle MFD = 90^\circ$ by I. 47 and I. 48.
  - (2) Congruence $\Delta HAC \equiv \Delta MDF$ via I. 26 giving $AC = DF$.
  - (3) Congruence $\Delta HAB \equiv \Delta MDE$ giving $AB = DE$.
  - (4) Congruence $\Delta ABC \equiv \Delta DEF$ via I. 4 giving $BC = EF$ and base angle equalities.
  - (5) Equality of complementary angles $\angle KBC = \angle NEF$ and $\angle KCB = \angle NFE$.
  - (6) Congruence $\Delta KBC \equiv \Delta NEF$ giving $KB = NE$ and $KC = NF$.
  - (7) Congruence of right triangles $KAC, NDF$ giving $AK = DN$.
  - (8) Equating hypotenuse squares $HK^2 + KA^2 = MN^2 + ND^2$.
