## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Commentary on Proposition 35**:
  - Step (9): SSS (I. 8) and SAS (I. 4) confirm $\angle HAK = \angle MDN$.
  - Legendre's Theorem: Proves that in equal trihedral angles, dihedral angles between corresponding faces are equal ($\angle HCK = \angle MFN$), using XI. Def. 6 and congruence of right triangles $HCK, MFN$.
  - Simson's Alternative Proof: Simson uses plane perpendicularity (XI. 18, XI. Def. 4, XI. Def. 3) to show $\angle ABH = 90^\circ$. Heath notes that Euclid's original metric proof using I. 47 is superior because Simson relies upon definitions that are effectively unproven theorems.
- **Proposition 36**:
  - Enunciation: If three lines are in continued proportion ($A : B = B : C$), the parallelepiped formed by $A, B, C$ is equal in volume to an equilateral parallelepiped whose edges all equal $B$ (the mean proportional) and which is equiangular to the first.
  - Setting out: Begins setting out solid angle at $E$ contained by $\angle DEG, \angle GEF, \angle FED$, cutting off mid-sentence at page bottom.
