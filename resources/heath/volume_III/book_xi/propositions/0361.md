<p class="sub-clause">Now, since, as <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ A /">$A$</span> is to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ B /">$B$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ B /">$B$</span> to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ C /">$C$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ A /">$A$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ B /">$B$</span> to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ L O /">$LO$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0361.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, and <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ C /">$C$</span> to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span> to <span data-ref="0361.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Thus the sides about the equal angles <span data-ref="0361.g1:< N L M )">$NLM$</span>, <span data-ref="0361.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span> are<br />
reciprocally proportional;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0361.g1:< M N >">$MN$</span> is equal to the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0361.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 14]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the angles <span data-ref="0361.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0361.g1:< N L M )">$NLM$</span> are two plane recti-<br />

are set up which are equal to one another and contain equal<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore the perpendiculars drawn from the points <span data-ref="0361.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0361.g1:O">$O$</span> to<br />

<p class="sub-clause">But parallelepipedal solids on equal bases and of the same<br />
