.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelepipedal solid formed out of :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` is equal to the solid on :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />` which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.

----

The edges of the parallelepiped :g:`$HL$ <0361.g1:< H L >>` being respectively equal to :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />`, and those of the equiangular parallelepiped :g:`$KE$ <0361.g1:< K E >>` being all equal to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, we regard :g:`$MN$ <0361.g1:< M N >>` (*not* containing the edge :g:`$OL$ <0361.g1:/ O L />` :eq:`equal <0361.g1:/ O L / = 0361.g1:/ B />` to :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`) as the base of the first parallelepiped, and consequently :g:`$FD$ <0361.g1:< F D >>`, equiangular to :g:`$MN$ <0361.g1:< M N >>`, as the base of :g:`$KE$ <0361.g1:< K E >>`.

Then the solids have the same height. [XI. 35, Por.]

Hence

$$\text{(solid } HL\text{)} : \text{(solid } KE\text{)} = \mathbin{▱} MN : \mathbin{▱} FD. \qquad \text{[XI. 32]}$$

But, since :g:`$A$ <0361.g1:/ A />`, :g:`$B$ <0361.g1:/ B />`, :g:`$C$ <0361.g1:/ C />` are in continued proportion,

$$A : B = B : C,$$

or

$$LM : EF = DE : LN.$$

Thus the sides of the equiangular ▱s :g:`$MN$ <0361.g1:< M N >>`, :g:`$FD$ <0361.g1:< F D >>` are reciprocally proportional, whence

$$\mathbin{▱} MN = \mathbin{▱} FD, \qquad \text{[VI. 14]}$$

and therefore

$$\text{(solid } HL\text{)} = \text{(solid } KE\text{)}.$$

----

PROPOSITION 37.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described will also be proportional; and, if the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described be proportional, the straight lines will themselves also be proportional.

Let :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />` be four straight lines in proportion, so that, as :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />`; and let there be described on :g:`$AB$ <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$EF$ <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$GH$ <0362.g1:/ G H />` the similar and similarly situated parallelepipedal solids :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>`, :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>`, :g:`$ME$ <0362.g1:< M E >>`, :g:`$NG$ <0362.g1:< N G >>`; I say that, as :g:`$KA$ <0362.g1:< K A >>` is to :g:`$LC$ <0362.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`$ME$ <0362.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`$NG$ <0362.g1:< N G >>`.
