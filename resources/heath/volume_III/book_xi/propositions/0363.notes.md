# Transcription Notes: Folio 0363 (Page 359)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 359 contains the direct and converse proof conclusions of **Proposition 37**, followed by Heath's detailed commentary discussing the tacit assumption regarding equality of triplicate ratios, and introducing Robert Simson's rigorous alternative proof (from manuscript **b** and Clavius).
- **Antecedent Continuity**: Carries forward from Folio 0362 (Page 358) where the direct proof began with XI. 33.
- **Subsequent Continuity**: Part II (the converse) of Simson's alternative proof continues on Folio 0364 (Page 360).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Running Head**: `XI. 37]` on the left margin, `PROPOSITIONS 36, 37` centered, and page number `359` on the right.
- **Scholarly Critique**:
  - Heath notes that Euclid tacitly assumes: (1) $A : B = C : D \implies A^3 : B^3 = C^3 : D^3$, and (2) $A^3 : B^3 = C^3 : D^3 \implies A : B = C : D$.
  - Simson's alternative proof avoids this by taking continuous proportionals: $AB, CD, O, P$ and $EF, GH, Q, R$, so that $AB : P = AB^3 : CD^3$ and $EF : R = EF^3 : GH^3$.
  - By V. 22 (*ex aequali*), $AB : P = EF : R$.
