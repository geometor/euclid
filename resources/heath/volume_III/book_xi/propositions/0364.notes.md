# Transcription Notes: Folio 0364 (Page 360)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 360 completes Part II of Simson's alternative proof for Proposition 37, examines an interpolated lemma found in certain manuscripts between Propositions 37 and 38, and begins **Proposition 38** (enunciation and initial setting-out).
- **Antecedent Continuity**: Directly continues Simson's alternative proof from Folio 0363 (Page 359).
- **Subsequent Continuity**: Proposition 38's setting-out continues on Folio 0365 (Page 361) with the plane specifications (*"and through the points of section let the planes KN, OR be carried..."*).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Running Head**: Page number `360` on the left, `BOOK XI` centered, and `[XI. 37, 38` on the right.
- **Interpolated Lemma Analysis**:
  - Found in the manuscript tradition between XI. 37 and XI. 38, but omitted in manuscript **b** and flagged by the scribe of **P**.
  - Serves as an unneeded lemma for XII. 17.
  - Proved false by *reductio ad absurdum*: assuming the perpendicular $EF$ does not fall on the intersection $AD$ yields a triangle $EGF$ with two right angles ($\angle EGF$ and $\angle EFG$), which is impossible.
- **Proposition 38**:
  - Enunciation: Bisecting sides of opposite planes of a cube produces planes whose common section and the cube's diameter bisect each other.
  - Important foundation for Book XIII (regular polyhedra, specifically XIII. 17).
