and through the points of section let the planes <span data-ref="0365.g1:< K N >">$KN$</span>, <span data-ref="0365.g1:< O R >">$OR$</span> be
carried;
let <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ U S /">$US$</span> be the common section of the planes, and <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D G /">$DG$</span> the
diameter of the cube <span data-ref="0365.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span>.

I say that <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ U T /">$UT$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ T S /">$TS$</span>, and <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D T /">$DT$</span> to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ T G /">$TG$</span>.

For let <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D U /">$DU$</span>, <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ U E /">$UE$</span>, <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ B S /">$BS$</span>, <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ S G /">$SG$</span> be joined.

Then, since <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D O /">$DO$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>,
the alternate angles <span data-ref="0365.g1:< D O U )">$DOU$</span>, <span data-ref="0365.g1:< U P E )">$UPE$</span> are equal to one another. [I. 29]

And, since <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D O /">$DO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ O U /">$OU$</span> to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ U P /">$UP$</span>,
and they contain equal angles,
therefore the base <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D U /">$DU$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ U E /">$UE$</span>,
the triangle <span data-ref="0365.g1:< D O U >">$DOU$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0365.g1:< P U E >">$PUE$</span>,
and the remaining angles are equal to the remaining angles; [I. 4]
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0365.g1:< O U D )">$OUD$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0365.g1:< P U E )">$PUE$</span>.

For this reason <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ D U E /">$DUE$</span> is a straight line. [I. 14]

For the same reason, <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ B S G /">$BSG$</span> is also a straight line,
and <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ B S /">$BS$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0365.g1:/ S G /">$SG$</span>.
