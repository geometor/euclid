| and through the points of section let the planes :g:`$KN$ <0365.g1:< K N >>`, :g:`$OR$ <0365.g1:< O R >>` be
| carried;
| let :g:`$US$ <0365.g1:/ U S />` be the common section of the planes, and :g:`$DG$ <0365.g1:/ D G />` the
| diameter of the cube :g:`$AF$ <0365.g1:< A F >>`.

I say that :g:`$UT$ <0365.g1:/ U T />` is equal to :g:`$TS$ <0365.g1:/ T S />`, and :g:`$DT$ <0365.g1:/ D T />` to :g:`$TG$ <0365.g1:/ T G />`.

For let :g:`$DU$ <0365.g1:/ D U />`, :g:`$UE$ <0365.g1:/ U E />`, :g:`$BS$ <0365.g1:/ B S />`, :g:`$SG$ <0365.g1:/ S G />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`$DO$ <0365.g1:/ D O />` is parallel to :g:`$PE$ <0365.g1:/ P E />`,

.. assertion::

   | the alternate angles :g:`$DOU$ <0365.g1:< D O U )>`, :g:`$UPE$ <0365.g1:< U P E )>` are equal to one another.
   | :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>`

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$DO$ <0365.g1:/ D O />` is equal to :g:`$PE$ <0365.g1:/ P E />`, and :g:`$OU$ <0365.g1:/ O U />` to :g:`$UP$ <0365.g1:/ U P />`,
   | and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the base :g:`$DU$ <0365.g1:/ D U />` is equal to the base :g:`$UE$ <0365.g1:/ U E />`,
   | the triangle :g:`$DOU$ <0365.g1:< D O U >>` is equal to the triangle :g:`$PUE$ <0365.g1:< P U E >>`,
   | and the remaining angles are equal to the remaining angles; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`
   | therefore the angle :g:`$OUD$ <0365.g1:< O U D )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$PUE$ <0365.g1:< P U E )>`.
