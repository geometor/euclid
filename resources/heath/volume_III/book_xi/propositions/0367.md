### PROPOSITION 39.

<p class="enunciation">If there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a<br />
parallelogram as base and the other a triangle, and if the<br />
parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms will be<br />
equal.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span> be two prisms of equal<br />
height,<br />
let one have the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> as base, and the other the<br />
triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>,<br />
and let the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> be double of the triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span> ;<br />
I say that the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is equal to the prism<br />
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span>.

![Proposition 39 diagram](0367.g1.svg)

For let the solids <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>, <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span> be completed.

<p class="sub-clause">Since the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is double of the triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>,<br />
while the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< H K >">$HK$</span> is also double of the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 34]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is equal to the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< H K >">$HK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and<br />
of the same height are equal to one another ; <span class="justification">[XI. 31]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> is equal to the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is half of the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>,<br />
and the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span> is half of the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span> ; <span class="justification">[XI. 28]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is equal to the prism<br />
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore etc.<br />
<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
