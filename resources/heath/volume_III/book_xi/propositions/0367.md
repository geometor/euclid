### PROPOSITION 39.

*If there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a
parallelogram as base and the other a triangle, and if the
parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms will be
equal.*

Let <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span> be two prisms of equal
height,
let one have the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> as base, and the other the
triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>,
and let the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> be double of the triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>;
I say that the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is equal to the prism
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span>.

![Proposition 39 diagram](0367.g1.svg)

For let the solids <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>, <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span> be completed.

Since the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is double of the triangle <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>,
while the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< H K >">$HK$</span> is also double of the triangle
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K >">$GHK$</span>, [I. 34]
therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is equal to the parallelogram
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< H K >">$HK$</span>.

But parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and
of the same height are equal to one another; [XI. 31]
therefore the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> is equal to the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span>.

And the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is half of the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>,
and the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span> is half of the solid <span data-ref="0367.g1:< G P >">$GP$</span>; [XI. 28]
therefore the prism <span data-ref="0367.g1:< A B C D E F >">$ABCDEF$</span> is equal to the prism
<span data-ref="0367.g1:< G H K L M N >">$GHKLMN$</span>.

Therefore etc.

Q. E. D.
