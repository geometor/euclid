PROPOSITION 39.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a parallelogram as base and the other a triangle, and if the parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms will be equal.

Let :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0367.g1:< A B C D E F >>`, :g:`$GHKLMN$ <0367.g1:< G H K L M N >>` be two prisms of equal height, let one have the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0367.g1:< A F >>` as base, and the other the triangle :g:`$GHK$ <0367.g1:< G H K >>`, and let the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0367.g1:< A F >>` be double of the triangle :g:`$GHK$ <0367.g1:< G H K >>` ; I say that the prism :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0367.g1:< A B C D E F >>` is equal to the prism :g:`$GHKLMN$ <0367.g1:< G H K L M N >>`.

.. picture:: 0367.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 39 diagram

For let the solids :g:`$AO$ <0367.g1:< A O >>`, :g:`$GP$ <0367.g1:< G P >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Since the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0367.g1:< A F >>` is double of the triangle :g:`$GHK$ <0367.g1:< G H K >>`, while the parallelogram :g:`$HK$ <0367.g1:< H K >>` is also double of the triangle :g:`$GHK$ <0367.g1:< G H K >>`, :just:`[I. 34] <I.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`$AF$ <0367.g1:< A F >>` is equal to the parallelogram :g:`$HK$ <0367.g1:< H K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another ; :just:`[XI. 31] <XI.31>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`$AO$ <0367.g1:< A O >>` is equal to the solid :g:`$GP$ <0367.g1:< G P >>`.

.. subclause::

   And the prism :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0367.g1:< A B C D E F >>` is half of the solid :g:`$AO$ <0367.g1:< A O >>`, and the prism :g:`$GHKLMN$ <0367.g1:< G H K L M N >>` is half of the solid :g:`$GP$ <0367.g1:< G P >>` ; :just:`[XI. 28] <XI.28>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the prism :g:`$ABCDEF$ <0367.g1:< A B C D E F >>` is equal to the prism :g:`$GHKLMN$ <0367.g1:< G H K L M N >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
