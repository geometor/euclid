This proposition is made use of in XII. 3, 4. The phraseology is interest-<br />
ing because we find one of the *parallelogrammic* faces of one of the triangular<br />
prisms called its *base*, and the perpendicular on this plane from that vertex of<br />
either *triangular* face which is not in this plane the *height*.

The proof is simple because we have only to complete parallelepipeds<br />
which are double the prisms respectively and then use XI. 31. It has to be<br />
borne in mind, however, that, if the parallelepipeds are not rectangular, the<br />
proof in XI. 28 is not sufficient to establish the fact that the parallelepipeds<br />
are double of the prisms, but has to be supplemented as shown in the note on<br />
that proposition. XII. 4 does, however, require the theorem in its general<br />
form.
