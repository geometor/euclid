# Transcription Notes: Folio 0368 (Page 364)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 364 contains Heath's commentary on **Proposition 39**, representing the concluding page and official completion of **Book XI** of Euclid's *Elements*.
- **Antecedent Continuity**: Directly follows the Q.E.D. of Proposition 39 on Folio 0367 (Page 363).
- **Subsequent Continuity**: Concludes Book XI. Volume III continues with Book XII (The Method of Exhaustion: Circles, Pyramids, Cones, Cylinders, and Spheres).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Running Head**: Page number `364` on the left, `BOOK XI` centered, and `[XI. 39` on the right.
- **Commentary Insights**:
  - **Role in Book XII**: Proposition 39 is vital for XII. 3 and XII. 4 (pyramids of the same height with triangular bases are divided into two equal prisms and two similar pyramids).
  - **Terminological Nuance**: Heath highlights the unusual phraseology where one of the *lateral parallelogrammic faces* of a triangular prism is treated as its "base", and the perpendicular from the opposing lateral edge/vertex is defined as the "height".
  - **Critique of Proof Foundation**: Heath reiterates that if the parallelepipeds are oblique (non-rectangular), Euclid's proof in XI. 28 requires supplementation to prove that the diagonal plane truly halves the solid into two symmetric/equal prisms.
- **Layout & Blank Space**: The printed commentary occupies only the top third of the page; the lower two-thirds are blank, marking the conclusion of the book.
