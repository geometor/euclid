Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Book XII Opening & Historical Note**: Introduces Book XII and the *method of exhaustion*, identifying its systematic application across Propositions 2, 3–5, 10, 11, 12, and 16–18.
- **Attribution to Eudoxus**: Heath presents the principal ancient evidence attributing the method of exhaustion and key stereometric theorems to Eudoxus of Cnidus, citing two passages from Archimedes:

  1. *On the Sphere and Cylinder* I (preface): Eudoxus's proofs for the pyramid (one third of prism, XII. 7) and cone (one third of cylinder, XII. 10).
  2. *Quadrature of the Parabola* (preface): The use of the "Axiom of Archimedes" (Euclid X. 1) by "earlier geometers" to prove circle areas (XII. 2), sphere volumes (XII. 18), pyramids (XII. 7), and cones (XII. 10).
