Transcription Notes: Folio 0370 (Page 366)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Pre-Eudoxian Discoveries**: Heath examines earlier precursors to the results in Book XII:

  - (*a*) **Hippocrates of Chios** (fl. c. 430 B.C.): Simplicius (citing Eudemus) records that Hippocrates knew that similar segments of circles are as the squares on their bases, which he established by showing that circles are as the squares on their diameters (Euclid XII. 2). Heath emphasizes this predated the fully developed method of exhaustion.
  - (*b*) **Democritus and Archimedes'** *Method*: Heiberg's 1906 discovery of Archimedes' *Method* (Palimpsest) reveals that Democritus first stated that the pyramid and cone are one third of the corresponding prism and cylinder, though "without proving it" (χωρὶς ἀποδείξεως). Archimedes credits Democritus with the initial discovery, while Eudoxus provided the first rigorous proof.

- **Continuity**: Text begins as the continuation from folio 0369 ("any other proof than that given by Eudoxus...") and ends mid-sentence at bottom ("The essential"), carrying directly over into folio 0371 ("feature of Archimedes' mechanical arguments...").
