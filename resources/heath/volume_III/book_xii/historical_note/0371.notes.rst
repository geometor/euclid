Transcription Notes: Folio 0371 (Page 367)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Archimedes' Mechanical Method & Infinitesimals**: Heath explains Archimedes' technique of treating planar areas as the sum of parallel line-elements and solid volumes as sums of parallel plane-sections (infinitesimal strips $dx$ and laminae $dz$).
- **Parabolic Segment Quadrature by Lever**: Heath reproduces the demonstration from Archimedes' *Method*, Proposition 1:

  - Tangent $CE$, chord $CA$, diameter $EBD$ through midpoint $E$ with $EB = BD$.
  - Line $AF \parallel ED$, meeting tangent $CE$ produced at $F$.
  - Lever $CH$ with fulcrum $K$ (where $CH$ intersects $AF$) and $CK = KH$.
  - Ordinates $MO$ of triangle $CFA$ balance parabolic elements $PO$ suspended at $H$ around fulcrum $K$.
  - Result: $(\triangle CFA) : (\text{segment}) = HK : KX = 3 : 1$.

- **Continuity**: Text begins as the continuation of "The essential" from folio 0370 and ends at the bottom with formula "$= 3 : 1,$", leading into folio 0372 ("from which it follows that...").

Textual & Lexical Observations
------------------------------

- **Mathematical Typography**: Modern infinitesimal notation ($dx$, $dz$) used by Heath alongside classic Euclidean proportion notation ($CA : AO = MO : OP$, $HK : KN = MO : OP$).
