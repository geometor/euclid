- **Proposition 1 Statement**: Similar polygons inscribed in circles are to one another as the squares on the diameters of the circles.
- **Construction & Steps**:
  - Inscribed similar polygons $ABCDE$ and $FGHKL$ in circles $ABC$, $FGH$ with diameters $BM$, $GN$.
  - Joining $BE$, $AM$, $GL$, $FN$.
  - Similarity gives $\angle BAE = \angle GFL$ and $BA : AE = GF : FL$ (VI. Def. 1).
  - Triangles $ABE$ and $FGL$ are equiangular (VI. 6), so $\angle AEB = \angle FLG$.
