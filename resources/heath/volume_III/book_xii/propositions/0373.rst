BOOK XII. PROPOSITIONS.
=======================

PROPOSITION 1.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Similar polygons inscribed in circles are to one another as the squares on the diameters.

Let :g:`ABC <0373.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`FGH <0373.g1:( F G H )>` be circles, let :g:`ABCDE <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`FGHKL <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>` be similar polygons inscribed in them, and let :g:`BM <0373.g1:/ B M />`, :g:`GN <0373.g1:/ G N />` be diameters of the circles; I say that, as the square on :g:`BM <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to the square on :g:`GN <0373.g1:/ G N />`, so is the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`.

.. picture:: 0373.g1.svg
   :alt: Similar polygons inscribed in circles

For let :g:`BE <0373.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`AM <0373.g1:/ A M />`, :g:`GL <0373.g1:/ G L />`, :g:`FN <0373.g1:/ F N />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the polygon :g:`ABCDE <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` is similar to the polygon :g:`FGHKL <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`, the angle :g:`BAE <0373.g1:< B A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0373.g1:< B A E ) = 0373.g1:< G F L )>` to the angle :g:`GFL <0373.g1:< G F L )>`, and, as :g:`BA <0373.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`AE <0373.g1:/ A E />`, so is :g:`GF <0373.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`FL <0373.g1:/ F L />`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

Thus :g:`BAE <0373.g1:< B A E >>`, :g:`GFL <0373.g1:< G F L >>` are two triangles which have one angle :eq:`equal <0373.g1:< B A E ) = 0373.g1:< G F L )>` to one angle, namely the angle :g:`BAE <0373.g1:< B A E )>` to the angle :g:`GFL <0373.g1:< G F L )>`, and the sides about the equal angles proportional;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`ABE <0373.g1:< A B E >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`FGL <0373.g1:< F G L >>`. :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`AEB <0373.g1:< A E B )>` is :eq:`equal <0373.g1:< A E B ) = 0373.g1:< F L G )>` to the angle :g:`FLG <0373.g1:< F L G )>`.
