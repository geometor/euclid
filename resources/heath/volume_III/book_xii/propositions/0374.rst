.. subclause::

   | But the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0373.g1:< A E B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>`,
   | for they stand on the same circumference; :just:`[III. 27] <III.27>`
   | and the angle :g:`$FLG$ <0373.g1:< F L G )>` to the angle :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`.

.. subclause::

   | But the right angle :g:`$BAM$ <0373.g1:< B A M )>` is also equal to the right angle
   | :g:`$GFN$ <0373.g1:< G F N )>`; :just:`[III. 31] <III.31>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remaining angle is equal to the remaining angle.
   | :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the triangle :g:`$ABM$ <0373.g1:< A B M >>` is equiangular with the
   | triangle :g:`$FGN$ <0373.g1:< F G N >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />`
   | to :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. subclause::

   | But the ratio of the square on :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` to the square on :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`
   | is duplicate of the ratio of :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` to :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`,
   | and the ratio of the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`
   | is duplicate of the ratio of :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`; :just:`[VI. 20] <VI.20>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the square on :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` is to the square on :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`,
   | so is the polygon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` to the polygon :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`

| As, from this point onward, the text of each proposition usually occupies
| considerable space, I shall generally give in the notes a summary of the
| argument, to enable it to be followed more easily.

| Here we have to prove that a pair of corresponding sides are in the ratio
| of the corresponding diameters.

| Since $\angle\text{s}$ :g:`$BAE$ <0373.g1:< B A E )>`, :g:`$GFL$ <0373.g1:< G F L )>` are equal, and the sides about those angles
| proportional,

.. assertion::

   $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$ABE$ <0373.g1:< A B E >>`, :g:`$FGL$ <0373.g1:< F G L >>` are equiangular,

so that

.. assertion::

   $\angle$ :g:`$AEB$ <0373.g1:< A E B )>` $= \angle$ :g:`$FLG$ <0373.g1:< F L G )>`

| Hence their equals in the same segments, $\angle\text{s}$ :g:`$AMB$ <0373.g1:< A M B )>`, :g:`$FNG$ <0373.g1:< F N G )>`, are equal.
| And the right angles :g:`$BAM$ <0373.g1:< B A M )>`, :g:`$GFN$ <0373.g1:< G F N )>` are equal.
| Therefore $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$ABM$ <0373.g1:< A B M >>`, :g:`$FGN$ <0373.g1:< F G N >>` are equiangular, so that

.. assertion::

   :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $:$ :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />` $=$ :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` $:$ :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`.

| The duplicates of these ratios are therefore equal,
| whence

.. assertion::

   | $(\text{polygon }$ :g:`$ABCDE$ <0373.g1:< A B C D E >>` $) : (\text{polygon }$ :g:`$FGHKL$ <0373.g1:< F G H K L >>` $)$
   | $= \text{duplicate ratio of }$ :g:`$BA$ <0373.g1:/ B A />` $\text{ to }$ :g:`$GF$ <0373.g1:/ G F />`
   | $= \text{duplicate ratio of }$ :g:`$BM$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $\text{ to }$ :g:`$GN$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`
   | $=$ :g:`$BM^2$ <0373.g1:/ B M />` $:$ :g:`$GN^2$ <0373.g1:/ G N />`.
