PROPOSITION 2.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   Circles are to one another as the squares on the diameters.

| Let :g:`$ABCD$ <0375.g1:( A B C D )>`, :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>` be circles, and :g:`$BD$ <0375.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$FH$ <0375.g1:/ F H />` their
| diameters;
| I say that, as the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`, so is
| the square on :g:`$BD$ <0375.g1:/ B D />` to the square on :g:`$FH$ <0375.g1:/ F H />`.

.. picture:: 0375.g1.svg
   :alt: Circles and inscribed polygons with areas S and T

.. subclause::

   | For, if the square on :g:`$BD$ <0375.g1:/ B D />` is not to the square on :g:`$FH$ <0375.g1:/ F H />` as
   | the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`,
   | then, as the square on :g:`$BD$ <0375.g1:/ B D />` is to the square on :g:`$FH$ <0375.g1:/ F H />`, so will
   | the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` be either to some less area than the circle
   | :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`, or to a greater.

.. subclause::

   First, let it be in that ratio to a less area :g:`$S$ <0375.g1:S>`.

| Let the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:< E F G H >>` be inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`;
| then the inscribed square is greater than the half of the circle
| :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`, inasmuch as, if through the points :g:`$E$ <0375.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0375.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0375.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0375.g1:H>` we
| draw tangents to the circle, the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:< E F G H >>` is half the
| square circumscribed about the circle, and the circle is less
| than the circumscribed square;

.. assertion::

   | hence the inscribed square :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:< E F G H >>` is greater than the half of
   | the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0375.g1:( E F G H )>`.

| Let the circumferences :g:`$EF$ <0375.g1:( E F )>`, :g:`$FG$ <0375.g1:( F G )>`, :g:`$GH$ <0375.g1:( G H )>`, :g:`$HE$ <0375.g1:( H E )>` be bisected at
| the points :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0375.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0375.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0375.g1:N>`,
| and let :g:`$EK$ <0375.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KF$ <0375.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$FL$ <0375.g1:/ F L />`, :g:`$LG$ <0375.g1:/ L G />`, :g:`$GM$ <0375.g1:/ G M />`, :g:`$MH$ <0375.g1:/ M H />`, :g:`$HN$ <0375.g1:/ H N />`, :g:`$NE$ <0375.g1:/ N E />` be joined;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the triangles :g:`$EKF$ <0375.g1:< E K F >>`, :g:`$FLG$ <0375.g1:< F L G >>`, :g:`$GMH$ <0375.g1:< G M H >>`, :g:`$HNE$ <0375.g1:< H N E >>` is
   | also greater than the half of the segment of the circle about
   | it, inasmuch as, if through the points :g:`$K$ <0375.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0375.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0375.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0375.g1:N>` we draw
   | tangents to the circle and complete the parallelograms on the
   | straight lines :g:`$EF$ <0375.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0375.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GH$ <0375.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`$HE$ <0375.g1:/ H E />`, each of the triangles :g:`$EKF$ <0375.g1:< E K F >>`,
