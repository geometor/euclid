<span data-ref="0375.g1:< F L G >">$FLG$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:< G M H >">$GMH$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:< H N E >">$HNE$</span> will be half of the parallelogram<br />
about it,<br />
while the segment about it is less than the parallelogram;
<p class="assertion">hence each of the triangles <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F >">$EKF$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:< F L G >">$FLG$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:< G M H >">$GMH$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:< H N E >">$HNE$</span><br />
is greater than the half of the segment of the circle<br />
about it.</p>

Thus, by bisecting the remaining circumferences and<br />
joining straight lines, and by doing this continually, we shall<br />
leave some segments of the circle which will be less than the<br />
excess by which the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> exceeds the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span>.<br />
For it was proved in the first theorem of the tenth book<br />
that, if two unequal magnitudes be set out, and if from the<br />
greater there be subtracted a magnitude greater than the half,<br />
and from that which is left a greater than the half, and if this<br />
be done continually, there will be left some magnitude which<br />
will be less than the lesser magnitude set out.

<p class="sub-clause">Let segments be left such as described, and let the<br />
segments of the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> on <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ L G /">$LG$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0375.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span>, <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ N E /">$NE$</span> be less than the excess by which the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0375.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> exceeds the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the remainder, the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>, is<br />
greater than the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span>.</p>

Let there be inscribed, also, in the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> the poly-<br />
gon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >">$AOBPCQDR$</span> similar to the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the square on <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, so is<br />
the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >">$AOBPCQDR$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XII. 1]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the square on <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0375.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, so also<br />
is the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> to the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span>, so is the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >">$AOBPCQDR$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, alternately, as the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the polygon<br />
inscribed in it, so is the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the circle <span data-ref="0375.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is greater than the polygon inscribed<br />
in it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the area <span data-ref="0375.g1:S">$S$</span> is also greater than the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >">$EKFLGMHN$</span>.</p>
