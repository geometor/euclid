:title: BOOK XII. PROPOSITION 2

:g:`FLG <0375.g1:< F L G >>`, :g:`GMH <0375.g1:< G M H >>`, :g:`HNE <0375.g1:< H N E >>` will be half of the parallelogram about it, while the segment about it is less than the parallelogram;

.. assertion::

   hence each of the triangles :g:`EKF <0375.g1:< E K F >>`, :g:`FLG <0375.g1:< F L G >>`, :g:`GMH <0375.g1:< G M H >>`, :g:`HNE <0375.g1:< H N E >>` is greater than the half of the segment of the circle about it.

Thus, by bisecting the remaining circumferences and joining straight lines, and by doing this continually, we shall leave some segments of the circle which will be less than the excess by which the circle :g:`EFGH <0375.g1:( E F G H )>` exceeds the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>`. For it was proved in the first theorem of the tenth book that, if two unequal magnitudes be set out, and if from the greater there be subtracted a magnitude greater than the half, and from that which is left a greater than the half, and if this be done continually, there will be left some magnitude which will be less than the lesser magnitude set out.

.. subclause::

   Let segments be left such as described, and let the segments of the circle :g:`EFGH <0375.g1:( E F G H )>` on :g:`EK <0375.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`KF <0375.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`FL <0375.g1:/ F L />`, :g:`LG <0375.g1:/ L G />`, :g:`GM <0375.g1:/ G M />`, :g:`MH <0375.g1:/ M H />`, :g:`HN <0375.g1:/ H N />`, :g:`NE <0375.g1:/ N E />` be less than the excess by which the circle :g:`EFGH <0375.g1:( E F G H )>` exceeds the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remainder, the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`, is greater than the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>`.

Let there be inscribed, also, in the circle :g:`ABCD <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` the polygon :g:`AOBPCQDR <0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >>` similar to the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the square on :g:`BD <0375.g1:/ B D />` is to the square on :g:`FH <0375.g1:/ F H />`, so is the polygon :g:`AOBPCQDR <0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >>` to the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`. :just:`[XII. 1] <XII.1>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the square on :g:`BD <0375.g1:/ B D />` is to the square on :g:`FH <0375.g1:/ F H />`, so also is the circle :g:`ABCD <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` to the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the circle :g:`ABCD <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>`, so is the polygon :g:`AOBPCQDR <0375.g1:< A O B P C Q D R >>` to the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`; :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, alternately, as the circle :g:`ABCD <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the polygon inscribed in it, so is the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>` to the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the circle :g:`ABCD <0375.g1:( A B C D )>` is greater than the polygon inscribed in it;

.. assertion::

   therefore the area :g:`S <0375.g1:S>` is also greater than the polygon :g:`EKFLGMHN <0375.g1:< E K F L G M H N >>`.
