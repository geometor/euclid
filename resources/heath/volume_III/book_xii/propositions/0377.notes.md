# Transcription Notes: Folio 0377 (Page 373)
**Volume III, Book XII, Proposition 2 (Proof Conclusion & Lemma)**

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 373 concludes Euclid's formal demonstration of Proposition 2 and presents the Lemma justifying the reduction of the "greater than" case to the "less than" case.
- **Mathematical Progression**:
  - Delivers the contradictory conclusion: polygon $EKFLGMHN$ is both greater than $S$ (by construction) and less than $S$ (by proportionality), ruling out the hypothesis that circle $ABCD : S = BD^2 : FH^2$ where $S < \text{circle } EFGH$.
  - Reverses the ratio to rule out $S > \text{circle } EFGH$ using the Lemma.
  - Concludes with Q.E.D. for Proposition 2.
  - Introduces and begins the proof of the `LEMMA` showing that if $S > \text{circle } EFGH$, then the fourth proportional $T$ such that $S : \text{circle } ABCD = \text{circle } EFGH : T$ satisfies $T < \text{circle } ABCD$.
- **Folio Continuity**: Carries directly over to folio 0378 (page 374), which finishes the Lemma and begins Heath's extended commentary.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Classical Proof by Double Contradiction**: Euclid avoids limits or infinitesimals by establishing that circle $ABCD : \text{circle } EFGH$ cannot be either greater than or less than $BD^2 : FH^2$, forcing equality.
- **Orientation of Letters**: In the inverse proportion sentence, Heath prints "square on FH is to the square on DB" rather than BD.
- **Typography**: The Lemma heading is centered in capital letters: `LEMMA.`
