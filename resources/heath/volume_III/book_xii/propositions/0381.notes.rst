Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Page Context**: Page 377 details Adrien-Marie Legendre's alternative proof of Proposition 2 from his *Éléments de géométrie*.
- **Mathematical Progression**:

  - Sets forth Legendre's lemma on concentric circles: inscribing in the outer circle a regular polygon not meeting the inner, and circumscribing about the inner a regular polygon not meeting the outer.
  - Construction of chord $MN$ whose distance from center $C$ is greater than tangent $DE$.
  - Radial projection of $MN$ via lines $CM, CN$ intersecting $DE$ at $P, Q$ to form the circumscribed polygon.
  - Setup of circle area proportionality: $(\text{circ. } CA) : (\text{circ. } OB) = CA^2 : OB^2$.
  - Begins Part I assuming ratio equals $(\text{circ. } CA) : (\text{circ. } OD)$ where $OD < OB$.

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Legendre vs. Euclid**: Heath critically remarks that Legendre's method is "equally rigorous but not, I think, possessing any advantages over Euclid's."
- **Notation**: Uses modern compact notation $(\text{circ. } CA)$ to represent the area of a circle of radius $CA$.
- **Connection to Euclid XII. 16**: Legendre's lemma corresponds directly to Euclid's Proposition XII. 16, which is used later in Book XII for spheres.
