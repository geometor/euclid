<p class="assertion">the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A D B >">$ADB$</span> is equiangular to the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D H K >">$DHK$</span>, <span class="justification">[I. 29]</span><br />
and they have their sides proportional;<br />
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A D B >">$ADB$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D H K >">$DHK$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. Def. 1]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D B C >">$DBC$</span> is also similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D K L >">$DKL$</span>, and<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A D C >">$ADC$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D L H >">$DLH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the two straight lines <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> meeting one<br />
another are parallel to the two straight lines <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span> meeting<br />
one another, not in the same plane, they will contain equal<br />
angles. <span class="justification">[XI. 10]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< K H L )">$KHL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, as <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is<br />
the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the vertex is similar to the pyramid<br />
of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the<br />
vertex.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the pyramid of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> is the base<br />
and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the vertex was proved similar to the pyramid<br />
of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G >">$AEG$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span> the<br />
vertex.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the pyramids <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G H >">$AEGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L D >">$HKLD$</span> is<br />
similar to the whole pyramid <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Next, since <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F G >">$EBFG$</span> is double of the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< G F C >">$GFC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, if there be two prisms of equal height, and one<br />
have a parallelogram as base, and the other a triangle, and if<br />
the parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms are<br />
equal, <span class="justification">[XI. 39]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the prism contained by the two triangles <span data-ref="0382.g1:< B K F >">$BKF$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0382.g1:< E H G >">$EHG$</span>, and the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F G >">$EBFG$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B K H >">$EBKH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K F G >">$HKFG$</span><br />
is equal to the prism contained by the two triangles <span data-ref="0382.g1:< G F C >">$GFC$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> and the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0382.g1:< K F C L >">$KFCL$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< L C G H >">$LCGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K F G >">$HKFG$</span>.</p>
