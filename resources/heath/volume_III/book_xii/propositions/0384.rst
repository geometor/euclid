.. assertion::

   the triangle :g:`$ADB$ <0382.g1:< A D B >>` is equiangular to the triangle :g:`$DHK$ <0382.g1:< D H K >>`, :just:`[I. 29] <I.29>` and they have their sides proportional; therefore the triangle :g:`$ADB$ <0382.g1:< A D B >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$DHK$ <0382.g1:< D H K >>`. :just:`[VI. Def. 1] <VI.def.1>`

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the triangle :g:`$DBC$ <0382.g1:< D B C >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`$DKL$ <0382.g1:< D K L >>`, and the triangle :g:`$ADC$ <0382.g1:< A D C >>` to the triangle :g:`$DLH$ <0382.g1:< D L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`$BA$ <0382.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AC$ <0382.g1:/ A C />` meeting one another are parallel to the two straight lines :g:`$KH$ <0382.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HL$ <0382.g1:/ H L />` meeting one another, not in the same plane, they will contain equal angles. :just:`[XI. 10] <XI.10>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0382.g1:< B A C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$KHL$ <0382.g1:< K H L )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`$BA$ <0382.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0382.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`$KH$ <0382.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`$HL$ <0382.g1:/ H L />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$HKL$ <0382.g1:< H K L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` the vertex is similar to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$HKL$ <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$HKL$ <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`$D$ <0382.g1:D>` the vertex was proved similar to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`$AEG$ <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`$H$ <0382.g1:H>` the vertex.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the pyramids :g:`$AEGH$ <0382.g1:< A E G H >>`, :g:`$HKLD$ <0382.g1:< H K L D >>` is similar to the whole pyramid :g:`$ABCD$ <0382.g1:< A B C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   Next, since :g:`$BF$ <0382.g1:/ B F />` is equal to :g:`$FC$ <0382.g1:/ F C />`,

.. assertion::

   the parallelogram :g:`$EBFG$ <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is double of the triangle :g:`$GFC$ <0382.g1:< G F C >>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, if there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a parallelogram as base, and the other a triangle, and if the parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms are equal, :just:`[XI. 39] <XI.39>`
