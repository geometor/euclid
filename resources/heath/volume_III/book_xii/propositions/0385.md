<p class="assertion">inasmuch as, if we join the straight lines <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, the prism<br />
in which the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F G >">$EBFG$</span> is the base and the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> its opposite is greater than the pyramid of which the<br />
triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F >">$EBF$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:K">$K$</span> the vertex.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the pyramid of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F >">$EBF$</span> is the base<br />
and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:K">$K$</span> the vertex is equal to the pyramid of which<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G >">$AEG$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span> the vertex;<br />
for they are contained by equal and similar planes.</p>

<p class="assertion">Hence also the prism in which the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F G >">$EBFG$</span><br />
is the base and the straight line <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> its opposite is greater<br />
than the pyramid of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G >">$AEG$</span> is the base and<br />
the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span> the vertex.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the prism in which the parallelogram <span data-ref="0382.g1:< E B F G >">$EBFG$</span> is the<br />
base and the straight line <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> its opposite is equal to the<br />
prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< G F C >">$GFC$</span> is the base and the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> its opposite,<br />
and the pyramid of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G >">$AEG$</span> is the base and<br />
the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span> the vertex is equal to the pyramid of which the<br />
triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the vertex.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the said two prisms are greater than the said<br />
two pyramids of which the triangles <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A E G >">$AEG$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H K L >">$HKL$</span> are the<br />
bases and the points <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the vertices.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the whole pyramid, of which the triangle <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span><br />
is the base and the point <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> the vertex, has been divided into<br />
two pyramids equal to one another and into two equal prisms,<br />
and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole<br />
pyramid.</p>

<p class="qed">Q. E. D.</p>

We will denote a pyramid with vertex <span data-ref="0382.g1:D">$D$</span> and base <span data-ref="0382.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> by <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D A B C >">$D(ABC)$</span> or<br />
<span data-ref="0382.g1:< D A B C >">$D\text{-}ABC$</span> and the triangular prism with triangles <span data-ref="0382.g1:< G C F >">$GCF$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H L K >">$HLK$</span> for bases by<br />
<span data-ref="0382.g1:< G C F H L K >">$(GCF, HLK)$</span>.

The following are the steps of the proof.

I. To prove pyramid <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H A E G >">$H(AEG)$</span> equal and similar to pyramid <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D H K L >">$D(HKL)$</span>.

Since sides of $\triangle$<span data-ref="0382.g1:< D A B >">$DAB$</span> are bisected at <span data-ref="0382.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:K">$K$</span>,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> $\parallel$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> $\parallel$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.
Hence
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ E A /">$EA$</span>,
<span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ D K /">$DK$</span>.
Therefore (1) $\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H A E >">$HAE$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D H K >">$DHK$</span> are equal and similar.
Similarly (2) $\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0382.g1:< H A G >">$HAG$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:< D H L >">$DHL$</span> are equal and similar.
Again, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ L H /">$LH$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> are respectively $\parallel$ to <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ G A /">$GA$</span>, <span data-ref="0382.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> in a different plane;
therefore
$\angle$<span data-ref="0382.g1:< G A E )">$GAE$</span> $= \angle$<span data-ref="0382.g1:< L H K )">$LHK$</span>.
