:title: Proposition 3 (Conclusion and Commentary)

.. assertion::

   inasmuch as, if we join the straight lines :g:`EF <0382.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`EK <0382.g1:/ E K />`, the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`EBFG <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is greater than the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`EBF <0382.g1:< E B F >>` is the base and the point :g:`K <0382.g1:K>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`EBF <0382.g1:< E B F >>` is the base and the point :g:`K <0382.g1:K>` the vertex is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< K E F B > = 0382.g1:< A E G H >>` to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`AEG <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex; for they are contained by equal and similar planes.

.. assertion::

   Hence also the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`EBFG <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is greater than the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`AEG <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`EBFG <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H G E K F B > = 0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` to the prism in which the triangle :g:`GFC <0382.g1:< G F C >>` is the base and the triangle :g:`HKL <0382.g1:< H K L >>` its opposite, and the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`AEG <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G H > = 0382.g1:< H K L D >>` to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`HKL <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the said two prisms are greater than the said two pyramids of which the triangles :g:`AEG <0382.g1:< A E G >>`, :g:`HKL <0382.g1:< H K L >>` are the bases and the points :g:`H <0382.g1:H>`, :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertices.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole pyramid, of which the triangle :g:`ABC <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex, has been divided into two pyramids :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G H > = 0382.g1:< H K L D >>` to one another and into two :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H G E K F B > = 0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` prisms, and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole pyramid.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.

----

We will denote a pyramid with vertex :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` and base :g:`ABC <0382.g1:< A B C >>` by :g:`D(ABC) <0382.g1:< D A B C >>` or :g:`D-ABC <0382.g1:< D A B C >>` and the triangular prism with triangles :g:`GCF <0382.g1:< G C F >>`, :g:`HLK <0382.g1:< H L K >>` for bases by :g:`(GCF, HLK) <0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>`.

The following are the steps of the proof.

I. To prove pyramid :g:`H(AEG) <0382.g1:< H A E G >>` :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H A E G > = 0382.g1:< D H K L >>` and similar to pyramid :g:`D(HKL) <0382.g1:< D H K L >>`.

Since sides of $\triangle$ :g:`DAB <0382.g1:< D A B >>` are :eq:`bisected <0382.g1:/ A H / = 0382.g1:/ H D /; 0382.g1:/ A E / = 0382.g1:/ E B /; 0382.g1:/ D K / = 0382.g1:/ K B />` at :g:`H <0382.g1:H>`, :g:`E <0382.g1:E>`, :g:`K <0382.g1:K>`,
:g:`HE <0382.g1:/ H E />` $\parallel$ :g:`DB <0382.g1:/ D B />`, and :g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` $\parallel$ :g:`AB <0382.g1:/ A B />`.
Hence
:g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` $=$ :g:`EB <0382.g1:/ E B />` $=$ :g:`EA <0382.g1:/ E A />`,
:g:`HE <0382.g1:/ H E />` $=$ :g:`KB <0382.g1:/ K B />` $=$ :g:`DK <0382.g1:/ D K />`.
Therefore (1) $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`HAE <0382.g1:< H A E >>`, :g:`DHK <0382.g1:< D H K >>` are :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H A E > = 0382.g1:< D H K >>` and similar.
Similarly (2) $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`HAG <0382.g1:< H A G >>`, :g:`DHL <0382.g1:< D H L >>` are :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H A G > = 0382.g1:< D H L >>` and similar.
Again, :g:`LH <0382.g1:/ L H />`, :g:`HK <0382.g1:/ H K />` are respectively $\parallel$ to :g:`GA <0382.g1:/ G A />`, :g:`AE <0382.g1:/ A E />` in a different plane;
therefore
$\angle$ :g:`GAE <0382.g1:< G A E )>` $= \angle$ :g:`LHK <0382.g1:< L H K )>`.
