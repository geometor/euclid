Similarly (2) $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$DLH$ <0382.g1:< D L H >>`, :g:`$DCA$ <0382.g1:< D C A >>` are similar, as also (3) the $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$DLK$ <0382.g1:< D L K >>`, :g:`$DCB$ <0382.g1:< D C B >>`.

:g:`$BA$ <0382.g1:/ B A />` $:$ :g:`$AC$ <0382.g1:/ A C />` $=$ :g:`$KH$ <0382.g1:/ K H />` $:$ :g:`$HL$ <0382.g1:/ H L />`.
Therefore (4) $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$BAC$ <0382.g1:< B A C >>`, :g:`$KHL$ <0382.g1:< K H L >>` are similar.

III. To prove prism :g:`$(GCF, HLK)$ <0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` equal to prism :g:`$(HGE, KFB)$ <0382.g1:< H G E K F B >>`.

The prisms may be regarded as having the same _height_ (the distance between the planes :g:`$HKL$ <0382.g1:< H K L >>`, :g:`$ABC$ <0382.g1:< A B C >>`) and having for bases (1) the $\triangle$ :g:`$CGF$ <0382.g1:< C G F >>` and (2) the $\square$ :g:`$EBFG$ <0382.g1:< E B F G >>`, which is the double of the $\triangle$ :g:`$CGF$ <0382.g1:< C G F >>`.
Therefore, by XI. 39, the prisms are equal.

IV. To prove the prisms greater than the small pyramids.

Prism :g:`$(HGE, KFB)$ <0382.g1:< H G E K F B >>` is clearly greater than pyramid :g:`$K(EFB)$ <0382.g1:< K E F B >>` and therefore greater than pyramid :g:`$H(AEG)$ <0382.g1:< H A E G >>`.
Therefore each of the prisms is greater than each of the small pyramids;
and the sum of the two prisms is greater than the sum of the two small pyramids, which, with the two prisms, make up the whole pyramid.

PROPOSITION 4.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If there be two pyramids of the same height which have triangular bases, and each of them be divided into two pyramids equal to one another and similar to the whole, and into two equal prisms, then, as the base of the one pyramid is to the base of the other pyramid, so will all the prisms in the one pyramid be to all the prisms, being equal in multitude, in the other pyramid.
