<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B O /">$BO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ O C /">$OC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ A L /">$AL$</span> to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ L O /">$LO$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>.</p>

![Proposition 4](0387.g1.svg)

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is also similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ C O /">$CO$</span>, and <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> of <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ F V /">$FV$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ C O /">$CO$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ F V /">$FV$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And on <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ C O /">$CO$</span> are described the similar and similarly<br />
situated rectilineal figures <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>,<br />
and on <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ F V /">$FV$</span> the similar and similarly situated figures<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>, so is<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 22]</span><br />
therefore, alternately, as the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so is the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>, so is<br />
the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is the base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> its<br />
opposite to the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> is the base<br />
and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> its opposite; <span class="justification">[Lemma following]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>,<br />
so is the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is the base and<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> its opposite to the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span><br />
is the base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> its opposite.</p>

<p class="assertion">But, as the said prisms are to one another, so is the prism<br />
in which the parallelogram <span data-ref="0387.g1:< K B O L >">$KBOL$</span> is the base and the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span> its opposite to the prism in which the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< Q E V R >">$QEVR$</span> is the base and the straight line <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span> its opposite.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. 39; cf. XII. 3]</span></p>
