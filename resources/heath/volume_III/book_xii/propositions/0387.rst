:title: Proposition 4 (Continued)

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$BO$ <0387.g1:/ B O />` is equal to :g:`$OC$ <0387.g1:/ O C />`, and :g:`$AL$ <0387.g1:/ A L />` to :g:`$LC$ <0387.g1:/ L C />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$LO$ <0387.g1:/ L O />` is parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0387.g1:/ A B />`,
   | and the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`.

.. picture:: 0387.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />` is double of :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />`, and :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />` of :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />` is to :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />`, so is :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />`.

.. subclause::

   | And on :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />` are described the similar and similarly
   | situated rectilineal figures :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`,
   | and on :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />` the similar and similarly situated figures
   | :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, so is
   | the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`; :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>`
   | therefore, alternately, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle
   | :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   | But, as the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, so is
   | the prism in which the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its
   | opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base
   | and :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite; :just:`[Lemma following]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the triangle :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`,
   | so is the prism in which the triangle :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and
   | :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`
   | is the base and :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite.

.. assertion::

   | But, as the said prisms are to one another, so is the prism
   | in which the parallelogram :g:`$KBOL$ <0387.g1:< K B O L >>` is the base and the straight
   | line :g:`$PM$ <0387.g1:/ P M />` its opposite to the prism in which the parallelogram
   | :g:`$QEVR$ <0387.g1:< Q E V R >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`$ST$ <0387.g1:/ S T />` its opposite.
   | :just:`[XI. 39; cf. XII. 3]`
