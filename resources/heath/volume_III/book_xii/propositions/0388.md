<p class="assertion">Therefore also the two prisms, that in which the parallelo-<br />
gram <span data-ref="0387.g1:< K B O L >">$KBOL$</span> is the base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span> its opposite, and that in<br />
which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is the base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> its opposite,<br />
are to the prisms in which <span data-ref="0387.g1:< Q E V R >">$QEVR$</span> is the base and the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span> its opposite and in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> is the<br />
base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> its opposite in the same ratio. <span class="justification">[V. 12]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so<br />
are the said two prisms to the said two prisms.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And similarly, if the pyramids <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N G >">$PMNG$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U H >">$STUH$</span> be divided<br />

<p class="assertion">as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span>, so will the two prisms<br />
in the pyramid <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N G >">$PMNG$</span> be to the two prisms in the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U H >">$STUH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span>, so is the base<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>;<br />

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so<br />

<p class="qed">And similarly also, if we divide the remaining pyramids<br />

<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so will all the prisms in the<br />

### LEMMA.

<p class="enunciation">But that, as the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is to the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>,<br />
so is the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is the base and<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> its opposite to the prism in which the triangle <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> is<br />
the base and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> its opposite, we must prove as follows.</p>

For in the same figure let perpendiculars be conceived<br />
drawn from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> to the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>; these are of<br />
course equal because, by hypothesis, the pyramids are of equal<br />
height.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the two straight lines <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> and the perpendicular<br />
from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span> are cut by the parallel planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">they will be cut in the same ratios. <span class="justification">[XI. 17]</span></p>
