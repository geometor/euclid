.. assertion::

   Therefore also the two prisms, that in which the parallelogram :g:`KBOL <0387.g1:< K B O L >>` is the base and :g:`PM <0387.g1:/ P M />` its opposite, and that in which the triangle :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite, are to the prisms in which :g:`QEVR <0387.g1:< Q E V R >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`ST <0387.g1:/ S T />` its opposite and in which the triangle :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite in the same ratio. :just:`[V. 12] <V.12>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the base :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so are the said two prisms to the said two prisms.

.. subclause::

   And similarly, if the pyramids :g:`PMNG <0387.g1:< P M N G >>`, :g:`STUH <0387.g1:< S T U H >>` be divided into two prisms and two pyramids,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` is to the base :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>`, so will the two prisms in the pyramid :g:`PMNG <0387.g1:< P M N G >>` be to the two prisms in the pyramid :g:`STUH <0387.g1:< S T U H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` is to the base :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>`, so is the base :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` to the base :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`; for the triangles :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` are :eq:`equal <0387.g1:< P M N > = 0387.g1:< L O C >; 0387.g1:< S T U > = 0387.g1:< R V F >>` to the triangles :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` respectively.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the base :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so are the four prisms to the four prisms.

.. qed::

   And similarly also, if we divide the remaining pyramids into two pyramids and into two prisms, then, as the base :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, so will all the prisms in the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0387.g1:< A B C G >>` be to all the prisms, being equal in multitude, in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0387.g1:< D E F H >>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

LEMMA.
~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   But that, as the triangle :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the triangle :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, so is the prism in which the triangle :g:`LOC <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is the base and :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>` its opposite to the prism in which the triangle :g:`RVF <0387.g1:< R V F >>` is the base and :g:`STU <0387.g1:< S T U >>` its opposite, we must prove as follows.

For in the same figure let perpendiculars be conceived drawn from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0387.g1:H>` to the planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`DEF <0387.g1:< D E F >>`; these are of course equal because, by hypothesis, the pyramids are of equal height.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`GC <0387.g1:/ G C />` and the perpendicular from :g:`G <0387.g1:G>` are cut by the parallel planes :g:`ABC <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`PMN <0387.g1:< P M N >>`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 17] <XI.17>

   they will be cut in the same ratios.
