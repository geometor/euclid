And <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> at <span data-ref="0387.g1:N">$N$</span>;
therefore the perpendicular from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span> to the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> will also be bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>.
For the same reason
the perpendicular from <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> to the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> will also be bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span>.
And the perpendiculars from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> to the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are equal;
therefore the perpendiculars from the triangles <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> to the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are also equal.
Therefore the prisms in which the triangles <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> are bases, and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> their opposites, are of equal height.
Hence also the parallelepipedal solids described from the said prisms are of equal height and are to one another as their bases; [XI. 32]
therefore their halves, namely the said prisms, are to one another as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>.

**Q. E. D.**

---

We can incorporate the lemma at the end of the proposition and summarise the proof thus.

Since <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ L O /">$LO$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
$\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> are similar.
In like manner
$\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> are similar.
And, since
<span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ C O /">$CO$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ F V /">$FV$</span>,
$\triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>, [VI. 22]
and, alternately,
$\triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> $= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>.

Now the prisms <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C P M N >">$(LOC, PMN)$</span> and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F S T U >">$(RVF, STU)$</span> are equal in height:
for the perpendiculars from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> on the bases <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are divided by the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> (parallel to the bases) in the same proportion as <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> are divided by those planes [XI. 17], i.e. they are bisected;
hence the heights of the prisms, being half the equal heights of the pyramids, are equal.

And the prisms are the halves respectively of parallelepipeds of the same height on parallelogrammic bases double of the $\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> respectively; [XI. 28 and note]
hence they are in the same ratio as those parallelepipeds, and therefore as their bases [XI. 32].

Therefore
$(\text{prism }$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C P M N >">$LOC, PMN$</span>$) : (\text{prism }$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F S T U >">$RVF, STU$</span>$) = \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>
$= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>.
