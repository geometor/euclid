<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> is bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span> at <span data-ref="0387.g1:N">$N$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the perpendicular from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span> to the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> will<br />
also be bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the perpendicular from <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> to the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> will also be<br />
bisected by the plane <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the perpendiculars from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> to the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are equal;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the perpendiculars from the triangles <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span><br />
to the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are also equal.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the prisms in which the triangles <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span><br />
are bases, and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> their opposites, are of equal<br />
height.</p>

<p class="assertion">Hence also the parallelepipedal solids described from the<br />
said prisms are of equal height and are to one another as their<br />
bases; <span class="justification">[XI. 32]</span><br />
therefore their halves, namely the said prisms, are to one another<br />
as the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>.<span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>

---

We can incorporate the lemma at the end of the proposition and sum-<br />
marise the proof thus.

Since <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ L O /">$LO$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
$\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> are similar.<br />
In like manner<br />
$\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> are similar.<br />
And, since<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ C O /">$CO$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ F V /">$FV$</span>,<br />
$\triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>, <span class="justification">[VI. 22]</span><br />
and, alternately,<br />
$\triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> $= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span>.

Now the prisms <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C P M N >">$(LOC, PMN)$</span> and <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F S T U >">$(RVF, STU)$</span> are equal in height:<br />
for the perpendiculars from <span data-ref="0387.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:H">$H$</span> on the bases <span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are divided by<br />
the planes <span data-ref="0387.g1:< P M N >">$PMN$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< S T U >">$STU$</span> (parallel to the bases) in the same proportion as <span data-ref="0387.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0387.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span> are divided by those planes <span class="justification">[XI. 17]</span>, i.e. they are bisected;<br />
hence the heights of the prisms, being half the equal heights of the pyramids,<br />
are equal.

And the prisms are the halves respectively of parallelepipeds of the same<br />
height on parallelogrammic bases double of the $\triangle\text{s}$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span>, <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span> respectively;<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. 28 and note]</span><br />
hence they are in the same ratio as those parallelepipeds, and therefore as<br />
their bases <span class="justification">[XI. 32]</span>.

Therefore<br />
$(\text{prism }$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C P M N >">$LOC, PMN$</span>$) : (\text{prism }$ <span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F S T U >">$RVF, STU$</span>$) = \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< L O C >">$LOC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< R V F >">$RVF$</span><br />
$= \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> $: \triangle$<span data-ref="0387.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>.
