:title: Proposition 4 (Lemma Conclusion and Commentary)

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$GC$ <0387.g1:/ G C />` is bisected by the plane :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>` at :g:`$N$ <0387.g1:N>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the perpendicular from :g:`$G$ <0387.g1:G>` to the plane :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` will
   | also be bisected by the plane :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>`.

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | the perpendicular from :g:`$H$ <0387.g1:H>` to the plane :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` will also be
   | bisected by the plane :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>`.

.. subclause::

   | And the perpendiculars from :g:`$G$ <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0387.g1:H>` to the planes :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`,
   | :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are equal;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the perpendiculars from the triangles :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>`
   | to the planes :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are also equal.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the prisms in which the triangles :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`
   | are bases, and :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` their opposites, are of equal
   | height.

.. assertion::

   | Hence also the parallelepipedal solids described from the
   | said prisms are of equal height and are to one another as their
   | bases; :just:`[XI. 32] <XI.32>`
   | therefore their halves, namely the said prisms, are to one another
   | as the base :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` is to the base :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`. :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

| We can incorporate the lemma at the end of the proposition and sum-
| marise the proof thus.

| Since :g:`$LO$ <0387.g1:/ L O />` is parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0387.g1:/ A B />`,
| $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` are similar.
| In like manner
| $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` are similar.
| And, since
| :g:`$BC$ <0387.g1:/ B C />` $:$ :g:`$CO$ <0387.g1:/ C O />` $=$ :g:`$EF$ <0387.g1:/ E F />` $:$ :g:`$FV$ <0387.g1:/ F V />`,
| $\triangle$ :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`, :just:`[VI. 22] <VI.22>`
| and, alternately,
| $\triangle$ :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` $= \triangle$ :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`.

| Now the prisms :g:`$(LOC, PMN)$ <0387.g1:< L O C P M N >>` and :g:`$(RVF, STU)$ <0387.g1:< R V F S T U >>` are equal in height:
| for the perpendiculars from :g:`$G$ <0387.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0387.g1:H>` on the bases :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>` are divided by
| the planes :g:`$PMN$ <0387.g1:< P M N >>`, :g:`$STU$ <0387.g1:< S T U >>` (parallel to the bases) in the same proportion as :g:`$GC$ <0387.g1:/ G C />`,
| :g:`$HF$ <0387.g1:/ H F />` are divided by those planes :just:`[XI. 17] <XI.17>`, i.e. they are bisected;
| hence the heights of the prisms, being half the equal heights of the pyramids,
| are equal.

| And the prisms are the halves respectively of parallelepipeds of the same
| height on parallelogrammic bases double of the $\triangle\text{s}$ :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>`, :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>` respectively;
| :just:`[XI. 28 and note]`
| hence they are in the same ratio as those parallelepipeds, and therefore as
| their bases :just:`[XI. 32] <XI.32>`.

| Therefore
| $(\text{prism }$ :g:`$LOC, PMN$ <0387.g1:< L O C P M N >>` $) : (\text{prism }$ :g:`$RVF, STU$ <0387.g1:< R V F S T U >>` $) = \triangle$ :g:`$LOC$ <0387.g1:< L O C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$RVF$ <0387.g1:< R V F >>`
| $= \triangle$ :g:`$ABC$ <0387.g1:< A B C >>` $: \triangle$ :g:`$DEF$ <0387.g1:< D E F >>`.
