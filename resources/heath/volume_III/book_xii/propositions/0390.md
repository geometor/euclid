### PROPOSITION 5.

<p class="enunciation">Pyramids which are of the same height and have triangular<br />
bases are to one another as the bases.</p>

Let there be pyramids of the same height, of which the<br />
triangles <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>, <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> are the bases and the points <span data-ref="0390.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0390.g1:H">$H$</span> the<br />
vertices;<br />
I say that, as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so is the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> to the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>.

![Proposition 5](0390.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">For, if the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is not to the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span><br />
as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">then, as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so will the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> be either to some solid less than the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> or to a greater.</p>

Let it, first, be in that ratio to a less solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>, and let the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> be divided into two pyramids equal to one<br />
another and similar to the whole and into two equal prisms;

<p class="assertion">then the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole<br />
pyramid. <span class="justification">[XII. 3]</span></p>
