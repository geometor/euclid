## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page 386** completes the proof of **Proposition 4** (showing that the ratio of the sum of prisms inscribed in two similar/equal-height pyramids equals the ratio of their triangular bases, even when the division is continued indefinitely).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 4 Conclusion**: Euclid/Heath notes that by repeated application of the prism-construction of XII. 3 and ratio-equivalence of XII. 4, the ratio of the sum of prisms remains invariant at $\triangle ABC : \triangle DEF$.
- **Proposition 5 Setting-out**: Pyramids $ABCG$ and $DEFH$ on triangular bases $ABC, DEF$ with vertices $G, H$.
- **Exhaustion Setup**: Heath introduces the auxiliary solid $W$. If $ABC : DEF \neq ABCG : DEFH$, then $ABC : DEF = ABCG : W$, where $W < DEFH$ or $W > DEFH$.
- **Application of XII. 3**: Each step of bisecting the edges leaves two prisms that together exceed half the pyramid, enabling the application of the bisection principle of X. 1.
