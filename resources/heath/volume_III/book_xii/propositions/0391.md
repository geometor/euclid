Again, let the pyramids arising from the division be<br />
similarly divided,<br />
and let this be done continually until there are left over from<br />
the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> some pyramids which are less than the<br />
excess by which the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> exceeds the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[X. 1]</span>

Let such be left, and let them be, for the sake of argument,<br />
<span data-ref="0390.g1:< D Q R S >">$DQRS$</span>, <span data-ref="0390.g1:< S T U H >">$STUH$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainders, the prisms in the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>,<br />
are greater than the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>.</p>

Let the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> also be divided similarly, and a<br />
similar number of times, with the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so are the<br />
prisms in the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> to the prisms in the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>. <span class="justification">[XII. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so also is the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> to the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is to the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>, so<br />
are the prisms in the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> to the prisms in the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>; <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span><br />
therefore, alternately, as the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is to the prisms<br />
in it, so is the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span> to the prisms in the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is greater than the prisms in it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span> is also greater than the prisms in the<br />
pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it is also less :</p>
<p class="assertion">which is impossible.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the prism <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is not to any solid less than<br />
the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly it can be proved that neither is the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> to any solid less than the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> as the base<br />
<span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>.</p>

I say next that neither is the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> to any<br />
solid greater than the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is<br />
to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For, if possible, let it be in that ratio to a greater solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, inversely, as the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span>,<br />
so is the solid <span data-ref="0390.g1:W">$W$</span> to the pyramid <span data-ref="0390.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>.</p>
