Again, let the pyramids arising from the division be similarly divided, and let this be done continually until there are left over from the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>` some pyramids which are less than the excess by which the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>` exceeds the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>`. :just:`[X. 1] <X.1>`

Let such be left, and let them be, for the sake of argument, :g:`DQRS <0390.g1:< D Q R S >>`, :g:`STUH <0390.g1:< S T U H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainders, the prisms in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`, are greater than the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>`.

Let the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` also be divided similarly, and a similar number of times, with the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>`, so are the prisms in the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` to the prisms in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`. :just:`[XII. 4] <XII.4>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>`, so also is the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` to the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` is to the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>`, so are the prisms in the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` to the prisms in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`; :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>` therefore, alternately, as the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` is to the prisms in it, so is the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>` to the prisms in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` is greater than the prisms in it;

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>` is also greater than the prisms in the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But it is also less :

.. assertion::

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the prism :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` is not to any solid less than the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>` as the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly it can be proved that neither is the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>` to any solid less than the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` as the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>` is to the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>`.

I say next that neither is the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>` to any solid greater than the pyramid :g:`DEFH <0390.g1:< D E F H >>` as the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let it be in that ratio to a greater solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, inversely, as the base :g:`DEF <0390.g1:< D E F >>` is to the base :g:`ABC <0390.g1:< A B C >>`, so is the solid :g:`W <0390.g1:W>` to the pyramid :g:`ABCG <0390.g1:< A B C G >>`.
