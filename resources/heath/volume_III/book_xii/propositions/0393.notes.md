## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Legendre's Approach**: Avoids Euclid's complex step-by-step exhaustion using prisms inscribed inside triangular pyramids, relying instead on parallel planar sections, similarity of polygons, and subsequently a slice-by-slice exhaustion resembling Cavalieri's principle.
- **Notation**: Uses lowercase letters $a, b, c, d, e$ and $o$ for the section parallel to base $A, B, C, D, E$ and $O$.
- **Parallel Line Symbol**: Standard `\parallel` ($\parallel$) for parallel line segments ($AB \parallel ab$).
