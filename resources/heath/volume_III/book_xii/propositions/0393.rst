II. Suppose, if possible, that $W > P'$. Then, inversely,

$$B' : B = W : P$$

$$= P' : V\text{,}$$

where $V$ is some solid less than $P$. :just:`[Cf. XII. 2, Lemma, and note.]` But this can be proved impossible exactly as in Part I.

.. assertion::

   Therefore $W$ is neither less nor greater than $P'$, so that __RSTBLOCKPLACEHOLDER_0003__

:person:`Legendre <legendre>`, followed by the American editors already mentioned, and by others, approaches the subject by a different route, proving the following propositions.

.. enunciation::

   1. *If a pyramid be cut by a plane parallel to the base, (a) the lateral edges and the height will be cut in the same proportion, (b) the section by the plane will be a polygon similar to the base.*

.. picture:: 0393.g1.svg
   :alt: Legendre Proposition 1

(a) Since a lateral face :g:`VAB <0393.g1:< V A B >>` of the pyramid :g:`V(ABCDE) <0393.g1:< V A B C D E >>` is cut by two parallel planes in :g:`AB <0393.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`ab <0393.g1:/ a b />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`AB <0393.g1:/ A B />` $\parallel$ :g:`ab <0393.g1:/ a b />`;

Similarly :g:`BC <0393.g1:/ B C />` $\parallel$ :g:`bc <0393.g1:/ b c />`, and so on. Therefore

.. assertion::

   :g:`VA <0393.g1:/ V A />` $:$ :g:`Va <0393.g1:/ V a />` $=$ :g:`VB <0393.g1:/ V B />` $:$ :g:`Vb <0393.g1:/ V b />` $=$ :g:`VC <0393.g1:/ V C />` $:$ :g:`Vc <0393.g1:/ V c />` $= \dots.$

And, if :g:`VO <0393.g1:/ V O />` the height be cut in :g:`O <0393.g1:O>`, :g:`o <0393.g1:o>`, 

.. assertion::

   :g:`BO <0393.g1:/ B O />` $\parallel$ :g:`bo <0393.g1:/ b o />`; and each of the above ratios is equal to :g:`VO <0393.g1:/ V O />` $:$ :g:`Vo <0393.g1:/ V o />`.

(b) Since :g:`BA <0393.g1:/ B A />` $\parallel$ :g:`ba <0393.g1:/ b a />`, and :g:`BC <0393.g1:/ B C />` $\parallel$ :g:`bc <0393.g1:/ b c />`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 10] <XI.10>

   $\angle$ :g:`ABC <0393.g1:< A B C )>` $= \angle$ :g:`abc <0393.g1:< a b c )>`.

Similarly for all the other angles of the polygons, which are therefore equiangular. Also, by similar triangles,

.. assertion::

   :g:`VA <0393.g1:/ V A />` $:$ :g:`Va <0393.g1:/ V a />` $=$ :g:`AB <0393.g1:/ A B />` $:$ :g:`ab <0393.g1:/ a b />`,

and so on. Therefore, by the ratios above,

.. assertion::

   :g:`AB <0393.g1:/ A B />` $:$ :g:`ab <0393.g1:/ a b />` $=$ :g:`BC <0393.g1:/ B C />` $:$ :g:`bc <0393.g1:/ b c />` $= \dots.$

.. assertion::

   Therefore the polygons are similar.
