<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V x /">$Vx$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V Y /">$VY$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V y /">$Vy$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V Z /">$VZ$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V z /">$Vz$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V O /">$VO$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V o /">$Vo$</span> $=$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V A /">$VA$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ V a /">$Va$</span> $= \dots,$</p>

<p class="assertion">(polygon <span data-ref="0393.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span>) $:$ (polygon <span data-ref="0393.g1:< a b c d e >">$abcde$</span>) $=$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ A B /">$AB^2$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0393.g1:/ a b /">$ab^2$</span><br />

<p class="enunciation">3. <em>Two triangular pyramids which have equivalent bases and equal heights<br />
are equivalent.</em></p>
