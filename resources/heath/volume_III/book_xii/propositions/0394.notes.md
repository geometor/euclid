## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Term "Equivalent" vs "Equal"**: Legendre and 19th-century geometers used "equivalent" for figures of equal content/volume that are not congruent.
- **Slice Construction**: Legendre uses horizontal equidistant slices of thickness $z < AX$ where $\text{prism}(ABC, AX) = VABC - vabc$. This is essentially a Darboux/Riemann upper and lower sum argument applied geometrically.
- **Step Citation**: Uses `[(2) above]` to justify section equivalence at each height.
