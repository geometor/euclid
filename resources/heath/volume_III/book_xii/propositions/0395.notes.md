## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Telescoping Sum Principle**: Heath highlights Legendre's telescoping sum:
  $$\sum \text{exterior} - \sum \text{interior} = \text{lowest exterior prism } ABCD.$$
  Since $ABCD < \text{prism}(ABC, AX) = VABC - vabc$, but also $\sum \text{exterior} - \sum \text{interior} > VABC - vabc$, a direct contradiction is reached.
- **Bridge to XII. 6 and XII. 7**: Heath notes how modern treatments reorder propositions (proving the volume formula first, then deriving the ratio theorems as immediate corollaries), whereas Euclid proves the ratio theorems (XII. 5, XII. 6) first by exhaustion, and only then determines the absolute fraction $\frac{1}{3}$ in XII. 7.
