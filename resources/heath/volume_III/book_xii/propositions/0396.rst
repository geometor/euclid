PROPOSITION 6.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Pyramids which are of the same height and have polygonal bases are to one another as the bases.

| Let there be pyramids of the same height of which the
| polygons :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$FGHKL$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L >>` are the bases and the points
| :g:`$M$ <0396.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0396.g1:N>` the vertices;
| I say that, as the base :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>` is to the base :g:`$FGHKL$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L >>`,
| so is the pyramid :g:`$ABCDEM$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E M >>` to the pyramid :g:`$FGHKLN$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L N >>`.

.. picture:: 0396.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 6

For let :g:`$AC$ <0396.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$AD$ <0396.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$FH$ <0396.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`$FK$ <0396.g1:/ F K />` be joined.

| Since then :g:`$ABCM$ <0396.g1:< A B C M >>`, :g:`$ACDM$ <0396.g1:< A C D M >>` are two pyramids which have
| triangular bases and equal height,
| they are to one another as the bases; :just:`[XII. 5] <XII.5>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0396.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$ACD$ <0396.g1:< A C D >>`, so is the
   | pyramid :g:`$ABCM$ <0396.g1:< A B C M >>` to the pyramid :g:`$ACDM$ <0396.g1:< A C D M >>`.

.. assertion::

   | And, *componendo*, as the base :g:`$ABCD$ <0396.g1:< A B C D >>` is to the base :g:`$ACD$ <0396.g1:< A C D >>`,
   | so is the pyramid :g:`$ABCDM$ <0396.g1:< A B C D M >>` to the pyramid :g:`$ACDM$ <0396.g1:< A C D M >>`. :just:`[V. 18] <V.18>`

| But also, as the base :g:`$ACD$ <0396.g1:< A C D >>` is to the base :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>`, so is the
| pyramid :g:`$ACDM$ <0396.g1:< A C D M >>` to the pyramid :g:`$ADEM$ <0396.g1:< A D E M >>`. :just:`[XII. 5] <XII.5>`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, *ex aequali*, as the base :g:`$ABCD$ <0396.g1:< A B C D >>` is to the base
   | :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>`, so is the pyramid :g:`$ABCDM$ <0396.g1:< A B C D M >>` to the pyramid :g:`$ADEM$ <0396.g1:< A D E M >>`.
   | :just:`[V. 22] <V.22>`

.. assertion::

   | And again *componendo*, as the base :g:`$ABCDE$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E >>` is to the
   | base :g:`$ADE$ <0396.g1:< A D E >>`, so is the pyramid :g:`$ABCDEM$ <0396.g1:< A B C D E M >>` to the pyramid
   | :g:`$ADEM$ <0396.g1:< A D E M >>`. :just:`[V. 18] <V.18>`

| Similarly also it can be proved that, as the base :g:`$FGHKL$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L >>`
| is to the base :g:`$FGH$ <0396.g1:< F G H >>`, so is the pyramid :g:`$FGHKLN$ <0396.g1:< F G H K L N >>` to the
| pyramid :g:`$FGHN$ <0396.g1:< F G H N >>`.
