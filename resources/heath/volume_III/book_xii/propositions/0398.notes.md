## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `394 BOOK XII [XII. 7`
- **Euclidean Logic**:
  - The prism $ABCDEF$ has base $\triangle ABC$ and opposite base $\triangle DEF$.
  - Dividing diagonal lines $BD, EC, CD$ are joined.
  - Parallelogram $ABED$ is bisected by diagonal $BD \implies \triangle ABD = \triangle EBD$ [I. 34].
  - Pyramids on equal bases with common vertex $C$ have equal volumes: $\text{pyr}(C, ABD) = \text{pyr}(C, DEB)$ [XII. 5].
  - $\text{pyr}(C, DEB)$ and $\text{pyr}(D, EBC)$ are the identical solid figure contained by the same four planes.
  - Parallelogram $FCBE$ is bisected by diagonal $CE \implies \triangle CEF = \triangle CBE$ [I. 34].
  - Hence $\text{pyr}(D, BCE) = \text{pyr}(D, ECF)$ [XII. 5].
  - Chaining equality establishes that all three pyramids are equal.
