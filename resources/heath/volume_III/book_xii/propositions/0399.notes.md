# Transcription Notes: Folio 0399 (Page 395)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Book**: Book XII

- **Continuity**: Continued from Folio 0398 (Page 394). Proposition 7 concludes with Q.E.D. and its famous Porism (a pyramid is one third of the prism having the same base and equal height). ## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `XII. 7, 8] PROPOSITIONS 7, 8 395`
- **Porism Significance**: The Porism to XII. 7 is a central theorem of stereometry attributed originally to Eudoxus (as noted by Archimedes), rigorously proved here by decomposing the prism into three equivalent pyramids.
- **Notation in Commentary**: Heath uses modern pyramid notation $C\text{-}ABD$ (vertex $C$, base $ABD$) and identity symbol $\equiv$ to indicate that two pyramids with different named vertices/bases represent the exact same tetrahedral solid figure contained by identical planes.
- **Folio Transition**: The bottom sentence breaks mid-clause: "Let there be similar and similarly situated pyramids of" / (Folio 0400: "which the triangles *ABC*, *DEF* are the bases...").
