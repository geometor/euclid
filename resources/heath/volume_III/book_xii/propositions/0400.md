For let the parallelepipedal solids <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> be<br />
completed.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the pyramid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is similar to the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>,<br />
the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< G B C )">$GBC$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span>,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B G )">$ABG$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E H )">$DEH$</span>;<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, as <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>,<br />
and the sides are proportional about equal angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$BM$</span> is similar to the parallelo-<br />
gram <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span>, and <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$MB$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> are similar<br />
to the three <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$MB$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> are equal and<br />
similar to their three opposites,<br />
and the three <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span> are equal and similar to their<br />
three opposites. <span class="justification">[XI. 24]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the solids <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> are contained by<br />
similar planes equal in multitude.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the solid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span> is similar to the solid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But similar parallelepipedal solids are in the triplicate ratio<br />
of their corresponding sides. <span class="justification">[XI. 33]</span></p>
