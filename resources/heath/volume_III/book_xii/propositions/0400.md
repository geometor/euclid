For let the parallelepipedal solids <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> be completed.

Now, since the pyramid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is similar to the pyramid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>, therefore the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E F )">$DEF$</span>,
the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< G B C )">$GBC$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< H E F )">$HEF$</span>,
and the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< A B G )">$ABG$</span> to the angle <span data-ref="0400.g1:< D E H )">$DEH$</span>;
and, as <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B G /">$BG$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>.

And since, as <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, and the sides are proportional about equal angles, therefore the parallelogram <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$BM$</span> is similar to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>.

For the same reason <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> is also similar to <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span>, and <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span> to <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>;
therefore the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$MB$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> are similar to the three <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span>.

But the three parallelograms <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C M A >">$MB$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G K A >">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B C N G >">$BN$</span> are equal and similar to their three opposites, and the three <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H O D >">$EO$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E F R H >">$ER$</span> are equal and similar to their three opposites. [XI. 24]

Therefore the solids <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>, <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> are contained by similar planes equal in multitude.

Therefore the solid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span> is similar to the solid <span data-ref="0400.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span>.

But similar parallelepipedal solids are in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides. [XI. 33]
