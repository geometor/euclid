## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `396 BOOK XII [XII. 8`
- **Euclidean Logic**:
  - Parallelepipeds $BGML$ and $EHQP$ are completed around the triangular pyramids $ABCG$ and $DEFH$.
  - By similarity of the pyramids, corresponding plane face angles are equal and adjacent edges are proportional: $AB : DE = BC : EF = BG : EH$.
  - Therefore, the bounding parallelograms $BM \sim EQ$, $BN \sim ER$, $BK \sim EO$.
  - By XI. 24, opposite faces of a parallelepiped are equal and similar.
  - Hence the parallelepipeds are contained by similar planes equal in multitude, making solid $BGML$ similar to solid $EHQP$.
  - By XI. 33, similar parallelepipeds are in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.
