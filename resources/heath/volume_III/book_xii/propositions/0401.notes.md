## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `XII. 8] PROPOSITION 8 397`
- **Volume Ratio Logic**:
  - Solid parallelepiped : pyramid = $6 : 1$, because prism is $\frac{1}{2}$ of parallelepiped [XI. 28] and pyramid is $\frac{1}{3}$ of prism [XII. 7].
  - Hence ratio of pyramids equals ratio of parallelepipeds, which is the triplicate ratio of corresponding sides ($BC^3 : EF^3$).
- **Porism Authenticity**: Heath observes that Euclid does not employ this Porism in XII. 12 where it would naturally apply; moreover, manuscript P (Vaticanus gr. 190) has the Porism only in the margin, suggesting it may be an ancient interpolation.
