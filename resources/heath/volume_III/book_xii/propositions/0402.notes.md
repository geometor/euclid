- **Continuity**: Folio 0402 begins Proposition 9, which establishes that triangular pyramids are equal if and only if their bases are reciprocally proportional to their heights. The proof of Part I continues onto Folio 0403 (Page 399).

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `398 BOOK XII [XII. 9`
- **Enunciation**: The reciprocal proportionality of bases and heights for equal pyramids (analogous to XI. 34 for parallelepipeds).
- **Proof Structure**:
  - Completes parallelepipeds $BGML$ and $EHQP$ on double bases ($BM = 2 \triangle ABC$, $EQ = 2 \triangle DEF$) with equal heights.
  - Since pyramids are equal, their sextuples (the parallelepipeds) are equal.
  - Applies XI. 34 (equal parallelepipeds have bases reciprocally proportional to heights).
  - Since base parallelograms $BM, EQ$ are double the triangular bases $ABC, DEF$, their ratio equals the ratio of the triangular bases [I. 34].
