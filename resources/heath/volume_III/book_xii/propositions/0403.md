<p class="sub-clause">But the height of the solid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> is the same with the<br />
height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>,<br />
and the height of the solid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span> is the same with the<br />
height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so is the<br />
height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> to the height of the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore in the pyramids <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>, <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> the bases are<br />
reciprocally proportional to the heights.</p>

Next, in the pyramids <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>, <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> let the bases be<br />
reciprocally proportional to the heights ;<br />
that is, as the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so let the height<br />
of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> be to the height of the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> ;<br />
I say that the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is equal to the pyramid<br />
<span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For, with the same construction,<br />
since, as the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so is the height<br />
of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> to the height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>,<br />
while, as the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C >">$ABC$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F >">$DEF$</span>, so is the<br />
parallelogram <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B C M A >">$BM$</span> to the parallelogram <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the parallelogram <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B C M A >">$BM$</span> is to the parallelogram<br />
<span data-ref="0402.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, so is the height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> to the height of<br />
the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> is the same with<br />
the height of the parallelepiped <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span>,<br />
and the height of the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is the same with the<br />
height of the parallelepiped <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B C M A >">$BM$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E F Q D >">$EQ$</span>, so is the height<br />
of the parallelepiped <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> to the height of the parallelepi-<br />
ped <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But those parallelepipedal solids in which the bases are<br />
reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal ; <span class="justification">[XI. 34]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span> is equal to the<br />
parallelepipedal solid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< A B C G >">$ABCG$</span> is a sixth part of <span data-ref="0402.g1:< B G M L >">$BGML$</span>, and<br />
the pyramid <span data-ref="0402.g1:< D E F H >">$DEFH$</span> a sixth part of the parallelepiped<br />
<span data-ref="0402.g1:< E H Q P >">$EHQP$</span> ;</p>
