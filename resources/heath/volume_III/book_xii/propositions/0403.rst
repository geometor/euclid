:title: Proposition 9

.. subclause::

   But the height of the solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` is the same with the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`, and the height of the solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is the same with the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the pyramids :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights.

Next, in the pyramids :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` let the bases be reciprocally proportional to the heights ; that is, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so let the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` be to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` ; I say that the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is equal to the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>`.

.. subclause::

   For, with the same construction, since, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`, while, as the base :g:`$ABC$ <0402.g1:< A B C >>` is to the base :g:`$DEF$ <0402.g1:< D E F >>`, so is the parallelogram :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` to the parallelogram :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the parallelogram :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to the parallelogram :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` to the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>`. :just:`[V. 11] <V.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the height of the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` is the same with the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>`, and the height of the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is the same with the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the base :g:`$BM$ <0402.g1:< B C M A >>` is to the base :g:`$EQ$ <0402.g1:< E F Q D >>`, so is the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` to the height of the parallelepiped :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`.

.. subclause::

   But those parallelepipedal solids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal ; :just:`[XI. 34] <XI.34>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>` is equal to the parallelepipedal solid :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>`.

.. subclause::

   And the pyramid :g:`$ABCG$ <0402.g1:< A B C G >>` is a sixth part of :g:`$BGML$ <0402.g1:< B G M L >>`, and the pyramid :g:`$DEFH$ <0402.g1:< D E F H >>` a sixth part of the parallelepiped :g:`$EHQP$ <0402.g1:< E H Q P >>` ;
