The volumes of the pyramids are respectively one sixth part of the volumes<br />
of the parallelepipeds described, as in the figure, on double the bases and with<br />
the same heights as the pyramids.

I. Thus the parallelepipeds are equal if the pyramids are equal.<br />
And, the parallelepipeds being equal, their bases are reciprocally propor-<br />
tional to their heights ; <span class="justification">[XI. 34]</span><br />
hence the bases of the equal pyramids (which are the halves of the bases of<br />
the parallelepipeds) are proportional to their heights.

II. If the bases of the pyramids are reciprocally proportional to their<br />
heights, so are the bases of the parallelepipeds to their heights (since the bases<br />
of the parallelepipeds are double of the bases of the pyramids respectively).<br />
Consequently the parallelepipeds are equal. <span class="justification">[XI. 34]</span><br />
Therefore their sixth parts, the pyramids, are also equal.
