  - The inscribed prism is half the circumscribed prism. Since cylinder < circumscribed prism, inscribed prism > half the cylinder.

  - By repeatedly subtracting more than half (Euclid X. 1 principle of exhaustion), the remaining cylindrical segments can be made less than any assigned volume excess.
