## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Running Header**: `XII. 10] PROPOSITIONS 9, 10 401`
- **Exhaustion Mechanics**:
  - The circumscribed square is twice the inscribed square [IV. 7].
  - Prisms of equal height are in the ratio of their bases [XI. 32].
  - The inscribed prism is half the circumscribed prism. Since cylinder < circumscribed prism, inscribed prism > half the cylinder.
  - Bisecting arcs $AB, BC, CD, DA$ at $E, F, G, H$ creates triangles each greater than half the circular segment [XII. 2].
  - Erecting triangular prisms of equal height on these triangles, each prism is greater than half the corresponding cylindrical segment.
  - By repeatedly subtracting more than half (Euclid X. 1 principle of exhaustion), the remaining cylindrical segments can be made less than any assigned volume excess.
- **Folio Transition**: Folio ends with the ongoing participle clause: "Thus, bisecting the circumferences that are left, joining".
