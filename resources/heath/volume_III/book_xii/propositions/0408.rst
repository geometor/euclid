:title: Proposition 10 (conclusion and commentary)
:proposition: 10

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$AEBFCGDH$ <0404.g1:< A E B F C G D H >>` is the base and the vertex the same with that of the cone, is greater than a third part of the cylinder.

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$AEBFCGDH$ <0404.g1:< A E B F C G D H >>` is the base and the vertex the same with that of the cone is a third part of the prism of which the polygon :g:`$AEBFCGDH$ <0404.g1:< A E B F C G D H >>` is the base and the height is the same with that of the cylinder;

.. assertion::

   therefore the prism of which the polygon :g:`$AEBFCGDH$ <0404.g1:< A E B F C G D H >>` is the base and the height is the same with that of the cylinder is greater than the cylinder of which the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0404.g1:( A B C D )>` is the base.

.. assertion::

   But it is also less, for it is enclosed by it: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the cylinder is not less than triple of the cone.

.. subclause::

   But it was proved that neither is it greater than triple;

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinder is triple of the cone; hence the cone is a third part of the cylinder.

.. assertion::

   Therefore etc.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

We observe the use in this proposition of the term "parallelepipedal prism," which recalls :person:`Heron <heron>`'s "parallelogrammic" or "parallel-sided prism."

The course of the proof is exactly the same as in XII. 2, except that an arithmetical fraction takes the place of a ratio which, being incommensurable, could only be expressed as a ratio. Consequently we do not need proportions in this proposition, as we did in XII. 2, and shall again in XII. 11, etc.

Euclid *exhausts* the cylinder and cone respectively by setting up prisms and pyramids of the same height on the successive regular polygons inscribed in the circle which is the common base, viz. the square, the regular polygon of 8 sides, that of 16 sides, etc.

If :g:`$AB$ <0408.g1:/ A B />` be the side of one polygon, we obtain two sides of the next by bisecting the arc :g:`$ACB$ <0408.g1:( A C B )>` and joining :g:`$AC$ <0408.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0408.g1:/ C B />`. Draw the tangent :g:`$DE$ <0408.g1:/ D E />` at :g:`$C$ <0408.g1:C>` and complete the parallelogram :g:`$ABED$ <0408.g1:< A B E D >>`.

Now suppose a prism erected on the polygon of which :g:`$AB$ <0408.g1:/ A B />` is a side, and of the same height as that of the cylinder.

To obtain the prism of the same height on the next polygon we add all the triangular prisms of the same height on the bases :g:`$ACB$ <0408.g1:< A C B >>` and the rest.

Now the prism on :g:`$ACB$ <0408.g1:< A C B >>` is half the prism of the same height on the $\square$ :g:`$ABED$ <0408.g1:< A B E D >>` as base. :just:`[cf. XI. 28]`
