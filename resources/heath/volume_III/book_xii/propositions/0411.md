Let the circumferences <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E Q F )">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( F R G )">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( G S H )">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( H P E )">$HE$</span> be bisected at the points <span data-ref="0410.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:Q">$Q$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:S">$S$</span>,  
and let <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ Q F /">$QF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ R G /">$RG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ S H /">$SH$</span> be joined.

Therefore each of the triangles <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E >">$HPE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E Q F >">$EQF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< F R G >">$FRG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< G S H >">$GSH$</span> is greater than the half of that segment of the circle which is about it.

On each of the triangles <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E >">$HPE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E Q F >">$EQF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< F R G >">$FRG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< G S H >">$GSH$</span> let there be set up a pyramid of equal height with the cone;  
therefore, also, each of the pyramids so set up is greater than the half of that segment of the cone which is about it.

Thus, bisecting the circumferences which are left, joining straight lines, setting up on each of the triangles pyramids of equal height with the cone,  
and doing this continually,  
we shall leave some segments of the cone which will be less than the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:X">$X$</span>. [X. 1]

Let such be left, and let them be the segments on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ Q F /">$QF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ R G /">$RG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ S H /">$SH$</span>;  
therefore the remainder, the pyramid of which the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span> is the base and the height the same with that of the cone, is greater than the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>.

Let there also be inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> similar and similarly situated to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>,  
and on it let a pyramid be set up of equal height with the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>.

Since then, as the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>, [XII. 1]  
while, as the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, [XII. 2]  
therefore also, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>.

But, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> to the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>,  
and, as the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> is to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>, so is the pyramid of which the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:L">$L$</span> the vertex to the pyramid of which the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:N">$N$</span> the vertex. [XII. 6]
