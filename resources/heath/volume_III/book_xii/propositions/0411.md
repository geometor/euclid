Let the circumferences <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E Q F )">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( F R G )">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( G S H )">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:( H P E )">$HE$</span> be bisected at<br />
the points <span data-ref="0410.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:Q">$Q$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:S">$S$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ Q F /">$QF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ R G /">$RG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ S H /">$SH$</span> be joined.

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the triangles <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E >">$HPE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E Q F >">$EQF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< F R G >">$FRG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< G S H >">$GSH$</span><br />
is greater than the half of that segment of the circle which is<br />
about it.</p>

On each of the triangles <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E >">$HPE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E Q F >">$EQF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< F R G >">$FRG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:< G S H >">$GSH$</span> let<br />
there be set up a pyramid of equal height with the cone;
<p class="assertion">therefore, also, each of the pyramids so set up is greater than<br />
the half of that segment of the cone which is about it.</p>

Thus, bisecting the circumferences which are left, joining<br />
straight lines, setting up on each of the triangles pyramids of<br />
equal height with the cone,<br />
and doing this continually,<br />
we shall leave some segments of the cone which will be less<br />
than the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:X">$X$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 1]</span>

Let such be left, and let them be the segments on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:/ P E /">$PE$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ Q F /">$QF$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ R G /">$RG$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ S H /">$SH$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the pyramid of which the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span> is the base and the height the same with that<br />
of the cone, is greater than the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>.</p>

Let there also be inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> similar and similarly situated to the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>,<br />
and on it let a pyramid be set up of equal height with the cone<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then, as the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so<br />
is the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>,<br />
<span class="justification">[XII. 1]</span><br />
while, as the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to the square on <span data-ref="0410.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>, so is the<br />
circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, <span class="justification">[XII. 2]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so<br />
is the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> to the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is the<br />
cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> to the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>,<br />
and, as the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> is to the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span>, so is the pyramid of which the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:L">$L$</span> the vertex to the<br />
pyramid of which the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span> is the base and<br />
the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:N">$N$</span> the vertex. <span class="justification">[XII. 6]</span></p>
