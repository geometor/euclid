| Let the circumferences :g:`$EF$ <0410.g1:( E Q F )>`, :g:`$FG$ <0410.g1:( F R G )>`, :g:`$GH$ <0410.g1:( G S H )>`, :g:`$HE$ <0410.g1:( H P E )>` be bisected at
| the points :g:`$P$ <0410.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0410.g1:Q>`, :g:`$R$ <0410.g1:R>`, :g:`$S$ <0410.g1:S>`,
| and let :g:`$HP$ <0410.g1:/ H P />`, :g:`$PE$ <0410.g1:/ P E />`, :g:`$EQ$ <0410.g1:/ E Q />`, :g:`$QF$ <0410.g1:/ Q F />`, :g:`$FR$ <0410.g1:/ F R />`, :g:`$RG$ <0410.g1:/ R G />`, :g:`$GS$ <0410.g1:/ G S />`, :g:`$SH$ <0410.g1:/ S H />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore each of the triangles :g:`$HPE$ <0410.g1:< H P E >>`, :g:`$EQF$ <0410.g1:< E Q F >>`, :g:`$FRG$ <0410.g1:< F R G >>`, :g:`$GSH$ <0410.g1:< G S H >>`
   | is greater than the half of that segment of the circle which is
   | about it.

| On each of the triangles :g:`$HPE$ <0410.g1:< H P E >>`, :g:`$EQF$ <0410.g1:< E Q F >>`, :g:`$FRG$ <0410.g1:< F R G >>`, :g:`$GSH$ <0410.g1:< G S H >>` let
| there be set up a pyramid of equal height with the cone;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, also, each of the pyramids so set up is greater than
   | the half of that segment of the cone which is about it.

| Thus, bisecting the circumferences which are left, joining
| straight lines, setting up on each of the triangles pyramids of
| equal height with the cone,
| and doing this continually,
| we shall leave some segments of the cone which will be less
| than the solid :g:`$X$ <0410.g1:X>`. :just:`[X. 1] <X.1>`

| Let such be left, and let them be the segments on :g:`$HP$ <0410.g1:/ H P />`,
| :g:`$PE$ <0410.g1:/ P E />`, :g:`$EQ$ <0410.g1:/ E Q />`, :g:`$QF$ <0410.g1:/ Q F />`, :g:`$FR$ <0410.g1:/ F R />`, :g:`$RG$ <0410.g1:/ R G />`, :g:`$GS$ <0410.g1:/ G S />`, :g:`$SH$ <0410.g1:/ S H />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, the pyramid of which the polygon
   | :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>` is the base and the height the same with that
   | of the cone, is greater than the solid :g:`$O$ <0410.g1:O>`.

| Let there also be inscribed in the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0410.g1:( A B C D )>` the
| polygon :g:`$DTAUBVCW$ <0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >>` similar and similarly situated to the
| polygon :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>`,
| and on it let a pyramid be set up of equal height with the cone
| :g:`$AL$ <0410.g1:< A L >>`.

.. subclause::

   | Since then, as the square on :g:`$AC$ <0410.g1:/ A C />` is to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0410.g1:/ E G />`, so
   | is the polygon :g:`$DTAUBVCW$ <0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >>` to the polygon :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>`,
   | :just:`[XII. 1] <XII.1>`
   | while, as the square on :g:`$AC$ <0410.g1:/ A C />` is to the square on :g:`$EG$ <0410.g1:/ E G />`, so is the
   | circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0410.g1:( A B C D )>` to the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0410.g1:( E F G H )>`, :just:`[XII. 2] <XII.2>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0410.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0410.g1:( E F G H )>`, so
   | is the polygon :g:`$DTAUBVCW$ <0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >>` to the polygon :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But, as the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0410.g1:( A B C D )>` is to the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0410.g1:( E F G H )>`, so is the
   | cone :g:`$AL$ <0410.g1:< A L >>` to the solid :g:`$O$ <0410.g1:O>`,
   | and, as the polygon :g:`$DTAUBVCW$ <0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >>` is to the polygon
   | :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>`, so is the pyramid of which the polygon
   | :g:`$DTAUBVCW$ <0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >>` is the base and the point :g:`$L$ <0410.g1:L>` the vertex to the
   | pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$HPEQFRGS$ <0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >>` is the base and
   | the point :g:`$N$ <0410.g1:N>` the vertex. :just:`[XII. 6] <XII.6>`
