<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> is to the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>, so is the<br />
pyramid of which the polygon <span data-ref="0410.g1:< D T A U B V C W >">$DTAUBVCW$</span> is the base and<br />
the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:L">$L$</span> the vertex to the pyramid of which the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:< H P E Q F R G S >">$HPEQFRGS$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0410.g1:N">$N$</span> the vertex; <span class="justification">[V. 11]</span><br />
therefore, alternately, as the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> is to the pyramid in it,<br />
so is the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span> to the pyramid in the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> is greater than the pyramid in it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span> is also greater than the pyramid in the<br />
cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">But it is also less:<br />
which is absurd.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> is not to any solid less than the<br />
cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span> as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that neither is the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span> to<br />
any solid less than the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> is to the<br />
circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>.</p>

I say next that neither is the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> to any solid greater<br />
than the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span> as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For, if possible, let it be in that ratio to a greater solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, inversely, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> is to the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>, so is the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span> to the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the solid <span data-ref="0410.g1:O">$O$</span> is to the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>, so is the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span><br />
to some solid less than the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>, so<br />
is the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span> to some solid less than the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span>:<br />
which was proved impossible.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> is not to any solid greater than<br />
the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span> as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it was proved that neither is it in this ratio to a less<br />
solid;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the circle <span data-ref="0410.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is<br />
the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< A L >">$AL$</span> to the cone <span data-ref="0410.g1:< E N >">$EN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the cone is to the cone, so is the cylinder to the<br />
cylinder,<br />
for each is triple of each; <span class="justification">[XII. 10]</span></p>
