PROPOSITION 12.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   Similar cones and cylinders are to one another in the triplicate ratio of the diameters in their bases.

| Let there be similar cones and cylinders,
| let the circles :g:`$ABCD$ <0414.g1:( A B C D )>`, :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:( E F G H )>` be their bases, :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />` the
| diameters of the bases, and :g:`$KL$ <0414.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$MN$ <0414.g1:/ M N />` the axes of the cones
| and cylinders;
| I say that the cone of which the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0414.g1:( A B C D )>` is the base and
| the point :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex has to the cone of which the circle
| :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:( E F G H )>` is the base and the point :g:`$N$ <0414.g1:N>` the vertex the ratio
| triplicate of that which :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` has to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`.

.. picture:: 0414.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12

.. subclause::

   | For, if the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` has not to the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>`
   | the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` has to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`,

.. assertion::

   | the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` will have that triplicate ratio either to
   | some solid less than the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>` or to a greater.

.. subclause::

   First, let it have that triplicate ratio to a less solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>`.

| Let the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:< E F G H >>` be inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:( E F G H )>`;
| :just:`[IV. 6] <IV.6>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:< E F G H >>` is greater than the half of the
   | circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:( E F G H )>`.

| Now let there be set up on the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:< E F G H >>` a pyramid
| having the same vertex with the cone;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the pyramid so set up is greater than the half part
   | of the cone.
