Let the circumferences <span data-ref="0414.g1:( E P F )">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:( F Q G )">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:( G R H )">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:( H S E )">$HE$</span> be bisected at<br />
the points <span data-ref="0414.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:Q">$Q$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:S">$S$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F Q /">$FQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ Q G /">$QG$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ G R /">$GR$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ R H /">$RH$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ H S /">$HS$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ S E /">$SE$</span> be joined.

<p class="assertion">Therefore each of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< E P F >">$EPF$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F Q G >">$FQG$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< G R H >">$GRH$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< H S E >">$HSE$</span><br />
is also greater than the half part of that segment of the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> which is about it.</p>

Now on each of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< E P F >">$EPF$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F Q G >">$FQG$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< G R H >">$GRH$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< H S E >">$HSE$</span><br />
let a pyramid be set up having the same vertex with the cone;
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the pyramids so set up is also greater than<br />
the half part of that segment of the cone which is about it.</p>

Thus, bisecting the circumferences so left, joining straight<br />
lines, setting up on each of the triangles pyramids having the<br />
same vertex with the cone,<br />
and doing this continually,
<p class="assertion">we shall leave some segments of the cone which will be less<br />
than the excess by which the cone <span data-ref="0414.g1:< E F G H N >">$EFGHN$</span> exceeds the<br />
solid <span data-ref="0414.g1:O">$O$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 1]</span></p>

Let such be left, and let them be the segments on <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F Q /">$FQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ Q G /">$QG$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ G R /">$GR$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ R H /">$RH$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ H S /">$HS$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ S E /">$SE$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the pyramid of which the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >">$EPFQGRHS$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0414.g1:N">$N$</span> the vertex, is<br />
greater than the solid <span data-ref="0414.g1:O">$O$</span>.</p>

Let there be also inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0414.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0414.g1:< A T B U C V D W >">$ATBUCVDW$</span> similar and similarly situated to the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >">$EPFQGRHS$</span>,<br />
and let there be set up on the polygon <span data-ref="0414.g1:< A T B U C V D W >">$ATBUCVDW$</span> a<br />
pyramid having the same vertex with the cone ;<br />
of the triangles containing the pyramid of which the polygon<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< A T B U C V D W >">$ATBUCVDW$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0414.g1:L">$L$</span> the vertex let<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< L B T >">$LBT$</span> be one,<br />
and of the triangles containing the pyramid of which the<br />
polygon <span data-ref="0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >">$EPFQGRHS$</span> is the base and the point <span data-ref="0414.g1:N">$N$</span> the vertex<br />
let <span data-ref="0414.g1:< N F P >">$NFP$</span> be one;<br />
and let <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the cone <span data-ref="0414.g1:< A B C D L >">$ABCDL$</span> is similar to the cone<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< E F G H N >">$EFGHN$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, so is the axis <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> to the axis <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. Def. 24]</span></p>
