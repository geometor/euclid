But, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>;
therefore also, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.

And, alternately, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>. [^412_1]

And the sides are proportional about equal angles, namely the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K L )">$BKL$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M N )">$FMN$</span>;
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K L >">$BKL$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M N >">$FMN$</span>. [^412_2]

Again, since, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span>,
and they are about equal angles, namely the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T )">$BKT$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P )">$FMP$</span>,
inasmuch as, whatever part the angle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T )">$BKT$</span> is of the four right angles at the centre <span data-ref="0414.g1:K">$K$</span>, the same part also is the angle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P )">$FMP$</span> of the four right angles at the centre <span data-ref="0414.g1:M">$M$</span>;
since then the sides are proportional about equal angles,
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T >">$BKT$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P >">$FMP$</span>. [^412_3]

Again, since it was proved that, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span>, and <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T K /">$TK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>;
and the sides are proportional about equal angles, namely the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< T K L )">$TKL$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< P M N )">$PMN$</span>, for they are right;
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L K T >">$LKT$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< N M P >">$NMP$</span>. [^412_4]

And since, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L K B >">$LKB$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< N M F >">$NMF$</span>,
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N F /">$NF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>,
and, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T >">$BKT$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P >">$FMP$</span>,
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B T /">$BT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F P /">$FP$</span>,
therefore, *ex aequali*, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B T /">$BT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N F /">$NF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F P /">$FP$</span>. [^412_5]

Again since, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L T K >">$LTK$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< N P M >">$NPM$</span>,
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L T /">$LT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T K /">$TK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span>,
and, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< T K B >">$TKB$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< P M F >">$PMF$</span>,
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T B /">$TB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span>;
therefore, *ex aequali*, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L T /">$LT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T B /">$TB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span>. [^412_6]
