<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">And, alternately, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the sides are proportional about equal angles, namely<br />
the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K L )">$BKL$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M N )">$FMN$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K L >">$BKL$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M N >">$FMN$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span>,<br />
and they are about equal angles, namely the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T )">$BKT$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P )">$FMP$</span>,<br />
inasmuch as, whatever part the angle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T )">$BKT$</span> is of the four<br />
right angles at the centre <span data-ref="0414.g1:K">$K$</span>, the same part also is the angle<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P )">$FMP$</span> of the four right angles at the centre <span data-ref="0414.g1:M">$M$</span>;<br />
since then the sides are proportional about equal angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T >">$BKT$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P >">$FMP$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since it was proved that, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span>, and <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T K /">$TK$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>;</p>
<p class="sub-clause">and the sides are proportional about equal angles, namely<br />
the angles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< T K L )">$TKL$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< P M N )">$PMN$</span>, for they are right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L K T >">$LKT$</span> is similar to the triangle <span data-ref="0414.g1:< N M P >">$NMP$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[VI. 6]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L K B >">$LKB$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< N M F >">$NMF$</span>,<br />
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N F /">$NF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F M /">$FM$</span>,<br />
and, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< B K T >">$BKT$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< F M P >">$FMP$</span>,<br />
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B T /">$BT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F P /">$FP$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, <em>ex aequali</em>, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ B T /">$BT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N F /">$NF$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ F P /">$FP$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again since, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< L T K >">$LTK$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0414.g1:< N P M >">$NPM$</span>,<br />
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L T /">$LT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T K /">$TK$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P M /">$PM$</span>,<br />
and, owing to the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0414.g1:< T K B >">$TKB$</span>, <span data-ref="0414.g1:< P M F >">$PMF$</span>,<br />
as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ K T /">$KT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T B /">$TB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ M P /">$MP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, <em>ex aequali</em>, as <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ L T /">$LT$</span> is to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ T B /">$TB$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> to <span data-ref="0414.g1:/ P F /">$PF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 22]</span></p>
