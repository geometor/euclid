.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` is to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`, so is :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` to :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` is to :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />`, so is :g:`$KL$ <0414.g1:/ K L />` to :g:`$MN$ <0414.g1:/ M N />`.

.. assertion::

   And, alternately, as :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` is to :g:`$KL$ <0414.g1:/ K L />`, so is :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />` to :g:`$MN$ <0414.g1:/ M N />`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   And the sides are proportional about :eq:`equal <0414.g1:< B K L ) = 0414.g1:< F M N )>` angles, namely the angles :g:`$BKL$ <0414.g1:< B K L )>`, :g:`$FMN$ <0414.g1:< F M N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$BKL$ <0414.g1:< B K L >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$FMN$ <0414.g1:< F M N >>`. :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since, as :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` is to :g:`$KT$ <0414.g1:/ K T />`, so is :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />` to :g:`$MP$ <0414.g1:/ M P />`, and they are about :eq:`equal <0414.g1:< B K T ) = 0414.g1:< F M P )>` angles, namely the angles :g:`$BKT$ <0414.g1:< B K T )>`, :g:`$FMP$ <0414.g1:< F M P )>`, inasmuch as, whatever part the angle :g:`$BKT$ <0414.g1:< B K T )>` is of the four right angles at the centre :g:`$K$ <0414.g1:K>`, the same part also is the angle :g:`$FMP$ <0414.g1:< F M P )>` of the four right angles at the centre :g:`$M$ <0414.g1:M>`; since then the sides are proportional about :eq:`equal <0414.g1:< B K T ) = 0414.g1:< F M P )>` angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$BKT$ <0414.g1:< B K T >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$FMP$ <0414.g1:< F M P >>`. :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since it was proved that, as :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` is to :g:`$KL$ <0414.g1:/ K L />`, so is :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />` to :g:`$MN$ <0414.g1:/ M N />`, while :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />` is :eq:`equal <0414.g1:/ B K / = 0414.g1:/ K T /; 0414.g1:/ F M / = 0414.g1:/ P M />` to :g:`$KT$ <0414.g1:/ K T />`, and :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />` to :g:`$PM$ <0414.g1:/ P M />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$TK$ <0414.g1:/ T K />` is to :g:`$KL$ <0414.g1:/ K L />`, so is :g:`$PM$ <0414.g1:/ P M />` to :g:`$MN$ <0414.g1:/ M N />`;

.. subclause::

   and the sides are proportional about :eq:`equal <0414.g1:< T K L ) = 0414.g1:< P M N )>` angles, namely the angles :g:`$TKL$ <0414.g1:< T K L )>`, :g:`$PMN$ <0414.g1:< P M N )>`, for they are :eq:`right <0414.g1:< T K L ) = 0414.g1:< P M N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$LKT$ <0414.g1:< L K T >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`$NMP$ <0414.g1:< N M P >>`. :just:`[VI. 6] <VI.6>`

.. subclause::

   And since, owing to the similarity of the triangles :g:`$LKB$ <0414.g1:< L K B >>`, :g:`$NMF$ <0414.g1:< N M F >>`, as :g:`$LB$ <0414.g1:/ L B />` is to :g:`$BK$ <0414.g1:/ B K />`, so is :g:`$NF$ <0414.g1:/ N F />` to :g:`$FM$ <0414.g1:/ F M />`, and, owing to the similarity of the triangles :g:`$BKT$ <0414.g1:< B K T >>`, :g:`$FMP$ <0414.g1:< F M P >>`, as :g:`$KB$ <0414.g1:/ K B />` is to :g:`$BT$ <0414.g1:/ B T />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0414.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FP$ <0414.g1:/ F P />`,

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$LB$ <0414.g1:/ L B />` is to :g:`$BT$ <0414.g1:/ B T />`, so is :g:`$NF$ <0414.g1:/ N F />` to :g:`$FP$ <0414.g1:/ F P />`.

.. subclause::

   Again since, owing to the similarity of the triangles :g:`$LTK$ <0414.g1:< L T K >>`, :g:`$NPM$ <0414.g1:< N P M >>`, as :g:`$LT$ <0414.g1:/ L T />` is to :g:`$TK$ <0414.g1:/ T K />`, so is :g:`$NP$ <0414.g1:/ N P />` to :g:`$PM$ <0414.g1:/ P M />`, and, owing to the similarity of the triangles :g:`$TKB$ <0414.g1:< T K B >>`, :g:`$PMF$ <0414.g1:< P M F >>`, as :g:`$KT$ <0414.g1:/ K T />` is to :g:`$TB$ <0414.g1:/ T B />`, so is :g:`$MP$ <0414.g1:/ M P />` to :g:`$PF$ <0414.g1:/ P F />` ;

.. assertion:: [V. 22] <V.22>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <ex-aequali>`, as :g:`$LT$ <0414.g1:/ L T />` is to :g:`$TB$ <0414.g1:/ T B />`, so is :g:`$NP$ <0414.g1:/ N P />` to :g:`$PF$ <0414.g1:/ P F />`.
