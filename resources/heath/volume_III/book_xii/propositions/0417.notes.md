# Transcription Notes: Folio 0417 (Page 413)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Page 413 continues Proposition 12.
- **Folio Continuity**: Follows Folio 0416 (Page 412) and continues without break into Folio 0418 (Page 414). The catch-phrase at the bottom ("But, by hypothesis, the cone of which the circle ABCD") continues directly with "is the base and the point L the vertex...".
- **Mathematical Scope**: Proves that the lateral faces of the partial pyramids $\triangle LTB$ and $\triangle NPF$ are similar (VI. 5, VI. Def. 1), establishing similarity of partial triangular pyramids (XI. Def. 9). By XII. 8, these partial pyramids are in the triplicate ratio of base radii. Summing antecedents and consequents (V. 12) yields that the whole inscribed polygonal pyramids are in the triplicate ratio of base diameters.
