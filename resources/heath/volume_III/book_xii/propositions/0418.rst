:title: Proposition 12

.. subclause::

   is the base and the point :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex has also to the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` has to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the cone of which the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0414.g1:( A B C D )>` is the base and the point :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex is to the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>`, so is the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$ATBUCVDW$ <0414.g1:< A T B U C V D W >>` is the base and :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex to the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$EPFQGRHS$ <0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >>` is the base and the point :g:`$N$ <0414.g1:N>` the vertex ; therefore, alternately, as the cone of which the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0414.g1:( A B C D )>` is the base and :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex is to the pyramid contained in it of which the polygon :g:`$ATBUCVDW$ <0414.g1:< A T B U C V D W >>` is the base and :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex, so is the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>` to the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$EPFQGRHS$ <0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >>` is the base and :g:`$N$ <0414.g1:N>` the vertex. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the said cone is greater than the pyramid in it; for it encloses it.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>` is also greater than the pyramid of which the polygon :g:`$EPFQGRHS$ <0414.g1:< E P F Q G R H S >>` is the base and :g:`$N$ <0414.g1:N>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But it is also less :

.. assertion::

   which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the cone of which the circle :g:`$ABCD$ <0414.g1:( A B C D )>` is the base and :g:`$L$ <0414.g1:L>` the vertex has not to any solid less than the cone of which the circle :g:`$EFGH$ <0414.g1:( E F G H )>` is the base and the point :g:`$N$ <0414.g1:N>` the vertex the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` has to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that neither has the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>` to any solid less than the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />` has to :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />`.

I say next that neither has the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` to any solid greater than the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />` has to :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   For, if possible, let it have that ratio to a greater solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, inversely, the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>` has to the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />` has to :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the solid :g:`$O$ <0414.g1:O>` is to the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>`, so is the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>` to some solid less than the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the cone :g:`$EFGHN$ <0414.g1:< E F G H N >>` also has to some solid less than the cone :g:`$ABCDL$ <0414.g1:< A B C D L >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$FH$ <0414.g1:/ F H />` has to :g:`$BD$ <0414.g1:/ B D />`: which was proved impossible.
