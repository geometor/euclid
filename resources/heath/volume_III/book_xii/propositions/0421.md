II. Suppose, if possible, that<br />
$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d : d') = X : O,$$<br />
where $O$ is *greater* than $X'$;<br />
then<br />
$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d : d') = Z : X',$$<br />
or, inversely,<br />
$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d' : d) = X' : Z,$$<br />
where $Z$ is *some solid less than* $X$.

This is proved impossible by the exact method of Part I.<br />
Hence $O$ cannot be either greater or less than $X'$,<br />
and<br />
$$X : X' = (\text{ratio triplicate of ratio } d : d').$$

### PROPOSITION 13.

<p class="enunciation">If a cylinder be cut by a plane which is parallel to its<br />
opposite planes, then, as the cylinder is to the cylinder, so will<br />
the axis be to the axis.</p>
