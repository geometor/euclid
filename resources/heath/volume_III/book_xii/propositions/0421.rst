II. Suppose, if possible, that 

$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d : d') = X : O,$$

where $O$ is *greater* than $X'$; then

$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d : d') = Z : X',$$

 
or, inversely, 

$$(\text{ratio triplicate of } d' : d) = X' : Z,$$

 
where $Z$ is *some solid less than* $X$.

This is proved impossible by the exact method of Part I. Hence $O$ cannot be either greater or less than $X'$, and

$$X : X' = (\text{ratio triplicate of ratio } d : d').$$

PROPOSITION 13.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   If a cylinder be cut by a plane which is parallel to its opposite planes, then, as the cylinder is to the cylinder, so will the axis be to the axis.

For let the cylinder :g:`AD <0421.g1:< A D >>` be cut by the plane :g:`GH <0421.g1:( G H )>` which is parallel to the opposite planes :g:`AB <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`CD <0421.g1:( C D )>`, and let the plane :g:`GH <0421.g1:( G H )>` meet the axis at the point :g:`K <0421.g1:K>`; I say that, as the cylinder :g:`BG <0421.g1:< B G >>` is to the cylinder :g:`GD <0421.g1:< G D >>`, so is the axis :g:`EK <0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`KF <0421.g1:/ K F />`.

For let the axis :g:`EF <0421.g1:/ E F />` be produced in both directions to the points :g:`L <0421.g1:L>`, :g:`M <0421.g1:M>`, and let there be set out any number whatever of axes :g:`EN <0421.g1:/ E N />`, :g:`NL <0421.g1:/ N L />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ E N / = 0421.g1:/ N L / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`EK <0421.g1:/ E K />`, and any number whatever :g:`FO <0421.g1:/ F O />`, :g:`OM <0421.g1:/ O M />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ F O / = 0421.g1:/ O M / = 0421.g1:/ F K />` to :g:`FK <0421.g1:/ F K />`; and let the cylinder :g:`PW <0421.g1:< P W >>` on the axis :g:`LM <0421.g1:/ L M />` be conceived of which the circles :g:`PQ <0421.g1:( P Q )>`, :g:`VW <0421.g1:( V W )>` are the bases.

Let planes be carried through the points :g:`N <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0421.g1:O>` parallel to :g:`AB <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`CD <0421.g1:( C D )>` and to the bases of the cylinder :g:`PW <0421.g1:< P W >>`, and let them produce the circles :g:`RS <0421.g1:( R S )>`, :g:`TU <0421.g1:( T U )>` about the centres :g:`N <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0421.g1:O>`.
