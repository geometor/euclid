<p class="assertion">therefore, whatever multiple the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is of the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>,<br />

<p class="assertion">For the same reason, whatever multiple the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is<br />

<p class="assertion">And, if the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is equal to the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, the cylinder<br />
<span data-ref="0421.g1:< Q G >">$QG$</span> will also be equal to the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< G W >">$GW$</span>,<br />

greater than the cylinder,<br />
and if less, less.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Thus, there being four magnitudes, the axes <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span><br />

and equimultiples of the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> and of the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< G D >">$GD$</span>,<br />
namely the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> and the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< G W >">$GW$</span>;<br />
and it has been proved that,<br />
if the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is in excess of the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< Q G >">$QG$</span><br />
is also in excess of the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< G W >">$GW$</span>,<br />
if equal, equal,<br />
and if less, less.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, as the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> is to the axis <span data-ref="0421.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, so is the<br />
cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< B G >">$BG$</span> to the cylinder <span data-ref="0421.g1:< G D >">$GD$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. Def. 5]</span></p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
