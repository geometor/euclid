## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Equimultiple Argument**: Classic Eudoxian proportion technique (Definition 5 of Book V) applied to cylinders and their axes. Multiples of axes *LN, NE, EK* correspond to multiples of cylinders *QR, RB, BG*.
- **Citations**: [XII. 11] (cylinders of equal height are as bases), [v. Def. 5] (definition of proportion via equimultiples), and references to [VI. 1] and [XI. 25].
