.. subclause::

   But the bases are equal;

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`$QR$ <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`$RB$ <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also equal to one another.

.. subclause::

   Since then the axes :g:`$LN$ <0421.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`$NE$ <0421.g1:/ N E />`, :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` are equal to one another, and the cylinders :g:`$QR$ <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`$RB$ <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also equal to one another, and the multitude of the former is equal to the multitude of the latter,

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever multiple the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is of the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />`, the same multiple also will the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` be of the cylinder :g:`$GB$ <0421.g1:< G B >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason, whatever multiple the axis :g:`$MK$ <0421.g1:/ M K />` is of the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />`, the same multiple also is the cylinder :g:`$WG$ <0421.g1:< W G >>` of the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`.

.. assertion::

   And, if the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is equal to the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` will also be equal to the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if the axis is greater than the axis, the cylinder will also be greater than the cylinder, and if less, less.

.. subclause::

   Thus, there being four magnitudes, the axes :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />` and the cylinders :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>`, :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`, there have been taken equimultiples of the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` and of the cylinder :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>`, namely the axis :g:`$LK$ <0421.g1:/ L K />` and the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>`, and equimultiples of the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />` and of the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`, namely the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />` and the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`; and it has been proved that, if the axis :g:`$KL$ <0421.g1:/ K L />` is in excess of the axis :g:`$KM$ <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`$QG$ <0421.g1:< Q G >>` is also in excess of the cylinder :g:`$GW$ <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if equal, equal, and if less, less.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as the axis :g:`$EK$ <0421.g1:/ E K />` is to the axis :g:`$KF$ <0421.g1:/ K F />`, so is the cylinder :g:`$BG$ <0421.g1:< B G >>` to the cylinder :g:`$GD$ <0421.g1:< G D >>`. :just:`[V. Def. 5] <V.def.5>`
