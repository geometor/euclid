### PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">Cones and cylinders which are on equal bases are to one<br />
another as their heights.</p>

For let <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span> be cylinders on equal bases, the circles<br />
<span data-ref="0423.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span>;<br />
I say that, as the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span> is<br />
to the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span>, so is the axis<br />
<span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> to the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.

![Proposition 14 Diagram](0423.g1.svg)

For let the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> be pro-<br />
duced to the point <span data-ref="0423.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> be made equal to the axis<br />
<span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>,<br />
and let the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C M >">$CM$</span> be conceived about <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> as axis.

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the cylinders <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C M >">$CM$</span> are of the same height,<br />
they are to one another as their bases. <span class="justification">[XII. 11]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the bases are equal to one another:</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the cylinders <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C M >">$CM$</span> are also equal.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F M >">$FM$</span> has been cut by the plane<br />
<span data-ref="0423.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> which is parallel to its opposite planes,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C M >">$CM$</span> is to the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span>, so is the<br />
axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> to the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>. <span class="justification">[XII. 13]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C M >">$CM$</span> is equal to the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span>,<br />
and the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> to the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span> is to the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span>, so is the<br />
axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> to the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span> is to the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span>, so is the<br />
cone <span data-ref="0423.g1:< A B G >">$ABG$</span> to the cone <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C D K >">$CDK$</span>. <span class="justification">[XII. 10]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore also, as the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span> is to the axis <span data-ref="0423.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, so is<br />
the cone <span data-ref="0423.g1:< A B G >">$ABG$</span> to the cone <span data-ref="0423.g1:< C D K >">$CDK$</span> and the cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< E B >">$EB$</span> to the<br />
cylinder <span data-ref="0423.g1:< F D >">$FD$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
