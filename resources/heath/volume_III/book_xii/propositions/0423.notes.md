## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 14 Structure**: Extends Proposition 13 to cones and cylinders on equal bases. Heath notes that no separate proposition was required for parallelepipeds because **XI. 25** already encompasses the ratio of heights as well as bases.
- **Mathematical Steps**: A cylinder *CM* is constructed on base *CD* with axis *LN* equal to *GH*. Cylinders *EB* and *CM* are equal by XII. 11. By XII. 13, cylinder *CM* is to cylinder *FD* as *LN* is to *KL*. Cones are one-third of the cylinders by XII. 10, completing the proof for cones as well.
