PROPOSITION 14.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Cones and cylinders which are on equal bases are to one another as their heights.

For let :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>`, :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>` be cylinders on :eq:`equal <0423.g1:( A B ) = 0423.g1:( C D )>` bases, the circles :g:`AB <0423.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`CD <0423.g1:( C D )>`; I say that, as the cylinder :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>` is to the cylinder :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>`, so is the axis :g:`GH <0423.g1:/ G H />` to the axis :g:`KL <0423.g1:/ K L />`.

.. picture:: 0423.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 14 Diagram

For let the axis :g:`KL <0423.g1:/ K L />` be produced to the point :g:`N <0423.g1:N>`, let :g:`LN <0423.g1:/ L N />` be made :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ L N / = 0423.g1:/ G H />` to the axis :g:`GH <0423.g1:/ G H />`, and let the cylinder :g:`CM <0423.g1:< C M >>` be conceived about :g:`LN <0423.g1:/ L N />` as axis.

.. subclause::

   Since then the cylinders :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>`, :g:`CM <0423.g1:< C M >>` are of the same height, they are to one another as their bases. :just:`[XII. 11] <XII.11>`

.. subclause::

   But the bases are :eq:`equal <0423.g1:( A B ) = 0423.g1:( C D )>` to one another:

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>`, :g:`CM <0423.g1:< C M >>` are also :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< E B > = 0423.g1:< C M >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the cylinder :g:`FM <0423.g1:< F M >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`CD <0423.g1:( C D )>` which is parallel to its opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the cylinder :g:`CM <0423.g1:< C M >>` is to the cylinder :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>`, so is the axis :g:`LN <0423.g1:/ L N />` to the axis :g:`KL <0423.g1:/ K L />`. :just:`[XII. 13] <XII.13>`

.. subclause::

   But the cylinder :g:`CM <0423.g1:< C M >>` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< C M > = 0423.g1:< E B >; 0423.g1:/ L N / = 0423.g1:/ G H />` to the cylinder :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>`, and the axis :g:`LN <0423.g1:/ L N />` to the axis :g:`GH <0423.g1:/ G H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as the cylinder :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>` is to the cylinder :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>`, so is the axis :g:`GH <0423.g1:/ G H />` to the axis :g:`KL <0423.g1:/ K L />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as the cylinder :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>` is to the cylinder :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>`, so is the cone :g:`ABG <0423.g1:< A B G >>` to the cone :g:`CDK <0423.g1:< C D K >>`. :just:`[XII. 10] <XII.10>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as the axis :g:`GH <0423.g1:/ G H />` is to the axis :g:`KL <0423.g1:/ K L />`, so is the cone :g:`ABG <0423.g1:< A B G >>` to the cone :g:`CDK <0423.g1:< C D K >>` and the cylinder :g:`EB <0423.g1:< E B >>` to the cylinder :g:`FD <0423.g1:< F D >>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
