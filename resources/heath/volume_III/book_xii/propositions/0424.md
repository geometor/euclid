### PROPOSITION 15.

<p class="enunciation">In equal cones and cylinders the bases are reciprocally<br />
proportional to the heights; and those cones and cylinders in<br />
which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are<br />
equal.</p>

Let there be equal cones and cylinders of which the circles<br />
<span data-ref="0424.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0424.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> are the bases;<br />
let <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> be the diameters of the bases,<br />
and <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> the axes, which are also the heights of the<br />
cones or cylinders;<br />
let the cylinders <span data-ref="0424.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>, <span data-ref="0424.g1:< E P >">$EP$</span> be completed.<br />
I say that in the cylinders <span data-ref="0424.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span>, <span data-ref="0424.g1:< E P >">$EP$</span> the bases are re-<br />
ciprocally proportional to the heights,<br />
that is, as the base <span data-ref="0424.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the base <span data-ref="0424.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is the<br />
height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> to the height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.

For the height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ L K /">$LK$</span> is either equal to the height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> or<br />
not equal.<br />
First, let it be equal.<br />
<p class="sub-clause">Now the cylinder <span data-ref="0424.g1:< A O >">$AO$</span> is also equal to the cylinder <span data-ref="0424.g1:< E P >">$EP$</span>.<br />
But cones and cylinders which are of the same height are<br />
to one another as their bases; <span class="justification">[XII. 11]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0424.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is also equal to the base <span data-ref="0424.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.<br />
Hence also, reciprocally, as the base <span data-ref="0424.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> is to the base<br />
<span data-ref="0424.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, so is the height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> to the height <span data-ref="0424.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.</p>
