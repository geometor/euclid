Let <span data-ref="0428.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> be the two given circles about the<br />
same centre <span data-ref="0428.g1:K">$K$</span> ;<br />
thus it is required to inscribe in the<br />
greater circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> an equilateral<br />
polygon with an even number of<br />
sides which does not touch the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.

![Proposition 16](0428.g1.svg)

For let the straight line <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ B K D /">$BKD$</span><br />
be drawn through the centre <span data-ref="0428.g1:K">$K$</span>,<br />
and from the point <span data-ref="0428.g1:G">$G$</span> let <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ G A /">$GA$</span> be<br />
drawn at right angles to the straight<br />
line <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> and carried through to <span data-ref="0428.g1:C">$C$</span> ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> touches the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>. <span class="justification">[III. 16, Por.]</span></p>

Then, bisecting the circumference <span data-ref="0428.g1:( B A D )">$BAD$</span>, bisecting the<br />
half of it, and doing this continually, we shall leave a circum-<br />
ference less than <span data-ref="0428.g1:( A D )">$AD$</span>. <span class="justification">[X. 1]</span><br />
Let such be left, and let it be <span data-ref="0428.g1:( L D )">$LD$</span> ;<br />
from <span data-ref="0428.g1:L">$L$</span> let <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> be drawn perpendicular to <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> and carried<br />
through to <span data-ref="0428.g1:N">$N$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L D /">$LD$</span>, <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span> be joined ;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L D /">$LD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span>. <span class="justification">[III. 3, I. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> touches the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> does not touch the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L D /">$LD$</span>, <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ D N /">$DN$</span> are far from touching the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed">If then we fit into the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> straight lines equal<br />
to the straight line <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L D /">$LD$</span> and placed continuously, there will<br />
be inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span> an equilateral polygon with<br />
an even number of sides which does not touch the lesser<br />
circle <span data-ref="0428.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

---

It must be carefully observed that the polygon inscribed in the outer circle<br />
in this proposition is such that not only do its own sides not touch the inner<br />
circle, but also *the chords, as <span data-ref="0428.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>, joining angular points next but one to each<br />
other do not touch the inner circle either*. In other words, the polygon is the<br />
second in order, not the first, which satisfies the condition of the enunciation.<br />
This is important, because such a polygon is wanted in the next proposition ;<br />
hence in that proposition the *exact* construction here given must be followed.
