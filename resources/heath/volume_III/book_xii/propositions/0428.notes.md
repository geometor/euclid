## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Formulaic Ending**: Concludes with **Q. E. F.** (*quod erat faciendum*) because Proposition 16 is a construction problem rather than a theorem (**Q. E. D.**).
- **Heath's Structural Insight**: Heath emphasizes that the inscribed polygon constructed by Euclid has its chords joining alternate vertices (*angular points next but one*, e.g., chord *LN*) strictly outside the inner circle *EFGH*. Thus, the sides of the polygon (*LD*, *DN*) are "far from touching" (*pollō mallon*) the inner circle. This extra separation is indispensable for the 3D construction of polyhedra in Proposition 17.
- **Citations**: [III. 16, Por.] (tangent perpendicular to diameter), [X. 1] (bisection principle of exhaustion), [III. 3, I. 4] (perpendicular bisector of chord and isosceles symmetry).
