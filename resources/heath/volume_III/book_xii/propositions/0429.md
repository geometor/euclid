### PROPOSITION 17.

<p class="enunciation">Given two spheres about the same centre, to inscribe in the<br />
greater sphere a polyhedral solid which does not touch the<br />
lesser sphere at its surface.</p>

Let two spheres be conceived about the same centre <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> ;<br />
thus it is required to inscribe in the greater sphere a poly-<br />
hedral solid which does not touch the lesser sphere at its<br />
surface.

![Proposition 17](0429.g1.svg)

Let the spheres be cut by any plane through the centre ;<br />
then the sections will be circles,<br />
inasmuch as the sphere was produced by the diameter<br />
remaining fixed and the semicircle being carried round it ;<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. Def. 14]</span><br />
hence, in whatever position we conceive the semicircle to be,<br />
the plane carried through it will produce a circle on the<br />
circumference of the sphere.
