and from <span data-ref="0429.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:S">$S$</span> let perpendiculars be drawn to the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span>; [XI. 11]
these will fall on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span>, the common sections of the planes, inasmuch as the planes of <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B O D )">$BOD$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:( K O N )">$KON$</span> are also at right angles to the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span>. [cf. XI. Def. 4]
Let them so fall, and let them be <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> be joined.

Now since, in the equal semicircles <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B O D )">$BOD$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:( K O N )">$KON$</span>, equal straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span> have been cut off,
and the perpendiculars <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span> have been drawn,
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span>, and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K W /">$KW$</span>. [III. 27, I. 26]
But the whole <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is also equal to the whole <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>;
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ V A /">$VA$</span> is also equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W A /">$WA$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ V A /">$VA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K W /">$KW$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W A /">$WA$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>. [VI. 2]
And, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span> is at right angles to the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span>. [XI. 6]
But it was also proved equal to it;
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> are also equal and parallel. [I. 33]
And, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span>,
while <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> is also parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>. [XI. 9]
And <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span> join their extremities;
therefore the quadrilateral <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span> is in one plane,
inasmuch as, if two straight lines be parallel, and points be taken at random on each of them, the straight line joining the points is in the same plane with the parallels. [XI. 7]
For the same reason
each of the quadrilaterals <span data-ref="0429.g1:< S P Q T >">$SPQT$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:< T Q R U >">$TQRU$</span> is also in one plane.
But the triangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:< U R O >">$URO$</span> is also in one plane. [XI. 2]
If then we conceive straight lines joined from the points <span data-ref="0429.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:S">$S$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:Q">$Q$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:T">$T$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:U">$U$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span>, there will be constructed a certain polyhedral solid figure between the circumferences <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B O )">$BO$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:( K O )">$KO$</span>, consisting of pyramids of which the quadrilaterals <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:< S P Q T >">$SPQT$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:< T Q R U >">$TQRU$</span> and the triangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:< U R O >">$URO$</span> are the bases and the point <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> the vertex.
