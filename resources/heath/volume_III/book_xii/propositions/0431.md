<p class="sub-clause">Now since, in the equal semicircles <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B O D )">$BOD$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:( K O N )">$KON$</span>, equal<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span>, and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K W /">$KW$</span>. <span class="justification">[III. 27, I. 26]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the whole <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is also equal to the whole <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ V A /">$VA$</span> is also equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W A /">$WA$</span> ;<br />
therefore, as <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ V A /">$VA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K W /">$KW$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W A /">$WA$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 2]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span> is at right<br />

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P V /">$PV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S W /">$SW$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 6]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it was also proved equal to it ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> are also equal and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> is also parallel to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 9]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span> join their extremities ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the quadrilateral <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span> is in one plane,<br />
inasmuch as, if two straight lines be parallel, and points be<br />
taken at random on each of them, the straight line joining the<br />
points is in the same plane with the parallels. <span class="justification">[XI. 7]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
each of the quadrilaterals <span data-ref="0429.g1:< S P Q T >">$SPQT$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:< T Q R U >">$TQRU$</span> is also in one plane.</p>

<p class="assertion">But the triangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:< U R O >">$URO$</span> is also in one plane. <span class="justification">[XI. 2]</span></p>
