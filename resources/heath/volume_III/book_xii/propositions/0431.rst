| and from :g:`$P$ <0429.g1:P>`, :g:`$S$ <0429.g1:S>` let perpendiculars be drawn to the plane of the
| circle :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>` ; :just:`[XI. 11] <XI.11>`
| these will fall on :g:`$BD$ <0429.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$KN$ <0429.g1:/ K N />`, the common sections of the planes,
| inasmuch as the planes of :g:`$BOD$ <0429.g1:( B O D )>`, :g:`$KON$ <0429.g1:( K O N )>` are also at right angles
| to the plane of the circle :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>`. :just:`[cf. XI. Def. 4]`
| Let them so fall, and let them be :g:`$PV$ <0429.g1:/ P V />`, :g:`$SW$ <0429.g1:/ S W />`,
| and let :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   | Now since, in the equal semicircles :g:`$BOD$ <0429.g1:( B O D )>`, :g:`$KON$ <0429.g1:( K O N )>`, equal
   | straight lines :g:`$BP$ <0429.g1:/ B P />`, :g:`$KS$ <0429.g1:/ K S />` have been cut off,
   | and the perpendiculars :g:`$PV$ <0429.g1:/ P V />`, :g:`$SW$ <0429.g1:/ S W />` have been drawn,

.. assertion:: [III. 27, I. 26] <III.27, I.26>

   therefore :g:`$PV$ <0429.g1:/ P V />` is equal to :g:`$SW$ <0429.g1:/ S W />`, and :g:`$BV$ <0429.g1:/ B V />` to :g:`$KW$ <0429.g1:/ K W />`.

.. subclause::

   But the whole :g:`$BA$ <0429.g1:/ B A />` is also equal to the whole :g:`$KA$ <0429.g1:/ K A />` ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder :g:`$VA$ <0429.g1:/ V A />` is also equal to the remainder :g:`$WA$ <0429.g1:/ W A />` ;
   | therefore, as :g:`$BV$ <0429.g1:/ B V />` is to :g:`$VA$ <0429.g1:/ V A />`, so is :g:`$KW$ <0429.g1:/ K W />` to :g:`$WA$ <0429.g1:/ W A />` ;
   | therefore :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />` is parallel to :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />`. :just:`[VI. 2] <VI.2>`

.. subclause::

   | And, since each of the straight lines :g:`$PV$ <0429.g1:/ P V />`, :g:`$SW$ <0429.g1:/ S W />` is at right
   | angles to the plane of the circle :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 6] <XI.6>

   therefore :g:`$PV$ <0429.g1:/ P V />` is parallel to :g:`$SW$ <0429.g1:/ S W />`.

.. subclause::

   But it was also proved equal to it ;

.. assertion:: [I. 33] <I.33>

   therefore :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />`, :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />` are also equal and parallel.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />` is parallel to :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />`,
   | while :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />` is parallel to :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />`,

.. assertion:: [XI. 9] <XI.9>

   therefore :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />` is also parallel to :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BP$ <0429.g1:/ B P />`, :g:`$KS$ <0429.g1:/ K S />` join their extremities ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the quadrilateral :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>` is in one plane,
   | inasmuch as, if two straight lines be parallel, and points be
   | taken at random on each of them, the straight line joining the
   | points is in the same plane with the parallels. :just:`[XI. 7] <XI.7>`

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | each of the quadrilaterals :g:`$SPQT$ <0429.g1:< S P Q T >>`, :g:`$TQRU$ <0429.g1:< T Q R U >>` is also in one plane.

.. assertion:: [XI. 2] <XI.2>

   But the triangle :g:`$URO$ <0429.g1:< U R O >>` is also in one plane.

| If then we conceive straight lines joined from the points
| :g:`$P$ <0429.g1:P>`, :g:`$S$ <0429.g1:S>`, :g:`$Q$ <0429.g1:Q>`, :g:`$T$ <0429.g1:T>`, :g:`$R$ <0429.g1:R>`, :g:`$U$ <0429.g1:U>` to :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>`, there will be constructed a certain
| polyhedral solid figure between the circumferences :g:`$BO$ <0429.g1:( B O )>`, :g:`$KO$ <0429.g1:( K O )>`,
| consisting of pyramids of which the quadrilaterals :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>`,
| :g:`$SPQT$ <0429.g1:< S P Q T >>`, :g:`$TQRU$ <0429.g1:< T Q R U >>` and the triangle :g:`$URO$ <0429.g1:< U R O >>` are the bases and the
| point :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` the vertex.
