And, if we make the same construction in the case of each<br />
of the sides <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ M E /">$ME$</span> as in the case of <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, and further<br />
in the case of the remaining three quadrants,<br />
there will be constructed a certain polyhedral figure in-<br />
scribed in the sphere and contained by pyramids, of which<br />
the said quadrilaterals and the triangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:< U R O >">$URO$</span>, and the others<br />
corresponding to them, are the bases and the point <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> the<br />
vertex.

I say that the said polyhedron will not touch the lesser<br />
sphere at the surface on which the circle <span data-ref="0429.g1:( F G H )">$FGH$</span> is.

Let <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> perpendicular to the<br />
plane of the quadrilateral <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span>, and let it meet the plane at<br />
the point <span data-ref="0429.g1:X">$X$</span> ; <span class="justification">[XI. 11]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X B /">$XB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> is at right angles to the plane of the<br />
quadrilateral <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore it is also at right angles to all the straight lines<br />
which meet it and are in the plane of the quadrilateral. <span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span><br />
Therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the squares on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X B /">$XB$</span> are equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
for the angle at <span data-ref="0429.g1:X">$X$</span> is right ; <span class="justification">[I. 47]</span><br />
and the squares on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span> are equal to the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>. <span class="justification">[*id.*]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X B /">$XB$</span> are equal to the squares<br />
on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span>.</p>

Let the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> be subtracted from each ;
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>, is equal to the<br />
remainder, the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span> ;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that the straight lines joined<br />
from <span data-ref="0429.g1:X">$X$</span> to <span data-ref="0429.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:S">$S$</span> are equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span>.</p>
