| And, if we make the same construction in the case of each
| of the sides :g:`$KL$ <0429.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$LM$ <0429.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$ME$ <0429.g1:/ M E />` as in the case of :g:`$BK$ <0429.g1:/ B K />`, and further
| in the case of the remaining three quadrants,
| there will be constructed a certain polyhedral figure in-
| scribed in the sphere and contained by pyramids, of which
| the said quadrilaterals and the triangle :g:`$URO$ <0429.g1:< U R O >>`, and the others
| corresponding to them, are the bases and the point :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` the
| vertex.

| I say that the said polyhedron will not touch the lesser
| sphere at the surface on which the circle :g:`$FGH$ <0429.g1:( F G H )>` is.

| Let :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` be drawn from the point :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` perpendicular to the
| plane of the quadrilateral :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>`, and let it meet the plane at
| the point :g:`$X$ <0429.g1:X>` ; :just:`[XI. 11] <XI.11>`
| let :g:`$XB$ <0429.g1:/ X B />`, :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   | Then, since :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` is at right angles to the plane of the
   | quadrilateral :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore it is also at right angles to all the straight lines
   | which meet it and are in the plane of the quadrilateral. :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`
   | Therefore :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` is at right angles to each of the straight
   | lines :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`, :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AB$ <0429.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$AK$ <0429.g1:/ A K />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`$AB$ <0429.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to the square on :g:`$AK$ <0429.g1:/ A K />`.

.. subclause::

   | And the squares on :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />`, :g:`$XB$ <0429.g1:/ X B />` are equal to the square
   | on :g:`$AB$ <0429.g1:/ A B />`,
   | for the angle at :g:`$X$ <0429.g1:X>` is right ; :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`
   | and the squares on :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />`, :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />` are equal to the square on :g:`$AK$ <0429.g1:/ A K />`. :just:`[*id.*]`

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the squares on :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />`, :g:`$XB$ <0429.g1:/ X B />` are equal to the squares
   | on :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />`, :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />`.

Let the square on :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` be subtracted from each ;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, the square on :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`, is equal to the
   | remainder, the square on :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />` ;
   | therefore :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />` is equal to :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />`.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly we can prove that the straight lines joined
   | from :g:`$X$ <0429.g1:X>` to :g:`$P$ <0429.g1:P>`, :g:`$S$ <0429.g1:S>` are equal to each of the straight lines :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`,
   | :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />`.
