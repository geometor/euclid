<p class="assertion">Therefore the circle described with centre <span data-ref="0429.g1:X">$X$</span> and distance<br />
one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X B /">$XB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X K /">$XK$</span> will pass through <span data-ref="0429.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:S">$S$</span> also,<br />
and <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span> will be a quadrilateral in a circle.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span> is a quadrilateral in a circle,<br />
and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B P /">$BP$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K S /">$KS$</span> are equal, and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span> less,<br />
and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span> is the radius of the circle,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is greater than double of the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K Z /">$KZ$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0429.g1:K">$K$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is less than double of <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D Z /">$DZ$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D Z /">$DZ$</span>, so is the rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B Z /">$BZ$</span> to the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D Z /">$DZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ Z B /">$ZB$</span>,<br />
if a square be described upon <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B Z /">$BZ$</span> and the parallelogram on<br />
<span data-ref="0429.g1:/ Z D /">$ZD$</span> be completed,</p>
<p class="assertion">then the rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B Z /">$BZ$</span> is also less than double of the<br />
rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D Z /">$DZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ Z B /">$ZB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, if <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K D /">$KD$</span> be joined,<br />
the rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B Z /">$BZ$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>,<br />
and the rectangle <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ D Z /">$DZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ Z B /">$ZB$</span> equal to the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K Z /">$KZ$</span>; <span class="justification">[III. 31, VI. 8 and Por.]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is less than double of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K Z /">$KZ$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is greater than double of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K Z /">$KZ$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>.</p>
