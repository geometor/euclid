.. assertion::

   Therefore the circle described with centre :g:`$X$ <0429.g1:X>` and distance one of the straight lines :g:`$XB$ <0429.g1:/ X B />`, :g:`$XK$ <0429.g1:/ X K />` will pass through :g:`$P$ <0429.g1:P>`, :g:`$S$ <0429.g1:S>` also, and :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>` will be a quadrilateral in a circle.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is greater than :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />`, while :g:`$WV$ <0429.g1:/ W V />` is :eq:`equal <0429.g1:/ W V / = 0429.g1:/ S P />` to :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is greater than :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is :eq:`equal <0429.g1:/ K B / = 0429.g1:/ K S / = 0429.g1:/ B P />` to each of the straight lines :g:`$KS$ <0429.g1:/ K S />`, :g:`$BP$ <0429.g1:/ B P />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$KS$ <0429.g1:/ K S />`, :g:`$BP$ <0429.g1:/ B P />` is greater than :g:`$SP$ <0429.g1:/ S P />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>` is a quadrilateral in a circle, and :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`$BP$ <0429.g1:/ B P />`, :g:`$KS$ <0429.g1:/ K S />` are :eq:`equal <0429.g1:/ K B / = 0429.g1:/ B P / = 0429.g1:/ K S />`, and :g:`$PS$ <0429.g1:/ P S />` less, and :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />` is the radius of the circle,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is greater than double of the square on :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`.

Let :g:`$KZ$ <0429.g1:/ K Z />` be drawn from :g:`$K$ <0429.g1:K>` perpendicular to :g:`$BV$ <0429.g1:/ B V />`.

.. assertion::

   then the rectangle :g:`$DB$ <0429.g1:/ D B />`, :g:`$BZ$ <0429.g1:/ B Z />` is also less than double of the rectangle :g:`$DZ$ <0429.g1:/ D Z />`, :g:`$ZB$ <0429.g1:/ Z B />`.

.. subclause::

   And, if :g:`$KD$ <0429.g1:/ K D />` be joined, the rectangle :g:`$DB$ <0429.g1:/ D B />`, :g:`$BZ$ <0429.g1:/ B Z />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BK$ <0429.g1:/ B K />`, and the rectangle :g:`$DZ$ <0429.g1:/ D Z />`, :g:`$ZB$ <0429.g1:/ Z B />` equal to the square on :g:`$KZ$ <0429.g1:/ K Z />`; :just:`[III. 31, VI. 8 and Por.]`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is less than double of the square on :g:`$KZ$ <0429.g1:/ K Z />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$KB$ <0429.g1:/ K B />` is greater than double of the square on :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`;

   therefore the square on :g:`$KZ$ <0429.g1:/ K Z />` is greater than the square on :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`.
