<p class="assertion">and of these the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ K Z /">$KZ$</span> is greater than the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ B X /">$BX$</span>;<br />
therefore the remainder, the square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ Z A /">$ZA$</span>, is less than the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ X A /">$XA$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A Z /">$AZ$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> is much greater than <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A X /">$AX$</span> is the perpendicular on one base of the poly-<br />
hedron,<br />
and <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> on the surface of the lesser sphere;</p>
<p class="assertion">hence the polyhedron will not touch the lesser sphere on its<br />
surface.</p>

<p class="qed">Therefore, given two spheres about the same centre, a<br />
polyhedral solid has been inscribed in the greater sphere<br />
which does not touch the lesser sphere at its surface.<br />
<span class="justification">Q. E. F.</span></p>

<p class="assertion">PORISM. *But if in another sphere also a polyhedral solid<br />
be inscribed similar to the solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span>,<br />
the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span> has to the poly-<br />
hedral solid in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of that<br />
which the diameter of the sphere <span data-ref="0429.g1:( B C D E )">$BCDE$</span> has to the diameter<br />
of the other sphere.*</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For, the solids being divided into their pyramids similar<br />
in multitude and arrangement, the pyramids will be similar.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But similar pyramids are to one another in the triplicate<br />
ratio of their corresponding sides; <span class="justification">[XII. 8, Por.]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the pyramid of which the quadrilateral <span data-ref="0429.g1:< K B P S >">$KBPS$</span> is<br />
the base, and the point <span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> the vertex, has to the similarly<br />
arranged pyramid in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of<br />
that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding<br />
side, that is, of that which the radius <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the sphere about<br />
<span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> as centre has to the radius of the other sphere.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly also each pyramid of those in the sphere about<br />
<span data-ref="0429.g1:A">$A$</span> as centre has to each similarly arranged pyramid of those<br />
in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0429.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has<br />
to the radius of the other sphere.</p>

<p class="assertion">And, as one of the antecedents is to one of the conse-<br />
