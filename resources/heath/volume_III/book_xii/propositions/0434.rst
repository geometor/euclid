.. assertion::

   | and of these the square on :g:`$KZ$ <0429.g1:/ K Z />` is greater than the square
   | on :g:`$BX$ <0429.g1:/ B X />`;
   | therefore the remainder, the square on :g:`$ZA$ <0429.g1:/ Z A />`, is less than the
   | square on :g:`$XA$ <0429.g1:/ X A />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` is greater than :g:`$AZ$ <0429.g1:/ A Z />`;
   | therefore :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` is much greater than :g:`$AG$ <0429.g1:/ A G />`.

.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$AX$ <0429.g1:/ A X />` is the perpendicular on one base of the poly-
   | hedron,
   | and :g:`$AG$ <0429.g1:/ A G />` on the surface of the lesser sphere;

.. assertion::

   | hence the polyhedron will not touch the lesser sphere on its
   | surface.

.. qed::

   | Therefore, given two spheres about the same centre, a
   | polyhedral solid has been inscribed in the greater sphere
   | which does not touch the lesser sphere at its surface.
   | :just:`Q. E. F.`

.. assertion::

   | PORISM. *But if in another sphere also a polyhedral solid
   | be inscribed similar to the solid in the sphere :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>`,
   | the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>` has to the poly-
   | hedral solid in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of that
   | which the diameter of the sphere :g:`$BCDE$ <0429.g1:( B C D E )>` has to the diameter
   | of the other sphere.*

.. subclause::

   | For, the solids being divided into their pyramids similar
   | in multitude and arrangement, the pyramids will be similar.

.. subclause::

   | But similar pyramids are to one another in the triplicate
   | ratio of their corresponding sides; :just:`[XII. 8, Por.]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the pyramid of which the quadrilateral :g:`$KBPS$ <0429.g1:< K B P S >>` is
   | the base, and the point :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` the vertex, has to the similarly
   | arranged pyramid in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of
   | that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding
   | side, that is, of that which the radius :g:`$AB$ <0429.g1:/ A B />` of the sphere about
   | :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` as centre has to the radius of the other sphere.

.. assertion::

   | Similarly also each pyramid of those in the sphere about
   | :g:`$A$ <0429.g1:A>` as centre has to each similarly arranged pyramid of those
   | in the other sphere the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$AB$ <0429.g1:/ A B />` has
   | to the radius of the other sphere.

   | quents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents;
