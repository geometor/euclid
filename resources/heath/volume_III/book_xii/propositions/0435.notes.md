## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Critical Scholarly Insight**: Heath points out: "the text and the figure treat two points *Z* and *V*, which are really one and the same, as different." Specifically, *V* is the foot of the perpendicular from *P* to diameter *BD*, and *Z* is the foot of the perpendicular from *K* to *BV* (i.e. to *BD*). Because *arc BP* = *arc KS*, their horizontal projections from *B* along the diameter are mathematically equal, so *V* and *Z* coincide on diameter *BD*.
