| while :g:`$AB$ <0437.g1:/ A B />` is not greater than :g:`$AD$ <0437.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$A'B'$ <0437.g1:/ A' B' />` = :g:`$CD$ <0437.g1:/ C D />`, and :g:`$AB$ <0437.g1:/ A B />` $>$ :g:`$CD$ <0437.g1:/ C D />` $>$ :g:`$C'D'$ <0437.g1:/ C' D' />`,
| then the radius :g:`$OA$ <0437.g1:/ O A />` of the circle circumscribing the first quadrilateral is greater

:person:`Clavius <clavius>`, and :person:`Simson <simson>` after him, prove this by :term:`*reductio ad absurdum* <reductio-ad-absurdum>`.

| (1)   *If* :g:`$OA$ <0437.g1:/ O A />` *=* :g:`$O'A'$ <0437.g1:/ O' A' />`,
| it follows that $\angle s$ :g:`$AOD$ <0437.g1:< A O D )>`, :g:`$BOC$ <0437.g1:< B O C )>`, :g:`$A'O'D'$ <0437.g1:< A' O' D' )>`, :g:`$B'O'C'$ <0437.g1:< B' O' C' )>` are all equal.
| Also                         $\angle$ :g:`$AOB$ <0437.g1:< A O B )>` $>$ $\angle$ :g:`$A'O'B'$ <0437.g1:< A' O' B' )>`,
