PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   Spheres are to one another in the triplicate ratio of their respective diameters.

| Let the spheres :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>`, :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` be conceived,
| and let :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` be their diameters;
| I say that the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has to the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio
| triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | For, if the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has not to the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the
   | ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion::

   | then the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` will have either to some less sphere
   | than the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`, or to a greater, the ratio triplicate of
   | that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.

| First, let it have that ratio to a less sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`,
| let :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` be conceived about the same centre with :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`,
| let there be inscribed in the greater sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` a poly-
| hedral solid which does not touch the lesser sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` at
| its surface, :just:`[XII. 17] <XII.17>`
