and let there also be inscribed in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> a polyhedral solid similar to the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>;
therefore the polyhedral solid in <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> has to the polyhedral solid in <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>. [XII. 17, Por.]

![Book XII, Proposition 18](0439.g1.svg)

But the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> also has to the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;
therefore, as the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is to the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span>, so is the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> to the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>;
and, alternately, as the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is to the polyhedron in it, so is the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> to the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>. [V. 16]

But the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is greater than the polyhedron in it;
therefore the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> is also greater than the polyhedron in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>.

But it is also less,
for it is enclosed by it.

Therefore the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> has not to a less sphere than the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.
