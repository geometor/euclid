and let there also be inscribed in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> a poly-<br />
hedral solid similar to the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>;
<p class="assertion">therefore the polyhedral solid in <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> has to the polyhedral<br />
solid in <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.<br />

<p class="sub-clause">But the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> also has to the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> the<br />
ratio triplicate of that which <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is to the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span>, so is<br />
the polyhedral solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> to the polyhedral<br />
solid in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>;<br />
and, alternately, as the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is to the polyhedron in<br />
it, so is the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> to the polyhedral solid in the<br />
sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>. <span class="justification">[V. 16]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is greater than the polyhedron in it;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( G H K )">$GHK$</span> is also greater than the polyhedron<br />
in the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">But it is also less,<br />
for it is enclosed by it.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> has not to a less sphere than<br />
the sphere <span data-ref="0439.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span> the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter<br />
<span data-ref="0439.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> has to <span data-ref="0439.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.</p>
