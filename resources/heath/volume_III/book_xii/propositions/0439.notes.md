# Transcription Notes: Folio 0439 (Page 435)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Folio Range**: Folio `0439` corresponds to page 435 of Volume III, Book XII.
- **Preceding Context**: Continues directly from folio `0438` (page 434), completing the inscription of a similar polyhedron in sphere $ABC$.
- **Current Folio**: Contains the central diagram of Proposition 18 and the exhaustion proof establishing that the sphere $ABC$ cannot have to any sphere less than $DEF$ the triplicate ratio of their diameters.
- **Succeeding Context**: Proof continues on folio `0440` (page 436): "Similarly we can prove that neither has the sphere $DEF$ to a less sphere than the sphere $ABC$...".

## Textual & Mathematical Observations
- **Text Layer**: Entirely Euclid's proof for Proposition 18.
- **Citations**: Cites Book XII, Proposition 17, Porism and Book V, Proposition 16.
- **Contradiction**: Beautifully concise reductio: $GHK$ is proven both greater than the inscribed polyhedron in $DEF$ (by proportionality) and less than it (because it is enclosed by it, with the faces not touching $GHK$).
- **Footnotes / Marginalia**: No footnotes or marginal line numbers.
