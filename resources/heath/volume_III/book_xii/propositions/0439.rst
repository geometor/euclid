:title: Proposition 18 (continued)

| and let there also be inscribed in the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` a poly-
| hedral solid similar to the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the polyhedral solid in :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has to the polyhedral
   | solid in :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.
   | :just:`[XII. 17, Por.]`

.. picture:: 0439.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XII, Proposition 18

.. subclause::

   | But the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` also has to the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` the
   | ratio triplicate of that which :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is to the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>`, so is
   | the polyhedral solid in the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` to the polyhedral
   | solid in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`;
   | and, alternately, as the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is to the polyhedron in
   | it, so is the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` to the polyhedral solid in the
   | sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`. :just:`[V. 16] <V.16>`

.. subclause::

   But the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` is greater than the polyhedron in it;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the sphere :g:`$GHK$ <0439.g1:( G H K )>` is also greater than the polyhedron
   | in the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   | But it is also less,
   | for it is enclosed by it.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the sphere :g:`$ABC$ <0439.g1:( A B C )>` has not to a less sphere than
   | the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>` the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter
   | :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />` has to :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />`.
