| Similarly we can prove that neither has the sphere :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`

   | the ratio triplicate of that which the diameter :g:`$EF$ <0439.g1:/ E F />` has to the
   | diameter :g:`$BC$ <0439.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   But, inasmuch as :g:`$LMN$ <0439.g1:( L M N )>` is greater than :g:`$DEF$ <0439.g1:( D E F )>`,

| It is the method of this proposition which :person:`Legendre <legendre>` adopted for his proof
| of XII. 2 (see note on that proposition).

| The argument can be put very shortly. We will suppose $S, S'$ to be the
| volumes of the spheres, and $d, d'$ to be their diameters; and we will for brevity
| express the triplicate ratio of $d$ to $d'$ by $d^3 : d'^3$.

If

$$d^3 : d'^3 \ne S : S',$$

then

$$d^3 : d'^3 = S : T,$$

where $T$ is the volume of some sphere either greater or less than $S'$.

I. Suppose, if possible, that $T < S'$.

Let $T$ be supposed concentric with $S'$.

| As in XII. 17, inscribe a polyhedron in $S'$ such that its faces do not any-
| where touch $T$;
| and inscribe in $S$ a polyhedron similar to that in $S'$.
