# Transcription Notes: Folio 0443 (Page 439)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 439 completes the Historical Note to Book XIII.
- **Continuity**: Follows from page 438 (Hypsicles quoting Aristaeus) and discusses Allman's and Bretschneider's analyses of Aristaeus' influence on Euclid's Book XIII, followed by Apollonius' treatise on the dodecahedron and icosahedron. Precedes the opening of Book XIII Propositions on page 440.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Scholarly & Historical References**:
  - G. J. Allman (*Greek Geometry from Thales to Euclid*, 1889, pp. 201–202): eight theorems used by Hypsicles in Book XIV, showing Book XIII Propositions 4, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16 (with Porism), and 17 recapitulate Aristaeus.
  - C. A. Bretschneider (p. 171).
  - Apollonius of Perga: second edition of *Comparison of the dodecahedron with the icosahedron*, establishing that ratio of surfaces equals ratio of volumes for dodecahedron and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere, due to equal inradii (perpendiculars from sphere center to faces).
- **Layout**: Text occupies the upper half of page 439; the lower half is blank before the start of the propositions.
