# BOOK XIII. PROPOSITIONS.

### PROPOSITION 1.

*If a straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, the square on the greater segment added to the half of the whole is five times the square on the half.*

For let the straight line <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the point <span data-ref="0444.g1:C">$C$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the greater segment; let the straight line <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be produced in a straight line with <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, and let <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be made half of <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>;
I say that the square on <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.

For let the squares <span data-ref="0444.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, and let the figure in <span data-ref="0444.g1:< D F >">$DF$</span> be drawn; let <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> be carried through to <span data-ref="0444.g1:G">$G$</span>.

![Book XIII, Proposition 1](0444.g1.svg)

Now, since <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0444.g1:C">$C$</span>, therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. [VI. Def. 3, VI. 17]

And <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0444.g1:< F H >">$FH$</span> the square on <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>; therefore <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0444.g1:< F H >">$FH$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, while <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span>, and <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> to <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, therefore <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>.

But, as <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ K A /">$KA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0444.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C K >">$CK$</span> to <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span>; [VI. 1] therefore <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C K >">$CK$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0444.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:< H C >">$HC$</span> are also double of <span data-ref="0444.g1:< C H >">$CH$</span>.

Therefore <span data-ref="0444.g1:< K C >">$KC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0444.g1:< L H >">$LH$</span>, <span data-ref="0444.g1:< H C >">$HC$</span>.
