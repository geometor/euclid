.. subclause::

.. assertion::

   | the square on :g:`$BA$ <0444.g1:/ B A />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`,
   | that is, :g:`$AE$ <0444.g1:< A E >>` is quadruple of :g:`$DH$ <0444.g1:< D H >>`.

.. assertion::

   | therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0444.g1:< M N O >>` is also quadruple of :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>`;
   | therefore the whole :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` is five times :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DF$ <0444.g1:< D F >>` is the square on :g:`$DC$ <0444.g1:/ D C />`, and :g:`$AP$ <0444.g1:< A P >>` the square on :g:`$DA$ <0444.g1:/ D A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0444.g1:/ C D />` is five times the square on :g:`$DA$ <0444.g1:/ D A />`.

| The first five propositions are in the nature of lemmas, which are required
| for later propositions but are not in themselves of much importance.

| It will be observed that, while the method of the propositions is that of
| Book II., being strictly geometrical and not algebraical, none of the results of
| that Book are made use of (except indeed in the Lemma to XIII. 2, which is
| probably not genuine). It would therefore appear as though these propositions
| were taken from an earlier treatise without being revised or rewritten in the
| light of Book II. It will be remembered that, according to :person:`Proclus <proclus>` (p. 67, 6),
| :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>` "greatly added to the number of the theorems which originated with
| :person:`Plato <plato>` regarding *the section*" (i.e. presumably the "*golden section*"); and it is
| therefore probable that the five theorems are due to :person:`Eudoxus <eudoxus>`.

| That, if :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />` is divided at :g:`$C$ <0444.g1:C>` in extreme and mean ratio, the rectangle
| :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0444.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0444.g1:/ A C />` is inferred from VI. 17.

| :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />` is made equal to half :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, and we have to prove that
| (sq. on :g:`$CD$ <0444.g1:/ C D />`) = 5 (sq. on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`).

| The figure shows at once that
| □ :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>` = □ :g:`$HL$ <0444.g1:< H L >>`,
| so that □ :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>` + □ :g:`$HL$ <0444.g1:< H L >>` = 2 (□ :g:`$CH$ <0444.g1:< C H >>`)
| = □ :g:`$AG$ <0444.g1:< A G >>`.
| Also sq. :g:`$HF$ <0444.g1:< H F >>` = (sq. on :g:`$AC$ <0444.g1:/ A C />`)
| = rect. :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0444.g1:/ B C />`
| = :g:`$CE$ <0444.g1:< C E >>`.
| By addition,
| (gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0444.g1:< M N O >>`) = sq. on :g:`$AB$ <0444.g1:/ A B />`
| = 4 (sq. on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`);
| whence, adding the sq. on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />` to each, we have
| (sq. on :g:`$CD$ <0444.g1:/ C D />`) = 5 (sq. on :g:`$AD$ <0444.g1:/ A D />`).

| The result here, and in the next propositions,
| is really seen more readily by means of the figure
| of II. 11.
