## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Ancient Interpolation**:
  - Definitions of Analysis and Synthesis, noting text corruption in MSS where synthesis mistakenly mirrors analysis ("something admitted to be true" vs. B/V's "the end or attainment of what is sought").
