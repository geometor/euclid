### PROPOSITION 2.

*If the square on a straight line be five times the square on a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original straight line.*

For let the square on the straight line <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be five times the square on the segment <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> of it, and let <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;
I say that, when <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

For let the squares <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> respectively, let the figure in <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> be drawn, and let <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be drawn through.

![Book XIII, Proposition 2](0447.g1.svg)

Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is five times <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.

Therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, therefore the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.

But the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> was also proved quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>; therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span>.

And, since <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, while <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C H /">$CH$</span>, therefore <span data-ref="0447.g1:< K B >">$KB$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< B H >">$BH$</span>.
