### PROPOSITION 2.

<p class="enunciation">
If the square on a straight line be five times the square on<br />
a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut<br />
in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining<br />
part of the original straight line.
</p>

For let the square on the straight line <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be five times<br />
the square on the segment <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span><br />
of it,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>;<br />
I say that, when <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is cut in extreme<br />
and mean ratio, the greater segment<br />
is <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

Let the squares <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> be de-<br />
scribed on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> respectively,<br />
let the figure in <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> be drawn,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> be drawn through.

![Book XIII, Proposition 2](0447.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is<br />
five times the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0447.g1:< A F >">$AF$</span> is five times <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> is<br />
quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> was also proved quadruple of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< A H >">$AH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0447.g1:< M N O >">$MNO$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0447.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> to <span data-ref="0447.g1:/ C H /">$CH$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0447.g1:< K B >">$KB$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0447.g1:< B H >">$BH$</span>. <span class="justification">[VI. 1]</span></p>
