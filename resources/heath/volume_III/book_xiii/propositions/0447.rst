----

PROPOSITION 2.
--------------

.. enunciation::

   If the square on a straight line be five times the square on a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original straight line.

| For let the square on the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0447.g1:/ A B />` be five times
| the square on the segment :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />`
| of it,
| and let :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` be double of :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />`;
| I say that, when :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` is cut in extreme
| and mean ratio, the greater segment
| is :g:`$CB$ <0447.g1:/ C B />`.

| Let the squares :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>`, :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>` be de-
| scribed on :g:`$AB$ <0447.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$CD$ <0447.g1:/ C D />` respectively,
| let the figure in :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>` be drawn,
| and let :g:`$BE$ <0447.g1:/ B E />` be drawn through.

.. picture:: 0447.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 2

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the square on :g:`$BA$ <0447.g1:/ B A />` is
   | five times the square on :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$AF$ <0447.g1:< A F >>` is five times :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` is
   | quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`,
   | that is, :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>` is quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` was also proved quadruple of :g:`$AH$ <0447.g1:< A H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the gnomon :g:`$MNO$ <0447.g1:< M N O >>` is equal to :g:`$CG$ <0447.g1:< C G >>`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$CA$ <0447.g1:/ C A />`,
   | while :g:`$DC$ <0447.g1:/ D C />` is equal to :g:`$CK$ <0447.g1:/ C K />`, and :g:`$AC$ <0447.g1:/ A C />` to :g:`$CH$ <0447.g1:/ C H />`,

.. assertion:: [VI. 1] <VI.1>

   therefore :g:`$KB$ <0447.g1:< K B >>` is also double of :g:`$BH$ <0447.g1:< B H >>`.
