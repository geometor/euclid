For let any straight line <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut in extreme and mean<br />
ratio at the point <span data-ref="0450.g1:C">$C$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the greater segment,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0450.g1:D">$D$</span> ;<br />
I say that the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is<br />
five times the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.

For let the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< A E >">$AE$</span> be<br />
described on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let the figure be drawn<br />
double.

![Book XIII, Proposition 3](0450.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Since <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is<br />
quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>,<br />
that is, <span data-ref="0450.g1:< R S >">$RS$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the rectangle <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is the rectangle <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:< R S >">$RS$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0450.g1:< R S >">$RS$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> is also quadruple of <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0450.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Hence the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span> is also equal to the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< H L >">$HL$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, that is, <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> to <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ N E /">$NE$</span> ;<br />
hence <span data-ref="0450.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0450.g1:< M F >">$MF$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C G >">$CG$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F E >">$FE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Let <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C N >">$CN$</span> be added to each ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0450.g1:< O P Q >">$OPQ$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0450.g1:< C E >">$CE$</span> was proved quadruple of <span data-ref="0450.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span> ;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0450.g1:< O P Q >">$OPQ$</span> is also quadruple of the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the gnomon <span data-ref="0450.g1:< O P Q >">$OPQ$</span> and the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span> are<br />
five times <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the gnomon <span data-ref="0450.g1:< O P Q >">$OPQ$</span> and the square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< F G >">$FG$</span> are the<br />
square <span data-ref="0450.g1:< D N >">$DN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0450.g1:< D N >">$DN$</span> is the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0450.g1:< G F >">$GF$</span> the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is five times the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0450.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.</p>

<p class="qed"><span class="justification">Q. E. D.</span></p>
