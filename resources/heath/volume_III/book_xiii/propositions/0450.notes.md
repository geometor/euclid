- **Proof Structure**:
  - $AC = 2 DC \implies AC^2 = 4 DC^2 \implies RS = 4 FG$.
  - $AB \cdot BC = AC^2 \implies CE = RS = 4 FG$.
  - Symmetry of complements and squares yields gnomon $OPQ = CE = 4 FG$.
  - Adding square $FG$ gives square $DN = \text{gnomon } OPQ + FG = 5 FG = 5 DC^2$, and $DN$ is the square on $DB$, Q.E.D.
