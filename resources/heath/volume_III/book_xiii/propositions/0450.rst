:title: Book XIII: Proposition 3 (cont.)

For let any straight line :g:`$AB$ <0450.g1:/ A B />` be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the point :g:`$C$ <0450.g1:C>`, let :g:`$AC$ <0450.g1:/ A C />` be the greater segment, and let :g:`$AC$ <0450.g1:/ A C />` be bisected at :g:`$D$ <0450.g1:D>` ; I say that the square on :g:`$BD$ <0450.g1:/ B D />` is five times the square on :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`.

For let the square :g:`$AE$ <0450.g1:< A E >>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0450.g1:/ A B />`, and let the figure be drawn double.

.. picture:: 0450.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 3

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$AC$ <0450.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AC$ <0450.g1:/ A C />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`, that is, :g:`$RS$ <0450.g1:< R S >>` is quadruple of :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0450.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0450.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0450.g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`$CE$ <0450.g1:< C E >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0450.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0450.g1:/ B C />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CE$ <0450.g1:< C E >>` is equal to :g:`$RS$ <0450.g1:< R S >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$RS$ <0450.g1:< R S >>` is quadruple of :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CE$ <0450.g1:< C E >>` is also quadruple of :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AD$ <0450.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`$HK$ <0450.g1:/ H K />` is also equal to :g:`$KF$ <0450.g1:/ K F />`.

.. assertion::

   Hence the square :g:`$GF$ <0450.g1:< G F >>` is also equal to the square :g:`$HL$ <0450.g1:< H L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$GK$ <0450.g1:/ G K />` is equal to :g:`$KL$ <0450.g1:/ K L />`, that is, :g:`$MN$ <0450.g1:/ M N />` to :g:`$NE$ <0450.g1:/ N E />` ; hence :g:`$MF$ <0450.g1:< M F >>` is also equal to :g:`$FE$ <0450.g1:< F E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$MF$ <0450.g1:< M F >>` is equal to :g:`$CG$ <0450.g1:< C G >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0450.g1:< C G >>` is also equal to :g:`$FE$ <0450.g1:< F E >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`$CN$ <0450.g1:< C N >>` be added to each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the gnomon :g:`$OPQ$ <0450.g1:< O P Q >>` is equal to :g:`$CE$ <0450.g1:< C E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$CE$ <0450.g1:< C E >>` was proved quadruple of :g:`$GF$ <0450.g1:< G F >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the gnomon :g:`$OPQ$ <0450.g1:< O P Q >>` is also quadruple of the square :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the gnomon :g:`$OPQ$ <0450.g1:< O P Q >>` and the square :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>` are five times :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`$OPQ$ <0450.g1:< O P Q >>` and the square :g:`$FG$ <0450.g1:< F G >>` are the square :g:`$DN$ <0450.g1:< D N >>`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DN$ <0450.g1:< D N >>` is the square on :g:`$DB$ <0450.g1:/ D B />`, and :g:`$GF$ <0450.g1:< G F >>` the square on :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$DB$ <0450.g1:/ D B />` is five times the square on :g:`$DC$ <0450.g1:/ D C />`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
