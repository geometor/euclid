### PROPOSITION 5.

*If a straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, and there be added to it a straight line equal to the greater segment, the whole straight line has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and the original straight line is the greater segment.*

Let the straight line <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the point <span data-ref="0452.g1:C">$C$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the greater segment,
and let <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.
