### PROPOSITION 5.

<p class="enunciation">
If a straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, and<br />
there be added to it a straight line equal to the greater segment,<br />
the whole straight line has been cut in extreme and mean ratio,<br />
and the original straight line is the greater segment.
</p>

For let the straight line <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> be cut in extreme and mean<br />
ratio at the point <span data-ref="0452.g1:C">$C$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be the greater segment,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> be equal to <span data-ref="0452.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.
