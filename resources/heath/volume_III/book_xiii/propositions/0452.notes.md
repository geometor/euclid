## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 4 Proof Mechanism**: Euclid demonstrates $AB^2 + BC^2 = 3AC^2$ by decomposing square $AE$ and square $CK$ into gnomon $LMN$ plus squares $CK, HG$. Since $AK = HG$ and $AK + CE = 2 AK = \text{gnomon } LMN + CK$, adding $HG$ gives $\text{gnomon } LMN + CK + HG = 3 HG = 3 AC^2$.
- **Heath's Commentary**: Heath observes that Euclid proves this *de novo* using the geometrical dissection method of Book II rather than citing Book II, Proposition 7 directly (which yields $AB^2 + BC^2 = 2 AB \cdot BC + AC^2 = 3 AC^2$).
- **Proposition 5 Enunciation**: Demonstrates the additive property of extreme and mean ratio: extending a line $AB$ cut in extreme and mean ratio at $C$ by a segment $AD$ equal to the greater segment $AC$ results in the whole line $DB$ being cut in extreme and mean ratio at $A$, with $AB$ as the greater segment.
