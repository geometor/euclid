PROPOSITION 5.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, and there be added to it a straight line equal to the greater segment, the whole straight line has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and the original straight line is the greater segment.

| For let the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` be cut in extreme and mean
| ratio at the point :g:`$C$ <0452.g1:C>`,
| let :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />` be the greater segment,
| and let :g:`$AD$ <0452.g1:/ A D />` be equal to :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`.

| straight line :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` is the greater
