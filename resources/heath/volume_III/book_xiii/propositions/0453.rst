.. subclause::

   Since :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`$C$ <0452.g1:C>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0452.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`.
   | :just:`[VI. Def. 3, VI. 17] <VI.def.3, VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$CE$ <0452.g1:< C E >>` is the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0452.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`$CH$ <0452.g1:< C H >>` the square
   | on :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CE$ <0452.g1:< C E >>` is equal to :g:`$HC$ <0452.g1:< H C >>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$HE$ <0452.g1:< H E >>` is equal to :g:`$CE$ <0452.g1:< C E >>`,
   | and :g:`$DH$ <0452.g1:< D H >>` is equal to :g:`$HC$ <0452.g1:< H C >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DH$ <0452.g1:< D H >>` is also equal to :g:`$HE$ <0452.g1:< H E >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole :g:`$DK$ <0452.g1:< D K >>` is equal to the whole :g:`$AE$ <0452.g1:< A E >>`.

.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$DK$ <0452.g1:< D K >>` is the rectangle :g:`$BD$ <0452.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0452.g1:/ D A />`,
   | for :g:`$AD$ <0452.g1:/ A D />` is equal to :g:`$DL$ <0452.g1:/ D L />`;
   | and :g:`$AE$ <0452.g1:< A E >>` is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the rectangle :g:`$BD$ <0452.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0452.g1:/ D A />` is equal to the square
   | on :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion:: [VI. 17] <VI.17>

   Therefore, as :g:`$DB$ <0452.g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />` to :g:`$AD$ <0452.g1:/ A D />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$DB$ <0452.g1:/ D B />` is greater than :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 14] <V.14>

   therefore :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />` is also greater than :g:`$AD$ <0452.g1:/ A D />`.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$DB$ <0452.g1:/ D B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at
   | :g:`$A$ <0452.g1:A>`, and :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` is the greater segment.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`

----

We have

.. container:: center

   | (sq. :g:`$DH$ <0452.g1:< D H >>`) = (sq. :g:`$HC$ <0452.g1:< H C >>`)
   | = (rect. :g:`$CE$ <0452.g1:< C E >>`), by hypothesis,
   | = (rect. :g:`$HE$ <0452.g1:< H E >>`).

Add to each side the rectangle :g:`$AK$ <0452.g1:< A K >>`, and

.. container:: center

   | (rect. :g:`$DK$ <0452.g1:< D K >>`) = (sq. :g:`$AE$ <0452.g1:< A E >>`),
   | or (rect. :g:`$BD$ <0452.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0452.g1:/ D A />`) = (sq. on :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />`).

The result is of course obvious from II. 11.

| There is an alternative proof given in P after XIII. 6, which depends on
| Book V.

By hypothesis,

.. container:: center

   :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />` : :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />` = :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />` : :g:`$CB$ <0452.g1:/ C B />`,

or, inversely,

.. container:: center

   :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />` : :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` = :g:`$CB$ <0452.g1:/ C B />` : :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`.

*Componendo*,

.. container:: center

   (:g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` + :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`) : :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` = :g:`$AB$ <0452.g1:/ A B />` : :g:`$AC$ <0452.g1:/ A C />`,

or

.. container:: center

   :g:`$DB$ <0452.g1:/ D B />` : :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />` = :g:`$BA$ <0452.g1:/ B A />` : :g:`$AD$ <0452.g1:/ A D />`.

PROPOSITION 6.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If a rational straight line be cut in extreme and mean ratio, each of the segments is the irrational straight line called apotome.
