## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Mathematical Significance**: Connects the golden section of a rational straight line to Book X classification of irrationals. By XIII. 1, $CD^2 = 5 DA^2$. Since $DA$ is rational ($\frac{1}{2}AB$), $CD^2$ is rational, so $CD$ is rational in square. But 5 is not a square number, so $CD$ is incommensurable in length with $DA$ (X. 9). Thus $CD, DA$ are commensurable in square only, making $AC = CD - DA$ an apotome (X. 73).
- **Application of Area (X. 97)**: The lesser segment $CB$ satisfies $AB \cdot CB = AC^2$. Applying the square of an apotome ($AC^2$) to the rational line $AB$ yields $CB$ as breadth, which by X. 97 is a first apotome.
- **Citations**: Cites XIII. 1, X. 6, X. Def. 4, X. 9, X. 73, VI. Def. 3, VI. 17, and X. 97.
