:title: Proposition 6

Let :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />` be a rational straight line, let it be cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`$C$ <0454.g1:C>`, and let :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />` be the greater segment; I say that each of the straight lines :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`$CB$ <0454.g1:/ C B />` is the irrational straight line called apotome.

.. picture:: 0454.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 6

For let :g:`$BA$ <0454.g1:/ B A />` be produced, and let :g:`$AD$ <0454.g1:/ A D />` be made half of :g:`$BA$ <0454.g1:/ B A />`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and to the greater segment :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />` is added :g:`$AD$ <0454.g1:/ A D />` which is half of :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion:: [XIII. 1] <XIII.1>

   therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` is five times the square on :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` has to the square on :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />` the ratio which a number has to a number; therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` is commensurable with the square on :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />`. :just:`[X. 6] <X.6>`

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />` is rational, for :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />` is rational, being half of :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />` which is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` is also rational; :just:`[X. Def. 4] <X.def.4>` therefore :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since the square on :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` has not to the square on :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />` the ratio which a square number has to a square number,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />`; :just:`[X. 9] <X.9>` therefore :g:`$CD$ <0454.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`$DA$ <0454.g1:/ D A />` are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />` is an apotome. :just:`[X. 73] <X.73>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />` is the greater segment,

.. assertion:: [VI. Def. 3, VI. 17] <VI.def.3, VI.17>

   therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0454.g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on the apotome :g:`$AC$ <0454.g1:/ A C />`, if applied to the rational straight line :g:`$AB$ <0454.g1:/ A B />`, produces :g:`$BC$ <0454.g1:/ B C />` as breadth.

.. subclause::

   But the square on an apotome, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a first apotome; :just:`[X. 97] <X.97>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CB$ <0454.g1:/ C B />` is a first apotome.
