## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Criticism of Proposition 6**: Heath provides an in-depth argument that Proposition 6 is an ancient interpolation, citing:
  1. A marginal note in Vatican MS 190 (P) distinguishing the "new recension" (Theonine) from the "old" (pre-Theonine).
  2. A scholium to XIII. 17 in P that proves the apotome property independently.
  3. The structural displacement of the alternative proof to XIII. 5.
  4. An internal discrepancy where the enunciation says "apotome" while the proof proves the lesser segment is a "first apotome".
- **Proposition 7 Enunciation & Case 1**: Examines an equilateral pentagon $ABCDE$ with three equal angles. Case 1 treats three angles taken in order ($A, B, C$). Uses triangles $ABC$ and $ABE$ (congruent by I. 4) to deduce $AC = BE$, $\angle BCA = \angle BEA$, $\angle ABE = \angle CAB$, and by I. 6 $AF = BF$.
