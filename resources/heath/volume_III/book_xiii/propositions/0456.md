<p class="sub-clause">But the whole <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> was also proved equal to the whole <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> is also equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ D E /">$DE$</span>.<br />
Therefore the two sides <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>;<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span> is common to them;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< F C D )">$FCD$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< F E D )">$FED$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B C A )">$BCA$</span> was also proved equal to the angle<br />
<span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E B )">$AEB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B C D )">$BCD$</span> is also equal to the whole<br />
angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E D )">$AED$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, by hypothesis, the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B C D )">$BCD$</span> is equal to the angles<br />
at <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:B">$B$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E D )">$AED$</span> is also equal to the angles at <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:B">$B$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span> is also equal<br />
to the angles at <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span> is equiangular.</p>

Next, let the given equal angles not be angles taken in<br />
order, but let the angles at the points <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:D">$D$</span> be equal;<br />
I say that in this case too the pentagon <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span> is equiangular.

For let <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since the two sides <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>,<br />
and they contain equal angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>,<br />
the triangle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A B E >">$ABE$</span> is equal to the triangle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B C D >">$BCD$</span>,<br />
and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles,<br />
namely those which the equal sides subtend; <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span><br />
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E B )">$AEB$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< C D B )">$CDB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B E D )">$BED$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< B D E )">$BDE$</span>,<br />
since the side <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B E /">$BE$</span> is also equal to the side <span data-ref="0455.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 5]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the whole angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E D )">$AED$</span> is equal to the whole<br />
angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< C D E )">$CDE$</span> is, by hypothesis, equal to the angles<br />
at <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:C">$C$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0455.g1:< A E D )">$AED$</span> is also equal to the angles at <span data-ref="0455.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0455.g1:C">$C$</span>.</p>
