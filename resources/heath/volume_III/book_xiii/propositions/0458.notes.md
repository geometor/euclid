## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Geometric Proof Architecture**:
  1. Congruence of $\Delta ABE \cong \Delta ABC$ (I. 4) gives $BE = AC$ and $\angle BAC = \angle ABE$.
  2. Exterior angle of $\Delta ABH$: $\angle AHE = \angle BAH + \angle ABH = 2 \angle BAH$ (I. 32).
  3. Circle arc relationship: Arc $EDC$ contains two pentagon sides ($ED + DC$), while arc $CB$ contains one, so $\text{arc } EDC = 2\,\text{arc } CB$. By III. 28 and VI. 33, inscribed angle $\angle EAC = 2 \angle BAC$.
  4. Triangle isosceles property: $\angle HAE = \angle AHE \implies HE = EA = AB$ (I. 6).
  5. Similar triangles: Triangles $ABE$ and $ABH$ share $\angle ABE$, and $\angle BEA = \angle BAH$, so remaining angles equal $\angle BAE = \angle AHB$ (I. 32). Thus $\Delta ABE \sim \Delta ABH$ (equiangular).
  6. Proportion: $EB : BA = BA : BH$ (VI. 4). Substituting $BA = EH$ yields $BE : EH = EH : HB$.
  7. Inequality: $BE > EH \implies EH > HB$ (V. 14).
