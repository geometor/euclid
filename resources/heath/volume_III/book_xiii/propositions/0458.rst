:title: Proposition 8

.. subclause::

   Then, since the two straight lines :g:`$EA$ <0457.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`$AB$ <0457.g1:/ A B />` are :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ E A / = 0457.g1:/ A B / = 0457.g1:/ B C />` to the two :g:`$AB$ <0457.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BC$ <0457.g1:/ B C />`, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$BE$ <0457.g1:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ B E / = 0457.g1:/ A C />` to the base :g:`$AC$ <0457.g1:/ A C />`, the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E >>` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< A B E > = 0457.g1:< A B C >>` to the triangle :g:`$ABC$ <0457.g1:< A B C >>`, and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles respectively, namely those which the equal sides subtend. :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0457.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< B A C ) = 0457.g1:< A B E )>` to the angle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E )>`; therefore the angle :g:`$AHE$ <0457.g1:< A H E )>` is double of the angle :g:`$BAH$ <0457.g1:< B A H )>`. :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$EAC$ <0457.g1:< E A C )>` is also double of the angle :g:`$BAC$ <0457.g1:< B A C )>`, inasmuch as the circumference :g:`$EDC$ <0457.g1:( E D C )>` is also double of the circumference :g:`$CB$ <0457.g1:( C B )>`; :just:`[III. 28, VI. 33] <III.28, VI.33>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$HAE$ <0457.g1:< H A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< H A E ) = 0457.g1:< A H E )>` to the angle :g:`$AHE$ <0457.g1:< A H E )>`; hence the straight line :g:`$HE$ <0457.g1:/ H E />` is also :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ H E / = 0457.g1:/ E A / = 0457.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$EA$ <0457.g1:/ E A />`, that is, to :g:`$AB$ <0457.g1:/ A B />`. :just:`[I. 6] <I.6>`

.. subclause::

   And, since the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0457.g1:/ B A />` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ B A / = 0457.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$AE$ <0457.g1:/ A E />`, the angle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E )>` is also :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< A B E ) = 0457.g1:< A E B )>` to the angle :g:`$AEB$ <0457.g1:< A E B )>`. :just:`[I. 5] <I.5>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E )>` was proved :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< A B E ) = 0457.g1:< B A H )>` to the angle :g:`$BAH$ <0457.g1:< B A H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$BEA$ <0457.g1:< B E A )>` is also :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< B E A ) = 0457.g1:< B A H )>` to the angle :g:`$BAH$ <0457.g1:< B A H )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E >>` and :g:`$ABH$ <0457.g1:< A B H >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$BAE$ <0457.g1:< B A E )>` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:< B A E ) = 0457.g1:< A H B )>` to the remaining angle :g:`$AHB$ <0457.g1:< A H B )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$ABE$ <0457.g1:< A B E >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$ABH$ <0457.g1:< A B H >>`; therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$EB$ <0457.g1:/ E B />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0457.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0457.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`$BH$ <0457.g1:/ B H />`. :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BA$ <0457.g1:/ B A />` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ B A / = 0457.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$EH$ <0457.g1:/ E H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$BE$ <0457.g1:/ B E />` is to :g:`$EH$ <0457.g1:/ E H />`, so is :g:`$EH$ <0457.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$HB$ <0457.g1:/ H B />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BE$ <0457.g1:/ B E />` is greater than :g:`$EH$ <0457.g1:/ E H />`;

.. assertion:: [V. 14] <V.14>

   therefore :g:`$EH$ <0457.g1:/ E H />` is also greater than :g:`$HB$ <0457.g1:/ H B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$BE$ <0457.g1:/ B E />` has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`$H$ <0457.g1:H>`, and the greater segment :g:`$HE$ <0457.g1:/ H E />` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ H E / = 0457.g1:/ A B / = 0457.g1:/ B C / = 0457.g1:/ C D / = 0457.g1:/ D E / = 0457.g1:/ E A />` to the side of the pentagon.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`$AC$ <0457.g1:/ A C />` has also been cut in extreme and mean ratio at :g:`$H$ <0457.g1:H>`, and its greater segment :g:`$CH$ <0457.g1:/ C H />` is :eq:`equal <0457.g1:/ C H / = 0457.g1:/ A B / = 0457.g1:/ B C / = 0457.g1:/ C D / = 0457.g1:/ D E / = 0457.g1:/ E A />` to the side of the pentagon.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
