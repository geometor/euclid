<p class="assertion">$\angle$<span data-ref="0457.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span> $= \angle$<span data-ref="0457.g1:< A B E )">$ABE$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">$\angle$<span data-ref="0457.g1:< A H E )">$AHE$</span> $= 2\angle$<span data-ref="0457.g1:< B A C )">$BAC$</span><br />

### PROPOSITION 9.

<p class="enunciation">If the side of the hexagon and that of the decagon inscribed<br />
in the same circle be added together, the whole straight line<br />
has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and its greater segment<br />
is the side of the hexagon.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0459.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> be a circle;<br />
of the figures inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0459.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> let <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> be the side<br />
of a decagon, <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> that of a hexagon,<br />
and let them be in a straight line;<br />
I say that the whole straight line<br />
<span data-ref="0459.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> has been cut in extreme and<br />
mean ratio, and <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is its greater<br />
segment.
