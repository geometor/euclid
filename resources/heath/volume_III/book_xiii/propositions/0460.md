And <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is also greater than <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

Therefore the straight line <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and <span data-ref="0459.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is its greater segment.
