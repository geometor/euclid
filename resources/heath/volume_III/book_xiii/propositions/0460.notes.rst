Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- Marginal Euclidean citations integrated in the text:

  - ``[VI. 33]``: Arcs are proportional to the angles subtended at the centre.
  - ``[I. 5]``: Base angles of isosceles triangles are equal ($\triangle EBC$ and $\triangle ECD$).
  - ``[I. 32]``: Exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  - ``[IV. 15, Por.]``: Side of the regular hexagon inscribed in a circle equals the circle's radius.
  - ``[VI. 4]``: Equiangular triangles have proportional sides ($DB : BE = EB : BC$).

- Chains of angle relationships:

  - $\text{arc } AC = 4\,\text{arc } CB \implies \angle AEC = 4\angle CEB$.
  - $\angle AEC = \angle EBC + \angle ECB = 2\angle ECB$.
  - $\angle ECB = \angle CED + \angle CDE = 2\angle EDC$.
  - Therefore $\angle AEC = 4\angle EDC = 4\angle BEC \implies \angle EDC = \angle BEC$.

- Proportional conclusion: $DB : BE = EB : BC \implies BD : CD = CD : CB$, proving $BD$ is cut in extreme and mean ratio with $CD$ as greater segment.
