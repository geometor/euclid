## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 10 Enunciation**: *If an equilateral pentagon be inscribed in a circle, the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the squares on the side of the hexagon and on that of the decagon inscribed in the same circle.*
  - Symbolically: $s_5^2 = s_6^2 + s_{10}^2$.
- In the original print of the specification ("I say that the square on the side of the pentagon *ABCDE* is equal to the squares on the side of the hexagon and on that of the decagor. inscribed..."), the word "decagon" has a slightly broken letter or typographical defect reading "decagor.", transcribed emended to "decagon".
- Algebraic derivation of decagon side: quadratic equation $(r + x)x = r^2 \iff x^2 + rx - r^2 = 0$, giving the positive root $x = \frac{r}{2}(\sqrt{5}-1)$.
