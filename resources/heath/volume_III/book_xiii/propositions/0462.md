let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> be joined,
let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> be again drawn from <span data-ref="0462.g1:F">$F$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, and be carried through to <span data-ref="0462.g1:M">$M$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 10](0462.g1.svg)

Since the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B C G )">$ABCG$</span> is equal to the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A E D G )">$AEDG$</span>,
and in them <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A E D )">$AED$</span>,
therefore the remainder, the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span>, is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0462.g1:( G D )">$GD$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> belongs to a pentagon;
therefore <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> belongs to a decagon.

And, since <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is perpendicular,
therefore the angle <span data-ref="0462.g1:< A F K )">$AFK$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0462.g1:< K F B )">$KFB$</span>. [I. 5, I. 26]

Hence the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$AK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K B )">$KB$</span>; [III. 26]
therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span> is double of the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>;
therefore the straight line <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is a side of a decagon.

For the same reason
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$AK$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>.

Now, since the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span> is double of the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>,
while the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is equal to the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span>,
therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is also double of the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>.

But the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span>;
therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> is equal to the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>, since <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$KA$</span> is so also;
therefore <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>.
