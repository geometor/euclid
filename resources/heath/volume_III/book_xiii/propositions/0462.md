let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> be joined,<br />
let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span> be again drawn from <span data-ref="0462.g1:F">$F$</span> perpendicular to <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, and be<br />
carried through to <span data-ref="0462.g1:M">$M$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ K N /">$KN$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 10](0462.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Since the circumference<br />
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B C G )">$ABCG$</span> is equal to the circum-<br />
ference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A E D G )">$AEDG$</span>,<br />
and in them <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to<br />
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( A E D )">$AED$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder, the<br />
circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span>, is equal to<br />
the remainder <span data-ref="0462.g1:( G D )">$GD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> belongs to a pen-<br />
tagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> belongs to a<br />
decagon.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ F H /">$FH$</span> is perpendicular,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0462.g1:< A F K )">$AFK$</span> is also equal to the angle <span data-ref="0462.g1:< K F B )">$KFB$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 5, I. 26]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Hence the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$AK$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K B )">$KB$</span>; <span class="justification">[III. 26]</span><br />
therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span> is double of the circumference<br />
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>;<br />
therefore the straight line <span data-ref="0462.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is a side of a decagon.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$AK$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span> is double of the circum-<br />
ference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>,<br />
while the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is equal to the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A B )">$AB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is also double of the circum-<br />
ference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C D )">$CD$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the circumference <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> is equal to the circumference<br />
<span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0462.g1:( B K )">$BK$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>, since <span data-ref="0462.g1:( A K )">$KA$</span> is so also;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0462.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0462.g1:( K M )">$KM$</span>.</p>
