:title: Proposition 10 (cont.)

| let :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`$KB$ <0462.g1:/ K B />` be joined,
| let :g:`$FL$ <0462.g1:/ F L />` be again drawn from :g:`$F$ <0462.g1:F>` perpendicular to :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, and be
| carried through to :g:`$M$ <0462.g1:M>`,
| and let :g:`$KN$ <0462.g1:/ K N />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0462.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 10

.. subclause::

   | Since the circumference
   | :g:`$ABCG$ <0462.g1:( A B C G )>` is equal to the circum-
   | ference :g:`$AEDG$ <0462.g1:( A E D G )>`,
   | and in them :g:`$ABC$ <0462.g1:( A B C )>` is equal to
   | :g:`$AED$ <0462.g1:( A E D )>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remainder, the
   | circumference :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>`, is equal to
   | the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0462.g1:( G D )>`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:/ C D />` belongs to a pen-
   | tagon;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` belongs to a
   | decagon.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$FA$ <0462.g1:/ F A />` is equal to :g:`$FB$ <0462.g1:/ F B />`,
   | and :g:`$FH$ <0462.g1:/ F H />` is perpendicular,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the angle :g:`$AFK$ <0462.g1:< A F K )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$KFB$ <0462.g1:< K F B )>`.
   | :just:`[I. 5, I. 26] <I.5, I.26>`

.. assertion::

   | Hence the circumference :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is also equal to :g:`$KB$ <0462.g1:( K B )>`; :just:`[III. 26] <III.26>`
   | therefore the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>` is double of the circumference
   | :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`;
   | therefore the straight line :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />` is a side of a decagon.

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is also double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>` is double of the circum-
   | ference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`,
   | while the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is equal to the circumference :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:( A B )>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is also double of the circum-
   | ference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the circumference :g:`$CD$ <0462.g1:( C D )>` is also double of :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the circumference :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` is equal to the circumference
   | :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>` is double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`, since :g:`$KA$ <0462.g1:( A K )>` is so also;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$CG$ <0462.g1:( C G )>` is also double of :g:`$KM$ <0462.g1:( K M )>`.
