:title: Proposition 10 (cont.)

.. subclause::

   But, further, the circumference :g:`$CB$ <0462.g1:( C B )>` is also double of the circumference :g:`$BK$ <0462.g1:( B K )>`, for the circumference :g:`$CB$ <0462.g1:( C B )>` is equal to :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:( B A )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole circumference :g:`$GB$ <0462.g1:( G B )>` is also double of :g:`$BM$ <0462.g1:( B M )>`; hence the angle :g:`$GFB$ <0462.g1:< G F B )>` is also double of the angle :g:`$BFM$ <0462.g1:< B F M )>`. :just:`[VI. 33] <VI.33>`

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$GFB$ <0462.g1:< G F B )>` is also double of the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>`, for the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$FAB$ <0462.g1:< F A B )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F >>` and :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`$AFB$ <0462.g1:< A F B )>` is equal to the remaining angle :g:`$BNF$ <0462.g1:< B N F )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$ABF$ <0462.g1:< A B F >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$BFN$ <0462.g1:< B F N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as the straight line :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`, so is :g:`$FB$ <0462.g1:/ F B />` to :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />`; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />` is equal to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`$AL$ <0462.g1:/ A L />` is equal to :g:`$LK$ <0462.g1:/ L K />`, while :g:`$LN$ <0462.g1:/ L N />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`$KN$ <0462.g1:/ K N />` is equal to the base :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />`; :just:`[I. 4] <I.4>` therefore the angle :g:`$LKN$ <0462.g1:< L K N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$LAN$ <0462.g1:< L A N )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`$LAN$ <0462.g1:< L A N )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$KBN$ <0462.g1:< K B N )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`$LKN$ <0462.g1:< L K N )>` is also equal to the angle :g:`$KBN$ <0462.g1:< K B N )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle at :g:`$A$ <0462.g1:A>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$AKB$ <0462.g1:< A K B >>` and :g:`$AKN$ <0462.g1:< A K N >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the remaining angle :g:`$AKB$ <0462.g1:< A K B )>` is equal to the remaining angle :g:`$KNA$ <0462.g1:< K N A )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>` therefore the triangle :g:`$KBA$ <0462.g1:< K B A >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`$KNA$ <0462.g1:< K N A >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, proportionally, as the straight line :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`, so is :g:`$KA$ <0462.g1:/ K A />` to :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />`; :just:`[VI. 4] <VI.4>` therefore the rectangle :g:`$BA$ <0462.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`$AN$ <0462.g1:/ A N />` is equal to the square on :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   But the rectangle :g:`$AB$ <0462.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$BN$ <0462.g1:/ B N />` was also proved equal to the square on :g:`$BF$ <0462.g1:/ B F />`;
