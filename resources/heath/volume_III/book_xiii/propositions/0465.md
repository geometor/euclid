### PROPOSITION 11.

<p class="enunciation">If in a circle which has its diameter rational an equilateral<br />
pentagon be inscribed, the side of the pentagon is the irrational<br />
straight line called minor.</p>

For in the circle <span data-ref="0466.g1:( A B C D E )">$ABCDE$</span> which has its diameter rational<br />
let the equilateral pentagon <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A B C D E >">$ABCDE$</span> be inscribed;<br />
I say that the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight<br />
line called minor.

For let the centre of the circle, the point <span data-ref="0466.g1:F">$F$</span>, be taken,<br />
let <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span> be joined and carried through to the points, <span data-ref="0466.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:H">$H$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be joined,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> be made a fourth part of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Now <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is also rational.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is also rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the whole <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the circumference <span data-ref="0466.g1:( A C G )">$ACG$</span> is equal to the circum-<br />
ference <span data-ref="0466.g1:( A D G )">$ADG$</span>,<br />
and in them <span data-ref="0466.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0466.g1:( A E D )">$AED$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0466.g1:( C G )">$CG$</span> is equal to the remainder <span data-ref="0466.g1:( G D )">$GD$</span>.</p>
