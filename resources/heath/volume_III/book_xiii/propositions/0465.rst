| If $r$ be the radius of the circle, we have seen (XIII. 9, note) that
| :g:`$AK$ <0462.g1:/ A K />` $= \frac{r}{2}(\sqrt{5} - 1)$.

Therefore

$$\begin{aligned}
(\text{side of pentagon})^2 &= r^2 + \frac{r^2}{4}(6 - 2\sqrt{5}) \\
&= \frac{r^2}{4}(10 - 2\sqrt{5}),
\end{aligned}$$

so that

$$(\text{side of pentagon}) = \frac{r}{2}\sqrt{10 - 2\sqrt{5}}.$$

PROPOSITION 11.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   If in a circle which has its diameter rational an equilateral pentagon be inscribed, the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight line called minor.

| For in the circle :g:`$ABCDE$ <0466.g1:( A B C D E )>` which has its diameter rational
| let the equilateral pentagon :g:`$ABCDE$ <0466.g1:< A B C D E >>` be inscribed;
| I say that the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight
| line called minor.

| For let the centre of the circle, the point :g:`$F$ <0466.g1:F>`, be taken,
| let :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />` be joined and carried through to the points, :g:`$G$ <0466.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0466.g1:H>`,
| let :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` be joined,
| and let :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` be made a fourth part of :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />`.

.. subclause::

   Now :g:`$AF$ <0466.g1:/ A F />` is rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` is also rational.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is also rational;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the circumference :g:`$ACG$ <0466.g1:( A C G )>` is equal to the circum-
   | ference :g:`$ADG$ <0466.g1:( A D G )>`,
   | and in them :g:`$ABC$ <0466.g1:( A B C )>` is equal to :g:`$AED$ <0466.g1:( A E D )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`$CG$ <0466.g1:( C G )>` is equal to the remainder :g:`$GD$ <0466.g1:( G D )>`.
