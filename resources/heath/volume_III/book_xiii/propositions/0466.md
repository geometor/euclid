<p class="assertion">And, if we join <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, we conclude that the angles at <span data-ref="0466.g1:L">$L$</span><br />
are right,<br />
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C L /">$CL$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">For the same reason<br />
the angles at <span data-ref="0466.g1:M">$M$</span> are also right,<br />
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>.</p>

![Proposition 11](0466.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A L C )">$ALC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F )">$AMF$</span>,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< L A C )">$LAC$</span> is common to the two triangles <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L >">$ACL$</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F >">$AMF$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L )">$ACL$</span> is equal to the remaining<br />
angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< M F A )">$MFA$</span>; <span class="justification">[I. 32]</span><br />
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L >">$ACL$</span> is equiangular with the triangle<br />
<span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F >">$AMF$</span>;<br />
therefore, proportionally, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the doubles of the antecedents may be taken;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is the double of<br />
<span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to the half<br />
of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to the<br />
half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And the halves of the consequents may be taken;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to the half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span><br />
to the fourth of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.</p>
