And, if we join <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, we conclude that the angles at <span data-ref="0466.g1:L">$L$</span> are right,
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C L /">$CL$</span>.

For the same reason
the angles at <span data-ref="0466.g1:M">$M$</span> are also right,
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>.

![Proposition 11](0466.g1.svg)

Since then the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A L C )">$ALC$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F )">$AMF$</span>,
and the angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< L A C )">$LAC$</span> is common to the two triangles <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L >">$ACL$</span> and <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F >">$AMF$</span>,
therefore the remaining angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L )">$ACL$</span> is equal to the remaining angle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< M F A )">$MFA$</span>; [I. 32]
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A C L >">$ACL$</span> is equiangular with the triangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A M F >">$AMF$</span>;
therefore, proportionally, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.

And the doubles of the antecedents may be taken;
therefore, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.

But, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to the half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>;
therefore also, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to the half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.

And the halves of the consequents may be taken;
therefore, as the double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span> is to the half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to the fourth of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>.
