# Transcription Notes: Folio 0466 (Page 462)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page 462** continues the Euclidean demonstration of **Proposition 11** of Book XIII.
- Establishes that diameters $AG$ and $BH$ bisect chords $CD$ and $AC$ at right angles at points $L$ and $M$ respectively.
- Demonstrates equiangularity of triangles $\Delta ACL$ and $\Delta AMF$, yielding the fundamental proportion:
  $$\frac{2\,LC}{\frac{1}{2}CA} = \frac{MF}{\frac{1}{4}FA} \iff \frac{CD}{CM} = \frac{MF}{FK}.$$
- Follows directly from folio 0465 (page 461) and continues onto folio 0467 (page 463).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- Marginal Euclidean citation: `[I. 32]` for equiangular triangles.
- Proportional transformations (manipulating antecedents and consequents by factors of 2 and $\frac{1}{2}$):
  - $\frac{LC}{CA} = \frac{MF}{FA}$
  - Taking doubles of antecedents: $\frac{2\,LC}{CA} = \frac{2\,MF}{FA} = \frac{MF}{\frac{1}{2}FA}$
  - Taking halves of consequents: $\frac{2\,LC}{\frac{1}{2}CA} = \frac{MF}{\frac{1}{4}FA}$
  - Here $2\,LC = CD$ (side of pentagon), $\frac{1}{2}CA = CM$ (half of pentagon diagonal), and $\frac{1}{4}FA = FK$.
