:title: Proposition 11 (cont.)

.. assertion::

   | And, if we join :g:`$AD$ <0466.g1:/ A D />`, we conclude that the angles at :g:`$L$ <0466.g1:L>`
   | are right,
   | and :g:`$CD$ <0466.g1:/ C D />` is double of :g:`$CL$ <0466.g1:/ C L />`.

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | the angles at :g:`$M$ <0466.g1:M>` are also right,
   | and :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`.

.. picture:: 0466.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 11

.. subclause::

   | Since then the angle :g:`$ALC$ <0466.g1:< A L C )>` is equal to the angle :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F )>`,
   | and the angle :g:`$LAC$ <0466.g1:< L A C )>` is common to the two triangles :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L >>`
   | and :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F >>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the remaining angle :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L )>` is equal to the remaining
   | angle :g:`$MFA$ <0466.g1:< M F A )>`; :just:`[I. 32] <I.32>`
   | therefore the triangle :g:`$ACL$ <0466.g1:< A C L >>` is equiangular with the triangle
   | :g:`$AMF$ <0466.g1:< A M F >>`;
   | therefore, proportionally, as :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   And the doubles of the antecedents may be taken;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is the double of
   | :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   | But, as the double of :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` is to :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to the half
   | of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to the
   | half of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.

.. subclause::

   And the halves of the consequents may be taken;

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as the double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />` is to the half of :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />`
   | to the fourth of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`.
