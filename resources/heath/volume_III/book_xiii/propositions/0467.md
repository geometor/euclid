And <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> a
fourth part of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>.
   *Componendo* also, as the sum of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is
<span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>; [v. 18]
therefore also, as the square on the sum of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is to the
square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.
   And since, when the straight line subtending two sides of
the pentagon, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the
greater segment is equal to the side of the pentagon, that is,
to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, [xiii. 8]
while the square on the greater segment added to the half
of the whole is five times the square on the half of the
whole, [xiii. 1]
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is half of the whole <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> taken as one straight line is
five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>.
   But it was proved that, as the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> taken
as one straight line is to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is the square
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.
   But the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is rational,
for the diameter is rational;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is also rational;
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is rational.
   And, since <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is five times <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is twenty-five times the square
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.
   But the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> has not to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>
the ratio which a square number has to a square number;
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. [x. 9]
   And each of them is rational.
   Therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> are rational straight lines commen-
surable in square only.
