<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ L C /">$LC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is half of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C A /">$CA$</span>, and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> a<br />
fourth part of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F A /">$FA$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">*Componendo* also, as the sum of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is<br />
<span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>; <span class="justification">[v. 18]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore also, as the square on the sum of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And since, when the straight line subtending two sides of<br />
the pentagon, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the<br />
greater segment is equal to the side of the pentagon, that is,<br />
to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span class="justification">[xiii. 8]</span><br />
while the square on the greater segment added to the half<br />
of the whole is five times the square on the half of the<br />
whole, <span class="justification">[xiii. 1]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> is half of the whole <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> taken as one straight line is<br />
five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But it was proved that, as the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span> taken<br />
as one straight line is to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ C M /">$CM$</span>, so is the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is rational,<br />
for the diameter is rational;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is also rational;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is rational.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is five times <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is twenty-five times the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> has not to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span><br />
the ratio which a square number has to a square number;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is incommensurable in length with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 9]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And each of them is rational.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> are rational straight lines commen-<br />
surable in square only.</p>
