.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` is double of :g:`$LC$ <0466.g1:/ L C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is half of :g:`$CA$ <0466.g1:/ C A />`, and :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />` a
   | fourth part of :g:`$FA$ <0466.g1:/ F A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />` is to :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is :g:`$MF$ <0466.g1:/ M F />` to :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`.

.. subclause::

   | *Componendo* also, as the sum of :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is to :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is
   | :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`; :just:`[v. 18]`

.. assertion::

   | therefore also, as the square on the sum of :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is to the
   | square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   | And since, when the straight line subtending two sides of
   | the pentagon, as :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />`, is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the
   | greater segment is equal to the side of the pentagon, that is,
   | to :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :just:`[xiii. 8]`
   | while the square on the greater segment added to the half
   | of the whole is five times the square on the half of the
   | whole, :just:`[xiii. 1]`
   | and :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` is half of the whole :g:`$AC$ <0466.g1:/ A C />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` taken as one straight line is
   | five times the square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`.

.. subclause::

   | But it was proved that, as the square on :g:`$DC$ <0466.g1:/ D C />`, :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />` taken
   | as one straight line is to the square on :g:`$CM$ <0466.g1:/ C M />`, so is the square
   | on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` to the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   | But the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` is rational,
   | for the diameter is rational;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is also rational;
   | therefore :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is rational.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is quadruple of :g:`$FK$ <0466.g1:/ F K />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`;
   | therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is twenty-five times the square
   | on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`;
   | therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has not to the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`
   | the ratio which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is incommensurable in length with :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`. :just:`[x. 9]`

.. subclause::

   And each of them is rational.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`, :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` are rational straight lines commen-
   | surable in square only.
