<p class="sub-clause">But, if from a rational straight line there be subtracted a<br />
rational straight line which is commensurable with the whole<br />
in square only, the remainder is irrational, namely an apotome;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span> is an apotome and <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M K /">$MK$</span> the annex to it. <span class="justification">[x. 73]</span></p>

I say next that <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span> is also a fourth apotome.

Let the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ N /">$N$</span> be equal to that by which the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span><br />
by the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ N /">$N$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">*componendo* also, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K B /">$KB$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ F B /">$FB$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 15]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B F /">$BF$</span> is commensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is also commensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>. <span class="justification">[x. 12]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is five times the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> has to the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> the<br />
ratio which $5$ has to $1$.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore, *convertendo*, the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> has to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ N /">$N$</span> the ratio which $5$ has to $4$ <span class="justification">[v. 19, Por.]</span>, and this is not the<br />
ratio which a square number has to a square number;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is incommensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ N /">$N$</span>; <span class="justification">[x. 9]</span><br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span><br />
by the square on a straight line incommensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Since then the square on the whole <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is greater than<br />
the square on the annex <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> by the square on a straight line<br />
incommensurable with <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span>,<br />
and the whole <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B K /">$BK$</span> is commensurable with the rational straight<br />
line, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B H /">$BH$</span>, set out,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span> is a fourth apotome. <span class="justification">[x. Deff. III. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and<br />
a fourth apotome is irrational,<br />
and its square root is irrational, and is called minor. <span class="justification">[x. 94]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">But the square on <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to the rectangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span>, <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>,<br />
because, when <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A H /">$AH$</span> is joined, the triangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A B H >">$ABH$</span> is equiangular<br />
with the triangle <span data-ref="0466.g1:< A B M >">$ABM$</span>, and, as <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ H B /">$HB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span><br />
to <span data-ref="0466.g1:/ B M /">$BM$</span>.</p>
