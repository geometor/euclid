.. subclause::

   | But, if from a rational straight line there be subtracted a
   | rational straight line which is commensurable with the whole
   | in square only, the remainder is irrational, namely an apotome;

.. assertion:: [x. 73]

   therefore :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is an apotome and :g:`$MK$ <0466.g1:/ M K />` the annex to it.

I say next that :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is also a fourth apotome.

| Let the square on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` be equal to that by which the square
| on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`;
| therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`
| by the square on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$KF$ <0466.g1:/ K F />` is commensurable with :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />`,

.. assertion:: [x. 15]

   :term:`*componendo* <componendo>` also, :g:`$KB$ <0466.g1:/ K B />` is commensurable with :g:`$FB$ <0466.g1:/ F B />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$BF$ <0466.g1:/ B F />` is commensurable with :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`;

.. assertion:: [x. 12]

   therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is also commensurable with :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is five times the square
   | on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has to the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` the
   | ratio which $5$ has to $1$.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` has to the square
   | on :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />` the ratio which $5$ has to $4$ :just:`[v. 19, Por.]`, and this is not the
   | ratio which a square number has to a square number;
   | therefore :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is incommensurable with :g:`$N$ <0466.g1:/ N />`; :just:`[x. 9]`
   | therefore the square on :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than the square on :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />`
   | by the square on a straight line incommensurable with :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`.

.. subclause::

   | Since then the square on the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is greater than
   | the square on the annex :g:`$KM$ <0466.g1:/ K M />` by the square on a straight line
   | incommensurable with :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />`,
   | and the whole :g:`$BK$ <0466.g1:/ B K />` is commensurable with the rational straight
   | line, :g:`$BH$ <0466.g1:/ B H />`, set out,

.. assertion:: [x. Deff. III. 4]

   therefore :g:`$MB$ <0466.g1:/ M B />` is a fourth apotome.

.. subclause::

   | But the rectangle contained by a rational straight line and
   | a fourth apotome is irrational,
   | and its square root is irrational, and is called minor. :just:`[x. 94]`

.. assertion::

   | But the square on :g:`$AB$ <0466.g1:/ A B />` is equal to the rectangle :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />`, :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />`,
   | because, when :g:`$AH$ <0466.g1:/ A H />` is joined, the triangle :g:`$ABH$ <0466.g1:< A B H >>` is equiangular
   | with the triangle :g:`$ABM$ <0466.g1:< A B M >>`, and, as :g:`$HB$ <0466.g1:/ H B />` is to :g:`$BA$ <0466.g1:/ B A />`, so is :g:`$AB$ <0466.g1:/ A B />`
   | to :g:`$BM$ <0466.g1:/ B M />`.
