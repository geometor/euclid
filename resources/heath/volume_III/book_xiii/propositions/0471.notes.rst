Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Proposition 12 Proof Strategy**:

  - Center $D$, diameter $ADE$, chord $BE$.
  - Arc $BEC = 1/3$ of circumference $\implies$ arc $BE = 1/6$ of circumference.
  - Chord $BE$ is side of regular hexagon $\implies BE = DE = r$ (IV. 15, Porism).
  - Triangle $ABE$ is right-angled at $B$ (III. 31), so $AE^2 = AB^2 + BE^2 = 4r^2$.
  - Since $BE^2 = r^2$, *separando* gives $AB^2 = 3BE^2 = 3r^2$.

- **Proposition 13 Enunciation**: Heath uses "pyramid" for the regular tetrahedron ($\pi\upsilon\rho\alpha\mu\acute{\iota}\varsigma$). Diameter of sphere $D^2 = \frac{3}{2} s^2$, i.e. $s^2 = \frac{2}{3} D^2$.
