Let the diameter <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere be set out,<br />
and let it be cut at the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:C">$C$</span> so that <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;<br />
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( A D B )">$ADB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> be joined;<br />
let the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span> which has its radius equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be<br />
set out,<br />
let the equilateral triangle <span data-ref="0472.g1:< E F G >">$EFG$</span> be inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>,<br />
<span class="justification">[IV. 2]</span><br />
let the centre of the circle, the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:H">$H$</span>, be taken, <span class="justification">[III. 1]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> be joined;<br />
from the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:H">$H$</span> let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be set up at right angles to the plane<br />
of the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>, <span class="justification">[XI. 12]</span><br />
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> equal to the straight line <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be cut off from <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 13 Diagrams](0472.g1.svg)

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is at right angles to the plane of the<br />
circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight<br />
lines which meet it and are in the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[XI. Def. 3]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> meets it:</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>,<br />
and they contain right angles,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the base <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span></p>
