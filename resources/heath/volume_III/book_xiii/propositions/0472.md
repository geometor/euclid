   Let the diameter <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere be set out,
and let it be cut at the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:C">$C$</span> so that <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( A D B )">$ADB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> be joined;
let the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span> which has its radius equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> be
set out,
let the equilateral triangle <span data-ref="0472.g1:< E F G >">$EFG$</span> be inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>, [iv. 2]
let the centre of the circle, the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:H">$H$</span>, be taken, [iii. 1]
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> be joined;
from the point <span data-ref="0472.g1:H">$H$</span> let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be set up at right angles to the plane
of the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>, [xi. 12]
let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> equal to the straight line <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> be cut off from <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 13 Diagrams](0472.g1.svg)

   Now, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is at right angles to the plane of the
circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>,
therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight
lines which meet it and are in the plane of the circle <span data-ref="0472.g1:( E F G )">$EFG$</span>. [xi. Def. 3]
   But each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span> meets it:
therefore <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is at right angles to each of the straight lines
<span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H G /">$HG$</span>.
   And, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>,
and they contain right angles,
therefore the base <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>. [i. 4]
