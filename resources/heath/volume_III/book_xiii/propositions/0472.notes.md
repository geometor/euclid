## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Construction of the Regular Tetrahedron**:
  - Sphere diameter $AB$ divided at $C$ such that $AC = 2CB$.
  - Semicircle $ADB$ on $AB$; ordinate $CD \perp AB$, chord $DA$ joined.
  - Base circle $EFG$ with radius equal to $DC$.
  - Inscribe equilateral triangle $EFG$; center $H$, radii $HE, HF, HG$.
  - Erect $HK \perp$ plane of $EFG$ at $H$ with $HK = AC$.
  - Join edges $KE, KF, KG$.
  - By right triangles $\Delta HKE \cong \Delta CAD$, base $KE = DA$.
