:title: Proposition 13 (construction and proof)

| Let the diameter :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere be set out,
| and let it be cut at the point :g:`$C$ <0472.g1:C>` so that :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`;
| let the semicircle :g:`$ADB$ <0472.g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />`,
| let :g:`$CD$ <0472.g1:/ C D />` be drawn from the point :g:`$C$ <0472.g1:C>` at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />`,
| and let :g:`$DA$ <0472.g1:/ D A />` be joined;
| let the circle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:( E F G )>` which has its radius equal to :g:`$DC$ <0472.g1:/ D C />` be
| set out,
| let the equilateral triangle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:< E F G >>` be inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:( E F G )>`,
| :just:`[IV. 2] <IV.2>`
| let the centre of the circle, the point :g:`$H$ <0472.g1:H>`, be taken, :just:`[III. 1] <III.1>`
| let :g:`$EH$ <0472.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`$HF$ <0472.g1:/ H F />`, :g:`$HG$ <0472.g1:/ H G />` be joined;
| from the point :g:`$H$ <0472.g1:H>` let :g:`$HK$ <0472.g1:/ H K />` be set up at right angles to the plane
| of the circle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:( E F G )>`, :just:`[XI. 12] <XI.12>`
| let :g:`$HK$ <0472.g1:/ H K />` equal to the straight line :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` be cut off from :g:`$HK$ <0472.g1:/ H K />`,
| and let :g:`$KE$ <0472.g1:/ K E />`, :g:`$KF$ <0472.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0472.g1:/ K G />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0472.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13 Diagrams

.. subclause::

   | Now, since :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />` is at right angles to the plane of the
   | circle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:( E F G )>`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight
   | lines which meet it and are in the plane of the circle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:( E F G )>`.
   | :just:`[XI. Def. 3] <XI.def.3>`

.. subclause::

   But each of the straight lines :g:`$HE$ <0472.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$HF$ <0472.g1:/ H F />`, :g:`$HG$ <0472.g1:/ H G />` meets it:

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$HK$ <0472.g1:/ H K />` is at right angles to each of the straight lines
   | :g:`$HE$ <0472.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`$HF$ <0472.g1:/ H F />`, :g:`$HG$ <0472.g1:/ H G />`.

.. subclause::

   | And, since :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$HK$ <0472.g1:/ H K />`, and :g:`$CD$ <0472.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$HE$ <0472.g1:/ H E />`,
   | and they contain right angles,

.. assertion:: [I. 4] <I.4>

   therefore the base :g:`$DA$ <0472.g1:/ D A />` is equal to the base :g:`$KE$ <0472.g1:/ K E />`.
