   For the same reason
each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>;
therefore the three straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span> are equal to
one another.
   And, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is triple of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.
   But, as <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> to the square
on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>, as will be proved afterwards.
   Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span>.
   But the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ F E /">$FE$</span> is also triple of the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>, [xiii. 12]
and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D C /">$DC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>.
   But <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span> was proved equal to each of the straight lines
<span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span>;
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ G E /">$GE$</span> is also equal
to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span>;
therefore the four triangles <span data-ref="0472.g1:< E F G >">$EFG$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:< K E F >">$KEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:< K F G >">$KFG$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:< K E G >">$KEG$</span> are
equilateral.
   Therefore a pyramid has been constructed out of four
equilateral triangles, the triangle <span data-ref="0472.g1:< E F G >">$EFG$</span> being its base and the
point <span data-ref="0472.g1:K">$K$</span> its vertex.
   It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere
and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere
is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.
   For let the straight line <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span> be produced in a straight
line with <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.
   Now, since, as <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>, [vi. 8, Por.]
while <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span>;
therefore the rectangle <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span> is equal to the square on
<span data-ref="0472.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>. [vi. 17]
   And each of the angles <span data-ref="0472.g1:< K H E )">$KHE$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:< E H L )">$EHL$</span> is right;
therefore the semicircle described on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> will pass through
<span data-ref="0472.g1:E">$E$</span> also. [cf. vi. 8, iii. 31]
