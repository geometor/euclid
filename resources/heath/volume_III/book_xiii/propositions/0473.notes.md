## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Equality of All Six Edges**:
  - Since $AC = 2CB$, $AB = 3BC$.
  - Uses the lemma ($AB : BC = AD^2 : DC^2$, proved at the end of the proposition on folio 0474) to establish $AD^2 = 3DC^2$.
  - By Proposition 12, $FE^2 = 3EH^2$. Since $DC = EH$, $DA = EF$.
  - Thus all 6 edges ($EF, FG, GE, KE, KF, KG$) are equal, proving the pyramid is regular.
- **Comprehending in the Sphere**:
  - $KH$ produced to $L$ with $HL = CB$.
  - $KH \cdot HL = AC \cdot CB = CD^2 = EH^2$.
  - Since $\angle KHE = \angle EHL = 90^\circ$, semicircle on diameter $KL$ passes through $E$ (by converse of VI. 8 and III. 31).
