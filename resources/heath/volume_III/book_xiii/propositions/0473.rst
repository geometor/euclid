:title: Proposition 13 (proof continued)

.. assertion::

   | For the same reason
   | each of the straight lines :g:`$KF$ <0472.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0472.g1:/ K G />` is also equal to :g:`$DA$ <0472.g1:/ D A />`;
   | therefore the three straight lines :g:`$KE$ <0472.g1:/ K E />`, :g:`$KF$ <0472.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0472.g1:/ K G />` are equal to
   | one another.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`$BC$ <0472.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   | But, as :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0472.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$AD$ <0472.g1:/ A D />` to the square
   | on :g:`$DC$ <0472.g1:/ D C />`, as will be proved afterwards.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$AD$ <0472.g1:/ A D />` is triple of the square on :g:`$DC$ <0472.g1:/ D C />`.

.. subclause::

   | But the square on :g:`$FE$ <0472.g1:/ F E />` is also triple of the square on :g:`$EH$ <0472.g1:/ E H />`,
   | :just:`[XIII. 12] <XIII.12>`
   | and :g:`$DC$ <0472.g1:/ D C />` is equal to :g:`$EH$ <0472.g1:/ E H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$DA$ <0472.g1:/ D A />` is also equal to :g:`$EF$ <0472.g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$DA$ <0472.g1:/ D A />` was proved equal to each of the straight lines
   | :g:`$KE$ <0472.g1:/ K E />`, :g:`$KF$ <0472.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0472.g1:/ K G />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore each of the straight lines :g:`$EF$ <0472.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`$FG$ <0472.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`$GE$ <0472.g1:/ G E />` is also equal
   | to each of the straight lines :g:`$KE$ <0472.g1:/ K E />`, :g:`$KF$ <0472.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`$KG$ <0472.g1:/ K G />`;
   | therefore the four triangles :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:< E F G >>`, :g:`$KEF$ <0472.g1:< K E F >>`, :g:`$KFG$ <0472.g1:< K F G >>`, :g:`$KEG$ <0472.g1:< K E G >>` are
   | equilateral.

.. assertion::

   | Therefore a pyramid has been constructed out of four
   | equilateral triangles, the triangle :g:`$EFG$ <0472.g1:< E F G >>` being its base and the
   | point :g:`$K$ <0472.g1:K>` its vertex.

| It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere
| and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere
| is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.

| For let the straight line :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />` be produced in a straight
| line with :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />`,
| and let :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />` be made equal to :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   | Now, since, as :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`$CD$ <0472.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`$CD$ <0472.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`, :just:`[VI. 8, Por.]`
   | while :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$CD$ <0472.g1:/ C D />` to :g:`$HE$ <0472.g1:/ H E />`, and :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />` to :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />`,

.. assertion::

   | therefore, as :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`$HE$ <0472.g1:/ H E />`, so is :g:`$EH$ <0472.g1:/ E H />` to :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />`;
   | therefore the rectangle :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />` is equal to the square on
   | :g:`$EH$ <0472.g1:/ E H />`. :just:`[VI. 17] <VI.17>`

.. subclause::

   And each of the angles :g:`$KHE$ <0472.g1:< K H E )>`, :g:`$EHL$ <0472.g1:< E H L )>` is right;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the semicircle described on :g:`$KL$ <0472.g1:/ K L />` will pass through
   | :g:`$E$ <0472.g1:E>` also. :just:`[cf. VI. 8, III. 31]`
