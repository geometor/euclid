   If then, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round
and restored to the same position from which it began to be
moved, it will also pass through the points <span data-ref="0472.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0472.g1:G">$G$</span>,

right angles;
and the pyramid will be comprehended in the given sphere.
   For <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, the diameter of the sphere, is equal to the
diameter <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere, inasmuch as <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> was
made equal to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span> to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.
   I say next that the square on the diameter of the sphere
is one and a half times the square on the side of the
pyramid.
   For, since <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,
therefore <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is triple of <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>;
and, *convertendo*, <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is one and a half times <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>.
   But, as <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> to the square
on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.
   Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is also one and a half times
the square on <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>.
   And <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> is the diameter of the given sphere, and <span data-ref="0472.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is
equal to the side of the pyramid.
   Therefore the square on the diameter of the sphere is
one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.
