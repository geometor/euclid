If then, :g:`$KL$ <0472.g1:/ K L />` remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round and restored to the same position from which it began to be moved, it will also pass through the points :g:`$F$ <0472.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0472.g1:G>`, since, if :g:`$FL$ <0472.g1:/ F L />`, :g:`$LG$ <0472.g1:/ L G />` be joined, the angles at :g:`$F$ <0472.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0472.g1:G>` similarly become right angles; and the pyramid will be comprehended in the given sphere.

.. subclause::

   For :g:`$KL$ <0472.g1:/ K L />`, the diameter of the sphere, is equal to the diameter :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere, inasmuch as :g:`$KH$ <0472.g1:/ K H />` was made equal to :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`$HL$ <0472.g1:/ H L />` to :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`.

I say next that the square on the diameter of the sphere is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`$CB$ <0472.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0472.g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`$BC$ <0472.g1:/ B C />`; and, :term:`*convertendo* <convertendo>`, :g:`$BA$ <0472.g1:/ B A />` is one and a half times :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`$BA$ <0472.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`$AC$ <0472.g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`$BA$ <0472.g1:/ B A />` to the square on :g:`$AD$ <0472.g1:/ A D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`$BA$ <0472.g1:/ B A />` is also one and a half times the square on :g:`$AD$ <0472.g1:/ A D />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$BA$ <0472.g1:/ B A />` is the diameter of the given sphere, and :g:`$AD$ <0472.g1:/ A D />` is equal to the side of the pyramid.

.. qed::

   Therefore the square on the diameter of the sphere is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid. :just:`Q. E. D.`
