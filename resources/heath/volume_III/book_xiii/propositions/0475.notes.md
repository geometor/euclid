## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Suspected Lemma**: Heath notes that the Lemma (showing $AB : BC = AD^2 : DC^2$) is suspect as an interpolation because Euclid commonly assumes more difficult stereometrical propositions without proof, and an interpolated gloss lacked knowledge of this Lemma.
- **Proof Decomposition**: Heath structures the demonstration of Euclid's construction into three clear algebraic/geometric steps:
  1. Showing that $KEFG$ has all edges equal to $AD$.
  2. Proving that $KEFG$ is inscribable in a sphere of diameter $AB$.
  3. Establishing the diameter-to-edge relation $AB^2 = \frac{3}{2}AD^2$.
