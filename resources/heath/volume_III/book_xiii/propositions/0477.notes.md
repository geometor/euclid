## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Pappus' Geometric Strategy**: Uses two equal and parallel circular sections of diameter $d'$, where $d'^2 = \frac{2}{3}d^2$. The edge of the tetrahedron is the diameter of these sections ($BC = AB = LM = d'$).
- **Division of Diameter**: Point $P$ on sphere diameter $BL$ is constructed such that $LP = 2PB$, meaning $LP : LB = 2 : 3$. The perpendicular ordinate $PM$ directly determines chord $LM$ since $LM^2 = LP \cdot LB$.
