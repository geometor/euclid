### PROPOSITION 14.

<p class="enunciation">
To construct an octahedron and comprehend it in a sphere,<br />
as in the preceding case; and to prove that the square on the<br />
diameter of the sphere is double of the square on the side of the<br />
octahedron.
</p>

Let the diameter <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere be set out,<br />
and let it be bisected at <span data-ref="0478.g1:C">$C$</span>;<br />
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0478.g1:( A D B )">$ADB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0478.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be joined;<br />
let the square <span data-ref="0478.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span>, having each of its sides equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,<br />
be set out,<br />
let <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ H F /">$HF$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span> be joined,<br />
from the point <span data-ref="0478.g1:K">$K$</span> let the straight line <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> be set up at right<br />
angles to the plane of the square <span data-ref="0478.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span> <span class="justification">[XI. 12]</span>, and let it be<br />
carried through to the other side of the plane, as <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>;<br />
from the straight lines <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> let <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span> be respectively<br />
cut off equal to one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L F /">$LF$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L G /">$LG$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L H /">$LH$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ M E /">$ME$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span> be joined.

![Proposition 14 Diagram](0478.g1.svg)

Then, since <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span>,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0478.g1:< E K H )">$EKH$</span> is right,<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>

Again, since <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L K /">$LK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0478.g1:< L K E )">$LKE$</span> is right,<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E L /">$EL$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[id.]</span>
