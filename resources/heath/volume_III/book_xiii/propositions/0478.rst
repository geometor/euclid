PROPOSITION 14.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To construct an octahedron and comprehend it in a sphere, as in the preceding case; and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is double of the square on the side of the octahedron.

| Let the diameter :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere be set out,
| and let it be bisected at :g:`$C$ <0478.g1:C>`;
| let the semicircle :g:`$ADB$ <0478.g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />`,
| let :g:`$CD$ <0478.g1:/ C D />` be drawn from :g:`$C$ <0478.g1:C>` at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />`,
| let :g:`$DB$ <0478.g1:/ D B />` be joined;
| let the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0478.g1:< E F G H >>`, having each of its sides equal to :g:`$DB$ <0478.g1:/ D B />`,
| be set out,
| let :g:`$HF$ <0478.g1:/ H F />`, :g:`$EG$ <0478.g1:/ E G />` be joined,
| from the point :g:`$K$ <0478.g1:K>` let the straight line :g:`$KL$ <0478.g1:/ K L />` be set up at right
| angles to the plane of the square :g:`$EFGH$ <0478.g1:< E F G H >>` :just:`[XI. 12] <XI.12>`, and let it be
| carried through to the other side of the plane, as :g:`$KM$ <0478.g1:/ K M />`;
| from the straight lines :g:`$KL$ <0478.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$KM$ <0478.g1:/ K M />` let :g:`$KL$ <0478.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$KM$ <0478.g1:/ K M />` be respectively
| cut off equal to one of the straight lines :g:`$EK$ <0478.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`$FK$ <0478.g1:/ F K />`, :g:`$GK$ <0478.g1:/ G K />`, :g:`$HK$ <0478.g1:/ H K />`,
| and let :g:`$LE$ <0478.g1:/ L E />`, :g:`$LF$ <0478.g1:/ L F />`, :g:`$LG$ <0478.g1:/ L G />`, :g:`$LH$ <0478.g1:/ L H />`, :g:`$ME$ <0478.g1:/ M E />`, :g:`$MF$ <0478.g1:/ M F />`, :g:`$MG$ <0478.g1:/ M G />`, :g:`$MH$ <0478.g1:/ M H />` be joined.

.. picture:: 0478.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 14 Diagram

| Then, since :g:`$KE$ <0478.g1:/ K E />` is equal to :g:`$KH$ <0478.g1:/ K H />`,
| and the angle :g:`$EKH$ <0478.g1:< E K H )>` is right,
| therefore the square on :g:`$HE$ <0478.g1:/ H E />` is double of the square on :g:`$EK$ <0478.g1:/ E K />`.
| :just:`[I. 47] <I.47>`

| Again, since :g:`$LK$ <0478.g1:/ L K />` is equal to :g:`$KE$ <0478.g1:/ K E />`,
| and the angle :g:`$LKE$ <0478.g1:< L K E )>` is right,
| therefore the square on :g:`$EL$ <0478.g1:/ E L />` is double of the square on :g:`$EK$ <0478.g1:/ E K />`.
| :just:`[id.]`
