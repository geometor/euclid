But the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span> was also proved double of the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>.

For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L H /">$LH$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>;<br />
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0478.g1:< L E H >">$LEH$</span> is equilateral.

Similarly we can prove that each of the remaining triangles<br />
of which the sides of the square <span data-ref="0478.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span> are the bases,<br />
and the points <span data-ref="0478.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:M">$M$</span> the vertices, is equilateral;<br />
therefore an octahedron has been constructed which is contained<br />
by eight equilateral triangles.

<p class="sub-clause">
It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere,<br />
and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is<br />
double of the square on the side of the octahedron.
</p>

For, since the three straight lines <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L K /">$LK$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> are equal<br />
to one another,<br />
therefore the semicircle described on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> will also pass<br />
through <span data-ref="0478.g1:E">$E$</span>.

And for the same reason,<br />
if, <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round and<br />
restored to the same position from which it began to be<br />
moved,<br />
it will also pass through the points <span data-ref="0478.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0478.g1:H">$H$</span>,<br />
and the octahedron will have been comprehended in a sphere.

<p class="assertion">
I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.
</p>

For, since <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L K /">$LK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>,<br />
while <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> is common,<br />
and they contain right angles,<br />
therefore the base <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 4]</span>

And, since the angle <span data-ref="0478.g1:< L E M )">$LEM$</span> is right, for it is in a semicircle,<br />
<span class="justification">[III. 31]</span><br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>.<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 47]</span>

Again, since <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.
