But, as <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> was also proved double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>.

And the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>, for <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> was made equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>.

Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the given sphere;
therefore <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is equal to the diameter of the given sphere.

Therefore the octahedron has been comprehended in the given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time that the square on the diameter of the sphere is double of the square on the side of the octahedron.

<div style="text-align: right;">Q. E. D.</div>

<span data-ref="0480.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span> being a square with side equal to <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>, it follows that <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K F /">$KF$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K G /">$KG$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are all equal to <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>.

So are <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>, by construction;
hence <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L F /">$LF$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L G /">$LG$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L H /">$LH$</span> and <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ M E /">$ME$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ M F /">$MF$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ M G /">$MG$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span> are all equal to <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> or <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

Thus (1) the figure is made up of eight equilateral triangles and is therefore a regular octahedron.

(2) Since
<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>,
the semicircle on <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> in the plane <span data-ref="0480.g1:< L K E >">$LKE$</span> passes through <span data-ref="0480.g1:E">$E$</span>.

Similarly <span data-ref="0480.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0480.g1:H">$H$</span> lie on semicircles on <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> as diameter.

Thus all the vertices of the tetrahedron lie on the sphere of which <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is a diameter.

(3)
<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ E M /">$EM$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;
therefore
<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>$^2 = 2$<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ E L /">$EL$</span>$^2 = 2$<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>$^2 = $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>$^2$,
or
<span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.
