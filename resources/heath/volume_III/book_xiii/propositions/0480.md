But, as <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> was also proved double of the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>.

And the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is equal to the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L E /">$LE$</span>, for<br />
<span data-ref="0478.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span> was made equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>.

Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the given sphere;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0478.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is equal to the diameter of the given sphere.

Therefore the octahedron has been comprehended in the<br />
given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time<br />
that the square on the diameter of the sphere is double of the<br />
square on the side of the octahedron.

<p class="qed">Q. E. D.</p>

(2) Since <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K E /">$KE$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> $= $ <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ K M /">$KM$</span>,<br />

Thus all the vertices of the tetrahedron lie on the sphere of which <span data-ref="0480.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> is<br />
