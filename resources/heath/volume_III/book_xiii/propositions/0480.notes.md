# Transcription Notes: Folio 0480 (Page 476)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page & Book**: Page 476; Book XIII, Proposition 14 (concluded) and Heath's commentary.
- **Folio Continuity**: Follows Page 475 (folio 0479), completing Euclid's proof (`Q. E. D.`). Heath's commentary introduces an alternative clearer diagram, carrying over to Page 477 (folio 0481).

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Textual Erratum / Lapsus Calami**: In part (2) of his commentary, Heath writes:
  *"Thus all the vertices of the **tetrahedron** lie on the sphere of which LM is a diameter."*
  This is an inadvertent slip of the pen for **octahedron**, preserved faithfully in the transcription.
- **Equivalence of Diameters**: The demonstration completes Euclid's proof that $LM = AB$ and that $AB^2 = 2BD^2 = 2 \cdot \text{edge}^2$.
