| But, as :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0478.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` to the square
| on :g:`$BD$ <0478.g1:/ B D />`;
| therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` is double of the square on :g:`$BD$ <0478.g1:/ B D />`.

| But the square on :g:`$LM$ <0478.g1:/ L M />` was also proved double of the
| square on :g:`$LE$ <0478.g1:/ L E />`.

| And the square on :g:`$DB$ <0478.g1:/ D B />` is equal to the square on :g:`$LE$ <0478.g1:/ L E />`, for
| :g:`$EH$ <0478.g1:/ E H />` was made equal to :g:`$DB$ <0478.g1:/ D B />`.

| Therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to the square
| on :g:`$LM$ <0478.g1:/ L M />`;
| therefore :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` is equal to :g:`$LM$ <0478.g1:/ L M />`.

| And :g:`$AB$ <0478.g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the given sphere;
| therefore :g:`$LM$ <0478.g1:/ L M />` is equal to the diameter of the given sphere.

| Therefore the octahedron has been comprehended in the
| given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time
| that the square on the diameter of the sphere is double of the
| square on the side of the octahedron.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.

| So are :g:`$KL$ <0480.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`$KM$ <0480.g1:/ K M />`, by construction;
| hence :g:`$LE$ <0480.g1:/ L E />`, :g:`$LF$ <0480.g1:/ L F />`, :g:`$LG$ <0480.g1:/ L G />`, :g:`$LH$ <0480.g1:/ L H />` and :g:`$ME$ <0480.g1:/ M E />`, :g:`$MF$ <0480.g1:/ M F />`, :g:`$MG$ <0480.g1:/ M G />`, :g:`$MH$ <0480.g1:/ M H />` are all equal to :g:`$EF$ <0480.g1:/ E F />` or :g:`$BD$ <0480.g1:/ B D />`.

| Thus (1) the figure is made up of eight equilateral triangles and is therefore
| a regular octahedron.

| (2) Since :g:`$KE$ <0480.g1:/ K E />` $= $ :g:`$KL$ <0480.g1:/ K L />` $= $ :g:`$KM$ <0480.g1:/ K M />`,
| the semicircle on :g:`$LM$ <0480.g1:/ L M />` in the plane :g:`$LKE$ <0480.g1:< L K E >>` passes through :g:`$E$ <0480.g1:E>`.

Similarly :g:`$F$ <0480.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0480.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0480.g1:H>` lie on semicircles on :g:`$LM$ <0480.g1:/ L M />` as diameter.

| Thus all the vertices of the tetrahedron lie on the sphere of which :g:`$LM$ <0480.g1:/ L M />` is
| a diameter.

| (3) :g:`$LE$ <0480.g1:/ L E />` $= $ :g:`$EM$ <0480.g1:/ E M />` $= $ :g:`$BD$ <0480.g1:/ B D />`;
| therefore :g:`$LM$ <0480.g1:/ L M />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$EL$ <0480.g1:/ E L />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$BD$ <0480.g1:/ B D />` $^2$
| $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0480.g1:/ A B />` $^2$,
| or :g:`$LM$ <0480.g1:/ L M />` $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0480.g1:/ A B />`.
