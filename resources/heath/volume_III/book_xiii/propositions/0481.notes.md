## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Octahedron Edge Metric**: $\text{edge} = \sqrt{2} \cdot r$.
- **Pappus' Geometry**: Circumscribes two opposite equilateral triangular faces ($ABC$ and $DEF$) by two equal and parallel circular sections of diameter $d'$, where $d^2 : d'^2 = 3 : 2$ ($d'^2 = \frac{2}{3}d^2$). Diameter $AF$ of the sphere connects opposite vertices across the two parallel sections.
- **Methodological Contrast**: Heath ends with a succinct structural observation comparing XIII. 13 and XIII. 14:
  - In XIII. 13 (tetrahedron): Euclid finds the circumscribed circle first; Pappus finds an edge first.
  - In XIII. 14 (octahedron): Euclid finds the edge first; Pappus finds the face circle first.
