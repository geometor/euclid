# PROPOSITION 15.

*To construct a cube and comprehend it in a sphere, like the pyramid; and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.*

Let the diameter <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere be set out,
and let it be cut at <span data-ref="0482.g1:C">$C$</span> so that <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0482.g1:( A D B )">$ADB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0482.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles to <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be joined;
let the square <span data-ref="0482.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span> having its side equal to <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be set out,
from <span data-ref="0482.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:H">$H$</span> let <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span> be drawn at right angles to the plane of the square <span data-ref="0482.g1:< E F G H >">$EFGH$</span>,
from <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span> let <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ F L /">$FL$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ G M /">$GM$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ H N /">$HN$</span> respectively be cut off equal to one of the straight lines <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ H E /">$HE$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ N K /">$NK$</span> be joined;
therefore the cube <span data-ref="0482.g1:< F N >">$FN$</span> has been constructed which is contained by six equal squares.
