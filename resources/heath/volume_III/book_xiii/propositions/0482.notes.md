# Transcription Notes: Folio 0482 (Page 478)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 478 is Book XIII, Proposition 15 (Cube construction and inscription in a sphere).
- **Note on Prompt Description**: The prompt header noted Pages 478–484 as Proposition 16 (Icosahedron), but Proposition 15 actually occupies pages 478–480 (folios 0482–0484), with Proposition 16 beginning on page 481 (folio 0485).
- **Content**: Enunciation of Proposition 15, setting-out of the diameter $AB$ with division at $C$ ($AC = 2CB$), semicircle $ADB$, perpendicular $CD$, side $DB$, construction of cube $FN$ on square $EFGH$, and commencement of the proof that the sphere circumscribes the cube.
- **Continuity**: Folio ends mid-sentence: "*hence also, if we join FK, GF will be at right angles to FK;*", which carries over to the top of folio 0483 (page 479): "*and for this reason again the semicircle described on GK will also pass through F.*"

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Proportional Division**: $AC = 2CB$, meaning $AB = 3CB$, which leads directly to the relation that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple the square on the side of the cube ($AB^2 = 3DB^2$).
- **Marginal Reference**: Includes `[XI. Def. 3]` in the right margin for perpendicularity of a line to a plane.
