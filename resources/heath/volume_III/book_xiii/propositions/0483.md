therefore the square on <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0482.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span> was also proved triple of the square<br />

sphere.

Therefore the cube has been comprehended in the given<br />
sphere; and it has been demonstrated at the same time that<br />
the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square<br />
on the side of the cube.
