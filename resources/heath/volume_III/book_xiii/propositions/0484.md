**Pappus' solution.**

In this case too Pappus (III. pp. 144—148) gives the full analysis and<br />
synthesis.

*Analysis.*

Suppose the problem solved, and let the vertices of the cube be<br />
<span data-ref="0484.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:D">$D$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:H">$H$</span>.<br />
Draw planes through <span data-ref="0484.g1:A">$A$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:B">$B$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:C">$C$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:D">$D$</span> and<br />
<span data-ref="0484.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:H">$H$</span> respectively; these will produce<br />
parallel circular sections, which are also equal<br />
since the inscribed squares are equal.<br />
And <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span> will be a diameter of the sphere.<br />
Join <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>.<br />
Now, since <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>$^2 = 2$<span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E H /">$EH$</span>$^2 = 2$<span data-ref="0484.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>$^2$,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0484.g1:< C G E )">$CGE$</span> is right,
<p class="assertion"><span data-ref="0484.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>$^2 = $ <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span>$^2 + $ <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>$^2 = \frac{3}{2}$<span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>$^2$.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ C E /">$CE$</span>$^2$ is given;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ E G /">$EG$</span>$^2$ is given, so that the circles<br />
<span data-ref="0484.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>, <span data-ref="0484.g1:( A B C D )">$ABCD$</span>, and the squares inscribed in them, are given.</p>

![Pappus' solution for the cube](0484.g1.svg)

*Synthesis.*

Draw two parallel circular sections with equal diameters $d'$, such that<br />
$$d^2 = \frac{3}{2}d'^2,$$
where $d$ is the diameter of the given sphere.<br />
Inscribe a square in one of the circles, as <span data-ref="0484.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span>.<br />
In the other circle draw <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, and complete the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0484.g1:/ F G /">$FG$</span> inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0484.g1:( E F G H )">$EFGH$</span>.<br />
<p class="assertion">The eight vertices of the required cube are thus determined.</p>
