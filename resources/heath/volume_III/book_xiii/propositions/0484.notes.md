## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Formula for the Edge**: $\text{(edge of cube)} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot r = \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3} \cdot r$.
- **Pappus' Method**: Determines the cube by finding two parallel circular sections of the sphere with equal diameters $d'$ such that $d^2 = \frac{3}{2}d'^2$, then inscribing congruent squares in them oriented with corresponding sides parallel.
