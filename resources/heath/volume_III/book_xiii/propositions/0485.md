### PROPOSITION 16.

<p class="enunciation">To construct an icosahedron and comprehend it in a sphere,<br />
like the aforesaid figures; and to prove that the side of the<br />
icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.</p>

Let the diameter <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> of the given sphere be set out,<br />
and let it be cut at <span data-ref="0485.g1:C">$C$</span> so that <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
let the semicircle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( A D B )">$ADB$</span> be described on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0485.g1:C">$C$</span> at right angles<br />
to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> be joined;

let the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span> be set out and let its radius be equal<br />
to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,<br />
let the equilateral and equiangular pentagon <span data-ref="0485.g1:< E F G H K >">$EFGHK$</span> be<br />
inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>,<br />
let the circumferences <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F )">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:( F G )">$FG$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:( G H )">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:( H K )">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:( K E )">$KE$</span> be bisected at<br />
the points <span data-ref="0485.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:M">$M$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:N">$N$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:O">$O$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:P">$P$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ O P /">$OP$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P L /">$PL$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span> be joined.
