# Transcription Notes: Folio 0485 (Page 481)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: Page 481 is the opening page of Book XIII, Proposition 16 (Construction and circumscription of the regular icosahedron, and proof that its edge is a minor irrational straight line).
- **Continuity**: Sets out the construction: division of sphere diameter $AB$ at $C$ ($AC = 4CB$), ordinate $CD$, chord $DB$ defining the radius of base circle $EFGHK$, regular pentagon $EFGHK$, circumference bisections at $L, M, N, O, P$, and chords forming pentagon $LMNOP$ and decagon side $EP$.
- **Folio Continuity Out**: Ends with "*and let LM, MN, NO, OP, PL, EP be joined.*", leading to folio 0486 (page 482): "*Therefore the pentagon LMNOP is also equilateral, and the straight line EP belongs to a decagon.*"

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Quadruple Division**: $AC = 4CB$, so $AB = 5CB$. By right-triangle relations, $DB^2 = AB \cdot CB = \frac{1}{5}AB^2$, establishing that the radius of the circumscribing circles for the pentagonal rings is $r_c = r / \sqrt{5}$ (or $DB = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} AB$).
- **Minor Straight Line**: Defined in Book X, Proposition 76; Euclid here constructs a 3D solid whose edge is the minor irrational line.
