PROPOSITION 16.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. enunciation::

   To construct an icosahedron and comprehend it in a sphere, like the aforesaid figures; and to prove that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.

| Let the diameter :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere be set out,
| and let it be cut at :g:`$C$ <0485.g1:C>` so that :g:`$AC$ <0485.g1:/ A C />` is quadruple of :g:`$CB$ <0485.g1:/ C B />`,
| let the semicircle :g:`$ADB$ <0485.g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />`,
| let the straight line :g:`$CD$ <0485.g1:/ C D />` be drawn from :g:`$C$ <0485.g1:C>` at right angles
| to :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />`,
| and let :g:`$DB$ <0485.g1:/ D B />` be joined;

.. picture:: 0485.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 16

| let the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>` be set out and let its radius be equal
| to :g:`$DB$ <0485.g1:/ D B />`,
| let the equilateral and equiangular pentagon :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:< E F G H K >>` be
| inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`,
| let the circumferences :g:`$EF$ <0485.g1:( E F )>`, :g:`$FG$ <0485.g1:( F G )>`, :g:`$GH$ <0485.g1:( G H )>`, :g:`$HK$ <0485.g1:( H K )>`, :g:`$KE$ <0485.g1:( K E )>` be bisected at
| the points :g:`$L$ <0485.g1:L>`, :g:`$M$ <0485.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0485.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0485.g1:O>`, :g:`$P$ <0485.g1:P>`,
| and let :g:`$LM$ <0485.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`$MN$ <0485.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`$NO$ <0485.g1:/ N O />`, :g:`$OP$ <0485.g1:/ O P />`, :g:`$PL$ <0485.g1:/ P L />`, :g:`$EP$ <0485.g1:/ E P />` be joined.
