Therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L M N O P >">$LMNOP$</span> is also equilateral,
and the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span> belongs to a decagon.

Now from the points <span data-ref="0485.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:K">$K$</span> let the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ H T /">$HT$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span> be set up at right angles to the plane of the circle, and let them be equal to the radius of the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>,
let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U Q /">$UQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q L /">$QL$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L R /">$LR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M S /">$MS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S N /">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ N T /">$NT$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T O /">$TO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ O U /">$OU$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U P /">$UP$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P Q /">$PQ$</span> be joined.

Now, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span> is at right angles to the same plane,
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span>. [XI. 6]

But it is also equal to it;
and the straight lines joining those extremities of equal and parallel straight lines which are in the same direction are equal and parallel. [I. 33]

Therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> belongs to an equilateral pentagon;
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>.

For the same reason
each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span> also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>;
therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q R S T U >">$QRSTU$</span> is equilateral.

And, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q E /">$QE$</span> belongs to a hexagon,
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span> to a decagon,
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q E P )">$QEP$</span> is right,
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q P /">$QP$</span> belongs to a pentagon;
for the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the square on the side of the hexagon and the square on the side of the decagon inscribed in the same circle. [XIII. 10]

For the same reason
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P U /">$PU$</span> is also a side of a pentagon.

But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> also belongs to a pentagon;
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q P U >">$QPU$</span> is equilateral.

For the same reason
each of the triangles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q L R >">$QLR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< R M S >">$RMS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< S N T >">$SNT$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< T O U >">$TOU$</span> is also equilateral.
