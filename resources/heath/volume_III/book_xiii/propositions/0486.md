Therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L M N O P >">$LMNOP$</span> is also equilateral,<br />
and the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span> belongs to a decagon.

Now from the points <span data-ref="0485.g1:E">$E$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:F">$F$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:K">$K$</span> let the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ F R /">$FR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ G S /">$GS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ H T /">$HT$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span> be set up at right angles to the plane<br />
of the circle, and let them be equal to the radius of the circle<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U Q /">$UQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q L /">$QL$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L R /">$LR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R M /">$RM$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M S /">$MS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S N /">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ N T /">$NT$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T O /">$TO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ O U /">$OU$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U P /">$UP$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P Q /">$PQ$</span> be joined.

Now, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span> is at right<br />
angles to the same plane,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Q /">$EQ$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ K U /">$KU$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 6]</span></p>

But it is also equal to it;<br />
and the straight lines joining those extremities of equal and<br />
parallel straight lines which are in the same direction are equal<br />
and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span>
<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span> belongs to an equilateral pentagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed<br />
in the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span> also belongs to<br />
the equilateral pentagon inscribed in the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q R S T U >">$QRSTU$</span> is equilateral.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q E /">$QE$</span> belongs to a hexagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E P /">$EP$</span> to a decagon,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q E P )">$QEP$</span> is right,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q P /">$QP$</span> belongs to a pentagon;</p>
for the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the<br />
square on the side of the hexagon and the square on the side<br />
of the decagon inscribed in the same circle. <span class="justification">[XIII. 10]</span>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P U /">$PU$</span> is also a side of a pentagon.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> also belongs to a pentagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q P U >">$QPU$</span> is equilateral.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
each of the triangles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q L R >">$QLR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< R M S >">$RMS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< S N T >">$SNT$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< T O U >">$TOU$</span> is also equi-<br />
lateral.</p>
