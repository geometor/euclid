## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Key Theorem (XIII. 10)**: The square on the side of a regular pentagon equals the sum of the squares on the sides of the regular hexagon and decagon inscribed in the same circle ($s_5^2 = s_6^2 + s_{10}^2$). Here $QE = s_6$ (radius), $EP = s_{10}$, $\angle QEP = 90^\circ$, so $QP^2 = QE^2 + EP^2 = s_5^2$, proving $QP = s_5$.
