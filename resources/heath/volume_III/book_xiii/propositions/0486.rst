:title: Proposition 16

| Therefore the pentagon :g:`$LMNOP$ <0485.g1:< L M N O P >>` is also equilateral,
| and the straight line :g:`$EP$ <0485.g1:/ E P />` belongs to a decagon.

| Now from the points :g:`$E$ <0485.g1:E>`, :g:`$F$ <0485.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0485.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0485.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0485.g1:K>` let the straight lines
| :g:`$EQ$ <0485.g1:/ E Q />`, :g:`$FR$ <0485.g1:/ F R />`, :g:`$GS$ <0485.g1:/ G S />`, :g:`$HT$ <0485.g1:/ H T />`, :g:`$KU$ <0485.g1:/ K U />` be set up at right angles to the plane
| of the circle, and let them be equal to the radius of the circle
| :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`,
| let :g:`$QR$ <0485.g1:/ Q R />`, :g:`$RS$ <0485.g1:/ R S />`, :g:`$ST$ <0485.g1:/ S T />`, :g:`$TU$ <0485.g1:/ T U />`, :g:`$UQ$ <0485.g1:/ U Q />`, :g:`$QL$ <0485.g1:/ Q L />`, :g:`$LR$ <0485.g1:/ L R />`, :g:`$RM$ <0485.g1:/ R M />`, :g:`$MS$ <0485.g1:/ M S />`, :g:`$SN$ <0485.g1:/ S N />`, :g:`$NT$ <0485.g1:/ N T />`,
| :g:`$TO$ <0485.g1:/ T O />`, :g:`$OU$ <0485.g1:/ O U />`, :g:`$UP$ <0485.g1:/ U P />`, :g:`$PQ$ <0485.g1:/ P Q />` be joined.

| Now, since each of the straight lines :g:`$EQ$ <0485.g1:/ E Q />`, :g:`$KU$ <0485.g1:/ K U />` is at right
| angles to the same plane,

.. assertion:: [XI. 6] <XI.6>

   therefore :g:`$EQ$ <0485.g1:/ E Q />` is parallel to :g:`$KU$ <0485.g1:/ K U />`.

| But it is also equal to it;
| and the straight lines joining those extremities of equal and
| parallel straight lines which are in the same direction are equal
| and parallel. :just:`[I. 33] <I.33>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`$QU$ <0485.g1:/ Q U />` is equal and parallel to :g:`$EK$ <0485.g1:/ E K />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EK$ <0485.g1:/ E K />` belongs to an equilateral pentagon;

.. assertion::

   | therefore :g:`$QU$ <0485.g1:/ Q U />` also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed
   | in the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | each of the straight lines :g:`$QR$ <0485.g1:/ Q R />`, :g:`$RS$ <0485.g1:/ R S />`, :g:`$ST$ <0485.g1:/ S T />`, :g:`$TU$ <0485.g1:/ T U />` also belongs to
   | the equilateral pentagon inscribed in the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the pentagon :g:`$QRSTU$ <0485.g1:< Q R S T U >>` is equilateral.

| And, since :g:`$QE$ <0485.g1:/ Q E />` belongs to a hexagon,
| and :g:`$EP$ <0485.g1:/ E P />` to a decagon,
| and the angle :g:`$QEP$ <0485.g1:< Q E P )>` is right,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$QP$ <0485.g1:/ Q P />` belongs to a pentagon;

| for the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the
| square on the side of the hexagon and the square on the side
| of the decagon inscribed in the same circle. :just:`[XIII. 10] <XIII.10>`

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | :g:`$PU$ <0485.g1:/ P U />` is also a side of a pentagon.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$QU$ <0485.g1:/ Q U />` also belongs to a pentagon;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$QPU$ <0485.g1:< Q P U >>` is equilateral.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | each of the triangles :g:`$QLR$ <0485.g1:< Q L R >>`, :g:`$RMS$ <0485.g1:< R M S >>`, :g:`$SNT$ <0485.g1:< S N T >>`, :g:`$TOU$ <0485.g1:< T O U >>` is also equi-
   | lateral.
