And, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q L /">$QL$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q P /">$QP$</span> was proved<br />
to belong to a pentagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span> also belongs to a pentagon,
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q L P >">$QLP$</span> is equilateral.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
each of the triangles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L R M >">$LRM$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< M S N >">$MSN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< N T O >">$NTO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< O U P >">$OUP$</span> is also equi-<br />
lateral.</p>

Let the centre of the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>, the point <span data-ref="0485.g1:V">$V$</span>, be<br />
taken;<br />
from <span data-ref="0485.g1:V">$V$</span> let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V Z /">$VZ$</span> be set up at right angles to the plane of the<br />
circle,<br />
let it be produced in the other direction, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>,<br />
let there be cut off <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, the side of a hexagon, and each of<br />
the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>, being sides of a decagon,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q Z /">$QZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E V /">$EV$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L V /">$LV$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L X /">$LX$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X M /">$XM$</span> be joined.

Now, since each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q E /">$QE$</span> is at right<br />
angles to the plane of the circle,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q E /">$QE$</span>. <span class="justification">[XI. 6]</span></p>

But they are also equal;
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E V /">$EV$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span> are also equal and parallel. <span class="justification">[I. 33]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E V /">$EV$</span> belongs to a hexagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span> also belongs to a hexagon.</p>

And, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span> belongs to a hexagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> to a decagon,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q W Z )">$QWZ$</span> is right,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q Z /">$QZ$</span> belongs to a pentagon. <span class="justification">[XIII. 10]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> also belongs to a pentagon,<br />
inasmuch as, if we join <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V K /">$VK$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W U /">$WU$</span>, they will be equal and<br />
opposite, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V K /">$VK$</span>, being a radius, belongs to a hexagon; <span class="justification">[IV. 15, Por.]</span><br />
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W U /">$WU$</span> also belongs to a hexagon.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> belongs to a decagon,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< U W Z )">$UWZ$</span> is right;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> belongs to a pentagon. <span class="justification">[XIII. 10]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q U /">$QU$</span> also belongs to a pentagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Q U Z >">$QUZ$</span> is equilateral.</p>
