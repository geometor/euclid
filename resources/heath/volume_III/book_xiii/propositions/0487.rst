:title: Proposition 16

| And, since each of the straight lines :g:`$QL$ <0485.g1:/ Q L />`, :g:`$QP$ <0485.g1:/ Q P />` was proved
| to belong to a pentagon,
| and :g:`$LP$ <0485.g1:/ L P />` also belongs to a pentagon,

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$QLP$ <0485.g1:< Q L P >>` is equilateral.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | each of the triangles :g:`$LRM$ <0485.g1:< L R M >>`, :g:`$MSN$ <0485.g1:< M S N >>`, :g:`$NTO$ <0485.g1:< N T O >>`, :g:`$OUP$ <0485.g1:< O U P >>` is also equi-
   | lateral.

| Let the centre of the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`, the point :g:`$V$ <0485.g1:V>`, be
| taken;
| from :g:`$V$ <0485.g1:V>` let :g:`$VZ$ <0485.g1:/ V Z />` be set up at right angles to the plane of the
| circle,
| let it be produced in the other direction, as :g:`$VX$ <0485.g1:/ V X />`,
| let there be cut off :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />`, the side of a hexagon, and each of
| the straight lines :g:`$VX$ <0485.g1:/ V X />`, :g:`$WZ$ <0485.g1:/ W Z />`, being sides of a decagon,
| and let :g:`$QZ$ <0485.g1:/ Q Z />`, :g:`$QW$ <0485.g1:/ Q W />`, :g:`$UZ$ <0485.g1:/ U Z />`, :g:`$EV$ <0485.g1:/ E V />`, :g:`$LV$ <0485.g1:/ L V />`, :g:`$LX$ <0485.g1:/ L X />`, :g:`$XM$ <0485.g1:/ X M />` be joined.

| Now, since each of the straight lines :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />`, :g:`$QE$ <0485.g1:/ Q E />` is at right
| angles to the plane of the circle,

.. assertion:: [XI. 6] <XI.6>

   therefore :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />` is parallel to :g:`$QE$ <0485.g1:/ Q E />`.

But they are also equal;

.. assertion:: [I. 33] <I.33>

   therefore :g:`$EV$ <0485.g1:/ E V />`, :g:`$QW$ <0485.g1:/ Q W />` are also equal and parallel.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$EV$ <0485.g1:/ E V />` belongs to a hexagon;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$QW$ <0485.g1:/ Q W />` also belongs to a hexagon.

| And, since :g:`$QW$ <0485.g1:/ Q W />` belongs to a hexagon,
| and :g:`$WZ$ <0485.g1:/ W Z />` to a decagon,
| and the angle :g:`$QWZ$ <0485.g1:< Q W Z )>` is right,

.. assertion:: [XIII. 10] <XIII.10>

   therefore :g:`$QZ$ <0485.g1:/ Q Z />` belongs to a pentagon.

.. subclause::

   | For the same reason
   | :g:`$UZ$ <0485.g1:/ U Z />` also belongs to a pentagon,
   | inasmuch as, if we join :g:`$VK$ <0485.g1:/ V K />`, :g:`$WU$ <0485.g1:/ W U />`, they will be equal and
   | opposite, and :g:`$VK$ <0485.g1:/ V K />`, being a radius, belongs to a hexagon; :just:`[IV. 15, Por.]`
   | therefore :g:`$WU$ <0485.g1:/ W U />` also belongs to a hexagon.

.. subclause::

   | But :g:`$WZ$ <0485.g1:/ W Z />` belongs to a decagon,
   | and the angle :g:`$UWZ$ <0485.g1:< U W Z )>` is right;

.. assertion:: [XIII. 10] <XIII.10>

   therefore :g:`$UZ$ <0485.g1:/ U Z />` belongs to a pentagon.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`$QU$ <0485.g1:/ Q U />` also belongs to a pentagon;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`$QUZ$ <0485.g1:< Q U Z >>` is equilateral.
