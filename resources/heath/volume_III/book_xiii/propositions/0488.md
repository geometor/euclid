For the same reason
each of the remaining triangles of which the straight lines <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span> are the bases, and the point <span data-ref="0485.g1:Z">$Z$</span> the vertex, is also equilateral.

Again, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V L /">$VL$</span> belongs to a hexagon,
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span> to a decagon,
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L V X )">$LVX$</span> is right,
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L X /">$LX$</span> belongs to a pentagon. [XIII. 10]

For the same reason,
if we join <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M V /">$MV$</span>, which belongs to a hexagon,
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M X /">$MX$</span> is also inferred to belong to a pentagon.

But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> also belongs to a pentagon;
therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L M X >">$LMX$</span> is equilateral.

Similarly it can be proved that each of the remaining triangles of which <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ O P /">$OP$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P L /">$PL$</span> are the bases, and the point <span data-ref="0485.g1:X">$X$</span> the vertex, is also equilateral.

Therefore an icosahedron has been constructed which is contained by twenty equilateral triangles.

It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere, and to prove that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.

For, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> belongs to a hexagon,
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> to a decagon,
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V Z /">$VZ$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0485.g1:W">$W$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> is its greater segment; [XIII. 9]
therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V E /">$VE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V E /">$VE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E V /">$EV$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>.

And the angles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Z V E )">$ZVE$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< E V X )">$EVX$</span> are right;
therefore, if we join the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Z /">$EZ$</span>, the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< X E Z )">$XEZ$</span> will be right because of the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< X E Z >">$XEZ$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< V E Z >">$VEZ$</span>.

For the same reason,
since, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X W /">$XW$</span>, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Q /">$WQ$</span>,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X W /">$XW$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Q /">$WQ$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>.
