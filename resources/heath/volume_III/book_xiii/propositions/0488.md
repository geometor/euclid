<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason<br />
each of the remaining triangles of which the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q R /">$QR$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ S T /">$ST$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ T U /">$TU$</span> are the bases, and the point <span data-ref="0485.g1:Z">$Z$</span> the vertex,<br />
is also equilateral.</p>

Again, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V L /">$VL$</span> belongs to a hexagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span> to a decagon,<br />
and the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L V X )">$LVX$</span> is right,
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L X /">$LX$</span> belongs to a pentagon. <span class="justification">[XIII. 10]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason,<br />
if we join <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M V /">$MV$</span>, which belongs to a hexagon,<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M X /">$MX$</span> is also inferred to belong to a pentagon.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> also belongs to a pentagon;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the triangle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< L M X >">$LMX$</span> is equilateral.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly it can be proved that each of the remaining<br />
triangles of which <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ O P /">$OP$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ P L /">$PL$</span> are the bases, and the<br />
point <span data-ref="0485.g1:X">$X$</span> the vertex, is also equilateral.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore an icosahedron has been constructed which is<br />
contained by twenty equilateral triangles.</p>

It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere,<br />
and to prove that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational<br />
straight line called minor.

For, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> belongs to a hexagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> to a decagon,<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V Z /">$VZ$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0485.g1:W">$W$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> is its greater segment; <span class="justification">[XIII. 9]</span>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V E /">$VE$</span>, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V E /">$VE$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E V /">$EV$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V X /">$VX$</span>.</p>

And the angles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< Z V E )">$ZVE$</span>, <span data-ref="0485.g1:< E V X )">$EVX$</span> are right;<br />
therefore, if we join the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ E Z /">$EZ$</span>, the angle <span data-ref="0485.g1:< X E Z )">$XEZ$</span><br />
will be right because of the similarity of the triangles <span data-ref="0485.g1:< X E Z >">$XEZ$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:< V E Z >">$VEZ$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">For the same reason,<br />
since, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z V /">$ZV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X W /">$XW$</span>, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Q /">$WQ$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X W /">$XW$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Q /">$WQ$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q W /">$QW$</span> to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W Z /">$WZ$</span>.</p>
