<p class="sub-clause">And for this reason again,<br />
if we join <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Q X /">$QX$</span>, the angle at <span data-ref="0485.g1:Q">$Q$</span> will be right; <span class="justification">[VI. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the semicircle described on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span> will also pass<br />
through <span data-ref="0485.g1:Q">$Q$</span>. <span class="justification">[III. 31]</span></p>

And if, <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span> remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried<br />
round and restored to the same position from which it began<br />
to be moved, it will also pass through <span data-ref="0485.g1:Q">$Q$</span> and the remaining<br />
angular points of the icosahedron,<br />
and the icosahedron will have been comprehended in a<br />
sphere.

<p class="assertion">I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.</p>

For let <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0485.g1:A'">$A'$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since the straight line <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V Z /">$VZ$</span> has been cut in extreme<br />
and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0485.g1:W">$W$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z W /">$ZW$</span> is its lesser segment,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z W /">$ZW$</span> added to the half of the greater<br />
segment, that is <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W A' /">$WA'$</span>, is five times the square on the half<br />
of the greater segment; <span class="justification">[XIII. 3]</span><br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z A' /">$ZA'$</span> is five times the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A' W /">$A'W$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z X /">$ZX$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z A' /">$ZA'$</span>, and <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span> double of <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A' W /">$A'W$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z X /">$ZX$</span> is five times the square on<br />
<span data-ref="0485.g1:/ W V /">$WV$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is quadruple of <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is five times <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 8, V. Def. 9]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ B D /">$BD$</span>.</p>

But the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ Z X /">$ZX$</span> was also proved to be five times<br />
the square on <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ V W /">$VW$</span>,<br />
for each of them is equal to the radius of the circle <span data-ref="0485.g1:( E F G H K )">$EFGHK$</span>;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the given sphere;</p>

<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0485.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span> is also equal to the diameter of the given sphere.</p>

Therefore the icosahedron has been comprehended in the<br />
given sphere
