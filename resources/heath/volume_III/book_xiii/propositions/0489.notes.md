- **Mathematical Argument**:
  - Since $VZ$ is divided in extreme and mean ratio at $W$ with lesser segment $ZW$, and $VW$ is bisected at $A'$, XIII. 3 gives $ZA'^2 = 5 A'W^2$.
  - Doubling gives $ZX^2 = 5 VW^2$.
  - From $AC = 4 CB$, $AB = 5 BC$.
  - By VI. 8 and V. Def. 9, $AB : BC = AB^2 : BD^2$, hence $AB^2 = 5 BD^2$.
  - Since $DB = VW$ (both equal the radius of circle $EFGHK$), $AB = XZ$, proving the circumscribing sphere equals the given sphere.
