.. subclause::

   | And for this reason again,
   | if we join :g:`$QX$ <0485.g1:/ Q X />`, the angle at :g:`$Q$ <0485.g1:Q>` will be right; :just:`[VI. 8] <VI.8>`

.. assertion::

   | therefore the semicircle described on :g:`$XZ$ <0485.g1:/ X Z />` will also pass
   | through :g:`$Q$ <0485.g1:Q>`. :just:`[III. 31] <III.31>`

| And if, :g:`$XZ$ <0485.g1:/ X Z />` remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried
| round and restored to the same position from which it began
| to be moved, it will also pass through :g:`$Q$ <0485.g1:Q>` and the remaining
| angular points of the icosahedron,
| and the icosahedron will have been comprehended in a
| sphere.

.. assertion::

   I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.

For let :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />` be bisected at :g:`$A'$ <0485.g1:A'>`.

.. subclause::

   | Then, since the straight line :g:`$VZ$ <0485.g1:/ V Z />` has been cut in extreme
   | and mean ratio at :g:`$W$ <0485.g1:W>`,
   | and :g:`$ZW$ <0485.g1:/ Z W />` is its lesser segment,

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$ZW$ <0485.g1:/ Z W />` added to the half of the greater
   | segment, that is :g:`$WA'$ <0485.g1:/ W A' />`, is five times the square on the half
   | of the greater segment; :just:`[XIII. 3] <XIII.3>`
   | therefore the square on :g:`$ZA'$ <0485.g1:/ Z A' />` is five times the square on
   | :g:`$A'W$ <0485.g1:/ A' W />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$ZX$ <0485.g1:/ Z X />` is double of :g:`$ZA'$ <0485.g1:/ Z A' />`, and :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />` double of :g:`$A'W$ <0485.g1:/ A' W />`;

.. assertion::

   | therefore the square on :g:`$ZX$ <0485.g1:/ Z X />` is five times the square on
   | :g:`$WV$ <0485.g1:/ W V />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`$AC$ <0485.g1:/ A C />` is quadruple of :g:`$CB$ <0485.g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` is five times :g:`$BC$ <0485.g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   | But, as :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`$BC$ <0485.g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` to the square
   | on :g:`$BD$ <0485.g1:/ B D />`; :just:`[VI. 8, V. Def. 9] <VI.8, V.def.9>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` is five times the square on :g:`$BD$ <0485.g1:/ B D />`.

| But the square on :g:`$ZX$ <0485.g1:/ Z X />` was also proved to be five times
| the square on :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />`.

.. subclause::

   | And :g:`$DB$ <0485.g1:/ D B />` is equal to :g:`$VW$ <0485.g1:/ V W />`,
   | for each of them is equal to the radius of the circle :g:`$EFGHK$ <0485.g1:( E F G H K )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` is also equal to :g:`$XZ$ <0485.g1:/ X Z />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`$AB$ <0485.g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the given sphere;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`$XZ$ <0485.g1:/ X Z />` is also equal to the diameter of the given sphere.

| Therefore the icosahedron has been comprehended in the
| given sphere
