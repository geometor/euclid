- **Porism**: Explicitly establishes:
  1. $(\text{diameter of sphere})^2 = 5 \times (\text{radius of circle } EFGHK)^2$.

- **Heath's Analytical Overview**: Summarizes Euclid's strategy into seven distinct logical phases:
  1. Base pentagons in parallel circular sections.
  2. Poles of the two sections.
  3. Equilateral triangular faces connecting the two pentagons (10 triangles).
  4. Equilateral triangular caps connecting poles to pentagons ($2 \times 5 = 10$ triangles).
  5. Concyclicity/spherical placement of vertices.
  6. Equality of circumscribing sphere with the given sphere.
  7. Classification of the edge as *minor* (irrational).
