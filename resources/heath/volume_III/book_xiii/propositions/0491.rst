:title: Proposition 16

:g:`$BD$ <0491.g1:/ B D />` *is the radius of the circular sections containing the pentagons.*

| [If $r$ is the radius of the sphere,
| since
| :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $:$ :g:`$BC$ <0491.g1:/ B C />` $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $^2 : $ :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $\cdot$ :g:`$BC$ <0491.g1:/ B C />`
| $= $ :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $^2 : $ :g:`$BD$ <0491.g1:/ B D />` $^2$,
| while
| :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $= 5$ :g:`$BC$ <0491.g1:/ B C />`,
| it follows that
| :g:`$AB$ <0491.g1:/ A B />` $^2 = 5$ :g:`$BD$ <0491.g1:/ B D />` $^2$,
| or

$$\text{(radius of section)}^2 = \frac{4}{5} r^2.$$

Thus :just:`[XIII. 10, note]` $\text{(side of pentagon)}^2 = \frac{r^2}{5} (10 - 2\sqrt{5})$.]
