Now <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> will be parallel to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> (in the pentagon <span data-ref="0494.g1:< A C F G E >">$ACFGE$</span>) and to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span><br />
(in the pentagon <span data-ref="0494.g1:< A K N H C >">$AKNHC$</span>), so that <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> are also parallel;<br />
and further <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> (in the pentagon <span data-ref="0494.g1:< L K D H M >">$LKDHM$</span>).

![Proposition 16, Pappus' Construction](0494.g1.svg)

Similarly <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E D /">$ED$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ G H /">$GH$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span> are all parallel ;<br />
and likewise <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ F D /">$FD$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span> are all parallel.

Since <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is equal and parallel to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ L N /">$LN$</span>, and <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ B A /">$BA$</span> to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ M N /">$MN$</span>, the circles <span data-ref="0494.g1:( A B C )">$ABC$</span>,<br />
<span data-ref="0494.g1:( L M N )">$LMN$</span> are equal and parallel.

Similarly the circles <span data-ref="0494.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:( K G H )">$KGH$</span> are equal and parallel; for the triangles<br />
inscribed in them are equal (since each of the sides in both is the chord<br />
subtending an angle of equal pentagons), and their sides are parallel re-<br />
spectively.

Now in the equal and parallel circles <span data-ref="0494.g1:( D E F )">$DEF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:( K G H )">$KGH$</span> the chords <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ K H /">$KH$</span><br />
are equal and parallel, and on opposite sides of the centres;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is a diameter of the sphere <span class="justification">[Pappus' lemma, pp. 136—8]</span>, and the<br />
angle <span data-ref="0494.g1:< F E K )">$FEK$</span> is right <span class="justification">[Pappus' lemma, p. 138, 20—26]</span>.

[The diameter <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span> is not actually drawn in the figure.]

In the pentagon <span data-ref="0494.g1:< G E A C F >">$GEACF$</span>, if <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> be divided in extreme and mean ratio,<br />
the greater segment is equal to <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>. <span class="justification">[Eucl. XIII. 8]</span>

Therefore <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span> $:$ <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> = (side of hexagon) : (side of decagon in same circle).<br />
<span class="justification">[XIII. 9]</span>

And <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>$^2 + $ <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>$^2 = $ <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>$^2 + $ <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E K /">$EK$</span>$^2 = d^2$,<br />
where $d$ is the diameter of the sphere.

Thus <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> are as the sides of the pentagon, hexagon and decagon<br />
respectively inscribed in the same circle. <span class="justification">[XIII. 10]</span>

But <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ F K /">$FK$</span>, the diameter of the sphere, is given;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> are given respectively;<br />
thus the radii of the circles <span data-ref="0494.g1:( E F D )">$EFD$</span>, <span data-ref="0494.g1:( A C B )">$ACB$</span> are given (if $r, r'$ are their radii,<br />
$r^2 = \frac{1}{3}$<span data-ref="0494.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>$^2$, $r'^2 = \frac{1}{3}$<span data-ref="0494.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>$^2$).
