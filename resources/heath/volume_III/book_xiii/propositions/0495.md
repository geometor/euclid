*Synthesis.*<br />

Draw (1) two equal and parallel circular sections in the sphere, with radii<br />

**Another construction.**

Mr H. M. Taylor has a neat construction for an icosahedron of edge $a$.<br />
Let $l$ be the length of the diagonal of a regular pentagon with side equal<br />
to $a$.

Then (figure of XIII. 8), by Ptolemy’s theorem,<br />
$l^2 = la + a^2$.

Construct a cube with edge equal to $l$.<br />
Let <span data-ref="0496.g1:O">$O$</span> be the centre of the cube.

From <span data-ref="0496.g1:O">$O$</span> draw <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ O L /">$OL$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ O M /">$OM$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ O N /">$ON$</span> perpendicular to three adjacent faces, and in<br />
these draw <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ P P' /">$PP'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ Q Q' /">$QQ'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ R R' /">$RR'$</span> parallel to <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ A E /">$AE$</span> respectively.

Make <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ L P /">$LP$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ L P' /">$LP'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ M Q /">$MQ$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ M Q' /">$MQ'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:/ N R' /">$NR'$</span> all equal to $\frac{1}{2}a$.

Let <span data-ref="0496.g1:p">$\bar{p}$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:p'">$\bar{p}'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:q">$\bar{q}$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:q'">$\bar{q}'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:r">$\bar{r}$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:r'">$\bar{r}'$</span> be the reflexes of <span data-ref="0496.g1:P">$P$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:P'">$P'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:Q">$Q$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:Q'">$Q'$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0496.g1:R'">$R'$</span> respectively.
