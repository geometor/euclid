**Another construction.**

| Mr H. M. Taylor has a neat construction for an icosahedron of edge $a$.
| Let $l$ be the length of the diagonal of a regular pentagon with side equal
| to $a$.

| Then (figure of XIII. 8), by Ptolemy’s theorem,
| $l^2 = la + a^2$.

| Construct a cube with edge equal to $l$.
| Let :g:`$O$ <0496.g1:O>` be the centre of the cube.

| From :g:`$O$ <0496.g1:O>` draw :g:`$OL$ <0496.g1:/ O L />`, :g:`$OM$ <0496.g1:/ O M />`, :g:`$ON$ <0496.g1:/ O N />` perpendicular to three adjacent faces, and in
| these draw :g:`$PP'$ <0496.g1:/ P P' />`, :g:`$QQ'$ <0496.g1:/ Q Q' />`, :g:`$RR'$ <0496.g1:/ R R' />` parallel to :g:`$AB$ <0496.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AD$ <0496.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$AE$ <0496.g1:/ A E />` respectively.

Make :g:`$LP$ <0496.g1:/ L P />`, :g:`$LP'$ <0496.g1:/ L P' />`, :g:`$MQ$ <0496.g1:/ M Q />`, :g:`$MQ'$ <0496.g1:/ M Q' />`, :g:`$NR$ <0496.g1:/ N R />`, :g:`$NR'$ <0496.g1:/ N R' />` all equal to $\frac{1}{2}a$.

Let :g:`$\bar{p}$ <0496.g1:p>`, :g:`$\bar{p}'$ <0496.g1:p'>`, :g:`$\bar{q}$ <0496.g1:q>`, :g:`$\bar{q}'$ <0496.g1:q'>`, :g:`$\bar{r}$ <0496.g1:r>`, :g:`$\bar{r}'$ <0496.g1:r'>` be the reflexes of :g:`$P$ <0496.g1:P>`, :g:`$P'$ <0496.g1:P'>`, :g:`$Q$ <0496.g1:Q>`, :g:`$Q'$ <0496.g1:Q'>`, :g:`$R$ <0496.g1:R>`, :g:`$R'$ <0496.g1:R'>` respectively.

| Then will :g:`$P$ <0496.g1:P>`, :g:`$P'$ <0496.g1:P'>`, :g:`$Q$ <0496.g1:Q>`, :g:`$Q'$ <0496.g1:Q'>`, :g:`$R$ <0496.g1:R>`, :g:`$R'$ <0496.g1:R'>`, :g:`$\bar{p}$ <0496.g1:p>`, :g:`$\bar{p}'$ <0496.g1:p'>`, :g:`$\bar{q}$ <0496.g1:q>`, :g:`$\bar{q}'$ <0496.g1:q'>`, :g:`$\bar{r}$ <0496.g1:r>`, :g:`$\bar{r}'$ <0496.g1:r'>` be the vertices of a regular
| icosahedron.

| The projections of :g:`$PQ$ <0496.g1:/ P Q />` on :g:`$AB$ <0496.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`$AD$ <0496.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`$AE$ <0496.g1:/ A E />` are equal to $\frac{1}{2}(l - a), \frac{1}{2}a, \frac{1}{2}l$
| respectively.

| Therefore :g:`$PQ$ <0496.g1:/ P Q />` $^2 = \frac{1}{4}(l - a)^2 + \frac{1}{4}a^2 + \frac{1}{4}l^2$
| = $\frac{1}{2}(l^2 - al + a^2)$
| = $a^2$.
