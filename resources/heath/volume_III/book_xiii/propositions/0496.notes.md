## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Algebraic Derivations**: Heath calculates the edge length $a$ of the regular icosahedron in terms of the circumscribing sphere's radius $r$:
  - Edge relationship: $l = \frac{a}{2}(\sqrt{5} + 1)$, where $l$ is the edge of the auxiliary circumscribing cube.
  - Sphere radius relation: $OP^2 = \frac{1}{4}(a^2 + l^2)$, yielding $a = \frac{r}{5}\sqrt{10(5 - \sqrt{5})}$.
- **Solid Angle Analysis**: Heath notes that each pentahedral solid angle consists of five equal plane angles of equilateral triangles, proving regularity.
