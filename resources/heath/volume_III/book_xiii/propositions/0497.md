### PROPOSITION 17.

*To construct a dodecahedron and comprehend it in a sphere, like the aforesaid figures, and to prove that the side of the dodecahedron is the irrational straight line called apotome.*

Let <span data-ref="0497.g1:< A B C D >">$ABCD$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:< C B E F >">$CBEF$</span>, two planes of the aforesaid cube at right angles to one another, be set out,
let the sides <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ D A /">$DA$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ E F /">$EF$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ E B /">$EB$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ F C /">$FC$</span> be bisected at <span data-ref="0497.g1:G">$G$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:H">$H$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:K">$K$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:L">$L$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:M">$M$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:N">$N$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:O">$O$</span> respectively,
let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ G K /">$GK$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H L /">$HL$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ M H /">$MH$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> be joined,
let the straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P O /">$PO$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the points <span data-ref="0497.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:S">$S$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:T">$T$</span> respectively,
and let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T Q /">$TQ$</span> be their greater segments;
from the points <span data-ref="0497.g1:R">$R$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:S">$S$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:T">$T$</span> let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span> be set up at right angles to the planes of the cube towards the outside of the cube,
let them be made equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T Q /">$TQ$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U B /">$UB$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B W /">$BW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W C /">$WC$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ C V /">$CV$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V U /">$VU$</span> be joined.

I say that the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< U B W C V >">$UBWCV$</span> is equilateral, and in one plane, and is further equiangular.
For let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R B /">$RB$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S B /">$SB$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V B /">$VB$</span> be joined.
