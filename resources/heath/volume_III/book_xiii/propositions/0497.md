### PROPOSITION 17.

*To construct a dodecahedron and comprehend it in a sphere, like the aforesaid figures, and to prove that the side of the dodecahedron is the irrational straight line called apotome.*

Let $ABCD$, $CBEF$, two planes of the aforesaid cube at right angles to one another, be set out,
let the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$, $EF$, $EB$, $FC$ be bisected at $G$, $H$, $K$, $L$, $M$, $N$, $O$ respectively,
let $GK$, $HL$, $MH$, $NO$ be joined,
let the straight lines $NP$, $PO$, $HQ$ be cut in extreme and mean ratio at the points $R$, $S$, $T$ respectively,
and let $RP$, $PS$, $TQ$ be their greater segments;
from the points $R$, $S$, $T$ let $RU$, $SV$, $TW$ be set up at right angles to the planes of the cube towards the outside of the cube,
let them be made equal to $RP$, $PS$, $TQ$,
and let $UB$, $BW$, $WC$, $CV$, $VU$ be joined.
