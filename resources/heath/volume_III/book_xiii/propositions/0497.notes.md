- **Page & Book**: Volume III, Book XIII, Page 493.

- **Antecedent**: Follows Folio 0496 (Page 492), the conclusion of Proposition 16.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Enunciation**: Construction of the regular dodecahedron inscribed in a sphere and demonstration that its edge is an irrational straight line called *apotome* (ἀποτομή).
- **Geometric Method**: Euclid uses two mutually perpendicular square faces of a cube ($ABCD$ and $CBEF$). Their midline segments are divided in extreme and mean ratio (*golden section*), and perpendicular roof-segments ($RU, SV, TW$) are erected outwards to create the pentagon vertices $U, V, W$.
