Then, since the straight line <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0497.g1:R">$R$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span> is the greater segment,
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span> are triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>. [XIII. 4]

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span> are triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span>; [I. 47]
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;
hence the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span> are quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V U /">$VU$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U R /">$UR$</span>,
inasmuch as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S R /">$SR$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>.

Similarly it can be proved that each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B W /">$BW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W C /">$WC$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ C V /">$CV$</span> is also equal to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>.

Therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V C W >">$BUVCW$</span> is equilateral.

I say next that it is also in one plane.

For let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0497.g1:P">$P$</span> parallel to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span> and towards the outside of the cube,
and let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X H /">$XH$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H W /">$HW$</span> be joined;
I say that <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X H W /">$XHW$</span> is a straight line.

For, since <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0497.g1:T">$T$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> is its greater segment,
therefore, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> to each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>;
therefore, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W T /">$WT$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span>.

And <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span>,
for each of them is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>; [XI. 6]
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>,
for each of them is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>. [id.]
