<p class="sub-clause">Then, since the straight line <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> has been cut in extreme<br />
and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0497.g1:R">$R$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span> is the greater segment,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span> are triple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>. <span class="justification">[XIII. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P N /">$PN$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span> are triple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span>;<br />
<span class="justification">[I. 47]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;<br />
hence the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span> are quadruple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V U /">$VU$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U R /">$UR$</span>,<br />
inasmuch as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S R /">$SR$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span>, that is, of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly it can be proved that each of the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B W /">$BW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W C /">$WC$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ C V /">$CV$</span> is also equal to each of the straight lines<br />
<span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V C W >">$BUVCW$</span> is equilateral.</p>

I say next that it is also in one plane.

For let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span> be drawn from <span data-ref="0497.g1:P">$P$</span> parallel to each of the<br />
straight lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span> and towards the outside of the cube,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X H /">$XH$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H W /">$HW$</span> be joined;<br />
I say that <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X H W /">$XHW$</span> is a straight line.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at<br />
<span data-ref="0497.g1:T">$T$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> is its greater segment,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H Q /">$HQ$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Q T /">$QT$</span> to each of the straight<br />
lines <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W T /">$WT$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H P /">$HP$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T W /">$TW$</span>,<br />
for each of them is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B D >">$BD$</span>; <span class="justification">[XI. 6]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ T H /">$TH$</span> is parallel to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P X /">$PX$</span>,<br />
for each of them is at right angles to the plane <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B F >">$BF$</span>. <span class="justification">[id.]</span></p>
