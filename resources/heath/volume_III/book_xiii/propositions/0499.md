<p class="sub-clause">But if two triangles, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:< X P H >">$XPH$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:< H T W >">$HTW$</span>, which have two<br />
sides proportional to two sides be placed together at one<br />
angle so that their corresponding sides are also parallel,<br />
the remaining straight lines will be in a straight line; <span class="justification">[VI. 32]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X H /">$XH$</span> is in a straight line with <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ H W /">$HW$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But every straight line is in one plane; <span class="justification">[XI. 1]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< U B W C V >">$UBWCV$</span> is in one plane.</p>

I say next that it is also equiangular.

<p class="sub-clause">For, since the straight line <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> has been cut in extreme<br />
and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0497.g1:R">$R$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> is the greater segment,<br />
while <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span> has also been cut in extreme and mean ratio<br />
at <span data-ref="0497.g1:P">$P$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is the greater segment; <span class="justification">[XIII. 5]</span><br />
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> are triple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>. <span class="justification">[XIII. 4]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span> are triple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>;<br />
hence the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V S /">$VS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S N /">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span> are quadruple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S B /">$SB$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S N /">$SN$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B S /">$BS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S V /">$SV$</span>, that is, the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span><br />
—for the angle <span data-ref="0497.g1:< V S B )">$VSB$</span> is right—is quadruple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N B /">$NB$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ V B /">$VB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B N /">$BN$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the two sides <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span> are equal to the two<br />
sides <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B W /">$BW$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ W C /">$WC$</span>,<br />
and the base <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B V /">$BV$</span> is equal to the base <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the angle <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V )">$BUV$</span> is equal to the angle <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B W C )">$BWC$</span>. <span class="justification">[I. 8]</span></p>

<p class="assertion">Similarly we can prove that the angle <span data-ref="0497.g1:< U V C )">$UVC$</span> is also equal<br />
to the angle <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B W C )">$BWC$</span>;<br />
therefore the three angles <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B W C )">$BWC$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V )">$BUV$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:< U V C )">$UVC$</span> are equal to<br />
one another.</p>
