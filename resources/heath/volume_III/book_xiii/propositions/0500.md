But if in an equilateral pentagon three angles are equal to one another, the pentagon will be equiangular, [XIII. 7]
therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V C W >">$BUVCW$</span> is equiangular.

And it was also proved equilateral;
therefore the pentagon <span data-ref="0497.g1:< B U V C W >">$BUVCW$</span> is equilateral and equiangular, and it is on one side <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ B C /">$BC$</span> of the cube.

Therefore, if we make the same construction in the case of each of the twelve sides of the cube,
a solid figure will have been constructed which is contained by twelve equilateral and equiangular pentagons, and which is called a dodecahedron.

It is then required to comprehend it in the given sphere, and to prove that the side of the dodecahedron is the irrational straight line called apotome.

For let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X P /">$XP$</span> be produced, and let the produced straight line be <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P Z /">$PZ$</span> meets the diameter of the cube, and they bisect one another,
for this has been proved in the last theorem but one of the eleventh book. [XI. 38]

Let them cut at <span data-ref="0497.g1:Z">$Z$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:Z">$Z$</span> is the centre of the sphere which comprehends the cube,
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Z P /">$ZP$</span> is half of the side of the cube.

Let <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> be joined.

Now, since the straight line <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at <span data-ref="0497.g1:P">$P$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is its greater segment,
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S P /">$SP$</span> are triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>. [XIII. 4]

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N S /">$NS$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X Z /">$XZ$</span>,
inasmuch as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P Z /">$PZ$</span>, and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X P /">$XP$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span>.

But further <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span> is also equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X U /">$XU$</span>,
since it is also equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>;
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Z X /">$ZX$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X U /">$XU$</span> are triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>.

But the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> is equal to the squares on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ Z X /">$ZX$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ X U /">$XU$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span>.
