But the square on the radius of the sphere which comprehends the cube is also triple of the square on the half of the side of the cube,
for it has previously been shown how to construct a cube and comprehend it in a sphere, and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube. [XIII. 15]

But, if whole is so related to whole, so is half to half also;
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is half of the side of the cube;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U Z /">$UZ$</span> is equal to the radius of the sphere which comprehends the cube.

And <span data-ref="0497.g1:Z">$Z$</span> is the centre of the sphere which comprehends the cube;
therefore the point <span data-ref="0497.g1:U">$U$</span> is on the surface of the sphere.

Similarly we can prove that each of the remaining angles of the dodecahedron is also on the surface of the sphere;
therefore the dodecahedron has been comprehended in the given sphere.

I say next that the side of the dodecahedron is the irrational straight line called apotome.

For since, when <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span> is the greater segment,
and, when <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P O /">$PO$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P S /">$PS$</span> is the greater segment,
therefore, when the whole <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is cut in extreme and mean ratio, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span> is the greater segment.

[Thus, since, as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N P /">$NP$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ P R /">$PR$</span> to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R N /">$RN$</span>,
the same is true of the doubles also,
for parts have the same ratio as their equimultiples; [v. 15]
therefore as <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span> to the sum of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S O /">$SO$</span>.

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span> is also greater than the sum of <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N R /">$NR$</span>, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ S O /">$SO$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> has been cut in extreme and mean ratio,
and <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span> is its greater segment.]

But <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ R S /">$RS$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>;
therefore, when <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ N O /">$NO$</span> is cut in extreme and mean ratio, <span data-ref="0497.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span> is the greater segment.
