## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Conclusion & Porism**:
  - The side of the cube is rational; therefore the greater segment of it cut in extreme and mean ratio ($UV$, the edge of the dodecahedron) is an apotome irrational line (`[XIII. 6]`).
  - *Porism*: When the edge of the cube is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the side of the dodecahedron.
- **Heath's Commentary**:
  - Highlights Euclid's unique reliance on Proposition VI. 32.
  - Connects Euclid's construction to modern crystal-formation methods (Henrici & Treutlein) and H. M. Taylor's formulation via Ptolemy's theorem ($l^2 = al + a^2$).
  - Outlines Euclid's 6-part proof roadmap ((1) equilateral, (2) planar, (3) equiangular, (4) vertex on sphere, (5) all vertices on sphere, (6) apotome edge).
