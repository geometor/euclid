(1) To prove that the pentagon <span data-ref="0503.g1:< B U V C W >">$BUVCW$</span> is equilateral.
We have
<span data-ref="0503.g1:/ B U /">$BU$</span>$^2 = $ <span data-ref="0503.g1:/ B R /">$BR$</span>$^2 + $ <span data-ref="0503.g1:/ R U /">$RU$</span>$^2$

$= 3$<span data-ref="0503.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>$^2 + $ <span data-ref="0503.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>$^2$ [XIII. 4]
$= 4$<span data-ref="0503.g1:/ R P /">$RP$</span>$^2$
$= $ <span data-ref="0503.g1:/ U V /">$UV$</span>$^2$.
