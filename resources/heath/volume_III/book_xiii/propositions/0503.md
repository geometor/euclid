(1) To prove that the pentagon $BUVCW$ is equilateral.
We have
$$\begin{aligned}
BU^2 &= BR^2 + RU^2 \\
&= (BN^2 + NR^2) + RP^2 \\
&= (PN^2 + NR^2) + RP^2 \\
&= 3RP^2 + RP^2 \qquad\qquad\text{[XIII. 4]} \\
&= 4RP^2 \\
&= UV^2.
\end{aligned}$$
Therefore
$$BU = UV.$$
