**Pappus' solution.**

| Here too :person:`Pappus <pappus>` (III. pp. 156—162) finds four circular sections of the
| sphere all parallel to one another and all passing through five of the vertices
| of the dodecahedron.

*Analysis.*

| Suppose (he says) the problem solved, and let the vertices of the
| dodecahedron be :g:`$A$ <0506.g1:A>`, :g:`$B$ <0506.g1:B>`, :g:`$C$ <0506.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0506.g1:D>`, :g:`$E$ <0506.g1:E>` ; :g:`$F$ <0506.g1:F>`, :g:`$G$ <0506.g1:G>`, :g:`$H$ <0506.g1:H>`, :g:`$K$ <0506.g1:K>`, :g:`$L$ <0506.g1:L>` ; :g:`$M$ <0506.g1:M>`, :g:`$N$ <0506.g1:N>`, :g:`$O$ <0506.g1:O>`, :g:`$P$ <0506.g1:P>`, :g:`$Q$ <0506.g1:Q>` ;
| :g:`$R$ <0506.g1:R>`, :g:`$S$ <0506.g1:S>`, :g:`$T$ <0506.g1:T>`, :g:`$U$ <0506.g1:U>`, :g:`$V$ <0506.g1:V>`.

| Then, as before, :g:`$ED$ <0506.g1:/ E D />` is parallel to :g:`$FL$ <0506.g1:/ F L />`, and :g:`$AE$ <0506.g1:/ A E />` to :g:`$FG$ <0506.g1:/ F G />` ; therefore the
| planes :g:`$ABCDE$ <0506.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$FGHKL$ <0506.g1:< F G H K L >>` are parallel.

| But, since :g:`$PA$ <0506.g1:/ P A />` is parallel to :g:`$BH$ <0506.g1:/ B H />`, and :g:`$BH$ <0506.g1:/ B H />` to :g:`$OC$ <0506.g1:/ O C />`, :g:`$PA$ <0506.g1:/ P A />` is parallel to :g:`$OC$ <0506.g1:/ O C />` ;
| and they are equal ; therefore :g:`$PO$ <0506.g1:/ P O />`, :g:`$AC$ <0506.g1:/ A C />` are parallel, so that :g:`$ST$ <0506.g1:/ S T />`, :g:`$ED$ <0506.g1:/ E D />` are also
| parallel.

| Similarly :g:`$RS$ <0506.g1:/ R S />`, :g:`$DC$ <0506.g1:/ D C />` are parallel, and likewise the pairs (:g:`$TU$ <0506.g1:/ T U />`, :g:`$EA$ <0506.g1:/ E A />`),
| (:g:`$UV$ <0506.g1:/ U V />`, :g:`$AB$ <0506.g1:/ A B />`), (:g:`$VR$ <0506.g1:/ V R />`, :g:`$BC$ <0506.g1:/ B C />`).

| Therefore the planes :g:`$ABCDE$ <0506.g1:< A B C D E >>`, :g:`$RSTUV$ <0506.g1:< R S T U V >>` are parallel ; and the circles
| :g:`$ABCDE$ <0506.g1:( A B C D E )>`, :g:`$RSTUV$ <0506.g1:( R S T U V )>` are equal, since the inscribed pentagons are equal.

| Similarly the circles :g:`$FGHKL$ <0506.g1:( F G H K L )>`, :g:`$MNOPQ$ <0506.g1:( M N O P Q )>` are equal, since the pentagons
| inscribed in them are equal.

| Now :g:`$CL$ <0506.g1:/ C L />`, :g:`$OU$ <0506.g1:/ O U />` are parallel because each is parallel to :g:`$KN$ <0506.g1:/ K N />` ;
| therefore :g:`$L$ <0506.g1:L>`, :g:`$C$ <0506.g1:C>`, :g:`$O$ <0506.g1:O>`, :g:`$U$ <0506.g1:U>` are in one plane.

| And :g:`$LC$ <0506.g1:/ L C />`, :g:`$CO$ <0506.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`$OU$ <0506.g1:/ O U />`, :g:`$UL$ <0506.g1:/ U L />` are all equal, since they subtend angles of equal
| pentagons.

| Also :g:`$L$ <0506.g1:L>`, :g:`$C$ <0506.g1:C>`, :g:`$O$ <0506.g1:O>`, :g:`$U$ <0506.g1:U>` are on a plane section, i.e. a circle ;
| therefore :g:`$LCOU$ <0506.g1:< L C O U >>` is a square.

| Therefore                 :g:`$OL$ <0506.g1:/ O L />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$LC$ <0506.g1:/ L C />` $^2 = 2$ :g:`$LF$ <0506.g1:/ L F />` $^2$
| (for :g:`$LC$ <0506.g1:/ L C />`, :g:`$LF$ <0506.g1:/ L F />` subtend angles of equal pentagons).
