# Transcription Notes: Folio 0506 (Page 502)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0506 (page 502) continues Heath's commentary and exposition of Pappus' alternative analysis and construction for inscribing a regular dodecahedron in a given sphere (following Proposition 17). It establishes that $OF$ is a diameter of the sphere, deduces proportional relations connecting the diameter $d$ and sides of the equilateral pentagon ($p$), triangle ($t$), and hexagon ($h$), introduces the decagon side ($d'$), and shows that the radii of the circular sections $ABCDE$ and $FGHKL$ are determined from the diameter of the sphere.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Scholarly Citations**: References Pappus' *Collection*, Book III (ed. Hultsch, pp. 136–138, 138 lines 20–26).
- **Proportional Arguments**: Uses classic Euclidean proportion operations: *alternando* and *ex aequali*.
- **Notation**: Employs $p, t, h$ for the sides of pentagon, triangle, and hexagon in circle $FGHKL$, $d$ for sphere diameter, and $d', p', h'$ for the corresponding figures in any reference circle.
