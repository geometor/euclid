# Transcription Notes: Folio 0507 (Page 503)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0507 (page 503) concludes the *Synthesis* of Pappus' dodecahedron construction and notes Pappus' remarkable observation regarding the common circumscribing circle of the icosahedral triangle and dodecahedral pentagon. The folio then opens **PROPOSITION 18**, the culminating proposition of the *Elements*, which sets out the sides of all five Platonic regular solids on a single diameter $AB$ and compares their metrics.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Proposition 18 Enunciation**: "To set out the sides of the five figures and to compare them with one another."
- **Initial Construction**: Diameter $AB$ is bisected at $C$ ($AC = CB$) and divided at $D$ in ratio $2:1$ ($AD = 2 DB$). Semicircle $AEB$ is described; perpendiculars $CE, DF$ meet the arc; chords $AF, FB, EB$ are joined.
- **Convertendo**: Deduces $BA = \frac{3}{2} AD$ from $AB = 3 BD$.
