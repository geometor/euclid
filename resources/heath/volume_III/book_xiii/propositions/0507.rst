PROPOSITION 18.
---------------

.. enunciation::

   To set out the sides of the five figures and to compare them with one another.

| Let :g:`$AB$ <0507.g1:/ A B />`, the diameter of the given sphere, be set out,
| and let it be cut at :g:`$C$ <0507.g1:C>` so that
| :g:`$AC$ <0507.g1:/ A C />` is equal to :g:`$CB$ <0507.g1:/ C B />`, and at :g:`$D$ <0507.g1:D>`
| so that :g:`$AD$ <0507.g1:/ A D />` is double of :g:`$DB$ <0507.g1:/ D B />`;
| let the semicircle :g:`$AEB$ <0507.g1:( A E B )>` be de-
| scribed on :g:`$AB$ <0507.g1:/ A B />`,
| from :g:`$C$ <0507.g1:C>`, :g:`$D$ <0507.g1:D>` let :g:`$CE$ <0507.g1:/ C E />`, :g:`$DF$ <0507.g1:/ D F />` be drawn
| at right angles to :g:`$AB$ <0507.g1:/ A B />`,
| and let :g:`$AF$ <0507.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`$FB$ <0507.g1:/ F B />`, :g:`$EB$ <0507.g1:/ E B />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   | Then, since :g:`$AD$ <0507.g1:/ A D />` is double
   | of :g:`$DB$ <0507.g1:/ D B />`,
   | therefore :g:`$AB$ <0507.g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`$BD$ <0507.g1:/ D B />`.

.. assertion::

   *Convertendo*, therefore, :g:`$BA$ <0507.g1:/ A B />` is one and a half times :g:`$AD$ <0507.g1:/ A D />`.
