<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> to the<br />
square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>, <span class="justification">[v. Def. 9, VI. 8]</span><br />
for the triangle <span data-ref="0507.g1:< A F B >">$AFB$</span> is equiangular with the triangle <span data-ref="0507.g1:< A F D >">$AFD$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$BA$</span> is one and a half times the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also one<br />
and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.<br />
<span class="justification">[XIII. 13]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A F /">$AF$</span> is equal to the side of the pyramid.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">Again, since <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is triple of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$BD$</span>.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$BD$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ F B /">$BF$</span>; <span class="justification">[VI. 8, v. Def. 9]</span></p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is triple of the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ F B /">$BF$</span>.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also triple<br />
of the square on the side of the cube. <span class="justification">[XIII. 15]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ F B /">$BF$</span> is the side of the cube.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span>.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But, as <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span>, so is the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> to the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span>.</p>
<p class="sub-clause">But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also double<br />
of the square on the side of the octahedron. <span class="justification">[XIII. 14]</span></p>
<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the given sphere;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ E B /">$BE$</span> is the side of the octahedron.</p>

Next, let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be drawn from the point <span data-ref="0507.g1:A">$A$</span> at right angles<br />
to the straight line <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A G /">$AG$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ G C /">$GC$</span> be joined,<br />
and from <span data-ref="0507.g1:H">$H$</span> let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> be drawn perpendicular to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>.

<p class="sub-clause">Then, since <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>,<br />
for <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and, as <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A G /">$GA$</span> is to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A C /">$AC$</span>, so is <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is also double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>.</p>
