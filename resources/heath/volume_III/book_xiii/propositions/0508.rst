.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`BA <0507.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`AD <0507.g1:/ A D />`, so is the square on :g:`BA <0507.g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`AF <0507.g1:/ A F />`, :just:`[v. Def. 9, VI. 8] <V.def.9, VI.8>` for the triangle :g:`AFB <0507.g1:< A F B >>` is equiangular with the triangle :g:`AFD <0507.g1:< A F D >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`BA <0507.g1:/ A B />` is one and a half times the square on :g:`AF <0507.g1:/ A F />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid. :just:`[XIII. 13] <XIII.13>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the sphere;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AF <0507.g1:/ A F />` is equal to the side of the pyramid.

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`AD <0507.g1:/ A D />` is double of :g:`DB <0507.g1:/ D B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`BD <0507.g1:/ D B />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`BD <0507.g1:/ D B />`, so is the square on :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`BF <0507.g1:/ F B />`; :just:`[VI. 8, v. Def. 9] <VI.8, V.def.9>`

   therefore the square on :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`BF <0507.g1:/ F B />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also triple of the square on the side of the cube. :just:`[XIII. 15] <XIII.15>`

.. subclause::

   And :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the sphere;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`BF <0507.g1:/ F B />` is the side of the cube.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`AC <0507.g1:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <0507.g1:/ A C / = 0507.g1:/ C B />` to :g:`CB <0507.g1:/ C B />`,

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`BC <0507.g1:/ C B />`, so is the square on :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`BE <0507.g1:/ E B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is double of the square on :g:`BE <0507.g1:/ E B />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also double of the square on the side of the octahedron. :just:`[XIII. 14] <XIII.14>`

   And :g:`AB <0507.g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the given sphere;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`BE <0507.g1:/ E B />` is the side of the octahedron.
