Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is quadruple of the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>;
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>, that is, the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span>, is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>.
But <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>.
And, since <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,
and, in them, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,
therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$BD$</span> is double of the remainder <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$DC$</span>.
Therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is triple of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is nine times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.
But the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>;
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;
therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.
Let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C L /">$CL$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,
from <span data-ref="0507.g1:L">$L$</span> let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> be drawn at right angles to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,
and let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span> be joined.
Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.
But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also five times the square on the radius of the circle from which the icosahedron has been described. [XIII. 16, Por.]
And <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere;
therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is the radius of the circle from which the icosahedron has been described;
therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is a side of the hexagon in the said circle. [IV. 15, Por.]
And, since the diameter of the sphere is made up of the side of the hexagon and two of the sides of the decagon inscribed in the same circle, [XIII. 16, Por.]
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere,
while <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is a side of the hexagon,
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span>,
therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is a side of the decagon inscribed in the circle from which the icosahedron has been described.
