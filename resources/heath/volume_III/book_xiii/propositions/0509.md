<p class="assertion">Therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span> is quadruple of the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>;<br />
therefore the squares on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H K /">$HK$</span>, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>, that is, the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span>,<br />
is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$KC$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ H C /">$HC$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$CB$</span>,<br />
and, in them, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A D /">$AD$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$DB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the remainder <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ D B /">$BD$</span> is double of the remainder <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$DC$</span>.</p>

<p class="assertion">Therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is triple of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;<br />
therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is nine times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is greater than the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span> is greater than <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C D /">$CD$</span>.</p>

Let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C L /">$CL$</span> be made equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,<br />
from <span data-ref="0507.g1:L">$L$</span> let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L M /">$LM$</span> be drawn at right angles to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span>,<br />
and let <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ M B /">$MB$</span> be joined.

<p class="sub-clause">Now, since the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span> is five times the square<br />
on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,<br />
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C B /">$BC$</span>, and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> double of <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ C K /">$CK$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is five times the square on <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span>.</p>

<p class="sub-clause">But the square on the diameter of the sphere is also five<br />
times the square on the radius of the circle from which the<br />
icosahedron has been described. <span class="justification">[XIII. 16, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere;</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is the radius of the circle from which the icosa-<br />
hedron has been described;<br />
therefore <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is a side of the hexagon in the said circle.<br />
<span class="justification">[IV. 15, Por.]</span></p>

<p class="sub-clause">And, since the diameter of the sphere is made up of the<br />
side of the hexagon and two of the sides of the decagon<br />
inscribed in the same circle, <span class="justification">[XIII. 16, Por.]</span><br />
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A B /">$AB$</span> is the diameter of the sphere,<br />
while <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ K L /">$KL$</span> is a side of the hexagon,<br />
and <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span> is equal to <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span>,</p>
<p class="assertion">therefore each of the straight lines <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ A K /">$AK$</span>, <span data-ref="0507.g1:/ L B /">$LB$</span> is a side of the<br />
decagon inscribed in the circle from which the icosahedron<br />
has been described.</p>
