## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0509 (page 505) continues Proposition 18, determining the side and circumscribing circle elements for the icosahedron:
1. Shows $HK^2 = 4 KC^2$, hence $HC^2 = 5 KC^2$. Since $HC = CB = r$, $BC^2 = 5 CK^2$.
2. Shows $BC = 3 CD$, hence $BC^2 = 9 CD^2$, which proves $CK > CD$.
3. Sets $CL = CK$ (making $KL = 2 CK$), and erects $LM \perp AB$.
4. Shows $AB^2 = 5 KL^2$, establishing that $KL$ is the radius of the circle circumscribing the icosahedron's pentagonal face, and hence the side of the inscribed regular hexagon.
5. Uses Book XIII Prop. 16 Porism to prove $AK = LB =$ side of the decagon in that circle.
