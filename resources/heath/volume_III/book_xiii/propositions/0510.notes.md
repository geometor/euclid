# Transcription Notes: Folio 0510 (Page 506)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0510 (page 506) continues Proposition 18:
1. Deduces that chord $MB$ is the side of the icosahedron via Book XIII Prop. 10 ($p^2 = h^2 + d_{10}^2$).
2. Divides cube edge $FB$ in extreme and mean ratio at $N$, showing $NB$ is the side of the dodecahedron (XIII. 17 Por.).
3. Compares the squares of the edges of the pyramid, octahedron, and cube, finding they are in the ratio $4 : 3 : 2$ and thus commensurable in square (rational ratios).
4. Classifies the edges of the icosahedron (minor irrational) and dodecahedron (apotome) as irrational.
5. Begins the proof that the edge of the icosahedron is greater than that of the dodecahedron ($MB > NB$).
