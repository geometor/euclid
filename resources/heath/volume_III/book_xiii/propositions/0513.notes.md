# Transcription Notes: Folio 0513 (Page 509)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0513 (page 509) concludes the Lemma proof showing the regular pentagon angle equals $1 \frac{1}{5}$ right angles ($108^\circ$), marked by **Q. E. D.** It then transitions into Heath's extensive commentary on Proposition 18. Heath tabulates the exact formulas for the edges of all five regular polyhedra inscribed in a sphere of radius $r$, and systematically provides modern algebraic and geometric verification of Euclid's constructions (1) tetrahedron, (2) cube, (3) octahedron, and starts (4) icosahedron.

## Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
- **Exact Expressions for Edges**:
  - Tetrahedron: $\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{6}r$
  - Octahedron: $\sqrt{2}r$
  - Cube: $\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{3}r$
  - Icosahedron: $\frac{r}{5}\sqrt{10(5-\sqrt{5})}$
  - Dodecahedron: $\frac{r}{3}(\sqrt{15}-\sqrt{3})$
- **Methodology**: Heath demonstrates how Euclid's geometric semicircle construction precisely yields these algebraic expressions without numerical coordinates.
