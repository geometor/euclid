# Transcription Notes: Folio 0515 (Page 511)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
Folio 0515 (page 511) continues Heath's commentary on Proposition 18:
1. Item (6): Establishes the canonical proportion between the squares of the sphere diameter ($2r$) and the edges of the tetrahedron ($t$), octahedron ($o$), and cube ($c$):
   $$(2r)^2 : t^2 : o^2 : c^2 = 6 : 4 : 3 : 2$$
   confirming that these edges are mutually commensurable in square (rational in Euclid's sense), whereas the edges of the icosahedron and dodecahedron are irrational.
2. Item (7): Summarizes Euclid's concluding proof establishing that the edge of the icosahedron is strictly greater than the edge of the dodecahedron in the same sphere ($MB > NB$).
