## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Hornlike Angle Controversies**: Exhaustive summary of the history of the angle of contact (*κερατοειδὴς γωνία*) through antiquity and the Renaissance (Democritus, Campanus, Cardano, Peletier, Clavius, Vieta, Galileo, Wallis).
- **Incommensurables & Irrationals**: Foundational entries distinguishing *incommensurable* (a natural mathematical property) from *irrational* (a conventional or postulatory property), side- and diagonal-numbers, sexagesimal approximations, and Apollonius' *unordered irrationals*.
- **Greek Vocabulary**:
  - *ἵππου πέδη* (hippopede)
  - *κερατοειδὴς γωνία* (hornlike angle)
  - *κλίσις* (inclination)
  - *ἄτομοι γραμμαί* (indivisible lines)
  - *ἀνάπαλιν* (inversely / inverse ratio)
  - *ἄρρητος λόγος* (irrational ratio)
