## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Key Entries**:
  - *Irrational*: Euclid's classification in Book X, quadratic and biquadratic equations, positive roots, compound irrationals.
  - *Isḥāq b. Ḥunain*: Translation of the *Elements*.
  - *Isosceles* (ἰσοσκελής): Even numbers, right-angled triangles, and IV. 10.
  - *Legendre, Adrien Marie*: Proving VI. 1 in two parts; proofs in Book XI; volumes of pyramids, cylinders, cones.
  - *Lemma*: Meaning, interpolated lemmas, Pappus lemmas, suspected lemmas in Books X, XII, XIII.
  - *Line*: Platonic and Aristotelian definitions; Apollonius, Heron, Geminus classifications; straight (εὐθεῖα), curved (καμπύλη), circular (περιφερής), spiral-shaped (ἑλικοειδής), bent (κεκαμμένη), broken (κεκλασμένη), round (τὸ στρογγύλον), composite (σύνθετος), incomposite (ἀσύνθετος), "forming a figure" (σχηματοποιοῦσα), determinate (ὡρισμένη), indeterminate (ἀόριστος), asymptotic (ἀσύμπτωτος), secant (συμπτωτός), homoeomeric.
