Transcription Notes: Folio 0543 (Page 539)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Page Context**: General Index, Page 539 (Letters N–P).
- **Continuity**: Continues from Page 538 with the conclusion of the *Number* entry ("Nicomachus and Iamblichus on...") and proceeds through *Perfect* (class of numbers), which continues onto Page 540.

Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

- **Key Entries**:

  - *Objection* (ἔνστασις): Technical term in Greek geometry and logic.
  - *Oblong*: Geometrical vs arithmetical meanings in Plato (προμήκης, ἑτερομήκης), Nicomachus, Theon of Smyrna, Iamblichus.
  - *Octahedron*: Definition and inscription in sphere by Euclid and Pappus.
  - *Odd (number)*: Definitions, Pythagorean definition, odd-times even, odd-times odd.
  - *Pappus*: Massive composite entry spanning both columns detailing his commentaries, Porisms, Treasury of Analysis, Arabic Book X, regular solids inscriptions, and geometric lemmas.
  - *Parallelepipedal* and *Parallelogram*: Definitions and terminology.
  - *Parallels*: Aristotle, Aganis, Geminus, Posidonius, Simplicius, equidistant and direction theories, Parallel Postulate.
  - *Peletarius*: Contact angle and semicircle angle.
  - *Pentagon* & *Pentagonal numbers*: Elementary triangles, pentagram.
