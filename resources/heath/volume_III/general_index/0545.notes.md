# Transcription Notes: Folio 0545 (Page 541)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: General Index, Page 541 (Letter P).
- **Continuity**: Continues from Page 540 with the detailed analysis of *Postulates* (1, 2, 4, 5) and proceeds through *Protarchus*.
- **Layout**: Two-column alphabetical index with volume and page citations.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Key Entries**:
  - *Postulate 5*: Exhaustive historical commentary on attempts to prove the Parallel Postulate (Ptolemy, Proclus, al-Ṭūsī, Wallis, Saccheri, Lambert) and equivalent axioms (Playfair, Legendre, Gauss, Bolyai, etc.).
  - *Prime (number)*: Definitions, status of 2, prime and incomposite (ἀσύνθετος), prime to one another.
  - *Problem*: Distinguished from theorem, classified into ordered (τεταγμένα), intermediate (μέσα), unordered (ἄτακτα), excess (πλεονάζον), and deficient (ἐλλιπὲς πρόβλημα).
  - *Proclus*: Detailed life, commentary on Book I, sources, summary, philosophical views.
  - *Proportion*: Eudoxus' general theory, Book V vs Book VII, Weierstrass and Dedekind connections, continuous (συνεχὴς / συνημμένη) vs discrete (διῃρημένη / διεζευγμένη).
