Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
---------------------------------------------

  - *Simson, Robert*: Monumental index entry documenting Simson's extensive commentary across all three volumes (textual restoration, Book V propositions, Book VI and XI additions).
  - *Solid* and *Solid angle*: Definitions, similar solids, solid loci, solid problems, solid numbers.
  - *Spiral* & *Spire*: Archimedean spiral, cylindrical helix in Pappus, spiric surfaces.
