# Transcription Notes: Folio 0550 (Page 546)

## Summary & Folio Continuity
- **Page Context**: General Index, Page 546 (Letters T–Z) — Final Folio and Page of Volume III.
- **Continuity**: Concludes the General Index from Page 545 (Speusippus... to Posidonius / Carpus under *Theorem and problem*), continuing through Z (*Zeuthen*), followed by the Cambridge University Press colophon.
- **Significance**: Marks the complete transcription conclusion of Sir Thomas L. Heath's three-volume edition of *The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements*.

## Textual / Lexical / Mathematical Observations
- **Key Entries**:
  - *Theorem and problem*: Distinction according to Speusippus, Amphinomus, Menaechmus, Zenodotus, Posidonius, Euclid, Carpus, Proclus, Geminus.
  - *Triangle*: Seven species, "four-sided" or barb-like (ἀκιδοειδές, κοιλογώνιον), Heron's area formula $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, Plato's *Timaeus*.
  - *Unit*: Definitions, Thymaridas, Aristotle, Euclid; etymology of μονάς from μόνος / μονή.
  - *Veronese, G.*: Foundations of geometry, motion without deformation.
  - *Woepcke, F.*: Discovery of Arabic texts of Euclid and Pappus.
  - *Zeuthen, H. G.*: Historical and mathematical commentary throughout all three volumes.
- **Colophon**: Centered at bottom: `CAMBRIDGE: PRINTED BY PHOTOGRAPHIC PROCESS FOR THE UNIVERSITY PRESS.`
