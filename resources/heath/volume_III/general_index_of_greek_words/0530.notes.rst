Transcription Notes: Folio 0530 (Page 526)
==========================================

Summary & Folio Continuity
--------------------------

- **Folio**: ``0530`` (Page 526, Volume III).
- **Position**: Concluding folio of the *General Index of Greek Words and Forms* in Heath's Euclid (Volume III, pp. 521-526). Followed in the printed volume by the *General Index* (English index of subjects and proper names).
- **Running Header**: ``526 GENERAL INDEX OF GREEK WORDS AND FORMS``.
- **Continuity**:

  - Column 1 concludes Sigma (**σχέσις**, **σχηματογραφεῖν**, **σχηματοποιοῦσα**) and covers Tau (**ταυτομήκης** through **τόπος**).
  - Column 2 completes Tau (**τόρνος** through **τυγχάνειν**), covers Upsilon (**ὑπερβολή** through **ὕψος**), Chi (**χωρίον**), and closes with Omega (**ὡρισμένη γραμμή**).

Textual, Lexical & Mathematical Observations
--------------------------------------------

- **Proportion & Ratio**:

  - **σχέσις** ("relation", in definition of ratio Book V Def. 3).
  - **τεταγμένη ἀναλογία** ("ordered" proportion) and **τεταραγμένη ἀναλογία** (*perturbed proportion*).
  - **τριπλάσιος** (triple) vs. **τριπλασίων** (triplicate ratio).

- **Geometric Loci and Analysis**:

  - **τοπικὸν θεώρημα** (locus-theorem).
  - **τόπος** (locus, space, room): includes Heath's references to Pappus' **τόπος ἀναλυόμενος** (*Treasury of Analysis*) and **παράδοξος τόπος** (*Treasury of Paradoxes*).

- **Geometric Construction and Figures**:

  - **σκυτοτομικὸς τομεύς** ("shoemaker's knife" / arbelos in Archimedes).
  - **τὸ τριπλοῦν, τὸ διʼ ἀλλήλων** (triple, interwoven triangle = Pythagorean pentagram).
  - **ὑπερβολή** (exceeding, in application of areas).
  - **ὑποκείμενον ἐπίπεδον** (plane of reference in stereometry).
  - **ὡρισμένη γραμμή** (determinate line/curve, forming a figure).

Graphic Calibration
-------------------

- Pure 2-column index folio; concludes the Greek index. Contains no diagrams or plates.
