Types / definition

Content of type 'definition'

Definition 1 [α΄ · Def. I]: Given in Magnitude

Spaces, lines, and angles are called given in magnitude to which we can procure equals.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 2 [β΄ · Def. II]: Given in Ratio

A ratio is said to be given to which we can procure an identical ratio.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 3 [γ΄ · Def. III]: Given in Species

Rectilineal figures are said to be given in species whose angles are severally given and the ratios of whose sides to one another are given.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 4 [δ΄ · Def. IV (cf. Def. A)]: Given in Position

Points, lines, and angles are said to be given in position which always occupy the same place.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 5 [ε΄ · Def. V]: Circle Given in Magnitude

A circle is said to be given in magnitude whose radius (straight line from the centre) is given in magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 6 [ϛ΄ · Def. VI]: Circle Given in Position & Magnitude

A circle is said to be given in position and in magnitude whose centre is given in position and whose radius is given in magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 7 [ζ΄ · Def. VII]: Segment Given in Magnitude

Segments of circles are said to be given in magnitude in which the inscribed angles are given and the bases of the segments are given in magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 8 [η΄ · Def. VIII]: Segment Given in Position & Magnitude

Segments of circles are said to be given in position and in magnitude in which the inscribed angles are given in magnitude and the bases of the segments are given in position and in magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 9 [θ΄ · Def. IX]: Affine Excess (δοθέντι μεῖζον)

A magnitude is greater than a magnitude by a given magnitude when, the given magnitude being subtracted, the remainder is equal to that same magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 10 [ι΄ · Def. X]: Affine Defect (δοθέντι ἔλασσον)

A magnitude is less than a magnitude by a given magnitude when, the given magnitude being added, the whole is equal to that same magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 11 [ια΄ · Omitted (expanded verbally in Props. XIV–XXVII)]: Affine-Ratio Excess (δοθέντι μεῖζον ἢ ἐν λόγῳ)

A magnitude is greater than a magnitude by a given magnitude than in ratio when, the given magnitude being subtracted, the remainder has a given ratio to that same magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 12 [ιβ΄ · Omitted (expanded verbally in Props. XIV–XXVII)]: Affine-Ratio Defect (δοθέντι ἔλασσον ἢ ἐν λόγῳ)

A magnitude is less than a magnitude by a given magnitude than in ratio when, the given magnitude being added, the whole has a given ratio to that same magnitude.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 13 [ιγ΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)]: Dropped Line / Katēgmenē [Scholium]

[A dropped line (katēgmenē) is the straight line drawn from a given point onto a straight line given in position in a given angle.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 14 [ιδ΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)]: Erected Line / Anēgmenē [Scholium]

An erected line (anēgmenē) is the straight line drawn from a given point upon a straight line given in position in a given angle.
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes

Definition 15 [ιε΄ · Omitted (late scholium; bracketed by Menge)]: Parallel to Position / Para Thesei [Scholium]

Parallel-to-position (para thesei) is the straight line drawn through a given point parallel to a straight line given in position.]
English · Greek (Menge 1896) · Summary · Notes
