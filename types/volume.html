Types / volume

Content of type 'volume'

Volume I: Introduction and Books I, II

Heath’s historical and manuscript introduction followed by Books I and II: elementary plane constructions, congruence, parallel lines, the Pythagorean theorem (I.47), geometric algebra, and division in extreme and mean ratio (II.11).

Volume II: Books III–IX

Books III through IX: geometry of the circle, inscribed and circumscribed regular polygons (pentagon, hexagon, 15-gon), Eudoxus’s general theory of proportion, similar figures, and Euclidean number theory (primes, GCD, and perfect numbers).

Volume III: Books X–XIII and Appendix

Books X through XIII and Appendix: Theaetetus’s classification of incommensurable quadratic irrational lines, solid geometry in space, the method of exhaustion for cones and spheres, and the construction of the five Platonic regular polyhedra.
